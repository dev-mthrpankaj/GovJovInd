How to Download Final Answer Key

  1. Visit the official Staff Selection Commission website at ssc.gov.in.
  2. Open the final answer key notice for Head Constable (Ministerial) in Delhi Police Examination, 2025.
  3. Use the link provided in the notice for final answer keys and question paper cum response sheets.
  4. Login with your Roll Number and Password.
  5. Download or print your final answer key and question paper cum response sheet before 06 July 2026, 6:00 PM.
  6. For marks, login on the SSC website with Registered ID and Password during the same availability window.
