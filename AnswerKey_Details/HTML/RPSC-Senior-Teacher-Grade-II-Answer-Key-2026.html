About This Answer Key

The Rajasthan Public Service Commission (RPSC) has officially published the provisional model answer key for the Senior Teacher Grade II (TGT) Competitive Examination 2025-26. Along with the answer key, RPSC has also made available the subject-wise Master Question Papers on its web portal. Candidates who appeared for the exam held between 12 July and 18 July 2026 can match their responses against the official master key to estimate their scores.

Since this answer key is provisional, RPSC provides an online objection module for candidates who identify discrepancies in any question or option. Candidates must cross-check their question paper against the official Master Question Paper published by the Commission, as the question numbering in the objection portal directly corresponds to the master paper, rather than individual set codes.

Valid objections accompanied by standard textbook references or authentic proofs submitted within the designated window (29 July to 31 July 2026) will be reviewed by expert committees. Based on their evaluation, RPSC will prepare and publish the Final Answer Key, which will form the basis for the declaration of results.

How to Download RPSC Senior Teacher Answer Key

  1. Visit the official portal of RPSC at rpsc.rajasthan.gov.in.
  2. Go to the Candidate Information menu and click on the Answer Keys section.
  3. Look for the link titled "Model Answer Key for Sr. Teacher (Sec. Edu. Deptt.) Comp. Exam - 2025".
  4. Select your respective subject (GK Group A, GK Group B, Hindi, Social Science, Science, Sanskrit, Mathematics, English, Urdu, etc.).
  5. Download the Answer Key PDF and also download the official Master Question Paper for the same subject.
  6. Match your answers carefully against the Master Question Paper for accuracy.

How to Raise Objection Against Answer Key

  1. Log in to the Rajasthan SSO portal at sso.rajasthan.gov.in using your credentials.
  2. Navigate to the Recruitment Portal option.
  3. Click on the Question Objection tab and select RPSC Senior Teacher Grade II Exam 2026.
  4. Select the specific Subject and Question Number as per RPSC's official Master Question Paper.
  5. Enter your proposed correct answer and upload authentic proof/documents from standard reference books.
  6. Pay the objection fee of INR 100 per question via e-Mitra or online payment gateway.
  7. Submit your representation before 31 July 2026 (12:00 Midnight).

Note: Objections submitted through offline mode, letter, or without fee payment will be rejected without any notice as per RPSC guidelines.

How to Calculate Expected Marks

Candidates can calculate their estimated scores by following the official RPSC marking scheme:

  • Paper I (General Knowledge): Consists of 100 questions carrying a total of 200 marks (2 marks per correct answer).
  • Paper II (Subject Specific): Consists of 150 questions carrying a total of 300 marks (2 marks per correct answer).
  • Negative Marking: 1/3rd of the marks assigned to that question (0.66 marks) will be deducted for each wrong answer.
  • Formula: Total Score = (Number of Correct Answers × 2) - (Number of Incorrect Answers × 0.66).
  • Check the official exam pattern in the recruitment notification for exact subject-wise paper weighting.
