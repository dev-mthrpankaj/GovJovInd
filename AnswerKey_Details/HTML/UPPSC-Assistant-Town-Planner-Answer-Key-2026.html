About This Answer Key

The Uttar Pradesh Public Service Commission has officially uploaded the answer keys for the Assistant Town Planner (Preliminary) Examination 2025, covering General Studies and Town & Country Planning. The questions along with their marked choices have been highlighted and securely underlined inside rectangular frames directly on the commission's official portal. Candidates who participated in the single-session examination can now evaluate their responses systematically.

This answer key publication allows students to perform a self-assessment by comparing their exam responses with the commission’s certified key. Since the current status is provisional, the commission welcomes genuine feedback or correction requests. If you discover an inconsistency or wrong option assignment, you can present an official representation backed by valid text references and documentary proof before the link is disabled.

Unlike contemporary computer-based exams, the challenge process here is strictly manual. Objections must match the structure of the master question paper booklet series barcode number 2000585. All documents need to be packed properly inside a closed envelope and reached within office hours to avoid summary rejection.

How to Download Answer Key

  1. Navigate to the official website of the commission at uppsc.up.nic.in.
  2. Look for the 'What's New' dashboard or notice board section on the landing page.
  3. Click on the specific text link corresponding to the Assistant Town Planner (Pre) Exam 2025 Answer Key.
  4. Open or download the scanned master booklet displaying rectangular highlights for correct answers.
  5. Match the provided solutions with your carbon copy or response sheet systematically.
  6. Save a local copy of the master PDF file on your computer for potential offline calculations.

How to Raise Objection

  1. Verify your doubtful questions precisely against the official master barcode sequence number 2000585.
  2. Draft separate applications for both distinct sections if needed: General Studies (Q. 1 to 50) and Town & Country Planning (Q. 51 to 150).
  3. Format the paper manually by typing out your Name, Roll Number, Exam Name, Subject Name, and a clear grid containing Question Number, Official Choice, Proposed Correction, and Evidence Details.
  4. Attach clear, legible, and authentic academic textbooks or reference materials as verification proof.
  5. Enclose the sheets carefully inside a secure envelope and address it explicitly to: "Controller of Examinations, Anugopan-5 Section, Uttar Pradesh Public Service Commission, Prayagraj - 211018".
  6. Deliver it personally at the commission's counter or send it through registered post before July 15, 2026, at 5:00 PM.

Note: Objections lacking proper academic evidence, illegible scripts, or matching arbitrary non-master booklet sequences will not be processed by the review committee.
