About This Answer Key

The UPSSSC Junior Assistant revised answer key is an important update for candidates who appeared in the Main Examination conducted on 1 February 2026. Earlier, the Commission had published the provisional answer key on 2 February 2026, and an objection link was uploaded on 3 February 2026 for candidates who wanted to challenge any answer.

After reviewing the objections received against the provisional answer key, the Commission has uploaded the revised master answer key for candidate reference. A revised key is usually more important than the first provisional key because it reflects the correction or confirmation process completed by the examination authority.

Candidates should use the revised key only along with official UPSSSC instructions. If any later notice is issued regarding final answer key, marks, result or document verification, candidates should follow the official website first.
