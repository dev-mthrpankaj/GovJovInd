About This Answer Key

The MPESB provisional answer key helps candidates check the answers recorded for the Van Rakshak, Kshetra Rakshak, Jail Prahari and Assistant Jail Superintendent Combined Recruitment Test 2026. By opening the candidate response sheet, a candidate can compare marked responses with the provisional key and understand where mistakes or doubts may exist.

This is a provisional stage, which means candidates get a limited chance to raise objections against doubtful questions or answers. Any objection should be filed only through the official online portal, with valid proof wherever required. Candidates should not rely on social media screenshots or unofficial PDFs for final action.

The objection window is important because the board reviews valid challenges before preparing the final answer key. After the deadline, late objections are usually not accepted, so candidates should save their response sheet and complete the challenge process well before 25 June 2026.

How to Download Answer Key

  1. Visit the official MPESB website at esb.mp.gov.in.
  2. Open the Question Paper and Candidate Response / Objection section.
  3. Select the Van Rakshak, Kshetra Rakshak, Jail Prahari and Assistant Jail Superintendent Combined Recruitment Test 2026 answer key link.
  4. Login with the details asked on the official portal.
  5. View your response sheet and provisional answer key carefully.
  6. Download or print the response sheet for personal analysis before the link closes.

How to Raise Objection

  1. Login through the official MPESB response sheet and objection portal.
  2. Open the objection/challenge option for the relevant question.
  3. Select the question or answer you want to challenge.
  4. Attach valid proof or reference if the portal asks for it.
  5. Check the objection fee and payment instructions shown on the official portal.
  6. Submit the objection before 25 June 2026 and save the confirmation for your record.

Candidates should follow only the official MPESB portal instructions for objection fee, document upload and final submission. Do not wait for the last hour.

How to Calculate Expected Marks

After downloading the response sheet, candidates can compare their selected answers with the provisional answer key. Mark the number of correct and incorrect responses separately and then apply the official exam marking scheme.

Check the official exam scheme before calculating expected marks. GovJobUpdates does not create unofficial cut-off or selection claims on the basis of answer key only.
