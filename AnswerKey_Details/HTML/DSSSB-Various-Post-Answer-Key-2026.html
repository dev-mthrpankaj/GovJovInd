About This Answer Key

The Delhi Subordinate Services Selection Board (DSSSB) has released the tentative answer key and response sheet for candidates who appeared in the 2026 recruitment examinations for various posts. This includes exams conducted between 21-24 July 2026, as well as several other dates for posts like Junior Engineer, Naib Tehsildar, Teacher, and TGT.

The response sheet helps candidates evaluate their performance by comparing their marked answers against the officially released provisional key. This transparency allows test-takers to calculate their expected marks and gauge their chances of qualifying.

Candidates can also utilize the objection/challenge window if they spot any discrepancies in the provisional key. Verifying the deadlines and objection rules strictly through the official DSSSB portal is highly necessary, as late or unsupported claims are usually rejected.

How to Download Answer Key / Response Sheet

  1. Visit the official website at dsssb.delhi.gov.in or dsssbonline.nic.in.
  2. Open the answer key or response sheet link provided on the homepage.
  3. Login using your required credentials (usually Application Number and Date of Birth).
  4. View and download the provisional answer key along with your candidate response sheet.
  5. Save the PDF or print it for personal analysis.
  6. Check the official instructions carefully regarding the objection process.

How to Raise Objection

  1. Login to the official DSSSB objection portal using your credentials.
  2. Select the specific question ID and the answer you wish to challenge.
  3. Upload valid documentary proof to support your claim if required.
  4. Pay the objection fee online if applicable (check official notice for fee details).
  5. Submit your challenge before the official deadline.
  6. Download and keep the submission confirmation for your records.

Note: Follow the objection rules strictly from the official notice. If objection details are not visible, please check the official website.
