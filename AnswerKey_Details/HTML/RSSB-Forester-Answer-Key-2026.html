About This Answer Key

The Rajasthan Staff Selection Board (RSSB / RSMSSB) conducted the written examination for Forester (Vanpal) 259 Posts on 28 June 2026 across designated examination centers in Rajasthan. The primary provisional answer key has been officially uploaded on 20 July 2026 to help examinees verify their marked answers against the official key.

Candidates who appeared in the examination can access the provisional answer key PDF directly from the RSSB portal without any login required for downloading. Matching the answers with your OMR copy enables you to calculate expected written examination scores prior to result declaration.

If any candidate notices discrepancies or incorrect answers in the provisional key, they can submit online representations during the specified objection window. Challenges must be supported by valid reference documents and submitted online via Rajasthan SSO along with the required processing fee.

How to Download RSSB Forester Answer Key

  1. Visit the official RSSB website at rssb.rajasthan.gov.in.
  2. Navigate to the "Candidate Corner" menu on the homepage and click on "Answer Key".
  3. Locate the link for "Forester 2026 Primary Answer Key".
  4. Click the link to open the answer key PDF in your browser.
  5. Download and save the PDF file to your device.
  6. Cross-check your marked answers with the master question paper and official answer key.

How to Raise Objection

  1. Go to the Rajasthan Single Sign-On portal at sso.rajasthan.gov.in.
  2. Log in using your SSO ID and Password.
  3. Open the Recruitment Portal and click on "Raise Objection".
  4. Select the Forester Exam 2026 and choose the question set/booklet code you wish to challenge.
  5. Select the question number and upload clear proof or reference from standard text books.
  6. Pay the required fee of INR 100 per question online using debit card, credit card, or net banking.
  7. Submit the challenge form and download the confirmation receipt.

Objections submitted without valid proof or after the notified deadline will not be accepted by RSSB.

How to Calculate Expected Marks

You can estimate your score using the official marking scheme specified in the recruitment notification:

  • Correct Answer: Add 1 mark for each correct response.
  • Incorrect Answer: Deduct 1/3rd (0.33) mark for every incorrect response.
  • Unattempted Questions: 0 marks (no deduction).
