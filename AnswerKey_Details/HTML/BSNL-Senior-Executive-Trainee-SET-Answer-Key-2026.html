About BSNL SET Answer Key

Bharat Sanchar Nigam Limited (BSNL) has officially made the provisional answer keys and individual response sheets available for the Senior Executive Trainee (SET) Computer Based Test (CBT) 2026. This recruitment drive aims to fill 120 positions under the Direct Recruitment scheme across the Telecom and Finance streams. Candidates who attended the exam on 5th July 2026 can now access their response files through the online portal.

The answer key allows candidates to visually map their attempted choices against the correct solutions evaluated by the board. In case of any discrepancies or incorrect answers in the tentative key, BSNL provides an online grievance/objection module. A dedicated timeframe has been enabled so candidates can put forward challenges along with valid supportive explanations and documents.

Candidates are strongly advised to check and securely save or print their response sheets immediately. The online portal link will expire after the stated time limit, and no separate request for answer key data will be accommodated afterwards by the administration.

How to Download BSNL SET Answer Key & Response Sheet

  1. Go to the official response portal designated for the exam at bsnl.examresponse.in.
  2. On the candidate login interface, input your official Roll Number.
  3. Provide your Password, which corresponds to your Date of Birth formatted as YYYYMMDD.
  4. Solve the captcha if applicable and click on the 'Login' button.
  5. Look for the response sheet tab or answer key download option inside the dashboard.
  6. Download the PDF file or print it for cross-verifying your scores and upcoming references.

How to Raise Objections / Challenges

  1. Log into the BSNL candidate response system portal during the active window.
  2. Navigate to the 'Grievance Panel' or 'Raise Objection' section.
  3. Identify the specific question ID or answer option sequence that you want to challenge.
  4. Upload substantial technical supporting documents or references to back your query.
  5. Pay the required processing fee of INR 500 per challenged question through the provided online payment mode.
  6. Review your submission and finalize it before 17 July 2026 (10:00 AM).

Please note that challenges submitted via letters, emails, or offline formats will not be processed under any circumstances.

How to Calculate Expected Marks

Candidates can estimate their expected score by matching their marked choices against BSNL's official answer key. Check the official exam scheme before calculating expected marks:

  • The written examination consists of objective type multiple choice questions carrying a total of 200 marks.
  • Award the designated positive marks for every correct answer based on the official stream structure.
  • Deduct the prescribed negative marking penalty for incorrect responses as specified in the recruitment brochure.
  • Unattempted questions will be awarded zero marks.

Frequently Asked Questions

Is BSNL Senior Executive Trainee SET Answer Key 2026 released?

Yes. Bharat Sanchar Nigam Limited (BSNL) has uploaded the tentative answer keys along with candidates' individual response sheets for the Senior Executive Trainee Examination 2026.

What is the last date to challenge the BSNL SET answer key?

Online representations or challenges against the answers can be submitted until 17 July 2026 up to 10:00 AM.

What is the objection fee for BSNL SET Answer Key 2026?

As per the official notification, the objection fee is INR 500 per question or answer challenged.

What login details are required to view the response sheet?

Candidates need to log in using their Roll Number and Password (which is their Date of Birth in YYYYMMDD format).

Can objections be submitted through offline mode?

No. The representations must be submitted online only through the designated grievance portal. No offline mode will be entertained.

When was the BSNL SET CBT examination conducted?

The Computer Based Test (CBT) for the Direct Recruitment of 120 Senior Executive Trainees (Telecom & Finance) was conducted on 5 July 2026.

Will the response sheet link be available after the deadline?

No. The portal will remain live only until 17 July 2026 (10:00 AM). Candidates must download and save their response sheets before this deadline.
