About This Answer Key

The Bihar Public Service Commission (BPSC) has uploaded the provisional answer keys for the objective competitive examination held for recruitment to the post of Assistant Environmental Scientist under Advertisement No. 88/2025. The answer keys have been published separately for General Studies (Paper-I) and all elective subjects under Paper-II, which include Physics, Chemistry, Life Science, and Environmental Science. This provisional release lets candidates evaluate their responses and calculate estimated scores early in the selection cycle.

Candidates who appeared for the exam can review the answers provided by the commission. If any discrepancy or error is found in the questions or their marked keys, an objection window is provided to challenge the provisional answers. Objections must be substantiated with valid, authoritative proof or references to be evaluated by the expert committee. BPSC will not accept any offline applications or entries received past the deadline.

Reviewing the provisional sheet is essential for competitive transparency. Candidates should log in to their respective dashboard portals well within the specified dates to submit challenges. A nominal fee is applicable for each individual question challenge raised during this window.

How to Download BPSC Assistant Environmental Scientist Answer Key

  1. Visit the official website of the Bihar Public Service Commission at bpsc.bihar.gov.in.
  2. Look at the homepage notifications dated 08.07.2026.
  3. Click on the subject-specific link matching your requirement: General Studies, Physics, Chemistry, Life Science, or Environmental Science.
  4. The provisional answer key PDF document will open on your screen.
  5. Download and print the document to perform a question-by-question comparative analysis.

How to Raise Objections Against Provisional Keys

  1. Navigate to the BPSC online portal at onlinebpsc.bihar.gov.in.
  2. Log in using your registered Username and Password to access your candidate dashboard.
  3. Locate the online objection link for Advertisement Number 88/2025.
  4. Select the specific question numbers you wish to challenge and state the required correction.
  5. Upload clean digital copies of authentic texts, books, or legitimate source proofs supporting your claim.
  6. Pay the required processing fee of INR 250 per challenged question through the online payment gateway.
  7. Submit the form before the final deadline on 12 July 2026. Keep a copy of your dashboard confirmation.

Note: Objections sent via post, email, or any offline media will be summarily rejected by the commission without review.

    Frequently Asked Questions

    Is BPSC Assistant Environmental Scientist Answer Key 2026 released?

    Yes. BPSC has published the provisional answer keys for both Paper-I (General Studies) and Paper-II optional subjects on 08 July 2026.

    What is the timeline for submitting answer key objections?

    Candidates can submit objections online through their BPSC dashboard from 08 July 2026 until 12 July 2026.

    What is the objection processing fee per question?

    A fee of INR 250 is required for each question challenged on the BPSC objection portal.

    What credentials are required to submit objections?

    Candidates must log in to the BPSC online portal using their designated Username and Password.

    Can I submit objections by post or speed email?

    No. Challenges submitted through any medium other than the official online candidate dashboard will be rejected.

    Which optional subjects are covered under Paper-II keys?

    The keys are provided for Physics, Chemistry, Life Science, and Environmental Science elective domains.

    What should I do if an answer key link does not open?

    If high web traffic slows down the servers, clear your browser cache or try downloading the PDF documents after some time directly from bpsc.bihar.gov.in.

    When will the final verified answer keys be published?

    The final keys will be formulated and updated after the panel reviews all submitted valid student challenges. The dates are currently to be notified.
