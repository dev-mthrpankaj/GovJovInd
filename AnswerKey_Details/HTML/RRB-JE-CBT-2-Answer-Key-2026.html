About This Answer Key

The Railway Recruitment Boards across all regional zones have released the provisional answer keys along with the individual candidate response sheets for the Stage 2 Computer Based Test (CBT-2). This exam was administered to fill technical roles including Junior Engineer, Depot Material Superintendent, and Chemical & Metallurgical Assistant positions across Indian Railways under notification CEN 05/2025. By introducing this portal, candidates can thoroughly evaluate their attempts alongside the officially recorded correct answers.

The provisional answer key allows applicants to gauge transparency within the evaluating scheme. Since this evaluation phase is tentative, candidates have a direct pathway to verify discrepancies or administrative errors in the question framework or primary options. Challenges must be accompanied by relevant documentary justifications and submitted entirely online before the designated closing window.

Candidates are strongly urged to download and securely store their personalized response sheets alongside the official keys before the active link expires. These logs will no longer remain accessible to individuals once the closing date arrives, and offline grievances or late applications will not be processed by regional boards under any scenario.

How to Download RRB JE CBT 2 Answer Key

  1. Open the centralized Railway Recruitment login portal at rrb.digialm.com or visit your respective regional RRB website.
  2. Locate and select the flashing notification link mentioning "CEN 05/2025 Junior Engineer CBT-2 Answer Key & Response Sheet".
  3. Provide your unique individual Registration Number and Password (Date of Birth) in the specified form fields.
  4. Complete the visual CAPTCHA verification correctly and click on the "Login" tab.
  5. Your unique response script, official question paper matrix, and tentative answers will load onto the dashboard.
  6. Click the download button or select print to securely save a soft copy PDF for your record.

How to Raise Online Objections

  1. Access the online dashboard through the dedicated portal using your active credentials.
  2. Open the active link labeled "Objection Tracker System" or "Raise Objection".
  3. Carefully pick the specific Question ID or Option ID from the dropdown menu that you believe contains an error.
  4. Specify the concrete reason explaining your objection and upload clean, supporting PDF evidence or reference materials where required.
  5. Proceed to the online payment gate by clicking the "Pay Now" option.
  6. Remit the mandatory fee of INR 50 per individual question using Debit Card, Credit Card, UPI, or Net Banking.
  7. Submit your challenge before 17 July 2026 (11:50 PM) and print out the acknowledgement sheet.

Refund Policy Note: The processing fee of ₹50 paid per valid question will be fully refunded to the candidate's active account if the official expert committee approves the objection as authentic.

How to Calculate Expected Marks

Candidates can follow the exact official marking scheme laid out in the recruitment guidelines to calculate their provisional evaluation scores:

  • Total Questions: 150 questions in the CBT-2 matrix.
  • Correct Attempts: Award exactly 1 mark for each verified answer matching the key.
  • Negative Marking: Deduct exactly 1/3 (0.33) marks for each incorrect response recorded.
  • Unattempted Items: No marks will be added or subtracted for left/blank questions.

Use this calculation structure: Expected Score = (Correct Answers × 1) - (Wrong Answers × 1/3).

Important Instructions for Candidates

  • Time Constraint: Response records and objection modules will be entirely deactivated after 17 July 2026, 11:50 PM.
  • Valid Mode: Only responses filed online via the integrated tracker will be monitored; postal letters or generic emails are completely rejected.
  • Refund Clause: Fee refunds apply only if the challenge is accepted; no reversals occur for unapproved challenges.
  • Final Formulation: The board will draft a definitive Final Answer Key post reviewing all grievances, which will govern the final results formatting.
