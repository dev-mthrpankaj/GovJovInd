About This Answer Key

The High Court of Delhi has officially issued the provisional Model Answer Keys for the Delhi Higher Judicial Service (HJS) Preliminary Examination 2026. The preliminary test was conducted on 26 July 2026 for 27 sanctioned vacancies. Candidates who appeared for the examination can now access the booklet-wise model answer keys for Series A, B, C, and D.

The released answer key serves as a model reference key, enabling candidates to evaluate their probable performance. If any candidate finds discrepancies or incorrect answers in the official model key, they can raise objections through the online link provided on the official portal within three days from the date of issuance of the notice.

Submitting valid objections is crucial because the final merit list and preliminary examination results will be prepared based on the verified final answer key after considering all legitimate candidate challenges.

How to Download Delhi High Court HJS Answer Key

  1. Go to the official website of the Delhi High Court at delhihighcourt.nic.in.
  2. Navigate to the "Public Notices" or "Recruitment" section on the homepage.
  3. Click on the link titled "Model Answer Keys and Inviting Objections for Delhi Higher Judicial Service Preliminary Examination 2026".
  4. Open or download the official PDF notice containing the Model Answer Keys for Series A, B, C, and D.
  5. Compare the official model answers with your question paper booklet series to calculate your expected score.
  6. Save or print the PDF file for your future reference.

How to Raise Objection

  1. Visit the official website portal of the Delhi High Court.
  2. Click on the online objection link provided specifically for the Delhi Higher Judicial Service Prelims 2026.
  3. Select your question paper booklet series and the specific question number you wish to challenge.
  4. Provide mandatory justification/reasons in support of your objection.
  5. Ensure you do not select the same answer option already given in the Model Answer Keys.
  6. Submit the objection online before 31 July 2026, 05:00 PM.

As per the official notice signed by the Registrar General, objections submitted through any mode other than the designated online link will not be entertained.

How to Calculate Expected Marks

Check the official exam scheme and marking pattern prescribed in the Delhi Higher Judicial Service recruitment notification before calculating expected marks.

  • Match your marked answers against the Model Answer Key for your specific booklet series (A, B, C, or D).
  • Count the total number of correct responses and incorrect responses.
  • Apply the official marking weightage and negative marking (if applicable as per rules) to estimate your raw score.
  • Note that the score calculated using the provisional model key is tentative and subject to change after the release of the final answer key.

Frequently Asked Questions

Is Delhi High Court HJS Answer Key 2026 released?

Yes. High Court of Delhi has released the Model Answer Keys for Question Paper Booklets of all series (A, B, C, and D) for the HJS Preliminary Examination 2026.

What is the last date to submit objections against Delhi HJS Answer Key?

Candidates can submit their objections online through the official website portal until 31 July 2026 up to 05:00 PM.

What is the mode of submitting objections for Delhi HJS Prelims Key?

Objections must be submitted through ONLINE mode ONLY via the link provided on the official website. No offline, email, or postal representations will be accepted.

Is justification required while filing an objection?

Yes. It is mandatory for candidates to provide valid justification/reasons in support of every objection raised.

Can a candidate select the same option given in the Model Answer Key?

No. The official notice clarifies that candidates are restricted from choosing the same answer option as already given in the Model Answer Keys.

Which booklet series answer keys have been released?

The High Court has released model answer keys for all four Question Paper Booklet Series: A, B, C, and D.

When was the Delhi HJS Prelims Exam 2026 conducted?
