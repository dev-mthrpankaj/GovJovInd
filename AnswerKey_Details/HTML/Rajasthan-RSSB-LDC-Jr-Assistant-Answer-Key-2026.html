About This Answer Key

The Rajasthan Staff Selection Board (RSSB) conducted the offline written examination for the posts of Clerk Grade-II, LDC, and Junior Assistant across various departments on 05 July 2026. Following the successfully conducted examination, the recruitment board has issued the official primary answer key on 24 July 2026.

Candidates who appeared in the examination can access the answer key to cross-check their responses against the official key provided by the board. This enables candidates to estimate their expected performance before the publication of official results.

If candidates find any discrepancies or incorrect responses in the preliminary answer key, RSSB provides a window to submit online objections along with requisite proof. Candidates are advised to keep a saved copy of their question paper and check the official guidelines for objection submission deadlines.

How to Download RSSB LDC Answer Key 2026

  1. Visit the official portal of Rajasthan Staff Selection Board at rssb.rajasthan.gov.in.
  2. Navigate to the "Answer Keys" section from the main navigation menu.
  3. Look for the link titled "LDC / Junior Assistant 2026: Primary Answer Key".
  4. Click the PDF download link to open the master answer key in your browser.
  5. Match your question booklet sequence against the official key to check correct answers.
  6. Save or print the PDF for future reference.

How to Raise Objection

  1. Visit the official RSSB portal or SSO Rajasthan portal at sso.rajasthan.gov.in.
  2. Login using your SSO ID and password.
  3. Go to the Recruitment Portal and select the RSSB LDC Objection / Challenge link.
  4. Select the Question Paper Set and the specific question number you wish to challenge.
  5. Attach standard credible reference books/proofs in support of your challenge.
  6. Pay the prescribed objection fee online as specified by the board and submit.

Check official website for specific objection window opening and closing dates.

How to Calculate Expected Marks

Candidates can calculate their estimated scores by following the official marking rules for RSSB examinations:

  • Award full marks assigned to each correct answer according to the question paper schema.
  • Deduct negative marking penalty (if applicable as per notification) for each wrong response.
  • Check the official exam scheme in the recruitment notification before arriving at your net estimated score.

Frequently Asked Questions

Is Rajasthan RSSB LDC Answer Key 2026 released?

Yes, Rajasthan Staff Selection Board (RSSB) officially released the Answer Key on 24 July 2026.

When was the RSSB LDC / Junior Assistant Exam conducted?

The offline written examination for RSSB LDC / Junior Assistant was held on 05 July 2026.

How many total posts are available under RSSB Advt 04/2026?

A total of 10,644 posts are available across various departments, including 9,642 Non-TSP and 1,002 TSP posts.

Where can I download the RSSB LDC Answer Key 2026?

You can download the answer key directly from the official portal rssb.rajasthan.gov.in.

How can I submit objections against the RSSB LDC answer key?

Objection dates and guidelines will be notified on the official website rssb.rajasthan.gov.in. Candidates can raise challenges online through the RSSB objection portal.

What is the One Time Registration (OTR) fee structure for Rajasthan examinations?

The OTR fee is INR 600 for General/Other State candidates and INR 400 for OBC/EWS/SC/ST candidates of Rajasthan.

What to do if the answer key link is not opening?

Due to heavy traffic, the server may slow down. Refresh the official website or try accessing the link after some time.

When will the RSSB LDC Final Answer Key and Result be declared?

The final answer key and official exam results will be notified on the official portal following the resolution of candidate objections.
