About This Answer Key

The Uttar Pradesh Subordinate Services Selection Commission (UPSSSC) conducted the Junior Engineer (Civil) Main Examination under Advt No. 08-Exam/2024 on 03 May 2026 across various exam centers in Uttar Pradesh[cite: 1]. Following the exam, the commission published the tentative master set answer key on 04 May 2026 and invited online representations from candidates[cite: 1].

After a detailed examination of all objections submitted by candidates, the expert panel prepared the final revised answer key[cite: 1]. UPSSSC officially uploaded this final answer key on 20 July 2026[cite: 1]. Candidates can match their responses against the master set PDF to calculate their score[cite: 1].

Since this is the revised final answer key, no further objections or challenges will be entertained[cite: 1]. UPSSSC will prepare and declare the final written examination results and merit list based on this final answer key[cite: 1].

How to Download UPSSSC JE Final Answer Key

  3. Click on the link titled "Advt. 08-Exam/2024 Junior Engineer (Civil) Revised Final Answer Key"[cite: 1].
  4. The final master set answer key PDF will open in your browser[cite: 1].
  5. Download and save the PDF file for checking your responses[cite: 1].
  6. Compare your answers with the master key to compute your revised score[cite: 1].

Objection Status

The objection window for the UPSSSC JE Civil 2026 examination was opened after the provisional answer key release in May 2026[cite: 1].
