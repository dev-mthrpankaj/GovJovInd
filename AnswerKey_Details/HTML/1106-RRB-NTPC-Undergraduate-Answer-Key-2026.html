About This Answer Key

RRB has provided the facility to view questions, candidate responses and answer keys for the NTPC Undergraduate CBT 1 exam. This helps candidates compare what they attempted in the examination with the answer key displayed on the official portal.

The answer key is important because candidates can submit objections against questions, options or answer keys if they find any issue. The objection must be filed only through the online link available on RRB websites during the given time period.

Candidates should carefully read the official instructions before paying the objection fee. RRB has clearly stated that objections submitted after the last date and time will not be considered, and the decision of RRB on objections will be final and binding.

How to Download RRB NTPC UG Answer Key

  1. Visit the official RRB website or open rrb.indianrailways.gov.in.
  2. Find the link for CEN 07/2025 NTPC Undergraduate CBT 1 question paper, responses and answer key.
  3. Open the link and login as per the instructions shown on the official portal.
  4. View your question paper, recorded responses and answer key carefully.
  5. Download or save the response sheet for personal analysis before the link closes.
  6. Use only the official portal for objection submission, if required.

How to Raise Objection

  1. Login through the answer key or objection link available on the official RRB website.
  2. Review the questions, options and answer keys shown in the candidate portal.
  3. Select the question, option or answer key against which you want to submit an objection.
  4. Follow the detailed objection procedure and guidelines shown on the official portal.
  5. Pay â‚¹50 per question along with applicable bank service charges through the available online mode.
  6. Submit the objection before 05 July 2026 at 23:55. After this time, no representation will be considered.

RRB has stated that its decision on the submitted objections will be final and binding, and no further correspondence will be entertained.

How to Calculate Expected Marks

Candidates can use the response sheet to compare their marked answers with the official answer key. However, the provided answer key notice does not mention the complete marking scheme on this page.

Check the official exam scheme before calculating expected marks. Candidates should not depend on unofficial cut-off predictions or unverified score claims while making decisions about the next stage.
