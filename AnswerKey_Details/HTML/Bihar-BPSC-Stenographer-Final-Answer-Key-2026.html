About This Final Answer Key

The Bihar Public Service Commission (BPSC) conducted the Stenographer Written (Objective) Competitive Examination under Advertisement No. 01/2026 on 11 June 2026 across designated centers. Following the successful conduct of the examination, the commission released the Provisional Answer Key on 08 July 2026 and invited online challenges from candidates until 12 July 2026 with a prescribed fee of Rs. 250 per question.

After thorough verification of all submitted objections by subject matter experts, BPSC finalized the official answers and published the Final Answer Key on 22 July 2026. The final answer key serves as the definitive reference point for assessing candidate responses, and no further objections or challenges will be entertained against these finalized answers.

Candidates can download the Final Answer Key PDF from the official BPSC website to re-verify their scores. The commission will declare the written examination result and prepare the merit list for subsequent selection rounds strictly on the basis of this final key.

How to Calculate Expected Marks

Candidates can estimate their written exam score by matching their marked responses with the official Final Answer Key. Follow these steps:

  • Refer to the official BPSC Stenographer examination scheme and marking guidelines before calculating marks.
  • Count the total number of correct answers matching the Final Answer Key.
  • Assign marks awarded per correct question as specified in the official exam notification.
  • Deduct marks for incorrect attempts if negative marking is applicable in the official scheme.
  • Calculate the net expected score to gauge your qualification chances for further selection stages.
