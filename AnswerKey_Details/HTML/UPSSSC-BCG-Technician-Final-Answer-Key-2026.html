About This Answer Key

The Uttar Pradesh Subordinate Service Selection Commission (UPSSSC) conducted the written examination for the post of BCG Technician on 30 June 2026 for a total of 255 vacancies. A provisional key was released earlier, allowing candidates to submit objections.

After reviewing all the valid representations submitted by the candidates, UPSSSC has now issued the Final Answer Key. Since this is the final key, no further objections will be entertained. Candidates can utilize this document to accurately calculate their final expected score before the official result declaration.

How to Download Final Answer Key

  1. Visit the official website of UPSSSC at upsssc.gov.in.
  2. Navigate to the 'Notice Board' or 'Important Links' section on the homepage.
  3. Click on the link corresponding to "UPSSSC BCG Technician Final Answer Key 2026".
  4. The Final Answer Key PDF file will open on your screen.
  5. Download the PDF and cross-check the answers with your question paper set.
  6. Save or print the PDF for your personal analysis and future reference.

How to Raise Objection

The objection window for the UPSSSC BCG Technician exam is currently closed. Objections were only accepted against the tentative answer key. The currently published key is final, and no further challenges will be accepted by the commission.

How to Calculate Expected Marks

Check the official exam scheme before calculating expected marks:

  • Match your attempted answers with the Final Answer Key PDF.
  • Award marks for every correct answer as per the official exam pattern.
  • Deduct marks for any incorrect answers based on the negative marking scheme (if applicable).
  • Unattempted questions will not impact your final score.
