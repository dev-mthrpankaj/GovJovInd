About This Answer Key

The National Testing Agency (NTA) has officially released the provisional answer keys for the Joint CSIR-UGC NET June 2026 examination, which was conducted in Computer-Based Test (CBT) mode across 224 cities. Along with the answer key, NTA has provided individual response sheets, allowing candidates to log in and compare their recorded answers with the official provisional solutions.

This answer key is provisional, meaning NTA provides a dedicated window for candidates to challenge any discrepancies. If a candidate finds a question or its answer incorrect, they can utilize the online challenge module by paying a prescribed processing fee. The submitted challenges are then carefully reviewed by a panel of subject experts.

Once all valid challenges are evaluated, NTA prepares and releases the final answer key. The final results are generated strictly based on this updated final answer key. Candidates are strongly advised to check their responses and submit objections, if any, before the strict deadline, as no modifications are permitted afterward.

How to Download CSIR NET Answer Key

  1. Visit the official NTA CSIR-NET portal at csirnet.nta.ac.in.
  2. Click on the link displaying "Joint CSIR UGC NET June 2026 Provisional Answer Key" or "View Response Sheet".
  3. Log in using your Application Number and Date of Birth or Password along with the Security Pin (Captcha).
  4. After successful login, click on "View Question Paper" to see your recorded responses or "Click to view / Challenge Answer Key" to see the provisional key.
  5. Download the answer key and response sheet PDF and save it for personal analysis.

How to Raise Objection

  1. Log in to the official answer key portal using your application credentials.
  2. Navigate to the "Challenge Answer Key" section.
  3. Identify the relevant question by its Question ID and select the correct Option ID you wish to claim.
  4. Upload any supporting documents as a single PDF file (up to 500 KB).
  5. Save your claims and proceed to the payment gateway.
  6. Pay the non-refundable processing fee of INR 200 per question challenged via Credit Card, Debit Card, Net Banking, or UPI before 18 August 2026, 11:00 PM.

Challenges submitted without successful fee payment will not be considered by NTA.

How to Calculate Expected Marks

  • Cross-check your recorded responses with the provisional answer key.
  • Keep in mind the specific marking scheme for Parts A, B, and C as it varies depending on the subject (Chemical Sciences, Life Sciences, Mathematical Sciences, Physical Sciences, Earth Sciences).
  • Add the stipulated positive marks for every correctly matched answer.
  • Deduct the precise negative marks for incorrect answers according to the official exam scheme for your specific subject and section.
  • Check the official exam scheme from the NTA CSIR NET bulletin before calculating expected marks to avoid inaccuracies.
