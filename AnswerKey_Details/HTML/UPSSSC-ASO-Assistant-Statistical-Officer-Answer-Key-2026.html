About This Answer Key

The Uttar Pradesh Subordinate Service Selection Commission (UPSSSC) conducted the written examination for Assistant Statistical Officer (ASO) and Assistant Research Officer (ARO) on 26 July 2026 under Advertisement No. 05/2026. Following the successfully conducted examination, UPSSSC released the official answer key on 27 July 2026.

This answer key enables candidates to review their attempted questions and calculate their expected marks before the official eligibility results and merit lists are published. Candidates are encouraged to download the official PDF and compare their responses carefully.

If any candidate finds discrepancies or incorrect answers in the tentative answer key, they must refer to the official notice released by UPSSSC for raising challenges within the specified objection window.

How to Download UPSSSC ASO Answer Key

  1. Visit the official portal of UPSSSC at upsssc.gov.in.
  2. Look for the Notice Board section on the homepage.
  3. Click on the link for "UPSSSC ASO/ARO Advt 05/2026 Answer Key".
  4. The Answer Key PDF / Response Sheet window will open on screen.
  5. Download the PDF file to your computer or mobile device.
  6. Cross-check your question paper booklet set with the official answer key keys.

How to Raise Objection

  1. Visit the official UPSSSC portal during the active objection window.
  2. Login using your Registration Number and Password / Date of Birth.
  3. Select the specific question paper set and question number you wish to challenge.
  4. Upload valid reference proof or document supporting your objection.
  5. Pay the required objection fee, if specified in the official notice.
  6. Submit the challenge before the deadline specified in the official notification.

Please read the official Answer Key Notice thoroughly for exact objection fee rules and deadlines.

How to Calculate Expected Marks

Check the official marking scheme provided in the UPSSSC ASO recruitment notification before calculating your tentative score:

  • Award prescribed positive marks for every correct answer marked.
  • Deduct prescribed negative marks for incorrect options as per UPSSSC exam rules.
  • Unattempted questions will carry zero marks.
  • Always verify your final score calculations against official answer keys only.
