About This Answer Key

The Uttar Pradesh Police Recruitment and Promotion Board has released the final answer key notice for the UP Home Guard Enrollment 2025 written examination. The written exam was held on 25 April 2026, 26 April 2026 and 27 April 2026 in six shifts. Earlier, the question papers and answer keys were displayed on the official website from 7 May 2026 to 10 May 2026 and candidates were allowed to submit online objections with references.

A tentative answer key helps candidates compare their attempted answers with the answers proposed by the board. After the objection window, the board reviews valid challenges through subject experts. In this case, the official notice says that objections were found valid for two questions across the six shifts.

The current notice is important because it clarifies that the final answer key is now available for candidates to view, and no further objection will be accepted on the questions, answer options or answer key. Candidates should check the final key only from the official UPPRPB website and save relevant details for personal record.

How to Download UP Home Guard Answer Key

  1. Visit the official UP Police Recruitment and Promotion Board website at uppbpb.gov.in.
  2. Open the UP Home Guard Enrollment 2025 final answer key link available on the website.
  3. Login with your roll number, date of birth and question booklet serial number.
  4. View the final answer key shown for your exam shift and question booklet.
  5. Save or print the answer key details for personal analysis before the availability period ends.
  6. Keep checking the official website for result and next-stage instructions.

How to Raise Objection

  1. The objection window was earlier available from 07 May 2026 to 10 May 2026.
  2. Candidates were required to submit objections online with suitable reference / evidence during that period.
  3. The objections received from candidates were reviewed by subject experts.
  4. The board has now issued the final answer key decision after considering valid objections.
  5. As per the final notice, no further objection will be accepted on questions, answer options or the final answer key.

Candidates should avoid relying on unofficial objection information and must read the latest notice on the official UPPRPB website.

How to Calculate Expected Marks

Use the final answer key only after confirming your exam shift and question booklet details. Compare your attempted answers with the final answer key shown in the candidate login panel.

  • For the question cancelled by the board, marks will be distributed as per the accepted arrangement mentioned in the official notice.
  • For the question where more than one option is correct, the notice says that marks will be awarded if the candidate has selected any one of the correct options.
  • Check the official exam scheme before calculating expected marks because the marking scheme is not explained in this answer key notice.
  • Do not treat self-calculated marks as final result. The official result will be published by the board separately.
