How to Download UPSC CAPF Answer Key

  1. Visit the official portal of Union Public Service Commission at upsc.gov.in.
  2. Look for the 'Answer Keys' or 'What's New' section on the homepage.
  3. Click on the link titled "Central Armed Police Forces (ACs) Examination, 2026 - Provisional Answer Key".
  4. The Answer Key PDF file for General Ability and Intelligence will open on your screen.
  5. Download the PDF file and save it to your local device.
  6. Cross-verify your attempted question booklet series with the key provided for calculation.

How to Raise Objection

If you find any discrepancy in the officially released answer key, UPSC usually provides a representation window (QP-PORTAL) for a stipulated duration.

  1. Visit the official UPSC online representation portal.
  2. Login using your Roll Number and Date of Birth / OTR details if prompted.
  3. Select the specific paper, set series, and Question Number to challenge.
  4. Upload valid documentary evidence/proof supporting your challenge.
  5. Submit the representation before the official cutoff deadline. Check official website for detailed objection rules.

How to Calculate Expected Marks

Check the official exam scheme before calculating expected marks:

  • Refer to the official marking pattern prescribed in Advt No. 08/2026-CAPF.
  • Award the specified positive marks for every correct response in Paper I.
  • Deduct negative marking penalty (usually 1/3rd of the marks assigned to that question) for incorrect answers.
  • Calculate total score = (Total Correct Answers × Marks per question) - (Total Incorrect Answers × Penalty).
