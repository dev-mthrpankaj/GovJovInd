About This Answer Key

The Indian Air Force conducts the Air Force Common Admission Test (AFCAT) twice a year to select Officers for Flying, Ground Duty (Technical), and Ground Duty (Non-Technical) branches. Following the successful completion of the AFCAT 02/2026 online written exam on 08 August 2026, answer keys and candidate login links have been made available on the official CDAC candidate portal.

Reviewing the official answer key and response sheet enables candidates to cross-verify their marked answers, evaluate overall accuracy, and estimate their probable written examination scores prior to the official result declaration. If any candidate finds discrepancies or incorrect answers in the key, they can submit online representations during the active objection window.

Candidates are strongly advised to save their question paper and response sheet for personal analysis immediately. Make sure to read all instructions in the candidate portal carefully regarding objection formats, deadlines, and applicable fees.

How to Download AFCAT Answer Key 2026

  1. Visit the official Indian Air Force AFCAT portal at afcat.cdac.in.
  2. Locate and click on the Candidate Login or AFCAT 02/2026 Answer Key link on the homepage.
  3. Enter your registered Email ID / Application Number and Password.
  4. Enter the required Security Code / Captcha and click on Submit.
  5. View your AFCAT 02/2026 question paper, answer key, and marked responses screen.
  6. Download or save a PDF copy of the response sheet for future personal reference.

How to Raise Objection

  1. Log into the official AFCAT candidate portal during the active challenge window.
  2. Navigate to the Objection / Challenge Module section.
  3. Select the specific Question ID or Answer Option you wish to challenge.
  4. Upload supporting documentation or academic proof as required by the IAF portal.
  5. Pay the required objection fee per question challenged (if applicable) through online payment modes.
  6. Submit your representation before the prescribed deadline and save the challenge confirmation receipt.

Please note that challenges submitted after the deadline or via offline modes (email, postal mail) will not be entertained by IAF.

How to Calculate Expected Marks

Candidates should check the official AFCAT 02/2026 examination scheme published in the notification to calculate their expected raw marks correctly:

  • Check the total number of correctly answered questions and multiply by the positive marks allotted per question.
  • Deduct negative marks for each incorrectly attempted question as specified in the marking scheme.
  • Unattempted questions carry zero marks.
  • Check the official exam scheme before concluding expected marks or qualifying predictions.
