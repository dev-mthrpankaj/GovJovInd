About This Answer Key

The Uttar Pradesh Subordinate Services Selection Commission (UPSSSC) conducted the written examination for Technical Assistant Group C (Agriculture Technical Assistant - AGTA) under Advertisement No. 06-Exam/2026 on 09 August 2026 across various examination centers in Uttar Pradesh[cite: 1]. The commission has released the answer key on 11 August 2026 to enable candidates to verify their answers[cite: 1].

The provisional answer key allows candidates to cross-check their marked answers with the official keys published by UPSSSC[cite: 1]. Cross-checking the answer key helps candidates calculate their expected scores based on the official marking scheme before the declaration of the final merit list and written examination results.

How to Download UPSSSC Technical Assistant Answer Key

  1. Visit the official website of UPSSSC at upsssc.gov.in[cite: 1].
  2. Look for the Notice Board or Important Announcements section on the homepage[cite: 1].
  3. Click on the link for "UPSSSC Technical Assistant Group C AGTA Answer Key 2026 (Advt 06-Exam/2026)"[cite: 1].
  4. If required, enter your Registration Number, Date of Birth / Password, and CAPTCHA code to log in[cite: 1].
  5. The answer key PDF or question paper set response sheet will appear on your screen[cite: 1].
  6. Compare your responses with the official key[cite: 1].
  7. Download and save the answer key PDF for future reference[cite: 1].

How to Raise Objection

  1. Log in to the official UPSSSC objection portal using your Registration credentials.
  2. Navigate to the "Raise Objection / Answer Key Challenge" section.
  3. Select the Question Set, Question Number, and Option you wish to challenge.
  4. Attach valid document proof / authentic reference standard book material supporting your objection.
  5. Pay the objection fee online if mandated by the official commission notice.
  6. Submit your representation before the official deadline. Late submissions will not be considered.

Note: Objections submitted via post, email, or any offline mode will not be accepted by UPSSSC.

How to Calculate Expected Marks

Candidates can estimate their written test score using the UPSSSC AGTA official exam scheme:

  • Total Marks: 100 Marks
