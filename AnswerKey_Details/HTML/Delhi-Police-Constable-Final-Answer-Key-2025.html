About This Answer Key

The Staff Selection Commission (SSC) has released the definitive final answer keys for the Constable (Executive) Male and Female in Delhi Police Examination, 2025. Following the announcement of the initial computer-based test results on 19 June 2026, the commission has finalized the answers after verifying all evaluation phases to ensure clarity and transparency in the grading process.

Along with the final keys, candidate response sheets and detailed question papers have been hosted on the login server. This system enables applicants to review their actual answers alongside the officially verified answers, which helps with strict self-analysis and assessment of individual performance benchmarks.

Furthermore, individual scorecards listing precise marks obtained by both qualified and non-qualified candidates are simultaneously available. Since this facility is bound to a strict institutional time limit, candidates should download and save their documents immediately, as SSC will not entertain separate personal requests later.

How to Download Final Answer Key & Response Sheet

  1. Click the official final answer key link or go to ssc.gov.in.
  2. Locate the official notification concerning the uploading of final answer keys for Delhi Police Constable 2025.
  3. Access the dedicated authentication page via the link given inside the official announcement.
  4. Enter your unique Examination Roll Number and Password as per your admission documentation.
  5. View the final answer key and your marked answers on the screen.
  6. Take a clean printout or save the page as a PDF file for self-analysis.

How to Check Individual Marks

  1. Visit the primary Staff Selection Commission website homepage.
  2. Log in to your candidate dashboard using your Registered ID and secure Password.
  3. Navigate to the designated Results/Marks tab available on the interface.
  4. Select the Constable (Executive) Male and Female in Delhi Police Examination, 2025 from the list.
  5. Your marks and detailed scorecard breakdown will be displayed on your screen.
  6. Save or print out your scorecard safely before the 21 July 2026 deadline.

How to Calculate Marks

Check the official exam scheme before calculating expected marks. Review your response sheet along with the final answer key to check total correct and incorrect responses based on the structural evaluation setup issued in the primary advertisement.
