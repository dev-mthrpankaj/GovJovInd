About This Answer Key

The Madhya Pradesh Employees Selection Board (MPESB) has officially activated the online objection tracker and response sheet portal for the Hospital Assistant Recruitment Test 2026. Candidates who participated in the written examination held for 1200 vacancies can now access their specific question papers along with the correct answers chosen by the board. This ensures transparency in the evaluation process.

By logging in, candidates can easily check which questions they answered correctly and calculate their tentative raw scores. If a candidate finds any discrepancy, typo, or incorrect evaluation in the provisional answer key, they can submit an online challenge. MPESB provides a limited window for raising these objections, after which no representations will be evaluated.

It is important to secure a copy of your response sheet before the link closes. The board will review all valid candidate responses and verify them with subject experts to draft the final answer key, which forms the core basis for the final results.

How to Download MPESB Hospital Assistant Answer Key

  1. Visit the official website of MPESB at esb.mp.gov.in or go straight to the exam portal.
  2. Click on the "Online Question Objection - Hospital Assistant Recruitment Test - 2026" link under the latest updates section.
  3. You will be redirected to the objection tracker login screen.
  4. Enter your unique Application Number, TAC Code (printed on your admit card), and Date of Birth in the DDMMYYYY format.
  5. Submit the details to view your official response sheet and provisional key on the screen.
  6. Download the PDF copy or print it out for your marks evaluation and future reference.

How to Raise Objection

  1. Login to the official portal using your Application Number, TAC Code, and Date of Birth.
  2. Locate the specific question ID or question number you intend to challenge.
  3. Select the type of objection and upload supporting reference material or document proofs if requested.
  4. Pay the required challenge fee (if applicable) through the online payment gateway.
  5. Review your details and submit the objection form before the final closing date on 13 July 2026.
  6. Keep a copy of the objection submission confirmation receipt.

Please note that challenges sent via email, post, or any offline mode will not be entertained by the board.

How to Calculate Expected Marks

Check the official exam scheme before calculating expected marks. Review the total questions and marking weightage assigned per question in the original recruitment brochure. Match your response sheet choices with the official correct options to compute your raw score, keeping any negative marking parameters in mind.
