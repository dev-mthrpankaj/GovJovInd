About This Answer Key

The Staff Selection Commission has released the tentative answer keys for the SSC GD Constable 2026 Computer Based Examination. Along with the answer key, candidates can also view their individual response sheets after logging in through the official SSC website. This helps candidates compare their marked responses with the tentative official answers before the result stage.

The answer key is called tentative because SSC allows candidates to submit representations against questions or answers within a fixed time window. If a candidate believes that a question or answer is incorrect, they can use the online challenge module and submit the objection with the prescribed fee. SSC will review valid objections as per its rules before finalizing the answer key.

Candidates should not wait until the last hour because the response sheet and tentative answer key will not remain available after the specified deadline. The saved question paper is strictly for personal use and self-analysis only, as stated in the official notice.

How to Download SSC GD Answer Key

  1. Visit the official Staff Selection Commission website at ssc.gov.in.
  2. Open the answer key or candidate response sheet link for Constable GD Examination 2026.
  3. Login with your Registration Number and Password.
  4. View the tentative answer key and your marked responses carefully.
  5. Download or save the response sheet for personal analysis before the deadline.
  6. If required, use the challenge module to submit objections within the notified dates.

How to Raise Objection

  1. Login to the SSC answer key or response sheet portal during the objection window.
  2. Open the challenge module and select the question or answer you want to challenge.
  3. Submit the objection only in the medium or language in which you chose and attempted the examination.
  4. Pay INR 50 per question or answer challenged through the online mode available on the portal.
  5. Submit the challenge before 20 June 2026, 6:00 PM. Late representations will not be entertained.
  6. Read SSC's Challenge/Objection Management System guidelines before final submission.

The official notice states that objections submitted in any mode other than online will not be entertained.

How to Use the Response Sheet

  • Compare each attempted question with the tentative answer key shown in the SSC portal.
  • Remember that the challenge module may show question and option sequence differently from the actual examination screen.
  • The answer selected by the candidate during the examination will be accurately reflected in the challenge module.
  • Use the saved question paper only for personal use and self-analysis.
  • Do not depend on unofficial answer keys for final decision; verify only through the official SSC portal.
