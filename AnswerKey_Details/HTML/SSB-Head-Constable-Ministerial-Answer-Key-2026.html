About This Answer Key

Sashastra Seema Bal (SSB) has officially uploaded the provisional answer key for the Head Constable (Ministerial) 2020 Computer Based Test (CBT). The assessment was conducted across assigned examination centers to fill positions in the department. Candidates can now evaluate their overall performance by cross-checking the response log with the verified master keys provided inside the digital interface.

The provisional key acts as a standard mechanism to bring transparency to competitive government examinations. If a candidate encounters any discrepancies or incorrect answers designated by the assessment panel, they are entitled to register objections. This challenge window allows administrative rectifications to take place before calculating final scores and merit ranking variables.

The objection management system is temporary and operates on strict timelines. Candidates must review their responses and download required copies for personal records before expiration. After the window closes, the administrative board closes candidate access and forwards submissions to specialized panels for standard verification processes.

How to Download Answer Key & Response Sheet

  1. Visit the official candidate assessment portal directly at DigialM SSB Login Link.
  2. Enter your unique exam credentials, including your login ID and designated portal password.
  3. Locate the answer key or response configuration link on the candidate command dashboard.
  4. View the detailed breakdown of question matrix allocations along with marked alternatives.
  5. Save the layout configuration directly as a PDF file or take a hard copy printout for offline analysis.

How to Raise Objection

  1. Access the official dashboard utilizing your authorized login parameters within the scheduled window.
  2. Navigate to the 'Objection Management Tab' to select specified questions you intend to contest.
  3. Follow instructions provided within the system workspace to outline points clearly.
  4. Attach supporting verification reference proofs or validation records if prompted inside the portal framework.
  5. Submit your documentation entries securely before 12 July 2026, 10:00 AM.

As specified in notice File No. 409/RC/SSB/CBT/2022/VOL-V-4071, alternative request forms via physical mail or external communication vectors are strictly rejected.

How to Calculate Expected Marks

Check the official exam scheme before calculating expected marks. Review structural guidelines regarding weight distribution per question block and look for updated regulatory parameters concerning negative evaluation mark adjustments before drawing a final score estimation.
