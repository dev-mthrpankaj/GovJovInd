About This Answer Key

The UPPSC Assistant Acharya answer key notice is related to the Government Degree College Preliminary Examination 2025 held on 31 May 2026. The Commission displayed the objective question paper and its answers on the official website for candidate review.

According to the notice, candidates were asked to match their answers with the question booklet barcode number 1005873 shown on the website. Correct answers were highlighted and underlined in rectangular boxes below the questions so candidates could compare them carefully.

The objection facility was time-bound and evidence-based. Candidates who found any discrepancy in the displayed question-answer were required to send their representation in the prescribed format with supporting proof before the deadline.

How to Download UPPSC Answer Key

  1. Visit the official UPPSC website at uppsc.up.nic.in.
  2. Open the Assistant Acharya, Government Degree College Preliminary Examination answer key notice.
  3. Check the displayed question paper and answers for the relevant subject or General Studies section.
  4. Match the displayed answers with question booklet barcode number 1005873 as instructed in the notice.
  5. Save the official notice or answer key PDF for personal reference.

How to Raise Objection

  1. Check the question number and answer from the question booklet barcode number 1005873 displayed on the official website.
  2. Write the full question in the prescribed format and mention the Commission's answer, your proposed answer and option details.
  3. Attach clear supporting evidence with the objection. The notice states that objections without evidence or with unclear evidence will not be considered.
  4. Submit the objection in a sealed envelope addressed to the Examination Controller, Atigopan-5 Section, Uttar Pradesh Public Service Commission, Prayagraj-211018.
  5. Submit it by post or at the Commission counter on any working day before 09 June 2026, 05:00 PM.
  6. Mention the subject on the envelope as instructed in the notice.

The objection deadline mentioned in the notice has passed. Candidates should check the official UPPSC website for any later update.

How to Calculate Expected Marks

The notice provides displayed answers for candidate checking, but it does not mention the complete marking formula or any result calculation rule. Candidates may compare their answers with the official displayed answer key for self-analysis.

Check the official exam scheme before calculating expected marks. Do not assume cutoff, marks or result status only from unofficial calculations.
