About This Answer Key

The UP Police Constable answer key helps candidates compare the official answers with the questions attempted in the written examination. The Board has displayed the question papers and answer keys for the exam conducted on 8 June 2026, 9 June 2026 and 10 June 2026 for both shifts.

This answer key is useful for checking responses and identifying any question or answer option where the candidate believes a correction is required. Candidates can submit online objections with proper evidence, reference or supportive document through the official link available on the Board website.

The objection window is time-bound, so candidates should check the answer key carefully and complete the online process before the last date. The official notice also makes it clear that objections through post, courier, email or any other offline mode will not be considered.

How to Download UP Police Constable Answer Key

  1. Visit the official Uttar Pradesh Police Recruitment and Promotion Board website at uppbpb.gov.in.
  2. Open the UP Police Constable direct recruitment 2025 answer key or objection notice link.
  3. Login using the required details such as roll number, date of birth and question booklet serial number.
  4. View the question paper and answer key for your exam date and shift.
  5. Download or save the answer key and question paper for personal analysis before the objection deadline.

How to Raise Objection

  1. Login through the objection link available on the official Board website.
  2. Select the question or answer option against which you want to submit an objection.
  3. Add the required evidence, reference, notice or supportive document for the objection.
  4. If the official portal asks for any objection fee, pay it only as per the official instructions. The provided notice does not mention a fee.
  5. Review the objection carefully before final submission because the notice states that a candidate cannot raise an objection again on the same question after submitting it once.
  6. Submit the objection online before 23 June 2026 midnight.

Objections sent through post, courier, email or any mode other than the official online link will not be considered.

How to Calculate Expected Marks

Use the official answer key to compare your attempted answers with the answers displayed by UPPBPB. Count the number of correct and incorrect responses only after confirming your exam date, shift and question booklet details.

Check the official exam scheme before calculating expected marks. The provided answer key notice does not mention the marking scheme, negative marking rule or expected cut-off. Candidates should not rely on unofficial score or cut-off claims without verifying the official recruitment notification and exam scheme.

Is UP Police Constable Answer Key 2025 released?

What is the objection last date?
