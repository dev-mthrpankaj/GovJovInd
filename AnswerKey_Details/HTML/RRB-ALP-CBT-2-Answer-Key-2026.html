About This Answer Key

The Railway Recruitment Board (RRB) has issued the tentative answer key and candidates' official response sheets for the Assistant Loco Pilot (ALP) CBT II examination under Notification CEN 01/2025. This stage of recruitment covers 9,970 total post vacancies across various regional RRBs.

The CBT 2 answer key provides candidates with their recorded responses alongside the official tentative key. This allows applicants to calculate their preliminary score and evaluate their performance in both Part A and Part B of the second stage computer-based test.

In case of any discrepancies or incorrect answers marked in the provisional key, candidates can submit online challenges through the objection link. All valid objections will be reviewed by subject experts before RRB releases the finalized CBT 2 evaluation and result.

How to Download RRB ALP CBT 2 Answer Key

  1. Visit the official RRB candidate portal or regional RRB web pages.
  2. Click on the login link for "CBT II Answer Key / Response Sheet - CEN 01/2025".
  3. Enter your Registration Number and Password / Date of Birth on the Digialm login screen.
  4. Click on the submit button to access your candidate dashboard.
  5. Select the "Candidate Response" or "Objection Form" tab to view your question paper and marked answers.
  6. Download and save the PDF copy of your CBT 2 response sheet for future reference.

How to Raise Objection

  1. Log into the official RRB Digialm portal using your credentials.
  2. Navigate to the "Objection Form" tab on the candidate dashboard.
  3. Select the Question ID and Option ID you wish to challenge.
  4. Upload valid documentary proof or technical justification in support of your claim if required.
  5. Pay the required online objection fee per question through net banking, UPI, or debit/credit card.
  6. Submit your representation before the specified objection closing deadline.

Objections submitted after the deadline or via offline modes (postal/email) will not be entertained by RRB under any circumstances.

How to Calculate Expected Marks

Check the official RRB marking scheme specified in the CEN 01/2025 notification before evaluating your score:

  • Each correct answer carries 1 mark.
  • Negative marking of 1/3 mark applies for every incorrect answer.
  • Unattempted questions receive zero marks.
