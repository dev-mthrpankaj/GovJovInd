How to Download Patna High Court Computer Operator Answer Key

  1. Visit the official website of Patna High Court at patnahighcourt.gov.in.
  2. Navigate to the "Recruitments" or "Notices" section on the homepage.
  3. Locate and click on the link for "Computer Operator Cum Typist Answer Key 2026 (Advt. No. PHC/02/2026)".
  4. Log in using your Registration Number / Application ID and Password / Date of Birth if prompted.
  5. The Answer Key PDF or Response Sheet display will appear on the screen.
  6. Download and save the file to your device for self-evaluation.

How to Raise Objection

  1. Log in to the official examination login portal using your credentials during the representation window.
  2. Select the specific Question ID and Option Key that you wish to challenge.
  3. Upload valid documentary evidence or standard reference justification as required by the High Court.
  4. Pay the requisite objection fee per question, if prescribed on the official portal.
  5. Submit your representation before the published closing deadline.

If objection submission instructions or fees are not explicitly specified in the short notice, write to or check the official Patna High Court recruitment page for updates.

How to Calculate Expected Marks

To compute your tentative score, cross-check your responses against the official answer key released by the Patna High Court. Check the official exam scheme for specific marking details, total marks, and negative marking rules prior to calculating your score.
