About This Answer Key

The Railway Recruitment Boards across India have opened the login module for candidates who appeared in the NTPC Graduate Level CEN 06/2025 Phase II Computer Based Test. The release of the tentative answer key and individual question sheets marks a critical step for aspirants targeting graduate-level positions like Station Master, Goods Train Manager, Chief Commercial Cum Ticket Supervisor, Senior Clerk, and Junior Accounts Assistant.

By downloading the official response sheet, candidates can systematically analyze their performance, verify the marked answers against the preliminary answers keys verified by the board, and approximate their net scores prior to the compilation of the final merit list. The challenge infrastructure allows applicants to flag discrepancies in question wording, layout, options, or provisional solutions.

Candidates are strictly advised to download and securely store copies of their dynamic response sheets within the active dates. The download link and the associated objection management window will close down strictly as per the server timeline, after which no offline letters, emails, or personal representations will be processed by the board administration.

How to Download RRB NTPC CBT II Answer Key

  1. Navigate to your primary regional RRB portal or directly load the authenticated login host at rrb.digialm.com.
  2. Locate the prominent notification section regarding "Candidate Response Sheet / Objection Tracker for CEN 06/2025 Graduate CBT II".
  3. Input your designated Registration/Roll number along with your password or date of birth as recorded on the admit card.
  4. Submit details to load the candidate dashboard interface.
  5. Click the tab labeled 'Candidate Response' or 'Answer Key Sheet' to view the full question list with marked options and official correct keys.
  6. Utilize the print button to save the entire page as a local PDF for calculation and future recruitment tracking.

How to Raise Objection

  1. Enter the official online challenge portal through the student link.
  2. Navigate to the 'Objection Tracker' tab shown on your exam dashboard.
  3. Select the specific Question ID or Option ID that you believe contains an error.
  4. Provide a clear, brief explanation in the text area and upload valid supporting documents or authoritative references if requested by the board.
  5. Pay the specified objection fee using the online transaction systems provided on the tracker gate.
  6. Review the entry and click submit before the server deadline. Keep a printout of the confirmation text.

Please note that challenges sent via speed post, courier, email attachments, or counter applications at the board offices will be rejected without any evaluation.

How to Calculate Expected Marks

Check the official exam scheme before calculating expected marks. Review the official notification document for the exact parameters regarding positive scoring additions and negative penalty fractions applicable specifically to the Stage II Graduate level examination structure.
