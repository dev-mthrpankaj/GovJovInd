How to Download Answer Key

  1. Visit the official UPPSC website at uppsc.up.nic.in.
  2. Open the View Answer Key option from the Candidates Help Desk or answer key area.
  3. Find the entry for Advertisement No. A-6/E-1/2025, Lecturer (Male/Female) Government Inter College Preliminary Examination 2025.
  4. Select the relevant paper or subject answer key such as General Studies, Hindi, English, Physics, Chemistry, Mathematics or other subject.
  5. Download or view the official answer key PDF from the UPPSC website.
  6. Save a copy for personal analysis and future reference.

How to Raise Objection

  1. Open the UPPSC official website and check whether an objection/challenge link is available for this answer key.
  2. If login is required, use the details asked by the official portal only.
  3. Select the question or answer you want to challenge, if the objection facility is provided.
  4. Upload proof or reference material only if required by UPPSC instructions.
  5. Pay the objection fee only if the official notice or portal asks for it.
  6. Submit the objection before the official deadline. Late objections may not be accepted as per normal exam rules.

Objection fee, mode and last date were not clearly available in the checked official listing. Candidates must verify these details from the official UPPSC notice or portal before submitting any objection.

How to Calculate Expected Marks

Download the official answer key for your subject and compare it with the answers you marked in the examination. If a response sheet is available, match your marked options with the official key question by question.

Check the official exam scheme before calculating expected marks. Do not assume marking scheme, negative marking, normalization, qualifying marks or cut-off from unofficial sources unless the official UPPSC notice confirms it.

  • Use only the official answer key for answer comparison.
  • Apply the official marking scheme from the recruitment/exam notice.
  • Do not treat unofficial expected cut-off as final.
  • Wait for UPPSC final answer key and result for confirmed status.
