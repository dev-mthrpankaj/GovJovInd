About This Answer Key

The Bihar Public Service Commission (BPSC) has initiated the answer key process for recruitment to the posts of Stenographer (आशुलिपिक) under Advertisement Number 01/2026. The provisional solutions for the General Knowledge subject paper have been made public. Registered candidates who appeared in this competitive objective examination can access the portal to analyze their responses against the board's provisional determinations.

This answer key release operates as a provisional update, inviting evaluation from the exam participants. If any candidate detects an error or finds discrepancies in the official tentative keys, they are permitted to challenge the question paper layout online. Every registered objection must be fully backed by verified documentary proof and academic references to meet the review threshold of the BPSC expert team.

Candidates must carefully check all timelines. Objections filed past the deadline or through standard offline mail, speed post, or manual hand delivery will not be considered under any circumstances. Ensure all proofs are clean and correctly linked to the specific question code before finishing your challenge entry.

How to Download BPSC Stenographer Answer Key

  1. Go to the official portal of Bihar Public Service Commission at bpsc.bih.nic.in.
  2. Locate the notification link titled "Provisional Answer Key: General Knowledge – Exam Advt. No. 01/2026" on the homepage.
  3. Click the PDF link to download and look at the official tentative answer key booklet.
  4. Match the answers given in the PDF with your exam shift's question paper series.
  5. Keep a copy of the downloaded PDF on your device for marking records.

How to Raise Objection

  1. Visit the official online registration site at onlinebpsc.bihar.gov.in.
  2. Enter your registered Username and Password to log in to your personal dashboard.
  3. Locate the specific "Objection/Challenge Link" provided for Advertisement No. 01/2026.
  4. Select the specific question number you want to contest from the General Knowledge paper.
  5. Upload valid, clear, and authentic source proofs or document evidence (प्रामाणिक साक्ष्य / स्रोत) for your claim.
  6. Pay the required processing fee of INR 250 for each distinct question challenged using the online payment gateway.
  7. Verify all entries and submit your objections before the final deadline on 12 July 2026.

Important Note: Any objection submitted without authentic source materials, or received after the closing date, will be rejected immediately without review.

Marking Evaluation Rules

Check the official exam scheme before calculating expected marks. Ensure you follow the official marking parameters and weightages set for the General Knowledge objective paper under Advertisement No. 01/2026 before estimating your final written test standing.

    Frequently Asked Questions

    Is the BPSC Stenographer Answer Key 2026 released?

    Yes. BPSC uploaded the provisional answer key for the General Knowledge subject paper on 08 July 2026.

    What is the final deadline to submit objections against the key?

    The online link to raise objections will remain active from 08 July 2026 to 12 July 2026.

    How much is the objection fee for the BPSC Stenographer key?

    Candidates must pay a processing fee of INR 250 for each question or answer key challenged.

    What parameters are required to login and submit questions objections?

    Candidates need to log in to their registration portal using their designated Username and Password credentials.

    Can I mail my objection documents using offline post?

    No. Objections are accepted strictly through the online candidate dashboard login. Offline applications will not be processed.

    Which subject paper can be checked under this notice?

    This update applies strictly to the written exam's General Knowledge (सामान्य ज्ञान) paper under Advt No. 01/2026.

    What sort of documents must be uploaded during challenges?

    Candidates must supply clear, verified documentary references, book sources, or authentic proof (प्रामाणिक साक्ष्य) along with their entry.

    When will the final revised answer key be published?

    The final answer keys will be compiled and announced after the expert board finishes evaluating all valid student challenges.
