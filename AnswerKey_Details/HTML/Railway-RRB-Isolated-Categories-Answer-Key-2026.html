About This Answer Key

The Railway Recruitment Board (RRB) has successfully issued the answer key and candidate response sheets for the Railway RRB Isolated Categories Various Posts under Centralized Employment Notice (CEN) 08/2025. This recruitment drive aims to fill 312 vacancies for positions including Junior Translator Hindi, Chief Law Assistant, Staff & Welfare Inspector, Lab Assistant Grade 3, and other isolated posts.

The Computer Based Test (CBT) was held nationwide on 14th and 15th July 2026. Through the released response sheet, candidates can cross-examine their marked options against the official master answer keys provided by RRB. This offers complete transparency before the publication of final results.

Candidates who identify any discrepancy or error in the official answer key can raise challenges online through the official candidate login link within the stipulated time frame as specified in the official RRB notice.

How to Download RRB Isolated Categories Answer Key

  1. Visit the candidate login portal or official website of your regional Railway Recruitment Board.
  2. Locate the link labeled "CEN 08/2025 Answer Key / Candidate Response Sheet".
  3. Enter your Registration Number and User Password / Date of Birth in the login credentials box.
  4. Click on the Login button to access your dashboard.
  5. View your attempted question paper, response sheet, and the official answer key.
  6. Download and save the PDF copy of your response sheet for future reference.

How to Raise Objections

  1. Log in to the official RRB objection portal using your registration credentials.
  2. Navigate to the "Raise Objection" section on the candidate dashboard.
  3. Select the specific Question ID and Option ID you wish to challenge.
  4. Upload valid documentary proof or reference standard justification supporting your claim.
  5. Pay the required objection fee (if applicable) per challenged question through the online payment gateway.
  6. Submit the challenge form and save the submission acknowledgment receipt.

Note: Objections submitted without proper documentation or past the official closing deadline will not be considered by RRB.

How to Calculate Expected Marks

Candidates can calculate their estimated scores by following the official RRB exam scheme:

  • Verify your marked answers against the correct answers indicated in the official key.
  • Check the official recruitment notification (CEN 08/2025) for specific positive marking and negative marking rules applicable to your post stage.
  • Check the official exam scheme before calculating final expected marks to ensure accurate self-evaluation.
