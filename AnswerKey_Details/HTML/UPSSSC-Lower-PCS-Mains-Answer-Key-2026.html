How to Download UPSSSC Answer Key

  1. Visit the official portal of UPSSSC at upsssc.gov.in[cite: 1].
  2. Look for the 'Notice Board' or 'Answer Key' section on the homepage.
  3. Click on the link corresponding to "Combined Lower Subordinate Mains Exam 2026 Answer Key / Notice".
  4. The Answer Key PDF file and notification will open on your screen[cite: 1].
  5. Download the PDF file and save it to your local device for personal analysis.
  6. Check official instructions carefully regarding the objection window.

How to Raise Objection

If you find any discrepancy in the officially released answer key, UPSSSC typically provides an objection window. Follow these general steps:

  1. Login to the official UPSSSC portal using your Registration Number and Date of Birth.
  2. Select the specific question paper set and the Question Number you wish to challenge.
  3. Upload valid documentary evidence/proof supporting your claim.
  4. Pay the requisite objection fee (if applicable) through the online payment gateway.
  5. Submit your representation before the official deadline. Check the official website for detailed objection rules.

How to Calculate Expected Marks

Check the official exam scheme before calculating expected marks:

  • Refer to the official marking pattern prescribed in the Combined Lower Subordinate Exam 2026 syllabus notice[cite: 1].
  • Award positive marks for every correct response as per the exam scheme.
  • Deduct the negative marking penalty for incorrect answers, if applicable.
  • Calculate total score = (Total Correct Marks) - (Total Penalty Marks).
