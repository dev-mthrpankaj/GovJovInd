About This Answer Key

The Rajasthan Staff Selection Board has released master question papers and preliminary answer keys for the Lab Assistant recruitment examinations. A preliminary answer key allows candidates to compare the board's initial answers with their own attempted answers before the final evaluation process.

RSSB has also provided an objection window for candidates who find any issue in a question or in its answer. Candidates should use the uploaded master question paper sequence while submitting objections because the notice states that question order in uploaded master papers may differ from the set provided during the exam.

The objection process is time-bound and fee-based. Candidates should submit objections only through the mode applicable to them and should attach valid standard/authentic book proof wherever required by the board.

How to Download RSSB Lab Assistant Answer Key

  1. Visit the official Rajasthan Staff Selection Board website at rssb.rajasthan.gov.in.
  2. Open the Lab Assistant 2026 answer key or master question paper notice link.
  3. Select the relevant exam or paper code such as 6Y89, PA22 or ZX4D.
  4. Download or view the master question paper and preliminary answer key carefully.
  5. Use the uploaded master question paper sequence for personal checking and objection reference.

How to Raise Objection

  1. Open the online objection link available on the RSSB website during the notified period.
  2. Login through SSO ID or the details required by the objection portal.
  3. Select the correct exam, paper and question number according to the uploaded master question paper.
  4. Select the objection type and add remarks wherever required.
  5. Upload valid standard/authentic book proof if the portal asks for supporting evidence.
  6. Pay INR 100 per question through the available e-Mitra payment gateway or e-Mitra kiosk option.
  7. Submit the objection before 18 June 2026 at 23:59. Late objections will not be accepted.

The notice states that online objections will be accepted only once and objections sent after the deadline or through any other medium will not be accepted.

Offline Objection Instructions

  • Offline objections are mentioned only for candidates who submitted offline applications and appeared in the examination.
  • Such candidates can submit objections in the prescribed format at the board office reception counter within the notified offline window.
  • The notice mentions a postal order of INR 100 per question in the name of Secretary, Rajasthan Staff Selection Board, Durgapura, Jaipur.
  • Candidates should follow the prescribed offline format and attach required proof as stated in the notice.

How to Calculate Expected Marks

The provided answer key notice does not mention the detailed marking scheme, negative marking rule or score calculation formula. Candidates should check the official exam scheme before calculating expected marks.

  • Use the official preliminary answer key only for answer matching.
  • Check the recruitment exam scheme before adding marks or applying negative marking.
  • Do not treat expected marks as final because objections and final answer key review may change the final evaluation.
