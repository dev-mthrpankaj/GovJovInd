About This Answer Key

The Union Public Service Commission conducts the Combined Medical Services Examination (CMSE) annually to recruit Medical Officers in central government health services, municipal corporations, and railways. Following the successful conduct of the written examination, UPSC releases the official provisional answer keys for candidates to verify their answers.

The provisional answer key allows candidates to cross-examine their attempted answers against the official key provided by subject matter experts. Candidates can review Paper 1 (General Medicine and Pediatrics) and Paper 2 (Surgery, Obstetrics & Gynecology, Preventive & Social Medicine) solution keys.

If candidates notice any discrepancy in questions or answer keys, UPSC provides an online window through its Online Question Paper Representation Portal (QPRA). The final evaluation and merit preparation are carried out after reviewing valid candidate representations.

How to Download UPSC CMS Answer Key 2026

  1. Visit the official website of the Union Public Service Commission at upsc.gov.in.
  2. Navigate to the Examination or What's New section on the homepage.
  3. Click on the link titled "Provisional Answer Key: Combined Medical Services Examination 2026".
  4. Open and download the PDF file corresponding to your paper (e.g., General Medicine & Pediatrics).
  5. Compare your marked responses with the official key provided in the PDF.
  6. Save or print a copy of the answer key PDF for personal evaluation.

How to Raise Objections / Submit Representations

  1. Visit the official UPSC portal at upsconline.nic.in.
  2. Click on the Online Question Paper Representation Portal (QPRA) link.
  3. Enter your Roll Number, Date of Birth, and the Captcha code shown on screen.
  4. Select the paper, question number, and set code against which you wish to submit a representation.
  5. Upload supporting valid references or documentary proof in the prescribed PDF/image format.
  6. Submit the representation within seven days of the opening of the QPRA portal.

Representations received through email, post, or hand delivery will not be entertained by UPSC.

How to Calculate Expected Marks

Candidates can calculate their estimated written exam score using the official UPSC marking scheme:

  • Each paper carries a total weightage as prescribed in the official exam scheme (typically 250 marks per paper).
  • Award the full prescribed marks for every correct response matched with the official answer key.
  • Deduct one-third (0.33) of the marks assigned to that question for every incorrect answer.
  • Unattempted questions carry zero marks and no negative penalty.

Check the official notification for exact subject-wise mark weightage before computing final scores.
