Step by step guide

How to use Rank Predictor after your exam

First submit your candidate and exam details. Later, check your rank again using the same exam, roll number, mobile number and date of birth.

Submit your data

  1. 1Open Rank Predictor Page

    Go to the GovJobUpdates Rank Predictor page.

  2. 2Select Your Exam

    Choose the correct exam before filling the form.

  3. 3Enter Candidate Details

    Add the candidate details requested in the form.

  4. 4Enter Exam Details

    Fill exam date, shift and other exam-specific details.

  5. 5Subject-wise Attempts

    Enter subject-wise attempted, right and wrong answers.

  6. 6Accept Consent

    Review the details and accept the consent checkbox.

  7. 7Submit Data

    Submit your record for expected rank prediction.

Check your rank again

  1. 1Select Exam

    Choose the same exam used during submission.

  2. 2Enter Roll Number, Mobile and DOB

    Use your roll number, mobile number and date of birth to find your record.

  3. 3Check Rank

    Submit the lookup form to view your expected rank again.

Student questions

GovJobUpdates FAQs

Quick clarity before using updates, quiz, rank predictor and live test tools.

Is GovJobUpdates a government website?

No. GovJobUpdates is an independent exam information and preparation platform. Always verify final details from the official authority website.

What can students do here?

Students can check jobs, admit cards, answer keys, results, quiz practice, rank prediction, document tools, UP services and live test details.

Is Rank Predictor an official result?

No. It gives an estimated rank or chance based on submitted or available data. Final result and selection are decided only by the official authority.

How should I use quiz and performance tools?

Use timed quizzes to practice speed and accuracy, then review performance history to identify weak subjects and improve your revision plan.
