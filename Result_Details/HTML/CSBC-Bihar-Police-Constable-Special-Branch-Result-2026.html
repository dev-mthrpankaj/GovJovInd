About This Result

The Central Selection Board of Constable (CSBC) conducted the written examination for the Bihar Police Constable General Duty Special Branch (Advt No: 01/2026) on 24 June 2026[cite: 2]. The official result for this examination has now been released on 18 August 2026[cite: 2].

The released result is a list of shortlisted candidates who have successfully qualified the written examination. These candidates are now eligible to appear for the Physical Efficiency Test (PET) and Physical Standard Test (PST).

Candidates must carefully search for their roll numbers in the official Result PDF to confirm their selection. It is highly advised to keep the result printout and original documents ready for the subsequent phases of the recruitment process.

How to Check Result

  1. Visit the official CSBC website at csbc.bihar.gov.in[cite: 2].
  2. On the homepage, navigate to the "Results" section or check the latest updates under the relevant tab.
  3. Click on the link for "CSBC Bihar Police Constable GD Special Branch Result 2026"[cite: 2].
  4. A PDF file containing the list of shortlisted candidates will open on your screen.
  5. Use the search function (Ctrl + F) to find your Roll Number carefully.
  6. Save or print the result PDF for future reference and next-stage requirements.

What After Result

Candidates who have successfully qualified in the written examination will proceed to the next stages of the recruitment process based only on official information:

  • PET/PST: Physical Efficiency Test and Physical Standard Test measuring endurance and physical parameters.
  • Document Verification (DV): Verification of all educational, identity, and category claims made in the application.
  • Medical Examination: To ensure the candidate meets the medical standards required for the police forces.

Documents Required for Next Stage

  • Original Admit Card
  • Printout of the Application Form
  • Result/Scorecard printout highlighting the roll number
  • Valid Photo ID proof (Aadhaar Card, Voter ID, PAN Card, etc.)
  • 10+2 Intermediate Exam Certificate and Marksheets[cite: 2]
  • Caste/Category certificate (if applicable)
  • Domicile certificate (if applicable)
  • Recent passport-size colour photographs
  • Other documents mentioned in the official CSBC notice
