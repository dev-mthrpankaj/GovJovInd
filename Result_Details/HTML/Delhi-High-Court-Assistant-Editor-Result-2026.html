How to Check Your Result

  1. Visit the official High Court of Delhi website at delhihighcourt.nic.in.
  2. Navigate to the "Public Notices" or "Recruitment Results" section on the homepage.
  3. Find the notice for the Assistant Editor Examination 2025 written test result.
  4. Refer to the file named Delhi High court Result.pdf[cite: 1].
  5. Open the downloaded file and use the search feature to find your roll number or name carefully.
  6. Save and print the result document for your future selection stages.

Cut Off & Merit Details

The official cut-off marks corresponding to the different categories will be updated once formally released by the High Court of Delhi. What After the Written Test Result?

  • Document Verification: Shortlisted candidates must present their original educational and identity documents.
  • Interview Stage: Qualified candidates will be called for a personal interview as part of the final evaluation.
  • Final Merit List: A final selection list will be drawn based on the combined performance of the written test and the interview.
  • The exact dates and venue for the upcoming stages will be declared via a separate notification on the official portal.

Documents Required for the Next Stage

  • Printed copy of the Examination Admit Card
  • Hard copy of the Application Form
  • Result/Scorecard printout (if available)
  • Valid Photo ID proof (Aadhar Card, Voter ID, PAN Card, etc.)
  • All original Educational Certificates & Marksheets
  • Caste / Category certificate (if claiming reservation)
  • Passport size recent photographs
  • Other specific documents as mentioned in the official call letter
