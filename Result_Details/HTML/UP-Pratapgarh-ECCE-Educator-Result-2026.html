About This Result

The District Basic Education Officer (BSA), Pratapgarh recently conducted a recruitment drive for 180 ECCE Educator vacancies. These posts were introduced to enhance early childhood care and education at the foundational level. The recruitment process was managed on an outsourcing basis (e.g., via Rojgaar Sangam and authorized agencies).

Earlier, a randomized list of 540 shortlisted candidates was published, and they were called for Document Verification and Interviews between 06 July and 10 July 2026. Following the thorough assessment of eligibility criteria, minimum marks, and home science/NTT qualifications, the administration has compiled the final merit list.

Candidates who successfully cleared the DV round can now find their names on the final selection list released on 13 August 2026. This final merit list serves as the basis for upcoming school allotments within the Pratapgarh district blocks.

How to Check Result & Merit List

  1. Visit the official NIC website of District Pratapgarh at pratapgarh.nic.in.
  2. Navigate to the "Notice" or "Recruitment" section from the main menu.
  3. Click on the link titled "Serial list for school allotment / Final Selection of 180 ECCE Educator posts".
  4. Download the provided PDF file containing the names and details of the selected candidates.
  5. Open the PDF and carefully search for your Name, Father's Name, or Registration Number.
  6. Save the PDF and take a printout for future correspondence during school joining.

Result Details Mentioned

The officially released PDF merit list typically includes the following important candidate particulars:

  • Serial Number / Rank
  • Candidate Name
  • Father's / Husband's Name
  • Category (UR/OBC/SC/ST)
  • Registration / Sewayojan Application Number
  • Educational Qualification (Graduation / NTT / DPSE)
  • Allotted Block / School Name (if released concurrently)
  • Remarks (Provisional status, if any)

What After Result: Joining & Allotment

The release of the final merit list indicates that the assessment phase is complete. Based on official norms:

  • School Allotment: The Basic Education Department will publish a detailed roster assigning blocks and specific schools to the selected 180 candidates.
  • Joining Letter: Candidates will either receive their appointment letters via email/post or they may have to collect them directly from the BSA Office, Pratapgarh.
  • Reporting: Appointees must report to their assigned Headmaster or Block Education Officer (BEO) on the scheduled date to formally commence duties.
  • If any discrepancies are found in a candidate's background verification later on, the department reserves the right to cancel their selection.

Documents Required for Next Stage

While reporting for final joining or school allotment, candidates should carry multiple self-attested sets of the following documents along with the originals:

  • Printout of the Final Merit List showing your name
  • Valid Photo ID Proof (Aadhaar Card, PAN Card, or Voter ID)
  • All Academic Certificates and Marksheets (10th, 12th, Graduation in Home Science)
  • Professional Training Certificates (NTT / DPSE Diploma)
  • Caste Certificate (if claiming reservation)
  • Domicile Certificate of Uttar Pradesh
  • Medical Fitness Certificate from CMO/District Hospital
  • Recent Passport Size Photographs
  • Character Certificate from last attended institution or Gazetted Officer

Is the UP Pratapgarh ECCE Educator Result 2026 released?

Where can I check the final selection list?

What is the next stage after the ECCE Educator result?

Yes, a standard medical fitness certificate issued by a competent medical authority is generally mandatory during the joining process for educational posts.
