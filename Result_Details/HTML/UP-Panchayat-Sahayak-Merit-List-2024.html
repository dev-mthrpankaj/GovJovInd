About This Merit List

The Uttar Pradesh Panchayati Raj Department invited offline applications for 4821 posts of Panchayat Sahayak, Accountant Cum Data Entry Operator (DEO) from 15th June to 30th June 2024. Since this recruitment did not involve a written exam, candidate selection is strictly based on an eligibility list prepared in descending order of the average percentage of marks obtained in High School (10th) and Intermediate (12th) examinations.

The preparation of the merit lists was scheduled between 7th July and 14th July 2024. These lists are generally displayed at the local Gram Panchayat offices or Block Development offices. If two candidates secured equal marks, preference is given to the candidate who is older in age.

Shortlisted candidates should expect the issuance of appointment letters by their respective Gram Panchayats. Please refer to UP GramPanchayat.pdf and local authorities for your final verification and joining details.

How to Check Your Selection Status

  1. Since the application process was offline, the final merit lists are typically not uploaded centrally on the internet.
  2. Visit your respective Gram Panchayat Office or Block Development Office.
  3. Ask the Gram Pradhan, Panchayat Secretary, or Block officials for the Panchayat Sahayak Merit List 2024.
  4. Search for your name, father's name, and average percentage on the official notice board.
  5. If you are selected as the top candidate, collect official information regarding your joining date and appointment letter.
  6. Review the UP GramPanchayat.pdf to understand joining instructions carefully.

Selection Process and Tie-Breaking Rules

  • Merit Calculation: Selection is based entirely on the average percentage of marks obtained in Class 10th and Class 12th.
  • Tie-Breaker 1 (Age): If the marks of two candidates are found to be equal, the older candidate will be given preference in selection.
  • Tie-Breaker 2 (Application Timing): If both educational marks and age limit are exactly the same, the candidate who submitted their application first will get the preference.
  • Candidates must be residents of the exact same Gram Panchayat from where they have applied.

What After Merit List (Next Stage)

Once the merit list is finalized and approved by the administrative committee, the next stage involves the issuance of joining letters. According to the official timeline, the Gram Panchayats will issue the appointment letters to the selected candidates between 22nd July and 24th July 2024. Selected candidates should be prepared to join duty immediately and sign the required contract with the Gram Panchayat.

Documents Required for Joining

  • Copy of the submitted Application Form
  • Original High School (10th) Marksheet and Certificate
  • Original Intermediate (12th) Marksheet and Certificate
  • Valid Photo ID Proof (Aadhaar Card, Voter ID, etc.)
  • Caste/Category Certificate (if applicable)
  • Domicile / Resident Certificate proving residency in the same Gram Panchayat
  • Recent Passport size photographs
  • Any other document mentioned by the Gram Panchayat or UP GramPanchayat.pdf

Frequently Asked Questions

Is UP Panchayat Sahayak Merit List 2024 released?

Yes, the merit list preparation was scheduled from 7th July to 14th July 2024 and lists are finalized at the Gram Panchayat level.

Where to check the UP Panchayat Sahayak merit list?

Candidates can check the merit list offline at their respective Gram Panchayat Office, Block Development Office, or District Panchayat Raj Officer Office.

How is the merit list prepared?

The merit list is prepared in descending order based on the average percentage of marks obtained in High School (10th) and Intermediate (12th) examinations.

What happens if two candidates have the same marks?

If two candidates have equal marks, the older candidate is given preference. If age is also the same, the candidate who applied first gets preference.

What is the next stage after the merit list?

Selected candidates will be issued appointment letters by their respective Gram Panchayats between 22nd July and 24th July 2024.

How many posts were available?

There were a total of 4821 vacancies for the post of Panchayat Sahayak, Accountant Cum Data Entry Operator DEO.

Is there any interview or written exam?

No, the selection is based entirely on academic merit and no written exam or interview is conducted.

Where can I find more instructions?

Candidates can review the official UP GramPanchayat.pdf or contact their local Block Development Office.
