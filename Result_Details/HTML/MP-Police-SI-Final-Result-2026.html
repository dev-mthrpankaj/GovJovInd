About This Result

The Madhya Pradesh Employees Selection Board (MPESB) successfully compiled the complete multi-stage evaluation process for the Subedar and Sub-Inspector Recruitment Test 2025. Following the successful completion of the Phase-I preliminary assessments, the Phase-II Main Examination was executed seamlessly across designated centers including Bhopal and Jabalpur on 29 April 2026. The initial phase outcomes were systematically made available on 18 May 2026.

Subsequent physical testing (PPT/PMT) and interview procedures led to the processing of the ultimate performance database. The final selection matrix has been drafted strictly in compliance with the state regulations, separating the main 87% structure (including the integrated 14% OBC standard quota alignment). Equal mark scenarios have been systematically resolved via age seniority parameters, positioning older candidates higher in alignment with the official rulebook guidelines.

Candidates can log into the selection board's official server framework utilizing their primary application keys to inspect individual detailed score sheets, merit tracking rankings, and definitive post allotment designations.

What Follows the Final Result Declaration?

As the final compilation metrics are entirely generated and verified by the MPESB, the complete operational records database is routed safely to the Police Headquarters (PHQ), Home (Police) Department of Madhya Pradesh. The subsequent administrative tasks will be handled directly through department coordination channels:

  • Medical Screening Board: All selected candidates must clear the departmentally prescribed physical fitness medical checks.
  • Document Attestation Check: Verification of original educational proofs, category declarations, and local residence certifications.
  • Character Assessment: Formal processing of background checks and police verification protocols.
  • Allotment Orders: Formal distribution of joining dates and placement training assignments.

Essential Documents for Joining Formalities

  • Printed copy of the official MPESB Final Selection Scorecard & Allotment Notice.
  • Original online application submission document alongside phase admit cards.
  • Class 10th & 12th official Board Marksheets and passing certifications.
  • Graduation degree or matching valid educational stream verification proofs.
  • Valid Domicile Certificate confirming Madhya Pradesh local residency status.
  • Recent caste validation certificates issued by authorized competent state bodies.
  • Recent color passport photographs matching the application records pattern.

Frequently Asked Questions

Is the MP Police SI Final Result 2026 declared?

Yes, the Madhya Pradesh Employees Selection Board (MPESB) officially declared the final result for the Subedar & Sub-Inspector Recruitment Test 2025 on 7 July 2026.

Where can I check the MP Police Subedar & SI Final Result?

Candidates can check their individual results and download the merit list PDFs on the official website of MPESB at esb.mp.gov.in.

What credentials are required to check the MP Police SI scorecard?

You will need your Application Number, Date of Birth, the first two letters of your mother's name, and the last four digits of your Aadhaar card number.

On what basis is the MP Police SI Final Merit List prepared?

The final selection list is prepared based on the cumulative marks obtained by candidates in the Written Exam (Mains), Physical Proficiency Test (PPT), and the Interview.

What is the next stage after the declaration of the MP Police SI final result?

Successfully selected candidates will be called by the Police Headquarters (PHQ), Madhya Pradesh, for final document verification, medical examination, and issuance of appointment letters.
