About This Result

The Madhya Pradesh Employees Selection Board (MPESB) successfully conducted the Pre Nursing Selection Test (PNST) 2026 as a Computer Based Test (CBT) starting on 10 July 2026 across major cities in Madhya Pradesh including Bhopal, Indore, Jabalpur, Ujjain, Ratlam, Rewa, Sagar, Satna, Neemuch, Sidhi, and Khandwa.

Following the release of the provisional answer key on 15 July 2026, MPESB has processed the final scores and declared the official MPESB PNST Result 2026 on 07 August 2026. The entrance examination serves as the gateway for admission into 4-year B.Sc Nursing degree courses offered by nursing institutions in MP.

Candidates can log into the official portal using their Application Number or Roll Number along with their Date of Birth and TAC Code to view their subject-wise normalized marks, merit rank, and qualifying status for state counselling.

How to Check MPESB PNST Result 2026

  1. Visit the official portal of MPESB at esb.mp.gov.in.
  2. Click on the Results tab or select the link titled "Result - Pre-Nursing Selection Test (PNST) - 2026".
  3. Enter your 13-digit Application Number or 8-digit Roll Number in the search box.
  4. Input your Date of Birth (DD/MM/YYYY) and First 2 Letters of Mother's Name + Last 4 Digits of Aadhar Card / TAC Code as instructed.
  5. Solve the mathematical captcha code displayed on the screen and click on Search.
  6. Your MPESB PNST 2026 scorecard displaying your subject-wise marks, total normalized score, and category merit rank will appear on screen.
  7. Download the scorecard PDF and print multiple copies for counselling and admission verification.

Details Mentioned on Scorecard

  • Candidate Name & Father's Name
  • Roll Number & Application Number
  • Category & Domicile Status (Madhya Pradesh Domicile)
  • Subject-wise Obtained Marks (Physics, Chemistry, Botany, Zoology, English)
  • Normalized Final Score
  • Overall Rank & Category Wise Rank
  • Qualifying Status for MP B.Sc Nursing State Counselling
  • Important Instructions regarding Centralized Counselling & Seat Allocation

Cut Off & Merit List

The cut-off marks for MPESB PNST 2026 depend on total candidates appeared, difficulty level of various CBT exam shifts, and availability of seat intake across state government nursing colleges. Candidates scoring above the qualifying percentile will be invited to participate in state-level web counselling.

Official category-wise cut-off marks and opening/closing ranks will be published by the Medical Education Department / Public Health Department during the choice filling and seat allotment rounds.

What After Result — Counselling & Admission Process

After the declaration of the PNST 2026 result, qualified candidates must participate in the online admission counselling process:

  • Registration & Choice Filling: Register on the state medical education / MP Online counselling portal and fill choices of preferred nursing colleges.
  • Seat Allotment Result: Seats will be allotted based on PNST merit rank, category reservations, and candidate preferences.
  • Document Verification: Report to designated nodal centers with original educational certificates and category documents.
  • College Reporting & Admission Fee: Confirm admission by submitting required course fees and medical fitness certificates.

Documents Required for Admission Counselling

  • MPESB PNST 2026 Scorecard & Admit Card Printout
  • Class 10th Mark Sheet & Passing Certificate (Proof of Date of Birth)
  • Class 12th Mark Sheet & Certificate (PCB Stream)
  • Madhya Pradesh Permanent Domicile Certificate (M.P. Mool Niwas Patra)
  • Category / Caste Certificate (SC / ST / OBC / EWS), if applicable
  • Income Certificate for Reserved/EWS Candidates
  • Transfer Certificate (TC) & Migration Certificate
  • Valid Photo ID Proof (Aadhaar Card, Voter ID, Passport, or DL)
  • Recent Passport Size Photographs (matching exam application)
  • Medical Fitness Certificate from a Registered Government Doctor
