About This Result

The Board of School Education Haryana (BSEH) conducted the Haryana Teacher Eligibility Test (HTET) 2025 for Level-1 (Primary), Level-2 (TGT), and Level-3 (PGT) on 04 and 05 July 2026. The official scorecard and marks have now been released.

This result serves as an eligibility certificate. Candidates who qualify the HTET examination will be eligible to apply for upcoming teacher recruitment vacancies in Haryana state schools. Candidates can check their qualifying status and subject-wise marks by downloading the scorecard.

Candidates must log in using their valid credentials on the official portal to download their certificates. It is advised to keep a printed copy of the scorecard safe for future document verification during teacher recruitment processes.

How to Check Result

  1. Visit the official portal of Haryana TET at htet.eapplynow.com or bseh.org.in.
  2. Click on the "Results" or "Download Scorecard/Certificate" section available on the homepage.
  3. Find and open the HTET 2025 Scorecard link.
  4. Enter your Application Number and Date of Birth, or Roll Number, as required.
  5. Complete any security verification and click Submit.
  6. Download the scorecard PDF and print it carefully for future recruitment use.

  • Marks Obtained in each section
  • Total Score

Cut Off / Passing Criteria

The HTET is a qualifying examination. The predefined passing criteria for different categories are as follows. Your exact status will be printed directly on your scorecard.

HTET Qualifying Marks Criteria
CategoryMinimum Qualifying Marks
General / OBC / Other State Candidates60% (90 Marks)
SC / PH Candidates (Haryana Domicile)55% (82 Marks)

What After Result

Candidates who have qualified the HTET will receive their official eligibility certificate. They are now eligible to apply for upcoming state government teacher vacancies in Haryana, including:

  • PRT (Primary Teacher - Level 1) vacancies
  • TGT (Trained Graduate Teacher - Level 2) vacancies
  • PGT (Post Graduate Teacher - Level 3) vacancies

Candidates must regularly check official state recruitment portals for upcoming vacancy notifications.

Documents Required for Next Stage

  • HTET Admit Card (for reference)
  • Printed copy of HTET Scorecard / Certificate
  • Valid Government Photo ID Proof (Aadhaar Card, PAN Card, Voter ID)
  • Original Educational Certificates (10th, 12th, Graduation, B.Ed/D.El.Ed)
  • Caste/Category Certificate (if applicable)
  • Haryana Domicile Certificate (if claiming state reservations)

Frequently Asked Questions

Is the HTET 2026 Scorecard released?

Yes. Board of School Education Haryana (BSEH) has released the HTET Scorecard on 22 August 2026.

How to download the HTET Scorecard 2026?

Candidates can download their HTET Scorecard from the official website or through the direct link provided on this page by logging in with their credentials.

What was the HTET 2026 exam date?

The Haryana Teacher Eligibility Test (HTET) 2025 was conducted on 04 and 05 July 2026.

What are the qualifying marks for HTET?

Generally, candidates need 60% marks to qualify, while SC/PH candidates from Haryana require 55% marks. Check your scorecard for the official qualifying status.

What is the next stage after qualifying HTET?

Qualified candidates will be awarded the HTET Certificate and will be eligible to apply for upcoming PRT, TGT, and PGT teacher recruitment in Haryana.

Where to check the HTET result?

You can check the result on the official website bseh.org.in or htet.eapplynow.com.

Is the HTET cut-off released?

HTET is a qualifying exam with predefined passing criteria rather than a traditional cut-off. Your qualifying status is mentioned directly on the scorecard.

What documents are required for the next stage?

You will need your printed HTET scorecard, educational certificates, category certificate, and ID proof when applying for upcoming teaching vacancies.
