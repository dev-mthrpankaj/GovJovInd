About IB Security Assistant Final Result 2026

The Intelligence Bureau, operating under the Ministry of Home Affairs (MHA), has successfully concluded the multi-tier selection process for recruiting 4987 eligible candidates for the post of Security Assistant/Executive. Following the Tier 1 objective exam, Tier 2 descriptive test, and the Tier 3 Interview/Personality Test held in June 2026, the final selection list has now been released.

The officially published MHA Result.pdf contains the roll numbers of the candidates who have been provisionally selected for the SA/Exe position. The roll numbers in the official list are arranged SIB-wise (Subsidiary Intelligence Bureaux) and numerical roll number-wise, not in order of merit.

Selected candidates will now move to the final joining formalities, which include thorough Character & Antecedent Verification and a Medical Examination. Those who clear these final checks will be granted official appointment letters by the Intelligence Bureau.

How to Check IB SA Final Result 2026

  1. Visit the official Ministry of Home Affairs website at mha.gov.in.
  2. Look for the 'What's New' or 'Vacancies' section on the homepage.
  3. Click on the link titled "Final Result of Security Assistant/Executive Exam 2025-26".
  4. Download the official MHA Result.pdf to your device.
  5. Open the PDF and use the search function (Ctrl+F or Mobile Search) to find your Roll Number.
  6. If your roll number is present under your applied SIB, you are provisionally selected. Save or print the PDF for future reference.

Details Mentioned in MHA Result.pdf

  • Name of the Recruiting Organization (Intelligence Bureau)
  • Name of the Examination and Post
  • Subsidiary Intelligence Bureaux (SIB) Name
  • Roll Numbers of Provisionally Selected Candidates
  • Important Instructions for the Next Stage (Medical/Verification)

Note: The merit list PDF currently displays roll numbers. The category-wise cut-off marks and individual candidate scorecards may be released separately by the MHA.

What After the Final Result?

Candidates whose roll numbers appear in the final selection list are selected "provisionally". The final appointment is subject to the following procedures:

  • Character and Antecedent Verification: A thorough background check will be conducted by the authorities.
  • Medical Examination: Candidates must clear the medical fitness standards as mandated by the Intelligence Bureau.
  • Document Verification: Re-verification of all original educational certificates, category certificates (SC/ST/OBC/EWS), and domicile certificates.

Candidates will receive further instructions regarding the medical examination schedule and venue via registered email/SMS or post from their respective SIBs.
