About This Result

The Oriental Insurance Company Limited (OICL) has announced the final results for the recruitment of Administrative Officers (AO) - Generalists and Hindi Officers. Following the completion of the main examination and subsequent selection stages, the final merit list has been officially published.

Candidates can now access their individual scorecards and check their qualifying status. The final result PDF contains the list of candidates shortlisted for the Pre-Recruitment Medical Examination. Candidates are advised to carefully check their roll numbers in the merit list.

Shortlisted candidates should regularly check their registered email IDs and the official OICL website for detailed instructions regarding the medical examination schedule and documentation requirements for joining.

How to Check Result & Scorecard

  1. Visit the official website of OICL at orientalinsurance.org.in.
  2. Navigate to the "Careers" section on the homepage.
  3. Click on the link for "Recruitment of AO - Final Result / Marks".
  4. To view the merit list, download the provided PDF and search for your roll number/name.
  5. To check your marks, click on the scorecard login link and enter your Registration Number/Roll Number and Password/DOB.
  6. Download and save your scorecard or result PDF for future reference.

What After Result

Candidates who have successfully cleared the final selection and made it to the merit list will have to undergo the following stages as per the official recruitment guidelines:

  • Pre-Recruitment Medical Examination: A mandatory medical fitness test to ensure candidates meet the physical standards required for the post.
  • Document Verification (DV): Original certificates verifying age, educational qualification, category, and identity will be scrutinized.
  • Joining & Training: Candidates declared medically fit and whose documents are verified will receive appointment letters followed by training.

Documents Required for Next Stage

  • System-generated printout of the valid Application Form
  • Printout of the OICL AO Scorecard/Result
  • Valid Photo ID proof (Aadhaar, PAN, Voter ID, Passport, etc.)
  • Original educational certificates and mark sheets (10th, 12th, Graduation, etc.)
  • Caste/Category certificate (if applicable) in the prescribed format
  • Disability (PwBD) certificate (if applicable)
  • Recent passport-size photographs
  • Any other documents specified in the official call letter or email
