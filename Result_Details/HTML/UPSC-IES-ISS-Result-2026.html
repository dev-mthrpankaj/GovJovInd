About This Result

The Union Public Service Commission (UPSC) successfully conducted the Indian Economic Service (IES) and Indian Statistical Service (ISS) Written Examination on 19 June 2026 across various exam centres in India. Based on their performance in the written test, qualified candidates have been shortlisted for the next phase of recruitment.

The released document is a written examination merit list containing roll numbers of qualified candidates. Those whose roll numbers appear on the list are eligible to appear for the Interview / Personality Test conducted by the Commission. Detailed schedules and call letters for the Interview will be made available on the official website in due course.

Candidates must carefully read the instructions in the official notice regarding the submission of the Detailed Application Form (DAF) and document verification before the interview round.

How to Check UPSC Result

  1. Visit the official portal of UPSC at upsc.gov.in.
  2. Navigate to the "Written Results" or "What's New" section on the homepage.
  3. Locate and click on the link for "Written Result: Indian Economic Service Examination 2026" or "Indian Statistical Service Examination 2026".
  4. The result PDF containing shortlisted candidates' roll numbers will open on your screen.
  5. Press Ctrl + F on keyboard (or use the PDF reader search icon) and type your Roll Number.
  6. Save and download the PDF file for future reference during the interview process.

Candidates can currently download the provisional merit list PDFs using the official direct links provided in the Important Links section below.

What After Result (Next Stage)

Candidates who have qualified in the written stage must participate in the following subsequent stages:

  • Interview / Personality Test: Shortlisted candidates must appear for the oral interview at the UPSC headquarters in New Delhi. The interview assesses general suitability, domain expertise, and analytical capability.
  • Document Verification (DV): Original certificates will be verified during the Personality Test stage.
  • Medical Examination: Candidates recommended after the interview stage undergo a medical assessment as specified by UPSC rules.
  • Final Merit List: Compiled based on total marks secured in the Written Examination and Personality Test combined.

Documents Required for Next Stage

  • Printout of E-Summon Letter / Interview Admit Card
  • Matriculation / Secondary School Certificate (Proof of Date of Birth)
  • Degree Certificate & Mark Sheets (Post Graduation for IES / Graduation/PG with Statistics for ISS)
  • Community / Caste Certificate (SC/ST/OBC/EWS) in prescribed format
  • Disability Certificate (for PwBD candidates)
  • Valid Photo Identity Proof (Aadhaar Card, Voter ID, Passport, PAN Card, etc.)
  • Two recent passport-size photographs

Frequently Asked Questions

Is the UPSC IES ISS Result 2026 declared?

Yes, Union Public Service Commission (UPSC) declared the written result for IES and ISS Exam 2026 on 04 August 2026.

What is the result date for UPSC IES ISS 2026?

The written exam result was declared officially on 04 August 2026.

Where can I check the UPSC IES ISS result PDF?

Candidates can download the official result PDFs directly from the UPSC official portal (upsc.gov.in) or through the download links provided on this page.

How do I search my roll number in the result PDF?

Open the downloaded IES or ISS result PDF, press Ctrl + F (or use the search bar on mobile), type your Roll Number, and press Enter to see if you are shortlisted.

What is the next stage after the written result?

Candidates shortlisted in the written examination will be called for the Interview / Personality Test conducted by UPSC, followed by Document Verification.

How many total posts are available under UPSC IES ISS 2026 recruitment?

There are a total of 44 posts, comprising 16 posts for Indian Economic Service (IES) and 28 posts for Indian Statistical Service (ISS).

When will the UPSC IES ISS cut-off and marks be released?

The official cut-off marks and individual mark sheets will be released on the UPSC website after the complete final result process is concluded.

What documents are required for the Interview / DV stage?

Candidates will need original certificates along with self-attested photocopies of Proof of Age, Educational Qualification Certificates, Caste/Category Certificate (if applicable), PwBD Certificate, and Photo ID proof.
