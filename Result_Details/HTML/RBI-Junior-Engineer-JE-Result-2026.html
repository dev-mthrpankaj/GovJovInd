About This Result

The Reserve Bank of India conducted the competitive Online Objective Examination for the recruitment of Junior Engineers in Civil and Electrical disciplines across various offices nationwide on June 6, 2026. Following thorough evaluation, the official zone-wise selection list was announced on July 14, 2026.

This result marks the completion of the first phase of selection. Candidates listed in the result sheets will have to clear the next phase, which is the Language Proficiency Test (LPT). The LPT tests local vernacular knowledge and is strictly qualifying in nature. Failing the test results in immediate disqualification regardless of objective marks.

Official communication regarding time slots, test venues, and precise details for the upcoming LPT and Document Verification will be released by respective regional offices in due course.

How to Check RBI JE Result 2026

  1. Navigate to the official web server of the Reserve Bank of India at rbi.org.in.
  2. Scroll down to the bottom panel and click on the 'Opportunities@RBI' or 'Recruitment Related Announcements' link.
  3. Find the notice labeled 'Result of Online Examination for Recruitment of Junior Engineer (Civil/Electrical) - 2026'.
  4. Open the separate links provided for Civil or Electrical discipline selection lists.
  5. Use the Ctrl+F functionality to lookup your Roll Number carefully within the PDF.
  6. Download and securely preserve a physical copy of the result layout for verification processes.

What After the Result (Next Selection Phase)

  • Language Proficiency Test (LPT): All shortlisted candidates must clear a local regional language evaluation test. This will check reading, writing, and speaking competencies. LPT is qualifying in nature.
  • Document Verification (DV): Candidates must prove educational credentials, age definitions, caste criteria, and required active experience records.
  • Medical Examination: Final selected lists will undergo clinical parameter checks prior to onboarding orders.

Documents Required for Verification

  • Printed layout copy of the Online Application Form.
  • Original Matriculation/10th standard certificate for date of birth verification.
  • Diploma or Degree Certificates in Civil/Electrical Engineering along with comprehensive semester mark sheets.
  • Experience Verification certificates (Minimum 2 years for Diploma holders, 1 year for Engineering Degree holders).
  • Valid Caste / Category / PwBD status certificates matching central government layouts (if applicable).
  • Recent passport size photographs identical to the one uploaded in application.
