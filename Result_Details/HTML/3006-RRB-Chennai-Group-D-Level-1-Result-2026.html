About This Result

RRB Chennai has published the CEN 08/2024 Level 1 result for candidates shortlisted to appear in the Physical Efficiency Test. The shortlist has been prepared on the basis of performance in the Computer Based Test conducted by RRBs from 27 November 2025 to 10 February 2026.

The official PDF contains 16-digit roll numbers of provisionally shortlisted candidates. The roll numbers are arranged horizontally in ascending order and are not shown in merit order, so candidates should search their roll number carefully in the official list.

After this CBT shortlist, the next activities such as PET, Document Verification, Medical Examination and panel publication will be managed by the concerned Railway Recruitment Cell. For RRB Chennai shortlisted candidates, future updates will be provided by RRC Chennai, Southern Railway.

How to Check RRB Chennai Group D Result

  1. Visit the official Railway Recruitment Board Chennai website at rrbchennai.gov.in.
  2. Open the CEN 08/2024 Level 1 result or PET shortlisted candidates notice.
  3. Download the roll number list PDF and cutoff PDF, if shown separately.
  4. Search your 16-digit roll number in the official PDF using the find/search option.
  5. For score and normalized marks, use the login link on the official RRB website with registration number and date of birth in DDMMYYYY format.
  6. Save or print the result notice and follow RRC Chennai for PET e-call letter and schedule updates.

What After Result

Shortlisted candidates will have to appear for the Physical Efficiency Test. The official notice states that PET schedule will be published by the Chairperson of the concerned Railway Recruitment Cell and candidates may also receive SMS/email on their registered mobile number and email address for downloading the PET e-call letter.

For RRB Chennai candidates, future communication for PET, Document Verification, Medical Examination and panel publication will be handled by RRC Chennai, Southern Railway. PwBD, ExSM and CCAA candidates are exempted from PET, and their separate shortlist will be published by the respective RRC as per the official notice.

Documents Required for Next Stage

  • PET e-call letter / admit card issued by RRC.
  • Online application form or registration details.
  • Valid photo ID proof such as Aadhaar card, voter ID, PAN card, passport or driving licence.
  • Educational qualification certificates and mark sheets.
  • Caste/category certificate, EWS certificate, PwBD certificate or ExSM documents, if applicable.
  • Domicile/residence certificate, if required in the official instructions.
  • Recent passport-size photographs.
  • Course Completed Act Apprentice certificate or other relevant certificates, if applicable.
  • Any other document mentioned in the official PET/DV notice.

Frequently Asked Questions

Is RRB Chennai Group D Level 1 Result 2026 released?

Yes. RRB Chennai has released the CEN 08/2024 Level 1 CBT result for candidates shortlisted for Physical Efficiency Test.

What is the result date for RRB Chennai Level 1 PET shortlist?

The cut-off notice is dated 01 July 2026. Candidates should verify the result write-up and roll number PDF from the official RRB Chennai website.

How can I check my RRB Chennai Group D result?

Download the official PET shortlist roll number PDF from the RRB Chennai website and search your 16-digit roll number in the list.

Are roll numbers arranged in merit order?

No. The official notice says roll numbers are arranged horizontally in ascending order and not in order of merit.

What is the next stage after RRB Chennai Group D result?

Shortlisted candidates will be called for PET. Document Verification, Medical Examination and panel publication will be carried out by the concerned Railway Recruitment Cell.

When can candidates view score and normalized marks?

The score and normalized marks link will be live on or before 01 July 2026 at 18:00 hrs and available up to 16 July 2026 at 23:55 hrs.

What is the official cutoff for RRB Chennai Level 1 PET shortlist?

The normalized cut off marks are UR 78.00694, SC 70.63490, ST 63.00000, OBC 76.65663 and EWS 71.11270.

Where will PET schedule be released?

The PET schedule and e-call letter updates will be published by RRC Chennai on its official website. SMS and email may also be sent to shortlisted candidates.

Are PwBD, ExSM and CCAA candidates included in the PET roll number list?

No. The official notice says PwBD, ExSM and CCAA candidates are exempted from PET, so their roll numbers are not included in this PET list.
