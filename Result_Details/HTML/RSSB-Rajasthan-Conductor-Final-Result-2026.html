About This Result

The Rajasthan Staff Selection Board (RSSB), formerly RSMSSB, had issued advertisement number 22/2024 for direct recruitment to 500 vacancies of Conductor (Parichalak) in the Rajasthan State Road Transport Corporation (RSRTC). The written examination was held on 06 November 2025, and initial exam results were released on 15 January 2026.

Following the completion of Document Verification (DV) for shortlisted applicants, RSSB has now finalized and declared the final merit list on 05 August 2026. A total of 429 candidates have been recommended for appointment, including 383 from Non-TSP areas and 46 from TSP areas.

The final selection list includes details such as candidate roll numbers, category-wise final cut-off scores, and guidelines for the remaining administrative steps. Candidates whose roll numbers feature in the selection list will soon receive formal appointment notifications from RSRTC.

How to Check RSSB Conductor Final Result 2026

  1. Visit the official portal of Rajasthan Staff Selection Board at rssb.rajasthan.gov.in.
  2. Click on the Results tab present on the main menu bar.
  3. Locate the link titled "Conductor Direct Recruitment 2024: Recommendation and Final Selection List".
  4. Download the Non-TSP or TSP Final Result PDF file to your device.
  5. Open the downloaded PDF file and press Ctrl + F (or search option in PDF viewer) to search your Roll Number.
  6. If your roll number appears in the list, you have been selected for final recommendation.
  7. Save or print a copy of the final result PDF for future reference during joining.

  • Roll numbers of finally selected candidates
  • Area designation (Non-TSP Sector / TSP Sector)
  • Category allocation (General, EWS, OBC, SC, ST, MBC, ESM, PwD)
  • Category-wise final cut-off marks
  • Details regarding withheld, provisional, or debarred candidates (if applicable)
  • Instructions regarding departmental joining and appointment dispatch

What After Final Result Declaration?

With the publication of the final result by RSSB, the role of the selection board is completed for recommended candidates. The next operational steps will be handled directly by the Rajasthan State Road Transport Corporation (RSRTC):

  • Document Check & Verification: Re-verification of original certificates and conductor license by RSRTC headquarters/divisional offices.
  • Medical Fitness Examination: Candidates must undergo a standard medical examination as prescribed by RSRTC rules.
  • Issuance of Appointment Letters: RSRTC will issue formal appointment orders with station/depot allotments.
  • Training & Induction: Joining candidates will undergo required departmental training prior to regular service deployment.

Documents Required During Joining

  • Printout of Application Form & Final Selection List PDF page
  • Original Conductor License and valid First Aid Certificate
  • 10th / Secondary School Certificate for Date of Birth proof
  • Educational qualification marksheets and certificates
  • Valid Category / Caste Certificate (OBC, EWS, SC, ST, MBC)
  • Domicile / Bonafide Resident Certificate of Rajasthan
  • Identity Proof (Aadhaar Card, Voter ID, or Driving License)
  • Character Certificates from required competent authorities
  • Recent passport-size photographs and medical fitness certificate
