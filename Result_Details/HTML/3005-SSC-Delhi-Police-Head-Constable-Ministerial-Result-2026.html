About This Result

The Staff Selection Commission conducted the Computer Based Examination for Head Constable (Ministerial) in Delhi Police Examination, 2025 from 7 January 2026 to 12 January 2026 at various centres across India. Since the examination was held in multiple shifts, SSC used normalized marks for processing the result.

The declared result is for shortlisting candidates to appear in the next stage: Physical Endurance & Measurement Test (PE&MT), Document Verification (DV), and Trade Test/Skill Test. The next stage will be conducted by Delhi Police, and its schedule will be communicated separately.

SSC has also stated that final answer keys, question papers cum response sheets, and marks of qualified/non-qualified candidates will be uploaded on the Commission website in due course.

How to Check SSC Result

  1. Visit the official Staff Selection Commission website at ssc.gov.in.
  2. Open the result section or latest result notice for Head Constable (Ministerial) in Delhi Police Examination, 2025.
  3. Download the official result PDF or write-up and the qualified candidates list, if separately provided.
  4. Search your roll number carefully in the official PDF/list.
  5. If shortlisted, follow Delhi Police website for PE&MT/DV/Trade Test admission certificate and schedule.
  6. Keep your result PDF, application details and identity documents ready for the next stage.

Next Stage Details

  • Shortlisted candidates will be called for PE&MT/DV/Trade Test.
  • The next stage will be conducted by Delhi Police.
  • The schedule will be communicated by Delhi Police in due course.
  • Candidates should follow delhipolice.gov.in for admission certificates.
  • NCC certificate and RRU degree/diploma bonus marks will be awarded in final result only to candidates who produce supporting documents at PE&MT stage.
  • Final answer key, response sheets and marks will be uploaded by SSC later.
