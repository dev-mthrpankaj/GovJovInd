About This Result

The Rajasthan Staff Selection Board (RSSB) successfully conducted the preliminary written examination for the recruitment of 259 Forester (Vanpal) posts on 28th June 2026[cite: 1]. The board has now released the official result and merit list for the same on 14th August 2026[cite: 1].

Candidates who appeared for the exam can download the provisional merit list PDF from the official website[cite: 1]. The PDF contains the roll numbers of the candidates who have qualified for the next stage of the selection process[cite: 1]. In addition to the merit list, the board has also published the category-wise cut-off marks[cite: 1].

Those who have successfully cleared the preliminary examination will be eligible to participate in the Physical Standard Test (PST) and Physical Efficiency Test (PET), which will be followed by Document Verification (DV) and a Medical Examination[cite: 1].

How to Check RSSB Forester Result

  1. Visit the official website of the Rajasthan Staff Selection Board at rssb.rajasthan.gov.in.
  2. Navigate to the "Results" section on the homepage.
  3. Look for the link that says "Forester 2026: Prelims Result & List of Provisionally Selected Candidates" and click on it.
  4. The official result PDF will open on your screen. You can download and save it.
  5. Use the search function (Ctrl+F) to find your Roll Number within the merit list carefully.
  6. Alternatively, you may log in to the portal using your Application Number and Date of Birth to view your scorecard.

Details Mentioned in the Result

  • Roll Numbers of provisionally selected candidates.
  • Category-wise cut-off marks for both TSP and Non-TSP areas.

What After Result?

  • Candidates whose roll numbers appear in the provisional merit list have qualified the written examination.
  • The next stage of the recruitment process is the Physical Standard Test (PST) and Physical Efficiency Test (PET).
  • After qualifying the physical tests, candidates will undergo Document Verification (DV) and a Medical Examination.
  • The detailed schedule and venue for the PST/PET will be announced by the RSSB shortly. Candidates are advised to keep a regular check on the official website.

Documents Required for Next Stage

  • Hard copy of the RSSB Forester Admit Card
  • Printout of the Result / Scorecard
  • Original and photocopies of Educational Certificates (10th/12th Marksheets)
  • Valid Caste / Category Certificate (if applicable)
  • Domicile Certificate (Mool Niwas)
  • Original valid Photo ID proof (Aadhar Card, Voter ID, PAN Card, etc.)
  • Recent passport-size photographs
  • Any other document mentioned in the official DV/PET notification.
