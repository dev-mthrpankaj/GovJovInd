About This Result

The Rajasthan Staff Selection Board (RSMSSB) carried out the offline written examination for the selection of Platoon Commanders under Advertisement Number 05/2025 on 22 November 2025. Following the evaluation and initial written updates declared on 17 January 2026, the department has finalized the allocation list.

This final result identifies the chosen candidates who successfully met all the required cut-off boundaries and structural norms. The selectees are designated for allocation across both TSP and Non-TSP segments as outlined in the initial rules document.

Candidates can access their definitive marks, check the qualified lists via the unified portal system, and proceed with checking their credentials against the verified final roster list released by the board authority.

How to Check RSSB Platoon Commander Final Result

  1. Visit the official portal of Rajasthan Staff Selection Board at rssb.rajasthan.gov.in.
  2. Click on the dedicated "Results" link visible on the homepage area.
  3. Locate the notification title: "Platoon Commander Direct Recruitment 2025 Final Result".
  4. Download the official selection list PDF file to your local drive.
  5. Open the PDF document and search for your specific Roll Number or Name carefully.
  6. Save or print out a hard copy of your final result details for validation during subsequent processes.
