About This Result

SSC has issued an additional result notice for the Head Constable (Ministerial) in Delhi Police Examination, 2025. This update is not a fresh recruitment notice; it is a supplementary shortlist connected with the already declared CBE result. Candidates who are included in the additional list will move to the next stage as per the official schedule.

The notice explains that some candidates who had applied under UR or EWS category submitted representations regarding non-inclusion in the result, stating that they should have been considered under the OBC from the NCT of Delhi category. After examination of such cases, SSC considered eligible claims while processing the additional result.

According to the notice, 51 additional candidates have been found eligible for shortlisting to appear in Physical Endurance & Measurement Test, Document Verification and Trade Test/Skill Test. Candidates must verify their roll number from the official SSC list and follow SSC/Delhi Police instructions for the next stage.

How to Check Result

  1. Visit the official Staff Selection Commission website at ssc.gov.in.
  2. Open the Result section or latest notice related to Delhi Police examination result.
  3. Download the official result write-up, additional candidates list or merit list PDF, if available separately.
  4. Search your roll number/name carefully in the official PDF or list.
  5. If shortlisted, follow the Delhi Police website and SSC instructions for PE&MT, Document Verification and Trade Test.
  6. Save or print the result PDF and keep your application details ready for the next stage.

What After Result

  • Shortlisted candidates should prepare for Physical Endurance and Measurement Test.
  • Document Verification will require original certificates and valid supporting documents.
  • Trade Test or Skill Test will be conducted as per the post-specific rules mentioned by the official authority.
  • The schedule, venue and admission certificate details should be verified from official SSC/Delhi Police updates.
  • Candidates whose details are not in the official list should avoid acting on unofficial screenshots or forwarded messages.

Documents Required for Next Stage

  • Printed result/shortlist PDF and admit card/admission certificate, if issued.
  • Online application form or registration details.
  • Valid photo identity proof such as Aadhaar card, voter ID, PAN card, passport or driving licence.
  • Class 10/12/degree certificates and marksheets as applicable to the post.
  • Caste/category certificate, OBC from the NCT of Delhi certificate, EWS certificate or other reservation documents, if claimed.
