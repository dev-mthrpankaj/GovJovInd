Monthly Stipend Structure Norms

As detailed under official structural announcements, engaged candidates will receive standard monthly stipends under fixed allocations:

  • Consolidated Monthly Stipend: Rs. 15,000/- per month flat rate.
  • Added Allowances Constraint: Apprentices are completely barred from drawing standard bank allowances, transport benefits, medical reimbursement, or alternative regular employee perks.
  • Attendance Deductions Rule: The final stipend compilation processed at the end of each operational block month will adjust automatically for loss-of-pay incidents or unscheduled leaves.
