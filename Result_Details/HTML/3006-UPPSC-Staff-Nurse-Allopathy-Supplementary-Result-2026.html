About This Result

UPPSC has issued a supplementary result notice for Staff Nurse Male/Female Allopathy Exam 2023 under Advertisement No. U-3/E-1/2023. The supplementary list has been released after cancellation of earlier provisional candidature in some cases, and fresh candidates have been provisionally selected against the resulting vacancies.

The notice states that the result is completely provisional. Candidates named in the supplementary list should not treat this as final appointment until UPPSC completes original certificate matching, verification and eligibility checks.

Candidates should regularly visit the official UPPSC website for any separate notice related to document verification, schedule, required certificates or further recommendation process.

What After Result

The next important step is original document matching and verification. The notice says recommendation will be sent to the government/department only after successful certificate verification and after candidates are found suitable as per rules.

  • Check UPPSC website for separate verification notice.
  • Keep all original certificates and photocopies ready.
  • Match personal details, category details and educational details with application records.
  • Do not rely on unofficial messages for verification date or venue.

Documents Required for Next Stage

  • UPPSC admit card / application form printout, if asked.
  • Valid photo ID proof and recent photographs.
  • High school, intermediate and nursing/educational qualification certificates.
  • Registration/council certificate, if required for the post.
  • Caste/category/EWS certificate, if applicable.
  • Domicile and other reservation-related documents, if applicable.
  • Any other document mentioned in the official UPPSC verification notice.
