About This Result

The Central Selection Board of Constable (CSBC) conducted the written examination for the recruitment of Bihar Police Constable Operators against Advt No. 02/2026 on 28 June 2026. Following the evaluation of the objective-type examination, the board has published the list of successful candidates on 18 August 2026.

This result constitutes the first stage of the selection process. Candidates whose roll numbers are mentioned in the official PDF have provisionally qualified to participate in the next stage, which includes the Physical Standard Test (PST) and Physical Efficiency Test (PET).

It is crucial for qualified candidates to download the result PDF, search for their roll numbers, and keep track of the upcoming official notices regarding the schedule and admit cards for the physical tests.

How to Check Result

  1. Visit the official Central Selection Board of Constable (CSBC) website at csbc.bihar.gov.in.
  2. On the homepage, navigate to the Results section or check the latest notice board.
  3. Click on the link titled "Result of Written Examination for the post of Constable Operator (Advt. 02/2026)".
  4. The result PDF containing the roll numbers of shortlisted candidates will open.
  5. Use the search function (Ctrl+F) and enter your Roll Number carefully.
  6. If your roll number is highlighted, you have qualified for the next stage. Save the PDF for future reference.

What After Result: PET & PST Details

Shortlisted candidates will now undergo Document Verification followed by a Physical Standard Test (PST) and Physical Efficiency Test (PET). Below are the official physical parameters required:

Documents Required for Next Stage

Candidates must carry original documents along with photocopies when appearing for the PET/PST stage. Expected documents include:

  • Admit Card for PET/PST
  • Written Exam Result/Scorecard print
  • Valid Photo ID Proof (Aadhaar, Voter ID, PAN Card, etc.)
  • Matriculation (10th) Certificate for Age Proof
  • Intermediate (12th Science/PCM) Marksheet and Certificate
  • Caste/Category Certificate (if applicable)
  • Domicile Certificate (for Bihar residents)
  • Recent Passport size photographs
  • Other documents mentioned in the official notice
