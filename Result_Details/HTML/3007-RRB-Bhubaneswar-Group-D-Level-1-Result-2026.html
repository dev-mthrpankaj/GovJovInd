About This Result

RRB Bhubaneswar has published the Level 1 result list for CEN 08/2024 after the Computer Based Test conducted by RRBs from 27 November 2025 to 10 February 2026. The PDF contains 16-digit roll numbers of candidates provisionally shortlisted for the Physical Efficiency Test.

The notice explains that candidates equal to three times the community-wise total vacancies, excluding PwBD, ExSM and CCAA candidates shortlisted in that community, have been shortlisted for PET. The roll numbers are arranged in ascending order horizontally, not in merit order.

Candidates can also check percentile score and normalized marks by logging in through the link provided on official RRB websites using registration number and date of birth in DDMMYYYY format. Further PET and next-stage communication will be issued by RRC Bhubaneswar, East Coast Railway.

What After Result

After this result, provisionally shortlisted candidates will have to appear for the Physical Efficiency Test. The PET schedule will be published by RRC Bhubaneswar, East Coast Railway on the official RRC website, and SMS/email may also be sent to the registered mobile number and email address of shortlisted candidates for downloading the PET e-call letter.

  • PET will be conducted by the concerned Railway Recruitment Cell.
  • PwBD, ExSM and CCAA candidates are exempted from PET; their lists will be published separately by the respective RRCs.
  • Document verification, medical examination and panel publication will be handled by the concerned RRC.
  • Shortlisting is provisional and does not guarantee appointment in Railways.

Documents Required for Next Stage

  • PET e-call letter / admit card issued by the concerned RRC
  • Original photo identity proof such as Aadhaar card, voter ID, PAN card, passport or driving licence

  • Recent passport size photographs and other documents mentioned in the official call letter

Frequently Asked Questions

Is RRB Bhubaneswar Group D Result 2026 released?

Yes. RRB Bhubaneswar has published the provisional list of candidates shortlisted for PET for CEN 08/2024 Level 1 posts.

What is the next stage after this result?

The next stage is Physical Efficiency Test followed by document verification, medical examination and panel publication by the concerned Railway Recruitment Cell.

How can candidates check their roll number?

Candidates should download the official result PDF and search their 16-digit roll number in the shortlisted list.

Can candidates check score and normalized marks?

Yes. Candidates can log in through the link on official RRB websites using registration number and date of birth in DDMMYYYY format.

How long will the score link be available?

The score and normalized marks link is scheduled to be available up to 16 July 2026 at 23:55 hours as per the result notice.

Are PwBD, ExSM and CCAA candidates included in this PET list?

No. PwBD, ExSM and CCAA candidates are exempted from PET, so their roll numbers are not included in this PET shortlist PDF.

Where will the PET schedule be released?

The PET schedule and e-call letter updates will be published by RRC Bhubaneswar, East Coast Railway on its official website.

Does PET shortlisting guarantee railway appointment?

No. The notice clearly states that calling a candidate for PET does not give any right to appointment in Railways.

Is the roll number list in merit order?

No. Roll numbers in the PDF are arranged horizontally in ascending order and not in order of merit.
