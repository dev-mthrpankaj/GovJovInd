About This Result

Railway Recruitment Board Bhubaneswar has published the list of candidates provisionally shortlisted for the Physical Efficiency Test for various Level 1 posts under CEN 08/2024. The shortlist is based on performance in the Computer Based Test conducted by RRBs from 27 November 2025 to 10 February 2026.

The result PDF contains 16-digit roll numbers arranged in ascending order from left to right. The list is not arranged in merit order, so candidates should search their roll number carefully instead of assuming rank or merit position from the sequence.

The official notice also explains the score/marks login facility, PET exemption rules for PwBD, Ex-Servicemen and CCAA candidates, and the role of the Railway Recruitment Cell for PET, Document Verification, Medical Examination and panel publication. Candidates should follow the official RRC Bhubaneswar website for PET schedule and e-call letter updates.

How to Check Result

  1. Visit the official RRB/RRC Bhubaneswar website.
  2. Open the CEN 08/2024 Level 1 result or PET shortlist notice.
  3. Download the result PDF / merit list PDF from the official result link.
  4. Use the search option in the PDF and enter your 16-digit roll number.
  5. For percentile score and normalized marks, use the official login link with registration number and date of birth in DDMMYYYY format.
  6. Save or print the result PDF, score details and PET instructions for future reference.

What After Result

After this result, provisionally shortlisted candidates have to appear for the Physical Efficiency Test. The PET schedule and PET e-call letter instructions will be published by the Chairperson of the concerned Railway Recruitment Cell on the official RRC website.

After PET, the recruitment process for Level 1 posts includes Document Verification, Medical Examination and panel publication by the Railway Recruitment Cell of the respective Zonal Railway. Merely being shortlisted for PET does not give any candidate a right to appointment in Railways.

PwBD, Ex-Servicemen and CCAA candidates are exempted from PET, so their roll numbers are not part of this PET list. Their shortlisted list will be published separately by the Railway Recruitment Cells of the respective Zonal Railways.

Documents Required for Next Stage

  • PET e-call letter / admit card issued by the concerned RRC.
  • Original photo ID proof such as Aadhaar card, voter ID, PAN card, passport or driving licence.
  • CBT admit card, application printout and result/score details if required.
  • Educational qualification certificates and mark sheets.
  • Caste/category certificate for SC, ST, OBC, EWS or other applicable category.
  • Domicile/residence certificate, if applicable as per official instructions.
  • Recent passport-size photographs as per RRC instructions.
  • PwBD, Ex-Servicemen, CCAA, apprenticeship, experience or skill certificates if applicable.
  • Any other document mentioned in the official PET/DV notice or e-call letter.
