About This Result

The Bihar Public Service Commission (BPSC) conducted the written examination for the recruitment of Auditors under the Panchayati Raj Dept., Govt. of Bihar, on 05 July 2026[cite: 2]. A total of 102 posts were advertised under Advt No. 09/2026[cite: 2].

The official result has now been declared as of 22 August 2026[cite: 2]. Candidates who have successfully cleared this stage of the examination are requested to check the merit list PDF and login to the official portal to view their OMR sheets and individual scores if made available[cite: 2].

Qualified candidates must regularly check the official website for further instructions regarding the next stages of the recruitment process, which may include the main examination or document verification.

How to Check BPSC Auditor Result

  1. Visit the official portal of BPSC at bpsc.bihar.gov.in[cite: 2].
  2. Look for the notification regarding the "Auditor (Panchayati Raj Dept) Result 2026" on the homepage.
  3. Click the link to open the result PDF file.
  4. Download the PDF and use Ctrl + F to search for your Roll Number carefully.
  5. To check your OMR sheet or marks, log in to the candidate portal at bpsconline.bihar.gov.in[cite: 2].
  6. Save or print the result page for your future reference.
