About This Result

The BPSC 70th Combined Competitive Examination final result is the final merit-list stage after the written examination and interview process. The result PDF is important because it confirms the recommended candidates and shows the service/post allotment details mentioned against their roll numbers.

Candidates should not rely only on screenshots or third-party summaries. Open the official BPSC result PDF, search the roll number carefully and match the name, merit serial and allotted service details before taking any further step.

After the final result, selected candidates should regularly check BPSC and the concerned department website for appointment, joining, medical, character verification or other document-related instructions. Any schedule or requirement must be treated as final only when issued through official sources.

How to Check BPSC 70th Final Result

  1. Visit the official Bihar Public Service Commission website at bpsc.bihar.gov.in.
  2. Open the notice/result link related to 70th Combined Competitive Examination Final Result.
  3. Download the result PDF or merit list PDF from the official notice section.
  4. Open the PDF and use the search option on mobile or computer.
  5. Enter your roll number carefully and check whether your roll number appears in the list.
  6. If shortlisted/recommended, verify your name, merit serial, gender and allotted service/post details.
  7. Save the PDF and take a printout for future reference.

Result Details Mentioned

  • Candidate roll number
  • Candidate name
  • Merit serial / final merit position
  • Gender as mentioned in the result table
  • Allotted reservation roster vacancy code
  • Service or post name in which candidate is recommended
  • Category-wise cutoff marks for written and final examination
  • Important notes related to reservation, disability and freedom fighter descendant categories
  • Next-stage or post-result instructions, if notified separately

What After BPSC 70th Final Result?

Because this is a final result, the next step is not another written test. Recommended candidates should wait for official instructions related to appointment, joining, posting, medical examination, character verification or additional document verification if required by the allotted department.

Candidates should keep their mobile number, email and documents ready. Any joining letter, appointment order or department-wise instruction should be followed only after checking the official source.

Documents Required for Next Stage

  • BPSC admit card and final result/merit list printout
  • Online application form / registration details
  • Valid photo identity proof such as Aadhaar Card, PAN Card, Voter ID or Passport
  • High school, intermediate, graduation and other educational certificates
  • Caste/category certificate, if applicable
  • EWS certificate, if applicable
  • Domicile/residence certificate, if applicable
  • Disability certificate, if applicable
  • Freedom fighter descendant certificate, if applicable
  • Recent passport-size photographs
  • Experience, service or other certificates if asked in the official instructions
  • Any other document mentioned by BPSC or the concerned department

Frequently Asked Questions

Is BPSC 70th Final Result 2026 declared?

Yes. Bihar Public Service Commission released the final result/merit list for the 70th Combined Competitive Examination on 20 June 2026.

Where can I check BPSC 70th Final Result 2026?

Candidates can check the result on the official BPSC website bpsc.bihar.gov.in and search their roll number in the final merit list PDF.

How do I search my roll number in the BPSC final result PDF?

Open the result PDF, use the search option, type your roll number carefully and verify the name, merit serial and allotted service details if your roll number appears.

What is the result type for BPSC 70th CCE?

Is BPSC 70th cutoff released?
