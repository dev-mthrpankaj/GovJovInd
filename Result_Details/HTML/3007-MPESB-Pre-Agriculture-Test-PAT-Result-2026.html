About This Result

MPESB Pre Agriculture Test (PAT) 2026 result has been published online for candidates who appeared in the entrance test for agriculture-related admission courses in Madhya Pradesh. The result page allows candidates to view their individual scorecard after entering the required login credentials.

This result page is focused on scorecard access, merit information and the next admission process. Candidates should not rely only on screenshots or third-party posts; the official MPESB result portal should be used to confirm the score, rank and result status.

How to Check MPESB PAT Result 2026

  1. Visit the official MPESB website at esb.mp.gov.in.
  2. Open the Results section and choose “Pre-Agriculture Test (PAT) - 2026 Final Result”.
  3. Enter your application number and date of birth in the required format.
  4. Enter the first two letters of mother name plus the last four digits of Aadhaar number, if asked on the result page.
  5. Solve the verification question shown on the page and submit the form.
  6. Check your scorecard, rank/status and other result details carefully.
  7. Download or print the result copy for counselling, document verification and admission use.

What After MPESB PAT Result 2026?

After the result, qualified candidates should prepare for the counselling and admission process. The admission authority may publish counselling registration dates, choice filling instructions, merit list, seat allotment result and reporting schedule separately.

Candidates should keep all original documents ready and should not wait until the last date. Any mismatch in name, category, domicile, educational qualification or reservation claim may create problems during document verification.

Documents Required for Counselling / Admission

  • MPESB PAT 2026 scorecard/result printout
  • PAT 2026 admit card, if required
  • Online application form or registration details
  • Class 10 marksheet/certificate for date of birth proof
