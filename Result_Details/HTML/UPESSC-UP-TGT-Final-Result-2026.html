About This Result

The Uttar Pradesh Secondary Education Service Selection Board (UPSESSB) / Uttar Pradesh Education Service Selection Commission (UPESSC), Prayagraj conducted the written examination for Trained Graduate Teacher (TGT) Advt No. 01/2022 on 03 and 04 June 2026 across various centers in Uttar Pradesh.

Following the declaration of written exam results on 30 June 2026, shortlisted candidates were called for Document Verification (DV) held between 09 July 2026 and 17 July 2026. UPESSC has officially declared the final merit list and subject-wise cut-off marks on 28 July 2026.

Candidates can check their selection status online by logging into the portal using their registration credentials or searching their roll number in the subject-wise result PDFs.

How to Check UP TGT Final Result 2026

  1. Visit the official UPESSC online portal at upessc.up.gov.in or upessc.net.
  2. Click on the Results / Notices link on the homepage.
  3. Locate the link for UP TGT Advt No. 01/2022 Final Result.
  4. Select your respective subject (e.g., Hindi, Mathematics, Social Science, Science, Arts, Sanskrit, Home Science).
  5. Download the subject-wise selection list PDF.
  6. Press Ctrl + F and enter your Roll Number or Registration Number to verify selection status.
  7. Save and print the final result PDF for future reference.

What After Final Result Declaration?

Candidates shortlisted in the UP TGT 2022 Final Result will receive final appointment letters and institution/school allotment orders issued by the Secondary Education Department, Uttar Pradesh. Selected candidates should stay tuned to official notifications regarding joining formalities, medical verification, and school reporting schedules.

Documents Required for Final Joining

  • UP TGT Application Form & Admit Card Copy
  • Final Result & Allotment Letter Printout
  • Educational Qualification Certificates (10th, 12th, Graduation, B.Ed degree/marksheets)
  • Caste / EWS / Category Certificate (issued by competent UP authorities)
  • Domicile Certificate
  • Government Photo ID Proof (Aadhaar, PAN, Voter ID)
  • Passport Size Photographs & Character Certificates
