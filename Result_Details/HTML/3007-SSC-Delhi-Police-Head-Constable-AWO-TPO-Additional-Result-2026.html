About This Result

SSC has released an additional result notice for Head Constable {Assistant Wireless Operator (AWO)/Tele-Printer Operator (TPO)} in Delhi Police Examination, 2025. This result update is a supplementary shortlist connected to the earlier CBE result and is meant for candidates who are now found eligible for the next stage.

The official notice states that some candidates who applied under UR or EWS category submitted representations regarding non-inclusion in the result. They claimed that they should have been considered under the OBC from the NCT of Delhi category. After examination, SSC considered the candidatures of candidates who claimed possession of such certificate.

As per the notice, 32 additional candidates have been shortlisted for Physical Endurance & Measurement Test, Document Verification and Trade Test. Candidates should check the official SSC website for the uploaded list and follow only official instructions for next-stage schedule and admission certificate.
