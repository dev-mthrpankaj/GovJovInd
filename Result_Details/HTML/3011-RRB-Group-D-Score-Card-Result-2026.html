About This Result

RRB Group D Score Card Result 2026 is connected with CEN 08/2024 for recruitment to various Level-1 posts in the 7th CPC Pay Matrix. The CBT was conducted in multiple phases, and the result update is used to shortlist candidates for the Physical Efficiency Test.

The scorecard link helps candidates view their CBT performance through the official login portal. Candidates should keep their registration number and date of birth/password ready before opening the scorecard page. If the direct login window shows maintenance or date-expired message, candidates should re-check the respective regional RRB website.

After the CBT result, shortlisted candidates have to follow the PET schedule and later Document Verification and Medical Examination instructions issued through official RRC/RRB portals. Candidates should not rely on social media screenshots or unofficial roll-number lists.

How to Check RRB Group D Score Card Result

  1. Visit your regional RRB official website or open the CEN 08/2024 Level-1 update page.
  2. Find the link for Result of CBT and list of candidates shortlisted for PET or Scorecard and QPHTML.
  3. For merit list, download the official PET shortlist PDF and search your roll number carefully.
  4. For scorecard, open the official login link and enter your registration number with date of birth/password as required.
  5. Check your CBT marks/normalized score and qualifying status from the official scorecard page.
  6. Download or print the scorecard and keep it safe for PET, Document Verification and future stages.

Result Details Mentioned

  • Candidate roll number or registration-linked scorecard details.
  • CBT marks / normalized score as displayed by the official login portal.
  • Qualifying status for Physical Efficiency Test, if shortlisted.
  • Zone-wise or railway-wise merit list / PET shortlist information.
  • Category-wise cut-off marks where published by the official regional portal.

What After RRB Group D Result?

Candidates shortlisted in CBT have to appear for the Physical Efficiency Test. The official CEN states that PET shortlisting is Railway/RRC-wise and based on CBT merit. PET will be followed by Document Verification and Medical Examination.

  • Check PET schedule on the official RRC/RRB website of your applied railway.
  • Download PET call letter or instructions only from the official portal when available.
  • Keep original documents ready for verification.
  • Follow medical standard and document instructions exactly as mentioned in official notices.
  • Do not pay any person for selection help; RRBs warn candidates against touts and job racketeers.

Documents Required for Next Stage

  • RRB Group D CBT admit card / e-call letter.
  • Downloaded scorecard or result proof, if required in instructions.
  • Application/registration printout and candidate login details.
  • Original photo ID proof such as Aadhaar card, voter ID, PAN card or passport.
  • Class 10 certificate / date of birth proof.
  • Educational qualification certificates as applicable.
  • Caste/category/EWS certificate in the prescribed format, if applicable.
  • PwBD, Ex-Servicemen, CCAA or other reservation/benefit certificates, if applicable.
  • Recent passport-size photographs and other documents mentioned in official PET/DV notice.

Frequently Asked Questions

Is RRB Group D Score Card Result 2026 released?

Yes. Regional RRB portals have listed the CEN 08/2024 CBT result, PET shortlist and scorecard link dated 1 July 2026.

What is the CEN number for RRB Group D result?

The result belongs to CEN 08/2024 for various Level-1 posts in the 7th CPC Pay Matrix.

How can candidates check RRB Group D scorecard?

Candidates should open the official RRB regional website, select CEN 08/2024 Level-1 and use the scorecard login link with registration details and date of birth or password.

What is the next stage after RRB Group D CBT result?

Candidates shortlisted in CBT have to appear for Physical Efficiency Test followed by Document Verification and Medical Examination as per official recruitment process.

Are RRB Group D marks normalized?

Yes. The official CEN states that marks will be normalized for CBT involving multiple sessions.

Where can I download the RRB Group D merit list PDF?

The merit list or PET shortlist PDF is available on the respective regional RRB or RRC website under CEN 08/2024 result updates.

Is RRB Group D cut off released?

Some official regional portals have published normalized cut-off marks of candidates shortlisted for PET. Candidates should verify their zone-wise cut-off from the official RRB or RRC page.

What details are mentioned in RRB Group D scorecard?

The scorecard generally shows candidate details, CBT marks or normalized score, qualifying status and related score information available through the official login.
