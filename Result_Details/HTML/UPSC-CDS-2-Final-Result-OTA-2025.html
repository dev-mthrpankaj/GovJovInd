About This Result

The Union Public Service Commission (UPSC) conducted the written examination for Combined Defence Services Examination (II), 2025 on 14 September 2025 across various exam centers nationwide. Candidates qualifying the written exam were called for the SSB Interview round conducted by the Services Selection Board (SSB) of the Ministry of Defence.

Following the declaration of the main final result for IMA, INA, and Air Force Academy on 30 March 2026, UPSC has now released the final result for Officers Training Academy (OTA) candidates on 07 August 2026. This final merit list includes candidates recommended for admission into Officers Training Academy, Chennai.

The selection list has been prepared on the basis of candidates' performance in the Written Examination followed by the SSB Interview and subject to medical fitness verification.

How to Check UPSC CDS 2 OTA Final Result 2025

  1. Visit the official Union Public Service Commission website at upsc.gov.in.
  2. Locate the "What's New" section or navigate to the "Final Results" tab on the homepage.
  3. Click on the link titled "Final Result: Combined Defence Services Examination (II), 2025 (OTA)".
  4. Open or download the official Result PDF file to your device.
  5. Use the search function (Ctrl + F) to enter your Roll Number or Name in the PDF document.
  6. Check your merit position, recommendation status, and save/print a copy of the PDF for future reference.

Details Mentioned in the Final Merit List

  • Candidate Roll Number
  • Candidate Full Name
  • Order of Merit / Rank
  • Academy / Course Allotment Details
  • Important instructions regarding Medical Fitness and Verification
  • Contact details of Army HQ / UPSC Facilitation Counter for queries

What After Final Result Declaration?

Candidates recommended in the OTA Final Merit List will receive joining instructions directly from Army Headquarters / Ministry of Defence based on their rank order, medical suitability, and original document verification.

The marks of all candidates (qualified and non-qualified) will be published on the UPSC website within 15 days from the date of publishing of the final result and will remain available on the portal for 30 days.

Documents Required During Verification / Joining

  • Original Matriculation / Secondary School Certificate (Proof of Date of Birth)
  • Original Degree / Provisional Passing Certificate for Graduation
  • Category / Caste Certificate (SC/ST/OBC/EWS) if applicable
  • SSB Call Letter and Interview Recommendation Certificates
  • Government-issued Identity Proof (Aadhaar Card, PAN Card, Voter ID, Passport)
  • Recent passport-size photographs as specified by the military authorities
