About This Result

The Bihar Public Service Commission (BPSC) conducts the Integrated Combined Competitive Examination to recruit eligible candidates for various State Civil Services posts across Bihar. Following the completion of the written examination and interview cycles, BPSC has published the official scorecards and marks breakdown for candidates.

Candidates can access their individualized scorecard by entering their login credentials on the official BPSC portal. The released scorecard contains comprehensive details regarding the candidate's performance in different papers, category cut-offs, and final qualifying status.

Checking the official scorecard is vital for verifying normalized scores, category merit rank, and document verification prerequisites. Candidates are advised to keep a downloaded copy of their scorecard safely for future reference during joining formalties.

How to Check BPSC Scorecard 2026

  1. Visit the official portal of Bihar Public Service Commission (bpsc.bih.nic.in) or access the candidate portal at bpsconline.bihar.gov.in.
  2. Locate the link for BPSC 70th Marks / Scorecard 2026 on the home notice board or login tab.
  3. Enter your mandatory login credentials such as Application Number / Registration ID and Password / Date of Birth.
  4. Click on the 'Submit' or 'Login' button to open your dashboard.
  5. Select the result / scorecard option to view your marks breakdown on the screen.
  6. Verify all details carefully including candidate name, roll number, subject-wise marks, total aggregate, and final status.
  7. Download and save a copy of the BPSC scorecard PDF, and take a printout for prospective selection procedures.

Cut-Off Marks & Merit List Details

The Bihar Public Service Commission determines cut-off scores depending on multiple key variables, including the total vacancies advertised, overall candidate performance, categorywise reservation policies, and paper difficulty levels.

Candidates obtaining marks equal to or greater than the official category-wise cut-off are included in the final merit list. Category cut-off marks are published alongside the official notification result PDF. Candidates are recommended to download the notice directly from BPSC official channels to view detailed cutoff tables.

What After Result & Documents Required

Candidates who qualify in the final merit list and secure necessary minimum cutoff scores will be invited for Document Verification (DV) and medical/administrative procedures prior to departmental allotment.

Key original documents required for verification include:

  • Printed copy of BPSC 70th Online Application Form & Scorecard.
  • Admit Card / Interview Letter of BPSC 70th Examination.
  • 10th Class / Matriculation Certificate as proof of Date of Birth.
  • Educational Certificates and Graduation Degree Marksheets.
  • Valid Caste / Category Certificate (for reserved categories).
  • Domicile / Permanent Resident Certificate of Bihar (if applicable).
  • Non-Creamy Layer (NCL) Certificate for OBC/EBC candidates.
  • Valid Photo Identification Proof (Aadhaar Card, Voter ID, PAN Card, Passport).
  • Recent Passport-size Photographs.

Frequently Asked Questions

Is BPSC 70th Scorecard 2026 released?

Yes, Bihar Public Service Commission (BPSC) has released the marks and scorecard for the 70th Combined Competitive Examination.

Where can I check BPSC 70th marks?

Candidates can check their marks by visiting the official BPSC candidate login portal at bpsconline.bihar.gov.in.

How to check BPSC 70th Scorecard 2026?

Log in to the BPSC online candidate portal using your Username and Password, then navigate to the result/marks section to view and download your scorecard.

What credentials are needed to log in for BPSC Marks?

You need your Application Number/Registration ID and Password or Date of Birth to log into the official portal.

Is the BPSC Integrated 70th Final Result available in PDF format?

Yes, the merit list PDF and result notices are uploaded on the official BPSC website at bpsc.bih.nic.in.

What details are mentioned in the BPSC 70th Scorecard?

The scorecard includes the candidate's name, roll number, registration number, category, total marks obtained, cut-off marks, and qualifying status.

What is the next stage after checking BPSC 70th result and marks?

Qualified candidates proceed for document verification, interview sessions, or final allotment as per the official recruitment cycle instructions.

What should I do if the official BPSC website link is slow?

Due to heavy traffic, the server may slow down. Candidates are advised to retry after a short period or clear browser cache before accessing bpsconline.bihar.gov.in.
