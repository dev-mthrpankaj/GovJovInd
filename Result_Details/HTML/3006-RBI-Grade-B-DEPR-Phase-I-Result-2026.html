About This Result

The Reserve Bank of India Services Board has released the Phase I result for Recruitment of Officers in Grade 'B' (DR) - Department of Economic and Policy Research (DEPR). The result is published as a roll-number-wise list of candidates shortlisted for Phase II.

The Phase I examination date mentioned in the result PDF is 14 June 2026. Candidates whose roll numbers appear in the official PDF should follow RBI instructions for Phase II, including admit card, reporting, documents and examination guidelines.

This page is a candidate-friendly summary of the uploaded result notice. Candidates should rely on the official RBI website for final confirmation, scorecard/cutoff updates and any correction or next-stage notice.

  1. Visit the official RBI opportunities website at opportunities.rbi.org.in.

What After Result

Candidates shortlisted in the Phase I result should prepare for Phase II of the RBI Grade B (DR) DEPR recruitment process. The detailed Phase II schedule, admit card and instructions must be verified from RBI's official recruitment portal.

  • Download and read the Phase I result PDF carefully.

Documents Required for Next Stage

  • Phase II admit card / call letter, when released.
  • Application form or registration details.
  • Valid photo ID proof such as Aadhaar card, PAN card, voter ID, passport or driving licence.
