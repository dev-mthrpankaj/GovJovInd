About This Result

The Railway Recruitment Boards administered the cross-country Computer Based Test (CBT) for multi-category Level-1 posts under Centralized Employment Notice (CEN) No. 08/2024 from 27 November 2025 to 10 February 2026. Based on individual metrics and multi-session normalized scoring trends, a collective list of candidates has been provisionally designated for the forthcoming physical filters.

The shortlisting ratio mirrors approximately three times the community-wise total vacancies allocated across respective departments (excluding qualified PwBD, ExSM, and CCAA category candidates who enjoy individual systemic exclusions). Shortlisted roll numbers are cataloged explicitly in horizontal ascending order rather than direct merit orientation.

Subsequent operational steps including physical infrastructure scheduling, verification frameworks, and definitive clinical assessments fall directly under regional jurisdiction. For the successful examinees belonging to RRB Ranchi, all future notifications, schedule itineraries, and printable e-call letters will be officially regulated by the Railway Recruitment Cell (RRC) Kolkata, South Eastern Railway.

What After Level 1 Written Result

Candidates provisionally identified through the CBT scores will have to clear the next physical threshold, the Physical Efficiency Test (PET), managed exclusively by RRC setup systems. Formal notification containing physical schedules, regional allocation layouts, and valid e-call letters will appear on the RRC Kolkata portal in due course. Direct informational triggers will also be delivered to your registered mobile networks and email accounts via SMS/email formats.

Passing the Physical Efficiency Test remains entirely mandatory for regular aspirants. Succeeding candidates will move directly to Document Verification (DV) checkposts, detailed clinical evaluations via specialized Medical Examinations, and final allocation matrices across Indian Railways service sectors.

Documents Required for Next Stage

Shortlisted candidates going forward must compile these mandatory records safely for subsequent physical checking stages:

  • Printed copy of the official PET E-Call Letter issued by RRC Kolkata.
  • Original online application registration copy along with valid Government Identification Cards (Aadhaar, Voter ID, Driving License, etc.).
  • Academic Qualification certificates including standard 10th standard marksheets or equivalent valid technical credentials.
  • Community/Caste validity proofs (SC, ST, OBC-NCL, EWS certificates formatted to strict central protocols).
  • Valid industrial apprentice tracking documents for those demanding corresponding category parameters.
  • Recent high-resolution color photographs matching identity portfolios.
