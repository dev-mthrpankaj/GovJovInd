About This Result

The Bihar Vidhan Parishad has successfully concluded the selection process for the post of Lower Division Clerk (LDC) for the year 2026. The recruitment involved written examinations followed by critical skill and typing tests (Hindi/English) and MS-Office Word Processing assessments.

Based on the consolidated performance of the candidates across all stages, the recruitment body has released the final merit list. This final result indicates the roll numbers of candidates who have been provisionally selected for the post.

Candidates are strongly advised to refer to the official notification and the final merit list PDF (Bihar LDC.pdf) to check their status and read the guidelines regarding the subsequent Document Verification (DV) and medical examination procedures.

How to Check Bihar LDC Final Result

  1. Visit the official Bihar Vidhan Parishad website at biharvidhanparishad.gov.in.
  2. Navigate to the "Recruitment" or "Latest Notices" section on the homepage.
  3. Click on the link for "Final Result / Merit List for Lower Division Clerk (LDC) 2026".
  4. Download the official result PDF (often named Bihar LDC.pdf).
  5. Open the file and use the search function to carefully look for your Roll Number or Registration Number.
  6. Save and print the final result page for your future reference and joining formalities.

  • Category (UR/EWS/BC/EBC/SC/ST)
  • Final Selection Status
  • Instructions for Document Verification
  • Remarks (Provisional/Withheld, if any)

Cut Off & Merit List

The final merit list contains the roll numbers of the successfully selected candidates. The official category-wise cut-off marks are determined based on the total number of vacancies, candidate performance, and category reservations.

Note: Official cutoff will be updated after release. Candidates should refer directly to the Bihar LDC.pdf or the official website for exact qualifying marks and scorecard links.

What After Final Result?

  • Document Verification (DV): All provisionally selected candidates will be called for comprehensive document verification.
  • Medical Fitness: Candidates may need to clear a standard medical fitness examination prior to appointment.
  • Joining & Training: After successful DV, official appointment letters will be dispatched with instructions regarding the joining date and department allocation.
  • Candidates must routinely check the official website for exact dates and venue details for the DV process.

Documents Required for Next Stage

Shortlisted candidates should prepare original and photocopies of the following documents for the Document Verification stage:

  • Printed copy of the Final Result / Merit List highlighting your Roll Number
  • Admit Card for written exam and skill test
  • Valid Photo ID Proof (Aadhaar Card, PAN Card, Voter ID, etc.)
  • 10th and 12th standard passing certificates and mark sheets
  • Computer / Typing Proficiency Certificates as claimed
  • Caste / Category Certificate (for reserved categories) issued by competent authority
  • Domicile Certificate (if applicable for state reservation)
  • Passport size recent photographs
  • Any other documents specifically mentioned in the official DV notice

Frequently Asked Questions

Is Bihar Vidhan Parishad LDC Final Result 2026 declared?

Yes, the final result and merit list for the Bihar Vidhan Parishad Lower Division Clerk (LDC) recruitment 2026 has been officially declared.

Where can I check the Bihar LDC result?

Candidates can check their result by downloading the official merit list PDF from the Bihar Vidhan Parishad official website (biharvidhanparishad.gov.in).

Is the scorecard available for LDC exam?

The scorecard or individual marks will be available as per the official notification. Candidates should check the official portal for login links.

Is the cut-off released?

Yes, the category-wise cut-off marks are generally published along with or shortly after the final merit list on the official website.

What is the next stage after the final result?

After the final result is declared, shortlisted candidates will be called for Final Document Verification (DV) and medical examination prior to joining.

What documents are required for Document Verification?

Candidates need to bring their admit card, photo ID, educational certificates, computer/typing certificates, caste/category certificate (if applicable), and passport-size photographs.

How to search my roll number in the merit list?

Open the official result PDF and use the 'Ctrl+F' (Find) shortcut to search for your specific roll number or registration number.

What if the official website is not opening?

Due to heavy traffic, the server might be down. Please wait for some time and try accessing the official link again.
