About This Result

The Madhya Pradesh Employees Selection Board (MPESB) conducted the Animal Husbandry and Dairy Technology Admission Test (ADDET) 2026 on 10th July 2026 across various online test centers in Madhya Pradesh. This state-level entrance examination is held for admission into 2-year diploma courses in Animal Husbandry and Dairy Technology in participating government and private colleges.

Candidates can now log in using their Roll Number or Application Number along with their Date of Birth to view and download their individual scorecard. The scorecard contains normalized marks, category-wise rank, and qualifying status for the counseling phase.

Participating institutes will soon publish the counseling schedule, merit cut-off lists, and choice-filling guidelines for candidates who have qualified in this entrance test.

How to Check MPESB ADDET Result 2026

  1. Visit the official portal of Madhya Pradesh Employees Selection Board at esb.mp.gov.in.
  2. Click on the "Results" tab present on the main navigation menu.
  3. Find and click on the link titled "Animal Husbandry and Dairy Technology Admission Test (ADDET) - 2026 Result".
  4. Enter your Application Number or Roll Number along with your Date of Birth and TAC Code (from admit card).
  5. Solve the CAPTCHA / Security question and click on Search / Submit.
  6. Your MPESB ADDET Scorecard 2026 will appear on the screen showing normalized marks and merit status.
  7. Download the scorecard PDF and print multiple clear copies for counseling and document verification.

Details Mentioned on ADDET Scorecard

  • Candidate's Full Name & Father's Name
  • Roll Number & Application Number
  • Category & Sub-category (if applicable)
  • Marks Obtained (Raw & Normalized)
  • Overall Merit Rank & Category Rank
  • Qualifying Status for Counseling
  • Official Stamp & Controller of Examination Signature Notice

What After Result & Documents Required for Counseling

Candidates who qualify in the MPESB ADDET 2026 result will be invited for centralized online counseling. During counseling, candidates must lock their preferred choices of colleges and courses based on their rank. Keep the following documents ready for verification:

  • MPESB ADDET 2026 Rank Card / Scorecard Printout
  • MPESB ADDET 2026 Admit Card
  • Class 10th Marksheet & Passing Certificate (Date of Birth Proof)
  • Class 12th Marksheet & Certificate (Science PCB Stream)
  • Madhya Pradesh Domicile / Permanent Resident Certificate
  • Caste / Category Certificate (SC/ST/OBC/EWS, if applicable)
  • Income Certificate for Reserved/EWS categories
  • Valid Identity Proof (Aadhaar Card, Voter ID, Passport, etc.)
  • Recent Passport Size Photographs
