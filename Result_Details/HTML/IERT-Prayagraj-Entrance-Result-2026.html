About This Result

The entrance examinations for admission into the prestigious Institute of Engineering and Rural Technology (IERT), Prayagraj for the 2026 academic calendar were successfully organized on June 30, 2026, for the flagship 3-Year Engineering Diploma courses. Following this, the entrance papers for the 2-Year Management Diploma and the 1.5-Year Post Graduate Diploma in Computer Application (PDCA) were conducted on July 3, 2026.

The newly announced result functions as an official qualification scorecard, indexing specific ranks based on performance. Candidates who clear the required minimum evaluation threshold will be declared eligible to join the offline seat configuration and branch allotment counseling rounds held directly at the Allahabad/Prayagraj campus.

Detailed verification of educational parameters, local state domicile eligibility, and age standards will be thoroughly enforced at the physical verification desks before formal branch registrations are locked.

How to Check IERT Entrance Result

  1. Navigate directly to the official IERT admission portal at iertentrance.in.
  2. Locate and click on the specific link designated for "Download Result" or proceed directly to the status page at iertentrance.in/viewstatus.aspx.
  3. Enter your unique Entrance Roll Number and Password into the requested text fields.
  4. Submit the captcha or press the security verification button to process your login request.
  5. Your official dashboard will present your secure scorecard detailing your aggregate scores and corresponding category rank.
  6. Download a digital copy and take a clear physical printout of the rank summary, as it is mandatory during seat allocation.

Details Mentioned on IERT Scorecard

Candidates must carefully check all the structured identity parameters present on the digital scorecard sheet:

  • Candidate's Full Name
  • Entrance Roll Number & Application Form ID
  • Father’s Name & Guardian Details
  • Applied Course Category (Engineering / Management / PDCA)
  • Category Classification (General, OBC, SC, ST, EWS)
  • Sub-Category Tags (Physically Handicapped, FF, Armed Forces, if applicable)
  • Total Marks Obtained
  • Open General Rank Merit Position
  • Category-Specific Allotted Rank

What After the Entrance Result?

Following the public release of the entrance evaluation, the institute establishes course-wise cutoff thresholds. The subsequent admission workflow follows these structured campus activities:

  • Counseling Call Letter: Registered applicants matching initial rank blocks must generate their digital counseling memos from the official portal.
  • Offline Campus Round: Shortlisted candidates must physically report at the IERT Prayagraj facility as per the day-wise rank configuration.
  • Document Verification: Original certificates are scrutinized by academic committees to confirm regulatory eligibility.
  • Branch Allotment: Desired discipline branches (such as Civil, Mechanical, Electrical, Electronics, etc.) are distributed dynamically based on rank availability and choices locked.
  • Fee Submission: Instantly lock the allotted branch by paying the annual institutional fee structured for the autonomous or aided regular seats.

Documents Required for Counseling Stage

Candidates must carry the original files along with two separate sets of self-attested document copies:

  • Original IERT Entrance Admit Card 2026
  • Printed Copy of IERT Result Scorecard/Rank Card 2026
  • Class 10th (High School) Marksheet and Passing Certificate
  • Class 12th (Intermediate) Marksheet and Passing Certificate (for Management / PDCA / Engineering options)
  • Qualifying Diploma or Graduation Marksheets (applicable exclusively for Post Graduate Diploma choices)
  • Valid Category Certificate (OBC / SC / ST / EWS issued by authorized Revenue Officers)
  • Domicile Certificate of Uttar Pradesh
  • Character Certificate issued by the last attended academic institution
  • Medical Fitness Certificate from a Government Chief Medical Officer (CMO)
  • Recent passport-sized color photographs matching application profiles

Frequently Asked Questions (FAQs)

Is the IERT Prayagraj Entrance Result 2026 declared?

Yes. The Institute of Engineering and Rural Technology, Prayagraj officially declared the entrance scorecards on July 7, 2026.

Where can I look up my individual score?

You can access your scores online on the dedicated login interface setup at iertentrance.in/viewstatus.aspx.

What data items are needed to log in?

You must authenticate into your candidate dashboard using your specific Entrance Exam Roll Number along with your registration Password.

How can I find out if I am selected for counseling?

Your online scorecard showcases specific category-wise ranks. IERT will publish cutoff schedules indicating the exact rank blocks invited on particular counseling dates.

Will the institute send physical call letters?

No, individual physical counseling letters are not sent by post. Candidates must monitor updates online and download copies directly from the portal.

What if I forgot my portal registration password?

You can access the recovery wizard located on the primary landing section of iertentrance.in to retrieve lost access credentials.

Are candidates from other states eligible?

Yes, but priority allocation is reserved for UP state domicile candidates. Other state candidates are accommodated inside open quotas or remaining vacant blocks as per rules.

Where does the document verification process take place?

The entire certificate verification and final seat registration are conducted offline at the IERT Prayagraj campus location.
