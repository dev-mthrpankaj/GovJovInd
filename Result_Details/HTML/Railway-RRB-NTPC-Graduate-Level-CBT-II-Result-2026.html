About This Result

The Railway Recruitment Board (RRB) successfully conducted the 2nd Stage Computer Based Test (CBT II) on 10 July 2026 for the Non-Technical Popular Categories (NTPC) Graduate Level Recruitment under CEN 06/2025. The result and regional cut-off marks have now been officially published.

Candidates can log into the candidate portal to download their individual CBT II scorecards, which display their normalized marks and shortlisting status. Depending on the post preferences filled by the candidates, shortlisted individuals will now be called for the Computer Based Aptitude Test (CBAT) for Station Master & Traffic Assistant posts, Typing Skill Test for Typist posts, or directly for Document Verification (DV).

It is advised to download the region-specific merit list PDF to verify roll numbers and carefully read instructions regarding the schedule and venues for the upcoming stages of selection.

How to Check RRB NTPC CBT II Result & Scorecard

  1. Visit the official RRB regional website (e.g., RRB Allahabad, RRB Chennai, RRB Mumbai, etc.) or the central application portal.
  2. Look for the link labeled "Score Card Link for CBT-2 CEN 06/2025 NTPC" or "Result of CBT-2 for Shortlisting Candidates for Next Stage".
  3. To download the scorecard, click the login link and enter your Registration Number and Date of Birth (Password).
  4. View your normalized score and shortlisting status on the dashboard.
  5. To check the merit list, download the PDF provided on your regional RRB website and search for your Roll Number.
  6. Download the cut-off marks PDF to analyze your score against the category-wise thresholds.
  7. Save and print your scorecard for future stages of the recruitment process.

Result Details Mentioned on Scorecard

  • Candidate Name and Roll Number
  • Registration Number
  • Applied RRB Region
  • Raw Score out of Total Valid Questions
  • Pro-rated Score out of 120
  • Normalized Score
  • Post-wise Shortlisting Status (Yes/No) for CBAT / Typing Test / DV

What After CBT II Result? (Next Stages)

Based on the merit in CBT II and the post preferences opted by candidates, they will be called for the following stages:

  • Computer Based Aptitude Test (CBAT): Mandatory for candidates opted for Station Master and Traffic Assistant posts.
  • Typing Skill Test (TST): Required for Senior Clerk Cum Typist, Junior Accounts Assistant Cum Typist posts. It is qualifying in nature.
  • Document Verification (DV) & Medical Exam: Candidates shortlisted for Goods Train Manager, Chief Commercial Cum Ticket Supervisor, and those who clear CBAT/TST will undergo DV and Medical Examination.

Documents Required for DV Stage

  • Printed copy of RRB NTPC application form and CBT I/II Admit Cards (lower half portion)
  • CBT II Scorecard printout
  • Matriculation / 10th Certificate for age verification
  • Graduation Degree / Marksheets
  • Valid Government Photo ID Proof (Aadhaar, PAN, Voter ID, Passport)
  • Caste / Category Certificate (SC/ST/OBC-NCL/EWS) in Central Government format
  • Income Certificate for Waiver of Examination Fee (if claimed)
  • Minority declaration on Non-judicial Stamp Paper (if applicable)
  • NOC from serving employees / Ex-Servicemen discharge documents
