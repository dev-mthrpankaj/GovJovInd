About This Result

National Aluminium Company Limited (NALCO) invited applications for 268 Non Executive positions under Advertisement No. 10260213. The online registration process began on May 21, 2026, and closed on June 17, 2026, after an extension. The first major phase of the selection process is the Computer Based Test (CBT), which is officially scheduled to take place on July 27, 2026.

The upcoming NALCO Non Executive Result 2026 will shortlist eligible candidates on the basis of normalized marks scored in the CBT. Shortlisted candidates will progress to the final stage, which includes a Medical Fitness evaluation to determine structural suitability for the respective executive and technical assignments across units in India.

Following standard procedural norms, NALCO will declare separate cut-off standards for differing administrative categories (General, OBC, EWS, SC, ST, PwD). Scorecards containing raw scores and normalized rank variables are expected to be available along with the shortlisting notification inside the career section.

How to Check NALCO Non Executive Result 2026

  1. Visit the official portal of NALCO recruitment at mudira.nalcoindia.co.in.
  2. Look for the active career panel or "Recruitment of Non-Executive Personnel" section.
  3. Locate the notification link labeled "List of Candidates Shortlisted for Medical Examination / CBT Scorecard 2026".
  4. Provide your login parameters such as Registration Number/ID and structural Password if directed to a scorecard screen.
  5. If a PDF file displays, execute a search configuration (Ctrl+F) to isolate your specific Name or Roll Number.
  6. Download the result sheet and print a duplicate copy for subsequent validation checks.

What After NALCO Non Executive Result

Candidates who clear the set cut-off criteria will be provisionally selected to move to the final stage of evaluation:

  • Medical Fitness Examination: Candidates must conform to the stringent physiological and medical parameters outlined by NALCO's institutional guidelines.
  • Document Verification (DV): Original verification of academic degrees, trade experience, ITI or Engineering Diplomas, and caste matrices will be checked concurrently or just prior to medical clearance.
  • Final Merit List: A consolidated final listing based cleanly on CBT performance and medical clearance parameters will precede institutional assignment offers.

Documents Required for Next Stage

Shortlisted aspirants must maintain the under-listed set of valid documentation credentials for evaluation phases:

  • Printed copy of the NALCO CBT Scorecard/Result sheet
  • Online Application Form submission printout
  • Original Matriculation (10th) Certificate for identity and age proof validation
  • ITI / Diploma Engineering / Graduation Certificates alongside seasonal semester sheets
  • Valid Category Certificate (OBC-NCL / EWS / SC / ST) if seeking allocation allowances
  • No Objection Certificate (NOC) if currently active under public or government frameworks
  • Recent color passport-sized photographic prints
