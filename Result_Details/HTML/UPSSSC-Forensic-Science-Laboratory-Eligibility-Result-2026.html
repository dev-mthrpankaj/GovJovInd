About This Result

The Uttar Pradesh Subordinate Service Selection Commission (UPSSSC) has released the Eligibility Result (Shortlisting Result) for the recruitment of 208 posts in the Forensic Science Laboratory under Advt No 15-Exam/2026. This result determines which candidates are eligible to appear for the main examination based on their preliminary qualifying scores.

Along with the eligibility status, the commission has also published the official Cut Off Marks notice category-wise. Candidates who meet the required cut-off criteria are advised to begin their preparations for the next stage of the selection process.

To check the result, candidates must use their UPSSSC Registration Number and other credentials on the official login portal provided by the commission.

How to Check Eligibility Result

  1. Visit the official website of UPSSSC at upsssc.gov.in.
  2. On the homepage, navigate to the "Results" segment.
  3. Click on the link for "Eligibility Result for Advt No 15-Exam/2026 (Forensic Science Laboratory)".
  4. A login page will open. Enter your Registration Number, Date of Birth, Gender, and the given Verification Code.
  5. Click on "See Result". Your shortlisting status for the Main Examination will be displayed on the screen.
  6. Download or print the result page for your records.

Result Details Mentioned

  • Candidate's Name
  • UPSSSC Registration Number
  • Father's / Husband's Name
  • Category (Gen/OBC/SC/ST/EWS)
  • Eligibility Status for Main Exam (Eligible / Not Eligible)
  • Cut Off Marks (available in a separate PDF notice)

Cut Off Marks

The official cut-off marks for the Forensic Science Laboratory Eligibility Result have been released. Candidates must review the official PDF notice to see the exact cut-off thresholds determined for different categories including UR, OBC, EWS, SC, and ST. Only candidates scoring equal to or above these cut-off marks are shortlisted for the Main Exam.

You can download the Cut-off PDF directly from the important links section below.

What After Eligibility Result?

Candidates who have been declared 'Eligible' in this result will be called to appear for the Main Examination. The UPSSSC will soon release a detailed schedule and syllabus (if not already published) for the main written examination.

Candidates will also be required to download a fresh admit card for the Main Examination once the dates are officially announced. Keep checking the official website for subsequent updates regarding the exam schedule.

Documents Required for Next Stage

While appearing for the upcoming stages (Main Exam and subsequent Document Verification), candidates must ensure they have:

  • Printout of the UPSSSC Application Form / OTR Details
  • UPSSSC PET Scorecard
  • Eligibility Result Printout
  • Valid Photo ID Proof (Aadhaar, Voter ID, PAN Card)
  • Educational Certificates (B.Sc./M.Sc., 'A' Level Diploma, etc.)
  • Category / Caste Certificate (if claiming reservation)
  • Domicile Certificate (if applicable)
