About This Result

The Madhya Pradesh Employee Selection Board (MPESB) conducted the Group-2 Sub Group-3 Combined Recruitment Test between 28 January 2026 and 31 January 2026. The examination was held for various posts across multiple districts including Bhopal, Indore, Jabalpur, Khandwa, Neemach, Ratlam, Rewa, Sagar, Satna, Sidhi, Anooppur, and Ujjain.

The board has now released the written examination results in the form of individual scorecards. Candidates can check their normalized marks, section-wise performance, and overall qualifying status by logging in with their Application Number or Roll Number along with their Date of Birth.

Candidates who have qualified this written stage are advised to download and safely preserve a printed copy of their scorecard, as it will be required during the upcoming Document Verification (DV) process.

How to Check Result

  1. Visit the official website of MPESB at esb.mp.gov.in.
  2. Choose your preferred language (English/Hindi) to enter the main portal.
  3. Click on the "Results" section or find the link titled "Result - Group-2 Sub Group-3 Combined Recruitment Test - 2025" in the latest updates.
  4. Enter your Application Number (Max 13 digits) or Roll Number (Max 8 digits) and your Date of Birth.
  5. Enter the TAC Code (if applicable) and solve the Captcha equation.
  6. Click on the 'Search' button to view your scorecard.
  7. Download the scorecard PDF and print it for document verification and future reference.

Cut Off & Merit List

The official cut-off marks vary based on the category of the candidates (UR, SC, ST, OBC, EWS) and the specific posts applied for under Group-2 Sub Group-3. The exact cut-off details are reflected directly on the candidate's scorecard. Candidates scoring equal to or above their respective category cut-off are considered qualified for the next stage.

Official cutoff will be updated after release. Check your official scorecard for detailed qualifying marks.

What After Result

Candidates who have successfully cleared the written examination will proceed to the next stage:

  • Document Verification (DV): Qualified candidates will be called for a thorough verification of their educational and category certificates.
  • Medical Examination: If applicable for specific posts, candidates may undergo a medical fitness test.
  • Joining & Appointment: Final appointment letters will be issued to candidates who clear all stages and are successfully placed in the final merit list.

Check the official notice for the exact schedule and venue details for the DV process.

Documents Required for Next Stage

  • Printed copy of MPESB Group-2 Sub Group-3 Result/Scorecard
  • Admit Card of the written examination
  • Class 10th High School Certificate (for Date of Birth verification)
  • Educational Qualification Certificates (Degree/Diploma as per post requirements)
  • Valid Government Photo ID Proof (Aadhaar, Voter ID, PAN, etc.)
  • Caste/Category Certificate (if claiming age relaxation or reservation, MP Domicile)
  • Candidate Live Photo and Recent Passport size photographs
  • Scan Signature (Running Hand) and Candidate Handwriting samples as specified
  • Any other documents mentioned in the official DV call letter
