About This Result

The centralized Computer Based Test (CBT) covering multiple Level 1 vacancies under standard advertisement CEN No. 08/2024 was administered across designated testing centers from 27 November 2025 to 10 February 2026. Following processing via shift-wise normalization systems, the recruitment boards have generated the provisional pass list for the successive Physical Efficiency Test (PET).

The volume of candidates designated for this physical test conforms to approximately three times the targeted community vacancy indices allocated for Western Railway segments. This allocation excludes specific functional reservation groups, namely Persons with Benchmark Disabilities (PwBD), Ex-Servicemen (ExSM), and Course Completed Act Apprentices (CCAA), who enjoy standalone administrative exemptions from clearing standard physical filters.

The final registration rolls published in this update are sorted strictly in horizontal ascending sequence and do not represent relative merit placement. All further selection procedures, data verification checkpoints, and certified medical tracking programs for qualifiers tracking under RRB Ahmedabad will be coordinated by the Chairperson of the Railway Recruitment Cell (RRC), Western Railway, Mumbai.

How to Check RRB Ahmedabad Level 1 Result

  1. Proceed to the central communications board of the Western Railway RRC division at rrc-wr.com.
  2. Locate the latest update block labeled "List of Candidates Shortlisted for Physical Efficiency Test (PET) under CEN No. 08/2024".
  3. Download the official provisional merit document tracking your sector.
  4. Open the index sheet and run a search lookup match for your unique 16-digit registration roll number.
  5. To look up personal marks, click the scorecard login link, provide your valid Registration Number plus your Date of Birth in DDMMYYYY formatting, and print out your validation summary.

Result Details Documented

  • Provisional Selection Numbers: 16-digit tracking sequences sorted horizontally in ascending order.
  • Operational Transfer Details: Official assignment transferring upcoming tasks directly to the RRC Western Railway hub.
  • Judicial Constraints Note: Selection status is entirely temporary and remains subject to final mandates issued by the Hon'ble High Court, Telangana in Writ Petition No. 7985/2026.

What After Level 1 Written Result

Candidates provisionally identified through the CBT scores will have to clear the next step, the Physical Efficiency Test (PET), managed entirely by the Western Railway cells. Detailed notification sheets tracking regional schedules, test ground layouts, and downloadable e-call letters will be made live on rrc-wr.com. Direct notifications will also be pushed via standard SMS updates and email logs.

Clearing these physical fitness benchmarks is absolutely mandatory for regular vacancies. Those who clear the physical test will move directly to Document Verification (DV) checkposts, followed by comprehensive medical screening tests to confirm final placements.

Documents Required for Next Stage

Shortlisted candidates going forward should prepare the following original documents for checking stages:

  • Printed copy of the official RRC Western Railway PET E-Call Letter.
  • Copy of the online application form paired with a valid, official photo ID card.
  • Matriculation/10th standard pass marksheets or certified equivalent industrial tracking data.
  • Community status credentials (SC, ST, OBC-NCL, EWS certificates compliant with central formatting guidelines).
  • Apprentice tracking logbooks or completion files for candidates matching those specific paths.
