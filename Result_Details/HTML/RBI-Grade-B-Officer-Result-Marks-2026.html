About This Result

The Reserve Bank of India conducted the competitive recruitment Phase I examination for the post of Officer Grade B (General) on 13 June 2026, and subsequent panels for DEPR and DSIM specializations on 14 June 2026. The selection process is strictly structured across Phase 1, Phase 2, and the final individual personal interview round to filter top professional talents for central banking duties across India.

The newly declared scorecard updates outline individual candidate performances, total secure responses, normalized sectional cut-offs, and final qualifying benchmarks. Shortlisted candidates are conditionally authorized to take part in the upcoming advanced specialized modules.

Candidates can access their specific scorecard dashboard using their validated registration numbers, application login credentials, or official roll indices published directly via the administrative server portal.

How to Check RBI Grade B Result Marks & Score Card

  1. Navigate to the official portal of the Reserve Bank of India at rbi.org.in or directly head to opportunities.rbi.org.in.
  2. Locate and expand the "Current Vacancies" panel and select the "Results" segment.
  3. Click on the highlighted hyperlink labeled "RBI Officer Grade B General Result Marks 2026".
  4. Enter your unique credentials such as Registration ID, Roll Number, and Password or Date of Birth into the secure login window.
  5. Submit the captcha prompt to load your detailed summary scores and sectional cut-off analysis spreadsheet.
  6. Download the digital document or secure a hardcopy printout for verification at subsequent assessment rounds.

Frequently Asked Questions (FAQs)

When was the RBI Officer Grade B Result Marks 2026 declared?

The detailed individual score cards and overall result marks lists were officially uploaded on 13 July 2026.

What are the general age limit criteria for RBI Grade B 2026 recruitment?

Candidates must possess a minimum age of 21 years and should not exceed the maximum age boundary of 30 years. Category-specific relaxations apply as per government rules.

What is the initial basic salary structure for an RBI Grade B Officer?

The initial monthly emoluments range between approximately ₹55,000 to ₹80,000 per month, supplemented by dynamic allowances, house configurations, and extra medical provisions.

How many posts are advertised for Officer Grade B General stream?

Out of the total 60 advertised posts across the specialized branches, the General stream comprises 40 exclusive vacancies.

What should I do if the score card authentication page fails to load?

Due to huge peak server traffic loads, the webpage may load slowly. It is recommended to clear your browser cache or re-attempt login access during off-peak operational intervals.
