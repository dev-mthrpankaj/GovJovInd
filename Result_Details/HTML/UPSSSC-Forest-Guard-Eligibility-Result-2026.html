About This Result

The Uttar Pradesh Subordinate Service Selection Commission (UPSSSC) has released the eligibility result for the Forest Guard Recruitment 2026 under Advertisement No. 12-Exam/2026. This result determines which candidates have been shortlisted based on their UPSSSC Preliminary Eligibility Test (PET) 2025 normalized scores.

Candidates who meet or exceed the newly released category-wise cut-off marks are eligible to participate in the next recruitment phases, primarily the Physical Standard Test (PST) and Physical Efficiency Test (PET).

Applicants are advised to log in to the official UPSSSC results portal using their Registration Number and Date of Birth to confirm their status. Detailed schedules and admit cards for the physical tests will be notified by the commission shortly.

How to Check UPSSSC Forest Guard Result

  1. Visit the official website of UPSSSC at upsssc.gov.in.
  2. Navigate to the "Results" segment on the homepage.
  3. Click on the link corresponding to "Eligibility Result for Forest Guard (Advt No. 12-Exam/2026)".
  4. Enter your Registration Number, Date of Birth, Gender, and the visible Verification Code.
  5. Click on the "See Result" button to view your shortlisting status.
  6. Download or take a printout of the result status for future reference.

  • Candidate's Name
  • Candidate's Registration Number
  • Category / Sub-category
  • UPSSSC PET 2025 Normalized Score
  • Shortlisting Status (Eligible / Not Eligible for Next Stage)
  • Next stage instructions

Cut Off & Merit List

The commission has published the official category-wise cut-off marks alongside the eligibility result. Candidates whose UPSSSC PET 2025 scores are equal to or higher than the official cut-off are considered shortlisted. You can download the official Cut Off PDF using the link in the Important Links section to view exact figures for General, OBC, SC, ST, and EWS categories.

What After Eligibility Result

Shortlisted candidates will undergo physical testing as the immediate next stage:

  • Physical Standard Test (PST): Measurement of Height and Chest (for males). Male candidates (UR/OBC/SC) require a minimum height of 168 CMS, and females require 152 CMS.
  • Physical Efficiency Test (PET): Candidates will have to qualify in the specified physical tasks/running as per official UPSSSC norms.
  • Document Verification: Conducted after clearing the physical stages.

Documents Required for Next Stage

  • Printed copy of the UPSSSC Forest Guard Application Form
  • UPSSSC PET 2025 Scorecard printout
  • Eligibility Result / Status Printout
  • Valid Photo ID Proof (Aadhaar, Voter ID, PAN Card, etc.)
  • 10th and 12th Marksheets & Certificates
  • Category/Caste Certificate (if claiming reservation)
  • Domicile Certificate (for UP residents)
  • Recent Passport Size Photographs
