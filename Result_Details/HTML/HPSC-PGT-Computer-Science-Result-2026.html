About This Result

Haryana Public Service Commission has released the result of the Subject Knowledge Test for the post of Post Graduate Teacher (PGT) in Computer Science under Secondary Education Department, Haryana. The result is related to Rest of Haryana Cadre and Mewat Cadre under Advertisement No. 23/2026 dated 05.02.2026.

The official notice contains roll numbers of candidates who have been declared qualified provisionally for Interview/Viva-Voce. The roll numbers are shown in ascending order, so candidates should search their roll number carefully in the official PDF.

The result is provisional and subject to fulfilment of all eligibility conditions. HPSC has also mentioned special notes regarding pending court cases, withheld results and correction rights, so candidates must read the official result notice before preparing for the next stage.

How to Check HPSC PGT Computer Science Result

  1. Visit the official Haryana Public Service Commission website at hpsc.gov.in.

  3. Download the official result PDF for Advt. No. 23/2026.

What After HPSC PGT Computer Science Result?
