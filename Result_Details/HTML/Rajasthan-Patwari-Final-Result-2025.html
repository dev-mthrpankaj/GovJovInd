About This Result

The Rajasthan Staff Selection Board (RSMSSB) initially declared the final exam result for the Patwari Direct Recruitment 2025 on 31 December 2025, recommending a total of 3,320 candidates out of 3,705 advertised vacancies. Following that announcement, several candidates were placed under a provisional status pending strict document and eligibility evaluation.

In continuation of the recruitment pipeline, the Board of Revenue, Ajmer conducted a comprehensive review of the pending validation files. Based on the clearance report, an additional 365 candidates (comprising 308 from the Non-Scheduled Area and 57 from the Scheduled Area) have been declared eligible. Their roll numbers are officially recommended to the parent department for final integration.

Please note that this updated final result remains fully subject to the ultimate legal judgements of pending Civil Writ Petitions in the High Court, specifically including WP No. 15085/2024, 2045/2026, 620/2026, 5695/2026, 2078/2026, and 4736/2026.

How to Check Rajasthan Patwari Final Result

  1. Navigate to the official portal of the Rajasthan Staff Selection Board at rsmsssb.rajasthan.gov.in.
  2. Click on the dedicated "Results" tab prominently displayed on the homepage interface.
  3. Locate the link titled "Patwari 2025: Amtim Parinam / Order regarding Recommendation of Provisional Candidates".
  4. Open and download the official notification PDF file locally.
  5. Utilize the search functionality (Ctrl + F) to input your specific Roll Number.
  6. Verify your corresponding category cut-off score listed alongside the official allocation orders.

What Follows the Final Result?

Candidates listed within the newly recommended roster will transition directly into service implementation procedures. The Board of Revenue (Revenue Mandal), Ajmer will evaluate localized administrative assignments and issue formal joining invitations.

Be advised that if any discrepancy regarding personal declarations, academic credentials, or identity validation surfaces during departmental alignment checks, the administration retains full statutory authority to nullify or modify recommendations.

Documents Required for Joining Formalities

  • Printed copy of the official RSMSSB Recommendation Order
  • Original online application form summary
  • Secondary School Certificate (Age Proof validation)
  • Graduation Degrees and relevant technical diplomas
  • Valid Rajasthan Domicile Certification
  • Recent Caste/Category verification credentials
  • Character references issued by gazetted authorities
  • Aadhar Card or valid alternative state identity proofs
