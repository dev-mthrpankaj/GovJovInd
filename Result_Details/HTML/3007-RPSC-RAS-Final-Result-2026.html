About This Result

Rajasthan Public Service Commission has declared the final result of Rajasthan State and Subordinate Services Combined Competitive Examination 2024 for Personnel (K-4/2) Department. The notice is dated 18 April 2026 and has been released after the interview stage.

According to the result notice, interviews of candidates who were provisionally successful in the mains result were conducted by the Commission from 01 December 2025 to 17 April 2026. After the interview, candidates have been listed with roll number and combined merit order. The recommendation is subject to eligibility conditions and service preference submitted by the candidates.

This result page is focused only on the final result and merit list information. Candidates should not treat a roll number listing as final appointment until the concerned department completes all required verification, document scrutiny and appointment formalities.

How to Check RPSC RAS Final Result

  1. Visit the official Rajasthan Public Service Commission website at rpsc.rajasthan.gov.in.
  2. Open the results or latest news section on the official website.
  3. Search for the result link related to Rajasthan State and Subordinate Services Combined Competitive Examination 2024.
  4. Download the official final result PDF or merit list file.
  5. Use Ctrl+F on desktop or Find in Page on mobile to search your roll number.
  6. Check the number shown in brackets beside the roll number, because it represents the combined merit order in the result PDF.
  7. Save or print the result PDF for personal reference and further department-level process.

What After Result?

After the final result, the shortlisted candidates will be recommended to the concerned department according to their combined merit order and service preference. The department may verify eligibility, documents, category claims and other conditions before issuing appointment-related instructions.

  • Check RPSC and concerned department websites for further instructions.
  • Keep original documents and photocopies ready for verification.
  • Ensure category, domicile, educational qualification and service preference documents are correct.
  • Follow official instructions if your result is marked provisional.
  • Do not ignore court-case, sealed-cover or withheld-result notes if your roll number is affected.
