About This Result

The Oriental Insurance Company Limited (OICL) has released the selection summary under the Administrative Officer (Scale-I) recruitment exercise. This follows the comprehensive Tier-I preliminary tests, Tier-II structural mains papers evaluated early in 2026, and the subsequent multi-center interview rounds managed across May 2026.

This deployment provides the definitive roll list of candidates who matched the prescribed corporate benchmarks. These candidates are provisionally cleared to approach specified institutional branches to execute their Pre-Recruitment Medical Assessments, ensuring full suitability before formal offer sheets are distributed.

Aspirants are advised that scorecards tracking absolute multi-tier point values, along with final category-wise cutoff tallies, are slated to open up via standard login prompts dynamically managed on the official domain in the weeks directly ahead.

What Follows After the Final Shortlist

Candidates holding active positions in the released shortlist must report for the Pre-Recruitment Medical Examination. Timelines, instructions, and target clinical addresses are conveyed directly via registered communication channels (Email and SMS) by the corresponding regional management hubs. Missing scheduled clinical reviews without corporate approval will result in immediate disqualification from the selection panel.

Essential Documentation Checklist for Verification

Ensure that you carry at least two independent sets of self-attested document duplications alongside your original records upon reporting:

  • Printed Copy of Online Application Form & Interview Call Sheets
  • Valid Government Identity Credentials (Aadhaar, Voter ID, PAN, Passport)
  • Proof of Birth Date (High School Matriculation Certificates)
  • Graduation and Post-Graduation Degree Records along with complete Semester Marksheets
  • Valid Caste/Community Certificates formatted matching central statutory layouts (for SC/ST/OBC)
  • EWS Income & Asset Status Verification Forms (if applicable)
  • Disability certificates matching formal formats for PwBD applicants
  • Recent color passport-sized photographs matching portal submissions
