About This Result

Bihar Police Subordinate Services Commission (BPSSC) successfully conducted the preliminary written examination for the recruitment of Assistant Sub-Inspector (Operation) in Bihar Police Radio (Wireless) against Advt. No. 04/2026. The examination was held on 22 July 2026 across various designated exam centres in Bihar state.

The commission has now officially declared the prelims result on 12 August 2026. Candidates who appeared for the exam can access the official merit list PDF to check if their roll numbers are included among the shortlisted candidates for the subsequent selection stages.

Shortlisted candidates in the preliminary test will now be eligible to participate in the Physical Efficiency Test (PET), Physical Measurement Test (PMT), and Document Verification (DV) as per the upcoming notification released by BPSSC.

How to Check BPSSC ASI Operation Result 2026

  1. Visit the official website of Bihar Police Subordinate Services Commission at bpssc.bihar.gov.in.
  2. Look for the notification section on the homepage dated 12.08.2026 regarding "Results of Preliminary Examination for Assistant Sub-Inspector (Operation) in Bihar Police Radio (Advt. No. 04/2026)".
  3. Click on the result PDF link to open or download the official write-up and roll number list.
  4. Open the downloaded PDF document in your PDF reader or web browser.
  5. Press Ctrl + F on PC or use the Search tool on mobile device and enter your 10-digit Roll Number.
  6. If your roll number appears highlighted in the PDF list, you have qualified for the next stage of recruitment (PET/PMT).
  7. Save and print a copy of the result PDF for future reference and verification.
