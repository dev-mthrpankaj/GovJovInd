About This Result

Punjab & Sind Bank invited online applications for 635 Apprentice posts from 21 May 2026 to 06 June 2026[cite: 1]. Based on the selection criteria set by the bank, the official merit list of shortlisted candidates has been published on 01 August 2026[cite: 1].

Candidates included in the published result list are provisionally selected for the engagement of Apprenticeship training. The final selection will depend upon successful Document Verification (DV), local language proficiency, and medical fitness checks as specified by the bank.

It is advised to download the official Result PDF and cross-check your Roll Number / Registration ID / Name carefully. Keep all original educational and category documents ready for submission during verification.

How to Check Punjab & Sind Bank Apprentice Result 2026

  1. Visit the official portal of Punjab & Sind Bank at punjabandsind.bank.in/content/recruitment[cite: 1].
  2. Navigate to the Recruitment / Careers section on the homepage.
  3. Locate the link titled Apprentice Result / Merit List 2026.
  4. Click on the link to download the Result PDF file.
  5. Use the search shortcut (Ctrl + F) to enter your Roll Number or Name.
  6. Save and print the result sheet for future reference and Document Verification processes.

Details Mentioned on Merit List PDF

Candidates checking the PSB Apprentice Result 2026 PDF should verify the following details:

  • Candidate's Name and Roll Number / Registration Number
  • Category / Sub-category
  • Assigned Region / State / Circle
  • Provisional Selection Status
  • Instructions for Document Verification & Joining

Cut Off & Scorecard Details

The official category-wise cut-off marks and individual marks/scorecard link will be uploaded by Punjab & Sind Bank on its portal in due course. Candidates are advised to keep checking the official website for score updates.

What After Result – Document Verification

Shortlisted candidates will receive individual communications or public notices regarding the Document Verification schedule and venue. You must bring the original and self-attested copies of the following documents:

  • Printout of Online Application Form & Result PDF page
  • Proof of Date of Birth (10th Marksheet / Birth Certificate)
  • Graduation Degree Certificate & All Semester Marksheets
  • Caste / Category Certificate (OBC / SC / ST / EWS) in valid format
  • Identity Proof (Aadhaar Card / PAN Card / Voter ID / Passport)
  • Disability / Ex-Servicemen Certificate (if applicable)
  • Recent Passport Size Photographs
