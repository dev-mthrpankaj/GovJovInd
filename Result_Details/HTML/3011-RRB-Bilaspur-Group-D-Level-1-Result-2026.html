About This Result

The Computer Based Test (CBT) corresponding to structural Level 1 profiles under advertisement CEN No. 08/2024 was executed by the Indian Railways recruitment branches between 27 November 2025 and 10 February 2026. The evaluation boards have deployed multi-session normalization algorithms to finalize the selection lists for the subsequent Physical Efficiency Test (PET).

The shortlisted candidate numbers approximate a three-times ratio matching overall community-wise positions opened for Zonal tracking (explicitly leaving out specific groups like PwBD, ExSM, and CCAA category candidates who follow independent paths). The cataloged index contains 16-digit roll configurations arranged strictly in ascending order without direct rank orientation.

Future operations including organizing physical evaluation fields, verifying original qualifications, and passing final clinical clearances will be conducted by Zonal systems. For qualifiers from RRB Bilaspur, subsequent communications will be managed by the Chairperson of the Railway Recruitment Cell (RRC), South East Central Railway, Bilaspur.

How to Check RRB Bilaspur Level 1 Result

  1. Navigate to the official portal directory of the South East Central Railway at secr.indianrailways.gov.in.
  2. Find the notification update stream marked "Shortlisted Candidates for Physical Efficiency Test (PET) under CEN 08/2024".
  3. Download and store the official publication file locally.
  4. Open the file view and use search queries to check if your 16-digit roll string is present within the columns.
  5. To find individual validation data, open the score link, enter your original Registration Number together with your verified Date of Birth (DDMMYYYY), and download your detailed performance scorecard.

Next Stage Details (PET Filters)

Provisionally selected candidates will have to pass the multi-step Physical Efficiency Test (PET). Authorized schedules, reporting layouts, and printable identity credentials (e-call letters) will appear in due course on the South East Central Railway network. Direct notifications will also be pushed to candidates via registered email strings and mobile text messages.

Aspirants successfully crossing the PET parameters will be directly forwarded to Document Verification checks, followed by standard Railway Medical Board checkups to confirm final placements.

Documents Required for Next Stage

Aspirants are required to preserve these original credentials for subsequent selection gates:

  • Printed copy of the RRC SECR issued PET E-Call Letter document.
  • Official online application log form accompanied by a valid photo ID card.
  • High school verification records (10th pass marksheet/certificate).
  • Valid community documents (SC, ST, OBC-NCL, EWS certificates matching central administrative formats).
  • Exemption proofs or Apprentice completion records for those demanding corresponding category parameters.
