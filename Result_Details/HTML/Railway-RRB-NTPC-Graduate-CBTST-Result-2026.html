About This Result

The Railway Recruitment Board (RRB) conducted the 2nd Stage Computer Based Test (CBT-2) on 13 October 2025, followed by the Computer Based Typing Skill Test (CBTST) on 28 December 2025 and 11 February 2026. This newly announced result specifically covers the posts requiring typing qualifications under CEN No. 05/2024.

The finalized merit lists showcase candidates shortlisted for the Category 4 (Senior Clerk cum Typist) and Category 5 (Junior Account Assistant cum Typist) vacancies. The compilation has taken into account the performance normalization metrics from CBT-2 alongside the qualifying performance metrics established in the CBTST phase.

Furthermore, an additional list of 31 candidates has been incorporated across various posts in continuous sequence with previous listings for Categories 1, 2, and 3. Qualified candidates are advised to download their official scorecards instantly since they will remain hosted for a fixed block window of 15 days.

How to Check RRB NTPC CBTST Result

  1. Navigate directly to the official digital portal of your respective regional Railway Recruitment Board (e.g., RRB Chennai, RRB Ajmer, RRB Bhopal).
  2. Locate the notification list updated under the notice ticker section for "CEN No. 05/2024 (NTPC Graduate Level)".
  3. Click on the specific text link displaying "Shortlisting of Candidates for Category 4 and 5 on the basis of CBTST".
  4. Open the generated result sheet PDF file on your screen layout.
  5. Utilize the structural shortcut search function (Ctrl + F) to key in your official assigned Roll Number.
  6. For accessing individual scorecards, click the scorecard login URL, enter your registration ID along with your Date of Birth, and secure a physical printout.

Details Highlighted in the Result Sheet

  • Provisionally selected candidate roll numbers arrayed horizontally in ascending sequences.

  • Normalized assessment scores achieved across written sections.
  • Qualifying criteria outcome status for the Computer Based Typing Skill Test.

    What Follows Next: DV & Medical

    Candidates discovering their roll codes inside the selection charts must configure themselves for the physical Document Verification (DV) exercises. The localized dates, reporting shifts, and destination desks will be dispatched via text alerts, emails, and regional announcements soon.

    Immediately trailing a successful structural authentication of certificates, aspirants will undergo a structured Medical Examination across designated Railway health centers to evaluate specified physical standards matching the post criteria.

    Mandatory Documents to Carry

    • Printed copy of the original online Application Form & DV Call Letter.
    • High school / Matriculation certificate verifying standard birth timing details.
    • Graduation degree certificate along with consolidated semester-wise mark sheets.
    • Original Category/Caste layout validations (SC/ST/OBC-NCL/EWS layout forms).
    • Valid Government visual identity passport codes (Aadhaar Card, PAN Card, Voter ID).
    • Income certificate for candidates claiming waivers or concession fees.
    • Medical exemption certificates or PwBD/ExSM formal verification slips if valid.

    Frequently Asked Questions

    When was the RRB NTPC Graduate CBTST Result 2026 released?

    The RRB NTPC Graduate Level CBTST and Final Result for Category 4 & 5 posts under CEN No. 05/2024 was officially released on 9 July 2026.

    Which categories and posts are covered in this result?

    This result covers Category 4 (Senior Clerk cum Typist) and Category 5 (Junior Account Assistant cum Typist) posts.

    How can I check my RRB NTPC Typing Test Result?

    Candidates can check their qualifying status and download the result PDF by visiting their respective regional RRB website and searching for their roll number.

    What credentials are required to check the CBTST scorecard?

    You need to log in using your Registration Number and Date of Birth on the official regional RRB scorecard link.

    For how long will the RRB NTPC scorecard link remain active?

    The online scorecard link will remain active for 15 days from the date of activation on the official RRB websites.

    What is the next stage after qualifying the CBTST Final Result?

    Shortlisted candidates will be called for Document Verification (DV) followed by a Medical Examination conducted by Indian Railways.
