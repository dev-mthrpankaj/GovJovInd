About This Result

The Intelligence Bureau, under the Ministry of Home Affairs, has issued the final result notice for Junior Intelligence Officer Grade-II/Tech exam 2025. The result has been prepared on the basis of combined performance in Tier-I, Tier-II and Tier-III stages.

The official PDF lists the roll numbers of candidates selected for appointment to the post of JIO-II/Tech in IB. The notice clearly says that roll numbers are placed in ascending order for candidate convenience and are not arranged in merit order.

Candidates should read the result note carefully because qualifying in the examination and interview stages does not automatically create a right to appointment. Final selection remains subject to satisfactory character and antecedents verification, medical examination and fulfilment of age, qualification, category and other eligibility conditions.

After publication of the final result, the selected candidates should keep checking official communication for further appointment-related instructions.   • Character and antecedents verification may be completed as per IB/MHA procedure.
  • Medical examination is required before final appointment.

Is IB JIO II Tech Final Result 2026 released?

How was the IB JIO II Tech final result prepared?

The final selection list is based on combined performance in Tier-I CBT held on 15 October 2025 and Tier-II & Tier-III held during 15 April to 5 May 2026.

How many candidates are selected in the final result?
