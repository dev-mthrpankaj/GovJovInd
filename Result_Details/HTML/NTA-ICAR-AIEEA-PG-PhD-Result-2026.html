About This Result

The National Testing Agency (NTA) conducted the ICAR AIEEA (All India Entrance Examination for Admission) PG and AICE-JRF/SRF (PhD) examinations on 04 July 2026 across various centers in India. These exams are held for admission to Master's and Doctoral degree programs in Agricultural Universities.

NTA has now declared the results in the form of individual scorecards. The scorecard contains the candidate's normalized score, percentile, and rank, which will form the basis for the subsequent counseling rounds.

Candidates must log in to the official result portal using their Application Number and Date of Birth to view and download their scorecards. It is advised to retain a printed copy of the scorecard as it is a mandatory document for the counseling and admission process.

How to Check Result & Download Scorecard

  1. Visit the official result portal of NTA ICAR or use the direct links given below.
  2. Select the respective link for either AIEEA PG Scorecard or PhD Scorecard.
  3. Enter your Application Number, Date of Birth, and the security pin displayed on the screen.
  4. Click on the "Submit" or "Login" button.
  5. Your scorecard will be displayed on the screen. Search for your marks and percentile carefully.
  6. Download the PDF and save/print a copy for future counseling use.

Result Details Mentioned on Scorecard

  • Candidate's Name
  • Application Number & Roll Number
  • Category (Gen/OBC/SC/ST/EWS)
  • Subject Group (e.g., Plant Science, Agronomy, etc.)
  • Total Marks Obtained
  • NTA Score / Percentile
  • All India Rank (if applicable)
  • Qualifying Status for Counseling
  • Remarks (Provisional/Withheld, if any)

Cut Off & Merit List

Official cutoff marks and subject-wise merit lists will be published by ICAR during the counseling process. Admission will be strictly based on the merit achieved in the entrance examination and availability of seats in the participating Agricultural Universities.

Status: Official cutoff will be updated after release by ICAR counseling authorities.

What After Result

Based on the scorecards released by NTA, ICAR will soon notify the schedule for online counseling. Qualified candidates must register for counseling, fill their choice of courses and universities, and participate in the seat allotment process.

Candidates are advised to keep checking the official ICAR counseling portal for the exact dates of choice filling, seat allotment, and reporting to the allotted university.

Documents Required for Next Stage (Counseling)

  • Printed copy of NTA ICAR Scorecard / Result
  • NTA ICAR AIEEA Admit Card
  • Application Form printout
  • Valid Photo ID proof (Aadhaar, Voter ID, PAN)
  • 10th / Matriculation Certificate (for Date of Birth)
  • 12th Marksheet & Certificate
  • Graduation / Post Graduation Degree & Marksheets (Provisional if final degree not received)
  • Caste / Category Certificate (OBC-NCL/SC/ST/EWS) in central government format
  • Domicile Certificate (if applicable)
  • Recent Passport size photographs
