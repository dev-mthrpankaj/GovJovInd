About This Result

The Rajasthan Public Service Commission (RPSC) successfully conducted the 1st Grade School Lecturer Examination from May 31 to June 11, 2026, for various subjects across the state. The recruitment drive aims to fill 3,225 vacancies under the School Education Department.

Currently, the commission has declared the result for the Hindi subject. This is a provisional list meant for eligibility checking and Document Verification (DV). Candidates whose roll numbers appear in the merit list have successfully qualified the written assessment.

It is important to note that merely appearing in the provisional merit list does not guarantee a final appointment. The final selection will be based on the verification of original documents and final merit list preparation. Results for other subjects (like English, Commerce, History, Mathematics, etc.) will be released in due course.

How to Check RPSC School Lecturer Result

  1. Visit the official RPSC website at rpsc.rajasthan.gov.in.
  2. Navigate to the "Results" section under the Candidate Information tab.
  3. Look for the notification titled "Result Preamble and Cutoff Marks (Provisional List for Eligibility Checking) for School Lecturer (School Edu.) – 2025".
  4. Click the link for your respective subject (e.g., Hindi) to open the PDF.
  5. Press Ctrl + F and enter your roll number to search in the list.
  6. Download and save the PDF for future reference and DV stage.

Details Mentioned in the Result PDF

  • Name of Exam Conducting Body (RPSC)
  • Exam Name and Subject
  • Roll Numbers of Provisionally Qualified Candidates
  • Category-wise Cut Off Marks
  • Result Declaration Date
  • Instructions regarding Document Verification

Cut Off Marks & Merit List

The official cut-off marks for the Hindi subject have been published along with the provisional merit list. RPSC determines these benchmarks based on factors like the number of vacancies, candidate volume, and exam difficulty level. Category-wise cut-off marks are embedded directly at the bottom of the result PDF.

Candidates can check their respective category (General, OBC, EWS, SC, ST, etc.) cut-offs directly from the official result document. If you belong to a subject for which the result is not yet out, the cut-off will be released alongside your subject's result.

What After RPSC 1st Grade Result?

  • Document Verification (DV): All provisionally shortlisted candidates must undergo thorough eligibility checking.
  • Candidates must produce original academic and reservation category certificates for verification.
  • RPSC will issue a detailed schedule and venue for the Document Verification process soon.
  • Final Merit List: After successful DV, the final merit list and allocation will be published.

Documents Required for Next Stage (DV)

  • Printed copy of RPSC School Lecturer Application Form
  • RPSC 1st Grade Exam Admit Card
  • Result/Merit List printout indicating your roll number
  • Valid Photo ID Proof (Aadhar Card, Voter ID, PAN Card, etc.)
  • Class 10th and 12th Marksheets and Certificates
  • Graduation and Post Graduation (PG) Degrees and Marksheets
  • B.Ed. Degree/Certificate
  • Category/Caste Certificate (if claiming reservation)
  • Domicile Certificate (for Rajasthan residents claiming reservation)
  • Passport size recent photographs

Frequently Asked Questions

Is RPSC School Lecturer Result 2026 released?

Yes. RPSC has released the 1st Grade Result 2026 for the Hindi subject on August 7, 2026. Results for other subjects will be released soon.

What is the result date for RPSC 1st Grade Hindi subject?

The RPSC School Lecturer result for the Hindi subject was declared on August 7, 2026.

Where to check RPSC 1st Grade result?

How to search my roll number in the result PDF?

After downloading the result PDF, open it and use the Ctrl+F shortcut to search for your roll number in the qualified candidates list.
