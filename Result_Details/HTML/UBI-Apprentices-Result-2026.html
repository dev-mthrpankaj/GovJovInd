About This Result

Union Bank of India (UBI) conducted the online written examination for the recruitment of 1865 Apprentice vacancies under the Apprentices Act, 1961 on 07 June 2026. The bank has now published the state-wise list of provisionally shortlisted candidates based on their written examination performance.

Shortlisted applicants must undergo Document Verification (DV), demonstrate proficiency in the specified local language (LLPT), and obtain a Medical Fitness Certificate from an MBBS doctor prior to final engagement. Individual reporting dates and venue details will be communicated to candidates via their registered email addresses.

The engagement duration is 1 year with a monthly stipend ranging from Rs. 15,000 to Rs. 20,000 based on the branch tier and location.

Selection Process & What After Result

  • Online Written Examination: Conducted on 07 June 2026.
  • Local Language Proficiency Test (LLPT): Candidates must read, write, speak, and understand the local language of the applied state. Candidates with 10th or 12th marksheets displaying the local language as a subject may be exempted.
  • Document Verification (DV): Physical verification of all academic and personal credentials.
  • Medical Examination: Submission of a valid Medical Fitness Certificate issued by a registered MBBS physician.

Documents Required for Verification

  • Printed copy of NATS Portal profile registration.
  • 10 recent passport-size photographs matching the application form.
  • Original 10th/Matriculation Certificate & Marksheet (Age Proof).
  • 12th Marksheet & Bachelor's Degree Certificate/Final Marksheet.
  • Caste Certificate (SC/ST/OBC-NCL) or EWS Certificate valid for FY 2025-26 (if applicable).
  • Permanent PwBD Certificate from a competent medical authority (if applicable).
  • Photo Identity Proof (Aadhaar Card, PAN Card, Passport, or Voter ID).
  • Proof of permanent and communication address.
  • Medical Fitness Certificate from a registered general physician (minimum MBBS).
  • One set of self-attested photocopies of all original documents.
