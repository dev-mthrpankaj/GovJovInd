About This Result

The National Testing Agency (NTA) conducted the Joint CSIR-UGC NET June 2026 Examination on 17th and 18th July 2026. The examination is conducted to determine the eligibility of Indian nationals for Junior Research Fellowship (JRF) and for Lectureship (LS) / Assistant Professor in Indian universities and colleges.

Following the successful completion of the exam and resolution of answer key objections, the NTA has now released the official Scorecards. Candidates can check their subject-wise percentile, total marks obtained, and their qualifying status for JRF and/or Assistant Professor categories.

Candidates must carefully check all details mentioned on their scorecard. The issuance of formal e-Certificates and JRF Award Letters to qualified candidates will follow in due course through the official CSIR/NTA web portals.

How to Check Result & Download Scorecard

  1. Visit the official NTA CSIR NET website at csirnet.nta.nic.in or use the direct link below.
  2. Click on the link titled "Joint CSIR-UGC NET JUNE-2026 Score Card".
  3. A login portal will appear. Enter your Application Number and Date of Birth.
  4. Enter the displayed Security Pin / Captcha code correctly.
  5. Click on the "Submit" or "Login" button.
  6. Your Scorecard will be displayed on the screen. Search and verify your qualifying status and subject marks carefully.
  7. Save the PDF or print the Scorecard for future use and certificate claim.

Result Details Mentioned

Your CSIR UGC NET Scorecard will contain the following important information:

  • Candidate's Name and Roll Number
  • Application Number
  • Mother's and Father's Name
  • Category and PwBD Status (if applicable)
  • Subject applied for
  • Marks / Percentile Score obtained in each Part (Part A, B, and C)
  • Total Marks / Percentile Score
  • Result Status (Qualified for JRF & Assistant Professor, OR Qualified for Assistant Professor only, OR Not Qualified)
  • Remarks (Provisional status, if any)

Cut Off & Merit List

Category-wise and subject-wise cut-off marks for JRF and Lectureship/Assistant Professor will be released officially by CSIR on their official website. The cut-off marks depend on the difficulty level of the exam, the number of candidates appearing, and the total available fellowships.

If the official cutoff is not yet visible on the NTA website, candidates should check the official notice board shortly as the cutoff PDF is usually updated after the scorecard release.

What After Result

Based on official information, the following are the next steps for qualified candidates:

  • For JRF Awardees: Candidates qualifying for JRF will be eligible to receive the CSIR JRF Award Letter. They must enroll in a Ph.D. program in a recognized university or institution within the stipulated time frame to activate their fellowship.
  • For Assistant Professor (LS): Candidates qualifying for Lectureship/Assistant Professor will be eligible to apply for Assistant Professor vacancies in Indian Universities/Colleges as per UGC rules.
  • E-Certificate Issuance: Qualified candidates will be able to download their official e-Certificate from the NTA/CSIR portal once they are uploaded. Document verification may be done online by uploading scanned copies of PG degrees and category certificates.

Documents Required for Next Stage (Certificate Claim)

Candidates may be asked to submit/upload the following documents to the CSIR portal to receive their final certificate:

  • CSIR NET Result / Scorecard Printout
  • Admit Card of CSIR UGC NET
  • Class 10th Certificate (for DOB verification)
  • Master’s Degree (M.Sc / Equivalent) Marks Sheet and Degree Certificate
  • Caste/Category Certificate (if claimed)
  • PwBD Certificate (if applicable)
  • Valid Photo ID Proof

Frequently Asked Questions

Is NTA CSIR UGC NET June 2026 result released?

Yes, NTA released the CSIR UGC NET June 2026 Result and Scorecard on 29 August 2026.

What is the NTA CSIR UGC NET June 2026 result date?

The result was declared on 29 August 2026.

Where to check the CSIR UGC NET Result 2026?

Candidates can check their result and download the scorecard from the official website csirnet.nta.nic.in.

How to search roll number or check the scorecard?

Visit the result portal link, enter your Application Number and Date of Birth along with the security pin to view your scorecard.

Is the scorecard released?

Yes, the individual scorecards with subject-wise percentile and marks have been released along with the result.

Is the cut-off released?

Category-wise and subject-wise cut-off details are typically released alongside or shortly after the scorecard. Candidates should check the official website.
