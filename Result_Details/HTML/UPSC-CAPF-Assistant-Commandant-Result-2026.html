About This Result

The Union Public Service Commission (UPSC) conducted the written part of the Central Armed Police Forces (Assistant Commandants) Examination 2026 (Advt No. 08/2026-CAPF) on 19 July 2026 across various examination centres nationwide. The examination was conducted to recruit 349 Assistant Commandants (Group A) in the Border Security Force (BSF), Central Reserve Police Force (CRPF), Central Industrial Security Force (CISF), Indo-Tibetan Border Police (ITBP), and Sashastra Seema Bal (SSB).

Candidates whose roll numbers appear in the written examination result PDF have provisionally qualified for the Physical Standards Test (PST), Physical Efficiency Test (PET), and Medical Standards Test. All qualified candidates are mandatorily required to register online and fill out the Detailed Application Form (DAF) through the official UPSC website before the specified deadline.

The Indo-Tibetan Border Police (ITBP) / designated nodal authority will intimate the schedule and e-Admit cards for PET/PST to qualified candidates. Candidates are advised to prepare their physical endurance and preserve all essential original educational and category certificates for the upcoming verification process.

How to Check UPSC CAPF Result 2026

  1. Visit the official website of the Union Public Service Commission at upsc.gov.in.
  2. Navigate to the What's New or Written Results section on the homepage.
  3. Click on the link titled Written Result: Central Armed Police Forces (ACs) Examination, 2026.
  4. The result PDF containing the roll numbers of qualified candidates will open.
  5. Press Ctrl + F on your keyboard (or use the search bar on mobile) and type your Roll Number.
  6. If your roll number is highlighted, you have successfully qualified for the PET, PST, and Medical Examination.
  7. Download and save the PDF document or print a copy for future reference and DAF filling.

What After Written Result

Candidates qualified in the written examination must successfully clear the following subsequent recruitment stages:

  • Submission of Detailed Application Form (DAF): Qualified candidates must fill out and submit the online DAF on the UPSC portal within the stipulated timeframe.
  • Physical Standards Test (PST) & Physical Efficiency Test (PET): Candidates will undergo 100-meter race, 800-meter race, Long Jump, and Shot Put (for males) conducted by the nodal recruitment authority.
  • Medical Standards Test (MST): Candidates qualifying PET will undergo comprehensive medical examination by designated Medical Boards.
  • Personality Test / Interview: Candidates declared medically fit will be summoned to UPSC Headquarters, New Delhi for the final Interview carrying 150 marks.
  • Final Merit List: Preparation of final selection list based on aggregate marks in the Written Examination and Personality Test.

Documents Required for Next Stage

  • Printed copy of submitted Detailed Application Form (DAF) with summary sheet
  • Matriculation / Secondary School Certificate (10th pass) showing date of birth
  • Graduation Degree Certificate or final year provisional mark sheet
  • Category / Caste Certificate (SC/ST/OBC/EWS) issued in the prescribed Central format
  • Valid Government Photo ID Proof (PAN Card, Passport, Driving License, Voter ID)
  • Recent passport-size colour photographs matching application upload
  • Ex-Servicemen Discharge Certificate (if applicable)
  • NOC from current employer for candidates serving in Government/PSU departments

Frequently Asked Questions

Is UPSC CAPF Assistant Commandant Result 2026 declared?

Yes, Union Public Service Commission (UPSC) declared the written examination result for Central Armed Police Forces (ACs) Examination 2026 on 25 August 2026.

Where can I download the UPSC CAPF Result 2026 PDF?

Candidates can download the result roll number list PDF directly from the official website upsc.gov.in or through the direct PDF link provided on this page.

How many vacancies are available in UPSC CAPF AC 2026?

A total of 349 vacancies are available across BSF (108), CRPF (106), CISF (70), ITBP (12), and SSB (53).

What is the next stage after UPSC CAPF written result?

Shortlisted candidates must submit the Detailed Application Form (DAF) and appear for Physical Standards / Physical Efficiency Tests (PST/PET) and Medical Standards Tests (MST).

When was the UPSC CAPF AC 2026 written exam conducted?

The written examination for UPSC CAPF Assistant Commandant 2026 was conducted on 19 July 2026.

Are individual marks released along with the written result?

No, UPSC releases the individual marks and final cut-off scores only after the declaration of the final selection list.

How can I search my roll number in the UPSC CAPF result list?

Open the downloaded result PDF file, press Ctrl + F on desktop (or use the search icon in mobile PDF readers), and enter your roll number to find your qualifying status.

What documents are required for the next stage (PET/PST/Medical)?

Candidates must carry a printed DAF copy, admit card, valid photo ID proof, matriculation certificate for DOB verification, graduation certificates, caste/category certificate, and recent photographs.
