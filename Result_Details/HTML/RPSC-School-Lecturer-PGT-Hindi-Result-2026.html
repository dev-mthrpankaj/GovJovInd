About This Result

The Rajasthan Public Service Commission (RPSC) conducted the competitive written examination for School Lecturer (PGT) Recruitment 2025–2026 from 31 May 2026 to 11 June 2026 across various examination centers in Rajasthan. The examination aimed to fill a total of 3,225 vacancies, including 710 vacancies dedicated to the subject of Hindi.

RPSC has declared the provisional list of shortlisted candidates eligible for the Document Verification (DV) process for the Hindi subject. The result PDF contains the roll numbers of all successful candidates who qualified based on performance and minimum cutoff criteria set by the commission.

Candidates listed in the merit selection list must keep all original educational degrees, B.Ed certificates, category certificates, and identity documents ready for the forthcoming verification round.

How to Check RPSC School Lecturer PGT Result

  1. Visit the official portal of Rajasthan Public Service Commission at rpsc.rajasthan.gov.in.
  2. Navigate to the News and Events section on the homepage.
  3. Look for the link titled "Result Preamble and Cutoff Marks for School Lecturer (Sec. Edu.) 2025 (Hindi)".
  4. Click on the result PDF link to open and download the document.
  5. Use the search shortcut (Ctrl + F or Find in phone) to search your Roll Number.
  6. If your roll number appears in the list, you have successfully qualified for the Document Verification stage.

Details Mentioned in Result PDF

  • Roll Numbers of Provisionally Selected Candidates
  • Category-wise Cut-off Marks (UR, EWS, OBC, SC, ST, MBC, etc.)
  • Date of Birth Cutoff for Tie-resolution

Documents Required for Document Verification (DV)

  • Printout of Detailed Application Form (DAF) submitted via SSO Portal
  • RPSC Written Exam Admit Card / Application Form Copy
  • 10th Class Marksheet & Certificate (Proof of Date of Birth)
  • 12th Class Marksheet & Passing Certificate
  • Master's Degree (Post Graduation) Marksheets and Provisional/Degree Certificate in relevant subject
  • B.Ed Degree / Professional Qualification Marksheets and Certificates
  • Domicile Certificate (Rajasthan Resident Proof, if applicable)
  • Caste / Category Certificate (OBC / SC / ST / EWS / MBC) issued by competent authority
  • No Objection Certificate (NOC) for currently employed candidates
  • Valid Photo Identity Proof (Aadhaar Card, Voter ID, PAN Card, etc.)
  • Recent Passport Size Photographs
