About This Result

The All India Institute of Medical Sciences (AIIMS) Examination Section conducted the Common Recruitment Examination (CRE 5th) for various Group B and Group C posts across participating AIIMS institutions in India under Notice No. 93/2026. A total of 1,484 vacancies were announced under this recruitment drive.

Candidates who appeared in the Computer Based Test (CBT) and re-examinations can now access their qualified status online through the official AIIMS examination portal. The result PDF includes roll numbers and qualifying status of shortlisted candidates for post-wise selection.

Shortlisted candidates are required to complete the subsequent stages of recruitment, including Document Verification, Skill Test/Trade Test (where applicable), and seat allotment as per the instructions issued by AIIMS.

How to Check AIIMS CRE 5th Result 2026

  1. Visit the official AIIMS examination website at aiimsexams.ac.in.
  2. Click on the Results tab present on the main homepage navigation bar.
  3. Locate and open the link titled "AIIMS CRE 5th Group B & C Result 2026" or Notice No. 93/2026 selection list.
  4. Login using your Application Number/Candidate ID and Password/Date of Birth, or open the merit list PDF directly if provided.
  5. Search your roll number or name carefully in the merit list using the Ctrl+F shortcut function.
  6. Download the result PDF or scorecard and print a copy for future reference and document verification.

Cut Off Marks & Selection Criteria

The cut-off marks for AIIMS CRE 5th Group B & C posts are prepared based on total vacancies, category-wise reservation rules, post-specific minimum qualifying percentile, and total candidates appearing in the CBT exam.

Official category-wise cut-off marks are published along with individual post results or candidate login dashboards. Candidates are advised to login to their account on aiimsexams.ac.in to check detailed percentile scores and post-wise rank.

What After AIIMS CRE 5th Result?

  • Skill Test / Trade Test: Posts requiring typing, technical, or trade proficiency will have post-specific practical tests.
  • Document Verification (DV): Shortlisted candidates must present original certificates, marksheets, and category documents before the verification committee.
  • Institute Allotment & Joining: Final seat allocation to respective AIIMS across India will be done based on candidate merit rank and institute preference filled during the recruitment process.

Documents Required for Next Stage / DV

  • Printout of AIIMS CRE 5th Online Application Form
  • Admit Card and Result / Scorecard Printout
  • Proof of Date of Birth (Class 10th Certificate / Birth Certificate)
  • Educational Qualification Certificates and Marksheets (10th, 12th, Degree, Diploma as applicable)
  • Valid Category Certificate (SC / ST / OBC-NCL / EWS / PwBD) issued by competent authority
  • Valid Photo Identity Proof (Aadhaar Card, Voter ID, Passport, PAN Card, Driving License)
  • Experience Certificate / NOC from current employer (if applicable for post)
  • Recent Passport-Size Photographs
