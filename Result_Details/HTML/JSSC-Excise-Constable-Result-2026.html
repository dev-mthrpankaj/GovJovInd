About This Result

The Jharkhand Staff Selection Commission (JSSC) conducted the Jharkhand Excise Constable Competitive Examination (JECCE-2023, Advt. No. 06/2023) to recruit eligible candidates for 583 Excise Constable posts in Jharkhand. Following the Physical Efficiency Test (PET), the written examination was held on 12 April 2026 across various examination centers in Jharkhand.

The declared result has been published in PDF format as a Merit List / Shortlist of candidates. Shortlisted candidates whose roll numbers appear in the merit list PDF are qualified to appear for the next phase of selection, which includes Document Verification and Medical Examination.

Candidates are strongly advised to check their roll numbers carefully in the official notification PDF and keep all required original documents prepared for the upcoming selection stages as instructed by JSSC.

How to Check JSSC Excise Constable Result

  1. Go to the official JSSC website at jssc.jharkhand.gov.in.
  2. Navigate to the "Results" section available on the homepage.
  3. Click on the active result link for "Result / Merit List of Jharkhand Excise Constable Competitive Examination (JECCE-2023)".
  4. The official JSSC Excise Constable Result PDF will open on screen.
  5. Use the search function (press Ctrl + F on PC or search option on mobile) and type your Roll Number or Registration Number.
  6. If your roll number is highlighted, you have qualified for the next stage. Save and print the PDF for reference.

Cut Off Marks

The official category-wise cut-off marks for JSSC Excise Constable written examination will be updated after official announcement by JSSC along with final selection details. The selection is processed through Physical Efficiency Test, Written Examination, Document Verification, and Medical Examination.

What After Result (Next Stage)

Candidates who have qualified in the JSSC Excise Constable written examination result will be called for the following final stages:

  • Document Verification (DV): Verification of original educational certificates, category status, residence certificates, and identity documents.
  • Medical Examination: Standard medical fitness tests as mandated under Jharkhand Excise Constable Recruitment rules.
  • Final Merit List: Final recommendation and appointment based on overall merit rank and fitness clearance.

Documents Required for Document Verification

  • Printed copy of Application Form and Admit Card
  • JSSC Excise Constable Result PDF copy
  • 10th / Matriculation Certificate & Marksheet (Date of Birth Proof)
  • Cast / Category Certificate (if claiming reservation)
  • Domicile / Local Resident Certificate of Jharkhand
  • Valid Photo ID Proof (Aadhaar Card, Voter ID, PAN Card, or Driving License)
  • Recent Passport-Size Color Photographs
  • No Objection Certificate (NOC) if currently employed in Government service

Frequently Asked Questions

Is JSSC Excise Constable Result 2026 declared?

Yes, Jharkhand Staff Selection Commission (JSSC) officially declared the result for JECCE-2023 on 28 July 2026.

What is the result date for JSSC Excise Constable 2026?

The result was officially released on 28 July 2026.

Where can I check the JSSC Excise Constable Result 2026?

You can check and download the merit list PDF on the official website jssc.jharkhand.gov.in.

How to search roll number in JSSC Excise Constable Merit List PDF?

Open the result PDF, press Ctrl + F (or use search in mobile PDF viewer), type your Roll Number, and hit Enter.

Is a separate scorecard released for JSSC Excise Constable?

The result is released as a combined merit list PDF. Individual scores, if applicable, are hosted on candidate login portals by JSSC.

What documents are required for the next stage?

Admit card, result printout, matriculation certificate, category certificate, Jharkhand domicile certificate, photo ID, and photographs.

Wait a few minutes and refresh the official portal jssc.jharkhand.gov.in during non-peak hours.
