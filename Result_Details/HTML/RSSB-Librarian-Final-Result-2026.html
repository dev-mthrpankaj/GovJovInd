About This Final Selection Result

The Rajasthan Staff Selection Board had initiated the online application window for Librarian Grade III vacancies from 05 March 2025 until 03 April 2025. Following standard computational evaluations and document vetting routines, the initial filtered result list was produced on 02 December 2025. This cumulative final list represents the structural baseline allocations for general postings across regular state departments.

The released allocation lists categorize select candidates separately into Non-TSP areas (483 total posts) and TSP areas (65 total posts). Candidates whose names appear in the verified selection lists are recommended to monitor instructions issued by the respective state deployment department for training schedules or physical joining locations.

How to Check RSSB Librarian Final Selection Status

  1. Navigate to the primary domain portal of the board at rssb.rajasthan.gov.in.
  2. Click directly on the "Results" link visible on the homepage navigation interface.
  3. Search specifically for the headline link labeled "Librarian 2024: Recommendation of Finally Selected Candidates".
  4. Open or download the dedicated Non-TSP and TSP roll number compilation sheets.
  5. Use the search shortcut functionality (Ctrl+F or search bar on mobile devices) to search your individual Roll Number.
  6. If recommended, proceed to save the respective file or check the SSO portal tracking board for structural document copies.

What After Final Selection?

Since this publication marks the conclusion of the evaluation cycle executed by the Rajasthan Staff Selection Board, the list of successful candidates is transmitted directly to the indenting state administration department. The designated department will subsequently issue notifications detailing:
