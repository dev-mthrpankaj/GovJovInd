About This Result

The Uttar Pradesh Police Recruitment and Promotion Board (UPPRPB) has concluded the final allocation phase for the Sub Inspector Civil Police, Platoon Commander (PAC), Sub Inspector (Special Security Force), and Women Sub Inspector (PC) posts under the 2025 recruitment cycle. The competitive offline/online written tests were organized on March 14 and March 15, 2026 across 75 districts in 4 distinct shifts. Normalization was applied to adjust score variations across shifts.

Following the written examinations, a total of 12,333 candidates (approximately 2.7 times the advertised vacancies) were called for the Document Verification and Physical Standard Test (DV & PST) held between June 15 and June 17, 2026. The successful candidates then qualified for the Physical Efficiency Test (PET) held between June 29 and July 4, 2026.

This final selection list is based systematically on the normalized score performance, order of post preferences submitted by candidates, and the strict adherence to state reservation provisions. Individual normalized score marks cards will be enabled online for validation by late July.

How to Check UP Police SI Final Selection Status

  1. Navigate to the official portal of UPPRPB at uppbpb.gov.in or use the direct results sub-domain links.
  2. Find the notice dated 14-07-2026 titled "Final Selection Result for Sub Inspector Civil Police and Equivalent Posts - 2025".
  3. Open the respective list (List-1 for overall select order, or post-wise and category-wise lists from List-2 to List-6).
  4. To look up personal status instantly, input your Unique Registration Number along with your exact Date of Birth inside the allocation dashboard link.
  5. Verify your name, alignment status, and final post code preference allocated.
  6. Save the selection list snippet and keep physical prints accessible for medical test invitations.

Mandatory Stages Following Final Selections

As per the statutory guidelines issued by the Board under section rules, these provisional selections are fully subject to succeeding validation parameters managed by appointing authorities:

  • Medical Examination Evaluation: Appointed medical boards will thoroughly check psychological fitness, eye vision ratios, and physical criteria standard checkups.
  • Document & Character Verification: Thorough cross-verification of academic certificates, category credentials, and local station character profiling.
  • Resolution of Tie-Break Situations: Handled effectively using institutional provisions (preferential qualifications, older age prioritization, and alphabetical order of names).

Documents Required for Joining Formalities

  • Online Selection Allocation Sheet Copy
  • Original Matricification & Higher Secondary Degree/Certificates
  • Graduation Passing Degrees and Marksheets
  • Recent Caste Certificate / EWS Category validation documents
  • Domicile Certificate of Uttar Pradesh
  • Horizontal Reservation Certificates (Ex-Servicemen / Freedom Fighter Dependent proofs if claimed)
  • Valid Government Identity Proofs (Aadhaar Card, PAN, Driving Licence)
  • Passport-size identical color photographs

Frequently Asked Questions (FAQs)

Is the UP Police SI Final Result 2026 declared?

Yes, the Uttar Pradesh Police Recruitment and Promotion Board (UPPRPB) officially declared the final selection result list on 14 July 2026.

Where can I check the UPPRPB SI Final Result?

Candidates can check their final results on the official website at uppbpb.gov.in or via the direct login result link provided by the board.

When will the normalized marks of the candidates be released?

UPPRPB will upload the normalized marks for all candidates participating in the written exam on the official website starting from 22 July 2026.

What is the next stage after the final selection result?

The selected candidates will have to undergo a Medical Examination followed by character verification and educational certificate verification by the appointing authority.

What is the final cut-off for the UR category under Sub Inspector Civil Police?

The final vertical reservation cut-off marks for the Unreserved (UR) category under Sub Inspector Civil Police is 373.93936.

Can I submit an RTI application for checking written exam response sheets now?

No, according to the official write-up notice, separate individual applications under the RTI Act 2005 concerning selection scores will not be entertained by the board.

What if multiple candidates secure identical marks at the cut-off boundary?

Tie-breaking rules apply, evaluating components like preferential qualifications (O/A Level, NCC), followed by age seniority, and alphabetical matching order.

Where will the schedule for medical examinations be published?

The detailed schedule, location allocations, and admit cards for the medical tests will be issued by the respective appointing authorities in due course.
