About This Result

The RBI Office Attendant Result 2026 has been issued as a roll-number-wise shortlist. The provided PDF does not contain a detailed result notice, marks, cutoff table or individual scorecard. It simply lists the roll numbers of candidates shortlisted under different RBI office regions.

Candidates whose roll number appears in the PDF should keep their application details and documents ready for the next stage. In RBI Office Attendant recruitment, the next stage generally includes Language Proficiency Test and document verification as per official recruitment rules.

Since the uploaded result sheet does not include detailed instructions, candidates should regularly check the RBI Opportunities portal for LPT schedule, document verification notice, scorecard, cutoff and any office-wise instructions.

How to Check RBI Office Attendant Result

  1. Visit the official RBI website at rbi.org.in.
  2. Open the Opportunities@RBI section and go to Current Vacancies or Results.
  3. Find the RBI Office Attendant Result 2026 link.
  4. Download the result PDF or shortlisted candidates list.
  5. Use the PDF search option and enter your roll number carefully.
  6. If your roll number is listed, save the PDF and wait for the official next-stage instructions.
