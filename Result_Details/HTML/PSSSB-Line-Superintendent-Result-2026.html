About This Result

The Punjab Subordinate Selection Board (PSSSB) conducted the written examination for the recruitment of Line Superintendents on 19 July 2026. The examination was held to fill a total of 06 vacancies across the state of Punjab. Candidates who appeared for this MCQ-based written exam can now check their qualifying status.

The result has been officially declared in the form of a PDF merit list. The list contains the roll numbers and names of the candidates who have been shortlisted for the next phase of the recruitment process based on their marks and category-wise cut-offs.

Candidates who have qualified the written examination are advised to prepare their original documents and certificates, as they will soon be called for Document Verification (DV). The exact schedule for DV will be notified by the board on its official website shortly.

How to Check PSSSB Line Superintendent Result

  1. Visit the official website of the Punjab Subordinate Selection Board at sssb.punjab.gov.in.
  2. Click on the "Results" section available on the homepage.
  3. Find and click on the link titled "PSSSB Line Superintendent Result 2026".
  4. The result PDF file will open on your screen.
  5. Use the search function (Ctrl + F) to find your Roll Number or Name carefully.
  6. Download and save the Result PDF for your future reference and document verification stage.

What After Result (Next Stage)

Candidates whose roll numbers are present in the merit list are provisionally shortlisted for the next stage, which is Document Verification (DV).

  • The official schedule, date, and venue for the Document Verification will be announced soon on the PSSSB website.
  • Candidates must attend the DV round in person on the allotted date and time.
  • Failure to produce the required original documents will lead to the cancellation of the candidature.

Documents Required for Next Stage

  • Printed copy of the Result / Scorecard
  • Admit Card of the Written Examination
  • Printed copy of the Online Application Form
  • 10th / Matriculation Certificate (Must show Punjabi Language passed)
  • Diploma in Electrical Engineering from a recognized University
  • Valid Photo ID Proof (Aadhaar Card, PAN Card, Voter ID, etc.)
  • Category / Caste Certificate (SC/ST/OBC) if applicable
  • Domicile Certificate (for Punjab residents claiming reservation)
  • Recent Passport size photographs
