About This Result

The State Bank of India (SBI) conducted the preliminary examination for the recruitment of Probationary Officers (PO) in August 2026. The official result for this stage was declared on 28 August 2026. Candidates who have successfully cleared the preliminary phase are now eligible to appear for the Mains examination.

The result is available in PDF format, containing the roll numbers of the shortlisted candidates. Candidates are advised to use the search function (Ctrl+F) within the PDF to locate their roll numbers easily. Separate instructions and admit cards for the upcoming Mains examination will be issued by SBI shortly on their official careers portal.

How to Check SBI PO Result

  1. Visit the official State Bank of India careers portal at sbi.bank.in/web/careers.
  2. Navigate to the "Current Openings" section.
  3. Locate the dropdown menu for "Recruitment of Probationary Officers".
  4. Click on the link for "Preliminary Examination Result".
  5. The result PDF containing shortlisted roll numbers will open.
  6. Press Ctrl + F and search for your Roll Number carefully.
  7. Save the result PDF for future reference.

Result Details Mentioned

  • Candidate Roll Number
  • Qualifying Status for Mains Examination
  • General instructions for the next recruitment stage

What After Preliminary Result

Candidates who find their roll numbers in the preliminary result PDF are shortlisted for the next stages of the SBI PO recruitment process. The subsequent stages include:

  • Mains Examination: A comprehensive objective and descriptive test.
  • Psychometric Test & Group Exercise: Evaluation of behavioral traits and group dynamics.
  • Personal Interview: Final evaluation by the SBI panel.
  • Document Verification: Conducted for provisionally selected candidates prior to joining.

Candidates must regularly check the official SBI website for updates regarding the Mains Examination admit card and exact schedule.
