About This Result

The Union Public Service Commission (UPSC) conducted the Engineering Services (Main) Examination 2026 on 21 June 2026 across various examination centres in India. Based on candidate performance in the written part of the Mains examination, UPSC declared the official result on 24 July 2026.

The declared result contains the roll-wise list of 1,340 provisionally qualified candidates eligible for the final stage—the Personality Test (Interview). Qualified engineering candidates belong to four primary branches: Civil Engineering, Mechanical Engineering, Electrical Engineering, and Electronics & Telecommunication Engineering.

Candidates whose roll numbers appear in the official notice are required to keep their educational certificates, caste certificates, and identity documents ready. Request for changes in scheduled interview dates or times will not be entertained by the Commission.

How to Check UPSC ESE Mains Result 2026

  1. Visit the official portal of Union Public Service Commission at upsc.gov.in.
  2. Look under the What's New or Written Results section on the homepage.
  3. Click on the link titled Written Result: Engineering Services (Main) Examination, 2026.
  4. Open or download the official Roll List PDF document.
  5. Press Ctrl + F on keyboard (or search icon on mobile PDF viewer) and enter your 7-digit Roll Number.
  6. If your roll number appears in the PDF, you have qualified for the Personality Test stage.
  7. Save or print a copy of the official PDF for future reference.

Details Mentioned in Result Notice

  • Full name of the examination: Engineering Services (Main) Examination 2026.
  • Branch-wise list of Roll Numbers of qualified candidates (Civil, Mechanical, Electrical, E&T).
  • Total count of shortlisted candidates (1,340 candidates).
  • Instructions regarding online window opening for submission of candidate details.
  • Guidelines regarding e-Summon letter download for Personality Test.
  • Verification of original documents during interview stage.

Cut Off & Scorecard Status

As per official UPSC guidelines, category-wise cut-off marks and individual candidate scorecards for Prelims and Mains examinations will be uploaded on the Commission's official website after the declaration of the Final Result (post-interview stage).

Official cutoff marks are updated only upon formal publication by UPSC. Candidates should not rely on unverified expected cutoffs.

What After Result & Next Stage Instructions

  • Step 1: Update Details Online: UPSC will activate an online window on upsconline.nic.in where shortlisted candidates must confirm/update contact details, higher qualifications, employment details, and service preferences.
  • Step 2: Interview e-Summon Letter: After processing online submissions, UPSC will upload e-Summon letters for Personality Test on its portal.
  • Step 3: Personality Test (Interview): Interviews will be held at UPSC premises in Dholpur House, Shahjahan Road, New Delhi. No request for changing date or time will be considered.
  • Step 4: Final Selection: Final merit list will be prepared based on combined marks obtained in Mains Written Exam and Personality Test.

Documents Required for Interview / Next Stage

  • e-Summon Letter printout for Personality Test.
  • Matriculation / Secondary School certificate showing Date of Birth.
  • Degree Certificate or provisional qualification degree in relevant Engineering branch.
  • Caste / Category Certificate (SC / ST / OBC / EWS) in prescribed format, if applicable.
  • Persons with Benchmark Disabilities (PwBD) certificate, if applicable.
  • Government issued Photo Identity Proof (Aadhaar Card, Passport, Voter ID, PAN Card, Driving License).
  • No Objection Certificate (NOC) from employer for candidates currently in government service.
  • Recent passport-size photographs as specified in e-Summon letter.
