About This Result

The Border Security Force (BSF) published the dynamic recruitment drive notification for the post of Constable (Tradesmen) in the Employment News on 26 July 2025, and subsequently hosted a detailed advertisement layout on the official portal on 25 July 2025. Following the completion of online application submissions ending 23 August 2025, corrigendum layouts were released on 06 January 2026 and 09 January 2026 respectively. The first screening benchmark, consisting of the Physical Efficiency Test and Physical Standard Test (PET/PST), was safely regulated w.e.f. 26 December 2025 through 28 March 2026 at highly diversified recruitment hubs across India.

The newly released notice officially features the roll numbers of prospective candidates who have safely cleared the physical evaluation benchmarks and stands provisionally shortlisted for the 2nd selection phase consisting of a written examination. Candidate shortlists have been categorized trade-wise, featuring specific configurations for Constable Barber, Carpenter, Cobbler, Cook, and other related tradesmen profiles.

As candidate parameters are categorized strictly on a provisional alignment basis, final validation parameters regarding merit standing, trade specialization efficiency, and medical fitness scores will be closely checked during the terminal evaluation cycles. Shortlisted individuals are recommended to start preparing for the multi-format written examination matrix immediately.
