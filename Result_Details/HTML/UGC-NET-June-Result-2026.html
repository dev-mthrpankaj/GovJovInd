About This Result

The National Testing Agency (NTA) conducted the UGC NET June 2026 examination via Computer-Based Test (CBT) mode on 22, 23, 24, 25, 29 & 30 June 2026[cite: 2]. A re-exam was also held on 09 September 2026 for English & Commerce and 10 September 2026 for Sociology[cite: 2]. The final result and scorecard have now been made available to all candidates[cite: 2].

The result determines the eligibility of Indian nationals for 'Assistant Professor' and 'Junior Research Fellowship (JRF) and Assistant Professor' in Indian universities and colleges[cite: 2]. Candidates must log in using their Application Number and Date of Birth to view their individual scores and qualifying status.

Alongside the scorecard, the NTA has also released the final answer key and official cut-off PDFs[cite: 2]. Candidates who have successfully qualified will subsequently be able to download their e-Certificates and JRF Award Letters from the official website.

How to Check Result

  1. Visit the official UGC NET portal at ugcnet.nta.nic.in[cite: 2].
  2. Click on the link for "UGC NET June 2026 Result / Scorecard" on the homepage[cite: 2].
  3. You will be redirected to the login portal.
  4. Enter your Application Number, Date of Birth, and the visible Security Pin[cite: 2].
  5. Click on the Submit button[cite: 2].
  6. Your scorecard and qualifying status will appear on the screen. Save and print it for future reference[cite: 2].

Result Details Mentioned

  • Candidate's Name and Roll Number
  • Application Number
  • Category and Subject details
  • Marks obtained in Paper 1 and Paper 2
  • Total Marks and Percentage/Percentile Score
  • Qualifying Status for Assistant Professor
  • Qualifying Status for JRF & Assistant Professor

Cut Off & Merit Details

The NTA has officially released the subject-wise and category-wise cut-off marks and percentiles for the June 2026 cycle[cite: 2]. Candidates can download the official cut-off PDF using the links provided below. The cut-off determines the minimum score required to qualify for JRF or Assistant Professor.

  • Assistant Professor: Candidates meeting the baseline cut-off are eligible for Assistant Professor roles.
  • JRF & Assistant Professor: Candidates scoring above the higher JRF cut-off are eligible for both the fellowship and the teaching role.

What After Result

  • e-Certificate: Qualified candidates will be issued their digital e-Certificates by the NTA shortly.
  • JRF Award Letter: Candidates who qualified for JRF will receive an Award Letter valid for 3 years to pursue research.
  • Job Application: Eligible candidates can apply for Assistant Professor vacancies in universities and colleges across India.

Documents Required for Next Stage

While checking the result online requires only the Application Number and DOB, candidates claiming JRF or applying for university posts will need:

  • Printed copy of UGC NET June 2026 Scorecard
  • UGC NET e-Certificate / JRF Award Letter
  • Master's Degree Certificates and Marksheets
  • Caste/Category Certificate (if applicable)
  • Valid Photo ID Proof
