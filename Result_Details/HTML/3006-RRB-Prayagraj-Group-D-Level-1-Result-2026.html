About This Result

RRB Prayagraj Group D Level 1 Result 2026 has been published for candidates who appeared in the Computer Based Test conducted for various Level 1 posts under CEN 08/2024. The official result PDF contains 16-digit roll numbers arranged in ascending order, not in merit order.

The shortlist is provisional and is meant for the Physical Efficiency Test. The notice states that candidates equal to three times the community-wise vacancies, excluding PwBD, ExSM and CCAA candidates shortlisted in that community, have been shortlisted for PET.

Candidates can also check their percentile score and normalized marks through the login link on official RRB websites by entering their registration number and date of birth in DDMMYYYY format. For future stages such as PET, document verification, medical examination and panel publication, candidates should follow RRC Prayagraj.

How to Check RRB Prayagraj Group D Result

  1. Visit the official RRB Prayagraj website or the official RRC Prayagraj website for latest result notices.
  2. Open the CEN 08/2024 Level 1 result / PET shortlist link.
  3. Download the official result PDF or merit list PDF.
  4. Use the search option in PDF and enter your 16-digit roll number carefully.
  5. For marks and percentile score, open the official login link and enter registration number with date of birth in DDMMYYYY format.
  6. If shortlisted, save the result PDF and keep checking the official website for PET e-call letter, schedule and instructions.
  7. Print or download the official notice for future reference during PET and document verification.

Result Details Mentioned

  • 16-digit roll numbers of candidates shortlisted for PET.
  • Result status and provisional shortlisting note.
  • CEN number and post level under 7th CPC Pay Matrix.
  • Normalized cutoff marks for UR, SC, ST, OBC and EWS categories.
  • Score and normalized marks login availability dates.
  • Next-stage instructions for PET, document verification and medical examination.
  • Official warning that shortlisting for PET does not guarantee appointment.
  • Note regarding PwBD, ExSM and CCAA candidates being exempted from PET.

What After RRB Group D Result?

After the CBT result, shortlisted candidates have to appear for the Physical Efficiency Test. The PET schedule will be published by the Chairperson of RRC Prayagraj on the official RRC website, and shortlisted candidates may also receive SMS or email for downloading the PET e-call letter.

After PET, the recruitment process will move towards document verification, medical examination and publication of panel by the concerned Railway Recruitment Cell. Candidates should treat every update from RRC Prayagraj as important because RRB has transferred future communication for these stages to the concerned RRC.

  • Physical Efficiency Test conducted by RRC Prayagraj.
  • Document Verification for candidates qualifying the required stage.
  • Medical Examination as per Railway rules and post requirement.
  • Panel publication by the concerned Railway Recruitment Cell.

Documents Required for Next Stage

  • PET e-call letter / admit card issued by RRC Prayagraj.
  • RRB Group D application form or registration details.
  • Valid photo ID proof such as Aadhaar Card, Voter ID, Passport, PAN Card or Driving Licence.
  • Recent passport size photographs as per official instructions.
  • Class 10th / ITI / educational qualification certificates, as applicable.
  • Caste / category certificate for SC, ST, OBC, EWS or other reserved categories, if applicable.
  • Domicile or other supporting certificates, if asked in the official instructions.
  • PwBD, ExSM or CCAA documents, if applicable for the candidate.
  • Any other document mentioned in the PET/DV e-call letter or official RRC notice.

Frequently Asked Questions

Is RRB Prayagraj Group D Level 1 Result 2026 released?

Yes. Railway Recruitment Board, Prayagraj has released the CEN 08/2024 Level 1 CBT shortlist result for Physical Efficiency Test on 01 July 2026.

What is the next stage after RRB Prayagraj Group D CBT result?

The next stage is Physical Efficiency Test. After PET, document verification, medical examination and panel publication will be handled by RRC Prayagraj as per official instructions.

How can I check my RRB Prayagraj Group D result?

Open the official RRB Prayagraj or RRC Prayagraj website, download the CEN 08/2024 Level 1 PET shortlist PDF, and search your 16-digit roll number in the list.

Can candidates check marks and percentile score?

Yes. The notice says candidates can log in with registration number and date of birth to view percentile score and normalized marks.

When will the score link be available?

The score and normalized marks link will be live on or before 01 July 2026 at 18:00 hours and will remain available up to 16 July 2026 at 23:55 hours.

What is the RRB Prayagraj Group D PET cutoff 2026?

The normalized cutoff marks are UR 78.12573, SC 72.40928, ST 66.6584, OBC 77.31772 and EWS 74.14863 as given in the cutoff notice.

Are PwBD, ExSM and CCAA candidates included in this PET roll number list?

No. The notice states that PwBD, ExSM and CCAA candidates are exempted from PET, so their roll numbers are not included in this PET list.

Where will the PET schedule and e-call letter be released?

The PET schedule and e-call letter updates for shortlisted RRB Prayagraj candidates will be published by RRC Prayagraj. SMS and email may also be sent to registered details.

Does being shortlisted for PET guarantee appointment?

No. The official notice clearly says that calling a candidate for PET does not provide any right to appointment in Railways.

What is the official website for next-stage updates?

Candidates should regularly check the official RRC Prayagraj website rrcpryj.org and the RRB Prayagraj website for authentic updates.
