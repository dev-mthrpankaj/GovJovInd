About This Result

The Madhya Pradesh Professional Examination Board (MPESB), formerly known as Vyapam, has completed all evaluation phases of the Madhya Pradesh Police Subedar Stenographer & Assistant Sub Inspector Ministerial Recruitment Test 2025. Following the written examination held in December 2025, phase 2 fee payments, and subsequent skill tests, the final merit list has been issued.

The final result covers 100 vacancies for Subedar (Stenographer) and 400 vacancies for Assistant Sub Inspector (ASI) Ministerial. Selection is strictly based on the normalized scores of the written test, performance benchmarks in typing/shorthand skill tests, and verification of eligibility rules specified in the official notification.

How to Download Final Result Card

  1. Go to the official website of MPESB at esb.mp.gov.in.
  2. Navigate to the "Results" tab on the main dashboard menu.
  3. Click on the link titled "MPESB MP Police Subedar & Sub Inspector SI Final Result 2026".
  4. Provide your unique Application Number or Roll Number along with your Date of Birth in the designated login fields.
  5. Solve the mandatory CAPTCHA security verification calculation.
  6. Click on "Submit" to view and download your full individual final selection result card.
  7. Take a clear printout to preserve for upcoming physical verification rounds.

  • Qualifying Status in Shorthand and Hindi Typing Skill Tests
  • Final Recommendation Status (Selected / Non-Selected / Waitlisted)
  • Allotted Post Preference Allocation Category code

Documents Required for Next Stage Verification

Shortlisted candidates recommended in the final selection list must present original copies along with self-attested sets of the following documents during joining formalities:

  1. Valid printout of the Final Selection Result Card
  2. Class 10th High School Certificate (serving as official proof of Date of Birth)
  3. Class 12th Intermediate Marksheet & Certificate
  4. Graduation Degree / MCA / BCA / M.Sc or valid Computer Diploma (DOEACC / COPA / ITI)
  5. Valid CPCT Scorecard with passing marks in Hindi Typing
  6. Shorthand Certificate (for Subedar Stenographer applicants)
  7. Domicile Certificate of Madhya Pradesh (if claiming reservation benefits)
  8. Valid Caste / Category Certificate issued by competent authority (SC/ST/OBC/EWS)
  9. Recent passport-size photographs matching application uploads
