About This Result

Central Bank of India (CBI) conducted the nationwide online competitive examination for 4500 Apprentice positions on 05 July 2026. Following the evaluation of candidates' performances, the bank has published the list of provisionally shortlisted candidates who qualified for the next selection rounds.

Candidates included in the merit PDF must verify their roll numbers and registration details. Selection is provisional subject to fulfillment of prescribed eligibility standards, age criteria, verification of original certificates, and performance in the Local Language Test (if applicable).

Failure to produce valid original documents at the time of verification will result in cancellation of candidature. Candidates are advised to keep track of official communications on centralbank.bank.in.

How to Check CBI Apprentice Result

  1. Visit the official portal of Central Bank of India at centralbank.bank.in/en/recruitments.
  2. Locate the recruitment section or active notice tab on the homepage.
  3. Click on the link titled "List of Provisionally Shortlisted Candidates for CBI Apprentice Recruitment 2026".
  4. Download and open the Annexure PDF file on your device.
  5. Use the search shortcut (Ctrl + F) to search for your Roll Number or Registration Number.
  6. If your details appear in the list, save a copy of the PDF for future reference during Document Verification.

Details Mentioned in the Result PDF

  • Candidate Roll Number / Registration Number
  • Candidate Name (if listed in the Annexure)
  • Applied State / Region / Zone
  • Provisional Shortlist Status
  • Category & PwBD Status
  • Instructions regarding Document Verification & Local Language Proficiency

What After Result: Next Stage Details

Candidates provisionally selected in the written examination shortlist must prepare for the subsequent stages:

  • Local Language Test: Candidates must demonstrate proficiency (reading, writing, speaking, and understanding) in the local language of the state opted for. Candidates who produce proof of studying the local language in 10th or 12th standard may be exempted as per official rules.
  • Document Verification (DV): Physical verification of all original certificates, educational qualifications, age proof, identity documents, and category certificates.
  • Medical Examination: Final engagement is subject to medical fitness as prescribed by the Central Bank of India.

Documents Required for Next Stage

  • Printout of Online Application Form & Admit Card.
  • Valid photo identity proof (Aadhaar Card, PAN Card, Voter ID, Passport).
  • 10th / Matriculation Certificate as proof of Date of Birth.
  • Graduation Degree Certificate & Mark sheets from a recognized University.
  • Caste Certificate (SC/ST/OBC-NCL/EWS) in prescribed Central Government format, if applicable.
  • Disability Certificate (PwBD) / Ex-Servicemen Discharge Certificate, if applicable.
  • Proof of local language studied in 10th/12th level (if applicable).
  • Recent passport-sized photographs matching application records.
