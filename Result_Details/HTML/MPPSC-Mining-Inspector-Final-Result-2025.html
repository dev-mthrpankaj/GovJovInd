About This Result

The Madhya Pradesh Public Service Commission executed the written examination for Mining Inspector positions on 21 December 2025. Following the evaluation of response sheets, a total of 123 candidates were provisionally selected to appear for the personal interview rounds. The selection process highlights a rigorous screening scheme evaluating comprehensive knowledge in General Studies and Geology/Mining Engineering streams.

On 9 July 2026, the commission formally posted the finalized selection order along with the dynamic 'Obtained Mark List'. The compiled sheet incorporates aggregated scores acquired across the 450-mark written module alongside the 50-mark personal interview parameters. Due to vacant slot variations across reserved tiers, 11 select vacancies have been left unfilled as no eligible applicants met the rigorous minimum threshold criteria.

Since this document lists the ultimate marks distributions post the complete exhaustion of the multi-tier selection matrix (Written + Interview + Core DV), the recommended candidates will now be issued direct selection and joining mandates by the Mineral Resources Department of the Madhya Pradesh state administration.
