About This Result

Bihar Police Subordinate Services Commission has declared the written examination result for the post of Havildar Clerk in Bihar Home Guard Service Cadre. The recruitment is being conducted under Advertisement No. 01/2026 for 64 vacancies.

The written examination was conducted on 10 June 2026. According to the official notice, 1,76,389 candidates were scheduled for the written examination, 79,791 candidates appeared and 96,598 candidates remained absent. After excluding candidates declared ineligible due to malpractice or OMR-related issues, 75,218 answer sheets were evaluated.

This result is not the final selection list. It is a shortlist for the next stage, where candidates will have to appear for certificate verification and Physical Efficiency Test. The official notice is important because it contains cutoff marks, shortlisted roll numbers, PET instructions, document requirements and the rule that PET admit card will not be sent by post.

What After Result

Candidates qualified in the written examination must appear in the next stage, which includes certificate verification and Physical Efficiency Test. The PET will include events such as running, high jump and shot put as per the rules mentioned in the recruitment notification.

  • Appearing in PET on the fixed date is compulsory for shortlisted candidates.
  • No relaxation will be given in the minimum physical standards fixed for eligibility.
  • No second chance will be given if a candidate does not appear on the scheduled PET date.
  • PET admit card will be uploaded on the BPSSC website under the Home Guard tab after the date is fixed.
  • No PET admit card will be sent by post.
  • The final merit list will be prepared on the basis of total marks obtained in all three PET events.

Documents Required for Next Stage

Shortlisted candidates should read the official PET notice carefully and keep original documents along with one set of self-attested photocopies ready. The official notice mentions the following documents for certificate verification:

  • PET admit card downloaded from the BPSSC website.
  • Copy of online application form / application acknowledgement.
  • Valid photo identity proof such as Aadhaar card, voter ID, driving licence or other approved ID.
  • Intermediate or equivalent educational qualification certificate.
  • Date of birth proof, generally matriculation certificate original copy.
  • Caste certificate for SC, ST, Extremely Backward Class and Backward Class candidates, wherever applicable.
  • Non-creamy layer certificate for Backward Class and Extremely Backward Class candidates, wherever applicable.
  • Bihar permanent resident / domicile certificate for reserved category candidates, wherever applicable.
  • EWS certificate for Bihar State EWS candidates, if applicable, based on the prescribed format and relevant financial year.
  • Recent passport-size photographs and any other document mentioned in the official PET notice.
