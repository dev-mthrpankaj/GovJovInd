About Indian Army Agniveer CEE Result 2026

The Indian Army conducted the Computer Based Online Common Entrance Examination (CEE) across various centers in India for recruitment under the Agniveer scheme. The result has been compiled based on candidate performance in the written CEE. The merit list contains the roll numbers of candidates shortlisted for the second stage of the recruitment process.

The next phase will be organized by individual Army Recruiting Offices (AROs) under their respective Headquarters Recruiting Zones (ZROs). This includes the Physical Fitness Test (1.6 Km run, beam pull-ups, zig-zag balance, and 9-feet ditch) and Physical Measurement parameters.

How to Check Indian Army Agniveer CEE Result

  1. Go to the official website of the Indian Army at joinindianarmy.nic.in.
  2. Enter the website captcha security code carefully to load the landing homepage.
  3. Scroll down or locate the navigation menu and click on the "CEE Results" tab.
  4. A complete table list of various AROs and ZROs will be displayed on your screen.
  5. Search for your specific Recruiting Zone (ZRO) and regional Army Recruiting Office (ARO).
  6. Click on the download PDF icon adjacent to your region's result notification title.
  7. Open the downloaded PDF file and use the search function (Ctrl + F) to look up your Roll Number.
  8. If your roll number appears in the PDF list, you are provisionally qualified for the next stage.

Details Mentioned in Agniveer Merit List PDF

  • Recruiting Zone (ZRO) Headquarters Name
  • Army Recruiting Office (ARO) Name
  • Category Designation (e.g., Agniveer General Duty, Agniveer Tradesmen 10th/8th pass)
  • Roll Numbers of Provisionally Shortlisted Candidates
  • Important regulatory notices and instructions for the Rally Stage

What After Indian Army Agniveer Written Result?

Candidates clearing the Online Common Entrance Examination will receive automated notification logs or admit cards via their registered logins for Stage 2. The second stage consists of:

  • Physical Fitness Test (PFT): 1.6 Km Run within specified timings, Pull-ups (Beam execution), 9 Feet Ditch jump crossing, and Zig-Zag Balance maintenance.
  • Physical Measurement Test (PMT): Verification of height, weight, and chest proportions as per regional army criteria.
  • Document Verification (DV): Matching of raw educational and demographic certificates.
  • Medical Examination: Comprehensive fitness verification conducted by military medical teams.

Documents Required for the Rally Stage

Shortlisted candidates must compile the following documents to present during the ARO rally physical phase:

  • Rally Physical Admit Card printout.
  • Twenty copies of unattested passport-size photographs with light backgrounds.
  • Educational Qualification Certificates (10th/12th/8th Mark Sheets & Passing Certificates).
  • Domicile Certificate issued by authorized state government revenue officers.
  • Caste Certificate and Religion Certificate.
  • School Character Certificate & Character Certificate from Village Sarpanch/Municipal Corporation.
  • Unmarried Candidate Certificate with photograph (applicable for candidates below 21 years).
  • Relationship Certificate (if applying under ESM/SOS/SOEX/SOW quotas).
  • NCC A/B/C Certificates or Sports Achievement Certificates (if applicable for bonus marks).
  • Aadhaar Card & PAN Card for identity verification.

Frequently Asked Questions (FAQs)

Is Indian Army Agniveer Result 2026 declared?

Yes. The Indian Army has begun releasing the regional CEE merit list PDFs for various ZROs/AROs on its portal.

Where can I look up the official merit list?

You can access the lists directly under the "CEE Results" tab on the official portal at joinindianarmy.nic.in.

What details do I need to enter to see my selection status?

No login is strictly needed to view the result lists. The results are published as PDFs containing selected roll numbers grouped by ARO and trade category.

What parameters are checked in the Agniveer Physical Fitness Test (PFT)?

It includes a 1.6 Km run, Pull-ups (Beam), 9 Feet Ditch jump crossing, and a Zig-Zag Balance test.

When will the next physical rally stage start?

The exact date and venue schedule will be declared individually by your regional ARO. Keep monitoring your registered email and the official army portal.

Are bonus marks awarded for NCC certificates?

Yes, bonus marks are calculated during the final selection process for candidates possessing NCC A, B, or C certifications who present verified original documents during the rally stage.
