About This Result

The Staff Selection Commission successfully conducted the Multi-Tasking (Non-Technical) Staff, and Havaldar (CBIC & CBN) Computer-Based Examination from 04 February 2026 to 15 February 2026 across various centers nationwide. Normalized marks have been used to evaluate candidates across multiple examination shifts.

The result declared on 03 August 2026 contains the list of candidates shortlisted for document verification and the subsequent physical test rounds required for the Havaldar post in CBIC & CBN.

Candidates listed in the official result merit PDF are eligible to proceed to the next stage of the recruitment process. Final scorecards and response keys will be uploaded on the official SSC portal shortly.

How to Check SSC MTS Result 2026

  1. Visit the official Staff Selection Commission website at ssc.gov.in.
  2. Click on the Results tab located at the top right of the homepage.
  3. Select the Others / PWD or relevant tab for MTS result notices.
  4. Click on the link titled "Multi-Tasking Staff and Havaldar Result 2026 List of Candidates Qualified".
  5. Download the PDF file and press Ctrl + F (or Search in mobile) to find your Roll Number or Name.
  6. If your roll number appears in the list, you have successfully qualified for the next stage. Save the PDF for future reference.

  • Category / Sub-Category Codes
  • Post Code Allocated / Shortlisted Category
  • Qualifying Status for Next Stage
  • Cut-off Marks Details (specified in Write-Up Notice)

Frequently Asked Questions

Is SSC MTS Result 2026 declared?

Yes, Staff Selection Commission declared the SSC MTS & Havaldar result on 03 August 2026.

How can I check my SSC MTS Result 2026?

You can check your result by opening the official candidate shortlist merit PDF and searching for your Roll Number or Name.

What is the next stage after qualifying the written exam?

Shortlisted candidates for Havaldar will undergo PET/PST followed by Document Verification. MTS post qualifiers will proceed to final verification/allotment.

What are the PET walking standards for Havaldar?

Male candidates must complete 1600 meters walking in 15 minutes, while female candidates must complete 1 km in 20 minutes.

When will SSC MTS Marks / Scorecards be uploaded?

SSC usually releases candidate scorecards and final answer keys within a few days following the result announcement on ssc.gov.in.

Where can I get the direct PDF link to check my result?

Direct PDF download links are available in the Important Links table on this page as well as on the SSC official portal.
