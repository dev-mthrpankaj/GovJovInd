About This Result

The Madhya Pradesh Employees Selection Board (MPESB) conducted the written examination for the Combined Recruitment Test of Forest Guard (Van Rakshak), Field Guard (Kshetra Rakshak), Jail Prahari, and Assistant Jail Superintendent starting from 4 June 2026. The First Phase Result is now declared.

The declared result is an eligibility shortlist for the second phase of the recruitment process. Out of all the applicants, 14,950 candidates have been successfully shortlisted for the next stage, which includes the Physical Proficiency Test (PET/PST) and Document Verification (DV).

Candidates need to log in to the official MPESB portal using their Application Number, Date of Birth, and other required credentials to access their individual qualifying status and scorecard.

How to Check MPESB Result

  1. Visit the official Madhya Pradesh Employees Selection Board website at esb.mp.gov.in.
  2. On the homepage, select your preferred language and go to the "Results" section.
  3. Click on the link for "First Phase Result - Van Rakshak & Kshetra Rakshak, Jail Prahari and Asst. Jail Superintendent Combined Recruitment Test- 2026".
  4. A login page will open. Enter your 13-digit Application Number and Date of Birth.
  5. Input the first two letters of your mother's name and the last four digits of your Aadhaar number as requested.
  6. Solve the captcha/calculation and click on the "Search" button.
  7. Your qualifying status and scorecard will appear. Download and print it for future stages.

What After First Phase Result?

  • Candidates who qualified in the First Phase are eligible for the Second Phase.
  • The Second Phase comprises the Physical Proficiency Test (PET/PST) and Document Verification (DV).
  • The schedule, venue, and admit cards for the physical tests will be announced on the MPESB website shortly.
  • Candidates must start preparing for the physical standards and endurance parameters specified in the official rulebook.

Documents Required for Next Stage (DV)

Shortlisted candidates should keep the following essential documents ready in original and photocopy formats for the Document Verification round:

  • Printout of the First Phase Result / Scorecard
  • Admit Card of the Written Examination and Physical Test
  • Valid Photo ID Proof (Aadhaar Card, Voter ID, Passport, etc.)
  • Class 10th (Matriculation) Marksheet and Certificate (for Date of Birth proof)
  • Class 12th Marksheet and Certificate
  • Caste / Category Certificate (if applicable, in the prescribed state format)
  • Madhya Pradesh Domicile Certificate (Mool Niwasi Praman Patra)
  • Employment Registration Certificate (Rojgar Panjiyan)
  • Passport-size photographs (matching the application form)
