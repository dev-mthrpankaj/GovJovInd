About This Result

The Uttar Pradesh Subordinate Service Selection Commission (UPSSSC) conducted the written examination for 5272 Health Worker (Female) posts on 11 January 2026. The commission has now released the written examination result for the candidates who appeared in the test.

Candidates can check their eligibility status for the next round by visiting the UPSSSC result portal. You will need your registration number and other required login credentials to view your qualifying status. The official category-wise cut-off marks document has also been uploaded separately.

Shortlisted candidates will now proceed to the Document Verification (DV) stage. It is crucial to regularly check the official UPSSSC website for further notices regarding the DV schedule and detailed instructions.

How to Check Result

  1. Visit the official website of UPSSSC at upsssc.gov.in.
  2. Click on the "Results" segment available on the homepage.
  3. Select the link for "Click Here to View Result for Advt No 11-Exam/2024 Health Worker Female".
  4. A login portal will appear. Enter your Registration Number, Date of Birth, Gender, and the given Verification Code.
  5. Click on the "See Result" button.
  6. Your qualifying status and scorecard (if applicable) will be displayed. Save or print it for future reference.

What After Result

Candidates who have been shortlisted in the written examination will undergo the following stage:

  • Document Verification (DV): Candidates must present all original documents supporting their eligibility (educational qualifications, age, category, UP Nursing Council registration) before the commission. Detailed schedules for DV will be published separately on the UPSSSC website.
  • Final Selection: Based on the written exam marks and successful document verification, the final merit list for appointment will be prepared.

Documents Required for Next Stage

  • UPSSSC PET 2023 Score Card printout
  • Admit card and Application form printout of Health Worker Female 2024
  • Result/Qualifying status printout
  • Valid Photo ID Proof (Aadhaar, PAN, Voter ID)
  • Matriculation (10th) Certificate for age proof
  • Intermediate (12th) Certificate & Marksheet
  • ANM Certificate and Marksheets
  • Valid Registration Certificate in UP Nursing Council
  • Caste/Category Certificate (if applicable)
  • Domicile Certificate (for UP residents claiming reservation)
  • Recent passport-size photographs
