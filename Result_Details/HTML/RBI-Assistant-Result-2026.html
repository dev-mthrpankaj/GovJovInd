About This Result

The Reserve Bank of India (RBI) conducted the Assistant Examination 2026 to recruit eligible candidates across its various offices for 650 vacancies. Following the successful completion of the examination phases, RBI has now released the official result and scorecard/marks for the candidates.

Candidates can view their section-wise and overall marks by logging into the candidate portal using their Registration Number/Roll Number and Password/Date of Birth. A merit list PDF containing the roll numbers of provisionally shortlisted candidates is also available for download.

Shortlisted candidates should carefully review the official notification to understand the requirements for the Language Proficiency Test (LPT) and Document Verification (DV) stages, which are mandatory for final selection.

How to Check RBI Assistant Marks & Result

  1. Visit the official RBI careers website at opportunities.rbi.org.in.
  2. Go to the "Current Vacancies" tab and click on the "Results" section.
  3. Select the link for "Marks sheet and cut-off marks for the post of Assistant - 2026".
  4. To check marks, open the IBPS login portal link provided on the page.
  5. Enter your Registration Number/Roll Number and Password/Date of Birth along with the captcha code.
  6. Your scorecard will be displayed. Download and print it for future reference.
  7. To check the merit list, download the Result PDF and search your Roll Number carefully.

Result Details Mentioned

  • Candidate's Name and Category
  • Roll Number and Registration Number
  • Section-wise marks obtained
  • Total aggregate marks scored
  • Category-wise cut-off marks
  • Qualifying status for the next round (Language Proficiency Test)

Cut Off & Merit List

The Reserve Bank of India determines the cut-off marks based on the difficulty level of the exam, number of vacancies, and category of candidates. Category-wise and region-wise cut-off details are typically mentioned alongside the scorecard. Candidates who meet or exceed the official cut-off are included in the merit list.

Please log in to the official scorecard portal to view the precise cut-off marks applicable to your category and applied zone.

What After Result

Candidates who have successfully qualified in the mains examination will have to undergo a Language Proficiency Test (LPT). The LPT will be conducted in the Official / Local Language of the State concerned. Candidates not proficient in the Official / Local Language will be disqualified.

Following the LPT, selected candidates will be called for Document Verification and pre-recruitment medical formalities. Please monitor the official RBI website for schedules related to LPT and DV.

Documents Required for Next Stage

  • System-generated printout of the online application form
  • Printout of the RBI Assistant Scorecard / Result
  • Valid original Photo ID proof (Aadhaar, PAN, Voter ID, etc.) and a photocopy
  • Proof of Date of Birth (10th Standard Marksheet/Certificate)
  • Graduation Marksheets and Degree Certificate
  • Category Certificate (SC/ST/OBC/EWS) in the prescribed format, if applicable
  • Discharge certificate for Ex-Servicemen
  • No Objection Certificate (NOC) if employed in Govt/PSU
