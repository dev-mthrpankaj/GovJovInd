About This Result

The Union Public Service Commission (UPSC) conducted the Combined Geo-Scientist (Main) Examination 2026 on 20th and 21st June 2026 across designated centers nationwide. Based on performance in the descriptive written paper, the Commission has released the list of provisionally qualified roll numbers for the interview stage.

Candidates whose roll numbers appear in the shortlist PDF are eligible for the Personality Test, which carries a crucial weightage in the final merit recommendation. Shortlisted applicants will be required to fill out the Detailed Application Form (DAF) on the UPSC portal before the schedule is published.

The individual marksheets and category-wise final cut-off marks for all candidates will be published on the Commission's official portal within 15 days after the final result declaration upon conclusion of the Personality Test interviews.

How to Check UPSC Geo Scientist Mains Result

  1. Visit the official portal of Union Public Service Commission at upsc.gov.in.
  2. Look under the "What's New" or "Written Results" section on the homepage.
  3. Click on "Written Result: Combined Geo-Scientist (Main) Examination, 2026".
  4. Open or download the official Result Roll List PDF.
  5. Use keyboard search (Ctrl + F or search icon) to search for your Roll Number.
  6. Save or print the Result PDF copy for future reference during document verification.

What After Mains Result?

Candidates who have qualified the Mains written examination will receive e-Summon letters for the Personality Test / Interview stage. During the interview stage, candidates will undergo thorough verification of original documents including educational degrees, age proof, and category certificates.

The final selection list will be prepared by compiling the combined marks obtained in the Mains Examination and the Personality Test.

Documents Required for Interview / DV

  • Printed copy of E-Summon Letter for Personality Test
  • Matriculation / Secondary Board certificate as proof of date of birth
  • Post-Graduate / Master's degree marksheets and provisional/degree certificates
  • Caste / Category certificate (EWS/OBC/SC/ST/PwBD) in prescribed format
  • Government-issued photo identification card (Aadhaar, PAN Card, Voter ID, Driving License)
  • Copy of submitted Detailed Application Form (DAF)
