About This Result

The Bihar Public Service Commission (BPSC) conducted the written Mains examination for District Statistical Officer / Assistant Director in the Planning & Development Department, Govt. of Bihar under Advertisement No. 38/2025 on 29 and 30 January 2026. The official written result has been released on 10 August 2026 in downloadable PDF format containing the roll numbers of successful candidates.

Candidates whose roll numbers appear in the merit list PDF are provisionally qualified for the next stage of selection, which comprises an Interview / Personality Test followed by Document Verification. Final merit lists will be compiled based on performance in the written mains exam and interview score as per BPSC guidelines.

It is strongly advised that all qualified candidates download the official result notification, verify their credentials, and keep all original educational degrees, domicile, and category documents ready for the forthcoming interview process.

How to Check BPSC DSO Mains Result 2026

  1. Visit the official portal of Bihar Public Service Commission at bpsc.bihar.gov.in.
  2. Look for the notification link titled "Results: District Statistical Officer Written (Mains) Competitive Examination (Advt. No. 38/2025)" on the homepage.
  3. Click on the result link to open the official PDF document.
  4. Use the search function (Ctrl + F on desktop or search in mobile PDF viewer) and enter your Roll Number.
  5. If your roll number is highlighted in the PDF list, you have successfully qualified for the Interview round.
  6. Download and save the result PDF, and take a printout for future reference and document verification.

Details Mentioned on Result PDF

  • Name of Recruitment Examination (District Statistical Officer / Assistant Director).
  • Advertisement Number (Advt. No. 38/2025).
  • Roll Numbers of Provisionally Shortlisted Candidates.
  • Category-wise qualification count summary.
  • Important instructions for the upcoming Interview & Document Verification stage.
  • Official Commission signature and date of issue (10 August 2026).

Cut-Off & Selection Criteria

BPSC releases category-wise cut-off marks along with the result notice or detailed scorecard update. Candidates must secure minimum qualifying marks in compulsory and optional subjects as per Commission standards. Official cut-off marks and individual marks updates will be accessible on the candidate portal (bpsconline.bihar.gov.in) in due course.

What After Result? (Next Stage Details)

Candidates who qualify in the Mains written examination will appear for the personal Interview / Personality Test. BPSC will issue individual Interview Call Letters on its portal. Final selection will be based on the cumulative marks obtained in the Written Mains Examination and Interview, subject to verification of all eligibility certificates.

Documents Required for Interview / DV

  • Printout of BPSC DSO Online Application Form and Interview Call Letter.
  • Matriculation / 10th Certificate (for Date of Birth verification).
  • Post Graduation Degree Certificate in Statistics, Mathematics, or Economics from a recognized university.
  • Marksheets for all academic semesters/years.
  • Cast / Category Certificate (for EWS, EBC, BC, SC, ST candidates issued by competent Bihar Govt authority).
  • Permanent Domicile / Residence Certificate of Bihar (if claiming reservation).
  • Non-Creamy Layer (NCL) Certificate for BC/EBC candidates.
  • Valid Photo Identity Proof (Aadhaar Card, Voter ID, PAN Card, or Passport).
  • Recent passport-size photographs.
