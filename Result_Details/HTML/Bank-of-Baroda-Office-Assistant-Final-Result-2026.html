About This Result

Bank of Baroda (BOB) conducted the Main Examination on 22 March 2026 for the recruitment of 500 Office Assistant (Peon) positions in the Subordinate Cadre on a regular basis. Following the completion of the evaluation process, the bank has officially published the Final Result and Merit List PDF on 28 July 2026.

The result PDF contains the registration numbers of candidates provisionally selected under both the Selected Category List and the Wait List, arranged state-wise and category-wise. Candidates can download the official document and search for their registration number to check their selection status.

Shortlisted candidates are required to attend the next stages, which include the Language Proficiency Test (LPT) in the local language of the respective state/UT, along with formal Document Verification (DV) and Pre-employment Medical Examination.

How to Check Bank of Baroda Peon Result 2026

  1. Visit the official website of Bank of Baroda at bankofbaroda.bank.in.
  2. Navigate to the Careers section located in the footer or menu bar.
  3. Click on Current Opportunities and select Recruitment of Office Assistant in Sub-Staff Cadre on Regular Basis.
  4. Click on the link titled List of Provisionally Selected Candidates / Final Result 2026.
  5. The result PDF will open on your screen.
  6. Press Ctrl + F (or search in mobile PDF viewer) and enter your Registration Number or Roll Number.
  7. Check if your number appears under the Selected List or Wait List for your state/category.
  8. Save and print a copy of the result PDF for future recruitment formalities.

Bank of Baroda releases category-wise and state-wise cut-off marks along with the candidate scorecard portal on its official website following the publication of the final selection list. Candidates are advised to check the official career page for individual marks logins.

Official cut-off marks and scorecard links are updated directly from the official portal once activated by the bank.

What After Result: Next Selection Stages

  • Language Proficiency Test (LPT): Candidates provisionally selected must possess proficiency in the specified local language of the applied State/UT. The test is qualifying in nature.
  • Document Verification (DV): Candidates must produce all original marksheets, age proof, identity documents, caste/category certificates, and domicile certificates for verification.
  • Pre-Employment Medical Examination: Final appointment is subject to being declared medically fit by authorized bank doctors.
  • Issuance of Offer Letter: Successful candidates will receive formal appointment letters with joining dates and allotted branches/offices.

Documents Required for Verification Stage

  • Printout of Online Application Form and Final Result / Selection Notice
  • High School (10th) Mark Sheet and Passing Certificate (Proof of Age and Qualification)
  • Valid Identity Proof (Aadhaar Card, PAN Card, Passport, Voter ID)
  • Caste / Category Certificate (SC/ST/OBC-NCL/EWS in prescribed central government format)
  • Domicile / Permanent Resident Certificate of applied State/UT
