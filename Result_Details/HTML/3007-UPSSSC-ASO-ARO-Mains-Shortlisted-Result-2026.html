About This Result

UPSSSC has released the shortlisted result for the Assistant Statistical Officer / Assistant Research Officer (Statistics) Main Examination under Advertisement No. 05-Exam/2026. This result is not the final selection list. It is a shortlist of candidates who have been allowed up to the availability limit for the Main Examination on the basis of PET 2025 normalized score.

The official notice states that candidates with zero, less than zero or negative marks in actual score or normalized score of PET 2025 were kept separate while preparing this shortlist. Candidates who applied online for the recruitment can check their individual result through the result link available on the UPSSSC website.

The next important step is the Main Examination. UPSSSC will separately notify the main exam date, admit card, exam instructions and any further document verification or selection-related process. Candidates should depend on the official notice and official website for every next-stage update.

What After Result?

Candidates shortlisted in this result will move to the Main Examination stage for Assistant Statistical Officer / Assistant Research Officer (Statistics). The main exam date, exam city/admit card, reporting instructions, syllabus-related instructions and other exam-day rules will be notified by UPSSSC separately.

  • Keep your application details and PET 2025 details ready.
  • Check the UPSSSC website regularly for main exam schedule and admit card.
  • Do not rely on social media screenshots for final information.
  • Follow only the official notice for any further document verification, medical, joining or selection-related instructions.

Documents Required for Next Stage

The uploaded result notice does not provide a complete document verification list. Candidates should keep the following commonly required documents ready and verify the final list from UPSSSC when the next-stage notice is released.

  • Main exam admit card or call letter, when released.
