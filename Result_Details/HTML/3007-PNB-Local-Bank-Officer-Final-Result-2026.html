About This Result

The Punjab National Bank result PDF is for Recruitment of Local Bank Officers in Scale I. It contains the list of candidates selected for provisional offer of appointment, along with roll number, registration number, candidate name and state applied.

The result is important for candidates who appeared in the Local Bank Officer selection process and were waiting for final selection status. Candidates whose details appear in the list should follow PNB official instructions for joining and appointment formalities.

The PDF clearly mentions that the joining schedule will be informed separately. Therefore, selected candidates should keep checking the official PNB recruitment section and their registered email/mobile communication for further instructions.

What After Result

Candidates listed in the PNB final result have been selected for provisional offer of appointment. The next step is to wait for joining schedule and official instructions issued by Punjab National Bank.

  • Monitor PNB official recruitment page for joining schedule.
  • Keep registered email and mobile number active for official communication.
  • Prepare original documents and photocopies for verification.
  • Follow reporting date, venue and instructions only from PNB official communication.

Documents Required for Next Stage

  • PNB application form/registration printout, if required.
  • Admit card/call letter of selection stages, if asked.
  • Valid photo ID proof and recent passport-size photographs.
  • Educational qualification certificates and mark sheets.
  • Category/EWS/PwBD certificate, if applicable.
  • Experience or service-related certificates, if applicable.
  • Any joining, medical, undertaking or verification document asked by PNB.
