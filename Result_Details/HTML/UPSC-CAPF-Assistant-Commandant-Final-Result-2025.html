About This Result

The Union Public Service Commission (UPSC) conducted the written part of the Central Armed Police Forces (Assistant Commandants) Examination 2025 on 03 August 2025 across various examination centres in India. The written result was declared on 10 October 2025, after which qualified candidates were called for Physical Efficiency Test (PET) / Physical Standard Test (PST), Medical Standards Test, and Personality Test (Interview).

Based on the combined marks obtained in the written examination and the personality test/interview, UPSC has now published the final merit list of recommended candidates for appointment as Assistant Commandants (Group A) in various forces including BSF, CRPF, CISF, ITBP, and SSB.

Candidates whose roll numbers appear in the final recommendation PDF are advised to keep their original documents ready for final document verification and joining formalities as directed by the Ministry of Home Affairs and respective force headquarters.

How to Check UPSC CAPF Final Result

  1. Visit the official portal of Union Public Service Commission at upsc.gov.in.
  2. Look for the "What's New" or "Final Results" section on the homepage.
  3. Click on the link titled "Final Result: Central Armed Police Forces (ACs) Examination, 2025".
  4. The final recommendation merit list PDF will open on the screen.
  5. Press Ctrl + F and search for your Roll Number or Name in the merit list.
  6. Download and save the PDF file or print out a copy for future reference.

What After Final Result

Candidates selected in the final merit list will undergo the following subsequent steps:

  • Document Verification: Verification of original educational certificates, caste, domicile, and identity proofs.
  • Offer of Appointment: Formal appointment letters will be issued by the respective Force Headquarter / Ministry of Home Affairs.
  • Pre-Joining Training: Joining guidelines, reporting dates, and basic military training schedule will be communicated individually.

Documents Required for Joining

  • Printed copy of Application Form / Detailed Application Form (DAF)
  • Original Matriculation / 10th Certificate for age verification
  • Graduation Degree / Final Provisional Pass Certificate
  • Category / Caste Certificate (SC/ST/OBC/EWS) in prescribed Central format
  • Valid Government Photo ID Proof (Aadhaar Card, PAN Card, Passport, Voter ID)
  • Recent Passport Size Colour Photographs
  • Discharge Certificate (for Ex-Servicemen candidates)
  • Character & Antecedent verification certificates
