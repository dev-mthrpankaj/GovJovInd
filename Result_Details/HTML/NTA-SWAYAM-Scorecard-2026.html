The National Testing Agency regularly organises the online semester endpoint evaluations for the SWAYAM courses nationwide. How to Check NTA SWAYAM Scorecard

  1. Navigate to the official NTA result authentication server at cnr.nic.in Portal.
  2. Enter your unique Application Reference Number alongside specified registration credentials securely.
  3. Click the verify/submit toggle option to populate your profile layout framework.
  4. Verify your marks, internal assessment indices, and gross score aggregates carefully.
  5. Select the print icon toggle or save the resulting layout structure locally in PDF format for future reference.

Cut Off / Merit List

The official cut-off criteria for individual SWAYAM courses are set based on predefined passing indices for internal assignments and the comprehensive CBT written test. Official course-specific cutoffs will be updated after release on the core institutional dashboard.
