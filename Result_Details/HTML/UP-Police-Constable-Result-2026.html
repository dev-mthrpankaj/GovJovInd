Latest UP Police Constable Result Update

The written examination for Constable Civil Police and equivalent posts was held on 8, 9 and 10 June 2026. On 31 July, UPPRPB published the final answer key and the result/list identifying candidates eligible for document scrutiny and the Physical Standard Test.

On 10 August 2026, the Board published further notices concerning the DV/PST process and download of admit cards and application forms. Therefore, the older status saying that DV/PST was still to be notified is no longer current.

The exact date, reporting time and venue applicable to a shortlisted candidate should be taken from the official UPPRPB admit card and notice.

What Shortlisted Candidates Should Do Now

  1. Open the official UPPRPB notice board and locate the Constable Civil Police & equivalent posts recruitment notices dated 10 August 2026.
  2. Use the official link to download the DV/PST admit card and application form.
  3. Read the process notice carefully before reporting.
  4. Carry the documents required for scrutiny in the prescribed form.
  5. Follow the date, reporting time and venue printed on your own admit card.

Required Documents for DV/PST Stage

  • DV/PST Admit Card and application form
  • 10th and 12th marksheets/certificates as applicable
  • Category / reservation certificates where applicable
  • Domicile or other eligibility documents where applicable
  • Valid government photo ID
  • Recent photographs as required by the official instructions
  • NCC / Home Guard / Ex-Servicemen or other claim certificates, if applicable

Use the official 10 August process notice as the final checklist because document requirements can vary by claim/category.
