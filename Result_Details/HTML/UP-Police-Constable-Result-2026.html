Latest UP Police Constable Result Update

The written examination was held on 8, 9 and 10 June 2026. On 31 July, UPPRPB published the final answer key and the result/list identifying candidates eligible for document scrutiny and the Physical Standard Test.

On 10 August 2026, the Board published further notices concerning the DV/PST process and the download of admit cards and application forms. Therefore, the earlier status “PET/PST/DV date to be notified” is no longer current for DV/PST.

The exact date, reporting time and venue applicable to a shortlisted candidate should be taken from the official UPPRPB admit card/notice.
