About This Result

The Uttar Pradesh Police Recruitment & Promotion Board (UPPRPB) conducted the written examination for 32,679 posts of Constable Civil Police, PAC, Special Force Battalion, Female Battalion, Horseman, and Jail Warden from 8th June 2026 to 10th June 2026 across various centers in Uttar Pradesh.

The written exam result has now been officially declared. Candidates who appeared for the OMR-based offline exam can log into the candidate portal using their roll number and birth details to download their scorecards and view qualifying status for the upcoming Physical Test (PET/PST) and Document Verification (DV).

Shortlisted candidates will receive instructions regarding PET/PST test centers, call letter availability, physical standards, and mandatory documents required during verification.

How to Check UP Police Constable Result 2026

  1. Visit the official website of UP Police Recruitment & Promotion Board at uppbpb.gov.in.
  2. Click on the result announcement banner for UP Police Constable Examination 2026.
  3. Enter your Registration Number, Date of Birth, and security captcha in the login portal.
  4. Click Submit to open your detailed written exam scorecard and qualification status.
  5. Download and print your result copy for future reference and physical test admit card download.

What After Result: Next Stage Guide

  • Candidates qualifying the written examination will be issued admit cards for DV/PST.
  • During Document Verification, educational marksheets, caste certificates, domicile, and identity proofs will be checked.
  • Physical Standard Test (PST) will measure candidate height and chest requirements.
  • Qualified candidates in PST will undergo Physical Efficiency Test (PET) running stage.
  • Final merit list will be prepared based on written test score, reservation norms, and document clearance.

Required Documents for DV/PST Stage

  • UP Police Written Exam Result / Scorecard Printout
  • DV/PST Admit Card / Call Letter
  • 10th & 12th Board Marksheet & Certificate
  • Caste / Category Certificate (OBC / SC / ST / EWS) in prescribed state format
  • UP State Domicile Certificate (if claiming reservation/age relaxation)
  • Valid Government Photo ID (Aadhaar Card / Voter ID / Driving License)
  • Passport size recent colored photographs (6 copies)
  • NCC / Home Guard / Ex-Servicemen certificates (if applicable)
