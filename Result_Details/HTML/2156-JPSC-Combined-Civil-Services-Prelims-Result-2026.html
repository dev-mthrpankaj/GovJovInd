About This Result

The Jharkhand Public Service Commission (JPSC) organized the Combined Civil Services Preliminary Competitive Examination-2025 on 19 April 2026 across various testing locations. Following the directive issued by the Honorable High Court of Jharkhand in W.P.(S) No. 1276/2026 (Vikash Chandra & Ors. Vs The State of Jharkhand & Others) along with identical cases on 30.06.2026 (uploaded on 02.07.2026), the official result has been validated and published on the commission's web portal.

This result release paves the way for the qualified applicants to step forward to the next level of the recruitment process, which is the Combined Civil Services Main (Written) Examination. The administrative panel retains full authorities to introduce typographical rectifications or resolve discrepancy points if any error is discovered at a later date.

Candidates listed in the merit roll can begin registering online for the written mains test starting 3 July 2026. Submitting hard copies of applications alongside self-certified educational, category, and residential proofs remains compulsory to confirm selection status.

How to Check JPSC Prelims Result

  1. Access the official website of the commission by typing www.jpsc.gov.in into your browser.
  2. Locate the latest press release link concerning the "Result of Combined Civil Services (P.T.) Examination-2025, Advt. No.-01/2026".
  3. Click open the published document to preview the complete list of qualified candidates.
  4. Utilize the find tool (Ctrl + F) to look up your unique roll number in the pages.
  5. If your roll number appears in the selection list, log on to jpscotr.com to kickstart your step-by-step registration for the Main written evaluation stage.
  6. Take a printout copy or download your result document for safe preservation.

Official Cut Off Marks

Official cutoff will be updated after release. The Jharkhand Public Service Commission has not highlighted category-wise marks cutoff details inside this primary screening press notice. Candidates are recommended to verify updates regularly on the official communication window.

What After JPSC Prelims Result

Shortlisted candidates need to navigate to the online application link setup at jpscotr.com. Log in via your registered credentials (Email ID / Phone Number / Candidate ID & Password) to initiate the registration steps. In Step-1, candidates must verify the details filled during the PT phase using the 'Start/View Application' option, and then submit the mandatory selections under Step-2.

Crucially, candidates must opt for their choice of Language and Literature under Paper-II from the provided language group according to advertisement criteria. Similarly, a choice between Hindi and English must be made for drafting descriptive text responses (excluding language subjects) and for addressing the interview board later. Priority rankings across 9 distinct civil service cadres must also be marked down conclusively.

Documents Required for Next Stage

Candidates are required to upload neat, readable scanned PDF copies of their documents during online registration, and subsequently dispatch a self-attested hard copy via Speed Post / Registered Post or hand-deliver it to the JPSC office by 14.07.2026 (05:00 PM). The top cover must highlight **Advt No. 01/2026, Registration Number, and Exam Name** clearly. The primary items include:

  • Main Examination Online Application Downloaded Printout Sheet
  • Academic Qualification Degrees & Marksheets matching criteria claims
  • Local Residential Certificate for claiming native reservation relaxations
  • Caste / Category Certificate (EWS, EBC-I, BC-II, SC, ST) as per prescribed parameters
  • Disability Medical Certificate countersigned by Chief Medical Officer (Min 40% condition)
  • No Objection Certificate (NOC) issued by Controlling Officer/HOD for continuous government employees
  • Ex-Servicemen discharge documents for age concession benefits

    Frequently Asked Questions

    Is the JPSC Combined Civil Services Prelims Result 2026 declared?

    Yes, the Jharkhand Public Service Commission declared the preliminary examination result on 2 July 2026.

    What is the next stage after qualifying the JPSC Prelims exam?

    All candidates who qualified in the preliminary exam are eligible to apply and appear for the Main (Written) competitive examination.

    When will the JPSC Civil Services Main Exam 2026 be conducted?

    The main written examination is tentatively scheduled to be held on 18 July, 19 July, and 20 July 2026.

    What are the online application dates for the JPSC Main Exam?

    Qualified candidates can fill out the online application form for the Main exam from 3 July 2026 to 9 July 2026 up to 5:00 PM.

    Is it mandatory to send a hard copy of the Main application form?

    Yes, candidates must send the downloaded hard copy of their application along with self-attested documents to the JPSC office by 14 July 2026 before 5:00 PM.

    Where can I check my JPSC Civil Services Prelims Result?

    Candidates can check their roll numbers on the official website of JPSC at www.jpsc.gov.in.

    What is the official helpline number for JPSC application queries?

    Candidates can call 8929883832 or email helpdesk@jpscotr.com on working days between 10:00 AM and 5:00 PM.
