About This Result

The Rajasthan Public Service Commission (RPSC) conducted the written competitive examination for the post of School Lecturer (1st Grade Teacher) and Coach under Advertisement No. 06/Exam/School Lect. & Coach/RPSC/EP-1/2025-26 from 31 May 2026 to 11 June 2026. The examination was held across various examination centers in Rajasthan state.

The commission has now published the subject-wise results listing candidates shortlisted for the next phase of selection, which is Document Verification (DV) and Counseling. Candidates can log in to the official portal or check the subject-wise PDF merit lists to verify their roll numbers and provisional selection status.

Detailed cut-off marks for various categories along with the Document Verification schedule will be updated on the official RPSC portal. Candidates are advised to download and retain their result PDF and application form for the counseling process.

How to Check RPSC School Lecturer Result

  1. Visit the official portal of Rajasthan Public Service Commission at rpsc.rajasthan.gov.in.
  2. Navigate to the News & Events or Results section on the homepage.
  3. Look for the link titled "Result Preamble and Cut-off Marks for School Lecturer (School Edu.) - 2025" for your specific subject.
  4. Click on the respective subject link to open or download the official Result PDF.
  5. Use the search function (Ctrl + F) inside the PDF to search for your Roll Number.
  6. Save and print out the result PDF for reference during Document Verification.

Details Mentioned in Result PDF

  • Roll numbers of provisionally shortlisted candidates
  • Category-wise cut-off marks (UR, SC, ST, OBC, MBC, EWS)
  • Horizontal reservation cut-offs (Ex-Servicemen, PwD, Widows, Divorced)
  • Total number of candidates qualified for Document Verification
  • Instructions for counseling and Document Verification process
  • Important instructions regarding withheld or provisional candidatures

What After Result: Next Stage Details

Candidates who qualify in the written result PDF will be invited for Document Verification (DV) and Counseling by RPSC. The official timetable and detailed form (Detailed Application Form - DAF) submission instructions will be released on the RPSC website.

After successful verification of eligibility documents, educational degrees, B.Ed/M.P.Ed certificates, category documents, and Rajasthani culture knowledge requirement, the final recommendation list and merit list will be published by RPSC for appointment in the School Education Department.

Documents Required for Document Verification

  • Printed copy of Online Application Form & Detailed Application Form (DAF)
  • 10th / Secondary Marksheet & Passing Certificate (Age Proof)
  • 12th / Senior Secondary Marksheet & Certificate
  • Graduation & Post-Graduation Marksheets and Degree Certificates in relevant subject
  • B.Ed. / M.P.Ed. / NIS Diploma Certificate recognized by NCTE / Government
  • Valid Category Certificate (SC / ST / OBC / MBC / EWS) if applicable
  • Rajasthan Domicile / Bonafide Residence Certificate
  • Character Certificates from gazetted officers / last institution
  • Valid Photo ID Proof (Aadhaar Card, Voter ID, Passport, PAN Card)
  • Passport-size recent color photographs
