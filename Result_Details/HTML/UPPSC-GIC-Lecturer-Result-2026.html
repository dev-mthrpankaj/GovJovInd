About This Result

The Uttar Pradesh Public Service Commission (UPPSC) conducted the preliminary examination for Pravakta Government Inter College (GIC Lecturer) Recruitment 2025 on 14 and 17 June 2026 across various examination centers in Uttar Pradesh. The recruitment drive aims to fill 1516 Lecturer vacancies across various subjects.

UPPSC has published subject-wise merit lists in PDF format containing roll numbers of candidates provisionally qualified for the Mains Written Examination. The result covers major subjects including English, Chemistry, Mathematics, History, Economics, Civics, Physics, Sociology, and Geography.

Candidates featured in the preliminary result PDF must fill out the separate UPPSC GIC Mains application form when notified on the official portal and submit required document copies to confirm their participation in the next phase.

How to Check UPPSC GIC Lecturer Result 2026

  1. Visit the official portal of Uttar Pradesh Public Service Commission at uppsc.up.nic.in.
  2. Go to the "Results" or "Information Bulletin / Candidate's Corner" section on the homepage.
  3. Click on the link titled "List of Candidates Provisionally Qualified for Mains in Advt. No. A-6/E-1/2025, Lect. (M/F) Govt. Inter College Exam-2025".
  4. Select your subject result link (e.g., English, Chemistry, Maths, History, Sociology, Geography, etc.).
  5. The result PDF containing subject-wise qualified roll numbers will open on your screen.
  6. Press Ctrl + F and enter your Roll Number or OTR Registration Number to check your qualifying status.
  7. Download and save the PDF file for your records and Mains examination process.

What After Prelims Result?

  • Candidates who qualify in the preliminary result are provisionally shortlisted for the UPPSC GIC Lecturer Mains Examination.
  • UPPSC will release a separate online application portal for Mains examination registration.
  • Qualified candidates must fill out the Mains form, pay the requisite fee (if applicable), and select their exam center preferences.
  • Hard copies of the Mains application form along with educational certificates, OTR printout, caste certificates, and photo ID must be submitted as per UPPSC guidelines.
  • Admission certificates for the Mains written exam will be issued on uppsc.up.nic.in prior to the exam date.
