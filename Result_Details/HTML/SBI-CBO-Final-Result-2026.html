About SBI CBO Final Result 2026

State Bank of India (SBI) conducted the recruitment process for 2050 Circle Based Officers (CBO) positions under Advertisement No. CRPD/ CBO/2025-26/18. The online examination was conducted in March 2026, followed by the declaration of written result on 30 June 2026.

Candidates shortlisted from the written phase were called for the personal interview round scheduled from 13 July 2026. The final merit list has now been compiled based on candidate performance in both the online examination and interview rounds.

How to Download SBI CBO Final Result 2026

  1. Visit the official State Bank of India career portal at sbi.bank.in.
  2. Navigate to the "Careers" section or "Latest Announcements".
  3. Locate the link for "Recruitment of Circle Based Officers (Advt. No. CRPD/ CBO/2025-26/18) - Final Result".
  4. Click the PDF link to open the merit list.
  5. Use keyboard shortcut Ctrl + F (or Search option on mobile) and type your Roll Number.
  6. If your roll number appears in the PDF, you have been selected for the appointment process. Save or print a copy for future reference.

What After Final Result?

  • Provisionally selected candidates will receive appointment offer letters from SBI via email/post in due course.
  • Candidates must undergo Document Verification (DV) and medical fitness checks as specified by the bank.
  • Keep original identity proofs, educational degrees, caste/category certificates, and prior experience certificates ready for verification.
