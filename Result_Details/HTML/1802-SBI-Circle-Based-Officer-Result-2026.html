About This Result

The State Bank of India's Central Recruitment & Promotion Department (CRPD), Corporate Centre in Mumbai, has announced the results for the Circle Based Officers selection phase. This selection path follows the guidelines and vacancy notices released under the official Advertisement Number CRPD/CBO/2025-26/18 on 29 January 2026.

Candidates who took part in the objective test processes can now check whether their designated Roll Numbers are visible in the formal list of qualified personnel. Those who find their roll numbers included have been shortlisted to attend the upcoming offline interview stage, where final round evaluation procedures will take place.

It is important for all participants to understand that these published selection lists are completely provisional. Final placement stands subject to meeting all structured age, qualification, experience parameters, and cross-verifying the details given during application registration.

How to Check Result

  1. Go to the authorized recruitment channel of the State Bank of India at sbi.co.in/careers.
  2. Navigate down to the "Latest Announcements" column grid.
  3. Locate the section labeled "Recruitment of Circle Based Officers (Advertisement No. CRPD/CBO/2025-26/18)".
  4. Click the link designated for "Roll Numbers of Candidates Qualified for Interview".
  5. Open or download the formal selection PDF document onto your machine or mobile device.
  6. Use the search shortcut command (Ctrl + F or search icon) to put in your exact 10-digit roll number.
  7. Save or print a copy of the list page if your number is listed.

Result Details Mentioned

The official qualifying list primarily highlights the structured criteria below:

  • Authorized Conducting Organization details (SBI CRPD).
  • Core Advertisement Identification Reference (CRPD/CBO/2025-26/18).
  • Comprehensive sequence list of qualified 10-digit candidate Roll Numbers.
  • Official signature authentication from the General Manager's desk.
  • Provisional guidelines warning regarding registration correctness and criteria validation.

Cut Off Marks & Merit List

Official cutoff will be updated after release. As of the current result announcement date, the bank has uploaded the roll-number-wise qualification list for shortlisting purposes. Numerical cutoff charts by category (UR, EWS, OBC, SC, ST, PwD) and standalone student scorecards are typically provided dynamically or following final result completion phases.

What After Result: Next Stage

Candidates featured in the document will undergo a structured selection verification process:

  • Personal Interview Round: Shortlisted aspirants will have to face interview evaluation panels testing core banking concepts, communication skill frameworks, and regulatory knowledge.
  • Eligibility Fulfillment: Verification protocols check parameters defined in the 29.01.2026 advertisement guidelines.
  • Data Correctness Check: Any discrepancies between details registered during online submission forms and original files can invalidate potential hiring.

Documents Required for Interview

Shortlisted banking aspirants must arrange the following records for validation during the final process:

  • Printed copy of the official SBI CBO Interview Call Letter.
  • Online registration application form printout.
  • Valid Identity verification card (Aadhaar Card, PAN Card, Passport, or equivalent).
  • Proof of Birth Date (High school certification/Birth registration files).
  • Academic mark sheets and degree credentials from graduation/post-graduation milestones.
  • Professional experience certificates validating your tenure track.
  • Category/Caste validation certificate or EWS asset structure declarations (if claiming benefits).
