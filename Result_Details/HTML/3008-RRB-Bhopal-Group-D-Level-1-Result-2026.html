About This Result

The Railway Recruitment Board - Bhopal result for CEN 08/2024 is a provisional shortlist for Physical Efficiency Test. It is based on the Computer Based Test conducted by Railway Recruitment Boards for various Level-1 posts under the 7th CPC pay matrix.

The official notice explains that roll numbers in the PDF are arranged horizontally in ascending order and not in merit order. Candidates equal to three times the community-wise vacancies, excluding PET-exempt PwBD, ExSM and CCAA candidates, have been shortlisted for PET.

After the result, upcoming activities such as PET, document verification, medical examination and panel publication will be handled by RRC Jabalpur, West Central Railway. Candidates should keep checking the concerned RRC website because PET date and e-call letter instructions will be issued separately.

How to Check Result

  1. Visit the official website of Railway Recruitment Board - Bhopal: https://rrbbhopal.gov.in/.
  2. Open the result notice for CEN 08/2024 Level-1 candidates shortlisted for PET.
  3. Download the official result PDF or merit list PDF.
  4. Use search/find option and enter your 16-digit roll number carefully.
  5. For individual percentile score and normalised marks, open the score login link provided by RRB and enter registration number with date of birth in DDMMYYYY format.
  6. If shortlisted, regularly check the RRC website for PET schedule and e-call letter updates.
  7. Save and print the result PDF, score details and future PET call letter for document verification.

What After Result?

Candidates shortlisted in the RRB Bhopal Level-1 result will move to the Physical Efficiency Test stage. The PET schedule will be published by RRC Jabalpur, West Central Railway; shortlisted candidates may also receive SMS/email updates on their registered contact details for downloading the PET e-call letter.

  • PET will be conducted by the concerned Railway Recruitment Cell.
  • After PET, eligible candidates may be called for document verification.
  • Medical examination will be conducted as per railway recruitment rules.
  • Final panel publication will depend on merit, eligibility, document verification, medical fitness and official rules.
  • Being called for PET is provisional and does not guarantee appointment in Railways.

Documents Required for Next Stage

  • PET e-call letter and result/score details.
  • Original photo identity proof such as Aadhaar, voter ID, PAN card, passport or driving licence.
  • Printout/copy of online application form, if required.
  • Class 10 certificate or proof of date of birth.
  • Educational qualification certificates and mark sheets.
  • Caste/category certificate for SC, ST, OBC-NCL or EWS candidates, if applicable.
  • PwBD, ExSM or CCAA documents where applicable and as required by RRC.
  • Recent passport-size photographs and any other document mentioned in official PET/DV instructions.

Frequently Asked Questions

Is RRB Bhopal Group D Level 1 Result 2026 released?

Yes. The RRB Bhopal Group D Level 1 Result 2026 PET shortlist result was published on 01 July 2026 as a roll number PDF for CEN 08/2024.

What is the result date of RRB Bhopal Group D Result 2026?

The result and normalised cut-off information are dated 01 July 2026.

How can I check my roll number in RRB Bhopal result PDF?

Download the official result PDF from the regional RRB website, open it, and use the search option to find your 16-digit roll number.

What is the next stage after RRB Bhopal Group D result?

The next stage is PET followed by document verification, medical examination and panel publication by RRC Jabalpur, West Central Railway as per official instructions.

What is the UR cut-off for RRB Bhopal Group D Result 2026?

The UR normalised cut-off mark for PET shortlisting is 77.73449.

Are PwBD, ExSM and CCAA candidates included in this PET list?

The notice states that PwBD, Ex-Servicemen and Course Completed Act Apprentice candidates are exempted from PET, so their roll numbers are not included in this PET list.

Can I check percentile score and normalised marks?

Yes. Candidates can view percentile score and normalised marks through the RRB login link during the official window: 01.07.2026 (18:00 hrs) to 16.07.2026 (23:55 hrs).

Does shortlisting for PET guarantee railway appointment?

No. The official notice clearly treats the shortlist as provisional and states that calling a candidate for PET does not give any right to appointment.

Where will RRB Bhopal PET e-call letter be released?

PET schedule and e-call letter updates will be issued by RRC Jabalpur, West Central Railway through its official website and SMS/email alerts.
