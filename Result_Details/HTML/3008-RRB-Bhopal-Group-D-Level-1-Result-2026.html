About This Result

The Railway Recruitment Board (RRB) Bhopal has published the result notice for the Level 1 recruitment under centralized notification CEN 08/2024. The selection list is determined by the candidate performances in the Computer Based Test (CBT) held between 27 November 2025 and 10 February 2026. Shortlisted individuals are chosen community-wise, matching roughly three times the total volume of vacancies available, excluding exempted special categories.

All candidates who participated can access their individualized normalized marks along with their percentile score values. Accessing this profile window requires standard credentials including the registration number and exact date of birth. This verification dashboard remains operational for a narrow timeframe, automatically closing on the night of 16 July 2026. Candidates are requested to take a screenshot or print out their scorecards early to avoid heavy server traffic towards the end.

The upcoming execution parameters—encompassing the physical testing parameters, detailed cross-verification of official certification papers, intensive medical compliance diagnostics, and ultimate panel selection rosters—stand delegated directly to the specialized Railway Recruitment Cells. For the selected candidate pool under RRB Bhopal, all forthcoming dispatch notices and coordination updates will filter directly through the Chairperson of RRC Jabalpur (West Central Railway).

How to Check RRB Bhopal Result & Scorecard

  1. Navigate to the authentic portal of RRB Bhopal at rrbbhopal.gov.in or alternative regional boards.
  2. Locate the notification feed tracking centralized employment notice CEN 08/2024.
  3. Click on the dedicated hyperlink reading "List of Candidates Shortlisted for Physical Efficiency Test (PET)" to open the PDF.
  4. Use the shortcut tool Ctrl + F (or search bar on mobile browsers) and insert your 16-digit Roll Number to check qualification status.
  5. To look at normalized marks, click the designated marks calculation login interface link.
  6. Input your designated Registration Number and official Date of Birth structured explicitly in DDMMYYYY format.
  7. Download the calculated scorecard printout securely for potential evaluations during subsequent stages.

Result Details Mentioned on the List

  • 16-Digit Roll Number: Arranged horizontally from left to right in ascending order (not in order of relative merit).
  • Total Selected Base Count: Explicit declaration confirming exactly 3,383 selected applicants.
  • Exemption Clarifications: Footnotes detailing exclusion parameters applied for PwBD, ExSM, and CCAA applicants.
  • Legal Jurisdiction Proviso: Note detailing compliance metrics aligned with the Hon'ble High Court of Telangana under active WP No. 7985/2026.

What After Result & PET Exemptions

Candidates provisionally identified through the roll number database must condition themselves physically for the Physical Efficiency Test (PET). The precise schedules, dates, and locations will be detailed exclusively by RRC cells via official portals. Registered candidates will additionally receive tracking messages via SMS or automated emails containing active direct links to pull down their e-call letters.

Crucial Exemptions from PET: As per paragraph 2.0 of the official release notice, individuals categorized under Persons with Benchmark Disabilities (PwBD), Ex-Servicemen (ExSM), and Course Completed Act Apprentices (CCAA) are entirely exempted from undergoing the physical test. Consequently, their roll numbers have not been included in this shortlist. Separate eligibility lists compiling these specific subsets for direct Document Verification steps will be circulated separately by corresponding regional RRC units.

Documents Required for Next Stage

Qualified candidates and exempted categories should proactively coordinate and organize their certification folders. The essential checklist consists of:

  • Printed copy of the original online application configuration framework.
  • CBT Examination Admit Card counterfoils (if saved) and newly generated PET E-Call Letters.
  • Matriculation/10th Standard educational certificate or equivalent board mark sheets (verifying legal date of birth).
  • National Apprenticeship Certificate (NAC) issued by NCVT (strictly applicable for CCAA applicants).
  • Valid Community Certificates (SC/ST/OBC-NCL templates structured on Central Government formats).
  • Income and Asset Certificates for EWS applicants or Fee Waiver proof documents.
  • Official Disability Certificates specifying benchmark criteria for PwBD candidates.
  • Discharge Certificates and ex-service validation papers for ExSM personnel.
  • Valid Photo Identification cards (Aadhaar Card, PAN Card, Voter ID, or Passport) along with recent passport-sized photographs.

Frequently Asked Questions

Is the RRB Bhopal Group D Level 1 Result 2026 declared?

Yes. RRB Bhopal announced the Level-1 CBT result on 1 July 2026 for candidates appearing in exams from late 2025 to early 2026.

How many candidates are shortlisted for the PET stage from RRB Bhopal?

Exactly 3,383 candidates have been selected provisionally to appear in the Physical Efficiency Test (PET).

How can I check my RRB Bhopal Group D percentile score and normalized marks?

Candidates can log in through the official link on the RRB website using their Registration Number and Date of Birth (DDMMYYYY format).

What are the active dates for viewing the scorecard and marks?

The login scorecard link will be made live on or before 01.07.2026 (18:00 Hrs) and will remain available until 16.07.2026 (23:55 Hrs).

Who all are exempted from appearing in the RRB Group D PET stage?

Persons with Benchmark Disabilities (PwBD), Ex-Servicemen (ExSM), and Course Completed Act Apprentices (CCAA) are entirely exempted from the PET.

Which website will host upcoming activities like PET schedule and DV for Bhopal candidates?

All future activities will be managed by RRC Jabalpur (West Central Railway) via their official website: wcr.indianrailways.gov.in.

What is the basis of selection for RRB Level-1 vacancies?

The selection process is fully computerized and strictly based on the merit of candidates in the Computer Based Test (CBT).

Is the current shortlisted merit list final for appointment?

No, the shortlisting is purely provisional for the PET stage and is subject to the final judgment of the Hon'ble High Court, Telangana in WP No. 7985/2026.
