About This Result

The Uttar Pradesh Public Service Commission (UPPSC) conducted the preliminary examination for recruitment to 1,253 Assistant Professor posts under Advertisement Number A-7/E-1/2025 (Government Degree College Examination) on 31 May 2026 across designated examination centres in Uttar Pradesh.

The declared result contains the list of candidates provisionally qualified for the Mains Examination based on their performance in the written screening test. A total of 4,755 candidates have cleared the preliminary stage in the subjects declared by the Commission including Chemistry, Economics, Geography, History, and Military Science (Defence Studies).

All shortlisted candidates must prepare for the Mains Examination and subsequent Document Verification process. Detailed guidelines regarding the Mains online application submission and exam dates will be published by UPPSC shortly.

How to Check UPPSC Assistant Professor Result

  1. Visit the official portal of Uttar Pradesh Public Service Commission at uppsc.up.nic.in.
  2. Navigate to the "Results / Latest Notices" section on the homepage.
  3. Click on the notification link for "Result of Assistant Professor Government Degree College Prelims Examination 2025".
  4. Open and download the subject-wise Result PDF document.
  5. Use the search shortcut (Ctrl+F) to enter your Roll Number.
  6. If your roll number appears in the list, you have provisionally qualified for the Mains Examination.
  7. Save or print a copy of the result PDF for future references.

Result Details Mentioned in PDF

  • Commission name and examination advertisement number (Advt. No. A-7/E-1/2025).
  • Subject name and subject code.
  • Roll numbers of provisionally selected candidates for Mains Examination.
  • Total count of qualified candidates per subject category.
  • Important directions regarding Mains exam application and eligibility verification.

What After Result

  • Mains Examination: Candidates who cleared the preliminary test must fill out the Mains online application form when enabled by UPPSC.
  • Document Verification: Original testimonials, educational certificates, caste certificates, and identity documents will be checked during DV.
  • Interview Stage: Final merit list will be prepared based on candidate performance in Mains examination and interview rounds as applicable.
  • Official Updates: Keep checking uppsc.up.nic.in for schedule notifications.

Documents Required for Next Stage

  • UPPSC Prelims Admit Card and Application Form printout.
  • High School (10th) certificate for Date of Birth proof.
  • Master's Degree mark sheets and passing certificates (minimum 55% aggregate marks).
  • UGC NET / SLET / SET qualification certificate or Ph.D. degree document as per UGC norms.
  • Caste / Category certificate (SC / ST / OBC / EWS) issued by competent UP authority, if applicable.
  • Domicile Certificate for UP state candidates claiming reservation.
  • Valid photo identification proof (Aadhaar Card, Voter ID, Passport, PAN Card).
  • Recent passport-size photographs.
