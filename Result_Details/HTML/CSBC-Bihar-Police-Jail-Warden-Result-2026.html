About This Result

The Central Selection Board of Constable (CSBC) conducted the written examination for the recruitment of Prohibition Constable, Jail Warder, and Mobile Squad Constable (Advt. No. 03/2025) on 14th and 17th June 2026. A massive number of candidates appeared for this objective-type test.

The board has now published the written exam result in the form of a PDF merit list containing the roll numbers of the provisionally qualified candidates. The recruitment drive aims to fill a total of 4,128 vacancies across various departments in Bihar Police.

Candidates who have qualified in this written stage will be called for the Physical Evaluation Test (PET) and Physical Standard Test (PST). The exact schedule and admit cards for the physical test will be notified shortly on the official website.

How to Check Result

  1. Visit the official CSBC Bihar portal at csbc.bihar.gov.in.
  2. On the homepage, navigate to the relevant tab (e.g., Prohibition Dept. or Prisons Dept.).
  3. Click on the link reading "Results of Written Examination for PET for Advt. No. 03/2025".
  4. A PDF file containing the list of qualified candidates will open.
  5. Use the search function (Ctrl + F) and carefully enter your Roll Number.
  6. If your roll number is highlighted, you have qualified for the PET/PST stage.
  7. Save or print the result PDF for future reference.

Cut Off & Merit List

The CSBC has released the list of shortlisted roll numbers for the physical stage. The official category-wise cut-off marks and individual scorecards have not been released at this stage. Official cutoff marks will be updated after release if provided by CSBC. Final merit will be determined later based on applicable recruitment rules.
