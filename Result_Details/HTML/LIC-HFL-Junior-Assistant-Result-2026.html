About This Result

LIC HFL Junior Assistant Result 2026 is an interview shortlist result. The official PDF contains roll numbers of candidates selected for interview on the basis of the online examination conducted on 06 June 2026.

Candidates should not treat the serial number in the PDF as merit rank. The notice clearly states that serial numbers are only for convenience and shall not be considered as order of merit.

Shortlisted candidates should keep checking LIC HFL official updates for interview call letter, city/venue instructions and document verification requirements. Any scorecard, marks or cut-off update should also be verified from the official website.

What After LIC HFL Junior Assistant Result?

After the online exam shortlist, candidates selected in the result PDF will be called for the interview stage. The interview is the next important stage of the LIC HFL Junior Assistant recruitment process.

  • Download and read the official result PDF carefully.
  • Keep checking the official website for interview call letter and city/venue update.
  • Prepare original documents and self-attested photocopies before the interview date.
  • Follow the reporting time, dress code and instructions given in the call letter.
  • Final selection will depend on LIC HFL rules and official recruitment process.

Documents Required for Interview

The final document list will be mentioned in the interview call letter or official instructions. Candidates should generally keep these documents ready:

  • LIC HFL Junior Assistant admit card / call letter.
  • Result PDF or scorecard print, if available.
  • Online application form printout.
  • Valid photo ID proof such as Aadhaar card, PAN card, voter ID or passport.
  • Recent passport size photographs.
  • Class 10th / 12th certificates and graduation marksheets or degree.
  • Category certificate, if applicable.
  • Domicile or city eligibility proof, if required by the notice.
  • PwBD / ESM certificate, if applicable.
  • Any other document mentioned by LIC HFL in the interview instructions.
