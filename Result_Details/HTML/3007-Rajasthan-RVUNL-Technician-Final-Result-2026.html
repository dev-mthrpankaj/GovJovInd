About This Result

Rajasthan RVUNL Technician Final Result 2026 is related to the recruitment of Technician-III (ITI), Operator-III (ITI) and Plant Attendant-III (ITI) posts. The recruitment covers 2163 vacancies across Rajasthan power companies and the result page is hosted on the Rajasthan Energy Department portal.

The final result is important because it comes after earlier recruitment stages such as preliminary or main examination and document verification. Candidates should check the group-wise or trade-wise list and confirm whether their roll number is included in the official selection list.

How to Check RVUNL Technician Final Result

  1. Visit the official Rajasthan Energy Department website at energy.rajasthan.gov.in.
  2. Open the RVUNL section and go to the recruitment page for Technician-III, Operator-III and Plant Attendant-III.

What After Result

After the final result, selected candidates should watch for appointment, joining and medical fitness instructions from the concerned Rajasthan power company or official recruitment portal. Candidates may be required to submit original documents, fulfill medical standards, complete joining formalities and report as per the allotted schedule.

Documents Required for Next Stage

  • Final result / scorecard printout, if required.
  • Admit card and application form printout.
  • Valid photo ID proof such as Aadhaar Card, PAN Card, Voter ID or Driving Licence.
  • 10th / Secondary certificate and mark sheet.
  • ITI certificate, NAC or trade qualification certificate as applicable.
  • Caste / category certificate for SC, ST, OBC, EWS or other reservation claim.
  • Rajasthan domicile certificate, if applicable.
  • PwBD, Ex-servicemen, apprenticeship or experience certificate, if applicable.
  • Passport size photographs and any other document mentioned in the official joining notice.
