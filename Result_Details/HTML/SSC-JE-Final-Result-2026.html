About This Result

The Staff Selection Commission has officially declared the First Round of Tentative Allocation (FRTA) results for the Junior Engineer (Civil, Mechanical & Electrical) Examination, 2025. Following the announcement of the Paper-I results on March 6, 2026, a total of 15,607 candidates were shortlisted, out of which 14,062 appeared for Paper-II on April 7, 2026. Online option-cum-preferences were collected from June 10 to June 14, 2026, with 11,052 candidates successfully submitting their choices.

This result marks the implementation of a new Sliding Mechanism to optimize vacancy utilization. Evaluation has been processed using normalized marks for Paper-I, along with inter-branch normalization between Mechanical and Electrical streams for Paper-II due to distinct question papers and difficulty variables.

Candidates allocated a provisional seat under FRTA must log into the portal to secure or opt for sliding. Physical presence at the designated regional offices for Identity Verification (IV) is strictly mandatory to maintain eligibility for the final merit list.

How to Check SSC JE Allocation List

  1. Navigate to the official Staff Selection Commission website at ssc.gov.in.
  2. Click on the 'Results' section or check the 'Latest News' carousel on the dashboard.
  3. Locate the link labeled 'Junior Engineer (Civil, Mechanical & Electrical) Examination, 2025 - First Round of Tentative Allocation (FRTA)'.

Tie-Breaker Resolution Protocol

In instances where multiple aspirants secure identical aggregate scores across Paper-I and Paper-II, positions are allocated using the hierarchical rules below:

  1. Total marks scored specifically in Paper-II.
  2. Total marks scored in Part (i) of Paper-I.
  3. Total marks scored in Part (ii) of Paper-I.
  4. Date of birth preference, giving higher rank to the older candidates.
  5. Alphabetical sequence of candidate names.

What After FRTA - The Sliding Mechanism Rules

Candidates provisionally allocated seats must log into their accounts between July 12 and July 14, 2026, to book their verification venue and slot. They must physically report for Identity Verification on July 20 or 21, 2026. This process facilitates candidate choice options ('FIX' or 'FLOAT') to fill left-over vacancies via subsequent reallocation.

Critical Warning: Any candidate who fails to report physically for the mandatory Identity Verification (IV) process on the specified dates will automatically forfeit their candidature, and they will not be integrated into the final selection list.

Documents Required for Identity Verification

  • Printed copy of the online application form and choice selection sheet.
  • Official SSC JE Allocation Status page printout.
  • Original Matriculation (Class 10) Certificate as age verification proof.
  • Diploma or Degree Certificates in relevant Engineering branches along with semester mark sheets.
  • Valid Caste / Category Certificate (SC/ST/OBC/EWS) if claiming structural reservations.
  • PwBD or Ex-Servicemen certificates from competent authorities, if applicable.
  • At least two recent color passport-sized photographs.
  • A valid government-issued Photo ID Proof (Aadhaar Card, PAN Card, Driving License, etc.).

Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)

Is the SSC JE 2025 Final Result 2026 declared?

Yes, the Staff Selection Commission has released the First Round of Tentative Allocation (FRTA) list on July 9, 2026, which acts as the provisional final selection list subject to sliding choices.

What is the total number of recommended candidates?

A total of 1,731 candidates have been allocated seats matching exactly with the total reported final vacancy strength across all categories.

Where can I look up my individual score and final answer sheets?

The commission explicitly noted that final answer keys, official question papers, and comprehensive mark sheets of all candidates will be made available on ssc.gov.in after the total completion of the final result block.

What is the deadline to pick options for the sliding process?

शॉर्टलिस्ट किए गए अभ्यर्थी candidate login dashboard के माध्यम से 12 जुलाई 2026 (शाम 5 बजे) से लेकर 14 जुलाई 2026 (शाम 5 बजे) तक अपनी पसंद का केंद्र, तारीख और स्लॉट चुन सकते हैं।

What happens if I skip the physical Identity Verification (IV)?

Any provisionally selected candidate failing to report physically for the Identity Verification stage on July 20 or 21, 2026, will have their candidature summarily canceled and will not be factored into the final result matrix.
