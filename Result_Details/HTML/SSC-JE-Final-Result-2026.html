About This Result

The Staff Selection Commission has successfully published the final recruitment result for the Junior Engineer (Civil, Mechanical & Electrical) Examination, 2025. Following the evaluation of Paper-I normalized marks and Paper-II scores along with the submission of option-cum-preference and the subsequent sliding/verification process, the final selection list has been compiled.

A total of 1,731 candidates have been provisionally selected and allocated to different government organizations, including CPWD, Military Engineering Services (MES), Border Roads Organization (BRO), Central Water Commission (CWC), and Farakka Barrage Project. Selection has been done strictly on the basis of merit-cum-preference.

Candidates included in the final merit list will now be called for Document Verification and joining formalities directly by their respective allotted departments. Final marks, question papers, and scorecard details will be made available on the official portal in due course.

How to Check SSC JE Final Result

  1. Visit the official portal of Staff Selection Commission at ssc.gov.in.
  2. Navigate to the "Results" tab present on the homepage navigation bar.
  3. Click on the link titled "Junior Engineer (Civil, Mechanical & Electrical) Examination, 2025 - Declaration of Final Result".
  4. Download the official Result PDF / Merit List document to your device.
  5. Open the downloaded PDF file and press Ctrl + F (or search icon on mobile) to enter your Roll Number or Full Name.
  6. Check your selection status, assigned post code, and allocated department carefully.
  7. Save and print a copy of the result PDF for future reference during document verification.

Details Mentioned on Result PDF

  • Candidate Roll Number
  • Full Name of the Candidate
  • Father's / Mother's Name
  • Category & Sub-Category Code
  • Allocated Post Code & Department Name
  • Selection Merit Rank / Status
  • Total Marks & Cut-off Marks criteria

What After Final Result?

With the release of the final result, the recruitment role of the Staff Selection Commission for JE Exam 2025 stands completed. The next procedures are as follows:

  • Department Allocation: User Departments will issue offer letters to provisionally selected candidates.
  • Document Verification (DV): All original certificates (educational qualification, caste, age proof, identity, etc.) will be verified by the respective department.
  • Medical Examination: Candidates will undergo medical checks as per department-specific medical standards.
  • Appointment & Training: Upon successful verification, final appointment orders and training schedules will be issued.

Documents Required for Next Stage

  • Printout of Online Application Form & Result Notification
  • Matriculation / 10th Class Certificate for Date of Birth verification
  • Diploma or Degree Certificates in Civil / Mechanical / Electrical Engineering
  • Year-wise / Semester-wise Marksheets
  • Valid Caste / Category Certificate (SC/ST/OBC/EWS) in prescribed Central Format
  • PwBD / Ex-Servicemen Certificate (if applicable)
  • No Objection Certificate (NOC) for currently employed government servants
  • Valid Photo Identity Card (Aadhaar Card, PAN Card, Voter ID, Driving License)
  • Recent Passport Size Photographs

Frequently Asked Questions

Is SSC JE 2025 Final Result 2026 declared?

Yes, Staff Selection Commission officially declared the final result and merit list on 03 August 2026 at ssc.gov.in.

How many candidates are recommended in SSC JE Final Result 2026?

A total of 1,731 candidates have been provisionally recommended for appointment to various Junior Engineer posts.

Where can candidates download the SSC JE Final Result PDF?

Candidates can download the result write-up PDF and merit list directly from the official SSC website at ssc.gov.in.

What are the minimum qualifying marks in SSC JE Paper-II?

Minimum qualifying marks in Paper-II are 30% (90 marks) for UR, 25% (75 marks) for OBC/EWS, and 20% (60 marks) for reserved categories.

How to check the roll number in SSC JE Merit List PDF?

What is the next stage after SSC JE Final Result release?

Will final marks and scorecard be released?

Yes, the Commission will upload individual scorecards and marks for both qualified and non-qualified candidates on ssc.gov.in in due course.

What to do if the official result link is not opening?

In case of high traffic on ssc.gov.in, wait for some time or clear your browser cache before retrying to open the page.
