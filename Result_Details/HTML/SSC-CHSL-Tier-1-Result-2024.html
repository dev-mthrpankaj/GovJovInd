About This Result

The Staff Selection Commission conducted Tier-I of the Combined Higher Secondary (10+2) Level Examination (CHSLE), 2024 from 1 July 2024 to 11 July 2024 in a computer-based format. Since the examination was held in multiple shifts, SSC used normalized scores to determine the cut-off marks and process the result.

The declared result is for shortlisting candidates to appear in the next stage: Tier 2 Examination. A total of 39,835 candidates have qualified for Lower Division Clerk (LDC)/Junior Secretariat Assistant (JSA), and 1,630 candidates have qualified for Data Entry Operator (DEO) posts.

The SSC has also stated that final answer keys and marks of both qualified and non-qualified candidates will be uploaded on the Commission's website shortly.

How to Check SSC CHSL Result

  1. Visit the official Staff Selection Commission website at ssc.gov.in.
  2. Go to the 'Result' tab available on the homepage.
  3. Click on the result link for "SSC CHSL Tier 1 Result 2024" (List 1 and List 2).
  4. A new PDF file will open.
  5. Press Ctrl + F and search for your roll number or name carefully in the result PDF.
  6. Download and keep a hard copy of the result page for further need.

What After Tier 1 Result?

  • Candidates who have qualified Tier 1 will be eligible to appear for the Tier 2 examination.
  • The schedule for the Tier 2 examination will be made available on the official website of the Commission.
  • Final answer keys and marks of both qualified and non-qualified candidates will be uploaded by SSC shortly.
  • Candidates should regularly visit ssc.gov.in for updates regarding the Tier 2 exam and admit cards.
