About This Result

The Staff Selection Commission conducted the Computer Based Examination for Constable (Executive) Male and Female in Delhi Police Examination, 2025 from 18 December 2025 to 06 January 2026 at different centres across the country. The examination was held in multiple shifts, so SSC processed the result using normalized marks.

This result is not the final selection list. It is a shortlist for the next stage of the recruitment process, which includes Physical Endurance & Measurement Test (PE&MT) and Document Verification (DV). The next-stage schedule and admission certificate will be communicated by Delhi Police in due course.

Candidates should read the official write-up carefully because it includes category-wise cutoff marks, shortlisted candidate counts, withheld cases, malpractice-related cases, cancelled candidature details and instructions about final answer keys, response sheets and marks.

How to Check Result

  1. Visit the official Staff Selection Commission website at ssc.gov.in.
  2. Open the Results section and find the Delhi Police Constable (Executive) Male and Female Examination, 2025 result link.
  3. Download the official result write-up, cutoff PDF and merit list/qualified candidates list if separately provided.
  4. Open the PDF and use Ctrl+F or the search option to find your roll number.
  5. Check the category, qualifying status, cutoff note and next-stage instructions carefully.
  6. Save or print the result PDF and keep it ready with your application details for PE&MT/DV.

What After Result

  • Shortlisted candidates will be called for PE&MT/DV conducted by Delhi Police.
  • The schedule of PE&MT/DV will be communicated by Delhi Police in due course.
  • Candidates should regularly check delhipolice.gov.in for admission certificate and next-stage instructions.
  • NCC certificate and RRU degree/diploma bonus marks will be awarded in the final result only to eligible candidates who produce supporting documents at PE&MT/DV stage.
  • SSC will upload final answer keys, question papers cum response sheets and marks of qualified/non-qualified candidates on its website in due course.

Documents Required for Next Stage

  • PE&MT/DV admission certificate or call letter issued by Delhi Police.
  • Printed application form or registration details, if asked in the official instructions.
  • Valid photo identity proof such as Aadhaar card, voter ID, PAN card, passport or driving licence.
  • Educational qualification certificates and mark sheets.
  • Caste/category certificate, EWS certificate, ESM documents or other reservation documents, if applicable.
  • Domicile/residence certificate, if applicable as per the official notice.
  • Recent passport-size photographs.
  • NCC certificate, RRU degree/diploma or other supporting documents, if claiming related bonus marks.
  • Any other document mentioned in the official PE&MT/DV admission certificate or Delhi Police instructions.
