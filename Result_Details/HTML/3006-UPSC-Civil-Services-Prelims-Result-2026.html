About This Result

UPSC has released the result of the Civil Services (Preliminary) Examination, 2026 on the basis of the examination held on 24 May 2026. The official result is published as a roll number list of candidates who have qualified for admission to the Civil Services (Main) Examination, 2026.

The candidature of the qualified candidates is provisional. As per the official press note, candidates must login during the notified window and update or reconfirm their required details so that the Commission can generate the e-Admit Card for the Civil Services Main Examination, 2026.

The official notice is important because it contains the mandatory Mains form window, fee submission instruction, cadre preference update and the status of marks, cutoff marks and answer keys. Candidates should rely on the official UPSC websites for final confirmation before taking any next step.

How to Check UPSC Prelims Result

  1. Visit the official Union Public Service Commission website at upsc.gov.in.
  2. Open the result section or the latest result notice for Civil Services (Preliminary) Examination, 2026.
  3. Download the official result PDF / roll number list from the UPSC website.
  4. Open the PDF and search your roll number carefully using the search option.
  5. If your roll number appears in the list, read the Mains form window and next-stage instructions carefully.
  6. Save or print the result PDF and keep your application details ready for the Civil Services Main Examination process.

Result Details Mentioned

  • Roll numbers of candidates qualified for admission to the Civil Services Main Examination, 2026.
  • Provisional candidature note for shortlisted candidates.
  • Mains form window for updating/reconfirming details and submitting the required application form.
  • Fee submission instruction for candidates who are not exempted from fee payment.
  • Scribe details, assistive device and large-font question paper update instructions where applicable.
  • Cadre preference filling instruction for Civil Services Main Examination, 2026.
  • Status of marks, cutoff marks and answer keys.
  • UPSC call centre contact numbers for examination-related clarification.

What After Result

After qualifying the Civil Services Preliminary Examination, candidates have to complete the next online formalities for the Civil Services Main Examination, 2026. UPSC has stated that the window for filling, updating and submitting required details will remain available from 19 June 2026 to 28 June 2026.

  • Qualified candidates must login to the portal at upsconline.nic.in during the official window.
  • Candidates must submit the fee of Rs. 200 for admission to the Civil Services Main Examination, except fee-exempted candidates such as Female, PwBD, SC and ST candidates.
  • Eligible candidates must submit or update scribe details, assistive device details and large-font question paper requirement, if applicable.
  • Candidates must fill the cadre preference for the Civil Services Main Examination, 2026.
  • Even candidates who have already uploaded information and do not need any update must login, verify details and finally submit the form.
  • Failure to submit the required form/details may lead to non-generation of e-Admit Card and candidates may not be allowed to participate in further stages.

Documents Required for Next Stage

Candidates should keep the following documents ready for the Civil Services Main Examination process and later verification stages. The final list must be checked from the official UPSC instructions.

  • Civil Services Preliminary Examination admit card and application details.
  • Printed copy or saved copy of the result PDF / roll number list for reference.
  • Valid photo ID proof such as Aadhaar, voter ID, passport, driving licence or other accepted ID.
  • Educational qualification certificates and mark sheets.
  • Category certificate for SC, ST, OBC, EWS or PwBD candidates, if applicable.
  • Domicile or other supporting certificates, if required by the official instructions.
  • Recent passport-size photographs as per UPSC requirements.
  • Scribe, assistive device, disability or large-font question paper related documents, if applicable.
  • Any other document mentioned in the official Civil Services Main Examination instructions.
