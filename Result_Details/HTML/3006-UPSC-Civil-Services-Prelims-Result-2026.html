About UPSC Civil Services Prelims 2026 Result

The Civil Services Preliminary Examination serves as a mandatory filtering stage for one of India's most prestigious administrative recruitment processes. The examination conducted on May 24, 2026, forms the baseline screening for shortlisting eligible candidates into key administrative posts such as the Indian Administrative Service (IAS), Indian Foreign Service (IFS), and Indian Police Service (IPS). The newly released publication list highlights only the roll numbers of the applicants who managed to score above the threshold parameters in General Studies Paper-I while successfully securing the minimum 33% qualifying mark in General Studies Paper-II (CSAT).

Candidatures published through this initial selection list remain entirely provisional. All shortlisted candidates have to proceed through a mandatory registration routine by logging into the official portal to provide, confirm, or modify their essential data parameters. Missing out on the validation frame will lead to immediate cancellation of admission clearances for the secondary descriptive phase.

How to Check UPSC Civil Services Prelims Result 2026

Follow these simple steps to verify if your roll number is present in the official selection list:

  1. Navigate directly to the official portal of the commission at https://www.upsc.gov.in.
  2. Look under the active "What's New" feed visible on the homepage screen.
  3. Click on the notification title: "Result: Civil Services (Preliminary) Examination, 2026".
  4. Open the resulting document link to download the structured roll number PDF to your local system environment.
  5. Open the downloaded document using any standard PDF Reader application.
  6. Utilize the structural keyboard shortcut command Ctrl + F (or choose the magnifying lens finder tool on mobile browsers).
  7. Input your official 7-digit assigned examination roll number inside the prompt input box and press enter.
  8. If your sequence becomes highlighted across the PDF file tables, you have successfully cleared the preliminary hurdle and qualified for the Mains stage.
  9. Save an archived copy or print the specific page snippet containing your record for upcoming reference procedures.

What Next After Qualifying UPSC Prelims 2026?

All candidates who have located their roll numbers in the preliminary list must prepare for the mandatory updates on the official website. Shortlisted candidates are required to log in to the portal at https://upsconline.nic.in between June 19, 2026, and June 28, 2026.

During this specified window, qualified candidates must submit the prescribed exam fee of ₹200 (except for fee-exempted categories like Female, SC, ST, and PwBD candidates), update scribe configurations if applicable, choose their large font paper layout metrics if needed, and finalize their service cadre or center parameters.

Completing this verification protocol is completely mandatory. Even if you have no changes or adjustments to perform, logging into your dashboard profile to save and submit the reconfirmation steps is necessary. Candidates who fail to complete this step before June 28, 2026, will not be issued an e-Admit Card for the Civil Services (Main) Written Examination, which is scheduled to begin on August 21, 2026.

Documents & Details Required for the Next Stage

While filling out forms and preparing for validation processing for the Main examination, keep the following credentials ready:

  • Primary Educational Matriculation / Age Validation Certificate.
  • Graduation Degree Qualification Documents or Equivalent Certificates.
  • Valid government-issued Identity Verification Document (Aadhaar Card, Voter ID, PAN, Passport).
  • Community Category/Caste Certificate (SC/ST/OBC status confirmations where applicable).
  • Income and Asset Certificate for EWS category applicants.
  • Disability/Medical Certificate for PwBD applicants.
  • Scanned Copy of Passport Size Photographs and clear signature files.

Frequently Asked Questions (FAQs)

When was the UPSC Civil Services Prelims Result 2026 declared?

The Union Public Service Commission (UPSC) officially declared the Civil Services Preliminary Examination 2026 result on June 15, 2026.

How many candidates qualified for the UPSC CSE Mains 2026 exam?

A total of 13,343 candidates successfully qualified for the Civil Services (Main) Examination 2026 out of the preliminary screening.

Where can I download the official UPSC Prelims 2026 Result PDF?

You can download the official roll number list PDF directly from the official website at upsc.gov.in or via the direct download link provided in the important links section of this page.

Are individual marks and official cut-off marks released with the Prelims result?

No, UPSC releases individual scores, final answer keys, and category-wise cut-off marks only after the entire selection process (including Main Exam and Personality Test) is completed and the final merit list is declared.

What is the mandatory next step for candidates who cleared the Prelims?

Qualified candidates must log in to the UPSC online portal (upsconline.nic.in) between June 19, 2026, and June 28, 2026, to pay the Mains exam fee, update scribe or personal details, and submit their preferences.

When will the UPSC Civil Services Main Examination 2026 begin?

The UPSC Civil Services Main Examination 2026 is scheduled to commence on August 21, 2026.

Can I check my result if I forgot my roll number?

The initial result is published roll-number-wise. If you forgot your roll number, you can log in to your UPSC OTR (One Time Registration) dashboard or check your registered email/admit card file to retrieve it.

What happens if a qualified candidate fails to submit the online portal updates by June 28?

If a candidate fails to log in and finalize their mandatory submission before the deadline on June 28, 2026, their candidature will be canceled, and their e-Admit Card for the Main Examination will not be generated.
