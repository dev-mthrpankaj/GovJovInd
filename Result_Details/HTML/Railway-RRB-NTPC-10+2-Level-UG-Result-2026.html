About This Result

The Railway Recruitment Board (RRB) has released the Stage-I Computer Based Test (CBT 1) results for the Under Graduate (10+2) level posts under Advt No. CEN 07/2025. This examination was conducted in multiple shifts across the country in May and June 2026 to recruit candidates for 3058 vacancies, including Commercial Cum Ticket Clerk, Accounts Clerk Cum Typist, and Trains Clerk.

The result is currently available in the form of zone-wise PDF merit lists containing the roll numbers of shortlisted candidates. Along with the result, RRB has also published the category-wise cut-off marks for each region.

Candidates who have qualified in CBT 1 are eligible to appear for the Stage-II CBT. The official scorecard, detailing raw marks, normalized marks, and qualifying status, will be accessible via candidate login starting 27 August 2026.

How to Check RRB NTPC Result

  1. Visit the official website of your respective regional RRB (e.g., RRB Allahabad, RRB Mumbai, RRB Chandigarh).
  2. On the homepage, look for the link titled "CEN 07/2025 NTPC (Under Graduate) CBT 1 Result & Cut-off Marks".
  3. Click the link to open the Merit List PDF.
  4. Press Ctrl + F and enter your Roll Number to search for your qualifying status.
  5. To view your exact marks, wait for the scorecard link activation on 27 August 2026, click on the login portal, and enter your Registration Number and Date of Birth.
  6. Download and print your result/scorecard for future reference in subsequent recruitment stages.

Result Details Mentioned

The merit list PDF and the upcoming individual scorecard will feature the following details:

  • Candidate's Roll Number (in Merit List)
  • Candidate Name & Registration Number (in Scorecard)
  • Applied RRB Zone
  • Category (UR, SC, ST, OBC, EWS)
  • Normalized Score / Raw Marks (in Scorecard)
  • Zone-wise and Category-wise Cut-off Marks
  • Shortlisting status for Stage-II CBT (Yes/No)

Cut Off & Merit List

The Railway Recruitment Board has released the official category-wise cut-off marks alongside the merit list for each zone. Because the exam was conducted in multiple shifts, the scores have been normalized before preparing the final cut-offs. Candidates are advised to visit their specific RRB regional portal (such as Bhubaneshwar, Guwahati, Ranchi, Chandigarh, Gorakhpur, etc.) to download the official PDF for exact cut-off marks.

What After CBT 1 Result?

Candidates who successfully clear the CBT 1 cut-off will move forward to the next stages of the recruitment process based on the official notification:

  • Stage-II Computer Based Test (CBT 2): A separate CBT will be conducted for the shortlisted candidates. The difficulty level will be aligned with the 10+2 educational standard.
  • Typing Skill Test (TST): For posts like Accounts Clerk Cum Typist and Junior Clerk Cum Typist, a qualifying typing test will follow CBT 2.
  • Document Verification (DV) & Medical Exam: Final stages before appointment.

Documents Required for Next Stages

  • Admit Card for CBT 2 / Next Stage
  • Printed copy of CBT 1 Scorecard
  • Original 10th and 12th standard mark sheets and passing certificates
  • Valid Photo ID Proof (Aadhaar Card, PAN Card, Voter ID, Passport)
  • Caste/Category Certificate in standard Government of India format (if applicable)
  • Income and Asset Certificate for EWS candidates
  • PwBD/ESM certificates (if applicable)
  • Passport size colour photographs
