About This Result

The Union Public Service Commission (UPSC) conducted the written examination for the Central Armed Police Forces (Assistant Commandants) across various centers in India. The declared result includes the roll numbers of candidates who have successfully cleared the written stage.

This is a written shortlist result. Candidates whose roll numbers feature in the merit list must prepare for the next stage of the selection process, which includes the Physical Efficiency Test (PET), Physical Standard Test (PST), and Medical Standards Test. Candidates are strictly advised to carefully read the UPSC CAPF.pdf for full instructions[cite: 1].

Additionally, successful candidates must fill in the Detailed Application Form (DAF) online within the stipulated timeline provided by the Commission. The final cut-off marks and scores will be made available only after the completion of the entire recruitment process.

How to Check UPSC CAPF AC Result

  1. Visit the official Union Public Service Commission website at upsc.gov.in.
  2. Go to the "Written Results" section on the homepage.
  3. Click on the link for "Central Armed Police Forces (ACs) Examination, 2026".
  4. Download the official result PDF, which may be referenced as UPSC CAPF.pdf[cite: 1].
  5. Search your roll number carefully within the PDF using the Find function (Ctrl+F).
  6. Save or print the result for your records and future reference.

Result Details Mentioned

  • Candidate roll number of shortlisted candidates.
  • Instructions for filling out the Detailed Application Form (DAF).
  • Details concerning the nodal authority conducting the PET/PST.
  • General guidelines for the Physical and Medical Standards Test.

Cut Off / Merit List

The official cutoff marks will be updated after release. As per UPSC norms, the final cut-off marks, along with the scorecard of candidates, are generally released after the final personality test/interview stage is concluded.

What After Result

  • Detailed Application Form (DAF): Qualified candidates must fill and submit the online DAF along with scanned copies of relevant certificates/documents.
  • PET / PST / Medical Test: Candidates will be called by the nodal CAPF authority to undergo the Physical Standards Test, Physical Efficiency Test, and Medical Standards Test.
  • Personality Test / Interview: Candidates who successfully pass the physical and medical stages will be invited for the interview round.
  • Check official notice for accurate dates and instructions.

Documents Required for Next Stage

  • UPSC CAPF Admit Card (Next Stage)
  • Printed copy of the submitted Detailed Application Form (DAF)
  • Result/scorecard print (when available)
  • Photo ID proof (Aadhar Card, Voter ID, Passport, etc.)
  • Original educational certificates (Degree/Provisional)
  • Caste/category certificate (if applicable)
  • Passport size photographs
  • Other documents mentioned in the official DAF notification

Frequently Asked Questions

Is UPSC CAPF AC Written Result 2026 declared?

Yes, the Union Public Service Commission has released the written exam results for CAPF AC 2026.

What is the next stage after UPSC CAPF AC Written Result?

Shortlisted candidates will be called for the Physical Efficiency Test (PET), Physical Standard Test (PST), and Medical Standards Test conducted by the Nodal Authority.

Where to check the UPSC CAPF AC Result?

Candidates can check their results on the official UPSC website at upsc.gov.in.

How to search roll number?

Download the official result PDF and use the Ctrl+F shortcut to search for your specific roll number in the list of shortlisted candidates.

Is the cut-off released?

The official cut-off marks are generally released after the final selection process is complete.

What documents are required for the next stage?

Candidates will need their admit card, original educational certificates, caste/category certificate (if applicable), photo ID proof, and passport size photographs for document verification during the PET/PST stage.

When will marks/final result be released?

The final marks and scorecard will be published by the UPSC after the culmination of the personality test/interview phase.

What to do if result link is not opening?

If the official website is unresponsive due to heavy traffic, candidates are advised to clear their browser cache, use a different browser, or try checking after some time.
