About This Result

Uttar Pradesh Police Recruitment and Promotion Board, Lucknow has published the shortlisted candidates list for Uttar Pradesh Home Guard posts under Enrollment 2025. This update is not the final joining result; it is a shortlist for the next stage of the selection process.

The written examination was conducted on 25, 26 and 27 April 2026. As per the official notice, candidates have been shortlisted by adding preferential qualification marks to normalized written exam marks and applying Uttar Pradesh reservation rules. The list has been prepared district-wise and category-wise against the advertised vacancies.

Candidates should carefully check the relevant district PDF, verify their roll number and read the official instructions for document verification and physical standard test. The DVPST date will be published separately on the UPPBPB website, so candidates should keep all documents ready and continue checking the official portal.

How to Check UP Home Guard Result

  1. Visit the official UPPBPB website at uppbpb.gov.in.
  2. Open the latest notice/result section related to Uttar Pradesh Home Guard Enrollment 2025.
  3. Download the district-wise and category-wise shortlisted candidates PDF for DVPST.
  4. Open the PDF and use the search/find option to check your roll number or details as available in the list.
  5. Check the cutoff marks given at the end of the relevant district PDF list, if applicable.
  6. Save or print the result PDF and keep checking the official website for DV/PST schedule and instructions.

Result Details Mentioned

  • Candidate roll number or candidate details as shown in the official district-wise PDF.
  • District-wise and category-wise shortlisted candidate information.
  • Qualifying status for document verification and physical standard test.
  • Cutoff marks for shortlisted candidates in each district PDF list, as stated in the official notice.
  • Next-stage instructions related to DV/PST, when released by the Board.
  • Marks or separate scorecard details: To be notified, if released separately by the Board.

Cut Off / Merit List

The official notice states that the cutoff marks of shortlisted candidates have been provided at the end of each district PDF list. Candidates should open their district PDF on the official UPPBPB website and verify the cutoff according to their category.

What After UP Home Guard Result

Shortlisted candidates will be called for document verification and physical standard test. The official notice says that the date of the next stage will be published soon on the Board website. Candidates should not wait for the last day and should prepare all original documents, photocopies and photographs in advance.

  • Check the official DVPST schedule when released.
  • Download any call letter, admit card or instruction PDF issued for DV/PST.
  • Carry original documents and required photocopies.
  • Follow the physical standard and document instructions strictly.
  • Final selection or further process will depend on official rules and Board instructions.

Documents Required for Next Stage

  • Printed result PDF / shortlisted list reference and DVPST admit card or call letter, if issued.
  • Application form / registration details.
  • Valid photo ID proof such as Aadhaar card, voter ID, PAN card or other accepted ID.
  • Educational qualification certificates and marksheets.
  • Caste/category certificate, if applicable.
  • Domicile/residence certificate, if applicable as per official instructions.
  • Recent passport-size photographs.
  • Preferential qualification, skill, service or experience certificates, if claimed in the application.
  • Any other document mentioned in the official DV/PST notice or call letter.
