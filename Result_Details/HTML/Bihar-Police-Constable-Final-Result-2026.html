About This Result

The Central Selection Board of Constables (CSBC) conducted the recruitment drive for 19,838 Constable posts in the Bihar Police and Bihar Special Armed Police[cite: 1]. Following the written exam held in July and August 2025, qualifying candidates were called for the Physical Efficiency Test (PET), which was conducted from 15 December 2025 to 11 March 2026[cite: 1].

The final merit list has been officially released on 27 May 2026[cite: 1]. This final selection is based primarily on the marks scored by the candidates in the Physical Efficiency Test (PET) subject to qualifying the written test. The result PDF lists the roll numbers of all successful candidates along with the respective Service and Allotted District/Battalion.

Out of the total selected candidates, 12,509 are males, 7,312 are females, and 17 are transgender candidates[cite: 1]. All shortlisted candidates will now undergo a mandatory Medical Examination and final Document Verification before their formal appointment into the police force.

How to Check Bihar Police Final Result

  1. Visit the new official CSBC website at csbc.bihar.gov.in.
  2. On the homepage, click on the link titled "Final Selection List of Candidates for the Post of Constable in Bihar Police (Advt. No. 01/2025)".
  3. A PDF file containing the merit list and cut-off marks will open on your screen.
  4. Use the search function (CTRL+F) to find your Roll Number within the PDF.
  5. Check your allotted District/Battalion mentioned next to your roll number.
  6. Download and save the final result PDF for future reference during the joining process.

What After Final Result?

  • Medical Examination: All 19,838 selected candidates will be called for a detailed Medical Examination to ensure they meet the strict health standards required for police service.
  • Document Verification (Final): Original documents regarding identity, education, category, and domicile will be re-verified at the time of joining.
  • Reporting and Joining: Candidates must report to their allotted districts/battalions on the dates specified in the upcoming official notice.
  • Failure to report within the stipulated timeline or failing the medical test may lead to cancellation of the candidature.

Documents Required for Next Stage

  • Valid Photo ID Proof (Aadhaar Card, Voter ID, Passport, etc.)
  • Printout of the Final Result PDF highlighting your Roll Number
  • Original Educational Certificates (10th and 12th Marksheets/Certificates)
  • Caste/Category Certificate (if applicable)
  • Bihar Domicile Certificate (if claiming reservation)
  • EWS Certificate (if applicable)
  • Passport size photographs
  • Any other documents specifically requested in the joining notice
