About This Result

The Bihar Technical Service Commission (BTSC) successfully conducted the written examination for the Staff Nurse posts on 30-31 July and 01 & 03 August 2025. Following the completion of the evaluation and prior stages, BTSC has now issued the final result and merit list for Advertisement No. 23/2025.

This result constitutes the final merit list of selected candidates. Candidates are advised to download the official PDF and carefully search for their roll numbers. The commission has also activated the candidate login portal where candidates may check their individual statuses or marks.

All candidates who have been successfully shortlisted in the final result will be called for document verification and joining processes by the relevant health departments. Candidates should regularly monitor the official BTSC website for subsequent joining directives.

How to Check Result

  1. Visit the official BTSC website at btsc.bihar.gov.in.
  2. On the homepage, navigate to the latest notices or result section.
  3. Click on the link for "Final Result - Staff Nurse Advt 23/2025".
  4. Download the final result PDF to your device.
  5. Open the PDF and search your Roll Number carefully.
  6. Save or print the result page for your future reference during joining.

Result Details Mentioned

  • Candidate Roll Number
  • Category (UR/EWS/BC/EBC/SC/ST)
  • Qualifying Status
  • Next-stage instructions / Document Verification remarks

What After Result

Candidates who have made it to the final merit list will undergo:

  • Document Verification (DV): Candidates must present all original documents verifying their eligibility, age, caste, and nursing qualifications.
  • Medical Examination: Standard medical fitness checks as prescribed by the department.
  • Final Joining: Issuance of appointment letters and posting allocations across state healthcare facilities.

Documents Required for Next Stage

  • Admit card and Application form printout
  • Result/scorecard print
  • Photo ID proof (Aadhaar, Voter ID, PAN)
  • Educational certificates (10th, 12th, Nursing Degree/Diploma)
  • Valid Nursing Council Registration Certificate
  • Caste/category certificate (if applicable)
  • Domicile certificate (if applicable)
  • Passport size photographs
  • Other documents mentioned in the official DV notice

FAQ

Is Bihar BTSC Staff Nurse Final Result 2025 released?
