About This Result

The Uttar Pradesh Subordinate Service Selection Commission has published the baseline status checklist for the Combined Lower Subordinate Services Exam 2026. This initial process sifts applicants using their normalized scoring records from the mandatory UP Preliminary Eligibility Test (PET) 2025 cycle.

Candidates verified as eligible through this screening will obtain clearance to register and pick up hall tickets for the definitive Main Written Examination. Individuals who acquired negative or zero net dynamic scores within their past PET credentials stand excluded from the final selection tier.
