About This Final Selection List

The Intelligence Bureau (Ministry of Home Affairs) conducted a rigorous multi-tier evaluation process for the ACIO-II/Exe 2025 recruitment. This process commenced with the online Tier-I Computer Based Test held between September 16 and September 18, 2025. Candidates matching the baseline cutoff parameters were advanced to the descriptive Tier-II examination on January 11, 2026. The final phase involved psychometric/personality interview evaluation cycles conducted through April 2026.

This final list establishes the cumulative selection roll numbers recommended for the central executive assignment. Candidates belonging to EWS, OBC, SC, and ST classifications who scored above the general unreserved benchmark standard are automatically categorized under the general standard rows. Any roll number suffixed with a '(P)' indicator denotes that their provisional selection depends on clearing document shortfalls reported during validation.

It is strictly specified by the Intelligence Bureau that entry into this qualification list does not serve as a definitive legal appointment guarantee. Final allocation depends on medical fitness, background screening checks, and category-wise verification protocols.

How to Check IB ACIO Final Selection Status

  1. Access the online home domain of the Ministry of Home Affairs at mha.gov.in.
  2. Locate the "Vacancies" or "Recent Updates" link section on the dashboard workspace.
  3. Download the document labeled "Final Result of ACIO-II/Exe Exam 2025".
  4. Open the compiled result sheet in a standard PDF viewer application.
  5. Use the shortcut command Ctrl + F (or choose the find feature on mobile platforms).
  6. Input your designated 12-digit structural roll number accurately and hit search.
  7. Verify whether your record contains a provisional classification marker '(P)' adjacent to it.

Key Selection Details on Result PDF

  • Roll Number Arrangement: All listed candidate rows are structured explicitly in ascending numerical alignment. This layout sequence is engineered solely for tracking speed and does NOT state candidate position or rank in the final merit sequence.
  • Provisional Qualification status: Roll records tagged with an explicit (P) indicator are flagged for pending credential documentation discrepancies. These candidates must produce authentic proof during subsequent confirmation reviews.
  • Category Standards Adjustments: Reserved category applicants (OBC/EWS/SC/ST) appearing under Unreserved tables cleared selection parameters using general unreserved eligibility limits without age or score relaxations.
  • Rectification Provisions: The Intelligence Bureau maintains complete rights to modify, correct, or adjust any manual printing discrepancies, misprints, or administrative omissions identified after the final release.

Next Stages After Selection Announcement

Candidates securing space in the selection roll table must prepare for subsequent administrative procedures managed directly by the Ministry of Home Affairs:

  • Character & Antecedents Verification: Comprehensive background verification checks regarding residential documentation, legal history, and academic integrity tracks.
  • Attestation Form Execution: Filling out the official Attestation Forms (AF) and Special Security Questionnaire (SSQ) forms with precise professional record accounts.
  • Medical Screening Evaluation: Final medical parameter clearance examinations supervised through central medical authority panels to establish physical suitability definitions.
  • Candidature Condition Checks: Cross-checking parameters relating to official age limits, authentic academic degree certificates, and category certification validity rules before generating final appointment files.

Documents to Keep Ready for Verification

  • Printout copy of the official IB ACIO Application form acknowledgement layout.
  • Primary educational identity records including Matriculation (10th) marksheets and original graduation degree validation certificates.
  • Valid community caste/category status validation certificate documents (OBC/EWS/SC/ST certifications matching central formats).
  • Valid Ex-Servicemen discharge certificate log details where applicable.
  • Government certified identity records featuring active passport, PAN card, Aadhaar record registration, or voters card.
  • Recent high-definition passport size coloration photographs matching application uploads.

    Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)

    When was the final merit result for IB ACIO-II/Exe 2025 published?

    The final selection list was officially verified and uploaded online on June 3, 2026.

    Are the selected roll records organized according to scoring ranks?

    No. All candidate roll numbers are listed systematically in ascending order for identification ease and do not show a rank position layout.

    What should I do if my qualification roll number is tagged with a (P) marker?

    The (P) represents a provisional status due to document shortfalls. You must contact validation authorities and present authentic certificates when asked during background verification updates.

    Does inclusion in this final selection PDF promise a confirmation job offer?

    No. True selection status is achieved only after completing character background checks, passing verification tests, and clearing central medical wellness guidelines.

    Where can I get the direct authentic PDF link for my verification record?

    Candidates can access or download copies from the central domain database setups visible at mha.gov.in.

    Will the board release separate marks or scorecard sheets for non-shortlisted applicants?

    Individual scoring metrics, aggregate performance numbers, and category cutoffs are typically released on the official website following the completion of the recruitment process.

    How do I fill out my Attestation Forms (AF) and Special Security Questionnaire (SSQ)?

    Selected applicants will receive official communications with instructions on downloading, filling out, and authenticating forms from their registered personal contact details.

    What parameters define matching results if applicants secure identical scores?

    Tie-breaking rules are determined by performance weights across the descriptive written section, tier-1 scoring balances, and senior age ranking parameters specified in the advertisement notice.
