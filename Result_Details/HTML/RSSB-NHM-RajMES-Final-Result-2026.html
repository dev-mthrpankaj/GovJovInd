About This Result

The Rajasthan Staff Selection Board successfully conducted the competitive contractual examinations for various healthcare, parameter, and administrative posts attached to the National Health Mission (NHM) and RajMES departments. The offline/written tests were scheduled and executed between 02 June 2025 and 13 June 2025 across selected testing nodes in Rajasthan.

Following primary evaluation, provisional shortlists, and subsequent rounds of verification of credentials and health parameters, the final lists of candidates recommended for provisional appointment have been structured. The selection lists contain the roll numbers categorized systematically according to reservation brackets and non-TSP/TSP area segments.

Candidates can log into the RSSB portal or the state recruitment portal using single sign-on (SSO) configurations to monitor official individual scorecards or download absolute PDF rosters published under the main results section.

Category Cut-Off Marks Structure

The category-specific final cut-off scores alongside horizontal reservation markers have been published individually inside the specific post-wise result PDFs. Candidates are advised to check their post-specific document files (such as Staff Nurse, ANM, or Administrative posts) on the RSSB results page to evaluate precise cut-off margins. For general understanding, actual cutoff ranges correspond directly to total evaluated weightage out of total maximum exam marks.

How to Check RSSB NHM & RajMES Final Result

  1. Launch an updated internet browser and point to the official portal: rssb.rajasthan.gov.in.
  2. Look carefully at the bottom task strip or main secondary menu and click on the "Results" tab.
  3. Locate the specific link matching your trade or vacancy titled under "NHM and RajMES Contractual Recruitment 2025 - Final Recommendation List".
  4. Open and download the published final merit list PDF file.
  5. Execute a finding command (Ctrl+F) and punch in your specific roll number string to look for matches.
  6. Alternatively, view your breakdown of marks through your registered credentials over the Rajasthan Single Sign-On (SSO) engine.

Details Highlighted in Selection PDFs

  • Official Notification Reference Numbers and date tags.
  • Post names along with departmental classification (NHM or RajMES).
  • Roster of Roll Numbers of provisionally selected candidates listed in order of rank/category.
  • Absolute category-wise cut-off points (Vertical & Horizontal classes).
  • Withheld list instructions or debarment lists where active.
  • Critical administrative advisory guidance points for prospective deployment.

Next Stage & Allocation Protocol

Candidates appearing on the final recommended list will have their data sets transmitted to the Medical, Health & Family Welfare Department, Government of Rajasthan. The department will release an online choice-filling module on the Rajswasthya portal to aggregate preferences for district locations or medical institutions. Postings will follow institutional vacancy positions combined with candidate rankings.

Essential Documents for Final Joining Verification

Candidates should prepare duplicate self-attested pairs of these document packets before reporting for final duties allocation:

  • Valid printed copy of the final selection list page and scorecard.
  • Original Matriculation (10th) certificate for proof of date of birth.
  • Senior Secondary (12th) marks sheets and pass certification.
  • Core Technical Diploma / Professional Degree Marks sheets (GNM, B.Sc. Nursing, or equivalent technical qualifications).
  • Valid active Registration Certificate issued by the Rajasthan Nursing Council (RNC) or respective state board.
  • Fresh Caste/Community certificate in standardized state formats (if claiming category accommodations).
  • Valid character certificates, marriage registration declarations, or domicile proofs.

Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)

When was the RSSB NHM and RajMES Final Result 2026 released?

The definitive category selection lists and structural final results were declared on 09 July 2026.

What are the exam dates for this recruitment?

The initial written multi-shift screening examination was organised between 02 June 2025 and 13 June 2025.

Where can I look up my absolute scored marks?

Candidates can access their specific mark sheets by logging into the state SSO portal utilizing their unique login IDs.

How do I search for my roll number within the long result list?

Open the downloaded PDF corresponding to your specific post and use the keyboard shortcut Ctrl+F to input and verify your roll number.

Is registration with the Rajasthan Nursing Council mandatory for final joining?

Yes, dynamic registration with the RNC or respective statutory council is compulsory during the physical joining procedure for paramedical/nursing trades.

What is the next stage after finding my roll number in the final list?

You must follow the updates on the Rajswasthya portal to submit your preferred choices for district and medical center allocation online.
