About This Result

The Madhya Pradesh Employees Selection Board (MPESB), Bhopal conducted the Group-1 Sub Group-2 Combined Recruitment Test 2025 starting on 17 February 2026 across multiple designated test centres in Madhya Pradesh. Following the successful conduct of the examination and release of provisional answer keys on 23 February 2026, MPESB has released the official result and scorecard on 03 August 2026.

This result status covers candidates competing for 474 Group-1 Sub Group-2 positions across various MP state departments. Candidates who appeared for the exam can now log in using their application credentials to download their individual scorecard, view normalized marks, and verify their merit position.

Candidates qualifying in this written examination stage will be invited for Document Verification (DV) by the concerned department authorities. Ensuring credentials and certificates are authentic and in line with official rulebooks is necessary prior to final appointment.

How to Check MPESB Result & Scorecard

  1. Visit the official portal of Madhya Pradesh Employees Selection Board at esb.mp.gov.in.
  2. Locate and click on the link titled "Result - Group-1 (Sub Group -2) Combined Recruitment Test - 2025" under the latest updates section.
  3. Enter your 13-digit Application Number or Roll Number along with your Date of Birth (DD/MM/YYYY).
  4. Enter the First 2 Letters of Mother Name + Last 4 Digits of Aadhaar Number if prompted as TAC code.
  5. Click on 'Search' or 'Submit' to display your detailed MPESB scorecard on screen.
  6. Check your marks, normalized score, category rank, and qualification status.
  7. Download and print a hard copy of your result for Document Verification requirements.

Details Mentioned in MPESB Scorecard

  • Candidate Full Name and Roll Number
  • Application Registration ID / Form Number
  • Father's Name & Candidate Category
  • Gender and MP Domicile Status
  • Raw Marks obtained in exam paper
  • Normalized Final Marks
  • Provisional Merit List / Waiting List Status
  • Important instructions for Document Verification

Cut Off Marks & Merit List Details

MPESB releases category-wise cutoff marks along with the final scorecard and press note on its portal. Factors such as overall paper difficulty, number of candidates appearing, normalized scores, and total post breakdown determine the category cutoff.

Candidates can check the post-wise and category-wise cut-off tables directly within the official Result PDF released on 03 August 2026 on esb.mp.gov.in.

What After Result: Next Stage

Candidates who obtain qualifying marks and feature in the provisional merit list will be invited for Document Verification (DV). The concerned Madhya Pradesh state government departments will publish the DV date sheet, reporting centers, and venue instructions separately.

Documents Required for Next Stage (DV)

  • Original Printed Application Form and Admit Card copy
  • Official MPESB Scorecard Printout
  • Class 10th High School Certificate (as Proof of Date of Birth)
  • Master’s Degree / Qualification Certificates in related trade/branch
  • Valid Madhya Pradesh Domicile Certificate (if applicable)
  • Caste Certificate for MP SC/ST/OBC Category candidates
  • Aadhaar Card or Photo ID proof
  • Recent Passport size colored photographs
