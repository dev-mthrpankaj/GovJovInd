About This Result

The Reserve Bank of India (RBI) conducted the Phase II (Main) examination for the recruitment of Officers in Grade 'B' (General/DEPR/DSIM) on 25 and 26 July 2026. The examination was held to fill a total of 60 vacancies across various departments within the RBI.

Based on the performance in the Phase II written examination, RBI has released the list of candidates shortlisted for the final phase, which is the Personality Test / Interview. The result has been published in PDF format containing the roll numbers of the successful candidates.

Shortlisted candidates are advised to prepare for the upcoming interview round and download their scorecards from the official portal. Detailed instructions regarding the interview schedule and venue will be communicated by the RBI shortly.

How to Check RBI Grade B Phase 2 Result

  1. Visit the official RBI careers portal at opportunities.rbi.org.in.
  2. Navigate to the "Current Vacancies" section and click on "Results".
  3. Click on the link for "Result of Phase-II Examination for Recruitment of Officers in Grade 'B' - 2026".
  4. The result PDF containing the roll numbers of shortlisted candidates will open.
  5. Use the search function (Ctrl + F) to locate your Roll Number carefully.
  6. Download and print the result document for future reference during the interview process.

Result Details Mentioned

  • Candidate Roll Number (in the shortlist PDF)
  • Qualifying status for Interview
  • Category-wise marks/scorecard (via login portal)
  • Instructions regarding document submission for the Interview

Cut Off / Merit List

The official cut-off marks for the Phase II examination and the final merit list will be published by the Reserve Bank of India after the completion of the entire recruitment process (post-interview stage). Candidates can currently access their individual scorecards via the official login portal provided by RBI.

Note: Official cutoff will be updated after release.

What After Result

Candidates whose roll numbers appear in the Phase II result PDF have been provisionally shortlisted for the final stage of the recruitment process:

  • Interview: A comprehensive Personality Test / Interview will be conducted by the RBI Selection Board.
  • Document Submission: Shortlisted candidates will be required to submit copies of their eligibility documents and bio-data before the interview.
  • Final Selection: The final merit list will be prepared based on the aggregate marks obtained in the Phase II examination and the Interview.

Documents Required for Next Stage

  • Printed copy of the RBI Grade B Application Form / DAF
  • Admit Card / Call Letter for the Interview
  • Result / Scorecard printout
  • Valid Government Photo ID Proof (Aadhaar, PAN, Voter ID, etc.)
  • Educational Certificates and Marksheets (Graduation/Post-Graduation)
  • Caste / Category Certificate (if applicable, in the prescribed central format)
  • No Objection Certificate (NOC) for currently employed candidates
  • Passport size photographs

Frequently Asked Questions

Is RBI Grade B Phase 2 Result 2026 released?

Yes, the Reserve Bank of India (RBI) released the Phase II result for the Officer Grade B examination on August 22, 2026.

How can I download the RBI Grade B Phase 2 Result PDF?

You can download the final merit list PDF from the official website rbi.org.in or through the direct link provided in the important links section.

What is the selection process for RBI Grade B 2026?

The selection process consists of Phase 1 (Preliminary Exam), Phase 2 (Main Exam), and an Interview.

What is the eligibility for RBI Grade B 2026?

Candidates must have a graduation or post-graduation degree from a recognized university. General requirement is minimum 60% marks in Graduation.

What is the salary of an RBI Grade B Officer?

The initial salary of an RBI Grade B Officer ranges from approximately ₹55,000 to ₹80,000 per month, which can increase with allowances and benefits.

What is the age limit for RBI Grade B 2026?

The general age limit is 21 to 30 years. Age relaxation is applicable for reserved categories as per government rules.

When will the RBI Grade B Scorecard be released?

The scorecard/marks portal link has been made available by RBI on their official website. Candidates can check it using their login credentials.

What is the next stage after the Phase 2 result?

Candidates shortlisted in the Phase 2 exam will be called for the final Personality Test / Interview round.
