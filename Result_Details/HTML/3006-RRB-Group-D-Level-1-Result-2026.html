About This Result

RRB Group D Level 1 Result 2026 has been released for CEN 08/2024 in region-wise PDF format. The result PDF contains roll numbers of candidates provisionally shortlisted for the Physical Efficiency Test. The roll numbers are arranged in ascending order and are not shown in merit order.

Candidates can also check their percentile score and normalized marks through the scorecard login link provided on official RRB websites. The next stage for most shortlisted candidates is PET, while PwBD, ExSM and CCAA candidates are exempted from PET as per the official result notices.

All PET, document verification, medical examination and panel publication updates will be handled by the concerned Railway Recruitment Cell. Therefore, candidates should use this page only as a quick reference and confirm every detail from their regional RRB/RRC website.

How to Check RRB Group D Result 2026

  1. Visit the official website of your regional Railway Recruitment Board.
  2. Open the CEN 08/2024 Level 1 result, PET shortlist or merit list link.
  3. Download the result PDF for your RRB region.
  4. Use Ctrl+F or the search option to find your 16-digit roll number.
  5. For marks and scorecard, open the scorecard login link and enter registration number with date of birth in DDMMYYYY format.
  6. Save the PDF and scorecard, and keep checking the concerned RRC website for PET e-call letter and schedule.

What After RRB Group D Result?

Candidates shortlisted in the CBT will have to appear in the Physical Efficiency Test. PET is qualifying in nature, and the detailed schedule, venue, e-call letter and instructions will be issued by the concerned Railway Recruitment Cell.

After PET, eligible candidates will be called for Document Verification and Medical Examination. Final panel publication will depend on merit, vacancy position, document verification, medical fitness and all official recruitment rules.

Documents Required for Next Stage

  • RRB Group D CBT admit card and PET e-call letter.
  • Online application printout and scorecard, if required.
  • Valid photo ID proof such as Aadhaar, voter ID, PAN card or driving licence.
  • Class 10th certificate, ITI/NAC or other required educational certificates.
  • Caste/category certificate for SC, ST, OBC-NCL or EWS candidates, if applicable.
  • PwBD, Ex-Servicemen or CCAA documents, if applicable.
  • Domicile, income, apprenticeship, experience or other supporting certificates as mentioned in official notice.
  • Recent passport-size photographs and self-attested photocopies of all required documents.

Frequently Asked Questions

Is RRB Group D Level 1 Result 2026 declared?

Yes. The RRB Group D Level 1 CBT result for CEN 08/2024 has been released zone-wise from 1 July 2026 for available RRB regions.

Which RRB regions are included on this page?

This page includes all 21 RRB regions: Ahmedabad, Ajmer, Bengaluru, Bhopal, Bhubaneswar, Bilaspur, Chandigarh, Chennai, Gorakhpur, Guwahati, Jammu-Srinagar, Kolkata, Malda, Mumbai, Muzaffarpur, Patna, Prayagraj, Ranchi, Secunderabad, Siliguri and Thiruvananthapuram.

What is the next stage after RRB Group D CBT result?

The next stage is Physical Efficiency Test (PET). After PET, candidates will proceed to Document Verification, Medical Examination and panel publication as per the concerned RRC.

How can I check my roll number in the result PDF?

Open the result PDF of your RRB region and use Ctrl+F or the Find option to search your 16-digit roll number.

How can candidates check marks and scorecard?

Candidates can log in through the scorecard link on official RRB websites using registration number and date of birth to view percentile score and normalized marks.

What is the RRB Group D scorecard availability period?

As per the result notices, the scorecard link is scheduled to be live on or before 1 July 2026 at 18:00 Hrs and available up to 16 July 2026 at 23:55 Hrs.

Are PwBD, ExSM and CCAA candidates included in the PET roll number list?

No. PwBD, ExSM and CCAA candidates are exempted from PET, so their roll numbers are generally not included in the PET shortlisted roll number list.

Where will PET schedule be released?

PET schedule and PET e-call letter updates will be released by the concerned Railway Recruitment Cell and may also be communicated through SMS or email.

Is the cut-off given in raw marks or normalized marks?

The cut-off table uses normalized cut-off marks for candidates shortlisted for PET wherever official/web-verified values are available.

Is GovJobUpdates an official railway website?

No. GovJobUpdates is an independent information portal. Candidates must verify all result, cutoff and next-stage instructions from official RRB/RRC websites.
