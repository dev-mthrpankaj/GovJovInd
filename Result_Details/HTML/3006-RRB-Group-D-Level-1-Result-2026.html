About This Result

RRB Group D Level 1 Result 2026 has been prepared on the basis of candidates' performance in the Computer Based Test conducted for various Level 1 posts under CEN 08/2024. The official result PDFs generally contain 16-digit roll numbers of candidates provisionally shortlisted for Physical Efficiency Test.

The roll numbers in the merit list are arranged in ascending order and are not shown in merit order. Candidates can also view percentile score and normalized marks through the scorecard link on official RRB websites by entering registration number and date of birth.

After the CBT result, the concerned Railway Recruitment Cell will handle PET, document verification, medical examination and panel publication. Therefore, candidates should follow both their regional RRB website and the concerned RRC website for the next-stage schedule and e-call letter.

How to Check RRB Group D Result

  1. Visit the official website of your regional RRB or the unified RRB portal.
  2. Open the CEN 08/2024 Level 1 result notice or result PDF link.
  3. Download the PET shortlisted candidates PDF or merit list.
  4. Use Ctrl+F or the search option and enter your 16-digit roll number.
  5. For scorecard and normalized marks, open the scorecard login link and enter registration number with date of birth in DDMMYYYY format.
  6. Save the PDF, scorecard and next-stage instructions for PET, document verification and medical examination.

Result Details Mentioned

  • RRB name and CEN 08/2024 Level 1 result heading.
  • 16-digit roll numbers of candidates provisionally shortlisted for PET.
  • Statement that roll numbers are arranged in ascending order, not merit order.
  • Total number of candidates shortlisted, wherever mentioned in the official PDF.
  • Scorecard login instructions for percentile score and normalized marks.
  • Official cut-off marks, wherever a separate official cut-off page/PDF is available.
  • Next-stage instructions for PET, document verification, medical examination and panel publication.

What After RRB Group D Result?

Shortlisted candidates will be called for Physical Efficiency Test. PET is the next qualifying stage for Level 1 posts, and the schedule will be published by the concerned Railway Recruitment Cell. Candidates may also receive SMS or email at their registered contact details for PET e-call letter information.

After PET, further stages include document verification, medical examination and panel publication. The candidature remains provisional at all stages and can be cancelled if eligibility, application data or document verification does not match official requirements.

Documents Required for Next Stage

  • RRB Group D CBT admit card and result/scorecard printout.
  • Application form or registration details, if asked by RRC.
  • Original photo identity proof such as Aadhaar card, voter ID, PAN card or passport.
  • Educational qualification certificates and mark sheets.
  • Caste/category certificate, EWS certificate, PwBD certificate or ExSM documents, if applicable.
  • Domicile or residence certificate, if required by official instructions.
  • Recent passport-size photographs.
  • Course Completed Act Apprentice documents, experience or skill certificates, if applicable.
  • Any other document specifically mentioned in the PET/DV e-call letter.

Frequently Asked Questions

Is RRB Group D Level 1 Result 2026 declared?

Yes. RRB Group D Level 1 CBT result for CEN 08/2024 has been released region-wise from 1 July 2026. Candidates should check their own regional RRB website for the official PDF and scorecard link.

What is the next stage after RRB Group D CBT result?

The next stage is Physical Efficiency Test (PET). After PET, shortlisted candidates move to document verification, medical examination and panel publication as per the concerned RRC instructions.

How can I search my roll number in RRB Group D result PDF?

Open the result PDF of your RRB region and use Ctrl+F or the search option to find your 16-digit roll number. Roll numbers in the list are arranged in ascending order, not in merit order.

Where can I check RRB Group D scorecard and normalized marks?

Candidates can use the scorecard login link provided on official RRB websites and enter registration number with date of birth in DDMMYYYY format.

What is the RRB Group D scorecard availability period?

The official notices state that the scorecard link will be live on or before 1 July 2026 at 18:00 Hrs and available up to 16 July 2026 at 23:55 Hrs.

Are PwBD, ExSM and CCAA candidates included in PET roll number list?

PwBD, ExSM and Course Completed Act Apprentice candidates are exempted from PET, so their roll numbers are not included in the PET shortlisted roll number list. Their lists are to be published by the respective RRCs.

Where will PET schedule and e-call letter be released?

PET schedule and PET e-call letter updates will be published by the concerned Railway Recruitment Cell. SMS and email may also be sent to shortlisted candidates.

Is the RRB Group D cut-off in raw marks or normalized marks?

The official cut-off table is described as normalized cut-off marks of candidates shortlisted for Physical Efficiency Test wherever the official cutoff page is available.

Are all RRB result links added on this page?

Yes. This page lists all 21 regional RRB websites and their concerned RRC or railway source so candidates can check official result and PET updates.

Is GovJobUpdates an official railway website?

No. GovJobUpdates is an independent information portal. Candidates must verify all result, cutoff, scorecard and next-stage details from official RRB and RRC websites.
