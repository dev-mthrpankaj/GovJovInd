About This Result

The Uttar Pradesh Education Service Selection Commission (UPESSC) successfully conducted the UPTET 2026 examination for Primary and Junior Levels between 02 and 04 July 2026. The result for this state-level teacher eligibility test is highly anticipated and will be published shortly.

The upcoming result will include the candidates' scorecards and qualifying status based on the official cutoff marks. UPTET is an eligibility exam, and candidates who secure the minimum qualifying marks will be awarded the UPTET Certificate, making them eligible for upcoming teacher recruitment drives in UP schools.

Candidates should keep their Application Number, Roll Number, and Date of Birth ready to access their scorecards. It is advised to follow the official UPESSC website for the exact date and time of the result link activation.

How to Check UPTET Result

  1. Visit the official UPESSC website at upessc.up.gov.in.
  2. Click on the "Results" section or search for the "UPTET 2026 Result" link on the homepage.
  3. Open the result link and you will be redirected to the candidate login portal.
  4. Enter your Registration Number / Roll Number and Date of Birth / Password carefully.
  5. Fill in the CAPTCHA code and click submit.
  6. Download your scorecard/result and save a printout for future recruitment and certificate verification.

Result Details Mentioned

Once you download your UPTET Scorecard, verify the following details carefully:

  • Candidate Name and Roll Number
  • Registration Number
  • Father's / Mother's Name
  • Category (Gen/OBC/SC/ST)
  • Subject opted (for Junior level)
  • Marks obtained in each section
  • Total Marks Scored
  • Qualifying Status (Qualified / Not Qualified)
  • Remarks (if any)

Cut Off Marks / Qualifying Criteria

The UPTET examination has pre-determined minimum qualifying marks based on the candidate's category. Candidates must score equal to or above these marks out of a total of 150 to qualify.

What After Result

The UPTET is an eligibility test and does not guarantee employment. Candidates who successfully clear the exam will experience the following next steps:

  • Certificate Issuance: Qualified candidates will receive the UPTET Eligibility Certificate. Historically, these are distributed through DIETs (District Institutes of Education and Training).
  • Validity: The UPTET certificate is generally valid for a lifetime.
  • Teacher Recruitment: Candidates can use this certificate to apply for upcoming Super TET (Primary Teacher Recruitment) or Junior High School Teacher vacancies released by the UP Government.

Documents Required for Next Stage

When collecting the UPTET Eligibility Certificate from the respective DIET, candidates will need to present certain documents:

  • UPTET 2026 Admit Card (Original/Printout)
  • Printed copy of UPTET Result/Scorecard
  • Original Photo ID proof (Aadhaar Card, Voter ID, PAN Card, Passport)
  • Educational and Training Certificates (B.Ed/D.El.Ed Marksheets)
  • Caste/Category Certificate (if applicable)
  • Passport size photographs matching the application form

Frequently Asked Questions

Is UPTET Result 2026 released?

No, the UPTET Result 2026 is expected to be released soon by the Uttar Pradesh Education Service Selection Commission (UPESSC).

What is the expected result date for UPTET 2026?

The exact result date is yet to be notified. Candidates should keep checking the official website for updates.

Where can I check the UPTET Result 2026?

Once released, you can check your UPTET Result 2026 on the official UPESSC website at upessc.up.gov.in.

How to search my roll number in the result?

You can log in to the official result portal using your Registration Number, Roll Number, and Password/Date of Birth to view your scorecard.

Is the UPTET scorecard released?
