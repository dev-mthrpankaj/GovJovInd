About This Result

UPSSSC Technical Assistant Group C Eligibility Result 2026 is a shortlisting/eligibility status update for candidates who applied under Advertisement No. 06-Exam/2026. This update helps candidates confirm whether they can move ahead for the mains examination stage of the Agriculture Technical Assistant recruitment process.

The recruitment is for 2759 Technical Assistant Group C / Agriculture Technical Assistant posts. The eligibility result is not the final selection result. Candidates who are shown eligible should continue checking the UPSSSC portal for mains exam date, admit card, exam city, exam instructions and any official notice related to the next stage.

Candidates should rely on the official UPSSSC result page and notice because final eligibility, rejection, shortlisting status, exam permission and later document verification instructions are decided only by the Commission.

How to Check UPSSSC Technical Assistant Eligibility Result

  1. Visit the official UPSSSC website at upsssc.gov.in.
  2. Open the latest result/notice section and find the Technical Assistant Group C / AGTA Eligibility Result 2026 link.
  3. Enter registration number, date of birth and captcha/verification details if the portal asks for login.
  4. Download the result PDF, eligibility notice or individual status page from the official portal.
  5. Search your registration number/roll number carefully if a PDF list is provided.
  6. Save and print the result/status page for the mains exam and future document verification use.

What After Result

After the eligibility result, eligible candidates should prepare for the mains written examination. UPSSSC will release the exam date, admit card and exam instructions separately on its official website.

  • Check the official website regularly for mains exam date and admit card notice.
  • Prepare according to the official syllabus and exam pattern.
  • Keep PET 2025 details and application information ready for login.
  • After the written exam, UPSSSC may release answer key, result, cutoff and document verification instructions as per the recruitment process.
  • Final selection will depend on the official selection rules, written exam performance, document verification and Commission instructions.

Documents Required for Next Stage

  • UPSSSC Technical Assistant application form printout.
  • UPSSSC PET 2025 scorecard / registration details.
  • Mains exam admit card when released.
  • Valid photo ID proof such as Aadhaar card, voter ID, PAN card or other accepted ID.
  • Educational qualification certificates and marksheets related to Agriculture / Horticulture / Forestry / Agricultural Engineering or equivalent qualification.
  • Caste/category certificate, if applicable.
  • Domicile certificate, if required by official instructions.
  • Recent passport-size photographs.
  • NCC ‘B’ certificate or Territorial Army service certificate, if applicable.
  • Any other document mentioned in the official UPSSSC notice or admit card.
