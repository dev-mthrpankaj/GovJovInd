About This Result

The Bihar Police Subordinate Services Commission (BPSSC) successfully conducted the written examination for the recruitment of 122 Havildar Instructor (Adhinayak Anudeshak) positions under Advertisement No. 05/2026 on 29 July 2026 across various examination centres in Bihar. Out of the registered applicants, 60,554 candidates took the examination, and 58,446 OMR answer sheets were evaluated.

The commission has declared the shortlisted candidates list based on standard reservation norms and minimum qualifying criteria. Candidates whose roll numbers are included in the official PDF notice are qualified for the next stage of selection, which includes Physical Endurance/Measurement Tests and Document Verification.

All candidates who appeared in the exam are advised to search their roll number using the Ctrl+F search tool inside the official BPSSC result PDF document.

How to Check BPSSC Havildar Instructor Result

  1. Visit the official portal of Bihar Police Subordinate Services Commission at bpssc.bihar.gov.in.
  2. Locate and click on the "Home Guard" or "Notice Board" tab on the homepage.
  3. Click on the link titled "Result of Preliminary Written Examination for the Post of Havildar Instructor (Advt. No. 05/2026)".
  4. The official PDF containing the list of shortlisted candidates will open on your screen.
  5. Press Ctrl + F on desktop or use the find icon on mobile PDF reader to search for your Roll Number.
  6. If your roll number appears in the list, you have qualified for the Physical Test stage.
  7. Save and download a copy of the PDF for future reference.

What After Result: Next Stage (PET / PMT)

Candidates who qualified in the written examination will receive admit cards for the Physical Efficiency Test (PET) and Physical Measurement Test (PMT). The physical standard test requirements are summarized below:

  • Height Standards: Male candidates require 165 cm (160 cm for SC/ST). Female candidates require 155 cm.
  • Chest Measurement (Male only): 81-86 cm (79-84 cm for SC/ST).
  • Running Event: Male: 1.6 KM in 6 Minutes. Female: 1 KM in 5 Minutes.
  • High Jump: Male: Minimum 4 Feet. Female: Minimum 3 Feet.
  • Shot Put (Gola Fek): Male: 16 Pound shot to 16 Feet. Female: 12 Pound shot to 12 Feet.

Documents Required for Verification

  • Printout of Online Application Form & Result PDF page.
  • Written Exam Admit Card & PET Call Letter.
  • Valid Photo ID Proof (Aadhaar Card, Voter ID, PAN, Driving License).
  • Class 10th (Matriculation) Certificate for proof of date of birth.
  • Class 12th (Intermediate) Marksheet and Passing Certificate.
  • Caste / Category Certificate (EWS, OBC/EBC, SC, ST) issued by competent Bihar authority.
  • Domicile Certificate of Bihar state, if claiming reservation benefits.
  • Recent passport-sized photographs.
