About This Result

The State Bank of India (SBI) conducted the online examination on 11 July 2026 to recruit 7,150 Apprentices across various states. The official result and provisional merit list containing the roll numbers of successful candidates were published on 25 August 2026. Out of all the applicants, approximately 6,867 candidates have provisionally qualified for the next stages.

Candidates can download the result PDF from the official SBI portal to search for their roll numbers. Additionally, SBI has activated the login portal where candidates can view their individual scorecards, sectional marks, and the official state/category-wise cut-off.

Those who have successfully qualified the online test will now be called for the Local Language Proficiency Test (if they do not have a 10th or 12th standard mark sheet as proof of having studied the specified local language), followed by Document Verification and Medical Examination.

How to Check SBI Apprentice Result 2026

  1. Visit the official SBI Careers website: sbi.bank.in/web/careers.
  2. Click on the "Current Openings" tab and expand the "Engagement of Apprentices" section.
  3. Click on the link to download the Result PDF or the link to view the Scorecard.
  4. To check the PDF: Open the downloaded file and press Ctrl+F to search for your roll number.
  5. To check the Scorecard: Enter your Registration Number/Roll Number and Password/Date of Birth along with the captcha to log in.
  6. Download and save the result PDF or print your scorecard for future reference.

What After Result (Next Stages)

Provisionally selected candidates whose roll numbers feature in the merit list must prepare for the next steps:

  • Local Language Proficiency Test (LPT): Candidates must prove proficiency (reading, writing, speaking, and understanding) in the local language of the applied state. Candidates producing a 10th or 12th standard mark sheet indicating they have studied the language will be exempted.
  • Document Verification (DV): Candidates must produce all original documents for verification at the assigned venue.
  • Medical Examination: Final selection is contingent upon meeting the bank’s medical fitness standards.

Documents Required for Next Stage

  • System-generated online application form printout
  • SBI Apprentice Result / Scorecard printout
  • Admit Card / Call Letter
  • Original 10th and 12th Mark sheets / Certificates (Proof of Date of Birth and Local Language)
  • Graduation Degree / Mark sheets from a recognized university
  • Valid Government Photo ID proof (Aadhaar, PAN, Voter ID, Passport)
  • Category / Caste Certificate (SC/ST/OBC/EWS) in the prescribed format
  • PwBD Certificate (if applicable)
  • Recent passport-size photographs
