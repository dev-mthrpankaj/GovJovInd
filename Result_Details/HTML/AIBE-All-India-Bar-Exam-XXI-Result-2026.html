About This Result

The Bar Council of India successfully organized the All India Bar Examination XXI (AIBE 21th) on 07 June 2026. The examination is mandatory for law graduates aiming to get the Certificate of Practice (COP) to practice law in Indian courts. Following the completion of the objection phase, the final answer key was deployed on 10 July 2026.

This eligibility result verifies whether a candidate has scored the minimum qualifying marks mandated by the council. Candidates who successfully qualify for the examination will be granted the Certificate of Practice by their respective State Bar Councils in due course.

Candidates can access their personal digital scorecard through the authenticated portal. Ensure all information matches your official enrollment criteria to prevent documentation discrepancies.

Frequently Asked Questions

Is AIBE All India Bar Exam XXI Result 2026 declared?

Yes. The Bar Council of India declared the AIBE XXI eligibility result officially on 18 July 2026.

Where can I view my result sheet?

You can verify your marks and pass criteria on the official web authentication portal located at aibe.digivarsity.online.

What are the minimum qualifying scores?

General and OBC candidates must achieve a minimum of 45% marks, while SC, ST, and PH candidates need 40% marks to qualify.

When was the AIBE XXI final key published?

The conclusive Final Answer Key PDF was issued by the council on 10 July 2026.

What should I do if the result login page is not opening?

Due to high server loads, the page might face downtime. Refresh after a few minutes or verify via the backup notifications on allindiabarexamination.com.

Will BCI issue a scorecard showing individual rank?

No. The exam determines certification status. Your profile will display either a "Passed" or "Failed" remarks value without rank profiling.
