About This Result

The Uttar Pradesh Education Service Selection Commission (UPESSC), formerly evaluated via UPSESSB, has completed the long-anticipated assessment for Post Graduate Teacher (PGT) under Advt No. 02/2022. The written exam was conducted across multiple test venues on May 9 and 10, 2026, subsequent to which a total of 2,253 candidates were declared qualified to face the interview stage.

The personality tests and interviews were seamlessly arranged at the Commission’s headquarters in Prayagraj from June 15 to June 27, 2026. This newly uploaded result constitutes the absolute compiled list based on standard criteria: Written Exam score (out of 425), Interview marks (out of 50), and applicable special educational weights (up to 25 marks).

Alongside subject-wise select merit panels, the commission has activated individual score links and subject cut-offs to maximize transparency. The next procedural track involves state college choice mapping and administrative verification updates.

How to Check UP PGT Final Result & Marks

  1. Navigate to the official portal page at upessc.up.gov.in.
  2. Locate the latest notification grid referencing "PGT Advt 02/2022 Final Selection Lists".
  3. Click the subject name relevant to your profile application to prompt the standard merit PDF viewer.
  4. Use your mobile viewer tool or standard computer command (Ctrl + F) to look up your 10-digit roll number.
  5. To verify absolute specific split marks, click on the official login server portal.
  6. Enter your unique Roll Number along with your exact Date of Birth as your security password.
  7. Review the overall evaluation marks out of 500 and print out the summary page for next-stage storage.

Details Mentioned on Scorecard Panel

  • Candidate’s Roll Number: Unique 10-digit primary identity assigned.
  • Candidate’s Full Name & Parentage: Matches basic matriculation board sheets.
  • Applied Subject: One of the 18 specific discipline designations.
  • Category Stream: UR, OBC, SC, ST, EWS along with horizontal sub-groups.
  • Written Score Breakdown: Out of the total 425 ceiling.
  • Interview Points Obtained: Marked out of the standard 50 marks.
  • Weightage Marks: Special premium credits awarded for Ph.D., M.Ed., or B.Ed. qualifications (Max 25).
  • Selection Status: Clear indication whether selected in provisional panel or waitlist.

What to Do Post Selection / Next Process

Candidates finding their name or roll numbers inside the main category sheets are deemed provisionally selected. The commission will soon roll out the choice mapping portal where selected candidates must indicate preferences for listed secondary educational institutions across Uttar Pradesh. Final appointment sheets and counseling letters will then be administered locally. Keep your original submission certificates safe for subsequent joining formalities.

Documents Required for School Allocation / Joining

  • Copy of the primary online registration application form.
  • Original identity documentation papers (Aadhaar Card, PAN, or Passport).
  • Academic portfolio items including post-graduation degree and mark sheets.
  • Professional degree credentials such as B.Ed. or M.Ed. certificates (if weightage claimed).
  • Category, caste validity, or EWS endorsement certifications issued by competitive local authorities.
  • Domicile validity verification sheets for candidates claiming reservation perks.
  • Character statements signed by reputable gazetted personnel.

    Frequently Asked Questions

    Is the UPESSB PGT Final Result 2026 declared?

    Yes, the Uttar Pradesh Education Service Selection Commission (UPESSC) officially uploaded the final merit panels on July 7, 2026.

    How many subjects are covered under this result notice?

    A total of 18 subjects are covered, including Hindi, English, Physics, Chemistry, Biology, History, Mathematics, and others.

    Where can I look up my absolute scored marks?

    Candidates can access the candidate login server at upessc.net using their unique 10-digit roll number and date of birth.

    What is the next phase after the final selection panel release?

    The commission will announce dates for online institutional preference choice mapping, following which final college allocation orders will be issued.

    What are the total maximum criteria marks for calculation?

    The final merit score is derived out of 500 marks total (Written Exam: 425, Interview: 50, special educational qualification weightage: 25).

    Are subject cut-off documents available?

    Yes, the commission has uploaded the final cut-off thresholds for all 18 subjects alongside the select lists on their official web portal.
