Complete context

About This Recruitment

RRB JE Junior Engineer Recruitment 2026 is a Railway Recruitment Board (RRB) drive to fill 3993 Junior Engineer posts across Indian Railways. The official notification was published under CEN 04/2026 in August 2026, and the online application window remained open from 14 August 2026 to 13 September 2026.

The Computer Based Test (CBT) is scheduled for 27 and 28 October 2026. The admit card has been released on the official RRB portal. Candidates must download the admit card and check their exam city, date, shift and reporting time carefully.

Basic eligibility is an Engineering Diploma / Degree / B.Sc in Engineering in the relevant trade or branch from a recognised institution. The age limit is 18 to 33 years as on 01 January 2027, with relaxation for reserved categories as per RRB rules.

What to do at the current stage

How to Download RRB JE CBT Admit Card

  1. Open the official RRB admit card portal from Important Links.
  2. Log in using your Registration Number and Date of Birth / Password.
  3. Download the CBT admit card PDF for RRB JE Junior Engineer 2026.
  4. Check your name, roll number, exam date, shift, reporting time and venue.
  5. Take a clear colour printout and carry it to the exam centre.
  6. Read all instructions printed on the admit card carefully.

Original Application Process

Preserved for recruitment history and reference after the application stage closes.

  1. Visit the official RRB website at rrbapply.gov.in.
  2. Read the official CEN 04/2026 notification carefully.
  3. Register on the RRB portal with valid email ID and mobile number.
  4. Fill the online application form with accurate personal and educational details.
  5. Upload photograph, signature and required documents.
  6. Pay the applicable examination fee online.
  7. Submit the form and save the confirmation page for future reference.
