Complete context

About This Recruitment

RRB JE Junior Engineer Recruitment 2026 (CEN No. 04/2026) is a Railway Recruitment Board drive to fill 3,993 vacancies for Junior Engineer (JE), Depot Material Superintendent (DMS) and Chemical & Metallurgical Assistant (CMA) posts across all RRB zones and Railway Production Units. The detailed notification was released on 13 August 2026, and the online application window remained open from 14 August 2026 to 13 September 2026.

The Computer Based Test (CBT-1) is the first stage of the selection process. The Exam City Intimation Slip for CBT-1 was released on 17 October 2026, and the CBT-1 examination is scheduled for 27 and 28 October 2026 in multiple shifts. The admit card is expected to be released closer to the exam date. Candidates must carry the exam city slip and a valid photo ID to the exam centre; however, the exam city slip is not the admit card and entry will be allowed only with the actual admit card.

Basic eligibility is a 3-year Engineering Diploma or B.E./B.Tech in a relevant branch, or B.Sc./PGDCA/BCA for specific posts as detailed in Annexure-A of the notification. The age limit is 18 to 33 years as on 01 January 2027, with upper age relaxation of 5 years for SC/ST and 3 years for OBC-NCL candidates. The pay level is Level-6 of the 7th CPC matrix, with an initial basic pay of Rs. 35,400 per month plus railway allowances.

The selection process comprises CBT-1, CBT-2, Document Verification (DV) and Medical Examination. This permanent page will be updated with CBT-2 admit card, result, DV notice, medical schedule and final merit list without changing the URL.

Eligibility Criteria

Age Limit

18 to 33 years as on 01 January 2027.

Upper age relaxation: 5 years for SC/ST, 3 years for OBC-NCL. Additional relaxation for Ex-Servicemen, PwBD and other categories as per Railway Board rules.

Education

3-year Engineering Diploma or B.E./B.Tech in a relevant branch, or B.Sc./PGDCA/BCA for specific posts as detailed in Annexure-A of CEN 04/2026.

Candidates awaiting final results of the prescribed qualification are not eligible.

Nationality / Other

Indian citizens and other categories as specified in the CEN. Candidates must meet the prescribed medical standards for the opted post.

Refer to official CEN for detailed post-specific conditions.

What to do at the current stage

How to Download RRB JE Exam City Slip

  1. Open the official RRB exam city slip portal from Important Links.
  2. Log in using your Registration Number and Date of Birth.
  3. Enter the captcha code shown on the screen.
  4. Download your CBT-1 Exam City Intimation Slip PDF.
  5. Check your allotted exam city, exam date and shift timing carefully.
  6. Plan your travel in advance. The city slip is not the admit card; carry the actual admit card and a valid photo ID to the exam centre.

Original Application Process

Preserved for recruitment history and reference after the application stage closes.

  1. Visit the official RRB application portal at rrbapply.gov.in.
  2. Read the CEN 04/2026 notification carefully.
  3. Create an account with a valid email ID and mobile number.
  4. Fill the online application form with accurate personal and educational details.
  5. Upload photograph, signature and required documents.
  6. Pay the applicable examination fee online.
  7. Submit the form and save the confirmation page for future reference.
