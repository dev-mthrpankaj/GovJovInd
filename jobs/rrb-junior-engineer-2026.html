About This Recruitment

RRB Junior Engineer JE Recruitment 2026 is a major central government recruitment drive conducted by the Railway Recruitment Boards (RRBs) under the Ministry of Railways. The recruitment is being conducted under CEN No. 04/2026 for a total of 3,993 vacancies across various technical posts including Junior Engineer (JE), Depot Material Superintendent (DMS), and Chemical & Metallurgical Assistant (CMA).

The online application process was conducted from 14 August 2026 to 13 September 2026, with a correction window open from 16 to 25 September 2026. The recruitment has now progressed to the examination stage, with the CBT 1 exam scheduled for 27 and 28 October 2026 (revised schedule).

Candidates who applied for RRB JE 2026 should now focus on downloading the exam city intimation slip (available approximately 10 days before the exam) and the e-call letter/admit card (available 4 days before the exam). This page will continue to carry every official update — from admit card release to final result and joining — on the same permanent URL.

Eligibility Criteria

Age Limit

18 to 33 years as on 01 January 2027.

Age relaxation: SC/ST – 5 years, OBC-NCL – 3 years, PwBD – 10 years, Ex-Servicemen – as per rules.

Education

Engineering Diploma or Degree in relevant discipline (Civil, Electrical, Mechanical, Electronics, etc.).

For JE (IT) and CMA posts, specific qualifications apply. Refer to CEN 04/2026 notification.

Nationality / Other

Indian nationals fulfilling post-specific eligibility conditions. Candidates must meet medical fitness standards.

Refer to official notification for detailed conditions.
