Complete context

About This Recruitment

RRB JE Junior Engineer Recruitment 2026 is a Railway Recruitment Board recruitment drive under Centralized Employment Notice (CEN) No. 04/2026 to fill 3993 posts of Junior Engineer (JE), Depot Material Superintendent (DMS) and Chemical & Metallurgical Assistant (CMA) across Indian Railways. The official detailed notification was released on 13 August 2026, and the online application window remained open from 14 August 2026 to 13 September 2026.

The Computer Based Test (CBT-1) is scheduled for 27-28 October 2026 as per the revised exam schedule. The exam city intimation slip is expected to be released about 10 days before the exam, and the E-Call Letter (admit card) will be available about 4 days before the exam date. Candidates who qualify CBT-1 will proceed to CBT-2, followed by Document Verification and Medical Examination.

The application status for CEN 04/2026 was released on 7 October 2026. Candidates can check whether their application is provisionally accepted, conditionally accepted, or rejected through the official RRB application portal. SMS and email alerts are also sent to candidates on their registered contact details.

Basic eligibility includes a Diploma or Degree in Engineering in a relevant trade/branch. The age limit is 18 to 33 years as on 01 January 2027, with relaxation for reserved categories as per railway rules. The initial basic pay for RRB JE posts is Rs. 35,400 per month under Pay Level 6 of the 7th Pay Commission.
