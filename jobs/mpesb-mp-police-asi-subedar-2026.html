Complete context

About This Recruitment

Madhya Pradesh Employees Selection Board (MPESB) has issued the recruitment notification for 655 Subedar Steno and ASI Ministerial posts in the Madhya Pradesh Police. Candidates who applied for the MP Police ASI Subedar Recruitment 2026 can now download their written examination admit cards.

The online application process was held from 24 September 2026 to 08 October 2026, and an application correction window remained open until 13 October 2026. The written examination has been officially scheduled for November 2026 across various district centres in Madhya Pradesh, including Bhopal, Indore, Jabalpur, Gwalior, Ujjain, and others.

Selection will be based on multiple phases: a Written Examination, a Physical Proficiency Test (PET/PST), Skill/Typing/Stenography tests (as applicable to the post), Document Verification, and a Medical Examination. Candidates must meet the specific minimum physical standard of 162 cm height for males and 152 cm for females.

The current stage of this recruitment lifecycle is the release of the Admit Card. Candidates must print a colour copy of their admit card and bring it along with a valid original photo ID to the exam centre. Regular updates on the answer key, result, and physical test schedules will be updated on this page permanently.
