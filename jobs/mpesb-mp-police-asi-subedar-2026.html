Complete context

About This Recruitment

Madhya Pradesh Employees Selection Board (MPESB) has released the official notification for MP Police Subedar, Stenographer and Assistant Sub-Inspector (Ministerial) Recruitment 2026, covering a total of 655 posts across six post codes. The recruitment is being conducted for various branches of the Madhya Pradesh Police, including the General Branch, Special Branch, Field Unit and Crime Investigation Department.

The online application process began on 24 September 2026 and the last date to apply is 08 October 2026. Candidates can also complete the correction process between 24 September 2026 and 13 October 2026, and pay the examination fee by 08 October 2026. The written examination (Computer Based Test) is scheduled to be conducted on 03 November 2026 across multiple districts of Madhya Pradesh.

The minimum age limit is 18 years, with an upper age limit of 33 years for male candidates and 38 years for female candidates as on 08 October 2026. Age relaxation is applicable for reserved categories as per Madhya Pradesh Government rules. The basic educational qualification is 10+2 (Intermediate) from a recognised board, along with the post-specific computer qualification and, for Subedar posts, a Hindi shorthand (stenography) certification.

The selection process includes a written examination, Physical Efficiency Test (PET/PST), document verification and medical examination.
