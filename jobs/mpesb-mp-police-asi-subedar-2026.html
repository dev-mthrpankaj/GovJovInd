Complete context

About This Recruitment

Madhya Pradesh Employees Selection Board (MPESB) has released the official notification for MP Police Subedar, Stenographer and Assistant Sub-Inspector (Ministerial) Recruitment 2026, covering a total of 655 posts across six post codes. The recruitment is being conducted for various branches of the Madhya Pradesh Police, including the General Branch, Special Branch, Field Unit and Crime Investigation Department.

The online application process began on 24 September 2026 and the last date to apply is 08 October 2026. Candidates can also complete the correction process between 24 September 2026 and 13 October 2026, and pay the examination fee by 08 October 2026. The written examination (Computer Based Test) is scheduled to be conducted on 03 November 2026 across multiple districts of Madhya Pradesh.

The minimum age limit is 18 years, with an upper age limit of 33 years for male candidates and 38 years for female candidates as on 08 October 2026. Age relaxation is applicable for reserved categories as per Madhya Pradesh Government rules. The basic educational qualification is 10+2 (Intermediate) from a recognised board, along with the post-specific computer qualification and, for Subedar posts, a Hindi shorthand (stenography) certification.

The selection process includes a written examination, Physical Efficiency Test (PET/PST), document verification and medical examination. Eligibility Criteria

Age Limit

Minimum 18 years. Maximum 33 years for male candidates and 38 years for female candidates as on 08 October 2026.

Age relaxation is applicable for SC, ST, OBC, PwBD, Ex-Servicemen and other reserved categories as per Madhya Pradesh Government rules.

Education

10+2 (Intermediate) from a recognised board. Post Code 01 & 02 additionally require Hindi Shorthand (Stenography) at 100 WPM. All posts require CPCT with Hindi Typing and one of the following computer qualifications: DOEACC Diploma, 1-year COPA certificate, Modern Office Management course, UGC-recognised Computer Diploma, or Engineering Degree / MCA / BCA / M.Sc (CS / IT) / AICTE-approved Polytechnic Diploma.

Hindi Shorthand certification is mandatory only for Post Code 01 and 02. The computer qualification must be from a government-recognised institution.

Nationality / Other

Indian citizens who fulfil the MP domicile and post-specific eligibility conditions may apply. Refer to the official MPESB notification for the complete eligibility and physical standards details.

What to do at the current stage

How to Apply for MPESB MP Police ASI Subedar 2026

  1. Visit the official MPESB portal at esb.mp.gov.in and open the Subedar, Steno & ASI Ministerial 2026 Apply Online link.
  2. Complete MP Profile Registration and MP Rojgar Panjiyan if you have not registered before.
  3. Register on the application portal using a valid email ID and mobile number.
  4. Log in with your registration ID and password and fill the application form with personal, educational and category details.
  5. Upload scanned photograph, signature and required certificates in the prescribed format.
  6. Pay the application fee online through Debit Card, Credit Card, Net Banking or MP Kiosk.
  7. Review all details carefully, submit the form and download the confirmation page for future reference.
