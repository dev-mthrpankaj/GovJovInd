Complete context

About This Recruitment

National Insurance Company Limited (NICL) is conducting the Administrative Officer (AO) Recruitment 2026 to fill 321 Administrative Officer (Scale I) posts across Generalist and Specialist disciplines. The official notification was released in October 2026, and the online application window remained open from 08 October 2026 to 28 October 2026.

The selection process includes a Preliminary Examination (Phase I), a Main Examination (Phase II), an Interview, Document Verification, and a Medical Examination. The Phase I exam is scheduled for 17 November 2026, and the Phase II exam is scheduled for 22 December 2026. The Prelims Admit Card was released on 10 November 2026.

Eligibility requires a Bachelor's or Master's degree in the relevant discipline with minimum marks (60% for General/OBC/EWS and 55% for SC/ST/PH). The age limit is 21 to 30 years as on 01 October 2026, with relaxation for reserved categories.
