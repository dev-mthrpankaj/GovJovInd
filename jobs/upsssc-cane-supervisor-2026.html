Complete context

About This Recruitment

Uttar Pradesh Subordinate Services Selection Commission (UPSSSC) published Advertisement No. 13/2026 to fill 1182 vacancies for Cane Supervisor (1000 posts) and Cane Supervisor Special (182 posts) across Uttar Pradesh. The online application window ran from 07 July 2026 to 28 July 2026, with the correction window open until 04 August 2026.

UPSSSC released the Cane Supervisor Admit Card 2026 on 25 November 2026 ahead of the Mains Examination scheduled on 29 November 2026. Candidates can download their hall ticket and check exam city details directly from the official UPSSSC portal links provided on this page.

To be eligible, candidates must hold a valid UPSSSC PET 2025 score card, a B.Sc in Agriculture degree from a recognised university or institute in India, and a CCC certificate from DOEACC Society / NIELIT. The age limit is 21 to 40 years as on 28 July 2026.

Selected candidates are appointed in Pay Level-4 with a pay scale of Rs.25,500 to Rs.81,000. The selection process consists of the Mains Written Examination followed by Document Verification. This permanent lifecycle page will be updated with answer key, result and final merit list updates.
