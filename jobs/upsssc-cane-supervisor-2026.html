Complete context

About This Recruitment

Uttar Pradesh Subordinate Services Selection Commission (UPSSSC) is conducting the UPSSSC Cane Supervisor (Ganna Parvekshak) Recruitment 2026 under Advt. No. 13-Exam/2026 to fill 1182 Cane Supervisor posts in the Sugar Industry and Cane Development Department, Uttar Pradesh. The official notification was published on 12 May 2026, online applications remained open from 07 July 2026 to 28 July 2026, and the application correction window closed on 04 August 2026.

UPSSSC declared the PET 2025 based Eligibility / Shortlisting Result on 12 October 2026 and issued the Mains examination fee payment notice on 12 November 2026. On 19 November 2026, UPSSSC released the Exam City Details for shortlisted candidates ahead of the Mains Written Examination scheduled on 29 November 2026 in a single shift from 10:00 AM to 12:00 PM.

Shortlisted candidates should check their allotted examination district through the official UPSSSC portal at upsssc.gov.in and complete the Mains examination fee payment if pending. The Mains Admit Card will be released before 29 November 2026 and must be carried along with a valid original photo ID to the examination centre.

To be eligible for UPSSSC Cane Supervisor (Ganna Parvekshak) Recruitment 2026, candidates must hold a Bachelor's Degree in Agriculture Science from a university established by law in India, a CCC Certificate in Computer Operations awarded by DOEACC Society (NIELIT), and a valid UPSSSC PET 2025 score. The age limit is 21 to 40 years as on 01 July 2026, with upper age relaxation for reserved categories of Uttar Pradesh as per UPSSSC rules.
