Complete context

About This Recruitment

UTET (Uttarakhand Teacher Eligibility Test) 2026 is conducted by the Uttarakhand Board of School Education for candidates aspiring to become teachers in Primary (Class 1 to 5) and Junior (Class 6 to 8) levels in Uttarakhand schools. The official notification was released in July 2026, and the online application window remained open from 14 July 2026 to 09 August 2026 (extended).

The exam was conducted on 29 September 2026 (Sunday). The answer key has now been released, and candidates can check their answers and estimate their scores. The result will be declared as per the official schedule.

Eligibility for Primary level includes Senior Secondary with at least 50% marks and D.El.Ed/B.T.C. or equivalent. For Junior level, candidates need Graduation with B.Ed. or equivalent qualification. A 5% relaxation in qualifying marks is applicable for reserved category candidates.

The application fee is Rs.600 for General/OBC candidates (Paper I or II) and Rs.1000 for both papers. SC/ST/PH candidates pay Rs.300 (Paper I or II) and Rs.500 for both papers.
