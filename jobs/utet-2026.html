About This Recruitment

Uttarakhand Teacher Eligibility Test (UTET) 2026 is a state-level eligibility examination conducted by the Uttarakhand Board of School Education (UBSE) for candidates aspiring to become teachers in primary (Classes I–V) and upper primary (Classes VI–VIII) schools across Uttarakhand. The exam was conducted on 29 September 2026 in two shifts.

This is an eligibility test, not a direct recruitment drive. Qualifying UTET awards a lifetime-valid TET certificate, which is mandatory for applying to teaching positions in government, government-aided and recognised unaided schools in Uttarakhand.

The admit card was released on 22 September 2026. The provisional answer key will be released on ukutet.com as per official schedule, followed by the objection window and result declaration.
