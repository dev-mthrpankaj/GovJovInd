Complete context

About This Recruitment

UTET 2026 (Uttarakhand Teacher Eligibility Test) is a state-level eligibility examination conducted by the Uttarakhand Board of School Education (UBSE). It is not a recruitment examination; it certifies candidates as eligible to apply for teaching posts in primary (Classes I–V) and upper primary (Classes VI–VIII) schools across Uttarakhand. The official notification was released on 14 July 2026, and the online application window remained open from 14 July 2026 to 09 August 2026 (extended from 03 August 2026).

The examination was conducted on 29 September 2026 for both Paper 1 and Paper 2. The provisional answer key was released on 05 October 2026, and the objection window remained open until 15 October 2026. The final answer key and results were declared on 13 November 2026. Candidates who qualify will be awarded the UTET eligibility certificate, which is a mandatory minimum qualification for appointment as a teacher in Uttarakhand schools.

Paper 1 (Primary, Classes I–V) requires Senior Secondary (or equivalent) with at least 50% marks and a 2-year Diploma in Elementary Education (D.El.Ed/B.T.C.) or equivalent qualifications. Paper 2 (Junior, Classes VI–VIII) requires Graduation with a 2-year Diploma in Elementary Education or Graduation with at least 50% marks and B.Ed or equivalent. There is no upper age limit prescribed for UTET 2026. The application fee is Rs.600 for one paper and Rs.1,000 for both papers for General/OBC candidates, while SC/ST/PwD candidates pay Rs.300 for one paper and Rs.500 for both.

Eligibility Criteria

Age Limit

No upper age limit prescribed for UTET 2026. Candidates must meet NCTE and state department rules.

Age relaxation is applicable as per Uttarakhand government rules for reserved categories.

Education

Paper I: Senior Secondary (or equivalent) with at least 50% marks and a 2-year Diploma in Elementary Education (D.El.Ed/B.T.C.) or equivalent. Paper II: Graduation with a 2-year Diploma in Elementary Education or Graduation with at least 50% marks and B.Ed or equivalent.

Both academic qualification and teacher training qualification must be completed from a recognised institution.

Nationality / Other

Indian citizens who fulfil the NCTE and Uttarakhand state eligibility conditions may apply. Refer to the official notification for complete details.

What to do at the current stage

How to Download UTET 2026 Scorecard

  1. Open the official UBSE or UKTET website at ukutet.com or ubse.uk.gov.in.
  2. Go to the 'Departmental Exam/UTET' or 'Result' section.
  3. Select the 'UTET 2026 Result (Paper I & II)' link.
  4. Enter your registration number and password or roll number and date of birth.
  5. Your UTET 2026 scorecard will appear on the screen.
  6. Download the scorecard and final answer key for future use.

Original Application Process

Preserved for recruitment history and reference after the application stage closes.

  1. Visit the official website at ukutet.com.
  2. Click on the UTET 2026 registration link.
  3. Register using a valid email ID and mobile number.
  4. Log in and fill the application form with personal and educational details.
  5. Upload scanned photograph and signature.
  6. Pay the application fee online.
  7. Submit the form and download the confirmation page.
