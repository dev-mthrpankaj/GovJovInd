About This Exam

The Uttarakhand Teacher Eligibility Test (UTET) 2026 is a state-level eligibility examination conducted for candidates who wish to teach in classes 1 to 8 across Uttarakhand. The test is held in two levels — Primary (Class 1 to 5) and Junior (Class 6 to 8).

The online application window ran from 14 July 2026 to 9 August 2026 (extended), with fee payment allowed until 11 August 2026 and a correction window from 12 to 15 August 2026. The UTET 2026 examination is scheduled for 29 September 2026.

Candidates who qualify UTET become eligible for teaching recruitment in Uttarakhand government schools. The admit card will be released on the official UTET portal shortly before the exam date.
