Complete context

About This Recruitment

MPESB MP Police Constable Driver Recruitment 2026 is a Madhya Pradesh Employees Selection Board recruitment drive to fill 1000 Constable Driver posts (Special Forces and District Police Force) in Madhya Pradesh. The official notification (Rulebook No. PCRT_Driver_2026_rulebook_07102026) was published in October 2026, and the online application window will remain open from 15 October 2026 to 29 October 2026.

The Computer Based Test (CBT) is scheduled for 04 January 2027. The selection process includes a written examination, Physical Endurance & Measurement Test (PE & MT), Driving Test, Document Verification and Medical Examination. The physical standards require a minimum height of 168 cm and chest measurement of 81-86 cm (SC candidates 76-81 cm).

Basic eligibility is a 10th class pass under the 10+2 system or Higher Secondary, or equivalent from a recognised board, plus a valid Heavy Motor Vehicle (HMV) driving licence. For ST category candidates, an 8th class pass is sufficient. The age limit is 18 to 33 years as on 29 October 2026, with relaxation for reserved categories as per rules.

Candidates must complete the MP Profile Registration before applying. The application fee is Rs. 760/- for General/Other State candidates and Rs. 410/- for OBC/EWS/SC/ST candidates, with portal charges included. Payment can be made online or through MP Kiosk.
