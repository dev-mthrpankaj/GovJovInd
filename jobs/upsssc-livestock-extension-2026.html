Complete context

About This Recruitment

Uttar Pradesh Subordinate Services Selection Commission (UPSSSC) published Advertisement No. 17-Exam/2026 on 10 August 2026 to recruit 1,251 Livestock Extension Officer (Pashudhan Prasar Adhikari) posts under the Directorate of Animal Husbandry, Uttar Pradesh. The total vacancies comprise 1,248 General Selection posts (UR 500, OBC 345, EWS 124, SC 254, ST 25) and 3 Special Selection posts reserved for OBC.

The online application window remained open from 09 September 2026 to 29 September 2026 with a uniform Rs.25 online processing fee for all categories, followed by application modification and fee reconciliation up to 06 October 2026. On 21 November 2026, UPSSSC declared the Eligibility Result based on UPSSSC PET 2025 shortlisting for the Mains Written Examination.

Shortlisted candidates can check their Eligibility Result status and download the official cut-off notice from upsssc.gov.in using their Candidate Registration Number and Date of Birth. The Mains examination date, admit card release schedule, document verification and final merit list will be announced separately by UPSSSC.

Essential eligibility requires a valid UPSSSC PET 2025 Score Card, 10+2 (Intermediate) from a recognised board, a 2-Year Livestock Extension Diploma from a recognised Veterinary University and knowledge of Hindi in Devanagari script. The age limit is 21 to 40 years as on 01 July 2026, and the post carries Pay Level-5 (Rs.29,200 to Rs.92,300).
