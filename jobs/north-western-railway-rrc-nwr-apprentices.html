Complete context

About This Recruitment

North Western Railway (RRC NWR Jaipur) has released the official notification for recruitment to 2008 Apprentice posts under Advertisement No. 04/2026 (NWR/AA). The online application window will remain open from 07 October 2026 to 06 November 2026. Eligible candidates can apply online through the official RRC Jaipur website.

The recruitment is for Apprentice posts across various trades under the Apprentices Act, 1961. Selection is merit-based and there is no written examination. The merit list will be prepared based on the marks obtained in the Matriculation (10th) examination and the ITI certificate in the relevant trade.

Candidates must have passed Matriculation (10th) from a recognised board and possess an ITI certificate in the relevant trade from a recognised institute. The age limit is 15 to 24 years as on 06 November 2026. Age relaxation is applicable for SC, ST, OBC and other reserved categories as per government rules.

The application fee is Rs.100 for General, OBC and EWS candidates, while SC, ST and PH candidates are exempted from paying any fee. What to do at the current stage

How to Apply for RRC NWR Apprentices 2026

  1. Visit the official RRC Jaipur website at rrcjaipur.in.
  2. Read the RRC NWR Apprentices 2026 notification carefully before applying.
  3. Click on the 'RRC NWR Apprentices 2026 Apply Online' link once activated on 07 October 2026.
  4. Register using a valid email ID and mobile number.
  5. Login using your registration ID and password.
  6. Fill the application form with personal, educational and category details.
  7. Upload scanned documents including photo, signature and ITI certificate.
  8. Pay the application fee through online mode if applicable.
  9. Submit the form and download/print the confirmation page for future reference.
