Complete context

About This Recruitment

IBPS RRB XV 2026 is a Common Recruitment Process (CRP RRBs XV) conducted by the Institute of Banking Personnel Selection for recruitment to Group A Officers (Scale I, II & III) and Group B Office Assistants (Multipurpose) in Regional Rural Banks across India. The official notification was released on 01 September 2026, and the online application window remained open from 01 September 2026 to 27 September 2026 (extended).

A total of 13,757 vacancies have been announced under the revised vacancy list. This includes 8,222 Office Assistant posts, 4,256 Officer Scale-I posts, 1,059 Officer Scale-II posts across various specialisations, and 220 Officer Scale-III posts.

The Officer Scale-I Preliminary Examination is scheduled for 21-22 November 2026, and the admit card for the same was released on 09 November 2026. The Office Assistant Preliminary Examination will be held on 06, 12 and 13 December 2026. The Officer Scale-I Mains and the Officer Scale-II & III Single Examination are scheduled for 20 December 2026, while the Office Assistant Mains will be conducted on 30 January 2027.

Basic eligibility for Office Assistant and Officer Scale-I is a Bachelor's degree in any discipline from a recognised university. Age limits vary by post: 18-28 years for Office Assistant, 18-30 years for Officer Scale-I, and up to 40 years for Officer Scale-III. Age relaxation is applicable for reserved categories as per government rules.
