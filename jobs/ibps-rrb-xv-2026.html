About This Recruitment

IBPS RRB XV Recruitment 2026 is the Common Recruitment Process for Group A Officers (Scale I, II and III) and Group B Office Assistants (Multipurpose) in participating Regional Rural Banks. The recruitment reports 13,706 vacancies across the covered posts.

Online registration, editing of application details and fee payment are open from 1 September to 21 September 2026. Candidates applying in the Officer cadre can apply for only one Officer post among Scale I, Scale II and Scale III, while an eligible candidate may separately apply for Office Assistant.

The official 2026-27 IBPS calendar schedules Officer Scale I prelims for 21-22 November 2026, Office Assistant prelims for 6, 12 and 13 December 2026, the Officer Scale I main and Scale II/III single examination for 20 December 2026, and the Office Assistant main examination for 30 January 2027. This permanent page will continue to carry later call-letter, result, interview and allotment updates.
