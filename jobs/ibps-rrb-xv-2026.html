Complete context

About This Recruitment

IBPS RRB XV Recruitment 2026 is the Common Recruitment Process for Group A Officers (Scale I, II and III) and Group B Office Assistants (Multipurpose) in participating Regional Rural Banks. The recruitment reports 13,706 vacancies across the covered posts.

Online registration, editing of application details and fee payment are open from 1 September to 21 September 2026. Candidates applying in the Officer cadre can apply for only one Officer post among Scale I, Scale II and Scale III, while an eligible candidate may separately apply for Office Assistant.

The official 2026-27 IBPS calendar schedules Officer Scale I prelims for 21-22 November 2026, Office Assistant prelims for 6, 12 and 13 December 2026, the Officer Scale I main and Scale II/III single examination for 20 December 2026, and the Office Assistant main examination for 30 January 2027. This permanent page will continue to carry later call-letter, result, interview and allotment updates.

Eligibility Criteria

Age Limit

As on 01 September 2026: Office Assistant 18-28 years; Officer Scale I 18-30 years; Officer Scale II 21-32 years; Officer Scale III 21-40 years.

Upper-age relaxation applies to eligible reserved and special categories as prescribed in the official CRP RRBs-XV notification.

Education

Office Assistant and Officer Scale I require a bachelor's degree. Officer Scale II and III posts have post-specific qualification, marks and relevant experience requirements.

Check the detailed notification for post-wise qualification, experience and local-language requirements.

Nationality / Other

Candidates must satisfy the nationality/citizenship, local-language and other post-specific eligibility conditions prescribed by IBPS and the participating RRBs.

Refer to official notification for detailed conditions.

What to do at the current stage

How to Apply for IBPS RRB XV

  1. Read the official CRP RRBs-XV notification and confirm the eligibility for your chosen post.
  2. Choose the official application link for Office Assistant (Multipurpose) or Officers Scale I, II & III.
  3. For the Officer cadre, apply for only one post among Scale I, Scale II and Scale III.
  4. Complete registration with an active mobile number and email ID.
  5. Fill personal, educational, category, RRB preference and experience details carefully.
  6. Upload the prescribed photograph, signature, left thumb impression, handwritten declaration and live photograph.
  7. Pay the applicable fee online and submit the application by 21 September 2026.
  8. Save the registration details, submitted form and fee receipt for later stages.
