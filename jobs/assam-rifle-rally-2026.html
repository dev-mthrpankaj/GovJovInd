About This Recruitment

Assam Rifle Rally Recruitment 2026 is a Technical and Tradesman Recruitment Rally conducted by the Directorate General Assam Rifles, Shillong, for 354 Group C vacancies across various technical and tradesman posts. The online application window is open from 28 September 2026 to 28 October 2026, up to 11:59 PM.

This recruitment covers posts such as Warrant Officer (Cipher), Rifleman (Electrical Fitter Signal), Rifleman (Lineman Field), Havildar (Instrument Mechanic), Warrant Officer (Physiotherapist) and several other trades. Eligible male Indian citizens who meet the post-wise educational, age and physical standards can apply online through the official Assam Rifles website.

The selection process includes Physical Standard Test (PST), Physical Efficiency Test (PET), Document Verification, Computer-Based Test (CBT), Trade Skill Test and Detailed Medical Examination (DME). Candidates should keep photo, signature, domicile/PRC, category certificate and other required documents ready before starting the online form.
