Complete context

About This Recruitment

SSC Combined Hindi Translator (CHT) Examination 2025 is a Staff Selection Commission recruitment drive to fill 437 Hindi Translator posts across various central government ministries, departments and organisations. The official notification was published on 05 June 2025, and the online application window remained open from 05 June 2025 to 26 June 2025.

The Computer Based Test (Paper-I) was conducted on 12 August 2025. The Paper-I result was declared on 04 November 2025, in which 3,642 candidates qualified for Paper-II. The final answer key, response sheet and marks were released on 30 December 2025 and remained available until 29 January 2026.

Paper-II (Descriptive Test) was conducted on 14 December 2025. The admit cards for Paper-II were released on 12 December 2025. The next stage is the declaration of the final result and document verification.

Basic eligibility is a Post Graduation degree in Hindi or English with the other language as a compulsory subject, or a Post Graduation degree in any subject with English medium and Hindi as a compulsory subject at degree level, along with a recognised diploma or certificate course in translation or two years of experience. The age limit is 18 to 30 years as on 26 June 2025, with relaxation for reserved categories as per SSC rules.

Eligibility Criteria

Age Limit

18 to 30 years as on 26 June 2025.

Age relaxation applicable for SC, ST, OBC, PwBD, Ex-Servicemen and other reserved categories as per SSC rules.

Education

Post Graduation degree in Hindi or English with the other language as a compulsory subject, or Post Graduation degree in any subject with English medium and Hindi as a compulsory subject at degree level, along with a recognised diploma or certificate course in translation from Hindi to English and vice versa, or two years of experience in translation.

Both the post-graduation degree and the translation diploma/experience are mandatory for eligibility.

Nationality / Other

Indian citizens fulfilling the post-specific eligibility conditions may apply. Refer to the official notification for complete details.

What to do at the current stage

How to Download Final Answer Key & Marks

  1. Open the official SSC login portal from Important Links.
  2. Log in using your Registration Number and Date of Birth.
  3. Click on the link for Combined Hindi Translators Examination 2025 – Paper-I Final Answer Key.
  4. Download the final answer key, response sheet and scorecard PDF.
  5. Take a printout of all documents for your records. The download facility was available until 29 January 2026.

Original Application Process

Preserved for recruitment history and reference after the application stage closes.

  1. Visit the official SSC recruitment website at ssc.gov.in.
  2. Read the official Notice of Advt CHT 2025 carefully.
  3. Register on the SSC portal with valid email ID and mobile number.
  4. Fill the online application form with accurate personal and educational details.
  5. Upload photograph, signature and required documents.
  6. Pay the applicable examination fee online.
  7. Submit the form and save the confirmation page for future reference.
