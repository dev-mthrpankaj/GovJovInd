Complete context

About This Recruitment

UPPSC Review Officer (RO) / Assistant Review Officer (ARO) Examination 2026 is a Uttar Pradesh Public Service Commission recruitment drive to fill 501 posts of Samiksha Adhikari (RO) and Sahayak Samiksha Adhikari (ARO) across the U.P. Secretariat, Board of Revenue and UPPSC departments. The official short notice was released in October 2026, and the online application window is open from 26 October 2026 to 26 November 2026.

The selection process consists of a Preliminary Examination, a Main Examination, and a Hindi Typing Test (for ARO posts). There is no interview stage. Candidates must complete One Time Registration (OTR) before applying. The preliminary exam date will be announced later on the official UPPSC portal.

Basic eligibility is a Bachelor's degree in any stream from a recognised university. For certain ARO posts, an 'O' Level certificate and Hindi typing speed of 25 WPM are required. The age limit is 21 to 40 years as on 01 July 2026, with relaxation for reserved categories as per UPPSC rules.

The application fee is Rs.125 for General/OBC/EWS, Rs.65 for SC/ST, and Rs.25 for PwD candidates. The last date for fee payment and application submission is 26 November 2026, while the correction window closes on 03 December 2026.
