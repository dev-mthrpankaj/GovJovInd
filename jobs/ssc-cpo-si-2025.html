Complete context

About This Recruitment

The Staff Selection Commission (SSC) conducted the CPO SI Examination 2025 to recruit Sub-Inspectors in Delhi Police and Central Armed Police Forces (CAPFs). The official notification was released in September 2025, with the online application window open from 26 September 2025 to 16 October 2025.

The Computer Based Examination (Paper-I) was held from 09 to 12 December 2025. The provisional answer key was released on 24 December 2025, followed by the final answer key and marks on 21 April 2026. The Physical Endurance Test (PET) and Physical Standard Test (PST) were conducted from 21 to 28 July 2026.

The PET/PST result has now been declared on 29 October 2026. Candidates who qualify will proceed to the next stage of the selection process, which includes Paper-II, a medical examination, and document verification. This page will be updated with every new official development.

Eligibility requires a Bachelor's degree in any discipline. Male candidates applying for the Sub-Inspector (Executive) post in Delhi Police must also possess a valid LMV driving license. The age limit is 20 to 25 years as on 01 August 2025, with relaxations for reserved categories.

What to do at the current stage

How to Check & Download PET/PST Result

  1. Visit the official website of the Staff Selection Commission (SSC).
  2. Click on the 'Login / Register' section.
  3. Enter your Registration Number and Password.
  4. Click on 'Login'.
  5. Go to the 'Result / Marks' section.
  6. Click on 'CPO SI PET/PST Result 2026'.
  7. Your result will appear on the screen. Download and save it for future reference.

Original Application Process

Preserved for recruitment history and reference after the application stage closes.

  1. Visit the official SSC recruitment website at ssc.gov.in.
  2. Read the official Notice of adv capf 2025 carefully.
  3. Register on the SSC portal with a valid email ID and mobile number.
  4. Fill the online application form with accurate personal and educational details.
  5. Upload photograph, signature, and required documents.
  6. Pay the applicable examination fee online.
  7. Submit the form and save the confirmation page for future reference.
