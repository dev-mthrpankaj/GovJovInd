Complete context

About This Recruitment

The Staff Selection Commission (SSC) conducts the Sub-Inspector in Delhi Police and Central Armed Police Forces (CAPFs) Examination 2025 (SSC CPO SI Examination 2025) to recruit 2861 Sub-Inspectors. The official notification was published on 26 September 2025, and the online application window remained open until 16 October 2025.

Paper-I Computer-Based Examination was held from 09 December to 12 December 2025, followed by the provisional answer key on 24 December 2025 and the final answer key and marks on 21 April 2026. Qualified candidates appeared for the Physical Standard Test (PST) and Physical Endurance Test (PET) from 21 July to 28 July 2026, and the PET/PST result was declared on 29 October 2026.

On 18 November 2026, SSC released the Paper-II Exam City Details on its official portal ssc.gov.in. Shortlisted candidates can log in with their Registration Number and password to view their allotted exam city for the Paper-II Computer-Based Exam scheduled on 28 November 2026.

Basic eligibility is a Bachelor's Degree in any discipline from a recognised university, with male candidates applying for Sub-Inspector (Executive) in Delhi Police also requiring a valid LMV driving licence for motorcycle and car. The age limit is 20 to 25 years as on 01 August 2025, with relaxation as per SSC rules.
