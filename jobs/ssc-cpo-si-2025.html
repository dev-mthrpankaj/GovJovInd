Complete context

About This Recruitment

Staff Selection Commission (SSC) is conducting the Sub-Inspector in Delhi Police and Central Armed Police Forces (CAPFs) Examination 2025 to fill 2,861 Sub-Inspector posts. Online applications were accepted from 26 September 2025 to 16 October 2025 for graduates aged 20 to 25 years as on 01 August 2025.

Paper-I Computer Based Examination was conducted from 09 December 2025 to 12 December 2025, followed by the provisional answer key on 24 December 2025 and final answer key and marks on 21 April 2026. Candidates who qualified in Paper-I appeared for the Physical Standard Test (PST) and Physical Endurance Test (PET) from 21 July 2026 to 28 July 2026, with the PET/PST result declared on 29 October 2026.

For candidates shortlisted in PET/PST, Staff Selection Commission released the Paper-II exam city intimation on 18 November 2026 and the Paper-II Admit Card on 25 November 2026. The Paper-II examination will be held on 28 November 2026, followed by Detailed Medical Examination and Document Verification.
