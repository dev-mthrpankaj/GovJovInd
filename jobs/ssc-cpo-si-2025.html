Complete context

About This Recruitment

Staff Selection Commission (SSC) is conducting the Sub-Inspector in Delhi Police and Central Armed Police Forces (CAPFs) Examination 2025 to fill 2,861 Sub-Inspector posts. Online applications were accepted from 26 September 2025 to 16 October 2025 for graduates aged 20 to 25 years as on 01 August 2025.

Paper-I Computer Based Examination was conducted from 09 December 2025 to 12 December 2025, followed by the provisional answer key on 24 December 2025 and final answer key and marks on 21 April 2026. Candidates who qualified in Paper-I appeared for the Physical Standard Test (PST) and Physical Endurance Test (PET) from 21 July 2026 to 28 July 2026, with the PET/PST result declared on 29 October 2026.

For candidates shortlisted in PET/PST, Staff Selection Commission released the Paper-II exam city intimation on 18 November 2026 and the Paper-II Admit Card on 25 November 2026. The Paper-II examination will be held on 28 November 2026, followed by Detailed Medical Examination and Document Verification.

What to do at the current stage

How to Download Paper-II Admit Card

  1. Open the official Staff Selection Commission login portal at ssc.gov.in from Important Links.
  2. Enter your Registration Number and Password or Date of Birth to log in.
  3. Select the Sub-Inspector in Delhi Police and CAPFs Examination 2025 Paper-II Admit Card link.
  4. Verify your name, roll number, exam date (28 November 2026), shift timing and test centre address.
  5. Download the Paper-II Admit Card PDF and take a clear printout for exam day.
  6. Carry the printed admit card, two recent passport-size photographs and an original valid photo ID to the centre.

Original Application Process

Preserved for recruitment history and reference after the application stage closes.

  1. Visit the official Staff Selection Commission portal at ssc.gov.in.
  2. Complete One-Time Registration (OTR) with verified mobile number and email ID.
  3. Log in and open the Sub-Inspector in Delhi Police and CAPFs Examination 2025 application form.
  4. Enter personal, educational, category and post-preference details accurately.
  5. Upload live photograph and scanned signature as per SSC specifications.
  6. Pay Rs.100 online fee (if applicable) and submit the application form before the deadline.
  7. Download and save the submitted application confirmation printout.
