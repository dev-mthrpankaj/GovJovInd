Complete context

About This Recruitment

Jamia Millia Islamia (JMI), New Delhi has released Advt No. 01/2026-27 for Jamia Millia Islamia Non Teaching Recruitment 2026 to fill 85 Group A, Group B and Group C posts across 35 administrative, engineering, library, medical, horticulture and technical cadres at Jamia Nagar, New Delhi.

The official notification was issued on 10 November 2026, and online applications opened on 12 November 2026 through the university recruitment portal at jobs.jmi.ac.in. Eligible candidates can submit the online application form and pay the application fee until 11 December 2026, followed by mandatory hard copy submission of the printed form with supporting documents by 21 December 2026.

Vacancies include Upper Division Clerk (10 posts), Beldar (6 posts), Electrician (5 posts), Carpenter (5 posts), Section Officer (4 posts), Urdu Typist (4 posts), Mate (4 posts), Assistant Registrar (3 posts), Assistant (3 posts), Plumber (3 posts), Wireman (3 posts), Pharmacist (3 posts), Chair Side Attendant (3 posts) and 22 other specialised cadres carrying 7th CPC pay scales from Pay Level 1 (Rs.18,000 - Rs.56,900) to Pay Level 13 (Rs.1,23,100 - Rs.2,15,900).

Post-wise educational qualifications range from 10th pass, 10+2 and ITI trade certificates to Bachelor's, B.Pharma, Civil Engineering and Master's degrees with relevant experience. The maximum age limit is 40 to 56 years depending on the post, with relaxation as per Government of India and university norms. All upcoming stages including exam dates, admit cards, results and document verification will be updated on this permanent page.

Eligibility Criteria

Age Limit

Maximum age limit is 40 to 56 years depending on the post (exact post-wise upper age and cut-off date as per Advt No. 01/2026-27).

Age relaxation is applicable for SC, ST, OBC, PwBD and other eligible categories as per Government of India and Jamia Millia Islamia rules.

Education

Post-wise qualifications range from 10th pass, 10+2, ITI trade certificates and Diplomas to Bachelor's, B.Pharma, Civil Engineering and Master's degrees.

Most posts require relevant work experience, trade competency or typing speed as specified in Advt No. 01/2026-27.

Nationality / Other

Candidates must meet post-specific experience, language proficiency (Hindi/Urdu/English where applicable) and deputation or direct recruitment criteria stated in the official notification.

Refer to official notification for detailed conditions.

What to do at the current stage

How to Apply for JMI Non Teaching Recruitment 2026

  1. Visit the official Jamia Millia Islamia recruitment portal at jobs.jmi.ac.in.
  2. Read Advt No. 01/2026-27 carefully to verify post-wise qualification, experience and pay level.
  3. Register on the portal using a valid email ID and active mobile number.
  4. Log in with your registration credentials and fill in personal, category, educational and experience details.
  5. Upload clear scanned copies of your passport-size photograph, signature and supporting documents.
  6. Pay the applicable application fee online on or before 11 December 2026.
  7. Submit the online form, download the confirmation printout and send the hard copy with self-attested enclosures by 21 December 2026 as instructed in the official notification.
