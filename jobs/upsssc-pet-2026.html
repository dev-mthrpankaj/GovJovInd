Complete context

About This Recruitment

UPSSSC Preliminary Eligibility Test (PET) 2026 is conducted by the Uttar Pradesh Subordinate Services Selection Commission (UPSSSC) as a mandatory screening examination for various Group C posts in Uttar Pradesh government departments. The PET score is used to shortlist candidates for the Main Examination of individual recruitments such as Lekhpal, Junior Assistant, VDO, Forest Guard and other Group C posts.

The official notification was released in August 2026, and the online application window was open from 3 August 2026 to 7 September 2026 (extended from the original 1 September deadline). The exam city intimation slip was released on 12 October 2026, and the admit card for the 25 October exam was released on 17 October 2026. The examination will be held on three dates: 25 October, 30 October and 1 November 2026.

PET 2026 is an objective-type OMR-based test with 100 questions carrying 100 marks. The duration is 2 hours, and there is a negative marking of 1/4 mark for each wrong answer. The minimum qualification is High School (Class 10) pass or equivalent, and the age limit is 18 to 40 years as on 1 July 2026. The PET score remains valid for three years, allowing candidates to use it for multiple recruitment cycles.

What to do at the current stage

How to Download UPSSSC PET Admit Card

  1. Visit the official UPSSSC website at upsssc.gov.in.
  2. Click on the PET 2026 admit card link in the Latest News or Examination section.
  3. Enter your registration number and date of birth (or password) as required.
  4. Submit the details to view your admit card.
  5. Download the PDF and take a clear printout for the exam day.
  6. Verify your name, roll number, exam date, shift, reporting time and centre address on the admit card.

Original Application Process

Preserved for recruitment history and reference after the application stage closes.

  1. Visit the official UPSSSC website at upsssc.gov.in.
  2. Complete One-Time Registration (OTR) if not already done.
  3. Read the PET 2026 official notification carefully.
  4. Fill the online application form with accurate personal and educational details.
  5. Upload photograph, signature and required documents.
  6. Pay the applicable examination fee online.
  7. Submit the form and save the confirmation page for future reference.
