Complete context

About This Recruitment

UPSSSC Preliminary Eligibility Test (PET) 2026 is a qualifying examination conducted by the Uttar Pradesh Subordinate Services Selection Commission (UPSSSC). It serves as a gateway for candidates seeking eligibility for various Group C posts in the Uttar Pradesh government. The official notification was released on 01 August 2026, and the online application window remained open from 03 August 2026 to 07 September 2026.

The written examination is scheduled to be held on 25 October, 30 October and 01 November 2026 in two shifts each day. The Admit Card for the examination was released on 17 October 2026, and the exam city intimation slip was made available on 12 October 2026. The PET scorecard remains valid for three years from the date of declaration of the result.

Eligibility requires candidates to have passed Class 10 (High School) or an equivalent examination from a recognised board. The age limit is 18 to 40 years as on 01 July 2026, with relaxation for reserved categories as per UPSSSC rules. The application fee is Rs.185 for General/OBC/EWS, Rs.95 for SC/ST, and Rs.25 for PH candidates.

What to do at the current stage

How to Download UPSSSC PET Admit Card 2026

  1. Open the official UPSSSC admit card portal from Important Links.
  2. Enter your registration number, date of birth and other required details.
  3. Download the PET Admit Card PDF for your exam shift.
  4. Check your name, roll number, exam date, shift timing and centre address.
  5. Take a clear colour printout and carry it to the exam centre.
  6. Read the admit card instructions carefully before the exam day.

Original Application Process

Preserved for recruitment history and reference after the application stage closes.

  1. Visit the official UPSSSC website at upsssc.gov.in.
  2. Complete One Time Registration (OTR) using a valid mobile number and email ID.
  3. Log in with your OTR number and fill the PET 2026 application form.
  4. Upload photograph, signature and required documents.
  5. Pay the applicable examination fee online.
  6. Submit the form and save the confirmation page for future reference.

Frequently Asked Questions

UPSSSC PET (Preliminary Eligibility Test) 2026 is a state-level qualifying examination conducted by the Uttar Pradesh Subordinate Services Selection Commission. It serves as an eligibility gateway for various Group C posts in the Uttar Pradesh government. Candidates who qualify the PET become eligible to apply for main recruitment exams conducted by UPSSSC and other departments.

Yes. The UPSSSC PET Admit Card 2026 was released on 17 October 2026. Candidates can download their hall ticket from the official UPSSSC portal at upsssc.gov.in. The direct download link is also available in the Important Links section of this page.

The UPSSSC PET 2026 written examination will be held on 25 October 2026, 30 October 2026 and 01 November 2026. The exam will be conducted in two shifts each day: 10:00 AM to 12:00 Noon and 3:00 PM to 5:00 PM.

The online application last date for UPSSSC PET 2026 was 07 September 2026 (extended from the earlier date). The fee payment deadline was also 07 September 2026, and the correction window closed on 08 September 2026. Applications are now closed.

Candidates must have passed Class 10 (High School) or an equivalent examination from a recognised board. The age limit is 18 to 40 years as on 01 July 2026. Age relaxation is available for SC, ST, OBC, PwBD and other reserved categories as per UPSSSC rules.

The application fee is Rs.185 for General, OBC and EWS candidates, Rs.95 for SC and ST candidates, and Rs.25 for PH (Divyang) candidates. Payment can be made online through debit card, credit card, internet banking or UPI.

The PET is a qualifying examination. The process includes One Time Registration (OTR), online application, appearing for the PET written exam, and obtaining a scorecard valid for three years. Shortlisting for main exams is done based on the PET score and other criteria set by the recruiting departments.

The PET written exam consists of 100 objective-type questions carrying 100 marks. The duration is 2 hours. There is negative marking of 1/4th mark for each wrong answer. Subjects include Indian History, National Movement, Geography, Indian Economy, Constitution, General Science, Arithmetic, Hindi, English, Reasoning, Current Affairs and General Awareness.

Open the official UPSSSC admit card portal at upsssc.gov.in. Enter your registration number, date of birth and other required details. Download the PET Admit Card PDF and check your name, roll number, exam date, shift timing and centre address. Take a clear colour printout to the exam centre.

Carry a printed copy of the PET Admit Card, a valid original photo ID (Aadhaar, PAN, Voter ID or Passport), recent passport-size photographs, and your Class 10 marksheet. Category certificates and domicile certificates may be required at later stages. Follow the instructions printed on your admit card.

The UPSSSC PET scorecard is valid for three years from the date of declaration of the result. Candidates can use the same PET score to apply for various Group C recruitments conducted during this period.

The UPSSSC PET 2026 result and scorecard will be released on the official UPSSSC website at upsssc.gov.in. This page will be updated with the result link and cut-off details as soon as they are announced.
