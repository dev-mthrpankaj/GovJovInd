About This Recruitment

UPSSSC Preliminary Eligibility Test (PET) 2026 is a qualifying examination conducted by the Uttar Pradesh Subordinate Service Selection Commission. Candidates who qualify PET become eligible to apply for various Group C and other subordinate services posts advertised by UPSSSC in the future. The PET score remains valid for a specified period, allowing candidates to skip the preliminary stage in subsequent UPSSSC recruitments.

The application window was open from 03 August 2026 to 07 September 2026 (extended). The correction window closed on 08 September 2026. The commission has now released the new exam date schedule.

The exam will be conducted in multiple shifts across three days. Candidates should download the official exam date notice from the Important Links section and keep checking this page for admit card, answer key, result and other updates.
