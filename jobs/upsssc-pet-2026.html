About This Recruitment

UPSSSC Preliminary Eligibility Test (PET) 2026 is an eligibility examination conducted by the Uttar Pradesh Subordinate Services Selection Commission under Advertisement No. 16/2026. The application stage has closed and the exam dates are now announced.

The PET examination is scheduled for 23, 24 and 25 October 2026. Candidates with Class 10 High School or a higher recognized qualification were eligible to apply, subject to the complete notification conditions.

Applicants should keep their UPSSSC registration details, identity proof and academic/category documents ready for the upcoming admit-card and examination stages.
