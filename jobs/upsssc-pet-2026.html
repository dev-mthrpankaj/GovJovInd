Complete context

About This Recruitment

UPSSSC Preliminary Eligibility Test (PET) 2026 is a state-level screening examination conducted by the Uttar Pradesh Subordinate Services Selection Commission (UPSSSC). It is the mandatory first stage for candidates aspiring to appear for various Group C recruitments such as Lekhpal, Junior Assistant, Village Development Officer (VDO), X-Ray Technician, Assistant Accountant, Stenographer and other UPSSSC posts.

The official notification (Advt. No. 16-Exam/2026) was released on 01 August 2026. The online application window remained open from 03 August 2026 to 07 September 2026 (extended). The correction window was available until 08 September 2026.

The PET 2026 examination was conducted in offline OMR-based mode on 25 October 2026, 30 October 2026 and 01 November 2026 in two shifts each day. The provisional answer key has been released on 02 November 2026. Candidates can now check their answers and raise objections if any discrepancy is found.

Basic eligibility requires passing Class 10th (High School) or an equivalent examination from a recognised board. The age limit is 18 to 40 years as on 01 July 2026, with relaxation for reserved categories as per UP Government rules. The application fee is Rs.185 for General/OBC, Rs.95 for SC/ST, and Rs.25 for PwD candidates.

What to do at the current stage

How to Download Answer Key and Raise Objection

  1. Visit the official UPSSSC website at upsssc.gov.in.
  2. Click on the 'PET 2026 Answer Key' link on the homepage.
  3. Log in using your registration number and date of birth.
  4. Download the provisional answer key and response sheet PDF.
  5. Match your marked answers with the key to estimate your score.
  6. If you find any discrepancy, use the 'Raise Objection' link and submit your challenge within the prescribed window.
  7. Pay the objection fee (if applicable) and save the acknowledgement for future reference.

Original Application Process

Preserved for recruitment history and reference after the application stage closes.

  1. Visit the official UPSSSC website at upsssc.gov.in.
  2. Complete One-Time Registration (OTR) if not already done.
  3. Log in with your OTR number and fill the PET 2026 application form.
  4. Upload photograph, signature and required documents.
  5. Pay the applicable application fee online.
  6. Submit the form and save the confirmation page for future reference.
