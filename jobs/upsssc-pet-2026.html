Complete context

About This Recruitment

UPSSSC Preliminary Eligibility Test (PET) 2026 is a qualifying eligibility test conducted by the Uttar Pradesh Subordinate Service Selection Commission (UPSSSC). Candidates who qualify in the PET become eligible to apply for various Group C posts in different departments of the Uttar Pradesh state government. PET scores are typically used as a screening criterion for these recruitments.

The UPSSSC PET 2026 notification (Advt No. 16/2026) was released in August 2026, and the online application window remained open from 03 August 2026 to 07 September 2026 (extended). The application correction window was open till 08 September 2026.

The UPSSSC PET 2026 exam city details were released on 12 October 2026. The Preliminary Eligibility Test is scheduled to be conducted on 25 October 2026, 30 October 2026 and 01 November 2026 across various exam centres in Uttar Pradesh. The UPSSSC PET admit card will be released as per the official schedule.

Basic eligibility for UPSSSC PET 2026 is a minimum of Class 10 (High School) qualification from a recognised board or any higher qualification. The age limit is 18 to 40 years as on 01 July 2026, with relaxation for reserved categories as per UPSSSC rules.

What to do at the current stage

How to Check UPSSSC PET Exam City Details

  1. Open the official UPSSSC PET exam city details portal from the Important Links section.
  2. Log in using your registration number, roll number or date of birth.
  3. Download the exam city intimation slip and check your allotted exam city.
  4. Note that the exam city intimation slip is NOT the admit card. It only indicates your exam city.
  5. Wait for the UPSSSC PET admit card release for the exact exam centre, date and reporting time.
  6. Read the official exam city notice carefully for instructions and important guidelines.

Original Application Process

Preserved for recruitment history and reference after the application stage closes.

  1. Visit the official UPSSSC website at upsssc.gov.in.
  2. Complete the One Time Registration (OTR) process on the UPSSSC portal.
  3. Click on the UPSSSC PET 2026 Apply Online link.
  4. Fill the online application form with accurate personal and educational details.
  5. Upload photograph, signature and required documents.
  6. Pay the applicable examination fee online.
  7. Submit the form and save the confirmation page for future reference.
