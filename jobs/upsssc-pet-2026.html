About This Recruitment

UPSSSC Preliminary Eligibility Test (PET) 2026 is the state-level preliminary eligibility examination conducted by the Uttar Pradesh Subordinate Services Selection Commission. PET is used as an eligibility/screening score for candidates who wish to participate in eligible UPSSSC recruitments notified after the examination.

The 2026 online application process started on 3 August 2026. The application and fee-payment deadline has been extended to 7 September 2026, while the available information lists 8 September 2026 as the correction last date.

Candidates should complete the PET form carefully and keep their registration details, photograph, signature, educational records, identity details and category documents ready. Later PET stages such as exam date, admit card, answer key and result will continue to be updated on this same permanent page.
