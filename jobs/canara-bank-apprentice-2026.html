About This Recruitment

Canara Bank Graduate Apprentice Recruitment 2026 is a bank apprenticeship drive for 3,500 posts under the Apprentices Act, 1961 for the financial year 2026-27. The online application window will open on 1 October 2026 and close on 17 October 2026.

This opportunity is suitable for graduates who have completed their degree between 1 January 2023 and 1 September 2026. The apprenticeship training lasts 12 months, and selected candidates will receive a monthly stipend of Rs.16,650.

Candidates must complete their NATS registration before applying. Keep graduation certificate, class 12 marksheet, photo, signature, and category documents ready before starting the online form.
