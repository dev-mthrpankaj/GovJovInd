About This Recruitment

Canara Bank has released the notification for the engagement of 3,500 Graduate Apprentices under the Apprentices Act, 1961 for the Financial Year 2026-27. This is a one-year apprenticeship training program across all states and union territories of India.

The online application window is open from 01 October 2026 to 17 October 2026. Eligible graduates aged between 20 and 28 years can apply online through the official Canara Bank website after completing their NATS portal registration.

Selected candidates will receive a monthly stipend of Rs. 16,650 during the 12-month training period. The selection will be based on a state-wise merit list prepared from 12th standard or Diploma examination marks, followed by document verification and a local language test.
