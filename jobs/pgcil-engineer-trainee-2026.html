Complete context

About This Recruitment

Power Grid Corporation of India Limited (PGCIL), a Maharatna PSU under the Ministry of Power, has released the notification for Engineer Trainee (ET) 2026 recruitment. The recruitment aims to fill 319 vacancies across Electrical (250), Civil (48), and Computer Science (21) disciplines. The selection is based on GATE 2026 scores, followed by Group Discussion and Personal Interview.

The online application process started on 3 November 2026 and will close on 18 November 2026. Candidates must have a valid GATE 2026 score in the corresponding paper (EE/CE/CS) and a full-time B.E./B.Tech/B.Sc (Engineering) degree with at least 60% marks. The age limit is 28 years as on 31 December 2025, with relaxation for reserved categories.

Eligibility Criteria

Age Limit

Maximum 28 years as on 31 December 2025.

Age relaxation: OBC (NCL) 3 years, SC/ST 5 years, PwBD 10-15 years as per Government of India rules.

Education

Full-time B.E./B.Tech/B.Sc (Engineering) in the relevant discipline with minimum 60% marks or equivalent CGPA.

Final year/semester students expecting results by 14 August 2026 are also eligible, provided they have 60% aggregate up to pre-final semester.

Nationality / Other

Only Indian Nationals are eligible. Candidates must have a valid GATE 2026 score in the corresponding paper (EE/CE/CS).

What to do at the current stage

How to Apply Online

  1. Visit the official POWERGRID careers portal at powergrid.in.
  2. Open the Engineer Trainee 2026 recruitment page.
  3. Register with your GATE 2026 registration number and application number.
  4. Fill in personal, educational, and GATE details accurately.
  5. Upload scanned photograph, signature, and required documents.
  6. Pay the application fee (Rs.500 for General/OBC/EWS; exempted for SC/ST/PwBD/Ex-SM).
  7. Submit the form before 18 November 2026, 11:59 PM and save the confirmation page.
