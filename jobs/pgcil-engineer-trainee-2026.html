How to Apply Online

  1. Open the official PGCIL careers portal at careers.powergrid.in.

Frequently Asked Questions

When is the last date to apply for PGCIL Engineer Trainee Recruitment 2026?

The last date to submit the PGCIL Engineer Trainee online application form is 18 November 2026. Candidates should complete the application and fee payment before 18 November 2026 to avoid server issues on the final day.

How many vacancies are available in PGCIL Engineer Trainee Recruitment 2026?

A total of 319 vacancies are available for the PGCIL Engineer Trainee post, split into 250 Electrical, 48 Civil and 21 Computer Science posts as per the official notification.

What is the starting date for PGCIL Engineer Trainee Online Form 2026?

The PGCIL Engineer Trainee online application process started on 03 November 2026 through the official Power Grid careers portal careers.powergrid.in. The last date to apply is 18 November 2026.

What is the eligibility for PGCIL Engineer Trainee Recruitment 2026?

Candidates must hold an Engineering Degree in the relevant branch (Electrical, Civil or Computer Science) and must have appeared for the corresponding paper of GATE 2026 as per the official PGCIL notification.

What is the age limit for PGCIL Engineer Trainee Recruitment 2026?

The age limit is 18 to 28 years as on 31 December 2026. Age relaxation is applicable for SC, ST, OBC, PwBD and Ex-Servicemen candidates as per government rules and the official notification.

What is the application fee for PGCIL Engineer Trainee 2026?

General and OBC candidates need to pay Rs.500 as the application fee. EWS, SC, ST and PH candidates are exempted from the fee as per the PGCIL Engineer Trainee 2026 official notification.

What is the salary of PGCIL Engineer Trainee in 2026?

The PGCIL Engineer Trainee salary and pay scale details are provided in the official notification. Selected candidates will receive pay as per the Power Grid pay scale along with applicable allowances and benefits.

How can I apply online for PGCIL Engineer Trainee Recruitment 2026?

Visit careers.powergrid.in, complete the registration with a valid email ID and mobile number, fill the application form, upload documents, pay the fee and submit before 18 November 2026.

What is the selection process for PGCIL Engineer Trainee 2026?

The PGCIL Engineer Trainee selection process is detailed in the official notification. Candidates must appear for the GATE 2026 examination in the relevant branch and meet all eligibility criteria.

Who can apply for PGCIL Engineer Trainee Recruitment 2026?

Candidates who have completed an Engineering Degree in Electrical, Civil or Computer Science and appeared for the corresponding GATE 2026 paper, and who meet the age limit of 18-28 years, can apply online.

What is the official website for PGCIL Engineer Trainee 2026?

The official website of Power Grid Corporation of India Limited is https://www.powergrid.in/en/home and the online application portal is available at https://careers.powergrid.in/recruitment-nextgen/h/login.aspx.

Will the PGCIL Engineer Trainee page URL change in later stages?

No. This is a permanent recruitment page for PGCIL Engineer Trainee 2026. The same URL will continue to show every later stage including admit card, exam, result and final selection details.
