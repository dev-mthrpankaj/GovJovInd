Complete context

About This Recruitment

Power Grid Corporation of India Limited (PGCIL), a Maharatna Public Sector Undertaking under the Ministry of Power, is recruiting 319 Engineer Trainees through GATE 2026 under Advertisement No. CC/04/2025. Vacancy Notice No. 2 was published on 03 November 2026, covering 250 Engineer Trainee (Electrical), 48 Engineer Trainee (Civil) and 21 Engineer Trainee (Computer Science) posts across India.

Online applications opened on 03 November 2026 at 5:00 PM and were initially scheduled to close on 18 November 2026. Through Notice-3, Power Grid Corporation of India Limited (PGCIL) extended the last date for online registration and fee payment to 23 November 2026.

Candidates must possess a full-time B.E./B.Tech./B.Sc. (Engineering) degree in the relevant discipline with at least 60% marks (pass marks for SC, ST and PwBD candidates on reserved posts) along with a valid qualifying GATE 2026 score in the corresponding EE, CE or CS paper. The age limit is 18 to 28 years as on 31 December 2025, with upper age relaxation for reserved categories as per Government of India rules.

Selection is based on GATE 2026 normalized marks (85% weightage), Group Discussion (3% weightage) and Personal Interview (12% weightage), followed by document verification and pre-employment medical examination. Selected trainees receive a pay scale of Rs.40,000 - Rs.1,40,000 (IDA) during the one-year training (approx. Rs.11 lakh CTC) and Rs.50,000 - Rs.1,60,000 (IDA) upon absorption as Engineer (E2) (approx. Rs.22.50 lakh CTC).

Eligibility Criteria

Age Limit

18 to 28 years as on 31 December 2025.

Upper age relaxation of 3 years for OBC (NCL), 5 years for SC/ST, and applicable relaxation for PwBD and Ex-Servicemen as per Government of India rules.

Education

Full-time B.E./B.Tech./B.Sc. (Engineering) degree in the relevant discipline with minimum 60% marks (pass marks for SC/ST/PwBD on reserved posts) plus a valid qualifying GATE 2026 score in EE, CE or CS.

Only GATE 2026 normalized marks are valid. GATE 2025 or earlier scores are not accepted.

Nationality / Other

Only Indian nationals meeting the discipline-specific degree, percentage, age limit and GATE 2026 qualification criteria may apply. Selected candidates must execute a service agreement bond for 3 years post-training.

Refer to official notification for detailed conditions.

How to Apply Online Before 23 November 2026

  1. Open the official Power Grid Corporation of India Limited (PGCIL) recruitment portal from Important Links.
  2. Register using a valid email ID, mobile number and your exact GATE 2026 registration and application numbers.
