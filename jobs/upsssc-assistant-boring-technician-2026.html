About This Recruitment

UPSSSC Assistant Boring Technician Recruitment 2026 is a recruitment drive conducted by the Uttar Pradesh Subordinate Services Selection Commission (UPSSSC) for 402 Assistant Boring Technician posts in the Minor Irrigation Department, Government of Uttar Pradesh. The online application window was open from 15 April 2026 to 5 May 2026.

This role is suitable for candidates with a 10th pass qualification and an ITI certificate in relevant trades such as Fitter, Wireman, Turner, or Machinist. A valid UPSSSC PET 2025 score card is mandatory for applying.

The selection process includes shortlisting based on PET 2025 scores, a main written examination, document verification, and a final merit list. The revised answer key has now been released, and candidates should check the Important Links section for the latest official downloads.
