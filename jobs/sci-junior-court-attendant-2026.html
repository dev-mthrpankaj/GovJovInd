Complete context

About This Recruitment

The Supreme Court of India (SCI) has released the official notification for the recruitment of Junior Court Attendant (Group 'C' Non-Clerical) on 15 October 2026. The recruitment drive aims to fill 130 tentative vacancies. The online application process started on 4 November 2026 and will remain open until 25 November 2026 (11:55 PM).

The selection process consists of an Objective Type Written Examination of 100 marks followed by an Interview of 25 marks. The objective test is tentatively scheduled for 26 December 2026. The answer key is expected to be uploaded on 28 December 2026, and the objection window will remain open from 28 December 2026 to 29 December 2026.

Eligible candidates must have passed Class 10 (Matriculation) from a recognised board. The maximum educational qualification allowed for the post is Class 12 (10+2). The age limit is 18 to 27 years as on 1 October 2026, with age relaxation applicable for reserved categories as per SCI rules.

The application fee is Rs. 1,500 for General/Unreserved candidates and Rs. 750 for SC/ST/OBC, Ex-Servicemen, and Physically Challenged candidates. Selected candidates will receive a pay scale of Rs. 21,700 to Rs. 69,100 (Pay Level 3).
