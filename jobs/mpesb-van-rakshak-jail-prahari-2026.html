About This Recruitment

MPESB Van Rakshak, Jail Prahari Recruitment 2026 is a combined recruitment drive by the Madhya Pradesh Employee Selection Board (MPESB) for 1679 posts of Vanrakshak, Kshetra Rakshak, Jail Prahari and Jail Superintendent. The written examination was held on 04 June 2026, and the provisional answer key was released on 22 June 2026.

The written exam result has been declared. The next stage is the Physical Efficiency Test (PET) / Physical Standard Test (PST), scheduled to be held from 07 to 18 October 2026. Candidates who have qualified in the written examination should download the physical exam schedule and prepare for the physical test as per the official notice.

This permanent page will continue to carry every future update, including the physical admit card, physical result, document verification, medical examination, final result and merit list, without changing the URL.
