Complete context

About This Recruitment

MPESB Van Rakshak / Jail Prahari Combined Recruitment Test 2026 is a Madhya Pradesh Employees Selection Board (MPESB) recruitment drive to fill 1679 vacancies across four posts: Van Rakshak (Forest Guard) – 728 posts, Kshetra Rakshak (Field Guard) – 169 posts, Jail Prahari (Jail Warder) – 757 posts, and Assistant Jail Superintendent – 25 posts. The official notification (Rule Book) was released on 27 February 2026, and the online application window remained open from 28 February 2026 to 30 April 2026 (extended from 14 March 2026).

The Computer Based Test (CBT) was conducted from 04 June 2026 to 19 June 2026 in multiple shifts across Madhya Pradesh. The provisional answer key was released on 22 June 2026 with an objection window from 22 to 25 June 2026. The written result was declared on 14 August 2026, and a total of 14,950 candidates qualified for the second phase of the selection process.

The Physical Efficiency Test (PET) is scheduled from 07 October to 18 October 2026, and the PET Admit Card was released on 06 October 2026. Candidates who qualified the written examination must download the PET Admit Card and carry it to the physical test centre along with valid photo identification.

Basic eligibility varies by post: 10th pass (High School) for Van Rakshak and Kshetra Rakshak, 12th pass (Higher Secondary) for Jail Prahari, and a Bachelor's Degree in any discipline for Assistant Jail Superintendent. The age limit is 18 to 33 years as on 01 January 2026 for most posts, with relaxation for reserved categories as per MP government rules.

What to do at the current stage

How to Download PET Admit Card

  1. Open the official MPESB PET admit card portal from Important Links.
  2. Enter your Application Number (up to 13 digits) and Date of Birth in DD/MM/YYYY format.
  3. Provide the first two letters of your mother's name and the last four digits of your Aadhaar Number.
  4. Solve the simple calculation displayed on the page and click Search.
  5. Download the PET Admit Card PDF for the Physical Efficiency Test.
  6. Check your name, roll number, PET date, reporting time and venue carefully.
  7. Take a clear colour printout and carry it to the PET centre along with a valid photo ID.
  8. Read the PET schedule notice and instructions carefully before the exam day.

Original Application Process

Preserved for recruitment history and reference after the application stage closes.

  1. Visit the official MPESB website at esb.mp.gov.in.
  2. Read the official JAIL_VAN_2026 Rule Book carefully.
  3. Register on the MPESB portal with valid email ID and mobile number.
  4. Fill the online application form with accurate personal and educational details.
  5. Upload photograph, signature and required documents.
  6. Pay the applicable examination fee online.
  7. Submit the form and save the confirmation page for future reference.
