Complete context

About This Recruitment

National Testing Agency (NTA) is conducting the Rashtriya Indian Military College Entrance Examination (RIMCEE) for Class VIII Admissions for the academic year 2027. The official admission notification was released on 01 September 2026, and the application window was open until 30 September 2026.

Following the application and correction phases, NTA has now released the Exam City Intimation slip on 17 November 2026. The written entrance examination is scheduled to take place on 06 December 2026 at various designated centres across India.

Candidates appearing or having passed the Class 7th examination from a recognized school are eligible for this admission. The age limit for applicants is strictly between 12.5 years and 14 years as on 01 July 2027.

Students must bring their printed admit card, a valid photo ID, and other required documents to the examination centre. The results for the entrance test are scheduled to be declared on 20 February 2027.
