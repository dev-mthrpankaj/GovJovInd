Complete context

About This Recruitment

Uttar Pradesh Public Service Commission (UPPSC) had released the notification for Combined State/Upper Subordinate Services Examination 2025 (PCS Pre) for a total of 210 posts. The online application process was conducted from 20 February 2025 to 24 March 2025. The Prelims examination was held on 12 October 2025, and its result was declared on 01 December 2025.

Candidates who qualified the Prelims appeared for the Mains examination, which was held from 29 March to 01 April 2026. The Mains Result has now been declared on 17 September 2026. This page provides the complete recruitment lifecycle, including all past stages, current status, and will be updated with future stages such as interview, document verification, and final merit list.

The selection process includes Prelims, Mains, and Interview. Candidates should carefully check the official notification for detailed eligibility, age limit, and post-wise vacancy distribution.
