Complete context

About This Recruitment

UPPSC PCS 2025 is the Combined State / Upper Subordinate Services Examination conducted by the Uttar Pradesh Public Service Commission for 210 posts in the Uttar Pradesh government. The recruitment cycle began with the notification released on 20 February 2025 and has since moved through the Prelims examination, the Mains examination and the interview stage.

This page is the permanent lifecycle record for UPPSC PCS 2025. It currently reflects the interview stage — the Mains result was declared on 17 September 2026 and the interview admit card has now been released. Interviews are scheduled between 28 September 2026 and 24 October 2026.

Candidates shortlisted for the interview must download the interview admit card, verify their individual interview date, reporting time and venue, and carry all original educational, category and domicile documents for verification. The final result, merit list and joining updates will be added to this same page without any change to the URL.

What to do at the current stage

What Candidates Should Do Now

  1. Download the interview admit card from the official UPPSC portal using your registration details.
  2. Check the interview date, reporting time and venue printed on the admit card carefully.
  3. Carry a printed copy of the interview admit card along with a valid photo identity proof.
  4. Carry all original educational certificates, category certificate and domicile documents for verification.
  5. Reach the interview venue well before the reporting time mentioned on the admit card.
  6. Follow the instructions and dress code mentioned in the official interview notice.

Original Application Process

Preserved for recruitment history and reference after the application stage closes.

  1. Visit the official UPPSC website.
  2. Read the UPPSC PCS notification carefully.
  3. Register on the UPPSC OTR portal.
  4. Fill the online application form.
  5. Upload photograph, signature and required documents.
  6. Pay the applicable fee.
  7. Submit the form and save the confirmation page.
