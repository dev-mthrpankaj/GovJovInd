About This Recruitment

The Institute of Banking Personal Selection (IBPS) has released the IBPS Clerk XVI Recruitment 2026 notification for 11,403 Customer Service Associate (CSA) Clerk vacancies across participating public sector banks in India. The online application window was open from 1 August 2026 to 28 August 2026 (extended), with a correction window on 4-5 September 2026.

The IBPS Clerk XVI Preliminary Examination 2026 is scheduled for 10-11 October 2026. The admit card has been released on the official IBPS portal on 1 October 2026. Candidates can download their call letter using their registration number and date of birth/password. The Mains examination is expected in December 2026, followed by the final result and provisional allotment.

This is a permanent recruitment lifecycle page. Every future update — answer key, result, mains admit card, final result and joining — will be added to this same URL without creating any stage-specific page. Candidates should bookmark this page for the complete IBPS Clerk XVI journey.
