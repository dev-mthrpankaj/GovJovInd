Complete context

About This Recruitment

RSSB Forester (Vanpal) Recruitment 2026 is a Rajasthan Staff Selection Board recruitment drive to fill 259 Forester posts across Non-TSP (213) and TSP (46) areas in the Rajasthan Forest Department. The official notification (Advt. No. 01/2026) was published in January 2026, and the online application window remained open from 06 January 2026 to 11 February 2026.

The written examination was conducted on 28 June 2026 in offline OMR-based mode across various examination centres, with a total of 279,587 candidates appearing. The provisional answer key was released on 20 July 2026, and the final answer key was released on 18 August 2026. The result was declared on 14 August 2026 along with the provisional merit list and cut-off marks.

The selection process includes a Written Examination, Physical Efficiency Test (PET) and Physical Standard Test (PST), Document Verification, and Medical Examination. The PET/PST schedule is yet to be announced.

Basic eligibility is 10+2 (Intermediate) from a recognised board. The age limit is 18 to 40 years as on 01 January 2027, with an additional 3-year relaxation due to no recruitment in the last three years. The pay scale is Pay Matrix Level 8.
