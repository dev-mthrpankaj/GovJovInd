Complete context

About This Recruitment

RSSB Forester (Vanpal) Recruitment 2026 is a Rajasthan Staff Selection Board recruitment drive to fill 259 Forester posts across Non-TSP (213) and TSP (46) areas in the Rajasthan Forest Department. The official notification (Advt. No. 01/2026) was published in January 2026, and the online application window remained open from 06 January 2026 to 11 February 2026.

The written examination was conducted on 28 June 2026 in offline OMR-based mode across various examination centres, with a total of 279,587 candidates appearing. The provisional answer key was released on 20 July 2026, and the final answer key was released on 18 August 2026. The result was declared on 14 August 2026 along with the provisional merit list and cut-off marks.

The selection process includes a Written Examination, Physical Efficiency Test (PET) and Physical Standard Test (PST), Document Verification, and Medical Examination. The PET/PST schedule is yet to be announced.

Basic eligibility is 10+2 (Intermediate) from a recognised board. The age limit is 18 to 40 years as on 01 January 2027, with an additional 3-year relaxation due to no recruitment in the last three years. The pay scale is Pay Matrix Level 8.

Eligibility Criteria

Age Limit

18 to 40 years as on 01 January 2027.

An additional 3-year age relaxation is granted to all applicants as recruitment was not held in the previous 3 years. SC/ST/OBC/MBC/EWS (Male, Rajasthan Domicile): 5 years. Age relaxation for other reserved categories as per RSSB rules.

Education

10+2 (Intermediate) / Senior Secondary Examination from any recognised board in India. Knowledge of working in written Hindi in Devnagari script and knowledge of Rajasthan culture.

Candidates must have passed the Senior Secondary (10+2) examination from a recognised board.

What to do at the current stage

How to Download Final Answer Key

  1. Visit the official RSSB website at rssb.rajasthan.gov.in.
  2. Look for the 'Candidate Information' or 'Answer Key' section on the homepage.
  3. Search for 'RSSB Forester Final Answer Key 2026' link.
  4. Click the link to open the final answer key PDF in a new window.
  5. Download and save the PDF for future reference.
  6. Compare your responses with the final answer key to estimate your score.

Original Application Process

Preserved for recruitment history and reference after the application stage closes.

  1. Visit the official RSSB website at rssb.rajasthan.gov.in.
  2. Read the official notification (Advt. No. 01/2026) carefully.
  3. Register on the Rajasthan SSO portal with a valid mobile number and email ID.
  4. Login using your SSO ID and open the Recruitment Portal.
  5. Click on 'RSSB Forester Recruitment 2026' and fill the online application form.
  6. Upload photograph, signature and required documents.
  7. Pay the applicable examination fee online.
  8. Submit the form and save the confirmation page for future reference.
