Complete context

About This Recruitment

NBEMS Group A, B, C Various Post Recruitment 2026 is a National Board of Examinations in Medical Sciences (NBEMS) recruitment drive to fill 53 posts across Group A, B and C categories. The official notification was published on 27 June 2026, and the online application window remained open from 07 July 2026 to 31 July 2026.

The Computer Based Test (CBT) was conducted on 15–16 September 2026. The provisional answer key was released on 02 October 2026, and candidates can raise objections from 02 October 2026 (3 PM) to 06 October 2026 (3 PM).

Basic eligibility varies by post: Deputy Director (Medical) requires a postgraduate medical qualification, Junior Programmer requires B.Tech/BE/BCA or equivalent, Junior Accountant requires a Bachelor’s degree in Commerce/Mathematics/Statistics, Stenographer requires 10+2 with shorthand and typing skills, and Junior Assistant requires 10+2 with computer proficiency. The age limit ranges from 18 to 35 years depending on the post.

Eligibility Criteria

Age Limit

Minimum 18 years. Maximum age varies by post: Deputy Director (Medical) - 35 years, Junior Programmer - 27 years, Junior Accountant - 27 years, Stenographer - 18 to 27 years, Junior Assistant - 27 years.

Age relaxation applicable for SC, ST, OBC, PwBD, Ex-Servicemen and other reserved categories as per government rules.

Education

Post-wise qualifications: Deputy Director (Medical) - Postgraduate Medical qualification; Junior Programmer - B.Tech/BE/BCA or equivalent; Junior Accountant - B.Com or Bachelor's in Mathematics/Statistics; Stenographer - 10+2 with shorthand and typing; Junior Assistant - 10+2 with computer proficiency.

Both academic qualification and any required skill certifications are mandatory for eligibility.

Nationality / Other

Indian citizens fulfilling the post-specific eligibility conditions may apply. Refer to the official notification for complete details.

What to do at the current stage

How to Download Answer Key & Raise Objection

  1. Open the official NBEMS recruitment exam portal from Important Links.
  2. Log in using your registration credentials.
  3. Click on the 'Provisional Answer Key' tab to view the question paper with recorded responses.
  4. Compare your responses with the provisional answers.
  5. To challenge a question, click on the 'Challenge Form' tab and submit your objection with valid explanation.
  6. The objection window is open from 02 October 2026 (3 PM) to 06 October 2026 (3 PM).

Original Application Process

Preserved for recruitment history and reference after the application stage closes.

  1. Visit the official NBEMS website at natboard.edu.in.
  2. Read the official notification A-12011/3/2025-Estb carefully.
  3. Register on the NBEMS portal with valid email ID and mobile number.
  4. Fill the online application form with accurate personal and educational details.
  5. Upload photograph, signature and required documents.
  6. Pay the applicable examination fee online.
  7. Submit the form and save the confirmation page for future reference.
