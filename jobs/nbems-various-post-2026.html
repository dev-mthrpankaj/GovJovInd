Complete context

About This Recruitment

NBEMS Group A, B, C Various Post Recruitment 2026 is a recruitment drive by the National Board of Examination in Medical Sciences (NBEMS) for 53 posts across various groups. The online application window was open from 07 July 2026 to 31 July 2026.

The recruitment covers posts such as Stenographer, Junior Assistant, Deputy Director (Medical), Junior Programmer, and Junior Accountant. Candidates with qualifications ranging from 10+2 to Master's degrees, depending on the post, were eligible to apply.

The admit card for the revised exam dates of 15–16 September 2026 is now the current stage. Candidates should download their admit cards and check exam city details from the official links provided on this page.
