About This Recruitment

MPPSC Assistant Professor Recruitment 2026 is conducted by the Madhya Pradesh Public Service Commission for 1930 Assistant Professor posts across various subjects in the Higher Education Department of Madhya Pradesh. The recruitment notification was released on 28 August 2026, with online applications accepted from 28 August 2026 to 18 September 2026.

This is a prestigious academic recruitment offering permanent, gazetted positions with a starting salary of Rs.57,700 per month at Academic Level-10. The selection process includes a written examination, interview, document verification, and final merit list preparation.

The written examination was conducted from 01 June to 27 July 2025. Subject-wise results for Law, Psychology, Sanskrit, and Geology were declared on 18 August 2026. Candidates should keep their application number, roll number, and original documents ready for subsequent stages.

Eligibility Criteria

Age Limit

21 to 40 years as on 18 September 2026.

Age relaxation: SC/ST 5 years, OBC (NCL) 3 years, PwBD as per rules.

Education

Post Graduate degree in relevant subject with minimum 55% marks and NET/SET/SLET qualified or PhD degree.

PhD holders are exempted from NET/SET/SLET requirement as per UGC norms.

Nationality / Other

Indian citizens fulfilling the post-specific eligibility conditions may apply.

Refer to official notification for detailed conditions.
