Complete context

About This Recruitment

UPSSSC Combined Lower Subordinate Services (Intermediate Level) Main Examination 2026 is a recruitment drive by the Uttar Pradesh Subordinate Services Selection Commission (UPSSSC) to fill 1784 posts across 12 categories in various departments of the Uttar Pradesh government. The official notification was released on 16 October 2026 under Advertisement No. 26-Exam/2026.

The online application window will open on 10 November 2026 and close on 01 December 2026. Candidates will be able to make corrections to their application forms until 08 December 2026. The main examination date will be announced later by the commission.

Eligibility requires a valid UPSSSC PET 2025 scorecard and Intermediate (10+2) qualification from a recognised board. Candidates with zero or negative PET scores will not be shortlisted for the main examination. The age limit is 18 to 40 years as on 01 July 2026, with relaxation for reserved categories as per UPSSSC rules.

The main examination will consist of 100 multiple-choice questions for 100 marks, to be completed in 2 hours, with a negative marking of 0.25 marks for each wrong answer. The selection process includes PET-based shortlisting, main written examination, and document verification.

How to Apply Online

  1. Visit the official UPSSSC website at upsssc.gov.in.
  2. Read the official Advertisement No. 26-Exam/2026 carefully.
  3. Complete the One-Time Registration (OTR) process if not already done.
  4. Log in using your registration number and password.
  5. Fill the online application form with accurate personal, educational, and category details.
  6. Upload scanned photograph, signature, and required documents.
  7. Pay the application fee of Rs.25 through online mode.
  8. Submit the form and save the confirmation page for future reference.
