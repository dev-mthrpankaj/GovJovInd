Complete context

About This Recruitment

Border Security Force (BSF) is recruiting for 1121 posts of Head Constable Radio Operator (910 posts) and Head Constable Radio Mechanic (211 posts) under the HC RO / RM Recruitment 2025. The official notification was released in August 2025, and the online application window remained open from 24 August 2025 to 23 September 2025.

The Physical Efficiency Test (PET) and Physical Standard Test (PST) were conducted in December 2025, with the admit card released on 17 December 2025. The PET/PST result was declared on 25 May 2026. The Computer Based Test (CBT) was held on 02 November 2026, after the CBT admit card was issued on 21 October 2026.

The BSF HC RO / RM Answer Key 2026 notice has now been released on 03 November 2026, and the answer key download link activates on 04 November 2026. Following the answer key, candidates will move to document verification, the dictation and paragraph reading test, medical examination, and finally the merit list.

Basic eligibility is 10th with a 2-year ITI in a relevant trade, or 12th with Physics, Chemistry and Mathematics. The age limit is 18 to 25 years for the General category as on 23 September 2025, with age relaxation for OBC, SC and ST candidates as per BSF rules.

How to Check BSF HC RO / RM Answer Key 2026

  1. Visit the official BSF recruitment portal at rectt.bsf.gov.in.
  2. Open the Latest Notices or Answer Key section on the portal.
  3. Click on the BSF HC RO / RM Answer Key 2026 notice link.
  4. Log in with your Registration Number / Roll Number and Date of Birth.
  5. Enter the captcha code shown on the screen.
  6. Download and save the answer key PDF for future reference.

BSF HC RO / RM Recruitment 2025 has 1121 total vacancies, including 910 Head Constable (Radio Operator) posts and 211 Head Constable (Radio Mechanic) posts across various categories.

Candidates must have passed 10th with a 2-year ITI in a relevant trade, or 12th with Physics, Chemistry and Mathematics. A valid certificate in the relevant trade from a recognised institute is required.

The age limit is 18 to 25 years for General category as on 23 September 2025. OBC candidates get up to 28 years and SC / ST candidates get up to 30 years, as per BSF relaxation rules.

The BSF HC RO / RM CBT examination was conducted on 02 November 2026. The CBT admit card was released on 21 October 2026 for all registered and shortlisted candidates.

General, OBC and EWS candidates paid Rs.100. SC, ST and female candidates were exempted from the fee. Fee payment was accepted online via debit card, credit card, net banking, IMPS or wallet.

The selection process includes PET and PST, followed by a CBT exam, document verification, dictation and paragraph reading test, and finally a medical examination. Each stage is qualifying in nature.

Male candidates need a height of 168 cm (162.5 cm for ST) with chest 80-85 cm. Female candidates need a height of 157 cm (154 cm for ST). A 1.6 km run in 6.5 minutes is required for male candidates.
