Complete context

About This Recruitment

Union Public Service Commission (UPSC) conducts the Combined Defence Services Examination (I), 2027 to recruit commissioned officers for the Indian Military Academy (IMA), Indian Naval Academy (INA), Air Force Academy (AFA), and Officers' Training Academy (OTA). According to the UPSC schedule, the official notification and online application form window will open on 02 December 2026 and close on 22 December 2026 at 06:00 PM.

Total vacancies for CDS I 2027 will be notified in the detailed official advertisement on 02 December 2026. The written examination is scheduled to be conducted on 11 April 2027 across designated exam centres in India, followed by the Services Selection Board (SSB) interview, document verification, and medical examination.

Candidates holding a bachelor's degree in any discipline from a recognised university can apply for IMA and OTA, while INA requires an engineering degree and AFA requires either a degree with Physics and Mathematics at the 10+2 level or an engineering degree. The age limit is 19 to 24 years for IMA and INA, 20 to 24 years for AFA, and 19 to 25 years for OTA as on 01 July 2027.

Eligibility Criteria

Age Limit

19 to 24 years for IMA & INA; 20 to 24 years for AFA; 19 to 25 years for OTA as on 01 July 2027.

Age relaxation and exact date-of-birth brackets apply as per UPSC CDS (I) 2027 notification rules.

Education

Bachelor's degree in any stream for IMA and OTA; Engineering degree for INA; Bachelor's degree with Physics and Mathematics at 10+2 level or Engineering degree for AFA.

Final-year degree candidates may also apply subject to submitting proof of passing by the prescribed date in the official notification.

Nationality / Other

Unmarried Indian citizens (and eligible subjects as per UPSC rules) meeting academy-specific physical and medical standards may apply.

Refer to official notification for detailed conditions.

What to do at the current stage

How to Apply for UPSC CDS 1 2027

  1. Visit the official UPSC online application portal at upsconline.nic.in from 02 December 2026.
  2. Complete One Time Registration (OTR) or Universal Registration on the portal if not already registered.
  3. Log in and select Combined Defence Services Examination (I), 2027 from the active examination list.
  4. Fill in personal, educational, and academy preference details for IMA, INA, AFA, and OTA carefully.
  5. Upload your recent photograph, signature, and valid photo identity proof in the prescribed format.
  6. Pay the application fee of Rs.200 online (exempted for SC, ST, and female candidates).
  7. Submit the application before 22 December 2026 (06:00 PM) and print the confirmation page.

    Frequently Asked Questions

    The online application process for UPSC Combined Defence Services Examination (I), 2027 starts on 02 December 2026 on the official portal upsconline.nic.in alongside the detailed notification.

    The last date to submit the UPSC CDS 1 2027 online application form and pay the fee is 22 December 2026 up to 06:00 PM. Candidates should submit forms early to avoid last-day server congestion.

    The Union Public Service Commission will conduct the UPSC CDS (I) 2027 written examination on 11 April 2027 across examination centres in India. Admit cards will be issued before the exam date.

    The exact number of vacancies for UPSC CDS 1 2027 has not yet been announced. Academy-wise seat details for IMA, INA, AFA, and OTA will be published in the official notification on 02 December 2026.

    Graduates in any stream can apply for IMA and OTA, while INA requires an Engineering degree and AFA requires a degree with Physics and Mathematics at 10+2 level or an Engineering degree.

    As on 01 July 2027, the age limit is 19 to 24 years for IMA and INA, 20 to 24 years for Air Force Academy (AFA), and 19 to 25 years for Officers' Training Academy (OTA).

    General, OBC, and EWS male candidates must pay an application fee of Rs.200 online or via SBI challan, while SC, ST, and female candidates are completely exempted from paying any fee.

    Visit upsconline.nic.in from 02 December 2026, complete One Time Registration, fill in the CDS (I) 2027 form with academy preferences, upload photo and signature, pay Rs.200 if applicable, and print the confirmation.

    Selection under UPSC CDS (I) 2027 consists of a written examination followed by the Services Selection Board (SSB) interview, document verification, medical examination, and final merit list.

    Commissioned officers appointed through UPSC CDS receive Level-10 pay matrix starting at Rs.56,100 per month along with Military Service Pay and allowances as prescribed in the official UPSC notification.
