Complete context

About This Recruitment

Jharkhand Staff Selection Commission (JSSC) has released the notification for the Jharkhand Trained Assistant Acharya Combined Competitive Examination (JTAACCE) 2026 under Advertisement No. 04/2026. The recruitment drive aims to fill 6101 regular vacancies for Para Teacher (Trained Assistant Acharya) posts in government schools across Jharkhand.

The posts are divided into Intermediate Trained Assistant Teacher for Classes 1–5 and Graduate Trained Assistant Teacher for Classes 6–8 in Language, Social Science, and Mathematics & Science subjects. A connected backlog recruitment (Advertisement No. 05/2026) covers an additional 1198 vacancies reserved for existing Para Teachers, taking the combined total to 7299 posts.

The online application window is open from 20 September 2026 to 18 October 2026. The last date for fee payment and photo/signature upload is 22 October 2026. The application correction window will remain open on 23–24 October 2026. The exam date and admit card schedule will be announced by JSSC in due course.

Selection is based on a single-stage written examination (OMR/CBT mode) followed by document verification. Candidates must have passed JTET/CTET or an equivalent Teacher Eligibility Test as prescribed in the official notification.
