Complete context

About This Recruitment

Union Public Service Commission (UPSC) conducts the National Defence Academy and Naval Academy Examination (I), 2027 twice a year to recruit unmarried male and female candidates into the Army, Navy, and Air Force wings of the National Defence Academy (NDA) and the 10+2 Cadet Entry Scheme at the Indian Naval Academy (INA).

As per the recruitment schedule, the detailed official notification and online application link for UPSC NDA & NA (I) 2027 will open on 02 December 2026 at upsconline.nic.in and upsc.gov.in. Eligible candidates can submit their online application forms until 22 December 2026 up to 06:00 PM, and the written examination is scheduled for 11 April 2027.

For the Army Wing of NDA, candidates must have passed or be appearing in Class 12 (10+2 pattern) from a recognised board in any stream. For the Air Force and Naval Wings of NDA and the 10+2 Cadet Entry Scheme at INA, candidates must have passed or be appearing in Class 12 with Physics, Chemistry, and Mathematics.

Selection consists of a 900-mark UPSC Written Examination (Mathematics and General Ability Test), followed by the 900-mark Services Selection Board (SSB) Intelligence and Personality Test, Medical Examination, and Final Merit List. This permanent page tracks every stage from notification to final result.

Eligibility Criteria

Age Limit

16.5 to 19.5 years (exact date-of-birth range to be confirmed in the 02 December 2026 notification).

Only unmarried male and female candidates born within the notified UPSC date-of-birth window are eligible; no category-based upper age relaxation applies in NDA.

Education

10+2 (Intermediate) pass or appearing in any stream for NDA Army Wing; 10+2 with Physics, Chemistry and Mathematics for Air Force, Navy and INA.

Candidates appearing in Class 12 may apply subject to submitting proof of passing by the deadline specified in the official UPSC notification.

Nationality / Other

Must be an unmarried Indian citizen (or eligible subject of Nepal / specified migrant category) meeting prescribed physical and medical standards.

Refer to official notification for detailed conditions.

What to do at the current stage

How to Apply Online for UPSC NDA & NA I 2027

  1. Visit the official UPSC online application portal at upsconline.nic.in once the link activates on 02 December 2026.
  2. Complete One Time Registration (OTR) if you are a new user, or log in with your existing OTR credentials.
  3. Select National Defence Academy and Naval Academy Examination (I), 2027 and fill in personal, educational, and wing preferences carefully.
  4. Upload scanned recent photograph, signature, and valid photo identity document as per UPSC specifications.
  5. Pay the Rs.100 application fee online (exempted for SC, ST, female candidates, and eligible wards of JCOs/NCOs/ORs).
  6. Submit the application before 22 December 2026 (06:00 PM) and print the confirmation page for future stages.

    Frequently Asked Questions

    The online application form for UPSC NDA & NA Examination (I), 2027 will open on 02 December 2026 on the official UPSC portal upsconline.nic.in.

    The last date to submit the online application form and pay the fee is 22 December 2026 up to 06:00 PM. Candidates should apply early to avoid last-day server load.

    The Union Public Service Commission will conduct the NDA & NA Examination (I), 2027 written test on 11 April 2027 across designated exam centres in India.

    The exact wing-wise vacancy count for Army, Navy, Air Force, and Naval Academy will be announced by UPSC in the detailed official notification on 02 December 2026.

    Unmarried male and female Indian citizens aged 16.5 to 19.5 years who have passed or are appearing in Class 12 from a recognised board can apply, subject to wing-specific subject rules.

    For the NDA Army Wing, Class 12 pass or appearing in any stream is accepted. For the Air Force, Navy Wing, and Indian Naval Academy, Class 12 with Physics, Chemistry, and Mathematics is mandatory.

    General, OBC, and EWS male candidates must pay Rs.100 online or via SBI challan. SC, ST, female candidates, and eligible wards of JCOs/NCOs/ORs are exempted from paying any fee.

    Visit upsconline.nic.in from 02 December 2026, complete One Time Registration (OTR), fill out the NDA & NA (I) 2027 form, upload photo and signature, pay the fee, and print the confirmation page.

    Selection comprises a 900-mark Written Examination (Mathematics and General Ability Test), a 900-mark SSB Intelligence and Personality Test, Document Verification, Medical Examination, and Final Merit List.

    During training at NDA and INA, cadets receive a fixed stipend as per defence pay rules, and upon commissioning as Lieutenant or equivalent rank, they are placed in Level 10 of the 7th CPC Pay Matrix.

    The official websites are https://upsc.gov.in/ for notifications and results, and https://upsconline.nic.in/ for One Time Registration and online application submission.
