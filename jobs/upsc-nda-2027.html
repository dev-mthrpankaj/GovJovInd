Complete context

About This Recruitment

Union Public Service Commission (UPSC) conducts the National Defence Academy and Naval Academy Examination (I), 2027 twice a year to recruit unmarried male and female candidates into the Army, Navy, and Air Force wings of the National Defence Academy (NDA) and the 10+2 Cadet Entry Scheme at the Indian Naval Academy (INA).

As per the recruitment schedule, the detailed official notification and online application link for UPSC NDA & NA (I) 2027 will open on 02 December 2026 at upsconline.nic.in and upsc.gov.in. Eligible candidates can submit their online application forms until 22 December 2026 up to 06:00 PM, and the written examination is scheduled for 11 April 2027.

For the Army Wing of NDA, candidates must have passed or be appearing in Class 12 (10+2 pattern) from a recognised board in any stream. For the Air Force and Naval Wings of NDA and the 10+2 Cadet Entry Scheme at INA, candidates must have passed or be appearing in Class 12 with Physics, Chemistry, and Mathematics.

Selection consists of a 900-mark UPSC Written Examination (Mathematics and General Ability Test), followed by the 900-mark Services Selection Board (SSB) Intelligence and Personality Test, Medical Examination, and Final Merit List.
