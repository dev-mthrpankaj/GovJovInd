About This Recruitment

NTA UGC NET June 2026 is a national-level eligibility exam conducted by the National Testing Agency (NTA) on behalf of the University Grants Commission (UGC) for Assistant Professor and Junior Research Fellowship (JRF) positions in Indian universities and colleges.

The exam was held on 22, 23, 24, 25, 29 and 30 June 2026, followed by a re-exam on 09 and 10 September 2026 for English, Commerce and Sociology subjects. The result has now been declared and candidates can download their score card, cut off and certificate from the official NTA portal.

This page tracks the complete UGC NET June 2026 lifecycle — from notification and application to final result and certificate issue — on a single permanent URL.

Eligibility Criteria

Age Limit

Minimum 21 years, Maximum 30 years for JRF. No upper age limit for Assistant Professor.

Age relaxation applicable as per UGC NET rules.

Education

Master's Degree in any subject with 55% marks (50% for SC/ST/OBC/PwD) from a recognized university.

Candidates appearing in the final year of Master's degree may also apply provisionally.

Nationality / Other

Indian citizens fulfilling the post-specific eligibility conditions may apply.

Refer to official notification for detailed conditions.

What to do at the current stage

How to Download UGC NET June 2026 Result

  1. Visit the official UGC NET website at ugcnet.nta.nic.in
  2. Click on the 'Result' section available on the homepage.
  3. Find and open the 'NTA UGC NET Result 2026' link.
  4. Enter your Application Number and Date of Birth or Roll Number, if required.
  5. Complete the CAPTCHA code or other security verification, if prompted.
  6. Click on the Submit button to view your NTA UGC NET June 2026 Result.
  7. Verify your responses and compare them with the official answer key.
  8. Download the UGC NET June 2026 Result PDF for future reference.
  9. Keep a copy of the downloaded result for future reference until the recruitment process is completed.

Original Application Process

Preserved for recruitment history and reference after the application stage closes.

  1. Visit the official UGC NET website at ugcnet.nta.nic.in
  2. Click on the 'Registration' or 'Apply Online' link.
  3. Register with your basic details and generate login credentials.
  4. Fill the application form carefully with educational and personal details.
  5. Upload scanned photograph, signature and required documents.
  6. Pay the applicable fee through online payment modes.
  7. Submit the form and save the confirmation page for future reference.

Quick answers

Frequently Asked Questions

NTA UGC NET June 2026 is a national-level eligibility exam conducted by the National Testing Agency (NTA) on behalf of the University Grants Commission (UGC) for Assistant Professor and Junior Research Fellowship (JRF) positions in Indian universities and colleges.

The UGC NET June 2026 exam was held on 22, 23, 24, 25, 29 and 30 June 2026. A re-exam was conducted on 09 September 2026 for English and Commerce, and on 10 September 2026 for Sociology.

Yes, the NTA UGC NET June 2026 Result has been declared. Candidates can download their score card from the official NTA portal using their application number and date of birth.

Visit the official UGC NET website at ugcnet.nta.nic.in, click on the Result section, enter your Application Number and Date of Birth, complete the CAPTCHA, and download your result PDF. Direct links are also available in the Important Links section on this page.

The application fee is Rs. 1150 for General category, Rs. 600 for Gen-EWS/OBC-NCL, and Rs. 325 for SC/ST/PH/Third Gender candidates. The fee can be paid through online modes like debit card, credit card, or net banking.

Candidates must have a Master's Degree in any subject with 55% marks (50% for SC/ST/OBC/PwD) from a recognized university. Candidates appearing in the final year of their Master's degree may also apply provisionally.

For JRF, the minimum age is 21 years and the maximum age is 30 years. For Assistant Professor, there is no upper age limit. Age relaxation is applicable as per UGC NET rules.

The exam is conducted in Computer-Based Test (CBT) mode with 2 papers, 150 questions, 300 marks, and a duration of 3 hours. There is no negative marking. Paper 1 covers Teaching & Research Aptitude, and Paper 2 is subject-specific.

Qualified candidates can download their certificate from the official NTA examination services portal. The direct link is available in the Important Links section on this page.

The selection process includes a written exam (CBT), answer key and objection window, result declaration, and certificate issue for qualified candidates. There is no interview or document verification stage in UGC NET.

No. This permanent recruitment page is designed to carry all later official stages on the same URL. The canonical URL will remain the same throughout the recruitment lifecycle.
