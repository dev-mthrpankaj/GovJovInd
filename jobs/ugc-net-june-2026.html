About This Recruitment

NTA UGC NET June 2026 is the national eligibility examination for Junior Research Fellowship and Assistant Professor eligibility. The main June examination was conducted across multiple dates from 22 to 30 June 2026.

The lifecycle has progressed through city intimation, admit card, examination, answer key and result. A September re-examination has now been scheduled for affected subjects, with English and Commerce on 9 September 2026 and Sociology on 10 September 2026.

Candidates covered by the re-examination should download the latest admit card from the official examination services portal and carefully verify their subject, examination date, reporting time, centre and instructions.
