About This Recruitment

NTA UGC NET June 2026 is a national-level examination conducted by the National Testing Agency (NTA) on behalf of the University Grants Commission (UGC). It determines eligibility for the post of Assistant Professor and for Junior Research Fellowship (JRF) in Indian universities and colleges.

The June 2026 exam was held on 22, 23, 24, 25, 29 and 30 June 2026. A re-exam for English, Commerce and Sociology was conducted on 09 and 10 September 2026. The result for the re-exam has now been declared on 18 September 2026.

The application process was open from 29 April 2026 to 24 May 2026. This permanent page will continue to carry all future updates, including final answer key, cut off, score card and final merit list, without changing the URL.
