Complete context

About This Recruitment

Haryana Teacher Eligibility Test (HTET) 2025 is the state teacher eligibility examination conducted for Level 1 Primary Teacher, Level 2 Trained Graduate Teacher (TGT), and Level 3 Post Graduate Teacher (PGT). The application process ran from 24 December 2025 to 4 January 2026.

The HTET 2025 examination was held on 4 and 5 July 2026 after the admit card was released on 1 July 2026. The recruitment lifecycle has since progressed through answer key, final answer key, result, score card/certificate and OMR-sheet availability.

Candidates who appeared in HTET can use the current official portal links to download their OMR sheet and score card/certificate. Earlier admit-card, answer-key and result links are retained below as useful lifecycle history.

Eligibility Criteria

Age Limit

No specific minimum or maximum age is stated in the supplied HTET information.

For age-related eligibility questions, refer to the official HTET information bulletin.

Education

Level 1, Level 2 and Level 3 have different educational requirements. Check the level-wise qualification details and official information bulletin before relying on eligibility.

Qualification conditions vary by HTET level and prescribed teacher-training route.

Nationality / Other

HTET is for teacher eligibility in Haryana. Candidates should verify all level-specific conditions in the official information bulletin.

Refer to official notification for detailed conditions.
