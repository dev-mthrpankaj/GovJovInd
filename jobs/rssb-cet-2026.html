Complete context

About This Recruitment

Rajasthan Staff Selection Board (RSSB) conducts the Common Eligibility Test (CET) Senior Secondary Level Examination 2026 (Advertisement No. 08/2026) as a state-level qualifying test for multiple 10+2 level government vacancies in Rajasthan. Qualifying candidates become eligible to apply for subsequent departmental recruitments such as Lower Division Clerk (LDC) / Clerk Grade-II, Junior Assistant, Rajasthan Police Constable, Forester (Vanpal), Hostel Superintendent, and Jamadar Grade-II.

The online application process for Rajasthan RSSB CET Senior Secondary Level Examination 2026 remained open from 24 June 2026 to 23 July 2026 via the Rajasthan SSO portal. Eligibility required passing Higher Secondary or Intermediate (10+2) from a recognised board in India, with an age limit of 18 to 40 years as on 01 January 2027.

RSSB released the Exam City Intimation Slip on 21 November 2026 and activated the provisional e-admit card download link on 26 November 2026. The offline OMR-based examination is scheduled for 01 December, 02 December, and 03 December 2026 across 38 districts in two shifts each day: 10:00 AM to 12:30 PM (gate closes at 09:00 AM) and 03:00 PM to 05:30 PM (gate closes at 02:00 PM).

Candidates must download and print their admit card from the official RSSB portal, Rajasthan Recruitment Portal, or Rajasthan SSO dashboard and carry a valid original photo ID proof along with a recent colour passport-size photograph to the examination centre. Upcoming stages including the provisional answer key, objection window, and CET result/scorecard will be updated on this permanent page.

What to do at the current stage

How to Download RSSB CET 12th Level Admit Card 2026

  1. Open the official RSSB Admit Card portal at rssb.rajasthan.gov.in/admitcards or visit recruitment.rajasthan.gov.in.
  2. Select Get Admit Card and choose Common Eligibility Test (Senior Secondary Level) 2026.
  3. Enter your Application Number, Date of Birth, and security captcha code, or log in using your Rajasthan SSO ID at sso.rajasthan.gov.in.
  4. Download the provisional e-admit card PDF and verify your name, roll number, exam date, shift timing, and exam centre address.
  5. Take a clear printout of the admit card and reach your examination centre before the gate closing time (09:00 AM for Shift 1 and 02:00 PM for Shift 2).
  6. Carry a valid original photo ID proof, a recent colour passport-size photograph, and a transparent blue ballpoint pen on exam day.

Original Application Process

Preserved for recruitment history and reference after the application stage closes.

  1. Visit the Rajasthan SSO portal at sso.rajasthan.gov.in and log in using your SSO ID and password.
  2. Open the Recruitment Portal under the Citizen Apps (G2C) section.
  3. Read Advertisement No. 08/2026 for Rajasthan RSSB CET Senior Secondary Level Examination 2026 carefully.
  4. Fill in accurate personal, address, and 10+2 educational qualification details.
  5. Upload scanned copies of your photograph, signature, and required documents.
  6. Pay the applicable fee (Rs.600 or Rs.400) via online payment or E-Mitra.
  7. Preview all entered fields, submit the form, and save the final printed application form.

Quick answers

Frequently Asked Questions

Yes, the Rajasthan Staff Selection Board (RSSB) released the CET Senior Secondary Level Admit Card 2026 on 26 November 2026. Registered candidates can download their provisional e-admit card from rssb.rajasthan.gov.in, recruitment.rajasthan.gov.in, or through Rajasthan SSO login.

The Rajasthan Staff Selection Board (RSSB) will conduct the CET Senior Secondary Level Examination 2026 on 01 December, 02 December, and 03 December 2026. The offline OMR-based test will be held in two daily shifts: 10:00 AM to 12:30 PM and 03:00 PM to 05:30 PM across 38 districts.

Visit rssb.rajasthan.gov.in/admitcards or recruitment.rajasthan.gov.in and select CET Senior Secondary Level 2026 under Get Admit Card. Enter your application number, date of birth, and captcha code, or log in via sso.rajasthan.gov.in to download and print the hall ticket PDF.

The Rajasthan Staff Selection Board (RSSB) released the CET Senior Secondary Level Exam City Intimation Slip 2026 on 21 November 2026. Candidates can verify their allotted examination district on sso.rajasthan.gov.in or recruitment.rajasthan.gov.in before downloading the admit card.

For the morning shift (10:00 AM to 12:30 PM), reporting starts at 08:00 AM and entry gates close at 09:00 AM. For the afternoon shift (03:00 PM to 05:30 PM), reporting starts at 01:00 PM and entry gates close at 02:00 PM. Always confirm exact timings on your printed admit card.

Candidates must carry a clear printed copy of the RSSB CET Senior Secondary Admit Card 2026, a valid original photo ID proof such as Aadhaar Card, PAN Card, Voter ID, or Driving Licence, a recent colour passport-size photograph, and a transparent blue ballpoint pen.

Candidates must have passed Higher Secondary or Intermediate (10+2) from a recognised education board in India to be eligible for the Rajasthan RSSB CET Senior Secondary Level Examination 2026 under Advertisement No. 08/2026.

The prescribed age limit for Rajasthan RSSB CET Senior Secondary Level 2026 is 18 to 40 years calculated as on 01 January 2027. Upper age relaxation applies to reserved category candidates of Rajasthan in accordance with state government recruitment rules.

The online application fee was Rs.600 for General and other state candidates, and Rs.400 for EWS, OBC, SBC, SC, ST, and PH candidates of Rajasthan. The application correction fee was Rs.300, payable through online banking or E-Mitra portals.

The Rajasthan RSSB CET Senior Secondary Level Examination 2026 is an offline OMR-based objective test carrying 120 multiple-choice questions for 240 marks. Candidates get 2 hours 30 minutes (150 minutes) to complete the paper, with each question carrying 2 marks.

Qualifying the Rajasthan RSSB CET Senior Secondary Level Examination 2026 makes candidates eligible to apply for state 12th-level posts including Clerk Grade-II (LDC), Junior Assistant, Rajasthan Police Constable, Forester (Vanpal), Hostel Superintendent, and Jamadar Grade-II.

The official website of the Rajasthan Staff Selection Board (RSSB) is rssb.rajasthan.gov.in. Candidates can also access admit cards, exam city slips, and recruitment notices through recruitment.rajasthan.gov.in and the Rajasthan SSO portal at sso.rajasthan.gov.in.
