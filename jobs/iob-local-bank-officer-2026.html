About This Recruitment

Indian Overseas Bank (IOB) has released the Local Bank Officer (LBO) Recruitment 2026 notification for 250 posts across Tamil Nadu, Karnataka, Maharashtra, and Gujarat. The online application process was open from 08 August 2026 to 24 August 2026.

This recruitment is a significant opportunity for graduates with mandatory local language proficiency looking for a banking career. The selection process typically involves a written examination, possibly followed by an interview or other stages as per the official notification. The admit card for the written examination has now been released.

Candidates who have successfully applied should download their admit card and prepare for the upcoming examination scheduled in September 2026. All future updates, including answer keys, results, and final merit lists, will be published on this same permanent page.
