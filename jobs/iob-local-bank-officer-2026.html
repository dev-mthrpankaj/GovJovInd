About This Recruitment

Indian Overseas Bank (IOB) has released the recruitment notification for Local Bank Officer (LBO) 2026 with a total of 250 vacancies across Tamil Nadu, Karnataka, Maharashtra, and Gujarat. The online application window was open from 08 August 2026 to 24 August 2026.

The recruitment is now progressing to the examination stage. The exam is scheduled for 24 and 29 September 2026. Candidates who successfully submitted their applications should prepare for the upcoming exam and watch for the admit card release on the official IOB portal.

This permanent lifecycle page will carry every official update for IOB LBO 2026 — including exam city intimation, admit card, answer key, result, and final merit list — without changing the URL.
