Complete context

About This Recruitment

AFCAT 02/2026 is the Indian Air Force officer-entry examination for courses commencing in July 2027. The official notification covers Flying Branch, Ground Duty (Technical and Non-Technical), NCC Special Entry and GATE Score Entry.

The official notification lists 353 AFCAT-entry vacancies across Flying, Ground Duty Technical and Ground Duty Non-Technical branches. NCC Special Entry and GATE Score Entry have separate vacancy provisions described in the notification and are not added to this AFCAT vacancy total.

The written AFCAT examination was scheduled for 8 August 2026. The supplied latest update dated 9 September 2026 reports that the AFCAT 02/2026 result has been released; candidates should use the official AFCAT portal for their result and subsequent AFSB instructions.

Eligibility Criteria

Age Limit

Flying Branch: 20–24 years as on 1 July 2027. Ground Duty (Technical and Non-Technical): 20–26 years as on 1 July 2027.

Relaxations/conditions, including applicable Flying Branch CPL provisions, are governed by the official notification.

Education

Branch-wise qualification applies: graduation / B.E. / B.Tech / specified professional or postgraduate qualification, generally with prescribed marks and subject requirements.

Eligibility differs by Flying, AE(L), AE(M), Weapon Systems, Administration, Logistics, Accounts, Education and Meteorology branches; verify the official notification.

Nationality / Other

Indian citizens (men and women) are invited subject to branch, marital-status, physical and medical eligibility conditions prescribed by the Indian Air Force.

Refer to official notification for detailed conditions.
