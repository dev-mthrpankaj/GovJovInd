Complete context

About This Recruitment

SSB Head Constable (Ministerial) Examination 2020 is a Sashastra Seema Bal recruitment drive to fill 115 Head Constable (Ministerial) posts under Advertisement No. 338/RC/SSB/HC(Min)/2020. The official notification was published in July 2021, and the online application window remained open from 24 July 2021 to 22 August 2021.

The Physical Efficiency Test (PET) and Physical Standard Test (PST) were conducted in two phases — Phase 1 from 28 January 2026 onwards and Phase 2 for remaining candidates from 09 February 2026 onwards. The Computer Based Test (CBT) was held on 07 July 2026, and the provisional answer key was released on 09 July 2026. CBT marks were released on 14 October 2026 and will remain available until 24 October 2026 (11:30 AM).

Basic eligibility is 12th (Intermediate) from a recognised board plus typing proficiency of 35 words per minute in English or 30 words per minute in Hindi. The age limit is 18 to 25 years as on 22 August 2021, with relaxation for reserved categories as per SSB rules. The selection process includes PET/PST, Written Exam (CBT), Skill Test (Typing Test), Document Verification, and Medical Examination.
