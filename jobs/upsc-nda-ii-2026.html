Complete context

About This Recruitment

UPSC NDA & NA Examination (II) 2026 is a Union Public Service Commission recruitment drive for admission to the National Defence Academy (158th Course) and Naval Academy (120th Course). The official notification was released on 20 May 2026 with 394 total vacancies across Army, Navy, Air Force and Naval Academy wings.

The online application window remained open from 20 May 2026 to 09 June 2026, later extended to 11 June 2026 (6:00 PM). The written examination was conducted on 13 September 2026. The provisional answer key was released on 16 September 2026. The written result was declared on 09 October 2026, and shortlisted candidates will proceed to the Services Selection Board (SSB) Interview.

Basic eligibility includes 10+2 (Intermediate) from a recognised board with Physics and Mathematics for Navy, Air Force and Naval Academy wings. The age limit is 16.5 to 19.5 years. Candidates must be unmarried male or female candidates fulfilling the physical and medical standards prescribed by UPSC.

The selection process includes a Written Examination (Mathematics & GAT), SSB Interview, and Medical Examination. The final merit list will be prepared based on the combined performance in the written test and SSB Interview.
