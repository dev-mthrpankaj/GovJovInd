About This Recruitment

Union Public Service Commission (UPSC) had invited applications for the National Defence Academy and Naval Academy Examination (II), 2026. This recruitment drive covers a total of 394 vacancies across Army, Navy, Air Force, and Naval Academy cadet entries.

The written examination was conducted on 13 September 2026. The provisional answer key has now been released, and candidates can raise objections within the official window. This page tracks every stage of the UPSC NDA II 2026 recruitment lifecycle, from notification to final merit list.

Candidates are advised to check the official UPSC website for the latest notices and download the answer key from the link provided in the Important Links section below.

Eligibility Criteria

Age Limit

16.5 to 19.5 years as per official notification.

Age relaxation applicable as per UPSC rules.

Education

10+2 Intermediate or equivalent from a recognized board.

For Navy, Air Force, and Naval Academy entries, Physics and Mathematics are mandatory.

Nationality / Other

Indian citizens, unmarried male and female candidates fulfilling the prescribed physical and medical standards.

Refer to official notification for detailed conditions.
