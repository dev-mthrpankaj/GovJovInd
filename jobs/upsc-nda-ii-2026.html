About This Recruitment

UPSC National Defence Academy and Naval Academy Examination (II), 2026 is conducted by the Union Public Service Commission for admission to the Army, Navy and Air Force wings of the NDA and the 10+2 Cadet Entry Scheme of the Indian Naval Academy. A total of 394 vacancies are listed in the available recruitment information.

The application process started on 20 May 2026 and, after extension, closed on 11 June 2026 at 6:00 PM. The written examination is scheduled for 13 September 2026. The admit-card stage is now active, so candidates should download their hall ticket and verify the reporting time, exam centre and instructions printed on it.

For Army Wing, candidates need 10+2 from a recognized board. Navy, Air Force and Naval Academy entries require 10+2 with Physics and Mathematics. Candidates should read the official UPSC notification for complete age, physical standards and service-specific conditions.
