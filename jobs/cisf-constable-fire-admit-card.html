Complete context

About This Recruitment

Central Industrial Security Force (CISF) initiated the CISF Constable / Fire Recruitment 2024 to fill 1130 Constable / Fire posts across India. The online application window remained open from 31 August 2024 to 30 September 2024, with the same deadline for online fee payment.

Following the application stage, CISF released the DV, PET, and PST admit cards on 16 December 2024 and conducted the Physical Efficiency Test (PET), Physical Standards Test (PST), and Document Verification from 24 December 2024 to 20 January 2025.

For candidates who qualified for the Written Examination, CISF released the Exam City Details on 17 November 2026 and published the CISF Constable Fire Admit Card 2026 on 27 November 2026. The Written Examination is scheduled for 01 December 2026. Candidates must carry a printed copy of the admit card along with a valid original photo ID to the examination centre.

Candidates applying for Constable / Fire posts must have passed 10+2 (Intermediate) with Science as a subject and meet the prescribed physical standards of minimum 170 cm height and 80 cm to 85 cm chest measurement. The prescribed age limit is 18 to 23 years as on 30 September 2024, with age relaxation as per CISF rules.
