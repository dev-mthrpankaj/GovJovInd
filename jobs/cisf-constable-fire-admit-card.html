Complete context

About This Recruitment

Central Industrial Security Force (CISF) initiated the CISF Constable / Fire Recruitment 2024 to fill 1130 Constable / Fire posts across India. The online application window remained open from 31 August 2024 to 30 September 2024, with the same deadline for online fee payment.

Following the application stage, CISF released the DV, PET, and PST admit cards on 16 December 2024 and conducted the Physical Efficiency Test (PET), Physical Standards Test (PST), and Document Verification from 24 December 2024 to 20 January 2025.

For candidates who qualified for the Written Examination, CISF released the Exam City Details on 17 November 2026 and published the CISF Constable Fire Admit Card 2026 on 27 November 2026. The Written Examination is scheduled for 01 December 2026. Candidates must carry a printed copy of the admit card along with a valid original photo ID to the examination centre.

Candidates applying for Constable / Fire posts must have passed 10+2 (Intermediate) with Science as a subject and meet the prescribed physical standards of minimum 170 cm height and 80 cm to 85 cm chest measurement. The prescribed age limit is 18 to 23 years as on 30 September 2024, with age relaxation as per CISF rules.

What to do at the current stage

How to Download CISF Constable Fire Admit Card 2026

  1. Click the Download Admit Card link in the Important Links section to open the official CISF exam portal.
  2. Enter your Enrollment Number, Registration Number, or Date of Birth in the candidate login form.
  3. Submit the credentials to view your CISF Constable Fire Admit Card 2026 on screen.
  4. Verify your name, roll number, examination date (01 December 2026), shift, reporting time, and exam venue.
  5. Download the admit card PDF and take a clear printout to carry to the examination centre.
  6. Keep a valid original photo ID proof and recent passport-size photographs ready for exam day verification.

Original Application Process

Preserved for recruitment history and reference after the application stage closes.

  1. Visit the official CISF recruitment portal at cisfrectt.in.
  2. Complete new registration using a valid email ID and mobile number.
  3. Log in with registration credentials and fill out the Constable / Fire 2024 application form.
  4. Upload scanned copies of photograph, signature, and required certificates.
  5. Pay the application fee of Rs.100 online (if applicable).
  6. Submit the application form and save the final confirmation printout for future stages.
