Complete context

About This Recruitment

Uttar Pradesh Public Service Commission (UPPSC) has published the official notification (Advt No. A-6/E-1/2026) for the UPPSC Civil Judge (Junior Division) PCS J Recruitment 2026 to fill 365 Civil Judge (Junior Division) posts in Uttar Pradesh. The online application window opened on 20 November 2026 and will remain active until 21 December 2026.

Of the 365 total vacancies, 148 posts are for General (UR), 98 for OBC, 36 for EWS, 76 for SC and 7 for ST candidates. Applicants must hold a Bachelor Degree in Law (LLB) from a recognised university in India, be enrolled as an Advocate and possess at least 1 year of legal practice as prescribed in the detailed advertisement.

The age limit is 22 to 35 years as on 01 July 2027 (born between 02 July 1992 and 01 July 2005), with upper age relaxation for eligible reserved categories as per UPPSC rules. The application fee is Rs.125 for General, OBC and EWS candidates, Rs.65 for SC and ST candidates, and Rs.25 for PH (Divyang) candidates.

Selection is conducted through Preliminary Examination, Main Written Examination and Interview, followed by Document Verification and Final Merit List. Candidates must complete One Time Registration (OTR) at otr.pariksha.nic.in before submitting the form on uppsc.up.nic.in, and corrections are permitted until 28 December 2026.
