About This Recruitment

Uttar Pradesh Education Service Selection Commission (UPESSC) has released the recruitment notification for the post of Principal in government colleges across Uttar Pradesh. This drive aims to fill 111 posts through a structured selection process. The online application window was open from 21 July 2026 to 20 August 2026.

The recruitment is for the post of Principal, a senior academic leadership role. Eligible candidates are those with a Ph.D. degree and at least 15 years of teaching/research experience, among other criteria. The selection process involves a written examination, followed by document verification and a final merit list.

This page is designed as a permanent lifecycle page. It will carry every official update—from admit card and exam date to answer key, result, and final merit list—without changing the URL. Candidates are advised to bookmark this page for continuous updates.
