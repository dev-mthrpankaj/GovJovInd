About This Recruitment

Uttar Pradesh Education Services Selection Commission (UPESSC) Principal Recruitment 2026 is a state-level recruitment drive for 111 Principal posts in government-aided degree colleges across Uttar Pradesh. The online application window was open from 21 July 2026 to 20 August 2026, and the written examination was conducted on 24 September 2026.

The provisional answer key was released on 26 September 2026 on the official website upessc.up.gov.in. Candidates who appeared in the written examination can now check their response sheet and raise objections against any incorrect answer from 26 September to 2 October 2026.

This permanent page will continue to carry every later official stage, including the revised answer key, result, interview schedule, document verification and final merit list, without changing the URL.
