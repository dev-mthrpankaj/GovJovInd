Complete context

About This Recruitment

Uttar Pradesh Education Service Selection Commission (UPESSC) released Advertisement No. 02/2026 for UPESSC Principal Recruitment 2026 to fill 111 Principal posts in higher education colleges across Uttar Pradesh. The online application window ran from 21 July 2026 to 20 August 2026, with fee payment closing on 20 August 2026 and form correction closing on 23 August 2026.

UPESSC issued exam city details on 14 September 2026 and admit cards on 21 September 2026. The written examination was conducted on 24 September 2026, followed by the provisional answer key on 26 September 2026. The UPESSC Principal Result 2026 and official result notice were declared on 20 November 2026 on upessc.up.gov.in.

Selected candidates are appointed in Pay Level 14 with a pay scale of Rs.144200 to Rs.218200 per month. The appointment tenure for a college Principal is 5 years, extendable for another 5 years based on satisfactory performance evaluation by a university-constituted committee, after which the incumbent returns to the parent institution as Professor.

Eligibility requires a Ph.D. degree, Professor or Associate Professor rank with at least 15 years of teaching or research experience in universities, colleges or higher education institutions, at least 10 research publications in peer-reviewed or UGC-listed journals, and a minimum research score of 110 as per Table 2. Maximum age is 62 years as on 01 July 2026, with BCI norms for Law colleges and NCTE norms for B.Ed. colleges.
