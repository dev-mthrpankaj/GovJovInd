Complete context

About This Recruitment

PFRDA Assistant Manager Recruitment 2026 is a Pension Fund Regulatory and Development Authority (PFRDA) recruitment drive to fill 30 Officer Grade ‘A’ (Assistant Manager) posts. The official notification (Advt. No. 03/2026) was released on 3 September 2026, and the online application window remained open from 3 September 2026 to 24 September 2026.

The Phase 1 online examination was conducted on 15 October 2026. The Phase 1 result was declared on 4 November 2026, and candidates shortlisted from this stage will appear for the Phase 2 online examination on 20 November 2026. The final stage is an interview, which carries 15% weightage in the final merit list.

The recruitment covers four streams: General (25 posts), Research (2 posts), Actuary (2 posts) and Official Language – Rajbhasha (1 post). The maximum age limit is 30 years as on 31 July 2026, with relaxation for SC, ST, OBC and other reserved categories as per government rules.

The pay scale for Officer Grade ‘A’ is Rs. 62,500 to Rs. 1,26,100, with gross emoluments around Rs. 2.05 lakh per month including allowances. The application fee is Rs. 1,000 for General, OBC and EWS candidates, while SC, ST, PwBD and Ex-Servicemen are exempted.

What to do at the current stage

What to Do After Phase 1 Result

  1. Download the Phase 1 result PDF from the official PFRDA website.
  2. Search your roll number in the result PDF to check your qualification status.
  3. If shortlisted, start preparing for the Phase 2 online examination on 20 November 2026.
  4. Phase 2 includes a descriptive English paper and a stream-specific MCQ paper.
  5. Keep your Phase 1 admit card and registration details safe for future stages.
  6. Check the official website regularly for Phase 2 admit card and exam city details.

Original Application Process

Preserved for recruitment history and reference after the application stage closes.

  1. Visit the official PFRDA website at pfrda.org.in.
  2. Open the Careers / Vacancies section and find Advt. No. 03/2026.
  3. Register with a valid email ID and mobile number.
  4. Fill the online application form with personal, educational and stream details.
  5. Upload photograph, signature and required documents.
  6. Pay the applicable fee online (Rs. 1,000 for General / OBC / EWS; exempted for SC / ST / PwBD / Ex-Servicemen).
  7. Submit the form and save the confirmation page for future reference.
