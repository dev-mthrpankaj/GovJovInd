Complete context

About This Recruitment

Karnataka Examinations Authority (KEA) has issued Advt. No. ED/KEA/04/2026 (RPC) on 17 November 2026 to fill 22 Textile Inspector posts in the Department of Handlooms and Textiles, Government of Karnataka. The online application window is active from 18 November 2026 to 16 December 2026, with online fee payment accepted until 17 December 2026 on cetonline.karnataka.gov.in/kea/.

Applicants must hold a Diploma in Textiles or Handloom Technology, Diploma in Textiles Technology, or Diploma in Handloom and Textile Technology from a recognised institution on or before 16 December 2026. The minimum age is 18 years, and the maximum age as on 16 December 2026 is 40 years for General Merit, 43 years for Category 2A, 2B, 3A and 3B, and 45 years for SC, ST and Category-1 candidates.

Selected Textile Inspectors receive a monthly pay scale of Rs.54,175 to Rs.99,400 with a two-year probation period under the National Pension System (NPS). The selection process comprises the qualifying Kannada Language Test scheduled for 08 January 2027 (150 marks, minimum 50 marks to qualify), an offline OMR Competitive Written Examination with 0.25 negative marking, Document Verification and Final Merit List.

Application fees are Rs.750 for General Merit and Category 2A, 2B, 3A and 3B candidates, Rs.500 for SC, ST, Category-1, Ex-Servicemen and Third Gender candidates, and Rs.250 for Persons with Disabilities.
