About This Recruitment

Rajasthan Staff Selection Board (RSSB) has released the Computer Instructor Answer Key 2026 for 3951 posts on its official website. The recruitment drive covers Senior Computer Instructor (322 posts) and Basic Computer Instructor (3629 posts). The written examination was held on 22–23 August 2026, and the provisional answer key is now available for candidates to download.

The answer key allows candidates to verify their responses and, if needed, raise objections within the prescribed window. The next stages will include the release of the revised answer key, result declaration, document verification, and final merit list. All future updates will be published on this same permanent page.

Candidates should keep their admit card, application number, and roll number ready to check and download the answer key from the official RSSB portal.
