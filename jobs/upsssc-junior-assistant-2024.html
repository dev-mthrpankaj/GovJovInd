About This Recruitment

UPSSSC Junior Assistant Recruitment 2024 (Advt No. 12-Exam/2024) is a major recruitment drive by the Uttar Pradesh Subordinate Service Selection Commission for 2,702 Junior Assistant posts across various departments of the Uttar Pradesh government. The recruitment was notified in December 2024.

The written examination was held on 1 February 2026. The revised answer key was released on 13 June 2026, and the result was declared on 15 September 2026. The typing test is scheduled for 30 October 2026.

Eligible candidates must have passed the UPSSSC PET 2023, completed 10+2 (Intermediate), possess a NIELIT CCC certificate, and meet the typing speed requirements of 25 WPM in Hindi and 30 WPM in English. The age limit is 18 to 40 years as on 1 July 2024.
