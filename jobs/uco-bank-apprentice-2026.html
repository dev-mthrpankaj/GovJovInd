Complete context

About This Recruitment

UCO Bank has invited online applications for 530 Apprentice posts under the "UCO BANK Apprenticeship Program FY 2026-27." The recruitment advertisement, numbered HO/HRM/RECR/2026-27/COM-02, was issued on 13 October 2026 by the bank's Human Resources Management Department.

The online application process commenced on 14 October 2026 and will close on 29 October 2026. Eligible graduates aged between 20 and 28 years can apply. Selected candidates will undergo one year of apprenticeship training and receive a monthly stipend of Rs. 16,650.

The selection process includes an objective-type online test conducted by the BFSI Sector Skill Council of India (BFSI SSC) on 07 November 2026 at 11:00 AM, followed by a local language test during document verification, document verification and a medical examination. Candidates must register on the National Apprenticeship Training Scheme (NATS) portal before proceeding with the application.

What to do at the current stage

How to Apply for UCO Bank Apprentice 2026

  1. Visit the official NATS portal at nats.education.gov.in.
  2. Register under the Student Register/Login section and complete your profile.
  3. Search for the UCO Bank Apprenticeship Program FY 2026-27 and apply.
  4. Save the unique Enrolment ID issued by the NATS portal.
  5. Check your email for a communication from BFSI SSC (info@bfsissc.com) with the application form and fee payment link.
  6. Visit https://beep.bfsissc.com and enter your caste category, training district preference and other required details.
  7. Pay the applicable examination fee online and submit the application before 29 October 2026.
