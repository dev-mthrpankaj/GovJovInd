Complete context

About This Recruitment

BTSC Food Safety Officer Recruitment 2026 is a Bihar Technical Service Commission recruitment drive to fill 105 Food Safety Officer (Khadya Suraksha Adhikari) posts under the Health Department, Government of Bihar. The official notification was released on 25 September 2026 under Advertisement No. 29/2026, and the online application window is open from 25 September 2026 to 24 October 2026.

Candidates with a Bachelor's degree in Food Technology, Dairy Technology, Biotechnology, Oil Technology, Agricultural Science, Veterinary Science, Biochemistry, Microbiology or Medicine (MBBS/BDS/BHMS/BUMS/BAMS), or a Post Graduate degree in Chemistry, are eligible to apply. The age limit is 21 to 37 years for Unreserved Male candidates, 21 to 40 years for Unreserved Female and BC/EBC candidates, and 21 to 42 years for SC/ST candidates, as on 01 August 2026.

The selection process consists of a Computer Based Test (CBT) of 100 marks followed by Document Verification. The application fee is Rs.100 for all categories. Selected candidates will be appointed in Pay Level 6 (Grade Pay Rs.4,200) of the Bihar government pay structure.
