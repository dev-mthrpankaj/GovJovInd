Complete context

About This Recruitment

UPSC Combined Defence Services Examination (II) 2026 is conducted by the Union Public Service Commission for admission to the Indian Military Academy (IMA), Indian Naval Academy (INA), Air Force Academy (AFA) and Officers' Training Academy (OTA). The official notification was published on 20 May 2026 and the online application window was open from 20 May 2026 to 11 June 2026.

The Computer Based Test (CBT) was conducted on 13 September 2026. The provisional answer key was released on the UPSC website, and the result has now been declared on 19 October 2026. Candidates who appeared in the written examination can check their result and download the PDF from the official UPSC portal.

The recruitment offers a total of 451 vacancies across four academies: IMA (100 posts), INA (26 posts), AFA (32 posts) and OTA (293 posts). The selection process includes a written examination, followed by an SSB interview and medical examination for qualified candidates.

Basic eligibility for IMA, INA and OTA is a Bachelor's Degree in any stream from a recognised university. For AFA, candidates must have a Bachelor's degree with Physics and Mathematics at 10+2 level or an Engineering degree. The age limit varies by academy, with the age calculated as on 01 July 2027.

Eligibility Criteria

Age Limit

Age as on 01 July 2027. Minimum 19 years for IMA, OTA & INA; 20 years for Air Force. Maximum 24 years for IMA, Air Force & INA; 25 years for OTA.

Age relaxation applicable for SC, ST, OBC and other reserved categories as per UPSC rules.

Education

IMA, INA & OTA: Bachelor's Degree in any stream from a recognised university. AFA: Bachelor's Degree with Physics & Mathematics at 10+2 level OR Engineering Degree.

Candidates must hold the prescribed qualification as on the closing date of the application.

Nationality / Other

Candidates must be Indian citizens and fulfill the physical and medical standards prescribed by UPSC for the respective academy.

Refer to official notification for detailed conditions.

How to Download UPSC CDS II Result 2026

  1. Visit the official UPSC website at upsc.gov.in.
  2. Click on the 'Results' section available on the homepage.
  3. Find and open the 'UPSC CDS II Result 2026' link.
  4. Enter your Application Number and Date of Birth or Roll Number, as required.
  5. Complete the CAPTCHA code or other security verification, if prompted.
  6. Click on the Submit button to view your UPSC CDS II Result 2026.
  7. Check your name, roll number, qualifying status, and marks displayed on the result page.
  8. Download the UPSC CDS II Result 2026 PDF or scorecard to your device.
  9. Take a clear printout of the result for document verification and future recruitment stages.
