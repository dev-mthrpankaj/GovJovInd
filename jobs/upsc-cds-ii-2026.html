Complete context

About This Recruitment

Union Public Service Commission (UPSC) has released the notification for Combined Defence Services Examination (II), 2026, for 451 posts across Indian Military Academy (IMA), Indian Naval Academy (INA), Air Force Academy (AFA), and Officers’ Training Academy (OTA). The online application window was open from 20 May 2026 to 11 June 2026 (06:00 PM).

The written examination was held on 13 September 2026. The provisional answer key has now been released on the official UPSC website. Candidates who appeared for the exam can download the answer key and, if the objection window is active, raise objections against any question within the prescribed period.

This page is designed to carry every future update—including the final answer key, result, cut off, score card, and final merit list—on the same permanent URL. Candidates are advised to bookmark this page for all official lifecycle updates.

Eligibility Criteria

Age Limit

19 to 24 years for IMA, INA & Air Force; 19 to 25 years for OTA (as on 01/07/2027).

Age relaxation applicable as per UPSC rules.

Education

Bachelor’s Degree in any stream for IMA & OTA; Engineering Degree for INA; Bachelor’s Degree with Physics & Math at 10+2 or Engineering Degree for Air Force Academy.

For detailed educational conditions, refer to the official notification.

Nationality / Other

Indian citizens fulfilling the post-specific eligibility conditions may apply. Marital status and physical standards as per UPSC notification.

What to do at the current stage

How to Check Answer Key & Raise Objection

  1. Visit the official UPSC website and open the CDS II 2026 answer key link.
  2. Download the provisional answer key PDF for the relevant paper.
  3. Compare your responses with the official answer key.
  4. If the objection window is active, use the official portal to raise objections with supporting evidence.
  5. Pay the prescribed objection fee per question, if applicable.
  6. Save the objection confirmation for future reference.

Original Application Process

Preserved for recruitment history and reference after the application stage closes.

  1. Visit the official UPSC website.
  2. Read the CDS II 2026 notification carefully.
  3. Open the online application link.
  4. Register and fill the form.
  5. Upload required documents.
  6. Pay the applicable fee.
  7. Submit and save the confirmation page.
