About This Recruitment

UPSC Combined Defence Services Examination (II), 2026 is conducted for admission to the Indian Military Academy, Indian Naval Academy, Air Force Academy and Officers' Training Academy. The supplied recruitment information lists 451 vacancies.

The online application started on 20 May 2026 and the extended deadline closed on 11 June 2026 at 6:00 PM. The written examination is scheduled for 13 September 2026 and the current candidate action is to download the CDS II e-Admit Card.

Candidates should verify academy-specific educational, age, physical and other eligibility conditions in the official UPSC notification before participating in later selection stages.
