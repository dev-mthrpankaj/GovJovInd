Complete context

About This Recruitment

UPSSSC Havaldar Instructor Recruitment 2026 is a Group-C recruitment drive by the Uttar Pradesh Subordinate Services Selection Commission for 209 Havaldar Instructor posts under the Home Guard Headquarters, Uttar Pradesh. The online application window was open from 19 June 2026 to 9 July 2026.

Eligible candidates must have passed Intermediate (10+2) and possess a valid UPSSSC PET 2025 scorecard. The age limit is 18 to 40 years as on 1 July 2026. The selection process includes PET-2025 based shortlisting, a Main Written Examination, Physical Standards Test (PST), Physical Efficiency Test (PET), and Document Verification.

The UPSSSC Havaldar Instructor Eligibility Result has been released on 30 September 2026. Candidates who applied for the 209 posts can now check their eligibility status. The Main Written Examination is scheduled for 22 November 2026 as per the UPSSSC exam calendar. Admit card, answer key, result, and all later stages will be updated on this same permanent page.

Eligibility Criteria

Age Limit

18 to 40 years as on 1 July 2026.

Age relaxation applicable as per UPSSSC and UP Government rules.

Education

Intermediate (10+2) from a recognised board. Valid UPSSSC PET 2025 scorecard is mandatory. For unpaid Home Guard departmental candidates, High School is acceptable.

Preference will be given to candidates with Territorial Army service, NCC 'B' Certificate, or Home Guard experience.

PET / Other

Indian citizen. Must possess a valid UPSSSC PET 2025 scorecard with a non-zero positive normalised score.

What to do at the current stage

How to Check Eligibility Result

  1. Visit the official UPSSSC website at upsssc.gov.in.
  2. Click on the Results section available on the homepage.
  3. Find and open the UPSSSC Havaldar Instructor Eligibility Result 2026 link.
  4. Enter your Application Number and Date of Birth or Roll Number as required.
  5. Complete the CAPTCHA or security verification if prompted.
  6. Click Submit to view your eligibility status.
  7. Download and save the result PDF or scorecard for future reference.
  8. Take a printout of the result for document verification and later stages.

Original Application Process

Preserved for recruitment history and reference after the application stage closes.

  1. Visit the official UPSSSC website at upsssc.gov.in.
  2. Click Applicant Segment and log in with PET 2025 registration number.
  3. Personal details auto-populate from PET 2025; fill educational qualification.
  4. Verify auto-fetched photo and signature.
  5. Select post preference and indicate preferential qualification, if applicable.
  6. Accept the declaration, enter verification code, and click Save & Submit.
  7. Pay the Rs. 25 online processing fee via the permitted payment modes.
  8. Download and print the submitted application form.
