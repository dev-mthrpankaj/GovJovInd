Complete context

About This Recruitment

Uttar Pradesh Subordinate Services Selection Commission (UPSSSC) is conducting the UPSSSC Havaldar Instructor Recruitment 2026 under Advertisement No. 11-Exam/2026 to fill 209 Havaldar Instructor posts in the Home Guard Headquarters, Uttar Pradesh. The official notification was published on 17 April 2026, and online applications remained open from 19 June 2026 to 09 July 2026, followed by fee reconciliation and form correction until 16 July 2026.

Based on normalized UPSSSC PET 2025 scores, the Commission declared the Eligibility / Shortlisting Result on 30 September 2026, shortlisting 10,025 candidates for the Main Written Examination. Exam city intimation details and the exam date notice were released on 12 November 2026.

UPSSSC released the Havaldar Instructor Main Exam Admit Card on 18 November 2026 at upsssc.gov.in for the written examination scheduled on 22 November 2026. Shortlisted candidates must download and print their admit card and carry it along with a valid original photo ID proof to the examination centre.

Basic eligibility for the post is Class 12 (Intermediate) from a recognised board along with a valid UPSSSC PET 2025 scorecard, while unpaid departmental Home Guard staff are eligible with Class 10 (High School). The age limit is 18 to 40 years as on 01 July 2026, and selected candidates are placed in Pay Level-3 (Rs.21,700 to Rs.69,100).
