About This Recruitment

UPSSSC Havaldar Instructor Recruitment 2026 is a state-level recruitment drive conducted by the Uttar Pradesh Subordinate Services Selection Commission (UPSSSC) under Advertisement No. 11-Exam/2026 to fill 209 Havaldar Instructor posts. Selected candidates receive a pay scale of Rs.21,700 to Rs.69,100 per month along with applicable Uttar Pradesh government allowances.

Online applications were accepted from 19 June 2026 to 09 July 2026 with an application fee of Rs.25 across all categories, and the correction window closed on 16 July 2026. UPSSSC declared the PET 2025 eligibility result on 30 September 2026, released exam city details on 12 November 2026, conducted the main written exam on 22 November 2026, and issued the official Answer Key on 24 November 2026.

Candidates must have passed 10+2 (Intermediate) from a recognised board in India, hold a valid UPSSSC PET 2025 Score Card, and be aged between 18 and 40 years as on 01 July 2026. For the Physical Efficiency and Measurement Test (PET/PST), male candidates require 167.7 cm height (160 cm for ST), 78.8 to 83.8 cm chest (76.5 to 81.5 cm for ST), and a 4.8 km run in 25 minutes, while female candidates require 152 cm height and a 2.4 km run in 14 minutes.
