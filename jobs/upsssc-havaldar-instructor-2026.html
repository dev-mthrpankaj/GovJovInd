Complete context

About This Recruitment

UPSSSC Havaldar Instructor Recruitment 2026 is a Uttar Pradesh Subordinate Services Selection Commission (UPSSSC) recruitment drive to fill 209 Havaldar Instructor posts in the Home Guard Headquarters, Uttar Pradesh (Lucknow). The official notification was published on 17 April 2026, and the online application window remained open from 19 June 2026 to 09 July 2026.

The selection process began with a PET-2025 score-based shortlist. The eligibility result was declared on 30 September 2026, shortlisting 10,025 candidates for the Main Written Examination. The exam city details were released on 12 November 2026, and the Main Written Examination is scheduled for 22 November 2026.

Basic eligibility is Intermediate (10+2) from a recognised board plus a valid UPSSSC PET 2025 scorecard. The age limit is 18 to 40 years as on 01 January 2026, with relaxation for reserved categories as per UP Government rules. The pay scale is Level-3, Rs. 21,700 to Rs. 69,100 per month.
