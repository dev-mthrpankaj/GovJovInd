About This Recruitment

UPSSSC Havaldar Instructor Recruitment 2026 is a Group-C recruitment drive by the Uttar Pradesh Subordinate Services Selection Commission for 209 Havaldar Instructor posts under the Home Guard Headquarters, Uttar Pradesh. The online application window was open from 19 June 2026 to 9 July 2026.

Eligible candidates must have passed Intermediate (10+2) and possess a valid UPSSSC PET 2025 scorecard. The age limit is 18 to 40 years as on 1 July 2026. The selection process includes PET-2025 based shortlisting, a Main Written Examination, Physical Standards Test (PST), Physical Efficiency Test (PET), and Document Verification.

The UPSSSC Havaldar Instructor Eligibility Result has been released on 30 September 2026. Candidates who applied for the 209 posts can now check their eligibility status. The Main Written Examination is scheduled for 22 November 2026 as per the UPSSSC exam calendar. Admit card, answer key, result, and all later stages will be updated on this same permanent page.
