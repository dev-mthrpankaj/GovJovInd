Complete context

About This Recruitment

Uttar Pradesh Public Service Commission (UPPSC) invited online applications for the Combined State / Upper Subordinate Services (PCS) Examination 2026 under Advertisement No. A-1/E-1/2026 to fill 500 posts across Uttar Pradesh. The online application window ran from 25 June 2026 to 03 August 2026 (extended), with fee payment closing on 03 August 2026 and form correction ending on 10 August 2026.

Candidates with a Bachelor's Degree in any stream from a recognised university in India and aged between 21 and 40 years as on 01 July 2026 were eligible to apply. Selection follows the Preliminary Examination, Main Examination, and Interview as prescribed by the Commission.

UPPSC released the Preliminary Exam City Intimation details on 27 November 2026. The UPPSC Pre Admit Card 2026 link will activate on 01 December 2026, and the Preliminary Examination is scheduled for 06 December 2026.
