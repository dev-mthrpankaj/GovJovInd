Complete context

About This Recruitment

Uttar Pradesh Public Service Commission (UPPSC) invited online applications for the Combined State / Upper Subordinate Services (PCS) Examination 2026 under Advertisement No. A-1/E-1/2026 to fill 500 posts across Uttar Pradesh. The online application window ran from 25 June 2026 to 03 August 2026 (extended), with fee payment closing on 03 August 2026 and form correction ending on 10 August 2026.

Candidates with a Bachelor's Degree in any stream from a recognised university in India and aged between 21 and 40 years as on 01 July 2026 were eligible to apply. Selection follows the Preliminary Examination, Main Examination, and Interview as prescribed by the Commission.

UPPSC released the Preliminary Exam City Intimation details on 27 November 2026. The UPPSC Pre Admit Card 2026 link will activate on 01 December 2026, and the Preliminary Examination is scheduled for 06 December 2026.

What to do at the current stage

How to Check UPPSC Pre Exam City & Download Admit Card

  1. Open the official UPPSC Exam City link (Server I or Server II) from Important Links below.
  2. Enter your OTR Number, Registration Number, Date of Birth, and the displayed CAPTCHA code.
  3. Submit your credentials to view your allotted examination district for the 06 December 2026 Prelims exam.
  4. Return to the official UPPSC portal on 01 December 2026 to download your UPPSC Pre Admit Card 2026 PDF.
  5. Verify your name, roll number, exam centre address, and reporting time printed on the admit card.
  6. Print a clear A4 copy of the admit card and carry it with a valid original photo ID to the examination centre.

Original Application Process

Preserved for recruitment history and reference after the application stage closes.

  1. Visit the official Uttar Pradesh Public Service Commission website at uppsc.up.nic.in.
  2. Complete One Time Registration (OTR) and verify personal details.
  3. Fill the online application form for Advertisement No. A-1/E-1/2026 carefully.
  4. Upload the required photograph, signature, and supporting documents.
  5. Pay the applicable category-wise examination fee online.
  6. Submit the form and print the final application confirmation for future reference.
