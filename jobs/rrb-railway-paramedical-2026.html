Complete context

About This Recruitment

Railway Recruitment Board (RRB) has released the Centralised Employment Notice (CEN) No. 05/2026 for recruitment to various paramedical categories in Indian Railways. The recruitment drive aims to fill 590 vacancies across posts such as Nursing Superintendent, Pharmacist (Entry Grade), Health & Malaria Inspector Gr II, Lab Assistant Gr II, Radiographer/X-Ray Technician, ECG Technician, Optometrist, Dialysis Technician, and Audiologist & Speech Therapist.

The official notification was released on 08 September 2026, and the online application window remained open from 15 September 2026 to 14 October 2026. The last date for fee payment was 16 October 2026, and the application modification window was available from 17 to 26 October 2026.

The Computer Based Test (CBT) is scheduled for 23 and 25 November 2026. The RRB Paramedical Application Status 2026 was released on 12 November 2026, allowing candidates to check whether their applications have been Provisionally Accepted, Conditionally Accepted, or Rejected. The admit card is expected around 19 November 2026, four days before the exam.

Eligibility varies by post: Nursing Superintendent requires B.Sc Nursing or GNM with Registered Nurse and Registered Midwife qualification; Pharmacist requires B.Pharm/Pharm.D with valid registration; other posts require relevant degrees or diplomas. The age limit is 18 to 40 years as on 01 January 2027, with category-wise relaxation as per government rules.

What to do at the current stage

How to Check Application Status

  1. Visit the official RRB application portal at rrbapply.gov.in.
  2. Log in using your registered User ID and Password.
  3. Click on the Application Status option for CEN 05/2026.
  4. Check whether your application is Provisionally Accepted, Conditionally Accepted or Rejected.
  5. If rejected, read the reason carefully and download the status for reference.
  6. If provisionally accepted, continue preparing for the CBT scheduled on 23 and 25 November 2026.

Original Application Process

Preserved for recruitment history and reference after the application stage closes.

  1. Visit the official RRB application portal at rrbapply.gov.in.
  2. Read CEN 05/2026 carefully before starting the application.
  3. Register with a valid mobile number and email ID.
  4. Fill the online application form with accurate personal and educational details.
  5. Upload photograph, signature and required documents.
  6. Pay the applicable examination fee online.
  7. Submit the form and save the confirmation page.
