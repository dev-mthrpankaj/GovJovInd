Complete context

About This Recruitment

Railway Recruitment Board (RRB) is conducting the RRB Paramedical Staff Recruitment 2026 under CEN 05/2026 to fill 590 posts across nine paramedical categories, including Nursing Superintendent (365 posts), Pharmacist Entry Grade (118 posts), Health and Malaria Inspector Grade-II (43 posts), Lab Assistant Grade-II (31 posts), Radiographer X-Ray Technician (25 posts), ECG Technician (4 posts), Optometrist (2 posts), Dialysis Technician (1 post), and Audiologist and Speech Therapist (1 post).

The online application window ran from 15 September 2026 to 14 October 2026, followed by fee payment until 16 October 2026 and application correction from 17 October 2026 to 26 October 2026. Exam city intimation details were released on 14 November 2026.

The RRB Paramedical Staff Admit Card 2026 was released on 18 November 2026 on the official login portal. The Computer Based Test (CBT) is scheduled on 23 November 2026 and 25 November 2026. Selection consists of three stages: Computer Based Test (CBT), Document Verification, and Medical Examination.

Post-wise educational qualifications range from 10+2 Science with relevant paramedical diploma or certificate to B.Sc., GNM/B.Sc. Nursing, B.Pharm/Pharm.D, or BASLP with council registration where applicable. The age limit is 18 to 40 years (post-wise) as on 01 January 2027, with category-wise age relaxation as per RRB rules.

Eligibility Criteria

Age Limit

18 to 40 years (post-wise) as on 01 January 2027.

Age relaxation is applicable for reserved categories as per Railway Recruitment Board (RRB) regulations.

Education

Post-wise qualifications include 10+2 Science with paramedical diploma/certificate, GNM/B.Sc. Nursing, B.Pharm/Pharm.D, B.Sc. with Chemistry, B.Sc. Optometry, or BASLP.

Candidates must hold the required professional registration (INC, Pharmacy Council, RCI, or concerned licensing body) wherever prescribed in CEN 05/2026.

Nationality / Other

Indian citizens meeting the post-specific educational, registration, and medical fitness standards prescribed in CEN 05/2026 are eligible.

Refer to official notification for detailed conditions.

What to do at the current stage

How to Download RRB Paramedical Staff Admit Card 2026

  1. Open the official RRB Admit Card login link from the Important Links section.
  2. Enter your Registration Number or Enrollment Number and Date of Birth.
  3. Submit the credentials to view your RRB Paramedical Staff Admit Card 2026.
  4. Verify your name, roll number, exam date (23 or 25 November 2026), shift, reporting time, and exam centre address.
  5. Download the admit card PDF and take a clear printout for exam day.
  6. Carry the printed admit card along with a valid original photo ID and recent passport-size photographs to the exam centre.

Original Application Process

Preserved for recruitment history and reference after the application stage closes.

  1. Visit the official RRB application portal at rrbapply.gov.in.
  2. Read the CEN 05/2026 Paramedical Categories notification carefully.
  3. Create an account using a valid email ID and mobile number.
  4. Fill in personal, post-preference, and educational qualification details accurately.
  5. Upload scanned photograph, signature, and required certificates.
  6. Pay the application fee online (Rs.500 for General/OBC/EWS or Rs.250 for SC/ST/EBC/Female).
  7. Submit the application form and save the confirmation receipt for future reference.
