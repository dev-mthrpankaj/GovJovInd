About This Recruitment

Railway Recruitment Board (RRB) has released the Railway Paramedical Recruitment 2026 notification for 590 posts across various paramedical categories. The online application window is open from 15 September 2026 to 14 October 2026.

This recruitment is suitable for candidates with a medical degree, diploma, or certificate in a related trade or branch. Candidates are advised to read the official notification carefully to confirm post-wise eligibility and other conditions before applying.

Keep your photograph, signature, educational certificates, category documents, and a valid ID proof ready before starting the online form. The fee payment last date is 16 October 2026.
