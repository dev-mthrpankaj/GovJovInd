Complete context

About This Recruitment

Rajasthan Staff Selection Board (RSSB) had released the recruitment notification for 10,644 posts of Clerk Grade-II (LDC) and Junior Assistant across various departments of the Rajasthan government. The written examination was held on 5 July 2026, and the result has now been declared on 28 September 2026.

This is a permanent lifecycle page that covers the entire RSSB LDC/Jr Assistant 2026 recruitment journey — from notification and application to exam, answer key, result, typing test, document verification and final merit list. All future official updates will be added to this same page without changing the URL.

Candidates who have appeared for the written examination can check their qualifying status in the result PDF. Shortlisted candidates will move to the next stage, which is the Hindi and English Typing Test, followed by document verification.
