Complete context

About This Recruitment

Dr. B.R. Ambedkar University Delhi has invited online applications for AUD Delhi Non-Teaching Recruitment 2026 under Advertisement No. AUD/55-1(154)/Rect./2026/1140 dated 12 November 2026. The recruitment drive aims to fill 6 regular non-teaching posts on a direct recruitment basis across Group A, Group B, and Group C cadres in New Delhi.

The 6 vacancies are distributed as 1 Deputy Registrar (SC, Pay Level 12), 1 Assistant Registrar (OBC, Pay Level 10), 1 Statistical Officer (UR, Pay Level 7), and 3 Steno (UR, Pay Level 4) posts. Pay scales follow the 7th Central Pay Commission from Rs.25,500 to Rs.2,09,200 per month with usual allowances.

Online applications are currently open from 12 November 2026 until 02 December 2026 through the official Dr. B.R. Ambedkar University Delhi Samarth recruitment portal. Candidates applying for more than one post must submit separate online applications and pay the applicable fee separately.

Educational qualifications range from a Bachelor's degree with 80 w.p.m. stenography and 35/30 w.p.m. typing speed for Steno to a Master's degree with at least 55% marks for Group A and Group B posts. Selection will be conducted through application screening, written examination, skill test where applicable, interview, and document verification as per the university Scheme of Examination.

Eligibility Criteria

Age Limit

Maximum age as on 02 December 2026: Deputy Registrar up to 50 years, Assistant Registrar and Statistical Officer up to 35 years, and Steno up to 32 years.

Age relaxation applies to SC, ST, OBC, PwBD, and other eligible categories as per Government of India and Dr. B.R. Ambedkar University Delhi rules.

Education

Post-wise qualifications include Bachelor's degree with 80 w.p.m. stenography and 35/30 w.p.m. typing for Steno, and Master's degree (55% marks) with prescribed experience where required for Group A and Group B posts.

All educational qualifications and work experience must be completed on or before the closing date of 02 December 2026.

Nationality / Other

Indian citizens fulfilling the post-specific essential qualifications, experience, and category reservation rules may apply. Separate applications and fees are required for multiple posts.

Refer to official notification for detailed conditions.

What to do at the current stage

How to Apply Online for AUD Delhi Non-Teaching Recruitment 2026

  1. Open the official Samarth application portal (audnt.samarth.edu.in) from the Important Links section.
  2. Read Advertisement No. AUD/55-1(154)/Rect./2026/1140 carefully to confirm post-wise eligibility, age limit, and experience.
  3. Register on the portal using an active email ID and mobile number, then log in with your credentials.
  4. Select the post you are applying for and enter personal, educational, category, and work experience details accurately.
  5. Upload scanned copies of your recent photograph, signature, and required qualification or experience certificates.
  6. Pay the applicable application fee online (Rs.1000 for Group A, Rs.500 for Group B, Rs.300 for Group C; Nil for SC/ST/PwBD/Women).
  7. Verify all details, submit the form before 02 December 2026, and download a printout of the final confirmation page.
