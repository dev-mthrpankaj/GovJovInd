Complete context

About This Recruitment

Nuclear Power Corporation of India Limited (NPCIL) has released Advertisement No. NAPS/HRM/APPRENTICE/02/2026 for engagement of apprentices at Narora Atomic Power Station (NAPS), Narora, District Bulandshahr, Uttar Pradesh. This recruitment drive offers a total of 80 apprenticeship seats across three categories: Trade Apprentice (50 seats), Diploma Apprentice (16 seats) and Graduate Apprentice (14 seats).

The online application process commenced on 29 October 2026 at 10:00 hrs and will close on 18 November 2026 at 16:00 hrs. The engagement is purely for apprenticeship training under the Apprentices Act, 1961 and Apprenticeship Rules, 1992, for a maximum duration of 12 months. There is no written examination or interview; selection is based on merit, determined by the percentage of marks obtained in the respective qualifying trade or discipline.

Eligible local candidates residing within a 16 km radius of Narora Atomic Power Station are given first preference, subject to reservation rules. Candidates from other areas will be considered if sufficient local candidates are not available in a particular category. The training location is Narora Atomic Power Station, Narora, PO NAPS Township, District Bulandshahr, Uttar Pradesh – 203389. No hostel or transport facility is provided.

This is a training engagement and does not guarantee employment with NPCIL upon completion. Candidates must register on the NAPS 2.0 portal (for Trade Apprentice) or the NATS 2.0 portal (for Diploma and Graduate Apprentice) before applying on the NPCIL careers website.
