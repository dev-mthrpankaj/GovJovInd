About This Recruitment

The Central Reserve Police Force (CRPF) released the Constable (Technical & Tradesmen and Pioneer) Recruitment 2026 notification for 9,175 posts. This is a major recruitment drive for 10th pass candidates seeking a government job in a central armed police force. Online applications were accepted from 20 April 2026 to 19 May 2026, and the application window has now closed.

The recruitment covers a wide range of trades including Driver, Motor Mechanic Vehicle, Cook, Bugler, Tailor, Barber, Washerman, Safai Karamchari, and more. Candidates must have passed Class 10 (Matriculation) from a recognised board. For Driver posts, a Heavy Motor Vehicle (HMV) driving license is also required. For technical trades like Motor Mechanic, an ITI certificate in the relevant trade is mandatory.

The selection process involves multiple stages: Physical Efficiency Test (PET), Physical Standard Test (PST), Computer-Based Test (CBT), Trade/Skill Test, Document Verification, and Detailed Medical Examination. The pay scale for all posts is Level-3 (Rs.21,700 – Rs.69,100). The next official stage — exam schedule, admit card, answer key or result — will be published on this same page as soon as CRPF releases it.
