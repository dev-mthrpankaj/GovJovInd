Complete context

About This Recruitment

Staff Selection Commission (SSC) has released the official notification for the Sub-Inspector in Delhi Police and Central Armed Police Forces (CAPF) Examination 2026. This recruitment drive aims to fill a total of 1871 Sub-Inspector vacancies across various forces including BSF, CISF, CRPF, ITBP, SSB and Delhi Police.

The online application process was conducted from 10 September 2026 to 30 September 2026. The last date for fee payment was 01 October 2026, and the application correction window remained open from 08 to 10 October 2026. The Staff Selection Commission has now released the exam date notice, confirming that the Computer Based Examination (Paper-I) will be held from 20 to 28 November 2026.

The selection process for SSC CPO SI 2026 includes Paper-I (Computer Based Examination), Physical Standard Test (PST) and Physical Endurance Test (PET), Paper-II (Computer Based Examination), and a Medical Examination. Candidates qualifying all stages will be considered for final appointment.

For Sub-Inspector in Delhi Police and Sub-Inspector (GD) in CAPFs, candidates must possess a Bachelor's degree from a recognized university. For Sub-Inspector (Fire) in CISF, candidates must have a Bachelor's degree in Science with Physics, Chemistry and Mathematics, or Matriculation with a 3-year Diploma in relevant engineering disciplines. The age limit for SI (GD) is 20 to 25 years, while for SI (Fire) it is 18 to 30 years as on 01 August 2026.
