Complete context

About This Recruitment

Staff Selection Commission (SSC) is conducting SSC CPO SI Recruitment 2026 to fill 1871 Sub-Inspector vacancies across Delhi Police (317 posts), Border Security Force (457 posts), Central Industrial Security Force GD (250 posts), Central Reserve Police Force (254 posts), Indo-Tibetan Border Police (187 posts), Sashastra Seema Bal (172 posts), and CISF Sub-Inspector Fire (234 posts). The official notification was released on 10 September 2026, and online applications closed on 30 September 2026.

Following the application correction window from 08 October 2026 to 10 October 2026, SSC published the revised exam schedule notice on 26 October 2026 and released exam city intimation details on 13 November 2026. The SSC CPO SI Admit Card 2026 was released on 20 November 2026 for the Paper-I computer-based examination scheduled from 23 November 2026 to 28 November 2026 across centres in India.

Candidates appearing in Paper-I must download their admit card from the official SSC portal at ssc.gov.in and carry a printed copy along with an original photo identity proof to the examination centre. Eligibility requires a Bachelor's degree in any stream (with a valid LMV driving licence for Delhi Police Male SI, and a B.Sc in PCM or a 3-year engineering diploma for CISF SI Fire) and an age between 20 and 25 years as on 01 August 2026.

The selection process consists of Paper-I (CBT), Physical Standard Test and Physical Endurance Test (PST/PET), Paper-II (CBT), Document Verification, and Detailed Medical Examination. All subsequent official updates including answer keys, results, cut-offs, physical schedules, and final merit lists will be updated on this permanent recruitment page.

Eligibility Criteria

Age Limit

20 to 25 years as on 01 August 2026.

Upper age relaxation is applicable for SC, ST, OBC, Ex-Servicemen, and other reserved categories as per Staff Selection Commission rules.

Education

Bachelor's Degree in any stream from a recognised university. Male candidates for Delhi Police SI require a valid LMV driving licence. CISF SI (Fire) requires B.Sc (PCM) or Matriculation with a 3-year Diploma in relevant Engineering.

For CISF SI (Fire), B.Tech/M.Tech in relevant fields may be considered as an additional qualification provided the basic Diploma qualification is met.

Nationality / Other

Indian citizens meeting post-wise physical, medical, and driving licence conditions may apply. Refer to the official notification PDF for complete eligibility norms.

What to do at the current stage

How to Download SSC CPO SI Admit Card 2026

  1. Open the official Staff Selection Commission portal at ssc.gov.in from the Important Links section.
  2. Sign in using your registration number and password on the candidate dashboard.
  3. Open the SSC CPO SI 2026 Admit Card download section and download the hall ticket PDF.
  4. Verify your name, roll number, exam date, shift timing, reporting time, and exam centre address.
  5. Take a clear printout of the admit card and pack an original government photo ID proof.
  6. Reach the allotted exam centre before the gate closing time mentioned on the admit card.

Original Application Process

Preserved for recruitment history and reference after the application stage closes.

  1. Visited the official Staff Selection Commission website at ssc.gov.in.
  2. Selected the SSC CPO SI 2026 Apply Online link under the candidate portal.
  3. Completed One-Time Registration using a valid email ID and mobile number.
  4. Logged in with registration ID and password to open the application form.
  5. Filled in personal, educational, force preference, and category details.
  6. Uploaded scanned photograph, signature, and required documents.
  7. Paid the applicable examination fee of Rs.100 online (if not exempted).
  8. Submitted the form before 30 September 2026 and printed the confirmation page.
