Complete context

About This Recruitment

Staff Selection Commission (SSC) has released the official notification for SSC Sub Inspector CPO SI Recruitment 2026 to fill 1871 vacancies across various Central Armed Police Forces (CAPFs) and Delhi Police. The recruitment covers posts of Sub Inspector in Delhi Police, BSF, CISF, CRPF, ITBP, SSB, and CISF Fire.

The online application process started on 10 September 2026 and will close on 30 September 2026. The last date for fee payment is also 30 September 2026. Candidates can make corrections to their applications from 08 to 10 October 2026. The Computer Based Test (CBT) is scheduled to be conducted from 23 to 28 November 2026.

The selection process includes a Computer Based Test (CBT), Physical Endurance & Measurement Test (PE & MT), Document Verification, and Medical Examination. Eligible candidates must have a Bachelor's Degree in any stream from a recognized university (specific qualifications apply for CISF SI Fire post). The age limit is 20 to 25 years as on 01 August 2026, with relaxation for reserved categories as per SSC rules.
