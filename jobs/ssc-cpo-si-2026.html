About This Recruitment

SSC CPO SI Recruitment 2026 is the Staff Selection Commission examination for recruitment of Sub-Inspectors in Delhi Police and Central Armed Police Forces (CAPFs). The available recruitment information lists 1,871 vacancies and an online application window from 10 September 2026 to 30 September 2026.

Candidates must hold a bachelor's degree. For the Delhi Police Sub-Inspector post, the available information also specifies a valid driving licence requirement for the applicable male candidates. The prescribed age range is 20 to 25 years as on 1 August 2026, with relaxation as per SSC rules.

Before submitting the form, candidates should complete SSC One-Time Registration if required, verify their personal and educational details, keep photograph/signature and category documents ready, and read the official SSC notice carefully.
