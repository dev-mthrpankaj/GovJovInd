About This Recruitment

HPRCA Assistant Staff Nurse Recruitment 2026 is a recruitment drive by the Himachal Pradesh Rajya Chayan Aayog for 312 Assistant Staff Nurse posts. The online application window was open from 30 July 2026 to 29 August 2026. The admit card has now been released for the examination scheduled on 25 September 2026.

This role is suitable for female candidates with a B.Sc. Nursing degree or GNM diploma. The key eligibility includes educational qualification, age limit, and the specific requirements mentioned in the official notification. Candidates should verify all details carefully before the exam day.
