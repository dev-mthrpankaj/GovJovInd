Complete context

About This Recruitment

National Testing Agency (NTA), an autonomous testing organization under the Ministry of Education, Government of India, has invited applications for 15 Office Assistant (OA) posts on a contractual basis. Selected candidates will be engaged initially for a period of 5 years at the NTA Head Office in New Delhi, extendable based on performance and organizational requirements.

The application window is open from 25 November 2026 to 10 December 2026. Unlike standard online portal forms, applicants must download the prescribed Annexure-I application proforma from the official notification PDF, complete it, and send it along with a detailed CV and self-attested supporting documents via email to recruitment@nta.ac.in.

Applicants must hold a graduate degree in any discipline from a recognized university or institution, possess working knowledge of MS Word, MS Excel, PowerPoint, email and internet applications, and have strong written and verbal communication skills in English and Hindi. Candidates must be below 50 years of age as on 10 December 2026.

Selection will be carried out through scrutiny of emailed applications followed by a personal interaction or interview as determined by NTA. Remuneration is indicative and equivalent to Pay Level 4 of the 7th Central Pay Commission (CPC).
