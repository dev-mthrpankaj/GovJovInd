Complete context

About This Recruitment

Coconut Development Board (CDB), under the Ministry of Agriculture and Farmers Welfare, Government of India, has released a direct recruitment notification for 35 vacancies across 13 distinct Group A, B, and C designations. The detailed notification was issued on 06 November 2026 and published in Employment News (21–27 November 2026 issue).

The online application process started on 25 November 2026 and will remain active until 21 December 2026 at the official portal coconutboard.gov.in. The vacancies cover clerical, technical, field, accounts, translation, and administrative positions including Lower Division Clerk (13 posts), Field Officer (5 posts), Deputy Director (4 posts), Development Officer (2 posts), Development Officer Technology (2 posts), Stenographer Grade II (2 posts), and 1 post each for Assistant Director, Administrative Officer, Accounts Officer, Assistant, Auditor, Junior Translation Officer, and Data Entry Operator.

Educational requirements range from 10+2 (Intermediate) with typing or science/agriculture diploma for Group C posts to Bachelor's, Master's, or professional CA/CMA qualifications with relevant experience for Group A and B posts. Selected candidates will be placed in 7th CPC Pay Levels 2 to 11 with All India transfer liability.
