Complete context

About This Recruitment

Coconut Development Board (CDB), under the Ministry of Agriculture and Farmers Welfare, Government of India, has released a direct recruitment notification for 35 vacancies across 13 distinct Group A, B, and C designations. The detailed notification was issued on 06 November 2026 and published in Employment News (21–27 November 2026 issue).

The online application process started on 25 November 2026 and will remain active until 21 December 2026 at the official portal coconutboard.gov.in. The vacancies cover clerical, technical, field, accounts, translation, and administrative positions including Lower Division Clerk (13 posts), Field Officer (5 posts), Deputy Director (4 posts), Development Officer (2 posts), Development Officer Technology (2 posts), Stenographer Grade II (2 posts), and 1 post each for Assistant Director, Administrative Officer, Accounts Officer, Assistant, Auditor, Junior Translation Officer, and Data Entry Operator.

Educational requirements range from 10+2 (Intermediate) with typing or science/agriculture diploma for Group C posts to Bachelor's, Master's, or professional CA/CMA qualifications with relevant experience for Group A and B posts. Selected candidates will be placed in 7th CPC Pay Levels 2 to 11 with All India transfer liability.

Eligibility Criteria

Age Limit

Maximum 40 years for Deputy Director, 35 years for Assistant Director, 30 years for Group B posts (Administrative Officer to Stenographer Grade II), and 27 years for Group C posts (Field Officer, Data Entry Operator, LDC) as on 21 December 2026.

Upper age relaxation of 5 years for SC/ST, 3 years for OBC, 10 years for PwBD, and up to 40 years for eligible Government servants (Group C posts) as per Central Government rules.

Education

12th Pass (Higher Secondary / Intermediate), Bachelor's Degree, Master's Degree, or CA/CMA qualification with post-specific typing speed or work experience.

Candidates must fulfill all educational, skill speed, and experience requirements on or before 21 December 2026.

Nationality / Other

Indian citizens fulfilling post-specific eligibility criteria may apply. Selected candidates are liable to be transferred anywhere in India under Coconut Development Board.

Refer to official notification for detailed conditions.

What to do at the current stage

How to Apply Online

  1. Visit the official Coconut Development Board Vacancy portal at coconutboard.gov.in/Vacancy.aspx.
  2. Open the recruitment notification dated 06 November 2026 and check post-wise eligibility, age limit, and experience rules.
  3. Click the online application link and register using a valid email ID and mobile number.
  4. Fill in your personal, educational, category, and work experience details carefully.
  5. Upload scanned copies of your photograph, signature, and required eligibility certificates in the prescribed format.
  6. Pay Rs.300 through SBI Collect (if applicable) and save the final submitted confirmation page.

    Frequently Asked Questions

    The last date to submit the online application form and pay the application fee for Coconut Development Board Recruitment 2026 is 21 December 2026. Applications opened on 25 November 2026 on coconutboard.gov.in.

    Coconut Development Board has advertised 35 direct recruitment vacancies across 13 Group A, B, and C posts, including 13 Lower Division Clerk, 5 Field Officer, and 4 Deputy Director posts.

    Visit coconutboard.gov.in/Vacancy.aspx, open the recruitment link dated 06 November 2026, complete registration, enter personal and educational details, upload documents, pay Rs.300 via SBI Collect, and submit.

    Lower Division Clerk requires a 12th pass certificate with 35 wpm English or 30 wpm Hindi computer typing speed. Field Officer requires 12th pass with Science and a diploma or certificate in Agriculture or Horticulture.

    As on 21 December 2026, the maximum age limit is 40 years for Deputy Director, 35 years for Assistant Director, 30 years for Group B posts, and 27 years for Group C posts, with category relaxations as per rules.

    General, OBC, and EWS candidates must pay Rs.300 online through SBI Collect. SC, ST, PwBD, Ex-Servicemen, and all female candidates are exempted from paying any application fee.

    Selection is conducted through a written examination or interview as decided by the Board, followed by a shorthand or typing skill test for Stenographer Grade II, DEO, and LDC posts, and document verification.

    Selected candidates receive 7th CPC pay scales from Level 2 (Rs.19,900–Rs.63,200) for Lower Division Clerk up to Level 11 (Rs.67,700–Rs.2,08,700) for Deputy Director (Development).

    The written examination, skill test, and interview dates have not been announced yet. Admit cards will be released on coconutboard.gov.in once the official schedule is published.

    The official website of the Coconut Development Board is https://coconutboard.gov.in/ and all recruitment notices and online application links are hosted on https://coconutboard.gov.in/Vacancy.aspx.
