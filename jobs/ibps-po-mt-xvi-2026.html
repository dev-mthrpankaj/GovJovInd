Complete context

About This Recruitment

IBPS PO MT XVI Recruitment 2026 is a Common Recruitment Process (CRP PO/MT-XVI) conducted by the Institute of Banking Personnel Selection (IBPS) for the recruitment of Probationary Officers (PO) and Management Trainees (MT) in 11 participating public sector banks across India. The recruitment drive is being held for vacancies in the 2027-28 cycle.

The online application process was conducted from 1 July 2026 to 26 July 2026 (extended). The Preliminary Examination was held on 22 and 23 August 2026, and the Prelims Result was declared on 23 September 2026. The Main Examination is scheduled for 4 October 2026.

Candidates who qualify in the Mains exam will be called for a Personality Test (non-qualifying but mandatory) followed by an Interview. The final merit list will be prepared based on the combined performance in the Mains exam and Interview. Selected candidates will be appointed as Probationary Officers/Management Trainees with a starting basic pay of Rs.48,480.

Eligibility Criteria

Age Limit

20 to 30 years as on 1 July 2026.

SC/ST: 5 years, OBC (NCL): 3 years, PwBD: 10 years, Ex-Servicemen: 5 years, Persons affected by 1984 riots: 5 years.

Education

Graduation in any discipline from a recognised university.

The qualifying degree must be completed by 21 July 2026 (the last date of application).

Nationality / Other

Citizen of India, subject of Nepal or Bhutan, Tibetan refugee (pre-1962), or person of Indian origin who migrated from specified countries with eligibility certificate.

Refer to the official notification for detailed conditions.
