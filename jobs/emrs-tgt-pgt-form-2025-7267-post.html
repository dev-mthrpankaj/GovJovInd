About This Recruitment

EMRS ESSE 2025 is a large teaching and non-teaching recruitment drive covering 7,267 posts, including Principal, PGT, TGT, Staff Nurse, Hostel Warden, Accountant, Junior Secretariat Assistant and Lab Attendant.

The application window ran from 19 September to 28 October 2025. Tier I was scheduled on 13–14 and 21 December 2025, followed by the Tier II examination from 22 to 25 March 2026 for applicable posts.

The latest lifecycle stage is the EMRS ESSE 2025 Tier II Result, released on 03 September 2026. This permanent page retains earlier exam-city, admit-card, answer-key, syllabus and recruitment links for candidate reference.
