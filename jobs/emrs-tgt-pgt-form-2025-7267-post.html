About This Recruitment

EMRS ESSE 2025 is a large teaching and non-teaching recruitment drive covering 7,267 posts, including Principal, PGT, TGT, Staff Nurse, Hostel Warden, Accountant, Junior Secretariat Assistant and Lab Attendant.

The application window ran from 19 September to 28 October 2025. Tier I was scheduled on 13–14 and 21 December 2025, followed by the Tier II examination from 22 to 25 March 2026 for applicable posts.

The latest lifecycle stage is the EMRS ESSE 2025 Tier II Result, released on 03 September 2026. This permanent page retains earlier exam-city, admit-card, answer-key, syllabus and recruitment links for candidate reference.

Eligibility Criteria

Age Limit

Principal: max 50 years; PGT: max 40 years; TGT: max 35 years; Staff Nurse & Hostel Warden: max 35 years; other listed posts: max 30 years. Source states age as on 23 October 2025.

Age relaxation: kindly refer to the detailed notification.

Education

Post-wise qualifications apply for Principal, PGT, TGT, Staff Nurse, Hostel Warden, Accountant, JSA and Lab Attendant.

Subject-wise and post-wise eligibility should be checked in the detailed notification.

Nationality / Other

Post-specific professional qualifications, CTET, registration, experience or typing requirements apply where prescribed.

Refer to official notification for detailed conditions.
