Complete context

About This Recruitment

Naval Dockyard Visakhapatnam, a naval ship repair yard under the Indian Navy and the Ministry of Defence, has invited online applications for engagement of Trade Apprentices for the 2026 intake. The recruitment notification was published under Advt No. DAS (MBI/APP/IT-28) and covers a total of 283 apprenticeship seats spread across 32 ITI trades.

Apprenticeship training at Naval Dockyard Visakhapatnam is a skill-development engagement governed by the Apprentices Act, 1961. Candidates selected as Trade Apprentices receive structured on-the-job training in naval ship repair, refit and industrial workshop trades at the Visakhapatnam yard. The trades on offer include Electrician, Fitter, Electronics Mechanic, Mechanic Diesel, Welder, COPA, Machinist, Marine Engine Fitter, Shipwright Wood, Pipe Fitter, Mechanic Mechatronics, Painter, Mason and several other technical trades.

The online application window opened on 07 October 2026 and will close on 28 October 2026. There is no application fee for any category — General, OBC, EWS, SC, ST and PwBD candidates can all apply free of cost. The application process is fully online through the official apprenticeship registration portal.

Basic eligibility is Class 10th (Matriculation) passed with a minimum of 50% marks along with an ITI NCVT or SCVT certificate in the relevant trade with a minimum of 65% marks. The minimum age is 14 years for non-hazardous trades and 18 years for hazardous trades as on 28 October 2026. Candidates are advised to read the complete official notification before filling the online form.
