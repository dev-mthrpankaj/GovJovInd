Complete context

About This Recruitment

IIM CAT 2026 is the Common Admission Test conducted by the Indian Institutes of Management (IIMs) for admission to Post Graduate Programmes in Management (PGP) and Fellow Programmes in Management (FPM) at 21 IIMs and other participating B-schools across India. The official notification was released on 26 July 2026, and the registration window was open from 03 August 2026 to 22 September 2026 (extended from 15 September 2026).

The CAT 2026 admit card was initially scheduled for 04 November 2026 but was postponed to 06 November 2026 by IIM Indore. The computer-based test will be conducted on 29 November 2026 in three sessions. The result is expected in the first week of January 2027, followed by the Written Ability Test (WAT) and Personal Interview (PI) rounds at individual IIMs.

Eligibility requires a bachelor's degree with at least 50% aggregate marks (45% for SC, ST and PwBD candidates). Final-year students are also eligible. The application fee is Rs.2,700 for General, EWS and NC-OBC candidates and Rs.1,350 for SC, ST and PwBD candidates. There is no age limit for appearing in CAT 2026.

What to do at the current stage

How to Download CAT 2026 Admit Card

  1. Open the official CAT website at iimcat.ac.in.
  2. Click on the Registered Candidate Login link.
  3. Enter your CAT user ID and password.
  4. Open the admit card section on your dashboard.
  5. Download the PDF and print at least two copies.
  6. Check your name, photograph, signature, test city, slot and reporting time carefully.

Original Application Process

Preserved for recruitment history and reference after the application stage closes.

  1. Visit the official CAT website at iimcat.ac.in.
  2. Read the CAT 2026 Information Bulletin carefully.
  3. Register with a valid email ID and mobile number.
  4. Fill the online application form with personal and academic details.
  5. Upload photograph, signature and required documents.
  6. Select preferred test cities.
  7. Pay the applicable registration fee online.
  8. Submit the form and save the confirmation page.
