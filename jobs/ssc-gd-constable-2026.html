Complete context

About This Recruitment

SSC GD Constable Examination 2026 is a Staff Selection Commission recruitment drive to fill 25,487 vacancies for Constable (General Duty) in Central Armed Police Forces (CAPFs) and SSF, and Rifleman (GD) in Assam Rifles. The official notification was released on 01 December 2025, and the online application window remained open from 01 December 2025 to 31 December 2025.

The Computer-Based Examination (CBE) was conducted from 27 April 2026 to 30 May 2026 across various centres in the country. The provisional answer key and response sheets were released on 15 June 2026, with the objection window open until 20 June 2026. The written result was declared on 12 October 2026, and 21,56,776 candidates had appeared for the examination.

The recruitment covers posts in forces such as BSF, CISF, CRPF, SSB, ITBP, Assam Rifles (AR), SSF, and NCB. Basic eligibility is Class 10th (Matriculation) from a recognised board. The age limit is 18 to 23 years as on 01 January 2026, with relaxation for reserved categories as per SSC rules.

Shortlisted candidates from the written result will now proceed to the Physical Efficiency Test (PET) and Physical Standard Test (PST), followed by Medical Examination and Document Verification. This page will be updated with PET/PST admit card, medical dates, and final result as soon as official announcements are made.

What to do at the current stage

How to Check SSC GD Result 2026

  1. Open the official SSC website at ssc.gov.in.
  2. Click on the 'Results' tab or the 'Candidate Result' link on the homepage.
  3. Look for the 'Constable (GD) Examination 2026' result notice.
  4. Download the relevant merit list PDF (List I for Female, List II for Male, List III for SSF).
  5. Search for your roll number using Ctrl+F in the PDF.
  6. If shortlisted, note down your PET/PST roll number and keep checking the CRPF recruitment portal for physical test call letters.

Original Application Process

Preserved for recruitment history and reference after the application stage closes.

  1. Visit the official SSC recruitment website at ssc.gov.in.
  2. Complete the One-Time Registration (OTR) if you are a new applicant.
  3. Log in with your registration number and password.
  4. Fill the online application form with accurate personal and educational details.
  5. Select your preferred forces and examination centres.
  6. Upload photograph, signature and required documents.
  7. Pay the applicable examination fee online.
  8. Submit the form and save the confirmation page for future reference.

Yes. The Staff Selection Commission declared the SSC GD Constable Result 2026 on 12 October 2026. A total of 21,56,776 candidates appeared for the Computer-Based Examination. The result is available in PDF merit list format on the official SSC website ssc.gov.in.

The SSC has released the category-wise cut-off marks along with the result. Minimum qualifying thresholds are: UR 30% (48 marks), OBC/EWS 25% (40 marks), SC/ST/ESM 20% (32 marks). Open ssc.gov.in, click on the 'Candidate Result' link, find the Constable (GD) Examination 2026 result notice, download the relevant merit list PDF (List I for Female, List II for Male excluding SSF, List III for SSF), and search for your roll number using Ctrl+F.

Shortlisted candidates will now proceed to the Physical Efficiency Test (PET) and Physical Standard Test (PST). The PET/PST will be conducted by the Central Reserve Police Force (CRPF). Candidates should check the CRPF recruitment portal rect.crpf.gov.in for PET/PST call letters and instructions.

A total of 25,487 vacancies for Constable (General Duty) and Rifleman (GD) posts were advertised in the CTGD 2026 notification. This includes 23,467 male vacancies and 2,020 female vacancies across BSF, CISF, CRPF, SSB, ITBP, Assam Rifles, and SSF.

Candidates must have passed the Class 10th (Matriculation) or equivalent examination from a recognised board. The age limit is 18 to 23 years as on 01 January 2026, with age relaxation for SC, ST, OBC, Ex-Servicemen, and other reserved categories as per SSC rules.

General, OBC, and EWS male candidates paid Rs.100. SC, ST, all female candidates, and Ex-Servicemen were exempted from the fee. The correction charge was Rs.200 for the first time and Rs.500 for the second time.

The selection process includes a Computer Based Test (CBT), followed by Physical Efficiency Test (PET) and Physical Standard Test (PST), then Medical Examination (DME/RME), and finally Document Verification.

For male candidates (General/OBC/SC): height 170 cm, chest 80-85 cm. For male ST: height 162.5 cm, chest 76-81 cm. For female candidates: height 157 cm. PET requires male candidates to run 5 km in 24 minutes and female candidates to run 1.6 km in 8.5 minutes.

The post carries Pay Level-3 in the pay matrix, with a basic pay range of Rs.21,700 to Rs.69,100. Selected candidates also receive Dearness Allowance (DA), House Rent Allowance (HRA), Transport Allowance, and other central government benefits as applicable.
