Complete context

About This Recruitment

Staff Selection Commission (SSC) conducted the Constable (GD) Examination 2026 for 25,487 vacancies in Central Armed Police Forces (CAPFs), SSF and Rifleman (GD) in Assam Rifles. The official notification was released on 01 December 2025, and online applications were accepted from 01 December 2025 to 31 December 2025.

The Computer Based Examination (CBE) was held from 27 April 2026 to 30 May 2026. The CBT result was declared on 12 October 2026, shortlisting candidates for the Physical Efficiency Test (PET) and Physical Standard Test (PST). The final answer key, question paper-cum-response sheet and candidate marks were uploaded on 03 November 2026.

The score card download window will remain open until 17 November 2026 (5:00 PM). Candidates must download and print their score card, final answer key and response sheet before the link expires. The next stage is the PET/PST, for which a separate schedule will be announced by SSC.

Eligibility for SSC GD Constable 2026 requires passing Class 10 (High School) from a recognised board. The age limit is 18 to 23 years as on 01 January 2026, with relaxation for SC, ST, OBC, PwBD and Ex-Servicemen as per government rules.

How to Download Score Card & Final Answer Key

  1. Visit the official SSC website at ssc.gov.in or the direct login link provided in Important Links.
  2. Log in using your Roll Number and Password for the final answer key and response sheet.
  3. For marks and score card, log in using your Registered ID and Password.
  4. Download and save the Final Answer Key, Question Paper-cum-Response Sheet and Score Card.
