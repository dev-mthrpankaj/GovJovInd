Complete context

About This Recruitment

SSC GD Constable Examination 2026 is a Staff Selection Commission recruitment drive to fill 25,487 General Duty Constable posts in central armed police forces including BSF, CISF, CRPF, SSB, ITBP, Assam Rifles (AR) and SSF. The official notification was published in December 2025, and the online application window remained open from 01 December 2025 to 31 December 2025.

The Computer Based Test (CBT) was conducted from 27 April 2026 to 30 May 2026. The provisional answer key was released on 15 June 2026, and the objection window remained open from 15 to 20 June 2026. The first result was declared on 12 October 2026, and a revised result was published on 23 October 2026.

Basic eligibility is Class 10 (High School) from a recognised board. The age limit is 18 to 23 years as on 01 January 2026, with relaxation for reserved categories as per SSC rules. The selection process includes CBT, Physical Efficiency Test (PET), Physical Standard Test (PST), Medical Examination and Document Verification.

The revised result released on 23 October 2026 includes List-1 and List-2 along with the cut-off. Candidates should download the revised result PDFs and cut-off from the Important Links section and check their selection status for the next stage.
