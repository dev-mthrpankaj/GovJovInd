Complete context

About This Recruitment

SSC GD Constable (General Duty) Examination 2026 is a Staff Selection Commission recruitment drive to fill 25,487 Constable (General Duty) posts in Central Armed Police Forces (CAPFs), Secretariat Security Force (SSF) and Assam Rifles. The official notification was published on 01 December 2025, and the online application window remained open from 01 December 2025 to 31 December 2025.

The Computer Based Test was conducted from 27 April 2026 to 30 May 2026. The result was declared on 12 October 2026, a revised result was issued on 23 October 2026, and the final answer key with individual marks was released on 03 November 2026. The PET/PST admit card was released on 12 November 2026.

The Physical Efficiency Test (PET) and Physical Standard Test (PST) will be conducted from 26 November 2026 to 11 December 2026. Candidates must carry the printed PET/PST admit card, a valid original photo ID and the documents listed on the admit card to the physical test centre.

Basic eligibility is Class 10 (High School) from a recognised board. The age limit is 18 to 23 years as on 01 January 2026, with relaxation for reserved categories as per SSC rules.
