About This Recruitment

The National Testing Agency (NTA) conducted the All India Ayush Post Graduate Entrance Test (AIAPGET) 2026 for admission to MD/MS/PG Diploma courses in Ayurveda, Unani, Siddha, and Homoeopathy. The examination was held on 22 August 2026 across various centres in India.

This permanent page tracks the complete lifecycle of the AIAPGET 2026 exam, from the initial notification and application process through to the final answer key, result, and score card. The result has now been declared, and candidates can check their scores and download the score card from the official links provided.

The final answer key has also been released. Candidates are advised to download the final answer key and their result/score card for future reference. All updates are published on this same URL.
