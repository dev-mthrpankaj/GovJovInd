About This Recruitment

NTA AIAPGET 2026 is the All India Ayush Post Graduate Entrance Test conducted by the National Testing Agency for admission to MD/MS/PG Diploma courses in Ayurveda, Unani, Siddha, and Homoeopathy. The examination was held on 22 August 2026 across various centres in India.

The final answer key has been released. Candidates who appeared for the exam can now check the final answer key and calculate their probable scores. The result and merit list are expected to be released soon on the official NTA AIAPGET portal.
