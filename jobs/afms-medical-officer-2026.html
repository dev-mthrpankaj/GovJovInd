Complete context

About This Recruitment

Armed Forces Medical Services (AFMS), Ministry of Defence, has announced 120 Medical Officer (Short Service Commission) vacancies for 2026. Of these, 90 posts are for male candidates and 30 posts are for female candidates. This recruitment is open to Indian citizens holding an MBBS degree and meeting the prescribed physical and mental fitness standards.

The short notice was released on 31 October 2026. The detailed advertisement is scheduled for publication in Employment News/Rozgar Samachar on 07 November 2026. Online registration will remain open from 09 November 2026 to 18 November 2026 through the official AFMS portal at join.afms.gov.in.

Age limit is below 30 years for MBBS/PG Diploma holders (born on or after 02 January 1997) and below 35 years for PG degree holders (born on or after 02 January 1992), calculated as on 31 December 2026. Selection will be through an interview tentatively held in Delhi during December 2026, followed by document verification and medical examination.

The application fee is Rs.200 for all categories. Eligible candidates should read the detailed notification carefully before applying, as it will contain the complete eligibility conditions, application format and other instructions.

Eligibility Criteria

Age Limit

Below 30 years for MBBS/PG Diploma; below 35 years for PG Degree (as on 31 December 2026).

Age relaxation for reserved categories as per rules.

Education

MBBS degree included in the National Medical Commission Act 2019 with permanent registration from State Medical Council/MCI/NMC.

Internship must be completed on or before 15 August 2025.

Nationality / Other

Indian citizens meeting physical and mental fitness standards of the Armed Forces.
