Complete context

About This Recruitment

Armed Forces Medical Services (AFMS), Ministry of Defence, has announced 120 Medical Officer (Short Service Commission) vacancies for 2026. Of these, 90 posts are for male candidates and 30 posts are for female candidates. This recruitment is open to Indian citizens holding an MBBS degree and meeting the prescribed physical and mental fitness standards.

The short notice was released on 31 October 2026. The detailed advertisement is scheduled for publication in Employment News/Rozgar Samachar on 07 November 2026. Online registration will remain open from 09 November 2026 to 18 November 2026 through the official AFMS portal at join.afms.gov.in.

Age limit is below 30 years for MBBS/PG Diploma holders (born on or after 02 January 1997) and below 35 years for PG degree holders (born on or after 02 January 1992), calculated as on 31 December 2026. Selection will be through an interview tentatively held in Delhi during December 2026, followed by document verification and medical examination.

The application fee is Rs.200 for all categories. Eligible candidates should read the detailed notification carefully before applying, as it will contain the complete eligibility conditions, application format and other instructions.

Eligibility Criteria

Age Limit

Below 30 years for MBBS/PG Diploma holders (born on or after 02 January 1997); below 35 years for PG Degree holders (born on or after 02 January 1992), as on 31 December 2026.

Age relaxation for reserved categories as per government rules.

Education

MBBS degree included in the National Medical Commission Act 2019 with permanent registration from State Medical Council/MCI/NMC. Internship must be completed on or before 15 August 2025.

Postgraduate degree holders recognised by State Medical Council/MCI/NBE/NMC may also apply.

Nationality / Other

Indian citizens meeting the physical and mental fitness standards of the Armed Forces. Candidates must be medically fit as per prescribed standards.

Refer to the detailed notification for complete conditions.

What to do at the current stage

How to Apply Online

  1. Visit the official AFMS recruitment portal at join.afms.gov.in.
  2. Click on the Medical Officer (Short Service Commission) 2026 registration link.
  3. Register using a valid mobile number and personal email address.
  4. Fill the online application form with personal, educational and medical details.
  5. Upload scanned photograph, signature, MBBS degree certificate, internship completion certificate and medical registration certificate.
  6. Pay the application fee of Rs.200 through Net Banking, Debit Card, Credit Card or UPI.
  7. Verify all details carefully and submit the application before 18 November 2026.
  8. Download and print the confirmation page for future reference.
