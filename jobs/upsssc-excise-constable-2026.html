Complete context

About This Recruitment

Uttar Pradesh Subordinate Services Selection Commission (UPSSSC) issued Advertisement No. 08-Exam/2026 to fill 722 Excise Constable (Abkari Sipahi) posts in the Uttar Pradesh Excise Department, including 612 general recruitment posts and 110 special selection posts. The online application window ran from 04 June 2026 to 24 June 2026, with form corrections allowed until 01 July 2026.

Candidates were shortlisted for the Mains Examination based on their UPSSSC PET 2025 score, with the eligibility result declared on 28 July 2026. The Mains Written Examination was conducted on 20 September 2026 after admit cards were released on 16 September 2026, followed by the provisional answer key on 22 September 2026.

On 23 November 2026, UPSSSC declared the Excise Constable Mains Result 2026 along with the official cut-off notice and PET/PST schedule. Shortlisted candidates are scheduled to appear for the Physical Efficiency Test (PET) and Physical Standard Test (PST) starting 07 December 2026, followed by Document Verification and Final Merit List.

Eligible applicants required a valid UPSSSC PET 2025 scorecard and a 10+2 (Intermediate) qualification from a recognised board in India. The age limit was 18 to 40 years as on 01 July 2026, with category-wise relaxation as per Uttar Pradesh government rules.

Frequently Asked Questions

Yes, Uttar Pradesh Subordinate Services Selection Commission (UPSSSC) declared the Excise Constable Mains Result 2026 on 23 November 2026. Candidates can check their qualifying status and cut-off notice using the official links on this page.
