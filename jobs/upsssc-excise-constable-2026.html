About This Recruitment

UPSSSC Excise Constable Recruitment 2026 is a state-level recruitment drive conducted by the Uttar Pradesh Subordinate Service Selection Commission (UPSSSC) for 722 Excise Constable posts, including 612 regular and 110 special selection vacancies. The online application window was open from 04 June 2026 to 24 June 2026.

This recruitment is suitable for candidates who have passed 10+2/Intermediate and possess a valid UPSSSC PET 2025 score card. The selection process typically includes a written examination, document verification, and a physical efficiency test as applicable. The admit card for the written exam has now been released.

Candidates should keep their registration details, photo ID, and admit card ready for the examination. All future stages—answer key, result, PET/PST, document verification, and final merit list—will be updated on this same permanent page.
