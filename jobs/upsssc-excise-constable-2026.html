Complete context

About This Recruitment

UPSSSC Excise Constable Recruitment 2026 is a Uttar Pradesh Subordinate Service Selection Commission (UPSSSC) drive for 722 Excise Constable posts, including 612 regular and 110 Special Selection vacancies. The online application window was open from 04 June 2026 to 24 June 2026, with a fee payment and correction window in June–July 2026.

Candidates needed a valid UPSSSC PET 2025 score card and 10+2/Intermediate qualification from a recognised board. The written examination was held on 20 September 2026, and the admit card was released on 16 September 2026. The answer key has now been released on 22 September 2026.
