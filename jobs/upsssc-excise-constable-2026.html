About This Recruitment

UPSSSC Excise Constable Recruitment 2026 is a major recruitment drive conducted by the Uttar Pradesh Subordinate Services Selection Commission (UPSSSC) for 820 Excise Constable (Aabkari Sipahi) posts under the Office of the Excise Commissioner, Uttar Pradesh. The recruitment was announced under Advertisement No. 08-Exam/2026, with the online application window open from 4 June 2026 to 24 June 2026.

The recruitment process began with a Preliminary Eligibility Test (PET) 2025 scorecard requirement. Based on PET-2025 normalized scores, UPSSSC released the eligibility result on 28 July 2026, shortlisting 32,891 candidates for the Main Examination. The Main Examination is scheduled for 20 September 2026 from 10:00 AM to 12:00 PM in offline mode across multiple districts in Uttar Pradesh.

This page serves as the permanent lifecycle page for UPSSSC Excise Constable Recruitment 2026. All future updates including admit card release, answer key, result, physical test schedule, document verification and final merit list will be published on this same URL without changing the web address.
