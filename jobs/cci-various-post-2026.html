Complete context

About This Recruitment

The Cotton Corporation of India Limited (CCI) is a public sector undertaking under the Ministry of Textiles, Government of India. The CCI Various Post Recruitment 2026 is a major drive to fill 148 vacancies across several posts, including Assistant Manager, Management Trainee, Junior Commercial Executive, and Junior Assistant roles.

The official notification was released in July 2026, with the online application window open from 09 July 2026 to 24 July 2026. The Computer Based Test (CBT) was conducted on 01 September 2026. The final result and merit list have now been declared on 23 October 2026, marking the completion of this recruitment cycle.

Eligibility varies by post, ranging from a Bachelor's degree in Commerce or Agriculture to an MBA or CA/CMA qualification. The age limit is generally between 18 and 30 years, with a maximum of 32 years for Assistant Manager posts. Age relaxation is available for reserved categories as per government rules.

This page serves as the permanent record for the CCI Various Post Recruitment 2026, tracking every stage from notification to final result. The canonical URL will remain the same for all future updates.

How to Check CCI Final Result

  1. Click on the 'Download Result' link in the Important Links section.
  2. The official merit list PDF will open in a new tab.
  3. Search for your roll number or name in the PDF using Ctrl+F.
  4. Check your selection status and other details carefully.
  5. Download and save the PDF for future reference.
  6. Keep checking the official website for further instructions.

Original Application Process

Preserved for recruitment history and reference after the application stage closes.

  1. Visit the official CCI recruitment website at cotcorp.org.in.
  2. Read the official notification carefully.
  3. Register with a valid email ID and mobile number.
  4. Fill the online application form with accurate personal and educational details.
  5. Upload photograph, signature and required documents.
  6. Pay the applicable examination fee online.
  7. Submit the form and save the confirmation page for future reference.
