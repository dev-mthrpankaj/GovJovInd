Complete context

About This Recruitment

The Supreme Court of India (SCI) has announced the recruitment of Court Master (Shorthand) for 20 tentative vacancies. The official notification (Advt No. F.6/2026/11/CM(S)/2026-SC(RC)) was released in October 2026, and the online application window will remain open from 25 October 2026 to 15 November 2026.

This is a prestigious government job opportunity for candidates with a Bachelor's degree, proficiency in English shorthand at 120 WPM, English typing at 40 WPM, and a minimum of 5 years of experience. The age limit is 30 to 45 years as on 01 October 2026.

The selection process will include a written examination, skill test (shorthand and typing), and document verification. The exact exam date and other stage details will be announced by the Supreme Court of India in due course.

This is a permanent recruitment lifecycle page. All future updates, including admit card, answer key, result, and final merit list, will be published on this same page without changing the URL.

What to do at the current stage

How to Apply Online

  1. Visit the official Supreme Court of India recruitment website at sci.gov.in/recruitments.
  2. Read the official notification (Advt No. F.6/2026/11/CM(S)/2026-SC(RC)) carefully.
  3. Register on the SCI recruitment portal with a valid email ID and mobile number.
  4. Fill the online application form with accurate personal and educational details.
  5. Upload scanned documents: photograph, signature, and required certificates.
  6. Pay the application fee online (Rs.1500 for General, Rs.750 for OBC/SC/ST).
  7. Submit the form and download/print the confirmation page for future reference.
