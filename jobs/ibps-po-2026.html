Complete context

About This Recruitment

IBPS PO/MT XVI Recruitment 2026 is a national-level banking recruitment drive conducted by the Institute of Banking Personnel Selection (IBPS) for 7,365 Probationary Officer (PO) and Management Trainee (MT) vacancies across 11 participating public sector banks. The online application window was open from 1 July 2026 to 26 July 2026 (extended).

The selection process includes a Preliminary Examination (qualifying), Main Examination, and Interview. The final merit list is prepared based on Mains and Interview performance in an 80:20 ratio. Candidates must hold a Bachelor's Degree in any stream from a recognised university and be between 20 and 30 years of age as on 1 July 2026.

The IBPS PO Preliminary Examination was held on 22 and 23 August 2026. The Prelims Result has been declared on 23 September 2026 and the result link will remain active until 30 September 2026. The Mains Examination is scheduled for 4 October 2026. This permanent page will continue to carry every later official stage without any URL change.
