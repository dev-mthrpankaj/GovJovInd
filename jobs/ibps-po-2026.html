About This Recruitment

IBPS PO Recruitment 2026 is a major banking recruitment drive conducted by the Institute of Banking Personnel Selection (IBPS) for 7,365 Probationary Officer (PO) and Management Trainee (MT) posts across 11 participating public sector banks. The online application window was open from 1 July 2026 to 26 July 2026 (extended).

Graduates between 20 and 30 years of age are eligible to apply. The selection process includes Preliminary Examination, Main Examination, Personality Test, and Interview. The Preliminary Exam was held on 22 and 23 August 2026, and the Main Exam is scheduled for 4 October 2026.

This permanent page carries every official stage of the IBPS PO 2026 lifecycle — from notification and application to admit card, answer key, result, and final allotment — on the same URL.
