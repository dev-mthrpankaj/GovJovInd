Complete context

About This Recruitment

The Institute of Banking Personnel Selection (IBPS) released the official CRP PO/MT-XVI notification on 30 June 2026 to recruit 7365 Probationary Officers (PO) and Management Trainees (MT) across 11 participating public sector banks in India. Online applications were accepted from 01 July 2026 to 26 July 2026, followed by an application correction window on 29-30 July 2026.

The Preliminary Examination was conducted on 23 August 2026, and the Prelims Result and Scorecard were released in September 2026. Candidates who qualified in the preliminary stage appeared for the Mains Examination in October 2026.

On 20 November 2026, the Institute of Banking Personnel Selection (IBPS) declared the IBPS PO Mains Result 2026. Shortlisted candidates will now be called for the Interview and Document Verification rounds as per the official schedule, followed by Provisional Allotment.

Eligibility for IBPS PO/MT XVI Recruitment 2026 required a Bachelor's Degree in any discipline from a recognised university and an age limit of 20 to 30 years as on 01 July 2026. The basic pay scale is Rs.48,480 to Rs.85,920 along with applicable banking allowances.

What to do at the current stage

How to Check IBPS PO Mains Result 2026

  1. Open the direct IBPS PO Mains Result 2026 link from the Important Links section below.
  2. Enter your Registration Number or Roll Number along with your Password or Date of Birth (DD-MM-YY).
  3. Type the captcha verification code shown on the login screen and click Login.
  4. View your Mains Exam qualifying status for the Interview round on the screen.
  5. Download and save a copy of the result status and take a clear printout for the Interview and Document Verification stages.
  6. Keep your login credentials and original educational and category certificates ready for the upcoming Interview.

Original Application Process

Preserved for recruitment history and reference after the application stage closes.

  1. Visit the official Institute of Banking Personnel Selection (IBPS) website at ibps.in.
  2. Open the CRP PO/MT-XVI online registration portal and complete new registration.
  3. Fill out the online application form with personal, educational and bank preference details.
  4. Upload scanned photograph, signature, left thumb impression and handwritten declaration.
  5. Pay the required application fee (Rs.850 for General/OBC/EWS or Rs.175 for SC/ST/PwBD) online.
  6. Submit the form and print the final application form for future reference.

Frequently Asked Questions

Yes, the Institute of Banking Personnel Selection (IBPS) declared the IBPS PO/MT XVI Mains Result 2026 on 20 November 2026. Candidates can check their qualifying status online using their Registration Number or Roll Number and Password or Date of Birth.

To check the IBPS PO Mains Result 2026, open the official IBPS result login link in the Important Links section, enter your Registration Number or Roll Number and Date of Birth or Password, complete the captcha, and submit to view and download your result.

Candidates who qualify in the IBPS PO/MT XVI Mains Examination 2026 will be called for the Interview and Document Verification rounds conducted by participating banks, followed by the final merit list and Provisional Allotment.

A total of 7365 Probationary Officer (PO) and Management Trainee (MT) vacancies are advertised under IBPS CRP PO/MT-XVI Recruitment 2026, including 2936 General, 2015 OBC, 721 EWS, 1131 SC, and 562 ST posts across participating banks.

The basic pay scale for IBPS PO/MT XVI is Rs.48,480-2000/7-62,480-2340/2-67,160-2680/7-85,920. Selected officers also receive Dearness Allowance, House Rent Allowance, Special Allowance, and other benefits as per bank rules.

The online application process for IBPS PO/MT XVI Recruitment 2026 started on 01 July 2026 on the official IBPS website following the release of the detailed notification on 30 June 2026.

The last date to submit the online application form and pay the fee for IBPS PO/MT XVI Recruitment 2026 was 26 July 2026. The application window is now closed, and an edit window was provided on 29-30 July 2026.

Candidates holding a Bachelor's Degree in any stream from a recognised university in India and meeting the prescribed age limit of 20 to 30 years as on 01 July 2026 were eligible for IBPS PO/MT XVI Recruitment 2026.

The age limit for IBPS PO/MT XVI Recruitment 2026 is 20 to 30 years as on 01 July 2026. Upper age relaxation is applicable for SC, ST, OBC, PwBD, and Ex-Servicemen candidates as per Government of India rules.

The online application fee for IBPS PO/MT XVI Recruitment 2026 was Rs.850 for General, OBC, and EWS candidates, and Rs.175 for SC, ST, and PwBD candidates, payable through online payment modes.

Candidates applied online on ibps.in by completing registration, filling in personal and academic details, uploading photo, signature, and documents, paying the application fee, and saving the final submitted form.

The official website for IBPS PO/MT XVI Recruitment 2026 is https://www.ibps.in/, where all official notices, admit cards, Mains results, interview call letters, and provisional allotment lists are published.
