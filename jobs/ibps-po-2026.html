Complete context

About This Recruitment

Institute of Banking Personnel Selection (IBPS) is conducting the IBPS CRP PO/MT-XVI (Probationary Officer) Recruitment 2026 to fill 7,565 Probationary Officer and Management Trainee posts across participating public sector banks in India. The detailed official notification was issued on 30 June 2026, and online registration remained open from 01 July 2026 to 26 July 2026 (extended), followed by an application correction window on 29 July 2026 and 30 July 2026.

The Online Preliminary Examination was conducted on 23 August 2026 after the Pre Admit Card release on 14 August 2026, and the Preliminary Result was declared on 24 September 2026 alongside Mains call letters. IBPS also published an updated vacancy annexure on 25 August 2026 confirming 7,565 posts. The Online Mains Examination took place on 04 October 2026, and the Mains Result was declared on 20 November 2026.

On 27 November 2026, IBPS released the CRP PO/MT-XVI Mains Score Card on its official portal ibps.in. Candidates who appeared in the Mains examination can log in using their Registration Number or Roll Number and Password or Date of Birth to check section-wise and overall marks. Shortlisted candidates will next appear for the Interview round, followed by Final Selection and Provisional Allotment.

Eligibility requires a Bachelor's degree in any discipline from a recognised university in India and an age limit of 20 to 30 years as on 01 July 2026, with upper age relaxation for reserved categories as per IBPS norms. The application fee was Rs.850 for General, OBC, and EWS candidates and Rs.175 for SC, ST, and PH (PwBD) candidates.

What to do at the current stage

How to Download IBPS PO Mains Score Card 2026

  1. Open the official IBPS Mains Score Card link from Important Links below.
  2. Enter your Registration Number or Roll Number in the login box.
  3. Enter your Password or Date of Birth in DD-MM-YY format.
  4. Type the displayed captcha code carefully and click Login.
  5. View your IBPS CRP PO/MT-XVI Mains Score Card and section-wise marks on screen.
  6. Download and print a copy of the score card and Mains result for Interview verification.

Original Application Process

Preserved for recruitment history and reference after the application stage closes.

  1. Visit the official IBPS website at ibps.in and open the CRP PO/MT-XVI section.
  2. Read the detailed CRP PO/MT-XVI notification dated 30 June 2026 carefully.
  3. Complete new registration using a valid email ID and active mobile number.
  4. Fill in personal, academic, and participating bank preference details accurately.
  5. Upload scanned photograph, signature, left thumb impression, and handwritten declaration.
  6. Pay the online application fee (Rs.850 for General/OBC/EWS or Rs.175 for SC/ST/PH).
  7. Submit the application form and save the confirmation printout for later stages.
