Complete context

About This Recruitment

IBPS PO/MT XVI Recruitment 2026 is a Common Recruitment Process conducted by the Institute of Banking Personnel Selection (IBPS) for the post of Probationary Officer (PO) / Management Trainee (MT) in 11 participating public sector banks. The recruitment drive aims to fill 7,365 vacancies across participating banks.

The online application window was open from 1 July 2026 to 26 July 2026 (extended). The Preliminary Examination was conducted on 22 and 23 August 2026, and the Main Examination is scheduled for 4 October 2026. The IBPS PO Mains Admit Card 2026 has been released on 24 September 2026 and is available for download until 4 October 2026.

This recruitment introduces a new mandatory Personality Test between the Mains examination and the Interview. The final merit list will be prepared based on 80% weightage to Mains marks and 20% weightage to Interview marks. Candidates are advised to check eligibility, exam pattern, and official notifications carefully before proceeding.

What to do at the current stage

How to Download Mains Admit Card

  1. Visit the official IBPS website at ibps.in.
  2. Click on the CRP PO/MT-XVI Mains Exam Call Letter link.
  3. Enter your Registration Number/Roll Number and Password/Date of Birth.
  4. Complete the CAPTCHA verification.
  5. Click on Submit to view your admit card.
  6. Download and take a printout of the admit card for the exam.
  7. Check all details carefully including exam date, shift, and centre address.
  8. Carry the admit card and a valid government-issued photo ID to the exam centre.

Original Application Process

Preserved for recruitment history and reference after the application stage closes.

  1. Visit the official IBPS website at ibps.in.
  2. Click on the CRP PO/MT-XVI application link.
  3. Register and fill in the application form carefully.
  4. Upload required documents in the prescribed format.
  5. Pay the applicable application fee.
  6. Review and submit the form before the deadline.
  7. Download and save the confirmation page for future reference.

Quick answers

Frequently Asked Questions

Yes, IBPS PO Mains Admit Card 2026 has been released on 24 September 2026. Candidates can download their call letter from the official IBPS website until 4 October 2026.

The IBPS PO Mains Examination 2026 is scheduled to be held on 4 October 2026. The exam will consist of objective questions carrying 200 marks and a descriptive test carrying 25 marks.

According to the official notification, there are a total of 7,365 Probationary Officer (PO) / Management Trainee (MT) vacancies across 11 participating public sector banks.

The application fee is Rs. 850 for General/OBC/EWS candidates and Rs. 175 for SC/ST/PwBD candidates. Payment can be made online through debit/credit card, net banking, UPI, IMPS, or wallets.

Candidates must be between 20 and 30 years of age as on 1 July 2026. Age relaxation is applicable as per government rules: SC/ST (5 years), OBC (3 years), PwBD (10 years), and Ex-Servicemen (5 years).

Candidates must possess a Bachelor's degree in any discipline from a recognized university. The final result must be declared on or before 21 July 2026.

The selection process consists of five stages: Preliminary Examination (qualifying), Main Examination, Personality Test (mandatory but non-qualifying), Interview, and Provisional Allotment. Final merit is based on 80% weightage to Mains marks and 20% weightage to Interview marks.

The basic pay for IBPS PO is Rs. 48,480 with a pay scale of Rs. 48,480-2000/7-62480-2340/2-67160-2680/7-85920. The approximate in-hand salary ranges from Rs. 65,000 to Rs. 70,000 per month including DA, HRA, and other allowances.

IBPS PO Prelims Scorecard 2026 has been released on 29 September 2026. Candidates can download their scorecard from the official IBPS website until 28 October 2026.

The IBPS PO Mains exam consists of 170 objective questions carrying 200 marks, with sections on Reasoning (40 Q, 60 marks), General/Economy/Banking/Digital/Financial Awareness (50 Q, 60 marks), English Language (40 Q, 20 marks), and Data Analysis & Interpretation (40 Q, 60 marks). There is also a descriptive test of 2 questions carrying 25 marks. Duration is 160 minutes for objective and 30 minutes for descriptive.

The provisional allotment for IBPS PO 2026 is expected to be released in January 2027. Final merit will be prepared based on Mains and Interview scores, and candidates will be allotted to participating banks based on merit-cum-preference.
