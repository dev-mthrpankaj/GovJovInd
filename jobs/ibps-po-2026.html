About This Recruitment

IBPS PO/MT XVI Recruitment 2026 is a Common Recruitment Process conducted by the Institute of Banking Personnel Selection (IBPS) for the post of Probationary Officer (PO) / Management Trainee (MT) in 11 participating public sector banks. The recruitment drive aims to fill 7,365 vacancies across participating banks.

The online application window was open from 1 July 2026 to 26 July 2026 (extended). The Preliminary Examination was conducted on 22 and 23 August 2026, and the Main Examination is scheduled for 4 October 2026. The IBPS PO Mains Admit Card 2026 has been released on 24 September 2026 and is available for download until 4 October 2026.

This recruitment introduces a new mandatory Personality Test between the Mains examination and the Interview. The final merit list will be prepared based on 80% weightage to Mains marks and 20% weightage to Interview marks. Candidates are advised to check eligibility, exam pattern, and official notifications carefully before proceeding.
