About This Recruitment

UPSSSC Physical Instructor & Other Post Recruitment 2026 is a Uttar Pradesh Subordinate Service Selection Commission (UPSSSC) recruitment drive for 412 posts across two distinct profiles — Regional Youth Welfare and Provincial Development Team Officer (368 posts) and Physical Training Instructor (44 posts). The online application window opens on 16 October 2026 and closes on 6 November 2026.

Both posts require a valid UPSSSC PET 2025 Score Card and a Bachelor's Degree in any stream from a recognised university. The Physical Training Instructor post additionally requires a DPED / BPED qualification. Candidates must also meet the prescribed physical standards for height and chest, with separate relaxations for ST candidates.

Eligibility Criteria

Age Limit

21 to 40 years as on 1 July 2026.

Age relaxation applicable for SC, ST, OBC, EWS, PH and other reserved categories as per UPSSSC rules.

Education

Bachelor's Degree in Any Stream from a recognised university. For Physical Training Instructor post, DPED / BPED degree is also required. Valid UPSSSC PET 2025 Score Card is mandatory for both posts.

Physical standards: Male Height 167.7 cm (ST: 160 cm), Female Height 152 cm (ST: 147 cm), Chest 78.8 cm to 83.8 cm. Refer to the official notification for complete details.

Physical Standards / Other

Indian citizens who meet the educational qualification, UPSSSC PET 2025 score requirement, age limit and physical standards prescribed for the respective post can apply.

Refer to official notification for detailed conditions.
