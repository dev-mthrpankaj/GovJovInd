Complete context

About This Recruitment

UPSSSC Physical Instructor (Vyayam Prashikshak) Recruitment 2026 is conducted by the Uttar Pradesh Subordinate Services Selection Commission (UPSSSC), Lucknow under Advertisement No. 23-Exam/2026. The recruitment is for 44 Physical Training Instructor posts under the Directorate of Youth Welfare and Provincial Guard (Yuva Kalyan evam Prantiya Rakshak Dal Mahanideshalaya), Uttar Pradesh. The post carries Pay Level 6 with a salary range of Rs. 35,400 to Rs. 1,12,400.

This is the Mains stage of UPSSSC's two-tier system. Only candidates who appeared in the Preliminary Eligibility Test (PET) 2025 and received a valid PET 2025 scorecard with non-zero positive marks are eligible to apply. Shortlisting for the Mains written examination will be based on the normalized PET 2025 score. A total of 412 posts are advertised under this notification, comprising 368 Regional Youth Welfare & Provincial Development Team Officer posts and 44 Physical Training Instructor posts.

The online application window opened on 16 October 2026 and will remain open until 06 November 2026. The last date for fee adjustment and application correction is 13 November 2026. The application fee is Rs. 25 for all categories. Candidates must be between 21 and 40 years of age as on 01 July 2026, with age relaxation for reserved categories as per UP government rules.

Eligibility for the Physical Training Instructor post requires a Bachelor's degree in any stream from a recognised university in India along with a D.P.Ed (Diploma in Physical Education), B.P.Ed (Bachelor of Physical Education), or B.P.E qualification. Preference will be given to candidates with an M.P.Ed, NIS coaching diploma, Territorial Army service, or NCC 'B' certificate. The selection process includes a written Mains examination (100 questions, 100 marks, 2 hours, with 25% negative marking), a qualifying Physical Efficiency Test, document verification, and final merit preparation based strictly on Mains examination marks.

What to do at the current stage

How to Apply Online for UPSSSC Physical Instructor 2026

  1. Visit the official UPSSSC website at upsssc.gov.in.
  2. Under the 'Live Advertisement Segment,' locate and open Advertisement No. 23-Exam/2026.
  3. Log in using your PET 2025 registration number, verified via OTP sent to your registered mobile number/email.
  4. Review the auto-filled personal details from your PET 2025 application.
  5. Select 'Physical Training Instructor (Vyayam Prashikshak)' as your preferred post.
  6. Confirm your qualification details including graduation degree and D.P.Ed/B.P.Ed certification.
  7. Verify the auto-displayed photo and signature from PET 2025.
  8. Indicate any preferential qualification (M.P.Ed, NIS coaching diploma, Territorial Army service, NCC 'B' certificate).
  9. Read and accept the declaration, enter the verification code, and submit the application.
  10. Note down your 11-digit registration number and take a printout.
  11. Pay the Rs. 25 application fee through SBI e-Pay, Debit Card, Credit Card, Internet Banking, or UPI.
  12. Download and verify the final printout after fee adjustment.
