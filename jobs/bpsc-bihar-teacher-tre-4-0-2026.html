About This Recruitment

BPSC Bihar Teacher TRE 4.0 Recruitment 2026 is a Bihar Public Service Commission (BPSC) drive to fill 33320 School Teacher posts across Bihar. The official notification (Advertisement No. 15/2026) was released in September 2026, and the online application window was opened from 25 September 2026. The last date to apply online has been extended to 03 November 2026.

The recruitment covers Primary School Teacher (Class 1-5), Middle School Teacher (Class 6-8), TGT Teacher (Class 9-10) and PGT Teacher (Class 11-12). Eligibility includes CTET/BTET/STET qualification along with the required educational qualifications for each post. The selection process will include a written examination followed by document verification and other stages as per the official notification.

The application fee is Rs. 100 for all categories. Candidates must complete BPSC OTR (One Time Registration) before applying. The exam date and admit card details will be announced later on the official BPSC website. This permanent page will track every stage from notification to final result.
