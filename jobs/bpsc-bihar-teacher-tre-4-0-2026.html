About This Recruitment

BPSC Bihar Teacher TRE 4.0 Recruitment 2026 is a major teacher recruitment drive by the Bihar Public Service Commission (BPSC) for 32,388 school teacher posts across Classes 1 to 12 in Bihar. The recruitment was re-announced under Advertisement No. 15/2026 dated 22 September 2026, replacing the earlier Advertisement No. 14/2026.

The online application window is open from 25 September 2026 to 26 October 2026, with the last date for fee payment on 25 October 2026. The examination was originally planned for September 2026 but has been postponed; a revised exam date is yet to be announced by BPSC.

Candidates must hold a graduate degree with B.Ed and have qualified CTET, BTET, or STET as applicable. Age limits range from 18 to 37/40/42 years depending on category and post level. Candidates should keep photo, signature, identity proof, educational certificates, and TET qualification details ready before applying.
