Complete context

About This Recruitment

BPSC Bihar Teacher TRE 4.0 Recruitment 2026 is a Bihar Public Service Commission (BPSC) drive to fill 33320 School Teacher posts across Bihar. The official notification (Advertisement No. 15/2026) was released in September 2026, and the online application window was opened from 25 September 2026. The last date to apply online has been extended to 03 November 2026.

The recruitment covers Primary School Teacher (Class 1-5), Middle School Teacher (Class 6-8), TGT Teacher (Class 9-10) and PGT Teacher (Class 11-12). Eligibility includes CTET/BTET/STET qualification along with the required educational qualifications for each post. The selection process will include a written examination followed by document verification and other stages as per the official notification.

The application fee is Rs. 100 for all categories. Candidates must complete BPSC OTR (One Time Registration) before applying. The exam date and admit card details will be announced later on the official BPSC website. This permanent page will track every stage from notification to final result.

Eligibility Criteria

Age Limit

Minimum 18 years for Primary Teacher, 21 years for TGT/PGT. Maximum 37 years for Male and 40 years for Female.

Age relaxation applicable for SC, ST, OBC, EWS, PwBD and other reserved categories as per Bihar Government rules.

Education

Varies by post: Primary Teacher requires Senior Secondary with 50% + 2 Year Diploma in Elementary Education + CTET/BTET Paper I. Middle School requires Bachelor Degree with 2 Year Diploma in Elementary Education OR Bachelor/Master with 50% + B.Ed + CTET/BTET Paper II. TGT requires Bachelor/Master in related subject with 50% + B.Ed + STET Paper I. PGT requires Master Degree in related subject with 50% + B.Ed + STET Paper II.

Candidates must have passed CTET/BTET/STET as applicable for the post. Read the official notification for detailed subject-wise eligibility.

Nationality / Other

Indian citizens fulfilling the post-specific eligibility conditions may apply. Bihar STET/CTET appearing candidates are not eligible. BPSC OTR registration is mandatory before applying.

Refer to official notification for detailed conditions.

What to do at the current stage

How to Apply Online

  1. Visit the official BPSC online portal at bpsconline.bihar.gov.in.
  2. Complete your BPSC OTR (One Time Registration) if not already done.
  3. Log in with your OTR credentials and select Bihar Teacher TRE 4.0.
  4. Fill the online application form with accurate personal and educational details.
  5. Upload photograph, signature and required documents.
  6. Pay the application fee of Rs. 100 through online modes.
  7. Submit the form and take a printout of the confirmation page.
