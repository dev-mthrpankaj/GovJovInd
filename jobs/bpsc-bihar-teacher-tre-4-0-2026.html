About This Recruitment

BPSC Bihar Teacher TRE 4.0 Recruitment 2026 is a massive teacher recruitment drive conducted by the Bihar Public Service Commission (BPSC) for 32,388 school teacher posts across Classes 1 to 12 in the Education Department, Government of Bihar.

The recruitment covers Primary Teacher (Classes 1-5), Middle School Teacher (Classes 6-8), Secondary Teacher (Classes 9-10) and Higher Secondary Teacher (Classes 11-12). The online application window is scheduled from 25 September 2026 to 26 October 2026, with the fee payment deadline on 25 October 2026.

This is a permanent recruitment page that will carry every future update — including exam date, admit card, answer key, result and final merit list — on the same URL.
