Complete context

About This Recruitment

AIIMS NORCET Nursing Officer 11th Recruitment 2026 is a national-level recruitment drive conducted by the All India Institute of Medical Sciences (AIIMS), New Delhi, to fill 2,218 Nursing Officer (Group B) posts across 27 participating institutions including AIIMS branches, ESIC, JIPMER, NITRD, and other central government hospitals. The official notification (Notice No. 103/2026) was released on 24 July 2026, and the online application window remained open from 24 July 2026 to 13 August 2026 (5:00 PM).

The selection process consists of two stages of computer-based tests followed by document verification and medical examination. The Stage I Preliminary CBT was held on 12 September 2026, and the result was declared on 18 September 2026, with 55,967 candidates qualifying for Stage II. A total of 1,00,804 candidates appeared for the Stage I examination. AIIMS shortlisted 12,638 candidates for Stage II.

The Stage II Mains CBT was conducted on 30 September 2026. The Stage II Mains result and cut off were declared on 10 October 2026, with 10,613 candidates qualifying the Mains examination. Candidates who qualify Stage II will be eligible for institute preference filling, seat allocation, document verification, and medical examination. The final merit list will be prepared based on Stage II marks.

The pay scale for the Nursing Officer post is Pay Level 7 in the Pay Matrix (Rs. 9,300 – Rs. 34,800 with Grade Pay Rs. 4,600 and entry basic pay of Rs. 44,900). The age limit is 18 to 30 years as on 13 August 2026, with relaxation for reserved categories as per Central Government guidelines.

What to do at the current stage

How to Check AIIMS NORCET 11th Stage II Result

  1. Visit the official AIIMS examination portal at aiimsexams.ac.in.
  2. Click on the 'Recruitments' tab on the homepage.
  3. Select the 'NORCET' option from the list.
  4. Click on 'View Details' and then select the 'Result' button.
  5. Click on the 'NORCET 11 Stage II Result' link.
  6. The result PDF will appear on the screen. Search for your roll number.
  7. Download the PDF and keep a copy for future reference.

Original Application Process

Preserved for recruitment history and reference after the application stage closes.

  1. Visit the official AIIMS examination portal at aiimsexams.ac.in.
