About This Recruitment

AIIMS NORCET 11th Recruitment 2026 is a national-level recruitment drive conducted by the All India Institute of Medical Sciences (AIIMS), New Delhi, for 2218 Nursing Officer posts across participating AIIMS institutions and other central government hospitals. The online application window was open from 24 July 2026 to 13 August 2026 (5:00 PM).

The selection process includes two computer-based tests — Stage-I Preliminary (qualifying) and Stage-II Mains (merit-determining), followed by document verification and medical examination. The Stage-I exam was held on 12 September 2026, and the Stage-II exam is scheduled for 30 September 2026.

This permanent page will carry every official update — from the Stage-II admit card to the final result and joining — on the same URL. Candidates should check the Important Links section for all current and historical official links.
