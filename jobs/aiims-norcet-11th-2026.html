About This Recruitment

AIIMS NORCET 11th 2026 is the Nursing Officer Recruitment Common Eligibility Test conducted by All India Institute of Medical Sciences (AIIMS). The online application window ran from 24 July to 13 August 2026.

The current supplied update is the Exam City information released on 3 September 2026. The admit card is scheduled for 9 September 2026, the Preliminary Examination for 12 September 2026, and the Main Examination for 30 September 2026.

Eligible candidates include B.Sc. Nursing candidates registered as Nurses and Midwives, or candidates with the specified General Nursing Midwifery diploma, registration, and required hospital experience. Candidates should check the detailed AIIMS notice for complete eligibility and participating-institute conditions.
