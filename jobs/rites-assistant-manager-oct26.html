Complete context

About This Recruitment

Rail India Technical and Economic Service (RITES), a Navratna CPSE under the Ministry of Railways, has announced recruitment for 27 Assistant Manager (Mechanical) posts. The vacancies are distributed across three levels — Assistant Manager (Mechanical-I), (Mechanical-II), and (Mechanical-III) — under advertisement numbers CL/09/26 to CL/11/26.

The online application window opened on 30 October 2026 and will close on 20 November 2026. The written examination is scheduled for 29 November 2026. Candidates with a Diploma in Mechanical Engineering and 8 years of experience, or a B.E./B.Tech in Mechanical Engineering with 2 years of experience, are eligible to apply.

The selection process includes a written examination, document verification, and a medical examination. The age limit is 18 to 40 years, with relaxation for reserved categories as per RITES rules. The application fee is Rs.600 for General/OBC candidates and Rs.300 for EWS/SC/ST/PwBD candidates.
