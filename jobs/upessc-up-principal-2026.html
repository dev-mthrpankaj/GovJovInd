Complete context

About This Recruitment

Uttar Pradesh Education Service Selection Commission (UPESSC) released Advt No. 08/2026 for UPESSC UP Principal Recruitment 2026 to fill 1475 Principal posts across Uttar Pradesh, including 1318 posts for Principal (Balak) and 157 posts for Principal (Balika). The online application window was open from 23 October 2026 to 22 November 2026.

The UPESSC UP Principal application correction window is currently open from 23 November 2026 to 26 November 2026. Registered candidates who submitted their forms can log in at apply.upessc.org/login to review and edit permitted details before the correction deadline closes.

Selected candidates are appointed in Pay Scale Level-12 (Rs.78,800 to Rs.2,09,200). Eligibility requires a Post Graduation degree with 50% marks, a B.Ed. degree from a recognised university, and at least 4 years of qualifying teaching or Headmaster experience, with an age limit of 30 to 62 years as on 01 July 2026.

The written examination is scheduled for 07 January 2027. Admit card and result dates will be announced by UPESSC as per the official schedule, and all upcoming stages will be updated on this permanent page.
