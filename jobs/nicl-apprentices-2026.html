Complete context

About This Recruitment

National Insurance Company Limited (NICL) is engaging 200 Graduate Apprentices for the year 2026-27 under the National Apprenticeship Training Scheme (NATS). The official notification was released on 04 November 2026, and the online application window is open from 04 November 2026 to 18 November 2026.

The apprenticeship is for a period of 12 months. Selected candidates will receive a monthly stipend of Rs.15,000 (NICL share: Rs.8,850 and Government share: Rs.6,150 through DBT). The recruitment covers 14 states, and candidates can apply for only one state.

Eligibility requires a regular, full-time graduation degree in any discipline from a university recognised by AICTE, DOTE, or UGC. The age limit is 21 to 30 years as on 01 April 2026. Selection is based on the percentage of marks obtained in the qualifying graduation examination, followed by document verification.

Candidates must apply through the NATS portal (nats.education.gov.in). A 100% complete NATS profile is mandatory before applying.

What to do at the current stage

How to Apply for NICL Graduate Apprentice 2026

  1. Visit the NATS portal at nats.education.gov.in and complete your student registration.
  2. Upload your graduation degree certificate, marksheets, Aadhaar card, bank passbook and photograph to achieve a 100% complete profile.
  3. Log in with your registered email ID and password.
  4. Under 'Apply against advertised vacancies', search for NATIONAL INSURANCE COMPANY LTD.
  5. Select the Graduate Apprentice vacancy and click Apply.
  6. Verify that your application status displays 'Applied'.
  7. Submit before 18 November 2026 and save the confirmation for future reference.
