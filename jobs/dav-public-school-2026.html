Complete context

About This Recruitment

DAV Public School Teaching & Non-Teaching Recruitment 2026 is a national-level Computer Based Test (CBT) drive conducted by the DAV College Managing Committee (DAVCMC) for the 2027-28 academic session. Vacancies for PRT, TGT, PGT, Nursery Teacher, Lab Assistant, LDC, UDC, Assistant, Library Assistant and Nurse posts are notified at the school and zone level across India.

The online registration window opened on 16 September 2026 and closed on 11 October 2026 (extended from 08 October 2026). Physical scrutiny of career graph and eligibility at nearby DAV Verification Centres ran from 25 September 2026 to 24 October 2026 (extended from 16 October 2026), and approved candidates paid the Rs.300 CBT fee online within 7 days of verification.

On 18 November 2026, DAVCMC announced the updated CBT exam pattern, shift timings and admit card schedule. Exam centre preference locking and CBT Admit Card download will activate on 22 November 2026 at 12:00 noon on a first-come, first-served basis on davrecruit.davcmc.in/CBT/. The 90-minute CBT is scheduled for the second week of December 2026.

Candidates who qualify the CBT receive a Provisional Eligibility Card (PEC), which makes them eligible for the school or zone-level classroom demonstration and interview round. Final appointment offers are issued by respective DAV Public Schools after the demo, interview and document verification.

What to do at the current stage

How to Select Centre & Download CBT Admit Card

  1. Visit the official DAVCMC CBT recruitment portal at davrecruit.davcmc.in/CBT/ on or after 22 November 2026 at 12:00 noon.
  2. Log in using your Registered Mobile Number (RMN), email ID and password created during registration.
  3. Select and lock your preferred CBT examination centre early because centre allotment follows a first-come, first-served basis.
  4. Download the DAV Public School CBT Admit Card 2026 PDF once centre confirmation is complete.
  5. Verify your name, post applied for, exam date, shift timing, reporting time and test centre address.
  6. Print a clear copy of the admit card and keep one recent colour passport-size photograph and an original government photo ID ready for exam day.

Original Application Process

Preserved for recruitment history and reference after the application stage closes.

  1. Visit the official DAV Staff Recruitment website at davrecruit.davcmc.in.
  2. Register using a valid Registered Mobile Number (RMN) and email ID.
  3. Log in with your registration credentials and fill in personal, educational and post details.
  4. Upload scanned photograph, signature and required supporting documents.
  5. Submit the form as ready for verification and print the application form.
  6. Visit the nearest DAV Verification Centre with original certificates for scrutiny and approval.
  7. After approval, pay the CBT examination fee of Rs.300 online within 7 days and save the confirmation receipt.

Frequently Asked Questions

The DAV Public School CBT Admit Card 2026 and exam centre preference link will activate on 22 November 2026 at 12:00 noon on davrecruit.davcmc.in/CBT/. Centre allotment is strictly on a first-come, first-served basis for verified candidates.

Visit davrecruit.davcmc.in/CBT/ on or after 22 November 2026 at 12:00 noon and log in with your Registered Mobile Number and password. Lock your preferred exam centre first, then download and print the CBT Admit Card PDF for exam day.

The DAV College Managing Committee (DAVCMC) will conduct the Computer Based Test (CBT) in the second week of December 2026. The exact exam date, shift timing, reporting time and test centre address will be printed on each candidate's admit card.

Selection includes online registration, physical scrutiny at a DAV Verification Centre, the Computer Based Test (CBT), issuance of a Provisional Eligibility Card (PEC) to qualifiers, and a final school or zone-level classroom demo and interview.

Verified candidates across all categories had to pay a CBT examination fee of Rs.300 online within 7 days after approval at the DAV Verification Centre. Only candidates who completed verification and fee payment are eligible for the CBT.

Candidates must carry a clear printed copy of the DAV CBT Admit Card 2026, one recent colour passport-size photograph, and a valid original government photo ID such as Aadhaar Card, PAN Card, Voter ID, Driving Licence or Passport.

The online application window for DAV Public School Teaching & Non-Teaching Recruitment 2026 is closed. The initial deadline was 08 October 2026, which DAVCMC extended to 11 October 2026, followed by verification up to 24 October 2026.

The online registration process for DAV Public School Teaching & Non-Teaching Recruitment 2026 started on 16 September 2026 on the official DAVCMC recruitment portal at davrecruit.davcmc.in for the 2027-28 academic recruitment session.

DAV College Managing Committee (DAVCMC) has not announced a single central vacancy count in the notification. Vacancies for PRT, TGT, PGT, Nursery Teacher, Lab Assistant, LDC, UDC, Library Assistant and Nurse are filled school and zone wise.

PRT and TGT require Graduation with B.Ed., PGT requires Post Graduation with B.Ed., and Nursery Teacher requires Graduation with NTT. Non-teaching posts require B.Sc. for Lab Assistant, Graduation for LDC/UDC, Library Science for Library Assistant and B.Sc. Nursing for Nurse.

Pay scale and salary for DAV Public School Teaching & Non-Teaching posts are governed by DAV College Managing Committee (DAVCMC) and respective school or state norms as detailed in the official recruitment notification and zone circulars.

Applications are now closed. Candidates registered on davrecruit.davcmc.in, filled personal and educational details, uploaded documents, completed physical eligibility scrutiny at a nearby DAV Verification Centre and paid the Rs.300 CBT fee online.
