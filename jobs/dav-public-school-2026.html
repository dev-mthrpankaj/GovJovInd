Complete context

About This Recruitment

DAV Public School Staff Recruitment 2026 is a centralised recruitment drive conducted by the DAV College Managing Committee (DAVCMC), New Delhi, for Teaching and Non-Teaching posts across DAV Public Schools in India. The recruitment is for the 2027-28 academic session, with vacancies available in various DAV schools and zones across the country.

The official notification was released on 16 September 2026 through the centralised DAV recruitment portal. Online applications are being accepted from 16 September 2026 to 8 October 2026. Candidates must complete the online registration form, take a printout, and visit the nearest DAV Verification Centre with original testimonials for document verification between 25 September and 16 October 2026.

After successful verification and approval, candidates must pay the CBT fee of Rs. 300 within 7 days. The Computer-Based Test (CBT) is scheduled to be conducted across India in December 2026. Candidates who qualify the CBT will receive a Provisional Eligibility Card (PEC) and become eligible for Phase 3, which includes a demo class and interview conducted by individual DAV schools and zones.

The recruitment covers posts such as PGT (Post Graduate Teacher), TGT (Trained Graduate Teacher), PRT (Primary Teacher), Nursery/Pre-Primary Teacher, Counsellor/Wellness Teacher, Lab Assistant, LDC/UDC/Assistant, Library Assistant, and Nurse. Eligibility criteria vary by post and subject, and candidates should check the specific school or zone advertisement for detailed requirements.

Indian citizens fulfilling the post-specific eligibility conditions may apply. Candidates must meet the DAVCMC, State and CBSE eligibility requirements applicable to their preferred post.

What to do at the current stage

How to Apply Online

  1. Visit the official DAV recruitment website at davrecruit.davcmc.in.
  2. Register using your own mobile number and email ID, and verify both through OTP.
  3. Complete the CBT Registration Form with personal, academic, and post-preference details.
  4. Submit the form as 'Form Ready for Verification'. The form remains editable at this stage.
  5. Take a printout of the registration form and visit the nearest DAV Verification Centre with original testimonials.
  6. After verification and approval, pay the CBT fee of Rs. 300 online within 7 days.
  7. Download and retain the confirmation receipt for future reference.
