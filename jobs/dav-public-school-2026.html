Complete context

About This Recruitment

DAV Public School Teaching & Non-Teaching Recruitment 2026 is a national-level Computer Based Test (CBT) drive conducted by the DAV College Managing Committee (DAVCMC) for the 2027-28 academic session. Vacancies for PRT, TGT, PGT, Nursery Teacher, Lab Assistant, LDC, UDC, Assistant, Library Assistant and Nurse posts are notified at the school and zone level across India.

The online registration window opened on 16 September 2026 and closed on 11 October 2026 (extended from 08 October 2026). Physical scrutiny of career graph and eligibility at nearby DAV Verification Centres ran from 25 September 2026 to 24 October 2026 (extended from 16 October 2026), and approved candidates paid the Rs.300 CBT fee online within 7 days of verification.

On 18 November 2026, DAVCMC announced the updated CBT exam pattern, shift timings and admit card schedule. Exam centre preference locking and CBT Admit Card download will activate on 22 November 2026 at 12:00 noon on a first-come, first-served basis on davrecruit.davcmc.in/CBT/. The 90-minute CBT is scheduled for the second week of December 2026.

Candidates who qualify the CBT receive a Provisional Eligibility Card (PEC), which makes them eligible for the school or zone-level classroom demonstration and interview round. Final appointment offers are issued by respective DAV Public Schools after the demo, interview and document verification.
