Complete context

About This Recruitment

UPSC CGSE 2027 (Combined Geo-Scientist Examination) is conducted by the Union Public Service Commission for recruitment to Group A and Group B scientific posts in the Geological Survey of India (GSI), Ministry of Mines, and the Central Ground Water Board (CGWB), Ministry of Jal Shakti. The examination notice was published on 2 September 2026 under Examination Notice No. 01/2027-GEOL.

The recruitment offers a total of 127 tentative vacancies across Geologist, Geophysicist, Chemist, Assistant Geologist, Assistant Geophysicist, Assistant Chemist, Scientist ‘B’ and Assistant Hydrogeologist posts. The selection journey consists of a Preliminary Examination, a Main Examination and a Personality Test/Interview.

The online application window remained open from 2 September 2026 to 22 September 2026 (6:00 PM). The Preliminary Examination is scheduled for 10 January 2027, and the Main Examination will be held on 19 and 20 June 2027. Candidates who applied successfully are now preparing for the prelims stage, while the Commission will release e-admit cards closer to the exam date.

The minimum age is 21 years and the maximum age is 32 years as on 1 January 2027, with upper-age relaxation for SC, ST, OBC, PwBD, ex-servicemen and certain government servants. A postgraduate degree in Geology, Applied Geology, Geophysics, Chemistry, Hydrogeology or an allied discipline is required, depending on the post applied for.

Quick answer: UPSC CGSE 2027 is a postgraduate-level geoscience recruitment exam for 127 GSI and CGWB posts. The prelims exam is on 10 January 2027, mains on 19–20 June 2027, and applications closed on 22 September 2026.
