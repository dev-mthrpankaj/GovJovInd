Complete context

About This Recruitment

UPSC Combined Geo-Scientist Examination 2027 is being conducted by the Union Public Service Commission for recruitment to 127 tentative Group A and Group B vacancies in the Geological Survey of India and Central Ground Water Board.

The online application window is open from 2 September 2026 to 22 September 2026 (6:00 PM). The Combined Geo-Scientist Preliminary Examination is scheduled for 10 January 2027, followed by the Main Examination on 19 and 20 June 2027.

Candidates should check the post-specific postgraduate qualification carefully, complete the UPSC Universal Registration Number (URN), Common Application Form (CAF) and examination-specific module, and keep the prescribed supporting documents ready before submitting the form.

Eligibility Criteria

Age Limit

21 to below 32 years as on 1 January 2027; candidates must have been born not earlier than 2 January 1995 and not later than 1 January 2006.

Upper-age relaxation applies as prescribed in the official rules, including applicable relaxations for SC/ST, OBC, eligible Government servants and other specified categories.

Education

A relevant Master's / M.Sc. / M.Sc. (Tech.) / Integrated M.Sc. or equivalent postgraduate qualification is required depending on the post and discipline.

Qualifications are post-specific. Check the detailed educational qualification for the exact post/stream in Examination Notice No. 01/2027-GEOL.

Nationality / Other

Nationality and certificate-of-eligibility conditions apply as prescribed by UPSC. Candidates must also satisfy the applicable physical and medical standards.

Refer to official notification for detailed conditions.

What to do at the current stage

How to Apply

  1. Open the UPSC online application portal.
  2. Complete or verify Account Creation and Universal Registration Number (URN).
  3. Complete the Common Application Form (CAF) and upload the prescribed photograph, live photograph, signature and supporting documents.
  4. Open the Combined Geo-Scientist Examination 2027 examination-specific module and enter the required examination, centre and fee details.
  5. Pay the applicable examination fee online and submit the application before 22 September 2026 at 6:00 PM.
  6. Save the URN, examination-specific Application Number and submitted application details for future stages.
