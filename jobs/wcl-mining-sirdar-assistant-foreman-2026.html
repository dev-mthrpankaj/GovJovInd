Complete context

About This Recruitment

Western Coalfields Limited (WCL), a Miniratna Public Sector Undertaking under Coal India Limited, has released Employment Notification No. 2026/69 for the recruitment of 444 Statutory Supervisory Staff posts. The recruitment drive aims to fill 220 Mining Sirdar (T&S Grade-C) and 224 Assistant Foreman (Trainee) (Electrical)/Electrical Supervisor (T&S Grade-C) vacancies on a regular basis.

The official notification was released on 16-17 July 2026, and the online application window remained open from 20 July 2026 to 10 August 2026. The WCL Mining Sirdar & Assistant Foreman Admit Card 2026 has been released on 11 October 2026. The Computer Based Test (CBT) will be conducted on 14 October 2026.

The selection process includes a Computer Based Test (CBT) followed by Document Verification and Medical Examination. The CBT will consist of 100 multiple-choice questions carrying 100 marks, to be completed in 90 minutes. There will be no negative marking.

Eligibility for Mining Sirdar requires a valid Mining Sirdar Certificate of Competency issued by DGMS with 3 years of underground mining experience, OR a Diploma in Mining Engineering with a valid Overman Competency Certificate. Assistant Foreman candidates must possess a 3-year Diploma in Electrical Engineering with a valid Electrical Supervisory Certificate for Mines.

Candidates must download their admit card using the direct link provided in the Important Links section and carry a printed copy along with a valid photo ID to the exam centre.
