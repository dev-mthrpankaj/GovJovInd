Complete context

About This Recruitment

RRB Ministerial & Isolated Categories Examination 2026 is a Railway Recruitment Board recruitment drive to fill 312 vacancies across various Ministerial and Isolated Category posts under Centralised Employment Notice (CEN) 08/2025. The official notification was published on 29 December 2025, and the online application window remained open from 30 December 2025 to 29 January 2026.

The Computer Based Test (CBT) was conducted on 14–15 July 2026. The provisional answer key was released on 31 July 2026, and the CBT result was declared on 5 October 2026. The recruitment is underway for posts including Chief Law Assistant, Public Prosecutor, Junior Translator (Hindi), Senior Publicity Inspector, Staff and Welfare Inspector, Lab Assistant Grade III (Chemist & Metallurgist), Scientific Assistant (Training), and Scientific Supervisor (Ergonomics & Training).

The next stage after the CBT result includes post-specific skill/translation/performance tests, document verification, and medical examination, as applicable to the specific post. Candidates shortlisted in the result must check their respective RRB zone's official notice for further details.

Basic eligibility varies by post and includes qualifications such as a Master's degree in Hindi or English, a Bachelor's degree with a Diploma or LLB in Labor Laws, a law degree with 3 years of practice, 10+2 with Science and a Diploma in Laboratory Technology, or a degree/diploma in Public Relations, Advertising, Journalism, or Mass Communication, depending on the specific post.
