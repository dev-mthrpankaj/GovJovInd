Complete context

About This Recruitment

The Bihar Technical Service Commission (BTSC), Patna, has invited online applications for 787 regular posts of Touring Veterinary Officer (Bharamanshil Pashu Chikitsa Padadhikari) and Equivalent under Advertisement No. 27/2026. These posts fall under the Dairy, Fisheries and Animal Resources Department, Government of Bihar.

The application window opened on 24 September 2026 and will close on 23 October 2026. The application fee is Rs.100 for all categories, payable online through debit card, credit card, net banking or UPI.

To be eligible, candidates must possess a Bachelor of Veterinary Science (B.V.Sc.) or Bachelor of Veterinary Science & Animal Husbandry (B.V.Sc. & A.H.) degree from a university recognised by the Veterinary Council of India. Permanent registration with the Bihar Veterinary Council is mandatory. The age limit is 21 years minimum, with upper limits varying by category (37 years for UR Male, 40 years for UR Female / BC / EBC, and 42 years for SC / ST), calculated as on 01 August 2026.

The selection process consists of a Computer Based Test (100 marks, 2 hours, with 0.25 negative marking), weightage for eligible contractual work experience up to 25 marks, and document verification. The CBT carries 75 marks weightage toward the final merit. Selected candidates will receive Pay Level-9 (Rs.53,100 to Rs.1,67,800 per month) plus applicable allowances.

Eligibility Criteria

Age Limit

21 years minimum. Upper age limit: 37 years for UR Male, 40 years for UR Female / BC / EBC (Male & Female), 42 years for SC / ST (Male & Female), as on 01 August 2026.

Age relaxation applicable for SC, ST, OBC, PwBD, Ex-Servicemen, Bihar government servants and other reserved categories as per BTSC rules. PwBD candidates get 10 years relaxation. Bihar government servants with 3+ years continuous service get 5 years relaxation.

Education

Bachelor of Veterinary Science (B.V.Sc.) or Bachelor of Veterinary Science & Animal Husbandry (B.V.Sc. & A.H.) degree from a university recognised by the Veterinary Council of India (VCI).

Permanent registration with the Bihar Veterinary Council is mandatory.

Registration / Other

Indian citizens fulfilling the post-specific eligibility conditions may apply. Bihar domicile required for reservation benefits. Candidates must be permanently registered with the Bihar Veterinary Council.

Refer to official notification for detailed conditions.

How to Apply Online

  1. Visit the official BTSC website at btsc.bihar.gov.in.
  2. Locate Advertisement No. 27/2026 for Touring Veterinary Officer and Equivalent.

  5. Enter your permanent Bihar Veterinary Council registration number.
  6. Upload a recent photograph, signature and required certificates.
  7. Pay the application fee of Rs.100 online via debit card, credit card, net banking or UPI.
