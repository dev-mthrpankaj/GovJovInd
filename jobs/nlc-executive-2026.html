Complete context

About This Recruitment

NLC India Limited has issued a recruitment notification (Advt No. : 10/2026) to fill 143 Graduate Executive posts across various engineering disciplines including Mechanical, Electrical, Civil, and Mining. The recruitment provides opportunities across Thermal Power Stations, Renewable Energy, Mines & Allied Services, Projects, and Planning divisions.

The online application window is currently active from 13 November 2026 and will remain open until 14 December 2026. Interested candidates must ensure they meet the eligibility criteria, which mandates a relevant engineering degree along with a valid GATE 2025 score.

Applicants are advised to thoroughly review the official notification for comprehensive details on the selection procedure, salary structures, category-wise vacancy distribution, and required documents. The application fee is Rs. 854 for General, OBC, and EWS candidates, while SC, ST, and PH candidates must pay Rs. 354.
