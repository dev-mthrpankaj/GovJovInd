Complete context

About This Recruitment

Jamia Millia Islamia (JMI) has released the JMI Teaching Recruitment 2026 notification for 46 vacancies across Professor, Associate Professor, and Assistant Professor posts. The online application window is open from 16 September 2026 to 06 October 2026.

The recruitment covers teaching positions in the Faculty of Engineering & Technology and University Polytechnic. Candidates must possess the relevant postgraduate degree and experience as prescribed post-wise in the official notification. The last date to send the hard copy of the application is 16 October 2026.

Candidates are advised to read the official notification carefully before applying, as eligibility criteria and experience requirements vary by post and department.

What to do at the current stage

How to Apply

  1. Visit the official website of JMI Teaching (https://www.jmi.ac.in/).
  2. Click on the JMI Teaching 2026 Apply Online link.
  3. Register using a valid email ID and mobile number.
  4. Login using registration ID and password.
  5. Fill the application form with personal, educational, and category details.
  6. Upload scanned documents (photo, signature, etc.).
  7. Pay the application fee through online mode.
  8. Submit the form and download/print the confirmation page.
  9. Send the hard copy of the application to reach by 16 October 2026.
