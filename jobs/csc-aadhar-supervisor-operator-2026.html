About This Recruitment

CSC Aadhaar Supervisor/Operator Recruitment 2026 invites applications for Aadhaar Operator and Supervisor roles at Aadhaar Seva Kendra (ASK) locations under CSC e-Governance Services India Ltd.

The available recruitment information shows state and district-wise application windows beginning from 7 August 2026, with the latest listed deadline extending to 3 October 2026. Individual state windows can close earlier.

The listed basic qualification is 10+2 from a recognized board, or Matriculation with a two-year ITI. Candidates should verify the relevant state or district opening before applying.
