Complete context

About This Recruitment

Uttar Pradesh Education Service Selection Commission (UPESSC) is recruiting Assistant Professors for 1936 posts across various subjects in Uttar Pradesh. The official notification (Advt No. 04/2026) was published in September 2026, and the online application window remained open from 08 September 2026 to 07 October 2026.

The written examination is scheduled for 19-20 November 2026. The exam city intimation and admit card have been released on 08 November 2026. Candidates must carry the printed admit card, a valid photo ID, and other required documents to the exam centre.

Eligibility requires a Post Graduation degree in the relevant subject with minimum 55% marks, along with NET/SET/SLET or Ph.D. The age limit is 62 years as on 01 July 2026, with relaxation for reserved categories as per rules.

The application fee is Rs.2000 for General/OBC/EWS, Rs.1500 for SC/ST, and Rs.1000 for PH candidates. The selection process includes a written exam, interview, and document verification.
