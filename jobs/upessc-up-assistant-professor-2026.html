Complete context

About This Recruitment

Uttar Pradesh Education Service Selection Commission (UPESSC) released UPESSC Assistant Professor Recruitment 2026 under Advt No. 04/2026 to fill 1,936 Assistant Professor vacancies across Uttar Pradesh. The total distribution comprises 1,564 posts for Male candidates (General: 636, OBC: 421, EWS: 138, SC: 347, ST: 22) and 372 posts for Female candidates (General: 160, OBC: 95, EWS: 23, SC: 92, ST: 2).

The online application process ran from 08 September 2026 to 07 October 2026, with fee payment closing on 07 October 2026 and form corrections closing on 11 October 2026. UPESSC released the written exam admit card on 15 November 2026 and conducted the written examination on 19-20 November 2026.

On 23 November 2026, Uttar Pradesh Education Service Selection Commission (UPESSC) released the provisional Answer Key and official notice on its portal. Candidates who appeared in the 19-20 November 2026 written examination can log in using the official link to download the answer key and check the notice instructions.

Eligibility requires a Post Graduation degree in the relevant subject with at least 55% marks along with NET, SET, SLET, or Ph.D. qualification, and a maximum age limit of 62 years as on 01 July 2026. Selected Assistant Professors are placed in Pay Level-10 (Rs.57,700 to Rs.1,82,400 per month) following the written examination, interview, document verification, and final merit list.
