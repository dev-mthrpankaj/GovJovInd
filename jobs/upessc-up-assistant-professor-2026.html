Complete context

About This Recruitment

The Uttar Pradesh Education Service Selection Commission (UPESSC) is conducting the UP Assistant Professor Examination 2026 to fill 1936 Assistant Professor vacancies across various subjects. The official notification (Advt. No. 04/2026) was released in September 2026, and the online application window was open from 08 September to 07 October 2026.

The selection process for this prestigious teaching role consists of a Written Examination, followed by a Personal Interview (Viva-Voce) and Document Verification. UPESSC released the exam city intimation details on 08 November 2026.

The UPESSC Assistant Professor Admit Card 2026 has been officially released on 15 November 2026. Candidates appearing for the test can log in using their enrollment number, registration number, or date of birth to download their hall tickets. The written examination is scheduled to be held on 19 and 20 November 2026.

Eligible candidates must hold a Master’s Degree in the relevant subject with at least 55% marks, along with a qualifying NET/SET/SLET or a PhD. The maximum age limit is 62 years, with relaxation applicable as per Uttar Pradesh state government rules.

What to do at the current stage

How to Download the Admit Card

  1. Visit the official UPESSC recruitment portal linked below.
  2. Enter your Registration Number, Enrollment Number, or Date of Birth.
  3. Click on submit to view your admit card.
  4. Verify your exam date (19-20 Nov), reporting time, and allotted center.
  5. Download and print the admit card in colour to carry to the examination hall.

Original Application Process

Preserved for recruitment history and reference after the application stage closes.

  1. Visit the official UPESSC recruitment portal apply.upessc.org.
  2. Read the Advt. No. 04/2026 notification carefully.
  3. Register on the portal with valid contact details.
  4. Fill the online application form with personal and educational details.
  5. Upload scanned photograph, signature, and required certificates.
  6. Pay the application fee as per your category.
  7. Submit the final form and take a printout for future reference.
