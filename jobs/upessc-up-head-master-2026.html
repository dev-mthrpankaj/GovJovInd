Complete context

About This Recruitment

Uttar Pradesh Education Service Selection Commission (UPESSC) has released the official notification for UP Head Master Recruitment 2026. This recruitment drive aims to fill 1172 Head Master posts in government schools across Uttar Pradesh. The posts are divided into Head Master (Balak) with 1037 vacancies and Head Master (Balika) with 135 vacancies.

The online application process commenced on 23 October 2026 and will remain open until 22 November 2026. Candidates must complete their fee payment by 22 November 2026, and the correction window will be available until 26 November 2026. The written examination is scheduled for 07 January 2027.

To be eligible, candidates must possess a Post Graduation degree with at least 50% marks and a B.Ed. or BPEd from a recognised university. Additionally, candidates need either four years of teaching experience as a Lecturer or Assistant Teacher in higher classes (Class 9 to 12) or four years of experience as a Principal of a Junior High School. The age limit is 30 to 62 years as on 01 July 2026, with relaxation for reserved categories as per rules.

This is a permanent recruitment lifecycle page. All future updates including admit card, answer key, result, document verification, and final merit list will be published on this same URL. The page will not change its address as the recruitment progresses.

Eligibility Criteria

Age Limit

30 to 62 years as on 01 July 2026.

Age relaxation applicable for SC, ST, OBC, PwBD, Ex-Servicemen and other reserved categories as per UPESSC rules.

Education

Post Graduation with at least 50% marks and B.Ed. or BPEd from a recognised university. Additionally, at least 4 years of teaching experience as a Lecturer or Assistant Teacher in higher classes (Class 9 to 12) of an institution recognised by Central/State Government OR at least 4 years of experience as Principal of a Junior High School, certified by a competent authority.

Both the academic qualification and the required teaching experience are mandatory for eligibility.

Nationality / Other

Indian citizens fulfilling the post-specific eligibility conditions may apply. Refer to the official notification for complete details.

How to Apply Online

  1. Visit the official UPESSC application portal at apply.upessc.org.

  5. Fill the application form with accurate personal, educational, and category details.
  6. Upload scanned documents including photograph, signature, and required certificates.
  7. Pay the application fee of Rs.1500 through online mode.
