Complete context

About This Recruitment

Uttar Pradesh Education Service Selection Commission (UPESSC) released Advt No. 09/2026 for UPESSC UP Head Master Recruitment 2026 to fill 1172 Head Master posts across Uttar Pradesh. The total vacancies comprise 1037 posts for Head Master (Balak) and 135 posts for Head Master (Balika) under Pay Scale Level-10 (Rs.56,100 to Rs.1,77,500).

The online application and fee payment window remained open from 23 October 2026 to 22 November 2026. Currently, the application correction window is active from 23 November 2026 to 26 November 2026, allowing registered applicants to review and edit permitted application details.

The UPESSC UP Head Master written examination is scheduled for 07 January 2027. Admit card release and result declaration dates will be announced by UPESSC as per the official schedule.

Eligibility requires a Post Graduation degree with 50% marks, B.Ed. or BPEd from a recognised university, and at least 4 years of teaching experience as a Lecturer or Assistant Teacher in Classes 9 to 12 or 4 years as Principal of a Junior High School. The age limit is 30 to 62 years as on 01 July 2026.

What to do at the current stage

How to Edit UPESSC UP Head Master Form 2026

  1. Open the official UPESSC candidate login portal at apply.upessc.org/login from Important Links.
  2. Sign in using your registered credentials (Registration ID and Password).
  3. Select the UPESSC UP Head Master Recruitment 2026 (Advt No. 09/2026) application.
  4. Review personal, academic, experience and category entries carefully and modify permitted fields.
  5. Submit the updated application form before 26 November 2026.
  6. Download and print the revised confirmation page for future reference.

Original Application Process

Preserved for recruitment history and reference after the application stage closes.

  1. Visit the official UPESSC website at upessc.up.gov.in or apply.upessc.org.
  2. Complete One Time Registration (OTR) using a valid email ID and mobile number.
  3. Log in with the generated registration ID and password.
  4. Fill in personal, educational, teaching experience and category details.
  5. Upload scanned photograph, signature and required supporting documents.
  6. Pay the Rs.1500 application fee through online mode.
  7. Submit the form and download the final confirmation page for reference.
