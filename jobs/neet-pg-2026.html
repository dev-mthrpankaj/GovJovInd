About This Recruitment

NEET PG 2026 is the National Eligibility cum Entrance Test for admission to MD, MS and PG Diploma courses in medical colleges across India. The examination is conducted by the National Board of Examinations in Medical Sciences (NBE).

The NEET PG 2026 examination was held on 30 August 2026. The admit card was released on 25 August 2026, and the exam city details were made available on 11 August 2026. The result has now been declared on 24 September 2026.

Candidates who appeared for the examination can check their result, scorecard and cut off details on the official NBE website. The counselling process will be conducted by the Medical Counselling Committee (MCC) for All India Quota seats and by respective state authorities for state quota seats.
