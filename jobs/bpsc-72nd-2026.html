Complete context

About This Recruitment

Bihar BPSC 72nd Combined Competitive Examination 2026 is conducted by the Bihar Public Service Commission (BPSC) to fill 1,230 posts in Bihar state services. The vacancies include 1,077 posts under Bihar BPSC Various Posts and 153 posts for Financial Administrative Officer and Other Posts. The official notification was released on 05 May 2026, and online applications were accepted from 07 May 2026 to 31 May 2026.

The Preliminary Examination was originally scheduled for 26 July 2026, but was postponed by the commission. On 20 November 2026, the revised schedule confirmed that the BPSC 72nd Prelims Examination will be conducted on 17 January 2027. The Prelims Admit Card will be published online on the official BPSC website prior to the examination.

Candidates who hold a Bachelor Degree from a recognised university in India were eligible to apply, with subject-specific graduation requirements for the Financial Administrative Officer and Other Posts. The age limit is 21 or 22 years minimum (post-wise) up to 37 years for male candidates and 40 years for female candidates as on 01 August 2026.

Selection for BPSC 72nd CCE 2026 is carried out in three stages: Preliminary Examination, Mains Examination, and Interview. Selected candidates receive pay in Level 6 to Level 9 as per post-wise norms.

What to do at the current stage

How to Prepare & Download Admit Card Once Released

  1. Note the revised BPSC 72nd Preliminary Examination date: 17 January 2027.
  2. Visit the official Bihar Public Service Commission website at bpsc.bihar.gov.in for the admit card release notice.
  3. Once active, log in at bpsconline.bihar.gov.in using your Registration Number and Password or Date of Birth.
  4. Verify your name, roll number, exam date, reporting time, and exam centre on the Prelims Admit Card.
  5. Download the admit card PDF and take a clear A4 printout for the examination day.
  6. Carry the printed admit card along with a valid original photo ID proof to the exam centre.

Original Application Process

Preserved for recruitment history and reference after the application stage closes.

  1. Visit the official BPSC online application portal at bpsconline.bihar.gov.in.
  2. Read the Integrated 72nd CCE 2026 notification carefully before registration.
  3. Complete One Time Registration (OTR) and log in to the candidate dashboard.
  4. Fill in personal, academic, and category details accurately in the application form.
  5. Upload the required scanned documents, photograph, and signature.
  6. Pay the Rs.100 application fee through online payment mode.
  7. Submit the application form and save the confirmation printout for future stages.

Frequently Asked Questions

The BPSC 72nd Preliminary Examination is now scheduled for 17 January 2027. The exam was earlier planned for 26 July 2026 before being postponed by the Bihar Public Service Commission.

The BPSC 72nd Prelims Admit Card will be released as per the official schedule before the 17 January 2027 exam. Candidates will be able to download it from bpsc.bihar.gov.in using their login credentials.

BPSC 72nd CCE 2026 is a state-level government recruitment conducted by the Bihar Public Service Commission for 1,230 posts, open to candidates holding a Bachelor Degree from a recognised university.

A total of 1,230 vacancies are advertised in BPSC 72nd CCE 2026. This includes 1,077 posts under Bihar BPSC Various Posts and 153 posts for Financial Administrative Officer and Other Posts.

Candidates must hold a Bachelor Degree in any stream from a recognised university for Various Posts. For Financial Administrative Officer and Other Posts, a degree in specified subjects like Commerce, Economics, Maths, Statistics, Sociology, Psychology, or Home Science is required.

The minimum age is 21 or 22 years (post-wise), and the maximum age limit is 37 years for Male candidates and 40 years for Female candidates as on 01 August 2026, with relaxation for reserved categories.

Candidates selected through the BPSC 72nd Combined Competitive Examination 2026 receive a salary in Pay Level 6 to Pay Level 9, depending on the specific post allotted.

The BPSC 72nd selection process consists of three stages: Preliminary Examination, Mains Examination, and Interview. Final selection is based on merit across the Mains and Interview stages.

The online application window for BPSC 72nd CCE 2026 ran from 07 May 2026 to 31 May 2026. The application and fee payment window is now closed.

The application fee for BPSC 72nd CCE 2026 was Rs.100 for all categories, including General, BC, Other State, SC, ST, PH, and all Female candidates, payable via online mode.

The official website of the Bihar Public Service Commission is https://bpsc.bihar.gov.in/, and the candidate login portal is https://bpsconline.bihar.gov.in/candidate/login.
