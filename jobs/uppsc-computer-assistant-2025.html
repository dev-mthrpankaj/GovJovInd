About This Recruitment

Uttar Pradesh Public Service Commission (UPPSC) Computer Assistant Recruitment 2025, Advt. No. A-4/E-1/2025, covers 13 Computer Assistant posts.

Applications were accepted from 01 July to 01 August 2025, with the correction / edit form deadline on 08 August 2025. The supplied eligibility is 10+2 with O Level passed or 10+2 with Diploma in Computer Science.

The recruitment has progressed through the written examination and computer typing test. The latest supplied update is the Typing Test Result declared on 01 September 2026; earlier result, admit-card and exam-notice history remains preserved on this permanent lifecycle page.
