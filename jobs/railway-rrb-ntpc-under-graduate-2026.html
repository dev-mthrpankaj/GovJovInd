Complete context

About This Recruitment

RRB NTPC Under Graduate 2026 is a Railway Recruitment Board recruitment drive under Centralised Employment Notice (CEN) No. 07/2026 to fill 1,688 tentative vacancies for Non-Technical Popular Categories (Undergraduate) posts. The posts included are Commercial Cum Ticket Clerk, Accounts Clerk Cum Typist, Junior Clerk Cum Typist and Trains Clerk.

The online application window opened on 15 October 2026 and will close on 13 November 2026 at 11:59 PM. Candidates must complete Aadhaar authentication during registration, and the details must match their Class 10 certificate. The age limit is 18 to 30 years as on 01 January 2027, with relaxation for reserved categories as per government rules.

The selection process consists of CBT Stage 1, CBT Stage 2, a Computer Based Typing Skill Test (for Accounts Clerk Cum Typist and Junior Clerk Cum Typist posts), Document Verification and Medical Examination. The detailed CEN will contain the final eligibility conditions, exam pattern and medical standards.

Basic eligibility is 12th (10+2) pass from a recognised board. For General category candidates, a minimum of 50% marks in 12th is required, though this condition is relaxed for SC/ST/PwBD/Ex-Servicemen and candidates with higher qualifications.

Eligibility Criteria

Age Limit

18 to 30 years as on 01 January 2027.

Age relaxation applicable for SC, ST, OBC-NCL, PwBD, Ex-Servicemen and other reserved categories as per government rules.

Education

12th (10+2 stage) pass or equivalent from a recognised board. For General category, minimum 50% marks required; relaxed for SC/ST/PwBD/Ex-Servicemen and candidates with higher qualifications.

Candidates waiting for Class 12 results are not eligible. Qualification must be completed on or before the last date of application.

Nationality / Other

Indian citizens fulfilling the post-specific medical standards and other conditions may apply. Aadhaar authentication is mandatory and details must match the Class 10 certificate.

Refer to official notification for detailed conditions.

What to do at the current stage

How to Apply Online

  1. Visit the official RRB application portal at rrbapply.gov.in.
  2. Complete Aadhaar authentication and create your account with a valid mobile number and email ID.
  3. Fill Part-1 of the application form with educational qualifications, category and post preferences.
  4. Upload scanned photograph, signature and required documents as per specifications.
  5. Pay the application fee through net banking, debit/credit card or UPI.
  6. Review all details carefully and submit the final application before 13 November 2026.
  7. Save and print the confirmation page for future reference.
