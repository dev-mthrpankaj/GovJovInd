Quick answers

RRB NTPC Under Graduate 2026 in Brief

RRB NTPC Under Graduate Recruitment 2026 is a Railway Recruitment Board recruitment under CEN 07/2026 for 1,688 Non-Technical Popular Categories posts open to 10+2 (Class 12) pass candidates: Commercial cum Ticket Clerk (700), Junior Clerk cum Typist (600), Accounts Clerk cum Typist (200) and Train Clerk (188).

Online applications are open from 15 October 2026 to 13 November 2026 on the official portal rrbapply.gov.in. The last date for fee payment is 15 November 2026 and the application correction window is 16 to 25 November 2026.

Candidates must be 18 to 30 years old on 01 January 2027, with relaxation for reserved categories. The fee is Rs.500 for General, OBC and EWS and Rs.250 for SC, ST and PwBD, with a partial or full refund after appearing in CBT Stage-I. Posts carry Pay Level 2 and Level 3 of the 7th CPC, and selection is through CBT Stage-I, CBT Stage-II, a Typing Skill Test for typist posts, Document Verification and Medical Examination. The exam date has not been announced yet.

Complete context

About This Recruitment

The Railway Recruitment Boards (RRBs) of the Ministry of Railways have issued Centralised Employment Notification (CEN) 07/2026 for Non-Technical Popular Categories (NTPC) posts at the Under Graduate level. The short notice was released on 21 September 2026 and the detailed notification carries the post-wise, category-wise and RRB-wise vacancy breakup. A separate recruitment, CEN 06/2026, covers the Graduate-level NTPC posts.

A total of 1,688 vacancies are distributed across 21 RRBs. RRB Kolkata has the highest number of posts (329), followed by RRB Chennai (168) and RRB Prayagraj (149). By category, 686 posts are for General (UR), 414 for OBC, 163 for EWS, 258 for SC and 167 for ST candidates.

Basic eligibility is 10+2 (Class 12) or equivalent from a recognised board and an age of 18 to 30 years as on 01 January 2027. Selection moves through two Computer Based Test stages, a Typing Skill Test for the typist posts, Document Verification and a Medical Examination. The posts are in Pay Level 2 and Level 3 of the 7th Pay Commission with a posting anywhere in India.

Online applications for RRB NTPC Under Graduate 2026 are open from 15 October 2026 to 13 November 2026. Track other openings on Latest Jobs, and check Admit Card and Results pages for later updates.

What to do at the current stage

How to Apply for RRB NTPC Under Graduate 2026

  1. Open the official RRB application portal (rrbapply.gov.in) from Important Links and select CEN 07/2026 - NTPC Under Graduate.
  2. Read the CEN 07/2026 notification on the official RRB website to confirm post-wise eligibility, age limit and medical standards before applying.
  3. Register with a valid mobile number and email ID, then log in with your registration details.
  4. Fill in personal, educational, category and post preference details carefully. Apply to only one RRB, as per the notification.
  5. Upload your photograph, signature and other required documents in the prescribed format.
  6. Pay the application fee online (Rs.500 for General / OBC / EWS, Rs.250 for SC / ST / PwBD) on or before 15 November 2026.
  7. Submit the form, download the confirmation page and keep a printout. Use the correction window of 16 to 25 November 2026 only if the notification allows changes.
