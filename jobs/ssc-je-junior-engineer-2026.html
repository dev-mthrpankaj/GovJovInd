Complete context

About This Recruitment

SSC Junior Engineer (JE) Examination 2026 is a Staff Selection Commission recruitment drive for Junior Engineer and Scientific Assistant posts in various central government departments and organisations, including CPWD, MES, CWC, BRO, NTRO, India Meteorological Department and others. The official notification was published in September 2026, and the online application window remained open from 02 September 2026 to 22 September 2026.

The Computer Based Test (Paper 1) will be conducted from 02 to 06 November 2026 for Civil, Electrical, Mechanical and IMD Scientific Assistant posts. The examination for Junior Engineer (Telecommunication) is scheduled separately from 30 November to 01 December 2026. The Paper 1 admit card was released on 30 October 2026.

The initial notification announced 1,748 tentative vacancies. A revised tentative vacancy notice was later issued, reducing the total to 1,699 posts. Candidates should refer to the latest official vacancy notice for the updated department-wise and category-wise breakup.

Basic eligibility requires a Degree or Diploma in the relevant engineering trade (Civil, Electrical, Mechanical, etc.) from a recognised university or institute, depending on the department. The age limit is 18 to 30 years (32 years for certain posts) as on 01 August 2026, with relaxation for reserved categories as per SSC rules.

Degree or Diploma in Civil / Electrical / Mechanical / Telecommunication Engineering or B.Sc. with relevant subjects, depending on the post and department.

Candidates must possess the required engineering qualification from a recognised university or institute. Refer to the official notification for post-wise qualification.

What to do at the current stage

How to Download SSC JE Paper 1 Admit Card

  1. Open the official SSC admit card portal at ssc.gov.in/login.
  2. Log in using your Registration Number and Date of Birth (or password).
  3. Select the SSC JE 2026 Paper 1 examination from the dropdown.
  4. Download and check the admit card PDF carefully.
  5. Verify your name, roll number, exam date, shift timing and centre address.
  6. Take a clear colour printout and carry it to the exam centre along with a valid photo ID.

Original Application Process

Preserved for recruitment history and reference after the application stage closes.

  1. Visit the official SSC recruitment website at ssc.gov.in.
  2. Read the official SSC JE 2026 notification carefully.
  3. Register on the SSC portal with valid email ID and mobile number.
  4. Fill the online application form with accurate personal and educational details.
  5. Upload photograph, signature and required documents.
  6. Pay the applicable examination fee online.
  7. Submit the form and save the confirmation page for future reference.
