Complete context

About This Recruitment

Staff Selection Commission (SSC) released the official notification for the SSC Junior Engineer (JE) Recruitment Examination 2026 on 02 September 2026 to fill 1,699 Group B (Non-Gazetted) vacancies across 12 central government departments, including CPWD, MES, BRO, CWC, DoT, and IMD. Online applications remained open from 02 September 2026 to 22 September 2026, followed by the correction window from 28 September 2026 to 30 September 2026.

Following the revised vacancy notice issued on 08 October 2026, exam city details were published on 23 October 2026 and Paper-I admit cards were released on 30 October 2026. The Paper-I Computer Based Test (CBT) was conducted from 02 November 2026 to 06 November 2026 across examination centres in India.

On 19 November 2026, Staff Selection Commission (SSC) released the Paper-I Tentative Answer Key along with individual candidate response sheets at ssc.gov.in. Candidates can log in using their Registration Number and Password to review marked responses and raise online objections until 22 November 2026 (6:00 PM) by paying Rs.50 per challenged question.

Eligibility requires a Degree or three-year Diploma in the relevant engineering discipline (Civil, Electrical, Mechanical, or Telecom), or a Bachelor's Degree in Science with Physics for Scientific Assistant posts in IMD. The upper age limit is 30 to 32 years depending on the department as on 01 August 2026, and selected candidates receive Pay Level 6 salary of Rs.35,400 to Rs.1,12,400 per month. The Paper-II examination is scheduled for December 2026.

Eligibility Criteria

Age Limit

Maximum age limit is 30 to 32 years (post-wise and department-wise) as on 01 August 2026. No minimum age limit is specified.

Upper age relaxation is applicable for SC, ST, OBC, PwBD, and Ex-Servicemen candidates as per Staff Selection Commission (SSC) rules.

Education

Degree or Three-Year Diploma in Civil, Electrical, Mechanical, or Telecom Engineering (post-wise), or Bachelor's Degree in Science with Physics for IMD Scientific Assistant.

Candidates applying for BRO and MES with a Diploma require 2 years of relevant work experience. Essential qualification must be completed by 22 September 2026.

Nationality / Other

Candidates must be citizens of India or meet the nationality criteria specified in the official notification. Border Roads Organization (BRO) vacancies are open for male candidates only.

Refer to official notification for detailed conditions.

What to do at the current stage

How to Download Paper-I Answer Key & Raise Objections

  1. Open the official Staff Selection Commission (SSC) login portal at ssc.gov.in from Important Links.
  2. Sign in using your Registration Number and Password.
  3. Open the SSC Junior Engineer Examination 2026 Paper-I Tentative Answer Key and Response Sheet link.
  4. Verify your recorded responses against the tentative answers and save a PDF copy for personal reference.
  5. To challenge any question or answer, open the online objection module before 22 November 2026 at 6:00 PM.
  6. Select the question ID, enter valid supporting details, pay Rs.50 per challenged question online, and save the payment receipt.

Original Application Process

Preserved for recruitment history and reference after the application stage closes.

  1. Visit the official Staff Selection Commission (SSC) portal at ssc.gov.in.
  2. Complete One-Time Registration (OTR) using a valid email ID and mobile number.
  3. Log in to the candidate dashboard and open the SSC Junior Engineer (JE) Examination 2026 application form.
  4. Enter personal, category, educational qualification, and department preference details accurately.
  5. Upload the required live photograph and scanned signature as per SSC specifications.
  6. Pay the application fee of Rs.100 online (if applicable) before 23 September 2026.
  7. Submit the application form before 22 September 2026 and save the confirmation PDF.

Quick answers

Frequently Asked Questions

The Staff Selection Commission (SSC) released the SSC JE Paper-I Tentative Answer Key 2026 on 19 November 2026. Candidates can download their response sheets and submit online objections until 22 November 2026 at 6:00 PM.

Candidates can check and download the SSC JE Paper-I Answer Key 2026 and response sheet on the official Staff Selection Commission portal at ssc.gov.in. Direct official login and notice links are also listed in Important Links.

Visit ssc.gov.in, log in with your Registration Number and Password, and open the SSC Junior Engineer Examination 2026 Paper-I answer key tab. Download the tentative answer key and candidate response sheet PDF for self-evaluation.

Candidates can raise online objections against the SSC JE Paper-I Tentative Answer Key 2026 from 19 November 2026 (6:00 PM) to 22 November 2026 (6:00 PM). The objection fee is Rs.50 per question or answer challenged.

A total of 1,699 tentative vacancies are announced under SSC Junior Engineer (JE) Recruitment 2026 across 12 central government departments. These include Junior Engineer posts in Civil, Electrical, Mechanical, Telecom, and Scientific Assistant in IMD.

Candidates require a Degree or three-year Diploma in Civil, Electrical, Mechanical, or relevant engineering disciplines depending on the department. For IMD Scientific Assistant, a Bachelor's Degree in Science with Physics or an eligible engineering degree is required.

The upper age limit for SSC Junior Engineer (JE) Recruitment 2026 is 30 to 32 years depending on the post and department, calculated as on 01 August 2026. Upper age relaxation applies for SC, ST, OBC, PwBD, and Ex-Servicemen candidates as per SSC rules.

General, OBC, and EWS candidates paid an application fee of Rs.100, while SC, ST, and Female candidates were exempted with Rs.0 fee. Online application correction charges were Rs.200 for the first correction and Rs.500 for the second correction.

The SSC JE 2026 selection process consists of Paper-I Computer Based Test (CBT), Paper-II Computer Based Test (CBT), Document Verification, and Final Merit List. Paper-I was conducted from 02 to 06 November 2026, and Paper-II is scheduled for December 2026.

Selected candidates for SSC Junior Engineer (JE) posts are placed in Pay Level 6 of the 7th Central Pay Commission with a pay scale of Rs.35,400 to Rs.1,12,400 per month. Allowances such as DA, HRA, and TA are provided as per central government norms.

Candidates should check the official Staff Selection Commission website at ssc.gov.in for SSC JE Paper-I Result, Paper-II Exam Date, and Admit Card notices. This permanent recruitment page also tracks every verified stage from notification to final result.

The official website of the Staff Selection Commission (SSC) is https://ssc.gov.in/. Candidates must verify all notices, tentative and final answer keys, results, cut-off marks, and admit cards directly on this official portal.
