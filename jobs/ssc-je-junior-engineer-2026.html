Complete context

About This Recruitment

The Staff Selection Commission (SSC) conducts the Junior Engineer (JE) Examination for recruitment to Junior Engineer (Civil, Mechanical, Electrical) and Scientific Assistant posts in various central government departments and organisations including Border Roads Organisation (BRO), Central Public Works Department (CPWD), Military Engineer Services (MES), Central Water Commission (CWC), India Meteorological Department (IMD), Department of Telecommunications (DoT), and others.

The SSC JE 2026 notification was released with an initial tentative vacancy of 1748 posts. The application window remained open from 02 September 2026 to 22 September 2026, with the fee payment last date on 23 September 2026 and a correction window from 28 to 30 September 2026. A revised tentative vacancy notice dated 08 October 2026 has reduced the total to 1699 posts across all departments and categories.

The Paper-I Computer Based Examination (CBE) is scheduled to be conducted from 02 to 06 November 2026, as confirmed by the official SSC examination schedule notice dated 01 October 2026. The Exam City Details (city intimation) were released on 23 October 2026 through the SSC login portal. The Paper-I admit card will follow separately before the exam.

The selection process consists of Paper-I (CBE), Paper-II (CBE), and Document Verification. Paper-I carries 200 marks across General Awareness, General Intelligence & Reasoning, and General Engineering sections. Paper-II is a technical paper carrying 300 marks based on the candidate's chosen engineering discipline. Candidates must qualify each stage to progress to the next.
