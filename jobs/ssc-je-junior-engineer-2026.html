Complete context

About This Recruitment

SSC JE Junior Engineer Examination 2026 is a Staff Selection Commission recruitment drive for Junior Engineer (Civil, Electrical, Mechanical, Telecommunication) and Scientific Assistant posts across various central government departments including CPWD, MES, BRO, CWC, IMD, DoT and others. The official notification was released on 02 September 2026 with 1,748 tentative vacancies, which was later revised to 1,699 posts in October 2026.

The online application window remained open from 02 September 2026 to 22 September 2026. The fee payment deadline was 23 September 2026, and the application correction window was open from 28 to 30 September 2026. The Computer Based Test (Paper 1) is scheduled from 02 to 06 November 2026, while Paper 2 will be held in December 2026.

The exam city intimation slip for Paper 1 was released on 23 October 2026. This slip only indicates the allotted exam city and is NOT a substitute for the admit card. The actual admit card (admission certificate) will be released separately, approximately 2-3 days before each candidate's exam date. Candidates must carry the printed admit card along with a valid photo ID to the exam centre.

Basic eligibility requires a Diploma or Degree in the relevant engineering discipline (Civil, Electrical, Mechanical, E&M, or Telecommunication) depending on the post. For Scientific Assistant (IMD), candidates with a Bachelor's degree in Science (Physics) or specified computer/electronics subjects are eligible. The age limit is 18 to 30/32 years depending on the post, calculated as on 01 August 2026.
