About This Recruitment

SSC Junior Engineer Examination 2026 is being conducted by the Staff Selection Commission for technical posts across multiple Central Government departments and organisations. The 2026 notice reports 1,748 tentative vacancies covering Junior Engineer posts in Civil, Electrical, Mechanical, Electrical & Mechanical and Telecom streams, along with Scientific Assistant posts in the India Meteorological Department.

The online application window is open from 2 September 2026 to 22 September 2026. Fee payment is allowed up to 23 September, followed by the official application correction window from 28 to 30 September 2026.

Paper-I is scheduled tentatively for October/November 2026 and Paper-II for December 2026. Eligibility varies by department, so candidates should match their engineering degree/diploma, experience and age limit with the exact post before submitting preferences.
