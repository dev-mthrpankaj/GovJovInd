About This Recruitment

SSC Selection Post Phase XIV Recruitment 2026 is a Staff Selection Commission drive for 3003 posts across various regions. The application window was open from 13 April 2026 to 4 May 2026, with a correction window from 11 to 13 May 2026.

The admit card for the written examination has been released on the official SSC portal. The exam is scheduled to be held from 16 to 26 September 2026. Candidates can download their admit card by logging into the SSC website with their registration number and date of birth.

What to do at the current stage

How to Download Admit Card

  1. Visit the official SSC website (ssc.gov.in) to download the admit card.
  2. Click on the 'Admit Card' or 'Download Admit Card' link available on the homepage.
  3. Enter your Registration Number, Date of Birth/Password, or other required login credentials.
  4. Complete the CAPTCHA code or security verification, if prompted.
  5. Click on the Submit/Login button to access your SSC Selection Post 14th Admit Card 2026.
  6. Verify all details printed on the admit card, including your Name, Roll Number, Exam Date, Exam Time, and Exam Centre.
  7. Download the SSC Selection Post 14th Admit Card 2026 PDF and save it to your device.
  8. Take a clear printout of the admit card on A4-size paper for the examination.
  9. Carry the printed Admit Card along with a valid Photo ID Proof (Aadhaar Card, PAN Card, Voter ID, Passport, or Driving License) to the examination centre.
  10. Keep your Admit Card safe until the recruitment process, document verification, and final selection are completed.
