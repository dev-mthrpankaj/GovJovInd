Complete context

About This Recruitment

SSC Selection Post Phase 14 Examination 2026 is a Staff Selection Commission recruitment drive to fill 3003 posts (later revised) across various ministries, departments and organisations in India. The official notification was released on 13 April 2026, and the online application window remained open from 13 April 2026 to 04 May 2026.

The Computer Based Examination (CBE) was conducted from 16 September 2026 to 26 September 2026. The provisional answer key along with candidate response sheets was released on 09 October 2026. Candidates can submit objections against the tentative answer key from 09 October 2026 (4:00 PM) to 12 October 2026 (4:00 PM) by paying Rs.50 per question challenged.

The recruitment covers posts at Matriculation (10th), Higher Secondary (10+2) and Graduation & Above levels. The selection process includes a Computer Based Examination, Skill Test (if applicable) and Document Verification. Basic eligibility varies by post, with age limits generally between 18 and 30 years as on the prescribed date.

Candidates who appeared for the CBE can now check their recorded responses, compare them with the tentative answer key, and raise objections if they find any discrepancy. The response sheet download facility will not be available after the objection window closes.
