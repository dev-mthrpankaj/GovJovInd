About This Recruitment

SSC Selection Post Phase XIV 2026 is a Staff Selection Commission recruitment examination for 3003 vacancies across Matriculation, Higher Secondary and Graduation-level posts advertised under Phase-XIV/2026/Selection Posts.

Online applications were accepted from 13 April to 4 May 2026, followed by the correction window from 11 to 13 May 2026. The examination is scheduled from 16 to 26 September 2026.

The latest official stage is exam-city information for candidates. Admission Certificate updates and all later stages such as examination, answer key and result will continue on this same permanent GovJobUpdates URL.
