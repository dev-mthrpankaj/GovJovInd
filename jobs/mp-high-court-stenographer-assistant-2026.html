Complete context

About This Recruitment

High Court of Madhya Pradesh (MPHC) is conducting the MP High Court Stenographer & Assistant Recruitment 2026 under Advertisement No. 666/Pariksha/2026 to fill 418 regular vacancies in District and Sessions Courts across Madhya Pradesh. The vacancy distribution includes 69 Stenographer Grade II posts, 299 Stenographer Grade III posts, 20 Stenographer Grade III (Court Manager Staff) posts, and 30 Assistant Grade III (English Knowing) posts.

The official recruitment notification was published on 06 October 2026. Online applications remained open from 06 October 2026 (12:00 PM) to 27 October 2026 (05:00 PM), followed by an online application correction window from 30 October 2026 to 01 November 2026.

On 20 November 2026, High Court of Madhya Pradesh (MPHC) issued Notification No. 738/Exam/26 confirming that the Online Preliminary Examination will be held on 15 January 2027 across 7 cities: Jabalpur, Bhopal, Indore, Gwalior, Sagar, Satna, and Ujjain. Admit cards will be released on mphc.gov.in about 7 days before the exam date.

Eligible candidates hold a Bachelor Degree in any stream, a valid MP CPCT Scorecard, a 1-Year Computer Application Diploma, and the post-specific shorthand qualification (Hindi 100 WPM for Grade II, Hindi 80 WPM for Grade III, or English 80 WPM for Assistant Grade III). Selection consists of the Online Preliminary Examination (100 marks), Main Exam / Skill Test (100 marks), Document Verification, and Medical Examination.

Eligibility Criteria

Age Limit

18 to 40 years for Male and 18 to 45 years for Female candidates as on 01 January 2026.

Upper age relaxation up to 45 years applies to SC, ST, OBC, Female, PH, and MP Government employee candidates who are Madhya Pradesh domiciles as per MPHC rules.

Education

Bachelor Degree in any stream from a recognised university, valid MP CPCT Scorecard, post-wise Hindi (100/80 WPM) or English (80 WPM) Shorthand certificate, and a 1-Year Diploma in Computer Application.

All required qualifications and certificates must have been passed on or before the closing date of 27 October 2026.

Nationality / Other

Indian citizens may apply. Reservation and age relaxation benefits apply only to Madhya Pradesh domiciles; candidates from other states are considered under the Unreserved (UR) category.

Refer to official notification for detailed conditions.

What to do at the current stage

How to Check Exam Date Notice & Prepare

  1. Open the official Exam Date Notice PDF (Notification No. 738/Exam/26) from the Important Links section.
  2. Verify the Online Preliminary Examination date of 15 January 2027 and note the 7 exam cities: Jabalpur, Bhopal, Indore, Gwalior, Sagar, Satna, and Ujjain.
  3. Keep your Application Number and Password ready to download the admit card about 7 days before the exam.
  4. Revise all 5 preliminary exam sections: General Knowledge (including MP GK), Maths and Reasoning, General Hindi, English Knowledge, and Computer Knowledge.
  5. Check the Candidate Login portal regularly for admit card release and shift timing instructions.

Original Application Process

Preserved for recruitment history and reference after the application stage closes.

  1. Visit the official website of the High Court of Madhya Pradesh at mphc.gov.in.
  2. Open the Recruitment / Result section and select Advt. No. 666/Pariksha/2026.
  3. Complete new registration using a valid email ID and mobile number.
  4. Log in with the generated Application Number and Password.
  5. Select the preferred post and enter personal, educational, CPCT, and shorthand details.
  6. Upload scanned photograph, signature, and required certificates.
  7. Pay the applicable application fee online and submit the form.
  8. Download and print the final submitted confirmation page for future reference.

Quick answers

Frequently Asked Questions

The MP High Court Stenographer and Assistant Grade III Online Preliminary Examination will be held on 15 January 2027. High Court of Madhya Pradesh (MPHC) confirmed this schedule through Notification No. 738/Exam/26 issued on 20 November 2026.

MPHC will release the Online Preliminary Examination admit card on mphc.gov.in approximately 7 days before the 15 January 2027 exam date. Registered candidates can download their hall ticket using their Application Number and Password.

According to Notification No. 738/Exam/26, the Online Preliminary Examination will be conducted across 7 cities in Madhya Pradesh: Jabalpur, Bhopal, Indore, Gwalior, Sagar, Satna, and Ujjain. Exact centre details will appear on the admit card.

High Court of Madhya Pradesh (MPHC) advertised 418 total vacancies under Advt. No. 666/Pariksha/2026. These include 69 Stenographer Grade II, 299 Stenographer Grade III, 20 Court Manager Staff, and 30 Assistant Grade III (English Knowing) posts.

The online application window for MP High Court Stenographer and Assistant Recruitment 2026 ran from 06 October 2026 to 27 October 2026 (05:00 PM). The application correction window closed on 01 November 2026, and fresh registrations are closed.

Under the 7th Pay Matrix, Stenographer Grade II carries Rs.28,700 - Rs.91,300, Stenographer Grade III carries Rs.25,300 - Rs.80,500, and Court Manager Staff and Assistant Grade III (English Knowing) carry Rs.19,500 - Rs.62,000 per month.

Candidates with a Bachelor Degree in any stream, a valid MP CPCT Scorecard, a 1-Year Computer Application Diploma, and Hindi Shorthand (100 WPM for Grade II or 80 WPM for Grade III) or English Shorthand (80 WPM for Assistant Grade III) were eligible.

The age limit is 18 to 40 years for Male candidates and 18 to 45 years for Female candidates as on 01 January 2026. Upper age relaxation up to 45 years applies to reserved categories and MP Government employees who hold Madhya Pradesh domicile.

General and Other State candidates paid Rs.943.40 as the online application fee. OBC, EWS, SC, ST, and PH candidates with Madhya Pradesh domicile paid Rs.743.40 through online payment modes before the 27 October 2026 deadline.

Applications were submitted online at mphc.gov.in between 06 October 2026 and 27 October 2026 by completing registration, entering educational and CPCT details, uploading photo and signature, paying the fee online, and printing the confirmation page.

Selection for MP High Court Stenographer and Assistant Grade III posts involves 4 stages: Online Preliminary Examination (100 marks screening test), Main Exam / Shorthand Skill Test (100 marks), Document Verification, and Medical Examination.

The official website of High Court of Madhya Pradesh (MPHC) is https://mphc.gov.in/ and its recruitment portal is https://portal.mphc.gov.in/. Candidates should verify all exam notices, admit cards, and results only on these official portals.
