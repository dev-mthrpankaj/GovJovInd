Complete context

About This Recruitment

RSSB Women Supervisor Recruitment 2026 is a Rajasthan Staff Selection Board recruitment drive to fill 72 Women Supervisor posts under Advertisement No. 02/2026. Of the total, 15 posts belong to the TSP (Tribal Sub Plan) area and 57 posts belong to the Non TSP area. The official notification was published on 6 January 2026 and the online application window opened on 7 January 2026.

The last date for submitting the online application was extended from 5 February 2026 to 11 February 2026 through an official RSSB extension notice. The written examination was conducted on 24 July 2026, and the admit card for the exam was released on 20 July 2026 on the Rajasthan recruitment portal.

The Rajasthan Staff Selection Board declared the Women Supervisor result on 4 November 2026. Separate result PDFs were published for TSP and Non TSP candidates along with the category-wise cut-off marks, and all three documents are linked on this page.

Basic eligibility is a graduation degree from a recognised university along with a computer qualification such as O Level, COPA, a degree or diploma in Computer Science or Computer Application, RSCIT, or 10+2 with Computer as a subject. The age limit is 18 to 40 years as on 1 January 2027, with relaxation for reserved categories as per RSSB rules.

Age Limit

Both the graduation degree and the computer qualification are mandatory as per RSSB Advertisement No. 02/2026.

What to do at the current stage

How to Check the RSSB Women Supervisor Result

  1. Open the official RSSB website or the direct result link given in Important Links.
  2. Download the TSP result PDF and the Non TSP result PDF separately.
  3. Press Ctrl and F on the PDF and search your roll number or name.
  4. Verify your roll number, category and the post area mentioned in the list.
  5. Download the cut-off PDF and compare your marks with the category-wise qualifying marks.
  6. Save the result PDF and keep printed copies ready for the document verification stage.

Original Application Process

Preserved for recruitment history and reference after the application stage closes.

  1. Visit the official Rajasthan SSO portal at sso.rajasthan.gov.in.
  2. Read RSSB Advertisement No. 02/2026 carefully before starting the form.
  3. Complete the one-time registration (OTR) using a valid mobile number and email ID.
  4. Fill the Women Supervisor online application form with accurate personal and educational details.
  5. Upload photograph, signature and the required documents.
  6. Pay the applicable application fee online.
  7. Submit the form and save the confirmation page for future reference.
