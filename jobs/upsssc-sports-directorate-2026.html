Complete context

About This Recruitment

UPSSSC Sports Directorate Recruitment 2026 is a recruitment drive by the Uttar Pradesh Subordinate Service Selection Commission (UPSSSC) to fill 108 posts under the Sports Directorate, Government of Uttar Pradesh. The official notification was published with Advt No 22-Exam/2026.

The online application window will remain open from 14 October 2026 to 04 November 2026. The last date for fee payment is also 04 November 2026, and the application correction window will remain open until 11 November 2026.

Eligible candidates must have a valid UPSSSC PET 2025 Score Card and possess the required knowledge and qualifications related to the post. The age limit is 18–21 years (minimum) to 40 years (maximum) as on 01 July 2026, with age relaxation applicable for reserved categories as per UPSSSC rules.

The selection process typically includes a written examination or PET score-based shortlisting, followed by document verification and other stages as specified in the official notification. The exam date, admit card release date and result date will be announced as per the official schedule.
