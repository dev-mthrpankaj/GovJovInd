Complete context

About This Recruitment

Government Institute of Medical Sciences (GIMS) issued Advertisement No. GIMS/2026/Manpower/NT-03/10 for GIMS Staff Nurse Recruitment 2026 to fill 100 Staff Nurse (Non Teaching Post) vacancies in Uttar Pradesh. The online application window ran from 10 August 2026 to the extended closing date of 07 October 2026.

The category-wise vacancy distribution includes 40 General, 27 OBC, 10 EWS, 21 SC, and 2 ST posts. Eligible candidates hold a B.Sc. Nursing (4-year) or Post Basic B.Sc. Nursing (2-year) degree, or a GNM Diploma with 2 years of experience in a minimum 50-bedded hospital, along with valid State or Indian Nursing Council registration. The prescribed age limit is 21 to 40 years as on 01 July 2026.

The GIMS Staff Nurse Admit Card 2026 was released on 24 November 2026 for the Computer Based Test (CBT) scheduled on 03 December 2026. Candidates must download and print their admit card and carry a valid original photo ID to the examination centre. Eligibility Criteria

Age Limit

21 to 40 years as on 01 July 2026.

Age relaxation applicable for reserved categories as per Uttar Pradesh government and GIMS recruitment rules.

Education

B.Sc. Nursing (4 years) or Post Basic B.Sc. Nursing (2 years) from an INC/State Medical Faculty recognized institute, OR Diploma in GNM from an INC recognized institute with 2 years experience in a minimum 50-bedded hospital.

Candidates must be registered as Nurse & Midwife in State or Indian Nursing Council.

Nationality / Other

Candidates must fulfill all eligibility, registration, and hospital experience conditions specified in Advt. No. GIMS/2026/Manpower/NT-03/10.

What to do at the current stage

How to Download GIMS Staff Nurse Admit Card 2026

  1. Open the official GIMS Staff Nurse Admit Card login link from Important Links.
  2. Enter your User ID / Registration Number and Password generated during registration.
  3. Submit the credentials and open the Admit Card tab on your dashboard.
  4. Download the GIMS Staff Nurse CBT Admit Card PDF.
  5. Verify your name, roll number, photograph, exam date (03 December 2026), shift timing, and exam centre address.
  6. Print a clear copy of the admit card and carry it with a valid original photo ID to the exam centre.

Original Application Process

Preserved for recruitment history and reference after the application stage closes.

  1. Visit the official GIMS recruitment portal at https://www.gims.ac.in/recruitment.html.
  2. Open the GIMS Staff Nurse 2026 Apply Online link.
  3. Register using a valid email ID and mobile number.
  4. Log in using the generated registration ID and password.
  5. Fill the online application form with personal, educational, experience, and category details.
  6. Upload scanned photograph, signature, and required supporting documents.
  7. Pay the applicable examination fee through online mode.
  8. Submit the application form and print the confirmation page for future reference.
