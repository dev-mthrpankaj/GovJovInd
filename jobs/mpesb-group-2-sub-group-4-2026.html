About This Recruitment

MPESB Group 2 Sub Group 4 Combined Recruitment Test 2026 is a recruitment drive by the Madhya Pradesh Employees Selection Board (MPESB) to fill 2106 posts including Patwari (Executive) and various other posts under Group-2 Sub-Group-4. The official notification was released on 04 August 2026, and the online application window remained open from 04 August 2026 to 21 August 2026 (extended from the original last date of 18 August 2026). The application correction window was available until 23 August 2026.

The Computer Based Test (CBT) was conducted from 23 September 2026 to 05 October 2026 across various exam centres in Madhya Pradesh. The provisional answer key was released on 06 October 2026, and candidates can raise objections until 09 October 2026 by paying Rs.150 per question. The admit card was released on 18 September 2026.

The recruitment covers posts in 61 departments and institutions, including the Madhya Pradesh State Agricultural Marketing Board, Panchayat Raj Directorate, Commissioner Co-operation, Women and Child Development Department, and MP State Civil Supplies Corporation. A total of 200 Patwari posts are reserved for Persons with Disabilities (Divyangjan) under a separate category.

Basic eligibility includes a Bachelor’s Degree from a recognised university and a valid CPCT scorecard (Hindi Typing & Computer Efficiency). The age limit is 18 to 40 years for UR/EWS and 18 to 45 years for SC/ST/OBC/PwBD/Female candidates as on 01 January 2026.
