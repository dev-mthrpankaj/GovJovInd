About This Recruitment

MPESB Group 2 Sub Group 4 Recruitment 2026 is being conducted by the Madhya Pradesh Employees Selection Board (MPESB) for 2106 posts of Patwari and other Group 2 Sub Group 4 positions. The online application window was open from 04 August 2026 to 21 August 2026.

The admit card for the written examination scheduled on 22 September 2026 has now been released. Candidates who applied successfully can download their admit card from the official MPESB portal. The exam will be conducted across various centres in Madhya Pradesh.

This is a permanent recruitment lifecycle page. All future updates including answer key, result, document verification, and final merit list will be published on this same URL without any change.
