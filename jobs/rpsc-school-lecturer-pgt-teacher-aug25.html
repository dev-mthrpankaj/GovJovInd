About This Recruitment

Rajasthan Public Service Commission (RPSC) School Lecturer PGT Teacher Recruitment 2025, Advt. No. 06/2025, covers 3,225 vacancies across multiple school subjects.

Online applications were accepted from 14 August to 12 September 2025. The admit card became available on 28 May 2026 and the examination was scheduled from 31 May to 11 June 2026.

The recruitment has now moved to the result stage. The latest supplied update includes official subject-wise result PDFs for Sanskrit, Biology, Public Administration, Music, Punjabi and Home Science, while earlier available results remain preserved on this permanent lifecycle page.
