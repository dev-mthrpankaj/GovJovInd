About This Recruitment

Rajasthan Public Service Commission (RPSC) Assistant Prosecution Officer (APO) Recruitment 2026 is being conducted for 371 posts under Advertisement No. 03/EXAM/APO/EP-1/2026-27.

The online application process ran from 8 June to 7 July 2026. The preliminary examination was held on 2 September 2026 and the preliminary answer key was released on 7 September 2026 along with an objection notice.

The supplied recruitment schedule lists the Main Examination for 27 December 2026. This permanent page is structured to carry the full recruitment lifecycle, so later result and selection-stage updates can be added here without changing the URL.

Eligibility Criteria

Age Limit

21 to 40 years as on 1 January 2027.

Age relaxation as per RPSC rules; refer to the official notification.

Education

Bachelor Degree in Law (LLB) or Integrated Law Course from a university established by law in India.

Working knowledge of Hindi written in Devanagari script and knowledge of Rajasthani culture are also listed.

Nationality / Other

Candidates must satisfy the detailed eligibility conditions prescribed in the official RPSC APO notification.

Refer to official notification for detailed conditions.
