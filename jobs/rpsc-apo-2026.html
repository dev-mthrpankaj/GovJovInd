About This Recruitment

Rajasthan Public Service Commission (RPSC) has released the RPSC APO Result 2026 for the Assistant Prosecution Officer (APO) recruitment on its official website. The preliminary examination was conducted on 02 September 2026, and the result for the same has now been declared on 01 October 2026.

This recruitment drive covers 371 Assistant Prosecution Officer posts under Advertisement No. 3/Exam/APO/EP-1/2026-27. Candidates who appeared in the preliminary examination can now check their result status and download the result PDF from the official RPSC portal. The mains examination is scheduled for 27 December 2026 for qualified candidates.

This is a permanent lifecycle page. Every future update — including the mains admit card, mains answer key, final result, cut off, document verification, and merit list — will be published on this same page without any URL change.

Eligibility Criteria

Age Limit

Minimum 21 years and maximum 40 years as on 01 January 2027.

Age relaxation applicable as per RPSC regulations for reserved categories.

Education

Bachelor Degree in Law (LLB) from a university established by law. Working knowledge of Hindi written in Devnagari Script and knowledge of Rajasthani culture.

All candidates must read the official information completely before applying. Refer to the official notification for detailed educational conditions.

Nationality / Other

Indian citizens fulfilling the post-specific eligibility conditions may apply. Candidates must have working knowledge of Hindi in Devnagari script and knowledge of Rajasthani culture.

Refer to official notification for detailed conditions.

What to do at the current stage

How to Check RPSC APO Result 2026

  1. Visit the official RPSC website at rpsc.rajasthan.gov.in.
  2. On the homepage, click on the Results section or the Latest News/Notifications section.
  3. Open the Assistant Prosecution Officer (APO) Result PDF link.
  4. Search your roll number or enrollment number in the result PDF.
  5. Verify all details mentioned in the result notification.
  6. Download the Result PDF and save it for future reference.
  7. Candidates qualified in prelims should prepare for the mains examination scheduled on 27 December 2026.

Original Application Process

Preserved for recruitment history and reference after the application stage closes.

  1. Visit the official RPSC recruitment website.
  2. Read the RPSC APO notification carefully.
  3. Open the application link from Important Links.
  4. Register and fill the form.
  5. Upload required documents.
  6. Pay the applicable fee.
  7. Submit and save the confirmation page.
