Complete context

About This Recruitment

Banaras Hindu University (BHU) has released Advertisement No. 03/2026-2027 dated 26 October 2026 for 11 Non-Teaching Group A and Group C posts at its Computer Centre, Varanasi. The posts include System Engineer, Maintenance Engineer (Junior), Computer Operator Grade-I, AC Operator, Laboratory Attendant, and Workshop Attendant.

The online application window is open from 26 October 2026 to 25 November 2026 (up to 5:00 PM). Candidates must also submit a downloaded hard copy of the application with enclosures to the Registrar (Recruitment and Assessment Cell), BHU, Varanasi, by 30 November 2026 (up to 5:00 PM).

Eligibility varies by post: System Engineer requires M.Tech/M.E./M.S. with 5 years experience (or B.Tech/MCA with 7 years), Maintenance Engineer (Junior) requires B.E./B.Tech with 5 years experience, and Group C posts require qualifications ranging from ITI to B.Sc/BCA. Age limits are post-specific: 50 years for System Engineer, 40 years for Maintenance Engineer, and 32 years for Group C posts.

The selection process includes a written test, skill test, and document verification. The syllabus and exam pattern will be notified by the University at a later stage.
