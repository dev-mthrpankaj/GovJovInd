About This Recruitment

Uttar Pradesh Subordinate Services Selection Commission (UPSSSC) has released the notification for Senior Instructor Cadre Recruitment 2026, offering 132 posts across various disciplines such as Agricultural Engineering, Plant Protection, Horticulture, Animal Husbandry, and more.

The online application window is open from 14 September 2026 to 05 October 2026. Candidates with the required educational qualifications and experience can apply through the official UPSSSC portal. The application fee is Rs. 25 for all categories.

This permanent page will be updated with every subsequent stage—exam date, admit card, answer key, result, and final merit list—without changing the URL. Candidates are advised to read the official notification carefully before applying.
