Complete context

About This Recruitment

UPSC EPFO APFC Recruitment 2026 is a central government recruitment drive for 80 Assistant Provident Fund Commissioner (APFC) posts in the Employees' Provident Fund Organisation (EPFO), Ministry of Labour & Employment. This is a Group 'A' Non-Ministerial position.

The online application window opened on 22 August 2026 and has been extended to 14 September 2026 (06:00 PM). The recruitment test (RT) is scheduled for 20 December 2026, followed by an interview for shortlisted candidates.

Candidates must hold a Bachelor's Degree from a recognized university. The selection process involves a Recruitment Test (300 marks) with 75% weightage and an Interview (100 marks) with 25% weightage.

Eligibility Criteria

Age Limit

Maximum 35 years for UR/EWS; 38 years for OBC; 40 years for SC/ST (as on last date of application).

PwBD candidates get up to 10 years additional relaxation as per rules. Other relaxations applicable as per UPSC norms.

Education

Bachelor's Degree from a recognized University or equivalent.

Diploma in Company Law, Labour Laws, or Public Administration is desirable (not mandatory).

Nationality / Other

Indian citizens fulfilling the post-specific eligibility conditions may apply. No essential work experience prescribed.

No essential work experience prescribed.

How to Apply

  1. Visit the UPSC Online Recruitment Application (ORA) portal.
  2. Complete One-Time Registration (OTR) if not already done.
  3. Fill the Common Application Form (CAF) and select APFC post.
  4. Upload scanned photograph, signature, and required documents.
  5. Pay the application fee (Rs. 25 for General/OBC/EWS male candidates).
  6. Submit the form before 14 September 2026 (06:00 PM).
