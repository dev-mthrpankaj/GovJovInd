About This Recruitment

UPSC EPFO APFC Recruitment 2026 is a recruitment drive conducted by the Union Public Service Commission for 80 Assistant Provident Fund Commissioner posts in the Employees' Provident Fund Organisation (EPFO), Ministry of Labour & Employment. The online application window started on 22 August 2026 and has now been extended to 14 September 2026 (6:00 PM).

The APFC post is a Group 'A' central government position with pay Level-10 in the Pay Matrix (7th CPC). Candidates must hold a bachelor's degree from a recognised university. The selection process consists of a Recruitment Test (300 marks) followed by an Interview (100 marks), with a 75:25 weightage between the two stages.

The Recruitment Test is scheduled for 20 December 2026. Candidates who successfully submit their applications should now focus on preparation and keep their registration details safe for future stages including admit card, result, and interview.
