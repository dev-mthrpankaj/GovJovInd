Complete context

About This Recruitment

DSSSB Junior Engineer and MTS Recruitment 2026 is a combined examination drive by the Delhi Subordinate Services Selection Board (DSSSB) to fill 1260 posts across Delhi government departments, autonomous bodies and local authorities. The official notification for Advertisement No. 06/2026 was released on 06 November 2026.

The vacancy split is 185 Junior Engineer (Electrical) posts under Post Code 809/26 and 1075 Multi-Tasking Staff (MTS) posts under Post Code 810/26. The JE posts are distributed across NDMC, Delhi Agricultural Marketing Board, MCD, Delhi Transco Limited and DUSIB.

The online application window opened on 12 November 2026 at 12:00 noon and will close on 11 December 2026 at 11:59 PM. Eligible candidates must apply through the official DSSSB online portal at dsssbonline.nic.in.

Junior Engineer applicants need a Diploma or Degree in Electrical Engineering, while MTS applicants need a Class 10 (Matriculation) pass. The age limit is 18 to 27 years as on 11 December 2026, with an upper limit of 32 years for the JE post in NDMC. Selection for JE includes Tier-I and Tier-II Computer Based Tests, while MTS has a single-tier CBT, followed by e-Dossier and document verification.
