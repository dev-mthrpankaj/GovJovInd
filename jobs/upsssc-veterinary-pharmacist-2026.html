Complete context

About This Recruitment

Uttar Pradesh Subordinate Services Selection Commission (UPSSSC) is conducting the UPSSSC Veterinary Pharmacist Recruitment 2026 under Advertisement No. 18-Exam/2026 to fill 1308 Veterinary Pharmacist posts in the Directorate of Animal Husbandry, Uttar Pradesh. The official notification was released on 14 August 2026, and online applications remained open from 14 September 2026 to 05 October 2026, with form correction closing on 12 October 2026.

On 21 November 2026, UPSSSC released the UPSSSC Veterinary Pharmacist Eligibility Result 2026 and shortlisting notice on upsssc.gov.in. Candidates who applied using their UPSSSC PET 2025 scorecard can now check whether they are shortlisted for the upcoming Mains Written Examination. The Mains Exam date and admit card schedule will be notified separately by the commission.

Out of the 1308 total vacancies, 576 posts are allotted to General (UR), 359 to OBC, 130 to EWS, 237 to SC and 6 to ST categories. Selected candidates are appointed in Pay Matrix Level-5 with a pay scale of Rs.29,200 to Rs.92,300 plus applicable Uttar Pradesh Government allowances.

Required eligibility includes a valid UPSSSC PET 2025 scorecard, Intermediate (10+2) with Biology, a Diploma in Veterinary Pharmacy from a recognised institution, mandatory council registration and working knowledge of Hindi in Devanagari script. The age limit is 18 to 40 years as on 01 July 2026, with category-wise upper age relaxation as per Uttar Pradesh Government rules.
