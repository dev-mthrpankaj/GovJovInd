Complete context

About This Recruitment

Government Medical College & Hospital (GMCH), Chandigarh, has announced the Chandigarh GMCH Recruitment 2026 for 242 posts across various medical and paramedical positions. The official notification was released in October 2026, and the online application window is open from 14 October 2026 to 28 October 2026 (up to 04:00 PM).

The selection process includes a Computer Based Test (CBT) scheduled for 03-04 November 2026, followed by document verification and a medical examination. The result is expected to be declared on 10 November 2026.

Eligibility varies by post and includes qualifications such as MD, MS, DNB, M.Ch, DM, MBBS, M.Sc Medical, and BDS, along with registration in the relevant Medical Council. The age limit is post-specific, with a maximum age ranging from 30 to 45 years as on 28 October 2026. Age relaxation is applicable as per rules.

Eligibility Criteria

Age Limit

Maximum age is 30 years, 37 years, or 45 years (post-wise) as on 28 October 2026.

Age relaxation is applicable for SC, ST, OBC, PwBD, Ex-Servicemen, and other reserved categories as per rules.

Education

Candidates must possess qualifications such as MD, MS, DNB, M.Ch, DM, MBBS, M.Sc Medical, or BDS, and must be registered with the relevant Medical Council.

The specific qualification required varies by post. Refer to the official notification for details.

Nationality / Other

Indian nationals fulfilling the post-specific eligibility conditions may apply.

Refer to official notification for detailed conditions.

How to Apply Online

  1. Visit the official GMCH website at https://gmch.gov.in/.
