Complete context

About This Recruitment

Uttarakhand Subordinate Service Selection Commission (UKSSSC) released the notification for Assistant Agriculture Officer (AAO), Senior Milk Inspector, and Pharmacist Grade-3 Recruitment 2026 to fill 90 vacancies under Advertisement No. 77/2026. The online application window was open from 01 June 2026 to 21 June 2026, and the application correction window was available from 24 June 2026 to 26 June 2026.

The recruitment includes Assistant Agriculture Officer Grade-3 (82 posts), Senior Milk Inspector (7 posts), and Pharmacist Grade-3 (1 post). Eligible candidates required a B.Sc. degree in Agriculture or a related field, along with the age criteria of 21 to 42 years as on 01 July 2026.

The selection process consists of a written competitive examination, document verification, and medical examination. According to the revised UKSSSC exam calendar released on 25 August 2026, the written examination for Agriculture Qualification Posts (Advertisement No. 77/2026) is scheduled to be held on 11 October 2026. The admit card has now been released and the direct download link is available on this permanent page.
