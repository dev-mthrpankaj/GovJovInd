Complete context

About This Recruitment

SSC Delhi Police Head Constable Ministerial 2025 is a recruitment drive by the Staff Selection Commission (SSC) for 509 Head Constable (Ministerial) posts in Delhi Police. The online application window was open from 29 September 2025 to 20 October 2025.

This role is suitable for 10+2 passed candidates with English typing speed of 30 WPM or Hindi typing speed of 25 WPM. The selection process includes a Computer Based Examination, Physical Endurance and Measurement Test (PE&MT), and Document Verification.

The PET & MT / DV stage was initially scheduled, then postponed. Now, the Staff Selection Commission has released the revised/fresh PET & MT / DV admit card for shortlisted candidates. The physical test for Head Constable (Ministerial) is scheduled for 28 and 29 September 2026. Candidates must download the new admit card as the previously issued admit card is no longer valid.

What to do at the current stage

How to Download Revised PET & MT / DV Admit Card

  1. Visit the official SSC NR website at sscnr.nic.in.
  2. Click on the link for 'Delhi Police HC (Ministerial) PE&MT Revised Admit Card 2026'.
  3. Enter your Registration ID / Roll Number and Date of Birth.
  4. Complete the captcha verification and submit.
  5. Download and print the revised admit card.
  6. Check the reporting time, venue, and date carefully (PET & MT for HC Ministerial is on 28 & 29 September 2026).
  7. Carry the printed admit card along with a valid photo ID to the examination venue.
  8. Do NOT use the old admit card as it is no longer valid.

Original Application Process

Preserved for recruitment history and reference after the application stage closes.

  1. Visit the official SSC website.
  2. Read the Delhi Police Head Constable Ministerial notification carefully.
  3. Open the application link from Important Links.
  4. Register and fill the form.
  5. Upload required documents.
  6. Pay the applicable fee.
  7. Submit and save the confirmation page.
