Complete context

About This Recruitment

State Bank of India (SBI) has released the notification for SBI Trade Finance Officer TFO Recruitment 2026, inviting online applications for 35 Specialist Cadre Officer posts. The application window was initially scheduled from 29 August 2026 to 19 September 2026, but the last date has now been extended to 28 September 2026.

The recruitment covers two posts: Trade Finance Officer – IBG (JMGS-I) with 14 vacancies, and Trade Finance Officer – IBG (MMGS-II) with 21 vacancies. Both roles require a bachelor's degree and relevant experience in forex operations. This is a Pan-India opportunity with postings across SBI branches.

Candidates are advised to complete the application process well before the extended deadline to avoid last-minute technical issues. All future updates regarding admit card, exam date, result, and document verification will be published on this same permanent page.
