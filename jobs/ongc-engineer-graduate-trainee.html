Complete context

About This Recruitment

ONGC Graduate Trainee Recruitment 2026 is a recruitment drive conducted by Oil and Natural Gas Corporation Limited (ONGC), a Maharatna Public Sector Undertaking, to fill 52 vacancies for Geologists and Engineers at E1 level. The official notification was published on 17 July 2026 under Advertisement No. 2/2026 (R&P), and the online application window remained open from 17 July 2026 to 31 July 2026.

The Computer Based Test (CBT) was conducted on 06 September 2026. The CBT admit card was released on 22 August 2026. The exam consisted of 85 objective-type questions covering Domain Knowledge, General Awareness, Aptitude Test, and English Language, with a total duration of 2 hours and no negative marking.

The interview call letter has now been released on 07 October 2026, and the download window will remain open until 29 October 2026. Candidates who have qualified the CBT are eligible to download the interview call letter and appear for the Personal Interview round.

Basic eligibility includes a Graduate degree in the relevant Engineering discipline or a Post Graduate degree in Geology with minimum 60% marks. The age limit is 21-26 years for Engineering streams and 21-27 years for Geology (UR/EWS), with relaxation for reserved categories as per ONGC rules.

What to do at the current stage

How to Download Interview Call Letter

  1. Open the official ONGC interview call letter download link from Important Links.
  2. Enter your Registration Number / Roll Number and Password / Date of Birth (dd-mm-yy).
  3. Enter the captcha code and click on Submit / Login.
  4. Your ONGC Graduate Trainee Interview Call Letter will appear on the screen.
  5. Check your name, roll number, interview date, reporting time and venue carefully.
  6. Download and take a clear printout of the call letter for the interview day.
  7. Carry the printed call letter along with a valid original photo ID and required documents.

Original Application Process

Preserved for recruitment history and reference after the application stage closes.

  1. Visit the official ONGC recruitment website at ongcindia.com.
  2. Read the official Advt. No. 2/2026 (R&P) notification carefully.
  3. Register on the ONGC recruitment portal with valid email ID and mobile number.
  4. Fill the online application form with accurate personal and educational details.
  5. Upload photograph, signature and required documents.
  6. Pay the applicable examination fee online.
  7. Submit the form and save the confirmation page for future reference.
