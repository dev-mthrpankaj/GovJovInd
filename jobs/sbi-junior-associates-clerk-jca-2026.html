Complete context

About This Recruitment

State Bank of India (SBI) Junior Associates Clerk (JCA) Recruitment 2026 is a nationwide drive for Junior Associate (Customer Support & Sales) posts in clerical cadre across SBI branches. The online application window was open from 11 August 2026 to 31 August 2026 and a total of 9124 vacancies were advertised under advertisement number CRPD/CR/2026-27/17.

The recruitment covers both regular and backlog vacancies. The basic eligibility is a graduation degree in any stream from a recognised university, and the age limit is 20 to 28 years as on 1 April 2026 with category-wise relaxation as per SBI rules. There is no application fee for SC / ST / PwBD candidates, while General, OBC and EWS candidates pay Rs.750.

The Preliminary examination is scheduled for 26-27 September 2026 and the SBI Clerk JCA Prelims admit card has been released on the official IBPS registration portal. This page will continue to carry every later stage — answer key, result, main exam, and final merit list — on the same permanent URL.
