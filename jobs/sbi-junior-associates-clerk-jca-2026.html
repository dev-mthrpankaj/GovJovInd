Complete context

About This Recruitment

State Bank of India (SBI) invited online applications for SBI Junior Associates Clerk Recruitment 2026 (Advt No. CRPD/CR/SPLDrive/2026-27/17) to fill 9124 Junior Associates Clerk vacancies, including 7680 regular posts and 1444 backlog posts. The online registration window remained open from 11 August 2026 to 31 August 2026.

Following Pre-Examination Training (PET) in September 2026, the Preliminary Examination was conducted on 26 September 2026 and 27 September 2026. On 18 November 2026, State Bank of India declared the Preliminary Examination Result and activated the Mains Admit Card download portal for shortlisted candidates.

The Main Examination is scheduled for 26 November 2026 and 27 November 2026. Qualified candidates must download their Mains call letter using their Registration Number or Roll Number and Password or Date of Birth, and carry a printed copy along with a valid original photo ID to the exam centre.

Eligibility requires a passed or appearing Bachelor's Degree in any discipline from a recognised university, with an age limit of 20 to 28 years as on 01 April 2026. Final selection is based on the Main Written Examination, Local Language Test, Document Verification and Medical Examination.

What to do at the current stage

How to Download Mains Admit Card & Check Pre Result

  1. Open the official SBI Pre Result link or Mains Admit Card portal from the Important Links section.
  2. Log in using your Registration Number or Roll Number and Password or Date of Birth.
  3. Download the SBI Junior Associates Clerk Mains Admit Card 2026 PDF.
  4. Verify your name, roll number, exam date (26 or 27 November 2026), reporting time and venue address.
  5. Take a clear printout of the Mains call letter and affix a recent passport-size photograph if required.
  6. Carry the printed Mains Admit Card along with a valid original photo ID and photocopy to the examination centre.

Original Application Process

Preserved for recruitment history and reference after the application stage closes.

  1. Visit the official State Bank of India Careers portal at sbi.bank.in/web/careers/current-openings.
  2. Open the SBI Junior Associates Clerk Recruitment 2026 notification (Advt No. CRPD/CR/SPLDrive/2026-27/17).
  3. Click on Apply Online and complete new registration with a valid mobile number and email ID.
  4. Fill in personal, academic and circle/state preference details accurately.
  5. Upload scanned photograph, signature, left thumb impression and handwritten declaration.
  6. Pay the applicable application fee online and submit the form.
  7. Download and save the submitted application confirmation for future stages.

Quick answers

Frequently Asked Questions

Yes. State Bank of India (SBI) released the Junior Associates Clerk Mains Admit Card on 18 November 2026 along with the Preliminary Examination Result. Shortlisted candidates can download their call letter using the official link on this page.

The State Bank of India (SBI) Junior Associates Clerk Main Written Examination 2026 is scheduled to be conducted on 26 November 2026 and 27 November 2026. Candidates must check their exact shift and reporting time on the Mains Admit Card.

Candidates can download the Mains call letter by opening the official SBI Mains Admit Card link in the Important Links section and logging in with their Registration Number or Roll Number and Password or Date of Birth.

The SBI Junior Associates Clerk call letter contains the candidate's name, roll number, registration number, examination date, reporting time, examination venue address and mandatory exam-day instructions.

State Bank of India conducted the Junior Associates Clerk Preliminary Written Examination on 26 September 2026 and 27 September 2026, and declared the Prelims Result on 18 November 2026 on its official website.

A total of 9124 vacancies are advertised under Advt No. CRPD/CR/SPLDrive/2026-27/17, comprising 3253 General, 760 EWS, 1716 OBC, 1114 SC, 837 ST regular posts and 1444 backlog posts.

Candidates must hold a passed or appearing Bachelor's Degree in any discipline from a recognised university. Proficiency in the opted local language of the state or union territory is also required.

The prescribed age limit is 20 to 28 years as on 01 April 2026. Upper age relaxation is applicable for SC, ST, OBC, PwBD and other reserved categories as per State Bank of India rules.

The application fee was Rs.750 for General, OBC and EWS candidates, while SC, ST and PwBD candidates were exempted (Nil). The fee was paid online before the 31 August 2026 closing date.

The selection process consists of five stages: Preliminary Written Examination, Main Written Examination, Local Language Test, Document Verification and Medical Examination.

The official recruitment portal of State Bank of India (SBI) is https://sbi.bank.in/web/careers/current-openings, where all official notifications, admit cards and results are published.
