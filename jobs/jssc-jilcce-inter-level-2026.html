About This Recruitment

Jharkhand Staff Selection Commission (JSSC) has reopened the online application window for the JSSC JILCCE Inter Level Recruitment 2026, offering 326 posts. This is a significant opportunity for candidates who have completed Intermediate (10+2) from a recognized board in India.

The recruitment is for various posts under the JILCCE Inter Level examination. The selection process typically includes a Written Exam (MCQ) followed by Document Verification. The application window was reopened, allowing more candidates to apply online between 05 August 2026 and the extended last date of 29 September 2026.

Candidates are advised to carefully read the official notification, check the detailed eligibility criteria including age limits and educational qualifications, and complete the application process before the deadline to avoid last-minute issues.
