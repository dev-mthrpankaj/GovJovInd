Eligibility Criteria

Age Limit

21 to 40 years as on 01 July 2026.

Age relaxation applicable as per UPSSSC rules.

Education

B.Sc. or M.Sc. degree in the relevant discipline (Physics, Chemistry, Biology, Forensic Science, Computer Science, etc.) from a recognized university. For some posts, a Bachelor's degree in Computer Science or 'A' Level Diploma from NIELIT is required.

Candidates must possess UPSSSC PET 2025 score card. Refer to the official notification for detailed post-wise qualification.

Indian citizens fulfilling the post-specific eligibility conditions may apply.
