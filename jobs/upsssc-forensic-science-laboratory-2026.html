About This Recruitment

UPSSSC Forensic Science Laboratory Recruitment 2026 is a recruitment drive by the Uttar Pradesh Subordinate Service Selection Commission (UPSSSC) for 208 posts of Scientific Assistant and Lab Assistant in the Forensic Science Laboratory, Uttar Pradesh. The online application window was open from 27 July 2026 to 24 August 2026 (extended).

The Mains examination is scheduled for 27 September 2026. The Mains Admit Card has now been released, and candidates can download it from the official UPSSSC portal. The exam city details and exam city notice have also been published.

This page is a permanent recruitment lifecycle page that will continue to carry every official update — from answer key and result to document verification and final merit list — without changing the URL.
