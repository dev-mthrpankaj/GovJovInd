About This Recruitment

UPSSSC Forensic Science Laboratory Recruitment 2026 is a Uttar Pradesh Subordinate Services Selection Commission recruitment under Advertisement No. 15-Exam/2026 for 208 posts.

The online application window ran from 27 July to 24 August 2026 after extension. The available recruitment update records the eligibility result on 27 August 2026 and a mains examination fee-payment update on 8 September 2026.
