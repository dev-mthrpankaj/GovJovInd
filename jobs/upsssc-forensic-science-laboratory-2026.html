About This Recruitment

UPSSSC Forensic Science Laboratory Recruitment 2026 is a recruitment drive by the Uttar Pradesh Subordinate Services Selection Commission (UPSSSC) for 208 posts of Scientific Assistant and Lab Assistant (Prayogshala Sahayak) in the Forensic Science Laboratory (Combined Cadre), Uttar Pradesh, Lucknow. The online application window was open from 27 July 2026 to 24 August 2026 (extended).

The main examination was held on 27 September 2026. The provisional answer key was released on 29 September 2026, and candidates can raise objections until 5 October 2026. This page covers the complete recruitment lifecycle, including notification, application, eligibility result, exam, answer key, objection window, and all upcoming stages such as final answer key, result, document verification, and final merit list.

Candidates must have qualified UPSSSC PET 2025 with a valid score card to be eligible for this recruitment. The selection process is based on the Main Written Examination, followed by document verification and final merit list preparation.
