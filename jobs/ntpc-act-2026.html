About This Recruitment

NTPC Assistant Chemist Trainee Recruitment 2026 is being conducted by NTPC Limited under Advertisement No. 09/2026 for 40 Assistant Chemist Trainee (ACT) posts. Online applications were accepted from 26 June to 10 July 2026.

The latest supplied update is the release of the NTPC ACT Admit Card on 3 September 2026. The examination is scheduled for 13 September 2026, so candidates should download their hall ticket from the official NTPC recruitment portal and check the reporting instructions carefully.

The supplied eligibility information requires an M.Sc. in Chemistry from a recognized university or institute in India with 60% marks. Detailed pay scale, selection conditions, reservation rules and other requirements should be checked in the official notification.
