Complete context

About This Recruitment

Consortium of National Law Universities conducts the Common Law Admission Test (CLAT 2027) for admission to 5-Year Integrated Undergraduate (B.A. LL.B. Hons.) and Postgraduate (LL.M.) law programmes across 24 participating National Law Universities in India. Online registration remained open from 03 August 2026 to 31 October 2026.

The CLAT 2027 Admit Card was released on 21 November 2026 on the official Consortium of National Law Universities portal. The entrance examination is scheduled for 06 December 2026, and candidates must carry a printed copy of the admit card along with a valid original photo ID to their allotted exam centre.

For CLAT UG, candidates who have passed or are appearing in 10+2 (Intermediate) with at least 45% marks (40% for reserved categories) are eligible. For CLAT PG, candidates who have passed or are appearing in an LL.B. degree with at least 55% marks (50% for reserved categories) are eligible. There is no upper age limit prescribed for appearing in the examination.

Participating National Law Universities include NLSIU Bengaluru, NALSAR Hyderabad, NLIU Bhopal, WBNUJS Kolkata, NLU Jodhpur, HNLU Raipur, GNLU Gandhinagar, GNLU Silvassa Campus, RMLNLU Lucknow, RGNUL Punjab, CNLU Patna, NUALS Kochi, NLUO Odisha, NUSRL Ranchi, NLUJA Assam, DSNLU Visakhapatnam, TNNLU Tiruchirappalli, MNLU Mumbai, MNLU Nagpur, MNLU Aurangabad, HPNLU Shimla, DNLU Jabalpur, DBRANLU Haryana, and NLUT Agartala.
