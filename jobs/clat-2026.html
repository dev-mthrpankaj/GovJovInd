Complete context

About This Recruitment

Consortium of National Law Universities conducts the Common Law Admission Test (CLAT 2027) for admission to 5-Year Integrated Undergraduate (B.A. LL.B. Hons.) and Postgraduate (LL.M.) law programmes across 24 participating National Law Universities in India. Online registration remained open from 03 August 2026 to 31 October 2026.

The CLAT 2027 Admit Card was released on 21 November 2026 on the official Consortium of National Law Universities portal. The entrance examination is scheduled for 06 December 2026, and candidates must carry a printed copy of the admit card along with a valid original photo ID to their allotted exam centre.

For CLAT UG, candidates who have passed or are appearing in 10+2 (Intermediate) with at least 45% marks (40% for reserved categories) are eligible. For CLAT PG, candidates who have passed or are appearing in an LL.B. degree with at least 55% marks (50% for reserved categories) are eligible. There is no upper age limit prescribed for appearing in the examination.

Participating National Law Universities include NLSIU Bengaluru, NALSAR Hyderabad, NLIU Bhopal, WBNUJS Kolkata, NLU Jodhpur, HNLU Raipur, GNLU Gandhinagar, GNLU Silvassa Campus, RMLNLU Lucknow, RGNUL Punjab, CNLU Patna, NUALS Kochi, NLUO Odisha, NUSRL Ranchi, NLUJA Assam, DSNLU Visakhapatnam, TNNLU Tiruchirappalli, MNLU Mumbai, MNLU Nagpur, MNLU Aurangabad, HPNLU Shimla, DNLU Jabalpur, DBRANLU Haryana, and NLUT Agartala.

What to do at the current stage

How to Download CLAT 2027 Admit Card

  1. Open the official CLAT 2027 portal at consortiumofnlus.ac.in/clat-2027/ from Important Links.
  2. Log in using your registered mobile number and password.
  3. Click the Download Admit Card button displayed on your candidate dashboard.
  4. Verify your name, photograph, roll number, exam centre address, and reporting time.
  5. Print two clear copies of the admit card and carry one with a valid original photo ID on 06 December 2026.

Original Application Process

Preserved for recruitment history and reference after the application stage closes.

  1. Visit the official Consortium of National Law Universities website at consortiumofnlus.ac.in.
  2. Read the CLAT 2027 information brochure and eligibility criteria carefully.
  3. Register online with a valid mobile number and email address.
  4. Fill in personal, academic, and NLU preference details accurately.
  5. Upload scanned photograph, signature, and required category documents.
  6. Pay the application fee (Rs.4000 for General/OBC/EWS or Rs.3500 for SC/ST) online.
  7. Submit the form and print the confirmation page for future reference.

Frequently Asked Questions

Yes, the Consortium of National Law Universities released the CLAT 2027 Admit Card on 21 November 2026. Registered UG and PG candidates can download their hall ticket from consortiumofnlus.ac.in/clat-2027/.

The CLAT 2027 entrance examination for both Undergraduate (5-Year B.A. LL.B. Hons.) and Postgraduate (LL.M.) programmes is scheduled to be held on 06 December 2026 across centres in India.

Candidates must visit consortiumofnlus.ac.in/clat-2027/, log in using their registered mobile number and password, and click the Download Admit Card button on their dashboard to save and print the PDF.

Candidates must carry a printed copy of the CLAT 2027 Admit Card along with a valid original photo ID such as Aadhaar, PAN, Voter ID, or Passport, and recent passport-size photographs.

A total of 24 National Law Universities participate in CLAT 2027, including NLSIU Bengaluru, NALSAR Hyderabad, NLIU Bhopal, WBNUJS Kolkata, NLU Jodhpur, GNLU Gandhinagar, RMLNLU Lucknow, and NLUT Agartala.

For CLAT UG (5-Year B.A. LL.B. Hons.), candidates must have passed or be appearing in 10+2 (Intermediate) from a recognised board with at least 45% marks, or 40% marks for reserved categories.

For CLAT PG (LL.M.), candidates must have passed or be appearing in an LL.B. degree from a recognised university with a minimum of 55% marks, or 50% marks for reserved category candidates.

No, there is no minimum or maximum age limit prescribed by the Consortium of National Law Universities for appearing in the CLAT 2027 UG or PG entrance examination.

The CLAT 2027 online application fee was Rs.4000 for General, OBC, and EWS candidates, and Rs.3500 for SC and ST candidates, paid online before the 31 October 2026 deadline.

The CLAT 2027 result date will be announced as per the official schedule after the 06 December 2026 examination. Candidates can check official result updates on consortiumofnlus.ac.in.
