Complete context

About This Recruitment

Indian Army TES 57 (10+2 Technical Entry Scheme) is a prestigious entry route for unmarried male candidates who have passed Class 12 with Physics, Chemistry and Mathematics (PCM) and appeared for JEE Main 2026. The scheme offers a four-year B.Tech programme at the Indian Military Academy (IMA), Dehradun, followed by a Permanent Commission as Lieutenant in the Indian Army.

The official notification for TES 57 (July 2027 course) was released in October 2026 in Employment News (10-16 October 2026). Online applications are being accepted from 14 October 2026 to 12 November 2026 through the official website joinindianarmy.nic.in. A total of 90 vacancies (tentative) have been announced for this batch.

Selection is based on shortlisting of candidates based on JEE Main 2026 score and Class 12 PCM marks. Shortlisted candidates will be called for the Services Selection Board (SSB) interview, followed by Document Verification and Medical Examination. The final merit list will be prepared based on SSB performance and medical fitness.

Selected candidates will undergo four years of training comprising engineering education leading to a B.Tech degree and military training. During the final phase of training, Gentlemen Cadets receive a stipend of Rs. 56,100 per month. Upon successful completion, candidates are commissioned as Lieutenant in Pay Level 10 (Rs. 56,100 – Rs. 1,77,500) with Military Service Pay (MSP) of Rs. 15,500.
