Complete context

About This Recruitment

Railway Recruitment Board (RRB) has released the Centralised Employment Notification CEN 02/2025 for recruitment to 6238 posts of Technician Grade I Signal (183 posts) and Technician Grade III (6055 posts). The online application window was open from 28 June 2025 to 07 August 2025.

The Computer Based Test (CBT) was conducted from 06 to 13 March 2026. The CBT results for Technician Grade I were declared on 21 May 2026, and for Technician Grade III on 29 May 2026. Shortlisted candidates are now being called for Document Verification and Medical Examination, with DV Admit Cards released on 16 June 2026.

This permanent recruitment page will carry every official update from notification through final result and joining, without changing the URL.
