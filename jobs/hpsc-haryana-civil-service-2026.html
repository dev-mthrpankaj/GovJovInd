About This Recruitment

The Haryana Public Service Commission (HPSC) has released the Haryana Civil Service (HCS) Mains Result 2026 for a total of 102 posts. The recruitment drive offers opportunities across various departments in the Haryana state government.

The selection process consists of a Preliminary examination, a Mains examination, and an interview. Candidates who qualify the Prelims are eligible to appear for the Mains examination. The final merit list will be prepared based on the performance in the Mains and Interview stages.

This permanent page tracks every stage of the HPSC HCS 2026 recruitment, from the initial notification through to the final result, ensuring candidates have a single reliable source for all official updates.
