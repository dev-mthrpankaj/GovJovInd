Complete context

About This Recruitment

HPSC Haryana Civil Service (HCS) & Allied Services Examination 2026 is a Haryana Public Service Commission recruitment drive to fill 102 posts across HCS (Executive Branch) and other allied services including DSP, ETO, DFSC, ARCS, DFSO, AETO, Traffic Manager, Tehsildar and Naib Tehsildar. The official notification (Advt. No. 22/2026) was published on 30 January 2026, and the online application window remained open from 06 February 2026 to 26 February 2026.

The Preliminary Examination was conducted on 26 April 2026. The Prelims result was declared on 04 May 2026, and qualified candidates appeared for the Mains Examination held from 27 to 29 June 2026. The Mains Admit Card was released on 22 June 2026.

The Mains Result was declared on 17 September 2026, and the Interview Schedule was released on 18 September 2026. Interviews will be conducted from 03 to 08 October 2026 at the HPSC Office, Sector-4, Panchkula. The Score Card / Marks for interview-qualified candidates was activated on 08 October 2026.

Basic eligibility is a Bachelor's Degree in any stream from a recognised university. The age limit is 18 to 42 years as on 01 January 2026 (18 to 27 years for DSP post), with relaxation for reserved categories as per Haryana Government rules.

What to do at the current stage

What Candidates Should Do Now

  1. Download the Mains Result PDF from the official HPSC website using the link in Important Links.
  2. Check your roll number in the Mains Result to confirm your interview eligibility.
  3. Download your Score Card / Marks from the official HPSC portal.
  4. If you have qualified for the interview, check the Interview Schedule for your exact date and reporting time.
  5. Carry all original documents for verification during the interview.
  6. Reach the HPSC Office, Sector-4, Panchkula at least 30 minutes before your reporting time.

Original Application Process

Preserved for recruitment history and reference after the application stage closes.

  1. Visit the official HPSC website at hpsc.gov.in.
  2. Read Advt. No. 22/2026 carefully.
  3. Register on the HPSC portal with valid email ID and mobile number.
  4. Fill the online application form with accurate personal and educational details.
  5. Upload photograph, signature and required documents.
  6. Pay the applicable examination fee online.
  7. Submit the form and save the confirmation page for future reference.
