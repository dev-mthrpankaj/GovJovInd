Complete context

About This Recruitment

The Haryana Public Service Commission (HPSC) has released the Haryana Civil Service (HCS) Mains Result 2026 for a total of 102 posts. The recruitment drive offers opportunities across various departments in the Haryana state government.

The selection process consists of a Preliminary examination, a Mains examination, and an interview. Candidates who qualify the Prelims are eligible to appear for the Mains examination. The final merit list will be prepared based on the performance in the Mains and Interview stages.

This permanent page tracks every stage of the HPSC HCS 2026 recruitment, from the initial notification through to the final result, ensuring candidates have a single reliable source for all official updates.

What to do at the current stage

How to Check Mains Result

  1. Open the official HPSC website from the Important Links section.
  2. Click on the Mains Result link provided on the homepage or in the notification section.
  3. The result PDF will open in a new tab.
  4. Search for your roll number in the PDF using Ctrl+F.
  5. Download and save the result PDF for future reference.
  6. Candidates who qualify will be eligible for the interview stage.

Original Application Process

Preserved for recruitment history and reference after the application stage closes.

  1. Visit the official HPSC recruitment website.
  2. Read the HCS 2026 notification carefully.
  3. Open the application link from Important Links.
  4. Register and fill the online application form.
  5. Upload required documents (photo, signature, certificates).
  6. Pay the applicable fee online.
  7. Submit and save the confirmation page.
