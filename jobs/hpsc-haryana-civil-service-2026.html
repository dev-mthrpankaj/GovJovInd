Complete context

About This Recruitment

Haryana Public Service Commission (HPSC) released the notification for the Haryana Civil Services (HCS) Examination 2026 to fill 102 Civil Services posts. The advertisement number is 22/2026. The online application window remained open from 06 February 2026 to 26 February 2026.

The Preliminary Examination was conducted on 26 April 2026, and the result was declared on 03 May 2026. After a revised preliminary result was published on 01 June 2026, the Mains Examination was held from 27-29 June 2026. The Mains result was declared on 17 September 2026, leading to the Interview stage conducted from 03-08 October 2026.

The Final Result was declared on 17 October 2026. Candidates can download the final merit list PDF and check their individual interview marks. The selection process includes the Preliminary Exam, Mains Exam, Interview, Document Verification, and Medical Examination.

Basic eligibility requires a Bachelor's degree from any recognised university in India. The age limit is 18 to 42 years for most posts and 18 to 27 years for the DSP post, as on 01 January 2026, with relaxation for reserved categories as per HPSC rules.

What to do at the current stage

How to Check HPSC HCS Final Result 2026

  1. Visit the official HPSC website at hpsc.gov.in.
  2. Open the 'Final Result / Notices' section.
  3. Click on the 'HCS (Executive Branch) Exam Final Result 2026' link.
  4. The Final Result will open in PDF format.
  5. Use Ctrl+F to search for your roll number.
  6. Download and save/print the PDF for future reference.

Original Application Process

Preserved for recruitment history and reference after the application stage closes.

  1. Visit the official HPSC website at hpsc.gov.in.
  2. Open the HCS Examination 2026 application portal.
  3. Register with a valid email ID and mobile number.
  4. Fill the online application form with accurate personal and educational details.
  5. Upload photograph, signature and required documents.
  6. Pay the applicable application fee online.
  7. Submit the form and save the confirmation page for future reference.
