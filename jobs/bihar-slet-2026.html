Complete context

About This Recruitment

Bihar School Examination Board (BSEB) conducts the School Librarian Eligibility Test (SLET) to determine eligibility for librarian posts in government schools across Bihar. The 2026 notification (Notice P.R. 221/2026) was released on 28 September 2026, and online applications were accepted from 01 October 2026 to 31 October 2026 (extended).

The Computer Based Test (CBT) was originally scheduled for 22 November 2026 but has been rescheduled to 23 November 2026 due to a timetable overlap with other state-level eligibility tests. The admit card will be available for download from 17 November 2026 on the official SLET registration portal, sletregistration.cbrt.co.in.

Eligibility requires a Bachelor’s degree in Library Science (B.Lib / B.Lib.I.Sc / BLIS) from a recognised university. The age limit is 37 years for male candidates and 40 years for female candidates as on 01 August 2026, with relaxation for reserved categories as per rules.

The examination is a Computer Based Test (CBT) with 150 multiple-choice questions (100 from Library Science and 50 from General Knowledge), to be completed in 150 minutes. There is no negative marking. This page will be updated with the admit card link, result, cut-off and final merit list as each stage is announced.

What to do at the current stage

How to Download Bihar SLET Admit Card

  1. Visit the official SLET registration portal at sletregistration.cbrt.co.in.
  2. Click on the link for Bihar School Librarian Eligibility Test 2026 Admit Card.
  3. Enter your application number, password or date of birth.
  4. Download and print the admit card on A4 paper.
  5. Check the exam date, reporting time and centre address carefully.
  6. Carry the admit card along with a valid photo ID to the exam centre.

Original Application Process

Preserved for recruitment history and reference after the application stage closes.

  1. Visit the official SLET registration portal at sletregistration.cbrt.co.in.
  2. Read the official notification (P.R. 221/2026) carefully.
  3. Register with a valid email ID and mobile number.
  4. Fill the online application form with accurate personal and educational details.
  5. Upload photograph, signature and required documents.
  6. Pay the applicable examination fee online.
  7. Submit the form and save the confirmation page for future reference.
