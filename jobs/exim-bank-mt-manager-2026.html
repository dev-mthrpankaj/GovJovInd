Complete context

About This Recruitment

Export-Import Bank of India (India Exim Bank) has released the recruitment notification for 120 Management Trainee (MT) and Manager (M) Grade / Scale MM II posts. The official notification (Advt No. HRM/ MT/M/2026-27/05) was published recently.

Eligible candidates can apply online through the official Exim Bank careers portal from 27 November 2026. The last date to submit online applications and pay the examination fee is 11 December 2026. The computer-based test (CBT) for the selection process is tentatively scheduled for December 2026.

Candidates must hold a Bachelor's Degree with a minimum of 60% marks to be eligible. For certain specialisations, a Master's degree in the relevant subject is required. The minimum age limit is 21 years for MT and 24 years for Manager, while the maximum age limit is 28 years for MT and 30 years for Manager as on 01 November 2026.
