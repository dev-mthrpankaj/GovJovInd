Complete context

About This Recruitment

Rajasthan Public Service Commission (RPSC) published Advertisement No. 02/2026-27 on 27 May 2026 for the Rajasthan State and Subordinate Services Combined Competitive Examination (RAS) 2026 to fill 607 vacancies, comprising 192 Rajasthan State Service posts and 415 Rajasthan Subordinate Service posts.

The online application window remained open from 04 June 2026 to 03 July 2026, followed by an online application correction and withdrawal window from 24 September 2026 to 30 September 2026. The Preliminary Examination is scheduled for 06 December 2026, while the Mains Examination is scheduled for 01–02 May 2027.

Eligibility requires a Bachelor's degree in any stream from a recognised university along with working knowledge of Hindi written in Devanagari script and knowledge of Rajasthani culture. The prescribed age limit is 21 to 40 years as on 01 January 2027, with category-wise age relaxation as per Rajasthan government rules.

Selection is conducted in three stages: Preliminary Examination (objective screening test), Mains Examination (descriptive), and Personality Test / Interview. This permanent lifecycle page will continue to carry the admit card, answer key, result, cut-off, and final merit list updates.

What to do at the current stage

What Candidates Should Do for Prelims Exam

  1. Visit the official RPSC news section or Rajasthan SSO portal for exam city intimation and Prelims Admit Card release.
  2. Keep your SSO ID, password, and application number ready to download the e-admit card once activated.
  3. Verify your name, roll number, exam centre address, reporting time, and exam day instructions on the admit card.
  4. Carry a printed copy of the Prelims Admit Card, a valid original photo ID, and a recent passport-size photograph to the centre.
  5. Reach the examination centre well before the reporting time mentioned on the admit card.

Original Application Process

Preserved for recruitment history and reference after the application stage closes.

  1. Visit the official Rajasthan SSO portal at sso.rajasthan.gov.in and log in using your SSO ID.
  2. Complete One Time Registration (OTR) if applying for the first time.
  3. Open the Recruitment Portal and select RPSC RAS Recruitment 2026 (Advt. No. 02/2026-27).
  4. Fill in personal, educational, category, and communication details carefully.
  5. Upload scanned passport-size photograph, signature, and required documents.
  6. Pay the applicable examination fee through online payment mode.
  7. Submit the application form and print the confirmation page for future reference.
