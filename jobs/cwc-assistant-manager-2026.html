Complete context

About This Recruitment

Central Warehousing Corporation (CWC) has published Advertisement No. CWC/I-Manpower/DR/Rectt/2026/01 for the recruitment of Assistant Manager and Junior Executive posts across multiple disciplines. A total of 327 vacancies are available, covering General, Technical, Finance, Civil, Electrical and Mechanical streams.

The online application window opened on 17 October 2026 and will close on 15 November 2026. Candidates must complete registration, form filling, document upload and fee payment within this period. The last date for fee payment is also 15 November 2026.

The selection process for Assistant Manager posts includes an online examination, document verification and interview. For Junior Executive posts, the selection process involves an online examination and document verification. The exam date will be announced later on the official CWC careers portal.

Eligibility varies by post: Assistant Manager (General) requires a two-year postgraduate degree or diploma, while technical and engineering posts require relevant B.E./B.Tech or B.Sc. degrees. The age limit is 18 to 28 years as on 15 November 2026, with relaxation for reserved categories as per government rules.
