About This Recruitment

CTET September 2026 is conducted by the Central Board of Secondary Education (CBSE) for candidates aspiring to become teachers in Central Government schools such as KVS, NVS, and other schools affiliated with CBSE. The exam is held for two levels — Primary (Class I to V) and Junior (Class VI to VIII).

The September 2026 session notification was released with applications opening from 11 May 2026. After the initial application window closed on 10 June 2026, CBSE provided a correction window from 7 to 10 September 2026 and also re-opened the application form until 1 September 2026 for fresh candidates.

The exam is scheduled for 12–13 December 2026. This permanent page will carry every later update — admit card, exam city intimation, answer key, objection window, result — without changing the URL.
