Complete context

About This Recruitment

The Central Board of Secondary Education (CBSE) conducts the Central Teacher Eligibility Test (CTET) December 2026 (22nd Edition) as a national-level eligibility examination for candidates seeking teaching appointments in Classes 1 to 8 in central government schools such as Kendriya Vidyalayas (KVS), Navodaya Vidyalayas (NVS), and other recognised institutions across India. The examination is held in two levels: Paper 1 for Primary Teachers (Classes 1 to 5) and Paper 2 for Elementary Teachers (Classes 6 to 8).

Originally notified on 11 May 2026 as the September 2026 session, the online application window remained open until 10 June 2026. CBSE subsequently re-opened the online application form until 01 September 2026 and provided a correction window from 07 September 2026 to 10 September 2026. On 14 September 2026, CBSE issued an official public notice scheduling the 22nd Edition examination for 12 December 2026 and 13 December 2026.

On 27 November 2026, CBSE activated the CTET December 2026 Exam City Intimation link on ctet.nic.in so registered candidates can verify their allotted examination city and exam date in advance. The official CTET Admit Card containing the exact examination centre address, roll number, and shift timings will be released two days before the examination date.

CTET is an objective multiple-choice examination carrying 150 marks per paper with no negative marking. General category candidates require 60% marks (90 out of 150) to qualify, while reserved category candidates (SC, ST, OBC, and PwBD) may receive a 5% relaxation (55% or 82 marks) as per extant reservation norms. The CTET qualifying certificate remains valid for a lifetime with no upper age limit or restriction on the number of attempts.
