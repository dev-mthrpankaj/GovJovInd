Complete context

About This Recruitment

IBPS Hindi Officer (Grade E) Recruitment 2026 is a direct recruitment drive by the Institute of Banking Personnel Selection (IBPS) for the post of Hindi Officer at its Mumbai headquarters. The official notification was released on 23 September 2026 under Advertisement No. IBPS/2026-27/04, and the online application window remained open from 23 September 2026 to 6 October 2026.

The selection process includes an Online Examination, Skill Test & Item Writing Exercise, Group Exercises, and Personal Interview. The online examination will be held on 15 October 2026, and the admit card was released on 8 October 2026. The exam will consist of 200 questions from Reasoning, English Language, General Awareness, and Hindi Language, carrying a total of 200 marks with a composite time of 140 minutes.

The pay scale for the Hindi Officer post is Rs.44,900 – Rs.91,070 (basic pay Rs.44,900), with an approximate annual CTC of Rs.17.39 lakh including benefits such as PF, medical benefits, medi-claim, telephone and newspaper reimbursement, canteen subsidy, performance-linked incentive, gratuity, and superannuation. Selected candidates must execute a service bond of Rs.2 lakh and serve IBPS for a minimum of 3 years.

Basic eligibility is a Master's Degree in Hindi with English as a major/elective subject at graduation, or Master's Degree in English with Hindi as a major/elective subject at graduation, or other prescribed combinations. The age limit is 23 to 30 years as on 1 September 2026, with relaxation for reserved categories as per IBPS rules. One year of translation experience and computer proficiency are desirable.

Eligibility Criteria

Age Limit

23 to 30 years as on 1 September 2026 (born between 2 September 1996 and 1 September 2003).

Age relaxation applicable for SC, ST, OBC, PwBD and other reserved categories as per IBPS rules.

Education

Master's Degree from a recognised university in Hindi with English as a major/elective subject at Graduation, OR Master's Degree in English with Hindi as a major/elective subject at Graduation, OR Master's Degree in any other subject with Hindi as major/elective and English as medium at degree level, OR Master's Degree in any other subject with English as major/elective and Hindi as medium at degree level.

The qualification must be from a recognised university. Degrees obtained through correspondence, distance education or open university system will not be accepted.

Nationality / Other

Candidates must have good knowledge of computer operations, particularly MS Office. One year of translation experience is desirable. Indian citizens fulfilling the post-specific eligibility conditions may apply.

Refer to official notification for detailed conditions.
