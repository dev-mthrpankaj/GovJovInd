About This Recruitment

Staff Selection Commission (SSC) has released the Combined Graduate Level Examination 2026 notification for recruitment to Group B and Group C posts in various central government ministries and departments. The Tier-I computer-based examination is scheduled from 30 September to 30 October 2026 across multiple shifts.

The online application window was open from 21 May to 22 June 2026. With over 12,000 vacancies and approximately 29 lakh registered candidates, this is one of the largest graduate-level recruitment drives conducted by SSC. The selection process includes Tier-I, Tier-II, and document verification stages.

Candidates who have successfully submitted their applications should now prepare for the Tier-I exam and keep track of city intimation slip and admit card release updates on the official SSC website.
