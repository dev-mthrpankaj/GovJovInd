Complete context

About This Recruitment

Staff Selection Commission (SSC) has released the Combined Graduate Level Examination 2026 notification for recruitment to Group B and Group C posts in various central government ministries and departments. The Tier-I computer-based examination is scheduled from 30 September to 30 October 2026 across multiple shifts.

The online application window was open from 21 May to 22 June 2026. With over 12,000 vacancies and approximately 29 lakh registered candidates, this is one of the largest graduate-level recruitment drives conducted by SSC. The selection process includes Tier-I, Tier-II, and document verification stages.

Candidates who have successfully submitted their applications should now prepare for the Tier-I exam and keep track of city intimation slip and admit card release updates on the official SSC website.

Eligibility Criteria

Age Limit

18 to 32 years as on 01 August 2026. Post-wise age limits may vary.

Age relaxation applicable for SC/ST/OBC/EWS/PwBD/Ex-Servicemen as per government rules.

Education

Bachelor's Degree from a recognized university. For JSO and Statistical Investigator posts, specific subject requirements apply.

Candidates must have obtained the degree before the cut-off date mentioned in the official notification.

Nationality / Other

Indian citizens fulfilling the post-specific eligibility conditions. Candidates must possess valid registration on the SSC portal.

Refer to official notification for detailed conditions.

What to do at the current stage

Exam Date Released – What Candidates Should Do Now

  1. Your Tier-I exam will be conducted between 30 September and 30 October 2026.
  2. The exam city intimation slip is expected to be released around 22 September 2026 on the official SSC website.
  3. The admit card will be available for download approximately 3 days before your allotted exam date.
  4. Check your registration details and ensure your photo and signature are valid as per SSC norms.
  5. Start your revision with focus on General Intelligence & Reasoning, General Awareness, Quantitative Aptitude, and English Comprehension.
  6. Keep your registration number, password, and date of birth ready for downloading city intimation and admit card.
  7. Monitor the official SSC website regularly for region-wise admit card links.

Original Application Process

Preserved for recruitment history and reference after the application stage closes.

  1. Visit the official SSC website at ssc.gov.in.
  2. Register for One-Time Registration (OTR) if not already done.
  3. Login and complete the CGL 2026 application form.
  4. Upload photograph and signature as per specifications.
  5. Pay the applicable examination fee.
  6. Review all details and submit the application.
  7. Save the confirmation page for future reference.
