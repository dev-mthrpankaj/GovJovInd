Complete context

About This Recruitment

The Staff Selection Commission (SSC) conducts the Combined Graduate Level Examination (CGL) every year to recruit candidates for various Group B and Group C posts in central government ministries, departments, and organisations. The SSC CGL 2026 notification was released on 21 May 2026, announcing 12,256 tentative vacancies across multiple posts.

The online application window remained open from 21 May 2026 to 22 June 2026, with the fee payment deadline on 23 June 2026. The application correction window was available from 29 June to 01 July 2026. The Tier-I Computer Based Examination was conducted from 30 September to 28 October 2026 at centres across the country.

The tentative answer key and candidate response sheets were released on 04 November 2026. Candidates can raise objections against the answer key from 04 November 2026 (09:00 PM) to 07 November 2026 (09:00 PM) by paying Rs.50 per question challenged. The Tier-II examination is tentatively scheduled for December 2026.

Eligibility for most posts requires a Bachelor's degree in any stream from a recognised university. The age limit is 18 to 32 years as on 01 August 2026, with relaxation for reserved categories as per SSC rules. The selection process includes Tier-I CBT, Tier-II CBT, Tier-III descriptive exam, Tier-IV skill test, and document verification.

What to do at the current stage

How to Raise Objection Against Answer Key

  1. Open the official SSC website and log in with your Registration Number and Password.
  2. Navigate to the Answer Key / Objection Management System link.
  3. Select the question(s) you want to challenge and upload supporting documents if required.
  4. Pay the objection fee of Rs.50 per question through the online payment gateway.
  5. Submit the objection and save the acknowledgement for future reference.

Original Application Process

Preserved for recruitment history and reference after the application stage closes.

  1. Visit the official SSC website at ssc.gov.in.
  2. Read the official CGL 2026 notification carefully.
  3. Register on the SSC portal with a valid email ID and mobile number.
  4. Fill the online application form with accurate personal and educational details.
  5. Upload photograph, signature and required documents.
  6. Pay the applicable examination fee online.
  7. Submit the form and save the confirmation page for future reference.
