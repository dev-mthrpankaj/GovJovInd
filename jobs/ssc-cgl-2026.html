About SSC CGL Recruitment 2026

Staff Selection Commission (SSC) has released the Combined Graduate Level (CGL) 2026 notification for 10,731 vacancies (revised from the originally announced 12,256) across various Group B and C posts in central government ministries, departments and organisations. This is one of India's largest graduate-level recruitment drives.

The online application process was conducted from 19 May 2026 with a re-open window from 23 June to 25 June 2026. The correction window was active from 1 July to 3 July 2026. The Tier-1 admit card has now been released, and the Tier-1 examination is scheduled from 30 September to 30 October 2026. Tier-2 examination is expected in December 2026.

The selection process includes Tier-1 computer-based examination, Tier-2 examination, and document verification. Candidates must carry the printed admit card along with a valid photo ID to the examination centre. This page will continue to carry every later SSC CGL stage — answer key, result, cut-off, DV and final merit list — on the same permanent URL.

Eligibility Criteria

Age Limit

18 to 32 years as on 1 August 2026 (post-wise).

Education

Nationality
