Complete context

About This Recruitment

Staff Selection Commission (SSC) has released the Combined Graduate Level Examination 2026 notification for recruitment to Group B and Group C posts. This permanent page contains the recruitment history, eligibility, vacancy, examination schedule and subsequent lifecycle updates.

The application window was initially scheduled from 21 May to 22 June 2026. SSC subsequently reopened the application window for two additional days, revising the application closing date to 25 June 2026, the online fee-payment deadline to 26 June 2026, and the correction window to 1–3 July 2026.

The Tier-I examination is scheduled from 30 September to 30 October 2026. SSC has now made the examination city details available through its designated login module. The Admission Certificate is tentatively available 2–3 days before the respective examination date.

Candidates using the Own Scribe facility should follow the fresh scribe-registration and Scribe OTR-mapping requirements and observe the 24 September 2026, 11:00 PM deadline.
