About This Recruitment

SBI Probationary Officer Recruitment 2026 is being conducted by the State Bank of India under Advertisement No. CRPD/PO/2026-27/09 for 1,500 vacancies. Online applications were accepted from 18 June to 8 July 2026, and the recruitment has now completed the Main Examination phase.

The preliminary examination was held on 1 and 2 August 2026, followed by the preliminary result on 28 August 2026 and preliminary marks on 2 September 2026. The Main Examination was conducted on 12 September 2026, and SBI has now released the Main Examination result on 15 October 2026.

This permanent page keeps the full SBI PO 2026 lifecycle together — from notification and application through prelims, mains, Phase III and final result — so candidates can return to the same URL for later official updates.

Eligibility Criteria

Age Limit

21 to 30 years as on 1 April 2026 (born between 2 April 1996 and 1 April 2005, both dates inclusive).

Upper-age relaxation: OBC (NCL) 3 years; SC/ST 5 years; PwBD 10/13/15 years by category; other relaxations as specified in the official advertisement.

Education

Graduation in any discipline from a recognised university or an equivalent qualification recognised by the Central Government. Final-year/semester candidates may apply subject to the notification conditions.

Candidates should verify the prescribed proof-of-qualification date and detailed conditions in the official advertisement.

Nationality / Other

Eligible Indian citizens may apply. Selected candidates may be posted anywhere in India.

Refer to official notification for detailed conditions.

How to Check & Download SBI PO Mains Result 2026

  1. Open the SBI PO Main Examination result link from Important Links.
  2. The result PDF (WTRESULT) will open on the official SBI website with a list of roll numbers.
  3. Search your roll number in the PDF using the browser search option (Ctrl + F).
  4. Download and save the PDF for future reference.
  5. Shortlisted candidates will be called for Phase III — Psychometric Test, Group Exercise & Interview.
  6. Keep the authenticated Preliminary and Main Examination admit cards and your photo ID ready for the next stage.
