Complete context

About This Recruitment

State Bank of India (SBI) invited online applications under Advertisement No. CRPD/PO/2026-27/09 for 1,500 Probationary Officer (PO) vacancies, comprising 1,446 regular posts and 54 backlog posts. The online application window remained open from 18 June 2026 to 08 July 2026.

The Preliminary Examination was held on 02 August 2026 and its result was declared on 28 August 2026. Shortlisted candidates appeared for the Mains Examination on 12 September 2026, and the Mains Result was published on 15 October 2026.

SBI released the PO Interview Letter 2026 on 21 November 2026 on the official IBPS/SBI portal. Candidates shortlisted for Phase III must download their Interview Call Letter using their Registration Number or Roll Number and Password or Date of Birth before the Interview begins on 01 December 2026.

Candidates who have passed or are appearing in the final year of a Bachelor's Degree in any discipline from a recognised university and are aged 21 to 30 years as on 01 April 2026 were eligible to apply. Selection is based on Preliminary Exam, Mains Exam, Group Exercise, and Interview.

What to do at the current stage

How to Download SBI PO Interview Letter 2026

  1. Open the official SBI PO Interview Letter download link from the Important Links section.
  2. Enter your Registration Number or Roll Number and Password or Date of Birth.
  3. Enter the captcha code displayed on the screen and click Login.
  4. Verify your name, roll number, interview date, reporting time, and venue address.
  5. Download and print the Interview Call Letter for the Group Exercise and Interview.
  6. Carry the printed call letter, valid original photo ID, and all required original certificates.

Original Application Process

Preserved for recruitment history and reference after the application stage closes.

  1. Visit the official State Bank of India (SBI) careers portal at sbi.co.in.
  2. Read the detailed Advt. No. CRPD/PO/2026-27/09 notification carefully.
  3. Complete online registration using a valid email ID and mobile number.
  4. Fill in personal, academic, and category details in the online application form.
  5. Upload scanned photograph, signature, and required documents.
  6. Pay the application fee online (Rs.750 for General/OBC/EWS; Nil for SC/ST/PH).
  7. Submit the application form and print the confirmation page for records.

Quick answers

Frequently Asked Questions

State Bank of India (SBI) released the SBI PO Interview Letter 2026 on 21 November 2026. Shortlisted Mains candidates can download the call letter using their Registration Number or Roll Number and Password or Date of Birth.

The SBI PO 2026 Group Exercise and Interview round begins on 01 December 2026. Candidates can verify their exact interview date, reporting time, and venue address on the downloaded Interview Call Letter.

Candidates can download the SBI PO Mains Result 2026 PDF from the official SBI website or the Important Links section above. The Mains Result was declared on 15 October 2026 for the exam held on 12 September 2026.

The SBI PO call letter contains the candidate's name, roll number, registration number, exam or interview date, reporting time, venue address, and essential instructions to follow at the centre.

Candidates must carry a printed copy of the SBI PO Interview Call Letter 2026, a valid original photo ID proof, recent passport-size photographs, and original educational and category certificates as specified in the call letter.

State Bank of India (SBI) announced 1,500 Probationary Officer (PO) vacancies under Advt. No. CRPD/PO/2026-27/09, including 1,446 regular posts and 54 backlog posts across General, OBC, EWS, SC, and ST categories.

Candidates must have passed or be appearing in the final year of a Bachelor's Degree in any discipline from a recognised university in India, subject to the conditions mentioned in the official SBI notification.

The age limit for SBI PO Recruitment 2026 is 21 to 30 years as on 01 April 2026. Upper age relaxation is provided to reserved category candidates in accordance with State Bank of India (SBI) rules.

The application fee for SBI PO 2026 was Rs.750 for General, OBC, and EWS candidates, while SC, ST, and PH candidates were exempted with Nil fee. Online applications closed on 08 July 2026.

The SBI Probationary Officer (PO) 2026 selection process consists of four stages: Preliminary Examination, Mains Examination, Group Exercise, and Interview, followed by the final merit list.

The official website of State Bank of India (SBI) is https://sbi.co.in/. All official recruitment notifications, call letters, results, and final merit lists are published on the SBI careers portal.
