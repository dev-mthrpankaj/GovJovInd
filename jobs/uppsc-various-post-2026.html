Complete context

About This Recruitment

Uttar Pradesh Public Service Commission (UPPSC) has released Advertisement No. A-5/E-1/2026 for the recruitment of 25 posts across three distinct profiles: Assistant Architect Planner (8 posts), Sports Officer (16 posts), and Programmer Grade-2 (1 post). The notification was published on 04 November 2026, and the online application process commenced on the same day.

The application window is scheduled to close on 04 December 2026, with the fee payment deadline also set for 04 December 2026. Candidates who submit their forms within the stipulated time will have the opportunity to make corrections to their application until 11 December 2026. The preliminary examination date will be announced later on the official UPPSC portal.

Eligibility varies by post. Assistant Architect Planner requires a Post Graduate Degree in Town/City/Urban Planning or a Bachelor of Planning/B.Tech Planning with two years of experience. Sports Officer requires a Bachelor’s degree in any stream with a recognized coaching diploma and two years of coaching experience. Programmer Grade-2 requires a Bachelor’s degree with a PG Diploma in Computer Science or ‘A’ Level certificate and knowledge of software development.

The age limit for all posts is 21 to 40 years as on 01 July 2026, with upper age relaxation applicable for SC, ST, OBC, PwBD, Ex-Servicemen, and other reserved categories as per Uttar Pradesh government rules. The application fee is Rs.225 for General/OBC/EWS candidates, Rs.105 for SC/ST candidates, and Rs.25 for PH candidates.
