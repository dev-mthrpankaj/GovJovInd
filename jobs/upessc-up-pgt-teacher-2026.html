Complete context

About This Recruitment

Uttar Pradesh Education Service Selection Commission (UPESSC) has released the recruitment notification for the post of Post Graduate Teacher (PGT) for 2607 vacancies. The online application process started on 18 September 2026 and the last date to apply was 17 October 2026. The application correction window is now open from 19 October to 21 October 2026.

The selection process consists of a written examination scheduled for 15-16 December 2026. Candidates must possess a Master's Degree in the relevant subject with the required teaching qualification (B.Ed. or equivalent) as per the official notification. The age limit is 62 years as on 01 July 2026, with relaxations applicable for reserved categories.

What to do at the current stage

How to Edit UPESSC UP PGT Application Form

  1. Open the official UPESSC application portal from the Important Links section.
  2. Log in using your registration number and password or date of birth.
  3. Navigate to the correction window and carefully review your submitted details.
  4. Make the necessary corrections in personal, educational, or category fields as permitted.
  5. Pay any additional fee if applicable for the changes made.
  6. Submit the corrected application and download the updated confirmation page.

Original Application Process

Preserved for recruitment history and reference after the application stage closes.

  1. Visit the official UPESSC website at upessc.up.gov.in.
  2. Read the official notification (Advt No. 06/2026) carefully.
  3. Register on the application portal with valid email ID and mobile number.
  4. Fill the online application form with accurate personal and educational details.
  5. Upload photograph, signature and required documents.
  6. Pay the applicable examination fee online.
  7. Submit the form and save the confirmation page for future reference.
