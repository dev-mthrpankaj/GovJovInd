Complete context

About This Recruitment

Bihar Secondary Teacher Eligibility Test (STET) 2026 is conducted by the Bihar School Examination Board (BSEB) to determine the eligibility of candidates for teaching positions in secondary (Classes 9-10) and higher secondary (Classes 11-12) schools across Bihar. The official notification was released on 14 August 2026 under Advertisement No. P.R. 183/2026.

The online application window was initially open from 17 August to 31 August 2026, later extended to 22 September 2026. The Computer Based Test (CBT) is scheduled to be conducted from 25 October to 20 November 2026 across various exam centres in Bihar.

The admit card for the examination was released on 18 October 2026. Candidates can download their admit card from the official portal at stetregistration.cbrt.co.in using their user ID and password. The admit card contains crucial details such as exam date, time, and centre address.

Basic eligibility for Paper 1 (Classes 9-10) is a graduation degree in the relevant subject with 50% marks and a B.Ed. qualification. For Paper 2 (Classes 11-12), candidates need a post-graduation degree in the relevant subject with 50% marks and a B.Ed. Age limit is 21 to 37 years for unreserved male candidates, with relaxation for female, OBC, and SC/ST candidates as per rules.

Eligibility Criteria

Age Limit

21 to 37 years for Unreserved Male; 40 years for Female (UR, BC, EBC) and BC/EBC Male; 42 years for SC/ST (Male & Female) as on 01 August 2026.

Age relaxation applicable for SC, ST, OBC, PwBD, Ex-Servicemen and other reserved categories as per BSEB rules.

Education

Paper 1 (Classes 9-10): Graduation in relevant subject with 50% marks and B.Ed. Paper 2 (Classes 11-12): Post-Graduation in relevant subject with 50% marks and B.Ed.

5% relaxation in educational qualification for SC, ST, EBC, BC and disabled candidates.

Nationality / Other

Indian citizens fulfilling the post-specific eligibility conditions may apply. Refer to the official notification for complete details.

What to do at the current stage

How to Download Bihar STET Admit Card

  1. Visit the official Bihar STET registration portal at stetregistration.cbrt.co.in.
  2. Click on the admit card download link for STET 2026.
  3. Enter your user ID and password to log in.
  4. Download the admit card PDF for your respective paper (Paper 1 or Paper 2).
  5. Check your name, roll number, exam date, time and centre details carefully.
  6. Take a clear colour printout and carry it to the exam centre along with a valid photo ID.

Original Application Process

Preserved for recruitment history and reference after the application stage closes.

  1. Visit the official BSEB STET website at bsebstet.org or stetregistration.cbrt.co.in.
  2. Read the official notification carefully.
  3. Register with valid email ID and mobile number.
  4. Fill the online application form with accurate personal and educational details.
  5. Upload photograph, signature and required documents.
  6. Pay the applicable examination fee online.
  7. Submit the form and save the confirmation page for future reference.
