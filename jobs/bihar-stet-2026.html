About This Recruitment

Bihar School Examination Board (BSEB) has released the notification for Bihar State Teacher Eligibility Test (STET) 2026. This is a state-level eligibility test for candidates aspiring to become teachers in Bihar. The online application window is open from 17 August 2026 to 22 September 2026.

This page is a permanent lifecycle page for Bihar STET 2026. It will be updated with every official stage—exam date, admit card, answer key, result, and final merit list—on the same URL.

There is no fixed number of posts in this recruitment as it is an eligibility test. Candidates who qualify STET become eligible to apply for teacher recruitment in Bihar. The exam is expected to be held in November 2026.

Eligibility Criteria

Age Limit

21 to 37 years for Male & 21 to 40 years for Female as on 01 August 2026.

Age relaxation applicable as per BSEB rules.

Education

Paper I: Graduation in related subject with 50% marks and B.Ed passed OR Post Graduation in related subject and B.Ed passed OR Graduation/PG with minimum 45% marks (as per NCTE norms) with B.Ed OR 4-year BA BEd / BSc BEd. Paper II: Post Graduation in related subject with 50% marks and B.Ed / BA BEd / BSc BEd passed OR PG with minimum 45% marks (as per NCTE norms) with B.Ed OR PG with 55% marks and 3-year B.Ed MEd course.

For subject-wise more eligibility kindly read the official notification.

Nationality / Other

Indian citizens fulfilling the post-specific eligibility conditions may apply.

Refer to official notification for detailed conditions.

What to do at the current stage

How to Apply

  1. Visit the official website of Bihar School Examination Board (https://biharboardonline.com/).
  2. Click on the Bihar State Teacher Eligibility Test STET Apply Online link.
  3. Register using a valid email ID and mobile number.
  4. Login using registration ID and password.
  5. Fill the application form with personal, educational, and category details.
  6. Upload scanned documents (photo, signature, etc.).
  7. Pay the application fee through online mode.
  8. Submit the form and download/print the confirmation page.
