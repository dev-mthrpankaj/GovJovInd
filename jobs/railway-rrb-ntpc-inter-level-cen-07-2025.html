Complete context

About This Recruitment

Railway Recruitment Board (RRB) is conducting the Railway RRB NTPC 10+2 Level UG Recruitment (CEN 07/2025) to fill 3,058 Under Graduate non-technical vacancies across 21 regional Railway Recruitment Boards. The recruitment covers four posts: Commercial Cum Ticket Clerk (2,424 posts), Accounts Clerk Cum Typist (394 posts), Junior Clerk Cum Typist (163 posts), and Trains Clerk (77 posts).

The detailed CEN 07/2025 notification was released on 27 October 2025, and online applications remained open from 28 October 2025 to 04 December 2025. The 1st Stage Computer Based Test (CBT 1) was held in phases on 07–09 May 2026, 13–14 June 2026, and 16–21 June 2026, followed by the CBT 1 answer key on 27 June 2026 and CBT 1 result on 24 August 2026. The 2nd Stage CBT (CBT 2) was conducted on 17 September 2026, and zone-wise CBT 2 results with CBTST shortlists were declared on 30 October 2026.

On 24 November 2026, Railway Recruitment Board (RRB) released the CBTST Admit Card 2026 for the Computer Based Typing Skill Test scheduled on 27 November 2026. Shortlisted candidates for Accounts Clerk Cum Typist and Junior Clerk Cum Typist must download their e-call letter and carry a printed copy along with a valid original government photo ID to the test centre.

Candidates must have passed 10+2 (Intermediate) from a recognised board, with computer typing proficiency in English or Hindi required for typist posts. The age limit is 18 to 30 years as on 01 January 2026, with category-wise upper age relaxation as per RRB rules. Subsequent stages include Document Verification, post-specific Medical Examination, and Final Empanelment.
