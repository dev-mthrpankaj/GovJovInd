Complete context

About This Recruitment

RRB NTPC Undergraduate (CEN 07/2025) is a Railway Recruitment Board recruitment drive to fill 3,058 posts of Commercial cum Ticket Clerk (2,424), Accounts Clerk cum Typist (394), Junior Clerk cum Typist (163), and Trains Clerk (77) across various Railway Zones. The official notification was published in October 2025, and the online application window remained open from 28 October 2025 to 04 December 2025.

The Computer-Based Test (CBT-I) was conducted in multiple phases from 07 May to 21 June 2026. The CBT-I result was declared on 24 August 2026. The CBT-II exam was held on 17 September 2026, and the CBT-II result was declared on 30 October 2026. The Computer-Based Typing Skill Test (CBTST) is tentatively scheduled for 27 November 2026.

The CBT-II result PDFs contain roll numbers of candidates provisionally shortlisted for CBTST, arranged in ascending order. Candidates can check their individual scorecards and cut-off marks on their respective regional RRB websites. The selection process includes CBT-I, CBT-II, CBTST (where applicable), Document Verification, and Medical Examination.

Basic eligibility is 12th (10+2) from a recognised board. The age limit is 18 to 30 years as on 01 January 2026, with relaxation for reserved categories as per RRB rules.

What to do at the current stage

How to Check CBT-II Result and Scorecard

  1. Visit the official website of your regional RRB.
  2. Locate the link for RRB NTPC UG CBT-II Result 2026 under CEN 07/2025.
  3. Open the region-wise result PDF and search for your roll number using Ctrl+F.
  4. Download the relevant category-wise cut-off PDF.
  5. Log in to the scorecard portal using your Registration Number and Date of Birth.
  6. Check your marks, qualifying status, and shortlisting for CBTST.

Original Application Process

Preserved for recruitment history and reference after the application stage closes.

  1. Visit the official RRB website at rrbapply.gov.in.
  2. Read the official CEN 07/2025 notification carefully.
  3. Register on the RRB portal with valid email ID and mobile number.
  4. Fill the online application form with accurate personal and educational details.
  5. Upload photograph, signature and required documents.
  6. Pay the applicable examination fee online.
  7. Submit the form and save the confirmation page for future reference.
