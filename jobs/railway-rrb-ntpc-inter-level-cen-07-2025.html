Complete context

About This Recruitment

Railway Recruitment Board (RRB) is conducting the Non Technical Popular Categories (NTPC) 10+2 Under Graduate Level recruitment under CEN 07/2025 for 3050 vacancies across Indian Railways zones. The posts included are Commercial Cum Ticket Clerk, Accounts Clerk Cum Typist, Junior Clerk Cum Typist and Trains Clerk.

The online application window was open from 28 October 2025 to 04 December 2025, and the fee payment and correction windows subsequently closed. CBT I was conducted in May and June 2026, and the CBT I result and score card were released in August 2026. CBT II was held on 17 September 2026, and the CBT II answer key was published on 24 September 2026.

This page is the permanent lifecycle page for RRB NTPC UG CEN 07/2025. Every later official stage — objection window, revised answer key, CBT II result, final merit list, document verification, medical and joining — will be added to this same URL without creating new pages.

Eligibility Criteria

Indian citizens who fulfil the post-specific eligibility conditions and medical standards prescribed by RRB may apply.

What to do at the current stage

How to Check CBT II Answer Key & Raise Objection

  1. Open the official RRB CBT II answer key login portal from Important Links.
  2. Log in using your registration number and date of birth (or password).
  3. View the CBT II question paper with your recorded responses.
  4. Download or save the answer key and response sheet for reference.
  5. If you find a discrepancy, raise an objection through the official objection tracker within the permitted window.
  6. Pay the applicable objection fee per question if required, and save the acknowledgement.

Original Application Process

Preserved for recruitment history and reference after the application stage closed.

  1. Visit the official RRB application portal at rrbapply.gov.in.
  2. Read the CEN 07/2025 notification carefully.
  3. Register using valid candidate details and generate registration number.
  4. Fill the online application form and upload photograph and signature.
  5. Pay the applicable examination fee through the permitted online mode.
  6. Verify the preview before final submission.
  7. Submit the form and save the confirmation page for future reference.
