Complete context

About This Recruitment

Railway Recruitment Board (RRB) has released the CBT II Admit Card for Railway RRB NTPC 10+2 Level Under Graduate (UG) CEN 07/2025. This recruitment drive covers 3058 vacancies for Non Technical Popular Categories (NTPC) posts including Commercial Cum Ticket Clerk, Accounts Clerk Cum Typist, Junior Clerk Cum Typist and Trains Clerk.

The Computer Based Test (CBT) II is scheduled to be held on 17 September 2026. Candidates who qualified the CBT I examination can now download their CBT II admit card from the official RRB portal. The admit card contains important details such as exam centre, date, time, and candidate instructions.

This permanent lifecycle page will continue to carry every later official update including answer key, objection window, result, score card, cut off, document verification, medical examination and final merit list on the same URL.

Eligibility Criteria

Age Limit

18 to 30 years as on 01 January 2026.

Age relaxation applicable as per RRB rules for SC/ST/OBC/PwBD/Ex-Servicemen candidates.

Education

Candidates must have passed 10+2 Intermediate Examination from any recognized board in India.

For detailed educational conditions, refer to the official notification.

Nationality / Other

Indian citizens fulfilling the post-specific eligibility conditions may apply.
