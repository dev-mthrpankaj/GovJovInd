Complete context

About This Recruitment

Railway Recruitment Board (RRB) is conducting the RRB NTPC Inter Level (10+2 UG) Recruitment CEN 07/2025 to fill 3,058 Non-Technical Popular Categories (NTPC) Undergraduate posts across Indian Railways. The advertised posts include Commercial Cum Ticket Clerk, Train Clerk, Accounts Clerk Cum Typist and Junior Clerk Cum Typist. The online application window ran from 28 October 2025 to 04 December 2025.

The Computer Based Test Stage I (CBT-I) was conducted on 07, 08, 09 May and 13, 14, 15, 16 and 20 June 2026, with results declared on 26 August 2026. The Stage II examination (CBT-II) was held on 17 September 2026, the provisional answer key was issued on 24 September 2026, and the zone-wise CBT-II result and cut-off were declared on 30 October 2026.

On 17 November 2026, Railway Recruitment Board (RRB) released the CBTST Exam City Details for shortlisted candidates. The Computer Based Typing Skill Test (CBTST) is tentatively scheduled for 27 November 2026. Shortlisted candidates can log in to the official RRB exam city portal using their Registration Number and Date of Birth to check their allotted exam city and shift details.

Candidates must have passed the 10+2 (Intermediate) examination from a recognised board in India with at least 50% marks for General, OBC and EWS categories (pass marks for SC, ST and PwBD). For Accounts Clerk Cum Typist and Junior Clerk Cum Typist posts, computer typing proficiency of 30 WPM in English or 25 WPM in Hindi is required. The age limit is 18 to 30 years as on 01 January 2026.
