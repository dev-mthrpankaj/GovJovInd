Complete context

About This Recruitment

Uttar Pradesh Subordinate Services Selection Commission (UPSSSC) issued Advertisement No. 07-Exam/2022 to recruit 92 Moharir posts in the Directorate of Geology and Mining, Uttar Pradesh. Online applications were accepted from 29 October 2022 to 18 November 2022, with the correction window closing on 25 November 2022.

Candidates who qualified in the UPSSSC Preliminary Eligibility Test (PET) 2021 and passed the 10+2 Intermediate examination from a recognised board were eligible to apply. The age limit for the post is 18 to 40 years as on 01 July 2022, and the pay scale is Rs.25,500 to Rs.81,000 under Pay Level-4.

UPSSSC released the Exam City intimation details on 19 November 2026 and issued the Mains Admit Card on 25 November 2026. The Mains Examination is scheduled to be conducted on 29 November 2026, followed by Document Verification for qualified candidates.

Shortlisted candidates should download the printed Mains Admit Card from the official UPSSSC portal and verify their exam centre address, reporting time, and exam-day instructions before 29 November 2026.
