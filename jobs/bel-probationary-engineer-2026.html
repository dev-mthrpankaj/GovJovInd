Complete context

About This Recruitment

Bharat Electronics Limited (BEL), a Navratna PSU under the Ministry of Defence, has released the official notification for BEL Probationary Engineer Recruitment 2026 (Advertisement No. 17556/HR/All-India/2026). The drive aims to fill 350 vacancies in the E-II Grade across Electronics and Mechanical engineering disciplines.

The online application window opened on 28 October 2026 and will close on 18 November 2026. The Computer-Based Test (CBT) is tentatively scheduled for 22 December 2026. Candidates must hold a first-class B.E./B.Tech./B.Sc. (Engineering) degree in the relevant discipline from an AICTE-approved institution (pass class for SC/ST/PwBD candidates).

The selection process consists of a CBT (85 marks) and a personal interview (15 marks). The CBT will have 125 questions (100 technical + 25 general aptitude/reasoning) to be completed in 120 minutes, with 1/4th negative marking for wrong answers. The pay scale is ₹40,000–₹1,40,000 with an approximate annual CTC of ₹13 lakh.

Eligibility Criteria

Age Limit

Maximum 25 years for UR/EWS as on 01 October 2026.

OBC (NCL): 3 years (up to 28 years); SC/ST: 5 years (up to 30 years); PwBD: 10 years (cumulative); Ex-Servicemen: as per government rules.

Education

First Class B.E./B.Tech./B.Sc. (4-year Engineering) in the relevant discipline from an AICTE-approved institution. SC/ST/PwBD candidates may apply with Pass Class. AMIE/AMIETE/GIETE holders in the specified disciplines are also eligible.

Dual specialisations and other equivalent branches are not considered. Candidates with colour blindness are not eligible.

Nationality / Other

Only Indian nationals are eligible. Candidates must fulfil the post-specific medical and physical standards as prescribed in the official notification.

Refer to official notification for detailed conditions.

What to do at the current stage

How to Apply Online

  1. Visit the official BEL website at bel-india.in or open the direct apply link from Important Links.
  2. Open Advertisement No. 17556/HR/All-India/2026 and read the eligibility conditions carefully.
  3. Register using a valid email ID and mobile number.
  4. Log in with your registration credentials and fill the online application form.
  5. Upload your recent photograph (100–200 KB, 3.5 cm × 4.5 cm) and signature (80–150 KB) in JPG/JPEG format.
  6. Pay the application fee (₹1,180 for GEN/OBC/EWS; exempted for SC/ST/PwBD/ESM) via SBI e-Pay Lite.
  7. Review all details and submit the form. Download and save the confirmation page for future reference.
