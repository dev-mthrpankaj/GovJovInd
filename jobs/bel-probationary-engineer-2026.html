Complete context

About This Recruitment

Bharat Electronics Limited (BEL), a Navratna PSU under the Ministry of Defence, has released the official notification for BEL Probationary Engineer Recruitment 2026 (Advertisement No. 17556/HR/All-India/2026). The drive aims to fill 350 vacancies in the E-II Grade across Electronics and Mechanical engineering disciplines.

The online application window opened on 28 October 2026 and will close on 18 November 2026. The Computer-Based Test (CBT) is tentatively scheduled for 22 December 2026. Candidates must hold a first-class B.E./B.Tech./B.Sc. (Engineering) degree in the relevant discipline from an AICTE-approved institution (pass class for SC/ST/PwBD candidates).

The selection process consists of a CBT (85 marks) and a personal interview (15 marks). The CBT will have 125 questions (100 technical + 25 general aptitude/reasoning) to be completed in 120 minutes, with 1/4th negative marking for wrong answers. The pay scale is ₹40,000–₹1,40,000 with an approximate annual CTC of ₹13 lakh.
