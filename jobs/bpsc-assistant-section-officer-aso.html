Complete context

About This Recruitment

The Bihar Public Service Commission (BPSC) has released the recruitment notification for the post of Assistant Section Officer (ASO) under Advertisement No. 37/2025. This recruitment drive aims to fill a total of 41 vacancies for the ASO post in various departments of the Bihar government.

The online application window was open from 29 May 2025 to 23 June 2025. The preliminary examination was conducted on 10 September 2025, followed by the release of the provisional answer key on 18 September 2025. The preliminary result was declared on 23 December 2025, shortlisting candidates for the Mains examination.

The Mains examination was held on 27 February 2026, and the Mains admit cards were released on 20 February 2026. The commission has now declared the Mains Result on 27 October 2026. Candidates who have qualified in the Mains examination will be called for the Document Verification (DV) stage. The final merit list will be prepared based on the performance in the Mains examination.

What to do at the current stage

How to Check BPSC ASO Mains Result

  1. Open the official BPSC website from the Important Links section.
  2. Click on the 'Download Mains Result' link provided on this page or visit the Results section on the BPSC portal.
  3. The result will be available in a PDF file format.
  4. Search for your roll number in the PDF using Ctrl+F.
  5. Download and save the PDF for future reference.

Original Application Process

Preserved for recruitment history and reference after the application stage closes.

  1. Visit the official BPSC recruitment website at bpsc.bihar.gov.in.
  2. Read the official notification (Advt. No. 37/2025) carefully.
  3. Register on the BPSC online portal with valid email ID and mobile number.
  4. Fill the online application form with accurate personal and educational details.
  5. Upload photograph, signature and required documents.
  6. Pay the applicable examination fee online.
  7. Submit the form and save the confirmation page for future reference.
