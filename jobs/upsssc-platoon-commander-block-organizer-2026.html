Complete context

About This Recruitment

UPSSSC Platoon Commander & Block Organizer Recruitment 2026 is an Uttar Pradesh Subordinate Services Selection Commission drive to fill 295 posts, including 243 Block Organizer and 52 Platoon Commander posts under Home Guards Headquarters, Uttar Pradesh. The advertisement was published as Advertisement No. 10-Exam/2026 in April 2026, and the online application window remained open from 16 June 2026 to 06 July 2026.

Shortlisting is done on the basis of the UPSSSC PET 2025 score, followed by the Main Written Examination. The eligibility result of shortlisted candidates was declared on 30 September 2026. The Main Examination is scheduled for 22 November 2026, and the Exam City Details for this examination were released on 12 November 2026.

Candidates must download the exam city intimation slip after paying the prescribed Main Examination fee. The city intimation slip only shows the allotted examination district and is not the admit card; the admit card will be released separately before the examination.

Basic eligibility is a Bachelor's Degree in any stream from a recognised university along with a valid UPSSSC PET 2025 scorecard. The age limit is 18 to 40 years as on 01 July 2026, with age relaxation for reserved categories as per Uttar Pradesh government rules.

What to do at the current stage

How to Download Exam City Intimation Slip

  1. Open the official UPSSSC website at upsssc.gov.in.
  2. Go to the Examination or Admit Card section on the homepage.
  3. Click the link for the advance examination district information for Platoon Commander / Block Organizer Main Examination 2026.
  4. Log in using your PET 2025 registration number and the required credentials.
  5. Pay the prescribed Main Examination fee, if it has not already been deposited.
  6. Submit the details to view your allotted examination district.
  7. Download the Exam City Intimation Slip and take a clear printout for reference.
  8. Note that the city intimation slip is not the admit card; the admit card will be released separately before the examination.

Original Application Process

Preserved for recruitment history and reference after the application stage closes.

  1. Visit the official UPSSSC website at upsssc.gov.in.
  2. Read Advertisement No. 10-Exam/2026 carefully.
  3. Log in with your PET 2025 registration number.
  4. Fill the online application form with accurate personal and educational details.
  5. Upload photograph, signature and required documents.
  6. Pay the application processing fee online.
  7. Submit the form and save the confirmation page for future reference.
