Complete context

About This Recruitment

UPSSSC Platoon Commander & Block Organizer Recruitment 2026 is a state-level recruitment drive conducted by the Uttar Pradesh Subordinate Services Selection Commission (UPSSSC) under Advt No. 10-Exam/2026 to fill 295 vacancies in Uttar Pradesh. Out of the 295 advertised posts, 243 posts are for Block Organizer and 52 posts are for Platoon Commander.

Online applications were accepted from 16 June 2026 to 06 July 2026, with the fee payment deadline on 06 July 2026 and the application correction window closing on 13 July 2026. Based on UPSSSC PET 2025 scores, the Mains shortlisting eligibility result was declared on 30 September 2026.

Exam city details were released on 12 November 2026, followed by the Mains admit card and the written examination conducted on 22 November 2026. On 24 November 2026, UPSSSC released the official Answer Key Notice on upsssc.gov.in so candidates can verify their responses and estimate their scores ahead of the Mains result and PET/PST rounds.

Eligible candidates hold a Bachelor Degree in any stream from a recognised university in India along with a valid UPSSSC PET 2025 Score Card, and meet the age limit of 18 to 40 years as on 01 July 2026.

What to do at the current stage

How to Check Answer Key & Submit Objections

  1. Open the official Answer Key Notice PDF or visit upsssc.gov.in from Important Links.
  2. Read the official notice dated 24 November 2026 for question paper set mapping and objection instructions.
  3. Log in on the UPSSSC portal using your Registration Number, Date of Birth, and verification code.
  4. Match your recorded responses against the provisional answer key for your exam shift and booklet series.
  5. If any discrepancy is found, submit online objections with valid documentary proof within the official objection window.
  6. Save a copy of the provisional answer key and objection receipt for future reference.

Original Application Process

Preserved for recruitment history and reference after the application stage closes.

  1. Visit the official Uttar Pradesh Subordinate Services Selection Commission (UPSSSC) website at upsssc.gov.in.
  2. Open the Live Advertisements section and select Advt No. 10-Exam/2026.
  3. Log in using your UPSSSC PET 2025 Registration Number and authentication details.
  4. Verify pre-filled personal and educational information and enter post preferences.
  5. Upload the required photograph, signature, and supporting details.
  6. Pay the Rs.25 online processing fee and submit the final application form.
  7. Download and print the submitted application confirmation receipt for future stages.

Quick answers

Frequently Asked Questions

Yes, the Uttar Pradesh Subordinate Services Selection Commission (UPSSSC) released the Answer Key and official notice on 24 November 2026. Candidates who appeared in the 22 November 2026 Mains exam can check it via the official portal upsssc.gov.in.

The UPSSSC Platoon Commander and Block Organizer Mains written examination under Advt No. 10-Exam/2026 was conducted on 22 November 2026. Exam city intimation details were issued earlier on 12 November 2026 on the official UPSSSC portal.

Candidates can download the UPSSSC Platoon Commander and Block Organizer Answer Key Notice from the official website upsssc.gov.in. Open the Notice Board or Answer Key section on the homepage and log in with registration details if required.

A total of 295 vacancies were advertised under UPSSSC Advt No. 10-Exam/2026. Out of these 295 posts, 243 vacancies are for Block Organizer and 52 vacancies are for Platoon Commander across General, OBC, EWS, SC, and ST categories.

For Block Organizer (243 posts), vacancies are 98 General, 71 OBC, 24 EWS, 48 SC, and 2 ST. For Platoon Commander (52 posts), vacancies are 23 General, 12 OBC, 5 EWS, 11 SC, and 1 ST as per the official notification.

Candidates must hold a Bachelor Degree in any stream from a recognised university in India along with a valid UPSSSC PET 2025 Score Card. Only candidates shortlisted in the PET 2025 eligibility result were permitted to appear in the Mains exam.

The prescribed age limit for UPSSSC Platoon Commander and Block Organizer Recruitment 2026 is 18 to 40 years as on 01 July 2026. Upper age relaxation is provided to reserved category candidates of Uttar Pradesh as per UPSSSC rules.

The online application processing fee was Rs.25 for all categories, including General, OBC, EWS, SC, ST, and PwBD candidates. The application window ran from 16 June 2026 to 06 July 2026, with form corrections allowed until 13 July 2026.

UPSSSC declared the Mains shortlisting eligibility result on 30 September 2026 based on UPSSSC PET 2025 scores. Shortlisted candidates were subsequently issued Mains admit cards for the written examination held on 22 November 2026.

The selection process comprises UPSSSC PET 2025 shortlisting, Mains Written Examination, Physical Standard and Efficiency Test (PET/PST), Document Verification, and Final Merit List preparation by Uttar Pradesh Subordinate Services Selection Commission.

The Mains written examination result date has not been announced yet by UPSSSC. Following the release of the provisional Answer Key on 24 November 2026, the commission will review objections and publish the written result on upsssc.gov.in.

The official website of Uttar Pradesh Subordinate Services Selection Commission (UPSSSC) is https://upsssc.gov.in/. All official notices, answer keys, results, and PET/PST schedules for Advt No. 10-Exam/2026 are published directly on this portal.
