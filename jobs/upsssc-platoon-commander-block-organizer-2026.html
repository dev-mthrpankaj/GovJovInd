Complete context

About This Recruitment

UPSSSC Platoon Commander & Block Organizer Recruitment 2026 is a state-level recruitment drive conducted by the Uttar Pradesh Subordinate Services Selection Commission (UPSSSC) under Advt No. 10-Exam/2026 to fill 295 vacancies in Uttar Pradesh. Out of the 295 advertised posts, 243 posts are for Block Organizer and 52 posts are for Platoon Commander.

Online applications were accepted from 16 June 2026 to 06 July 2026, with the fee payment deadline on 06 July 2026 and the application correction window closing on 13 July 2026. Based on UPSSSC PET 2025 scores, the Mains shortlisting eligibility result was declared on 30 September 2026.

Exam city details were released on 12 November 2026, followed by the Mains admit card and the written examination conducted on 22 November 2026. On 24 November 2026, UPSSSC released the official Answer Key Notice on upsssc.gov.in so candidates can verify their responses and estimate their scores ahead of the Mains result and PET/PST rounds.

Eligible candidates hold a Bachelor Degree in any stream from a recognised university in India along with a valid UPSSSC PET 2025 Score Card, and meet the age limit of 18 to 40 years as on 01 July 2026.
