Complete context

About This Recruitment

UPSSSC Platoon Commander and Block Organizer Recruitment 2026 is a Uttar Pradesh Subordinate Services Selection Commission (UPSSSC) recruitment drive for 295 posts under the Home Guards Headquarters, Uttar Pradesh. The online application window was open from 16 June 2026 to 6 July 2026.

The recruitment is based on the Preliminary Eligibility Test (PET) 2025 score. Candidates shortlisted through PET 2025 were required to apply online. The eligibility result, which lists candidates shortlisted for the mains examination, has now been released on 30 September 2026.

The next stage is the Mains Written Examination, tentatively scheduled for 22 November 2026 as per the UPSSSC Exam Calendar 2026. Candidates should check the official website for the admit card and exam city details.

Eligibility Criteria

Age Limit

21 to 40 years as on 1 July 2026.

Age relaxation applicable as per UPSSSC rules: SC/ST – 5 years, OBC – 5 years, Home Guards unpaid member (3 yrs service) – 2 years.

Education

Bachelor's Degree in any stream from a recognized university.

Candidates must have a valid UPSSSC PET 2025 score card. Preference will be given to candidates who have served in Territorial Army, possess NCC 'B' certificate, or have worked in Home Guards for 3 years.

Nationality / Other

Indian citizens fulfilling the post-specific eligibility conditions may apply.

Refer to official notification for detailed conditions.

What to do at the current stage

How to Download Eligibility Result

  1. Visit the official UPSSSC website at upsssc.gov.in.
  2. Click on the 'Results' section available on the homepage.
  3. Find and open the 'UPSSSC Platoon Commander, Block Organizer Eligibility Result 2026' link.
  4. Enter your Application Number and Date of Birth or Roll Number, as required.
  5. Complete the CAPTCHA code or other security verification, if prompted.
  6. Click on the Submit button to view your result.
  7. Check your name, roll number, qualifying status, and marks displayed on the result page.
  8. Download the result PDF or scorecard to your device.
  9. Take a clear printout of the result for document verification and future recruitment stages.

Original Application Process

Preserved for recruitment history and reference after the application stage closes.

  1. Visit the official UPSSSC website at upsssc.gov.in.
  2. Login using your PET-2025 Registration Number via OTP sent to your registered mobile number/email.
  3. Select the post(s) you wish to apply for and confirm your educational qualification details.
  4. Enter all required personal details, category, reservation details, educational qualifications, and other information carefully.
  5. Complete fee payment via Credit Card / Debit Card / Internet Banking / UPI or SBI e-Challan.
  6. After successful fee payment, the application form will be submitted automatically.
  7. Download and print the application form for future reference.
