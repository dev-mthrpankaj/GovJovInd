Complete context

About This Recruitment

Uttar Pradesh Subordinate Services Selection Commission (UPSSSC) is conducting the Platoon Commander and Block Organizer Recruitment 2026 under Advertisement No. 10-Exam/2026 to fill 295 posts in the Uttar Pradesh Home Guards Headquarters. The recruitment includes 243 vacancies for Block Organizer and 52 vacancies for Platoon Commander.

The official notification was published on 15 April 2026, and online applications were accepted from 16 June 2026 to 06 July 2026, with application corrections open until 13 July 2026. Based on UPSSSC PET 2025 normalised scores, the shortlisting eligibility result was declared on 30 September 2026, followed by the examination district intimation slip on 12 November 2026.

On 18 November 2026, UPSSSC released the Main Written Examination Admit Card on upsssc.gov.in. The Main Written Examination is scheduled for 22 November 2026 in a single shift from 10:00 AM to 12:00 PM. Shortlisted candidates must pay the prescribed Main Examination fee before downloading the hall ticket and must carry a printed admit card along with a valid original government photo ID to the examination centre.

Applicants must hold a Bachelor's Degree in any stream from a recognised university along with a valid UPSSSC PET 2025 score card, while Intermediate (10+2) pass is accepted for unpaid departmental employees. The age limit is 21 to 40 years as on 01 July 2026, with upper age relaxation as per Uttar Pradesh government rules. Selection proceeds through PET 2025 shortlisting, Main Written Examination, Physical Measurement and Standard Test (PMT/PST), and Document Verification.

Eligibility Criteria

Age Limit

21 to 40 years as on 01 July 2026.

Upper age relaxation is applicable for SC, ST, OBC, Home Guards volunteers, and eligible Uttar Pradesh categories as per state government rules.

Education

Bachelor's Degree (Graduation) in any stream from a recognised university along with a valid UPSSSC PET 2025 Score Card.

For unpaid departmental employees, Intermediate (10+2) pass is eligible. Preferential weightage applies for Territorial Army service (2 years), NCC 'B' Certificate, or 3 years Home Guards service.

Nationality / Other

Candidates must have appeared in UPSSSC PET 2025 with a valid normalised score and must meet the prescribed Physical Measurement and Standard Test (PMT/PST) norms.

Refer to official notification for detailed conditions.

What to do at the current stage

How to Download UPSSSC Platoon Commander & Block Organizer Admit Card 2026

  1. Open the official UPSSSC Mains Admit Card portal from the Important Links section.
  2. Ensure you have paid the prescribed Main Examination fee (Rs.200 for General/OBC/EWS, Rs.80 for SC/ST, Nil for PwBD) before downloading.
  3. Enter your UPSSSC Registration Number, Date of Birth, Gender, and the verification captcha code.
  4. Click on Download Admit Card to view your Mains hall ticket for Advt. No. 10-Exam/2026.
  5. Verify your name, roll number, examination centre address, exam date (22 November 2026), shift timing (10:00 AM to 12:00 PM), and reporting time.
  6. Take a clear printout of the admit card and carry it with a valid original government photo ID to the exam centre.

Original Application Process

Preserved for recruitment history and reference after the application stage closes.

  1. Visit the official UPSSSC website at upsssc.gov.in and open the Live Advertisements section.
  2. Select Advertisement No. 10-Exam/2026 for Platoon Commander and Block Organizer Recruitment 2026.
  3. Authenticate using your UPSSSC PET 2025 Registration Number through personal details or OTP.
  4. Verify pre-filled details, enter post-specific educational qualifications, and check the preview carefully.
  5. Pay the Rs.25 online application processing fee using Debit Card, Credit Card, Net Banking, or SBI e-Challan.
  6. Submit the completed application form before 06 July 2026 and save the printed confirmation receipt.
