About This Recruitment

MPPSC Assistant Town Planner Recruitment 2026 is being conducted by the Madhya Pradesh Public Service Commission for 39 Assistant Town Planner posts under Advertisement No. 02/2026.

Online applications started on 20 February 2026. The regular application and fee window closed on 19 March 2026, followed by correction and late-fee phases. The recruitment has now moved to the examination stage.

The admit card was released on 10 September 2026 and the written examination is scheduled for 20 September 2026. Candidates should download the admit card, verify the reporting instructions, and carry the required photo ID to the examination centre.
