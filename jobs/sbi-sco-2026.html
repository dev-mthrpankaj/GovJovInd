Complete context

About This Recruitment

State Bank of India (SBI) has released the Specialist Cadre Officer (SCO) Recruitment 2026 notification for 207 posts under Advertisement No. CRPD/SCO/2026-27/23. The online application window has been extended, and eligible graduates can now apply online from 04 September 2026 to 05 October 2026 through the official SBI recruitment portal.

This recruitment is a significant opportunity for candidates seeking a specialist officer role in India's largest public sector bank. The positions fall under Junior Management Grade Scale-I (JMGS-I) and Middle Management Grade Scale-II (MMGS-II) with attractive pay scales. Post-wise eligibility, experience requirements, and age limits vary, so candidates must carefully review the official notification before applying.

The selection process typically involves an online written examination and/or interview, followed by document verification and medical examination. The final merit list will determine the selection of candidates. Keep photograph, signature, educational certificates, experience documents, and category certificates ready before starting the online form.

This is a permanent recruitment lifecycle page. All future updates — including exam date, admit card, answer key, result, scorecard, and final selection — will be published on this same URL without creating separate stage-specific pages.
