Complete context

About This Recruitment

National Insurance Company Limited (NICL) has released the recruitment notification for 500 Assistant (Class III) posts. The official notification was published on 17 July 2026, and the online application window remained open from 18 July 2026 to 07 August 2026.

The Phase I (Preliminary) examination was conducted on 27 August 2026. The Prelims result was declared on 08 October 2026, and the Phase II (Mains) examination is scheduled for 30 October 2026. A total of 16,575 candidates have been shortlisted for the Mains round.

Basic eligibility is a Bachelor's degree in any stream from a recognised university. The age limit is 21 to 30 years as on 01 July 2026, with relaxation for reserved categories as per government rules. The application fee is Rs. 850 for General/OBC/EWS candidates, while SC/ST/PwBD candidates are exempted.

The selection process includes a Preliminary exam, Mains exam, Document Verification, and Medical Examination. The pay scale is approximately Rs. 53,000 per month at the starting level. This permanent page will be updated with every official stage including Mains admit card, Mains result, final merit list, and joining.
