Complete context

About This Recruitment

NICL Assistant Recruitment 2026 is a nationwide recruitment drive conducted by National Insurance Company Limited (NICL) to fill 500 Assistant posts across various regional offices in India. The official notification was released in July 2026, and online applications were accepted from 18 July 2026 to 07 August 2026.

The selection process consists of the Phase I Preliminary Examination, Phase II Main Examination, Regional Language Test, Document Verification, and Medical Examination. The Phase I examination was conducted on 27 August 2026, followed by the Phase I result declaration on 08 October 2026 and the Phase II examination on 30 October 2026.

National Insurance Company Limited released the NICL Assistant Score Card 2026 on 23 November 2026. Candidates can download their score cards online through the direct IBPS login link provided in the Important Links section and save a printed copy for upcoming recruitment stages.

Basic educational eligibility for the Assistant post is a Bachelor's degree in any discipline from a recognised university in India. Applicants must be between 21 and 30 years of age as on 01 July 2026, with upper age relaxation applicable for reserved categories as per government rules.
