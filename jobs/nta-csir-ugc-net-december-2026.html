Complete context

About This Examination

The NTA CSIR UGC NET December 2026 Examination is a national-level eligibility test conducted by the National Testing Agency (NTA) on behalf of the Council of Scientific and Industrial Research (CSIR). It determines eligibility for Junior Research Fellowship (JRF) and Lectureship (LS) / Assistant Professor positions in Indian universities and colleges across five core science disciplines.

The official notification was released on 07 October 2026, and online applications were accepted until 05 November 2026, followed by a late fee window until 09 November 2026 and an application correction window from 10 November to 12 November 2026.

On 20 November 2026, NTA published the official subject-wise exam schedule confirming that the examination will take place on 19, 22 and 27 December 2026. Admit cards will be released on the official portal as per schedule prior to the exam dates.

Candidates holding an M.Sc or equivalent degree with at least 55% marks (50% for SC, ST and PwBD candidates), as well as eligible Integrated Course, B.E., B.Tech, B.Pharma and MBBS candidates, applied for Chemical Science, Earth, Atmospheric, Ocean and Planetary Science, Life Science, Mathematical Science and Physical Science.

Eligibility requirements

Eligibility Criteria

Age Limit

Maximum 30 years for Junior Research Fellowship (JRF). No upper age limit for Lectureship (LS) / Assistant Professor.

Upper age relaxation for reserved categories applies as per official CSIR UGC NET notification rules.

Education

M.Sc or equivalent degree with 55% marks for General and OBC candidates, and 50% marks for SC, ST and PH candidates.

Candidates with Integrated Course, B.E., B.Tech, B.Pharma and MBBS degrees are also eligible as per official norms.

Nationality / Other

Candidates must fulfill all category, subject and fellowship eligibility conditions specified in the CSIR UGC NET December 2026 notification.

Refer to official notification for detailed conditions.

How to Check Subject-wise Exam Schedule

  1. Open the official Subject-wise Exam Schedule PDF from the Important Links section.
  2. Locate your chosen subject among Chemical Science, Earth Science, Life Science, Mathematical Science and Physical Science.
  3. Note your exact examination date among 19, 22 and 27 December 2026 and check the assigned shift timing.
  4. Keep your application number and date of birth ready for downloading the admit card once released.
  5. Monitor the official portal csirnet.nta.nic.in for city intimation slip and admit card updates.

Original Application Process

Preserved for examination history and reference after the application stage closes.

  1. Visit the official NTA CSIR UGC NET website at csirnet.nta.nic.in.
  2. Open the NTA CSIR UGC NET December 2026 online application link.
  3. Register using a valid email ID and mobile number.
  4. Log in using the generated registration ID and password.
  5. Fill in personal, educational, subject and category details accurately.
  6. Upload scanned photograph, signature and required documents.
  7. Pay the applicable examination fee through online mode.
  8. Submit the form and download the confirmation page for future reference.
