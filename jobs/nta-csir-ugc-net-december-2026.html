Complete context

About This Recruitment

CSIR UGC NET December 2026 is a national-level eligibility test conducted by the National Testing Agency (NTA) on behalf of the Council of Scientific & Industrial Research (CSIR). The exam determines eligibility for Junior Research Fellowship (JRF), appointment as Assistant Professor, and admission to PhD programs in Indian universities and colleges.

The official notification was released on 07 October 2026, and the online application window is open from 07 October 2026 to 05 November 2026. Candidates who miss the deadline can apply with a late fee of Rs. 500 between 06 November and 09 November 2026. The application correction window is open from 10 November to 12 November 2026.

The exam will be conducted in Computer Based Test (CBT) mode on 19, 22, and 27 December 2026 in two shifts. The test covers five science subjects: Chemical Science, Earth Atmospheric Ocean and Planetary Science, Life Science, Mathematical Science, and Physical Science.

Eligibility requires a Master's degree or equivalent with at least 55% marks for General/EWS candidates and 50% for OBC-NCL, SC, ST, PwD, and Third Gender candidates. The maximum age limit for JRF is 30 years, while there is no upper age limit for Assistant Professor and PhD admission.
