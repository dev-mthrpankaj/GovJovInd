Complete context

About This Recruitment

The Joint CSIR-UGC NET December 2026 is conducted by the National Testing Agency (NTA) on behalf of the Council of Scientific & Industrial Research (CSIR). It determines eligibility for the award of Junior Research Fellowship (JRF), appointment as Assistant Professor, and admission to PhD programmes in Indian universities and colleges.

The official notification was released on 07 October 2026, and online applications were accepted from 07 October to 05 November 2026. A late fee window was available from 06 to 09 November 2026. The application correction window is currently open from 10 to 12 November 2026, and the exam will be held on 19, 22, and 27 December 2026 in Computer Based Test (CBT) mode.

A total of 7150 vacancies (JRF and Assistant Professor positions) are covered under this recruitment cycle. Candidates must hold an M.Sc or equivalent degree with at least 55% marks (50% for SC/ST/PwBD) to be eligible. The exam will be conducted in five subjects: Chemical Sciences, Earth, Atmospheric, Ocean and Planetary Sciences, Life Sciences, Mathematical Sciences, and Physical Sciences.
