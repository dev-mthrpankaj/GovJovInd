Complete context

About This Recruitment

NTA CSIR UGC NET December 2026 Examination is conducted by the National Testing Agency (NTA) for the Council of Scientific & Industrial Research (CSIR) to determine Indian nationals' eligibility for Junior Research Fellowship (JRF), Assistant Professor (Lectureship), and PhD admissions in Indian universities and research institutions. Rather than fixed job vacancies, the examination covers five core science disciplines: Chemical Science, Earth, Atmospheric, Ocean and Planetary Science, Life Science, Mathematical Science, and Physical Science.

The official notification was released on 07 October 2026, and online applications were accepted until 05 November 2026, with late fee payment open up to 09 November 2026. The application correction window operated from 10 November 2026 to 12 November 2026, followed by the subject-wise exam schedule notice on 20 November 2026.

On 27 November 2026, NTA activated the Advance Exam City Intimation Slip portal so candidates can check their allotted examination city before travel planning. The Computer Based Test (CBT) will be conducted on 19, 22, and 27 December 2026, and the official admit card will be released separately as per schedule.

Candidates with an M.Sc or equivalent degree (55% marks for General and OBC; 50% marks for SC, ST, and PwBD) or eligible Integrated, B.E., B.Tech, B.Pharma, and MBBS qualifications were eligible to apply. The maximum age limit is 30 years for JRF, while there is no upper age limit for Lectureship or Assistant Professor.
