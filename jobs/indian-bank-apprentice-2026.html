Complete context

About This Recruitment

Indian Bank Apprentice Recruitment 2026 is a engagement drive under the Apprentices Act, 1961 for the financial year 2026-27. The bank aims to fill 1500 Apprentice training seats across various states and Union Territories. The official notification was released on 05 November 2026, and the online application window is open from 05 November 2026 to 25 November 2026.

Candidates with a bachelor's degree in any discipline from a recognised university are eligible to apply. The age limit is 20 to 28 years as on 01 November 2026. The selection process includes an online written examination followed by a local language proficiency test. Selected apprentices will receive a monthly stipend of Rs.15,000 in metro/urban areas and Rs.12,300 in rural/semi-urban areas.

Applicants must be proficient in the local language of the state/UT they are applying for. They must produce an 8th, 10th, or 12th standard mark sheet evidencing the local language. The application fee is Rs.800 plus GST for General/OBC/EWS candidates and Rs.175 plus GST for SC/ST/PwBD candidates.
