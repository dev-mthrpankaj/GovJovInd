Complete context

About This Recruitment

UKPSC Upper PCS Recruitment 2026 is a Uttarakhand Public Service Commission drive to fill 67 Group-B posts across various departments, including Deputy Collector, Deputy Superintendent of Police (DSP), Finance Officer and Block Development Officer. The official notification (Advt No. A-1/E-1/2026-27) was published on 09 September 2026, and the online application window remained open from 09 September 2026 to 29 September 2026.

The Preliminary Examination is scheduled for 29 November 2026 (Sunday) in two shifts across 18 cities in Uttarakhand. The Prelims Admit Card was released on 13 November 2026. Candidates must download the hall ticket from the official website psc.uk.gov.in using their registration number and date of birth. The exam will be OMR-based, with General Studies (Paper 1) from 10:00 AM to 12:00 Noon and General Aptitude Test (Paper 2) from 2:00 PM to 4:00 PM.

Basic eligibility requires a Bachelor's Degree from a recognised university, with post-specific requirements for certain positions. The age limit is 21 to 42 years as on 01 July 2026, with relaxation for SC (5 years), OBC (3 years) and other reserved categories as per Uttarakhand Government rules.

What to do at the current stage

How to Download Prelims Admit Card

  1. Open the official UKPSC website at psc.uk.gov.in.
  2. Click on the 'Admit Card' link under the 'Candidate Corner' section.
  3. Select the link for Uttarakhand Combined State Civil/Upper Subordinate Services Examination-2026.
  4. Enter your registration number or application number and date of birth.
  5. Download and print the admit card for the Preliminary Examination.
  6. Check your name, roll number, exam date, shift timings and exam centre carefully.

Original Application Process

Preserved for recruitment history and reference after the application stage closes.

  1. Visit the official UKPSC website at psc.uk.gov.in.
  2. Read the official notification (Advt No. A-1/E-1/2026-27) carefully.
  3. Register on the UKPSC portal with valid email ID and mobile number.
  4. Fill the online application form with accurate personal and educational details.
  5. Upload photograph, signature and required documents.
  6. Pay the applicable examination fee online.
  7. Submit the form and save the confirmation page for future reference.
