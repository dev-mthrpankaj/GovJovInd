Complete context

About This Recruitment

Office of the Divisional Forest Officer, Nicobar Division has published Advt No. ND/LS/1-35(A)/ for Andaman & Nicobar Mazdoor Recruitment 2026 to fill 64 regular Mazdoor posts in Andaman & Nicobar. Selected candidates will be placed in Pay Matrix Level-1 (Rs.18,000 to Rs.56,900).

The online application process began on 26 November 2026 and closes on 26 December 2026. Candidates can also pay the application fee online up to 26 December 2026, and an online correction window will remain available from 26 December 2026 to 05 January 2027.

Applicants must have passed Class 10th (High School) from a recognized board or institution, possess a good physique, and qualify in the Physical Test. Knowledge of Hindi is desirable. The age limit is 18 to 33 years for male candidates and 18 to 38 years for female candidates as on 26 December 2026.

Final selection will be made based on merit secured in the Physical Test, followed by verification of certificates and testimonials, character and antecedents verification, and medical examination by the appropriate authority. What to do at the current stage

How to Apply Online for Andaman & Nicobar Mazdoor 2026

  1. Visit the official Andaman & Nicobar e-Recruitment portal at erecruitment.andamannicobar.gov.in.
  2. Read the official notification (Advt No. ND/LS/1-35(A)/) carefully to verify eligibility and category vacancies.
  3. Click on the Apply Online link and register using a valid email ID and mobile number.
  4. Log in with your registration ID and password to open the Mazdoor application form.
  5. Enter your personal, educational (Class 10th), and category details accurately.
  6. Upload scanned copies of your recent passport-size photograph, signature, and required certificates.
  7. Pay the Rs.100 application fee online (Women and ST candidates are exempted).
  8. Submit the completed form before 26 December 2026 and print the confirmation page for future reference.
