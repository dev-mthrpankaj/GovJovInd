About This Recruitment

GSRTC Helper Recruitment 2026 is a recruitment drive by Gujarat State Road Transport Corporation for 2,510 Helper posts. Online applications are scheduled from 7 September 2026 to 6 October 2026.

Candidates must have at least a one-year ITI qualification from a recognized institution in a relevant trade. The available recruitment information lists trades including Mechanic Motor Vehicle, Mechanic Diesel, General Mechanic, Fitter, Turner, Electrician, Sheet Metal Worker, Automobile Body Repairer and Welder, among others.

Candidates should verify their trade eligibility, age, category and documents in the official notification before submitting the OJAS online application.
