Complete context

About This Recruitment

BPSC Assistant Environmental Scientist (AES) Recruitment 2025 is a Bihar Public Service Commission drive to fill 17 posts of Assistant Environmental Scientist in the Bihar State Pollution Control Board under the Environment, Forest and Climate Change Department, Government of Bihar. The official notification (Advt. No. 88/2025) was released on 22 August 2025, and online applications were accepted from 27 August 2025 to 19 September 2025.

The written (objective) competitive examination was conducted on 11 June 2026. The provisional answer key was released on 7 July 2026, and the objection window remained open from 8 July 2026 to 12 July 2026. The final answer key was published on 27 July 2026. The written examination result was declared on 30 October 2026, shortlisting 48 candidates for the interview stage out of 1,310 candidates who appeared.

The selection process consists of a written examination (Paper-I General Studies for 100 marks and Paper-II Optional Subject for 300 marks) followed by an interview for 50 marks. The final merit list will be prepared based on the combined performance in the written examination and the interview, along with applicable weightage for relevant contractual work experience. Candidates shortlisted for the interview must keep their educational certificates, identity documents, category certificates, and experience certificates ready for document verification.

The basic eligibility for the post is a postgraduate degree in Chemistry, Biology, Environmental Science, or Physics from a recognised university. The age limit is 21 to 37 years for Unreserved Male candidates, 21 to 40 years for Unreserved Female, BC, and EBC candidates, and 21 to 42 years for SC/ST candidates as on 1 August 2025. The pay scale is Level-9 (Rs. 53,100 – Rs. 1,67,800) under the 7th Pay Commission, plus admissible allowances.
