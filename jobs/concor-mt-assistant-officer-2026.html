Complete context

About This Recruitment

Container Corporation of India Limited (CONCOR), a Navratna CPSE under the Ministry of Railways, has released Employment Notification No. 05/2026 for the recruitment of Management Trainee (MT) and Assistant Officer (AO) posts. A total of 77 vacancies are available across Commercial & Operations, Accounts, Technical, MIS, Civil, and Personnel & Administration disciplines.

The online application window was open from 31 August 2026 to 30 September 2026. The Computer Based Test (CBT) is now scheduled for 21 November 2026. Management Trainee selection includes CBT followed by Group Discussion and/or Interview, while Assistant Officer selection is based on CBT and Document Verification.

Eligibility varies by post: Management Trainee posts require a graduate degree with 55% marks plus a relevant MBA/PG degree/diploma or professional qualification like CA/CS, while Assistant Officer posts require a graduate degree or diploma with 50% marks and relevant work experience. The age limit is 18–28 years for MT and 18–32 years for AO as on 31 August 2026, with relaxation for reserved categories.

The pay scale is ₹50,000–3%–₹1,60,000 for Management Trainee and ₹40,000–3%–₹1,36,000 for Assistant Officer, with approximate CTC of ₹20.11 lakh and ₹16.09 lakh per annum respectively. This page will be updated with admit card, answer key, result, and final merit list as they are released.
