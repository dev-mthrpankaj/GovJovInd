Complete context

About This Recruitment

Container Corporation of India Ltd. (CONCOR) published Advt. No. 05/2026 on 18 August 2026 to fill 77 posts of Management Trainee and Assistant Officer across Commercial & Operations, Accounts/Compliance, Technical, MIS, Civil, and Personnel & Administration. Online applications were accepted from 31 August 2026 to 30 September 2026.

Admit cards for the Computer Based Test (CBT) were released on 05 November 2026, and the examination was conducted on 21 November 2026. On 26 November 2026, CONCOR released the provisional Answer Key on its official login portal so candidates can verify their recorded responses and calculate expected scores.

Selection for CONCOR Management Trainee & Assistant Officer Recruitment 2026 consists of a Computer Based Test (CBT), Group Discussion and Interview (if applicable), Document Verification, and Medical Examination. Eligible candidates hold a Graduate, Postgraduate, Engineering, CA, CS, or Diploma qualification with post-specific marks and experience as prescribed in Advt. No. 05/2026.

What to do at the current stage

How to Download CONCOR MT & Assistant Officer Answer Key 2026

  1. Open the official CONCOR Answer Key login portal from the Important Links section.
  2. Enter your Enrollment Number or Registration Number along with your Date of Birth or password.
  3. Submit the login credentials to open your candidate dashboard.
  4. Click on the CONCOR MT, Assistant Officer Answer Key 2026 link to view your response sheet.
  5. Download and save the Answer Key PDF on your device to verify your answers and estimate your score.

Original Application Process

Preserved for recruitment history and reference after the application stage closes.

  1. Visit the official CONCOR recruitment portal at concorindia.co.in.
  2. Read Advt. No. 05/2026 carefully to check post-wise eligibility.
  3. Register online with a valid email ID and mobile number.
  4. Fill in personal, academic, and work experience details accurately.
  5. Upload scanned photograph, signature, and supporting documents.
  6. Pay the applicable fee online (Rs.750 + GST for MT; Rs.500 + GST for AO; Nil for SC/ST/PwBD).
  7. Submit the application form and save the confirmation receipt.
