About This Recruitment

Container Corporation of India Limited (CONCOR) Recruitment 2026, Advertisement No. 05/2026, covers 77 vacancies across Management Trainee and Assistant Officer disciplines. The online application window runs from 31 August 2026 to 30 September 2026.

The recruitment includes Commercial & Operations, Accounts, Compliance, Technical, MIS, Civil and Personnel & Administration disciplines. Qualification and experience requirements vary by post, so candidates should check the exact discipline before applying.

Management Trainee and Assistant Officer posts have different age ceilings and application fees. Candidates should keep educational certificates, experience proof where required, category documents, photograph, signature and valid identity documents ready before submitting the online form.
