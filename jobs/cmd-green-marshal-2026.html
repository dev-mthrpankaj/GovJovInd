Complete context

About This Recruitment

CMD Green Marshal Recruitment 2026 is being conducted by the Centre for Management Development (CMD) on behalf of the Directorate of Tourism, Government of Kerala. The recruitment is for 162 Green Marshal positions to be deployed at tourist destinations across Kerala.

The engagement is on a daily-wage basis for one year with remuneration of ₹810 per day. Candidates must have passed SSLC, be 20 to 45 years old as on 1 September 2026, and choose one district while applying.

Online applications are accepted from 5 September 2026 at 10:00 AM to 11 September 2026 at 5:00 PM. Candidates should read the official notification, keep qualification and identity documents ready, and submit the form within the prescribed window.
