Complete context

About This Recruitment

BHEL Artisan Recruitment 2026 is a recruitment drive by Bharat Heavy Electricals Limited (BHEL) to engage 660 Artisan Grade IV personnel across its manufacturing units. The official notification (Advt. No. 01/2026) was released on 13 October 2026.

The online application window is open from 21 October 2026 to 18 November 2026. Candidates can apply for only one manufacturing unit and one trade. The Computer Based Test (CBT) is tentatively scheduled for 11 December 2026.

The recruitment offers regular pay scale of Rs.29,500 to Rs.65,000 per month after one year as a Temporary Artisan at Rs.24,000 per month. Selection is based on CBT, Document Verification, Skill Test, and Medical Examination.

Eligibility requires Class 10 pass plus ITI (NTC) and National Apprenticeship Certificate (NAC) in the relevant trade. The age limit is 27 years for General/EWS, 30 years for OBC-NCL, and 32 years for SC/ST as on 01 October 2026.

Eligibility Criteria

Age Limit

27 years for General/EWS, 30 years for OBC-NCL, 32 years for SC/ST as on 01 October 2026.

Age relaxation applicable for SC/ST (5 years), OBC-NCL (3 years), PwBD (10-15 years), Ex-Servicemen, and others as per BHEL rules.

Education

Class 10 pass plus National Trade Certificate (NTC/ITI) and National Apprenticeship Certificate (NAC) in the relevant trade.

Minimum 60% marks for General/OBC and 55% for SC/ST in ITI and NAC. Certificates must be issued up to 01 October 2026.

What to do at the current stage

How to Apply for BHEL Artisan Recruitment 2026

  1. Visit the official BHEL careers website at careers.bhel.in.
  2. Read the official notification (Advt. No. 01/2026) carefully.
  3. Click on the online application link for Artisan Grade IV.
  4. Register with a valid email ID and mobile number.
  5. Fill the application form with personal, educational and trade details.
  6. Upload scanned photograph, signature and required certificates.
  7. Pay the application fee online (Rs.1,072 for General/OBC/EWS, Rs.472 for SC/ST/PwD/ESM).
  8. Submit the form and download the confirmation page.
