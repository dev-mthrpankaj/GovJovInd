Complete context

About This Recruitment

RRB Railway Section Controller Recruitment 2026 (CEN 03/2026) is a Railway Recruitment Board recruitment drive to fill 119 Section Controller posts across Indian Railways. The official notification was published in July 2026, and the online application window remained open from 15 July 2026 to 14 August 2026.

The Computer Based Test (CBT) was conducted on 12-13 October 2026. The provisional answer key was released on 16 October 2026. Candidates who appeared in the CBT can now download the answer key and estimate their expected scores before the result is declared.

Eligibility requires a Graduation Degree in any stream from a recognised university. The age limit is 20 to 33 years as on 01 July 2026, with relaxation for reserved categories as per RRB rules. The application fee was Rs.500 for General/OBC/EWS candidates and Rs.250 for SC/ST/PwBD candidates, with a partial refund after appearing in the Stage I exam.

The selection process includes the Computer Based Test (CBT) followed by Document Verification and Medical Examination as per the official notification. The final merit list will be prepared based on CBT performance and subsequent stages.

What to do at the current stage

How to Download Answer Key

  1. Open the official RRB answer key portal from Important Links.
  2. Enter your Registration Number and Date of Birth.
  3. Complete the CAPTCHA verification.
  4. View and download the provisional answer key PDF.
  5. Compare your responses and calculate your expected score.
  6. If an objection window is available, raise objections before the last date.

Original Application Process

Preserved for recruitment history and reference after the application stage closes.

  1. Visit the official RRB application portal at rrbapply.gov.in.
  2. Read the official CEN 03/2026 notification carefully.
  3. Register on the RRB portal with valid email ID and mobile number.
  4. Fill the online application form with accurate personal and educational details.
  5. Upload photograph, signature and required documents.
  6. Pay the applicable examination fee online.
  7. Submit the form and save the confirmation page for future reference.
