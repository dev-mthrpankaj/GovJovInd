Complete context

About This Recruitment

RRB Section Controller Recruitment 2026 under CEN 03/2026 is a Railway Recruitment Board drive to fill 119 Section Controller posts (Level 6, 7th CPC) across various zonal railways. The official notification was published on 14 July 2026, and the online application window remained open from 15 July 2026 to 14 August 2026.

The Computer Based Test (CBT) is scheduled for 12-13 October 2026. The exam city and date intimation slip was released on 02 October 2026, and the admit card (e-call letter) was released on 08 October 2026. The Computer Based Aptitude Test (CBAT) will follow as the next stage for shortlisted candidates.

Basic eligibility is a Bachelor's degree in any discipline from a recognised university. The age limit is 20 to 33 years as on 01 July 2026, with relaxation for reserved categories as per RRB rules. The post carries an A-2 medical standard and involves operational railway duties.

The selection process includes CBT, CBAT, Document Verification, and Medical Examination. This page will continue to carry every later stage including answer key, result, CBAT admit card, document verification, medical and final result without changing the URL.
