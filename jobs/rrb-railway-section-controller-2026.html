Complete context

About This Recruitment

RRB Railway Section Controller Recruitment 2026 is a Railway Recruitment Board (RRB) recruitment drive to fill 119 Section Controller posts across various Zonal Railways in India. The official notification was released under CEN No. 03/2026 in July 2026, and the online application window remained open from 15 July 2026 to 14 August 2026.

The Computer Based Test (CBT) is scheduled to be conducted on 12-13 October 2026. The exam city intimation slip was released on 2 October 2026, allowing candidates to check their allotted exam city and date. The admit card or e-call letter is expected to be released around 8 October 2026, approximately 4 days before the exam.

The selection process includes a single-stage CBT, followed by a Computer Based Aptitude Test (CBAT), Document Verification, and Medical Examination. Selected candidates will be placed in Level 6 of the 7th CPC Pay Matrix with an initial basic pay of Rs. 35,400.

Basic eligibility is a Graduate Degree in any stream from a recognised university. The age limit is 20 to 33 years as on 01 July 2026, with relaxation for reserved categories as per Railway rules. A-2 Medical Standard is mandatory for this operational railway post.
