Complete context

About This Recruitment

The Delhi Subordinate Services Selection Board (DSSSB) has released Advertisement No. 05/2026 for recruitment to 25 posts of Sweeper/Safai Karamchari and Chowkidar in the District & Sessions Courts, Delhi. Both are Group 'C' non-gazetted posts in the pay scale of Rs. 5,200–20,200 with a Grade Pay of Rs. 2,000.

The official notification was released on 09 October 2026. The online application window opens on 13 October 2026 at 12:00 Noon and closes on 11 November 2026 at 11:00 PM. Candidates must complete their registration on the DSSSB online portal (dsssbonline.nic.in) and pay the application fee before the deadline.

Eligibility requires a Matriculation (Class 10) pass from a recognised board. The age limit is 18 to 27 years as on 01 June 2026, with age relaxation applicable for SC, ST, OBC, PwBD and Ex-Servicemen candidates as per government rules. The selection process comprises an Objective/MCQ Test (100 marks) followed by an Interview (15 marks), and subsequently Document Verification and Medical Examination as applicable.

What to do at the current stage

How to Apply Online

  1. Visit the official DSSSB online application portal at dsssbonline.nic.in.
  2. Complete the One Time Registration (OARS) using Class 10th roll number, year of passing and basic personal details.
  3. Log in using your registered credentials (Date of Birth, Class 10th Roll No, Passing Year and Password).
  4. Select Post Code 46/26 (Sweeper/Safai Karamchari) or Post Code 47/26 (Chowkidar).
  5. Fill in your educational qualifications, communication address and category details carefully.
  6. Upload scanned copies of photograph, signature, left thumb impression and right thumb impression as per specifications.
  7. Pay the application fee of Rs. 100 online through SBI e-pay (exempted for SC, ST, PwBD, ESM and Female candidates).
  8. Submit the form and save/print the confirmation page for future reference.
