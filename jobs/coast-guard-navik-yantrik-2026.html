Complete context

About This Recruitment

Join Indian Coast Guard (Ministry of Defence) is recruiting for CGEPT 01/2027 and 02/2027 batches to fill 190 Navik (General Duty) and Yantrik posts. The official notification was released in October 2026, and online applications were accepted from 06 October 2026 to 21 October 2026.

The recruitment covers Navik (General Duty) and Yantrik (Mechanical, Electrical and Electronics) posts. The Stage 1 Computer Based Test is scheduled for 17-18 November 2026. The exam city slip was released on 06 November 2026, allowing candidates to check their allotted city and plan travel.

Admit cards will be available 48-72 hours before the exam date. Candidates must carry a printed admit card, a valid original photo ID and the documents specified in the official notification. The Stage 2 physical fitness test, document verification and medical examination will follow as per the official schedule.

Basic eligibility for Navik (General Duty) is 10+2 with Physics and Mathematics. Yantrik posts require Class 10 plus a relevant Engineering Diploma. The age limit and date of birth range vary by post and are detailed in the official notification.

What to do at the current stage

How to Check Exam City

  1. Open the CGEPT candidate portal from Important Links.
  2. Log in using your registration number and password.
  3. Open the exam city intimation slip to see your allotted city and exam date.
  4. Download and save the slip for travel planning.
  5. Remember that the exam city slip is not the admit card; carry a printed admit card to the centre.
  6. Admit card will be available separately 48-72 hours before the exam date.

Original Application Process

Preserved for recruitment history and reference after the application stage closes.

  1. Visit the official CGEPT portal at cgept.parikshasaarthi.in.
  2. Register with a valid email ID and mobile number.
  3. Fill the online application form with personal and educational details.
  4. Upload photograph, signature and required documents.
  5. Pay the applicable fee online.
  6. Submit the form and save the confirmation page.

Yes. Indian Coast Guard released the CGEPT 01/2027 and 02/2027 exam city slip on 06 November 2026. Candidates can log in to the official candidate portal to check their allotted exam city and exam date.

The admit card for the 17 November 2026 Stage 1 exam will be available from 15 November 2026, and for the 18 November 2026 exam from 16 November 2026. Candidates should download it 48-72 hours before their exam date.

The Stage 1 Computer Based Test will be held on 17 and 18 November 2026. Candidates must check their admit card for the exact shift timing and exam centre address.

A total of 190 vacancies are advertised under CGEPT 01/2027 for Navik (General Duty) and Yantrik posts. The break-up includes 130 Navik GD, 28 Yantrik Mechanical, 23 Yantrik Electrical and 09 Yantrik Electronics posts.

The online application window closed on 21 October 2026 at 5:30 PM. Candidates who submitted their forms before the deadline can now check the exam city slip and prepare for the Stage 1 CBT.

Navik (General Duty) candidates need 10+2 with Physics and Mathematics. Yantrik candidates need Class 10 plus a Diploma in Mechanical, Electrical, Electronics or Telecommunication Engineering. Post-wise eligibility is detailed in the official notification.
