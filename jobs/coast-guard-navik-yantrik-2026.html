Complete context

About This Recruitment

Indian Coast Guard (ICG), under the Ministry of Defence, has invited online applications for the Coast Guard Enrolled Persons Test (CGEPT) 01/2027 and 02/2027 batches to fill 365 vacancies for Navik (General Duty), Navik (Domestic Branch), Yantrik (Mechanical/Electrical/Electronics) and Hygienist-I posts. The official notification was released on 06 October 2026, and the application window remained open from 06 October 2026 to 21 October 2026 (up to 05:30 PM).

The selection process involves Stage-I Computer Based Examination (CBE), Stage-II Physical Fitness Test (PFT) and assessment, Stage-III document verification and medical examination, followed by training at INS Chilka. The Stage-I exam city intimation slip was released on 06 November 2026, and the computer-based test is scheduled for 17-18 November 2026. The e-admit card with detailed exam centre address and shift timings will be available for download 48 to 72 hours before the exam.

Basic eligibility is 10+2 (Intermediate) with Physics and Mathematics for Navik (GD), Class 10th for Navik (DB), Class 10th with a Diploma in the relevant engineering trade for Yantrik, and Class 10th or ITI for Hygienist-I. The age limit is generally 18 to 22 years, with post-specific date-of-birth criteria and upper-age relaxation for SC, ST and OBC (NCL) candidates as per Indian Coast Guard rules.
