About This Recruitment

Bank of India Specialist Officer Recruitment 2026 is a recruitment drive for 205 Specialist Officer vacancies. The available vacancy distribution includes Chief Manager, Senior Manager, Manager / Law Officer / Fire Officer / Security Officer, and Officer / Civil Engineer levels.

The online application window is open from 10 September 2026 to 25 September 2026. Eligibility is post-specific and can require graduation, post-graduation or engineering qualifications together with prescribed professional experience. The listed age limits vary by post, broadly from 25 to 42 years as on 1 August 2026.

Candidates should read the official notification and the separate post-wise eligibility annexure before applying, because qualification discipline, experience and age conditions differ across Specialist Officer posts.
