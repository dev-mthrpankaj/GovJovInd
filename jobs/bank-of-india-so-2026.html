Complete context

About This Recruitment

Bank of India Specialist Officer (SO) Recruitment 2026 under Project No. 2026-27/02 (Notice dated 01 August 2026) is a nationwide recruitment drive to fill 205 regular officer posts across four grades: 10 Chief Manager (Scale IV), 49 Senior Manager (Scale III), 118 Manager, Law Officer, Fire Officer and Security Officer (Scale II), and 28 Officer and Civil Engineer (Scale I) positions.

The online application process started on 10 September 2026 and closed on 05 October 2026 after an extension from the original 25 September 2026 deadline. Bank of India released the online examination call letter on 18 November 2026 through the IBPS portal, and the computer-based examination is scheduled for 27 November 2026.

Shortlisted candidates from the online examination will be called for a Personal Interview carrying 100 marks, followed by Document Verification and Final Merit List preparation. Basic pay across Scale I to Scale IV ranges from Rs.48,480 to Rs.1,20,940 per month along with applicable allowances.

Eligibility requires Graduation, Post Graduation, Engineering, Law, or Fire Safety degrees in the relevant stream with post-qualification experience as specified in Annexure I. The age limit is 21 to 42 years (post-wise) as on 01 August 2026, with category-wise relaxation as per Government of India rules.

What to do at the current stage

How to Download Bank of India SO Admit Card 2026

  1. Open the official IBPS call letter download link from Important Links.
  2. Select your preferred language (English or Hindi) on the login page.
  3. Enter your Registration Number or Roll Number and Password or Date of Birth (DD-MM-YY).
  4. Type the captcha security code shown on the screen and click Login.
  5. Verify your name, photograph, post applied, exam date (27 November 2026), reporting time, and test centre address.
  6. Download the PDF and print two clear copies to carry with a valid original photo ID and its photocopy on exam day.

Original Application Process

Preserved for recruitment history and reference after the application stage closes.

  1. Visit the official Bank of India website at bankofindia.bank.in and open the Career section.
  2. Open the Project No. 2026-27/02 Specialist Officer recruitment link on the IBPS registration portal.
  3. Complete new registration using a valid email ID and mobile number to generate Registration Number and Password.
  4. Log in and fill in personal, category, educational qualification, and work experience details.
  5. Upload scanned photograph, signature, left thumb impression, handwritten declaration, and required certificates.
  6. Pay the applicable application fee (Rs.1180 for General/OBC/EWS or Rs.175 for SC/ST/PwBD) online.
  7. Submit the application form and save the confirmation printout for future stages.

Quick answers

Frequently Asked Questions

Yes, Bank of India released the Specialist Officer (SO) Admit Card on 18 November 2026 for the online examination under Project No. 2026-27/02. Registered candidates can download their call letter through the official IBPS login portal until 27 November 2026.

The Bank of India Specialist Officer (SO) online examination is scheduled to be conducted on 27 November 2026 across designated test centres. Candidates must verify their exact exam shift, reporting time, and venue address printed on the admit card.

Candidates can download the Bank of India SO Admit Card 2026 online via the official IBPS call letter portal linked in Important Links. Enter your Registration Number or Roll Number along with your Password or Date of Birth and captcha code to download and print the PDF.

Bank of India has advertised a total of 205 Specialist Officer vacancies under Project No. 2026-27/02 across four officer grades. These include 10 Chief Manager (Scale IV), 49 Senior Manager (Scale III), 118 Manager (Scale II), and 28 Officer (Scale I) posts.

The online application window for Bank of India SO Recruitment 2026 closed on 05 October 2026 after an extension from the initial 25 September 2026 deadline. Applications opened on 10 September 2026, and the online fee payment window also closed on 05 October 2026.

Bank of India Specialist Officer basic pay ranges from Rs.48,480 to Rs.1,20,940 per month depending on the officer scale. Scale I offers Rs.48,480-85,920, Scale II Rs.64,820-93,960, Scale III Rs.85,920-1,05,280, and Scale IV Rs.1,02,300-1,20,940 plus allowances.

Indian citizens holding a Graduation, Post Graduation, Engineering, Law, or Fire Safety degree from a recognised university with relevant post-qualification experience were eligible for Bank of India SO Recruitment 2026. Exact qualifications vary by stream in Annexure I.

The post-wise age limit for Bank of India SO Recruitment 2026 is 21 to 42 years as on 01 August 2026 across Scale I to Scale IV posts. Upper age relaxation is applicable for SC, ST, OBC, PwBD, and Ex-Servicemen candidates as per Government of India guidelines.

General, OBC, and EWS candidates paid Rs.1180 (including GST) as the application fee for Bank of India SO Recruitment 2026. SC, ST, and PwBD candidates paid Rs.175 as intimation charges through online payment modes before the 05 October 2026 deadline.

The Bank of India Specialist Officer selection process consists of an Online Examination, a Personal Interview carrying 100 marks, and Document Verification. Final merit is prepared using 60 percent weightage for the online exam and 40 percent for the personal interview.

Candidates appearing for the 27 November 2026 exam must carry a printed copy of the Bank of India SO Admit Card 2026, a valid original photo ID such as Aadhaar, PAN, Passport, or Driving Licence, a photocopy of the ID, and recent passport-size photographs.

The official website of Bank of India is bankofindia.bank.in, where the Project No. 2026-27/02 notification and Annexure I are hosted. The admit card download portal is hosted on the official IBPS recruitment platform at ibpsreg.ibps.in.
