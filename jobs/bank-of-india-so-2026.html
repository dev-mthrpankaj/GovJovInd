Complete context

About This Recruitment

Bank of India (BOI) has released the Specialist Officer (SO) Recruitment 2026 notification under Project No. 2026-27/02 for 205 Specialist Officer vacancies across Scale I to Scale IV. The recruitment covers multiple specialist streams including IT, Law, Civil Engineering, Fire Safety, Security, and other technical disciplines.

The online application window was originally scheduled from 10 September 2026 to 25 September 2026, and the last date has now been extended to 05 October 2026. The application fee payment deadline is also 05 October 2026. Candidates who have not applied yet should complete the online form before the revised deadline to avoid last-minute server issues.

Eligibility varies by post. Candidates generally need Graduation / Post Graduation / B.E. / B.Tech / LLB / CA or an equivalent qualification along with the prescribed post-qualification work experience. Age is calculated as on 01 August 2026, with a minimum of 25 years and a maximum of 42 years depending on the post. Selection will be through an online examination and/or personal interview as prescribed in the official notification, followed by document verification and medical examination.
