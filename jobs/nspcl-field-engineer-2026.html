Complete context

About This Recruitment

NTPC-SAIL Power Company Limited (NSPCL), a joint venture of Maharatna PSUs NTPC Limited and SAIL, has invited online applications under Advertisement No. 03/2026 to fill 17 Field Engineer vacancies. The positions cover Power Station O&M (12 posts), Power Station Civil (4 posts), and Power Station Safety (1 post) across NSPCL business units and projects including Bhilai, Durgapur, and Rourkela.

The engagement is on a Fixed Term Engagement (FTE) basis for an initial period of up to 3 years, extendable by up to 2 additional years based on organizational requirements and performance. Selected candidates receive a consolidated monthly remuneration of Rs.90,000 along with company accommodation or HRA, applicable night shift allowance, and medical facilities for self and eligible family members.

Online registration opened on 20 November 2026 at 10:00 AM and will remain active until 04 December 2026 at 11:59 PM. Candidates must hold a recognised Engineering Degree with at least 60% marks (passing marks for SC/ST/PwBD) and a minimum of 2 years of relevant post-qualification experience. Eligible applicants will be shortlisted for a Personal Interview based on merit and post requirements.

Eligibility Criteria

Age Limit

Maximum 32 years for Field Engineer (O&M) and 45 years for Field Engineer (Civil & Safety) as on 04 December 2026.

Upper age relaxation of 5 years for SC/ST, 3 years for OBC (NCL), 10 years for PwBD, and Ex-Servicemen as per Government of India guidelines.

Education

Recognised Engineering Degree in the relevant discipline with at least 60% marks (passing marks for SC/ST/PwBD) and a minimum of 2 years of post-qualification experience.

For Field Engineer (Safety), a Diploma, Advance Diploma, or PG Diploma in Industrial Safety is additionally required. All qualifications and experience are calculated as on 04 December 2026.

Nationality / Other

Only Indian Nationals are eligible to apply. Candidates must possess sound health and clear the pre-employment medical examination at NSPCL notified or empanelled hospitals before joining.

Refer to official notification for detailed conditions.

What to do at the current stage

How to Apply Online for NSPCL Field Engineer 2026

  1. Read the official NSPCL Advertisement No. 03/2026 PDF carefully to verify discipline-wise qualification and experience requirements.
  2. Open the official NSPCL online application portal from the Important Links section below.
  3. Register using a valid email ID and active mobile number to generate your Unique Registration Number.
  4. Log in and fill in personal, educational, category, and post-qualification work experience details accurately.
  5. Upload scanned copies of your recent passport-size photograph, signature, and required supporting certificates.
  6. Pay the Rs.300 application fee online (if applicable; SC/ST/PwBD/Women/Ex-Servicemen candidates are exempted).
  7. Submit the application before 04 December 2026 (11:59 PM) and download the system-generated application slip for future reference.
