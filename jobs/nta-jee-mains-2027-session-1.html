Complete context

About This Recruitment

The National Testing Agency (NTA) has released the official notification for JEE Main 2027 Session 1. This examination is the gateway for admission to undergraduate engineering, architecture, and planning programs at NITs, IIITs, CFTIs, and other participating institutions across India.

The online application window is open from 24 October 2026 to 22 November 2026. Candidates can apply with late fees from 23 to 26 November 2026, and the application correction window will remain open from 27 to 29 November 2026. The exam city details will be released on 30 December 2026, and the admit card is expected on 13 January 2027.

The JEE Main 2027 Session 1 examination is scheduled to be held on 20, 21, 22, 23, 24 and 28 January 2027. Session 2 registration will begin from 05 January 2027 to 03 February 2027. Candidates who wish to improve their scores can appear in both sessions.

Eligibility requires candidates to have passed or appeared for the 10+2 Intermediate examination from any recognised board in India. Students who passed Class 12 in 2025 or 2026, as well as those appearing in 2027, are eligible to apply.

What to do at the current stage

How to Apply Online

  1. Visit the official NTA JEE Main website at jeemain.nta.nic.in.
  2. Click on the 'NTA JEE Main 2027 Apply Online' link.
  3. Register using a valid email ID and mobile number.
  4. Login using the registration ID and password.
  5. Fill the application form with personal, educational, and category details.
  6. Upload scanned documents (photo, signature, etc.).
  7. Pay the application fee through online mode.
  8. Submit the form and download or print the confirmation page.
