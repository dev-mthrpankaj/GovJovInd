Complete context

About This Recruitment

SSC MTS Havaldar 2025 is a Staff Selection Commission recruitment drive to fill 8,021 posts — 6,810 Multi-Tasking Staff (Non-Technical) posts and 1,211 Havaldar posts in the Central Board of Indirect Taxes and Customs (CBIC) and Central Bureau of Narcotics (CBN). The official notification was published on 26 June 2025, and the online application window remained open from 26 June 2025 to 24 July 2025.

The Computer Based Test (CBT) was conducted from 4 February 2026 to 15 February 2026 across multiple shifts. The provisional answer key was released on 3 March 2026 with an objection window open until 6 March 2026. The Paper-I result was declared on 3 August 2026, shortlisting 8,071 candidates for the Havaldar PET/PST round. An additional result was released on 7 October 2026, shortlisting 542 more candidates for PET/PST.

The Physical Efficiency Test (PET) and Physical Standard Test (PST) for Havaldar posts are scheduled from 24 August to 31 August 2026. The PET/PST admit card has been released by the respective SSC Regional Offices and can be downloaded from the official regional websites.

Basic eligibility is Class 10 (High School) passed from a recognised board. The age limit is 18 to 25 years for MTS posts and 18 to 27 years for Havaldar posts as on 01 August 2025, with relaxation for reserved categories as per SSC rules.

What to do at the current stage

How to Download PET/PST Admit Card

  1. Open the official SSC regional website or SSC login portal from Important Links.
  2. Click on the link for PET/PST admit card for MTS & Havaldar Examination 2025.
  3. Log in using Registration Number/Roll Number and Date of Birth.
  4. Download the PET/PST Admit Card PDF for the physical test round.
  5. Check your name, roll number, physical exam date, reporting time and venue.
  6. Take a clear colour printout and carry it to the physical test centre.
  7. Read the admit card notice and instructions carefully before the exam day.

Original Application Process

Preserved for recruitment history and reference after the application stage closes.

  1. Visit the official SSC recruitment website at ssc.gov.in.
  2. Read the official Notice of adv_mts_2025 carefully.
  3. Register on the SSC portal with valid email ID and mobile number.
  4. Fill the online application form with accurate personal and educational details.
  5. Upload photograph, signature and required documents.
  6. Pay the applicable examination fee online.
  7. Submit the form and save the confirmation page for future reference.
