About DSSSB Result 2026

Delhi Subordinate Service Selection Board (DSSSB) has declared the Result 2026 for Assistant Sanitary Inspector (Post Code 33/24) in Municipal Corporation of Delhi (MCD) and various other posts. The result PDFs and merit lists are now available on the official DSSSB website.

Candidates who appeared in the DSSSB 2026 written examination for posts including Assistant Teacher (Nursery), TGT Sanskrit, PGT Sanskrit, PGT Chemistry, TGT English and other posts can download their result from the direct links provided in the Important Links section.

Qualified candidates will be called for the next stage, which typically includes Document Verification (DV), medical examination and final merit list preparation. All future updates for these stages will be published on this same page without changing the URL.
