Complete context

About This Recruitment

Delhi Subordinate Services Selection Board (DSSSB) conducts recruitment for Group B and Group C posts across various departments of the Government of NCT of Delhi. The DSSSB Result 2026 covers multiple advertisements, including Advertisement 03/2024 for MTS and Advertisement 03/2026 for 1,979 posts.

The result for many posts was declared on 27 March 2026. Post-specific results, such as MTS (Post Code 812/24) on 3 September 2026 and PGT Mathematics on 1 November 2026, were released later. Candidates can check their merit list and cut-off marks on dsssb.delhi.gov.in and view individual marks on the OARS portal at dsssbonline.nic.in.

Shortlisted candidates must upload their e-dossier documents and appear for the Skill Test or Physical Test (as applicable to the post). The final selection is based on written exam, skill test, document verification, and medical examination where required.

What to do at the current stage

How to Check DSSSB Result 2026

  1. Visit the official DSSSB website at dsssb.delhi.gov.in.
  2. Go to the 'Results' or 'Latest Result' section.
  3. Find the result notice for your post code.
  4. Download the PDF and search for your roll number using Ctrl+F.
  5. Log in to the OARS portal at dsssbonline.nic.in to view your individual marks and scorecard.

Original Application Process

Preserved for recruitment history and reference after the application stage closes.

  1. Visit the official DSSSB online portal at dsssbonline.nic.in.
  2. Read the official advertisement carefully.
  3. Register with a valid email ID and mobile number.
  4. Fill the online application form with accurate personal and educational details.
  5. Upload photograph, signature and required documents.
  6. Pay the applicable examination fee online.
  7. Submit the form and save the confirmation page for future reference.
