About This Recruitment

The Delhi Subordinate Service Selection Board (DSSSB) has released the Result 2026 for various posts, including Assistant Teacher (Nursery) Female under Post Code 817/23 and TGT Sanskrit Female under Post Code 808/24. Candidates who appeared for the DSSSB examinations can now check their results and download the merit list from the official website.

This page is your permanent source for all DSSSB Result 2026 updates. As the recruitment progresses, information about document verification, medical examinations, and the final merit list will be added here.

Candidates are advised to download and save their result and scorecard for future reference. Keep your registration number, roll number, and date of birth handy to check your result.
