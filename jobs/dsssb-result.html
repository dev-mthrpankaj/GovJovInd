Complete context

About This Recruitment

DSSSB Assistant Teacher (Primary) Recruitment 2025-2026 is a Delhi Subordinate Services Selection Board drive to fill 1,169 Assistant Teacher (Primary) vacancies across the Directorate of Education (1,044 posts) and the New Delhi Municipal Council (125 posts). The official notification (Advt. No. 05/2025) was released on 10 September 2025, and online applications were accepted from 17 September 2025 to 16 October 2025.

The Computer-Based Tier-I examination was conducted in multiple shifts between 16 February 2026 and 10 April 2026. A total of 83,614 candidates appeared in the exam. The board released the marks and category-wise cut-off on 13 October 2026. Candidates who scored equal to or above the cut-off have been provisionally shortlisted for the next stage.

The next critical stage is e-dossier submission, which will remain open from 19 October 2026 to 30 October 2026. Provisionally shortlisted candidates must upload their educational certificates, category certificates, and other supporting documents through the OARS module. The final merit list will be prepared after successful document verification.

Basic eligibility includes a Senior Secondary qualification with at least 50% marks and a two-year Diploma in Elementary Education (or equivalent), along with a valid CTET certificate. The age limit is up to 30 years as on the last date of application, with relaxations for reserved categories as per DSSSB rules.

Eligibility Criteria

Age Limit

Maximum 30 years as on the last date of application (16 October 2025). Minimum age is 18 years.

Education

Candidates must have passed Hindi, Urdu, Punjabi, or English as a subject at the secondary level.

  1. Visit the official DSSSB OARS portal at dsssbonline.nic.in.
  2. Log in using your Registration ID and Password.
  3. Open the Assistant Teacher (Primary) recruitment section for Post Code 802/25.
  4. Click on the marks/result link to view your scorecard.
  5. Download and save the marks PDF for future reference.

Preserved for recruitment history and reference after the application stage closes.

Yes. DSSSB released the Assistant Teacher (Primary) Tier-I marks and cut-off for Post Code 802/25 on 13 October 2026. Candidates can check their marks on the official OARS portal at dsssbonline.nic.in.

A total of 1,169 Assistant Teacher (Primary) vacancies are being filled across the Directorate of Education (1,044 posts) and the New Delhi Municipal Council (125 posts).

The category-wise cut-off out of 200 marks is: UR – 172.06, EWS – 160.95, OBC – 160.09, SC – 158.01, ST – 139.64, PwBD Cat A – 149.73, PwBD Cat B – 132.47, PwBD Cat C – 152.75, PwBD Cat D & E – 118.81.

Provisionally shortlisted candidates must upload their documents through the e-dossier module on the OARS portal. The e-dossier submission window is open from 19 October 2026 to 30 October 2026.

Candidates must upload educational and professional certificates, degree certificates and marksheets, caste certificates (if applicable), experience certificates (if required), government service or ex-servicemen documents (if applicable), admit cards, and any other documents specified in Notice No. 382.

Candidates must have Senior Secondary with at least 50% marks and a 2-year Diploma in Elementary Education (or equivalent), along with a valid CTET certificate. For NDMC posts, 12th pass with 50% and a 2-year Diploma in Primary Education is required.

The maximum age limit is 30 years as on the last date of application (16 October 2025). The minimum age is 18 years. Age relaxation applies for SC, ST, OBC, PwBD and Ex-Servicemen as per rules.

General, OBC and EWS candidates paid Rs.100. Women, SC, ST, PwBD and Ex-Servicemen candidates were exempted from the fee. Payment was accepted through SBI e-pay only.

Selected candidates will receive a salary in the pay scale of Rs.35,400 to Rs.1,12,400 (Pay Level 6). The approximate in-hand salary ranges from Rs.64,000 to Rs.65,000 per month.

The selection process includes a Computer Based Test (Tier-I), e-Dossier submission, Document Verification, and Final Merit List preparation.

The official website of the Delhi Subordinate Services Selection Board is https://dsssb.delhi.gov.in/. The OARS portal for results and e-dossier is at https://dsssbonline.nic.in.
