Complete context

About This Recruitment

Bihar Technical Service Commission (BTSC) has released the Fishery Extension Officer Recruitment 2026 notification for 231 posts. The online application window is open from 24 September 2026 to 23 October 2026.

This recruitment is for candidates holding a Bachelor Degree in Fisheries Science from a recognized Agricultural University in India. The post offers Pay Level-7 under the Bihar state government and is a permanent support-staff opportunity in the fisheries department.

Candidates should keep their educational certificates, photo, signature, identity proof, category documents, and active mobile number and email ID ready before starting the online application form.
