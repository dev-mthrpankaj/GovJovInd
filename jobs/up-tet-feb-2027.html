Complete context

About This Recruitment

The Uttar Pradesh Education Service Selection Commission (UPESSC) released the official short notice for the Uttar Pradesh Teacher Eligibility Test (UPTET) February 2027 under Advertisement No. 10/2026 on 18 November 2026. UPTET is a state-level eligibility examination conducted for candidates seeking teaching eligibility at the Primary Level (Class 1–5) and Upper Primary Level (Class 6–8) in Uttar Pradesh.

As per the official schedule, the detailed notification and online application process will begin on 24 November 2026 after mandatory One Time Registration (OTR) on upessc.up.gov.in. The last date to submit the online form and pay the examination fee is 23 December 2026, while the online form correction window will remain open until 27 December 2026.

UPESSC has scheduled the UPTET February 2027 written examination for 26 and 27 February 2027. Admit cards will be issued online before the exam, followed by the provisional answer key and result declaration on the official commission website.

Eligible graduates with recognised teacher training qualifications such as BTC, D.El.Ed, B.El.Ed, B.Ed, or RCI Special Education (including final-year trainees) can apply according to level-specific norms. Application fees are Rs.1000 for General, EWS, and OBC candidates, Rs.500 for SC and ST candidates, and Rs.300 for PH candidates.
