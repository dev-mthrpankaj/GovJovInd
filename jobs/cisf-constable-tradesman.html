Complete context

About This Recruitment

The Central Industrial Security Force (CISF) published the Constable Tradesmen Examination 2025 notification to recruit 1161 eligible candidates for various trades across India. The online application process was open from 05 March 2025 to 03 April 2025.

The selection process for CISF Tradesman involves multiple stages. The Physical Efficiency Test (PET), Physical Standard Test (PST), Document Verification (DV), and Trade Test have already been successfully completed in late 2025. Candidates who qualified these initial stages are now eligible for the written examination.

CISF released the Exam City Details for the written examination on 17 November 2026. The written examination is officially scheduled to take place on 01 December 2026. Candidates are advised to check their allocated exam city online and arrange their travel accordingly. The final Admit Card with exact venue details will be available shortly before the exam date.

To be eligible for final selection, candidates must pass the written examination followed by a Detailed Medical Examination (DME). Minimum educational qualification required is 10th Standard (Matriculation) from a recognized board, along with meeting the strict physical standards.

What to do at the current stage

How to Check Exam City

  1. Click on the Check Exam City Details link provided in the Important Links section.
  2. Enter your Enrollment Number, Registration Number, or Date of Birth on the login portal.
  3. View and download your Exam City slip.
  4. Check your allocated test city and exact exam date (01 December 2026).
  5. Note that this is not the final admit card; the final admit card with the venue will be released later.

Original Application Process

Preserved for recruitment history and reference after the application stage closes.

  1. Visit the official CISF recruitment portal.
  2. Click on the Constable Tradesman 2025 apply link.
  3. Fill in personal and educational details.
  4. Upload scanned photograph and signature.
  5. Pay the application fee of Rs.100 (if applicable).
  6. Submit the form and print the confirmation page.
