Complete context

About This Recruitment

Central Industrial Security Force (CISF) initiated the CISF Constable Tradesman Recruitment 2025 under Advertisement Constable/Tradesmen-2024 to fill 1,161 Group C posts across India. Online applications were accepted from 05 March 2025 to 03 April 2025 for 13 trades including Cook (493), Washerman (262), Barber (199), Sweeper (152), Tailor (23), Cobbler (9), Carpenter (9), Electrician (4), Mali (4), Painter (2), MP Attendant (2), Welder (1) and Charge Mechanic (1).

The Physical Efficiency Test (PET) and Physical Standard Test (PST) commenced on 26 September 2025, followed by the PET/PST result declaration on 02 December 2025. Qualified candidates then appeared for the Trade Test and Document Verification starting from 11 December 2025.

For the next stage, Central Industrial Security Force (CISF) released the exam city intimation on 17 November 2026 and issued the Written Exam Admit Card on 27 November 2026. The written examination is scheduled for 01 December 2026, and candidates must carry a printed hall ticket along with a valid original government photo ID to their allotted centre.

Eligibility requires Matriculation (10th Class) or equivalent from a recognised board, with preference given to Industrial Training Institute (ITI) trained candidates for skilled trades. The age limit is 18 to 23 years as on 01 August 2025 with category-wise relaxation as per Government of India rules, and selected candidates receive Pay Level-3 salary of Rs.21,700 to Rs.69,100 per month plus allowances.

What to do at the current stage

How to Download CISF Constable Tradesman Admit Card

  1. Open the official CISF written exam admit card login portal from Important Links below.
  2. Enter your Registration Number or Enrollment Number along with your Date of Birth or Password.
  3. Complete the captcha verification and submit your credentials to view your hall ticket.
  4. Download the CISF Constable Tradesman Admit Card 2026 PDF and verify your name, roll number, exam date, shift and centre address.
  5. Take two clear printouts of the admit card and carry a valid original government photo ID to the examination centre on 01 December 2026.
  6. Reach the exam venue before the reporting time printed on the hall ticket and follow all exam-day instructions.

Original Application Process

Preserved for recruitment history and reference after the application stage closes.

  1. Visit the official CISF recruitment website at cisfrectt.cisf.gov.in.
  2. Complete one-time registration using a valid mobile number and email address.
  3. Log in to the Constable/Tradesmen-2024 recruitment portal and fill in personal, trade and educational details.
  4. Upload scanned copies of the recent photograph, signature and required certificates.
  5. Pay the Rs.100 application fee online (if applicable) and verify all entered details.
  6. Submit the application form and download the confirmation copy for future stages.

A total of 1,161 vacancies were advertised under CISF Constable Tradesman Recruitment 2025 across 13 trades, including 493 Cook, 262 Washerman, 199 Barber, 152 Sweeper, 23 Tailor and other skilled trade posts.

Candidates must have passed Matriculation (10th Class) or an equivalent examination from a recognised board. For skilled trades, candidates with Industrial Training Institute (ITI) training are given preference as per CISF rules.
