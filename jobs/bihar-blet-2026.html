Complete context

About This Recruitment

Bihar Library Eligibility Test (BLET) 2026 is conducted by the Bihar School Examination Board (BSEB) for candidates who wish to qualify as school librarians in Bihar. The official notification was released in September 2026, and the online application window opened on 01 October 2026.

The application last date has been extended to 31 October 2026. Candidates must possess a Graduation Degree in B.Lib / B.Lib.I.Sc / BLIS (Bachelor of Library & Information Science) from a recognised university in India. The age limit is 37 years for General Male and 40 years for General Female candidates as on 01 August 2026, with relaxation for reserved categories.

The BLET 2026 examination is scheduled to be held from 19 November to 10 December 2026. Admit cards will be released before the examination. This page will be updated with every official stage including admit card, exam, answer key, result and final merit list.

What to do at the current stage

How to Apply for Bihar BLET 2026

  1. Visit the official BLET application portal at sletregistration.cbrt.co.in.
  2. Click on the Bihar Library Eligibility Test BLET Apply Online link.
  3. Register using a valid email ID and mobile number.
  4. Login using your registration ID and password.
  5. Fill the application form with personal, educational, and category details.
  6. Upload scanned documents including photograph and signature.
  7. Pay the application fee through online mode.
  8. Submit the form and download/print the confirmation page for future reference.
