About Bihar BLET 2026

Bihar Library Eligibility Test (BLET) 2026 is conducted by the Bihar School Examination Board (BSEB) for candidates who wish to qualify as librarians in Bihar. Unlike a recruitment exam, BLET is an eligibility test — qualifying it makes candidates eligible to apply for librarian posts in schools, colleges and other institutions across Bihar.

The online application window for BLET 2026 is open from 1 October 2026 to 21 October 2026. The application fee is Rs.960 for General/Other State candidates and Rs.760 for SC/ST/PH candidates who are Bihar domiciles. The examination date will be announced separately by BSEB.

Eligibility requires a Graduation degree in B.Lib / B.Lib.I.Sc / BLIS (Bachelor of Library & Information Science) from a recognised university. The maximum age limit is 37 years for male candidates and 40 years for female candidates as on 1 August 2026. Candidates should read the official notification carefully before applying.
