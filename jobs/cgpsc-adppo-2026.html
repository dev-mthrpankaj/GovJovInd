Complete context

About This Recruitment

CGPSC Assistant District Public Prosecution Officer (ADPPO) Recruitment 2026 is a Chhattisgarh Public Service Commission recruitment drive to fill 15 Assistant District Public Prosecution Officer posts in the Home Department. The official notification (Advertisement No. 03/2026/Exam) was published on 17 June 2026, and the online application window remained open from 22 June 2026 to 21 July 2026.

The written examination was conducted on 20 September 2026 (Sunday) from 10:00 AM to 1:00 PM. The admit card for the written examination was released on 10 September 2026. The answer key and result are awaited as per the official schedule. The selection process includes a written examination followed by an interview, document verification, and medical examination.

Basic eligibility is a Bachelor's Degree in Law (LLB) from a recognised university. The age limit is 21 to 30 years as on 01 January 2026, with age relaxation for Chhattisgarh domicile candidates and reserved categories as per CGPSC rules. The pay scale is Pay Matrix Level-09 (₹38,100 – ₹1,20,400 per month).

Frequently Asked Questions

CGPSC ADPPO stands for Chhattisgarh Public Service Commission Assistant District Public Prosecution Officer.

A total of 15 vacancies for the Assistant District Public Prosecution Officer post have been announced under Advertisement No. 03/2026.

Candidates must hold a Bachelor's Degree in Law (LLB) from a university recognised by the University Grants Commission (UGC).

The age limit is 21 to 30 years as on 01 January 2026 for general candidates. Chhattisgarh domicile candidates receive a 5-year relaxation, making their upper age limit 40 years.

General and other state candidates paid Rs.400. Chhattisgarh OBC, EWS, SC, ST and PwD candidates paid Rs.300. Portal charges and GST were applicable additionally.

The selection process includes a Written Examination (300 marks), Interview (30 marks), Document Verification, and Medical Examination.

The written exam is objective type with 150 MCQs worth 300 marks. Part 1 covers General Knowledge (50 questions, 100 marks) and Part 2 covers Constitution and Law (100 questions, 200 marks). Duration is 3 hours with 1/3 negative marking for wrong answers.
