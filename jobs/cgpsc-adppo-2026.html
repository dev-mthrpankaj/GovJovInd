About This Recruitment

CGPSC Assistant District Public Prosecution Officer (ADPPO) Recruitment 2026 is a Chhattisgarh Public Service Commission recruitment drive to fill 15 Assistant District Public Prosecution Officer posts in the Home Department. The official notification (Advertisement No. 03/2026/Exam) was published on 17 June 2026, and the online application window remained open from 22 June 2026 to 21 July 2026.

The written examination was conducted on 20 September 2026 (Sunday) from 10:00 AM to 1:00 PM. The admit card for the written examination was released on 10 September 2026. The answer key and result are awaited as per the official schedule. The selection process includes a written examination followed by an interview, document verification, and medical examination.

Basic eligibility is a Bachelor's Degree in Law (LLB) from a recognised university. The age limit is 21 to 30 years as on 01 January 2026, with age relaxation for Chhattisgarh domicile candidates and reserved categories as per CGPSC rules. The pay scale is Pay Matrix Level-09 (₹38,100 – ₹1,20,400 per month).
