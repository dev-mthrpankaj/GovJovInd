Complete context

About This Recruitment

Electronics Corporation of India Limited (ECIL) has invited online applications under Advertisement No. 12/2026 for 242 one-year apprenticeship training seats at its Corporate Learning & Development Centre, Hyderabad. The recruitment covers 200 Graduate Engineer Apprentice (GEA) seats and 42 Technician (Diploma) Apprentice (TA) seats across seven engineering disciplines: ECE, CSE/IT, Mechanical, EEE, EIE, Civil, and Chemical.

Candidates who passed a B.E./B.Tech degree or a three-year Engineering Diploma in the notified branches on or after 01 April 2024 from an AICTE-approved institution or recognised university are eligible to apply. Prior registration on the National Apprenticeship Training Scheme (NATS) portal is mandatory before submitting the online application on the ECIL recruitment portal.

The online registration window started on 24 November 2026 and will close on 13 December 2026 at 11:59 PM. There is no application fee for any category. Selected Graduate Engineer Apprentices receive a monthly stipend of Rs.12,300, while Technician (Diploma) Apprentices receive Rs.10,900 per month during the one-year training period.

Shortlisted applicants based on qualifying examination marks will attend document verification from 17 December 2026 to 19 December 2026 at Hyderabad. Provisional selection offer letters will be issued by email on 23 December 2026 and 24 December 2026, and apprenticeship training will commence on 01 January 2027.

Eligibility Criteria

Age Limit

18 to 25 years as on 31 December 2026.

Upper age relaxation of 5 years for SC/ST, 3 years for OBC (NCL), and 10 years for PwD (40% and above) candidates as per ECIL rules.

Education

B.E./B.Tech degree for Graduate Engineer Apprentice (GEA) or 3-year Engineering Diploma for Technician Apprentice (TA) in ECE, CSE/IT, Mechanical, EEE, EIE, Civil, or Chemical.

Candidates must have passed the qualifying degree or diploma on or after 01 April 2024 from an AICTE-approved institute or recognised university.

Nationality / Other

Indian residents registered on the National Apprenticeship Training Scheme (NATS) portal are eligible. Candidates who have already undergone or are undergoing apprenticeship training are not eligible.

Refer to official notification for detailed conditions.

What to do at the current stage

How to Apply Online for ECIL Apprentice 2026

  1. Complete mandatory student registration on the NATS portal at nats.education.gov.in first and note your NATS enrolment ID.
  2. Open the ECIL Apprentice Apply Online portal from the Important Links section on this page.
  3. Select Advertisement No. 12/2026 and enter your personal, academic, branch and NATS registration details accurately.
  4. Upload scanned copies of your recent passport-size photograph, signature and required certificates.
  5. Verify all filled entries carefully because no application fee is required for any category.
  6. Submit the online form before 13 December 2026 and print the registered application form with the serial number for document verification.
