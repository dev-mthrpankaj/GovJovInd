Complete context

About This Recruitment

Chhattisgarh State Power Transmission Company Limited (CSPTCL) has issued Advertisement No. 01-01/HR/Recruitment/739106 for CSPTCL Various Post Recruitment 2026 to fill 1501 regular vacancies in Raipur, Chhattisgarh. The recruitment covers Assistant Engineer, Junior Engineer, Fire Officer, Security Officer, Programmer, Accounts Officer, Medical Officer, Stenographer Grade III, Data Entry Operator, Assistant Lab Technician, and other technical and support posts.

Online registration opened on 06 November 2026 and the application deadline has been extended from 26 November 2026 to 03 December 2026. Fee payment is also open until 03 December 2026 through online mode. Out of the 1501 total posts, 637 are for General, 192 for OBC, 173 for SC, and 499 for ST candidates.

Candidates aged 18 to 40 years as on 01 January 2026 who meet the post-wise educational qualifications—such as B.E./B.Tech, Fire Engineering, Graduation, BCA/B.Sc. (CS/IT), or 10+2 with DMLT/BMLT—can submit their online form on the official portal. Exam date, admit card, and result schedules will be updated on this permanent page as soon as CSPTCL announces them.
