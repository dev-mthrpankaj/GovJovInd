About This Recruitment

UPPSC Various Post Recruitment 2024 is a direct recruitment drive by the Uttar Pradesh Public Service Commission for 109 posts across multiple departments and disciplines in Uttar Pradesh. The online application window was open from 17 October 2024 to 18 November 2024.

The recruitment covers posts such as Registrar, Assistant Architect, Reader (Upacharya), Professor (Acharya), Professor Sanskrit, Professor Arabic, and Inspector (Government Office). The selection process and eligibility vary by post, so candidates are advised to verify post-specific details in the official notification.

The written examination result has now been declared. This permanent page will continue to carry every later stage — including document verification, medical examination, and final merit list — without changing the URL.
