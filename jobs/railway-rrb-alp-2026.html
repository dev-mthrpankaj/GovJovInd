Complete context

About This Recruitment

RRB Assistant Loco Pilot (ALP) Recruitment 2026 is a Railway Recruitment Board drive under CEN 01/2026 to fill 11,127 Assistant Loco Pilot posts across Indian Railways zones. The official notification was published on 14 May 2026, and online applications were accepted from 15 May 2026 to 14 June 2026.

The Computer Based Test (CBT 1) is scheduled for 3–5 November 2026. The exam city intimation slip was released on 24 October 2026, and the CBT 1 admit card was released on 30 October 2026. The selection process includes CBT 1, CBT 2, CBAT (Computer Based Aptitude Test), Document Verification and Medical Examination.

Eligibility requires 10th (Matriculation) with ITI from NCVT/SCVT in a relevant trade, or a Diploma in Mechanical/Electrical/Electronics/Automobile Engineering, or a BE/B.Tech degree in a relevant discipline. The age limit is 18 to 30 years as on 01 July 2026, with relaxation for reserved categories as per RRB rules.

The application fee is Rs. 500 for General/OBC/EWS candidates and Rs. 250 for SC/ST/Ex-Servicemen/Female/Transgender/Minorities/EBC candidates. A refund of Rs. 400 (for Gen/OBC/EWS) and Rs. 250 (for others) is provided after appearing in CBT 1.
