Complete context

About This Recruitment

The Railway Recruitment Boards (RRBs) are conducting the Assistant Loco Pilot (ALP) recruitment 2026 under Centralised Employment Notification CEN 01/2026. The detailed notification was published on 14 May 2026 for 11,127 ALP posts across Indian Railways, and the online application window remained open from 15 May 2026 to 14 June 2026.

The Computer Based Test (CBT 1) is scheduled for 3, 4 and 5 November 2026. The exam city intimation slip was released on 24 October 2026, and the CBT 1 admit card was released on 30 October 2026. Candidates must download the e-call letter from the official RRB portal and carry it to the exam centre along with a valid photo ID.

The selection process includes CBT 1, CBT 2, the Computer Based Aptitude Test (CBAT), document verification and a medical examination. The CBT 1 result, cut-off and next-stage schedule will be published on the official RRB websites after the exam.

Eligibility requires Matriculation (10th) plus an ITI certificate in a relevant trade from NCVT/SCVT, or a Diploma/Degree in Mechanical, Electrical, Electronics or Automobile Engineering. The age limit is 18 to 30 years as on 01 July 2026, with relaxation for reserved categories as per Railway Recruitment Board rules.

What to do at the current stage

How to Download RRB ALP CBT 1 Admit Card

  1. Open the official RRB admit card portal from the Important Links section.
  2. Log in using your registration number and date of birth or password.
  3. Select the RRB ALP CEN 01/2026 CBT 1 admit card link.
  4. Check your name, roll number, exam date, shift, reporting time and centre address.
  5. Download and take a clear colour printout of the e-call letter.
  6. Read the exam-day instructions printed on the admit card carefully.

Original Application Process

Preserved for recruitment history and reference after the application stage closes.

  1. Visit the official RRB application portal at rrbapply.gov.in.
  2. Read the CEN 01/2026 notification carefully.
  3. Register with a valid email ID and mobile number.
  4. Fill the online application form with accurate personal and educational details.
  5. Upload photograph, signature and required documents.
  6. Pay the applicable examination fee online.
  7. Submit the form and save the confirmation page for future reference.
