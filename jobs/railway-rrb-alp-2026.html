Complete context

About This Recruitment

Railway Recruitment Board (RRB) Assistant Loco Pilot (ALP) Recruitment 2026 (CEN 01/2026) is a Ministry of Railways recruitment drive for 14,627 Assistant Loco Pilot posts under Pay Level 2. The original notification was published on 14 May 2026, and applications were accepted from 15 May 2026 to 14 June 2026.

The Computer Based Test (CBT 1) was conducted on 3–5 November 2026. RRB released the provisional answer key, question paper and recorded responses on 12 November 2026. Candidates who appeared in CBT 1 can view their response sheet and raise objections against any question, option or key until 19 November 2026 (11:59 PM) by paying Rs.50 per question plus applicable bank charges.

A corrigendum dated 10 November 2026 increased the total vacancies from 11,127 to 14,627 (an addition of 3,500 posts). The selection process includes CBT 1, CBT 2, Computer-Based Aptitude Test (CBAT), Document Verification and Medical Examination.

Basic eligibility is 10th pass with ITI in a relevant trade, or a Diploma/Degree in Engineering. The age limit is 18 to 30 years, with relaxation for reserved categories as per railway rules.

What to do at the current stage

How to Raise Objection Against RRB ALP Answer Key

  1. Visit the official website of your regional RRB (rrb.indianrailways.gov.in).
  2. Open the CEN 01/2026 ALP CBT 1 answer key / objection link.
  3. Log in using your registration number and date of birth.
  4. View the question paper, your recorded responses and the provisional answer key.
  5. Select the question(s) you want to challenge and click the objection button.
  6. Pay Rs.50 per question plus applicable bank charges through UPI, debit card, credit card or net banking.
  7. Submit the objection before 19 November 2026, 11:59 PM and save the acknowledgement.

Original Application Process

Preserved for recruitment history and reference after the application stage closes.

  1. Visit the official RRB application portal at rrbapply.gov.in.
  2. Read the CEN 01/2026 notification carefully.
  3. Register with a valid email ID and mobile number.
  4. Fill the online application form with accurate personal and educational details.
  5. Upload photograph, signature and required documents.
  6. Pay the applicable examination fee online.
  7. Submit the form and save the confirmation page for future reference.
