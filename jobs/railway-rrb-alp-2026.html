Complete context

About This Recruitment

Railway RRB ALP Assistant Loco Pilot 2026 is a Railway Recruitment Board (RRB) recruitment drive to fill 11,127 Assistant Loco Pilot (ALP) posts across various railway zones. The official notification (CEN 01/2026) was released in May 2026, and the online application window remained open from 15 May 2026 to 14 June 2026.

The Computer Based Test (CBT) is scheduled to be conducted from 03 to 05 November 2026. The exam city details link is now active, allowing candidates to check their allotted exam city. The admit card is expected to be released shortly before the exam date.

The selection process for RRB ALP typically includes a Computer Based Test (CBT) Stage I, a Computer Based Test (CBT) Stage II, a Computer Based Aptitude Test (CBAT), Document Verification, and a Medical Examination. Candidates must have passed Class 10th with an ITI certificate in relevant trades, or a Diploma/Degree in Engineering to be eligible.

The age limit is 18 to 30 years as on 01 July 2026, with relaxation for reserved categories as per government rules. The application fee is Rs. 500 for General/OBC/EWS candidates, with a refund of Rs. 400 after appearing in Stage I exam. SC/ST/PwBD candidates pay Rs. 250 with a full refund.

What to do at the current stage

How to Check Exam City Details

  1. Open the official RRB exam city details portal from Important Links.
  2. Log in using your Registration Number, Roll Number or Date of Birth.
  3. Check your allotted exam city and exam date on the screen.
  4. Download the exam city intimation slip for reference.
  5. Check the official exam notice for detailed instructions and exam day guidelines.
  6. The admit card will be released separately a few days before the exam.

Original Application Process

Preserved for recruitment history and reference after the application stage closes.

  1. Visit the official RRB recruitment website at rrbapply.gov.in.
  2. Read the official CEN 01/2026 notification carefully.
  3. Register on the RRB portal with valid email ID and mobile number.
  4. Fill the online application form with accurate personal and educational details.
  5. Upload photograph, signature and required documents.
  6. Pay the applicable examination fee online.
  7. Submit the form and save the confirmation page for future reference.
