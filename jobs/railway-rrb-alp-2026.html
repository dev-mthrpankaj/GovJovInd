About This Recruitment

Railway Recruitment Board (RRB) has released the official notification for Assistant Loco Pilot (ALP) Recruitment 2026 under CEN 01/2026, offering a total of 11,127 posts across various railway zones in India. This is one of the largest technical recruitment drives conducted by Indian Railways for the ALP post.

The online application window was open from 15 May 2026 to 14 June 2026, with the fee payment last date on 16 June 2026 and a correction window from 17 to 26 June 2026. The RRB ALP Exam Date 2026 has now been officially announced as 03 to 05 November 2026. Candidates who have successfully submitted their applications should now focus on exam preparation and keep track of the admit card release.

The selection process for RRB ALP involves a Computer Based Test (CBT) Stage I, CBT Stage II, Computer Based Aptitude Test (CBAT), and Document Verification with Medical Examination. Candidates must qualify each stage to move forward in the selection process.
