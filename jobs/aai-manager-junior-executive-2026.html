About This Recruitment

Airports Authority of India (AAI) has released a major recruitment notification (Advt. No. 12/2026/CHQ/DR-CBT) for Manager and Junior Executive posts across multiple disciplines, with a total of 389 vacancies. The online application window was open from 08 August 2026 to 07 September 2026.

The Computer Based Test (CBT) is scheduled from 21 October 2026 to 26 October 2026 as per the official exam notice. Candidates who applied successfully should now prepare for the CBT and watch for the admit card release. The exam is expected to be held across multiple cities in India.

This page will carry every later stage — admit card, answer key, result, document verification, medical and final merit list — on the same permanent URL. No new page will be created for any stage.
