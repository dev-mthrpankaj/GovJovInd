Complete context

About This Recruitment

Airports Authority of India (AAI) has released the official notification for direct recruitment of Managers and Junior Executives in various disciplines under Advertisement No. 12/2026/CHQ/DR-CBT. A total of 389 vacancies are available across 15 different post codes including Civil, Electrical, Finance, IT, Architecture, Law, Fire Services, Operations, Commercial, and more.

The online application window was open from 08 August 2026 to 07 September 2026. The Computer Based Test (CBT) admit card has been released on 14 October 2026. The CBT exam is scheduled to be held on 21 October 2026 and 26 October 2026, depending on the post code.

The selection process includes a Computer Based Test (CBT) for all posts, followed by Application Verification and Interview for Manager posts (Post Code 1 to 11). For Manager (Fire Services), additional tests include Physical Measurement Test, Driving Test, and Physical Endurance Test.

Eligibility requires a relevant degree (B.E./B.Tech, MBA, MCA, LL.B, etc.) depending on the post. The maximum age limit is 32 years for Manager posts and 27 years for Junior Executive posts as on 07 September 2026, with age relaxation for reserved categories as per Government of India rules.
