Complete context

About This Recruitment

University of Delhi (DU) has invited online applications for the recruitment of Assistant and Senior Assistant posts. The official notification (Advt No. R&P/323/2026) was released in October 2026, announcing a total of 126 vacancies — 80 for Assistant and 46 for Senior Assistant.

The online application window opened on 23 October 2026 and will remain active until 09 November 2026. Candidates must complete their registration, fill in the application form, upload documents, and pay the application fee before the deadline to be considered for the recruitment process.

The selection process typically involves a Computer Based Test (CBT) or written examination, followed by document verification and other stages as specified in the official notification. Candidates are advised to read the full notification carefully before applying.

Eligibility requires a Bachelor's degree from a recognised university, along with relevant work experience — three years as Assistant or equivalent for Senior Assistant, and two years as Junior Assistant or equivalent for Assistant. The age limit is 35 years for Senior Assistant and 32 years for Assistant, with age relaxation applicable as per rules.

Eligibility Criteria

Age Limit

Maximum 35 years for Senior Assistant and 32 years for Assistant.

Age relaxation applicable for SC, ST, OBC, PwBD, Ex-Servicemen and other reserved categories as per DU rules.

Education

Bachelor's Degree from any recognised University/Institution. For Senior Assistant: 3 years experience as Assistant or equivalent. For Assistant: 2 years experience as Junior Assistant or equivalent.

Both the academic qualification and relevant work experience are mandatory for eligibility.

How to Apply

  1. Visit the official DU recruitment portal at https://dunt.uod.ac.in/index.php/site/login.
  2. Register using a valid email ID and mobile number.
  3. Login using your registration ID and password.
  4. Fill the application form with personal, educational, and category details.
  5. Upload scanned documents (photograph, signature, and required certificates).
  6. Pay the application fee through the available online payment modes.
  7. Submit the form and download/print the confirmation page for future reference.
