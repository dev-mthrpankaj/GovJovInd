Complete context

About This Recruitment

University of Delhi (DU) has released the official notification for the recruitment of Assistant and Senior Assistant posts. The recruitment drive aims to fill 126 vacancies, with 46 posts for Senior Assistant and 80 posts for Assistant.

The online application window will open on 23 October 2026 and close on 09 November 2026. Eligible candidates must apply through the official University of Delhi website. The selection process typically involves a written examination followed by document verification.

The application fee is Rs. 1000 for General category candidates, Rs. 800 for OBC/EWS/Female candidates, and Rs. 600 for SC/ST/PH candidates. The age limit is 35 years for Senior Assistant and 32 years for Assistant, with age relaxation applicable as per University of Delhi rules.

Candidates must hold a Bachelor's degree from a recognized university. For Senior Assistant, three years of experience as Assistant or equivalent in Level 4 is required, while for Assistant, two years of experience as Junior Assistant or equivalent is required.
