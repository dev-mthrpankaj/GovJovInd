About This Recruitment

Uttar Pradesh Education Service Selection Commission (UPESSC) has announced the UP Special Teacher Eligibility Test 2026 for teachers working at Primary Level (Classes 1 to 5) and Upper Primary / Junior High School Level (Classes 6 to 8).

As per the available recruitment information, online applications are scheduled from 5 September 2026 to 4 October 2026, the correction deadline is 8 October 2026, and the examination is scheduled for 3 November 2026.

This Special TET is intended for eligible working teachers covered by the notified school categories. Candidates should carefully read Advertisement No. 03/2026 and verify their level-specific eligibility before submitting the online form.
