About This Recruitment

Himachal Pradesh Public Service Commission (HPPSC) has released the H.P. Administrative Service Combined Competitive Examination (HPAS) notification 2026 for 22 posts across multiple state services. The online application window was open from 26 August 2026 to 22 September 2026.

The recruitment covers posts such as H.P. Administrative Services, Block Development Officer, District Controller, District Treasury Officer and Tehsildar. Candidates must hold a Bachelor's degree in any stream from a recognised university and have knowledge of the customs, manners and dialects of Himachal Pradesh.

The Commission has now released the examination notice and announced 13 December 2026 as the HPAS 2026 exam date. The admit card is expected in November or December 2026. This permanent page will carry every later stage including admit card, answer key, result and final merit list.
