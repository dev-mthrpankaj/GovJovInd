Complete context

About This Recruitment

Rajasthan Staff Selection Board (RSSB) is conducting the RSSB Junior Engineer Combined Direct Recruitment 2026 under Advt. No. 10/2026 to fill 1909 Junior Engineer posts across six Government of Rajasthan departments: Public Works Department (PWD), Water Resources Department (WRD), Public Health Engineering Department (PHED), Urban Development and Housing Department (UDHD), Local Self Government Department (LSG), and Rajasthan State Agricultural Marketing Board (RSAMB).

Originally notified on 15 August 2026 for 874 posts with an initial application window from 16 August 2026 to 14 September 2026, the recruitment was expanded on 17 November 2026 by adding 1035 new posts. The revised vacancy total stands at 1909 posts, comprising 1814 Non-Scheduled Area (Non-TSP) and 95 Scheduled Area (TSP) positions across Civil (Degree), Civil (Diploma), Electrical (Degree), and Mechanical/Electrical (Diploma) cadres.

Reopened online applications are currently active from 18 November 2026 to 17 December 2026 (11:59 PM) through the Rajasthan SSO Portal (sso.rajasthan.gov.in). Candidates who already applied during the earlier window do not need to submit a fresh form; they can modify their department preference order free of cost during the 22 December 2026 to 31 December 2026 correction window.

Selection is based on a 120-mark Written Examination (40 marks Rajasthan General Knowledge and 80 marks core engineering discipline, 2 hours duration, 1/3 negative marking) scheduled from 03 February 2027 to 05 February 2027, followed by Document Verification and Medical Examination. Selected candidates are appointed under 7th CPC Pay Matrix Level 10 (Rs.29,100 to Rs.1,04,400 per month).
