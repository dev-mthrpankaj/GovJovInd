Complete context

About This Recruitment

Rajasthan Staff Selection Board (RSSB) is conducting the RSSB Junior Engineer Combined Direct Recruitment 2026 under Advt. No. 10/2026 to fill 1909 Junior Engineer posts across six Government of Rajasthan departments: Public Works Department (PWD), Water Resources Department (WRD), Public Health Engineering Department (PHED), Urban Development and Housing Department (UDHD), Local Self Government Department (LSG), and Rajasthan State Agricultural Marketing Board (RSAMB).

Originally notified on 15 August 2026 for 874 posts with an initial application window from 16 August 2026 to 14 September 2026, the recruitment was expanded on 17 November 2026 by adding 1035 new posts. The revised vacancy total stands at 1909 posts, comprising 1814 Non-Scheduled Area (Non-TSP) and 95 Scheduled Area (TSP) positions across Civil (Degree), Civil (Diploma), Electrical (Degree), and Mechanical/Electrical (Diploma) cadres.

Reopened online applications are currently active from 18 November 2026 to 17 December 2026 (11:59 PM) through the Rajasthan SSO Portal (sso.rajasthan.gov.in). Candidates who already applied during the earlier window do not need to submit a fresh form; they can modify their department preference order free of cost during the 22 December 2026 to 31 December 2026 correction window.

Selection is based on a 120-mark Written Examination (40 marks Rajasthan General Knowledge and 80 marks core engineering discipline, 2 hours duration, 1/3 negative marking) scheduled from 03 February 2027 to 05 February 2027, followed by Document Verification and Medical Examination. Selected candidates are appointed under 7th CPC Pay Matrix Level 10 (Rs.29,100 to Rs.1,04,400 per month).

Eligibility Criteria

Age Limit

18 to 40 years as on 01 January 2027.

Rajasthan domicile SC, ST, OBC, MBC, and EWS male candidates and General female candidates get 5 years relaxation; Rajasthan SC, ST, OBC, MBC, and EWS female candidates get 10 years relaxation as per Rajasthan government rules.

Education

Engineering Degree (B.E. / B.Tech) or 3-year Diploma in Civil, Electrical, or Mechanical Engineering from a recognised university or technical institution, matching the applied post.

Final-year candidates may apply provided they acquire the required qualification on or before the written examination date.

Nationality / Other

Candidates must have working knowledge of Hindi written in Devanagari script and knowledge of Rajasthani culture. Indian citizens meeting the post-wise norms may apply.

Refer to official notification for detailed conditions.

What to do at the current stage

How to Apply Online for RSSB Junior Engineer 2026

  1. Visit the official Rajasthan SSO Portal at sso.rajasthan.gov.in and log in with your SSO ID and password, or register a new SSO ID.
  2. Open Citizen Apps (G2C) and select the Recruitment Portal option.
  3. Complete One Time Registration (OTR) and pay the applicable OTR fee if you have not already paid it.
  4. Select Junior Engineer Combined Direct Recruitment 2026 (Advt. No. 10/2026) and click Apply Now.
  5. Enter personal details, engineering degree or diploma details, category information, and department preferences carefully.
  6. Upload your recent live photograph, signature, and required documents in the prescribed format.
  7. Review all entries, submit the form before 17 December 2026 (11:59 PM), and download the confirmation receipt.

    Frequently Asked Questions

    The last date to submit the reopened RSSB Junior Engineer Recruitment 2026 online form is 17 December 2026 up to 11:59 PM on the Rajasthan SSO Portal. The earlier application window had closed on 14 September 2026 before vacancies were revised.

    Rajasthan Staff Selection Board (RSSB) has notified 1909 Junior Engineer vacancies under revised Advt. No. 10/2026, increased from the earlier 874 posts. This includes 1814 Non-Scheduled Area posts and 95 Scheduled Area posts across six departments.

    The reopened online application process for RSSB Junior Engineer Recruitment 2026 started on 18 November 2026 via sso.rajasthan.gov.in. Previously, the first application round had opened on 16 August 2026 under Advt. No. 10/2026.

    No, candidates who already submitted their application between 16 August 2026 and 14 September 2026 do not need to apply again. They can update their department preference order free of cost from 22 December 2026 to 31 December 2026.

    Selected RSSB Junior Engineers are placed in 7th CPC Pay Matrix Level 10 with a pay scale of Rs.29,100 to Rs.1,04,400 per month and Grade Pay Rs.3,600. During the two-year probation period, fixed monthly remuneration is paid as per state rules.

    Candidates holding an Engineering Degree (B.E. or B.Tech) or Diploma in Civil, Electrical, or Mechanical Engineering from a recognised institution can apply. Working knowledge of Hindi in Devanagari script and Rajasthani culture is also mandatory.

    Log in at sso.rajasthan.gov.in, open Citizen Apps, select Recruitment Portal, complete One Time Registration (OTR), choose RSSB Junior Engineer Recruitment 2026, upload photo and signature, pay the fee if applicable, and submit before 17 December 2026.

    Candidates must be between 18 and 40 years of age as on 01 January 2027 to apply for RSSB Junior Engineer Recruitment 2026. Upper age relaxation of 5 to 10 years applies to eligible Rajasthan reserved categories and women as per state rules.

    The One Time Registration (OTR) fee is Rs.600 for General, Creamy Layer OBC/MBC, and other-state applicants, and Rs.400 for Rajasthan Non-Creamy Layer OBC/MBC, EWS, SC, ST, and PwBD candidates. Application edits carry a Rs.300 correction fee.

    The RSSB Junior Engineer Combined Direct Recruitment 2026 written examination is scheduled from 03 February 2027 to 05 February 2027. The 2-hour paper carries 120 marks (40 marks Rajasthan GK and 80 marks technical subject) with 1/3 negative marking.

    Selection is based on a 120-mark Written Examination followed by Document Verification and Medical Examination. There is no interview stage, and final department allotment is decided on written exam merit and candidate department preferences.

    The official website of Rajasthan Staff Selection Board (RSSB) is rssb.rajasthan.gov.in for notifications, syllabus, admit cards, and results. Online applications and OTR fee payments are submitted through the Rajasthan SSO Portal at sso.rajasthan.gov.in.
