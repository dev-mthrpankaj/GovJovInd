Complete context

About This Recruitment

MPPSC Assistant Professor Recruitment 2026 is a major state-level recruitment drive conducted by the Madhya Pradesh Public Service Commission (MPPSC) for 949 Assistant Professor posts in the Higher Education Department, Government of Madhya Pradesh. These are permanent, gazetted academic positions with a starting pay scale of Rs. 57,700 (Academic Level 10).

The recruitment is being conducted in three phases based on subject groups. The admit card for the first phase (Sanskrit, Geography, Psychology, Law, Geology, and Yogic Science) has been released. The exam is scheduled for 12 July 2026 in Indore. Candidates who applied for the relevant subject group can download their hall ticket from the official MPPSC website.

This permanent lifecycle page will continue to carry every official update — from admit card and answer key to result, interview, and final merit list — on the same URL without creating stage-specific pages.
