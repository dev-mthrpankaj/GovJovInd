About This Recruitment

UPSSSC Pharmaceutical Ayurvedic Recruitment 2024 is a recruitment drive by the Uttar Pradesh Subordinate Service Selection Commission (UPSSSC) for 1002 Pharmaceutical Ayurvedic posts. The online application window was open from 12 February 2024 to 03 March 2024.

This role is suitable for candidates with a Diploma in Pharmaceutical Ayurvedic and registration in the council. The selection process involves a written exam, and the latest update is the release of exam city details. Candidates should keep their admit card, photo ID, and educational documents ready for the examination stage.

The exam is scheduled for 20 September 2026. Candidates are advised to check the official website for the latest updates on admit card release and exam city intimation.
