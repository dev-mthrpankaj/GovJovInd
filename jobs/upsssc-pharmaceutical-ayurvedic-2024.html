About This Recruitment

UPSSSC Pharmaceutical Ayurvedic Recruitment 2024 is a Uttar Pradesh Subordinate Service Selection Commission (UPSSSC) drive for 1002 Pharmaceutical Ayurvedic posts. The online application window was open from 12 February 2024 to 03 March 2024.

This page tracks the complete recruitment lifecycle. The admit card and exam date are currently awaited. All future official updates, including admit card, answer key, result and final merit list, will be added to this same permanent URL.

Candidates who applied should keep their registration details, photo ID and educational certificates ready for later stages.

Eligibility Criteria

Age Limit

18 to 40 years as on the prescribed date.

Age relaxation applicable as per UPSSSC rules.

Education

Intermediate Exam with Biology / Mathematics subject and Diploma in Pharmaceutical Ayurvedic with registration in Council. UPSSSC PET 2023 Score Card required.

For detailed educational conditions, refer to the official notification.

Nationality / Other

Indian citizens fulfilling the post-specific eligibility conditions may apply.

Refer to official notification for detailed conditions.
