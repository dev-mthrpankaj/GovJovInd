Complete context

About This Recruitment

Southern Railway (RRC SR) has released a notification for the engagement of Act Apprentices for the year 2026-27. This recruitment drive aims to fill a total of 4,471 vacancies across various trades in the Southern Railway zone, including Palghat, Trivandrum, and Chennai divisions. The online application process is currently active and will remain open until 27 September 2026.

This is a significant opportunity for candidates who have completed their 10th standard and possess an ITI certificate in a relevant trade. The apprenticeship training is governed by the Apprentices Act, 1961, and offers a stipend as per government norms. Candidates are advised to carefully review the trade-wise eligibility and vacancy distribution before applying.

All future updates regarding this recruitment, including exam dates (if any), document verification schedules, and the final merit list, will be published on this same permanent page. Candidates should bookmark this URL for the latest official information.
