About This Recruitment

UPPSC Assistant Professor Government Degree College Recruitment is a Uttar Pradesh Public Service Commission recruitment drive for 1,253 Assistant Professor posts across multiple subjects.

The application process ran from 4 September to 6 October 2025, followed by the preliminary examination, answer key and subject-wise results. The recruitment has now moved to the Mains stage, with the supplied update listing Mains examinations from 15 September to 1 October 2026 and the Mains Admit Card released on 8 September 2026.

Candidates should download the Mains Admit Card from the official UPPSC portal and carefully check their subject-wise examination date, centre, reporting time and instructions.
