Complete context

About This Recruitment

High Court of Judicature at Allahabad is conducting the Private Secretary Grade-I Recruitment Examination 2026 to fill 195 vacancies in Uttar Pradesh under Advertisement No. 01/Private Secretary Grade-I/2026. The official notification was released on 12 March 2026, and online applications remained open from 12 March 2026 to 01 April 2026, with fee payment closing on 02 April 2026 and the correction window running on 03 April 2026 and 04 April 2026.

The Stage-I Computer Based Test (CBT) was conducted on 17 November 2026 across 10 cities in Uttar Pradesh, including Agra, Bareilly, Gorakhpur, Kanpur, Lucknow, Meerut, Moradabad, Noida, Prayagraj and Varanasi. On 20 November 2026, the High Court released the Stage-I Part A (General Studies) Answer Key and opened a free online objection window through 22 November 2026 (11:55 PM).

Candidates who appeared in the Stage-I examination can now review the answer key and await the Stage-I result. Shortlisted candidates numbering 10 times the vacancies in each category will advance to the Stage-II English Shorthand Dictation and Transcription Test (200 marks), followed by the Stage-III Interview and Document Verification.

Eligibility requires a Bachelor's Degree in any stream from a recognized university in India, English Shorthand speed of 100 WPM, English Typewriting speed of 40 WPM, and computer operations knowledge. The age limit is 21 to 35 years as on 01 July 2026, and selected officers are placed in Pay Matrix Level 10 (Rs.56,100 to Rs.1,77,500).

Eligibility Criteria

Age Limit

21 to 35 years as on 01 July 2026.

Upper age relaxation is applicable for SC, ST, OBC, PwBD, Ex-Servicemen and other eligible categories domiciled in Uttar Pradesh as per Allahabad High Court rules.

Education

Bachelor's Degree in any stream from a recognized university in India, along with English Shorthand speed of 100 WPM, English Typewriting speed of 40 WPM, and knowledge of Data Entry, Word Processing and Computer Operations.

Candidates must fulfill both the graduation requirement and the prescribed shorthand, typing and computer proficiency standards.

Nationality / Other

Must be a citizen of India. Reservation benefits apply only to candidates domiciled in Uttar Pradesh with valid category and domicile certificates issued by a competent authority.

What to do at the current stage

How to Check Stage-I Answer Key

  1. Open the official Allahabad High Court candidate login link from the Important Links section.
  2. Enter your registered User ID and Password to log in to the exam portal.
  3. Open the Stage-I Part A (General Studies) response sheet and provisional answer key tab.
  4. Match your marked responses with the official answer key to estimate your Stage-I score.
  5. Save a PDF copy of your response sheet for reference until the Stage-I result is declared.
  6. Begin preparing for the Stage-II English Shorthand Dictation and Transcription test if your estimated score is strong.

Original Application Process

Preserved for recruitment history and reference after the application stage closes.

  1. Visit the official Allahabad High Court website at www.allahabadhighcourt.in or the recruitment portal.
  2. Read Advertisement No. 01/Private Secretary Grade-I/2026 carefully before registration.
  3. Complete online registration using a valid email ID and active mobile number.
  4. Fill in personal, academic, shorthand, typing and category details accurately.
  5. Upload scanned photograph, signature and required supporting documents in the prescribed format.
  6. Pay the category-wise examination fee online (Rs.1900 for General/OBC or Rs.1700 for EWS/SC/ST).
  7. Submit the final application form and download the confirmation page for future stages.
