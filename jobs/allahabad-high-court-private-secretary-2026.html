Complete context

About This Recruitment

Allahabad High Court Private Secretary Grade I Recruitment 2026 is a recruitment drive by the High Court of Judicature at Allahabad to fill 195 Private Secretary Grade I posts. The official notification was published on 12 March 2026, and the online application window remained open from 12 March 2026 to 01 April 2026.

The written examination is scheduled for 17 November 2026. The admit card for the written exam has been released on the official Allahabad High Court website. Candidates must download the admit card and carry it along with a valid photo ID to the examination centre.

Eligibility requires a Bachelor's degree in any stream from a recognised university, along with good knowledge of English Shorthand (100 WPM) and Typewriting (40 WPM). Knowledge of Data Entry, Word Processing and Computer Operations is also required. The age limit is 21 to 35 years as on 01 July 2026, with relaxation for reserved categories as per rules.

The selection process includes a written examination, shorthand and typing test, document verification and final merit list. Candidates should read the official notification for detailed eligibility and selection criteria.
