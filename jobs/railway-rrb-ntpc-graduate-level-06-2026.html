Complete context

About This Recruitment

Railway Recruitment Board (RRB) has released the RRB NTPC Graduate Level Recruitment 2026 notification under Centralised Employment Notice (CEN) No. 06/2026. A total of 3477 graduate-level vacancies have been announced across Indian Railways for posts such as Goods Train Manager, Chief Commercial-cum-Ticket Supervisor, Junior Accounts Assistant cum Typist, and Senior Clerk cum Typist.

The online application process will start on 08 October 2026 and the last date to submit the application is 06 November 2026. The application fee payment can be made until 08 November 2026, and the application modification window will be open from 09 November 2026 to 18 November 2026.

The selection process for RRB NTPC Graduate Level 2026 includes CBT 1, CBT 2, Skill Test or CBAT (where applicable), Document Verification, and Medical Examination. As per the new CEN 06/2026, there will be no CBTST or CBAT for certain posts, and the selection will be based on CBT 1 and CBT 2 only.

Eligible candidates must possess a Bachelor's Degree in any stream from a recognized university. The age limit is 18 to 33 years as on 01 January 2027, with age relaxation applicable for reserved categories as per government rules.

Eligibility Criteria

Age Limit

18 to 33 years as on 01 January 2027.

Age relaxation applicable for OBC-NCL (3 years), SC/ST (5 years), PwBD (10-15 years) and other reserved categories as per government rules.

Bachelor's Degree in any stream from a recognized university. For Junior Accounts Assistant cum Typist and Senior Clerk cum Typist posts, typing proficiency in English/Hindi on computer is also required.

Candidates must possess the required qualification as on the closing date of application.
