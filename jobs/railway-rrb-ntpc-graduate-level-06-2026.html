Complete context

About This Recruitment

Railway Recruitment Boards (RRBs) have released Centralised Employment Notice (CEN) No. 06/2026 for the recruitment of Graduate Level posts under Non-Technical Popular Categories (NTPC). The notification was published in October 2026 and covers 3,548 vacancies across four posts: Goods Train Manager, Chief Commercial cum Ticket Supervisor, Junior Accounts Assistant cum Typist and Senior Clerk cum Typist.

The online application window started on 08 October 2026 and the last date has been extended to 08 November 2026. Candidates must complete registration, form filling and fee payment by the extended deadline. A modification window will be available from 09 to 18 November 2026 for corrections, with a fee of Rs.250 per attempt.

The selection process consists of a 1st Stage Computer Based Test (CBT 1), 2nd Stage CBT (CBT 2), Document Verification and Medical Examination. The Computer Based Aptitude Test (CBAT) and Typing Skill Test have been dispensed with for this CEN.

Eligibility requires a Bachelor's degree from a recognised university. The age limit is 18 to 33 years as on 01 January 2027, with relaxation for SC, ST, OBC, PwBD and Ex-Servicemen as per government rules. The application fee is Rs.500 for General/OBC/EWS and Rs.250 for SC/ST/PwBD/Women/Ex-Servicemen, with a refund provision for candidates who appear in CBT 1.

How to Apply Online

  1. Visit the official RRB application portal at rrbapply.gov.in.
  2. Register with a valid email ID and mobile number to create your account.
  3. Log in and select CEN 06/2026 – NTPC Graduate Level recruitment.
  4. Fill the application form with accurate personal, educational and category details.
  5. Upload scanned photograph, signature and required documents in the prescribed format.
  6. Pay the application fee online and submit the form before 08 November 2026.
