Complete context

About This Recruitment

RRB NTPC Graduate Level Recruitment 2026 (CEN 06/2026) is a central railway recruitment drive conducted by the Railway Recruitment Board (RRB) to fill 3,548 Non-Technical Popular Categories (Graduate Level) vacancies across 21 RRB zones in India. The posts include Goods Train Manager (2,750 posts), Senior Clerk Cum Typist (371 posts), Junior Accounts Assistant Cum Typist (300 posts), and Chief Commercial Cum Ticket Supervisor (127 posts).

The online application process started on 08 October 2026 and closed on 08 November 2026 after an official deadline extension, followed by the application correction window from 09 November 2026 to 18 November 2026. On 26 November 2026, RRB released the official Application Status notice and activated the status check link on the central RRB portal.

Candidates who submitted forms under CEN 06/2026 should verify their provisional acceptance status using their registration credentials and SMS/email alerts. The Computer Based Test (CBT-1) is scheduled for December 2026, with city intimation slips and e-admit cards to follow on official RRB portals.

Applicants must hold a Bachelor's degree in any discipline from a recognised university and be aged 18 to 33 years as on 01 January 2027, with category-wise age relaxation and fee refund provisions after appearing in CBT Stage 1 as per 7th CPC Level-5 and Level-6 recruitment rules.

What to do at the current stage

How to Check Application Status

  1. Open the official RRB application portal (rrbapply.gov.in) from Important Links.
  2. Sign in using your registered mobile number or email ID along with your password.
  3. Open the CEN 06/2026 (NTPC Graduate Level) application dashboard.
  4. Verify whether your form status shows Provisionally Accepted or Rejected with reasons.
  5. Check your registered SMS and email inbox for official status alerts from RRB.
  6. Save or print a copy of your accepted application status for CBT admit card reference.

Original Application Process

Preserved for recruitment history and reference after the application stage closes.

  1. Visit the official RRB application portal at rrbapply.gov.in.
  2. Complete One-Time Registration (OTR) using a valid email ID and mobile number.
  3. Log in with registration credentials and select CEN 06/2026 (NTPC Graduate Level).
  4. Enter personal, educational, category, RRB zone and post preference details.
  5. Upload scanned passport photograph, signature and required certificates.
  6. Pay the applicable examination fee online and submit the final form.
  7. Download and print the submitted application confirmation page.

A total of 3,548 vacancies are advertised under CEN 06/2026 across 21 RRB zones, including 2,750 Goods Train Manager, 371 Senior Clerk Cum Typist, 300 Junior Accounts Assistant Cum Typist, and 127 Chief Commercial Cum Ticket Supervisor posts.

RRB Bilaspur has the highest allocation with 542 posts, followed by RRB Ranchi (377 posts), RRB Bengaluru (346 posts), RRB Kolkata (332 posts), RRB Ajmer (304 posts), RRB Siliguri (291 posts), and RRB Prayagraj (265 posts).
