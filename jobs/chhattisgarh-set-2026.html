Complete context

About This Recruitment

Chhattisgarh State Eligibility Test (CG SET) 2026 is conducted by the Chhattisgarh Staff Selection Board (CSSB) / CG Vyapam for candidates aspiring to become Assistant Professors in colleges and universities of Chhattisgarh. The exam determines eligibility for the post of Assistant Professor across 28+ subjects including Hindi, English, Political Science, Economics, History, Geography, Science streams, Commerce, Law, and more.

The online application window was open from 23 July 2026 to 17 August 2026. The examination is scheduled for 04 October 2026. Candidates who successfully applied can now download their admit card from the official portal.

The CG SET exam consists of two papers — Paper I (General) and Paper II (Subject-specific). Candidates must secure the minimum qualifying marks to earn SET certification, which remains valid for Assistant Professor recruitment in Chhattisgarh state universities and colleges.

What to do at the current stage

How to Download Admit Card

  1. Visit the official CG Vyapam portal (vyapamprofile.cgstate.gov.in/online/).
  2. Click on the Admit Card / Download Admit Card link.
  3. Enter your Registration Number and Date of Birth / Password.
  4. Complete the CAPTCHA verification if prompted.
  5. Click Submit to access your CG SET Admit Card 2026.
  6. Verify Name, Roll Number, Exam Date, Time and Centre.
  7. Download and save the admit card PDF.
  8. Take a clear printout on A4 paper for the examination.
  9. Carry the printed admit card with a valid photo ID proof.

Original Application Process

Preserved for recruitment history and reference after the application stage closes.

  1. Visit the official CG Vyapam website (vyapamcg.cgstate.gov.in).
  2. Read the CG SET 2026 notification carefully.
  3. Open the application link from Important Links.
  4. Register and fill the form.
  5. Upload required documents.
  6. Pay the applicable fee.
  7. Submit and save the confirmation page.
