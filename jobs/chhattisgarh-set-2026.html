About This Recruitment

Chhattisgarh State Eligibility Test (CG SET) 2026 is conducted by the Chhattisgarh Staff Selection Board (CSSB) / CG Vyapam for candidates aspiring to become Assistant Professors in colleges and universities of Chhattisgarh. The exam determines eligibility for the post of Assistant Professor across 28+ subjects including Hindi, English, Political Science, Economics, History, Geography, Science streams, Commerce, Law, and more.

The online application window was open from 23 July 2026 to 17 August 2026. The examination is scheduled for 04 October 2026. Candidates who successfully applied can now download their admit card from the official portal.

The CG SET exam consists of two papers — Paper I (General) and Paper II (Subject-specific). Candidates must secure the minimum qualifying marks to earn SET certification, which remains valid for Assistant Professor recruitment in Chhattisgarh state universities and colleges.
