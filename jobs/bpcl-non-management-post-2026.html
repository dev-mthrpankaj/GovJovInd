Complete context

About This Recruitment

Bharat Petroleum Corporation Limited (BPCL) released Advertisement No. BPCL.HR.ER.03.NMGT.RECT for 154 Non-Management posts across Mumbai Refinery and Kochi Refinery. The posts include Process Technician (Chemical), Process Operator/Operator (Chemical), Technician/Operator (Mechanical) and Technician (Electrical).

The online application window opened on 29 July 2026 and was later extended to 19 August 2026. The Computer Based Test (CBT) was conducted on 12 September 2026. The CBT result has now been declared, and shortlisted candidates will be called for the Skill/Proficiency Test (SPT), which is qualifying in nature.

The selection journey includes CBT, Skill/Proficiency Test, Document Verification, Pre-Employment Medical Examination and final appointment. Candidates must carry their original documents for verification during the SPT stage.

Eligibility required a full-time AICTE-approved Diploma in the relevant engineering discipline with minimum 60% marks for General/EWS/OBC-NCL and 50% for SC/ST/PwBD, plus one year of post-qualification experience or apprenticeship as on 01 July 2026. The age limit was 18 to 25 years, with relaxation for reserved categories.
