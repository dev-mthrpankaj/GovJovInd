About This Recruitment

BPCL Non Management Recruitment 2026 covers 154 Process Technician, Process Operator/Operator and Technician vacancies at Mumbai and Kochi refineries under Advertisement No. BPCL.HR.ER.03.NMGT.RECT.

Online applications were accepted from 29 July to 13 August 2026. The current stage is the CBT admit card, and the examination is scheduled for 12 September 2026.

Candidates should download the admit card, verify the reporting time and examination centre, and follow all identity-proof and examination-day instructions printed on it.
