Complete context

About This Recruitment

United India Insurance Company Limited (UIIC) has released the notification for Administrative Officer (Scale I) Recruitment 2026 on 07 September 2026. A total of 225 vacancies are available — 200 posts for Administrative Officer – Generalists and 25 posts for Administrative Officer – Hindi Officers. Online applications are open from 08 September 2026 to 28 September 2026.

The selection process includes a Preliminary Examination (tentatively scheduled for 22 October 2026), Main Examination (tentatively 30 November 2026), Descriptive Test and Interview. The preliminary exam will consist of English Language, Reasoning Ability, and Quantitative Aptitude sections, each carrying 100 marks.

This is a pan-India recruitment with the job location across India. The scale I pay starts at a basic pay of Rs.82,485 with the published pay scale extending to Rs.1,56,745, and the gross monthly salary is approximately Rs.1.10 lakh at metropolitan centres, subject to applicable allowances and deductions.

Candidates are advised to keep their photograph, signature, educational certificates, category certificate (if applicable), and valid ID proof ready before starting the online application. The application fee is Rs.1,000 for General/OBC/EWS candidates and Rs.250 for SC/ST/PwBD candidates (plus applicable GST and service charges).
