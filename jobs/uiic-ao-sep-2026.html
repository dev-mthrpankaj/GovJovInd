Complete context

About This Recruitment

United India Insurance Company (UIIC) Administrative Officer (AO) Recruitment 2026 is a recruitment drive to fill 225 Administrative Officer posts, including 200 AO Generalists and 25 AO Hindi Officers. The official notification was published with advertisement number HO:HRM:REC:AO:1:2026, and the online application window remained open from 08 September 2026 to 01 October 2026.

The Prelims Computer Based Test was conducted on 22 October 2026, and the result was declared on 11 November 2026. Candidates who qualify the prelims will appear for the Mains Examination scheduled on 30 November 2026. The selection process further includes Document Verification, Medical Examination and Final Merit List preparation.

Eligibility for AO Generalists requires a Bachelor’s Degree or Post Graduate degree in any discipline with 60% marks for General/OBC/EWS and 55% for SC/ST/PH candidates. For AO Hindi Officers, a Master’s Degree is required with the same percentage criteria. The age limit is 21 to 30 years as on 30 June 2026, with relaxation for reserved categories.
