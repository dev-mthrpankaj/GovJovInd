About This Recruitment

United India Insurance Company (UIIC) has released the Administrative Officer (AO) Recruitment 2026 notification for 225 posts — 200 AO Generalists and 25 AO Hindi Officers. The advertisement number is HO:HRM:REC:AO:1:2026. Online applications were accepted from 08 September 2026 to 01 October 2026 through the official IBPS registration portal.

The selection process includes a Preliminary Examination (CBT), Mains Examination (CBT), Interview, Document Verification and Medical Examination. The Preliminary Examination is scheduled for 22 October 2026 and the Mains Examination for 30 November 2026. The UIIC AO Prelims Admit Card 2026 has now been released for candidates appearing in the preliminary round.

Eligibility for AO Generalists requires a Bachelor's Degree or Post Graduate Degree in any discipline with 60% marks for General/OBC/EWS candidates and 55% marks for SC/ST/PH candidates. For AO Hindi Officers, a Master's Degree is required with the same percentage criteria. The age limit is 21 to 30 years as on 30 June 2026, with relaxation for reserved categories as per rules.
