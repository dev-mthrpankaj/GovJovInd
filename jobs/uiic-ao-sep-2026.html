Complete context

About This Recruitment

United India Insurance Company (UIIC) issued Advertisement No. HO:HRM:REC:AO:1:2026 to recruit 225 Administrative Officers (AO) across India, comprising 200 Generalists posts and 25 Hindi Officers posts. The online application window remained open from 08 September 2026 to 01 October 2026.

The Preliminary Examination was conducted on 22 October 2026, and the Prelims Result was declared on 11 November 2026. Candidates who qualified in the preliminary stage are eligible to appear for the Mains Examination scheduled on 30 November 2026.

The UIIC AO Mains Admit Card 2026 was released on 21 November 2026 through the official IBPS call letter portal. Candidates must download and print their call letter and carry a valid original photo ID proof to the examination centre.

Applicants were required to be between 21 and 30 years of age as on 30 June 2026, with a Bachelor's or Master's degree (60% marks for General/OBC/EWS and 55% marks for SC/ST/PH) for Generalists, and a Master's degree with the same minimum marks for Hindi Officers.

What to do at the current stage

How to Download UIIC AO Mains Admit Card 2026

  1. Open the official IBPS call letter link from the Important Links section below.
  2. Select your preferred language (English or Hindi) on the login page.
  3. Enter your Registration Number or Roll Number along with Password or Date of Birth.
  4. Fill in the captcha code and click Login to view your Mains Admit Card.
  5. Verify your name, roll number, exam date (30 November 2026), reporting time and exam centre.
  6. Download the PDF and take a clear printout to carry with a valid original photo ID proof.

Original Application Process

Preserved for recruitment history and reference after the application stage closes.

  1. Visit the official United India Insurance Company (UIIC) website at https://uiic.co.in/.
  2. Open the UIIC AO Administrative Officers 2026 online application link.
  3. Complete new registration using a valid email ID and mobile number.
  4. Log in with the generated Registration ID and Password.
  5. Fill in personal, educational and category details accurately.
  6. Upload scanned photograph, signature and required documents.
  7. Pay the applicable application fee through online mode.
  8. Submit the form and print the confirmation page for future reference.
