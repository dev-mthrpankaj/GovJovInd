Complete context

About This Recruitment

Madhya Pradesh Employees Selection Board (MPESB) has released the official notification for MPESB Naib Tehsildar Recruitment 2026. This recruitment drive aims to fill a total of 73 vacancies across two cadres — Clerical Service Cadre (46 posts) and Patwari / Revenue Inspector Cadre (27 posts).

The online application window is scheduled to remain open from 17 September 2026 to 01 October 2026. Candidates are advised to read the complete rule book / notification carefully before applying. The recruitment is open to eligible candidates who have completed graduation in relevant disciplines and, for certain posts, have the required experience in revenue or clerical roles.

The selection process will include a written examination, and may include further stages such as document verification and medical examination as prescribed by MPESB. Candidates should keep scanned copies of their photograph, signature, identity proof, educational certificates, and category documents ready before starting the online form.

Eligibility Criteria

Age Limit

Maximum 55 years as on 01/01/2026. Minimum age not specified (NA).

Age relaxation applicable as per MPESB / Madhya Pradesh government rules.

Education

Graduation in Arts, Science, Commerce, Agriculture, or Technical / Engineering streams from a recognised university. For specific cadres, candidates must have at least 5 years of service in the revenue department as Clerk, Patwari, or Revenue Inspector (permanent or officiating).

For detailed educational and experience requirements, refer to the official MPESB rule book.

Nationality / Other

Indian citizens fulfilling the post-specific eligibility conditions may apply. Candidate must meet the experience criteria for the respective cadre.

Refer to official notification for detailed conditions.
