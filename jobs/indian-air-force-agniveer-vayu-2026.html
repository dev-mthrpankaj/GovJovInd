About This Recruitment

Indian Air Force (IAF) has released the Agniveer Vayu Intake 02/2027 recruitment notification for unmarried male and female candidates. The online application window was open from 06 July 2026 to 26 July 2026. The selection test was conducted on 22–23 September 2026, and the official answer key has now been released on the official IAF recruitment portal.

Agniveer Vayu is a four-year service entry scheme under the Agnipath model. Candidates who qualify the online selection test proceed to physical fitness test (PFT), medical examination, and final merit list. The recruitment offers a unique opportunity to serve in the Indian Air Force in technical and non-technical streams.

Candidates should keep their registration number, date of birth, and admit card details ready to download the answer key and check their responses. The official answer key link is available in the Important Links section below.
