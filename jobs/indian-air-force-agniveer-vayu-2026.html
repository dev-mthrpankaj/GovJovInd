About This Recruitment

Indian Air Force Agniveer Vayu Recruitment 2026 (Intake 02/2027) is a national-level recruitment drive for unmarried male and female candidates. The online application window was open from 06 July 2026 to 26 July 2026.

The selection process includes a computer-based examination scheduled on 22-23 September 2026, followed by physical fitness test, medical examination, and final merit list. The admit card for the written exam has now been released.

This page serves as the permanent lifecycle record for the IAF Agniveer Vayu 2026 recruitment, from notification to final result.

Eligibility Criteria

Age Limit

17.5 to 22 years as on the prescribed date.

Age relaxation applicable as per IAF rules.

Education

Science Stream: 10+2 with Mathematics, Physics and English with 50% aggregate and 50% in English OR 3-year Diploma in Engineering. Non-Science Stream: 10+2 in any stream with 50% aggregate and 50% in English.

For detailed educational conditions, refer to the official notification.

Nationality / Other

Indian citizens (male and female), unmarried, fulfilling prescribed physical and medical standards.

Unmarried candidates only. Refer to official notification for detailed conditions.
