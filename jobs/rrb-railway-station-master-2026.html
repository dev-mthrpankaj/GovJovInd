Complete context

About This Recruitment

Railway Recruitment Board (RRB) has released the official notification for the recruitment of Railway Station Master in 2026. This recruitment drive aims to fill 1265 vacancies for the Station Master post across various railway zones in India. The official notification was published on 26 October 2026, and the online application process starts on 27 October 2026.

The online application window will remain open from 27 October 2026 to 25 November 2026. The last date for fee payment is also 25 November 2026. A correction window will be available from 28 November 2026 to 07 December 2026. The exam date and admit card release date will be announced later on the official RRB portal.

Candidates must have a Bachelor's Degree from any recognised university in India to be eligible for this post. The age limit is 20 to 33 years as on 01 January 2027, with age relaxation applicable for reserved categories as per government rules. The application fee is Rs. 500 for General/OBC/EWS candidates and Rs. 250 for SC/ST/PwBD candidates.

The selection process includes a Computer Based Test (CBT) followed by document verification and medical examination. This is a permanent recruitment lifecycle page where every future stage, from admit card to final result, will be updated on this same URL.

How to Apply Online

  1. Visit the official RRB application portal at rrbapply.gov.in.
  2. Read the official CEN 08/2026 notification carefully before applying.
  3. Register with a valid email ID and mobile number to create your account.
  4. Login using your registration ID and password.
  5. Fill the application form with accurate personal, educational and category details.
  6. Upload scanned photographs, signature and required documents as per specifications.
  7. Pay the application fee through the available online payment modes.
  8. Submit the form and download/print the confirmation page for future reference.
