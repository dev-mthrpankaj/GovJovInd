Complete context

About This Recruitment

UP Police Home Guard Recruitment 2025 is a Uttar Pradesh Police Recruitment and Promotion Board (UPPRPB) recruitment drive to fill 41,424 Home Guard posts across Uttar Pradesh. The official notification was published in November 2025, and the online application window remained open from 18 November 2025 to 17 December 2025.

The written examination was conducted on 25, 26 and 27 April 2026. The answer key was released on 07 May 2026, the final answer key and written result were declared on 28 June 2026. The Document Verification / Physical Standard Test (DV/PST) was conducted from 13 July to 07 August 2026, and the Physical Efficiency Test (PET) was held on 15 September 2026 after a postponement from the earlier scheduled date.

The Final Result was declared on 03 October 2026 along with the final merit list. District-wise cut-off marks for all categories have also been released. Candidates can check their final selection status and download the merit list from the official portal.

Basic eligibility is Class 10th (High School) or equivalent from a recognised board. The age limit is 18 to 30 years as on 01 July 2025, with relaxation for reserved categories as per UPPRPB rules.
