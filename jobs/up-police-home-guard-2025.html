About This Recruitment

UP Police Home Guard Enrollment 2025 is being conducted by the Uttar Pradesh Police Recruitment & Promotion Board (UPPRPB) for 41,424 Home Guard vacancies across Uttar Pradesh under Advertisement No. 02/2025. The original online application stage closed in December 2025 and the written examination was held on 25, 26 and 27 April 2026.

The recruitment has since moved through the written answer-key/result and DV/PST stages. The current stage is the Physical Efficiency Test (PET): UPPRPB released the PET admit-card link and PET schedule information on 1 September 2026, with PET scheduled from 8 to 15 September 2026.

This permanent page keeps the original vacancy, eligibility, fee and application information together with later exam, answer-key, DV/PST and PET updates, so candidates can follow the complete recruitment lifecycle from one URL.
