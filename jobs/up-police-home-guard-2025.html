Complete context

About This Recruitment

The UP Police Home Guard Recruitment 2025 is a massive recruitment drive by the Uttar Pradesh Police Recruitment & Promotion Board (UPPRPB) for 41,424 Home Guard posts. The online application process was conducted from 17 November 2025 to 17 December 2025.

The recruitment has progressed through multiple stages, including a written exam, document verification (DV), and physical efficiency test (PET). Candidates who have successfully cleared the written exam and DV are now eligible for the PET stage. A new PET exam date has been announced after a postponement.

This page serves as the permanent lifecycle hub for this recruitment, tracking every major development from notification to the final merit list. Candidates should regularly check this page for the latest official notices and download links.
