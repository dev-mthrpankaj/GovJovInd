About This Recruitment

UP Police Home Guard Recruitment 2025 is being conducted by the Uttar Pradesh Police Recruitment & Promotion Board for 41,424 Home Guard posts. The online application window ran from 17 November to 17 December 2025, followed by correction, written examination and later physical-selection stages.

The written examination was held from 25 to 27 April 2026. Candidates shortlisted for the next stage were called for document verification and physical standard proceedings from 13 July to 7 August 2026.

The Physical Efficiency Test (PET) scheduled for 14 September 2026 has been postponed. Candidates should use the current official postponement notice and wait for the revised PET schedule from the board.
