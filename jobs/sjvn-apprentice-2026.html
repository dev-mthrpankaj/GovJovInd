Complete context

About This Recruitment

SJVN Limited, a Navratna Public Sector Undertaking and joint venture of the Government of India and the Government of Himachal Pradesh, has published Advertisement No. 128/2026 for one-year apprenticeship training under the Apprentices Act, 1961. The drive offers 100 seats across Graduate Apprentice (40 posts), Technician (Diploma) Apprentice (25 posts) and Technician (ITI) Apprentice (35 posts) categories in projects and offices across Himachal Pradesh.

Online registration began on 18 November 2026 and will remain open until 17 December 2026 on the official SJVN e-recruitment portal. Only candidates who hold a Himachal Pradesh domicile certificate are eligible to apply, and 25% of total seats are earmarked for Project Affected Families and Areas (PAF/PAA) of SJVN.

Selection is strictly based on an academic merit list prepared from Class 10, Class 12, ITI, Diploma or Engineering Degree marks, with no written examination or interview. Shortlisted candidates will be called for document verification and medical fitness clearance before joining.

Selected trainees receive a fixed monthly stipend of Rs.15,000 for Graduate Apprentices, Rs.13,500 for Technician (Diploma) Apprentices and Rs.12,500 for Technician (ITI) Apprentices during the one-year training period. The age limit is 18 to 30 years as on 17 December 2026, with category-wise relaxation as per government norms.

Eligibility Criteria

Age Limit

18 to 30 years as on 17 December 2026.

Upper age relaxation of 5 years for SC/ST, 3 years for OBC (Non-Creamy Layer), and 10 years for UR/EWS PwBD (13 years OBC-NCL PwBD, 15 years SC/ST PwBD).

Education

Full-time 4-year Engineering Degree for Graduate Apprentice, 3-year Polytechnic Diploma for Diploma Apprentice, or Class 10 with regular ITI (NCVT/SCVT) for ITI Apprentice.

Candidates must have completed their qualification by 17 December 2026. Result-awaited candidates are not eligible.

Nationality / Other

Only Himachal Pradesh domicile candidates can apply. Candidates with 1 year or more job/training experience or over 5 years gap after result declaration are ineligible.

Refer to official notification for detailed conditions.

What to do at the current stage

How to Apply Online

  1. Open the official SJVN e-recruitment portal from Important Links.
  2. Read Advertisement No. 128/2026 carefully and confirm Himachal Pradesh domicile eligibility.
  3. Register with a valid email ID and mobile number that will remain active for at least one year.
  4. Log in and fill in personal, category, domicile and academic marks details accurately.
  5. Select one apprenticeship category and trade based on your qualification.
  6. Upload a scanned passport-size photograph and signature in JPG or JPEG format under 500 KB.
  7. Pay the Rs.100 application fee online if applicable (SC, ST, EWS and PwBD candidates are exempted).
  8. Submit the application before 17 December 2026 and save the confirmation copy.

    Quick answers

    Frequently Asked Questions

    The last date to submit the online application form for SJVN Apprentice Recruitment 2026 is 17 December 2026. Online registration opened on 18 November 2026 on the official SJVN e-recruitment portal, and candidates should apply early to avoid last-minute server issues.

    SJVN Limited has advertised 100 apprentice vacancies under Advertisement No. 128/2026. The seats are divided into 40 Graduate Apprentices, 25 Technician (Diploma) Apprentices and 35 Technician (ITI) Apprentices for projects and offices in Himachal Pradesh.

    Selected trainees receive a fixed monthly stipend of Rs.15,000 for Graduate Apprentices, Rs.13,500 for Technician (Diploma) Apprentices and Rs.12,500 for Technician (ITI) Apprentices. The apprenticeship training duration is one year under the Apprentices Act, 1961.

    Only domicile candidates of Himachal Pradesh who meet the prescribed educational qualification and age limit can apply. Applicants must hold a relevant 4-year Engineering Degree, 3-year Polytechnic Diploma or Class 10 with regular NCVT/SCVT ITI qualification.

    Candidates must be between 18 and 30 years of age as on 17 December 2026. Upper age relaxation of 5 years is provided to SC/ST, 3 years to OBC (Non-Creamy Layer), and 10 to 15 years to PwBD candidates according to their category.

    General and OBC candidates must pay a non-refundable application fee of Rs.100 online via Debit Card, Credit Card or Internet Banking. SC, ST, EWS and PwBD candidates are completely exempted from paying the application fee.

    No, there is no written examination or interview for SJVN Apprentice Recruitment 2026. Shortlisting is based purely on an academic merit list prepared from Class 10, Class 12, ITI, Diploma or Engineering Degree marks, followed by document verification.

    For Graduate Apprentices, weightage is 20% to 10th, 20% to 12th or Diploma, and 60% to Engineering aggregate. For Diploma and ITI Apprentices, weightage is 30% to 10th and 70% to Diploma or ITI marks respectively.

    Visit sjvn.nic.in, open the Careers section and click the Advt. No. 128/2026 apply link. Register with a valid email ID and mobile number, fill in academic details, upload photo and signature under 500 KB, pay the fee if applicable, and submit.

    The official website of SJVN Limited is https://sjvn.nic.in/, and online applications are submitted at https://recruitment.sjvn.co.in/ErecruitLogin/Login.jsp. All merit lists, document verification notices and final selection updates are published on sjvn.nic.in.
