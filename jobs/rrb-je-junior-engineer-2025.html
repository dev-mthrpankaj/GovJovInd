About This Recruitment

Railway Recruitment Board (RRB) Junior Engineer Recruitment 2025 was announced under CEN 05/2025 for a total of 2,569 posts of Junior Engineer, Depot Material Superintendent (DMS), and Chemical & Metallurgical Assistant (CMA) across various railway zones. The online application process was conducted from 31 October 2025 to 10 December 2025.

The recruitment involved a Computer Based Test (CBT-I), followed by CBT-II for shortlisted candidates. The CBT-II examination was held on 02 July 2026 after the exam city intimation was released on 22 June 2026.

The CBT-II result, cut-off, and the list of candidates shortlisted for Document Verification have now been declared. Candidates can check their score card and download the result PDF from the official RRB portals. This page will continue to carry every later stage including DV, medical, and final merit list on the same URL.
