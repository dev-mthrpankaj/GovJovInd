Complete context

About This Recruitment

Railway Recruitment Board (RRB) has released the Junior Engineer (JE) recruitment notification under CEN 05/2025 for 2569 posts across various railway zones. The online application process was conducted from 31 October 2025 to 10 December 2025.

The recruitment includes posts of Junior Engineer, Depot Material Superintendent (DMS), and Chemical & Metallurgical Assistant (CMA). Selection is based on CBT I, CBT II, document verification, and medical examination. Candidates must have an Engineering Diploma/Degree or B.Sc in a relevant trade.

The CBT I examination was held in February 2026, and the CBT II result has now been released. The CBT II examination was conducted on 02 July 2026. Candidates can check their results and score cards from the official RRB portal. This same page will carry every future update including document verification, medical, and final result.
