Complete context

About This Recruitment

Railway Recruitment Board (RRB) Junior Engineer Recruitment 2025 was announced under CEN 05/2025 for a total of 2,569 posts of Junior Engineer, Depot Material Superintendent (DMS), and Chemical & Metallurgical Assistant (CMA) across various railway zones. The online application process was conducted from 31 October 2025 to 10 December 2025.

The recruitment involved a Computer Based Test (CBT-I), followed by CBT-II for shortlisted candidates. The CBT-II examination was held on 02 July 2026 after the exam city intimation was released on 22 June 2026.

The CBT-II result, cut-off, and the list of candidates shortlisted for Document Verification have now been declared. Candidates can check their score card and download the result PDF from the official RRB portals. This page will continue to carry every later stage including DV, medical, and final merit list on the same URL.

What to do at the current stage

What to Do After CBT-II Result

  1. Download the CBT-II result PDF and check if your roll number is in the DV shortlist.
  2. Log in to the official RRB portal to view and download your score card.
  3. Check the cut-off marks for your category and post.
  4. Keep all original educational and category documents ready for Document Verification.
  5. Wait for the official DV schedule and venue details to be released by your RRB zone.
  6. Monitor this page and official RRB websites for DV and medical updates.

Original Application Process

Preserved for recruitment history and reference after the application stage closes.

  1. Visit the official RRB application portal.
  2. Read the CEN 05/2025 notification carefully.
  3. Register with valid email ID and mobile number.
  4. Fill the online application form with correct details.
  5. Upload photograph, signature, and required documents.
  6. Pay the examination fee through the permitted online mode.
  7. Submit the form and save the confirmation page for future reference.
