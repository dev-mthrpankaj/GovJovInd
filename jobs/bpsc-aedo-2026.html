Complete context

About This Recruitment

Bihar Public Service Commission (BPSC) has published Advertisement No. 16/2026 to recruit 935 Assistant Education Development Officer (AEDO) posts in Bihar. The official notification and important dates notice were released on 26 November 2026 on the official portal bpsc.bihar.gov.in.

Eligible graduates from any stream can apply online from 01 December 2026. The last date for online fee payment is 30 December 2026, and the last date for final submission of the online application form is 31 December 2026.

Candidates must be at least 21 years old as on 01 August 2025, with a maximum age limit of 37 years for male candidates and 40 years for female candidates. The application fee is Rs.100 across General, OBC, EWS, SC, and ST categories, payable online.

What to do at the current stage

How to Apply for BPSC AEDO 2026

  1. Download and read the official BPSC AEDO Advt No. 16/2026 PDF carefully to verify eligibility and reservation rules.
  2. Visit the official BPSC online application portal at bpsconline.bihar.gov.in once the link activates on 01 December 2026.
  3. Complete One Time Registration or log in using your valid email ID and mobile number.
  4. Fill in personal, academic, and category details accurately in the AEDO application form.
  5. Upload scanned copies of your recent photograph, signature, and required certificates in the prescribed format.
  6. Pay the Rs.100 application fee online before 30 December 2026.
  7. Submit the final application form by 31 December 2026 and download the confirmation printout for future reference.
