Complete context

About This Recruitment

The Rajasthan Public Service Commission (RPSC) originally released the Sub Inspector (SI) notification in 2021 to fill 857 vacancies. The initial application window closed on 23 June 2021.

Recently, RPSC announced a Re-Examination for this recruitment. The RPSC SI Re-Exam is officially scheduled to be conducted on 20 September 2026. Candidates who had successfully registered for the 2021 recruitment cycle are eligible to download their fresh admit cards and appear for the examination.

This master page tracks the complete history and future lifecycle events of the RPSC Sub Inspector 2021 recruitment, including the upcoming answer key, result, PMT/PET, and final merit list announcements.

What to do at the current stage

How to Download Admit Card

  1. Visit the official SSO Rajasthan portal or RPSC website.
  2. Log in using your SSO ID / Registration Number and Password.
  3. Navigate to the 'Recruitment Portal' section.
  4. Click on the 'Get Admit Card' link for Sub Inspector 2021 Re-Exam.
  5. Download and print the admit card clearly on an A4 size paper.
  6. Carry the printed admit card along with a valid original photo ID proof to the exam center.

Original Application Process

Preserved for recruitment history and reference after the application stage closes.

  1. Visit the official SSO Rajasthan portal.
  2. Login with your credentials (SSO ID and password).
  3. Navigate to the Recruitment Portal and look for the 'Get Admit Card' link.
  4. Download the SI 2021 Re-Exam Admit Card.
  5. Print it clearly on an A4 sheet.
  6. Verify all details such as Exam Center and timings thoroughly.
