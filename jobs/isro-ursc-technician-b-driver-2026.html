Complete context

About This Recruitment

Indian Space Research Organisation (ISRO), U R Rao Satellite Centre (URSC) has released a recruitment notification for the posts of Technician B, Heavy Vehicle Driver, Light Vehicle Driver and Staff Car Driver. The advertisement number is URSC: 04/2026, and a total of 61 vacancies are available across various trades and driver positions.

The online application process started on 24 October 2026 and will remain open until 13 November 2026. The last date for fee payment is also 13 November 2026. The exam date and admit card release date will be announced later on the official ISRO URSC website.

Candidates must have passed SSLC/SSC/Matriculation and possess an ITI/NTC/NAC certificate in the relevant trade from NCVT for Technician B posts. For driver posts, candidates must have a valid driving licence and relevant driving experience as specified in the official notification. The age limit is 18 to 35 years as on 13 November 2026, with age relaxation for reserved categories as per government rules.

The application fee is Rs. 500 for all category candidates, and Rs. 400 will be refunded to candidates who appear for the written examination. The selection process includes a written examination, skill test/trade test (for Technician posts), driving test (for Driver posts), and document verification.
