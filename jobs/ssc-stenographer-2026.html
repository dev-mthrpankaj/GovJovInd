About This Recruitment

Staff Selection Commission (SSC) has released the Stenographer Grade C and Grade D Recruitment 2026 for a total of 731 posts across various departments and ministries of the Government of India. The online application window was open from 24 April 2026 to 15 May 2026, and the computer-based examination was conducted from 09 to 15 September 2026.

The recruitment is a stenography-based selection process. Candidates applying for Grade C are required to possess a higher transcription speed as compared to Grade D, as mentioned in the official notification. The minimum educational qualification is Intermediate (Class 12) pass from a recognised board.

The provisional answer key has now been released on the official SSC website on 23 September 2026. Candidates who appeared in the examination can download the answer key and, where applicable, raise objections within the official window. This is a permanent recruitment page and will continue to carry the result, cut-off, document verification, and final merit list stages as soon as they are officially announced.
