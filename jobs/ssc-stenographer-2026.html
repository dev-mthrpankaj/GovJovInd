About This Recruitment

SSC Stenographer Grade C & D Examination 2026 is a Staff Selection Commission recruitment for Stenographer Grade 'C' (Group B, Non-Gazetted) and Grade 'D' (Group C) posts in various Ministries, Departments and Organizations of the Government of India.

The 2026 notification listed approximately 731 tentative vacancies. The application window ran from 24 April to 15 May 2026, followed by a correction window on 20-21 May 2026.

The Computer Based Examination is being conducted from 9 to 15 September 2026 after SSC revised the earlier 9-12 September schedule. Exam city information and admission certificates are available through the SSC login module according to the candidate's examination date.
