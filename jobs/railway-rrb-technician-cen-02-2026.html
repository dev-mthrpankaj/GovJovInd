Complete context

About This Recruitment

RRB Technician CEN 02/2026 is a Centralised Employment Notification (CEN) issued by the Railway Recruitment Board for recruitment to Technician Grade I Signal and Technician Grade III posts across Indian Railways. The official notification was published on 30 June 2026, and the online application window remained open from 30 June 2026 to 29 July 2026.

A total of 6,557 vacancies were announced, including 323 posts for Technician Grade I Signal (Pay Level 5) and 6,234 posts for Technician Grade III (Pay Level 2) across 24 trades. The Computer Based Test (CBT) for both grades was conducted from 06 to 09 October 2026. The provisional answer key and response sheet have now been released on 15 October 2026.

The application correction window was open from 01 August to 10 August 2026. The application status link was activated on 11 September 2026. The tentative exam date notice was released on 10 September 2026. The exam city intimation slip was released on 27 September 2026, and the CBT admit card was released on 02 October 2026.

Eligibility for Technician Grade I Signal requires a Bachelor's Degree in Science (Physics/Electronics/Computer Science/IT/Instrumentation) or BE/B.Tech/3-year Engineering Diploma in relevant streams. Technician Grade III requires Class 10th with an ITI certificate from NCVT/SCVT in the related trade. The age limit is 18 to 33 years for Grade I Signal and 18 to 30 years for Grade III as on 01 July 2026, with relaxation for reserved categories as per rules.

Eligibility Criteria

Age Limit

Grade I Signal: 18 to 33 years. Grade III: 18 to 30 years. Age reckoned as on 01 July 2026.

Age relaxation applicable for SC, ST, OBC, PwBD, Ex-Servicemen and other reserved categories as per Railway recruitment rules.

Education

Grade I Signal: B.Sc in Physics/Electronics/Computer Science/IT/Instrumentation OR BE/B.Tech OR 3-Year Engineering Polytechnic Diploma. Grade III: Class 10th with ITI Certificate from NCVT/SCVT in the related trade.

Candidates whose final results of prescribed qualifications were pending as on 29 July 2026 were not eligible to apply.

Nationality / Other

Indian citizens fulfilling the post-specific eligibility and medical standards may apply. PwBD suitability indicated against each post in Annexure A of the detailed CEN.

Refer to official notification for detailed conditions.

What to do at the current stage

How to Download RRB Technician Answer Key 2026 and Raise Objection

  1. Visit the official RRB portal at rrbapply.gov.in or your regional RRB website.
  2. Find the link for CEN 02/2026 Technician answer key, response sheet and objection tracker.
  3. Enter your Registration Number and Date of Birth/Password on the login screen.
  4. Complete the CAPTCHA verification and click Submit.
  5. Your question paper, recorded responses and the provisional answer key will appear on the screen.
  6. Compare each of your responses with the answer key and save a PDF copy for reference.
  7. To raise an objection, select the question you want to challenge, choose the correct option, enter justification and pay Rs.50 per question plus bank charges.
  8. Save the payment receipt and acknowledgement for every objection submitted.

Original Application Process

Preserved for recruitment history and reference after the application stage closes.

  1. Visit the official RRB application website at rrbapply.gov.in.
  2. Read the detailed CEN 02/2026 notification carefully.
  3. Register with a valid email ID and mobile number to create an account.
  4. Fill the online application form with accurate personal, educational and category details.
  5. Upload photograph, signature and required documents as per specifications.
  6. Pay the applicable examination fee through online modes.
  7. Submit the form and save the confirmation page for future reference.
