About This Recruitment

Railway Recruitment Board (RRB) has released the Centralised Employment Notification (CEN) No. 02/2026 for recruitment of Technician Grade I Signal and Technician Grade III posts across Indian Railways. The notification was released on 29 June 2026, and online applications were accepted from 30 June 2026 to 29 July 2026.

A total of 6,557 vacancies have been announced — 323 for Technician Grade I Signal (Level 5, starting pay Rs. 29,200) and 6,234 for Technician Grade III across 25 trade categories (Level 2, starting pay Rs. 19,900). The Computer-Based Test (CBT) is tentatively scheduled from 6 to 9 October 2026.

The exam city intimation slip has been released on 27 September 2026. Candidates can now check their allotted exam city, date and shift details through the official RRB portal. The e-Call Letter (admit card) will be released approximately 4 days before the respective exam date.
