Complete context

About This Recruitment

High Court of Madhya Pradesh (MPHC), Jabalpur, has released the recruitment notification for Assistant Grade III posts in District and Sessions Courts across Madhya Pradesh. The recruitment drive aims to fill 1,174 vacancies under Advertisement No. 614/Exam/2026 dated 14 August 2026.

The online application window was open from 17 August 2026 to 30 September 2026 (extended from the original last date of 15 September 2026). The application correction window was available from 06 to 08 October 2026. The preliminary examination is scheduled for 23 and 25 November 2026 across Jabalpur, Bhopal, Indore, Gwalior, Sagar, Satna, and Ujjain.

Eligibility requires a graduation degree in any discipline from a recognised university, a valid CPCT score card from MAP-IT or any government-recognised institution, and a one-year Diploma in Computer Application. The age limit is 18 to 40 years for male candidates and 18 to 45 years for female candidates as on 01 January 2026, with relaxation for reserved categories as per rules.

The selection process includes an online preliminary examination (100 questions, 100 marks, 120 minutes), a Hindi typing skill test, document verification, and medical examination. The salary is in the pay matrix of Rs. 19,500 to Rs. 62,000.

What to do at the current stage

How to Prepare for the Preliminary Exam

  1. Download and read the official exam date notice carefully.
  2. Check the exam pattern: 100 questions, 100 marks, 120 minutes.
  3. Study the five subjects: General Knowledge, Maths & Reasoning, Hindi, English, and Computer Knowledge.
  4. Practice mock tests and previous year papers to improve speed and accuracy.
  5. Keep your application number and password ready for admit card download.
  6. Admit cards will be available approximately 7 days before the exam on mphc.gov.in.

Original Application Process

Preserved for recruitment history and reference after the application stage closes.

  1. Visit the official MP High Court website at mphc.gov.in.
  2. Go to the Recruitment/Result section and find the Assistant Grade III recruitment notification.
  3. Register using a valid email ID and mobile number.
  4. Login using registration ID and password.
  5. Fill the application form with personal, educational, and category details.
  6. Upload scanned documents (photo, signature, etc.).
  7. Pay the application fee through online mode.
  8. Submit the form and download/print the confirmation page.
