Complete context

About This Recruitment

High Court of Madhya Pradesh (MPHC), Jabalpur invited online applications under Advertisement No. 614/Pariksha/2026 for 1174 Assistant Grade III posts across District Courts in Madhya Pradesh. The online application window ran from 17 August 2026 to the extended last date of 30 September 2026, followed by an application correction window from 06 October 2026 to 08 October 2026.

Admit cards for the online preliminary examination were issued on 16 November 2026, and the computer-based examination was conducted from 23 November 2026 to 25 November 2026. On 25 November 2026, the High Court of Madhya Pradesh published the notification along with the Proposed Model Answer Key PDF on its official portal.

To be eligible for MP High Court Assistant Grade III Recruitment 2026, candidates must hold a Bachelor Degree in any stream from a recognized university, a valid C.P.C.T. score card passed from MAP-IT or an MP Government-recognized institution, and a one-year Diploma in Computer Application from an institution recognized by the Government of Madhya Pradesh. The age limit is 18 to 40 years for male candidates and 18 to 45 years for female candidates as on 01 January 2026.

Candidates who appeared in the online examination can now download the proposed model answer key PDF to match their responses and calculate probable scores ahead of the preliminary examination result declaration.

What to do at the current stage

How to Check Proposed Model Answer Key

  1. Click the Download Answer Key button or open the official link from Important Links.
  2. Download the official Notification along with Proposed Model Answer Key PDF hosted on portal.mphc.gov.in.
  3. Locate your exam date and shift section inside the model answer key document.
  4. Match your attempted responses against the official proposed answers to calculate your raw score.
  5. Read the attached notice instructions carefully regarding representation or objection submission timelines, if applicable.
  6. Save a copy of the PDF for reference until the preliminary examination result is declared.

Original Application Process

Preserved for recruitment history and reference after the application stage closes.

  1. Visit the official MP High Court recruitment portal at portal.mphc.gov.in/recruitment-result.
  2. Select the MP High Court Assistant Grade III Recruitment 2026 online application link.
  3. Complete new registration using a valid mobile number and email address.
  4. Log in with generated credentials and enter personal, category, and educational details.
  5. Upload scanned photograph, signature, and required eligibility certificates.
  6. Pay the prescribed online examination fee according to candidate category.
  7. Submit the completed form and download the confirmation receipt for records.

Frequently Asked Questions

The online preliminary examination for MP High Court Assistant Grade III was held from 23 November 2026 to 25 November 2026 across designated test centres in Madhya Pradesh.

A total of 1174 vacancies for Assistant Grade III are advertised under Advt No. 614/Pariksha/2026, including 314 General, 325 OBC, 118 EWS, 181 SC, and 236 ST posts.

Candidates must hold a Bachelor Degree in any stream, a valid C.P.C.T. score card from MAP-IT or an MP Government-recognized body, and a one-year Diploma in Computer Application.
