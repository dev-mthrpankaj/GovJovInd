About This Recruitment

High Court of Madhya Pradesh (MPHC) has released the notification for MP High Court Assistant Grade III Recruitment 2026 for 1174 posts. The online application window is open from 17 August 2026, and the last date to apply has been extended to 30 September 2026.

This recruitment is for Assistant Grade III posts in the District Courts of Madhya Pradesh. Candidates with a Bachelor's degree in any stream and a valid CPCT score card or one-year computer diploma from a recognized institution are eligible to apply.

The selection process may include a written examination followed by document verification and other stages as prescribed by the High Court of Madhya Pradesh. Candidates should read the official notification carefully before applying.
