How to Apply

  1. Read the official notification and confirm eligibility before starting the form.
  2. Visit the official website of MECL (https://mecl.co.in/).
  3. Click on the 'MECL Non Executive Post 2026 Apply Online' link.

  5. Login using registration ID and password.
  6. Fill the application form with personal, educational, and category details.
  7. Upload scanned documents (photo, signature, etc.).
  8. Pay the application fee through online mode.
