Complete context

About This Recruitment

UPSSSC Computer Cadre Recruitment 2026 is being conducted by the Uttar Pradesh Subordinate Services Selection Commission for 12 posts in the General's Office Computer Cadre. The advertised posts are Computer Operator Grade ‘A’ and Assistant Computer Operator.

Online applications are scheduled from 7 September to 28 September 2026, with the correction window available up to 5 October 2026. Candidates should check their UPSSSC PET 2025 score-card requirement, educational qualification and prescribed experience before applying.

The available recruitment details require a bachelor's degree along with a Diploma in Computer Science or NIELIT ‘O’ Level qualification. Relevant computer-application experience differs by post, so applicants should verify the post-specific conditions in the official notification.
