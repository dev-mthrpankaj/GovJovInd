Complete context

About This Recruitment

Uttar Pradesh Subordinate Services Selection Commission (UPSSSC) issued Advertisement No. 20/2026 for the Advocate General's Office Computer Cadre Recruitment 2026 to fill 12 posts in Uttar Pradesh. The vacancies include 11 posts of Computer Operator Grade 'A' and 1 post of Assistant Computer Operator.

Online applications were accepted from 07 September 2026 to 28 September 2026, with the fee payment deadline on 28 September 2026 and the application correction window closing on 05 October 2026. Candidates were required to hold a valid UPSSSC PET 2025 Score Card along with a Bachelor's Degree, a Diploma in Computer Science or NIELIT 'O' Level Certificate, and relevant computer applications experience.

On 21 November 2026, UPSSSC declared the Eligibility Result for the Computer Cadre recruitment. Shortlisted candidates can check their result status on the official UPSSSC portal and await the official schedule for the Main Examination, admit card, and subsequent stages.

Eligibility Criteria

Age Limit

21 to 40 years as on 01 July 2026.

Age relaxation is applicable for reserved categories as per Uttar Pradesh government and UPSSSC rules.

Education

UPSSSC PET 2025 Score Card, Bachelor's Degree from a recognised university, Diploma in Computer Science or NIELIT 'O' Level Certificate, and 2 to 3 years' relevant computer applications experience.

Computer Operator Grade 'A' requires 3 years' experience and Assistant Computer Operator requires 2 years' experience in Computer Applications.

Nationality / Other

Candidates must have appeared in UPSSSC PET 2025 and satisfy all eligibility terms mentioned in Advt No. 20/2026.

What to do at the current stage

How to Check UPSSSC Computer Cadre Eligibility Result 2026

  1. Open the official UPSSSC Eligibility Result link from the Important Links section.
  2. Enter your Registration Number, Date of Birth, Gender and verification code on the UPSSSC result portal.
  3. Submit the details to view your Computer Cadre Eligibility Result status on the screen.
  4. Download the Eligibility Result Notice PDF to review the official cut-off and shortlisting instructions.
  5. Save and print a copy of your result status for the upcoming Main Examination and Document Verification stages.

Original Application Process

Preserved for recruitment history and reference after the application stage closes.

  1. Visit the official UPSSSC website at https://upsssc.gov.in/.
  2. Open the Advt No. 20/2026 UPSSSC Computer Cadre 2026 Apply Online link.
  3. Register or authenticate using UPSSSC PET 2025 registration details, email ID and mobile number.
  4. Fill in personal, educational, computer qualification and experience details carefully.
  5. Upload scanned photograph, signature and required documents.
  6. Pay the Rs.25 online application fee through the prescribed online payment mode.
  7. Submit the application form and print the confirmation page for future reference.
