Complete context

About This Recruitment

UPSC Civil Services (IAS/IFS) Examination 2026 is a national-level recruitment conducted by the Union Public Service Commission to fill 1013 vacancies across the Indian Administrative Service (IAS), Indian Foreign Service (IFS), Indian Police Service (IPS) and other Group A and B central services. The official notification was released on 14 January 2026, and online applications were accepted from 04 February 2026 to 27 February 2026.

The Preliminary Examination was held on 24 May 2026, and its result was declared on 17 June 2026. The Main Examination was conducted from 21 August to 30 August 2026. The Mains Result was declared on 05 November 2026, and 2,526 candidates have qualified for the Personality Test (Interview). The final result is expected in March or April 2027.

Candidates must possess a graduation degree in any stream from a recognised university. The age limit is 21 to 32 years as on 01 August 2026, with relaxation for reserved categories as per UPSC rules. The application fee is Rs.100 for General, OBC and EWS candidates, while SC, ST, PwBD and women candidates are exempted.

What to do at the current stage

How to Check Mains Result

  1. Open the official UPSC website from the Important Links section.
  2. Click on the Mains Result PDF link for roll number wise or name wise list.
  3. Download the PDF and search for your roll number or name.
  4. Check your qualification status for the Personality Test (Interview).
  5. Keep your documents ready for the interview stage.

Original Application Process

Preserved for recruitment history and reference after the application stage closes.

  1. Visit the official UPSC website at upsc.gov.in.
  2. Read the official notification carefully.
  3. Register on the UPSC portal with valid email ID and mobile number.
  4. Fill the online application form with accurate details.
  5. Upload photograph, signature and required documents.
  6. Pay the applicable examination fee online.
  7. Submit the form and save the confirmation page.
