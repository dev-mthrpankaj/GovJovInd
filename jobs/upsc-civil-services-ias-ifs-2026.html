Complete context

About This Recruitment

UPSC Civil Services (IAS) and Indian Forest Service (IFS) Examination 2026 is a combined recruitment examination conducted by the Union Public Service Commission for selection to the Indian Administrative Service (IAS) and Indian Forest Service (IFS). The official notification (Advt. No. 05/2026) was published in February 2026, and the online application window remained open from 04 February 2026 to 27 February 2026 (extended).

The Civil Services (Preliminary) Examination 2026 was conducted on 24 May 2026. The Prelims admit card was released on 15 May 2026, and the provisional answer key was published on 27 May 2026. The Prelims result was declared on 15 June 2026, and candidates who qualified were eligible to appear for the Mains examination.

The IAS Mains application window was open from 19 June 2026 to 29 June 2026, while the IFS Mains application window remained open from 13 July 2026 to 22 July 2026. The IAS Mains admit card was released on 14 August 2026, and the IAS Mains examination was conducted from 21 to 30 August 2026. The IFS Mains Exam Date Notice has now been released, and the IFS Mains examination is scheduled to be held from 22 to 29 November 2026.

The examination is conducted in three stages: Preliminary Examination (objective type), Main Examination (written examination), and Personality Test (Interview). The final merit list is prepared based on the combined scores of the Main Examination and the Personality Test. The total number of advertised vacancies is 1013, comprising 933 posts in the Indian Administrative Service (IAS) and 80 posts in the Indian Forest Service (IFS).

Basic eligibility requires a bachelor's degree from a recognised university. For the Indian Forest Service, candidates must hold a bachelor's degree in specific science subjects including Animal Husbandry, Veterinary Science, Botany, Chemistry, Geology, Mathematics, Physics, Statistics, Zoology, Agriculture, or equivalent qualifications. The age limit is 21 to 32 years as on 01 August 2026, with relaxation for reserved categories as per UPSC rules.
