Complete context

About This Recruitment

The Union Public Service Commission (UPSC) published the official notification for the Civil Services Examination (CSE) and Indian Forest Service (IFS) Examination 2026 to recruit eligible candidates for 1013 high-profile administrative posts, including Indian Administrative Service (IAS) and Officers Grade B DEPR.

The online application process was successfully completed between 04 February 2026 and 27 February 2026. The Preliminary Examination took place on 24 May 2026, and its result was formally declared on 17 June 2026. Successful candidates appeared for the Mains Examination, and the Civil Services Mains Result was announced on 05 November 2026.

Currently, the recruitment is at the Personality Test / Interview stage. UPSC released the detailed IAS Interview Schedule on 17 November 2026. Interviews are set to commence from 07 December 2026. Meanwhile, the IFS Mains Examination is scheduled to be held from 22 to 29 November 2026.

Candidates preparing for the interview must ensure they have all necessary original documents along with their Detailed Application Form (DAF) and e-Summon letter before appearing on their respective dates.

What to do at the current stage

Preparation for Interview Stage

  1. Download the official interview schedule and check your roll number.
  2. Note your specific date, session, and reporting time.
  3. Prepare all original educational and category documents for verification.
  4. Bring the e-Summon letter and recent passport-size photographs.
  5. Review your Detailed Application Form (DAF) thoroughly before the interview.

Original Application Process

Preserved for recruitment history and reference after the application stage closes.

  1. UPSC Civil Services IAS IFS Recruitment 2026 Candidates could apply between 04 February 2026 to 27 February 2026.
  2. Candidates must read the UPSC Civil Services IAS IFS Notification 2026 before applying.
  3. Check and collect all documents – Eligibility, ID Proof, Address Details, Basic Details.
