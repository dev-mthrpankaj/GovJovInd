Complete context

About This Recruitment

UPSC Civil Services IAS & IFS Examination 2026 is a Union Public Service Commission recruitment drive to fill 1013 posts, including 933 posts in the Indian Administrative Service (IAS) and 80 posts in the Indian Forest Service (IFS). The official notification was published on 4 February 2026, and the online application window remained open from 4 February 2026 to 27 February 2026.

The Civil Services Preliminary Examination was conducted on 24 May 2026, and the result was declared on 15 June 2026. A total of 13,343 candidates qualified for the Main Examination. The IFS Mains application window was open from 13 July to 22 July 2026, and the IAS Mains Admit Card was released on 14 August 2026 for the IAS Main Examination held from 21 to 30 August 2026.

The IFS Mains Admit Card was released on 13 November 2026, and the Indian Forest Service Main Examination will be conducted from 22 to 29 November 2026. Candidates must carry the printed e-Admit Card and a valid original photo ID to the examination centre.

Basic eligibility is a graduation degree in any stream from a recognised university. The age limit is 21 to 32 years as on 1 August 2026, with relaxation for SC, ST, OBC, PwBD, and Ex-Servicemen as per UPSC rules.

What to do at the current stage

How to Download IFS Mains Admit Card

  1. Open the official UPSC online portal at upsconline.nic.in.
  2. Click on the e-Admit Card link for the Indian Forest Service Main Examination 2026.
  3. Log in using your Registration ID or Roll Number and Date of Birth.
  4. Download the e-Admit Card PDF and check your name, roll number, exam dates, venue and instructions.
  5. Take a clear colour printout of the e-Admit Card.
  6. Carry the printed e-Admit Card and a valid original photo ID to the examination centre on each exam day.

Original Application Process

Preserved for recruitment history and reference after the application stage closes.

  1. Visit the official UPSC website at upsc.gov.in.
  2. Read the official notification for Civil Services IAS and IFS Examination 2026.
  3. Register on the UPSC online portal with valid email ID and mobile number.
  4. Fill the online application form with accurate personal and educational details.
  5. Upload photograph, signature and required documents.
  6. Pay the applicable examination fee online.
  7. Submit the form and save the confirmation page for future reference.
