About This Recruitment

Indian Overseas Bank (IOB) has released the result for the engagement of Apprentices under the Apprentices Act, 1961. The recruitment drive aims to fill 750 apprentice posts across various states and union territories. The online application process was conducted from 07 July 2026 to 27 July 2026 (extended).

The selection is based on an online examination, which was held on 02 August 2026. Candidates who qualify in the written exam will be called for document verification and other subsequent stages as per the bank's policy. The final merit list will be prepared based on the performance in the online exam and fulfilment of eligibility criteria.

This page is a permanent recruitment lifecycle page. It will be updated with the admit card, answer key, result, and final merit list as and when officially announced, without changing the URL.
