Complete context

About This Recruitment

Hindustan Copper Limited (HCL) is conducting this recruitment for engagement of Trade Apprentices at its Malanjkhand Copper Project (MCP) in Balaghat, Madhya Pradesh. The official notification No. HCL/MCP/HR/Trade App./01/2026 was released on 03 November 2026, and the online application window is open from 05 November 2026 to 24 November 2026.

A total of 171 vacancies are available across 19 trades, including Electrician, Fitter, Welder, Computer Assistant (COPA), Shotfirer / Blaster, Mechanic Diesel and others. Eligible candidates must have passed Class 10th and possess an ITI certificate in the relevant trade. The age limit is 18 to 25 years as on 01 November 2026, with relaxation for reserved categories as per rules.

The selection process is merit-based, followed by document verification and medical fitness. HCL will publish the list of shortlisted candidates on 08 December 2026. This is an apprenticeship training engagement under the Apprentices Act, 1961, and does not offer permanent employment.
