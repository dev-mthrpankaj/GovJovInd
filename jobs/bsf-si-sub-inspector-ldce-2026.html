Complete context

About This Recruitment

Directorate General Border Security Force (BSF) has released the CAPF SI LDCE Recruitment 2026 notification for 1167 Sub-Inspector (General Duty) vacancies across BSF, CRPF, SSB and ITBP. The advertisement number is A.2/SI (LDCE)/CAPFs Exam-2026/Pers(Rectt)/BSF/2026, and the official notification was published on 10 November 2026.

The online application window is active from 12 November 2026 to 31 December 2026. This is a departmental examination, so only serving Constables (GD/Tradesmen), Head Constables (GD) and ASIs (GD) of BSF, CRPF, ITBP and SSB who meet the service and educational eligibility can apply.

Candidates must have a Bachelor’s degree from a recognised university and at least 4 years of regular service, including basic training. The maximum age limit is 32 years as on 31 December 2026, with a 5-year relaxation for SC/ST candidates. Selection will be through a Computer Based Test (CBT), Physical Standard/Endurance Test (PST/PET), Document Verification and Medical Examination.

The pay scale is Pay Matrix Level-6 (Rs. 35,400 – Rs. 1,12,400). There is no application fee for any category. Eligible personnel should complete their application well before the deadline to avoid last-minute technical issues.
