Complete context

About This Recruitment

Indian Air Force (IAF) conducts the Air Force Common Admission Test (AFCAT) twice a year for selection of officers in Flying and Ground Duty branches. AFCAT 01/2027 is the first cycle of 2027, offering 284 tentative vacancies for courses commencing in January 2028 at the Air Force Academy, Dundigal.

The official notification was released on 11 November 2026. Online applications started on 12 November 2026 and will close on 11 December 2026 at 11:30 PM. The AFCAT written examination is scheduled for 31 January 2027 (Sunday). Candidates shortlisted in the written exam will appear for AFSB testing, followed by medical examination and merit list.

The recruitment covers Permanent Commission (PC) and Short Service Commission (SSC) entries. NCC Special Entry and GATE Score Entry are also available for eligible candidates. Basic eligibility includes Indian citizenship, unmarried status at the time of training, and prescribed age and educational qualifications for each branch.
