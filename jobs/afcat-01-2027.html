Complete context

About This Recruitment

Indian Air Force (IAF) conducts the Air Force Common Admission Test (AFCAT) twice a year for selection of officers in Flying and Ground Duty branches. AFCAT 01/2027 is the first cycle of 2027, offering 284 tentative vacancies for courses commencing in January 2028 at the Air Force Academy, Dundigal.

The official notification was released on 11 November 2026. Online applications started on 12 November 2026 and will close on 11 December 2026 at 11:30 PM. The AFCAT written examination is scheduled for 31 January 2027 (Sunday). Candidates shortlisted in the written exam will appear for AFSB testing, followed by medical examination and merit list.

The recruitment covers Permanent Commission (PC) and Short Service Commission (SSC) entries. NCC Special Entry and GATE Score Entry are also available for eligible candidates. Basic eligibility includes Indian citizenship, unmarried status at the time of training, and prescribed age and educational qualifications for each branch.

Eligibility Criteria

Age Limit

Flying Branch: 20 to 24 years. Ground Duty (Technical & Non-Technical): 20 to 26 years as on 01 January 2028.

Age relaxation for CPL holders in Flying Branch (up to 26 years) and other categories as per IAF rules.

Education

Flying Branch: Graduation in any discipline with Physics and Mathematics at 10+2 level, or BE/B.Tech. Ground Duty (Technical): 4-year Engineering/Technology degree in relevant discipline. Ground Duty (Non-Technical): Graduation/Post-Graduation as per branch requirement.

Candidates must fulfil branch-specific educational qualifications. Refer to the official notification for detailed discipline-wise requirements.

Nationality / Other

Indian citizens, unmarried at the time of course commencement. Medical and physical standards as prescribed by the Indian Air Force.

Refer to official notification for detailed conditions.

What to do at the current stage

How to Apply for AFCAT 01/2027

  1. Visit the official AFCAT portal at afcat.edcil.co.in.
  2. Click on the AFCAT 01/2027 apply online link.
  3. Select the entry: AFCAT, NCC Special Entry, or GATE Score Entry.
  4. Register with a valid email ID, mobile number and Aadhaar details.
  5. Fill the application form with personal, educational and branch preferences.
  6. Upload photograph, signature and required documents in the prescribed format.
  7. Pay the application fee (if applicable) and submit the form.
  8. Download and save the confirmation page for future reference.
