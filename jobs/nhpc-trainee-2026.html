Complete context

About This Recruitment

NHPC Limited, a Government of India Navratna Public Sector Undertaking, has published Advertisement No. NH/Talent Acquisition/09/2026 on 25 November 2026 for the recruitment of 47 Trainee Engineers and Trainee Officers in E2 Grade. The openings cover five specialised disciplines: Electronics & Communication (E&C), Environment, Information Technology (IT), Geology, and Geophysicist.

Candidates must hold a full-time regular Bachelor's degree in Engineering/Technology/B.Sc. (Engineering) or the prescribed Master's degree (M.Sc./M.Tech./MCA) in the relevant discipline with at least 60% marks, along with a valid GATE-2026 score and registration number in the corresponding paper. Final-year appearing students are not eligible to apply.

The selection process comprises shortlisting based on GATE-2026 normalized marks (75% weightage), followed by Group Discussion (5% weightage) and Personal Interview (20% weightage). Selected candidates are appointed in the E2 pay scale of Rs.50,000 to Rs.1,60,000 (IDA pattern), with approximate annual emoluments of Rs.16 to 17 lakh.

The online application portal on nhpcindia.com opens on 27 November 2026 at 10:00 AM and closes on 26 December 2026 at 5:00 PM. This permanent lifecycle page tracks every milestone from notification release to final empanelment and joining.

Eligibility Criteria

Age Limit

Maximum 30 years as on 26 December 2026.

Upper age limit is relaxable by 5 years for SC/ST, 3 years for OBC (Non-Creamy Layer), 10 years for PwBD (General), 13 years for PwBD (OBC-NCL), and 15 years for PwBD (SC/ST) candidates.

Education

Full-time regular B.E./B.Tech/B.Sc. (Engineering) or M.Sc./M.Tech/MCA in the prescribed discipline with minimum 60% marks (50% for SC/ST/PwBD) plus a valid GATE-2026 score.

Only GATE-2026 normalized marks out of 100 in the corresponding paper are valid. Final-year appearing students are not eligible.

Nationality / Other

Only Indian Nationals are eligible to apply. Selected candidates must execute a service agreement surety bond for a minimum period of 4 years including 1-year training.

Refer to official notification for detailed conditions.
