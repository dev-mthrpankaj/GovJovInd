About This Recruitment

Madhya Pradesh Power Generating Company Limited (MPPGCL) has released a notification for engagement of ITI Apprentices at Amarkantak Thermal Power Station (ATPS), Chachai, District Anuppur. The recruitment is for one year of apprenticeship training under the Apprentices Act, 1961. A total of 29 seats are available across six trades: Electrician (10), Fitter (8), Turner (3), Welder (3), Motor Mechanic (3) and COPA (2).

The online application process is conducted through the Apprenticeship India portal (apprenticeshipindia.gov.in). The application window opened on 21 October 2026 and will close on 20 November 2026 at 11:59 PM. Candidates must be permanent residents of Madhya Pradesh and must hold an ITI certificate (SCVT/NCVT) in the relevant trade from a recognised institute. The monthly stipend is Rs 9,600. There is no application fee.

Selection is purely merit-based. A merit list will be prepared based on the percentage equivalent of the average semester CGPA obtained in the relevant ITI trade. In case of equal marks, the older candidate will be given preference. There is no written examination or interview. Shortlisted candidates will undergo document verification and medical fitness verification before joining.

Eligibility Criteria

Age Limit

18 to 25 years as on 20 November 2026.

5 years relaxation for SC/ST/OBC (Non-Creamy Layer); 10 years for PwD candidates.

Education

ITI pass (SCVT/NCVT) in the relevant trade from a Government or non-Government ITI recognised in Madhya Pradesh.

Candidates must be permanent residents (domicile) of Madhya Pradesh.

Other Conditions

Candidates who have previously registered for apprenticeship in any establishment or have worked for one year or more are not eligible.

Preference will be given to candidates from Anuppur, Shahdol, Umaria, Katni and Jabalpur districts.
