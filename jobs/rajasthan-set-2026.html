Complete context

About This Recruitment

The Rajasthan State Eligibility Test (SET) 2026 is conducted by the University of Kota (UOK) on behalf of the Government of Rajasthan. It determines eligibility for appointment as Assistant Professor in Rajasthan state universities and colleges. The exam was held on 13 September 2026, and the result was declared on 6 November 2026.

A total of 9,191 candidates have qualified the SET 2026 examination. The result PDF containing roll numbers of qualified candidates along with cut-off details is available on the official website uok.ac.in. Candidates who do not find their roll numbers in the PDF have not qualified.

Eligibility requires a Master’s degree in the relevant subject with at least 55% marks (50% for SC/ST/OBC/PwD/Third Gender). There is no age limit. The application window was open from 14 June 2026 to 10 August 2026 (extended). The examination consists of two objective papers with no negative marking.

Qualified candidates can download their e-certificate and scorecard through SSO ID login after two weeks from the result date. The SET certificate is valid for lifetime and is accepted for Assistant Professor recruitment across Rajasthan.

What to do at the current stage

How to Check Rajasthan SET Result 2026

  1. Open the official University of Kota website at uok.ac.in.
  2. Click on the SET Result 2026 link available on the homepage.
  3. Download the result PDF containing roll numbers of qualified candidates.
  4. Search for your roll number in the PDF using Ctrl+F.
  5. If your roll number is listed, you have qualified the SET 2026 examination.
  6. Download and save the result PDF for future reference.
  7. E-certificates and scorecards will be available after two weeks via SSO ID login.

Original Application Process

Preserved for recruitment history and reference after the application stage closes.

  1. Visit the official University of Kota website at uok.ac.in.
  2. Read the SET 2026 notification carefully.
  3. Complete SSO registration if not already registered.
  4. Fill the online application form with accurate details.
  5. Upload photograph, signature and required documents.
  6. Pay the application fee online as per your category.
  7. Submit the form and save the confirmation page.
