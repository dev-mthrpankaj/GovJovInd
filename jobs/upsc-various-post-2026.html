Complete context

About This Recruitment

UPSC Various Post Recruitment 2026 is a direct recruitment drive for 212 positions across multiple central government departments and union territories. The Commission has released Advertisement No. 11/2026 covering medical, legal, editorial, and specialist posts.

The online application window opens on 12 September 2026 and closes on 2 October 2026 for most posts. Candidates applying for Public Law Officer posts under UT of Ladakh have a separate deadline of 9 October 2026.

The largest vacancy category is Assistant Public Prosecutor with 140 posts, followed by Specialist Grade III Assistant Professor (General Medicine) with 60 posts. Candidates should verify post-specific eligibility, age limits, and experience requirements before applying.

What to do at the current stage

How to Apply

  1. Read Advertisement No. 11/2026 carefully and confirm post-wise eligibility.
  2. Visit upsconline.nic.in and complete Account Creation and Universal Registration (URN).
  3. Fill the Common Application Form (CAF) and select the specific post module for Advt No. 11/2026.
  4. Capture a live photograph and upload the prescribed photograph and signature.
  5. Upload supporting documents for age, qualification, experience, and category as required.
  6. Pay the applicable fee (Rs. 25 for UR/OBC/EWS male; exempted for others).
  7. Submit before 2 October 2026 (6 PM); Ladakh post deadline is 9 October 2026.
  8. Save the submitted application and confirmation page for future reference.
