Complete context

About This Recruitment

MPPSC ADPO Examination 2026 is a recruitment drive by the Madhya Pradesh Public Service Commission to fill 107 Assistant District Prosecution Officer (ADPO) posts. The official notification (Advt. No. 03/2026) was published in March 2026, and the online application window remained open from 10 July 2026 to 24 July 2026.

The written examination is scheduled for 01 November 2026. The admit card for the same has been released on 21 October 2026. Candidates can download their hall tickets using their Enrollment Number, Registration Number, or Date of Birth from the official MPPSC portal.

Basic eligibility is a Bachelor’s Degree in Law (LLB) from a recognized university in India. The age limit is 21 to 40 years as on 01 January 2027, with relaxation for reserved categories as per MPPSC rules.

The selection process includes a Written Exam, Personal Interview, Document Verification, and Medical Examination. This page will be updated with the result, cut-off, interview schedule, and final merit list as they are released.

What to do at the current stage

How to Download MPPSC ADPO Admit Card

  1. Visit the official MPPSC ADPO admit card portal from Important Links.
  2. Enter your Enrollment Number, Registration Number, or Date of Birth.
  3. Download the Admit Card PDF for the written examination.
  4. Check your name, roll number, exam date, shift, reporting time and venue.
  5. Take a clear printout and carry it to the exam centre.
  6. Read all instructions on the admit card carefully before the exam day.

Original Application Process

Preserved for recruitment history and reference after the application stage closes.

  1. Visit the official MPPSC recruitment website at mppsc.mp.gov.in.
  2. Read the official notification (Advt. No. 03/2026) carefully.
  3. Register on the MPPSC portal with valid email ID and mobile number.
  4. Fill the online application form with accurate personal and educational details.
  5. Upload photograph, signature and required documents.
  6. Pay the applicable examination fee online.
  7. Submit the form and save the confirmation page for future reference.
