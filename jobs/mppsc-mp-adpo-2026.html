Complete context

About This Recruitment

MPPSC ADPO Examination 2026 is a recruitment drive by the Madhya Pradesh Public Service Commission to fill 107 Assistant District Prosecution Officer (ADPO) posts. The official notification (Advt. No. 03/2026) was published in March 2026, and the online application window remained open from 10 July 2026 to 24 July 2026.

The written examination is scheduled for 01 November 2026. The admit card for the same has been released on 21 October 2026. Candidates can download their hall tickets using their Enrollment Number, Registration Number, or Date of Birth from the official MPPSC portal.

Basic eligibility is a Bachelor’s Degree in Law (LLB) from a recognized university in India. The age limit is 21 to 40 years as on 01 January 2027, with relaxation for reserved categories as per MPPSC rules.

The selection process includes a Written Exam, Personal Interview, Document Verification, and Medical Examination. This page will be updated with the result, cut-off, interview schedule, and final merit list as they are released.
