About This Recruitment

MPESB Agriculture Extension Officer Recruitment 2026 is a Madhya Pradesh government recruitment drive for 2784 Agriculture Extension Officer (Krashi Vistar Adhikari) posts. The Madhya Pradesh Employees Selection Board (MPESB) has released the admit card for the written examination.

The online application window was open from 03 July 2026 to 17 July 2026. The revised exam date has been announced as 17 September 2026. Candidates who applied successfully can now download their admit card from the official MPESB portal.

The recruitment offers a significant opportunity for agriculture graduates seeking a stable government job in Madhya Pradesh. Candidates should download and print the admit card well before the exam date to avoid last-minute issues.

What to do at the current stage

How to Download Admit Card

  1. Visit the official MPESB website and open the Admit Card download link.
  2. Enter your Registration Number, Date of Birth/Password and required login credentials.
  3. Complete the CAPTCHA or security verification if prompted.
  4. Click on Submit/Login to access your MPESB Agriculture Extension Officer Admit Card 2026.
  5. Verify all details printed on the admit card including Name, Roll Number, Exam Date, Exam Time and Exam Centre.
  6. Download the admit card PDF and save it to your device.
  7. Take a clear printout on A4-size paper for the examination.
  8. Carry the printed admit card along with a valid Photo ID Proof (Aadhaar, PAN, Voter ID, Passport or Driving License) to the exam centre.

Original Application Process

Preserved for recruitment history and reference after the application stage closes.

  1. Visit the official MPESB recruitment website.
  2. Read the MPESB Agriculture Extension Officer notification carefully.
  3. Open the application link from Important Links.
