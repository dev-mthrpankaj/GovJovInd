About This Recruitment

MPESB Agriculture Extension Officer Recruitment 2026 is being conducted by the Madhya Pradesh Employees Selection Board for 2,784 Agriculture Extension Officer (Krashi Vistar Adhikari) posts in Madhya Pradesh.

Online applications were accepted from 3 July to 17 July 2026, with the correction facility available from 3 to 22 July 2026. The recruitment requires a Bachelor Degree in Agriculture from a recognized university in India.

The examination date was revised to 17 September 2026 and the admit card is now available through the MPESB portal. Candidates should download the admit card, verify the exam centre and reporting details, and carry the required photo identity proof to the examination centre.
