About This Recruitment

Central Board of Secondary Education (CBSE) released the KVS & NVS Teaching and Non-Teaching Recruitment 2025 notification for 15,762 posts. The online application window was open from 14 November 2025 to 11 December 2025. The Tier II examination was conducted from 27 to 31 March 2026.

The Tier II Result along with the Final Answer Key has now been declared on 17 August 2026. Candidates can download their results and the final answer key from the official links provided on this page. This permanent page tracks every stage of the CBSE KVS NVS recruitment lifecycle from notification to final result.

Candidates should keep their admit card, registration number, and date of birth ready to check their Tier II result. All future updates including DV, medical, and final merit list will be added to this same URL.
