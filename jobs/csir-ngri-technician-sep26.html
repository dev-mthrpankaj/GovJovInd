About This Recruitment

CSIR National Geophysical Research Institute (NGRI) Technician Recruitment 2026 is a central government recruitment drive for 21 Technician posts across trades such as Fitter, Plumber, Electrician, Computer/IT Assistant, Digital Photographer, Lab Assistant, Medical Lab Technician, Physiotherapy Technician and Catering & Hospitality Assistant. The online application window is open from 05 September 2026 to 04 October 2026.

This recruitment is suitable for candidates who have completed SSC / 10th standard with Science subjects (minimum 55% marks) along with an ITI Certificate or National / State Trade Certificate in the relevant trade. The age limit is 18 to 28 years as on 04 October 2026, with age relaxation applicable as per government rules.

Candidates should keep their photo, signature, educational certificates, ITI trade certificate, category certificate (if applicable) and a valid mobile number / email ID ready before starting the online form. This page will continue to carry every future stage — exam date, admit card, answer key, result and final merit list — on the same permanent URL.
