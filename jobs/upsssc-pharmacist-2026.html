About This Recruitment

UPSSSC Pharmacist Recruitment 2026 is a recruitment drive by the Uttar Pradesh Subordinate Service Selection Commission (UPSSSC) to fill 560 Pharmacist posts across various departments in Uttar Pradesh. The official notification was released in early 2026, and the online application window remained open from 09 March 2026 to 29 March 2026.

The Computer Based Test (CBT) was conducted on 29 June 2026. The provisional answer key was released on 01 July 2026, and candidates were allowed to raise objections. The final answer key has now been released on 20 August 2026. The mains result and subsequent stages, including document verification and final merit list, will be announced in due course.

Basic eligibility includes a Diploma in Pharmacy with registration in the State Pharmacy Council and a valid UPSSSC PET 2025 scorecard. The age limit is 18 to 40 years as on 01 July 2026, with relaxation for reserved categories as per UPSSSC rules.
