Complete context

About This Recruitment

Maulana Azad National Institute of Technology (MANIT) Bhopal has released a notification for the recruitment of various Group B and Group C Non-Teaching positions. A total of 55 vacancies have been announced across multiple roles including Superintendent, Technical Assistant, Senior Technician, Technician, Junior Assistant, Senior Assistant, SAS Assistant, and Library & Information Assistant.

The online application process commenced on 13 November 2026. Interested and eligible candidates can submit their online applications until 10 December 2026. Candidates are advised to complete the fee payment of Rs.1,770 (for UR/OBC/EWS) or Rs.885 (for SC/ST/PwD) within the application deadline.

Eligibility criteria vary by post, requiring qualifications ranging from 10th/12th pass with ITI to Master's degrees, along with specific skill sets such as computer proficiency and typing speed for administrative roles. Selection will be based on written examinations, skill tests (if applicable), and document verification.

    Frequently Asked Questions

    The last date to submit the online application form is 10 December 2026. Candidates should apply early to avoid last-minute server issues.

    A total of 55 vacancies are available across various Group B and Group C posts such as Superintendent, Technical Assistant, Senior Technician, and Junior Assistant.

    The application fee is Rs.1,770 for General, OBC, and EWS candidates. For SC, ST, and PwD candidates, the fee is Rs.885.

    For the Junior Assistant post, candidates must have passed the 10+2 (Intermediate) examination and possess a minimum typing speed of 35 words per minute along with computer proficiency.

    The minimum age is 18 years, and the maximum age limit varies between 27 to 33 years depending on the specific post applied for, as on 10 December 2026.

    The selection process generally involves a written examination, a skill/trade/typing test (if applicable for the post), document verification, and a medical examination.

    Eligible candidates can apply online through the official IBPS registration portal linked on the MANIT Bhopal website before the 10 December 2026 deadline.

    Candidates must possess the required educational qualifications as of the closing date of the application (10 December 2026) to be eligible.
