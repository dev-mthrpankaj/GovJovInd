About This Recruitment

Maulana Azad National Institute of Technology (MANIT) Bhopal has released a notification for the recruitment of various Group B and Group C Non-Teaching positions. A total of 55 vacancies have been announced across multiple roles including Superintendent, Technical Assistant, Senior Technician, Technician, Junior Assistant, Senior Assistant, SAS Assistant, and Library & Information Assistant.

The online application process commenced on 13 November 2026. Interested and eligible candidates can submit their online applications until 10 December 2026. Candidates are advised to complete the fee payment of Rs.1,770 (for UR/OBC/EWS) or Rs.885 (for SC/ST/PwD) within the application deadline.

Eligibility criteria vary by post, requiring qualifications ranging from 10th/12th pass with ITI to Master's degrees, along with specific skill sets such as computer proficiency and typing speed for administrative roles. Selection will be based on written examinations, skill tests (if applicable), and document verification.
