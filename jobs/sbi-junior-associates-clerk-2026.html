Complete context

About This Recruitment

State Bank of India (SBI) released the Junior Associates (Customer Support & Sales) Special Recruitment Drive 2026 notification for 1,538 backlog vacancies. The advertisement number is CRPD/CR/SPLDRIVE/2026-27/16. The online application window remained open from 7 August 2026 to 27 August 2026.

The Preliminary Examination was conducted on 16 September 2026. SBI declared the prelims result on 17 October 2026 along with individual scorecards and cut-off marks. Candidates who have qualified in the prelims will appear for the Mains examination, which is tentatively scheduled for 29 October 2026.

This recruitment drive is specifically for backlog vacancies reserved for SC, ST, and OBC categories. The selection process includes Prelims, Mains, and a Local Language Proficiency Test (LLPT) wherever applicable. The final merit list will be prepared based on Mains examination marks.

Basic eligibility is a graduation degree in any discipline from a recognised university. Final-year students who complete their graduation by 31 December 2026 are also eligible to apply provisionally. The age limit is 20 to 28 years as on 01 April 2026, with relaxation for reserved categories as per SBI rules.
