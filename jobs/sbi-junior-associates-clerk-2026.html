Complete context

About This Recruitment

State Bank of India (SBI) released a Special Recruitment Drive notification for Junior Associates (Customer Support & Sales) against 1,538 backlog vacancies reserved for SC, ST and OBC categories. The advertisement number is CRPD/CR/SPLDRIVE/2026-27/16, and the online application window remained open from 07 August 2026 to 27 August 2026.

The Preliminary Examination was conducted on 16 September 2026. SBI declared the Prelims Result on 17 October 2026, and candidates who qualified are eligible to appear for the Mains Examination. The Mains Admit Card was released on 19 October 2026, and the Mains exam is scheduled for 29 October 2026.

The selection process comprises the Preliminary Examination (qualifying in nature), the Mains Examination (marks counted for final merit), and the Local Language Proficiency Test (LLPT) for candidates who have not studied the specified local language at the 10th or 12th standard level. The final merit list is prepared state-wise and category-wise based on Mains examination marks.

Eligibility requires a Graduation degree in any discipline from a recognised university. The age limit is 20 to 28 years as on 01 April 2026, with upper age relaxation applicable for reserved categories as per SBI rules. The starting basic pay is Rs.26,730/- and starting emoluments in a metro city are approximately Rs.46,000/- per month.

What to do at the current stage

How to Download SBI Clerk Mains Admit Card

  1. Open the SBI Clerk Mains Admit Card download link from Important Links or visit sbi.bank.in.
  2. Log in using your Registration Number/Roll Number and Password or Date of Birth.
  3. Download the Mains Admit Card PDF and check all details carefully.
  4. Verify your name, roll number, exam date (29 October 2026), reporting time and exam centre address.
  5. Take a clear colour printout of the admit card to carry to the examination centre.
  6. Read all exam day instructions mentioned on the admit card before the exam.

Original Application Process

Preserved for recruitment history and reference after the application stage closes.

  1. Visit the official SBI careers website at sbi.bank.in.
  2. Read the Special Recruitment Drive notification (CRPD/CR/SPLDRIVE/2026-27/16) carefully.
  3. Register on the SBI portal with valid email ID and mobile number.
  4. Fill the online application form with accurate personal and educational details.
  5. Select one State/UT for which you wish to apply.
  6. Upload photograph, signature, left thumb impression and handwritten declaration.
  7. Pay the applicable application fee online.
  8. Submit the form and save the confirmation page for future reference.
