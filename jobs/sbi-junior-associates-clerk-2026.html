About This Recruitment

SBI Junior Associates Clerk Recruitment 2026 is a State Bank of India backlog recruitment drive for 1,538 posts under Advertisement No. CRPD/CR/SPLDRIVE/2026-27/16. The application window has closed and the admit card is now available.

Candidates with a graduation degree in any stream were eligible subject to the complete SBI notification. The supplied schedule lists the backlog examination for 16 September 2026.

Candidates should download the admit card, verify the examination instructions, and keep valid identity proof and required documents ready for the exam.
