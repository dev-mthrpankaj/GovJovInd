Complete context

About This Recruitment

State Bank of India (SBI) initiated the Special Recruitment Drive for Junior Associates (Customer Support & Sales) in the clerical cadre under Advertisement No. CRPD/CR/SPLDRIVE/2026-27/16 to fill 1,538 backlog vacancies across SC, ST, and OBC categories nationwide. The official notification was issued on 07 August 2026, and online applications remained open from 07 August 2026 to 27 August 2026.

The selection process comprises Phase-I Preliminary Examination, Phase-II Main Examination, and the Test of Specified Opted Local Language (where applicable), followed by Document Verification and Medical Examination. The Preliminary Examination was conducted on 16 September 2026, and its result was declared on 17 October 2026. Shortlisted candidates appeared for the online Main Examination on 29 October 2026.

On 25 November 2026, State Bank of India declared the Mains Result PDF listing the roll numbers of provisionally selected candidates. Provisional selection is subject to qualifying the Local Language Proficiency Test (for candidates who did not study the opted local language in Class 10 or Class 12), verifying original eligibility documents, and meeting SBI medical fitness standards.

Applicants had to hold a graduation degree in any discipline from a recognised university and be aged between 20 and 28 years as on 01 April 2026, with upper age relaxation as per Government of India rules. Selected Junior Associates receive a starting Basic Pay of Rs.26,730 per month (including two advance increments for graduates) in the pay scale of Rs.24,050 to Rs.64,480.

Eligibility Criteria

Age Limit

20 to 28 years as on 01 April 2026.

Upper age relaxation applicable for SC/ST (5 years), OBC Non-Creamy Layer (3 years), PwBD, Ex-Servicemen, and trained SBI Apprentices as per Government of India rules.

Education

Graduation in any discipline from a recognised University or any equivalent qualification recognised as such by the Central Government.

Candidates must also be able to read, write, speak, and understand the specified opted local language of the state/UT applied for.

Nationality / Other

Indian citizens belonging to eligible SC, ST, or OBC (Non-Creamy Layer) backlog categories. Candidates currently working in SBI in the clerical cadre or who resigned from SBI clerical cadre are not eligible.

Refer to official notification for detailed conditions.

What to do at the current stage

How to Check Mains Result & Next Steps

  1. Download the official SBI Junior Associates Backlog Mains Result PDF from Important Links.
  2. Open the PDF and press Ctrl+F (or use Find on mobile) to search your Roll Number.
  3. Save and print a copy of the Mains Result PDF for Document Verification records.
  4. Keep original graduation certificates, category certificates (OBC NCL issued on or after 01 April 2026, SC, or ST), and identity proof ready.
  5. Check your registered email and SBI Careers portal for the Local Language Proficiency Test (LLPT) and Document Verification schedule.

Original Application Process

Preserved for recruitment history and reference after the application stage closes.

  1. Visit the official SBI Careers portal at sbi.bank.in/web/careers/current-openings.
  2. Open the Special Recruitment Drive for Junior Associates (Advt. No. CRPD/CR/SPLDRIVE/2026-27/16) link.
  3. Complete online registration using a valid email ID and mobile number.
  4. Fill in personal, category, state/circle preference, and educational qualification details.
  5. Upload scanned photograph, signature, left thumb impression, and handwritten declaration.
  6. Pay the prescribed application fee (Rs.750 for OBC; Nil for SC/ST/PwBD) through online mode.
  7. Submit the final application form and print the confirmation page for future reference.
