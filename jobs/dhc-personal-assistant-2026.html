Complete context

About This Recruitment

The High Court of Delhi (DHC) is conducting the Senior Personal Assistant (SPA) and Personal Assistant (PA) Examination 2026 to fill 150 Group 'B' posts — 117 Senior Personal Assistant and 33 Personal Assistant vacancies. The official vacancy notice was published on 05 September 2026, and the online application window was open from 15 September 2026 to 05 October 2026.

The selection process includes an English Typing Test (qualifying), English Shorthand Test, Main Descriptive Examination and Interview. The Stage-I English Typing Test for SPA will be held on 14 November 2026, and for PA on 27 November 2026. The exam city intimation slip was released on 06 November 2026, and admit cards are expected from 11 November 2026.

Eligibility requires a graduation degree from a recognised university, with shorthand speed of 110 w.p.m. for SPA and 100 w.p.m. for PA, plus 40 w.p.m. English typing speed on computer for both posts. The age limit is 18 to 32 years as on 01 January 2026, with relaxation for reserved categories as per rules.
