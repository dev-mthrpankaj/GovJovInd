Complete context

About This Recruitment

High Court of Delhi (DHC) has invited online applications for Senior Personal Assistant (SPA) and Personal Assistant (PA) posts under the SPA & PA (Open) Examinations 2026. The recruitment covers 150 vacancies — 117 for Senior Personal Assistant and 33 for Personal Assistant — both Group-B posts. The official vacancy notice was released on 05 September 2026, and applications were accepted from 15 September to 05 October 2026.

The selection process begins with a Stage-I English Typing Test (qualifying), followed by an English Shorthand Test and a Main Descriptive Examination & Interview. The SPA exam is scheduled for 14 November 2026 and the PA exam for 27 November 2026, both in the Delhi-NCR region. The PA admit card will be live from 24 November 2026; the SPA admit card date will be notified on the official website.

Eligibility requires a Bachelor's degree from a recognised university. SPA candidates need English shorthand speed of 110 wpm and typing speed of 40 wpm; PA candidates need 100 wpm shorthand and 40 wpm typing, along with good computer knowledge. The age limit is 18 to 32 years as on 01 January 2026, with relaxation for reserved categories as per rules.
