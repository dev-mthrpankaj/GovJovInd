Complete context

About This Recruitment

High Court of Delhi is conducting the Senior Personal Assistant and Personal Assistant Examination 2026 to fill 150 Group B vacancies in New Delhi, comprising 117 Senior Personal Assistant posts (Pay Matrix Level 8, initial basic pay Rs.47600) and 33 Personal Assistant posts (Pay Matrix Level 7, initial basic pay Rs.44900). The official vacancy circular was published on 02 September 2026, online applications ran from 15 September 2026 to 05 October 2026, and the correction window remained open from 12 October 2026 to 14 October 2026.

The selection process consists of Stage I English Typing Test (40 w.p.m., 10 minutes, qualifying), Stage II English Shorthand Test (110 w.p.m. for Senior Personal Assistant and 100 w.p.m. for Personal Assistant), Stage III Main Descriptive Examination, Stage IV Personal Interview, and Document Verification. The Senior Personal Assistant typing test was conducted on 14 November 2026.

High Court of Delhi released the Personal Assistant Typing Test Admit Card on 23 November 2026 for the computer-based typing test scheduled on 27 November 2026. Registered candidates must download their hall ticket through the official login link in Important Links and carry a printed copy along with a valid original photo ID to the examination venue.

Eligibility requires a graduation degree from a recognised university, good working knowledge of computers, 40 w.p.m. English typing speed, and the post-specific English shorthand speed. The prescribed age limit is 18 to 32 years as on 01 January 2026, with category-wise upper age relaxation as per High Court of Delhi rules.

Eligibility Criteria

Age Limit

18 to 32 years as on 01 January 2026 (candidates must be born between 02 January 1994 and 01 January 2008).

Upper age relaxation is 5 years for SC/ST, 3 years for OBC-NCL, 10 years for PwBD (UR/EWS), 13 years for PwBD (OBC), and 15 years for PwBD (SC/ST) as per High Court of Delhi rules.

Education

Graduation degree from a recognised university with 40 w.p.m. English typing speed on computer, good working knowledge of computers, and English shorthand speed of 110 w.p.m. (SPA) or 100 w.p.m. (PA).

All educational and skill qualifications must be fulfilled on or before the closing date of online application (05 October 2026).

Nationality / Other

Candidates must be citizens of India. Separate online applications were required for candidates applying to both Senior Personal Assistant and Personal Assistant posts.

Refer to official notification for detailed conditions.

What to do at the current stage

How to Download DHC Personal Assistant Admit Card

  1. Open the official admit card login portal from the Important Links section.
  2. Enter your registered User ID and Password generated during online application.
  3. Verify your name, roll number, exam date (27 November 2026), reporting time, and test centre address.
  4. Download the admit card PDF and print two clear copies on A4 paper.
  5. Carry the printed admit card, a valid original government photo ID, and recent passport-size photographs to the exam venue.
  6. Reach the examination centre well before the gate closing time mentioned on the hall ticket.

Original Application Process

Preserved for recruitment history and reference after the application stage closes.

  1. Visit the official High Court of Delhi website at https://delhihighcourt.nic.in/web/.
  2. Open the Senior Personal Assistant and Personal Assistant Examination 2026 recruitment portal link.
  3. Complete new registration using a valid email ID and active mobile number.
  4. Log in with the generated User ID and password to access the application form.
  5. Enter personal, category, educational qualification, and post preference details accurately.
  6. Upload scanned copies of the passport-size photograph, signature, and required documents.
  7. Pay the applicable application fee (Rs.1500 for General/OBC/EWS or Rs.1300 for SC/ST/PwBD) online.
  8. Submit the completed form and save the confirmation printout for future reference.

Frequently Asked Questions

Yes, the High Court of Delhi released the Personal Assistant Typing Test Admit Card on 23 November 2026. Registered candidates can download the hall ticket using their User ID and Password via the official login link in Important Links.

The Senior Personal Assistant English Typing Test was conducted on 14 November 2026, while the Personal Assistant English Typing Test is scheduled for 27 November 2026. Exact shift timings and venue details are printed on each admit card.

The High Court of Delhi announced 150 total vacancies under the 2026 recruitment drive. This includes 117 posts for Senior Personal Assistant (Group B) and 33 posts for Personal Assistant (Group B) across eligible reservation categories.

The online application process for High Court of Delhi Senior Personal Assistant and Personal Assistant Recruitment 2026 started on 15 September 2026. The official vacancy notification was published earlier on 02 September 2026.

The last date to submit the online application form and pay the exam fee was 05 October 2026. Online applications are now closed, and the application correction window also concluded between 12 October 2026 and 14 October 2026.

Senior Personal Assistant is a Group B post placed in Pay Matrix Level 8 of the 7th CPC with initial basic pay of Rs.47600, while Personal Assistant is in Pay Matrix Level 7 with initial basic pay of Rs.44900 plus applicable allowances.

Graduates from a recognised university with good computer knowledge and 40 w.p.m. English typing speed were eligible. Candidates also needed English shorthand speed of 110 w.p.m. for Senior Personal Assistant or 100 w.p.m. for Personal Assistant.

Candidates must be between 18 and 32 years of age as on 01 January 2026 (born between 02 January 1994 and 01 January 2008). Upper age relaxation applies to SC, ST, OBC-NCL, PwBD, and Ex-Servicemen categories as per official rules.

General, OBC, and EWS category applicants paid Rs.1500, while SC, ST, and PwBD (PH) candidates paid Rs.1300. All examination fees were collected exclusively through online payment modes before the 05 October 2026 closing deadline.

Applicants registered on the High Court of Delhi recruitment portal, filled in personal and academic details, uploaded scanned photo and signature, paid the online fee, and printed the confirmation page before applications closed on 05 October 2026.

Selection involves five stages: Stage I qualifying English Typing Test (40 w.p.m.), Stage II English Shorthand Test, Stage III Main Descriptive Examination, Stage IV Personal Interview, followed by Document Verification before final appointment.
