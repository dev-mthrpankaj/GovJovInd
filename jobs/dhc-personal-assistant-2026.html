About This Recruitment

Delhi High Court Personal Assistant Recruitment 2026 covers 150 vacancies for Senior Personal Assistant and Personal Assistant posts in New Delhi. Online applications are scheduled from 15 September 2026 to 5 October 2026.

Senior Personal Assistant requires graduation with at least 110 w.p.m. English shorthand and 40 w.p.m. English typing on computer. Personal Assistant requires graduation with at least 100 w.p.m. English shorthand and 40 w.p.m. English typing on computer, along with good working knowledge of computers.

Candidates should read the official Delhi High Court recruitment notification carefully before applying. The correction window is scheduled for 12 to 14 October 2026; later exam, admit-card and result dates are awaited.
