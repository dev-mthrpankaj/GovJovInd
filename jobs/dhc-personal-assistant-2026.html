About This Recruitment

The High Court of Delhi (DHC) has released a notification for the recruitment of Senior Personal Assistant (SPA) and Personal Assistant (PA) posts. This recruitment drive aims to fill a total of 150 vacancies.

The online application process is active from 15 September 2026 to 05 October 2026. Candidates with a graduate degree and the specified shorthand and typing speeds are eligible to apply. It is a significant opportunity for candidates seeking a government job in the Delhi court system.

This is a permanent recruitment lifecycle page. All future updates, including the admit card, exam date, answer key, and results, will be published on this same URL. Candidates should bookmark this page and check the official notification for detailed information before applying.
