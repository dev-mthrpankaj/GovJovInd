About This Recruitment

The Union Public Service Commission conducts the Combined Medical Services Examination for recruitment to medical posts/services covered by the examination rules. The CMS 2026 notification was issued on 11 March 2026 and applications closed on 31 March 2026 at 6:00 PM.

UPSC uploaded the e-Admit Card on 24 July 2026 and conducted the written examination on 02 August 2026. Provisional answer keys for both papers were subsequently made available.

The latest stage is the UPSC CMS 2026 Written Result, released on 01 September 2026. Both roll-number-wise and name-wise official result PDFs are retained here, along with earlier admit-card, answer-key and exam-stage links.
