About This Recruitment

UP Scholarship 2026-27 is an Uttar Pradesh government scholarship application cycle for eligible Post Matric and Dashmottar students. Post Matric applications are open from 22 July to 21 September 2026 according to the supplied schedule.

The scholarship covers Post Matric Class 11-12 and Dashmottar courses including UG, PG, Diploma and Certificate programmes. Both fresh and renewal candidates should use the appropriate official login.

Applicants should keep their marksheet, caste and income certificates where applicable, bank passbook, fee details, enrollment number, Aadhaar number and recent photograph ready before completing the form.
