Complete context

About This Recruitment

The Supreme Court of India (SCI) has released the recruitment notification for the post of Junior Court Assistant (JCA) 2026. The advertisement number is F.6/2026-SC(RC). A total of 250 posts are notified on a tentative basis, and the recruitment is open to candidates from all over India.

The online application window opened on 07 October 2026 and will remain active till 28 October 2026. The last date for payment of the examination fee is also 28 October 2026. Candidates who wish to apply must complete registration, form filling, document upload and fee payment before the deadline.

The minimum educational qualification is a Bachelor's Degree in any stream from a recognised university or institute in India. Along with the degree, candidates must be able to type in English at a speed of 35 words per minute (WPM). The age limit is 18 to 30 years as on 01 September 2026, with relaxation for reserved categories as per government rules.

The application fee is Rs.1500 for General, OBC and EWS candidates, and Rs.750 for SC and ST candidates.
