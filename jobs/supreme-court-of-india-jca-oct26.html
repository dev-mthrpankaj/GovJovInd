Complete context

About This Recruitment

Supreme Court of India Junior Court Assistant Examination 2026 is a recruitment drive to fill 250 Group-B Non-Gazetted Junior Court Assistant posts. The official notification (Advertisement No. F.6/2026-SC (RC)) was published on 30 September 2026, and the online application window remained open from 7 October 2026 to 28 October 2026.

The Objective Type Test is scheduled for 20 November 2026. The Exam City Intimation Slip was released on 9 November 2026, allowing candidates to check their allotted examination city. The admit card with complete centre details will be released separately before the exam.

Basic eligibility is a Bachelor's degree in any stream from a recognised university plus English typing speed of 35 words per minute. The age limit is 18 to 30 years as on 1 September 2026, with relaxation for reserved categories as per Government of India rules.

The selection process includes an Objective Type Test, a Typing Speed Test on Computer, a Descriptive Written Test, and an Interview. The pay scale is Level 6 of the Pay Matrix with an initial basic pay of Rs.35,400 plus allowances.

What to do at the current stage

How to Check Exam City Intimation Slip

  1. Open the official SCI JCA exam city login portal from Important Links.
  2. Enter your User ID / Registration Number and Password / Date of Birth.
  3. Complete the CAPTCHA verification and click Login.
  4. Your Exam City Intimation Slip will appear on the screen.
  5. Check your allotted examination city and exam date carefully.
  6. Download and save the slip for future reference.

Original Application Process

Preserved for recruitment history and reference after the application stage closes.

  1. Visit the official Supreme Court of India recruitment website at sci.gov.in.
  2. Read the detailed advertisement (Advt. No. F.6/2026-SC (RC)) carefully.
  3. Register on the online application portal with a valid email ID and mobile number.
  4. Fill the online application form with accurate personal and educational details.
  5. Upload photograph, signature and required documents.
  6. Pay the applicable examination fee online.
  7. Submit the form and save the confirmation page for future reference.
