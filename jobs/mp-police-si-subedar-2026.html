Complete context

About This Recruitment

MP Police SI Subedar Recruitment 2026 is a Madhya Pradesh Employees Selection Board (MPESB) recruitment drive to fill 507 Subedar and Sub-Inspector posts in the Madhya Pradesh Home (Police) Department. The official notification was published in September 2026, and the online application window remained open from 9 September 2026 to 23 September 2026.

The written examination was conducted from 30 October to 5 November 2026 under the revised schedule. The provisional answer key was released on 10 November 2026, and candidates can raise objections until 13 November 2026. The final answer key and result will follow after the objection window closes.

Basic eligibility is a Bachelor's Degree in any stream from a recognised university for most posts, while technical posts such as Sub-Inspector (Arms) require a Diploma in Mechanical Engineering. The age limit is 18 to 33 years for male candidates and 18 to 38 years for female candidates as on 1 January 2026, with relaxation for reserved categories as per MPESB rules.

What to do at the current stage

How to Raise Objection Against Answer Key

  1. Open the official objection portal from Important Links.
  2. Log in using your application number, TAC code and date of birth.
  3. Enter the captcha code and submit to view the provisional answer key and your response sheet.
  4. Compare your recorded responses with the provisional key.
  5. Select the question you want to challenge and submit your objection with supporting evidence.
  6. Pay the prescribed objection fee (if applicable) and save the acknowledgement for future reference.

Original Application Process

Preserved for recruitment history and reference after the application stage closes.

  1. Visit the official MPESB website at esb.mp.gov.in.
  2. Read the official notification for Subedar and Sub-Inspector Recruitment Test 2026 carefully.
  3. Register on the MPESB portal with a valid email ID and mobile number.
  4. Fill the online application form with accurate personal and educational details.
  5. Upload photograph, signature and required documents.
  6. Pay the applicable examination fee online.
  7. Submit the form and save the confirmation page for future reference.
