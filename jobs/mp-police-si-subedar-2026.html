Complete context

About This Recruitment

Madhya Pradesh Employees Selection Board (MPESB) has released the MP Police SI Sub Inspector Subedar Recruitment 2026 notification for 507 posts in the Madhya Pradesh Police Department. The recruitment covers multiple posts including Subedar, Sub Inspector (Special Force), Sub Inspector (Civil Police), Sub Inspector (Ayudh), Sub Inspector (Photo), Sub Inspector Q.D and Sub Inspector (Finger Print).

The online application window remained open from 09 September 2026 to 23 September 2026. The fee payment last date was 23 September 2026, and the form correction window closed on 28 September 2026. The written examination is scheduled for 28 October 2026, and the admit card has been released on the official MPESB portal.

Candidates must carry the printed MP Police SI Admit Card, a valid original photo ID, and all required documents to the examination centre. The admit card contains the exam date, reporting time, venue and important instructions.

Basic eligibility includes a Bachelor Degree in any stream from a recognised university for most posts. Certain posts such as Sub Inspector (Ayudh) require a Diploma in Mechanical Engineering, while Sub Inspector (Photo), Sub Inspector Q.D and Sub Inspector (Finger Print) require a Bachelor Degree with Physics, Chemistry and Mathematics as subjects. The age limit is 18 to 33 years for male candidates and 18 to 38 years for female candidates as on 01 January 2026, with relaxation for reserved categories as per MP government rules.

Eligibility Criteria

Age Limit

18 to 33 years for male candidates and 18 to 38 years for female candidates as on 01 January 2026.

Age relaxation applicable for SC, ST, OBC, PwBD, Ex-Servicemen and other reserved categories as per MP government rules.

Education

Bachelor Degree in any stream from a recognised university. Post-specific qualifications apply for Sub Inspector (Ayudh) – Diploma in Mechanical Engineering, and Sub Inspector (Photo), Sub Inspector Q.D, Sub Inspector (Finger Print) – Bachelor Degree with Physics, Chemistry and Mathematics.

Candidates must fulfill the specific educational qualification for the post they are applying for.

Physical Standards

Height: Male 167.5 cm, Female 152.4 cm. Chest (Male only): 81-86 cm. Candidates must meet the physical standards prescribed in the official notification.

What to do at the current stage

How to Download MP Police SI Admit Card 2026

  1. Open the official MPESB admit card portal from Important Links.
  2. Click on the MP Police SI Sub Inspector Subedar Admit Card link.
  3. Enter your Registration Number, Roll Number or Date of Birth.
  4. Download the admit card PDF for the written examination.
  5. Check your name, roll number, exam date, reporting time and venue.
  6. Take a clear colour printout and carry it to the exam centre along with a valid photo ID.
  7. Read all instructions printed on the admit card carefully before the exam day.

Original Application Process

Preserved for recruitment history and reference after the application stage closes.

  1. Visit the official MPESB website at esb.mp.gov.in.
  2. Read the official MP Police SI Sub Inspector Subedar notification 2026 carefully.
  3. Complete MP Profile Registration on the MPESB portal.
  4. Register for the MP Police SI Sub Inspector Subedar 2026 examination.
  5. Fill the online application form with accurate personal, educational and category details.
  6. Upload photograph, signature and required documents.
  7. Pay the applicable examination fee online or through MP Kiosk.
  8. Submit the form and save the confirmation page for future reference.
