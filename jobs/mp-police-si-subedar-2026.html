About This Recruitment

MP Police SI Subedar Recruitment 2026 is being conducted through the Madhya Pradesh Employees Selection Board (MPESB) for 507 posts across Subedar and multiple Sub Inspector cadres. The recruitment includes general police posts as well as technical/specialized SI posts with post-specific educational qualifications.

The online application window is scheduled from 9 September to 23 September 2026, with fee payment up to 23 September and form correction up to 28 September 2026. The examination is listed for 28 October 2026.

Candidates should check the post-wise qualification and physical standards carefully before applying. The supplied recruitment information lists minimum physical standards of 167.5 cm height for male candidates, 152.4 cm for female candidates, and 81–86 cm chest for male candidates.

Eligibility Criteria

Age Limit

Age as on 01 January 2026: minimum 18 years; maximum 33 years for male candidates and 38 years for female candidates as listed in the supplied recruitment information.

Check the official MPESB rulebook for category-wise age relaxation and detailed conditions.

Education

Bachelor's degree for Subedar, Special Force SI and Civil Police SI. Technical/specialized SI posts require the specific diploma or science-subject qualification shown in the vacancy table.

Post-wise educational qualification differs; verify the official rulebook before applying.

Nationality / Other

Physical standards listed in the supplied information: male height 167.5 cm, female height 152.4 cm, and male chest 81–86 cm. Check the official rulebook for complete physical and other eligibility conditions.

Refer to official notification for detailed conditions.
