Complete context

About This Recruitment

JKSSB Junior Engineer (Mechanical) Recruitment 2026 is a Jammu & Kashmir Services Selection Board recruitment drive to fill 127 Junior Engineer (Mechanical) posts in the Public Works (R&B) Department under the UT Cadre. The official notification (Advertisement No. 10 of 2026) was released on 21 October 2026.

The online application window will open on 16 November 2026 and close on 15 December 2026. Candidates must hold a valid J&K Domicile Certificate, a 3-year Diploma in Mechanical Engineering, a Degree in Mechanical Engineering, or AMIE Section (A&B) India. The age limit is 18 to 40 years for Open Merit candidates as on 01 January 2026, with relaxation for reserved categories.

The selection process includes a Written/OMR Examination (objective multiple-choice questions in English with one-fourth negative marking) followed by Document Verification. There is no interview. The exam date and admit card release date will be notified separately on the official JKSSB website.

The application fee is Rs.600 for Open Merit/General candidates and Rs.500 for SC, ST-1, ST-2, EWS and PwBD candidates. Payment is online only. This permanent page will track every stage from notification to final selection.

Eligibility Criteria

Age Limit

18 to 40 years as on 01 January 2026 for Open Merit candidates.

Upper age limit is 43 years for SC, ST-1, ST-2, RBA, ALC/IB, EWS and OBC candidates; 42 years for Physically Challenged candidates. Ex-Servicemen can deduct military service period from their actual age.

Education

3-year Diploma in Mechanical Engineering from a Government-recognised institute, OR a Degree in Mechanical Engineering from an Indian University, OR AMIE Section (A&B) India.

The qualification must be completed on or before 15 December 2026. A valid J&K Domicile Certificate is mandatory.

Nationality / Other

Candidates must be domiciles of the Union Territory of Jammu & Kashmir with a valid Domicile Certificate. Reserved category candidates must upload a valid category certificate in the prescribed format.

Refer to official notification for detailed conditions.

What to do at the current stage

How to Apply for JKSSB JE (Mechanical) 2026

  1. Visit the official JKSSB website at jkssb.nic.in and open the online application portal.
  2. Register with a valid email ID and mobile number.
  3. Fill in your personal, educational and category details exactly as per your certificates.
  4. Upload a live photograph and signature (10 KB to 20 KB), category certificate (PDF, max 200 KB) and qualification certificates (PDF, max 200 KB).
  5. Pay the application fee online (Rs.600 for OM/General; Rs.500 for SC/ST-1/ST-2/EWS/PwBD).
  6. Review the form carefully, submit it and download the confirmation page for Document Verification.
