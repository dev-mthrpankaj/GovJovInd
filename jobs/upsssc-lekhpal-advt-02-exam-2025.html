About This Recruitment

UPSSSC Lekhpal Recruitment 2025-2026 is a large-scale recruitment drive by the Uttar Pradesh Subordinate Service Selection Commission (UPSSSC) for 7794 Lekhpal posts across Uttar Pradesh. The recruitment process began with the notification in December 2025, followed by a mains examination on 21 May 2026.

The written examination result has been declared, and successful candidates are now required to upload their documents for the Document Verification (DV) stage. The document upload window is active from 15 September to 29 September 2026. This is a mandatory step for all shortlisted candidates to progress toward final selection.

This page serves as the permanent lifecycle hub for this recruitment. All future official updates, including the final result, merit list, and joining details, will be added here without changing the URL.

Eligibility Criteria

Age Limit

18 to 40 years as on 01/07/2025.

Age relaxation applicable as per UPSSSC rules for SC/ST/OBC/PwD/Ex-Servicemen.

Education

Candidates must have passed 10+2 Intermediate Exam from any recognized board in India and possess a valid UPSSSC PET 2025 Score Card.

For detailed educational conditions, refer to the official notification.

Nationality / Other

Indian citizen with required domicile and physical standards as per the notification.

Refer to official notification for detailed conditions.

What to do at the current stage

How to Upload Documents for DV

  1. Visit the official UPSSSC website and open the DV document upload link.
  2. Log in using your application number and date of birth.
  3. Upload scanned copies of all required documents in the prescribed format.
  4. Ensure documents are clear, legible, and within the specified file size limits.
  5. Verify all uploaded documents before final submission.
  6. Download and save the confirmation page for future reference.
  7. Keep checking this page for the final result and merit list updates.

Original Application Process

Preserved for recruitment history and reference after the application stage closes.

  1. Visit the official UPSSSC website.
  2. Read the UPSSSC Lekhpal notification carefully.
  3. Open the application link from Important Links.
  4. Register and fill the form.
  5. Upload required documents.
  6. Pay the applicable fee.
  7. Submit and save the confirmation page.
