About This Recruitment

UPSSSC Lekhpal Recruitment 2025-2026 is a major recruitment drive by the Uttar Pradesh Subordinate Service Selection Commission (UPSSSC) for 7994 Lekhpal (Accountant/Revenue Officer) posts. The online application window was open from 29 December 2025 to 28 January 2026.

The recruitment process includes a Preliminary Eligibility Test (PET) score, a Mains examination, and document verification. The Mains exam was conducted on 21 May 2026, and the provisional answer key was released on 22 May 2026, followed by a revised answer key on 1 July 2026.

The UPSSSC Lekhpal Result 2026 has now been declared. Candidates who appeared in the Mains examination can check their result and qualifying status on the official website. This page will continue to carry every future update, including the final merit list and joining details.
