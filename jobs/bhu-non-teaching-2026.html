Complete context

About This Recruitment

Banaras Hindu University (BHU) has invited online applications under Advertisement No. 06/2026-27 for BHU Non-Teaching Group A, B & C Recruitment 2026. The recruitment drive covers 578 vacancies across Sir Sunderlal Hospital, Trauma Centre, Centenary Super Specialty Complex, Institute of Medical Sciences (IMS), National Centre of Ageing (NCA), Faculty of Ayurveda, and RGSC in Varanasi, Uttar Pradesh.

The 578 vacancies include 39 Group A posts (such as Medical Officer, Deputy Medical Superintendent, Nursing Superintendent, and Senior Optometrist), 382 Group B posts (led by Nursing Officer Female and Male, Physiotherapist, and Medical Social Worker), and 157 Group C posts (including Laboratory Attendant, Technical Assistant, and Junior Assistant).

Online registration and fee payment opened on 18 November 2026 and will remain active until 15 December 2026 up to 5:00 PM through the official BHU Samarth portal. General, EWS, and OBC candidates must pay Rs.1000 for Group A posts and Rs.500 for Group B and C posts, while SC, ST, PwBD, and female candidates are exempted from the application fee.

Educational qualifications, experience requirements, and upper age limits (up to 50 years) vary by post as detailed in the official notification. Admit card, examination, and result dates will be announced on the official BHU recruitment portal and updated on this permanent lifecycle page.

What to do at the current stage

How to Apply Online for BHU Non-Teaching 2026

  1. Download and read Advt. No. 06/2026-27 to verify post-wise qualification, age limit and experience.
  2. Open the official BHU Samarth application portal from Important Links.
  3. Register using a valid email ID and active mobile number.
  4. Log in with your registration credentials and select the desired Group A, B or C post.
  5. Enter personal, educational, category and experience details accurately.
  6. Upload scanned copies of photograph, signature and required certificates.
  7. Pay the applicable application fee online before 15 December 2026 up to 5:00 PM.
  8. Submit the form and download or print the confirmation page for future reference.
