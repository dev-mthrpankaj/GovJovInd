About This Recruitment

National Testing Agency (NTA) has released the Council of Scientific & Industrial Research CSIR UGC National Eligibility Test NET Assistant Professor / JRF June 2026 Examination result and score card. The online application process was conducted from 27 May 2026 to 19 June 2026.

CSIR NET is conducted for determining eligibility for Junior Research Fellowship (JRF) and Assistant Professor positions in Indian universities and colleges. The examination was held on 17-18 July 2026, and the result has now been declared on 29 August 2026.

Candidates who appeared for the examination can now download their score card and check their result status using the official links provided on this page. The final answer key has also been released.

Eligibility Criteria

Age Limit

No upper age limit for Assistant Professor. For JRF, upper age limit is 28 years as per CSIR norms.

Age relaxation for SC/ST/OBC/PwD and other categories as per government rules.

Education

M.Sc or equivalent degree with 55% marks for General/OBC candidates. For SC/ST/PwD candidates, minimum 50% marks.

Integrated Course and B.E/B.Tech/B.Pharma/MBBS candidates are also eligible for CSIR NET.

Nationality / Other

Indian citizens meeting the educational and age criteria can apply for JRF and Assistant Professor positions.

Refer to official notification for detailed conditions.

What to do at the current stage

How to Check Result & Download Score Card

  1. Visit the official NTA CSIR result portal using the link provided.
  2. Enter your registration number, date of birth, and other required credentials.
  3. Complete the CAPTCHA verification if prompted.
  4. Click on Submit/Login to access your result.
  5. Check your qualifying status, marks, and percentile.
  6. Download the score card PDF and save it for future reference.
  7. Take a printout of the score card for your records.

Original Application Process

Preserved for recruitment history and reference after the application stage closes.

  1. Visit the official CSIR NET website.
  2. Read the CSIR UGC NET June 2026 information brochure carefully.
  3. Open the application link and register with basic details.
  4. Fill the application form with accurate information.
  5. Upload photograph, signature, and required documents.
  6. Select exam city and language preference.
  7. Pay the applicable fee through online mode.
  8. Submit the form and save the confirmation page.
