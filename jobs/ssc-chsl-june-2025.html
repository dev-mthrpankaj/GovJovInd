Complete context

About This Recruitment

SSC Combined Higher Secondary (10+2) Level Examination 2025 is a Staff Selection Commission recruitment drive conducted to fill 3522 vacancies across Lower Division Clerk (LDC)/Junior Secretariat Assistant (JSA), Data Entry Operator (DEO), Data Entry Operator Grade-A, Postal Assistant/Sorting Assistant, and Junior Health Administrative Assistant posts in various Ministries, Departments and Government Organizations.

The official notification was published on 23 June 2025, and the online application window remained open from 23 June 2025 to 18 July 2025. The fee payment last date was 19 July 2025, and the application correction window was available on 23-24 July 2025.

The Tier-I Computer Based Examination (CBT) was conducted from 12 November 2025 to 30 November 2025. The Tier-I result was declared on 27 February 2026, with 39,901 candidates qualifying for Tier-II. The Tier-II examination was held on 10 April 2026, and the Skill Test/Typing Test (re-examination) was conducted on 22 April 2026 after the original test scheduled for 10 April 2026 was cancelled due to technical errors.

The Tier-II answer key was released on 20 April 2026, and the objection window remained open until 23 April 2026. The First Round of Tentative Allocation (FRTA) result was declared on 17 August 2026, with 3515 candidates provisionally recommended for Document Verification. The Document Verification slot selection was held from 20 to 22 August 2026, and the physical sliding process was conducted from 01 to 03 September 2026. The final result was declared on 07 October 2026.

Basic eligibility is 10+2 (Intermediate) from a recognised board. The age limit is 18 to 27 years as on 01 August 2025, with relaxation for reserved categories as per SSC rules.
