Complete context

About This Recruitment

The Combined Higher Secondary (10+2) Level Examination 2025 was conducted by the Staff Selection Commission to recruit eligible candidates for various government posts including Lower Division Clerk (LDC), Junior Secretariat Assistant (JSA), Postal Assistant (PA), Sorting Assistant (SA), and Data Entry Operator (DEO). A total of 3131 vacancies were announced in the official notification.

The application window opened on 23 June 2025 and concluded on 18 July 2025. The Tier I CBT examination commenced on 12 November 2025, and its result was published on 27 February 2026. Qualified candidates appeared for the Tier II examination and Skill Test on 22 April 2026.

After compiling the final vacancies and candidate preferences submitted in June 2026, SSC declared the FRTA results in August, followed by the Final Result on 07 October 2026. The official marks and score cards for the candidates have now been released on 18 November 2026 on the SSC portal.

What to do at the current stage

How to Download Score Card

  1. Click on the Download Score Card link to visit the official SSC portal.
  2. Log in using your Registration Number and Password.
  3. Navigate to the Results/Marks section on your candidate dashboard.
  4. Select 'Combined Higher Secondary (10+2) Level Examination 2025'.
  5. View and download your score card for future reference.

Original Application Process

Preserved for recruitment history and reference after the application stage closes.

  1. Visit the SSC OTR portal at ssc.gov.in and complete your One-Time Registration.
  2. Log in using your registration credentials.
  3. Fill out the SSC CHSL 2025 application form carefully.
  4. Upload scanned photographs and signatures in the required format.
  5. Pay the application fee if applicable.
  6. Submit the final application and download the confirmation page.
