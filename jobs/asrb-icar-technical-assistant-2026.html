Complete context

About This Recruitment

The Agricultural Scientists Recruitment Board (ASRB) has announced the Centralized Combined Examination 2026 for recruitment to 1393 technical posts under the Indian Council of Agricultural Research (ICAR). The vacancies include 1254 Technical Assistant (T-3) posts in ICAR institutes and 139 Senior Technical Assistant (T-4) posts in ICAR Krishi Vigyan Kendras (KVKs).

The official notification (F. No. 2(2)/2025-Exam) was released on 11 November 2026. Online applications will be accepted from 18 November 2026 to 17 December 2026. The application correction window will remain open from 18 to 20 December 2026. The preliminary Computer Based Test (CBT) is tentatively scheduled for February 2027, followed by the Mains CBT.

Eligibility requires a bachelor’s degree in the relevant subject, with a minimum age of 21 years and a maximum of 30 years as on 17 December 2026. The application fee is Rs.300 for UR/General candidates (single post), Rs.150 for OBC/EWS, and nil for SC/ST/PwBD/female/Ex-Servicemen candidates. Selection will be through Preliminary CBT, Mains CBT and document verification; there is no interview.
