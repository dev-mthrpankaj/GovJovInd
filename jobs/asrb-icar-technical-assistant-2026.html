Complete context

About This Recruitment

Agricultural Scientists Recruitment Board (ASRB) – ICAR has published Advt. No. 2(2)/2025-Exam on 11 November 2026 for the Centralized Combined Examination 2026 to fill 1393 direct recruitment vacancies. The drive covers 1254 posts of Technical Assistant (T-3) in ICAR Research Institutes and 139 posts of Senior Technical Assistant (T-4) in ICAR Krishi Vigyan Kendras (KVKs) across India.

Online registration and application submission opened on 18 November 2026 and will remain active until 17 December 2026 (11:59 PM). Candidates who submit their forms can use the online application correction window from 18 December 2026 to 20 December 2026. Fee payment for applicable categories is processed online through the Bharatkosh portal.

Applicants must hold a relevant Bachelor's Degree in Agriculture, Horticulture, Agriculture Engineering, Agro-forestry, Food Science, Biotechnology, Environmental Science, Home Science, Community Science, Natural Farming, IT, Computer Science, ECE, or a prescribed Engineering Diploma for the chosen functional group. The age limit is 21 to 30 years as on 17 December 2026, with category-wise upper age relaxation as per Government of India and ASRB norms.

Selection consists of a qualifying Preliminary Computer Based Test tentatively scheduled for February 2027, followed by a subject-specific Mains Computer Based Test that determines final merit, and Document Verification. There is no interview stage. Selected Technical Assistant (T-3) candidates receive Pay Level 5 (Rs.29,200 to Rs.92,300) and Senior Technical Assistant (T-4) candidates receive Pay Level 6 (Rs.35,400 to Rs.1,12,400) under the 7th CPC.
