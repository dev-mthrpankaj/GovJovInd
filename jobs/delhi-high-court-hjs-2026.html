Complete context

About This Recruitment

Delhi High Court Higher Judicial Service (HJS) Examination 2026 is a direct recruitment drive by the High Court of Delhi to fill 27 vacancies (24 existing and 3 anticipated) for the post of District Judge (Entry Level) in the Delhi Higher Judicial Service under the 25% direct recruitment quota from the Bar. The official notification was published on 1 July 2026, and the online application window remained open from 1 July 2026 to 15 July 2026 (5:30 PM).

The Preliminary Examination (objective type with 25% negative marking) was conducted on 26 July 2026 (11:00 AM to 1:00 PM). The prelims admit card was released on 21 July 2026, the model answer key was published on 28 July 2026, and the prelims result was declared on 14 August 2026.

The Mains Written Examination was originally scheduled for 12-13 September 2026 but was postponed to 10-11 October 2026 due to traffic restrictions associated with the 18th BRICS Summit at Bharat Mandapam. The Mains Admit Card was released on 5 October 2026.

Eligibility requires a Bachelor's Degree in Law (LL.B.) from a recognised university and a minimum of 7 years of continuous practice as an Advocate or combined judicial service experience as on 15 July 2026. The age limit is 35 to 45 years as on 15 July 2026, with relaxation for reserved categories as per Delhi High Court rules. The pay scale for the post is (J-5) Rs.1,44,840 to Rs.1,94,660.
