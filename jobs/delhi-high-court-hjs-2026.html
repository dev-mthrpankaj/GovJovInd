Complete context

About This Recruitment

Delhi High Court Higher Judicial Service Examination 2026 is being conducted for 27 vacancies in the Delhi Higher Judicial Service. Online applications were accepted from 1 July to 15 July 2026.

The preliminary examination was held on 26 July 2026 and its result was declared on 14 August 2026. Candidate marks and the preliminary-stage notices are also available through the official Delhi High Court links retained on this page.

The recruitment has now moved to the Main Examination stage. The Delhi High Court HJS Mains Examination is scheduled for 10 and 11 October 2026, so candidates shortlisted from the preliminary examination should follow the latest official notice and prepare for the next stage.
