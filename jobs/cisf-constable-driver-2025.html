Complete context

About This Recruitment

Central Industrial Security Force (CISF) is conducting CISF Constable Driver & DCPO Recruitment 2025 to fill 1124 posts across India, including 845 Constable / Driver (Direct) posts and 279 Constable / Driver Cum Pump Operator (DCPO) posts. Online applications were accepted from 03 February 2025 to 04 March 2025 through the official CISF recruitment portal.

Earlier stages of the recruitment included the examination held on 15 September 2025, the result declared on 30 October 2025, and the Trade Test conducted from 07 November 2025 to 22 November 2025. Candidates shortlisted through the recruitment stages can now appear for the upcoming examination scheduled on 01 December 2026.

The CISF Constable Driver Admit Card 2026 was released on 27 November 2026. Candidates must download and print their admit card and carry it along with a valid original photo identity proof and required driving licence documents to the examination centre on 01 December 2026.

Eligibility is restricted to male Indian citizens who have passed High School (Class 10) from a recognised board and hold a valid Heavy Motor Vehicle (HMV) or Transport Vehicle, Light Motor Vehicle (LMV), and Motor Cycle with Gear driving licence with 3 years of driving experience. The age limit is 21 to 27 years as on 04 March 2025, with physical standards of 167 cm height and 80-85 cm chest.

What to do at the current stage

How to Download CISF Constable Driver Admit Card 2026

  1. Open the official CISF Admit Card download link from the Important Links section.
  2. Log in using your Registration Number / Roll Number and Date of Birth or Password.
  3. Download the CISF Constable Driver Admit Card 2026 PDF and check all printed details.
  4. Verify your name, roll number, examination date (01 December 2026), reporting time, and exam centre address.
  5. Take a clear printout of the admit card and carry a valid original photo ID and passport-size photographs to the exam centre.

Original Application Process

Preserved for recruitment history and reference after the application stage closes.

  1. Visit the official CISF recruitment website at cisfrectt.cisf.gov.in.
  2. Open the Recruitment / Career section and read the CISF Constable Driver & DCPO 2025 notification carefully.
  3. Click on the Apply Online link and complete candidate registration.
  4. Fill in personal details, address, educational qualification, and driving licence details accurately.
  5. Upload required documents, photograph, and signature in the prescribed PDF or JPEG format and size.
  6. Pay the applicable application fee of Rs.100 online (if applicable), review all entries, submit the form, and save the printout for future reference.
