About This Recruitment

Uttar Pradesh Police Recruitment & Promotion Board (UPPBPB) has released the UP Police Constable Recruitment 2026 notification for a total of 32,679 posts. This is a landmark recruitment drive covering Constable Civil Police, Constable PAC/Armed Police, Constable Special Force Battalion, Constable Female Battalion, Constable Horseman, and Jail Warden posts across Uttar Pradesh.

The online application window ran from 31 December 2025 to 30 January 2026, with a correction window from 31 January to 03 February 2026. The written examination was conducted from 08 June to 10 June 2026. The answer key was released on 20 June 2026, and the written result has been declared.

The latest stage is the PET/PST (Physical Efficiency Test / Physical Standard Test) schedule. Candidates who qualified the written examination should download the PET exam notice, the DV/PST admit card, and the DV/PST exam notice from the Important Links section below. This page is the permanent home for every UP Police Constable 2026 update from notification to final merit list.
