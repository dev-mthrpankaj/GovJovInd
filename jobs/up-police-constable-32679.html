Complete context

About This Recruitment

The Uttar Pradesh Police Recruitment and Promotion Board (UPPRPB) announced the UP Police Constable Recruitment 2026 to fill a total of 32,679 Constable vacancies across various categories, including Civil Police, PAC, and Jail Warder.

The selection process has successfully concluded through multiple stages, beginning with the written examination on 08, 09, and 10 June 2026. Following the written test results on 31 July 2026, eligible candidates proceeded to the Physical Standard Test (PST) and Document Verification (DV) in August, and subsequently to the Physical Efficiency Test (PET) on 08 October 2026.

The Final Result and Merit List were declared on 14 October 2026. Now, the board has released the official Score Card on 16 November 2026, allowing all participating candidates to view their detailed performance metrics and marks.
