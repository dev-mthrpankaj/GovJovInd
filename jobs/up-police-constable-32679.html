Complete context

About This Recruitment

UP Police Constable Recruitment 2025-26 is a large-scale recruitment drive conducted by the Uttar Pradesh Police Recruitment & Promotion Board (UPPRPB) to fill 32,679 Constable posts across multiple units — Civil Police (Male/Female), PAC/Armed Police (Male), Special Security Force (Male), Female Battalion, Horseman (Male), and Jail Warden (Male/Female).

The official notification was released in December 2025, and online applications were accepted from 31 December 2025 to 30 January 2026. The application correction window remained open from 31 January to 03 February 2026, and the sample OMR sheet was released on 05 February 2026.

The Computer Based Test (CBT) was conducted from 08 June to 10 June 2026, and the provisional answer key was released on 20 June 2026. The written result was declared thereafter, and candidates who qualified were called for the Physical Endurance Test (PET) and Physical Standard Test (PST), which was conducted from 01 October to 08 October 2026. The DV/PST admit card was also released for the document verification round.

The Final Result was declared on 14 October 2026. Candidates can check their post-wise final result and download the official cut off notice from the Important Links section of this page. The final merit list includes selected candidates across all categories and horizontal reservations.

Basic eligibility is 10+2 (Intermediate) from a recognised board. The age limit is 18 to 25 years for UR Male candidates and 18 to 28 years for OBC/SC/ST Male candidates as on 01 July 2025, with similar relaxation for female candidates. The application fee was Rs.500 for General/OBC/EWS and Rs.400 for SC/ST candidates.
