Complete context

About This Recruitment

UP Police Constable Recruitment 2026 is a large-scale recruitment drive by the Uttar Pradesh Police Recruitment & Promotion Board (UPPRPB) for 32,679 posts of Constable Civil Police, PAC, Jail Warder, and equivalent posts. The written examination was conducted from 08 to 10 June 2026, and the result was declared on 31 July 2026.

Candidates shortlisted after the written examination appeared for Document Verification (DV) and Physical Standard Test (PST) from 17 to 31 August 2026. The Physical Efficiency Test (PET) is now scheduled from 01 to 08 October 2026 for all qualified candidates.

The PET admit card has been released on the official UPPRPB website. Candidates must carry a printed admit card along with a valid photo ID to the PET centre. The final merit list will be prepared based on written examination marks after the completion of all physical tests.
