About This Recruitment

Rashtriya Chemicals and Fertilizers Limited (RCFL) has announced the recruitment for 94 Management Trainee (MT) posts across various engineering and management disciplines. The application process started on 8 August 2026 and the last date to apply has been extended to 20 September 2026.

This is a significant opportunity for candidates with a B.E./B.Tech/MBA/PG degree in relevant fields to join a Mini Ratna Category-I PSU under the Government of India. The recruitment covers disciplines like Chemical, Boiler, Mechanical, Electrical, Instrumentation, Finance, Marketing, and more.

Interested candidates should review the detailed eligibility, vacancy, and selection process below and ensure they submit their online application before the extended deadline.

Eligibility Criteria

Age Limit

Maximum 27 years for UR/EWS as on 01 February 2026.

Education

Full-time B.E./B.Tech / M.Tech / MBA / PG Degree or equivalent in the relevant discipline.

Qualification cut-off date has been revised to 1 August 2026.

Nationality / Other

Indian citizens fulfilling the post-specific eligibility conditions may apply.
