Complete context

About This Recruitment

UPPSC Assistant Town Planner (Special Recruitment) Examination 2025 is a recruitment drive by the Uttar Pradesh Public Service Commission for 08 Assistant Town Planner posts under Urban Development Authorities of Uttar Pradesh. The advertisement number is A-10/E-1/2025.

The recruitment is for Group-B, Non-Gazetted posts. The selection process includes a Preliminary Examination, a Main (Written) Examination, Document Verification, and Medical Examination. The pay scale is Pay Matrix Level-10 (Rs. 15,600 - Rs. 39,100 with Grade Pay Rs. 5,400).

The preliminary examination was conducted on 07 July 2026 and the result was declared on 09 September 2026, with 84 candidates shortlisted for the Mains examination. The Mains examination is scheduled for 06 October 2026 in two sessions. This permanent page will carry every future update including the Mains admit card, Mains result, final merit list and joining details.
