About This Recruitment

UPPSC Assistant Town Planner Recruitment 2025 was notified under Advertisement No. A-10/E-1/2025 for 8 posts. The recruitment has progressed through the examination and answer-key stages, and the result has now been declared.

The advertised vacancies were distributed as 4 OBC and 4 SC posts. The eligibility includes Town & Country Planning qualifications or specified professional membership/equivalent qualifications as stated in the official notification.

Candidates should download and preserve the official result and continue checking UPPSC instructions for any subsequent recruitment stage.
