Complete context

About This Recruitment

UPPSC Assistant Town Planner Recruitment 2025 was notified under Advertisement No. A-10/E-1/2025 for 8 posts. The recruitment has progressed through the examination and answer-key stages, and the result has now been declared.

The advertised vacancies were distributed as 4 OBC and 4 SC posts. The eligibility includes Town & Country Planning qualifications or specified professional membership/equivalent qualifications as stated in the official notification.

Candidates should download and preserve the official result and continue checking UPPSC instructions for any subsequent recruitment stage.

Eligibility Criteria

Age Limit

Minimum 21 years and maximum 40 years as on 1 July 2025.

Age relaxation is applicable as per the official UPPSC notification.

Education

Bachelor Degree OR PG Diploma in Town & Country Planning from a recognized institution; OR specified associate membership / equivalent qualification of recognized town-planning institutions as stated in the notification.

Candidates should check the official notification for the complete wording and equivalence conditions.

Nationality / Other

Applicants must satisfy all eligibility and documentation conditions prescribed by UPPSC.

Refer to official notification for detailed conditions.
