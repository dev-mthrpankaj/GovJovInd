Complete context

About This Recruitment

UPSSSC Combined Junior Engineer (Mechanical) Recruitment 2026 is conducted by the Uttar Pradesh Subordinate Service Selection Commission (UPSSSC) to fill 1,156 posts across eight mechanical and technical cadres in Uttar Pradesh government departments. The official advertisement number is 24-Exam/2026 and the notification was published on 07 October 2026.

The online application window opens on 03 November 2026 and closes on 25 November 2026. The last date for payment of the application fee is also 25 November 2026. The examination date, admit card release date and result date will be announced by UPSSSC through separate official notices.

To be eligible, candidates must possess a valid UPSSSC PET (Preliminary Eligibility Test) score card along with a Diploma in Mechanical Engineering or Automobile Engineering from a recognised institution. The age limit varies post-wise, with the minimum age between 18 and 21 years and the maximum age of 40 years as on 01 July 2026.

The recruitment will progress through the UPSSSC PET eligibility check, written examination, document verification, medical examination and final merit list.
