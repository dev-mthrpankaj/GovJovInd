Complete context

About This Recruitment

UPSSSC Combined Junior Engineer (Mechanical) Main Examination 2026 is a recruitment drive by the Uttar Pradesh Subordinate Services Selection Commission (UPSSSC) to fill 1,156 technical posts across eight departments, including Irrigation, PWD, UPSRTC, Panchayati Raj, Ground Water, and Prison Administration. The official notification was released on 12 October 2026 under Advertisement No. 24-Exam/2026.

The online application window is open from 03 November 2026 to 25 November 2026. Candidates must have appeared in the UPSSSC Preliminary Eligibility Test (PET-2025) and hold a valid scorecard. A zero, negative, or below-zero PET score makes a candidate ineligible for shortlisting. The selection process includes PET-2025 shortlisting, a main written examination of 100 marks, document verification, and a medical examination.

The basic eligibility is a three-year Diploma in Mechanical or Automobile Engineering from a recognised institution, along with a valid PET-2025 scorecard. The age limit is 18 to 40 years for most posts and 21 to 40 years for Technical Assistant (Mechanical) and ADPRO (Technical), calculated as on 01 July 2026. Age relaxation is available for reserved categories as per Uttar Pradesh government rules.

What to do at the current stage

How to Apply Online

  1. Visit the official UPSSSC website at upsssc.gov.in.
  2. Click on Advt. No. 24-Exam/2026 under the Live Advertisement section.
  3. Log in using your PET-2025 registration number and the OTP sent to your registered mobile number.
  4. Fill in your personal details, educational qualifications, and category details.
  5. Select the post and department you wish to apply for.
  6. Upload your photograph, signature, and required documents.
  7. Pay the Rs.25 application fee online via debit card, credit card, net banking, or UPI.
  8. Submit the form and download the confirmation page for future reference.
