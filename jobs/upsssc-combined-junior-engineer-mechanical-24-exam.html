Complete context

About This Recruitment

UPSSSC Combined Junior Engineer (Mechanical) Recruitment 2026 is conducted by the Uttar Pradesh Subordinate Service Selection Commission (UPSSSC) to fill 1,156 posts across eight mechanical and technical cadres in Uttar Pradesh government departments. The official advertisement number is 24-Exam/2026 and the notification was published on 07 October 2026.

The online application window opens on 03 November 2026 and closes on 25 November 2026. The last date for payment of the application fee is also 25 November 2026. The examination date, admit card release date and result date will be announced by UPSSSC through separate official notices.

To be eligible, candidates must possess a valid UPSSSC PET (Preliminary Eligibility Test) score card along with a Diploma in Mechanical Engineering or Automobile Engineering from a recognised institution. The age limit varies post-wise, with the minimum age between 18 and 21 years and the maximum age of 40 years as on 01 July 2026.

The recruitment will progress through the UPSSSC PET eligibility check, written examination, document verification, medical examination and final merit list. Eligibility Criteria

Age Limit

Minimum age 18 to 21 years (post-wise) and maximum age 40 years as on 01 July 2026.

Age relaxation is applicable for SC, ST, OBC, PwBD and other reserved categories as per UPSSSC rules. Post-wise age limits are mentioned in the official notification.

Education

Diploma in Mechanical Engineering or Automobile Engineering from a recognised institution, along with a valid UPSSSC PET (Preliminary Eligibility Test) Score Card.

Post-wise qualifications vary. The Instructor (Machine) post additionally requires 10+2. A valid UPSSSC PET score card is mandatory for all posts.

Nationality / Other

Candidates must be Indian citizens and must fulfil the post-wise eligibility conditions, including a valid UPSSSC PET score card. Refer to the official notification for complete details.

What to do at the current stage

How to Apply for UPSSSC JE Mechanical 2026

  1. Visit the official UPSSSC website at upsssc.gov.in.
  2. Open the All Notifications section and click the UPSSSC Combined Junior Engineer (Mechanical) 2026 Apply Online link (active from 03 November 2026).
  3. Register using a valid email ID and mobile number.
  4. Log in using your registration ID and password.
  5. Fill the application form with personal, educational and category details.
  6. Upload scanned photograph, signature and required documents.
  7. Pay the application fee of Rs.25 through the online payment gateway.
  8. Submit the form and download or print the confirmation page for future reference.
  9. Read the official notification carefully before final submission.
