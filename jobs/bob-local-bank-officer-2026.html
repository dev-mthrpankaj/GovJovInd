About This Recruitment

Bank of Baroda (BOB) has released the notification for Local Bank Officer (LBO) Recruitment 2026 covering 2482 vacancies across various states. The online application window was open from 18 August 2026 to 17 September 2026 (extended).

The selection process includes a written examination scheduled for 03 October 2026, followed by document verification and medical examination. Candidates must possess a bachelor's degree in any stream and proficiency in the local language of the state applied for.

The admit card for the written examination has been released. Candidates can download their call letters from the official IBPS registration portal. All future updates including answer key, result, and final merit list will be published on this same permanent page.

How to Download Admit Card

  1. Open the official IBPS registration portal from Important Links.
  2. Enter your registration number and password/date of birth.
  3. Verify the admit card details including name, roll number, exam date, and venue.
  4. Download and take multiple printouts of the admit card.
  5. Carry the admit card along with a valid photo ID to the examination centre.
  6. Check the exam day guidelines and reporting time mentioned in the call letter.

Preserved for recruitment history and reference after the application stage closes.
