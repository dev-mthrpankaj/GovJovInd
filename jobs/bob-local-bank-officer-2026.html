About This Recruitment

Bank of Baroda (BOB) has released the recruitment notification for Local Bank Officer (LBO) posts with a total of 2,482 vacancies across India. The online application window was open from 18 August 2026 to 17 September 2026 (extended from the original last date). Applications are now closed.

The exam date for BOB LBO 2026 has been officially released. As per the latest exam notice, the written examination is scheduled for 03 October 2026. Candidates who successfully submitted their applications should now prepare for the exam and keep their registration details ready for admit card download.

The admit card, answer key, result and all subsequent stages will be published on this same permanent page. Candidates are advised to bookmark this URL and check back for every official update.
