Complete context

About This Recruitment

Bihar Staff Selection Commission (BSSC) advertised 432 vacancies for Stenographer / Steno Typist Grade-III under Advertisement No. 07/2025. The online application window ran from 25 September 2025 to 03 November 2025, with final form submission closing on 05 November 2025.

Following the exam schedule notice issued on 09 November 2026, BSSC released the Exam City Details on 24 November 2026 so registered candidates can check their allotted examination city before the test. The official Admit Card will be issued on 27 November 2026, and the Written Examination will take place on 01 December 2026.

Eligible candidates hold a 10+2 (Intermediate) qualification along with stenography skills, and fall within the 18 to 37 years age bracket for males or 18 to 40 years for females as on 01 August 2025. Selection proceeds through the Written Examination, Stenography and Typing Skill Test, Document Verification, and Final Merit List.
