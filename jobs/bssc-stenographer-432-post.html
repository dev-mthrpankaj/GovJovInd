Complete context

About This Recruitment

Bihar Staff Selection Commission (BSSC) is conducting the Stenographer/Steno Typist Grade-III recruitment for 432 posts under Advertisement No. 07/2025. The posts are spread across various departments and offices of the Bihar government, and the recruitment is being held on a state-wide basis.

The online application window opened on 25 September 2025 and closed on 03 November 2025, with the final submit window open until 05 November 2025. The written examination is an online Computer Based Test (CBT) scheduled for 01 December 2026, as confirmed by the official BSSC exam notice dated 09 November 2026.

The selection process includes the written CBT, a stenography and typing skill test, document verification and a medical examination. Candidates who clear the written exam will be called for the skill test, where their Hindi or English stenography speed and computer typing ability will be assessed.

Eligibility requires Intermediate (10+2) or an equivalent qualification from a recognised board, along with knowledge of Hindi and/or English stenography and computer typing. The age limit is 18 to 37 years for male and 18 to 40 years for female candidates as on 01 August 2025, with category-wise relaxation as per Bihar government rules.
