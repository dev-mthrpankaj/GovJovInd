Complete context

About This Recruitment

Bihar SSC BSSC Stenographer Grade-III Recruitment 2025 is an official recruitment drive by the Bihar Staff Selection Commission (BSSC) under Advertisement No. 07/2025 to fill 432 posts of Stenographer / Steno Typist Grade-III across departments and offices of the Government of Bihar. The notification was released on 25 September 2025, online registration and fee payment closed on 03 November 2025, and final form submission ended on 05 November 2025.

Following the examination notice dated 09 November 2026, the Bihar Staff Selection Commission (BSSC) released exam city details on 24 November 2026 and activated the Admit Card download link on 27 November 2026. The written examination is scheduled for 01 December 2026, and candidates must carry a printed admit card along with a valid original photo ID to the examination centre.

Eligibility requires passing the 10+2 (Intermediate) examination from a recognised board along with stenography and computer typing proficiency. The age limit is 18 to 37 years for Male candidates and 18 to 40 years for Female candidates as on 01 August 2025, with upper age relaxation for reserved categories as per BSSC rules. Selected candidates receive Pay Level 4 salary from Rs.25,500 to Rs.81,100 through a selection process comprising the Written Examination, Practical Skill Test, Document Verification and Final Merit List.

What to do at the current stage

How to Download BSSC Stenographer Admit Card 2026

  1. Open the official BSSC Stenographer candidate login portal from the Important Links section below.
  2. Enter your Registration Number and Date of Birth or Password carefully to sign in.
  3. Click the link to view and download your BSSC Stenographer Admit Card 2026 PDF.
  4. Verify your name, roll number, photograph, exam date (01 December 2026), shift timing and test centre address.
  5. Take a clear printout of the admit card and read all examination day instructions printed on it.
  6. Carry the printed admit card along with a valid original government photo ID to the examination centre.

Original Application Process

Preserved for recruitment history and reference after the application stage closes.

  1. Visit the official Bihar Staff Selection Commission (BSSC) website at bssc.bihar.gov.in or the Advt. No. 07/2025 application portal.
  2. Read Advertisement No. 07/2025 carefully to verify eligibility, age limit and syllabus before applying.
  3. Complete online registration and fill personal, address and educational qualification details accurately.
  4. Upload scanned photograph, signature and required certificates in the prescribed size and format.
  5. Pay the Rs.100 examination fee through online mode before 03 November 2025.
  6. Verify all entered details, complete final submission by 05 November 2025 and save a printed PDF copy.
