Complete context

About This Recruitment

Uttarakhand Subordinate Service Selection Commission (UKSSSC) has released the official recruitment notification (Advertisement No. 85/UKSSSC/2026) to fill 200 Group C uniformed vacancies in the Uttarakhand Police Department and Excise Department, Uttarakhand. The advertised posts include Sub-Inspector Civil Police (93 posts), Sub-Inspector Intelligence (19 posts), Platoon Commander Male PAC/IRB (81 posts), Fire Officer II (5 posts), and Deputy Excise Inspector (2 posts).

Online applications will be accepted on the official portal sssc.uk.gov.in from 01 December 2026 to 31 December 2026, with an online application correction window scheduled from 04 January 2027 to 06 January 2027. The written examination is scheduled for 18 April 2027, followed by Physical Efficiency & Standard Tests (PET/PST), Document Verification, and Medical Examination.

Candidates must hold a Bachelor's degree in any stream from a recognised university in India and meet the prescribed physical standards. The age limit as on 01 July 2026 is 21 to 35 years for Sub-Inspector, Platoon Commander, and Fire Officer II posts, and 21 to 42 years for the Deputy Excise Inspector post.

What to do at the current stage

How to Prepare & Apply Online (From 01 December 2026)

  1. Download and read Advertisement No. 85/UKSSSC/2026 carefully to verify post-wise physical standards and category-wise vacancies.
  2. Keep scanned copies of your recent passport-size photograph, signature, Bachelor's degree mark sheets, and category/domicile certificates ready.
  3. Visit the official UKSSSC website at sssc.uk.gov.in once the online portal activates on 01 December 2026.
  4. Complete One-Time Registration (OTR) or log in using your valid mobile number and email ID.
  5. Fill in personal, educational, and post-preference details accurately and upload required documents.
  6. Pay the application fee online (Rs.300 for General/OBC, Rs.150 for SC/ST/EWS, Rs.0 for Orphan candidates) on or before 31 December 2026 and print the confirmation page.

    Frequently Asked Questions

    Online applications for UKSSSC Sub Inspector Recruitment 2026 open on 01 December 2026 on the official website sssc.uk.gov.in under Advertisement No. 85/UKSSSC/2026.

    The last date to submit the online application form and pay the examination fee for UKSSSC SI Recruitment 2026 is 31 December 2026.

    The written examination for UKSSSC Sub Inspector and allied posts is scheduled for 18 April 2027, while the application correction window runs from 04 January 2027 to 06 January 2027.

    UKSSSC has announced 200 total vacancies: 93 Sub-Inspector Civil Police, 81 Platoon Commander PAC/IRB, 19 Sub-Inspector Intelligence, 5 Fire Officer II, and 2 Deputy Excise Inspector posts.

    Candidates must hold a Bachelor's degree in any stream from a recognised university in India and fulfill the prescribed physical efficiency and measurement standards.

    As on 01 July 2026, the age limit is 21 to 35 years for Sub-Inspector, Platoon Commander, and Fire Officer II posts, and 21 to 42 years for the Deputy Excise Inspector post.

    Sub-Inspector, Platoon Commander, and Fire Officer II carry a Pay Level 7 scale of Rs.44,900 to Rs.1,42,400 per month, while Deputy Excise Inspector carries Rs.29,200 to Rs.92,300 per month.
