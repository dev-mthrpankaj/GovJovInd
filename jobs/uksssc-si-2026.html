Complete context

About This Recruitment

Uttarakhand Subordinate Service Selection Commission (UKSSSC) has released the official recruitment notification (Advertisement No. 85/UKSSSC/2026) to fill 200 Group C uniformed vacancies in the Uttarakhand Police Department and Excise Department, Uttarakhand. The advertised posts include Sub-Inspector Civil Police (93 posts), Sub-Inspector Intelligence (19 posts), Platoon Commander Male PAC/IRB (81 posts), Fire Officer II (5 posts), and Deputy Excise Inspector (2 posts).

Online applications will be accepted on the official portal sssc.uk.gov.in from 01 December 2026 to 31 December 2026, with an online application correction window scheduled from 04 January 2027 to 06 January 2027. The written examination is scheduled for 18 April 2027, followed by Physical Efficiency & Standard Tests (PET/PST), Document Verification, and Medical Examination.

Candidates must hold a Bachelor's degree in any stream from a recognised university in India and meet the prescribed physical standards. The age limit as on 01 July 2026 is 21 to 35 years for Sub-Inspector, Platoon Commander, and Fire Officer II posts, and 21 to 42 years for the Deputy Excise Inspector post.
