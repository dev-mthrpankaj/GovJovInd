Complete context

About This Recruitment

Indian Air Force Airmen Group Y Medical Assistant Recruitment 2026 (Intake 02/2027) is a defence recruitment drive conducted by the Central Airmen Selection Board (CASB) for male Indian citizens and Gorkha candidates. The online examination was conducted on 22-23 September 2026, and the answer key has now been released.

This recruitment offers a prestigious opportunity to serve in the medical branch of the Indian Air Force. Selected candidates undergo training with a stipend of Rs. 14,600 per month and receive a starting salary of Rs. 26,900 per month after enrolment, along with various allowances and defence benefits.

The selection process includes an online written examination, Physical Fitness Test (PFT), Adaptability Tests, and Medical Examination. Candidates must meet strict physical and medical standards, including a minimum height of 152 cm and chest measurement of 77 cm with 5 cm expansion.

Eligibility Criteria

Age Limit

10+2 candidates: Born between 1 July 2006 and 1 July 2010 (max 21 years). Pharmacy candidates: Born between 1 July 2003 and 1 July 2008 (unmarried) or 1 July 2003 to 1 July 2006 (married) (max 24 years).

Age relaxation applicable as per Indian Air Force rules for reserved categories.

Education

10+2/Intermediate with Physics, Chemistry, Biology and English with minimum 50% aggregate marks and 50% in English OR Diploma/B.Pharma/B.Sc Pharmacy with 50% marks and valid Pharmacy Council registration.

Aggregate marks will not be rounded off. 49.99% will not be considered as 50%.

Nationality / Other

Only male Indian citizens and Gorkha (subject of Nepal) candidates are eligible. Candidates must be unmarried (for 10+2 entry). Minimum height 152 cm, chest 77 cm with 5 cm expansion. Vision: 6/36 each eye correctable to 6/6, CP-II colour vision.

Refer to official notification for detailed conditions.
