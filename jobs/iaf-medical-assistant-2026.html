Complete context

About This Recruitment

Indian Air Force (IAF) has released the Airmen Group Y Medical Assistant recruitment notification for Intake 02/2027. This drive offers Group Y Medical Assistant vacancies across two entry routes: candidates who have completed 10+2 with Physics, Chemistry, Biology and English, and candidates holding a Diploma or B.Sc in Pharmacy with valid State Pharmacy Council or PCI registration.

The online application window remained open from 6 July 2026 to 26 July 2026. The written examination is scheduled for 22-23 September 2026, and the admit card is expected to be released shortly on the official IAF recruitment portal. Candidates who applied successfully should keep their registration details ready to download the admit card and check the exam city details.

This is a permanent lifecycle page. As the recruitment progresses through admit card, exam, answer key, result, physical and medical stages, every update will be added to this same page without changing the URL.

Eligibility Criteria

Age Limit

For 10+2 entry: Unmarried candidates born between 01 July 2006 and 01 July 2010. For Diploma/B.Sc Pharmacy entry: Unmarried born between 01 July 2003 and 01 July 2008; Married born between 01 July 2003 and 01 July 2006.

Age relaxation as per IAF Airmen recruitment rules.

Education

10+2 / Intermediate with Physics, Chemistry, Biology and English (50% aggregate and 50% in English) OR Diploma/B.Sc in Pharmacy with 50% marks and valid State Pharmacy Council or PCI registration.

For detailed educational conditions, refer to the official notification.

Nationality / Other

Indian citizens fulfilling the post-specific eligibility conditions may apply. Candidates must be unmarried for the 10+2 entry; specific marital conditions apply for the Diploma/B.Sc entry as per notification.

Refer to official notification for detailed conditions.
