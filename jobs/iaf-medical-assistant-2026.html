About This Recruitment

The Indian Air Force (IAF) has released the Exam City Intimation Slip for Airmen Group ‘Y’ (Medical Assistant) Intake 02/2027 on 11 September 2026. Candidates who applied for this intake can now check their allotted exam city through the candidate login on the official recruitment portal.

This is a regular Airmen recruitment (not Agniveer) for the Medical Assistant trade under Group ‘Y’ (Non-Technical). The online examination is scheduled for 22-23 September 2026. The admit card will be released separately 48-72 hours before the exam.

Male Indian citizens (including Gorkhas from Nepal) who meet the educational and age criteria are eligible for this recruitment. Candidates should keep their registration details ready to download the exam city slip and admit card from the official portal.
