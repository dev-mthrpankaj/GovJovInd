About This Recruitment

District Court Rewari Stenographer Grade III Recruitment 2026 is an offline recruitment drive for 18 posts. Applications are accepted from 28 August 2026, and the hard-copy application must reach the prescribed office by 17 September 2026 at 5:00 PM.

Candidates need a Bachelor Degree from a recognized university and must qualify the English shorthand test at 80 W.P.M. with transcription at 20 W.P.M. on computer. The shorthand test schedule is listed as 28 September to 5 October 2026.

There is no application fee. Candidates should download the prescribed form and official notification, complete the offline application carefully, attach the required documents, and ensure it reaches the designated office before the deadline.
