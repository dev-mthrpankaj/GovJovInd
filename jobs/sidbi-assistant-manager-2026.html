Complete context

About This Recruitment

Small Industries Development Bank of India (SIDBI) has published the official notification (Advertisement No. 8/Grade 'A' and 'B'/2026-27) to fill 68 Officer posts in India. The recruitment covers 63 vacancies for Assistant Manager Grade 'A' (General, Hindi, Legal, and Systems/IT streams) and 5 vacancies for Manager Grade 'B' (Hindi, Legal, and Systems/IT specialist streams).

Category-wise, the 68 advertised posts are distributed as 35 Unreserved (UR), 12 OBC, 11 SC, 5 EWS, and 5 ST vacancies. Candidates can apply for only one post under this recruitment drive. Online registration and fee payment are open from 21 November 2026 to 11 December 2026 through the official IBPS application portal linked from sidbi.in.

Selected Assistant Manager Grade 'A' officers receive a basic pay scale of Rs.62500 to Rs.126100 with approximate gross monthly emoluments of Rs.1,20,000 (annual CTC Rs.30 lakh to Rs.34 lakh). Manager Grade 'B' officers receive a pay scale of Rs.78450 to Rs.141600 with approximate gross monthly emoluments of Rs.1,45,000 (annual CTC Rs.40 lakh to Rs.43 lakh).

Selection is conducted through a Phase I online screening test and Phase II objective and descriptive examination tentatively scheduled on 27 December 2026, followed by psychometric assessment, personal interview, document verification, and medical examination. This permanent lifecycle page will carry every subsequent update including admit cards, exam notices, results, and final merit lists.

Eligibility Criteria

Age Limit

As on 21 November 2026: 21 to 30 years for Assistant Manager Grade 'A' (born 21 November 1996 to 21 November 2005) and 25 to 33 years for Manager Grade 'B' (born 21 November 1993 to 21 November 2001).

Upper age relaxation: SC/ST 5 years, OBC (NCL) 3 years, PwBD 10 years (UR), 13 years (OBC), 15 years (SC/ST), and Ex-Servicemen 5 years as per SIDBI rules.

Education

Stream-wise Graduation (min. 60% marks; 50% to 55% for reserved categories as applicable) in Commerce, Economics, Mathematics, Statistics, Business Administration, Engineering, Law, MCA, or Hindi/English with PG Diploma in Translation, plus prescribed work experience.

Educational qualification and work experience cut-off date is 11 December 2026. Work experience in Grade 'A' General stream is waived for CA and 2-year full-time MBA/PGDM holders.

Nationality / Other

Must be a citizen of India or meet nationality norms in the SIDBI notification. Each applicant may apply for only one post under Advertisement No. 8/Grade 'A' and 'B'/2026-27.

Refer to official notification for detailed conditions.

What to do at the current stage

How to Apply Online for SIDBI Grade A and B 2026

  1. Read Advertisement No. 8/Grade 'A' and 'B'/2026-27 on sidbi.in to confirm stream-wise marks and experience eligibility.
  2. Open the official IBPS registration portal from Important Links and click 'Click here for New Registration'.
  3. Enter your name, active mobile number, and email ID to generate your Provisional Registration Number and Password.
  4. Log in and fill in personal, category, educational qualification, and work experience details accurately.
  5. Upload scanned photograph, signature, left thumb impression, and handwritten declaration in the prescribed format.
  6. Pay the application fee online (Rs.1100 for General/OBC/EWS or Rs.175 for SC/ST/PwBD) on or before 11 December 2026.
  7. Verify all entries, submit the final form, and download the confirmation page and fee receipt for future stages.
