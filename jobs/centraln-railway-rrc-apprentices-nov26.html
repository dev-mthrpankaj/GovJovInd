Complete context

About This Recruitment

Railway Recruitment Cell (RRC), Central Railway has invited online applications for engagement of 2861 Act Apprentices under the Apprentices Act 1961 for the year 2026-27. The notification was published on 03 November 2026, and the online application window is open from 03 November 2026 to 02 December 2026 (5:00 PM).

The recruitment covers apprentices in various ITI trades across Mumbai, Bhusawal, Pune, Nagpur, Solapur clusters and the Track Machine Organisation. Candidates must have passed Matriculation (10th) and possess an ITI certificate in the relevant trade.

The age limit is 15 to 24 years as on 03 November 2026. The application fee is Rs.100 for General, OBC and EWS candidates, while SC, ST, PwBD and Women candidates are exempted. Selection will be based on a merit list prepared from ITI marks, followed by document verification and medical examination.
