Complete context

About This Recruitment

National Capital Region Transport Corporation (NCRTC) published Advt. No. 32/2026 (Operations & Maintenance Staff — 67 posts) and Advt. No. 33/2026 (Supervisor and Non-Supervisor — 23 posts) to recruit 90 candidates across Supervisor-I and Junior Maintainer roles. The online application window ran from 10 September 2026 to 09 October 2026.

Eligible candidates hold an ITI (NCVT/SCVT) certificate, a 3-year Engineering Diploma, B.E./B.Tech, BCA, B.Sc (IT), MCA, or a relevant Graduate/PG qualification depending on the post. The age limit is 18 to 28 years as on 10 September 2026, with category-wise relaxation as per NCRTC rules.

The NCRTC Various Post Admit Card 2026 was released on 26 November 2026 on the official examination portal. Candidates must download and print their admit card and carry a valid original photo ID to the examination centre as per the schedule printed on the hall ticket.
