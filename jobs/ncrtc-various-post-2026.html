Complete context

About This Recruitment

National Capital Region Transport Corporation (NCRTC) has released two vacancy notices (32/2026 & 33/2026) for the recruitment of 90 Supervisor-I and Junior Maintainer posts. This includes 23 posts on a contract basis and 67 posts on a direct recruitment basis. The online application window is open from 10 September 2026 to 9 October 2026.

This recruitment is suitable for candidates with engineering degrees, diplomas, or ITI qualifications in relevant trades. The posts are part of the Operations & Maintenance cadre for the Namo Bharat Project. Selected candidates will receive pay in the S1 or NS3 level, along with allowances as per company rules.

Candidates should read both official vacancy notices carefully to verify post-specific eligibility, medical standards, and the mandatory three-year service bond before applying.

Eligibility Criteria

Age Limit

Maximum 28 years as on 10 September 2026.

SC/ST: 5 years; OBC (NCL): 3 years; PwBD: 10-15 years; Ex-Servicemen: length of service + 3 years (max 50 years).

Education

Engineering Degree / Diploma / ITI in relevant trade from a recognised institute.

Only full-time regular courses from UGC-recognised universities or AICTE-approved institutions are accepted.

Nationality / Other

Indian nationals fulfilling the post-specific eligibility conditions may apply.

Refer to official notification for detailed conditions.

What to do at the current stage

How to Apply

  1. Read both official vacancy notices (32/2026 & 33/2026) carefully and confirm eligibility for your preferred post.
  2. Visit the official NCRTC website and open the Careers section.
  3. Register using a valid email ID and mobile number to generate login credentials.
  4. Log in and fill the application form with personal, educational and experience details.
  5. Upload scanned photograph, signature and relevant certificates in the prescribed format.
  6. Pay the applicable application fee online (Rs.500 for UR/OBC/EWS/ESM; Nil for SC/ST/PwBD).
  7. Submit the form before 9 October 2026 (23:55 hrs) and save the confirmation page.
