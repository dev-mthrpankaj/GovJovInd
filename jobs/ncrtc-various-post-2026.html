Complete context

About This Recruitment

National Capital Region Transport Corporation (NCRTC) published Advt. No. 32/2026 (Operations & Maintenance Staff — 67 posts) and Advt. No. 33/2026 (Supervisor and Non-Supervisor — 23 posts) to recruit 90 candidates across Supervisor-I and Junior Maintainer roles. The online application window ran from 10 September 2026 to 09 October 2026.

Eligible candidates hold an ITI (NCVT/SCVT) certificate, a 3-year Engineering Diploma, B.E./B.Tech, BCA, B.Sc (IT), MCA, or a relevant Graduate/PG qualification depending on the post. The age limit is 18 to 28 years as on 10 September 2026, with category-wise relaxation as per NCRTC rules.

The NCRTC Various Post Admit Card 2026 was released on 26 November 2026 on the official examination portal. Candidates must download and print their admit card and carry a valid original photo ID to the examination centre as per the schedule printed on the hall ticket.

What to do at the current stage

How to Download NCRTC Admit Card 2026

  1. Open the official NCRTC admit card login portal from the Important Links section.
  2. Enter your Registration ID / User ID and Password generated during application.
  3. Submit the credentials to view your NCRTC Various Post Admit Card 2026.
  4. Verify your name, roll number, post code, exam date, reporting time and test centre address.
  5. Download the admit card PDF and take a clear printout for exam day.
  6. Carry the printed admit card along with a valid original photo ID to the examination centre.

Original Application Process

Preserved for recruitment history and reference after the application stage closes.

  1. Visit the official NCRTC website at https://ncrtc.in/.
  2. Open the NCRTC Various Post 2026 online application link for Advt. No. 32/2026 or 33/2026.
  3. Register using a valid email ID and mobile number.
  4. Log in with the generated registration ID and password.
  5. Fill in personal, educational and category details in the online form.
  6. Upload scanned copies of photograph, signature and required documents.
  7. Pay the application fee (Rs.500 for General/OBC/EWS; Nil for SC/ST/PH) via Online mode or E-Challan.
  8. Submit the form and print the final confirmation page for future reference.
