Complete context

About This Recruitment

BSF Constable Tradesman Examination 2025 is a Border Security Force (BSF) recruitment drive to fill 3588 Constable (Tradesman) posts (3406 Male and 182 Female). The official notification was published in July 2025, and online applications were accepted from 25 July to 23 August 2025.

The selection process begins with the Physical Efficiency Test (PET) and Physical Standard Test (PST), which were conducted in December 2025. Following the declaration of the PET/PST result on 10 June 2026, the recruitment authority proceeds with the Written Examination for the qualified candidates.

The Written Exam Admit Card was officially released on 17 November 2026. The written examination is scheduled to be held on 02 December 2026. Candidates must carry the printed admit card along with an original valid photo ID to the examination centre.

Basic eligibility requires candidates to have passed the 10th standard (Matriculation) from a recognised board, along with an ITI certificate or proven proficiency in the applied trade. The age limit is 18 to 25 years as on 23 August 2025, with standard age relaxation for reserved categories.

What to do at the current stage

How to Download Written Exam Admit Card

  1. Open the official BSF recruitment login portal via Important Links.
  2. Enter your registration ID or Enrollment Number along with your password/Date of Birth.
  3. Navigate to the dashboard and select the download link for the Written Exam Admit Card.
  4. Verify your personal details, exam centre, and reporting time carefully.
  5. Print a clear colour copy of the admit card.
  6. Carry the printed admit card along with a valid original photo ID (like Aadhaar or PAN card) to the exam venue.

Original Application Process

Preserved for recruitment history and reference after the application stage closes.

  1. Visit the official BSF recruitment portal at rectt.bsf.gov.in.
  2. Click on 'New Registration' if not already registered, and complete basic details.
  3. Log in using credentials and fill out the Constable Tradesman online form.
  4. Upload required scanned documents, signature, and a recent photograph.
  5. Pay the application fee via the specified online payment gateway.
  6. Submit the application and print the confirmation page for future records.
