Complete context

About This Recruitment

Border Security Force (BSF) Constable (Tradesman) Recruitment 2025 is a central government recruitment drive to fill 3,588 Constable (Tradesman) posts across various trades including Cook, Water Carrier, Sweeper, Washer Man, Cobbler, Tailor, Carpenter, Plumber, Painter, Electrician, and Pump Operator. The official notification was published on 22 July 2025, and the online application window remained open from 25 July 2025 to 23 August 2025.

The PET/PST was conducted from 26 December 2025 to 28 March 2026 across various recruitment centres in India. The PET/PST result was declared on 10 June 2026, and a total of 1,81,143 candidates have been shortlisted for the Phase 2 written examination. Among the shortlisted candidates, 1,76,361 are male and 4,782 are female.

The selection process includes PET/PST, Written Examination, Trade Test, Document Verification, Medical Examination, and Final Merit List. Basic eligibility requires Class 10th (Matric) with an ITI certificate in the relevant trade. The age limit is 18 to 25 years as on 25 August 2025, with relaxation for reserved categories as per BSF rules.

The selected candidates will be placed in Pay Matrix Level 3 with a salary of Rs.21,700 to Rs.69,100 along with other allowances as per Central Government rules.

What to do at the current stage

How to Check BSF Constable Tradesman PET/PST Result

  1. Visit the official BSF recruitment portal at rectt.bsf.gov.in.
  2. Click on the Constable Tradesman PET/PST Result 2026 link under latest notifications.
  3. Enter your Registration Number or Roll Number, Date of Birth, Gender, and Verification Code.
  4. Submit the details and view your PET/PST result on the screen.
  5. Download the result PDF and search for your roll number using Ctrl+F.
  6. Take a printout and save multiple copies for future reference.

Original Application Process

Preserved for recruitment history and reference after the application stage closes.

  1. Visit the official BSF recruitment website at rectt.bsf.gov.in.
  2. Read the official notification (Advt No. 9A/Advt/CT(TM)-2024-25/Rectt/BSF/2025) carefully.
  3. Register on the BSF portal with valid email ID and mobile number.
  4. Fill the online application form with accurate personal and educational details.
  5. Upload photograph, signature and required documents.
  6. Pay the applicable examination fee online.
