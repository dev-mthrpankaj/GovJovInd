Complete context

About This Recruitment

RPSC Senior Teacher Grade II (TGT) Recruitment 2025 is conducted by the Rajasthan Public Service Commission to fill Senior Teacher (Grade-II) posts in the Secondary Education Department, Government of Rajasthan. The official notification (Advt. No. 07/Exam/Sr. Teach./2025-26) was released on 17 July 2025, and online applications were accepted from 19 August 2025 to 17 September 2025.

The total vacancy was originally advertised as 6,500 posts. After official corrigenda, the vacancy was revised to 9,651 posts (Corrigendum dated 04 July 2026), covering ten subjects including Hindi, English, Mathematics, Science, Social Science, Sanskrit, Urdu, Punjabi, Sindhi and Gujarati. The written examination was conducted from 12 to 18 July 2026, and the answer key was released on 28 July 2026.

The provisional result for Mathematics and Sindhi was declared on 28 October 2026. A total of 1,659 candidates were shortlisted in Mathematics and 2 candidates in Sindhi for eligibility checking. Shortlisted candidates must complete the online Detailed Form Cum Scrutiny between 03 and 09 November 2026, followed by document verification.

Eligibility requires a graduate degree in the relevant subject with B.Ed. or an equivalent education degree/diploma. The age limit is 18 to 40 years as on 01 January 2026, with relaxation for reserved categories as per Rajasthan government rules.
