Complete context

About This Recruitment

RPSC Senior Teacher Grade II (TGT) Recruitment 2025 is conducted by the Rajasthan Public Service Commission to fill Senior Teacher (Grade-II) posts in the Secondary Education Department, Government of Rajasthan. The official notification (Advt. No. 07/Exam/Sr. Teach./2025-26) was released on 17 July 2025, and online applications were accepted from 19 August 2025 to 17 September 2025.

The total vacancy was originally advertised as 6,500 posts. After official corrigenda, the vacancy was revised to 9,651 posts (Corrigendum dated 04 July 2026), covering ten subjects including Hindi, English, Mathematics, Science, Social Science, Sanskrit, Urdu, Punjabi, Sindhi and Gujarati. The written examination was conducted from 12 to 18 July 2026, and the answer key was released on 28 July 2026.

The provisional result for Mathematics and Sindhi was declared on 28 October 2026. A total of 1,659 candidates were shortlisted in Mathematics and 2 candidates in Sindhi for eligibility checking. Shortlisted candidates must complete the online Detailed Form Cum Scrutiny between 03 and 09 November 2026, followed by document verification.

Eligibility requires a graduate degree in the relevant subject with B.Ed. or an equivalent education degree/diploma. The age limit is 18 to 40 years as on 01 January 2026, with relaxation for reserved categories as per Rajasthan government rules.

Eligibility Criteria

Age Limit

18 to 40 years as on 01 January 2026.

Age relaxation applicable for SC, ST, OBC, MBC, women and other reserved categories as per Rajasthan government rules.

Education

Graduate or equivalent degree with the concerned subject as an optional subject, plus B.Ed. or Degree/Diploma in Education recognized by NCTE/Government.

For Science and Social Science, at least two relevant optional subjects are required at graduation level. Working knowledge of Hindi in Devanagari script and knowledge of Rajasthani culture is also required.

Nationality / Other

Indian citizens fulfilling the post-specific eligibility conditions may apply. Rajasthan domicile requirements apply as per rules.

Refer to official notification for detailed conditions.

What to do at the current stage

What Shortlisted Candidates Should Do Now

  1. Download the Mathematics or Sindhi result PDF from the Important Links section.
  2. Search your roll number in the provisional list using Ctrl+F.
  3. If shortlisted, note the scrutiny form submission window: 03 November to 09 November 2026.
  4. Log in to the RPSC SSO portal and complete the online Detailed Form Cum Scrutiny.
  5. Upload self-attested copies of all required educational and category certificates.
  6. Submit the form before 09 November 2026 (11:59 PM) to avoid cancellation of candidature.
  7. Keep a printed copy of the submitted scrutiny form for future reference.

Original Application Process

Preserved for recruitment history and reference after the application stage closes.

  1. Visit the official RPSC website at rpsc.rajasthan.gov.in.
  2. Complete One-Time Registration (OTR) on the SSO portal with Aadhaar details.
  3. Read the official notification (Advt. No. 07/2025-26) carefully.
  4. Fill the online application form with accurate personal and educational details.
  5. Upload photograph, signature and required documents.
  6. Pay the applicable examination fee online.
  7. Submit the form and save the confirmation page for future reference.
