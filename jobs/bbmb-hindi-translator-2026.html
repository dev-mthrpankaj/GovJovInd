About This Recruitment

Bhakra Beas Management Board (BBMB) has invited online applications for 09 Hindi Translator posts under Advertisement No. 02 of 2026. The application window runs from 31 August 2026 to 21 September 2026, while the fee can be paid up to 23 September 2026.

The advertised Hindi Translator posts are distributed across General, OBC, EWS and ST categories. Candidates must satisfy the prescribed Hindi-English academic qualification and have at least three years of translator experience in government, semi-government or a reputed daily newspaper.

After submitting the online application, applicants should also follow the notification instructions regarding sending a copy of the online application form to the Centralized Staff Selection Committee, BBMB, Chandigarh.
