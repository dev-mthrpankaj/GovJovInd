Complete context

About This Recruitment

All India Bar Examination (AIBE XXII) 2026 is a national-level certification examination conducted by the Bar Council of India (BCI) to assess law graduates for the Certificate of Practice (CoP) in Indian courts. The official press release was issued on 18 July 2026, and online registrations remained open from 19 August 2026 to 27 October 2026.

The fee payment window closed on 28 October 2026, followed by the application correction window until 30 October 2026. On 18 November 2026, the Bar Council of India released the AIBE XXII Admit Card 2026 on its official examination portal for all eligible registered candidates.

The AIBE XXII written examination is scheduled to be held on 29 November 2026 across designated test centres in India. Candidates must log in with their Registration Number or Enrollment Number and Date of Birth to download the hall ticket and verify their reporting time and centre address.

Eligibility requires a 3-year or 5-year Bachelor Degree in Law (LLB) from a university recognised by the Bar Council of India, with no upper or lower age limit. There are no fixed vacancies because AIBE is a qualifying certification examination rather than a vacancy-based recruitment.
