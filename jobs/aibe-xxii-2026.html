Complete context

About This Recruitment

AIBE XXII (All India Bar Examination 22) is conducted by the Bar Council of India as the qualifying examination for law graduates who wish to obtain a Certificate of Practice and practice law in Indian courts. The examination is mandatory for advocates enrolled with a State Bar Council.

The AIBE XXII notification was released on 18 July 2026. Online registration began on 19 August 2026 and will close on 27 October 2026. The last date for fee payment is 28 October 2026, and the application correction window closes on 30 October 2026. The admit card is scheduled for release on 14 November 2026.

The AIBE XXII examination will be held on 29 November 2026 in offline pen-and-paper mode. It is an open-book examination where candidates may carry Bare Acts without notes or commentary. The paper consists of 100 multiple-choice questions with no negative marking. The qualifying marks are 45% for General and OBC candidates and 40% for SC, ST and PwD candidates.

Eligibility requires a 3-year or 5-year LLB degree from a BCI-recognised university, or final-year/final-semester status with no backlogs under the undertaking framework. Candidates who appeared in AIBE XX or XXI but did not clear do not need to register afresh and can log in with their existing credentials.
