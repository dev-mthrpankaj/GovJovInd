Complete context

About This Recruitment

All India Bar Examination (AIBE XXII) 2026 is a national-level certification examination conducted by the Bar Council of India (BCI) to assess law graduates for the Certificate of Practice (CoP) in Indian courts. The official press release was issued on 18 July 2026, and online registrations remained open from 19 August 2026 to 27 October 2026.

The fee payment window closed on 28 October 2026, followed by the application correction window until 30 October 2026. On 18 November 2026, the Bar Council of India released the AIBE XXII Admit Card 2026 on its official examination portal for all eligible registered candidates.

The AIBE XXII written examination is scheduled to be held on 29 November 2026 across designated test centres in India. Candidates must log in with their Registration Number or Enrollment Number and Date of Birth to download the hall ticket and verify their reporting time and centre address.

Eligibility requires a 3-year or 5-year Bachelor Degree in Law (LLB) from a university recognised by the Bar Council of India, with no upper or lower age limit. There are no fixed vacancies because AIBE is a qualifying certification examination rather than a vacancy-based recruitment.

What to do at the current stage

How to Download AIBE XXII Admit Card 2026

  1. Open the official AIBE XXII candidate login portal from the Important Links section.
  2. Enter your Registration Number or Enrollment Number and Date of Birth to log in.
  3. Locate and open the AIBE XXII Admit Card 2026 download link on your dashboard.
  4. Verify your name, roll number, exam date (29 November 2026), reporting time, and test centre address.
  5. Download the admit card PDF and take a clear colour printout for exam day.
  6. Carry the printed admit card along with a valid original photo ID to the examination centre.

Original Application Process

Preserved for recruitment history and reference after the application stage closes.

  1. Visit the official Bar Council of India examination portal at allindiabarexamination.com.
  2. Read the AIBE XXII 2026 official press release and instructions carefully.
  3. Complete online registration using a valid email ID and mobile number.
  4. Fill in personal, academic, and LLB degree details accurately in the application form.
  5. Upload the required photograph, signature, and supporting documents.
  6. Pay the application fee of Rs.3500 (General/EWS/OBC) or Rs.2500 (SC/ST/PwBD) online.
  7. Submit the form and save the confirmation page for future reference.
