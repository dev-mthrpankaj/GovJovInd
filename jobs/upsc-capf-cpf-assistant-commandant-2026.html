About This Recruitment

UPSC Central Armed Police Forces (Assistant Commandants) Examination 2026 is conducted by the Union Public Service Commission for Assistant Commandant vacancies in BSF, CRPF, CISF, ITBP and SSB.

The recruitment notified 349 vacancies. Applications were accepted from 20 February to 12 March 2026, the written examination was held on 19 July 2026, and UPSC uploaded the written result on 25 August 2026.

Candidates who have qualified the written examination should follow subsequent UPSC and designated CAPF instructions for the next stages of the selection process. This permanent page will continue to carry later physical, interview and final-result updates when officially announced.
