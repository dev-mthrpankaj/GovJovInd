Complete context

About This Recruitment

Uttarakhand Subordinate Service Selection Commission (UKSSSC) published Advertisement No. 84/UKSSSC/2026 on 17 November 2026 to fill 1694 Group C uniformed constable and guard vacancies across multiple Uttarakhand government departments, including Uttarakhand Police, Fire Service, Forest Department, Prison Administration and Reform Services, Secretariat Administration, Excise Department, and Transport Department.

Online registration and fee payment opened on 25 November 2026 and will close on 25 December 2026. Registered applicants will also get a three-day online correction window from 28 December 2026 to 30 December 2026 to rectify permitted errors in their submitted forms.

Candidates who have passed the 10+2 (Intermediate) examination from a recognised board and meet the post-specific age limits as on 01 July 2026 and physical measurement standards are eligible to apply. The selection process comprises Physical Standard Test (PST) and Physical Efficiency Test (PET), followed by an offline OMR-based written examination tentatively scheduled for 04 April 2027, Document Verification, and Medical Examination.

Selected candidates across all 11 uniformed cadres are placed in Pay Level-3 with a pay scale of Rs.21,700 to Rs.69,100 per month under Uttarakhand government rules.

Eligibility Criteria

Age Limit

Calculated as on 01 July 2026: 18 to 24 years for Police Constable, PAC/IRB, Fireman, Forest Guard, and Special Tiger Guard; 18 to 29 years for Secretariat Guard and Transport Constable; 18 to 34 years for Excise Constable; 21 to 34 years for Jail Warder.

Upper age relaxation of 5 years for Uttarakhand SC, ST, OBC, and Freedom Fighter dependants; 10 years for eligible PwBD candidates; and service period plus 3 years for Ex-Servicemen as per Uttarakhand rules.

Education

10+2 (Intermediate) examination passed from Uttarakhand Board of School Education, Ramnagar, or any recognised board in India.

Candidates must also fulfill Uttarakhand domicile, schooling, or employment exchange registration conditions specified in Advt. No. 84/UKSSSC/2026.

Nationality / Other

Indian citizens meeting Uttarakhand state eligibility conditions and post-wise Physical Standard Test (PST) measurements (with relaxation for ST and hill-area Parvatiya certificate holders) can apply.

Refer to official notification for detailed conditions.

What to do at the current stage

How to Apply Online

  1. Visit the official UKSSSC portal at sssc.uk.gov.in and open the link for Advertisement No. 84/UKSSSC/2026.
  2. Complete One Time Registration (OTR) or log in using your Aadhaar number, email ID, and mobile number.
  3. Enter personal details, Uttarakhand domicile/schooling eligibility details, and 10+2 educational qualifications accurately.
  4. Select your preferred uniformed posts across Police, Fireman, Forest Guard, Jail Warder, Excise, Transport, or Secretariat Guard.
  5. Upload your live photograph, signature, left thumb impression, High School certificate, both sides of Aadhaar card, and category certificates.
  6. Pay the prescribed application fee online before 25 December 2026 and print the submitted confirmation form.
