Complete context

About This Recruitment

Uttarakhand Subordinate Service Selection Commission (UKSSSC) has issued the official notification (Advt No. 84/UKSSSC/2026) for the recruitment of 1694 Constables, Fireman, Forest Guards, Jail Warders, and other posts across various departments of the Uttarakhand Government. The notification was officially released on 17 November 2026.

Eligible candidates can apply online for these positions through the official UKSSSC website. The online application window is scheduled to remain open from 25 November 2026 to 25 December 2026. After the application phase, a correction window will be available from 28 December to 30 December 2026 to rectify any errors in the submitted forms.

To be eligible, candidates must have completed their 10+2 (Intermediate) education from a recognized board. The age limit varies between 18 and 35 years depending on the specific post applied for, with the cutoff date being 01 July 2026. Selection will be based on a Physical Standard Test (PST), Physical Efficiency Test (PET), followed by a Written Examination which is tentatively scheduled for 04 April 2027.

Preserved for recruitment history and reference after the application stage closes.

Frequently Asked Questions

The online application process for UKSSSC Constable and Other Posts will start on 25 November 2026 on the official website sssc.uk.gov.in.

The last date to submit the online application form and pay the fee is 25 December 2026.

A total of 1694 vacancies have been announced for Constable, Fireman, Forest Guard, and other posts under UKSSSC Advt No. 84/2026.

Candidates must have passed the 10+2 (Intermediate) examination from a recognized board to be eligible for these posts.

The minimum age is 18 years, and the maximum age varies from 25 to 35 years depending on the specific post. The age is calculated as on 01 July 2026.

General and OBC candidates need to pay Rs. 300, while SC, ST, EWS, and PH candidates need to pay Rs. 150.

The written examination for UKSSSC Constable and Other Posts is tentatively scheduled for 04 April 2027.

The selection process consists of a Physical Standard Test (PST), Physical Efficiency Test (PET), Written Examination, Document Verification, and Medical Examination.
