Complete context

About This Recruitment

Damodar Valley Corporation (DVC) has released Advt No. PLR/ET/04/2026/05 to recruit 108 Executive Trainees in Mechanical, Electrical, Civil, Control and Instrumentation (C&I), and Mining engineering disciplines. The recruitment is conducted through GATE 2025 scores, and the job location is Kolkata.

Online registration and fee payment opened on 06 November 2026 and will remain active until 06 December 2026 on the official DVC recruitment portal at dvc.gov.in. Selected candidates will receive a pay scale of Rs.56,100 to Rs.1,77,500 per month.

Applicants must hold a full-time Bachelor's Degree in Engineering or Technology (B.E./B.Tech) in the relevant discipline from an AICTE or statutory-approved university or institution. The upper age limit is 29 years as on 06 December 2026, with category-wise age relaxation as per DVC rules.

The category-wise vacancy distribution covers 48 General, 18 OBC, 10 EWS, 24 SC, and 8 ST posts. General, OBC, and EWS candidates must pay an online application fee of Rs.300, whereas SC, ST, and PH candidates are exempted from the fee.

Eligibility Criteria

Age Limit

Maximum age limit is 29 years as on 06 December 2026. Minimum age is not specified.

Upper age relaxation is applicable for reserved categories as per Damodar Valley Corporation (DVC) rules mentioned in the official notification.

Education

Full-time Bachelor's Degree in Engineering or Technology (B.E./B.Tech) from an AICTE or statutory-approved University/Institution in Mechanical, Electrical, Civil, C&I (Electronics & Instrumentation / Instrumentation & Control), or Mining Engineering.

Candidates must also have a valid GATE 2025 score in the relevant engineering discipline.

Nationality / Other

Allied branches specified in the notification (such as Production, Industrial, Thermal, Power, Power Electronics, and Electronics Instrumentation & Control) are eligible for respective disciplines.

Refer to official notification for detailed conditions.

What to do at the current stage

How to Apply Online for DVC Executive Trainees 2026

  1. Visit the official Damodar Valley Corporation recruitment portal at dvc.gov.in.
  2. Open the DVC Executive Trainees 2026 (Advt No. PLR/ET/04/2026/05) apply link.
  3. Register using a valid email ID and active mobile number to generate login credentials.
  4. Log in and fill the application form with personal, educational, category, and GATE 2025 details.
  5. Upload scanned copies of your recent photograph, signature, and required certificates.
  6. Pay the application fee of Rs.300 online (SC/ST/PH candidates are exempted).
  7. Verify all details, submit the form, and download the confirmation page for future reference.
