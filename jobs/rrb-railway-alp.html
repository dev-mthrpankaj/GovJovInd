About This Recruitment

RRB ALP Recruitment 2026 is being conducted by the Railway Recruitment Board for 10,970 Assistant Loco Pilot (ALP) posts under CEN 01/2025. The recruitment covers CBT-I, CBT-II, CBAT, document verification and medical examination stages across RRB regions.

The CBT-II Result 2026 has been declared on 01 October 2026. Candidates can check their zone-wise result and cutoff PDF from the respective RRB region website. The next stage, CBAT, is scheduled for 30 October 2026.

This page preserves the full recruitment journey from the original notification to the final merit list, so candidates do not need to search for separate pages for admit card, answer key, result, CBAT or document verification updates.
