Complete context

About This Recruitment

RRB Railway ALP Assistant Loco Pilot Recruitment 2026 (CEN 01/2025) is one of the largest technical recruitment drives conducted by the Railway Recruitment Board for 9,970 Assistant Loco Pilot posts across Indian Railways zones. Online applications were accepted from 12 April 2025 to 19 May 2025.

The recruitment has already progressed through CBT Stage I, CBT Stage II and the answer-key / objection stage. The RRB ALP CBT II Result 2026 is now awaited and will be published on the official RRB / Digialm candidate login portal. Candidates who appeared in CBT II on 28 July 2026 should keep their registration number and date of birth ready to check the result.

This is a permanent recruitment lifecycle page. Every future RRB ALP update — CBT II result, CBAT, document verification, medical examination and final merit list — will be added here without changing the URL.
