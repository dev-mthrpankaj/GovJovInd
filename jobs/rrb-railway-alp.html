Complete context

About This Recruitment

Railway Recruitment Board (RRB) is conducting the Assistant Loco Pilot (ALP) recruitment under CEN 01/2025 for a total of 10,970 posts across all railway zones. The official notification was published on 12 April 2025, and the online application window remained open from 12 April 2025 to 19 May 2025.

The Computer Based Test (CBT-I) was conducted in multiple phases in February and March 2026. The CBT-I result was declared on 12 June 2026. The CBT-II exam was held on 28 July 2026, and the CBT-II result was declared on 01 October 2026 along with the cut-off marks.

The Computer Based Aptitude Test (CBAT) is the next stage, scheduled for 30 October 2026. RRB released the CBAT Exam City Details on 20 October 2026. Candidates can check their exam city and download the admit card from the official portal.

Basic eligibility is 10th pass plus ITI in the relevant trade or a Diploma/Degree in Engineering. The age limit is 18 to 30 years as on 01 July 2025, with relaxation for reserved categories as per railway rules.

What to do at the current stage

How to Check CBAT Exam City Details

  1. Open the official RRB CBAT exam city portal from Important Links.
  2. Log in using your Registration Number, Enrollment Number or Date of Birth.
  3. Check your CBAT exam city, date and reporting time.
  4. Download and save the exam city intimation slip for reference.
  5. Read the official CBAT exam date notice carefully before the exam day.
  6. The CBAT admit card will be released a few days before the exam.

Original Application Process

Preserved for recruitment history and reference after the application stage closes.

  1. Visit the official RRB recruitment website at rrbapply.gov.in.
  2. Read the official CEN 01/2025 notification carefully.
  3. Register on the RRB portal with valid email ID and mobile number.
  4. Fill the online application form with accurate personal and educational details.
  5. Upload photograph, signature and required documents.
  6. Pay the applicable examination fee online.
  7. Submit the form and save the confirmation page for future reference.
