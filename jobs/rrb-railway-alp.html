Complete context

About This Recruitment

Railway Recruitment Board (RRB) is conducting the Assistant Loco Pilot (ALP) recruitment under Centralised Employment Notice No. CEN 01/2025. The recruitment drive was announced on 12 April 2025 for a total of 10,970 posts (revised from 9,970) across all zonal railways in India.

The selection process for ALP posts includes Computer Based Test Stage 1 (CBT-1), Computer Based Test Stage 2 (CBT-2), Computer Based Aptitude Test (CBAT), Document Verification, and Medical Examination. The CBT-1 exam was conducted on 13, 16, 17 & 18 February 2026. The CBT-2 exam was held on 28 July 2026.

As of the latest update, RRB has declared the CBT-II Result on 1 October 2026. Candidates who have qualified in CBT-2 will now appear for the Computer Based Aptitude Test (CBAT) scheduled on 30 October 2026. The final merit list will be prepared based on CBT-2 Part A marks (50% weightage) and CBAT marks (50% weightage) as per the revised merit calculation for ALP 2026.

Eligibility Criteria

Age Limit

18 to 30 years as on 01 July 2025.

Age relaxation: SC/ST 5 years, OBC-NCL 3 years, Ex-Servicemen as per rules.

Education

10th pass with ITI certificate in relevant trade OR Diploma/Degree in Engineering in respective field.

For zone-wise eligibility, refer to the official notification.

Nationality / Other

Indian citizen, subject of Nepal/Bhutan, or other categories as specified. Must meet A-1 medical standard with strict vision requirements.

Vision: Distant 6/6, 6/6 without glasses; Near: Sn 0.6, 0.6 without glasses.
