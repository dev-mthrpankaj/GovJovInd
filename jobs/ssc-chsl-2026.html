Complete context

About This Recruitment

The Staff Selection Commission (SSC) Combined Higher Secondary (10+2) Level Examination 2026 is a national-level recruitment drive to fill 2,536 tentative Group C vacancies for Lower Division Clerk (LDC), Junior Secretariat Assistant (JSA), and Data Entry Operator (DEO) posts across central government ministries, departments, and offices. The official notification (F. No. HQ-C1102/5/2026-C-1) was released on 07 September 2026, and online applications remained open until 07 October 2026, followed by the fee payment deadline on 08 October 2026 and the correction window from 14 October 2026 to 16 October 2026.

On 23 November 2026, SSC activated the Tier-I Exam City Intimation link and published the official exam city and scribe notice on ssc.gov.in. Registered candidates can log in with their Registration Number and Password to check their allotted examination city and exam date ahead of the Tier-I Computer Based Examination scheduled from 03 December 2026 to 31 December 2026.

The Tier-I Admit Card will be released on ssc.gov.in 2 to 3 days before each candidate's exam date. Selection is based on Tier-I Computer Based Examination (screening), Tier-II Computer Based Examination, qualifying Skill Test or Typing Test, and Document Verification, with no interview stage.

Eligibility requires passing 12th Standard (10+2) or equivalent from a recognised board on or before 07 October 2026 (with Science and Mathematics for specified DEO posts). The prescribed age limit is 18 to 27 years as on 01 August 2026, with relaxation for reserved categories as per Government of India rules, and pay scales range from Rs.19,900 to Rs.92,300 across Pay Levels 2, 4, and 5.
