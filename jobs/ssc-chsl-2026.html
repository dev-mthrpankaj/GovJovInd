Complete context

About This Recruitment

The Staff Selection Commission (SSC) Combined Higher Secondary (10+2) Level Examination 2026 is a national-level recruitment drive to fill 2,536 tentative Group C vacancies for Lower Division Clerk (LDC), Junior Secretariat Assistant (JSA), and Data Entry Operator (DEO) posts across central government ministries, departments, and offices. The official notification (F. No. HQ-C1102/5/2026-C-1) was released on 07 September 2026, and online applications remained open until 07 October 2026, followed by the fee payment deadline on 08 October 2026 and the correction window from 14 October 2026 to 16 October 2026.

On 23 November 2026, SSC activated the Tier-I Exam City Intimation link and published the official exam city and scribe notice on ssc.gov.in. Registered candidates can log in with their Registration Number and Password to check their allotted examination city and exam date ahead of the Tier-I Computer Based Examination scheduled from 03 December 2026 to 31 December 2026.

The Tier-I Admit Card will be released on ssc.gov.in 2 to 3 days before each candidate's exam date. Selection is based on Tier-I Computer Based Examination (screening), Tier-II Computer Based Examination, qualifying Skill Test or Typing Test, and Document Verification, with no interview stage.

Eligibility requires passing 12th Standard (10+2) or equivalent from a recognised board on or before 07 October 2026 (with Science and Mathematics for specified DEO posts). The prescribed age limit is 18 to 27 years as on 01 August 2026, with relaxation for reserved categories as per Government of India rules, and pay scales range from Rs.19,900 to Rs.92,300 across Pay Levels 2, 4, and 5.

Eligibility Criteria

Age Limit

18 to 27 years as on 01 August 2026 (candidates born not before 02 August 1999 and not later than 01 August 2008).

Upper age relaxation is applicable for SC, ST, OBC, PwBD, Ex-Servicemen, and eligible central government employees as per Government of India rules.

Education

Passed 12th Standard (10+2) or equivalent examination from a recognised Board or University on or before 07 October 2026.

For Data Entry Operator (DEO) posts in specified ministries and departments, 12th Standard pass in Science stream with Mathematics as a subject is required.

Nationality / Other

Candidates must be citizens of India or meet the nationality criteria stated in the official SSC CHSL 2026 notification.

Refer to official notification for detailed conditions.

What to do at the current stage

How to Check SSC CHSL Exam City 2026

  1. Open the official Staff Selection Commission candidate login portal at ssc.gov.in from Important Links.
  2. Enter your SSC Registration Number, Password, and the security captcha code to sign in.
  3. Navigate to the Combined Higher Secondary (10+2) Level Examination 2026 section on your dashboard.
  4. Click the Tier-I Exam City Intimation link to view your allotted examination city and exam date.
  5. Verify your name, registration number, exam date, and allotted city carefully, then save a copy for travel planning.
  6. Note that the exam city slip is not an entry pass; download your official Tier-I Admit Card from ssc.gov.in when released 2 to 3 days before your exam date.

Original Application Process

Preserved for recruitment history and reference after the application stage closes.

  1. Visit the official Staff Selection Commission website at ssc.gov.in.
  2. Complete One-Time Registration (OTR) using a valid mobile number and email address.
  3. Log in with your SSC Registration Number and Password and select Combined Higher Secondary (10+2) Level Examination 2026.
  4. Fill in personal, educational, and exam centre preference details carefully.
  5. Capture a live photograph and upload your scanned signature as per SSC specifications.
  6. Pay the Rs.100 examination fee online if applicable (exempted for female, SC, ST, PwBD, and ESM candidates).
  7. Submit the application form and print the final confirmation page for future reference.
