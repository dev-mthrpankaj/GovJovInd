MP Police ASI and Head Constable Computer Recruitment 2026 is an MPESB recruitment for 89 technical police posts: 14 Assistant Sub Inspector (Computer) and 75 Head Constable (Computer). The recruitment has progressed through written examination and result stages.

Candidates appearing for the current stage should carry the admit card, valid identity proof and all educational/category documents required by MPESB.

Eligibility Criteria

Age Limit

Minimum 18 years and maximum 33 years as on 19 February 2026.

Age relaxation is applicable as per the official MPESB rulebook.

Education

ASI Computer: Bachelor/Master degree in Computer or Information Technology OR Polytechnic Diploma in Computer/IT. Head Constable Computer: 10+2 Intermediate with PCM plus 1-year Computer Diploma OR Polytechnic Diploma in Computer/IT.

Candidates should check the official rulebook for full equivalence and post-specific qualification conditions.

Nationality / Other

The supplied information lists minimum height of 160 cm for male candidates and 155 cm for female candidates; verify detailed physical/category conditions in the official rulebook.
