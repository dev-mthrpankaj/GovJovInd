About This Recruitment

MP Police ASI and Head Constable Computer Recruitment 2026 is an MPESB recruitment for 89 technical police posts: 14 Assistant Sub Inspector (Computer) and 75 Head Constable (Computer). The recruitment has progressed through written examination and result stages.

The current candidate action is the PET/practical-stage admit card. Earlier written result, PPT/second-phase status and answer-key links are retained on the same permanent page for complete lifecycle tracking.

Candidates appearing for the current stage should carry the admit card, valid identity proof and all educational/category documents required by MPESB.
