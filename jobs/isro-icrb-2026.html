About This Recruitment

The Indian Space Research Organisation (ISRO) Centralised Recruitment Board (ICRB) has released the admit card for the recruitment of various posts including Assistant, Junior Personal Assistant, Upper Division Clerk, and Stenographer. A total of 244 vacancies have been advertised under Advt No. ISRO:ICRB:01(A-JPA):2026.

The Computer Based Test (CBT) is scheduled to be held on 21 September 2026. Candidates who have successfully submitted their online applications can now download their admit cards from the official ISRO ICRB portal. The admit card is a mandatory document for entry to the examination hall.

This is a permanent recruitment lifecycle page. All future updates, including answer key, result, and final merit list, will be published on this same URL for candidate convenience.

Eligibility Criteria

Age Limit

Maximum 28 years as on 16 August 2026.

Age relaxation is applicable as per ISRO/Government of India rules.

Education

Bachelor's Degree in any stream with a minimum of 60% marks or CGPA 6.32/10 from a recognized university. For JPA/Stenographer posts, English Stenography speed of 60 WPM is required.

For detailed educational conditions, refer to the official notification.

Nationality / Other

Indian citizens fulfilling the post-specific eligibility conditions may apply.

Refer to official notification for detailed conditions.

How to Download Admit Card

  1. Visit the official ISRO ICRB admit card portal from Important Links.
  2. Click on the 'Download Admit Card' link.
  3. Enter your Registration Number and Date of Birth/Password.
  4. Complete the CAPTCHA verification if prompted.
  5. Click Submit/Login to view your admit card.
  6. Verify all details like Name, Roll Number, Exam Date, and Centre.
  7. Download and take a clear printout on A4-size paper.
  8. Carry the admit card along with a valid photo ID to the exam centre.
