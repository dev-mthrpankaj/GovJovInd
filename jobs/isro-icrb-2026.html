About This Recruitment

The Indian Space Research Organisation (ISRO) Centralised Recruitment Board (ICRB) has released the admit card for the recruitment of various posts including Assistant, Junior Personal Assistant, Upper Division Clerk, and Stenographer. A total of 244 vacancies have been advertised under Advt No. ISRO:ICRB:01(A-JPA):2026.

The Computer Based Test (CBT) is scheduled to be held on 21 September 2026. Candidates who have successfully submitted their online applications can now download their admit cards from the official ISRO ICRB portal. The admit card is a mandatory document for entry to the examination hall.

This is a permanent recruitment lifecycle page. All future updates, including answer key, result, and final merit list, will be published on this same URL for candidate convenience.
