About This Recruitment

ISRO ICRB Various Post Recruitment 2026 is a recruitment drive conducted by the Indian Space Research Organisation (ISRO) through its Centralised Recruitment Board (ICRB). The official notification was released for 244 posts of Assistant, Junior Personal Assistant, Upper Division Clerk, and Stenographer. The online application window was open from 27 July 2026 to 16 August 2026.

The Computer Based Test (CBT) was conducted on 21 September 2026. The result for the same has now been declared on the official ISRO website. Candidates can check their result using the direct link provided in the Important Links section. The result is a crucial step towards final selection, which will include further stages like Document Verification and Medical Examination.

Basic eligibility for these posts includes a Bachelor's Degree in any stream with minimum 60% marks or CGPA 6.32/10 from a recognised university. For Junior Personal Assistant and Stenographer posts, candidates also need English Stenography Speed of 60 WPM and computer proficiency. The age limit is 28 years as on 16 August 2026, with relaxation for reserved categories as per government rules.

This page will continue to be updated with all future stages, including the final merit list and joining details, without changing the URL. Candidates are advised to bookmark this page for the latest updates.
