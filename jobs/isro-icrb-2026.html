Complete context

About This Recruitment

ISRO Centralised Recruitment Board (ICRB) has released the Answer Key for the 2026 recruitment drive covering 244 posts of Assistant, Junior Personal Assistant, Upper Division Clerk and Stenographer. The Computer Based Test (CBT) was conducted on 21 September 2026 across multiple centres in India.

The answer key is now available on the official ISRO recruitment portal. Candidates who appeared in the CBT can download the provisional answer key and, where the objection window is open, raise objections against any question as per the official procedure.

The recruitment offers opportunities at ISRO centres and autonomous institutions across India. Candidates must have a Bachelor's degree with minimum 60% marks or 6.32 CGPA for all posts; JPA and Stenographer posts additionally require English stenography speed of 60 WPM and computer proficiency.

This permanent page will continue to carry every later stage — final answer key, result, cut-off, document verification, medical and final merit list — on the same URL without any change.
