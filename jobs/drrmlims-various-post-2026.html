Complete context

About This Recruitment

Dr. Ram Manohar Lohia Institute of Medical Sciences (DRRMLIMS), Lucknow has released a recruitment notification for 74 various posts. The advertisement number is 78-83/Estb.-2/Rectt./Dr.RMLIMS/2026. The online application process started on 19 October 2026 and will close on 20 November 2026.

The posts include Programmer (02), Medical Physicist (01), Radiation Safety Officer (01), Hospital Attendant Grade-II (32), Stenographer (30), and Receptionist (08). Candidates with qualifications ranging from 10th pass to postgraduate degrees are eligible to apply for different posts.

The selection process typically involves a written examination or skill test, followed by document verification. The exact selection procedure for each post is detailed in the official notification. Candidates must read the official notification carefully before applying.

The age limit is 18 to 40 years, with relaxation for reserved categories as per government rules. The application fee is Rs. 1180 for General/OBC/EWS candidates and Rs. 708 for SC/ST candidates. PH candidates are exempted from paying any fee.
