Complete context

About This Recruitment

UPCISB UP Cooperative Bank Recruitment 2026 is conducted by the U.P. Co-Operative Institutional Service Board (UPCISB), Lucknow, for the Combined Co-operative Banking and Technical Services Examination (Special Recruitment) 2026. The recruitment aims to fill 116 vacancies across five posts: Manager (7), Junior Manager (45), Assistant/Cashier (57), Assistant/Typist (2) and Assistant Engineer (Civil) (5).

The online application window was open from 25 March 2026 to 15 April 2026. The written examination was originally scheduled for 20-22 July 2026 but was postponed, and the revised examination was conducted on 7-8 September 2026 across multiple shifts. The exam city details were released on 31 August 2026 and the admit card was issued on 5 September 2026.

The UPCISB Result 2026 was declared on 30 October 2026. A total of 184 candidates have been shortlisted for the next stage of the selection process, which includes Interview for Manager, Junior Manager and Assistant Engineer (Civil) posts, and Typing Test for Assistant/Typist posts, followed by Document Verification and Medical Examination.

Basic eligibility requires a Bachelor's degree with at least 55% marks in relevant disciplines. The age limit is 21 to 40 years as on 01 January 2026, with relaxation for reserved categories as per rules. The application fee was Rs.500 for OBC/EWS, Rs.250 for SC, and nil for PH candidates.

What to do at the current stage

How to Download UPCISB Result 2026

  1. Open the official UPCISB website at upcisb.upsdc.gov.in.
  2. Navigate to the Results or Recruitment section.
  3. Click on the UPCISB UP Cooperative Bank Result 2026 link.
  4. Open the result PDF and search for your roll number using Ctrl+F.
  5. Download and save the result PDF for future reference.
  6. Check the cut-off marks and shortlisting status in the result notice.
  7. Prepare for the next stage (Interview/Typing Test) if shortlisted.

Original Application Process

Preserved for recruitment history and reference after the application stage closes.

  1. Visit the official UPCISB website at upcisb.upsdc.gov.in.
  2. Read the official notification for Combined Co-operative Banking and Technical Services Examination 2026.
  3. Register with valid email ID and mobile number.
  4. Fill the online application form with accurate personal and educational details.
  5. Upload photograph, signature and required documents.
  6. Pay the applicable examination fee online.
  7. Submit the form and save the confirmation page for future reference.
