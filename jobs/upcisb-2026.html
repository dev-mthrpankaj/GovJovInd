About This Recruitment

UPCISB UP Cooperative Bank Recruitment 2026 is being conducted by the UP Co-Operative Institutional Service Board, Lucknow for 116 vacancies under Advertisement No. A-1/S.R./2026.

The application window ran from 25 March to 15 April 2026. The current recruitment stage is the admit card and written examination, with the exam scheduled for 7–8 September 2026.

Candidates appearing in the examination should download their admit card, verify the printed exam date, time and centre, and carry the required photo identity proof and other documents mentioned in the admit-card instructions.
