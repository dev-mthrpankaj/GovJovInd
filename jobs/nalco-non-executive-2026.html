Complete context

About This Recruitment

National Aluminium Company Limited (NALCO) has released Advt No. 10260213 for NALCO Non Executive Recruitment 2026 covering a total of 268 vacancies across multiple trades and disciplines. This is a central PSU recruitment that invites ITI, Diploma and Graduation qualified candidates from across India.

The online application window was open from 21 May 2026 to 17 June 2026 (extended). The NALCO Non Executive Admit Card 2026 was released on 15 July 2026, and the Computer Based Test (CBT) is scheduled for 27 July 2026. Selection is based on CBT performance followed by medical fitness verification and final merit list preparation.

Candidates who have applied should download the admit card from the official portal, carry a valid photo ID to the exam centre, and keep checking this permanent page for the answer key, result and later recruitment stages.

What to do at the current stage

How to Download NALCO Non Executive Admit Card

  1. Open the official NALCO / IBPS admit card portal from the Important Links section.
  2. Enter your registration number / roll number and password or date of birth.
  3. Verify your name, photo, exam centre and exam timing on the admit card.
  4. Download and take a clear colour printout of the admit card.
  5. Read the exam day instructions, reporting time and prohibited items list.
  6. Carry the admit card along with a valid original photo ID to the exam centre.

Original Application Process

Preserved for recruitment history and reference after the application stage closed on 17 June 2026.

  1. Visit the official NALCO recruitment portal.
  2. Read Advt No. 10260213 carefully before starting the form.
  3. Open the NALCO Non Executive 2026 application link.
  4. Register using a valid email ID and mobile number.
  5. Login and fill personal, educational and category details.
  6. Upload scanned photo, signature and required documents.
  7. Pay the applicable application fee through online mode.
  8. Submit the form and save the confirmation page for future reference.
