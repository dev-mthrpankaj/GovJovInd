About This Recruitment

National Aluminium Company Limited (NALCO) has released Advt No. 10260213 for NALCO Non Executive Recruitment 2026 covering a total of 268 vacancies across multiple trades and disciplines. This is a central PSU recruitment that invites ITI, Diploma and Graduation qualified candidates from across India.

The online application window was open from 21 May 2026 to 17 June 2026 (extended). The NALCO Non Executive Admit Card 2026 was released on 15 July 2026, and the Computer Based Test (CBT) is scheduled for 27 July 2026. Selection is based on CBT performance followed by medical fitness verification and final merit list preparation.

Candidates who have applied should download the admit card from the official portal, carry a valid photo ID to the exam centre, and keep checking this permanent page for the answer key, result and later recruitment stages.
