Complete context

About This Recruitment

Join Indian Army has released the notification for Technical Graduate Course TGC 145 Entry (January 2027 Batch) for 30 vacancies. This is a permanent commission entry for engineering graduates who wish to serve in the Indian Army as officers.

The online application window will open on 30 September 2026 and close on 29 October 2026. There is no application fee for any category. Candidates must hold a Bachelor's degree in Engineering from a recognised university and meet the prescribed age criteria.

The selection process includes shortlisting, SSB interview, medical examination, and final merit list. This page will be updated with every official stage including admit card, SSB schedule, and final result as and when announced.

Eligibility Criteria

Age Limit

20 to 27 years. Born between 02 January 2000 and 01 January 2007 (both dates inclusive).

Age relaxation applicable as per Indian Army rules for reserved categories.

Education

Bachelor Degree in Engineering (B.E. / B.Tech) from any recognised university in India.

Candidates must have completed the engineering degree by the prescribed date. Final year students may also be eligible as per notification.

Nationality / Other

Indian citizens (male candidates only as per TGC entry). Must meet physical and medical standards prescribed by the Indian Army.

Refer to official notification for detailed conditions.

What to do at the current stage

What Candidates Should Do Now

  1. Read the official TGC 145 notification carefully and verify your eligibility.
  2. Ensure you have a Bachelor's degree in Engineering from a recognised university.
  3. Check the age criteria: born between 02 January 2000 and 01 January 2007 (both dates inclusive).
  4. Keep scanned copies of photograph, signature, and educational documents ready.
  5. Mark the application window: 30 September 2026 to 29 October 2026.
  6. There is no application fee for any category, so no payment is required.

Original Application Process

Preserved for recruitment history and reference after the application stage closes.

  1. Visit the official website of Join Indian Army.
  2. Read the TGC 145 notification carefully.
  3. Click on 'Indian Army Apply Online' link.
  4. Register using valid email ID and mobile number.
  5. Login using registration ID and password.
  6. Fill the application form with personal, educational and category details.
  7. Upload scanned documents (photo, signature, etc.).
  8. No application fee is applicable.
  9. Submit the form and download/print the confirmation page.
