About This Recruitment

Madhya Pradesh Employees Selection Board (MPESB) has released the Group 3 Sub Engineer Recruitment 2026 notification for 1700 posts including Sub Engineer, Sahayak Manchitrakar, Technician and other equivalent posts. The online application window was initially open from 29 August 2026 to 12 September 2026.

The Board has now issued a re-open notice for the online application form. Eligible candidates who missed the initial window can apply online from 13 October 2026 to 27 October 2026. The fee payment last date is 27 October 2026 and the form correction window will remain open until 28 October 2026.

The examination is scheduled for December 2026 or January 2027 as per the official re-open and exam date notice. Candidates are advised to complete the application process within the re-open window and keep all required documents ready well in advance.
