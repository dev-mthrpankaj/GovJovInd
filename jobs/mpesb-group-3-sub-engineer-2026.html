Complete context

About This Recruitment

MPESB Group 3 Sub Engineer & Other Post Recruitment 2026 is a combined recruitment drive conducted by the Madhya Pradesh Employees Selection Board (MPESB) to fill 1,700 posts of Group-3 Sub Engineer, Sahayak Manchitrakar, Technician and other equivalent posts across various departments of the Madhya Pradesh Government.

The official notification was released in August 2026, and the online application window was initially open from 29 August 2026 to 12 September 2026. MPESB has now re-opened the online application window from 13 October 2026 to 27 October 2026, giving eligible candidates another opportunity to apply. The last date for fee payment is 27 October 2026, and the form correction window will close on 28 October 2026.

The written examination is scheduled for December 2026 or January 2027. The exam will be conducted at centres in Bhopal, Indore, Jabalpur, Khandwa, Neemuch, Ratlam, Rewa, Sagar, Satna, Sidhi and Ujjain. The exact exam date and admit card release date will be announced by MPESB on the official portal.

Basic eligibility includes ITI, Diploma or Degree in the relevant trade or discipline depending on the post applied for. The age limit is 18 to 40 years as on 01 January 2026, with relaxation for reserved categories as per MP Government rules. Candidates are advised to read the official notification carefully before applying.

What to do at the current stage

How to Apply Online (Re-Open)

  1. Visit the official MPESB portal at esb.mp.gov.in or the MP Online portal.
  2. Complete MP Profile Registration if not already done.
  3. Click on the MPESB Group 3 Sub Engineer 2026 Apply Online link.
  4. Register using a valid email ID and mobile number.
  5. Fill the application form with personal, educational and category details.
  6. Upload scanned photo, signature and required documents.
  7. Pay the application fee through online mode or MP Kiosk.
  8. Submit the form and download the confirmation page for future reference.
