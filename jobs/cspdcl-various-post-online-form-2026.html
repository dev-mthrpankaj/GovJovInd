Complete context

About This Recruitment

Chhattisgarh State Power Transmission Company Limited (CSPDCL) has published Advertisement No. 01-01/HR/Recruitment/739106 for CSPDCL Various Post Recruitment 2026 to fill 1501 regular vacancies in Raipur, Chhattisgarh. The recruitment covers officer, engineering, technical, clerical, and paramedical posts including Assistant Engineer, Junior Engineer, Programmer, Accounts Officer, Medical Officer, Stenographer Grade III, Data Entry Operator, Assistant Lab Technician, and Attendant posts.

The online application window opened on 06 November 2026 and remains active until 26 November 2026. Candidates must also complete the online application fee payment on or before 26 November 2026. Out of 1501 total posts, 637 are for General, 192 for OBC, 173 for SC, and 499 for ST categories.

Eligibility varies by post and includes four-year B.E./B.Tech./B.Sc. (Engineering)/AMIE/PTDC degrees, Fire Engineering degrees, Computer Science/IT degrees or BCA, Graduate degrees with relevant service experience, and 10+2 with DMLT/BMLT and Chhattisgarh Paramedical Council registration. The prescribed age limit is 18 to 40 years as on 01 January 2026, with category-wise relaxation as per official rules.
