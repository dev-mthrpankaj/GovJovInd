Complete context

About This Recruitment

The Railway Recruitment Boards (RRB) issued Advertisement No. CEN 09/2025 to recruit candidates for 22,195 Group-D (Level-1) posts across various railway zones in India. These posts include Track Maintainer Gr. IV, Pointsman-B, and Assistant positions in electrical, mechanical, and engineering departments.

The online application window was open from 31 January 2026 to 09 March 2026. Following the application screening and correction window, the Computer Based Test (CBT-I) was conducted in multiple shifts on 03, 06, 09, 17, 21, and 25 August 2026.

The CBT-I Result and Cut-off marks were officially declared by all regional RRBs on 12 November 2026. Candidates who appeared for the written examination can now access their individual Score Cards and marks as of 16 November 2026 through the official RRB login portal.

Shortlisted candidates will proceed to the Physical Efficiency Test (PET), which evaluates candidates' physical fitness for the demanding roles in Level-1. The dates for the PET will be announced shortly by the respective regional boards.
