Complete context

About This Recruitment

RRB Group D Recruitment 2026 (CEN 09/2025) is a Railway Recruitment Board drive to fill 22,195 Level-1 posts across 16 railway zones in Indian Railways. The official notification was published on 30 January 2026, and the online application window remained open from 31 January 2026 to 09 March 2026 (extended from 02 March 2026).

The Computer Based Test (CBT) was conducted from 03 to 25 August 2026 across multiple shifts. The provisional answer key and response sheet were released on 07 September 2026, and the objection window remained open until 15 September 2026. The CBT result with score card was declared on 12 November 2026.

Basic eligibility is 10th pass (Matriculation) or ITI/NAC from a recognised institution. The age limit is 18 to 33 years as on 01 January 2026, with relaxation for OBC-NCL (3 years), SC/ST (5 years), and other reserved categories as per RRB rules. The application fee is Rs.500 for General/OBC/EWS (Rs.400 refunded after appearing in CBT) and Rs.250 for SC/ST/PwBD/Female/Ex-Servicemen (fully refunded after appearing in CBT).

The selection process includes Computer Based Test (CBT), Physical Efficiency Test (PET), Document Verification, and Medical Examination. Shortlisted candidates will be called for the PET round, and the schedule will be announced on the official RRB website.

What to do at the current stage

How to Download RRB Group D Score Card 2026

  1. Open the official RRB score card portal from Important Links.
  2. Log in using your Registration Number and Date of Birth (or password).
  3. Download the score card PDF showing your section-wise and total marks.
  4. Check the zone-wise result on the RRB Indian Railways website.
  5. Compare your score with the expected cut-off for your category and zone.
  6. Save a copy of the score card for the upcoming PET and document verification stages.
  7. Keep checking the official portal for PET schedule and shortlist updates.

Original Application Process

Preserved for recruitment history and reference after the application stage closes.

  1. Visit the official RRB application portal at rrbapply.gov.in.
  2. Read the CEN 09/2025 notification carefully.
  3. Register on the RRB portal with valid email ID and mobile number.
  4. Fill the online application form with accurate personal and educational details.
  5. Upload photograph, signature and required documents.
  6. Pay the applicable examination fee online.
  7. Submit the form and save the confirmation page for future reference.
