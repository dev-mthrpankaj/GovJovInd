About This Recruitment

Railway Recruitment Board (RRB) Group D CEN 09/2025 is a Level 1 recruitment drive for 22,195 posts. Online applications were accepted from 31 January 2026 to 9 March 2026, and the CBT was scheduled from 3 August to 25 August 2026.

The answer key stage is the latest update on this permanent recruitment page. Candidates can use the official candidate login to access the answer key and related examination information. The same page will continue to carry later result, PET, document verification and final-selection updates when officially available.

Eligibility shown in the supplied recruitment information is High School/Matriculation from a recognized board. The recruitment also includes prescribed physical efficiency standards for male and female candidates; candidates should verify complete conditions in the official CEN 09/2025 notification.
