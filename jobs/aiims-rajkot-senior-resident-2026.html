Complete context

About This Recruitment

AIIMS Rajkot invited online applications for 64 Senior Resident (Non-Academic) posts across 25 departments. The advertisement was released on 17 September 2026 with advertisement number AIIMS/RJT/AS/SR/R-3/2026/3194. The application window remained open until 03 October 2026 at 11:00 AM.

The selection process for this recruitment depended on the number of applicants per department. For departments with more than six applicants, a written screening test of 40 MCQs (80 marks, 40 minutes, 0.50 negative marking) was to be conducted, carrying 80% weightage. The interview carried 20% weightage. For departments with six or fewer applicants, selection was based solely on the interview.

The walk-in interview was scheduled for 08 October 2026 from 09:00 AM onwards. Candidates were required to bring the printed online application form, original documents, and one set of self-attested photocopies for verification. The interview has now been concluded, and the final result and merit list are awaited.

Selected candidates will receive a consolidated monthly remuneration of Rs 1,29,984. The appointment is for six months or until a regular incumbent joins, whichever is earlier. The post is full-time, private practice is prohibited, and candidates may be posted at any place within the Institute.
