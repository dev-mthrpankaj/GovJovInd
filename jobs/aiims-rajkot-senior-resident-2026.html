About This Recruitment

All India Institute of Medical Sciences (AIIMS) Rajkot has released the notification for Senior Resident recruitment 2026. The online application process is active for 64 posts across various medical departments.

Eligible candidates with MD/MS/DNB/MCh qualifications in relevant specialities can apply online from 17 September 2026 to 3 October 2026. The interview is scheduled for 8 October 2026.

This is a permanent recruitment lifecycle page. All future updates including interview call letters, results, and document verification notices will be published on this same URL. Candidates should bookmark this page for regular updates.

Eligibility Criteria

Age Limit

Maximum 45 years as on the prescribed date.

Age relaxation applicable for SC/ST/OBC/PwBD and other categories as per AIIMS rules.

Education

MD/MS/DNB/MCh in the relevant medical speciality as specified for each post.

Qualification must be recognized by MCI/NMC. Refer to the official notification for post-wise detailed qualifications.

Nationality / Other

Indian citizens fulfilling the post-specific eligibility conditions may apply.

Refer to official notification for detailed conditions.
