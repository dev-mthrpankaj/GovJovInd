About This Recruitment

UP TGT Teacher Recruitment 2022 was advertised under Advertisement No. 01/2022 for 3,539 Trained Graduate Teacher posts. The recruitment has progressed through examination, result, document verification and final-result stages.

The latest available stage is College / Institute Allotment Result 2026. Subject- and category-wise official allotment PDFs are available, while final and revised final result links remain preserved on this permanent page.

Candidates should check the allotment PDF relevant to their subject/category and preserve the result, DV letter and allotment document for any further official instructions.
