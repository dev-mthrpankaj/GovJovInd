About This Recruitment

NCERT Non Academic Recruitment 2025, Advertisement No. 01/2025/Non-Academic, covers 173 Group A, B and C non-academic vacancies across different pay levels.

Applications were accepted from 27 December 2025 to 16 January 2026. The recruitment subsequently moved through Phase I and Phase II examinations, LDC examination, admit-card and answer-key stages.

The latest supplied update is the NCERT Non Academic LDC Result 2026. Candidates who appeared in the LDC examination held on 15 May 2026 can use the official result PDF preserved in Important Links.
