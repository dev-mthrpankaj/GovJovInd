Complete context

About This Recruitment

Bihar Public Service Commission (BPSC) has released the official notification for the recruitment of 300 Assistant Prosecution Officer (APO) posts under Advertisement No. 13/2026. The recruitment is conducted under the Home Department (Prosecution Directorate), Government of Bihar. The online application window was open from 27 February 2026 to 20 March 2026.

The Preliminary Examination was conducted on 15 July 2026. The provisional answer key was released on 12 August 2026, and the Final Answer Key was released on 13 October 2026. The Main Examination and Interview will follow as subsequent stages of the selection process.

Eligibility for the APO post requires a Bachelor's Degree in Law (LLB – 3 Year or 5 Year integrated course) from any recognised university in India. The age limit is 21 to 37 years for Unreserved Male candidates as on 01 August 2026, with age relaxation applicable for reserved categories as per Bihar government rules.

Eligibility Criteria

Age Limit

21 to 37 years for Unreserved Male candidates as on 01 August 2026.

Upper age limit is 40 years for Unreserved Female, BC and EBC candidates. Upper age limit is 42 years for SC/ST candidates. Age relaxation applicable as per Bihar government rules.

Education

Bachelor's Degree in Law (LLB – 3 Year or 5 Year integrated course) from any recognised university in India.

Both 3-year and 5-year integrated law courses are accepted.

Nationality / Other

Candidates must be Indian citizens and fulfil all conditions mentioned in the official notification. Refer to the official notification for complete details.

What to do at the current stage

How to Download Final Answer Key

  1. Visit the official BPSC website at bpsc.bihar.gov.in.
  2. Navigate to the 'Notices' or 'Latest Updates' section.
  3. Find the link for 'Final Answer Key – Advt. No. 13/2026 – Prosecution Officer'.
  4. Click on the General Studies and Law answer key links separately.
  5. Download the PDF files and save them for future reference.
  6. Compare your answers and calculate your expected score.

Original Application Process

Preserved for recruitment history and reference after the application stage closes.

  1. Visit the official BPSC website at bpsc.bihar.gov.in.
  2. Read the official notification (Advt. No. 13/2026) carefully.
  3. Complete One-Time Registration (OTR) if you are a new user.
  4. Fill the online application form with accurate personal and educational details.
  5. Upload photograph, signature and required documents.
  6. Pay the applicable examination fee online.
