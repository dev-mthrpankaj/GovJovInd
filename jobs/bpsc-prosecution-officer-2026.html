Complete context

About This Recruitment

Bihar Public Service Commission (BPSC) has released the official notification for the recruitment of 300 Assistant Prosecution Officer (APO) posts under Advertisement No. 13/2026. The recruitment is conducted under the Home Department (Prosecution Directorate), Government of Bihar. The online application window was open from 27 February 2026 to 20 March 2026.

The Preliminary Examination was conducted on 15 July 2026. The provisional answer key was released on 12 August 2026, and the Final Answer Key was released on 13 October 2026. The Main Examination and Interview will follow as subsequent stages of the selection process.

Eligibility for the APO post requires a Bachelor's Degree in Law (LLB – 3 Year or 5 Year integrated course) from any recognised university in India. The age limit is 21 to 37 years for Unreserved Male candidates as on 01 August 2026, with age relaxation applicable for reserved categories as per Bihar government rules.
