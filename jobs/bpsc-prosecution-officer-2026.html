Complete context

About This Recruitment

Bihar Public Service Commission (BPSC) is conducting the Prosecution Officer Recruitment 2026 under Advertisement No. 13/2026 to fill 300 Prosecution Officer posts in the Home Department (Prosecution Directorate), Government of Bihar. The official notification was released on 25 February 2026, and online applications were accepted from 27 February 2026 to 20 March 2026.

The Preliminary Competitive Examination (General Studies and Law) was conducted on 15 July 2026 after the release of e-Admit Cards on 09 July 2026. Provisional answer keys and the objection notice were issued on 12 August 2026, followed by the final answer keys on 12 October 2026.

On 19 November 2026, Bihar Public Service Commission (BPSC) declared the Preliminary Examination Result in roll-number-wise PDF format. Out of 23,218 candidates who appeared in the examination, 3,175 candidates have been shortlisted for the descriptive Main (Written) Examination, which will be followed by the Interview and Document Verification.

Eligible candidates required a Bachelor Degree in Law (LLB or BA LLB) from a recognised university in India, with an age limit of 21 to 37 years for Unreserved Male candidates and 21 to 40 years for Women candidates as on 01 August 2026. Selected officers are appointed in Pay Level 9 (Rs.53,100 to Rs.1,67,800).

What to do at the current stage

How to Check BPSC Prosecution Officer Result 2026

  1. Open the official Preliminary Result PDF link from Important Links or visit bpsc.bihar.gov.in.
  2. Locate the notice titled Results: Prosecution Officer Preliminary Examination (Advt. No. 13/2026).
  3. Download the roll-number-wise merit list PDF to your device.
  4. Use Ctrl+F (or PDF search on mobile) to search your Roll Number among the 3,175 qualified candidates.
  5. Save and print a copy of the result PDF for future reference during Mains registration.
  6. Log in to bpsconline.bihar.gov.in when individual marks are made available in candidate dashboards and prepare for the Main (Written) Examination.

Original Application Process

Preserved for recruitment history and reference after the application stage closes.

  1. Visit the official BPSC online application portal at bpsconline.bihar.gov.in.
  2. Read the official Advt. No. 13/2026 notification carefully before registration.
  3. Complete one-time registration and fill personal, address and educational details.
  4. Upload scanned photograph, signature and required eligibility documents.
  5. Pay the Rs.100 application fee through online payment mode.
  6. Preview all entered fields carefully, submit the form and save the final printout.

Frequently Asked Questions

Yes, the Bihar Public Service Commission (BPSC) declared the Prosecution Officer Preliminary Examination Result 2026 on 19 November 2026 for Advt. No. 13/2026. The roll-number-wise result PDF is available on bpsc.bihar.gov.in.

Out of 23,218 candidates who appeared in the Preliminary Examination on 15 July 2026, a total of 3,175 candidates have been declared qualified for the Main (Written) Examination based on combined General Studies and Law marks.

Visit bpsc.bihar.gov.in or click the Preliminary Result PDF link in Important Links on this page. Open the roll-number-wise PDF for Advt. No. 13/2026 and press Ctrl+F to search your roll number and verify your qualifying status.

Candidates who qualified in the Preliminary Examination will appear for the descriptive Main (Written) Examination, followed by the Interview and Document Verification. BPSC will publish the Mains form and exam dates separately.

The Bihar Public Service Commission (BPSC) conducted the Prosecution Officer Preliminary Examination on 15 July 2026. Admit cards were released on 09 July 2026, provisional answer keys on 12 August 2026, and final keys on 12 October 2026.

A total of 300 Prosecution Officer vacancies were advertised under Advt. No. 13/2026. Category-wise posts include 129 UR, 29 EWS, 42 SC, 7 ST, 64 EBC, 25 BC, and 4 BC Female vacancies under the Home Department, Government of Bihar.

Candidates must possess a Bachelor Degree in Law (LLB or BA LLB) from a recognised university in India. Complete academic and eligibility conditions are specified in the official BPSC Advt. No. 13/2026 notification.

The minimum age is 21 years and the maximum age is 37 years for Unreserved Male candidates and 40 years for Women candidates as on 01 August 2026. Reserved categories receive upper age relaxation as per Bihar Government and BPSC rules.

The application fee for BPSC Prosecution Officer Recruitment 2026 (Advt. No. 13/2026) was Rs.100 for General, OBC, EWS, SC, ST, and PH (Divyang) candidates, paid online. The application window closed on 20 March 2026.

Selected Prosecution Officers under the Home Department (Prosecution Directorate), Government of Bihar are placed in Pay Level 9 of the 7th Pay Commission matrix, with a pay scale of Rs.53,100 to Rs.1,67,800 per month plus admissible allowances.

The selection process consists of a Preliminary Examination (screening test), followed by a Main (Written) Examination, Interview, and Document Verification. Final merit is determined on the basis of Main Examination and Interview marks.

The official website of the Bihar Public Service Commission is https://bpsc.bihar.gov.in/ for notices, answer keys, and results, and https://bpsconline.bihar.gov.in/candidate/login for the candidate login dashboard.
