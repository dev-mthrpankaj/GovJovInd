Complete context

About This Recruitment

BPSC 71st Combined Competitive Examination 2025 is a Bihar Public Service Commission recruitment drive to fill 1,298 vacancies across various departments of the Bihar government. The official notification was released on 30 May 2025, and the online application window remained open from 02 June 2025 to 30 June 2025.

The Preliminary Examination was held on 13 September 2025, and the Prelims result was declared on 18 November 2025. Candidates who qualified then appeared for the Mains Examination, which was conducted from 25 April 2026 to 30 April 2026. The Mains result has now been declared on 06 November 2026.

A total of 3,392 candidates have qualified in the Mains examination and will now proceed to the interview round. The final merit list will be prepared after the interview stage. The recruitment offers posts such as Senior Deputy Collector, Deputy Superintendent of Police, Financial Administrative Officer, Block Cooperative Officer, Revenue Officer and various other Bihar government positions.

Basic eligibility requires a Bachelor’s degree from a recognised university. The age limit is 21 to 37 years for General Male candidates as on 01 August 2025, with upper age relaxation up to 40 years for General Female, BC and EBC candidates, and up to 42 years for SC/ST candidates.

What to do at the current stage

How to Check BPSC 71st Mains Result

  1. Visit the official BPSC website at bpsc.bihar.gov.in.
  2. Look for the link titled 'BPSC 71st Mains Result 2026' on the homepage.
  3. Click the link to open the result PDF.
  4. Search for your roll number using Ctrl+F in the PDF.
  5. Download and save the result PDF for future reference.
  6. Qualified candidates should prepare for the interview round; details will be announced soon.

Original Application Process

Preserved for recruitment history and reference after the application stage closes.

  1. Visit the official BPSC website at bpsc.bihar.gov.in.
  2. Read the official notification for BPSC 71st CCE 2025.
  3. Register on the BPSC online portal with valid email ID and mobile number.
  4. Fill the online application form with accurate personal and educational details.
  5. Upload photograph, signature and required documents.
  6. Pay the applicable examination fee and biometric fee online.
  7. Submit the form and save the confirmation page for future reference.
