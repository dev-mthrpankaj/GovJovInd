Complete context

About This Recruitment

Bank of Baroda (BOB) has announced a recruitment drive for Specialist Officer (Various Post) positions. The official notification (Adv No. BOB/HRM/REC/ADVT/2026/15) was released to fill a total of 206 vacancies across multiple specialist roles including Digital Product, Civil Engineering, Electrical Engineering, Information Security, Data Science, Taxation, Accounts, and more.

The online application process was open from 06 August 2026 to 26 August 2026. The Bank has now released the Admit Card for the online examination. The exam is scheduled to be held on 01 November 2026. Candidates who have applied successfully can download their admit cards from the official IBPS portal.

Selection will be through a multi-stage process including an Online Test, followed by a Personal Interview. The final merit list will be prepared based on performance in these stages. Candidates are advised to regularly check this page for updates on the result, cut-off, and interview schedule.

Eligibility requirements vary by post. For most technical positions, a B.E./B.Tech degree in the relevant discipline is required. For other positions, a Bachelor's degree in any stream or professional qualifications like CA/ICWA are needed. The age limit is post-wise and will be calculated as on 01 August 2026.
