Complete context

About This Recruitment

SKAU Non Teaching Post Recruitment 2026 is a direct recruitment drive by Shri Krishna Ayush University (SKAU), Kurukshetra, Haryana, to fill 60 non-teaching posts across three categories — Staff Nurse (25 posts under Advt. No. NT-01/2026), Ayurvedic Pharmacist (06 posts under Advt. No. NT-02/2026), and Clerk (29 posts under Advt. No. NT-03/2026). The official recruitment notice was issued in July 2026, and the online application window remained open from 30 July 2026 to 21 August 2026 (up to 04:00 PM).

The selection process for SKAU Non Teaching posts includes a Computer-Based Test / Written Test followed by a Typing Test for Clerk posts, Document Verification, and a Medical Examination. For Clerk posts, candidates must qualify a data entry test at a speed of 30 words per minute along with computer skills. The Phase 1 Computer-Based Typing Test for the Clerk post is scheduled from 10 October 2026 to 14 October 2026, and the admit card for the same was released on 08 October 2026.

Candidates who applied for Staff Nurse and Ayurvedic Pharmacist posts should await further official schedule announcements for their respective examination dates. The university will release separate exam date notices for these posts in due course.

Basic eligibility varies by post. Staff Nurse requires Matriculation with an A-grade Nursing qualification registered with the Haryana Nurses Registration Council. Ayurvedic Pharmacist requires Up-Vaidya or a Diploma in Ayurvedic Pharmacy. Clerk requires a Bachelor's degree from a recognised university with Hindi or Sanskrit at the Matric level. The age limit for all posts is 18 to 42 years as on the last date of application, with relaxation for reserved categories as per Haryana Government rules.
