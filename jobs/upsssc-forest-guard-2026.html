About This Recruitment

UPSSSC Forest Guard Recruitment 2026 is a Uttar Pradesh Subordinate Services Selection Commission recruitment under Advertisement No. 12-Exam/2026 for 708 Forest Guard posts.

Applications were accepted from 30 June to 20 July 2026, with correction listed up to 27 July 2026. The latest supplied update is the Mains Examination Fee Payment notice dated 8 September 2026, following the eligibility result and cutoff stage.

Candidates shortlisted for the mains stage should use the official UPSSSC fee-payment link and notice. This permanent page will continue to carry the mains exam date, admit card, physical stages, result and later recruitment updates on the same URL.
