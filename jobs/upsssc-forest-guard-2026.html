About This Recruitment

UPSSSC Forest Guard Recruitment 2026 is a Uttar Pradesh Subordinate Services Selection Commission (UPSSSC) recruitment drive for 708 Forest Guard and Wildlife Guard posts. The official notification was released on 23 April 2026, and the online application window was open from 30 June 2026 to 20 July 2026.

Only candidates who appeared for UPSSSC PET 2025 and possess a valid PET scorecard were eligible to apply. The selection journey includes shortlisting based on PET 2025 normalized scores, a Main Written Examination of 100 marks, Physical Standard Test (PST), Physical Efficiency Test (PET), Document Verification and Medical Examination.

The Main Written Examination is scheduled for 27 September 2026 (10:00 AM to 12:00 PM). The Mains Admit Card has been released on 23 September 2026. Candidates can download their hall ticket from the official UPSSSC portal using their registration details.
