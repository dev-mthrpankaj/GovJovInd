Complete context

About This Recruitment

UPSSSC Forest Guard Recruitment 2026 is conducted by the Uttar Pradesh Subordinate Services Selection Commission (UPSSSC) for 708 Forest Guard posts under Advertisement No. 12-Exam/2026. The online application window ran from 30 June 2026 to 20 July 2026, and the Mains examination is scheduled for 27 September 2026.

The recruitment uses the UPSSSC PET 2025 score card as a mandatory eligibility requirement, along with 10+2 Intermediate qualification and prescribed physical standards. Category-wise reservations apply for General, OBC, EWS, SC and ST candidates, with additional physical eligibility criteria for male and female applicants.

Eligible candidates who cleared the initial stage can now check the Mains Exam City Details and complete the Mains exam fee payment. Admit cards and further stage updates will be published on this same permanent page as soon as UPSSSC releases them.
