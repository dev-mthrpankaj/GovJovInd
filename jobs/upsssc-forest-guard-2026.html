About This Recruitment

UPSSSC Forest Guard Recruitment 2026 is a major state-level recruitment drive conducted by the Uttar Pradesh Subordinate Services Selection Commission (UPSSSC) to fill 708 posts of Forest Guard (Van Rakshak) and Wildlife Guard (Vanya Jeev Rakshak).

The official notification (Advt No. 12-Exam/2026) was released on 23 April 2026, and online applications were accepted from 30 June 2026 to 20 July 2026. The written examination was held on 27 September 2026, and the provisional answer key has now been released on 29 September 2026.

Candidates who appeared in the exam can download their response sheet, calculate their estimated score, and raise objections against any incorrect answer within the official objection window.
