About This Recruitment

Join Indian Navy Agniveer SSR & MR 01/2027 & 02/2027 Batch INET 2026 recruitment is a major opportunity for candidates aspiring to serve in the Indian Navy. The recruitment is for Agniveer SSR (Senior Secondary Recruit) and Agniveer MR (Matric Recruit) entries. The online application process was conducted from 14 March 2026 to 06 April 2026.

The Indian Navy Entrance Test (INET) 2026 was held from 13 to 15 May 2026. The result for the same has now been declared on 17 September 2026. This page will continue to provide all subsequent updates, including details for the next stage (II Stage Admit Card) and final merit list, on this same permanent URL.

Candidates who appeared for the INET exam can now check their result using the official link provided in the Important Links section. It is advisable to download and save the result page for future reference.
