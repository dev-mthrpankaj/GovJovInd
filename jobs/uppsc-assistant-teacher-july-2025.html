Complete context

About This Recruitment

UPPSC Assistant Teacher LT Grade Recruitment 2025 is a Uttar Pradesh Public Service Commission drive to fill 7,466 Assistant Teacher (Trained Graduate Category) posts across various subjects in Uttar Pradesh. The official notification (Advt No. A-5/E-1/2025) was released in July 2025, and the online application window remained open from 28 July 2025 to 28 August 2025.

The selection process includes a Preliminary Examination (Phase-I), a Mains Examination (Phase-II, Computer Based), and Document Verification. The Phase-I exam was held on 06-07 and 21 December 2025, and the Mains examination was conducted on 16 August 2026. Subject-wise Mains results have been declared progressively, with the Mathematics result released on 28 October 2026.

Basic eligibility is a Bachelor's Degree in the relevant subject from a recognised university in India with B.Ed. The age limit is 21 to 40 years as on 01 July 2025, with relaxation for reserved categories as per UPPSC rules. The application fee is Rs. 125 for General/OBC/EWS, Rs. 65 for SC/ST, and Rs. 25 for PH candidates.
