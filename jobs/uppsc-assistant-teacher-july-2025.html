Complete context

About This Recruitment

UPPSC Assistant Teacher (LT Grade) Examination 2025 is a Uttar Pradesh Public Service Commission recruitment drive to fill 7,466 Assistant Teacher (Trained Graduate Grade) posts in government secondary schools across Uttar Pradesh. The official notification (Advt. No. A-5/E-1/2025) was released in July 2025, and the online application window remained open from 28 July 2025 to 28 August 2025.

For the first time, the recruitment introduced a two-tier examination system with Prelims and Mains. The Phase I (Prelims) exam was conducted on 06-07 and 21 December 2025. The Phase II (Mains) exam was held from 16 May to 16 August 2026 for various subjects. The final result for Sanskrit, Commerce, Agriculture/Horticulture was declared on 07 October 2026, and results for other subjects are being released in phases.

The selection process includes Prelims (objective), Mains (written), and Document Verification. The pay scale for the post is Rs. 9,300–34,800 with Grade Pay of Rs. 4,800 (Level 8) plus DA and HRA as per rules.

Basic eligibility is a Bachelor's degree in the relevant subject from a recognised university plus a B.Ed. or equivalent NCTE-recognised teaching qualification. The age limit is 21 to 40 years as on 01 July 2025, with relaxation for reserved categories as per UPPSC rules.
