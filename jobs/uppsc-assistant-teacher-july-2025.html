Complete context

About This Recruitment

UPPSC Assistant Teacher (LT Grade) Examination 2025 is a Uttar Pradesh Public Service Commission recruitment drive to fill 7,466 Assistant Teacher (Trained Graduate) posts in government secondary schools across Uttar Pradesh. The official notification (Advt. No. A-5/E-1/2025) was released in July 2025, and the online application window remained open from 28 July 2025 to 28 August 2025.

The preliminary examination was conducted in two phases: 06–07 and 21 December 2025, and 17–25 January 2026. The mains examination was held on 23 April 2026 for Sanskrit and on 16–17 May 2026 for other subjects. The mains result for Sanskrit, Science, Home Science, Commerce, and other subjects was declared between February and September 2026. The document verification schedule was released on 19 September 2026.

Final results are being published subject by subject. The Sanskrit final result was declared on 07 October 2026, with 182 candidates selected (90 male, 92 female). The Home Science final result has also been released. Final results for other subjects including Commerce, Agriculture/Horticulture, Social Science, and others are expected soon.

Basic eligibility is a Bachelor's degree in the relevant subject with B.Ed. from a recognised university. The age limit is 21 to 40 years as on 01 July 2025, with relaxation for reserved categories as per UPPSC rules.
