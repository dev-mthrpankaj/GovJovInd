About This Recruitment

Uttar Pradesh Public Service Commission (UPPSC) Assistant Teacher LT Grade Recruitment 2025, Advt. No. A-5/E-1/2025, covers 7,466 Assistant Teacher posts.

The application window ran from 28 July to 28 August 2025. The basic eligibility listed in the supplied recruitment information is a Bachelor Degree in the related subject with B.Ed, with subject-wise conditions to be checked in the notification.

The recruitment has progressed to subject-wise results. The latest supplied update is the LT Grade Mains Result 2026 for Commerce and Agriculture / Horticulture, while earlier Pre results, Social Science Mains result, admit-card and exam-notice history remain available on this permanent lifecycle page.
