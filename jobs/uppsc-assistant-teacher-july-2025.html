Complete context

About This Recruitment

Uttar Pradesh Public Service Commission (UPPSC) is conducting the Assistant Teacher (Trained Graduate / LT Grade) Examination 2025 under Advertisement No. A-5/E-1/2025 to fill 7,466 teaching vacancies across Uttar Pradesh. Out of the total 7,466 posts, 4,860 vacancies belong to the Male Branch, 2,525 belong to the Female Branch, and 81 posts are under the Department of Empowerment of Persons with Disabilities.

The official notification was released on 28 July 2025, and online applications were accepted via UPPSC OTR until 28 August 2025, with correction and fee reconciliation open until 04 September 2025. The Preliminary Written Examination was conducted in phases starting 06, 07, and 21 December 2025, followed by subject-wise Mains examinations across 2026 including Computer on 16 August 2026.

On 18 November 2026, UPPSC declared the Assistant Teacher (LT Grade) Mains Result 2026 for Music and Mathematics. Earlier, UPPSC released Mains results for Science, Commerce, Agriculture/Horticulture, and Social Science, as well as Final Results for Home Science and Sanskrit. Shortlisted candidates will now appear for Document Verification as per the official UPPSC schedule.

Basic eligibility requires a Bachelor Degree in the relevant subject from a recognised university in India with B.Ed or the prescribed subject-specific training qualification, and an age limit of 21 to 40 years as on 01 July 2025. Selected candidates are appointed in Pay Matrix Level-7 (Rs.44,900 to Rs.1,42,400, Grade Pay Rs.4,600).

Eligibility Criteria

Age Limit

21 to 40 years as on 01 July 2025 (candidates must have been born between 02 July 1985 and 01 July 2004).

Upper age relaxation is applicable for SC, ST, OBC, PwD, and Ex-Servicemen candidates of Uttar Pradesh as per UPPSC rules.

Education

Bachelor Degree in the relevant teaching subject from a recognised university in India along with a B.Ed degree or equivalent prescribed training qualification.

Computer, Physical Education, Art, and Special Education posts carry subject-specific technical or RCI qualifications as specified in Advt. No. A-5/E-1/2025.

Nationality / Other

Candidates must be citizens of India and must have possessed all required educational qualifications on or before the application closing date of 28 August 2025.

Refer to official notification for detailed conditions.

What to do at the current stage

How to Check UPPSC LT Grade Mains Result 2026

  1. Open the Music or Mathematics Mains Result PDF link from the Important Links section below.
  2. Wait for the official UPPSC provisional selection list PDF to load in your browser.
  3. Press Ctrl+F (or use Find in Page on mobile) and enter your Roll Number.
  4. Verify your Roll Number, category, and branch (Male or Female) carefully in the merit list.
  5. Save and print a copy of the result PDF for upcoming Document Verification.
  6. Keep original academic degrees, B.Ed certificates, and category documents ready as per UPPSC notice.

Original Application Process

Preserved for recruitment history and reference after the application stage closes.

  1. Visited the official UPPSC portal at uppsc.up.nic.in and completed One Time Registration (OTR).
  2. Opened Advertisement No. A-5/E-1/2025 under All Notifications / Advertisements.
  3. Logged in with OTR credentials and filled personal, subject, and educational details.
  4. Uploaded scanned photograph, signature, and required supporting documents.
  5. Paid the category-wise examination fee online (Rs.125 for Gen/OBC/EWS, Rs.65 for SC/ST, Rs.25 for PH).
  6. Verified all entries in the preview and submitted the form before 28 August 2025.
  7. Saved and printed the final submitted application form for future stages.

Frequently Asked Questions

Yes, the Uttar Pradesh Public Service Commission (UPPSC) declared the Assistant Teacher (LT Grade) Mains Result 2026 for Music and Mathematics on 18 November 2026. Candidates can download both official result PDFs directly from the Important Links section.

Open the official Mathematics or Music Mains Result PDF link from Important Links or visit uppsc.up.nic.in. Use Ctrl+F or Find in Page to search your Roll Number in the provisional selection list and save a copy for Document Verification.

UPPSC has declared Mains Results for Music, Mathematics, Science, Commerce, Agriculture/Horticulture, and Social Science, along with Final Results for Home Science and Sanskrit under Advertisement No. A-5/E-1/2025.

A total of 7,466 Assistant Teacher (LT Grade) vacancies were advertised under Advt. No. A-5/E-1/2025. This includes 4,860 posts in the Male Branch, 2,525 posts in the Female Branch, and 81 posts under the Empowerment of Persons with Disabilities Department.

Candidates provisionally selected in the UPPSC Assistant Teacher (LT Grade) Mains Result 2026 will be called for Document Verification (DV). UPPSC will publish the subject-wise DV schedule and call letters separately on uppsc.up.nic.in.

Candidates required a Bachelor Degree in the relevant subject from a recognised Indian university along with a B.Ed degree or equivalent training qualification. Computer and Special Education posts carried specific technical or RCI qualifications.

The age limit was 21 to 40 years as on 01 July 2025, meaning candidates had to be born between 02 July 1985 and 01 July 2004. Upper age relaxation applied for SC, ST, OBC, PwD, and Ex-Servicemen of Uttar Pradesh as per UPPSC rules.

Selected UPPSC Assistant Teachers (Trained Graduate / LT Grade) are placed in Pay Matrix Level-7 with a pay scale of Rs.44,900 to Rs.1,42,400 and Grade Pay Rs.4,600, plus Dearness Allowance and other Uttar Pradesh Government allowances.

The online application fee was Rs.125 for General, OBC, and EWS candidates, Rs.65 for SC and ST candidates, and Rs.25 for PH/PwD candidates. The fee was paid online via Net Banking, Debit Card, Credit Card, or UPI during the application window.

The UPPSC Assistant Teacher (LT Grade) selection process consists of four stages: Preliminary Written Examination, Mains Written Examination, Document Verification, and Final Merit List. Final selection is based on Mains exam performance and DV.

Shortlisted candidates must carry the printed Mains Result PDF, UPPSC OTR application form, valid photo ID, 10th and 12th certificates, Graduation degree and marksheets, B.Ed or prescribed training certificate, and UP domicile or category proofs.

The only official website for UPPSC Assistant Teacher (LT Grade) Recruitment 2025 updates, subject-wise Mains results, DV notices, and final merit lists is https://uppsc.up.nic.in/. Candidates should verify all notices directly on the UPPSC portal.
