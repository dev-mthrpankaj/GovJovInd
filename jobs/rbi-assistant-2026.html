About This Recruitment

Reserve Bank of India (RBI) has conducted the Assistant Recruitment 2026 for 650 posts across various offices. The recruitment process included a preliminary examination, mains examination, and language proficiency test. The online application window was open from 16 February 2026 to 10 March 2026 (extended), and the mains result has now been declared on 24 September 2026.

This permanent page carries every stage of the RBI Assistant 2026 recruitment lifecycle—from notification to final result—without changing the URL. Candidates who appeared for the mains examination can download the result PDF and check their marks through the official links provided below.

The selection process for RBI Assistant includes Prelims, Mains, and Language Proficiency Test (LPT). Final selection will be based on the candidate's performance in the mains examination and the language proficiency test, subject to document verification and eligibility conditions.
