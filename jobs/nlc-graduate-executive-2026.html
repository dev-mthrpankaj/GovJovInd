Complete context

About This Recruitment

NLC India Limited has published an official notification (Advt No. 10/2026) for the recruitment of Graduate Executives across various engineering disciplines. A total of 143 vacancies have been announced for Mechanical, Electrical, Civil, and Mining branches.

Candidates will be evaluated and shortlisted exclusively based on their performance in the GATE 2025 examination in the relevant subject code. Only marks obtained in GATE 2025 are considered valid; previous GATE scores will not be accepted. The online application portal opened on 13 November 2026 and will remain active until 14 December 2026.

The maximum age limit for applying is 30 years as on 01 November 2026, with relaxations applicable for reserved categories. Following the conclusion of the application window, NLC will release the merit list for document verification and medical examination based on GATE 2025 rankings.

What to do at the current stage

How to Apply Online

  1. Visit the official website of NLC India Limited at www.nlcindia.in.
  2. Navigate to the recruitment section and click on 'NLC India Limited 2026 Apply Online'.
  3. Register using a valid email ID and mobile number to obtain your registration credentials.
  4. Log in and fill the application form with your personal, educational, and category details.
  5. Enter your valid GATE 2025 registration number and details.
  6. Upload scanned passport-size photographs, signature, and other required documents.
  7. Pay the application fee through the online payment gateway.
  8. Submit the application and print the confirmation page for future reference.
