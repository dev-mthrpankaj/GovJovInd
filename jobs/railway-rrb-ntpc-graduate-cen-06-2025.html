Complete context

About This Recruitment

Railway Recruitment Board (RRB) has released the NTPC Graduate Level recruitment notification under CEN 06/2025 for 5,810 posts across six different posts including Station Master, Goods Train Manager, Chief Commercial Cum Ticket Supervisor, Junior Accounts Assistant Cum Typist, Senior Clerk Cum Typist and Traffic Assistant. Online applications were accepted from 27 October 2025 to 27 November 2025.

The recruitment follows a multi-stage selection process beginning with Computer Based Test (CBT) Stage I, followed by CBT Stage II, Computer Based Typing Skill Test (CBTST) for applicable posts, Document Verification and Medical Examination. CBT I was conducted from 16 to 27 March 2026 and CBT II was held on 10 July 2026.

The CBTST (Typing Skill Test) Admit Card has now been released for eligible candidates. The typing test is scheduled for 30 September 2026. Candidates appearing for CBTST must carry the admit card along with a valid photo ID to the examination centre.

What to do at the current stage

How to Download CBTST Admit Card

  1. Open the official RRB CBTST admit card download link from Important Links.
  2. Enter your registration number and date of birth (or password as applicable).
  3. Verify your details including name, roll number, exam date and centre on the admit card.
  4. Download and take a clear printout of the admit card.
  5. Check the reporting time and exam centre address carefully.
  6. Carry the admit card along with a valid original photo ID to the examination centre.
  7. Practice the CBTST mock test available on the official portal before the exam date.

Original Application Process

Preserved for recruitment history and reference after the application stage closes.

  1. Visit the official RRB application portal rrbapply.gov.in.
  2. Read the CEN 06/2025 notification carefully.
  3. Register with valid mobile number and email ID.
  4. Fill the application form with correct personal and educational details.
  5. Upload photo, signature and required documents as per specifications.
  6. Pay the applicable fee through online mode.
  7. Submit and save the confirmation page for future reference.
