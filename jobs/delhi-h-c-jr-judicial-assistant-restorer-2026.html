Complete context

About This Recruitment

Delhi High Court Junior Judicial Assistant (JJA) / Restorer Recruitment 2026 is a Group 'B' court establishment recruitment drive for 152 posts at Delhi High Court, New Delhi. The online application window was open from 4 February 2026 to 23 February 2026.

The selection process includes Stage-I Preliminary (Computer Based Test), Stage-II Mains (Descriptive) Examination, English Typing Test (35 WPM), Interview and Document Verification. The Stage-I CBT was held on 22 March 2026 and the revised result was declared on 8 September 2026.

Candidates who qualified Stage-I are eligible to appear for the Stage-II Mains (Descriptive) Examination scheduled on 4 October 2026. The Mains Admit Card has been released on 29 September 2026 on the official website delhihighcourt.nic.in.
