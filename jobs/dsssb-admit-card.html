Complete context

About This Recruitment

Delhi DSSSB Various Post Recruitment Examination 2026 is conducted by the Delhi Subordinate Services Selection Board (DSSSB) for various posts advertised under Advertisement No. 2023 in Delhi. Earlier examination phases under this cycle were held from 01 July 2026 to 01 September 2026, 01 September 2026 to 20 September 2026, and 21 September 2026 to 24 October 2026.

On 23 November 2026, DSSSB published the official exam schedule notice for the upcoming Computer Based Test on 15 December 2026 along with the online admit card download facility on dsssbonline.nic.in and dsssb.delhi.gov.in.

Candidates appearing in the 15 December 2026 examination must download their e-admit card, check their name, roll number, exam date, shift timing, exam centre code, and venue address, and carry a printed copy along with an original valid photo ID proof to the examination centre. Upcoming stages including the provisional answer key and result will be added on this permanent page as per the official DSSSB schedule.

What to do at the current stage

How to Download DSSSB Admit Card 2026

  1. Open the official DSSSB portal at dsssbonline.nic.in or dsssb.delhi.gov.in from Important Links.
  2. Go to the Download Admit Card section for your advertised post code.
  3. Enter your Application Number or Registration Number along with your Date of Birth or Password.
  4. Submit your login credentials to view the DSSSB Admit Card 2026 on screen.
  5. Verify your name, parents' names, roll number, exam date, shift timing, exam centre code, and venue address.
  6. Download the admit card PDF and take a clear colour or black-and-white printout as instructed for exam day.

Original Application Process

Preserved for recruitment history and reference after the application stage closes.

  1. Visit the official DSSSB online recruitment portal at dsssbonline.nic.in.
  2. Complete One-Time Registration (OTR) using valid personal, mobile and email details.
  3. Log in and select the desired post code under the active DSSSB recruitment advertisement.
  4. Fill in educational qualifications, address and category details accurately.
  5. Upload scanned photograph, signature and required documents in the prescribed format.
  6. Pay the applicable examination fee online and save the submitted application confirmation.

Frequently Asked Questions

The upcoming DSSSB Computer Based Test is scheduled for 15 December 2026 as per the official exam schedule notice. Earlier DSSSB examination phases were conducted between July 2026 and October 2026, and each candidate's exact exam date and shift are printed on their admit card.

Candidates can download the DSSSB Admit Card 2026 by visiting dsssbonline.nic.in or dsssb.delhi.gov.in and opening the admit card section. Log in using your Application Number or Registration Number along with your Date of Birth or Password, then save and print the hall ticket.

DSSSB releases post-wise e-admit cards a few days prior to the scheduled Computer Based Test date. The latest admit card and 15 December 2026 exam schedule update was published on 23 November 2026 on the official Delhi Subordinate Services Selection Board portal.

The official websites for DSSSB Admit Card 2026 are dsssbonline.nic.in and dsssb.delhi.gov.in. Candidates should use only these official Delhi Subordinate Services Selection Board portals to check exam schedules and download their examination hall tickets.

The DSSSB Admit Card 2026 mentions the candidate's name, father's or mother's name, roll number, date of birth, gender, post applied, exam date, shift timing, exam centre code, venue address, photograph, signature, and mandatory exam-day instructions.

Your allotted exam city, centre code, complete venue address, and reporting time are printed directly on the DSSSB Admit Card 2026. Download the hall ticket from dsssbonline.nic.in well ahead of the exam date to verify your centre location and travel plan.

Candidates must carry a clear printed copy of their DSSSB Admit Card 2026 along with an original valid photo ID proof such as Aadhaar Card, PAN Card, Voter ID, or Driving License. Follow all colour or black-and-white print instructions stated on the hall ticket.

If the DSSSB Admit Card 2026 fails to download, refresh the page, switch to another web browser, or retry during off-peak hours when server traffic is lower. Also recheck your Application Number, Date of Birth, and Password carefully before logging in again.

If you find any discrepancy in your name, parents' names, date of birth, gender, roll number, or post code on the DSSSB Admit Card 2026, contact the Delhi Subordinate Services Selection Board recruitment helpdesk immediately before the examination date.
