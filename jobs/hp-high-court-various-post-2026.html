About This Recruitment

The High Court of Himachal Pradesh has advertised 388 vacancies on the Establishment of District Judiciary in Himachal Pradesh under Advertisement Notice No. DJ-ADVT./2026 (1). The recruitment covers Court Manager, Clerk, Stenographer Grade-III, Driver, Process Server and multiple Group-D support posts.

Online applications opened on 10 August 2026 and the closing date for both online application and fee payment is 10 September 2026 at 11:59 PM. The recruitment portal is the official HP High Court recruitment portal.

Eligibility and selection vary by post. Candidates should verify the post-specific qualification, experience, typing or stenography standards, driving requirements, category conditions and documents in the official advertisement before submitting the form.
