Complete context

About This Recruitment

Punjab Agricultural University (PAU), Ludhiana has released Advertisement No. 03/2026 inviting online applications for 15 Teaching and Non-Teaching posts across Group A, Group B, and Group D pay levels. The recruitment covers key academic, research, medical, administrative, and support roles including Additional Director Communication, Plant Pathologist, Scientist (Forestry), Horticulture Scientist, Bioinformatician, Biochemist, District Extension Scientist, Agronomist, Agro-Meteorologist, Assistant Professor, Deputy Controller of Examination, Medical Officer (General), Sanitary Inspector, and Packer.

Online registration and fee payment opened on 26 November 2026 and will remain active until 09 December 2026 on the official PAU Recruitment Management Portal (r.pau-apms.in). In addition to online submission, candidates must send or submit the printed hard copy of the completed application form along with self-attested certificates and fee payment receipt to the designated PAU Ludhiana office by 16 December 2026 up to 04:00 PM.

The pay scale ranges from Rs.18,000 (Level-01) for Packer and Rs.35,400 (Level-06) for Sanitary Inspector up to Rs.53,100 (Level-09), Rs.57,700 (Level-10), Rs.78,800 (Level-12), and Rs.1,44,200 (Level-14) plus Rs.800 special allowance for senior Group-A posts. The age limit is 18 to 37 years as on 01 January 2026 for applicable non-teaching posts, with upper age relaxation up to 45 years for in-service government employees as per Punjab Government and PAU rules.

Selection is conducted through scrutiny of applications followed by post-specific assessment stages—written competitive examination for Sanitary Inspector, practical test for Packer, and interview for Teaching, Scientist, and shortlisted posts—leading to document verification and final merit list publication. This permanent page tracks every stage of PAU Ludhiana Recruitment 2026 from notification to final selection.

Eligibility Criteria

Age Limit

18 to 37 years as on 01 January 2026 (applicable for non-teaching posts Sr. No. 12 to 15 and as specified post-wise).

Upper age limit is relaxed up to 45 years for in-service government employees and as per Punjab Government / PAU rules for reserved categories.

Education

Post-wise qualifications range from 10+2 with experience (Packer), Graduation + Diploma (Sanitary Inspector), and MBBS (Medical Officer) to Master's / Ph.D. with NET and experience for Teaching & Scientist posts.

Matric-level certificate of Punjabi language is mandatory for applicable posts. Verify exact post-wise academic and experience conditions in Advt No. 03/2026.

Nationality / Other

In-service candidates must apply through proper channel with an advance copy reaching by the closing date. Campus residence is mandatory for Medical Officer (General).

Refer to official notification for detailed conditions.

What to do at the current stage

How to Apply Online for PAU Ludhiana Recruitment 2026

  1. Visit the official PAU recruitment registration portal at r.pau-apms.in or pau.edu and read Advertisement No. 03/2026.
  2. Select your target post (Sr. No. 1 to 15) and register using a valid email ID and mobile number.
  3. Log in with your registration credentials and enter accurate personal, academic, publication, and experience details.
  4. Upload scanned copies of your recent passport-size photograph, signature, and required certificates.
  5. Pay the prescribed non-refundable application fee online (Rs.1,298, Rs.590, or Rs.118 depending on the post) by 09 December 2026.
  6. Submit the online form, print the completed application and fee receipt, and attach self-attested copies of all certificates.
  7. Send or submit the hard copy to the designated PAU Ludhiana office (Registrar Office, Estate Office, or Director of Extension Education) by 16 December 2026 up to 04:00 PM.
