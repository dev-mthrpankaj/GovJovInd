Complete context

About This Examination

The National Testing Agency (NTA) conducts the UGC National Eligibility Test (UGC NET) on behalf of the University Grants Commission (UGC) to determine the eligibility of Indian nationals for (i) award of Junior Research Fellowship (JRF) and appointment as Assistant Professor, (ii) appointment as Assistant Professor and admission to PhD, and (iii) admission to PhD only, in Indian universities and colleges.

The UGC NET December 2026 examination will be conducted in Computer Based Test (CBT) mode across 87 subjects from 14 December 2026 to 19 December 2026. The online application process started on 08 October 2026 and will close on 28 October 2026. Candidates who miss the regular deadline can apply with a late fee of Rs.500 from 29 October to 31 October 2026.

The application correction window will be available from 02 November 2026 to 04 November 2026. The exam city intimation slip, admit card, answer key and result dates will be announced by NTA separately through the official website ugcnet.nta.nic.in.
