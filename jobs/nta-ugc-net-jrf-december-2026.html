Complete context

About This Recruitment

NTA UGC NET JRF December 2026 Eligibility Examination is conducted by the National Testing Agency (NTA) on behalf of the University Grants Commission (UGC) across 85 subjects. Qualifying candidates become eligible for the award of Junior Research Fellowship (JRF), appointment as Assistant Professor in Indian universities and colleges, and admission to Ph.D. programmes.

The official Information Bulletin was released on 08 October 2026. Online applications remained open from 08 October 2026 to 31 October 2026 after NTA extended the original 28 October 2026 deadline without late fees, followed by an online application correction window from 02 November 2026 to 04 November 2026.

On 19 November 2026, NTA released the official subject-wise exam schedule for UGC NET December 2026. The Computer Based Test (CBT) will be conducted on 14, 15, 16, 17, 18 and 21 December 2026 in two daily shifts: Shift I from 09:00 AM to 12:00 PM and Shift II from 03:00 PM to 06:00 PM.

Basic eligibility requires a Master's Degree in the concerned subject with at least 55% marks for General and EWS candidates (50% for OBC-NCL, SC, ST, PwBD and Third Gender), or a 4-Year Bachelor's Degree with 75% marks. The upper age limit is 30 years for JRF with category-wise relaxation up to 5 years, while there is no upper age limit for Assistant Professor eligibility or Ph.D. admission.
