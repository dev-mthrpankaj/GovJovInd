Complete context

About This Recruitment

NTA UGC NET JRF December 2026 Eligibility Examination is conducted by the National Testing Agency (NTA) on behalf of the University Grants Commission (UGC) across 85 subjects. Qualifying candidates become eligible for the award of Junior Research Fellowship (JRF), appointment as Assistant Professor in Indian universities and colleges, and admission to Ph.D. programmes.

The official Information Bulletin was released on 08 October 2026. Online applications remained open from 08 October 2026 to 31 October 2026 after NTA extended the original 28 October 2026 deadline without late fees, followed by an online application correction window from 02 November 2026 to 04 November 2026.

On 19 November 2026, NTA released the official subject-wise exam schedule for UGC NET December 2026. The Computer Based Test (CBT) will be conducted on 14, 15, 16, 17, 18 and 21 December 2026 in two daily shifts: Shift I from 09:00 AM to 12:00 PM and Shift II from 03:00 PM to 06:00 PM.

Basic eligibility requires a Master's Degree in the concerned subject with at least 55% marks for General and EWS candidates (50% for OBC-NCL, SC, ST, PwBD and Third Gender), or a 4-Year Bachelor's Degree with 75% marks. The upper age limit is 30 years for JRF with category-wise relaxation up to 5 years, while there is no upper age limit for Assistant Professor eligibility or Ph.D. admission.

Eligibility Criteria

Age Limit

Maximum 30 years for Junior Research Fellowship (JRF). There is no upper age limit for Assistant Professor eligibility or Ph.D. admission.

Up to 5 years JRF age relaxation for OBC-NCL, SC, ST, PwBD, Third Gender and women candidates, and up to 3 years for LL.M. degree holders, as per UGC NET rules.

Education

Passed or appearing in a Master's Degree in a concerned subject with at least 55% marks for General/EWS (50% for OBC-NCL, SC, ST, PwBD and Third Gender), or a 4-Year Bachelor's Degree with 75% marks.

Candidates qualifying through a 4-Year Bachelor's Degree are eligible for JRF and Ph.D. admission, with 5% marks relaxation for eligible reserved categories.

Nationality / Other

Indian citizens fulfilling the UGC NET subject and qualification criteria may appear. There is no limit on the number of attempts for UGC NET.

Refer to official notification for detailed conditions.

What to do at the current stage

How to Check Subject-Wise Exam Schedule

  1. Open the official NTA UGC NET December 2026 Exam Schedule PDF from the Important Links section.
  2. Locate your subject code and subject name in the date-wise timetable for 14 to 18 December 2026 and 21 December 2026.
  3. Note your allotted shift timing: Shift I runs from 09:00 AM to 12:00 PM and Shift II runs from 03:00 PM to 06:00 PM.
  4. Keep your NTA application number and date of birth ready for the upcoming Exam City Intimation Slip.
  5. Plan travel only after downloading the official Exam City Intimation Slip from ugcnet.nta.nic.in.
  6. Review your CBT syllabus and keep a valid original photo ID ready for the admit card and exam day.

Original Application Process

Preserved for recruitment history and reference after the application stage closes.

  1. Visit the official NTA UGC NET portal at ugcnet.nta.nic.in.
  2. Open the UGC NET December 2026 Online Application Form link.
  3. Complete new registration using a valid email ID and active mobile number.
  4. Log in with the generated application number and password.
  5. Fill in personal, academic, subject and exam city preference details carefully.
  6. Upload scanned passport-size photograph, signature and required category certificates.
  7. Pay the applicable examination fee online before 31 October 2026.
  8. Submit the application form and download the confirmation page for future reference.
