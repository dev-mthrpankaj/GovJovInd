Complete context

About This Exam

UGC NET December 2026 is a national-level eligibility test conducted by the National Testing Agency (NTA) on behalf of the University Grants Commission (UGC). It determines eligibility for the award of Junior Research Fellowship (JRF), appointment as Assistant Professor, and admission to PhD programmes in Indian universities and colleges.

The examination will be held in Computer Based Test (CBT) mode from 14 to 19 December 2026. The test covers 87 subjects, including new additions such as Disaster Management, Ayurveda Biology, Forestry and Statistics. Paper 1 is common for all candidates and tests teaching and research aptitude, while Paper 2 is subject-specific.

The online application process started on 8 October 2026. The last date to apply without late fee was 28 October 2026, and with a late fee of Rs.500, applications are accepted until 31 October 2026. The application correction window is scheduled from 2 to 4 November 2026.

Candidates applying for JRF must not exceed 30 years of age as on 1 December 2026. There is no upper age limit for Assistant Professor. A Master's degree with at least 55% marks (50% for reserved categories) is the minimum educational qualification. Final-year students whose results are awaited are also eligible to apply.

Eligibility Criteria

Age Limit

JRF: Maximum 30 years as on 1 December 2026. Assistant Professor: No upper age limit.

Age relaxation for JRF: 5 years for SC/ST/OBC-NCL/PwD and 3 years for OBC-NCL (non-creamy layer).

Education

Master's degree from a UGC-recognised university with at least 55% marks (50% for SC/ST/OBC-NCL/PwD/Third Gender). Final-year/appearing candidates are eligible.

4-year Bachelor's degree candidates are also eligible as per UGC regulations.

Nationality / Other

Indian nationals fulfilling the eligibility conditions. Candidates awaiting final results may apply provisionally.

Refer to official notification for detailed conditions.
