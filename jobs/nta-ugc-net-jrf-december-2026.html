Complete context

About This Examination

The National Testing Agency (NTA) conducts the UGC National Eligibility Test (UGC NET) on behalf of the University Grants Commission (UGC) to determine the eligibility of Indian nationals for (i) award of Junior Research Fellowship (JRF) and appointment as Assistant Professor, (ii) appointment as Assistant Professor and admission to PhD, and (iii) admission to PhD only, in Indian universities and colleges.

The UGC NET December 2026 examination will be conducted in Computer Based Test (CBT) mode across 87 subjects from 14 December 2026 to 19 December 2026. The online application process started on 08 October 2026 and will close on 28 October 2026. Candidates who miss the regular deadline can apply with a late fee of Rs.500 from 29 October to 31 October 2026.

The application correction window will be available from 02 November 2026 to 04 November 2026. The exam city intimation slip, admit card, answer key and result dates will be announced by NTA separately through the official website ugcnet.nta.nic.in.

Eligibility Criteria

Age Limit

For JRF: Maximum 30 years as on 01 December 2026. For Assistant Professor & PhD: No upper age limit.

Age relaxation applicable for OBC-NCL, SC, ST, PwD, Third Gender, and women candidates as per UGC rules.

Education

Master's degree or equivalent examination from a UGC-recognized university with at least 55% marks (50% for OBC-NCL, SC, ST, PwD, Third Gender). Candidates pursuing Master's degree or appeared in final year can also apply. Four-year Bachelor's degree holders are eligible for JRF and PhD admission but not for Assistant Professor.

Both the academic qualification and the minimum percentage requirement must be fulfilled.

Nationality / Other

Indian nationals fulfilling the post-specific eligibility conditions may apply. Refer to the official Information Bulletin for complete details.

Refer to official notification for detailed conditions.

How to Apply Online

  1. Visit the official UGC NET website at ugcnet.nta.nic.in.
  2. Click on the UGC NET December 2026 Application Form link.
  3. Complete registration using a valid email ID and mobile number.
  4. Fill the application form with personal, educational, and subject details.
  5. Upload scanned photograph and signature as per specifications.
  6. Pay the application fee through online modes (Debit Card, Credit Card, Net Banking, UPI).
  7. Submit the form and download the confirmation page for future reference.
