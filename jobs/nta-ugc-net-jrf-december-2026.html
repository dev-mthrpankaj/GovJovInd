Complete context

About This Examination

The NTA UGC NET JRF December 2026 National Eligibility Test is conducted by the National Testing Agency (NTA) on behalf of the University Grants Commission (UGC) across 87 subjects to determine eligibility for Junior Research Fellowship (JRF), appointment as Assistant Professor, and admission to PhD programmes in Indian universities and colleges. The official notification was released on 08 October 2026, and online applications remained open until 31 October 2026 (extended from 28 October 2026 with a Rs.500 late fee window), followed by the correction window from 02 November 2026 to 04 November 2026.

On 19 November 2026, NTA published the subject-wise Computer Based Test (CBT) schedule for 14, 15, 16, 17, 18, and 21 December 2026 in two daily shifts: Shift 1 from 9:00 AM to 12:00 PM and Shift 2 from 3:00 PM to 6:00 PM. The Advance Exam City Intimation Slip is scheduled to activate on 25 November 2026 (revised from 23 November 2026), and the official Admit Card will be released on 07 December 2026 on ugcnet.nta.nic.in.

Eligible candidates hold or are appearing in a Master's degree in the concerned subject with at least 55% marks (50% for OBC-NCL, SC, ST, PwD, and Third Gender categories), or hold a recognised four-year undergraduate degree meeting UGC norms. The upper age limit for JRF is 30 years as on 01 December 2026 with applicable category relaxation, while Assistant Professor eligibility and PhD admission carry no upper age limit.

Eligibility Criteria

Age Limit

JRF: Maximum 30 years as on 01 December 2026. Assistant Professor & PhD Admission: No upper age limit.

Up to 5 years age relaxation for OBC-NCL, SC, ST, PwD, Third Gender, Women, and candidates with research experience; 3 years for LLM holders as per UGC rules.

Education

Passed or appearing in a Master's Degree or equivalent examination in the concerned subject with at least 55% marks (50% for OBC-NCL, SC, ST, PwD, and Third Gender). Four-year Bachelor's Degree candidates meeting UGC criteria are also eligible.

Candidates appearing in their final year must complete the degree with required marks within the stipulated period mentioned in the Information Bulletin.

Nationality / Other

Candidates should select the subject of their post-graduation or a related subject from the 87 notified UGC NET subjects. Refer to the official Information Bulletin for PhD-only and 4-year UG eligibility norms.

Refer to official Information Bulletin for detailed conditions.

What to do at the current stage

How to Check Exam City Intimation Slip

  1. Visit the official NTA UGC NET portal at ugcnet.nta.nic.in on 25 November 2026 once the link activates.
  2. Open the link titled Advance Intimation of Examination City for UGC-NET December 2026 under Latest News or Candidate Activity.
  3. Enter your Application Number, Password or Date of Birth, and the displayed security pin.
  4. Submit the credentials to view your allotted examination city and subject exam date.
  5. Download and save the City Intimation Slip PDF to plan travel arrangements in advance.
  6. Remember that the City Intimation Slip is not the Admit Card; download your Admit Card separately from 07 December 2026.

Original Application Process

Preserved for examination history and reference after the application stage closes.

  1. Visited the official NTA UGC NET portal at ugcnet.nta.nic.in between 08 October 2026 and 31 October 2026.
  2. Completed new candidate registration using a valid email ID and mobile number to generate an Application Number.
  3. Filled personal, academic qualification, subject code, and preferred exam city details in the online form.
  4. Uploaded scanned recent passport photograph and signature in the prescribed format.
  5. Paid the category-wise examination fee (Rs.1150 / Rs.600 / Rs.325, plus Rs.500 late fee if submitted on 29 to 31 October 2026) online.
  6. Submitted the form and downloaded the Confirmation Page for future stages.

Quick answers

Frequently Asked Questions

The National Testing Agency (NTA) is scheduled to release the UGC NET December 2026 Exam City Intimation Slip on 25 November 2026 at ugcnet.nta.nic.in. It was postponed from 23 November 2026 and informs candidates of their allotted exam city before the admit card.

Candidates can check their allotted exam city by visiting ugcnet.nta.nic.in once the link activates on 25 November 2026. Log in using your Application Number, Password or Date of Birth, and security pin to view and download the advance city intimation slip PDF.

The NTA UGC NET December 2026 Admit Card is scheduled to be released on 07 December 2026 on ugcnet.nta.nic.in. Unlike the exam city slip, the admit card contains the exact exam centre address, shift timing, roll number, and mandatory reporting instructions.

NTA will conduct the UGC NET December 2026 Computer Based Test on 14, 15, 16, 17, 18, and 21 December 2026 across India. The exam runs in two shifts daily: Shift 1 from 9:00 AM to 12:00 PM and Shift 2 from 3:00 PM to 6:00 PM as per the subject-wise schedule.

The last date to submit the NTA UGC NET JRF December 2026 online application form and pay fees was 31 October 2026, extended from 28 October 2026 with a Rs.500 late fee for 29 to 31 October. The application and correction windows are now closed.

The NTA UGC NET December 2026 examination covers 87 subjects across humanities, social sciences, commerce, computer science, languages, law, management, Ayurveda Biology, and Disaster Management. Each subject is scheduled on a specific date and shift.

Candidates who have passed or are appearing in a Master's degree in the concerned subject with at least 55% marks (50% for OBC-NCL, SC, ST, PwD, and Third Gender) are eligible. Four-year undergraduate degree holders meeting UGC criteria can also appear.

The maximum age limit for Junior Research Fellowship (JRF) is 30 years as on 01 December 2026, with up to 5 years relaxation for reserved categories and women as per UGC norms. There is no upper age limit for Assistant Professor eligibility or PhD admission.

For NTA UGC NET December 2026, the application fee was Rs.1150 for General (Unreserved), Rs.600 for Gen-EWS and OBC-NCL, and Rs.325 for SC, ST, PwD, and Third Gender candidates. An additional Rs.500 late fee applied for submissions from 29 to 31 October 2026.

NTA UGC NET December 2026 is a single 3-hour Computer Based Test with Paper 1 (50 questions, 100 marks) and Paper 2 (100 subject questions, 200 marks), carrying 300 marks with no negative marking. Qualification is decided by merit and category-wise cut-offs.

UGC NET is a national eligibility test rather than a direct job recruitment. JRF qualifiers receive a monthly UGC research fellowship during PhD, while Assistant Professor qualifiers become eligible for college and university teaching posts under UGC pay scales.

Candidates submitted the NTA UGC NET December 2026 form online at ugcnet.nta.nic.in between 08 October and 31 October 2026 by registering, entering academic details, uploading photo and signature, paying the fee online, and saving the confirmation page.
