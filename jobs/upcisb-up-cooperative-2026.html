Complete context

About This Recruitment

UPCISB UP Cooperative Bank Combined Recruitment 2026 is a Uttar Pradesh Co-operative Institutional Service Board recruitment drive to fill 2085 posts across 29 designations in various cooperative banks and institutions across Uttar Pradesh. The official notification was published on 13 April 2026, and the online application window remained open from 25 April 2026 to 15 May 2026.

The written examination is scheduled for 14, 16, 22 and 26 October 2026. The Admit Card for the written examination was released on 15 September 2026. The selection process includes an Online Examination followed by Interview/Skill Test and Document Verification depending on the post applied for.

The recruitment covers a wide range of posts including Manager, Junior Manager, Assistant/Cashier, Assistant Manager, Accountant, Stenographer, Junior Assistant, Assistant/Typist, Assistant Engineer, Junior Engineer and various other technical and clerical positions across District Co-operative Banks, U.P. Co-operative Bank Limited, U.P. State Construction Co-operative Federation Limited and other cooperative institutions.

Basic eligibility varies by post, with most positions requiring a Bachelor's Degree from a recognised university. The age limit is 21 to 40 years as on 01 January 2026, with relaxation for reserved categories as per government rules.
