About This Recruitment

UPCISB UP Cooperative Bank Various Post Recruitment 2026 is a major recruitment drive by the Uttar Pradesh Co-Operative Institutional Service Board for 2085 posts across multiple roles including Manager, Junior Manager, Assistant/Cashier, Assistant Manager, and various technical and clerical positions. The online application window was open from 25 April 2026 to 15 May 2026.

The admit card for the written examination scheduled from 17 to 21 September 2026 has now been released. Candidates who applied successfully can download their admit card from the official IBPS portal. The recruitment covers a wide range of qualifications from graduation to B.Tech, with specific percentage requirements varying by post.

What to do at the current stage

How to Download Admit Card

  1. Visit the official IBPS admit card portal from Important Links.
  2. Enter your Registration Number and Date of Birth / Password.
  3. Complete the CAPTCHA or security verification if prompted.
  4. Click on Submit/Login to access your admit card.
  5. Verify all details including Name, Roll Number, Exam Date, and Exam Centre.
  6. Download the admit card PDF and take a clear printout on A4 paper.
  7. Carry the printed admit card along with a valid photo ID to the exam centre.

Original Application Process

Preserved for recruitment history and reference after the application stage closes.

  1. Visit the official IBPS application portal.
  2. Read the UPCISB UP Cooperative Bank notification carefully.
  3. Register with valid email ID and mobile number.
  4. Fill the application form with personal and educational details.
  5. Upload photograph, signature, and required documents.
  6. Pay the applicable fee through online mode.
  7. Submit the form and save the confirmation page.
