About This Recruitment

Uttarakhand Subordinate Service Selection Commission (UKSSSC) has released the notification for Scaler Group C Recruitment 2026, advertising a total of 200 posts. The online application window is scheduled from 22 September 2026 to 22 October 2026.

This recruitment is for candidates with a 10+2 (Intermediate) qualification and who meet the physical standards specified for the role. The selection process includes a written examination and a Physical Efficiency Test (PET), followed by document verification.

Candidates are advised to read the official notification carefully for detailed eligibility, physical standards, and category-wise vacancy distribution before applying online.
