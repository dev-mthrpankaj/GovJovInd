About This Recruitment

The High Court of Judicature at Patna has released the answer key for the Group 'C' Assistant recruitment examination 2026. This recruitment drive covers 68 Assistant posts under advertisement number PHC/03/2026.

The written examination was conducted on 18 September 2026 and the provisional answer key has now been released. Candidates who appeared in the examination can download the answer key and raise objections, if any, through the official portal within the prescribed window.

The selection process includes a written examination, document verification, and a final merit list. Candidates should retain their admit card and examination details for later stages of the recruitment process.
