About This Recruitment

Patna High Court Ex-Cadre Assistant Recruitment Examination 2026 is being conducted under Advertisement No. PHC/03/2026 for 68 Group C Assistant posts in Bihar.

The online application window ran from 28 July to 27 August 2026. Patna High Court has now issued the examination notice, with the written examination scheduled for 18 September 2026 and the supplied recruitment information listing the admit card from 12 September 2026.

Candidates need a bachelor’s degree in any stream along with at least a six-month Diploma or Certificate in Computer Application from a recognized institution.
