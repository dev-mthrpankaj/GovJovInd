Complete context

About This Recruitment

The High Court of Judicature at Patna has released the recruitment notification for Group 'C' Assistant posts. This drive aims to fill 68 vacancies. The online application process was conducted from 28 July 2026 to 27 August 2026.

The recruitment is for the post of Group 'C' Assistant, a support-staff role within the court system. Candidates were required to possess a Bachelor's Degree in any stream and a Diploma/Certificate in Computer Application of at least six months. The selection process involves a written examination, document verification, and a medical examination.

The admit card for the written examination has been released. The exam is scheduled for 18 September 2026. This permanent page will continue to provide updates on the result, final merit list, and other stages as they are announced by the Patna High Court.
