Complete context

About This Recruitment

State Bank of India (SBI), through its Central Recruitment & Promotion Department (CRPD), Corporate Centre, Mumbai, has released Advertisement No. CRPD/SCO/2026-27/31 on 20 November 2026 for SBI SCO Specialist Cadre Officer (Data Scientist) Recruitment 2026. The recruitment drive offers 20 regular posts: 2 Chief Manager (Data Scientist) posts in SMGS-IV, 14 Manager (Data Scientist) posts in MMGS-III, and 4 Deputy Manager (Data Scientist) posts in MMGS-II.

Online registration, application editing, and online fee payment are active from 20 November 2026 to 10 December 2026, while candidates can print their submitted application until 25 December 2026. General, OBC, and EWS applicants must pay Rs.750 online, whereas SC, ST, and PwBD candidates are exempted from paying any fee. Candidates may apply for only one post under this notification.

Eligibility is calculated as on 31 October 2026. Applicants must hold a B.E./B.Tech/M.Tech in Computer Science, IT, ECE, EEE, Data Science, or AI & ML, or an M.Sc (Data Science/Statistics/Computer Science), M.A. (Statistics), M.Stat, or MCA with at least 60% marks in both graduation and post-graduation, along with 3 to 6 years of post-qualification AI/ML experience depending on the grade. Age limits are 27 to 37 years for Chief Manager, 26 to 36 years for Manager, and 24 to 32 years for Deputy Manager.

Selection for Chief Manager (SMGS-IV) is based on shortlisting and a 100-mark personal interview. Selection for Manager (MMGS-III) and Deputy Manager (MMGS-II) includes an online written test tentatively scheduled for December 2026 or January 2027 followed by a 100-mark interview, with final merit prepared on a 70:30 written-to-interview weightage. Basic pay scales range from Rs.64,820 to Rs.1,20,940 per month plus DA, HRA, CCA, NPS, LFC, and medical benefits.

Eligibility Criteria

Age Limit

27 to 37 years for Chief Manager, 26 to 36 years for Manager, and 24 to 32 years for Deputy Manager as on 31 October 2026.

Upper age relaxation is applicable for SC, ST, OBC, and PwBD candidates as per Government of India guidelines.

Education

B.E./B.Tech/M.Tech in CS, IT, ECE, EEE, Data Science or AI & ML, or M.Sc (Data Science/Statistics/CS), M.A. (Statistics), M.Stat, or MCA with minimum 60% marks in both graduation and post-graduation.

Mandatory post-qualification experience (6 years for Chief Manager, 4+ years for Manager, 3 years for Deputy Manager) is calculated as on 31 October 2026. Training and teaching experience are excluded.

Nationality / Other

Only Indian citizens may apply. Candidates can apply for only one post under Advertisement No. CRPD/SCO/2026-27/31. Preferred certifications include GenAI, Agentic AI, MLOps, LLMOps, or AI Security.

What to do at the current stage

How to Apply Online for SBI SCO 2026

  1. Download and read Advertisement No. CRPD/SCO/2026-27/31 carefully to confirm your post-wise age, degree, and experience eligibility as on 31 October 2026.
  2. Open the official IBPS-hosted SBI registration portal from Important Links and click on New Registration.
  3. Register with a valid email ID and active mobile number to generate your provisional registration number and password.
  4. Fill in personal, academic, and post-qualification AI/ML experience details accurately; candidates can apply for only one post under this advertisement.
  5. Upload scanned copies of your photograph, signature, detailed resume, ID proof, age proof, qualification certificates, experience proof, and category certificate if applicable.
  6. Pay the non-refundable Rs.750 fee online (General/OBC/EWS) via Debit Card, Credit Card, Internet Banking, or UPI; SC/ST/PwBD candidates are exempted.
  7. Submit the application before 10 December 2026 and print the confirmation page and e-receipt on or before 25 December 2026.
