Complete context

About This Recruitment

Allahabad High Court (AHC) issued Advertisement No. 02/2026 to recruit 543 candidates across three posts in Uttar Pradesh: Review Officer (RO) with 138 posts, Assistant Review Officer (ARO) with 380 posts, and Computer Assistant with 25 posts. The online application window was open from 01 June 2026 to 21 June 2026, followed by an application correction window on 24 and 25 June 2026.

On 27 November 2026, Allahabad High Court (AHC) released the Exam City intimation slip and official exam schedule notice. Registered candidates can now log in to check their allotted examination city and shift schedule ahead of the examination scheduled for 08, 09, and 10 December 2026.

Applicants must hold a Bachelor Degree in any stream along with a Degree or Diploma in Computer Science, O Level certificate, or CCC certificate, plus an English computer typing speed of 25 WPM. The prescribed age limit is 21 to 35 years as on 01 July 2026, with age relaxation for reserved categories as per Allahabad High Court rules.

What to do at the current stage

How to Check Exam City Intimation Slip

  1. Open the official Exam City intimation link from the Important Links section.
  2. Enter your application number, password or date of birth, and security captcha.
  3. Submit the login details to view your allotted examination city and exam date.
  4. Download and save the city intimation slip for travel planning.
  5. Check the official Exam Schedule PDF to confirm your post-wise exam date between 08 and 10 December 2026.
  6. Wait for the official Admit Card release to get your exact examination centre address and reporting time.

Original Application Process

Preserved for recruitment history and reference after the application stage closes.

  1. Visit the official Allahabad High Court (AHC) recruitment portal.
  2. Read Advertisement No. 02/2026 carefully before applying.
  3. Click on the AHC RO, ARO & Computer Assistant 2026 Apply Online link.
  4. Register using a valid email ID and mobile number.
  5. Log in with the generated registration ID and password.
  6. Fill in personal, educational, and category details accurately.
  7. Upload scanned photograph, signature, and required documents.
  8. Pay the applicable application fee online and print the confirmation page.
