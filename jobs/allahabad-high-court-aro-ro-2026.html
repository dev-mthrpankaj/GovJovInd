Complete context

About This Recruitment

Allahabad High Court (AHC) issued Advertisement No. 02/2026 to recruit 543 candidates across three posts in Uttar Pradesh: Review Officer (RO) with 138 posts, Assistant Review Officer (ARO) with 380 posts, and Computer Assistant with 25 posts. The online application window was open from 01 June 2026 to 21 June 2026, followed by an application correction window on 24 and 25 June 2026.

On 27 November 2026, Allahabad High Court (AHC) released the Exam City intimation slip and official exam schedule notice. Registered candidates can now log in to check their allotted examination city and shift schedule ahead of the examination scheduled for 08, 09, and 10 December 2026.

Applicants must hold a Bachelor Degree in any stream along with a Degree or Diploma in Computer Science, O Level certificate, or CCC certificate, plus an English computer typing speed of 25 WPM. The prescribed age limit is 21 to 35 years as on 01 July 2026, with age relaxation for reserved categories as per Allahabad High Court rules.
