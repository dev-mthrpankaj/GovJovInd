Complete context

About This Recruitment

Indo-Tibetan Border Police Force (ITBP), under the Ministry of Home Affairs, has released the notification for Head Constable Recruitment 2026. The recruitment drive aims to fill a total of 37 posts across two trades: Head Constable (Education & Stress Counsellor) and Head Constable (Motor Mechanic).

The online application process began on 28 September 2026 and will close on 27 October 2026 at 11:59 PM. Candidates must apply through the official ITBP recruitment portal at recruitment.itbpolice.nic.in. The last date for fee payment is also 27 October 2026.

The Head Constable (Education & Stress Counsellor) post requires a degree with Psychology as a subject, or a degree along with B.Ed./Bachelor of Teaching or equivalent. The Head Constable (Motor Mechanic) post requires 10+2 pass along with a Motor Mechanic certificate/ITI with three years practical experience, or a three-year Diploma in Automobile Engineering.

The selection process includes Physical Efficiency Test (PET), Physical Standard Test (PST), Written Examination, Document Verification and Medical Examination. Selected candidates will receive Pay Level-4 salary of Rs. 25,500 to Rs. 81,100 as per 7th CPC.
