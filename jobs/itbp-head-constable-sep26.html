About This Recruitment

The Indo-Tibetan Border Police Force (ITBP) has released the recruitment notification for Head Constable posts for 2026. The recruitment drive covers 37 vacancies across two posts: Head Constable (Education & Stress Counsellor) and Head Constable (Motor Mechanic).

The online application process is currently active. Eligible candidates can apply online from 28 September 2026 to 27 October 2026 (11:59 PM) through the official ITBP recruitment portal at recruitment.itbpolice.nic.in.

Selected candidates will be placed in Pay Level 4 of the 7th Central Pay Commission with a salary of Rs. 25,500–81,100. The selection process includes a Physical Efficiency Test (PET), Physical Standard Test (PST), Written Examination, and Document Verification, followed by a Detailed Medical Examination.
