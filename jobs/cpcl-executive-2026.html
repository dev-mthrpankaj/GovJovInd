Complete context

About This Recruitment

Chennai Petroleum Corporation Limited (CPCL), a Navratna Central Public Sector Enterprise and a group company of Indian Oil, has released the official notification for CPCL Executive Recruitment 2026. The recruitment aims to fill 41 Executive posts across engineering and non-engineering disciplines, including Engineer (Chemical, Mechanical, Electrical, Civil, Instrumentation), Officer (Information Systems), Junior Officer (Official Language), and Manager/Senior Manager (Secretarial).

The online application window opened on 4 November 2026 and will close on 3 December 2026. Eligible candidates must apply online through the official CPCL website at www.cpcl.co.in. The Computer Based Test (CBT) is tentatively scheduled for December 2026 or January 2027, followed by Group Discussion/Task and Personal Interview for shortlisted candidates.

The age limit varies by post: maximum 28 years for Post Codes 01 to 07, 42 years for Manager (Secretarial), and 45 years for Senior Manager (Secretarial), calculated as on 1 October 2026. The application fee is Rs.500 for General/OBC/EWS candidates, while SC/ST/PwBD/Women/Ex-Servicemen are exempted.

All posts require a first-class Engineering degree (60% marks) with minimum 2 years of post-qualification experience, except Junior Officer (Official Language) which requires an M.A. with 60% and 2 years experience, and Manager/Senior Manager (Secretarial) which requires ACS with 50% and 12/15 years experience.

Eligibility Criteria

Age Limit

Maximum 28 years for Post Codes 01 to 07; 42 years for Manager (Secretarial); 45 years for Senior Manager (Secretarial) as on 1 October 2026.

Age relaxation applicable for SC, ST, OBC, PwBD and Ex-Servicemen as per Government of India rules.

Education

First class Engineering degree in relevant discipline with 60% marks and minimum 2 years post-qualification experience. For Junior Officer (Official Language): M.A. in Hindi/English with 60% and 2 years experience. For Manager/Senior Manager (Secretarial): ACS with 50% and 12/15 years experience.

Candidates must possess the required qualification and experience as on 1 October 2026.

What to do at the current stage

How to Apply Online

  1. Visit the official CPCL website at www.cpcl.co.in.
  2. Open the Careers or Recruitment section.
  3. Find the link for CPCL Executive Recruitment 2026 (Advt. No. 02 of 2026).
  4. Read the official notification and verify eligibility for your preferred post.
  5. Complete the registration process with a valid email ID and mobile number.
  6. Fill the online application form with personal, educational and professional details.
  7. Upload photograph, signature and required documents.
  8. Pay the application fee (Rs.500 for General/OBC/EWS; exempted for SC/ST/PwBD/Women/Ex-Servicemen).
  9. Review the form and submit it before 3 December 2026.
  10. Download the confirmation page and payment receipt for future reference.
