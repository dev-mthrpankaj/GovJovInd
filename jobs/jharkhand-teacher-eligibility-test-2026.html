Complete context

About This Recruitment

The Jharkhand Teacher Eligibility Test (JTET) 2026 is conducted by the Jharkhand Academic Council (JAC), Ranchi, to determine the eligibility of candidates for teaching positions in Primary (Classes 1–5) and Upper Primary (Classes 6–8) schools across Jharkhand. The official notification was released on 28 March 2026, and the online application window remained open from 21 April 2026 to 20 July 2026 (extended from the initial 21 May 2026 deadline).

The JTET Admit Card 2026 was released on 15 October 2026, and the examination is scheduled for 01 November 2026. The exam will be conducted in offline OMR-based mode across multiple centres in Jharkhand. Around 4.40 lakh candidates are expected to appear, with over 1,000 examination centres being set up. The result is expected to be declared in December 2026.

Eligibility for Paper 1 (Primary Level) requires 10+2 Intermediate with at least 50% marks plus a 2-year Diploma in Elementary Education (D.El.Ed), or equivalent qualifications. For Paper 2 (Upper Primary Level), candidates need a Bachelor's degree with a 2-year Diploma in Elementary Education or B.Ed. The minimum age is 21 years, with no upper age limit. There is no limit on the number of attempts.

Candidates must carry the printed Admit Card along with a valid original photo ID (Aadhaar Card) to the examination centre. The admit card contains details such as the candidate's name, roll number, exam date, exam time, and exam centre. Candidates should verify all details carefully and contact the examination authorities immediately if any discrepancy is found.

Eligibility Criteria

Age Limit

Minimum 21 years. No upper age limit. Age relaxation as per government rules for reserved category candidates (SC/ST/OBC/PwD).

Special one-time age relaxation for candidates who crossed the normal age limit due to a delay in the JTET exam cycle.

Education

Paper 1 (Primary): 10+2 Intermediate with at least 50% marks plus a 2-year Diploma in Elementary Education (D.El.Ed), or 4-year Bachelor of Elementary Education (B.El.Ed), or Graduation with D.El.Ed. Paper 2 (Upper Primary): Graduation plus a 2-year Diploma in Elementary Education or B.Ed.

Candidates in the final year of their teacher training course can also apply. Refer to the official notification for complete qualification details.

Nationality / Other

Indian citizen. Candidates must have knowledge of Jharkhand's language, culture, and environment. Reserved category candidates (SC/ST/OBC/PwD) are exempt from the Jharkhand school condition. No limit on the number of attempts. No prior teaching experience required.

Refer to official notification for detailed conditions.

What to do at the current stage

How to Download JTET Admit Card 2026

  1. Open the official JTET admit card portal at jtet.jacportal.in.
  2. Click on the 'Admit Card' or 'Download Admit Card' link available on the homepage.
  3. Enter your Registration Number, Date of Birth/Password, or other required login credentials.
  4. Complete the CAPTCHA code or security verification, if prompted.
  5. Click on the Submit/Login button to access your JTET Admit Card 2026.
  6. Verify all details printed on the admit card, including your Name, Roll Number, Exam Date, Exam Time, and Exam Centre.
  7. Download the JTET Admit Card 2026 PDF and save it to your device.
  8. Take a clear printout of the admit card on A4-size paper for the examination.
  9. Carry the printed Admit Card along with a valid Photo ID Proof (Aadhaar Card, PAN Card, Voter ID, Passport, or Driving License) to the examination centre.
  10. Keep your Admit Card safe until the recruitment process, document verification, and final selection are completed.

Original Application Process

Preserved for recruitment history and reference after the application stage closes.

  1. Visit the official JTET portal at jactetonline.com or jac.jharkhand.gov.in.
  2. Click on the JTET 2026 registration link.
  3. Complete the registration process using a valid mobile number and email ID.
  4. Fill in personal and educational details in the application form.
  5. Upload the required documents including photograph and signature.
  6. Pay the application fee online as per your category and paper selection.
  7. Submit the form and download the confirmation page for future reference.
