Complete context

About This Recruitment

Jharkhand Teacher Eligibility Test (JTET) 2026 is conducted by the Jharkhand Academic Council (JAC), Ranchi to determine the eligibility of candidates for appointment as teachers in primary (Class I-V) and junior (Class VI-VIII) schools in Jharkhand. The official notification was released in 2026, and the online application window remained open from 21 April 2026 to 02 June 2026.

The examination is scheduled to be held on 01 November 2026. The admit card for the same has been released on the official portal. Candidates who have successfully applied for the JTET 2026 can download their admit card using their registration credentials.

The application fee is Rs. 1300 for General/BC/EWS candidates for a single paper (I or II) and Rs. 1500 for both papers. For SC/ST candidates, the fee is Rs. 700 for a single paper and Rs. 800 for both papers. The payment can be made through online modes.

Eligibility criteria differ for Primary and Junior levels. For Primary (Class I-V), candidates need 10+2 with at least 50% marks and a 2-year Diploma in Elementary Education (B.T.C./D.El.Ed.), among other qualifications. For Junior (Class VI-VIII), candidates need a Bachelor's degree with a 2-year Diploma in Elementary Education or a Bachelor's degree with at least 50% marks and a B.Ed. degree, among other qualifications.

What to do at the current stage

How to Download JTET Admit Card 2026

  1. Visit the official Jharkhand Teacher Eligibility Test (JTET) website from the Important Links section.
  2. Click on the 'Admit Card' or 'Download Admit Card' link available on the homepage.
  3. Enter your Registration Number, Date of Birth/Password, or other required login credentials.
  4. Complete the CAPTCHA code or security verification, if prompted.
  5. Click on the Submit/Login button to access your JTET Admit Card 2026.
  6. Verify all details printed on the admit card, including your Name, Roll Number, Exam Date, Exam Time, and Exam Centre.
  7. Download the JTET Admit Card 2026 PDF and save it to your device.
  8. Take a clear printout of the admit card on A4-size paper for the examination.
  9. Carry the printed Admit Card along with a valid Photo ID Proof (Aadhaar Card, PAN Card, Voter ID, Passport, or Driving License) to the examination centre.
  10. Keep your Admit Card safe until the recruitment process, document verification, and final selection are completed.

Original Application Process

Preserved for recruitment history and reference after the application stage closes.

  1. Visit the official JTET website at jactetonline.com.
  2. Read the official JTET 2026 notification carefully.
  3. Register on the portal with valid email ID and mobile number.
  4. Fill the online application form with accurate personal and educational details.
  5. Upload photograph, signature and required documents.
  6. Pay the applicable examination fee online.
  7. Submit the form and save the confirmation page for future reference.
