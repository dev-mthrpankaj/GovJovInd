Complete context

About This Recruitment

Rajasthan Rajya Vidyut Utpadan Nigam Ltd. (RVUNL) is conducting a common recruitment drive for 2005 posts across Rajasthan state power companies — RVUNL, RVPN, JVVN, AVVN and JdVVN. The recruitment covers Junior Engineer-I (Electrical, Mechanical, Civil), Junior Accountant and Junior Assistant / Commercial Assistant-II posts.

The official notification was released on 4 August 2026, and online applications were accepted from 5 August 2026 to 25 August 2026. The Computer Based Test (CBT) schedule was released on 29 October 2026. As per the schedule, JE-I (Electrical) exams will be held on 29–30 November 2026, JE-I (Mechanical) and JE-I (Civil) on 30 November 2026, Junior Assistant / CA-II from 4 December 2026 to 1 January 2027, and Junior Accountant on 13 and 15 January 2027.

Eligibility varies by post: JE-I requires a full-time four-year BE/B.Tech/AMIE degree in the relevant engineering discipline; Junior Accountant requires a commerce graduation or related qualification with computer knowledge; Junior Assistant / CA-II requires 10+2 with a recognised computer qualification. The age limit is 21–40 years for JE-I and 18–40 years for Junior Accountant and Junior Assistant, with relaxation as per rules. The selection process includes a Computer Based Test, Document Verification and Medical Examination.

Candidates should carry the printed admit card, a valid original photo ID and all relevant documents to the exam centre. Admit cards are expected to be released before the exam date.
