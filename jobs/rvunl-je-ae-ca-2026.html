Complete context

About This Recruitment

Rajasthan Rajya Vidyut Utpadan Nigam Limited (RVUNL) issued Advertisement No. RVUN/P&A/Rectt.2026-27 to fill 2005 vacancies across five Rajasthan state power utilities: RVUN, RVPN, JVVN, AVVN, and JDVVN. The advertised posts include Junior Engineer (Electrical, Mechanical, Civil), Junior Accountant, and Junior Assistant / Commercial Assistant-II.

The online application window remained open from 05 August 2026 to 25 August 2026, with the fee payment deadline also ending on 25 August 2026. Following the release of the official exam date schedule, RVUNL activated the online admit card download link on 25 November 2026.

The written examinations for the 2005 posts are scheduled to take place from 29 November 2026 to 15 January 2027. Candidates must download their call letter from the official IBPS/RVUNL portal and carry a printed copy along with a valid original photo identity proof to their allotted examination centre.

Educational requirements vary by post from 10+2 with a computer qualification for Junior Assistant / Commercial Assistant-II to B.Com/M.Com/MBA/IPCC with computer qualification for Junior Accountant and B.E./B.Tech in the relevant engineering branch for Junior Engineer posts. The age limit is 21 to 40 years with category-wise age relaxation as per Rajasthan government norms.

What to do at the current stage

How to Download RVUNL Admit Card 2026

  1. Open the official RVUNL Admit Card portal from the Important Links section.
  2. Select your preferred call letter language (English or Hindi).
  3. Enter your Registration Number / Roll Number and Password / Date of Birth (DD-MM-YY).
  4. Fill in the security captcha code and click Login to view your call letter.
  5. Verify your name, post applied for, exam date, reporting time, and exam centre address.
  6. Print a clear copy of the admit card and carry it along with a valid original photo ID to the exam centre.

Original Application Process

Preserved for recruitment history and reference after the application stage closes.

  1. Visit the official Rajasthan Energy / RVUNL portal at energy.rajasthan.gov.in/home.
  2. Open the recruitment link for Advt. No. RVUN/P&A/Rectt.2026-27 on the IBPS registration portal.
  3. Complete new registration using a valid email ID and active mobile number.
  4. Log in with the generated registration number and password.
  5. Enter personal, category, post preference, and educational qualification details accurately.
  6. Upload scanned copies of photograph, signature, and required declarations.
  7. Pay the applicable application fee online and print the final submitted application form.

Quick answers

Frequently Asked Questions

Yes, Rajasthan Rajya Vidyut Utpadan Nigam Limited (RVUNL) released the RVUNL JE, AE, CA Admit Card 2026 on 25 November 2026. Candidates can download their call letter using their registration number and password or date of birth via the official link on this page.

The online written examination for Rajasthan RVUNL Junior Engineer, Junior Accountant, and Junior Assistant / Commercial Assistant-II is scheduled from 29 November 2026 to 15 January 2027 across designated examination centres.

Rajasthan Rajya Vidyut Utpadan Nigam Limited advertised 2005 total vacancies across five power companies (RVUN, RVPN, JVVN, AVVN, and JDVVN), including 869 Junior Engineer, 371 Junior Accountant, and 765 Commercial Assistant-II posts.

The online application window for Rajasthan RVUNL Recruitment 2026 opened on 05 August 2026 and closed on 25 August 2026. The deadline for paying the online application fee also ended on 25 August 2026.

Junior Engineer requires a B.E./B.Tech in the relevant engineering branch; Junior Accountant requires B.Com, M.Com, MBA, or CA/CMA Inter with a computer certificate; Junior Assistant / Commercial Assistant-II requires 10+2 with a computer certificate.

Candidates must be between 21 and 40 years of age to be eligible for Rajasthan RVUNL Recruitment 2026. Upper age relaxation is provided to reserved category candidates in accordance with Rajasthan government rules.

General and other category candidates paid Rs.1000, while EWS, BC, MBC, SC, ST, and PwBD candidates paid Rs.500 through online payment modes during the registration window.

Salary and pay scale details for Junior Engineer, Junior Accountant, and Junior Assistant / Commercial Assistant-II are governed by the probation and pay matrix rules specified in Advertisement No. RVUN/P&A/Rectt.2026-27.

Candidates must carry a clear printed copy of their RVUNL Admit Card 2026, a valid original photo identity proof such as Aadhaar Card, PAN Card, Voter ID, or Passport, and recent passport-size photographs.

The official portal for Rajasthan Rajya Vidyut Utpadan Nigam Limited and the Rajasthan Energy Department is https://energy.rajasthan.gov.in/home, where all recruitment notices and updates are published.
