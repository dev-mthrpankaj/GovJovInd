Complete context

About This Recruitment

SSC Stenographer Grade C & D Examination 2025 is a Staff Selection Commission recruitment drive to fill 1,715 Stenographer posts (232 Grade C and 1,483 Grade D) across various Ministries, Departments, and Offices of the Government of India. The official notification was published in June 2025, and the online application window remained open from 6 June 2025 to 26 June 2025.

The Computer Based Test (CBT) was conducted on 6-11 August 2025. The written result was declared on 28 November 2025, the Skill Test was held on 28-29 January 2026, and the final answer key was released on 2 January 2026. The First Round of Tentative Allocation (FRTA) was initially declared on 18 June 2026, later revised on 31 July 2026, and the final result was declared on 25 August 2026.

The Score Card for both qualified and non-qualified candidates was released on 7 October 2026. Candidates can access their final marks by logging in with their Registered ID and Password on the official SSC website until 6 November 2026 (6:00 PM).

Basic eligibility is 12th (Intermediate) pass from a recognised board. The age limit is 18 to 30 years for Grade C and 18 to 27 years for Grade D as on 1 August 2025, with relaxation for reserved categories as per SSC rules.
