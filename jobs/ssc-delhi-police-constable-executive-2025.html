Complete context

About This Recruitment

The Staff Selection Commission (SSC) is conducting the Delhi Police Constable (Executive) Examination 2025 for recruitment to 7565 posts (revised to 7201) of Constable (Executive) Male and Female in Delhi Police. The Computer-Based Examination (CBE) was held from 18 December 2025 to 6 January 2026, and the result was declared on 19 June 2026.

Shortlisted candidates are now required to appear for the Physical Endurance and Measurement Test (PE&MT) and Document Verification. The PE&MT admit card has been released on 10 September 2026 for the revised schedule. The physical test will be held from 18 to 26 September 2026. Candidates must download the fresh admit card from the official website and carry it along with valid photo ID and required documents.

Candidates must carry the printed fresh e-Admit Card, valid photo ID, and all original certificates along with self-attested photocopies for document verification at the PE&MT venue.

Eligibility Criteria

Age Limit

18 to 25 years as on 01 July 2025.

Age relaxation applicable as per SSC and Delhi Police rules (SC/ST: 5 years, OBC: 3 years, Ex-Servicemen: as per rules).

Education

10+2 (Senior Secondary) passed from a recognized Board. Educational qualification is relaxable up to 11th passed for sons/daughters of Delhi Police personnel and certain categories.

Male candidates must possess a valid LMV driving license (motorcycle or car) as on the date of PE&MT. Learner license is not acceptable.

Nationality / Other

Indian citizens fulfilling the post-specific eligibility conditions may apply. The post is not identified as suitable for Persons with Benchmark Disabilities (PwBD).

Refer to official notification for detailed conditions.

What to do at the current stage

How to Download PE&MT Admit Card

  1. Visit the official Delhi Police website at delhipolice.gov.in.
  2. Open the Recruitment section on the homepage.
  3. Click on the link for Delhi Police Constable (Executive) PE&MT e-Admit Card 2025-2026.
  4. Enter your Registration Number / Roll Number and Date of Birth.
  5. Submit the details after entering the captcha code.
  6. Download and print the admit card for the physical test.
  7. Carry the printed admit card along with valid photo ID and required documents to the PE&MT venue.

Original Application Process

Preserved for recruitment history and reference after the application stage closes.

  1. Visit the official SSC website at ssc.gov.in.
  2. Complete One-Time Registration (OTR) on the new SSC website.
  3. Read the Delhi Police Constable notification carefully.
  4. Open the application link and fill the online form.
  5. Upload the required documents (photograph and signature).
  6. Pay the applicable fee (Rs. 100 for General/OBC/EWS).
  7. Submit and save the confirmation page for future reference.
