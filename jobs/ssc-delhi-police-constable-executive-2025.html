About This Recruitment

SSC Delhi Police Constable Executive Recruitment 2025 is a Staff Selection Commission recruitment drive for 7,565 Constable (Executive) vacancies. The computer-based examination, result and final answer key stages are complete, and the recruitment has moved to PE&MT/PET.

Candidates who qualified the CBT stage should focus on the current physical-stage admit card and Delhi Police PE&MT/PET notice. The page preserves earlier application, exam, answer-key and result milestones for reference.

Shortlisted candidates should carry the PE&MT admit card, photo identity proof and all documents required in the physical-stage instructions.
