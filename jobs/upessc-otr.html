Understand the service

What is UPESSC OTR?

UPESSC OTR stands for One Time Registration of the Uttar Pradesh Education Service Selection Commission. It is a permanent candidate profile for recruitment exams run by the commission for education-sector posts in Uttar Pradesh. The portal describes it simply: register once, then apply to every examination.

Once your profile is created you receive an OTR ID. Whenever the commission opens an advertisement, you sign in with that profile and submit the application for that exam. The OTR was also required before applying for UPTET 2026, so it is the first step for most UPESSC aspirants.

Important: Creating an OTR does not mean you have applied for any exam. OTR and the individual exam application are separate steps, each with its own deadline.

03

Why it matters

Why UPESSC OTR is Useful

One Candidate Profile

A single OTR ID covers all recruitment of the commission, so you do not register again for each exam.

Less Repeated Entry

Your core personal, contact and education details live in one profile instead of being typed for every form.

Faster Applications

When a new advertisement opens, you sign in and apply from your existing profile.

Multiple Applications

Where an advertisement allows several subjects or posts, each is a separate application from the same OTR.

Online Access

Registration, sign in and applications all happen on the official UPESSC portal.

Consistent Details

One profile helps avoid small mismatches in details you would otherwise retype many times.

09

Review before you submit

UPESSC OTR Update & Correction

Check every personal, contact and education entry carefully before you submit your OTR. A wrong name, date of birth or qualification can cause problems later in an application.

Signed-in candidates can update their details on the portal, but which fields can be edited may depend on the stage and the exam. Use only the official option you see after login, and do not create a second OTR to fix a mistake unless the commission tells you to.

Do not mix them up

UPESSC OTR vs UPSSSC OTR

UPESSC OTR

For exams of the Uttar Pradesh Education Service Selection Commission. Register at apply.upessc.org.

UPSSSC OTR

For exams of the Uttar Pradesh Subordinate Services Selection Commission, with its own separate portal. See our UPSSSC OTR guide.

The two are different commissions with separate registrations. An OTR ID from one cannot be used on the other.

हिंदी में जानकारी

UPESSC OTR क्या है और कैसे करें?

UPESSC OTR यानी उत्तर प्रदेश शिक्षा सेवा चयन आयोग का वन टाइम रजिस्ट्रेशन। यह एक स्थायी प्रोफाइल है, जिसे एक बार बनाने के बाद आप आयोग की हर खुली भर्ती के लिए आवेदन कर सकते हैं।

रजिस्ट्रेशन के लिए apply.upessc.org/register खोलें, नया अभ्यर्थी के रूप में OTR ID बनाएं, व्यक्तिगत, संपर्क और शैक्षणिक विवरण भरें, सभी जानकारी जांचकर सबमिट करें और अपनी OTR ID सुरक्षित रखें। लॉगिन के लिए OTR ID, मोबाइल नंबर या ईमेल का उपयोग किया जा सकता है।

ध्यान रखें कि OTR बनाना किसी परीक्षा का आवेदन नहीं है। हर भर्ती का आवेदन अलग से करना होता है, जिसकी तिथि और शुल्क अलग हो सकते हैं।
