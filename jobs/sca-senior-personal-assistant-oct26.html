Complete context

About This Recruitment

Supreme Court of India (SCI) Senior Personal Assistant Recruitment 2026 is a direct recruitment drive to fill 65 tentative vacancies for the post of Senior Personal Assistant (Group 'B', Non-Gazetted) in the Supreme Court of India Registry, New Delhi. The official notification (Advt. No. F.6/11/SPA/2026-SC (RC)) was released on 12 October 2026.

The online application window will remain open from 20 October 2026 to 10 November 2026 (up to 11:55 PM). The objective-type written examination is tentatively scheduled for 7 December 2026. The answer key will be displayed on 8 December 2026, and the objection window will remain open from 8 to 9 December 2026.

The selection process consists of four stages: Objective Type Test (100 marks), English Shorthand Test (100 marks), Computer Typing Speed Test (20 marks), and Interview (30 marks). Candidates must qualify each stage to be considered for final selection. The pay scale is Level 8 of the Pay Matrix (Rs. 47,600 to Rs. 1,51,100).

Basic eligibility includes a Bachelor's Degree from a recognised university, English shorthand speed of 110 words per minute, and computer typing speed of 40 words per minute. The age limit is 18 to 30 years as on 1 October 2026, with age relaxation for reserved categories as per Government of India rules.

Eligibility Criteria

Age Limit

18 to 30 years as on 1 October 2026. Candidate must not be born after 01/10/2008 and not before 02/10/1996.

Age relaxation applicable for SC, ST, OBC (NCL), PwD, Ex-Servicemen, and Dependants of Freedom Fighters as per Government of India rules. Departmental candidates of Supreme Court Registry have no upper age limit.

Education

Bachelor's Degree from a recognised University in India. Proficiency in English Shorthand with a speed of 110 words per minute. Knowledge of computer operation with English typing speed of 40 words per minute.

Candidates must possess a valid degree and meet the prescribed shorthand and typing speed requirements.

Nationality / Other

Indian citizens fulfilling the post-specific eligibility conditions may apply. Refer to the official notification for complete details.
