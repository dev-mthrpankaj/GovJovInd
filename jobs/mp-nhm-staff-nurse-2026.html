Complete context

About This Recruitment

National Health Mission, Madhya Pradesh (MP NHM) has released the official notification for MP NHM Samvida Staff Nurse Recruitment 2026 to fill 301 contractual Staff Nurse posts across Madhya Pradesh. The online application window started on 20 November 2026 and remains active until 05 December 2026 through the official MPOnline portal at iforms.mponline.gov.in.

Candidates who have passed Class 12th with Physics, Chemistry, and Biology (PCB) and completed a 4-year B.Sc. Nursing course or a 2-year Post Basic B.Sc. Nursing course after General Nursing Training are eligible to apply. Applicants must also hold a valid and active registration with the Madhya Pradesh Nurses Registration Council.

The age limit for MP NHM Samvida Staff Nurse Recruitment 2026 is 21 to 40 years as on 01 January 2026, with category-wise age relaxation applicable as per Madhya Pradesh government rules. Selected candidates will receive a monthly salary of Rs.28,700, and there is no application fee (Rs.0) for any category.
