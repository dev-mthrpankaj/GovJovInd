Complete context

About This Recruitment

Madhya Pradesh Employees Selection Board (MPESB) released the official notification for MPESB MP Police Constable General Duty (GD) Recruitment 2026 to fill 7,500 posts in Madhya Pradesh Police. The vacancy distribution includes 6,800 posts for Constable GD (General Duty) and 700 posts for Constable GD (General Duty Special).

The online application window remained open from 22 September 2026 to 06 October 2026, with the fee payment deadline on 06 October 2026 and the form correction window closing on 11 October 2026. On 22 November 2026, MPESB published an official notice postponing the written examination that was scheduled for 19 November 2026. The revised exam date and admit card schedule will be notified on esb.mp.gov.in.

Candidates who passed 10th Class under the 10+2 system, Higher Secondary, or equivalent from a recognized board were eligible to apply, while ST category candidates required an 8th Class pass or equivalent. The prescribed age limit is 18 to 33 years for Male candidates and 18 to 38 years for Female candidates as on 06 October 2026.

Physical eligibility standards require a minimum height of 168 cm for Male candidates and 155 cm for Female candidates, along with a chest measurement of 81 to 86 cm for Male candidates. Examination centres are designated across 13 districts in Madhya Pradesh: Bhopal, Indore, Jabalpur, Khandwa, Neemuch, Ratlam, Rewa, Sagar, Satna, Sidhi, Ujjain, Badwani, and Anoopur.
