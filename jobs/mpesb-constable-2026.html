Complete context

About This Recruitment

MPESB MP Police Constable General Duty (GD) Recruitment 2026 is conducted by Madhya Pradesh Employees Selection Board (MPESB) to fill 7,500 Constable vacancies in Madhya Pradesh Police, comprising 6,800 posts for Constable GD (General Duty) and 700 posts for Constable GD (General Duty Special). The official rulebook (PCRT GD 2026) was published on 09 September 2026, and the online application window remained open from 22 September 2026 to 06 October 2026, with form corrections permitted until 11 October 2026.

On 22 November 2026, MPESB issued an official postponement notice deferring the written examination earlier scheduled for 19 November 2026. On 23 November 2026, MPESB released the New Exam Date Notice confirming that the rescheduled Computer Based Test (CBT) will be conducted on 09 December 2026 across 13 exam districts in Madhya Pradesh: Bhopal, Indore, Jabalpur, Khandwa, Neemuch, Ratlam, Rewa, Sagar, Satna, Sidhi, Ujjain, Badwani and Anoopur.

Basic educational eligibility requires passing the Class 10th examination under the 10+2 system or Higher Secondary from a recognised board, while Scheduled Tribe (ST) candidates are eligible with a Class 8th pass qualification. The age limit is 18 to 33 years for male candidates and 18 to 38 years for female candidates as on 06 October 2026, with reserved category age relaxation as per MPESB rules.

Selection involves five sequential stages: Written Examination (CBT), Physical Efficiency and Measurement Test (PET/PST) requiring 168 cm height and 81–86 cm chest for males and 155 cm height for females, Document Verification, Medical Examination and Final Merit List. Admit cards for the 09 December 2026 exam will be released on esb.mp.gov.in as per the official schedule.

What to do at the current stage

What Candidates Should Do for the Rescheduled Exam

  1. Download the official New Exam Date Notice dated 23 November 2026 from Important Links and verify the rescheduled exam date of 09 December 2026.
  2. Keep your MPESB application number, registration credentials and date of birth ready to download the admit card once issued.
  3. Regularly monitor the official portal esb.mp.gov.in for test city intimation and admit card release ahead of 09 December 2026.
  4. Check your allotted exam district among the 13 Madhya Pradesh centres (Bhopal, Indore, Jabalpur, Khandwa, Neemuch, Ratlam, Rewa, Sagar, Satna, Sidhi, Ujjain, Badwani and Anoopur).
  5. Keep a valid original photo identity proof (Aadhaar Card, Voter ID, PAN Card or Driving Licence) ready for exam day verification.
  6. Continue preparing for the Written Examination (CBT) and Physical Efficiency & Measurement Test (PET/PST) standards specified in the rulebook.

Original Application Process

Preserved for recruitment history and reference after the application stage closes.

  1. Visited the official MPESB portal at esb.mp.gov.in or esb.mponline.gov.in.
  2. Completed MPESB Profile Registration using a valid mobile number, email ID and e-KYC.
  3. Selected the MP Police Constable General Duty (GD) Recruitment 2026 application link.
  4. Filled in personal, domicile, category, exam district preference and educational details.
  5. Uploaded scanned photograph, signature and handwritten declaration in prescribed format.
  6. Paid the application fee (Rs.560 for General/Other State or Rs.310 for MP reserved categories).
  7. Submitted the online form before 06 October 2026 and saved the confirmation printout.

Frequently Asked Questions

The new written exam date for MPESB MP Police Constable General Duty (GD) Recruitment 2026 is 09 December 2026. Madhya Pradesh Employees Selection Board (MPESB) issued the revised schedule notice on 23 November 2026 after postponing the earlier 19 November 2026 exam date.

Madhya Pradesh Employees Selection Board (MPESB) postponed the written examination originally scheduled for 19 November 2026 via an official notice on 22 November 2026. A subsequent notice released on 23 November 2026 confirmed the rescheduled exam date as 09 December 2026.

The MPESB MP Police Constable Admit Card 2026 will be released on the official portal esb.mp.gov.in as per the official schedule ahead of the 09 December 2026 written examination. Candidates can download it using their application number and date of birth.

Madhya Pradesh Employees Selection Board (MPESB) has advertised a total of 7,500 vacancies under MP Police Constable Recruitment 2026. This includes 6,800 posts for Constable GD (General Duty) and 700 posts for Constable GD (General Duty Special).

The online application window for MPESB MP Police Constable Recruitment 2026 ran from 22 September 2026 to 06 October 2026. Fee payment closed on 06 October 2026, and the online application form correction window remained open until 11 October 2026.

Candidates who passed the Class 10th examination under the 10+2 system or Higher Secondary from a recognised board are eligible for MPESB MP Police Constable 2026. Candidates belonging to the Scheduled Tribe (ST) category are eligible with a Class 8th pass qualification.
