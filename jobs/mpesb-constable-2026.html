Complete context

About This Recruitment

MPESB MP Police Constable Recruitment 2026 is a Madhya Pradesh police recruitment drive for 7,500 Constable GD posts. The official rulebook is available and online applications are scheduled from 22 September to 6 October 2026.

The recruitment includes 6,800 Constable GD posts and 700 Constable GD Special posts. Candidates should check educational, age, category and physical eligibility carefully before applying.

Candidates should keep their photograph, signature, identity proof, educational certificates, category documents and applicable registration details ready before starting the online form.

Eligibility Criteria

Age Limit

Minimum 18 years. Maximum 33 years for male candidates and 38 years for female candidates, as on 6 October 2026.

Age relaxation is applicable as per the MPESB notification/rulebook.

Education

10th Class under the 10+2 system, Higher Secondary, or equivalent from a recognized board. For ST category, 8th Class or equivalent is stated in the supplied recruitment information.

Candidates should verify post/category-specific qualification requirements in the official rulebook.

Nationality / Other

Candidates must satisfy the category, domicile, physical and other eligibility conditions prescribed in the official rulebook.

Refer to official notification for detailed conditions.
