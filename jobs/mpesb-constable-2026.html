Complete context

About This Recruitment

Madhya Pradesh Employees Selection Board (MPESB) has released the notification for MP Police Constable General Duty (GD) Recruitment 2026 for a total of 7,500 posts. The online application window is open from 22 September 2026 to 06 October 2026.

This recruitment covers Constable GD (General Duty) and Constable GD (Special) posts under the Home Department, Government of Madhya Pradesh. Eligible candidates who have passed Class 10 (or Class 8 for ST category) can apply online through the official MPESB portal.

The selection process includes a written examination, physical efficiency test (PET/PST), document verification, and medical examination. The written exam is scheduled for 19 November 2026.
