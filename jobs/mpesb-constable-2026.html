About This Recruitment

MPESB MP Police Constable Recruitment 2026 is a Madhya Pradesh police recruitment drive for 7,500 Constable GD posts. The official rulebook is available and online applications are scheduled from 22 September to 6 October 2026.

The recruitment includes 6,800 Constable GD posts and 700 Constable GD Special posts. Candidates should check educational, age, category and physical eligibility carefully before applying.

Candidates should keep their photograph, signature, identity proof, educational certificates, category documents and applicable registration details ready before starting the online form.
