Complete context

About This Recruitment

CSBC Bihar Police Constable GD Special Branch Recruitment 2026 is a Central Selection Board of Constable (CSBC) recruitment drive to fill 83 Constable (General Duty Close Cadre) posts in the Special Branch of Bihar Police. The official notification (Advt. No. 01/2026) was published on 29 January 2026, and the online application window remained open from 06 February 2026 to 05 March 2026.

The written examination was conducted on 24 June 2026 across 38 revenue districts of Bihar. A total of 64,408 candidates appeared in the written exam, and 254 candidates were shortlisted for the Physical Standard Test (PST). The written result was declared on 18 August 2026, and the PST/DV admit card was released on 05 September 2026.

The Physical Standard Test (PST) and Document Verification (DV) were held on 12 September 2026 at Bihar Veterinary College, Patna. A total of 250 candidates appeared in the physical test (156 male and 94 female). Based on the PST and written exam performance, the final merit list was prepared and the final result was declared on 09 October 2026.

A total of 83 candidates (52 male and 31 female) have been selected. Joining is scheduled from 25 October to 20 November 2026. Basic eligibility is 10+2 (Intermediate) from a recognised board. The age limit is 18 to 25 years as on 01 August 2026, with relaxation for reserved categories as per Bihar Police rules.
