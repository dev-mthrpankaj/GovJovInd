About This Recruitment

Delhi Subordinate Services Selection Board (DSSSB) has released the Answer Key 2026 for various posts. This is a multi-stage recruitment covering numerous post codes across Delhi government departments. The answer key and objection window are now available for candidates who appeared in the written examinations conducted between November 2025 and July 2026.

The DSSSB recruitment process typically includes a written examination (Tier-I and/or Tier-II), followed by skill tests, document verification, and medical examination, depending on the specific post. This permanent lifecycle page will continue to track every stage from answer key to final result and joining.

Candidates should download the answer key, compare their responses, and raise objections within the official window if required. All future updates—including revised answer keys, results, cut-offs, and DV schedules—will be published on this same URL.
