About This Recruitment

PGIMER Group C Various Post Recruitment 2026 is a direct-recruitment drive under Advertisement No. PGI/RC/2026/053/2192 dated 1 September 2026. The recruitment covers 60 advertised vacancies across PGIMER, Chandigarh and the PGI Satellite Centre, Sangrur, Punjab.

For PGIMER Chandigarh, the notified posts include Library Attendant Grade-I, Animal Attendant, Barber and Multi Tasking Staff (Sanitation) Grade-II. For the PGI Satellite Centre, Sangrur, the notified posts include Medical Officer, Computer Programmer and Pharmacist Grade-II.

Online applications are scheduled from 3 September 2026 to 19 October 2026. Candidates should verify the post-specific qualification, experience, age conditions and supporting documents in the official detailed guidelines before submitting the form.
