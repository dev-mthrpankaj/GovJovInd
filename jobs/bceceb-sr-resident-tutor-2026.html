About This Recruitment

BCECEB Senior Resident & Tutor Recruitment 2026 is a Bihar Combined Entrance Competitive Examination Board (BCECEB) recruitment drive to fill 575 Senior Resident/Tutor posts in the Health Department, Government of Bihar. The official notification (Advertisement No. BCECEB/Health(SR)/2026/(III)/01) was released in November 2026, and the online application window is open from 02 November 2026 to 23 November 2026.

The recruitment offers a three-year tenure appointment across 10 medical departments, including Radiotherapy (72 posts), Radiology (68 posts), and Tuberculosis & Chest Diseases (68 posts). Selected candidates will receive a monthly remuneration of Rs. 1,15,000.

Eligibility requires a postgraduate medical qualification (MD/MS/DNB) in the concerned speciality and valid medical registration under the National Medical Commission (NMC). The age limit is up to 37 years for Unreserved Male, 40 years for Unreserved Female/BC/EBC, and 42 years for SC/ST candidates, as on 01 August 2026. Age relaxation is applicable as per rules.
