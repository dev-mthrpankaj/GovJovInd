Complete context

About This Recruitment

BCECEB Senior Resident & Tutor Recruitment 2026 is a Bihar Combined Entrance Competitive Examination Board (BCECEB) recruitment drive to fill 575 Senior Resident/Tutor posts in the Health Department, Government of Bihar. The official notification (Advertisement No. BCECEB/Health(SR)/2026/(III)/01) was released in November 2026, and the online application window is open from 02 November 2026 to 23 November 2026.

The recruitment offers a three-year tenure appointment across 10 medical departments, including Radiotherapy (72 posts), Radiology (68 posts), and Tuberculosis & Chest Diseases (68 posts). Selected candidates will receive a monthly remuneration of Rs. 1,15,000.

Eligibility requires a postgraduate medical qualification (MD/MS/DNB) in the concerned speciality and valid medical registration under the National Medical Commission (NMC). The age limit is up to 37 years for Unreserved Male, 40 years for Unreserved Female/BC/EBC, and 42 years for SC/ST candidates, as on 01 August 2026. Age relaxation is applicable as per rules.

Eligibility Criteria

Age Limit

Maximum age: 37 years for Unreserved Male, 40 years for Unreserved Female/BC/EBC, and 42 years for SC/ST candidates as on 01 August 2026.

Age relaxation applicable for reserved categories, Bihar Health Service cadre members (5 years), and PwD candidates (10 years) as per rules.

Education

Postgraduate medical qualification (MD/MS/DNB) in the concerned speciality from a recognised institution. Valid medical registration under NMC/Medical Council is mandatory.

DNB Super Speciality and other prescribed postgraduate qualifications recognised under NMC rules are also accepted.

Nationality / Other

Indian citizens fulfilling the post-specific eligibility conditions may apply. Candidates who have already completed a three-year Senior Resident/Tutor tenure in the same subject are not eligible.

Refer to official notification for detailed conditions.

What to do at the current stage

How to Apply Online

  1. Visit the official BCECEB website at bceceboard.bihar.gov.in.
  2. Click on the 'Senior Resident/Tutor Recruitment 2026' link.
  3. Register with a valid email ID and mobile number.
  4. Fill the online application form with personal, educational and category details.
  5. Upload scanned photograph, signature and required documents.
  6. Pay the counselling fee of Rs. 2,250 online.
  7. Submit the form and download the confirmation page for future reference.
