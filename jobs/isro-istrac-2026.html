About This Recruitment

The Indian Space Research Organisation (ISRO) has announced the exam date for its ISTRAC Various Post Recruitment 2026. This permanent page tracks the entire recruitment lifecycle for 26 posts including Technical Assistant, Scientific Assistant, Library Assistant, Technician-B, Draughtsman, and Cook.

The online application window was held from 27 June 2026 to 20 July 2026. The written examination is now scheduled for 25 September 2026. Candidates who have successfully applied should now prepare for the exam and check this page regularly for admit card updates.

This page will be updated with the admit card link, answer key, result, and all subsequent stages. Bookmark this permanent URL to avoid missing any official update.
