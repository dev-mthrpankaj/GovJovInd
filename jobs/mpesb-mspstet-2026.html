About This Recruitment

Madhya Pradesh Employees Selection Board (MPESB) has released the notification for MPESB Primary and Secondary Teachers MSPSTET 2026. This is an eligibility test for teachers currently working in primary and secondary schools in Madhya Pradesh.

The online application window opened on 21 August 2026 and the last date has now been extended to 05 October 2026. The application fee is Rs.1060 for all category candidates, which includes portal charges of Rs.60.

The exam is scheduled for 12 October 2026. Candidates should keep their registration details, photograph, signature, and eligibility documents ready before starting the online form.
