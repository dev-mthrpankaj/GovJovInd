Complete context

About This Recruitment

IBPS Specialist Officers XVI (CRP SPL-XVI) is a Common Recruitment Process conducted by the Institute of Banking Personnel Selection to recruit Specialist Officers (Scale I) in participating public sector banks for the 2027-28 recruitment cycle. The official notification was released on 30 June 2026, and the online application window remained open from 1 July 2026 to 26 July 2026 (extended from 21 July).

The recruitment covers posts including IT Officer, Agricultural Field Officer (AFO), Rajbhasha Adhikari, Law Officer, and HR/Personnel Officer. The initial vacancy count of 745 was revised to 1,043 on 27 August 2026 after Indian Bank reported additional vacancies. The prelims exam was held on 29 August 2026, and the prelims result was declared on 15 October 2026.

The mains exam is scheduled for 1 November 2026, followed by personal interviews in November-December 2026 and provisional allotment in January 2027. Selection is based on the mains examination (80% weightage for most posts) and interview performance.

Basic eligibility varies by post: IT Officer requires a 4-year engineering degree in Computer Science/IT or equivalent; AFO requires a 4-year agriculture-related degree; Law Officer requires an LLB with Bar Council enrollment; Rajbhasha Adhikari requires a PG in Hindi/Sanskrit with English; HR/Personnel Officer requires a PG in HR/Personnel Management. The age limit is 20 to 30 years as on 1 July 2026, with relaxation for reserved categories.

Original Application Process

Preserved for recruitment history and reference after the application stage closes.

  1. Visit the official IBPS website at ibps.in.
  2. Click on the CRP Specialist Officers (CRP-SPL-XVI) link.
  3. Complete the new registration with valid email ID and mobile number.
  4. Fill the online application form with accurate personal and educational details.
  5. Upload photograph, signature and required documents.
  6. Pay the applicable examination fee online.
