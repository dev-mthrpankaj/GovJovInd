Complete context

About This Recruitment

IBPS Specialist Officer (SO) XVI Recruitment 2026 is conducted through the Common Recruitment Process (CRP SPL-XVI) for selection of Specialist Officers (Scale I) in participating public sector banks across India. The recruitment covers posts including IT Officer, Agricultural Field Officer, Rajbhasha Adhikari, Law Officer, HR/Personnel Officer, and Marketing Officer.

The official notification was released in June 2026, with online applications accepted from 01 July to 26 July 2026 (extended). The Preliminary examination was held in August 2026, and the Prelims result was declared on 15 October 2026. Candidates who qualified in the Prelims are now eligible to appear for the Mains examination.

The Mains admit card was released on 22 October 2026, and the Mains examination is scheduled for 01 November 2026. The selection process continues with an Interview for shortlisted candidates, followed by Document Verification and Medical Examination as applicable. Provisional allotment is expected in January 2027.

The age limit is 20 to 30 years as on 01 July 2026, with relaxation for reserved categories. Educational qualifications vary by post, ranging from a 4-year engineering degree for IT Officer to a postgraduate degree in Hindi/Sanskrit for Rajbhasha Adhikari, LL.B. for Law Officer, and relevant postgraduate diplomas for HR and Marketing Officer posts.
