Complete context

About This Recruitment

UPSSSC Combined Lower Subordinate Services (Graduate Level) Main Examination 2026 is a Uttar Pradesh Subordinate Services Selection Commission (UPSSSC) recruitment drive to fill 2,587 Group B and Group C posts across various departments of the Uttar Pradesh government. The official notification (Advertisement No. 07-Exam/2026) was released on 30 March 2026, and the online application window remained open from 29 May 2026 to 25 June 2026.

The Mains written examination was conducted on 23 August 2026. The provisional answer key was released on 24 August 2026, and the revised master-set answer key was published on 13 November 2026 after the objection process. The next stages include the declaration of the final result, cut-off marks and document verification.

Candidates must have a Graduation degree in any stream from a recognised university and a valid UP PET 2025 score card. The age limit is 18 to 40 years as on 01 July 2026, with relaxation for reserved categories as per UPSSSC rules. The application fee is Rs. 25 for all categories.

How to Check Revised Answer Key

  1. Visit the official UPSSSC website at upsssc.gov.in.

  3. Look for the link 'Revised Answer Key – Lower PCS Mains 2026'.
  4. Download the master-set answer key PDF.
  5. Match your question paper set and compare your responses.
  6. Calculate your expected score for the Mains examination.

Original Application Process

Preserved for recruitment history and reference after the application stage closes.

  1. Visit the official UPSSSC website at upsssc.gov.in.
  2. Read the official notification (Advt. No. 07-Exam/2026) carefully.
  3. Register on the UPSSSC portal with a valid email ID and mobile number.
  4. Fill the online application form with accurate personal and educational details.
