Complete context

About This Recruitment

SSC Delhi Police Constable (Driver) Examination 2025 is a Staff Selection Commission recruitment drive to fill 660 Driver posts (Constable/Driver – Male) in Delhi Police. The official notification was published in September 2025, and the online application window remained open from 24 September 2025 to 15 October 2025.

The Computer Based Test (CBT) was conducted on 16–17 December 2025. The written result was declared on 29 April 2026, a final answer key notice was issued on 26 May 2026, and an additional result was published on 25 June 2026. The Physical Endurance & Measurement Test (PE & MT) is scheduled for 08–09 September 2026, and the Driving Test follows as the next stage.

The Physical Admit Card for the PET/PST round was released on 26 September 2026, and the physical examination will be held on 03 October 2026. Candidates must carry the printed Physical Admit Card, a valid original photo ID, and their HMV driving licence to the physical test centre.

Basic eligibility is 10+2 (Intermediate) from a recognised board plus a valid Heavy Motor Vehicle (HMV) driving licence. The age limit is 21 to 30 years as on 01 July 2025, with relaxation for reserved categories as per SSC rules.

Eligibility Criteria

Age Limit

21 to 40 years for Urban Primary Schools and 21 to 62 years for Aided Primary Schools as on 01 July 2026.

Age relaxation is applicable for SC, ST, OBC, PwBD and other reserved categories as per UPESSC rules.

Education

Bachelor's degree from a university established by law in India or recognised as equivalent, plus BTC, D.El.Ed, B.El.Ed or an equivalent teacher training qualification.

Candidates must have qualified the Teacher Eligibility Test (TET) for Classes 1 to 5 conducted by the Uttar Pradesh Government or Government of India.

Nationality / Other

Indian citizens fulfilling the post-specific eligibility conditions may apply. Refer to Advt. No. 05/2026 for complete details.

What to do at the current stage

How to Edit Your Application

  1. Open the official UPESSC application login portal.
  2. Log in using your registration number and password.
  3. Open the correction link for Advt. No. 05/2026.
  4. Edit only the fields permitted by UPESSC in the correction window.
  5. Review the changes and submit the corrected form.
  6. Download the updated confirmation page for your records.

Original Application Process

Preserved for recruitment history and reference after the application stage closes.

  1. Visit the official UPESSC application portal at apply.upessc.org.
  2. Complete OTR registration with a valid email ID and mobile number.
  3. Fill the online application form for Advt. No. 05/2026.
  4. Upload photograph, signature and required documents.
  5. Pay the applicable examination fee online.
  6. Submit the form and download the confirmation page.
