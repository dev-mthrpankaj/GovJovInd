Complete context

About This Recruitment

The Uttar Pradesh Education Service Selection Commission (UPESSC) has announced the recruitment of Primary Teachers (PRT) for 12,405 posts across the state. The official notification was released in September 2026, and the online application process commenced on 16 September 2026.

The application deadline has been extended from its original date to 04 November 2026, providing candidates with additional time to complete their submissions. The application correction window will be open until 07 November 2026.

This recruitment offers a significant opportunity for candidates holding a Bachelor’s degree and a recognized teacher training qualification such as BTC, D.El.Ed., or B.El.Ed., along with a valid UP TET qualification. The selection process will likely involve a written examination followed by document verification.

The pay scale for the Assistant Teacher post is Level-6, with a minimum salary of Rs. 35,400 and a maximum of Rs. 1,12,400 per month.

Eligibility Criteria

Age Limit

21 to 40 years for Urban Area posts; 21 to 62 years for Aided Schools (as on 01 July 2026).

Age relaxation applicable for SC, ST, OBC, PwBD, Ex-Servicemen and other reserved categories as per UP government rules.

Education

Bachelor's degree from a recognized university plus a recognized teacher training qualification (BTC, D.El.Ed., B.El.Ed., or equivalent) and a valid UP TET (Classes 1-5) qualification.

Candidates must possess a valid Teacher Eligibility Test (TET) certificate issued by the Government of UP or India.

Nationality / Other

Indian citizens fulfilling the post-specific eligibility conditions may apply. Refer to the official notification for complete details.

What to do at the current stage

How to Apply Online

  1. Visit the official UPESSC application portal at apply.upessc.org.
  2. Register using a valid email ID and mobile number to create an OTR (One Time Registration).
  3. Login using your registration ID and password.
  4. Fill the application form with personal, educational, and category details.
  5. Upload scanned documents (photo, signature, etc.) as per the specified format.
  6. Pay the application fee through online mode.
  7. Submit the form and download/print the confirmation page for future reference.
