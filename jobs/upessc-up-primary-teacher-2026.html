Complete context

About This Recruitment

Uttar Pradesh Education Service Selection Commission (UPESSC) has released the notification for UP Primary Teacher (Assistant Teacher) Recruitment 2026 under Advertisement No. 05/2026. The recruitment drive aims to fill a total of 12,405 posts of Assistant Teacher in Urban Area Primary Schools and Assistant Teacher in Affiliated Primary Schools (Aided Schools).

The online application window is open from 16 September 2026 to 15 October 2026. The written examination is scheduled to be held on 03 and 04 December 2026. Candidates must possess a graduation degree along with a recognized teacher training qualification (BTC, D.El.Ed, B.El.Ed, etc.) and must have qualified TET (Paper-I) conducted by the UP Government or the Central Government.

Eligibility Criteria

Age Limit

21 to 40 years as on 01 July 2026 (For Urban Area). For Aided Schools: Minimum 21 years, retirement age 62 years.

Age relaxation applicable as per UPESSC rules for SC/ST/OBC and other reserved categories.

Education

Graduation degree from a recognized university along with BTC / D.El.Ed / B.El.Ed / Special BTC / equivalent teacher training qualification recognized by NCTE. Must have qualified TET (Paper-I) conducted by UP Government or Central Government.

For detailed educational conditions and subject-specific requirements, refer to the official notification.

Nationality / Other

Indian citizens fulfilling the post-specific eligibility conditions may apply. Domicile requirements as per UPESSC rules.

Refer to official notification for detailed conditions.

How to Apply

  1. Visit the official UPESSC website and complete One-Time Registration (OTR) if not already done.
  2. Log in to the UPESSC application portal using your OTR credentials.
  3. Select the UP Primary Teacher Recruitment 2026 (Advt. No. 05/2026) application link.
  4. Fill the application form carefully with personal, educational and category details.
  5. Upload scanned photograph, signature and required documents in the prescribed format.
  6. Pay the applicable application fee online through Debit Card, Credit Card, Net Banking or UPI.
  7. Review all entries and submit the form before 15 October 2026. Save the confirmation page.
