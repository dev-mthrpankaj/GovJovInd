About This Recruitment

Uttar Pradesh Education Service Selection Commission (UPESSC) has released the notification for UP Primary Teacher (Assistant Teacher) Recruitment 2026 under Advertisement No. 05/2026. The recruitment drive aims to fill a total of 12,405 posts of Assistant Teacher in Urban Area Primary Schools and Assistant Teacher in Affiliated Primary Schools (Aided Schools).

The online application window is open from 16 September 2026 to 15 October 2026. The written examination is scheduled to be held on 03 and 04 December 2026. Candidates must possess a graduation degree along with a recognized teacher training qualification (BTC, D.El.Ed, B.El.Ed, etc.) and must have qualified TET (Paper-I) conducted by the UP Government or the Central Government.
