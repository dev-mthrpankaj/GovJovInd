Complete context

About This Recruitment

Uttar Pradesh Education Service Selection Commission (UPESSC) issued Advertisement No. 05/2026 on 15 September 2026 to recruit 12405 Assistant Teachers (Primary Teacher / PRT) across Uttar Pradesh. The online One Time Registration (OTR) and application window opened on 16 September 2026, closed after extension on 11 November 2026, and completed the form correction window on 13 November 2026.

Out of the 12405 advertised vacancies, 11508 posts are for Assistant Teacher in Urban Area Primary Schools across 47 districts of Uttar Pradesh (General 4746, OBC 3069, EWS 1116, SC 2379, ST 198). The remaining 897 posts are for Assistant Teacher in Affiliated Primary Schools for Boys and Girls in non-government aided schools (General 378, OBC 164, EWS 7, SC 348, ST 0, comprising 473 male and 424 female posts).

UPESSC released the Exam City Details on 23 November 2026 so applicants can check their allotted exam district prior to the written examination scheduled on 03 December 2026 and 04 December 2026. Final admit cards mentioning exact exam centre addresses and shift timings will be issued as per the official commission schedule before the exam.

Eligible candidates hold a Bachelor's degree from a recognised university, a government-recognised primary teacher training qualification such as BTC, D.El.Ed, B.El.Ed, or a two-year Diploma in Special Education, and a valid pass certificate in the Primary Level Teacher Eligibility Test (UPTET or CTET for Classes 1 to 5). Selected Assistant Teachers are placed in Pay Level-6 (Rs.35,400 to Rs.1,12,400).

Eligibility Criteria

Age Limit

21 to 40 years as on 01 July 2026 for Urban Area Primary Schools; minimum 21 years and superannuation age 62 years for Affiliated Aided Primary Schools.

Upper age relaxation is applicable for SC, ST, OBC, Divyang (PH), and other reserved categories of Uttar Pradesh as per UPESSC rules.

Education

Bachelor's degree from a recognised university along with BTC / D.El.Ed / B.El.Ed / RCI-approved 2-year Diploma in Special Education and Primary Level TET / CTET (Classes 1 to 5).

Urban Area Primary Schools (11508 posts: Gen 4746, OBC 3069, EWS 1116, SC 2379, ST 198); Affiliated Aided Primary Schools (897 posts: Gen 378, OBC 164, EWS 7, SC 348, ST 0).

Nationality / Other

Indian citizens fulfilling the prescribed academic, teacher training, and Primary TET/CTET criteria in Advertisement No. 05/2026 are eligible.

What to do at the current stage

How to Check UPESSC Primary Teacher Exam City 2026

  1. Open the official UPESSC candidate login portal at apply.upessc.org/login from Important Links.
  2. Enter your OTR Registration Number, Password or Date of Birth, and the security captcha code.
  3. Click on the Exam City Intimation link in your candidate dashboard.
  4. Verify your allotted exam district and scheduled exam date (03 December 2026 or 04 December 2026).
  5. Download and save a copy of the exam city slip for travel planning.
  6. Keep checking the official UPESSC website for the final Admit Card release containing exact centre address and shift timing.

Original Application Process

Preserved for recruitment history and reference after the application stage closes.

  1. Visit the official UPESSC website at upessc.up.gov.in or apply.upessc.org.
  2. Complete One Time Registration (OTR) using a valid email ID and mobile number.
  3. Log in with your OTR Registration Number and password to access the Advt. No. 05/2026 form.
  4. Select the post wing and fill in personal, educational, teacher training, and TET details.
  5. Upload scanned passport-size photograph, signature, and required documents.
  6. Pay the applicable examination fee online and download the confirmation page for reference.

Quick answers

Frequently Asked Questions

Yes, Uttar Pradesh Education Service Selection Commission (UPESSC) released the UP Primary Teacher Exam City Details on 23 November 2026. Candidates can log in at apply.upessc.org/login to view their allotted exam district.

The UPESSC UP Primary Teacher (Assistant Teacher) written examination will be conducted on 03 December 2026 and 04 December 2026 across Uttar Pradesh. Exact shift timings and exam centre addresses will appear on the admit card.

The UPESSC UP Primary Teacher Admit Card 2026 will be released on the official portal as per the commission schedule ahead of the 03 December 2026 exam. Candidates can download it using their OTR registration number and password.

UPESSC advertised 12405 total Assistant Teacher vacancies under Advertisement No. 05/2026. This includes 11508 posts in Urban Area Primary Schools and 897 posts in Affiliated Aided Primary Schools for Boys and Girls.

The online application window for UPESSC UP Primary Teacher Recruitment 2026 ran from 16 September 2026 to the extended deadline of 11 November 2026. Online form correction closed on 13 November 2026, and applications are now closed.

Candidates must hold a Bachelor degree from a recognised university along with BTC, D.El.Ed, B.El.Ed, or a two-year Diploma in Special Education. Passing the Class 1 to 5 Teacher Eligibility Test (UPTET or CTET) is mandatory.

The minimum age is 21 years and the maximum age is 40 years for Urban Area Primary Schools as on 01 July 2026, while Aided Primary Schools specify a minimum age of 21 years and superannuation at 62 years. Category age relaxation applies.

Selected UPESSC UP Primary Teachers (Assistant Teachers) are appointed under Pay Level-6 with a pay scale ranging from Rs.35,400 to Rs.1,12,400 per month. Allowances are admissible as per Uttar Pradesh Government rules.

The application fee was Rs.1000 for General, OBC, and EWS candidates, Rs.500 for SC and ST candidates, and Rs.300 for PH (Divyang) candidates. Fees were paid online through debit card, credit card, or internet banking.

Candidates can check their allotted exam district by visiting the official UPESSC candidate login portal at apply.upessc.org/login. Log in with your OTR registration number and password or date of birth to download the slip.

Selection for UPESSC UP Primary Teacher (Assistant Teacher) posts consists of a Written Examination followed by Document Verification and Final Merit List preparation. Shortlisted candidates must present original certificates at verification.

The official website of the Uttar Pradesh Education Service Selection Commission is https://upessc.up.gov.in/ and the candidate portal is https://apply.upessc.org/. All notices, exam city slips, admit cards, and results appear there.
