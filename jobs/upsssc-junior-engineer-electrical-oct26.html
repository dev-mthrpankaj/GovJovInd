Complete context

About This Recruitment

UPSSSC Combined Junior Engineer (Electrical) Recruitment 2026 is a Uttar Pradesh Subordinate Services Selection Commission recruitment drive to fill 380 Junior Engineer (Electrical), Electrician, Technical Assistant and Research Assistant posts across various departments of the Uttar Pradesh government. The official notification (Advt. No. 25-Exam/2026) was published on 12 October 2026.

The online application window will open on 06 November 2026 and will close on 27 November 2026. The application fee is Rs. 25 for all categories. Candidates must possess a three-year Diploma in Electrical Engineering from the UP Board of Technical Education or an equivalent AICTE-approved qualification, along with a valid UPSSSC PET-2025 scorecard.

The selection process typically includes a written examination followed by document verification. The exam date will be announced by UPSSSC in due course. Frequently Asked Questions

The last date to submit the online application for UPSSSC JE Electrical Recruitment 2026 is 27 November 2026. Candidates should complete registration and fee payment before the deadline to avoid last-minute issues.

A total of 380 vacancies for the Combined Junior Engineer (Electrical) and equivalent posts have been announced by UPSSSC. The category-wise breakup is General 177, OBC 85, EWS 33, SC 78 and ST 07.

The minimum age is 18 years and the maximum age is 40 years as on 01 July 2026. Age relaxation is applicable for reserved categories as per Uttar Pradesh government rules.

Candidates must possess a three-year Diploma in Electrical Engineering from the UP Board of Technical Education or an equivalent AICTE-approved qualification. A valid UPSSSC PET-2025 scorecard with positive normalized marks is also required.
