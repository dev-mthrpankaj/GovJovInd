About This Recruitment

SSC Delhi Police Head Constable (AWO/TPO) Examination 2025 is a recruitment drive conducted by the Staff Selection Commission for Head Constable (Assistant Wireless Operator / Tele-Printer Operator) posts in Delhi Police. The original notification advertised 552 vacancies, later revised to 443 posts. The notification was released on 24 September 2025, and online applications were accepted from 24 September to 15 October 2025.

The Computer Based Examination (CBT) was held from 15 January to 22 January 2026, and the result was declared on 29 May 2026. Shortlisted candidates are now appearing for the Physical Endurance & Measurement Test (PE&MT) and Document Verification, scheduled from 30 September to 1 October 2026.

The recruitment follows a multi-stage selection process including CBT, PE&MT, Trade Test, Computer Proficiency Test, and Document Verification. The admit card for the physical test has been released on 26 September 2026 and can be downloaded from the official SSC Northern Region website.
