Complete context

About This Recruitment

SSC Delhi Police Head Constable (AWO/TPO) Examination 2025 is a recruitment drive conducted by the Staff Selection Commission for Head Constable (Assistant Wireless Operator / Tele-Printer Operator) posts in Delhi Police. The original notification advertised 552 vacancies, later revised to 443 posts. The notification was released on 24 September 2025, and online applications were accepted from 24 September to 15 October 2025.

The Computer Based Examination (CBT) was held from 15 January to 22 January 2026, and the result was declared on 29 May 2026. Shortlisted candidates have now been issued the Physical Admit Card for the PE&MT and Document Verification, which will be conducted from 30 September to 1 October 2026.

The recruitment follows a multi-stage selection process including CBT, PE&MT, Trade Test, Computer Proficiency Test, and Document Verification. The admit card for the physical test was released on 26 September 2026 and can be downloaded from the official SSC Northern Region website.

Eligibility Criteria

Age Limit

18 to 27 years as on 01 July 2025.

SC/ST: 5 years; OBC: 3 years; Ex-Servicemen: 3 years; Departmental candidates: up to 40 years (UR/EWS), 43 years (OBC), 45 years (SC/ST).

Education

Passed 10+2 (Senior Secondary) with Science and Mathematics from a recognised board, OR National Trade Certificate (NTC) in Mechanic-cum-Operator Electronic Communication System.

Candidates must also have proficiency in computer operation and English word processing speed of 1000 key depressions in 15 minutes.

Nationality / Other

Indian citizens fulfilling the post-specific eligibility conditions. Candidates from all parts of the country are eligible to apply.

Refer to official notification for detailed conditions.

What to do at the current stage

How to Download Physical Admit Card

  1. Visit the official SSC Northern Region website at sscnr.nic.in.
  2. Click on the link for Delhi Police HC AWO/TPO Physical Admit Card 2026.
  3. Enter your registration number, date of birth, and other login details.
  4. Download the admit card PDF and take a clear printout.
  5. Carry the printed admit card along with a valid photo ID to the PE&MT centre.
  6. Read all instructions on the admit card carefully and comply strictly.

Original Application Process

Preserved for recruitment history and reference after the application stage closes.

  1. Visit the official SSC website at ssc.gov.in.
  2. Complete One-Time Registration (OTR) before filling the form.
  3. Read the Delhi Police HC AWO/TPO notification carefully.
  4. Open the application link and fill in the required details.
  5. Upload photograph, signature, and required documents.
  6. Pay the applicable fee online.
  7. Submit the form and save the confirmation page.
