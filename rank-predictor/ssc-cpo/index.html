SSC CPO Rank Predictor for Paper 1 Expected Rank

SSC CPO candidates can use the main Rank Predictor after checking the answer key or response sheet. Enter Maths, Reasoning, English and General Awareness attempts to estimate raw marks, normalized marks and category-wise comparison.

Official verification: Final SSC CPO cutoff, physical test shortlisting and merit decisions are issued only by SSC.
