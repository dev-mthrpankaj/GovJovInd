About BPSC Prosecution Officer Prelims Exam Notice

The Bihar Public Service Commission (BPSC) has officially published the preliminary competitive examination schedule and e-admit card availability guidelines for the 300 available vacancies of Assistant Prosecution Officer (APO) under Advertisement Number 13/2026. Law graduates who successfully completed their online registration between February 27 and March 20, 2026, can now log into the application system to check their respective details.

As per the official notice, the BPSC PO Preliminary examination will be carried out in an offline OMR-based setting on July 15, 2026. The examination is designed to test candidates across two comprehensive papers. The morning shift will evaluate General Studies, followed by the afternoon shift covering Law topics. Candidates are advised to download their e-admit cards early to avoid heavy server traffic on the day of the exam.

Securing a printed copy of the online hall ticket is a vital prerequisite for entry inside the examination venue. No individual will receive any physical admit card via post or courier. All particulars regarding the specific venue code, district name, and personal mapping will be clearly mentioned in the downloadable document.

How to Download BPSC Prosecution Officer Admit Card

  1. Open your web browser and go to the official BPSC online application portal at bpsconline.bihar.gov.in.
  2. Locate the candidate login panel displayed on the homepage.
  3. Provide your unique Username and secure Password created during the initial application process.
  4. Enter the verification captcha code accurately as shown on the screen and hit the 'Login' button.
  5. Once inside the candidate dashboard, click on the active link labeled 'Download e-Admit Card'.
  6. Review the details displayed, check the undertaking box if applicable, and proceed to download the file.
  7. Generate a high-quality physical printout of the admit card on an A4 size paper sheet for exam day usage.

Details Mentioned on BPSC PO Admit Card

  • Full Name of the Candidate.
  • Roll Number and Unique Registration Number.
  • Scanned Photograph and Signature.
  • Advertisement Number (13/2026) and Name of Post.
  • Date of the Written Examination (15 July 2026).
  • Shift Names and Exact Exam Timings.
  • Name and Address of the Allocated Exam Center.
  • Reporting Time and Gate Closure Limits.
  • Important Instructions and COVID/General discipline protocols.

Documents to Carry on Exam Day

  • Clear physical printout of the downloaded BPSC e-Admit Card.
  • Valid photo identity proof in original (Aadhaar Card, Voter ID, PAN Card, Driving License, or Passport).
  • Photocopy of the photo identity card, if specifically instructed in the guidelines.
  • Two recent passport-sized color photographs matching the one uploaded in the online form.
  • Reliable blue or black ballpoint pens for marking responses on the OMR sheet.

Exam Day Instructions

  • Reach the assigned examination center well ahead of the reporting time mentioned on the call letter.
  • Gate entry will be closed strictly prior to the commencement of each shift; late arrivals will not be permitted entry.
  • Electronic items such as mobile phones, calculators, smartwatches, Bluetooth devices, and log tables are strictly barred.
  • Ensure that the photograph and signature printed on the admit card are clear and legible.
  • Preserve the authenticated copy of the admit card safely throughout the selection stages until final joining.

Frequently Asked Questions

What is the BPSC Prosecution Officer Prelims exam date 2026?

The preliminary written competitive exam will be conducted on 15 July 2026.

When will the BPSC Prosecution Officer admit card download start?

The online e-admit card download facility goes live starting from 09 July 2026.

Where can I access the BPSC Prosecution Officer Admit Card download link?

You can access the official download option by logging in with your registration details at bpsconline.bihar.gov.in.

What login details are required to download the hall ticket?

Candidates must input the Username and Password provided during the application process.

What are the shift timings for the BPSC Prosecution Officer exam?

The exam takes place in two shifts: General Studies runs from 10:00 AM to 12:00 PM, and the Law paper runs from 2:30 PM to 4:30 PM.

How many posts are available under Advertisement 13/2026?

There are a total of 300 vacancies announced for the Prosecution Officer post in the Home Department of Bihar.

Can I change my allotted examination center?

No, the examination center assigned by the Bihar Public Service Commission is final, and no requests for modifications will be evaluated.

What items are prohibited inside the BPSC examination hall?

All electronic gadgets, books, notebooks, whiteboards, mobile communication tools, smart bands, and bags are entirely barred inside the center.
