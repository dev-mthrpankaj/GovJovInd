About RPSC JLO Admit Card & Written Exam 2026

The Rajasthan Public Service Commission (RPSC) has released official press notes regarding the written competitive examination for the recruitment of Junior Legal Officer (JLO) 2025-26. The recruitment drive is aimed at filling posts for the Jaipur Development Authority (JDA).

The written examination will take place across designated district headquarters in Rajasthan on 26 July 2026 and 27 July 2026. The exam consists of four objective-type papers spread across morning (09:00 AM to 12:00 PM) and afternoon (02:30 PM to 05:30 PM) sessions over the two days.

Candidates can access their e-admit card from 23 July 2026 using their Application Number and Date of Birth on the RPSC portal or directly through the Recruitment Portal inside the Rajasthan SSO portal (sso.rajasthan.gov.in).

How to Download RPSC JLO Admit Card 2026

  1. Visit the official RPSC website at rpsc.rajasthan.gov.in or log in to sso.rajasthan.gov.in.
  2. Click on the Admit Card for Junior Legal Officer 2025-26 link on the home page / Recruitment Portal.
  3. Select 'Get Admit Card' and enter your Application Number and Date of Birth.
  4. Enter the captcha code as shown on screen and submit the details.
  5. Your RPSC JLO e-Admit Card will appear on the screen. Verify all details.
  6. Download and print a clear copy of the e-admit card to bring to the examination centre.

Details Mentioned on Hall Ticket

  • Candidate Name and Roll Number
  • Application Number & Category
  • Exam Name and Post Title (Junior Legal Officer)
  • Exam Dates (26 & 27 July 2026) and Shift Timings
  • Exam Centre Name, Address, and District Headquarters
  • Candidate Photograph and Signature
  • Important OMR and Identification Instructions

Documents to Carry to the Exam Centre

  • Printed copy of RPSC JLO E-Admit Card with a recent clear colour photograph pasted on it.
  • Original latest colour Aadhaar Card for identity verification.
  • If the Aadhaar photo is old or unclear, carry an alternative original photo ID (Voter ID, Driving License, Passport) with a recent clear photo.
  • Blue/Black transparent ballpoint pen for marking the OMR sheet.

Exam Day Guidelines & OMR Rules

  • Entry Deadline: Entry into the examination centre will strictly close 60 minutes before the start time of each exam session. Arrive early to complete biometric and identity checks.
  • OMR 5th Option Rule: Each question has 5 options. If you do not wish to attempt a question, you must darken the 5th option circle. An extra 10 minutes is allotted at the end of each paper for this purpose.
  • Prohibited Items: Smartwatches, mobile phones, calculators, Bluetooth devices, and study material are completely banned inside the exam hall.

Frequently Asked Questions

When is the RPSC Junior Legal Officer JLO exam date 2026?

The RPSC Junior Legal Officer (JLO) written examination is scheduled for 26 July and 27 July 2026.

When can candidates download RPSC JLO Admit Card 2026?

The e-admit card download link will go live on 23 July 2026 on the official RPSC portal and Rajasthan SSO portal.

What details are required to download RPSC JLO Admit Card?

Candidates can download the hall ticket using their Application Number and Date of Birth on the RPSC portal or via SSO login.

What is the reporting time for RPSC JLO Examination?

Candidates must reach the exam centre at least 60 minutes before the commencement of the exam. Entry closes 60 minutes prior to exam start time.

Where can candidates download the RPSC JLO e-admit card?

Candidates can download the admit card from rpsc.rajasthan.gov.in or sso.rajasthan.gov.in.

What photo ID documents are allowed at the exam centre?

Candidates must carry an original latest color Aadhaar card. If the Aadhaar photo is unclear, carry a Voter ID, Passport, or Driving License with a clear photo.

How many papers are conducted in RPSC JLO Written Exam?

The examination consists of 4 objective-type papers conducted over two days in morning and afternoon sessions.

Is there extra time provided for marking OMR options?

Yes, an extra 10 minutes is provided at the end of each session to fill the 5th option in the OMR answer sheet.
