How to Download CBSE Skill Test Admit Card 2026

  1. Open your internet browser and navigate to the official recruitment webpage at cbse.gov.in.
  2. Locate and click on the specific notification title designated for "Public Notice: Download of Admit Card for DRQ2026 (Skill Test)".
  3. You will be redirected to the secure NIC Examination Services portal link.
  4. Enter your accurate Application Number / Registration Number and select your true Date of Birth.
  5. Type the security captcha verification pin visible on your screen accurately.
  6. Click on the login or submit element to populate your dedicated hall ticket on the device window.
  7. Save the file as a PDF document and acquire clear hardcopy prints to preserve for the examination day.

Details Mentioned on the Admit Card

  • Candidate Personal Data: Full Name, Father's Name, Roll Number, and Registration Identifier.
  • Photograph & Signature: Uploaded bio-images for identification verification.
  • Post Description: Particular non-teaching vacancy designation applied for.
  • Exam Parameters: Scheduled Skill Test Date, Session Timings, and Reporting Closure hours.
  • Venue Specifics: Comprehensive allotted venue laboratory address with location landmarks.
  • General Directives: Explicitly structural instructions layout for handling system hardware during assessment.

Essential Documents to Carry at the Test Venue

Candidates must ensure they possess the following verification documents to obtain access into the skill assessment lab spaces:

  • A clearly printed physical paper copy of the downloaded CBSE Skill Test Admit Card.
  • An authentic original government-issued photo identity proof card (Aadhaar Card, Passport, Voter ID, or Driving License).
  • Recent passport-sized coloration photographs matching the online registration form upload.

Key Exam Day Instructions

  • Reporting Early: Candidates must register presence at the test facility premises prior to the stated closure deadline. Late arrivals will strictly not be entertained.
  • Prohibited Possessions: Standard digital hardware items like computing bands, cellular links, portable calculators, or physical textual guides are strictly banned.
  • Hardware Usage: Candidates must strictly comply with instruction norms regarding the keyboard peripheral hardware provided at the venue.

Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)

Is the CBSE Group A B C Skill Test Admit Card 2026 released?

Yes, the CBSE Group A, B, and C Skill Test Admit Card 2026 has been officially released on 9th July 2026.

What is the CBSE Group A B C Skill Test Exam Date?

The CBSE Group A, B, and C Skill Test is scheduled to be conducted on 11th July 2026.

Where can I download the CBSE Skill Test Hall Ticket?

Shortlisted candidates can download their hall tickets online using the official link available at cbse.gov.in or directly via the examination services login portal.

What login details are required to download the CBSE Admit Card?

Candidates require their Application Number/Registration Number and Date of Birth along with the security pin to access the download portal.

How many total vacancies are advertised under this CBSE DRQ2026 recruitment?

A total of 124 provisional posts across various Group A, B, and C categories are advertised under this notification.

Is city intimation different from the final admit card?

Yes, the City Intimation slip informs candidates only about the exam district/city to plan travel, while the final Admit Card details the specific venue address and shift timing.

What documents must be carried to the CBSE skill test centre?

Candidates must carry a clear printout of the downloaded Admit Card along with a valid original photo ID card (like Aadhar Card, PAN Card, or Driving License).

What should I do if my CBSE Skill Test Admit Card is not downloading?

Ensure your login details match your initial application form. If the problem persists, check the official website announcements or reach out to the CBSE recruitment helpdesk immediately.
