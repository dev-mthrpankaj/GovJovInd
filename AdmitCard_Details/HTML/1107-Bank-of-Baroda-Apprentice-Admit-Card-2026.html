About This Admit Card

Bank of Baroda has notified the online examination date for Apprentice engagement. The selection process mentioned in the notice includes online examination, document verification and local language test of the State applied for by the candidate.

For this examination, admit card or login credentials are important because candidates need them to access the online test and follow test-day guidelines. The notice says eligible candidates will receive details from BFSI SSC on their registered email IDs.

Candidates should check only official Bank of Baroda or BFSI SSC communication and avoid depending on unofficial messages. If any detail such as exam timing or login link is missing, candidates should wait for the official intimation.
