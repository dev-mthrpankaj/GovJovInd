About This Admit Card Update

The State Bank of India (SBI) is conducting a recruitment drive to fill 49 Specialist Officer vacancies, which include 20 posts for Assistant Manager (Law) and 29 posts for Deputy Manager (Law). Candidates who successfully applied for these positions must now appear for the online written examination.

SBI has activated the call letter download link. Candidates are advised to download and print their admit cards well in advance. The written exam will test candidates on Reasoning, English, and Professional Knowledge. Please ensure you secure minimum qualifying marks in the Professional Knowledge section as specified in the official guidelines.

For exhaustive examination instructions and guidelines, candidates can refer to the official notification document named SBI.pdf, and always verify their specific reporting time and exact examination center address printed on their individual admit cards.

How to Download SBI Law Officer Admit Card 2026

  1. Visit the official State Bank of India website at sbi.co.in/web/careers.
  2. Navigate to the "Current Openings" tab under the Join SBI section.
  3. Look for the relevant recruitment link for "Assistant Manager & Deputy Manager (Law)".
  4. Click on the link provided to download the Online Examination Call Letter.
  5. Enter your Registration Number/Roll Number and Password/Date of Birth along with the displayed captcha code.
  6. Click on 'Login', verify the details on your admit card, download it, and print a clear copy for exam day.

Details Mentioned on Admit Card

Candidates must carefully check the following details on their printed call letter. In case of discrepancies, contact SBI authorities immediately:

  • Candidate's Name and Roll/Registration Number.
  • Category and Date of Birth.
  • Exam Date and Reporting Time.
  • Exam Venue/Center Name and Complete Address.
  • Photograph and space for Signature.
  • Important Exam Day Instructions and Guidelines.

Documents to Carry on Exam Day

Candidates are required to bring the following mandatory items to the examination venue:

  • A clear printout of the downloaded SBI Law Officer Admit Card 2026.
  • An original valid Photo Identity Proof (e.g., Aadhaar Card, PAN Card, Passport, Voter ID, or Driving License).
  • A photocopy of the same Photo Identity Proof (to be submitted along with the call letter).
  • Recent passport-size photographs (if specifically instructed on the call letter).

Exam Day Instructions

  • Candidates must reach the exam center strictly as per the reporting time mentioned on their admit card. Late entry will not be permitted.
  • Electronic gadgets, mobile phones, calculators, smartwatches, and notes are strictly prohibited inside the examination hall.
  • Listen carefully to the instructions provided by the invigilator at the exam center.
  • Keep your admit card safe even after the examination, as it may be required during the interview stage.
