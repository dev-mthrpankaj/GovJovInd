About This Admit Card Notice

The Central Board of Secondary Education (CBSE) has officially published an advance notice for candidates registered to appear in the Direct Recruitment Quota Examination 2026 (DRQ2026) Skill Test. Under this notification, the department has made the exam city intimation slips accessible through the online candidate login window starting 30 June 2026.

This process is mainly intended to help candidates plan their necessary travel arrangements and local accommodation in advance. The notice clearly establishes that this specific announcement is NOT the actual admit card. The formal admit card containing detailed instructions, precise dates, shifts, and testing centers will be published separately in the coming days.

Candidates can log onto the online portal using their registered credentials to securely view their specific details, alongside the detailed guidelines issued for both English and Hindi language typing/skill test formats.

How to Download CBSE DRQ Skill Test City Slip / Admit Card

  1. Go to the official portal of Central Board of Secondary Education at www.cbse.gov.in.
  2. Find and open the link related to Advance Information for Allotment of City of examination for DRQ2026.
  3. Provide your registered credentials (such as application number or password) on the secure login screen.
  4. View your allotted examination city and review the printed guidelines for the typing/skill test.
  5. Download the city information slip for your own reference.
  6. Revisit the official website regularly to download the final admit card when the download link goes live.

Details Mentioned on Advance City Slip / Admit Card

  • Candidate Full Name and Registration Number.
  • Recruitment Year / Advertisement Details (DRQ2026).
  • Allotted City of Examination.
  • Guidelines for English and Hindi Typing / Skill Test.
  • Official instructions regarding travelling and accommodation rules.
  • Final Examination Centre Name and Detailed Address (Only on final Admit Card).
  • Exact Date, Batch, Shift, and Reporting Time (Only on final Admit Card).

Documents to Carry on Exam Day

  • Clear printout of the final CBSE DRQ 2026 Skill Test Admit Card.
  • Valid photo identity proof (such as Aadhaar Card, PAN Card, Voter ID, or Driving License).
  • Recent passport-size color photographs as instructed on the hall ticket.
  • Application form acknowledgement copy or category certificates if specifically indicated by CBSE.
  • Any extra document listed in the candidate instructions page of the final admit card.

Exam Day Instructions

  • Candidates must report at their assigned test centers strictly within the given reporting window.
  • Please note that the advance city information slip cannot be used as an entry pass; only the final admit card guarantees entry.
  • No TA/DA (Travelling Allowance / Daily Allowance) will be paid by CBSE for travel and accommodation expenses.
  • Carefully read the language guidelines (English or Hindi) chosen for your specific typing test batch.
  • Prohibited communication devices, electronic items, and study material will not be allowed inside the testing hall.

Duplicate Admit Card / Correction Instructions

If you encounter technical issues, errors in candidate details, or problems accessing the portal, please contact the official helpdesk during working hours:

Frequently Asked Questions

When was the CBSE DRQ 2026 Skill Test city intimation released?

The Advance Information for Allotment of City of examination was released on 30 June 2026.

Is the CBSE city intimation slip the final admit card?

No. The public notice explicitly clarifies that this city intimation is not the admit card. The actual admit card will be issued separately.

How can I download my CBSE DRQ 2026 Skill Test city slip?

Candidates can download it by logging in with their registered credentials on the official CBSE application portal at cbse.gov.in.

What details are required to login to the portal?

Candidates must enter their registered login credentials on the official website to view the details.

What details are provided on the city intimation slip?

The slip includes the allotted examination city and Guidelines for typing/skill test in English or Hindi.

Will CBSE pay any travel allowance for attending the skill test?

No. The official notification states that no TA/DA shall be paid by the Board for traveling or accommodation.

What should I do if I have queries regarding the city allotment?

Candidates can contact the CBSE helpdesk numbers at 011-24050353 or 011-24050354, or email at drq2026@cbseshiksha.in during working hours.

When will the final CBSE DRQ 2026 admit card be released?

The final admit card containing the exact examination centre, date, and timing details will be issued separately as per the notified schedule.
