About Cotton Corporation CCI Admit Card 2026

The Cotton Corporation of India Limited has issued the admit card for the recruitment examination covering 148 vacancies across multiple administrative, commercial, and management positions. Candidates who successfully completed their online application process are eligible to appear for the computer-based test.

The hall ticket acts as a mandatory pass for entry into the examination hall. It features crucial details such as the candidate's roll number, exam shift, reporting time, and exact venue address. Aspirants are strongly advised to secure a physical printout well ahead of the exam day.

Candidates must carefully cross-check all printed information against their original identity proof. Any discrepancies should be reported immediately to the recruitment board authorities for necessary corrections.

How to Download CCI Various Post Admit Card 2026

  1. Visit the official website of the Cotton Corporation of India at cotcorp.org.in.
  2. Navigate to the "Public Notice" section and open the "Recruitment" tab.
  3. Click on the link titled "CCI Various Post Admit Card / Exam City Link (Advt. No. DR/CCI/2026)".
  4. Enter your User ID and Password/Date of Birth in the login portal.
  5. Fill in the captcha verification code and click on Login.
  6. View and download your hall ticket, then take a clear printout for the examination day.

Details Mentioned on Admit Card

  • Candidate's full name and roll number / registration number.
  • Post name and examination title (Advt. No. DR/CCI/2026).
  • Photograph and signature of the applicant.
  • Exam date, shift timing, and reporting time.
  • Exam centre name, code, and complete venue address.
  • Important exam guidelines and instructions for candidates.

Documents to Carry

Candidates must ensure they bring valid credentials to gain entry into the examination hall:

  • Printed copy of the CCI Various Post Admit Card 2026.
  • Original photo identity proof (Aadhaar Card, Voter ID, PAN Card, Driving Licence, or Passport).
  • Passport-size photographs, if instructed on the admit card.

Exam Day Instructions

  • Arrive at the designated test centre well before the reporting time printed on the admit card.
  • Strictly avoid carrying prohibited electronic gadgets, calculators, or mobile phones inside the exam hall.
  • Verify all seating arrangement details and follow instructions given by invigilators.
  • Preserve the admit card safely for future recruitment processes like document verification.

Frequently Asked Questions

Is Cotton Corporation CCI Various Post Admit Card 2026 released?

Yes, the Cotton Corporation of India has officially released the admit card for various posts on 21st August 2026.

What is the CCI Various Post exam date 2026?

The online Computer-Based Test (CBT) exam is scheduled to be held on 1st September 2026.

Where can I download the CCI Various Post admit card?

Candidates can download the admit card from the official website cotcorp.org.in using their login credentials.

What login details are required to download CCI admit card?

Candidates need their User ID / Registration Number and Password / Date of Birth to log in.

What is the selection process for CCI Recruitment 2026?

The selection process consists of an online Computer-Based Test (CBT) followed by Document Verification.

What documents should I carry to the CCI exam centre?

Candidates must carry a printed copy of the admit card along with a valid original photo ID proof.

Is exam city intimation slip the same as admit card?

No, the city intimation slip shows the allotted exam city, whereas the admit card contains full exam venue details and shift timings.

What should I do if my CCI admit card is not downloading?

Recheck your credentials, try using an updated web browser, clear cache, or contact the official CCI support helpdesk.
