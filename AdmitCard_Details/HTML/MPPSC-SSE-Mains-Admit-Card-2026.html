How to Download MPPSC Mains Admit Card 2026

  1. Visit the official MP Online examination portal or MPPSC website at mppsc.mp.gov.in.
  2. Locate and click on the link titled "Admit Card - State Service Mains Examination 2026".
  3. Enter your Application Number / Roll Number and Date of Birth in the candidate login section.
  4. Solve the captcha security code and click on the "Submit" or "Login" button.
  5. Your MPPSC Mains Hall Ticket will appear on the screen. Verify all personal details and exam schedule carefully.
  6. Download the PDF and print at least two copies on clean A4 paper for exam day reference.

Details Mentioned on MPPSC Mains Hall Ticket

  • Candidate Full Name and Application / Roll Number.
  • Photograph and Signature of the candidate.
  • Father's Name and Category / Gender details.
  • Exam Dates (08 to 13 August 2026) and paper-wise shift timings.
  • Exact Exam Centre Address and Venue Code.
  • Reporting time and gate closure rules.
  • Commission guidelines for conventional written papers.

Mandatory Documents to Carry

  • Printed copy of MPPSC State Service Mains Admit Card 2026.
  • Original Valid Photo Identity Proof (Aadhaar Card, PAN Card, Passport, Voter ID, or Driving License).
  • Recent passport-size photographs matching the application form (if instructed).
  • Permitted stationery (Blue/Black ballpoint pens as per exam rules).

Exam Day Guidelines

  • Report to the assigned examination center strictly before the gate closure time mentioned on the admit card.
  • Electronic devices including smartwatches, mobile phones, calculators, and bluetooth gadgets are strictly banned inside the hall.
  • Maintain silence and adhere to seated arrangements in the examination room.
  • Retain your printed admit card safely for post-mains evaluation and interview stages.
