About This Admit Card Update

The Madhya Pradesh Employees Selection Board (MPESB), Bhopal released the Test Admit Card for the Primary School Teacher Selection Test (PSTST) 2025 on 30 September 2025. This selection test was organized to fill 10,150 vacancies for Primary School Teacher posts across various government schools in Madhya Pradesh.

Candidates who completed the online application process between 18 July 2025 and 25 August 2025 can access their admit card by visiting the official MPESB candidate portal. The computer-based examination (CBT) commenced on 09 October 2025 at allotted exam centres throughout the state.

It is mandatory for all candidates to download and print the e-admit card well in advance. Candidates must bring a valid printed admit card along with an original photo identity card and follow all biometric authentication procedures at the examination venue.

How to Download MPESB PSTST Admit Card 2025

  1. Visit the official website of MPESB at esb.mp.gov.in.
  2. Click on the Admit Card section on the homepage header menu.
  3. Select the link titled Primary School Teacher Selection Test - 2025 Test Admit Card.
  4. Read the instructions popup and proceed to the login window.
  5. Enter your 13-digit Application Number and Date of Birth (DD/MM/YYYY).
  6. Input the security code string: First 2 letters of your Mother's name + Last 4 digits of your Aadhar Number.
  7. Solve the simple mathematical captcha and click on the Search button.
  8. Your MPESB PSTST Admit Card will be displayed on screen. Download and print a high-quality copy for the exam.

Details Mentioned on MPESB PSTST Admit Card

  • Candidate Name, Father's Name, and Roll Number
  • Application Number and Registration ID
  • Candidate Photo and Digital Signature
  • Exam Date, Shift Timing, and Reporting Time
  • Gate Closure Time
  • Exam Center Name, Code, and Detailed Address
  • Category (UR / OBC / SC / ST / EWS) and Gender
  • Exam Day Guidelines and Subject Code

Documents to Carry to Examination Centre

  • Printed copy of MPESB PSTST Test Admit Card (Tac Part-1 & Part-2).
  • Original valid Photo Identity Card (Aadhaar Card, Voter ID, PAN Card, Driving License, or Passport).
  • Two recent colored passport-size photographs (same as uploaded in the application form).
  • Simple transparent ballpoint pen (Black/Blue).

Exam Day Instructions & Guidelines

  • Report strictly as per the reporting time mentioned on your test admit card. Late entry after gate closure is prohibited.
  • Multi-stage biometric authentication (Aadhaar-based) will be performed prior to entering the computer lab.
  • Do not carry electronic items including mobile phones, smartwatches, Bluetooth devices, calculators, or bags inside the premises.
  • Preserve the candidate copy of the admit card carefully, as it will be required during post-exam verification and counseling stages.

Duplicate Admit Card & Correction Assistance

Candidates experiencing difficulty in downloading their Test Admit Card should double-check their application registration number and security combination. For technical support, contact MPESB through official helpdesk channels:

Frequently Asked Questions

Is the MPESB PSTST Admit Card 2025 released?

Yes, the admit card for MPESB Primary School Teacher Selection Test PSTST 2025 has been released on the official portal.

When was the MPESB Primary School Teacher exam conducted?

The examination commenced on 09 October 2025 across designated examination centres in Madhya Pradesh.

Where can candidates download the PSTST Admit Card?
