About Delhi High Court HJS Admit Card 2026

The High Court of Delhi has released the official hall ticket download link for the Higher Judicial Service (HJS) Examination 2026. Candidates who submitted online applications between 1 July 2026 and 15 July 2026 for 27 vacancies in the Delhi Higher Judicial Service Cadre can now access their e-Admit Card online.

The Preliminary Examination (Screening Test) is scheduled to take place on 26 July 2026 across various designated test centres in New Delhi. Candidates must log in using their Registration Number / User ID and Password or Date of Birth to download their admit card.

It is mandatory to carry a printed copy of the e-Admit Card along with an original valid photo identity card to the exam hall. Entry without a valid hall ticket and photo proof will strictly not be allowed.

How to Download Delhi High Court HJS Admit Card 2026

  1. Visit the official website of High Court of Delhi at delhihighcourt.nic.in or direct link on applycareer.co.in.
  2. Navigate to Public Notices / Recruitment section on the homepage.
  3. Click on the link titled "Download Admit Card for Delhi Higher Judicial Service Preliminary Examination 2026".
  4. Enter your Registration Number / User ID and Password or Date of Birth.
  5. Fill in the security captcha code as displayed on screen and click on Login / Submit.
  6. Your Delhi High Court HJS Admit Card 2026 will be displayed on screen.
  7. Verify all details like Candidate Name, Roll Number, Exam Centre Address, and Shift Time carefully.
  8. Download the PDF and take a clear printout on A4 paper for exam day.

  • Candidate's Full Name & Roll Number
  • Registration / Application ID

Documents to Carry to the Examination Centre

  • Printed Admit Card: Hard copy of Delhi High Court HJS e-Admit Card 2026.
  • Original Photo ID Proof: Aadhaar Card, Passport, Voter ID, Driving License, or PAN Card.
  • Passport Size Photographs: Recent color passport size photographs matching the uploaded application photo.
  • Stationery Items: As specified in the exam instruction guidelines printed on the hall ticket.

Exam Day Guidelines for Candidates

  • Report at the examination centre well before the reporting time mentioned on the admit card.
  • No candidate will be allowed entry inside the examination venue after gate closure time.
  • Electronic gadgets such as mobile phones, smartwatches, calculators, bluetooth devices, and digital notes are strictly prohibited.
  • Follow all instructions given by the invigilators inside the examination hall.
  • Preserve a copy of the admit card safely until the completion of the recruitment process.

Frequently Asked Questions

Is the Delhi High Court HJS Admit Card 2026 released?

Yes, High Court of Delhi has released the e-admit card for the Higher Judicial Service (HJS) Preliminary Examination 2026.

What is the exam date for Delhi High Court HJS 2026?

The Delhi High Court HJS preliminary examination is scheduled to be held on 26 July 2026.

How can I download Delhi High Court HJS Admit Card 2026?

Candidates can download the admit card from the official website delhihighcourt.nic.in or applycareer.co.in by entering Application Number / Registration ID and Password or Date of Birth.

How many vacancies are announced for Delhi High Court HJS 2026?

A total of 27 vacancies have been released for Delhi Higher Judicial Service Examination 2026.

What login details are required to download the hall ticket?
