How to Download MPESB ADDET Admit Card 2026

  1. Visit the official MPESB website at esb.mp.gov.in.
  2. Open the Test Admit Card section and select Animal Husbandry and Dairy Technology Diploma Entrance Test (ADDET) - 2026.
  3. Enter your Application Number and Date of Birth in the required fields.
  4. Enter the first two letters of your mother's name plus the last four digits of Aadhaar number, if asked on the portal.
  5. Fill the security code / captcha and submit the details.
  6. Download the admit card and print a clear copy for the exam day.
  7. Verify exam date, reporting time, centre address, photo, signature and instructions carefully.
