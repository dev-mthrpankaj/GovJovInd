How to Download UPPSC LT Grade Biology Main Exam Admit Card

  1. Go to the official online destination of the commission at uppsc.up.nic.in.
  2. Locate the explicit download link designated for "Assistant Teacher Trained Graduate Grade (Biology) Main Exam-2025 Admit Card".
  3. Provide your valid One Time Registration (OTR) number along with required authentication elements.
  4. Verify your credentials and submit the request to fetch your individual hall ticket records.
  5. Carefully read the allocated centre layout, room instructions, and basic personal information printed on the document.
  6. Download the file copy and generate clear printed versions to bring on the assigned day.

  • One clearly printed physical copy of the downloaded UPPSC Admit Card.

Exam Day Instructions

  • Candidates will be granted initial entry access to the venue premises beginning at 08:00 AM, which marks exactly 1 hour 30 minutes prior to exam startup.
  • The entry gates will be securely locked exactly 45 minutes before the exam starts, which is precisely at 08:45 AM. Absolutely no entry request will be entertained past this threshold.
  • Candidates must strictly submit a photocopy of their ID verification proof directly to the invigilator stationed inside their allotted room.

Frequently Asked Questions

What is the UPPSC LT Grade Biology Main Exam Date 2026?

The written examination for Assistant Teacher (Biology) Main Exam 2025 is officially scheduled for 13 June 2026.

Where will the UPPSC LT Grade Biology Main Exam be conducted?

The examination will be conducted entirely in the Lucknow district of Uttar Pradesh.

What login details are required to download the UPPSC admit card?

Candidates need their One Time Registration (OTR) number to download the admit card from the official website.

What is the exam timing for UPPSC Biology Main Exam?

The exam will take place in a single session from 09:30 AM to 12:30 PM.

What is the reporting time at the exam centre?

Candidates will be allowed entry starting from 1 hour 30 minutes prior to the exam (08:00 AM), and gates will close strictly 45 minutes before the exam (08:45 AM).

What documents should I carry to the UPPSC exam centre?

Candidates must carry a clear print of the admit card, two passport-sized photographs, and the original along with a photocopy of a valid ID proof.

Is there any penalty for unfair practices in the exam?

Yes, under the UP Public Examinations Act 2024, using unfair means can result in up to a 1 Crore INR fine and life imprisonment.
