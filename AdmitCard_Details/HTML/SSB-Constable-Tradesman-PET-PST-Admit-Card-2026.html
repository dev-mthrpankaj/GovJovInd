About SSB Constable Tradesman PET PST Notice

Sashastra Seema Bal (SSB) is conducting the recruitment drive for 827 vacancies of Constable (Tradesman) including Driver, Veterinary, Barber, Washerman, Carpenter, Tailor, Cobbler, Gardener, Water Carrier, Cook, and Waiter posts[cite: 3]. The official physical measurement and efficiency tests (PET/PST) are scheduled to start in the third week of August 2026[cite: 3].

Candidates who submitted their online applications between 21 March 2026 and 04 May 2026 are required to clear the Physical Standards Test and Physical Efficiency Test as the first phase of selection[cite: 3]. Admit cards will be issued online on the official SSB recruitment portal around 7 to 10 days prior to the commencement of the physical tests[cite: 3].

How to Download SSB Constable Tradesman Admit Card

  1. Visit the official portal of Sashastra Seema Bal Recruitment at recruitment.ssb.gov.in[cite: 3].
  2. Locate the latest news or ticker link reading "Download Admit Card for PET/PST of Constable (Tradesman) 2026".
  3. Click on the admit card download link to navigate to the candidate login portal[cite: 3].
  4. Enter your SSB Registration Number and Password or Date of Birth as created during form submission[cite: 3].
  5. Enter the captcha code shown on the screen and click on Submit/Login[cite: 3].
  6. Your SSB Tradesman PET/PST Call Letter will be rendered on screen. Verify all ground, time, and photo details[cite: 3].
  7. Download the PDF file and print 2-3 clear copies for official verification at the physical center[cite: 3].

Details Mentioned on PET/PST Admit Card

  • Candidate Name, Roll Number, and Registration Number.
  • Father's / Mother's Name.
  • Applied Trade (e.g., Driver, Barber, Cook, Washerman, etc.).
  • Date of PET/PST Examination.
  • Reporting Time & Entry Gate Closure Time.
  • Allotted Physical Test Ground / Unit Address & Center Code.
  • Candidate Photograph and Specimen Signature.
  • Important Physical Test Rules, Mandatory Documents, & Entry Guidelines.

Documents to Carry for PET / PST Stage

  • Printed copy of SSB Constable Tradesman PET/PST Admit Card.
  • Original Valid Photo ID Proof (Aadhaar Card, Voter ID, Driving Licence, PAN Card, or Passport).
  • Recent Passport Size Color Photographs (at least 4-6 copies).
  • Copy of Online Application Form acknowledgment slip.
  • Original Category / Caste Certificate (SC/ST/OBC/EWS) and Domicile Certificate, if specified in instructions.
  • Trade-related experience certificates / Driving Licence (for Driver post), if required at the time of document check.

PET / PST Ground Instructions

  • Reach the assigned SSB Battalion / Center ground well before the reporting time specified on the call letter.
  • Ensure adequate physical preparation for running, physical efficiency rounds, and height/chest measurements.
  • Prohibited electronic items like mobile phones, smartwatches, or recording devices are strictly restricted inside test grounds.
  • Wear proper sports clothing and shoes suitable for outdoor track running and physical activities.
  • Strictly comply with all commands issued by the SSB recruitment board officers present on ground.
