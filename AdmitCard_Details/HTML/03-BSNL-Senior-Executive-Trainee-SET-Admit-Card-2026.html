How to Download BSNL SET Admit Card 2026

  1. Visit the official BSNL website at bsnl.co.in or open the official admit card portal.
  2. Open the Senior Executive Trainee SET Admit Card / Hall Ticket link for the Computer Based Test.
  3. Enter your Registration Number and Password or Date of Birth as required on the portal.
  4. Complete the security check or captcha shown on the login screen.
  5. Verify your exam date, shift, reporting time and exam centre address.
  6. Download the admit card and print a clear copy for the examination day.
  7. Read the instructions printed on the admit card carefully before visiting the exam centre.

Details Mentioned on Admit Card

  • Candidate name and registration number.
  • Roll number or application number, if printed.
  • Exam name and post/stream details.
  • Exam date, shift and reporting time.
  • Exam centre name and full address.
  • Candidate photograph and signature, if applicable.
  • Important instructions for entry, ID verification and exam conduct.
  • Helpdesk or official contact instructions, if printed on the hall ticket.

Documents to Carry

  • Printed BSNL SET admit card / hall ticket.
  • Original valid photo ID such as Aadhaar Card, PAN Card, Voter ID, Passport or Driving Licence.
  • Passport size photograph, if required in the admit card instructions.
  • Application acknowledgement or registration proof, if required for verification.
  • Category, PwBD or other supporting document only if instructed by BSNL or printed on the admit card.
  • Any other document mentioned in the official admit card instructions.

Exam Day Instructions

  • Reach the exam centre as per the reporting time printed on your admit card.
  • Carry a clear printed copy of the admit card and a valid photo identity card.
  • Do not carry prohibited items if they are restricted in the admit card instructions.
  • Follow biometric, frisking, document verification and seating instructions at the centre.
  • Check the centre route in advance to avoid late entry issues.
  • Follow only official BSNL instructions for exam timing, entry gate closing time and allowed items.

Duplicate Admit Card / Correction Instructions

Specific duplicate admit card or correction schedule has not been clearly mentioned on the available admit card portal content. Candidates who face login, registration number, password or download issues should use the recover option on the admit card portal and check the official helpdesk/guidelines section.

For correction or duplicate hall ticket related instructions, candidates should check the official website. Do not rely on unofficial contact numbers or social media messages.
