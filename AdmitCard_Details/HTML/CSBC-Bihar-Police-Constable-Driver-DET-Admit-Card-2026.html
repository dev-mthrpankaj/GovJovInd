About CSBC Bihar Police Driver DET Admit Card 2026

The Central Selection Board of Constable (CSBC) Bihar has officially released the Driving Efficiency Test (DET) Admit Card for the recruitment of 4,361 Constable Drivers under Advertisement No. 02/2025. Candidates who successfully passed the Physical Efficiency Test (PET) are eligible to participate in this practical test stage.

The DET test schedule commences on 24 August 2026 across designated test centers in Bihar. Candidates are advised to download their call letters well in advance using their registration credentials and verify all details, including reporting time, venue details, and driving test requirements.

Carrying the printed call letter along with valid photo identity proof and an original valid LMV/HMV driving license is strictly mandatory for entering the test venue.

How to Download CSBC Driver DET Admit Card 2026

  1. Visit the official CSBC portal at csbc.bihar.gov.in or direct link on apply-csbc.com.
  2. Click on the notification link titled "Download DET Admit Card for Bihar Police Constable Driver (Advt No. 02/2025)".
  3. Enter your Registration Number / Roll Number and Date of Birth on the login window.
  4. Click on the Submit / Login button to view your DET call letter.
  5. Carefully check your driving test date, batch timing, venue details, and candidate particulars.
  6. Download and print a clear copy of the DET Admit Card for test day presentation.

Details Mentioned on DET Call Letter

  • Candidate Full Name and Roll Number / Registration Number.
  • Father's Name and Category details.
  • Exam Stage: Driving Efficiency Test (DET).
  • Date of Test and Allotted Batch / Reporting Time.
  • DET Centre Name and Complete Venue Address.
  • Candidate Passport Photo and Digital Signature.
  • Driving License Details required for physical verification.
  • Essential Instructions for Driving Test and COVID/General protocols.

Documents to Carry at DET Test Venue

  • Printed copy of CSBC Driver DET Call Letter / Admit Card.
  • Original Valid LMV or HMV Driving License (as submitted during application).
  • Original Valid Photo ID Proof (Aadhaar Card, Voter ID, PAN Card, or Passport).
  • Recent Passport Size Photographs (matching application photo).
  • Photocopy of Application Form / Acknowledgement slip if required for document verification.

DET Test Day Instructions

  • Report strictly at the venue before or at the specified reporting time.
  • Late arrivals will not be entertained under any circumstances.
  • Candidates must strictly adhere to field supervisor instructions during driving vehicle checks.
  • Electronic gadgets, smartwatches, and unauthorized items are prohibited inside the test area.
  • Keep the admit card signed by the invigilator safe for final merit verification.

Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)

When was the CSBC Bihar Police Driver DET Admit Card 2026 released?

The CSBC Bihar Police Constable Driver DET Admit Card was released on 10 August 2026.

When does the CSBC Bihar Police Driver DET exam start?

How many posts are advertised under CSBC Constable Driver Advt 02/2025?

A total of 4,361 Constable Driver vacancies are being filled through this recruitment.

Where can candidates download the DET Call Letter?

Candidates can download their DET Call Letter from the official portal at apply-csbc.com or csbc.bihar.gov.in.

What login details are required to download the DET Admit Card?

Candidates need their Registration Number or Roll Number along with Date of Birth to log in and download the admit card.

What to do if the DET admit card is not downloading?

Verify your registration credentials properly, check the official portal announcements, or contact CSBC helpdesk support.
