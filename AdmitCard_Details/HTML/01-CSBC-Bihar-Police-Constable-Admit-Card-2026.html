About This Admit card

The Central Selection Board of Constable (CSBC), Bihar administers the written assessment system for hiring 83 Special Branch Constable positions under Advertisement No. 01/2026. Reviewing the answer framework helps candidates evaluate performance objectivity and project preliminary outcomes well before final result tabs open up officially.

A response sheet breakdown ensures transparency throughout the written phase. For this cycle, CSBC introduces a structured evaluation format across two distinct papers organized back-to-back in a single examination setting. Candidates have been given the specific privilege to carry a verified duplicate copy of their marked OMR solution worksheet for accurate post-exam assessments.

The objection and verification window operates as an administrative safety guard. It allows candidates to point out discrepancy items or key inaccuracies against published provisional drafts. Authentic proofs matching up to standardized reference literature must support each challenge to secure consideration by the evaluation review committees.

How to Download Admit card

  1. Go to the main online address of the Central Selection Board of Constable at csbc.bihar.gov.in.
  2. Find the designated tab or notification link relating to Advertisement No. 01/2026 Admit card.
  3. Enter your login parameters such as Registration Number or registered Mobile Number and Date of Birth if requested.
  4. Access the question sets along with corresponding official keys published by the administration.
  5. Download the respective paper code files to store a local copy for personal metric analysis.

How to Raise Objection

  1. Log in securely through the specific objection application interface using your unique board parameters.
  2. Identify the target question item number or paper series variant you wish to contest.
  3. Provide authentic supporting files or academic reference citations validating your counter-claim.
  4. Pay the required processing fee for challenging the key option if applicable by the board.
  5. Complete and submit the objection application review form before the final time limit passes.

Note: Objection criteria and modalities are strictly subjected to the rules highlighted in subsequent notifications. Challenges without clear proof references are rejected automatically.

How to Calculate Expected Marks

Candidates can accurately calculate their estimated scores using the official testing matrix parameters defined in the recruitment advertisement:

  • The test comprises 2 papers with each containing 100 multiple-choice questions.
  • Every accurate answer rewards a value of +1 Mark.
  • A definite negative grading system applies: every incorrect response incurs a deduction of -0.25 Marks.
  • Use the formulation: Total Score = (Total Correct Items × 1) - (Total Incorrect Items × 0.25).
  • Cross-verify your answers against the third carbonless OMR sheet copy retained from your testing venue.

Important Instructions

  • OMR Carbon Backup: The OMR response sheet is generated in three parts. The original and second layers remain inside administrative care, while the third carbonless sheet is given directly to the candidate for personal analysis.
  • Booklet Restrictions: Candidates are strictly forbidden from taking the original printed Question Booklet outside the testing hall boundaries.
  • Biometric Protocols: Separate biometric identification and physical logs are registered independently for Paper 1 and Paper 2 stages.
  • Late Submissions: Any grievance or option correction inputs filed after the scheduled objection close date will not be entertained.
  • Disqualification Risks: Erroneous entries regarding Roll Numbers or Question Booklet numbers on the OMR system invalidate sheet scanning processes automatically.

Frequently Asked Questions

What is the release date for CSBC Bihar Police Constable Admit card 2026?

The official Admit card release date will be notified soon by the Central Selection Board of Constable (CSBC) on its official website after the exam.

When is the objection deadline for the CSBC Bihar Police Constable exam?

The objection schedule, fees, and instructions for challenging the tentative Admit cards are to be notified by the board along with the Admit card notice.

Is there a negative marking rule in CSBC Bihar Police Constable Advt 01/2026?

Yes, a negative marking system is active for this exam. For every correct response, 1 mark is given, while 0.25 marks are deducted for each incorrect answer.

Do candidates get a copy of their OMR sheet response sheet?

Yes, candidates are allowed to take the third carbonless copy of their OMR answer sheet home after the examination for personal verification.

What details are needed to check the CSBC Admit card?

Candidates can visit the official website or log into the candidate portal using their registration details or registered mobile number and date of birth.

How many questions are there in the CSBC Constable written test?

The exam consists of two papers, each containing 100 objective-type questions. Both papers must be attempted in a continuous single-session arrangement.

Are candidates allowed to bring the question booklet home?

No. The official recruitment notice states that carrying the question booklet outside the exam hall is strictly prohibited.

Where can I find the official CSBC Admit card and results updates?

All official updates regarding keys, answers, responses, and results can be tracked through the official portal at csbc.bihar.gov.in.
