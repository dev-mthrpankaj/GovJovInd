About CTET September 2026 Admit Card

The Central Board of Secondary Education (CBSE) is conducting the Central Teacher Eligibility Test (CTET) for the September 2026 cycle. Before issuing the final admit card, CBSE typically provides an Exam City Intimation Slip to help candidates make travel arrangements in advance. The actual admit card containing the exam centre address and shift timings is issued a few days prior to the test.

It is mandatory for candidates to download their admit cards online as no physical copies will be dispatched by post. Candidates must verify all personal details on the hall ticket and contact the CBSE CTET helpdesk immediately in case of any discrepancies.

How to Download CTET Admit Card / Check City Intimation

  1. Visit the official CTET website at ctet.nic.in.
  2. Scroll down to the candidate activity board on the homepage.
  3. Click on the link for "Download Admit Card for CTET Sept-2026" or "View Exam City Intimation".
  4. A login portal will appear. Enter your Application Number and Date of Birth.
  5. Fill in the security pin displayed on the screen and click 'Submit'.
  6. Verify your exam date, city, centre address, and shift timings carefully.
  7. Download the PDF and take a clear printout to carry on the day of the examination.

Details Mentioned on Admit Card / City Slip

  • Candidate's Name and Roll Number.
  • Application Registration Number.
  • Date of Birth and Category.
  • Exam Papers Opted (Paper I, Paper II, or both).
  • Language I and Language II opted.
  • Exam Date (06 September 2026) and Exam Shift Timings.
  • Reporting Time and Gate Closing Time.
  • Exam Centre Name and complete Address (City only in the case of City Intimation Slip).
  • Candidate's Photograph and Signature.
  • Important instructions for the examination day.

Documents to Carry

Candidates must carry the following essential documents to gain entry into the CTET examination hall:

  • A clear, printed copy of the downloaded CTET Admit Card.
  • One original, valid photo ID proof (e.g., PAN Card, Aadhaar Card, Passport, Driving License, or Voter ID).
  • A transparent ballpoint pen (Blue/Black) as specified in official instructions.
  • Any other specific document mentioned on the admit card (e.g., PwD certificate if applicable).

Exam Day Instructions

  • Report to the exam centre well in advance of the reporting time printed on the admit card.
  • No candidate will be allowed entry after the gate closing time under any circumstances.
  • Electronic devices, smartwatches, calculators, books, and loose papers are strictly prohibited inside the exam hall.
  • The exam city slip is NOT valid for entry; candidates must present the final admit card.
  • Follow all guidelines and instructions issued by the invigilators and centre superintendent.

Duplicate Admit Card / Correction Instructions

If candidates find any discrepancy in their particulars (such as name, photograph, or signature) on the admit card, they should immediately contact the CTET unit for necessary corrections. Check the official website for helpdesk numbers and email support.

Frequently Asked Questions

Is the CTET September 2026 admit card released?

The CTET September 2026 admit card will be released 2 days before the examination date. Currently, it is expected soon.

What is the exam date for CTET September 2026?

The CTET September 2026 exam is officially scheduled to be held on 06 September 2026.

Where can I check the CTET 2026 Exam City Intimation?

Candidates can check their exam city intimation slip on the official website ctet.nic.in once it is released.

What login details are required to download the CTET admit card?

Candidates must enter their Application Number, Date of Birth, and the displayed security pin to download the CTET admit card.

What documents are required at the CTET exam centre?

Candidates must carry a printed copy of the CTET admit card along with a valid original photo ID proof (such as PAN Card, Aadhaar Card, Passport, or Voter ID).

Is the city intimation slip the same as the admit card?

No. The city intimation slip only informs candidates about their allotted exam city to plan travel. The admit card contains the exact exam centre address and entry timings, and is the mandatory document for exam entry.

What should I do if my CTET admit card is not downloading?

Check your internet connection, clear browser cache, and verify that your Application Number and Date of Birth are correct. If the issue persists, wait for some time or contact CBSE support.
