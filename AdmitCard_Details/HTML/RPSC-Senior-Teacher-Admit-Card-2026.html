About This Admit Card Update

The Rajasthan Public Service Commission (RPSC) has released an explicit press note dated July 1, 2026, defining the operational layout for the Senior Teacher Competitive Examination 2025 (Secondary Education Department). The written board evaluations will take place across various centers from July 12, 2026, through July 18, 2026.

Candidates can access their specific exam district name on the SSO Portal exactly seven days prior to their particular exam date. The authentic printable e-admit cards will go live on the official website and SSO Rajasthan dashboard three days prior to the day of evaluation. Candidates should download their copies immediately to prevent high traffic errors.

A specialized policy introduction dictates that candidates will receive an additional 10 minutes in each question paper session specifically to review and record selections on the 5th OMR circle pattern. Strict safety check requirements have moved up entry deadlines significantly.

How to Download RPSC Senior Teacher Admit Card

  1. Navigate directly onto the RPSC portal dashboard at rpsc.rajasthan.gov.in.
  2. Locate the designated 'Admit Card' link on the homepage sidebar columns.
  3. Input your authentic Application Number alongside your accurate Date of Birth details.
  4. Alternatively, log on to the Rajasthan Single Sign-On engine at sso.rajasthan.gov.in.
  5. Select the 'Recruitment Portal' application module within Citizen Apps (G2C).
  6. Click down onto your relevant hall ticket option link, download the layout, and capture a high-clarity color printout.

Details Mentioned on Admit Card

  • Full name of the designated candidate.
  • Allocated Roll Number & registration index block.
  • Specific Subject Exam Date, time frameworks, and shift tags.
  • Complete Address structure of the designated examination center venue.
  • Crucial reporting timelines and closing window thresholds.
  • Photograph box space along with system authorization signatures.
  • Mandatory written guidelines on the deployment of fifth OMR alternatives.

Documents to Carry on Exam Day

  • A clearly printed copy of your official RPSC E-Admit Card.
  • Latest clear passport-size colored photograph to paste on the attendance form block.
  • An updated, latest, original colorful Aadhaar Card containing clear imagery.
  • In cases of an outdated photo on the Aadhaar card, carry alternative colorful valid identities like a Driving License, valid Passport, or Voter ID Card.

Exam Day Instructions & Entry Rules

  • 60-Minute Cutoff: Complete entry checks well in advance. Gate entry closes 60 minutes prior to session activation; no exceptions will be accommodated.
  • Physical Venue Crosschecking: RPSC flags that candidates should not solely lean on Google Maps trackers for exact lane tracking due to technical glitches. Confirm structural layouts a day early.
  • 5th OMR Option Rule: Every question sheet layout includes 5 option slots. An additional 10 minutes are dedicated at the close of the exam session to ensure proper completion of unattempted questions via the 5th alternative.
  • Strict Warning Protocols: Any involvement in unlawful means or coordination with fraudulent brokers attracts life-long imprisonment sentences and asset forfeiture under the Rajasthan Public Examination Act of 2022.

Duplicate Admit Card / Correction Instructions

Check official website. For extreme anomalies or emergency situations regarding downloading failure or verification errors, reach out to the board control system extensions via the official telephone desk strings: 0145-2635200, 2635212, or 2635255.

Frequently Asked Questions

What are the RPSC Senior Teacher exam dates 2026?

The written competitive examination will be held from 12 July 2026 to 18 July 2026.

When can candidates check their exam district location?

Candidates can check their exam city/district location information 7 days prior to their specific exam date on the SSO Portal.

When will the RPSC Senior Teacher admit card be available for download?

The official hall ticket will be uploaded on the RPSC portal and SSO Rajasthan 3 days before the scheduled exam date.

What login credentials are required to download the admit card?

Candidates will require their Application Number and Date of Birth to log into the official download portal.

Is there extra time allowed for filling the 5th option in OMR?

Yes, an extra time of 10 minutes will be given to candidates in each paper specifically to fill the mandatory 5th option on the OMR answer sheet.

What is the deadline for entering the examination hall?

Candidates must enter the examination center at least 60 minutes before the start of the session. No entry is allowed after this deadline.

Which identity document is preferred on the examination day?

An updated/latest original color Aadhaar Card is highly preferred. If the photo is unclear, carry alternative valid color photo IDs like a Driving License or Voter ID.

Where can I find the exact address verification instruction?

Candidates are strictly advised to physically verify the address printed on their admit card instead of completely relying on Google Maps locations.
