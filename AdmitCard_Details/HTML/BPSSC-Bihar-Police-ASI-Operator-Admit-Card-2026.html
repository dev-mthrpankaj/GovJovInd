About BPSSC ASI Operator Written Exam Schedule

The Bihar Police Subordinate Services Commission (BPSSC) has officially formalized the written examination process for the recruitment of Assistant Sub-Inspector (Operator) positions in Bihar Police Radio (Wireless) under Advt No. 04/2026. The initial level screening test will be carried out on 22 July 2026, which falls on a Wednesday, utilizing a traditional single-shift format across dedicated centers.

Candidates can access their official e-admit cards beginning 07 July 2026. The registration credentials must be provided on the BPSSC portal under the specific Bihar Police section. The commission has clearly pointed out that no separate hall tickets will be issued or dispatched via regular postal mail services.

Candidates must carefully check all entry limits, as security thresholds and sitting configurations are synchronized strictly with assigned roll numbers. An aggregate duplicate card system is also available for emergency rectifications at the Patna headquarters.

How to Download BPSSC ASI Operator Admit Card

  1. Go to the official online destination at bpssc.bihar.gov.in.
  2. Locate and navigate straight into the "Bihar Police" specialized navigation tab.
  3. Click on the specific text link corresponding to downloading the e-Admit Card for Advt. No. 04/2026.
  4. Provide your login details such as Registration Number or Registered Mobile Number along with Date of Birth.
  5. Review the test center particulars displayed on the summary framework.
  6. Initiate the download process and secure a sharp, readable printout for your records.

Details Displayed on the e-Admit Card

  • Full name of the candidate and specific application number.
  • Roll Number allocated for the competitive testing stage.
  • Advertisement tracking notice reference (04/2026).
  • Assigned examination center code, name, and venue location.
  • Reporting timeline benchmark (09:30 AM) and shift allocations.
  • Candidate photograph and signature validations.
  • Mandatory OMR marking and structural guidelines.

Duplicate e-Admit Card Procurement Guidelines

For candidates encountering systematic issues or errors while trying to pull up their online call letters, an alternative path is set up to issue physical duplicates:

  • Counter Date & Time: 17 July 2026, working continuously from 10:00 AM up to 05:00 PM.
  • Physical Address: Bihar Police Subordinate Services Commission Office, 5, Harding Road, Patna - 800001.
  • Mandatory Verification Items: Candidates must personally bring a valid photo ID along with a photocopy of the original application form submission receipt/acknowledgement token.
  • Charges: Issued at the candidate's personal expense under official supervision.

Documents to Carry to the Examination Room

  • Clear physical printout of the downloaded e-Admit Card document.
  • Valid original government identity confirmation (such as Aadhaar Card, PAN Card, Voter ID, driving license, or Passport).
  • Two (2) identical color passport photos matching the application file (mandatory if the printed photo on the admit card appears blurred or blank).
  • High-quality Blue or Black ballpoint pens for marking circles on the answer sheet (Gel pens or pencils are prohibited).

Crucial OMR and Center Management Instructions

  • Candidates must sit only on the precise seat designated by the Roll Number sticker configuration. Seating deviations result in total disqualification.
  • The final center boundary gate completely seals at 10:30 AM. No candidate is allowed entry beyond this timestamp under any condition.
  • The answer booklet consists of an original copy and a duplicate carbon layer below. Do not try to separate, detach, or pull apart these sheets while filling.
  • You must reproduce the given text paragraph inside the OMR booklet accurately using your personal flowing handwriting in either Hindi or English.
  • Always check that Box No. 1 and Box No. 2 relating to Roll Number and Question Booklet Number are filled completely without manual scratching errors.
  • Any presence of stray pen markings, calculation rough sheets, electronic gadgets, or mobile phones inside the facility results in strict elimination.

    Frequently Asked Questions

    What is the BPSSC Bihar Police ASI Operator exam date 2026?

    The preliminary written competitive exam will take place on 22 July 2026, Wednesday.

    When will the e-admit card link open?

    Online downloading starts officially on 07 July 2026 through the web portal.

    What is the exact test shift timeline for Assistant Sub-Inspector Operator?

    The exam is structured as a single session starting exactly at 11:00 AM and ending at 01:00 PM.

    What is the reporting limit set for test venues?

    Candidates must report at 09:30 AM. No one will be allowed into the testing centers after gates close at 10:30 AM.

    Will I receive my hall ticket by post?

    No, the commission does not send physical admit cards by post. You must download it digitally.

    What can I do if my face print is missing or blurred on the paper?

    You must bring two extra passport photos that match your application form to the examination center supervisor.

    How can I get a duplicate hall ticket if the portal fails?

    You can visit the physical BPSSC office at 5, Harding Road, Patna on 17 July 2026 between 10:00 AM and 05:00 PM with your application receipt copy.

    Can I use a pencil or gel pen to fill the answers?

    No, using pencils or gel pens is strictly forbidden. Only standard Blue or Black ballpoint pens are permitted.
