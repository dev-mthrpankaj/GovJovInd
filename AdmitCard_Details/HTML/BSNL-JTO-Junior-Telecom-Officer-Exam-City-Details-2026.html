How to Check BSNL JTO Exam City Details 2026

  1. Visit the official BSNL external examination portal at externalexam.bsnl.co.in or direct portal link.
  2. Click on the link for "BSNL JTO Junior Telecom Officer Exam City Details 2026".
  3. Enter your Registration ID / Application Number and Password / Date of Birth on the candidate login page.
  4. Click the Submit button to access your dashboard.
  5. Your Exam City Intimation slip will appear on the screen displaying your assigned exam city and test date.
  6. Download and save or print a copy of the slip for reference.

BSNL JTO Selection Procedure & Exam Pattern

Selection for BSNL JTO 2026 will be conducted through a single-stage Computer Based Test (CBT) consisting of three sectional papers with a total duration as specified in the official scheme.

BSNL JTO CBT Examination Structure
Section Subject / Stream Number of Questions Maximum Marks
Section I Engineering Stream Paper I 50 Questions 200 Marks
Section II Engineering Stream Paper II 50 Questions 200 Marks
Section III General Ability Test 20 Questions 80 Marks
Total 3 Sectional Papers 120 Questions 480 Marks

Documents to Carry on Exam Day

  • Printout of BSNL JTO Admit Card: A clear printout of the official call letter downloaded from the portal.
  • Valid Original Photo Identity Proof: Aadhaar Card, Passport, PAN Card, Voter ID, or Driving License.
  • Passport Size Photographs: 2 recent color photographs (same as uploaded in application form).
  • Category / PwD Certificates: If applicable, as specified in the admit card instructions.

Exam Day Instructions for Candidates

  • Reach the allocated examination centre well before the reporting time printed on your hall ticket.
  • Strictly note that late entries will not be permitted after gate closing time under any circumstances.
  • Electronic gadgets such as mobile phones, smartwatches, Bluetooth devices, calculators, and study materials are strictly banned inside the exam premises.
  • Keep your admit card safely throughout the selection process as it may be required during subsequent document verification.

Is BSNL JTO Exam City Details 2026 released?

What is the BSNL JTO 2026 Exam Date?

When will BSNL JTO Admit Card 2026 be available for download?

How to check BSNL JTO Exam City 2026?
