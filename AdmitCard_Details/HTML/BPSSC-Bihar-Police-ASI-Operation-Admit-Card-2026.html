About This Admit Card Notice

The Bihar Police Subordinate Services Commission (BPSSC) has activated the formal digital gateway for accessing the e-Admit Cards related to the upcoming preliminary written examination for Advertisement No. 04/2026. This selection cycle aims to fill 462 structural vacancies for the role of Assistant Sub-Inspector (Operation) allocated within the specialized Bihar Police Radio (Wireless) wing.

As per the official notification framework, the examination will take place on 22 July 2026 across various pre-designated offline testing centers spread across Bihar. The login portal requires candidates to authenticate using their private registration particulars established during the application period. Physical possession of a highly clear, printed copy of this document is mandatory for entry protocols.

In addition to online access, the commission has outlined a formal emergency duplicate provision. Applicants facing persistent technical failures or download irregularities on the official domain can claim an offline duplicate hall ticket directly at the commission's regional facility situated in Patna under rigorous documentation guidelines.

How to Download BPSSC ASI Operation Admit Card

  1. Access your internet browser and navigate directly to the official platform at bpssc.bihar.gov.in.
  2. Look under the active notification list on the homepage and select the link titled "Download E-Admit Card of Preliminary Written Examination for the Post of Assistant Sub-Inspector (Operation) in Bihar Police Radio (Wireless)".
  3. Upon being redirected to the secure portal, locate the credential submission boxes.
  4. Provide your unique Registration ID or your registered Mobile Number coupled with your exact Date of Birth parameters.
  5. Solve the alphanumeric visual security Captcha precisely and hit the submit button.
  6. Review the populated hall ticket layout for total precision, save the underlying PDF, and construct a crisp printout on standard white paper.

Details Mentioned on Admit Card

  • Full Legal Name of the Candidate.
  • Assigned Roll Number and Application Registration Number.
  • Clear Photograph and Digital Signature imprint.
  • Official Advertisement Reference and Targeted Post Cadre.
  • Allotted Written Examination Centre Name and exact physical Address.
  • Specified Reporting Time and strict Gate Closing thresholds.
  • Core structural guidelines laid out by BPSSC for the test day.

Duplicate e-Admit Card Facility

Candidates encountering technical gridlocks that completely prevent the extraction of their e-admit card from the web portal can utilize the commission's designated offline collection process.

  • Office Venue: Bihar Police Subordinate Services Commission, 5, Harding Road, near Secretariat Halt, Patna – 800001.
  • Allotted Date & Time: 17 July 2026 strictly between 10:00 AM and 05:00 PM.
  • Mandatory Actions: The candidate must appear physically at the facility. Proxy representation will not be entertained.
  • Required Paperwork: Present a clear photocopy of the initial application confirmation acknowledgement along with a valid government photo identification card.
  • Notice Note: Duplicate print provisions are issued directly at the candidate's personal operational expense.

Documents to Carry on Exam Day

  • Clean physical printout of the officially issued BPSSC ASI Operation e-Admit Card.
  • Original government-authorized photo identity proof (Aadhaar Card, Voter ID Card, PAN Card, Driving Licence, or Passport).
  • Clear duplicate photocopy of the same provided identity document.
  • Two recent passport-sized color photographs matching the structural database profile.
  • Functional blue or black ballpoint pens for marking response grids on the OMR document.

Exam Day Instructions

  • Reach the allocated venue before the gate opening line at 09:30 AM to undergo multi-tiered security checks smoothly.
  • Strictly note that security guards will lock entry gates precisely at 10:30 AM; latecomers will be denied entry.
  • Do not bring prohibited electronic elements including mobile devices, smartwatches, calculators, or communication gadgets inside the boundary.
  • Preserve the physical verified hall ticket cautiously post-exam as it remains mandatory for succeeding mains and physical test modules.
