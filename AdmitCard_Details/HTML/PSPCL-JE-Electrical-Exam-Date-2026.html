About This Exam Date Notice

Punjab State Power Corporation Limited (PSPCL) has released the public notice regarding the online examination schedule for the post of Junior Engineer/Electrical against CRA 316/26. The notification was originally advertised to recruit eligible candidates for a total of 622 Junior Engineer vacancies across various categories in the power sector of Punjab.

As per the latest updates from the Office of Chief Engineer/HRD, the online test will be organized on July 31, 2026, across designated centers. The exam is planned to take place in two separate shifts, specifically Shift-2 and Shift-3. Candidates who successfully submitted their application forms within the deadline are advised to keep a regular check on their registered email IDs and the official web portal for hall ticket download updates.

The selection methodology relies heavily on the performance in this Computer Based Test (CBT), which will carry a set pattern covering technical electrical engineering knowledge alongside general aptitudes and language awareness. Official verification of credentials will succeed the online test for top-ranking candidates.

How to Download PSPCL JE Admit Card / Check Updates

  1. Go to the official website portal of Punjab State Power Corporation Limited at www.pspcl.in.
  2. Navigate towards the "Recruitment" or "Public Notices" link displayed on the main page.
  3. Search for the link stating "Download Online Exam Admit Card for Junior Engineer/Electrical against CRA 316/26".
  4. Provide your login credentials such as Application User ID and Password correctly.
  5. Your personal admit card showing your assigned shift, test city, and exact center location will load on screen.
  6. Save the document, print out a clear copy, and read the exam rules carefully.

Details to Verify on Hall Ticket

Documents and Exam Day Rules

Candidates must carry specific documents to gain valid entry into the exam premises:

  • A clear printout of the official PSPCL JE Online Exam Admit Card.
  • An original valid Government-issued photo ID card (Aadhaar Card, PAN Card, Voter ID, Driving License, Passport).
  • Passport-size identical color photographs if requested explicitly in the printout instructions.
  • Prohibited elements: Mobile devices, smartwatches, calculators, electronic gadgets, or study reference materials are strictly barred inside the labs.
  • Applicants are recommended to reach the assigned venue precisely as per their reporting timeline to avoid entry denials after gate closing.

    Frequently Asked Questions

    What is the official PSPCL JE Electrical exam date 2026?

    The Computer-Based Test for PSPCL Junior Engineer Electrical under Advertisement No. CRA 316/26 will be held on 31 July 2026.

    How many shifts are there for the PSPCL JE Electrical examination?

    The online examination will be conducted in two shifts: Shift-2 and Shift-3 on the scheduled date.

    What is the timing for Shift-2 of the PSPCL JE Exam?

    For Shift-2, reporting time is 11:30 AM, gate closing is 12:30 PM, and the exam timing is from 01:00 PM to 03:00 PM.

    What is the timing for Shift-3 of the PSPCL JE Exam?

    For Shift-3, reporting time is 03:30 PM, gate closing is 04:30 PM, and the exam timing is from 05:00 PM to 07:00 PM.

    When will the PSPCL JE Electrical admit card be released?

    The admit cards are expected to be available on the official portal a few days before the examination scheduled on 31 July 2026.

    What details are required to download the PSPCL JE admit card?

    Candidates will need their registration number/user ID and password to access the download portal on the official website.

    Is there negative marking in the PSPCL JE Electrical CBT exam?

    Yes, there is a negative marking of 0.25 marks (1/4 mark) for each incorrect answer.

    Where can I check the official exam date notice for PSPCL JE Electrical?

    Candidates can verify the notice on the official recruitment portal of Punjab State Power Corporation Limited at www.pspcl.in.
