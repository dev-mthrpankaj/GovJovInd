About NICL Assistant Admit Card 2026

National Insurance Company Limited (NICL) is conducting the Phase 1 preliminary examination for the recruitment of 500 Assistants. The admit card (call letter) for Phase 1 has been released on the official portal, and it is a mandatory document for entry into the examination hall.

The call letter contains vital information including the candidate's name, roll number, exact exam center address, shift timing, and reporting instructions. Since this exam is conducted online, the admit card download portal is hosted by IBPS.

Candidates are strongly advised to download and print the admit card well before the exam date (27 August 2026) to avoid last-minute server congestion. Also, ensure you read the attached instruction handout carefully to understand the exam day protocols and required ID proofs.

How to Download NICL Assistant Admit Card 2026

  1. Visit the official National Insurance Company Limited (NICL) website at nationalinsurance.nic.co.in.
  2. Navigate to the "Recruitment" section at the top or bottom of the homepage.
  3. Click on the link for "Recruitment of Assistants - 2026".
  4. Click on the "Download Phase 1 Call Letter / Admit Card" link. You will be redirected to the IBPS login portal.
  5. Enter your Registration Number or Roll Number.
  6. Enter your Password or Date of Birth (in DD-MM-YY format) and fill in the Captcha code.
  7. Click on "Login" to access your admit card. Download the PDF and take a clear printout on A4 size paper.

  • Reporting Time at the center

  • Detailed Exam Day Guidelines

Documents to Carry

Candidates must carry the following documents to the exam center. Failure to produce these will result in disqualification from the exam:

  • Printed copy of the NICL Assistant Admit Card (Call Letter).
  • Original valid Photo ID Proof (e.g., Aadhaar Card, PAN Card, Voter ID, Passport, or Driving License). Note: Ration Card and Learner's License are typically not accepted.
  • A clear photocopy of the same Photo ID Proof stapled to the call letter.
  • Recent passport-size photographs (same as uploaded during the application process).
  • Blue/Black ballpoint pen.

Exam Day Instructions

  • Reach the exam center at least 30 minutes before the reporting time specified on your call letter.
  • Ensure that your name on the admit card exactly matches the name on your photo ID proof.
  • Electronic gadgets, smartwatches, calculators, and mobile phones are strictly prohibited inside the examination hall.
  • Follow the seating arrangement and do not leave the exam hall until the test is over and permitted by the invigilator.
  • Retain the authenticated Phase 1 admit card safely, as it may be required if you qualify for Phase 2.

Duplicate Admit Card / Correction Instructions

If you face issues downloading the admit card (e.g., "Invalid Login"), ensure you are using the exact Registration Number and Password/DOB sent to your email/SMS during registration. If discrepancies are found in your personal details on the admit card, check the official website or contact NICL / IBPS helpdesk immediately. No duplicate admit cards are issued at the exam center.

Frequently Asked Questions

Is NICL Assistant Admit Card 2026 released?

Yes, the NICL Assistant Phase 1 Admit Card 2026 is released and is available to download on the official portal via the IBPS login page.

What is the NICL Assistant Exam Date 2026?

The Phase 1 online examination for NICL Assistant 2026 is scheduled to be held on 27 August 2026. Candidates who qualify will appear for Phase 2 on 30 October 2026.

When will the admit card for NICL Assistant be released?

The admit card has already been released and is currently available for download prior to the exam on 27 August 2026.

Where can I download the NICL Assistant admit card?

You can download it from the official NICL website (nationalinsurance.nic.co.in) under the Recruitment section or by using the direct IBPS portal link shared above.

What login details are required?

To download the call letter, you must log in using your Registration Number or Roll Number along with your Password or Date of Birth (DD-MM-YY format).

What is the exam timing and reporting time?

The exact exam shift and reporting time are candidate-specific and will be clearly mentioned on your downloaded admit card. Arrive at least 30 minutes before the specified reporting time.

What documents are required at the exam centre?

You must carry the printed admit card, an original valid photo ID proof, a photocopy of the same ID, and passport-size photographs.

What to do if the admit card is not downloading?

Double-check your Registration Number and Password. Ensure you are entering the Date of Birth in the exact format shown on the portal. If the issue persists, clear your browser cache or try a different device.
