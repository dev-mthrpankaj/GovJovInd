About This Exam City Intimation

The Uttar Pradesh Subordinate Services Selection Commission (UPSSSC) has released the web link for checking the allocated exam city and district location for the upcoming Assistant Statistical Officer and Assistant Research Officer (Statistics) recruitment examination. Candidates who registered under Advt No. 05/2026 can now find out which district their test center is located in.

The allocation slip serves to inform applicants beforehand so they can make required travel arrangements in an organized manner. Please note that this link is specifically for checking the city and district name only, and does not constitute a full admit card or entry pass for the examination hall.

The formal written test is scheduled for 26 July 2026. Candidates are advised to keep their registration credentials ready to query the database and double-check their scheduling information on the official board server link.

How to Check UPSSSC ASO Exam City Slip

  1. Go to the main website of the Commission at upsssc.gov.in.
  2. Locate the notification segment or dynamic link relating to Advertisement No. 05/2026 exam district allocation.
  3. Click on the dedicated link to launch the applicant identification window.
  4. Enter your unique Registration Number, Date of Birth, Gender, and the alphanumeric Verification Captcha Code.
  5. Submit the parameters to see your designated exam district location.
  6. Review the information and download or note the details for transition planning.

Details Displayed on City Slip

  • Candidate Registration ID and Profile Data.
  • Recruitment Advertisement Code (05/2026).
  • Assigned Exam City / District Zone Name only.
  • Scheduled Date of Written Examination.
  • Important administrative parameters or safety notices.

Documents to Bring on Exam Day

  • Clear printout copy of the final official Admit Card (to be issued separately).
  • Valid original photo identification credential (such as Aadhaar Card, PAN Card, or Driving License).
  • Recent passport-size photographs as dictated by the board guidelines.
  • Photocopy of the valid ID document if requested in instructions.

Exam Day General Guidelines

  • Plan your transit in accordance with the allocated city to reach the destination well before the entry gate schedules close.
  • Please note that the city intimation page does not substitute for a venue pass. Do not fail to acquire the complete hall ticket printout.
  • Prohibited gadgets, electronic smart devices, calculators, and textual bags are strictly forbidden inside test blocks.
  • Abide by safety and invigilation desk operations throughout the execution of the verification protocols.

Frequently Asked Questions

Is the UPSSSC ASO exam city slip released?

Yes, the UPSSSC ASO and ARO exam city intimation details were made available on 17 July 2026.

What is the exam date for UPSSSC ASO 2026?

The written examination for UPSSSC ASO and Assistant Research Officer is scheduled to be held on 26 July 2026.

When will the actual admit card be released?

The final admit card containing full venue details will be released online a few days before the scheduled exam date as per the board rules.

Where can I check my exam city district?

Candidates can check their exam city allocation details online by visiting the official link on the UPSSSC portal.

What login details are required to check the exam city?

You will need your registration number, date of birth, gender, and verification code to log in.

How many vacancies are announced for this recruitment?

A total of 1565 vacancies have been advertised for the Assistant Statistical Officer and Assistant Research Officer positions.

Is the city intimation slip the same as the admit card?

No, the city intimation slip only informs you about the designated exam city or district to help plan your journey. It cannot be used as a hall ticket to enter the venue.

What should I do if my city intimation page does not load?

If the portal is slow due to high user traffic, please clear your browser cache or try logging in again after some time or check the official website announcements.
