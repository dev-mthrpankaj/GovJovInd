About This Exam Date & Admit Card Notice

The Bihar Police Subordinate Services Commission (BPSSC) has officially published the written examination schedule for Advertisement No. 05/2026. This recruitment drive is organized to fill 122 regular vacancies for the post of Havildar Instructor (Adhinayak Anudeshak) in the Bihar Home Guard Service Cadre under the Home Department (Special), Government of Bihar.

As per the commission's latest announcement, the written test will be conducted on 29 July 2026 (Wednesday) across various allocated test centres in Bihar. The exam will run for a total duration of 2 hours in a single shift. Candidates must ensure that they retrieve their individual allocation data and print their hall tickets before heading to the examination hall.

The online link to download the e-admit card will become active on 14 July 2026. Board guidelines clearly specify that no candidate will receive physical admit cards via post. It is mandatory to carry a clear printout of the downloaded hall ticket along with authentic government-approved identification cards to gain entry into the testing venue.

How to Download BPSSC Havildar Instructor Admit Card

  1. Go to the official website of the commission at bpssc.bihar.gov.in.
  2. Look for the notification tab relating to the Bihar Home Guard section or check the main notice board.
  3. Click on the link titled "Download e-Admit Card for the Post of Havildar Instructor (Advt. No. 05/2026)".
  4. Enter your Registration Identification Number or your registered Mobile Number on the login screen.
  5. Provide your exact Date of Birth (Date, Month, Year) and fill out the security verification captcha code correctly.
  6. Submit the credentials to see your call letter details display on the screen.
  7. Verify your exam date, center allocation address, and reporting time rules before downloading the PDF file.
  8. Print a clean, legible hard copy of the admit card on white paper to bring to the test center.

Details Printed on the Hall Ticket

  • Full name of the applicant and registration number.
  • Roll number allocated by BPSSC.
  • Advertisement details and specific post name (Havildar Instructor).
  • Date of the written examination and shift details.
  • Reporting time window and gate closure time limits.
  • Complete address of the designated exam center venue.
  • Scanned photograph and official signature print.
  • Important exam day code of conduct and instruction notes.

Documents to Carry on Exam Day

  • A clear physical printout of the BPSSC e-admit card.
  • Valid original photo identification proof (Aadhaar Card, Voter ID, PAN Card, Driving License, or Passport).
  • Two recent passport-size color photographs (required if the picture on the printed admit card is faint, blur, or missing).
  • Simple blue or black ballpoint pens to complete the OMR responses.

Exam Day Guidelines & Instructions

  • Candidates must report at the designated center starting from 09:30 AM to undergo security checks and identity authentication.
  • No candidate will be allowed entry inside the center venue past the gate closure timing stated in the official document.
  • Possession of smart devices, calculators, textbooks, notebooks, mobile phones, or Bluetooth equipment is strictly illegal inside the examination room.
  • Do not tear, scratch, fold, or overwrite information circles on the OMR answer sheets.
  • Preserve the authenticated physical copy of the admit card carefully after the written test, as it is mandatory to produce it during subsequent physical efficiency test (PET) and document verification stages.

    Frequently Asked Questions

    What is the official BPSSC Havildar Instructor exam date 2026?

    The written competitive exam will take place on 29 July 2026, Wednesday.

    When can I download the BPSSC Havildar Instructor admit card?

    The e-admit card download link will be available starting 14 July 2026 on the official portal.

    What is the exam timing and shift system?

    The exam will be organized in a single shift running from 11:00 AM to 01:00 PM.

    What is the required reporting time at the exam hall?

    Applicants are requested to reach their designated test venues from 09:30 AM onwards to finish validation checks early.

    What login details are required to download the admit card?

    You need your Registration Identification Number or registered Mobile Number along with your exact Date of Birth.

    What documents should I carry along with the hall ticket?

    Bring a clean printed copy of the e-admit card, a valid original government photo identity proof, and two extra passport photos if the print image is unclear.

    In case the photo print is missing or unidentifiable, candidates must bring two passport-sized color photographs matching their application document to the exam venue.
