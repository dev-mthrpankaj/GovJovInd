About This Admit Card

RRB JE CBT-II admit card is the official e-call letter for candidates shortlisted for the second stage Computer Based Test under CEN 05/2025. The recruitment covers Junior Engineer, Depot Material Superintendent and Chemical & Metallurgical Assistant posts under Railway Recruitment Boards.

The admit card is important because it contains candidate details, exam date, exam city, centre address, reporting time, gate closing time, shift and exam-day instructions. Candidates should not depend only on city intimation slip; the printed e-call letter is required for exam centre entry.

Before the exam, candidates should verify every detail printed on the admit card from the official RRB login. If any detail looks incorrect, candidates should contact the concerned RRB/helpdesk immediately and follow only official instructions.

How to Download RRB JE CBT II Admit Card

  1. Visit your official regional RRB website or open the official RRB e-call letter login link.
  2. Find the CEN 05/2025 JE/DMS/CMA CBT-II e-call letter or admit card link.
  3. Enter your Registration Number and User Password/Date of Birth as asked on the login page.
  4. Complete captcha or portal verification and submit the login form.
  5. Check the exam date, exam centre, shift, reporting time and instructions carefully.
  6. Download the admit card PDF and take a clear printout for exam-day use.

Details Mentioned on Admit Card

  • Candidate name and registration/roll number.
  • CEN number and post/exam group details.
  • Exam date, shift and CBT-II details.
  • Reporting time, gate closing time and exam timing.
  • Exam centre name and full address.
  • Candidate photograph, signature or identification details, if printed.
  • RRB exam-day instructions and prohibited item rules.

Documents to Carry

  • Printed RRB JE CBT-II e-call letter/admit card.
  • Valid original photo ID proof as accepted by RRB.
  • Passport size photograph, if required on the admit card.
  • Category/PwBD/scribe-related document, if applicable and instructed.
  • Any other document specifically mentioned in the official e-call letter.

Exam Day Instructions

  • Reach the exam centre before the reporting time printed on your e-call letter.
  • Do not wait for the last minute to download or print the admit card.
  • Carry original ID proof and keep candidate details ready for verification.
  • Follow biometric/Aadhaar or identity verification instructions if mentioned on the admit card.
  • Do not carry prohibited items inside the examination centre.
  • Follow only the instructions printed on the official RRB admit card and displayed at the centre.

Duplicate Admit Card / Correction Instructions

RRB has not provided a separate duplicate admit card schedule on the checked official notice page. Candidates who face login, password, printing or admit card detail issues should use the official candidate login helpdesk or contact their concerned RRB immediately.

  • Helpdesk contact shown on candidate login: +91 7996339995.
  • Helpdesk timing shown on login page: Monday to Saturday, 09:00 AM to 06:00 PM.
  • For correction or discrepancy, check official website and candidate login instructions.

Frequently Asked Questions

Is RRB JE CBT II Admit Card 2026 released?

Yes. The official RRB notice page shows the link for downloading e-call letter for CBT-II under CEN 05/2025 dated 29 June 2026.

What is the RRB JE CBT II exam date 2026?

RRB CBT-II for CEN 05/2025 JE/DMS/CMA is scheduled on 02 July 2026 as per the official tentative CBT 2 schedule.

Where can I download RRB JE CBT II admit card?

Candidates can download the e-call letter from the official regional RRB websites or the RRB candidate login linked on official RRB notice pages.

Which login details are required?

The candidate login asks for Registration Number and User Password, which is Date of Birth, along with captcha or other portal verification.

What is the exam timing?

Exact exam timing, shift and gate closing time are printed on the candidate's admit card. Follow the official e-call letter only.

What is the reporting time?

Reporting time is mentioned on the e-call letter. Candidates should reach before the reporting time and avoid last-minute entry risk.

Is city intimation slip the same as admit card?

No. City intimation slip is for exam city/date information. The e-call letter/admit card is the mandatory entry document.

What documents are required with the admit card?

Carry printed e-call letter, valid original photo ID, photograph if required and any document mentioned in the official admit card.

What should I do if there is an error in admit card?

Contact the concerned RRB/helpdesk immediately and follow official correction instructions available on the RRB website or login portal.
