About This Admit Card & Exam Notice

Punjab State Power Corporation Limited (PSPCL) has published the official download link and notification for the online Computer-Based Test (CBT) for the post of Junior Engineer (Electrical) under Advertisement CRA 316/26. A total of 622 vacancies are being filled through this recruitment process.

The online examination will be held across various designated test centres on 31 July 2026 in multiple shifts (Shift-2 and Shift-3). Candidates who submitted their application form successfully between 20 April 2026 and 10 May 2026 can now download their e-admit cards using their registration credentials.

The hall ticket contains crucial exam details including candidate name, roll number, exact examination centre address, shift schedule, gate closing time, and mandatory candidate instructions. Entry inside the examination premises without a printed hall ticket and valid photo ID is strictly prohibited.

How to Download PSPCL JE Electrical Admit Card 2026

  1. Visit the official recruitment portal of PSPCL at pspcl.in.
  2. Navigate to the "Recruitment" / "Careers" section on the homepage.
  3. Click on the link titled "Download Admit Card for CBT Exam of Junior Engineer (Electrical) against CRA 316/26".
  4. You will be redirected to the online login portal (digialm portal).
  5. Enter your Application Registration Number / User ID and Password or Date of Birth.
  6. Submit the credentials to view your hall ticket on the screen.
  7. Verify all personal and exam-related details printed on the hall ticket carefully.
  8. Download the PDF and take a clear printout to carry on exam day.

Details Mentioned on PSPCL JE Hall Ticket

  • Candidate's Full Name
  • Roll Number / Registration Number
  • Father's / Mother's Name
  • Category & Gender
  • Post Name: Junior Engineer (Electrical)
  • Advertisement Number: CRA 316/26
  • Exam Date: 31 July 2026
  • Assigned Shift Number & Exam Duration
  • Reporting Time & Gate Closing Time
  • Exact Exam Centre Address & Centre Code
  • Candidate's Photograph and Digital Signature
  • Important Exam Day Instructions

Documents to Carry to the Exam Centre

  • A clear printout of the downloaded PSPCL JE Electrical Admit Card 2026.
  • Original Government Photo Identity Proof (Aadhaar Card, Voter ID, PAN Card, Driving Licence, Passport).
  • Two recent passport-size photographs (matching the photograph on the application form).
  • Category / PwBD Certificate copy, if applicable under guidelines.

Exam Day Instructions

  • Reach the allocated examination centre well before the reporting time specified on your admit card.
  • Note that entry gates will be closed at 12:30 PM for Shift-2 and 04:30 PM for Shift-3; late entry is not allowed.
  • Mobile phones, smartwatches, calculators, electronic Bluetooth devices, and study materials are strictly prohibited inside the hall.
  • Biometric attendance and identity verification will take place before entry into the examination lab.
  • Follow all guidelines issued by invigilators and preserve the admit card copy safely for post-exam document verification steps.
