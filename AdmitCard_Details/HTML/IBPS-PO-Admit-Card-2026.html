About This Admit Card

The Institute of Banking Personnel Selection (IBPS) has activated the admit card download link for the Probationary Officer / Management Trainee (CRP PO/MT XVI) Preliminary Examination 2026. The update was released on 14 August 2026, allowing candidates to access their exam centre and timing details.

Candidates appearing for the exam on 22 and 23 August 2026 must download and secure their hall tickets immediately. The admit card acts as the primary identity document on the exam day; without it, candidates will not be permitted to enter the examination venue. Candidates are advised to cross-check all details printed on the admit card against their application to ensure full accuracy.

After generating the hall ticket, it is crucial to save the IBPS Admit Card.pdf file securely on your device and take a high-quality printout. Ensure you thoroughly read the accompanying instruction pages to understand centre protocols and valid ID requirements.

How to Download IBPS PO Admit Card

  1. Visit the official Institute of Banking Personnel Selection website at ibps.in.
  2. Click on the prominent link flashing on the homepage: "Click here to Download Online Preliminary Exam Call Letter for CRP-PO/MTs-XVI".
  3. You will be redirected to the secure login page.
  4. Enter your Registration Number or Roll Number.
  5. Enter your Password or Date of Birth (in DD-MM-YY format).
  6. Fill in the captcha verification code and click "Login".
  7. Verify your exam date, shift, centre address, and reporting time.
  8. Download the IBPS Admit Card.pdf and print a clear copy for the exam day.

Details Mentioned on Admit Card

  • Candidate's Full Name
  • Roll Number and Registration Number
  • Password for Exam Login
  • Date of Birth
  • Exam Date and Shift Timing
  • Reporting Time
  • Exam Centre Name and Complete Address
  • Candidate's Photograph and Space for Signature
  • Space for Invigilator's Signature
  • Exam Day Instructions and Guidelines

Documents to Carry

  • A clear printout of the downloaded IBPS Admit Card.pdf.
  • Original Valid Photo Identity Proof (Aadhaar Card, PAN Card, Passport, Driving License, or Voter ID).
  • A clear photocopy of the same Photo ID Proof (to be submitted).
  • Recent passport-size photographs (same as uploaded during the application process).
  • Personal transparent water bottle and simple pen (if permitted by instructions).
  • PwBD/Scribe documents, if applicable as per official norms.

Exam Day Instructions

  • Candidates must strictly reach the venue before the designated reporting time. Gate closing time will be firmly enforced.
  • The call letter and ID proof photocopy must be handed over to the invigilator. Do not forget to get your admit card stamped if the preliminary admit card is to be retained for the Mains exam (read specific IBPS instructions carefully).
  • Calculators, mobile phones, smartwatches, and all electronic gadgets are strictly prohibited inside the hall.
  • Biometric verification and iris scan (if applicable) will be conducted at the centre.
  • Maintain decorum and follow all instructions given by the exam centre staff.

Frequently Asked Questions

Is IBPS PO admit card released?

Yes, the IBPS PO Prelims Admit Card 2026 has been officially released on 14 August 2026.

What is the IBPS PO Prelims exam date 2026?

The IBPS PO Preliminary examination is scheduled to be held on 22 August and 23 August 2026.

Where to download the IBPS PO admit card?

Candidates can download the admit card exclusively from the official IBPS website at ibps.in.

What login details are required?

You will need your Registration Number or Roll Number along with your Password or Date of Birth to log in and download the hall ticket.

What is the exam timing for IBPS PO?

The online examination is typically held in four shifts: 9:00 AM, 11:30 AM, 2:00 PM, and 4:30 PM. Exact reporting and shift timings are mentioned on the candidate's admit card.

What is the reporting time?

Reporting time varies by shift. Candidates must refer to their specific admit card and arrive at the centre prior to the mentioned reporting time.

What documents are required at the exam centre?

Candidates must carry the printed IBPS Admit Card.pdf, a valid original photo ID (like Aadhaar, PAN card, or Voter ID), a photocopy of the same ID, and extra passport-size photographs.

What to do if admit card is not downloading?

Ensure you have a stable internet connection and are entering the correct credentials. If the issue persists, clear your browser cache or try downloading during off-peak hours. Contact IBPS support if the problem continues.
