About This Admit Card Update

The Uttarakhand Subordinate Service Selection Commission (UKSSSC) has moved to the next phase of selection for the Group C Driver posts advertised under Advt No. 75/2026. Following the written examination conducted on 31 May 2026 and subsequent results published on 19 June 2026, the commission has issued the hall tickets for the formal Driving Test / Skill Test phase.

Eligible candidates who secured qualifying marks in the initial competitive test can now download their individual call letters starting from 13 July 2026. The official notice specifies the method of credential validation needed on the server to print the hall ticket securely.

It is mandatory to preserve and carry the physical printed call letter to the designated assessment venue. Candidates must strictly adhere to the reporting time window printed on their sheets to avoid disqualification from entry.

How to Download UKSSSC Group C Driver Admit Card

  1. Go to the main online operations portal of the commission at ukssscrecruitment.in or visit sssc.uk.gov.in.
  2. Find the highlighted live link referring to the Driving Test Admit Card for post name Vehicle Driver (DRIVER) / Enforcement Driver.
  3. Click the link to clear redirection to the dedicated admit card utility.
  4. Provide your valid registration credentials, such as Application Number, Registration Number, and Date of Birth / Password.
  5. Verify the loaded details on screen including name, photo, test district, and timing.
  6. Download the document and take a clear physical printout to carry on the day of the test.

Details Mentioned on Admit Card

  • Full name of the candidate.
  • Roll number and unique Registration ID.
  • Exam category name and post applied for (Driver).
  • Official Advertisement number (75/2026).
  • Scheduled date and shift allocation for the Driving Test.
  • Reporting time and gate closure regulations.
  • Allocated test venue address and district identifier.
  • Candidate photograph and digital signature imprint.
  • Mandatory guidelines issued by UKSSSC.

Documents to Carry on Exam Day

  • Valid printed copy of the UKSSSC Driving Test Admit Card.
  • Original valid Driving License (Heavy or Light vehicle as applicable) matching eligibility rules.
  • Original valid Government photo identity card (Aadhaar Card, Voter ID, PAN Card, Passport).
  • Recent passport-size colored photographs.
  • Photocopy of application acknowledgment form, if explicitly requested.
  • Category or reservation authentication certificates if instructed by the call letter.

Exam Day Instructions

  • Candidates must strictly reach the venue ahead of the designated reporting time.
  • Entry will be barred post the gate closure time specified inside the hall ticket.
  • The driving license must fulfill the prerequisite tenure criteria of at least three years prior history.
  • Any electronic items, mobile gadgets, or prohibited assets are completely banned from inside the venue premises.
  • Candidates must follow the specific safety and execution parameters defined by the assessment board on ground.
