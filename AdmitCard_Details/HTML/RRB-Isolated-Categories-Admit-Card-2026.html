About This Admit Card Update

The Railway Recruitment Boards across India have opened the download link for the e-admit cards of the Ministerial & Isolated Categories under Employment Notice CEN 08/2025. This specialized recruitment aims to fulfill 312 openings, including positions such as Junior Translator (Hindi), Chief Law Assistant, Staff and Welfare Inspector, Public Prosecutor, and Lab Assistant Grade III.

The single-stage Computer Based Test (CBT) is set to be organized nationwide on July 14 and July 15, 2026. Registered candidates must access the customized online download link to check their assigned test center location, precise exam timings, and shift slots. Travel arrangements should be planned accordingly as the exact street address of the test center is specified on this hall ticket document.

Downloading the document requires typing the unique registration identification number along with the associated password or birth date onto the official RRB login screen. No printed paper copies will be delivered via postal mail, making the digital download step strictly necessary.

How to Download RRB Isolated Categories Admit Card

  1. Open your web browser and click on the official direct link: rrb.digialm.com Login Portal.
  2. Alternatively, go to the primary recruitment portal at rrbapply.gov.in or your specific regional RRB website.
  3. Locate and click the banner link pointing to "CEN 08/2025 e-Call Letter / City Slip Download".
  4. Enter your system-generated Registration Number and the registered Password / Date of Birth carefully.
  5. Submit the accurate Captcha verification text displayed on the login layout.
  6. Click the login or submit command button to view your applicant dashboard area.
  7. Review the visible personal data and click the "Download Admit Card" button to save the file.
  8. Print at least two clear physical copies on standard white paper for presentation at the exam gate.

Details Verified on the Call Letter

Applicants must check every single text field on the printed sheet to confirm consistency with their personal identification papers. The sheet outlines:

  • Full Legal Name of the Candidate
  • Unique Roll Number and Registration Code
  • Scanned Photograph and Signature graphic elements
  • Specific Post Applied (e.g., Junior Translator, Chief Law Assistant)
  • Allotted Exam Date (14 July or 15 July 2026)
  • Shift Assignment and Reporting Time limit
  • Gate Closure Time limit (No entry allowed past this mark)
  • Complete Address details of the Computer Test Venue
  • Space for self-declaration text string to be penned inside the hall

Mandatory Documents to Carry

  • Printed Hall Ticket: A clear layout printout of the official RRB e-Call Letter document.
  • Original Photo ID: A valid government identity document such as Aadhaar Card (preferably original e-Aadhaar printed copies), PAN Card, Driving Licence, Voter ID, or original Passport. Photocopies or digital phone screen versions are strictly rejected.
  • Passport Pictures: Two matching recent passport-sized color photographs, matching the frame uploaded during initial online registration.
  • Category Papers: Valid community certificates if applying for specific travel concessions or PwBD provisions as allowed in the official notice rules.

Key Exam Day Instructions

  • Arrive well before the designated reporting gate timeline to complete formal biometric identity scanning steps smoothly.
  • Electronic gadgets, digital smartwatches, advanced calculators, bluetooth communication tools, books, bags, or metallic ornaments are banned inside the test floor area.
  • The exam structure contains 100 multiple-choice questions over a 90-minute testing period. A negative marking penalty of 1/3 mark applies to each wrong entry.
  • Candidates must write down a specified running handwriting declaration text phrase inside the designated blank box on the call letter, strictly in front of the invigilator during the testing session.
  • Keep the stamped lower slip of the admit card securely preserved after the test conclusion, as it remains mandatory for downstream document verification levels.

    Is the RRB Isolated Categories Admit Card 2026 released?
