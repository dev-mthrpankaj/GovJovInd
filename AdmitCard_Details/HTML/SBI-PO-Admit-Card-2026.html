About This Pre Exam Training Notice

State Bank of India has issued the online Pre Exam Training Letter for candidates who successfully registered for the 1500 Probationary Officers recruitment scheme (Advt No. CRPD/PO/2026-27/09). The application window concluded on 08 July 2026, and the board has initiated the pre-examination phases on schedule.

The pre-exam training is arranged in line with government guidelines to familiarize candidates belonging to SC, ST, OBC, and Minority communities with the computer-based testing interface and structure. Eligible candidates can download their training materials and schedules starting 17 July 2026.

The standard preliminary recruitment examinations are tentatively set to follow closely in August 2026. Candidates are advised to leverage this pre-examination window to verify their testing configurations, study patterns, and platform requirements ahead of time.

How to Download SBI PO Training Call Letter

  1. Navigate to the official career section of State Bank of India at sbi.bank.in/web/careers.
  2. Locate the "Current Openings" tab and select the recruitment section for Probationary Officers (Advt No. CRPD/PO/2026-27/09).
  3. Click on the specific link titled "Download Pre Exam Training Letter".
  4. Enter your unique Registration Number received during the application phase and your password or Date of Birth.
  5. Verify the loaded profile details and training schedules visible on the screen.
  6. Download the static PDF of the letter and take a clear printout for reference.

Details Indicated on the Call Letter / Slip

  • Full Name of the Applicant.
  • Registration Number and Roll Number.
  • Official Advertisement Number and Post Title.
  • Category Classification (General/OBC/EWS/SC/ST).
  • Allotted Training Schedule or Online Platform Instructions.
  • Scanned Photo and Signature of the candidate.
  • Core general instructions issued by the State Bank of India.

Documents to Keep Ready

  • A clean printed copy of the Pre Exam Training Call Letter / Preliminary Admit Card.
  • Original valid photo identification (e.g., Aadhaar Card, PAN Card, Voter Card, Passport, or Driving License).
  • A clear photocopy of the same photo identification card.
  • Recent passport-sized photographs matching the application upload.
  • A print copy of the application form acknowledgement, if required for verification.

General Examination Day Guidance

  • Arrive at the designated examination venue well within the official reporting window mentioned on your final hall ticket.
  • Late entry inside the assessment hall will strictly not be permitted under any circumstances once registration gates close.
  • Biometric registration and identity verification will take place before candidates are assigned terminal systems.
  • Electronic equipment, calculation aids, smart watches, bags, and reference books are completely barred inside the premises.
  • Keep your validated hall ticket safe through the subsequent testing rounds, interview cycles, and final document verification.

When was the SBI PO Pre Exam Training Letter 2026 released?
