About This Admit Card & Exam Update

The High Court of Judicature at Allahabad is undertaking a recruitment drive to fill 195 vacancies for the post of Private Secretary Grade-I. Eligible candidates who successfully applied for this recruitment between 12 March 2026 and 2 April 2026 are now awaiting their written examination schedule.

Before the actual admit card release, the recruitment board may issue an advance city intimation slip to help candidates with their travel arrangements. The final e-admit card, containing specific details regarding the exam centre address, exact timing, and reporting instructions, will be made available online on the official website about a week before the exam.

Candidates are strongly advised to keep their registration credentials ready. Once the hall ticket is officially published, it will be mandatory for candidates to print a hard copy, as digital copies on mobile devices will not be accepted for entry at the examination centre.

How to Download AHC Private Secretary Admit Card

  1. Visit the official Allahabad High Court website at allahabadhighcourt.in.
  2. Navigate to the "Recruitment" or "Admit Card" section on the homepage.
  3. Find and click on the link for "Private Secretary Grade-I Admit Card 2026".
  4. Enter your Registration Number and Password / Date of Birth carefully in the login window.
  5. Submit the details to view your city intimation slip or e-admit card.
  6. Download the PDF and print at least two clear hard copies for exam day use.

Details Mentioned on the Admit Card

  • Candidate's full name and Registration/Roll Number.
  • Category and gender of the candidate.
  • Date of Birth.
  • Exam date, shift, and duration.
  • Reporting time and gate closing time.
  • Full address of the allocated exam centre (only available on the final admit card, not city slip).
  • Candidateâ€™s scanned photograph and signature.
  • Specific guidelines and instructions for the examination day.

Documents to Carry on Exam Day

  • A clear printout of the officially issued e-admit card.
  • A valid, original photo ID proof (Aadhaar Card, PAN Card, Voter ID, Passport, or Driving License).
  • Passport-size photographs matching the one uploaded during the application process.
  • Proof of Date of Birth, if specifically asked in the instructions.

Exam Day Instructions

  • Candidates must reach the exam venue at least 60 minutes prior to the reporting time.
  • Entry will strictly be denied after the designated gate closing time.
  • No electronic devices, mobile phones, smartwatches, or calculators are permitted inside the examination premises.
  • Follow all guidelines provided by the invigilators and mentioned on the admit card printout.
  • Retain the admit card safely even after the written test, as it will be required during the skill test and document verification stages.

Frequently Asked Questions

Is the Allahabad High Court Private Secretary Admit Card 2026 released?

No, the admit card status is currently 'To be notified'. It will be released a few days before the written examination.

What is the exam date for Allahabad High Court Private Secretary?

The official exam date for the Private Secretary Grade-I recruitment has not been announced yet. Please check the official website regularly for updates.

Where can candidates download the AHC Private Secretary admit card?

Candidates can download their e-admit card from the official Allahabad High Court website at allahabadhighcourt.in once it is officially released.

What login details are required to download the hall ticket?

Candidates will need their Registration Number and Password or Date of Birth to log into the portal and download the admit card.

What documents are required to carry to the exam centre?

Candidates must carry a printed copy of the e-admit card, an original valid photo ID proof (like Aadhaar Card or PAN Card), and passport-size photographs.

What should I do if my admit card is not downloading?

Ensure you have a stable internet connection and are entering the correct login credentials. If the issue persists, contact the AHC recruitment helpdesk.

Will the AHC send the admit card by post?

No, the admit card will not be sent by post. It must be downloaded online from the official portal.

Is city intimation slip the same as an admit card?

No, a city intimation slip only informs the candidate of their exam district to help with travel plans. The actual admit card contains the precise exam centre address and entry time.
