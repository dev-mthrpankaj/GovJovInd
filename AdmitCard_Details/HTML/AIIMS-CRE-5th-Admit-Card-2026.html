About This Admit Card Update

The All India Institute of Medical Sciences (AIIMS), New Delhi has issued the official hall tickets for the Common Recruitment Examination 5 (CRE-5) 2026 under Notice No. 93/2026. This recruitment drive aims to fill 1,484 Group B and Group C non-faculty positions across various AIIMS institutes located throughout the country.

The examination will be conducted as an online Computer-Based Test (CBT) from 25 July 2026 to 27 July 2026. Candidates who submitted their application form between 13 June 2026 and 03 July 2026 can now access their admit cards after checking their exam city intimation slip which was released earlier on 17 July 2026.

Admit cards are mandatory for obtaining entry into the exam hall. Candidates are advised to check all key details such as assigned Group Code, exam centre address, shift timing, and reporting time mentioned on their printed e-admit cards before setting out for the test centre.

How to Download AIIMS CRE 5th Admit Card 2026

  1. Go to the official AIIMS examination portal at aiimsexams.ac.in.
  2. Navigate to the "Recruitments" tab on the main page.
  3. Select the link titled "Common Recruitment Examination - 5 (CRE-5)".
  4. Click on the "Admit Card / Download Hall Ticket" login link.
  5. Enter your Candidate ID (or Registered Mobile Number) and Password along with the displayed Security Captcha.
  6. Click on the Submit/Login button to view your e-Admit Card.
  7. Carefully check all exam details including centre name, group code, and timing, then download and print a copy for exam day.

Details Mentioned on Admit Card

  • Candidate's Full Name and Candidate ID / Roll Number.
  • Applied Post Name and assigned Group Code.
  • Exam Date, Shift Timing, and Reporting Time.
  • Examination Centre Name, District, and Detailed Address.
  • Candidate's Photograph and Digital Signature.
  • Important Exam Day Instructions and Dress Code / Entry Rules.

Documents to Carry to the Exam Centre

  • Clear hard copy printout of AIIMS CRE 5th Admit Card 2026.
  • Original and valid Photo ID Proof (Aadhaar Card / PAN Card / Passport / Voter ID / Driving Licence).
  • Two recent passport-sized color photographs (same as uploaded in the application form).
  • Transparent water bottle (as per AIIMS guidelines).

Exam Day Guidelines

  • Arrive at the examination centre strictly before the gate closing time indicated on your admit card.
  • Follow the instructions provided by test centre invigilators during biometric verification and attendance process.
  • Electronic gadgets, smartwatches, calculators, Bluetooth devices, and study material are strictly prohibited inside the CBT premises.
  • Retain your admit card copy safely as it may be required during subsequent stages like document verification.

Admit Card Issues & Correction

If candidates experience technical difficulties in downloading the admit card or notice discrepancies in personal particulars (such as incorrect spelling or category), they should immediately contact the official AIIMS support desk prior to the exam date.

  • Official Website: aiimsexams.ac.in
  • Helpdesk Portal: Available under "Support" on the official AIIMS Exams main page.

Frequently Asked Questions

Is AIIMS CRE 5th Admit Card 2026 released?

Yes, AIIMS released the Common Recruitment Examination (CRE-5) Group B & C admit card on 21 July 2026.

What is the AIIMS CRE 5th CBT exam date 2026?

The computer-based test (CBT) for AIIMS CRE-5 is scheduled to be held from 25 July to 27 July 2026.

Where can candidates download the AIIMS CRE 5 hall ticket?

Candidates can download their admit card from the official website, aiimsexams.ac.in.
