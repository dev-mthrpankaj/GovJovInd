How to Download NTA CSIR NET Admit Card & City Slip

  1. Go directly to the official web domain at csirnet.nta.nic.in.
  2. Navigate to the active links designated for "Download Admit Card" or "Advanced City Intimation" on the dashboard page.
  3. Input your verified Application Number and exact Date of Birth parameters into the secure login window.
  4. Solve the Captcha/Security Pin challenge accurately and hit the submit button.
  5. Review the visible PDF structure for correct venue allocations and identification information.
  6. Download the file onto your machine and generate multiple high-contrast printed hardcopies.

Details Displayed on the Hall Ticket

  • Full Name of the Candidate and Photograph.
  • Unique Roll Number and Application Identification.
  • Exam Category, Subject Code, and Opted Medium.
  • Assigned Test Center Name and Complete Address.
  • Date of the Scheduled Shift and Session Timings.
  • Gate Closing Deadlines and Reporting Windows.
  • Important candidate protocols and signature fields.

Documents Required on the Exam Day

  • A clear printout of the CSIR UGC NET June 2026 Admit Card (all pages required).
  • Original valid Government Photo ID (Aadhaar Card, PAN Card, Passport, or Driving License).
  • Additional passport-size photographs matching the one uploaded during application submission.
  • PwBD certificate issued by competent authorities, if seeking structural relaxations.

Exam Day Protocols & Guidelines

  • Arrive strictly within the allocated reporting window to facilitate security checks and biometric logs.
  • No entries will be permitted after the gate closing time under any situation.
  • Electronic devices, mathematical calculators, textbooks, bags, and metallic jewelry are explicitly prohibited.
  • Retain the authenticated admit card copy post-examination for subsequent counseling stages.
