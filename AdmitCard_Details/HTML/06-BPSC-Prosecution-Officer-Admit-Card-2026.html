About This Admit Card

Bihar Public Service Commission has released the examination programme for Advertisement No. 13/2026, Prosecution Officer Preliminary Competitive Examination. The preliminary examination is scheduled on 15 July 2026 at examination centres located in Patna, Bhojpur, Muzaffarpur, Bhagalpur and Nalanda.

The admit card is important because it will carry the candidate-specific centre details, exam timing, roll details and instructions. Candidates should download it only from the official BPSC website when the link is released.

The programme also mentions the paper-wise schedule and marking instruction. Candidates should read the official admit card and examination programme carefully before appearing in the preliminary examination.

How to Download Admit Card

  1. Visit the official BPSC website at bpsc.bihar.gov.in.
  2. Open the admit card link for Prosecution Officer Preliminary Competitive Examination, Advertisement No. 13/2026.
  3. Enter the login details required by the official portal.
  4. Download the admit card and verify exam centre, paper timing and candidate details.
  5. Print the admit card clearly and keep it safe.
  6. Read the instructions printed on the admit card and official examination programme.

Details Mentioned on Admit Card

  • Candidate name and roll/registration number.
  • Advertisement number and exam name.
  • Paper-wise exam date and timing.
  • Exam centre address and reporting time.
  • Photo, signature and identity-related details, if printed.
  • BPSC exam-day instructions.

Documents to Carry

  • Printed BPSC admit card.
  • Valid photo identity proof.
  • Passport size photograph, if instructed.
  • Application acknowledgement, if required.
  • Any other document mentioned in the official admit card.

Exam Day Instructions

  • Check paper-wise timing carefully before reaching the centre.
  • Carry printed admit card and valid photo ID.
  • Follow reporting time and centre rules mentioned on admit card.
  • Each question will have five options A, B, C, D and E as per the programme.
  • Wrong answers will attract negative marking of 1/3 mark as per the notice.

Duplicate Admit Card / Correction Instructions

Duplicate admit card or correction instructions are not mentioned in the provided examination programme. Candidates should check the official BPSC website for admit card, correction and helpdesk updates.
