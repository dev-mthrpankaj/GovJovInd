About IBPS SO Specialist Officers XVI Admit Card 2026

The Institute of Banking Personnel Selection (IBPS) has officially released the Preliminary examination admit cards for the CRP Specialist Officers (SO) XVI recruitment[cite: 1]. This recruitment drive aims to fill 1035 specialist posts, including IT Officer, Agriculture Field Officer (AFO), Rajbhasha Adhikari, Law Officer, HR/Personnel Officer, and Marketing Officer[cite: 1].

Candidates must download the call letter online using their Registration Number or Roll Number and Password or Date of Birth. The admit card is a mandatory document that contains your specific exam centre details, reporting time, shift schedule, and strict guidelines for the exam day. No candidate will be permitted entry without a valid printed admit card and recognized photo identity proof.

It is highly recommended to print a clear copy of the admit card well in advance and read all the instructions carefully, especially regarding the documents to carry, such as the staple requirement for the photocopy of the ID proof.

How to Download IBPS SO Admit Card 2026

  1. Visit the official IBPS website at ibps.in[cite: 1].
  2. Click on the ticker or link that says "Click here to download Online Preliminary Exam Call Letter for CRP SPL-XVI"[cite: 1].
  3. You will be redirected to the candidate login portal[cite: 1].
  4. Choose your preferred language (English or Hindi) for the admit card.
  5. Enter your Registration Number or Roll Number.
  6. Enter your Password or Date of Birth in the exact DD-MM-YY format.
  7. Type the captcha code and click on the "Login" button.
  8. Your IBPS SO Prelims Admit Card will be displayed. Download and take a clear printout.

Documents to Carry

According to standard IBPS rules, candidates must carry specific documents for verification. Failure to bring these will result in denied entry.

  • A clear printed copy of the IBPS SO Preliminary Exam Call Letter.
  • Original valid photo identity proof (e.g., PAN Card, Passport, Driving License, Voter’s Card, Bank Passbook with photograph, or Aadhaar Card with a photograph).
  • A photocopy of the same original photo ID proof (this must be stapled to the call letter).
  • Passport-size photographs (same as uploaded in the application form), if instructed.
  • Ration Card and Learner's Driving License are generally NOT accepted as valid ID proof by IBPS.

Exam Day Instructions

  • Reach the exam centre well before the reporting time printed on your call letter to complete biometric and document verification.
  • Latecomers will not be allowed inside the examination venue under any circumstances.
  • Make sure the name on your admit card exactly matches the name on your original photo ID proof.
  • Do not carry prohibited items such as mobile phones, smartwatches, calculators, or any electronic gadgets inside the exam hall.
  • Candidates must retain the Preliminary Exam call letter safely after the exam, as authenticated copies are required during the Mains exam if you qualify.
  • Follow all instructions given by the invigilators and centre staff.

Duplicate Admit Card / Correction Instructions

If you face issues downloading the admit card or notice discrepancies, check the official website. IBPS does not issue physical duplicate admit cards.

  • Ensure you are entering the correct Registration Number and Password/DOB received during the initial application process.
  • If the page is slow, try accessing the portal during off-peak hours or use a different internet connection.
  • In case of major discrepancies (like a completely wrong photograph), contact the IBPS helpdesk immediately using the contact details provided on ibps.in.

Frequently Asked Questions

Is the IBPS SO XVI Admit Card 2026 released?

Yes, the IBPS SO Specialist Officers XVI Admit Card 2026 was released on 20 August 2026.

What is the IBPS SO XVI Prelims Exam Date 2026?

The IBPS SO XVI Preliminary exam is scheduled to be held on 29 August 2026.

Where can I download the IBPS SO Admit Card 2026?

Candidates can download the admit card from the official IBPS recruitment portal using the direct link provided in our important links section or by visiting ibps.in.

What login details are required for the IBPS SO admit card?

You need your Registration Number or Roll Number and your Password or Date of Birth (DOB) along with the captcha code to log in and download the call letter.

What is the exam mode for IBPS SO XVI 2026?

The IBPS SO XVI Preliminary examination will be conducted in an online Computer Based Test (CBT) mode.

What is the reporting time for IBPS SO 2026?

The exact reporting time is printed on your IBPS SO admit card. Candidates must strictly adhere to the time mentioned on their individual call letters.

What documents are required for the IBPS SO exam?

Candidates must carry a printed copy of their admit card, an original valid photo ID proof, a photocopy of the same ID proof stapled to the admit card, and passport-size photographs.

Is city intimation the same as the admit card for IBPS?

No, IBPS directly issues the call letter (admit card) which contains the complete exam centre address, shift, and timing. There is no separate city intimation slip for this exam.

What to do if the IBPS SO admit card is not downloading?

Ensure you are entering the correct Registration Number and Password/DOB format. Try clearing your browser cache, using a desktop computer, or accessing the site during off-peak hours.
