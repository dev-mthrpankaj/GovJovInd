About Punjab and Haryana High Court Clerk Admit Card 2026

The High Court of Punjab and Haryana (SSSC) has officially released the admit cards for the Clerk Recruitment 2026. The examination is scheduled to fill 1265 vacancies across subordinate courts in Haryana and Punjab.

The admit card is a mandatory document that grants candidates entry to the examination hall. It contains crucial details including the candidate's exact exam date (allotted between August 24 and September 7, 2026), exam centre address, reporting time, and specific instructions for the exam day.

Candidates are advised to download the admit card using their Registration ID and Password well in advance to avoid last-minute server issues. Any discrepancy found on the admit card should be immediately brought to the notice of the conducting authority for rectification.

How to Download SSSC Clerk Admit Card 2026

  1. Visit the official SSSC website at sssc.gov.in.
  2. Look for the link titled "Admit Card for the post of Clerk 2026" under the latest notifications.
  3. Enter your Registration ID and Password in the required login fields.
  4. Fill in the security captcha code if prompted.
  5. Click the submit/login button to view your hall ticket.
  6. Download the admit card PDF and print a clear copy for the exam day.
  7. Verify your exam date, reporting time, centre address, photo, and signature carefully.

Details Mentioned on the Admit Card

  • Candidate's Name and Roll Number / Registration Number
  • Exam Name: High Court of Punjab and Haryana Clerk 2026
  • Candidate's Photograph and Signature
  • Exact Exam Date and Shift Timing
  • Reporting Time and Gate Closing Time
  • Exam Centre Name and Full Address
  • Category details (if applicable)
  • Important instructions for the examination day

Documents to Carry

Candidates must ensure they bring the following documents to the exam centre. Entry will not be permitted without proper verification.

  • A clear printed copy of the SSSC Clerk Admit Card 2026.
  • Original and valid photo ID proof (such as Aadhaar Card, PAN Card, Voter ID, Passport, or Driving Licence).
  • Passport size photographs (carry 2 identical to the one uploaded in the form).
  • Category or PwD certificate (if instructed by the official admit card).

Exam Day Instructions

  • Reach the examination centre well before the reporting time mentioned on the admit card.
  • Do not rely on digital copies; carry a physical printout of the admit card.
  • Ensure your photo ID details match the details provided during the application process.
  • Prohibited items such as mobile phones, smartwatches, calculators, and bluetooth devices are strictly banned inside the hall.
  • Follow all guidelines provided by the invigilators and exam centre staff.
  • Keep the admit card secure even after the exam for future stages (results, document verification).

Duplicate Admit Card / Correction Instructions

If there are any discrepancies in your admit card (e.g., misspelled name, blurred photograph), or if you are unable to download it, you should immediately contact the SSSC helpdesk.

  • Double-check that you are entering the correct Registration ID and Password.
  • If you have forgotten your password, use the "Forgot Password" link on the login page.
  • For corrections, check the official website for contact numbers or email support provided by the SSSC.

Is Punjab and Haryana High Court Clerk Admit Card 2026 released?
