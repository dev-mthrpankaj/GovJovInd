About This Admit Card Notice

The Bihar Police Subordinate Services Commission (BPSSC) has officially declared the written examination schedule and issued e-admit card download permissions for the recruitment of Havildar Instructor vacancies. This vacancy notice carries Advertisement Number 05/2026, aimed at regular direct induction to fill up 122 permanent structural positions.

Registered eligible aspirants can download their dynamic hall ticket from July 14, 2026 onwards by signing in using their individual valid registration data or registered telephone number along with birth date specifications. Verification parameters such as venue placement, specific rules of conduct, shift metrics, and timing schedules are systematically recorded on the online printable document.

Checking the allocation data early is strongly advised to help candidates confirm entry rules, understand mandatory identity verifications, and prevent peak-hour digital congestion on public recruitment board links. Entry permission stands canceled if candidates fail to produce a legible physical print of their officially validated hall copy.

How to Download Admit Card

  1. Visit the official portal dashboard setup at bpssc.bihar.gov.in.
  2. Locate the specific updates row carrying notification references for Advt. No. 05/2026 Admit Card.
  3. Click the dedicated download link structure to reach the login gateway interface.
  4. Enter your system-generated application registration identification number or your registered mobile phone number.
  5. Select your correct Date of Birth (DD/MM/YYYY) format parameters, clear the verification captcha image code, and submit details.
  6. Verify all personal profile records displayed on screen before printing a clear physical copy on A4 size media.

Details Mentioned on Admit Card

  • Full Name of the Candidate.
  • Unique Roll Number & Registration Reference.
  • Clear Photograph and Signature.
  • Exam Category / Gender Profile.
  • Advertisement Number (05/2026) & Post Name.
  • Official Date and Assigned Test Timings.
  • Reporting Time & Main Gates Closing Hours.
  • Allocated Test Centre Venue with Full Location Address.
  • Mandatory Exam Day Guidelines.

Documents to Carry on Exam Day

  • Legible physical paper printout of the downloaded e-admit card.
  • Original valid government-issued photo identification proof (e.g., Aadhaar Card, PAN Card, Voter Identification, Passport, Driving License).
  • Identical passport-sized color photographs matching the one uploaded in the primary form, if required.
  • Ballpoint pens matching specified instructions (Black or Blue ink variants).

Exam Day Instructions

  • Report strictly inside your allocated test venue within the designated window prior to final entry timings.
  • Do not bring prohibited digital communication gear, electronic wearable items, computational log tables, study texts, or wallets inside the venue.
  • Cooperate cleanly with the biological scanning protocols and verification steps arranged at the entry doors.
  • Retain your signed physical hall permission document carefully after conclusion for subsequent physical verification validation stages.

Frequently Asked Questions

What is the BPSSC Havildar Instructor exam date 2026?

The written evaluation examination is officially scheduled to take place on 29 July 2026.

When will BPSSC Havildar Instructor admit card download start?

Eligible applicants can access and download their online e-admit card from 14 July 2026 onwards.

Where to download the online e-admit card document?

You can retrieve your official hall copy exclusively via the portal systems of the Bihar Police Subordinate Services Commission at bpssc.bihar.gov.in.

What login details are required?

Candidates need their unique system registration credentials, or their registered mobile contact numbers alongside their exact Date of Birth parameters.

What is the scale of pay for a BPSSC Havildar Instructor?

The position is budgeted under Level-4 pay scale specifications, offering basic pays starting from Rs. 25,500 to Rs. 81,100.

What is the total number of vacancies advertised under Advt No. 05/2026?

There are a total of 122 permanent open vacancies allocated across various distribution categories.

What selection process is implemented for this post?

The multi-stage selection structure requires clearing a Written Examination first, passing a Physical Efficiency Test (PET), and successfully completing Document Verification.

What to do if my admit card is not downloading?

Verify that your entered credentials match your registration receipt precisely. If problems persist, check for official announcements or connect with the technical support desk links listed on bpssc.bihar.gov.in.
