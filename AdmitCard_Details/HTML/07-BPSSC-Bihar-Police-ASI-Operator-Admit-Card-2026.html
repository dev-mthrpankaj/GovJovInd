About This Admit Card

BPSSC has issued the official notice for the preliminary written competition examination and e-admit card for Assistant Sub Inspector (Operator) posts in Bihar Police Radio (Wireless). This notice is for candidates linked with Advertisement No. 04/2026.

The admit card is mandatory for appearing at the allotted exam centre. It will not be sent by post, so candidates need to download and print the e-admit card from the official BPSSC website and carry it with a valid photo identity card.

Candidates should check the roll-number-wise exam centre list on the BPSSC website and verify that the downloaded e-admit card belongs to them. BPSSC has also advised candidates to study OMR sheet instructions carefully because wrong entries on OMR can lead to cancellation of candidature or non-evaluation of the answer sheet.

How to Download Admit Card

  1. Visit the official website: bpssc.bihar.gov.in.
  2. Open the career/admit card/recruitment section mentioned in the official notice.
  3. Select the admit card link for Assistant Sub Inspector (Operator).
  4. Enter the required login details such as registration number, mobile number, date of birth or other details asked by the portal.
  5. Verify exam date, exam centre, reporting time and candidate details before printing.
  6. Download and print the admit card, then read all instructions carefully before going to the exam centre.

Details Mentioned on Admit Card

  • Candidate name, photo and roll number details.
  • Advertisement number and post name.
  • Preliminary written exam date and shift.
  • Exam timing and reporting time.
  • Allotted exam centre and centre-related instructions.
  • Valid photo identity card requirement.
  • OMR sheet and written exam instructions.

Documents to Carry

  • Printed e-admit card downloaded from BPSSC website.
  • Valid photo identity proof such as Voter ID, Aadhaar Card, Driving Licence, PAN Card or Passport.
  • Two photographs same as application form if the photo on e-admit card is not clear or not available.
  • Application acknowledgement copy for duplicate e-admit card, if needed.
  • Any other document mentioned in the official e-admit card instructions.

Exam Day Instructions

  • Report at the examination centre at 09:30 AM as per the official notice.
  • Entry to the examination centre will be allowed only up to 10:30 AM. Late entry will not be allowed under any circumstances.
  • Carry printed e-admit card and a valid photo identity card.
  • Sit only at the allotted place according to the name, photo and roll number sticker.
  • Read OMR instructions carefully and fill required details correctly.
  • Preserve the admit card after the exam because it may be required in main written exam, PET, document verification and joining process.

Duplicate e-Admit Card Facility

Candidates who are unable to download the e-admit card from the website can obtain a duplicate e-admit card from the BPSSC office as per the official schedule.

Frequently Asked Questions

When will BPSSC ASI Operator Admit Card 2026 be available?

The e-admit card will be available on the BPSSC website from 7 July 2026.

What is the BPSSC ASI Operator exam date 2026?

The preliminary written examination will be held on 22 July 2026, Wednesday.

What is the exam timing for BPSSC ASI Operator prelims?

The exam will be held from 11:00 AM to 1:00 PM in one shift.

What is the reporting time for BPSSC ASI Operator exam?

Candidates must report at the exam centre at 09:30 AM.

What is the last entry time at the exam centre?

Entry will be allowed only up to 10:30 AM. No entry will be allowed after the fixed time.

Where can candidates download BPSSC ASI Operator admit card?

Candidates can download it from the Bihar Police tab on the official BPSSC website at bpssc.bihar.gov.in.

Will BPSSC send admit card by post?

No. The notice clearly says that admit card will not be sent by post.

What documents are required on exam day?

Candidates should carry printed e-admit card and one valid photo identity card such as Aadhaar, Voter ID, Driving Licence, PAN Card or Passport.

What if the photo on BPSSC admit card is not clear?

If the photo is not clear or not available on the e-admit card, candidates should carry two photographs same as the application form.

How can I get duplicate BPSSC e-admit card?

Candidates who cannot download it can visit BPSSC office at 5, Harding Road, Patna on 17 July 2026 from 10:00 AM to 5:00 PM with application acknowledgement and photo ID.
