How to Download UPPSC Assistant Town Planner Admit Card

  1. Visit the official UPPSC website at uppsc.up.nic.in.
  2. Open the admit card link for Advt No. A-10/E-1/2025, Assistant Town Planner (Special Recruitment) Preliminary Examination.
  3. Enter the required login details such as OTR number, registration details, date of birth and captcha, as shown on the official portal.
  4. Verify exam date, roll details, exam centre address and reporting instructions after the admit card opens.
  5. Download the admit card PDF and print a clear copy for exam-day entry.
  6. Read all instructions printed on the admit card before going to the examination centre.

Details Mentioned on Admit Card

  • Candidate name and personal details.
  • Roll number, registration number or OTR-related details.
  • Advertisement number and examination name.
  • Exam date, shift and timing details.
  • Reporting time and gate closing instructions.
  • Exam centre name and full address.
  • Candidate photograph, signature and identity-related details.
  • Important exam-day instructions issued by UPPSC.

Documents to Carry

  • Printed copy of UPPSC Assistant Town Planner admit card.
  • Valid original photo identity proof such as Aadhaar card, voter ID, passport, driving licence or any ID accepted by UPPSC.
  • Passport-size photograph, if required on the admit card.
  • Application acknowledgement or registration details, if instructed.
  • Category, PwBD or other certificate, if specifically mentioned in the admit card instructions.
  • Any other document mentioned in the official admit card or notice.

Exam Day Instructions

  • Reach the exam centre as per the reporting time printed on the admit card.
  • Carry a clear printed copy of the admit card and valid photo ID proof.
  • Check the exam centre location before the exam day to avoid last-minute delay.
  • Do not carry prohibited items if they are restricted in the admit card instructions.
  • Follow the invigilator instructions and centre rules during the examination.
  • Keep the admit card safely after the exam for future selection stages.

Duplicate Admit Card / Correction Instructions
