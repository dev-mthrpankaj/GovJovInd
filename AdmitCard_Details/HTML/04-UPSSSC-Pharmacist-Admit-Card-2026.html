How to Download Admit Card

  1. Visit the official UPSSSC website at upsssc.gov.in.
  2. Open the admit card or candidate login link related to Pharmacist.
  3. Enter the required details such as registration number, date of birth, gender or verification code as asked on the official portal.
  4. Complete the prescribed main examination fee step first, if the official portal requires it for admit card download.
  5. Check the exam date, exam district or exam centre details carefully before printing.
  6. Download the admit card and take a clear printout for the examination centre.
  7. Read every instruction printed on the admit card before exam day.

Details Mentioned on Admit Card

  • Candidate name, roll number or registration number.
  • Advertisement number and exam/post name.
  • Exam date, shift, timing and reporting instructions.
  • Exam centre district and complete centre address, if allotted.
  • Candidate photograph, signature and identity details, if printed.
  • Important instructions related to entry, verification and conduct at the centre.

Documents to Carry

  • Printed copy of the valid admit card downloaded from UPSSSC website.
  • Valid photo identity proof in original, such as Aadhaar card, voter ID, driving licence, passport or any ID accepted by the commission.
  • Passport size photograph, if asked in the admit card instructions.
  • Application acknowledgement, category or PwBD document, if required by the official instructions.
  • Any other document specifically mentioned on the official admit card.

Exam Day Instructions

  • Reach the examination centre on the date and time mentioned in the admit card.
  • Carry the valid admit card because it is mandatory for entry into the main examination.
  • Verify the centre address, travel time and reporting instructions before leaving for the exam.
  • Do not carry prohibited items if they are restricted in the official admit card instructions.
  • Follow the instructions of centre staff and the UPSSSC notice carefully.
