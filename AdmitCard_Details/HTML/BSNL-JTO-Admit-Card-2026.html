About BSNL JTO Admit Card / Exam Date 2026

Bharat Sanchar Nigam Limited (BSNL) has officially announced the exam dates and released the hall tickets for the recruitment of 100 Junior Telecom Officer (JTO) vacancies. The Computer Based Test (CBT) is scheduled to be conducted on 23 August 2026 across various centers in India.

Earlier this month, on 12 August 2026, the board activated the exam city intimation link to help candidates make advance travel arrangements. The final admit card, which contains the complete exam centre address, shift, and reporting time, was released on 20 August 2026.

Candidates are advised to download the admit card at the earliest to avoid last-minute server congestion. Carefully read the instructions mentioned on the admit card regarding permitted items, identity verification rules, and the reporting schedule.

How to Download BSNL JTO Admit Card / Check City

  1. Visit the official BSNL recruitment portal at externalexam.bsnl.co.in.
  2. Look for the link that says "Download Admit Card for Direct Recruitment of JTO (T) 2026".
  3. Enter your Registration ID/Number and Password or Date of Birth.
  4. Submit the security captcha code to proceed.
  5. Your BSNL JTO Exam City Slip or Admit Card will appear on the screen.
  6. Verify your exam date, city, centre address, and shift timing carefully.
  7. Download the PDF file and print a clear hard copy to carry on exam day.

Details Mentioned on Admit Card / City Slip

  • Candidate's Name and Roll / Registration Number
  • Exam Name: BSNL Junior Telecom Officer (JTO) 2026
  • Candidate's clear Photograph and Signature
  • Exam Date (23 August 2026)
  • Exam Shift and Timing
  • Reporting Time and Gate Closing Time
  • Exam City (on City Intimation Slip)
  • Complete Exam Centre Address (on Final Admit Card)
  • Category and PwBD status (if applicable)
  • Important Exam Day Instructions

Documents to Carry

Candidates will not be allowed entry without the mandatory documents. Please ensure you have the following ready before leaving for the exam centre:

  • Clear printed copy of the BSNL JTO Admit Card 2026.
  • Original and valid photo ID proof (Aadhaar Card, PAN Card, Voter ID, Passport, or Driving License).
  • 2 Recent passport-size photographs (matching the one uploaded during application).
  • Category/PwBD certificate in original (only if required by official instructions).

Exam Day Instructions

  • Reach the exam centre well in advance of the reporting time to complete biometric/frisking formalities.
  • Entry will strictly close after the gate closing time mentioned on the admit card.
  • Do not bring prohibited items like mobile phones, smartwatches, calculators, Bluetooth devices, or bags inside the exam hall.
  • Keep your admit card safe even after the exam, as it may be required during the document verification stage.
  • Follow all instructions given by the invigilators and exam centre staff.

Duplicate Admit Card / Correction Instructions

If you encounter issues downloading your admit card or find discrepancies in your details (like misspelled name, wrong photo, etc.), immediately check the official website for contact details or the designated helpdesk email/number. There is no official mention of issuing a duplicate admit card at the exam centre.

  • Try an alternate web browser or clear your cache if the page fails to load.
  • Contact BSNL Recruitment helpdesk immediately if the problem persists.

Frequently Asked Questions

Is BSNL JTO Admit Card 2026 released?

Yes, the BSNL JTO Admit Card 2026 was released on 20 August 2026 on the official portal.

What is the BSNL JTO 2026 exam date?

The Computer Based Test (CBT) for BSNL Junior Telecom Officer (JTO) is scheduled to be held on 23 August 2026.

Where can I download the BSNL JTO Admit Card?

Candidates can download their admit cards from the official portal at externalexam.bsnl.co.in using their login credentials.

What login details are required for the BSNL JTO admit card?

You will need your Registration ID/Number and Password/Date of Birth to download the admit card.

Is the BSNL JTO city intimation link active?

What is the exam mode for BSNL JTO 2026?
