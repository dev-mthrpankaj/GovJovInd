About NTA AIAPGET Admit Card 2026

The National Testing Agency (NTA) has officially released the admit cards for the All India Ayush Post Graduate Entrance Test (AIAPGET) 2026[cite: 1]. The examination is scheduled to be held on 22 August 2026[cite: 1]. The admit card is an essential document that grants candidates entry to the examination hall and contains vital details regarding their identity, exam centre, and schedule.

Before the admit cards were released, the NTA provided an Exam City Intimation slip on 13 August 2026[cite: 1]. This allowed candidates to confirm their allotted exam district and make necessary travel arrangements in advance. Candidates should use only the official NTA login portal to download their documents[cite: 1].

It is crucial that candidates verify all the details printed on their admit card. They should ensure that their name, photograph, roll number, and exam centre location match their official records. In case of any discrepancies, candidates must contact the NTA immediately.

How to Download NTA AIAPGET Admit Card 2026

  1. Visit the official NTA AIAPGET website at exams.nta.nic.in/aiapget/.[cite: 1]
  2. Look for the link that says "Download Admit Card for AIAPGET 2026".
  3. Enter your Application Number, Date of Birth (or Password), and the security pin.
  4. Click on the submit or login button.
  5. Your admit card will be displayed on the screen. Verify the exam date, city, centre, and timing carefully.
  6. Download the admit card in PDF format and take a clear, legible printout for exam day.
  7. Read the official candidate instructions printed on the admit card thoroughly.

Details Mentioned on AIAPGET Admit Card

  • Candidate's Name and Roll Number.
  • Application Registration Number.
  • Exam Name (AIAPGET 2026) and Subject/Course applied for.
  • Candidate's Photograph and Signature.
  • Exam Date: 22 August 2026.[cite: 1]
  • Exam Shift and Exam Timing.
  • Reporting Time and Gate Closing Time.
  • Full Exam Centre Name and Address.
  • Important instructions regarding COVID guidelines (if applicable), prohibited items, and exam conduct.

Documents to Carry

Candidates must carry specific documents for verification at the exam centre. Entry will not be allowed without them.

  • A clear, printed copy of the NTA AIAPGET Admit Card 2026.
  • An original, valid photo ID proof (such as Aadhaar Card, Voter ID, PAN Card, Driving Licence, or Passport).
  • A passport-size photograph (to be pasted on the attendance sheet), if instructed.
  • PwBD certificate (if claiming relaxation under the category).

Exam Day Instructions

  • Reach the exam centre well before the reporting time to complete biometric/frisking formalities.
  • Strictly note the gate closing time; no candidate will be allowed entry after the gates close.
  • Carry only the permitted items (Admit Card, ID proof, and photo) inside the examination hall.
  • Electronic gadgets, smartwatches, calculators, and bags are strictly prohibited.
  • Follow all instructions given by the invigilators and the exam centre staff.

Duplicate Admit Card / Correction Instructions

If you face issues downloading the admit card or spot errors in your personal details, follow these steps:

  • Check the official website for helpdesk contact details.
  • Ensure you are entering the correct Application Number and Date of Birth as provided during registration.
  • If an error is found in the admit card details (name, photo, signature), contact the NTA AIAPGET helpline immediately for correction guidelines.
  • Check official website notifications for any updates regarding admit card discrepancies.

Frequently Asked Questions

Is NTA AIAPGET Admit Card 2026 released?

Yes. The NTA has released the admit card for the All India Ayush Post Graduate Entrance Test (AIAPGET) 2026 on 18 August 2026.[cite: 1]

What is the NTA AIAPGET 2026 exam date?

The official exam date for AIAPGET 2026 is 22 August 2026. Candidates must verify their exact shift and reporting time from the admit card.[cite: 1]

When was the AIAPGET Exam City Intimation slip released?

The AIAPGET exam city intimation details were made available on 13 August 2026.[cite: 1]

Where can I download NTA AIAPGET Admit Card 2026?

Candidates can download the admit card from the official NTA AIAPGET portal at exams.nta.nic.in/aiapget/.[cite: 1]

What login details are required for the NTA AIAPGET admit card?

Candidates need their Application Number, Date of Birth/Password, and the security pin to log in and download their admit card.

What is the exam mode for NTA AIAPGET 2026?

The exam mode details and specific instructions will be clearly printed on the candidate's admit card.

What is the reporting time for AIAPGET 2026?

Reporting time is printed on the candidate's admit card. Candidates must strictly adhere to the gate closing time mentioned.

What documents should I carry for the AIAPGET exam?

Carry a printed admit card, an original valid photo identity proof, and any other specific document or photograph mentioned in the admit card instructions.

Is the city intimation slip the same as the admit card?

No, the city intimation slip only informs you about your exam city to help with travel arrangements. The admit card is mandatory for entry and contains the full exam centre address.

What should I do if my AIAPGET admit card is not downloading?

Ensure your login credentials are correct and try again. If the issue persists, contact the official NTA helpdesk for assistance.
