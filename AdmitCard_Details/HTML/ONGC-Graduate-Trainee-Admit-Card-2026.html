About ONGC Graduate Trainee Admit Card 2026

The Oil and Natural Gas Corporation Limited (ONGC) has scheduled the Computer Based Test (CBT) for the recruitment of Geologists and Engineers (Graduate Trainees) for 52 posts[cite: 1]. The admit card is an essential entry document, carrying candidate identity details, roll number, exam date, shift, centre details, and official instructions[cite: 1].

The official admit card portal asks candidates to log in with their Registration Number and Password or Date of Birth[cite: 1]. Candidates should use only the official login link for downloading their hall tickets securely.

Before leaving for the exam centre, candidates should meticulously check every detail printed on the admit card. Verification of the exact exam date (tentatively 06 September 2026), examination centre, and reporting time is critical for a smooth exam experience[cite: 1].

How to Download ONGC Engineer Admit Card 2026

  1. Visit the official ONGC website at www.ongcindia.com[cite: 1].
  2. Navigate to the Careers / Recruitment section[cite: 1].
  3. Click on the link for ONGC Graduate Trainee Admit Card 2026[cite: 1].
  4. Enter your Registration Number and Password/Date of Birth in the login portal[cite: 1].
  5. Enter the captcha code and click on Submit[cite: 1].
  6. Your ONGC Graduate Trainee Admit Card 2026 will appear on the screen[cite: 1].
  7. Verify your Exam Date, Exam Centre, and Reporting Time carefully[cite: 1].
  8. Download and print a clear copy of the admit card for future reference[cite: 1].

Frequently Asked Questions

Is ONGC Graduate Trainee Admit Card 2026 released?

Yes, ONGC released the admit card on 22 August 2026[cite: 1].

What is the ONGC Graduate Trainee 2026 exam date?

The CBT Exam is tentatively scheduled for 06 September 2026[cite: 1].

Where can I download the ONGC Graduate Trainee Admit Card 2026?

Candidates can download the admit card from the official ONGC website or the direct IBPS login link provided[cite: 1].

What login details are required to download the admit card?

Candidates require their Registration Number and Password or Date of Birth[cite: 1].

What is the exam mode for ONGC Graduate Trainee 2026?

The examination is conducted as a Computer Based Test (CBT)[cite: 1].

What is the reporting time for the ONGC CBT exam?

Reporting time varies by shift. Candidates must follow the exact reporting time printed on their individual admit cards[cite: 1].

What documents should I carry for the ONGC exam?

Carry a printed admit card, an original valid photo identity proof, and any other items specifically mentioned in the admit card instructions.

Is exam city intimation the same as the admit card?

No, the full admit card contains the exact centre address, whereas a city slip only shows the exam district. ONGC has released the full admit card.

What should I do if my ONGC admit card is not downloading?

Recheck your Registration Number and Password/Date of Birth[cite: 1]. Try using a stable internet connection and an updated desktop browser. Contact ONGC support if the issue persists.
