About This Admit Card

The ICFRE FRI Various Post Admit Card 2026 is for Group C recruitment under Advertisement No. 1/FRI/GC/2026. The recruitment covers 91 vacancies in multiple posts such as Library Information Assistant, Technical Assistant, Stenographer Grade II, Technician, Store Keeper, Lower Division Clerk, Forest Guard and Multi Tasking Staff.

The admit card is an important exam document because it confirms the candidate identity, exam schedule, exam city, centre address and entry instructions. Candidates should download the hall ticket only from the official portal and check every detail carefully before the exam day.

No separate detailed admit card notice has been provided here. Therefore, exam date, reporting time, shift and centre address should be treated as final only after checking the official admit card or official notice. Candidates should not depend on screenshots, forwarded messages or unofficial claims for exam-day instructions.

How to Download ICFRE FRI Admit Card

  1. Visit the official exam portal at friexam.in.
  2. Find the admit card link for the post you applied for under FRI Group C Recruitment 2026.
  3. Open the login page and enter the required details such as application number, registration number, date of birth or password.
  4. Submit the details and check the admit card shown on the screen.
  5. Verify candidate name, post name, exam date, exam city, centre address and reporting time carefully.
  6. Download the admit card PDF and take a clear printout for exam day.
  7. Read all exam-day instructions printed on the admit card before leaving for the examination centre.

Details Mentioned on Admit Card

  • Candidate name and registration/application details.
  • Advertisement number and post name.
  • Candidate photograph and signature, if printed.
  • Exam date, shift and reporting time.
  • Exam city and complete centre address.
  • Roll number or candidate ID, if allotted.
  • Important instructions for entry, identity proof and exam conduct.
  • Items allowed and not allowed inside the examination centre.

  • Carry a valid original photo identity proof as mentioned in the admit card.
  • Reach the centre before the reporting time printed on the admit card.
  • Check exam city and centre address in advance to avoid last-minute travel issues.
  • Do not carry prohibited items such as electronic gadgets, notes or any banned material.

  • Keep the admit card safely after the exam for answer key, result or next-stage reference.
