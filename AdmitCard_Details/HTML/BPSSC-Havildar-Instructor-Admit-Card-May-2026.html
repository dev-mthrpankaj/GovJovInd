About BPSSC Havildar Instructor Admit Card 2026

Bihar Police Sub-Ordinate Services Commission (BPSSC) has officially issued the e-Admit Card notice for the recruitment of Havildar Instructor under Advertisement No. 06/2026. This recruitment drive aims to fill 122 vacancies in Bihar Home Guard under the Home Department (Special), Government of Bihar. Candidates who applied successfully within the application window from 09 May 2026 to 09 June 2026 can now download their hall tickets.

The written examination is scheduled to be conducted on 05 August 2026 across designated examination centers in Bihar. Candidates can log in to the official portal using their Registration Number or registered Mobile Number along with Date of Birth to access the document.

Candidates must carefully check all details printed on the e-admit card, including their name, roll number, registration number, allocated exam center, reporting time, and shift details. It is mandatory to bring a printed copy of the e-admit card along with a valid original photo ID proof to the exam hall.

How to Download BPSSC Havildar Instructor Admit Card 2026

  1. Visit the official portal of BPSSC at bpssc.bihar.gov.in.
  2. Navigate to the "Bihar Home Guard" tab or check the latest notice section on the homepage.
  3. Click on the link for "Download e-Admit Card for BPSSC Havildar Instructor Written Exam (Advt No. 06/2026)".
  4. Enter your Registration Number or Registered Mobile Number and Date of Birth / Password in the login fields.
  5. Fill in the CAPTCHA code accurately and click on the "Submit / Login" button.
  6. Your e-Admit Card will appear on the screen. Verify all details such as Candidate Name, Exam Date, Shift, Reporting Time, and Center Address.
  7. Download the PDF and print a clear copy on A4 size paper for exam day reference.

Details Mentioned on Admit Card

  • Candidate Name and Parent/Spouse Name
  • Roll Number and Registration Number
  • Post Name and Advertisement Number (Advt No. 06/2026)
  • Candidate Photograph and Signature
  • Exam Date and Shift Timings
  • Reporting Time and Gate Closure Time
  • Exam Center Name, Code, and Detailed Address
  • Category and Gender
  • Important Instructions for Exam Day

Documents to Carry to the Exam Center

  • Hard copy printout of the BPSSC Havildar Instructor e-Admit Card 2026.
  • Original valid Photo Identity Proof (Aadhaar Card, Voter ID, PAN Card, Passport, or Driving License).
  • Two recent passport-size photographs (matching the photograph on the application form).
  • Simple black or blue ballpoint pen for OMR sheet marking.

Exam Day Instructions

  • Reach the allocated examination center strictly before the reporting time mentioned on the hall ticket.
  • No candidate will be allowed entry into the exam center after gate closure.
  • Electronic gadgets such as mobile phones, smartwatches, calculators, Bluetooth devices, and earphones are strictly prohibited inside the hall.
  • Ensure the printed photograph and details on your admit card are clear and legible.
  • Preserve your admit card safely after the exam as it will be required during Physical Efficiency Test (PET) and Document Verification.

Duplicate Admit Card / Correction Instructions

If you encounter issues while downloading the admit card or notice any error in your personal details, immediately contact the BPSSC helpline or check for official office visitation notifications prior to the exam date on the official website bpssc.bihar.gov.in.
