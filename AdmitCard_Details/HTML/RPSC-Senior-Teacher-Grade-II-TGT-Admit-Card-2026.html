About This Admit Card Notice

The Rajasthan Public Service Commission (RPSC) has finalized the preparation and uploaded the official online e-admit cards for the Senior Teacher Grade II TGT Competitive Examination 2026. Registered applicants can now access the official links using their dynamic login credentials to check their precise examination venues, shift assignments, and exam dates spread between July 12 and July 18, 2026.

This state-level offline recruitment examination aims to fill 6,500 vacant positions for Senior Teachers within the Secondary Education Department of Rajasthan. Earlier, the commission distributed the preliminary district city intimation slips on July 5, 2026, allowing candidates to arrange their travel plans well in advance before the main admit cards went live on July 9, 2026.

Candidates must remember that presenting a physical printout of the online e-admit card at the entrance gate is mandatory. Entry without a proper hall ticket, validation papers, and structural identification will be denied by the test venue administrators.

How to Download RPSC Senior Teacher Grade II Admit Card

Candidates have two distinct official methods to fetch their hall tickets online. Follow the structured step sequences below:

Method 1: Via Rajasthan Recruitment Portal

  1. Open the direct server link at recruitment.rajasthan.gov.in.
  2. Navigate to the section marked for active "Admit Card Link".
  3. Select "Senior Teacher Grade II Competitive Examination 2026".
  4. Type in your Application Number and input your exact Date of Birth.
  5. Provide the visible security captcha string and hit "Get Admit Card".
  6. Save the generated PDF locally and get a high-quality printout.

Method 2: Via Single Sign-On (SSO) Dashboard

  1. Log on to the state gateway at sso.rajasthan.gov.in.
  2. Provide your valid SSO ID (Username) and account Password.
  3. Locate and click on the "Recruitment Portal" application icon.
  4. Select the prominent "Get Admit Card" tab found near the header navbar.
  5. Find the relevant row matching RPSC Senior Teacher Recruitment 2026.
  6. Click the download button to pull the hall ticket document directly.

Details Printed on the RPSC Hall Ticket

Carefully read through all textual entries printed on your download copy. Verify these specific fields:

  • Full Name of the Candidate and Father's/Husband's Name.
  • Roll Number and Registration/Application Reference Number.
  • Categorization group details along with chosen subject paper options.
  • Allotted Written Examination Date, Shift Window, and exact session duration.
  • Complete physical postal address of the designated Examination Center.
  • Scanned photograph image and digitized signature check block.
  • Mandatory examination day execution guidelines issued by RPSC.

Documents to Carry on Exam Day

  • A clearly visible printed copy of the RPSC 2nd Grade e-Admit Card.
  • Original valid government-issued photo identification (Aadhaar Card is highly preferred; alternatively PAN Card, Driving License, Passport, or Voter ID).
  • Two recent passport-size color photographs identical to the one uploaded during application submission.
  • A transparent blue or black ballpoint pen for filling out the OMR answer sheets.

Important Exam Day Guidelines

To avoid cancellation or issues at the venue gate, candidates must strictly follow these administrative instructions:

  • Reporting Timings: Arrive at the test center location well in advance. The entrance gates close strictly 1 hour prior to the examination start time.
  • Prohibited Items: Do not bring electronics, mobile devices, smartwatches, calculators, bags, logbooks, books, loose sheets, or metallic accessories inside the building premises.
  • OMR Compliance: Carefully bubble in your Roll Number, Question Booklet Series code, and Subject details. Double-bubbling or using correcting fluid can invalidate the scoring engine results.

    Frequently Asked Questions

    Is the RPSC Senior Teacher Grade II TGT Admit Card 2026 released?

    Yes, the Rajasthan Public Service Commission officially launched the e-admit card download links on 9 July 2026.

    What are the actual examination dates?

    The offline OMR-based written exams are scheduled from 12 July 2026 to 18 July 2026 across multiple centers in Rajasthan.

    Which details are mandatory to fetch the admit card?

    You can use your Application ID paired with your Date of Birth on the recruitment page, or log in via your SSO ID credentials.

    What are the session shift timings?

    Generally, Morning shift papers occur between 10:00 AM and 12:00 Noon, while Evening shift specialty subject exams are held from 3:00 PM to 5:30 PM (or up to 12:30 PM for some morning single shifts).

    Is the city intimation slip different from the actual admit card?

    Yes, the city intimation slip released on July 5 only specified the district location. The actual admit card released on July 9 outlines the exact test center address.

    What should I do if there is a mistake printed on my admit card?

    If you encounter typos or blurry details, immediately reach out to the RPSC helpdesk numbers listed on their official website for correction procedures.

    What is the strict closing time for center gates?

    According to current RPSC rules, entrance gates close exactly 1 hour before the start of the paper. Late arrivals are strictly forbidden entry.

    Can I change my allotted examination center?

    No, the commission does not accept requests to change designated examination venues or shifts under any circumstances.
