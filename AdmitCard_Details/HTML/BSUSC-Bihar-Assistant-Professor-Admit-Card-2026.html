About This Admit Card & Interview Update

The Bihar State University Service Commission (BSUSC), Patna has initiated the phase-wise evaluation and interview process for the position of Assistant Professor. With 3,687 vacancies distributed across primary core subjects such as Hindi, History, English, Economics, Sociology, and Political Science, the commission has finalized the interview calendar spanning from July 20 to July 27, 2026.

Candidates who successfully registered up until the closing window of July 15, 2026, can check their eligibility status. Individual interview call letters containing absolute reporting timestamps, panel details, and guidelines are accessible via the centralized Higher Education Department dashboard system powered by Samarth.

Verifying all documents and carrying the printed verification slips is mandatory. Candidates will not be evaluated or permitted onto the official assessment panel premises if they fail to display a verified, legible, print version of their interview letters.

How to Download BSUSC Assistant Professor Interview Letter

  1. Navigate to the official institutional login URL at hedbiharrec.samarth.edu.in.
  2. Look up the primary authentication blocks or designated "Assistant Professor Login" modules.
  3. Enter your unique registered email identity or username alongside your secret access password.
  4. Resolve the CAPTCHA check cleanly if prompted by the platform security configurations.
  5. Locate the active option reading "Download Interview Letter / Admit Card" on the dashboard.
  6. Review the listed interview dates, panel number, and reporting hours displayed.
  7. Download the file copy locally and create crisp, physical A4 sheets prints.

Details Mentioned on Admit Card

  • Full candidate name and digital signature layout.
  • Unique roll sequence or allocation number.
  • Alloted Subject Name (e.g., Hindi, History, Economics, English, etc.).
  • Advertisement tracking serial code.
  • Exact date of interview panel reporting.
  • Detailed venue address and office building indicators in Patna.
  • Crucial document screening rules compiled by BSUSC.

Documents to Carry on Interview Day

  • Legible physical copy of the downloaded BSUSC Interview Call Letter.
  • Original online application acknowledgement copy printed at the time of form submission.
  • Valid original government identity documentation (Aadhaar, Passport, PAN Card, or DL).
  • All original educational transcripts (Master's Degree Certificate, UGC NET / CSIR NET clearance files, or Ph.D. documentation).
  • Caste / Category documentation certificates issued by competent authorities, if applying under reservation quotas.
  • Recent passport-size colour illustrations/photographs.

Interview Day Instructions

  • Arrive at the designated Patna venue exactly as per the specified shift timing window.
  • Maintain order inside the document verification rooms prior to facing the academic panel.
  • Electronic transmission tools, storage arrays, or support phones are completely barred inside assessment halls.
  • Review the official commission notifications frequently for subject-specific daily panels alignment shifts.

Frequently Asked Questions

Is the BSUSC Bihar Assistant Professor Admit Card 2026 released?

Yes, the interview letters/admit cards for the Bihar Assistant Professor recruitment have been released on the official Samarth portal link.

What are the BSUSC Assistant Professor interview dates?

How many vacancies are filled through this BSUSC recruitment?

A total of 3,687 posts are being filled for various subjects including Hindi, History, English, Economics, Sociology, and Political Science.
