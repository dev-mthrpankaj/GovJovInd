About PNB Local Bank Officer LBO Admit Card 2026

The PNB Local Bank Officer (LBO) 2026 admit card is a mandatory document for all candidates appearing in the online examination for the 545 available posts. It serves as your official entry pass to the examination hall and carries essential identity verification details.

The admit card can be downloaded through the official IBPS portal linked by Punjab National Bank. Candidates must log in using their Registration Number and Password or Date of Birth. It is highly recommended to use a desktop or laptop to download the admit card smoothly.

Upon downloading, thoroughly verify all printed details. Ensure that your name, photograph, signature, and registration number match your application. Familiarize yourself with the allotted exam centre location in advance to ensure timely arrival on the exam day.

How to Download PNB LBO Admit Card 2026

  1. Visit the official Punjab National Bank (PNB) website at pnb.bank.in and navigate to the Recruitments section.
  2. Click on the link for "Download Call Letter for Local Bank Officer (LBO) Exam 2026".
  3. You will be redirected to the IBPS login portal.
  4. Select your preferred language (English or Hindi).
  5. Enter your Registration Number or Roll Number.
  6. Enter your Password or Date of Birth (in DD-MM-YY format).
  7. Fill in the security captcha code and click on the Login button.
  8. Verify all details printed on the screen and download the PDF.
  9. Take a clear printout of the admit card on A4-size paper for the examination day.

Details Mentioned on PNB LBO Call Letter

  • Candidate's Name and Roll Number / Registration Number.
  • Exam Name: Local Bank Officer (LBO) 2026.
  • Candidate's Photograph and blank space for affixing a matching passport-size photo.
  • Exam Date: 06 September 2026.
  • Exam Shift and strictly allotted Reporting Time.
  • Full address of the Examination Centre.
  • Space for Candidate's Signature and Invigilator's Signature.
  • Important instructions regarding permitted items, identity verification, and exam protocol.

Documents to Carry

Banking exams strictly enforce identity verification protocols. Candidates must ensure they carry the exact combination of documents as instructed in the call letter.

  • Printed copy of the PNB Local Bank Officer Admit Card 2026.
  • Original valid photo identity proof (e.g., Aadhaar Card, PAN Card, Voter ID, Passport, or Driving License).
  • A clear photocopy of the same original photo ID proof (to be stapled with the admit card).
  • Recent passport-size photographs (same as uploaded in the application form).
  • Category / PwBD related documents only if claiming specific exemptions/scribe.

Exam Day Instructions

  • Reach the exam centre strictly before the reporting time printed on your admit card. Latecomers will not be allowed entry.
  • Carry the printed admit card along with the original ID and a photocopy of the ID.
  • Ensure your name on the ID proof exactly matches the name on your admit card.
  • Electronic gadgets, mobile phones, calculators, and smartwatches are strictly prohibited inside the exam hall.
  • Follow all instructions given by the invigilators and exam centre officials.
  • Retain a copy of the admit card securely even after the exam, as it will be required during document verification/interview stages.

Frequently Asked Questions

Is PNB Local Bank Officer Admit Card 2026 released?

Yes. Punjab National Bank has released the call letters for the Local Bank Officer (LBO) 2026 examination.

What is the PNB LBO 2026 exam date?

The PNB Local Bank Officer (LBO) 2026 exam is scheduled to be held on 06 September 2026.

Where can I download the PNB LBO Admit Card 2026?

Candidates can download the admit card from the official Punjab National Bank website or directly through the IBPS portal link provided for this recruitment.

What login details are required for the PNB LBO admit card?

You will need your Registration Number/Roll Number and Password/Date of Birth to download the admit card.

What is the exam mode for PNB LBO 2026?

The PNB Local Bank Officer exam is conducted in an Online Computer-Based Test (CBT) mode.

What is the reporting time for the PNB LBO Exam?

Reporting time varies by shift and is explicitly printed on the candidate's individual admit card. Candidates must strictly adhere to the time mentioned on their call letter.

What documents should I carry for the PNB LBO exam?

Candidates must carry the printed admit card, an original valid photo ID proof, a photocopy of the same ID proof, and passport-size photographs as specified in the call letter instructions.

What should I do if my PNB LBO admit card is not downloading?

Ensure you are entering the correct Registration Number and Password/DOB. If the issue persists, try clearing your browser cache, using a desktop browser, or contacting the IBPS/PNB helpdesk.
