About UP Police Constable DV / PST Stage

The Uttar Pradesh Police Recruitment & Promotion Board (UPPRPB) is conducting the second stage of recruitment for 32,679 Constable posts. Candidates who have been shortlisted based on the written examination cut-off marks are eligible to appear for Document Verification (DV) and Physical Standard Test (PST).

During the DV stage, candidate eligibility including age limit, educational qualification (10+2 Intermediate), domicile status, and category reservations will be authenticated against original certificates. Concurrently, the Physical Standard Test (PST) verifies physical attributes like height and chest expansion for male candidates, and height and weight standards for female candidates.

Candidates must carry the official printed copy of their DV/PST call letter along with all required original educational and identity certificates to their allotted district center.

How to Download UP Police DV / PST Admit Card

  1. Visit the official portal of UPPRPB at uppbpb.gov.in.
  2. Locate and click on the "UP Police Constable DV / PST Admit Card 2026" link under the main notices section.
  3. Enter your Registration Number and Date of Birth on the candidate login window.
  4. Submit the security captcha code shown on screen.
  5. Your DV / PST Call Letter displaying reporting center, time, and date will appear on the screen.
  6. Download the PDF and print 2 clear copies for presentation at the venue.

Documents Required During Verification (DV)

  • Printed copy of the UP Police DV / PST Admit Card.
  • 10th (Matriculation) Marksheet & Passing Certificate.
  • 12th (Intermediate) Marksheet & Passing Certificate.
  • Valid UP Domicile Certificate (for reservation/age relaxation benefit).
  • Cast Certificate (OBC / SC / ST / EWS) in official UP state format.
  • Government-issued valid Photo ID (Aadhaar Card, Driving License, Voter ID, Passport).
  • Preferential qualification certificates (NCC 'B' Certificate, O-Level Computer Certificate, Ex-Serviceman Discharge certificate if applicable).
  • Two recent passport-sized color photographs matching application form.
  • One set of self-attested photocopies of all above documents.

Important Rules & Exam Day Instructions

  • Report strictly at the designated DV/PST venue at the exact reporting time mentioned on your call letter.
  • Entry will not be permitted without a valid printed DV/PST Admit Card and Original Photo Identity Proof.
  • Maintain order and follow instructions provided by the verification officers and physical assessment staff.
  • Keep all original certificates organized in a single folder for smooth verification.

Frequently Asked Questions

Is UP Police Constable DV PST Admit Card 2026 released?

The UP Police Recruitment and Promotion Board (UPPRPB) publishes the DV/PST call letter and schedule on its official portal uppbpb.gov.in.

When does UP Police Constable DV PST start?

As per official schedule notices, the Document Verification (DV) and Physical Standard Test (PST) process begins from 17 August 2026.

What login credentials are required to download UP Police DV PST Admit Card?

Candidates require their Registration Number and Date of Birth to log in and download the hall ticket.

What height is required for Male candidates in UP Police Constable PST?

The standard minimum height for General/OBC/SC male candidates is 168 cm, and for ST male candidates, it is 160 cm.

What documents are required for UP Police Document Verification?

Candidates must carry 10th & 12th certificates, domicile certificate, category certificate (if applicable), photo ID proof, and passport-size photographs along with self-attested photocopies.

Where can candidates download the UP Police DV PST Call Letter?

Candidates can download the call letter directly from the official UPPRPB website at uppbpb.gov.in.

What running standard is required for Physical Efficiency Test (PET)?

Male candidates must complete 4.8 km in 25 minutes, while female candidates must complete 2.4 km in 14 minutes.

Will physical hall tickets be sent by post?

No, admit cards are issued purely online through uppbpb.gov.in and will not be dispatched by post.
