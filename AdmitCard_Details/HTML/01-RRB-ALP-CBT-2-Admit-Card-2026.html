About This Admit Card Notice

The Railway Recruitment Board (RRB) has officially finalized the tentative examination date for the Second Stage Computer Based Test (CBT-2) for the selection of the Assistant Loco Pilot (ALP) post under Centralized Employment Notice (CEN) 01/2025. This notice guides candidates regarding the timeline for downloading travel passes and individual e-call letters.

As per the board's instructions, the written phase of CBT-2 will take place on 28 July 2026. Registered applicants can view their allotted examination city, session dates, and print free travel authority cards (exclusively for eligible SC/ST candidates) exactly 10 days ahead of the test window.

Securing the physical e-admit card copy is vital for authentication checks at the venue gates. RRB has explicitly declared that individuals lacking the authentic original identification documents specified during the application phase will be barred from attempting the examination.

How to Download RRB ALP CBT-2 Admit Card

  1. Go to the central hub website at rrcb.gov.in or navigate to your regional RRB portal.
  2. Locate and click on the specific link titled "CEN 01/2025 Assistant Loco Pilot CBT-2 Exam City / Call Letter".
  3. Key in your personal Registration Number and valid login credentials securely.
  4. Examine the assigned exam centre, scheduled slot timing, and personal information thoroughly.
  5. Download the electronic call letter file and secure a high-quality print copy.
  6. Carefully study all general rules and exam-day regulations mentioned on the subsequent pages.

Details Mentioned on Admit Card

  • Full name of the candidate and formal photograph.
  • Unique roll number and central registration index.
  • CEN 01/2025 notice code along with post specification.
  • Detailed address of the assigned Computer Based Test laboratory.
  • Specific shift timing, reporting guidelines, and gate closure hour.
  • Scanned legal signature or thumb impression placeholder.
  • Essential disciplinary parameters outlined by the board.

Duplicate Admit Card / Correction Instructions

Check official website.

Documents to Carry on Exam Day

  • A clearly printed physical copy of the official RRB e-call letter.
  • The identical original Photo Identity Document as registered inside the application file.
  • Passport-size colored photographs matching application specifications.
  • Valid SC/ST caste authentication certificate if using the free travel authority pass.

Exam Day Instructions

  • Report accurately within the allocated timeline; entry is prohibited post gate closure.
  • Candidates must ensure that their Aadhaar account remains in an UNLOCKED CONDITION inside the UIDAI system to avoid biometric friction.
  • Electronic systems, smartphones, watches, and unauthorized stationery materials remain completely banned.
  • Selections remain purely objective and based systematically upon Computer Based Test merit scoring.
  • Retain the authenticated call letter copy carefully for verification in subsequent processing stages.

Frequently Asked Questions

What is the RRB ALP CBT-2 exam date 2026?

The Computer Based Test-2 (CBT-2) for the post of Assistant Loco Pilot (ALP) under CEN 01/2025 is tentatively scheduled to be held on 28 July 2026.

When can candidates download RRB ALP CBT-2 Admit Card 2026?

The downloading of e-call letters will start about 4 days prior to the exam date mentioned in the exam city and date intimation link.

When will the RRB ALP CBT-2 Exam City and Date link open?

The link for viewing the Exam City & Date and downloading the Travel Authority for SC/ST candidates will be made live 10 days prior to the final exam date.

Where can candidates download the RRB ALP CBT-2 call letter?

Candidates can access the direct download link on the official websites of their respective regional RRBs.

What details are required to download the RRB ALP admit card?

Candidates need to log in using their registration credentials on the official RRB web portals.

What identity documents are required at the exam centre?

Candidates must bring the same original photo identity document that was mentioned in their application form.

Is there any specific Aadhaar requirement for the RRB ALP CBT-2 exam?

Yes, candidates must ensure that their Aadhaar account remains in an unlocked condition in the UIDAI system before arriving at the exam centre to facilitate biometrics.

How is the final selection made for the RRB ALP recruitment?

RRB selections are based strictly on merit obtained through the Computer Based Test (CBT) process.
