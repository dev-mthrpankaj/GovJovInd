About This Admit Card Update

The Uttar Pradesh Co-operative Institutional Service Board (UPCISB), Lucknow has officially announced the computer-based online test schedule for 116 vacancies under Advertisement No. A-1 / S.R. / 2026. The examination is being organized for various positions including Manager, Junior Manager, Assistant / Cashier, Assistant / Typist, and Assistant Engineer (Civil) across District Co-operative Banks and UP State Construction Co-operative Federation Ltd.

As per the official notification published on June 30, 2026, a total of 14,883 unique applications were received before the closing date. The testing authority will make the download portal live for online call letters exactly 10 days before the scheduled test dates. Candidates will also be kept informed via text message alerts and registered email updates regarding their shift assignments.

Carrying a physical copy of the online e-admit card is completely mandatory for entry into the testing facility. Any candidate reporting without a verified admit card print and proper identity proof will be denied access to the computer terminals.

How to Download UPCISB Admit Card 2026

  1. Go to the official portal of UP Co-operative Institutional Service Board at upcisb.upsdc.gov.in.
  2. Locate and click on the "Important Notice / Admit Card Link" section on the homepage.
  3. Select the link designated for Advertisement No. A-1 / S.R. / 2026 Co-operative Banking Call Letter.
  4. Provide your login details such as Registration Number and Password/Date of Birth in the required fields.
  5. Verify your specific exam date, post name, session shift, and venue configuration layout.
  6. Download the digital call letter file and take a clear, legible printout on an A4 sheet.

Details Mentioned on Admit Card

  • Full name of the candidate and dynamic registration index.
  • Roll number / seating control designation number.
  • Applied post title (Manager / Junior Manager / Assistant / Cashier / Typist / AE).
  • Assigned examination date, venue address, and specific location mapping.
  • Shift details, reporting deadline window, and gate closing timings.
  • Candidate photograph and signature verification block.
  • Important computer-based testing layout rules and board guidance instructions.

Documents to Carry on Exam Day

  • Hard copy print of the downloaded UPCISB e-Admit Card.
  • Valid original photo identification card (Aadhaar Card, PAN Card, Voter ID, or Passport).
  • Two recent passport-sized color photographs identical to the one uploaded.
  • Black or blue simple ballpoint pen for rough sheets paperwork requirements.
  • Additional identity documentation details as specified on your formal printed instructions card.

Exam Day Instructions

  • Report at least 60 to 90 minutes before the session starts to complete biometric logs and scanning rules comfortably.
  • Electronic gear, mobile communication layouts, calculators, or smart wearables are completely prohibited inside the test zone.
  • Ensure that the photograph printed on your hall ticket is completely visible and matching your current identity layout.
  • Retain your signed admit card counterfoil securely as it may be verified during subsequent selection and document screening cycles.
  • Check the official web updates channel regularly for shift changes or venue updates.

Duplicate Admit Card / Correction Instructions

Check official website or contact the board directly via the provided organizational channels if you face login issues or discover errors in your registration name spelling on the downloaded hall ticket.

Frequently Asked Questions

What are the UPCISB Co-operative Banking exam dates 2026?

The written online examination will be conducted on July 20, July 21, and July 22, 2026, in multiple shifts depending on the post.

When can candidates download the UPCISB Admit Card 2026?

Candidates can download the admit card from the official website 10 days prior to the examination date, which is expected around July 10, 2026.

What are the exam timings for Assistant/Cashier posts?

The online exam for Assistant / Cashier posts will be held in the first shift from 09:00 AM to 11:00 AM on July 20, 2026.

How will candidates be notified about the UPCISB Call Letter?

UPCISB will send notification alerts via SMS to the registered mobile numbers and e-mails to the registered e-mail IDs of eligible candidates.

Where can candidates download the official UPCISB Admit Card?

Eligible candidates can access and download their hall tickets from the official portal at upcisb.upsdc.gov.in.

What details are required to download the UPCISB hall ticket?

Candidates will require their login credentials such as registration number, password, or date of birth on the official portal.

What is the total number of vacancies under Advertisement No. A-1/S.R./2026?

There are a total of 116 vacancies across various posts including Manager, Assistant, Cashier, Typist, and Assistant Engineer.

Will the board accept separate RTI queries regarding the exam schedule?

No, as per the official notification, separate RTI applications regarding the examination schedule will not be accepted by the board.
