About RSSB Computer Instructor Admit Card 2026

The Rajasthan Staff Selection Board (RSSB) has issued the admit card for the Computer Instructor (Senior and Basic) Recruitment 2026. This recruitment drive aims to fill 3951 vacancies across the state. The written examination is a crucial step in the selection process, making the admit card a mandatory document for entry into the examination hall.

Candidates can access their admit cards and exam city intimation slips using their Application Number, Date of Birth, or through their Rajasthan SSO login credentials. The city intimation slip helps candidates make prior travel arrangements, while the final admit card provides the exact exam centre address and shift timings.

It is strongly advised to verify all details printed on the admit card. Any discrepancy regarding candidate name, category, photograph, or signature should be immediately brought to the notice of the RSSB helpdesk to avoid issues on the exam day.

How to Download Admit Card / Check City Intimation

  1. Visit the official RSSB website at rssb.rajasthan.gov.in or the recruitment portal.
  2. Click on the "Admit Card" section or the link for "RSSB Computer Instructor Admit Card/City Slip 2026".
  3. Choose the appropriate login method (SSO login or Application Number & DOB).
  4. Enter your Registration/Application Number, Date of Birth, and the security captcha code.
  5. Click on the 'Get Admit Card' or 'Check Location' button.
  6. Verify your exam date, city, centre, and timing details displayed on the screen.
  7. Download the PDF and print a clear copy of the admit card for the exam day.
  8. Read the official instructions printed at the bottom of the admit card carefully.

Details Mentioned on Admit Card / City Slip

  • Candidate's Full Name and Father's Name.
  • Roll Number and Application/Registration Number.
  • Exam Name and Applied Post (Senior or Basic Computer Instructor).
  • Exam Date (22 or 23 August 2026).
  • Exam Shift and Timing.
  • Reporting Time and Gate Closing Time.
  • Exam City / District (available in City Slip).
  • Full Exam Centre Name and Address (available in final Admit Card).
  • Candidate's Photograph and Signature.
  • Important Exam Day Guidelines and Dress Code Instructions.

Documents to Carry

Candidates must ensure they carry the necessary documents to the exam hall. Failure to produce valid identification may result in disqualification.

  • A clear printed copy of the RSSB Computer Instructor Admit Card 2026.
  • Valid original photo ID proof (Aadhaar Card, Voter ID, PAN Card, Driving Licence, or Passport).
  • Recent passport-size color photographs (if explicitly required by the admit card instructions).
  • Blue/Black transparent ballpoint pen (check admit card if provided at the centre or needs to be carried).
  • Category/PwBD certificate (if applicable and instructed for verification).

Exam Day Instructions

  • Reach the exam centre well in advance, ideally 1-1.5 hours before the reporting time.
  • Entry will strictly close at the gate closing time mentioned on the admit card.
  • Strictly follow the RSSB dress code (usually light clothes, half sleeves, and simple footwear like slippers/sandals).
  • Do not carry electronic gadgets, mobile phones, smartwatches, calculators, or Bluetooth devices.
  • Do not carry prohibited items, heavy jewelry, or large bags into the exam premises.
  • Cooperate with security staff during the frisking and biometric verification process.
  • Retain the admit card safely even after the exam, as it may be required during the document verification stage.

Duplicate Admit Card / Correction Instructions

If you find any discrepancies in your admit card (like a spelling mistake in your name or an incorrect photograph), check the official website for correction windows or contact the RSSB helpdesk immediately. If your admit card is lost, you can re-download a duplicate copy by logging into the portal using the same Application Number/SSO ID and Date of Birth.
