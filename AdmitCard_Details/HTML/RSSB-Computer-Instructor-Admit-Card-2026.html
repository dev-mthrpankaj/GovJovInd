About RSSB Computer Instructor Admit Card & Exam City Notice 2026

The Rajasthan Staff Selection Board (RSSB) has announced the examination city notification for the 3951 Computer Instructor Recruitment under Advertisement Number 07/2026. Candidates who applied online between 25 May 2026 and 23 June 2026 can now verify their designated examination district prior to downloading their formal admit cards.

As per the official schedule, the written examination for both Senior Computer Instructor (322 posts) and Basic Computer Instructor (3629 posts) will take place across various exam centers in Rajasthan on 22 and 23 August 2026. The full-fledged e-admit cards will be available for download starting 19 August 2026.

Candidates must log in through the official Rajasthan Recruitment Portal or their SSO ID to retrieve their call letter. It is essential to download and print the hall ticket in advance to prevent last-minute server connectivity issues.

How to Download RSSB Computer Instructor Admit Card 2026

  1. Visit the official Rajasthan recruitment portal at recruitment.rajasthan.gov.in or login through sso.rajasthan.gov.in.
  2. Navigate to the "Get Admit Card" section on the homepage.
  3. Locate and click on the link titled "RSSB Computer Instructor Recruitment 2026 (Advt 07/2026)".
  4. Enter your Application Number, Date of Birth, and the security text (captcha code).
  5. Click on the 'Get Admit Card' button to display your hall ticket on the screen.
  6. Review your exam centre details, exam date, shift timings, and reporting instructions carefully.
  7. Download and print 2â€“3 clear copies of the admit card for examination day usage.

Details Mentioned on RSSB Computer Instructor Admit Card

After downloading your e-admit card, ensure that all the following details are clearly visible and correct:

  • Candidate's Full Name and Roll Number
  • Application Registration Number & SSO ID
  • Father's / Husband's Name
  • Exam Date, Shift, and Exact Reporting Time
  • Exam Centre Name, Code, and Detailed Address
  • Post Applied For (Basic or Senior Computer Instructor)
  • Candidate's Photograph and Digital Signature
  • Important Exam Day Guidelines and Subject Instructions

Documents to Carry to the Examination Centre

  • Clear hard copy of RSSB Computer Instructor E-Admit Card.
  • Original and valid Photo Identity Card (Aadhaar Card, Voter ID, PAN Card, Driving License, or Passport).
  • Recent passport-size color photographs (matching the application photo).
  • Transparent blue ballpoint pen for filling the OMR sheet.

Exam Day Instructions

  • Reach the examination venue strictly before the designated reporting time mentioned on the admit card.
  • Gate closing times are strictly enforced; latecomers will not be granted entry under any circumstances.
  • Electronic devices such as mobile phones, smartwatches, calculators, Bluetooth equipment, and study materials are completely prohibited inside the premises.
  • Follow all guidelines specified on the admit card regarding dress code and mandatory identity check routines.

Frequently Asked Questions

Is the RSSB Computer Instructor Admit Card 2026 released?

The Exam City Intimation Notice has been released on 14 August 2026. The full e-admit card will be released on 19 August 2026.

What is the exam date for RSSB Computer Instructor 2026?

The RSSB Computer Instructor examination is scheduled to be conducted on 22 and 23 August 2026.

When will the RSSB Computer Instructor Admit Card be available for download?

Candidates can download their admit card starting from 19 August 2026 onwards.

Where can candidates download the RSSB Computer Instructor Admit Card?

Candidates can download the admit card from the official recruitment portal at recruitment.rajasthan.gov.in or sso.rajasthan.gov.in.

What login details are required to download the admit card?

Candidates require their Application Number, Date of Birth, or Rajasthan SSO Login ID and Password to download the hall ticket.

How many total vacancies are advertised for RSSB Computer Instructor 2026?

A total of 3951 posts are advertised (322 Senior Computer Instructor and 3629 Basic Computer Instructor posts).

Is the exam city intimation slip the same as the admit card?

No. The exam city intimation slip only informs candidates about their exam district, whereas the admit card contains the exact exam venue address, shift, and reporting time.

What documents are required at the exam centre?

Candidates must carry a clear printed admit card, a original valid photo ID proof, recent passport-size photos, and a transparent blue ballpoint pen.
