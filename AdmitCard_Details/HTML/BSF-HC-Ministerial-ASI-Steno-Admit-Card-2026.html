About BSF HC Ministerial and ASI Steno Admit Card

The Border Security Force (BSF) has issued the admit cards for candidates participating in the recruitment process for 1526 vacancies of Head Constable (Ministerial) and Assistant Sub Inspector (Stenographer). The admit card is a mandatory entry document and serves as proof of identity and candidature at the examination centre.

The official BSF admit card portal requires candidates to log in using their credentials (User ID and Password). Candidates are advised to use the official login portal (hosted on Digialm) to securely download their hall tickets. Ensure that you are downloading the admit card well in advance to avoid last-minute server issues.

After downloading, candidates must thoroughly check the printed details. The admit card contains critical information such as the exam date, shift timings, full centre address, and specific instructions for the exam day. Any discrepancy in personal details should be immediately brought to the attention of the BSF recruitment cell.

How to Download BSF Admit Card 2026

  1. Visit the official BSF recruitment website at rectt.bsf.gov.in or use the direct Digialm login link.
  2. Navigate to the 'Candidate Login' section on the portal.
  3. Enter your Registration Number (User ID) and Password in the required fields.
  4. Fill in the correct security code / captcha as displayed on the screen.
  5. Click on the 'Login' button to access your dashboard.
  6. Click on the 'Download Admit Card / Hall Ticket' button for the HC (Min) & ASI (Steno) exam.
  7. Verify your exam date, reporting time, centre address, photograph, and signature.
  8. Download the PDF and take a clear printout for the exam day.

Documents to Carry

To gain entry to the exam hall, candidates must verify their identity. Follow the specific document instructions printed on your admit card.

  • A clear printed copy of the BSF HC & ASI Admit Card 2026.
  • Original and valid photo identity proof (e.g., Aadhaar Card, Voter ID, PAN Card, Driving Licence, or Passport).
  • Passport-size photographs (as per admit card instructions).
  • Category certificate or PwBD document, only if specifically required for verification.

Exam Day Instructions

  • Arrive at the exam centre well before the reporting time printed on the admit card.
  • Ensure you carry a physical printout of the admit card; digital copies on mobile phones will not be accepted.
  • Read the instructions carefully to know what items (like electronic gadgets, smartwatches, or bags) are prohibited inside the centre.
  • Check your exam centre location a day in advance to avoid last-minute delays.
  • Follow all guidelines provided by BSF personnel and invigilators at the exam centre.
  • Preserve the admit card safely after the exam, as it may be needed for further recruitment stages like skill tests and document verification.

Correction & Discrepancy Instructions

If your admit card contains errors or fails to download, candidates should take immediate action. Check the official website for helpdesk contact details.

  • Double-check that you are entering the correct User ID and Password.
  • Try using a stable internet connection and clearing your browser cache.
  • If personal details like your name or photo are misprinted, contact the BSF recruitment helpdesk immediately before the exam date.

Frequently Asked Questions

Is the BSF HC Ministerial and ASI Steno Admit Card released?

Yes, the Border Security Force (BSF) has released the admit cards for the Head Constable (Ministerial) and Assistant Sub Inspector (Stenographer) posts.

What is the BSF HC Ministerial and ASI Steno exam date?

The exact exam date will be conducted as per the official schedule. Candidates must verify their specific exam date, shift, and timing directly from their downloaded admit card.

Where can I download the BSF HC Ministerial Admit Card?

You can download the admit card from the official BSF recruitment portal or through the direct Digialm login link provided on the official website.

What login details are required for downloading the admit card?

Candidates will typically need their User ID (Registration Number) and Password to log into the admit card download portal.

What is the reporting time for the BSF HC Ministerial exam?

Reporting time is printed on each candidate's admit card. Candidates should strictly follow the reporting time mentioned on their personal hall ticket to avoid gate closure issues.

What documents are required at the BSF exam centre?

Candidates must carry a clear printout of their admit card, an original valid photo ID (like Aadhaar, Voter ID, PAN Card), and passport-size photographs as per the official instructions.

Is city intimation the same as the admit card?

No, a city intimation slip only provides the exam city to help with travel planning, whereas the final admit card contains the exact exam centre address, timing, and entry instructions.

What should I do if my BSF admit card is not downloading?

If you face issues, check your internet connection, ensure you are entering the correct User ID and Password, and try using a desktop browser. If the issue persists, contact the official BSF recruitment helpdesk.
