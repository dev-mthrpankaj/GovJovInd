About CGPSC State Engineering Service Admit Card 2026

Chhattisgarh Public Service Commission (CGPSC) conducts the State Engineering Service (SES) Exam to recruit qualified Civil Engineers for various state departments. Under Advertisement No. 02/2026, the commission announced a total of 46 vacancies. Candidates who successfully registered before the closing date (11 June 2026) are eligible to appear for the written test.

The written examination is scheduled to take place on 16 August 2026. Candidates can download their e-admit cards using their official login portal by providing registration identifiers and date of birth or password once released by the board.

It is mandatory for all applicants to carry a printed copy of the hall ticket to the assigned examination venue along with standard government photo identification. Please review all personal details, roll number, shift timing, and exam site guidelines mentioned on your downloaded copy.

How to Download CGPSC SES Admit Card 2026

  1. Visit the official website of Chhattisgarh Public Service Commission at psc.cg.gov.in.
  2. Locate and click on the "Admit Card" tab or "Download Admit Card - State Engineering Service Exam 2026" link on the homepage.
  3. Enter your Registration Number, Application Number, or User Name along with Password / Date of Birth.
  4. Complete security verification / CAPTCHA code if prompted on the login panel.
  5. Click on the "Login" or "Submit" button to display your e-admit card on screen.
  6. Verify your printed information including candidate name, roll number, photo, signature, exam date, shift timing, and venue address.
  7. Download the PDF copy and take a legible printout on A4 size paper for exam day use.

Details Mentioned on Admit Card

  • Candidate's Full Name & Application Registration Number
  • Roll Number & Candidate Category / Gender
  • Exam Name (CGPSC State Engineering Service Exam 2026)
  • Post Name & Advertisement Reference Number (Advt 02/2026)
  • Exam Date (16 August 2026) & Shift Timings
  • Reporting Time & Gate Closing Time
  • Assigned Exam Centre Name and Complete Venue Address
  • Candidate Photo and Digital Signature
  • Important Examination Day Rules & Guidelines

Documents to Carry to the Exam Hall

  • Clear printout of CGPSC SES Admit Card 2026.
  • Valid Original Photo Identity Proof (e.g., Aadhaar Card, PAN Card, Voter ID, Driving License, or Passport).
  • Recent passport-size color photographs (if specified in hall ticket instructions).
  • Required stationery items as permitted by CGPSC exam rules.

Exam Day Instructions

  • Arrive at the examination venue well before the reporting time specified on your admit card to complete security screening smoothly.
  • Electronic devices such as mobile phones, smartwatches, calculators, Bluetooth equipment, or unauthorised printed material are strictly forbidden inside the hall.
  • Verify all particulars printed on your admit card beforehand. In case of any discrepancy, notify the CGPSC helpdesk promptly.
  • Retain your printed admit card securely after the written test, as it may be required during subsequent selection stages and document verification.

What is the CGPSC State Engineering Service exam date 2026?

When will the CGPSC SES Admit Card 2026 be released?

Where can candidates download the CGPSC SES admit card?

What login details are required to download the CGPSC SES admit card?
