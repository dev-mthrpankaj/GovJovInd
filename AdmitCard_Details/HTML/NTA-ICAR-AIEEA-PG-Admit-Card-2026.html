About This Admit Card & City Slip Notice

The National Testing Agency (NTA) is organizing the national level entrance test on behalf of the Indian Council of Agricultural Research (ICAR). This exam facilitates admissions into various Postgraduate (PG) and Doctoral (Ph.D.) degree programmes across Agricultural and Allied Sciences branches for the academic session 2026-27. The written entrance tests will be organized systematically across various designated centers.

As per the public notice released on 25 June 2026, NTA has activated the advance exam city allotment facility. Candidates can now verify the location of their test city to plan their transit and logistics efficiently. Logins can be managed securely using application credentials provided during registration.

Candidates are explicitly cautioned that the city intimation document must not be treated as the actual entry hall ticket. The main admit card, containing precise verification instructions, sitting arrangements, room allotments, and roll numbers, will follow separately before the test date.

How to Download ICAR AIEEA PG Exam City Slip & Admit Card

  1. Navigate to the official web link hosted at exams.nta.nic.in/icar/.
  2. Locate the designated link for checking the Advance City Allotted or Admit Card download section.
  3. Enter your unique Application Number along with your secure Password or Date of Birth in the fields.
  4. Submit details and carefully read the allotted district name shown on the slip.
  5. Keep monitoring the identical dashboard portal to save your official hall ticket once published online.
  6. Print a neat copy of the documents and preserve them carefully for examination venue verification.

Details Mentioned on Admit Card

  • Full Name and signature pattern of the candidate.
  • Unique Application Form Number and newly allotted Roll Number.
  • Exact examination category name (PG or Ph.D. branch stream).
  • Assigned Date of the test along with individual shift windows.
  • Comprehensive address layout of the assigned test venue.
  • Latest guidelines and regulatory practices framed by NTA.

Documents to Carry on Exam Day

  • Clean and legible print copy of the official NTA ICAR Admit Card.
  • Valid original government-issued photo identity identification (Aadhaar, Voter Card, Driving License, or Passport).
  • Passport size colored photographs as prescribed in individual form profiles.
  • Additional identity proofs or medical exemptions if applicable.

Exam Day Instructions

  • Reach the examination venue well before the gate closure window mentioned inside the hall ticket.
  • Remember that the advance city slip document cannot be presented for entry verification at the test gate.
  • Electronic systems, modern smart watches, calculation logs, and unauthorized stationeries remain strictly banned.
  • Follow the instructions provided by the room invigilator inside the venue during compliance checks.

Frequently Asked Questions

What is the NTA ICAR AIEEA PG exam date 2026?

The entrance examination for ICAR AIEEA PG and AICE JRF/SRF Ph.D. 2026 will be conducted on 4 July 2026, Saturday.

When can candidates download the ICAR AIEEA PG Admit Card 2026?

The official hall ticket or admit card will be issued by NTA in due course before the exam. Currently, only the advance exam city intimation slip is available.

Is the city intimation slip the same as the admit card?

No, the advance intimation slip only informs candidates about their allotted exam city to help them plan travel. It cannot be used to enter the examination hall.

What details are required to download the ICAR AIEEA PG exam city slip?

Candidates need to log in using their Application Number and Password or Date of Birth on the NTA portal.

Where is the official link to download the city slip and admit card?

Candidates can access these updates directly on the official NTA website at exams.nta.nic.in/icar/.

What should I do if there is an error or difficulty downloading the slip?

Candidates facing issues can reach the official NTA Helpdesk at 011-40759000 or 011-69227700, or email their queries to icar@nta.ac.in.

What documents are required at the exam venue?

Candidates must bring a printed copy of the official admit card (when released), along with a valid photo identification card and other items specified in the final instructions.

What is the exam timing for ICAR AIEEA PG 2026?

The official shift schedule and exact exam timings will be notified on the final admit card copy. Candidates should verify details there.
