About RRB Group D Exam City Details 2026

Railway Recruitment Boards (RRBs) have officially issued the City Intimation Slip for the upcoming Computer Based Test (CBT) for Level-1 Group D posts under Advertisement No. CEN 09/2025. This recruitment drive covers 22,195 vacancies for various Level-1 roles across Indian Railways regions.

The CBT examination is scheduled to be held from 03 August 2026 to 21 August 2026 in multiple shifts across nationwide exam centres. Candidates can now log into the unified examination portal using their application registration number and date of birth to check their allotted exam city, exam date, shift timing, and SC/ST travel pass authority (if applicable).

Candidates should note that the City Intimation Slip is strictly an informative document to help candidates plan their travel to the allotted exam city. The final official e-Call Letter (Admit Card) containing full exam centre name, address, centre code, and reporting instructions will be released 4 days prior to each candidate's respective exam date.

How to Check RRB Group D Exam City Details 2026

  1. Visit the official RRB examination portal at rrb.digialm.com or your respective regional RRB website.
  2. Click on the link titled "RRB Group D Exam City Details 2026 / City Intimation Slip CEN 09/2025".
  3. Enter your Application Registration Number and Password / Date of Birth.
  4. Enter the displayed Security Captcha code and click on the "Submit / Login" button.
  5. Your RRB Group D City Intimation Slip will appear on the screen.
  6. Check your allotted exam city, CBT date, shift timing, and reporting time.
  7. Download and save a copy of the city slip for reference.

Details Mentioned on City Slip vs Admit Card

Details on City Intimation Slip

  • Candidate's Name & Registration Number
  • Exam Date & Scheduled Shift
  • Allotted Exam City & State
  • Reporting Time & Shift Start Time
  • SC/ST Travel Authority details (if eligible)

Details on Official Admit Card

  • Exact Exam Centre Name & Address
  • Exam Centre Code & Landmark
  • Candidate Photo & Signature Space
  • Roll Number & Invigilator Signature Box
  • Detailed Exam Day Rules & Instructions

Documents to Carry to the Examination Centre

  • Printed copy of official RRB Group D e-Call Letter (Admit Card) printed on A4 paper.
  • Original Valid Photo Identity Proof (Aadhaar Card, E-Aadhaar, Passport, PAN Card, Voter ID, or Driving License).
  • One recent passport-size color photograph (matching application photo).
  • Free Travel Authority pass printout for eligible SC/ST candidates along with caste certificate copy.

Exam Day Instructions

  • Report at the allotted exam centre strictly within the specified reporting window. Late entry will not be permitted.
  • Aadhaar-linked biometric authentication will be conducted at the entry gate before candidate admission.
  • Prohibited items such as mobile phones, smartwatches, calculators, Bluetooth devices, bags, or metallic ornaments are strictly banned.
  • Follow all instructions issued by exam centre officials and invigilators during the Computer Based Test.
  • Keep your admit card safely after CBT for future stages like Physical Efficiency Test (PET) and Document Verification (DV).

Frequently Asked Questions

Is RRB Group D Exam City Details 2026 released?

Yes, Railway Recruitment Board has activated the link to check RRB Group D City Intimation Slip 2026 for CEN 09/2025.

When is the RRB Group D CBT exam date 2026?

The CBT examination for CEN 09/2025 is scheduled from 03 August 2026 to 21 August 2026.

Is the city intimation slip the same as the final admit card?

No. The city intimation slip shows your allotted exam city and date. The e-call letter (admit card) with exact centre details comes out 4 days prior to your exam date.

How can candidates check their RRB Group D Exam City?

Log in at rrb.digialm.com using your Registration Number and Password or Date of Birth.

When will the official RRB Group D Admit Card 2026 be released?

The e-call letter will be available for download 4 days prior to your specific exam date.

What login credentials are required to check RRB Group D City Slip?

You need your Registration Number and Date of Birth along with the captcha code.

What is the total number of vacancies in RRB Group D CEN 09/2025?

There are 22,195 Level-1 vacancies across Indian Railway zones.

What documents are required to carry on RRB Group D exam day?

Bring a printed e-Call Letter (Admit Card), an original valid Photo ID (Aadhaar, Voter ID, PAN Card, etc.), and passport-size photographs.
