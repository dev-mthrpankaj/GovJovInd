About This Admit Card

POWERGRID has published an admit card notice for the recruitment of professionals in Company Secretary discipline as Officer Trainee. The notice is related to Advertisement Ref. No. CC/02/2026 dated 28 April 2026 and the Special Recruitment Drive for ST category.

The admit card is important because it works as the candidate entry document for the Computer Based Test. Candidates should download it from the POWERGRID career page and check the exam venue, reporting time, identity proof requirement and candidate information carefully.

The official notice specifically asks candidates to carry the original Government ID proof mentioned in the admit card for identity verification. Candidates should keep checking the POWERGRID website because official updates, if any, will be released there only.

How to Download Admit Card

  1. Visit the official website: powergrid.in.
  2. Open the career/admit card/recruitment section mentioned in the official notice.
  3. Select the admit card link for Officer Trainee (Company Secretary).
  4. Enter the required login details such as registration number, mobile number, date of birth or other details asked by the portal.
  5. Verify exam date, exam centre, reporting time and candidate details before printing.
  6. Download and print the admit card, then read all instructions carefully before going to the exam centre.
