About This Admit Card Update

The Karnataka Examination Authority (KEA) is organizing the written competitive examination for the post of Police Constable (Civil) to fill 3,991 vacancies across Kalyana Karnataka and Non-Kalyana Karnataka cadres. Candidates who submitted their online applications are required to stay updated regarding hall ticket release dates.

The hall ticket acts as an essential document granting entry into the designated examination center. It contains pivotal information including roll number, candidate details, exam timings, shift duration, reporting time, and specific instructions for exam day adherence.

Candidates are strongly advised to check the details mentioned on their hall ticket carefully after downloading it and follow all rules prescribed by KEA for a seamless examination experience.

How to Download Karnataka Police Constable Admit Card

  1. Visit the official portal of Karnataka Examination Authority at cetonline.karnataka.gov.in/kea/.
  2. Locate the latest recruitment section on the homepage and click on "Police Constable Recruitment 2026 Admit Card".
  3. Enter your Registration Number and Password/Date of Birth in the login portal.
  4. Submit details to view your admit card on the screen.
  5. Verify all printed details including candidate name, exam date, time, and allotted center address.
  6. Download and print multiple clear copies of the admit card for use on examination day.

Details Mentioned on Admit Card

  • Full Candidate Name
  • Roll Number and Application/Registration Number
  • Photograph and Digital Signature of the Candidate
  • Exam Date and Shift Timings
  • Reporting Time and Gate Closing Time
  • Examination Center Name, Code, and Detailed Address
  • Category and Gender
  • Important Instructions and COVID-19 / Center Rules

Documents to Carry on Exam Day

  • Clear hard copy printout of Karnataka Police Constable Admit Card.
  • Original Valid Photo Identity Card (Aadhaar Card, Voter ID, Driving License, PAN Card, or Passport).
  • Recent Passport Size Photographs (if specified on the hall ticket).
  • Blue/Black Ballpoint Pen for filling OMR sheets (as permitted by exam guidelines).

Exam Day Instructions

  • Reach the examination venue well before the designated reporting time to complete verification smoothly.
  • Electronic items such as mobile phones, smartwatches, calculators, Bluetooth devices, and digital bands are strictly prohibited.
  • Do not wear prohibited accessories, jewelry, or footwear with thick soles as per board instructions.
  • Maintain decorum and strictly adhere to all guidelines stated on your admit card and issued by exam invigilators.

Correction & Helpline Instructions

In case candidates observe any spelling mistake or discrepancy in candidate personal details, photo, or signature on the downloaded hall ticket, they must immediately contact the Karnataka Examination Authority (KEA) helpdesk/support before the exam date for necessary rectification.

Frequently Asked Questions

Is the Karnataka Police Constable Admit Card 2026 released?

What is the exam date for Karnataka Police Constable 2026?

How many vacancies are available in Karnataka Police Constable Recruitment 2026?

A total of 3,991 Civil Police Constable posts are to be filled through this recruitment.

Where can candidates download the Karnataka Police Constable Admit Card?

What login details are required to download the admit card?
