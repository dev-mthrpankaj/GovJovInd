About SSC JHT Admit Card / Exam Date 2026

The Staff Selection Commission (SSC) has officially announced the exam dates for the Junior Hindi Translator (JHT), Junior Translation Officer (JTO), and Senior Hindi Translator (SHT) Examination 2026. The examination for a total of 84 posts is scheduled to be conducted on 08 September 2026[cite: 1]. Candidates should regularly monitor the official website to download their respective admit cards as soon as the link goes live.

The SSC admit card is a mandatory document that provides critical details such as the candidate's name, roll number, exact exam city, test center location, reporting time, and shift timings. Typically, SSC releases the exam city intimation slip about 10 days before the exam, and the final admit card is made available 3 to 4 days prior to the test date.

Candidates are strongly advised to thoroughly verify all the details printed on their admit card. It is also important to read the attached instructions to ensure compliance with identity verification procedures and center rules on the day of the exam. Use only the official SSC website or regional portals to check your application status and download the hall ticket.

How to Download SSC JHT Admit Card 2026

  1. Visit the official Staff Selection Commission website at ssc.gov.in or your respective regional SSC portal[cite: 1].
  2. Look for the "Admit Card" tab on the homepage and click on it[cite: 1].
  3. Click on the specific link indicating "Download Admit Card for Junior Hindi Translator Examination 2026"[cite: 1].
  4. Enter your login credentials: Registration Number and Date of Birth / Password[cite: 1].
  5. Complete the CAPTCHA code or security verification if prompted[cite: 1].
  6. Click the Submit/Login button[cite: 1].
  7. Your admit card will appear on the screen. Verify the exam date, shift, city, center address, and reporting time carefully[cite: 1].
  8. Download the admit card in PDF format and take a clear printout to carry to the examination center[cite: 1].

Details Mentioned on SSC JHT Admit Card / City Slip

  • Exam Name (Junior Hindi Translator 2026)

Documents to Carry to the Exam Centre

Candidates appearing for the SSC JHT 2026 examination must ensure they have the following documents ready for verification on the exam day:

  • A clear printed copy of the SSC JHT Admit Card 2026[cite: 1].
  • A valid, original photo ID proof (e.g., Aadhaar Card, PAN Card, Voter ID, Passport, or Driving License)[cite: 1].
  • Passport-size photographs (usually 2 copies, as mentioned in the admit card instructions).
  • Category/PwBD certificates only if specially requested by the official instructions.

Exam Day Instructions

  • Arrive at the exam center strictly before the reporting time indicated on your admit card to complete frisking and registration.
  • The entry gate will close sharply at the stipulated time; no candidate will be allowed entry afterward.
  • Ensure your original photo ID proof matches the name and Date of Birth exactly as printed on the admit card.
  • Electronic gadgets, smartwatches, calculators, and study materials are strictly prohibited inside the exam hall.
  • Follow all instructions given by the invigilators and read the printed guidelines on your admit card carefully.

Duplicate Admit Card / Correction Instructions

If you face issues downloading the admit card or notice any discrepancies in the printed details (like name spelling or photo mismatch), you should immediately check the official website or contact the respective regional SSC office. Generally, no separate duplicate admit card is sent offline; it must be fetched from the portal online.
