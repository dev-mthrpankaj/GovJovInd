About This Admit Card Update

The Board of School Education Haryana (BSEH), Bhiwani has declared that the HTET 2025 assessment will take place on 04 July 2026 and 05 July 2026 across various assigned centers. Eligible candidates are authorized to fetch their online hall tickets beginning 01 July 2026 through the integrated board utility. Access requires authentication using unique credentials assigned during registration.

Possessing a genuine admit card is necessary for securing access to the exam facility. Candidates must carefully check the information printed on it, such as their name, structural roll allocation, specific timings, and exam instructions. Any discrepancies must be rectified ahead of the target examination window.

BSEH has strictly stated that only physical color copies of the admit card will be accepted at the exam venue gates. Candidates are advised to keep multiple copies ready and review safety security guidelines to prevent issues at the venue layout.

How to Download HTET Admit Card

  1. Go to the main online destination of BSEH at www.bseh.org.in.
  2. Locate the dynamic active button labeled for downloading HTET 2025 Admit Cards.
  3. Provide your unique Registration Number or Application ID along with your Date of Birth in the login screen form elements.
  4. Review the information concerning exam shifts, schedule structure, and instructions layout.
  5. Download the document files and print clear, clean copies of the admit card pages.
  6. Read the printed guidelines completely to prevent any violations at the exam venue gates.

Details Mentioned on Admit Card

Candidates must carefully check that the following information on the document matches their details:

  • Candidate Name and Signature profiles.
  • Roll Number and system registration codes.
  • Allotted Exam Dates (04 or 05 July 2026).
  • Assigned Shift window details and specific test block times.
  • Precise structural address details of the assigned venue.
  • Separate dedicated placeholders for Center Copy and Candidate Copy profiles.
  • Official set of exam day compliance regulations.

Documents to Carry on Exam Day

  • Mandatory Colour Printout: One copy each of the Center Copy page and Candidate Copy page of the admit card.
  • Photo Identity Card: The original valid photo identification document that was used during the online form submission (details are listed on the admit card).
  • Verified Photograph: A recent colored photograph (identical to the registration upload image) pasted in the specified slot on the admit card and duly attested by a Gazetted Officer.

Exam Day Instructions

  • Reporting Requirement: Candidates must report to the exam centre 02 hours and 10 minutes prior to the start time to complete mandatory procedures such as biometric registration, thumb indexing, and security scanning checks smoothly.
  • Printing Condition: Only clear Colour Printouts of the admit card are valid. Black and white prints will not be accepted.
  • Venue and Subject Changes: Modifying structural allocations for the testing venue or selected paper subjects is strictly prohibited.
  • Prohibited Items: Do not bring metal objects, rings, earrings, necklaces, chains, brooches, cameras, watches, calculators, mobile phones, pagers, bluetooth systems, earphones, wallets, log tables, health bands, or electronic items. Plastic pouches and empty or written papers are also banned.
  • Allowed Religious/Traditional Items: Female candidates are permitted to wear Bindi, Sindoor, and Mangalsutra. Baptized Sikh candidates are allowed to carry their mandatory religious articles.

Scribe Facility & Compensatory Time

Visually impaired candidates and other candidates with disabilities who are unable to write by hand are eligible for 50 minutes of compensatory time per paper. This applies whether they use a scribe facility or write independently.

Candidates requiring a scribe (qualification must not exceed Senior Secondary) can choose their own assistant or request one from the board by following the procedures below:

  • Board Office Registration: Contact Room No. 28 at the Board Office between 09:00 AM and 04:30 PM at least 4 days before the exam.
  • Centre Superintendent Verification: Alternatively, present valid medical documents and the scribe's identity/qualification credentials to the Centre Superintendent at the venue between 01:00 PM and 04:00 PM at least 2 days before the exam.
  • Forms SPL-1, SPL-2, and Appendix-G are available for download on the official website.

Duplicate Admit Card / Correction Instructions

If you encounter any issues or errors while downloading your admit card, you can contact the official Board helpline number below for assistance:

  • BSEH Helpline Phone Support: 01664-254305
  • For correction or alternate access instructions, please check the official website.

Frequently Asked Questions

What is the HTET 2025 exam date?

The Haryana Teacher Eligibility Test (HTET) 2025 will be conducted on 04 July 2026 (Saturday) and 05 July 2026 (Sunday).

When can candidates download the HTET Admit Card 2026?

Candidates can download their HTET admit card starting from 01 July 2026 onwards.

Which website can I use to download the HTET admit card?

You can download the admit card from the official website of the Board of School Education Haryana at www.bseh.org.in.

What details are required to log in and download the HTET hall ticket?

Candidates need to fill in their Registration Number or Application ID along with their Date of Birth.

Is a black and white printout of the HTET admit card allowed?

No, the official notice states that it is mandatory to bring a clear Colour Printout of the admit card (both Center Copy and Candidate Copy).

What is the reporting time at the HTET exam centre?

Candidates must report at least 2 hours and 10 minutes prior to the commencement of the exam for biometric data capturing and metal detector checks.

Can I request a change in my HTET exam centre or subject?

No, changes to the allocated exam centre or subject are strictly prohibited according to the board guidelines.

How can I contact the BSEH board if I face issues downloading my HTET admit card?

In case of any difficulty during download, candidates can reach out to the board helpline at 01664-254305.
