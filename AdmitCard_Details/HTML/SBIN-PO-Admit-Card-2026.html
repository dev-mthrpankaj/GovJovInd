About SBI PO Admit Card 2026

State Bank of India (SBI) conducts the Probationary Officers recruitment annually to select eligible candidates across its branches nationwide. For the 2026 drive, SBI announced 1500 vacancies for Probationary Officers. Candidates who successfully submitted their online application forms within the deadline can now access their admit card portal.

The SBI PO Call Letter contains critical details including candidate registration particulars, allocated examination centre name, address, roll number, reporting time, and exam day instructions. It is mandatory for every applicant to bring a clear printed copy of their hall ticket along with a original photo identity card to gain entry into the examination center.

Candidates are encouraged to download their admission ticket early to avoid server traffic toward the close of the download window. Verify every detail printed on your admit card immediately upon downloading.

How to Download SBI PO Admit Card 2026

  1. Visit the official career portal of State Bank of India at sbi.bank.in/web/careers or click the direct admit card link below.
  2. Locate the section titled "Recruitment of Probationary Officers (Advertisement No. 2026)".
  3. Click on the "Download Call Letter / Admit Card" portal link.
  4. On the login page, select your preferred language (English or Hindi).
  5. Enter your Registration Number / Roll Number and Password / Date of Birth (DD-MM-YY).
  6. Fill in the Captcha code shown on the screen and click Submit / Login.
  7. Your SBI PO Admit Card 2026 will appear on the screen. Verify all details and print a copy for examination day.

Details Mentioned on Call Letter

  • Candidate's Full Name & Father's Name
  • Roll Number and Registration Number
  • Exam Date, Shift, and Reporting Time
  • Exam Centre Name, Code, and Detailed Venue Address
  • Candidate's Scanned Photograph and Signature
  • Category / Community Details
  • Important Instructions regarding exam guidelines and prohibited items

Documents to Carry on Exam Day

  • Hard copy of printed SBI PO Admit Card 2026 with photograph pasted as instructed.
  • Original Valid Photo Identity Proof (Aadhaar Card, PAN Card, Passport, Driving License, Voter ID, or Bank Passbook with photo).
  • Clear photocopy of the same Photo Identity Proof.
  • Additional passport-size photographs as specified on the call letter.
  • Transparent ballpoint pen.

Exam Day Guidelines

  • Report to the exam venue strictly within the reporting time mentioned on your admit card. Late entry will not be permitted.
  • Ensure that the photograph pasted on the admit card matches the uploaded image in your application.
  • Electronic items such as mobile phones, smartwatches, calculators, Bluetooth devices, or electronic gadgets are strictly banned.
  • Follow all guidelines provided by the invigilators inside the test lab.
  • Keep your admit card safe after the exam for subsequent selection rounds.

Admit Card Verification & Queries

If you observe any discrepancy in your name, category, or photograph on the e-admit card, or encounter technical errors while logging in, check official website or contact the official recruitment helpline provided on the SBI Careers portal immediately prior to the examination date.
