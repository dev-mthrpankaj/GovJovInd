About This UP Homeguard DV/PST Process Update

The Uttar Pradesh Police Recruitment and Promotion Board (UPPRPB) has published notification letter No. PRPB-B (Homeguard Cadre)-02/2025, detailing the explicit methodologies that will govern the Document Verification and Physical Standard Testing phases for the Homeguards Enrollment-2025. This notice builds upon the foundational advertisement issued on November 18, 2025, and governmental order number 60/2025/1780/95-2025-08Misc/2012.

The procedure clarifies that a highly regulated board consisting of administration, police, education, and medical officials will review structural benchmarks on-site. The candidate documentation checking involves validating eligibility, vertical/horizontal reservations, age parameters, and specific weightages given to mandatory or preferential educational certifications. Any attempt to introduce falsified, deceptive, or manipulated paperwork will result in instant cancellation of candidature alongside immediate initiation of legal criminal proceedings.

Composition of the Verification Committee

A multi-departmental committee has been set up to oversee the selection process at each center to ensure transparency. The structural layout of the panel is as follows:

  • Chairman: A Deputy Collector nominated specifically by the respective District Magistrate (DM).
  • Member (Police Representative): A Deputy Superintendent of Police (DSP) nominated by the district police chief.
  • Member (Education Representative): A Gazetted Officer designated by the District Inspector of Schools (DIOS).
  • Member (Medical Representative): A Medical Officer authorized by the Chief Medical Officer (CMO) of the district.
  • Additional Representational Members: If matching representation criteria for SC, ST, OBC, or other specific communities is missing, supplementary officers (not below Inspector rank) can be introduced via the local Police Commissioner or SP/SSP.

Live Grievance Redressal and Absentees Rule

Immediate Appeal Right: Candidates who are unsatisfied with their physical test results can file an official appeal or objection on the same day, immediately following the completion of their measurement. A designated Additional Superintendent of Police (ASP) will serve as the Nodal Officer at each location. The evaluation will be re-conducted immediately in the presence of the ASP Nodal Officer. No further requests, complaints, or legal claims will be entertained once a candidate leaves the premises after failing the review round.

Rescheduling Absentee Candidates: If an applicant is unable to attend on the designated date due to unforeseen emergencies, they can submit a detailed written representation explaining the reasons to the district Nodal Officer before the final scheduled date or reserve day. If approved, they may be accommodated on a designated reserve date. No extensions or testing sessions will be arranged after the conclusion of the final reserve day, regardless of medical or alternative emergencies.

How to Prepare for the Process When Admit Cards Open

  1. Monitor the main link on the official portal at uppbpb.gov.in.
  2. Enter your Registration details and Date of Birth on the Homeguard Enrollment portal when prompted.
  3. Download the printed copy of the dynamic call letter and note the assigned verification center and date window.
  4. Arrange all original document certificates along with sets of self-attested photocopies corresponding to information provided during the initial online application.

Essential Documents to Carry for Verification

  • A clear printout of the UPPRPB Homeguard Call Letter.
  • Original educational qualification certificates alongside matching age proofs.
  • Valid category certificates validating vertical/horizontal claims (OBC, SC, ST, EWS, Ex-Servicemen, etc.).
  • Preferential documents if claimed in the application (such as NCC 'C' or 'B' certificates, Computer courses, etc.).
  • A valid government-approved Photo Identity card (Aadhaar Card, PAN Card, Driving License, Voter ID).

Frequently Asked Questions

When was the UP Homeguard DV/PST process notice released?

The official process notice was issued by the UPPRPB on July 03, 2026.

What is the minimum height required for general male candidates?

Male candidates belonging to General, OBC, and SC categories must have a minimum height of 168 cm.

What is the chest expansion criteria for male candidates?

General, OBC, and SC male candidates require a minimum unexpanded chest of 79 cm and an expanded chest of 84 cm. A minimum expansion of 5 cm is mandatory for all.

What are the physical standards for female candidates?

General, OBC, and SC female candidates require a minimum height of 152 cm. ST female candidates require 147 cm. A minimum weight of 40 kg is mandatory for all female candidates.

Can a candidate appeal against the physical measurement results?

Yes. If a candidate is dissatisfied with their physical measurement, they can file an appeal on the same day immediately after the test. A designated Additional Superintendent of Police (ASP) will act as the Nodal Officer to conduct a re-measurement.

What happens if someone misses the assigned DV/PST date?

Candidates can submit a detailed representation explaining the valid reasons to the district Nodal Officer before the final scheduled/reserve day. If approved, they may be allowed on an alternate date or reserve day.

Will measurements from previous recruitments be valid?

No. Measurements from previous recruitment processes or external institutions will not be considered valid. Only the measurements taken live by the official center committee during this round are valid.

What action is taken if falsified documents are detected?

The candidate's enrollment will be cancelled immediately, and legal criminal proceedings can be initiated against them by the board or appointing authority.
