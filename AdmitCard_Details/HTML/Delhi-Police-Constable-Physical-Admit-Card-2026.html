About This Admit Card Notice

Delhi Police, in coordination with the Staff Selection Commission (SSC), has officially released the Physical Endurance & Measurement Test (PE & MT) Admit Card 2026 for Constable (Executive) recruitment. Candidates who cleared the Computer-Based Test (CBT) stage are eligible to appear for this physical test round.

The physical examination is scheduled to take place from 19 August 2026 to 1 September 2026 across designated physical test grounds in Delhi. Shortlisted candidates must download their e-admit card using their Registration Number / Roll Number and Password or Date of Birth.

The PE & MT round is qualifying in nature but mandatory for selection to the next stages, which include document verification and medical examination. Candidates must thoroughly check their reporting time, assigned venue, and physical measurement standards prior to attending the test.

How to Download Delhi Police Constable Physical Admit Card

  1. Visit the official recruitment portal of Delhi Police at delhipolice.gov.in or SSC portal.
  2. Navigate to the Recruitment / Latest Updates section on the homepage.
  3. Click on the link titled "Admit Card for PE & MT for Constable Executive Examination".
  4. Enter your Registration Number or Roll Number along with Date of Birth or Password.
  5. Submit the credentials to view your physical test admit card.
  6. Verify your name, roll number, assigned ground/venue, test date, and reporting time.
  7. Download and print a clear copy of the hall ticket for exam day entry.

Details Mentioned on Admit Card

  • Candidate Name, Father Name, and Category.
  • Roll Number and Registration Number.
  • Physical Test Venue Address and Ground Number.
  • Date of Physical Test (PE & MT).
  • Reporting Time and Entry Gate details.
  • Candidate Photograph and Signature.
  • Important Exam Day Instructions and List of Documents required.

Physical Standards (PET & PMT) Requirements

Candidates must meet the prescribed physical endurance and measurement standards to qualify for the next stage:

Documents to Carry on PE & MT Day

  • Printed Delhi Police Constable PE & MT Admit Card.
  • Original Valid Photo Identity Card (Aadhaar Card, Voter ID, Driving License, Passport, etc.).
  • Recent Passport-size Colour Photographs (at least 4 copies).
  • Original Educational Qualification Certificates (10th, 10+2 Marksheets/Certificates).
  • Original Category Certificate (SC/ST/OBC/EWS) if applicable.
  • Valid LMV Driving License (Mandatory for Male candidates appearing for Constable Executive).
  • Photocopies of all original documents (self-attested).

Exam Day Instructions

  • Report strictly at the test venue as per the reporting time printed on your admit card.
  • Late arrival will result in disqualification from attending the physical test.
  • Ensure you bring a valid LMV Driving License (for Male candidates) as required by recruitment rules.
  • Wear appropriate sports footwear and comfortable athletic apparel for physical endurance tests.
  • Electronic gadgets, smartwatches, and unauthorized items are prohibited inside the testing premises.
  • Follow all guidelines issued by physical test marshals and recruitment officials at the venue.

Frequently Asked Questions

What is the Delhi Police Constable Physical Exam Date 2026?

The Physical Endurance & Measurement Test (PE & MT) for Delhi Police Constable Executive will be conducted from 19 August 2026 to 1 September 2026.

When was the Delhi Police Constable Physical Admit Card 2026 released?

The PE & MT hall ticket was officially released on 8 August 2026.

Where can I download the Delhi Police Physical Admit Card 2026?

Candidates can download the PE & MT admit card from the official recruitment portal at delhipolice.gov.in or regional SSC websites.

What login credentials are required to download the hall ticket?

Candidates need their Registration Number or Roll Number along with Password or Date of Birth.

What physical efficiency standards are required for Male candidates?

Male candidates under 30 years must complete a 1600-meter race in 6 minutes, achieve a Long Jump of 14 feet, and High Jump of 3 feet 9 inches.

What physical measurement standards are required for Height and Chest?

General male candidates require a minimum height of 170 cm and chest of 81 cm (with 4 cm expansion). Female candidates require a minimum height of 157 cm.

Is entry allowed without the physical test admit card?

No. Candidates must present a printed copy of the PE & MT e-admit card along with a original valid photo ID to enter the venue.

What documents should candidates bring for the Delhi Police physical test?

Candidates must carry the printed admit card, valid photo ID card, passport-size photographs, and original educational/category certificates along with photocopies.
