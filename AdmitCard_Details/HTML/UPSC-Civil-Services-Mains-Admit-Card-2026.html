About UPSC CSE Mains Admit Card 2026

The Union Public Service Commission (UPSC) has officially scheduled the Civil Services (Main) Examination 2026. Candidates who successfully cleared the Preliminary examination held on 24 May 2026 are required to download their e-admit cards for the Main exam. A total of 13,343 candidates have been shortlisted to compete for approximately 1016 prestigious administrative vacancies.

The mains exam is descriptive in nature and is conducted across five days over two weekends: 21, 22, 23, 29, and 30 August 2026. The e-admit cards are generally released 1-2 weeks prior to the examination commencement. Candidates must verify their name, roll number, photo, identity proof details, and their chosen optional subjects on the admit card once downloaded.

A physical copy of the admit card will not be sent by post. It is mandatory to carry a printed copy of the downloaded e-admit card along with the original photo ID card (as mentioned in the Detailed Application Form) to secure entry at the exam centre.

How to Download UPSC CSE Mains Admit Card

  1. Visit the official UPSC website at upsc.gov.in or directly go to upsconline.nic.in.
  2. Click on the "e-Admit Cards for Various Examinations of UPSC" link available on the homepage.
  3. Select the link for "Civil Services (Main) Examination, 2026".
  4. Read all the essential instructions carefully and print them if required, then proceed.
  5. Select the login option: "By Registration ID" or "By Roll Number".
  6. Enter the required credentials along with your Date of Birth and the security captcha code.
  7. Your UPSC Mains e-admit card will be displayed on the screen. Download it and take a clear printout.

Details Mentioned on Admit Card

  • Candidate's Full Name and Roll Number.
  • Registration ID (RID) number.
  • Father’s and Mother’s Name.
  • Exam Centre address and precise venue details.
  • Exam Date and detailed time-table (subject-wise shifts).
  • Chosen Indian Language and Optional Subject Papers.
  • Candidate's Photograph and Scanned Signature.
  • Specified Photo ID Card Number (e.g., Aadhar, Voter ID, PAN).
  • Important instructions for the candidate.

Documents to Carry on Exam Day

  • A clear printout of the downloaded e-admit card for each session.
  • The original valid photo identity card (whose number is printed on the e-admit card).
  • Two identical passport-size photographs (only if the photograph on the admit card is not clear or missing).
  • A simple black ballpoint pen is required for marking attendance and writing answers.

Exam Day Instructions

  • Candidates must reach the exam venue at least 30 minutes before the scheduled commencement of each session.
  • Entry to the examination venue closes 10 minutes prior to the commencement (i.e., 8:50 AM for the Forenoon session and 2:20 PM for the Afternoon session).
  • Electronic devices, including mobile phones, smartwatches, Bluetooth devices, and calculators, are strictly prohibited inside the premises.
  • Normal analog watches are allowed, but smartwatches or fitness bands will be confiscated.
  • Read the "Important Instructions" attached with the e-admit card PDF meticulously regarding the usage of authorized items and discipline during the exam.
