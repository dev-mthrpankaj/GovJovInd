About This Admit Card

UPPSC has published the exam schedule and admit card instructions for the Assistant Prosecution Officer Main Examination 2025. The notice confirms that the examination will be held in Lucknow on multiple dates and sessions.

The admit card is required for entry into the examination centre. It helps candidates confirm their centre, paper/session timing, personal details and mandatory instructions. Candidates should download it early and take a clean printout.

Candidates should rely only on the UPPSC official website and instructions printed on the admit card. The official notice also mentions strict action for unfair means and impersonation under the Uttar Pradesh Public Examination Act, 2024.

How to Download Admit Card

  1. Visit the official UPPSC website at uppsc.up.nic.in.
  2. Open the admit card link for Assistant Prosecution Officer Main Examination 2025.
  3. Enter your OTR number or required login details as shown on the official portal.
  4. Download the admit card and exam instructions.
  5. Check your exam centre, exam date, paper timing and reporting instructions carefully.
  6. Print the admit card and carry all documents mentioned in the official notice and admit card.

Details Mentioned on Admit Card

  • Candidate name and roll or registration details
  • OTR-related candidate details
  • Exam name and examination stage
  • Exam date and session timing
  • Exam centre name and address
  • Reporting time and entry closing instruction
  • Candidate photograph/signature if printed
  • Important exam instructions issued by UPPSC

Documents to Carry

  • Printed admit card
  • Two photographs as mentioned in the notice
  • Original photo ID proof
  • One photocopy of the same photo ID proof
  • Any document specifically printed on the admit card
  • Category or PwBD document if applicable and required by the official instructions

Exam Day Instructions

  • The examination will be held in Lucknow in the sessions mentioned in the official schedule.
  • Candidates will be allowed entry 1 hour 30 minutes before the scheduled exam start time.
  • Entry will close 45 minutes before the scheduled exam start time.
  • Carry two photos, original ID proof and one photocopy of the ID proof.
  • Follow the seating, centre and verification instructions given by UPPSC staff.
  • Do not use unfair means, impersonation or any prohibited activity in the examination.
  • Read all instructions printed on the admit card before visiting the centre.
