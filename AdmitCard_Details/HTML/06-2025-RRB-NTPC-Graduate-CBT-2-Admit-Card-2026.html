About This Admit Card Update

The Railway Recruitment Boards have issued the formal notice outlining the tentative exam window and essential guidelines for candidates qualifying for the 2nd Stage Computer Based Test (CBT-II) under CEN No. 06/2025. This examination is being conducted to fill up various graduate level vacancies under the Non-Technical Popular Categories (NTPC).

As per the official notice dated 12.06.2026, the CBT-II exam is scheduled to take place on 10 July 2026 (Friday). Candidates will be able to check their assigned exam cities and specific dates 10 days prior to the commencement of the exam. This facility will also serve as the portal for SC/ST candidates to download their designated travel authority passes.

The boards have introduced mandatory security measures for the examination day. All candidates must undergo Aadhaar-linked biometric authentication before entering the test venue. It is crucial that candidates keep their Aadhaar card in an unlocked status in the UIDAI registry to ensure seamless verification on the exam day.

How to Download RRB NTPC Graduate CBT 2 Admit Card

  1. Visit the official centralised application portal at www.rrbapply.gov.in or navigate to your specific regional RRB website.
  2. Look for the link designated for "CEN No. 06/2025 Exam City Intimation & E-Call Letter download".
  3. Provide your login credentials (such as registration number and password/date of birth) on the portal window.
  4. Review your allotted exam city, exam shift details, and verification requirements.
  5. Download the E-Call letter starting from 4 days before your specific exam date.
  6. Print a clear copy of the call letter and carefully read through all instructions provided by the board.

Details Mentioned on E-Call Letter

  • Candidate Name, Photograph, and Signature.
  • Registration Number and Roll Number.
  • Centralized Employment Notice Number (CEN No. 06/2025).
  • Exam Date, Assigned Shift, and Test Timing.
  • Reporting Time and Gate Closing Time.
  • Detailed Exam Centre Name and Address.
  • Important instructions regarding Aadhaar biometric authentication.

Documents to Carry on Exam Day

  • Printed copy of the official RRB E-Call Letter.
  • Original Aadhaar Card or a clear printout of e-verified Aadhaar (Mandatory for Biometric Authentication).
  • Valid photo identity proof (such as Voter ID, Passport, Driving License, or PAN card) for general matching.
  • Passport size photographs as instructed on the call letter.
  • Downloaded Travel Authority Pass (applicable for SC/ST candidates availing free travel).

Exam Day Instructions & Aadhaar Rules

  • Aadhaar Biometric Verification: Aadhaar-linked biometric authentication will be executed at the exam centre prior to entry into the examination hall. Candidates are advised to authenticate identity early by logging into the application portal if not completed during submission.
  • Aadhaar Lock Status: Candidates must verify that their Aadhaar profile is in an UNLOCKED condition in the UIDAI registry system before proceeding to the venue to prevent registration delays.
  • Original Documents: Photocopies or digital copies of Aadhaar on mobile devices will not suffice for biometric authentication; bring the original card.
  • Banned Items: Electronic gadgets, mobile devices, calculators, and unapproved materials are strictly forbidden inside the testing rooms.
  • Selection Transparency: Selections are completely merit-based, handled through computer-based tests. Beware of touts making fake appointment promises.

Duplicate Admit Card / Correction Instructions

The official notification does not list provisions for issuing duplicate hall tickets or rectification workflows at the exam venue. Candidates facing download errors or mismatched entries are advised to check the official website or contact their respective regional Railway Recruitment Board helpdesk for guidance.

Frequently Asked Questions

What is the exam date for RRB NTPC Graduate CBT 2 in 2026?

The tentative exam date for RRB NTPC Graduate CBT-II under CEN No. 06/2025 is 10 July 2026 (Friday).

When will the link for viewing RRB NTPC CBT 2 Exam City be live?

The link for viewing the Exam City & Date and downloading the Travel Authority for SC/ST candidates will be made live 10 days prior to the exam date.

When can candidates download the RRB NTPC Graduate CBT 2 e-Call Letter?

The downloading of E-Call letters will start 4 days prior to the exam date mentioned in the candidate's Exam City and Date intimation link.

What login details are required to download the admit card?
