About This Interview Letter Notice

The Rajasthan Staff Selection Board (RSSB), Jaipur has officially compiled the interview plan for the 84 vacant positions under the Platoon Commander Direct Recruitment 2025 advertisement. This notice applies specifically to all candidates who successfully cleared the rigorous physical efficiency test (PET) and physical standard verification checks conducted earlier.

According to the official board release (Ref No: 14 (115) RSSB/Arthna/Platoon Commander/2025/), the interview panel proceedings will officially commence on 08 July 2026 and continue dynamically across various blocks up to 17 July 2026. Shortlisted candidates are required to print out their documentation forms and present them on their designated slot date.

Candidates must take note that the board has explicitly moved away from traditional offline courier dispatches; all interview letters are exclusively distributed via an automated online portal. Entry without a validated online printout and completed forms will remain strictly restricted at the selection desk.

How to Download RSSB Platoon Commander Interview Letter

  1. Open your web browser and load the dedicated verification gateway link at rssbasonline.com.
  2. Locate and click on the dedicated tab labeled "Platoon Commander Direct Recruitment 2025 Interview Call Letter".
  3. In the form field, enter your Registered Mobile Number exactly as filled in the primary registration.
  4. Click on the submit or generate token button to request a secure One Time Password (OTP).
  5. Enter the OTP received on your mobile device to authorize and display your profile dashboard.
  6. Download the PDF version of your interview letter, and take a high-quality physical printout.
  7. Thoroughly study the specific time slot, reporting counter, and localized guidelines highlighted inside the document.

Documents Required at Interview Venue

Shortlisted candidates must present themselves at the selection desk along with a complete dossier of mandatory records. Ensure the following files are arranged in order:

  • Detailed Application Form (विस्तृत आवेदन पत्र): Two complete filled print copies.
  • Scrutiny / Attestation Form (परिनिरीक्षण फॉर्म): Pre-filled and finalized copy as prescribed.
  • Original Testimonials: All core marksheets, certificates, category records, and age proofs.
  • Self-Attested Photocopies: Exactly one full set of self-signed duplicates matching all original files.
  • Valid Identity Card: Government-issued id proof (Aadhaar Card, PAN card, or Voter ID).

Important Interview Venue Instructions

  • Strict Timelines: Candidates must reach the specified Board Office address precisely as highlighted in their individual slot details.
  • No Offline Letters: No physical letters are being sent by post. Candidates are entirely responsible for bringing their online generated letter.
  • Verification Check: Final eligibility is subject to a review of original certificates. Any discrepancies found in the detailed application form can lead to immediate rejection.
  • Periodic Updates: Candidates are strongly advised to continuously track official updates on the RSSB website to notice any sudden updates in reporting formats or dynamic alerts.
