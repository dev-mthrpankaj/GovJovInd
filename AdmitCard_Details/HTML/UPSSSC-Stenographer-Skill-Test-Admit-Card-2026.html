About UPSSSC Stenographer Skill Test Admit Card 2026

The Uttar Pradesh Subordinate Service Selection Commission (UPSSSC) has successfully declared the results of the written exam and is now proceeding with the next phase of recruitment: the Skill Test. The admit card for this crucial stage is a mandatory document that provides your entry clearance to the test venue.

Candidates appearing for the UPSSSC Stenographer Advt No. 09-Exam/2023 Skill Test must log in to the official UPSSSC portal to download their hall ticket. It contains highly specific details about the venue location, date, reporting shift, and the prescribed format for the shorthand and typing assessment.

It is strongly advised to download the admit card well in advance and not wait until the last minute. Thoroughly read and understand the official guidelines to ensure you have the required documents and are fully aware of the skill test rules before arriving at the center.

How to Download UPSSSC Stenographer Skill Test Admit Card

  1. Visit the official UPSSSC website at upsssc.gov.in.
  2. Look for the "Examination / Interview" segment or check the "Notice Board" for the Stenographer Skill Test Admit Card link.
  3. Click on the link labeled "Download Admit Card for the Skill Test under Advt. No. 09-Exam/2023".
  4. Enter your Registration Number, Date of Birth, Gender, and the on-screen Verification Code.
  5. Click the "Download Admit Card" button.
  6. Verify your exam center, shift, reporting time, and instructions.
  7. Download the PDF and take a clear, legible printout to carry on the day of the test.

Details Mentioned on Skill Test Admit Card

  • Candidate's Full Name
  • Registration Number and Roll Number
  • Exam Name (Stenographer Skill Test - 09/2023)
  • Candidate's Photograph and Signature
  • Skill Test Date and Shift Timing
  • Reporting Time and Gate Closing Time
  • Full Exam Center Address / Venue
  • Specific instructions for shorthand and typing assessments

Documents to Carry to the Exam Center

Candidates must carry the following essential documents to gain entry to the skill test venue. Failure to present these may lead to disqualification.

  • A clear, printed copy of the UPSSSC Stenographer Skill Test Admit Card.
  • Valid original Photo ID proof (Aadhaar Card, PAN Card, Voter ID, Passport, or Driving License).
  • 2 recent passport-size photographs (matching the one uploaded in the application form).
  • Any specific self-declaration or category certificate, if explicitly demanded by the official instructions.

Exam Day Instructions

  • Arrive at the examination venue strictly before the reporting time to complete biometric and identity verification.
  • Entry will not be permitted after the designated gate closing time.
  • Do not carry prohibited items such as mobile phones, Bluetooth devices, smartwatches, or unauthorized study material.
  • Pay close attention to the invigilator's instructions regarding the keyboard layout (e.g., Mangal/Kruti Dev) and shorthand dictation rules.
  • Maintain discipline and silence throughout the skill test process.

Frequently Asked Questions

Is UPSSSC Stenographer Skill Test Admit Card 2026 released?

Yes, the UPSSSC Stenographer Skill Test Admit Card for Advt No. 09-Exam/2023 is available for download on the official website.

What is the UPSSSC Stenographer Skill Test date?

The exact skill test date is mentioned on the candidate's official admit card and the latest exam date notice.

Where can I download the UPSSSC Stenographer admit card?

Candidates can download their admit card directly from the official UPSSSC portal at upsssc.gov.in using their login credentials.

What login details are required for the UPSSSC admit card?

You will need your Registration Number, Date of Birth, Gender, and the verification code displayed on the screen to log in.

What documents should I carry to the UPSSSC skill test?

You must carry a clear printout of your admit card, a valid original photo ID proof, and passport-size photographs as per the admit card instructions.

What is the reporting time for the UPSSSC skill test?

Reporting time varies by shift. Candidates must strictly follow the reporting time printed on their individual admit cards.

Is city intimation the same as the admit card?

No, city intimation only informs you of the exam district, while the admit card provides the exact test center address, shift, and entry instructions.

What should I do if my UPSSSC admit card is not downloading?

Ensure you are entering the correct registration number and DOB. If the issue persists, clear your browser cache, try during off-peak hours, or contact the UPSSSC helpdesk.
