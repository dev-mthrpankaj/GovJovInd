About This Admit Card

Uttar Pradesh Education Service Selection Commission has released an important notice for the Uttar Pradesh Teacher Eligibility Test 2026. The notice confirms the exam schedule for Primary and Upper Primary levels and also informs candidates about the advance exam city information and admit card download date.

The UPTET admit card is an important document because it helps candidates verify their exam date, shift, centre address and instructions before the examination. Candidates should download the admit card as soon as it is available and check every detail carefully.

Candidates should not rely only on social media messages or unofficial screenshots. The final details such as exam centre, reporting time, allowed documents and exam-day rules must be checked from the official admit card and the official UPESSC website.

How to Download UPTET Admit Card 2026

  1. Visit the official website of Uttar Pradesh Education Service Selection Commission at upessc.up.gov.in.
  2. Open the UPTET 2026 admit card or प्रवेश पत्र link available on the homepage.
  3. Enter the required registration or login details as asked on the official portal.
  4. Verify your exam date, paper level, shift and exam centre details carefully.
  5. Download the admit card and take a clear printout for exam day use.
  6. Read all instructions printed on the admit card before visiting the examination centre.

Details Mentioned on Admit Card

  • Candidate name and basic identity details.
  • Roll number, registration number or application number.
  • UPTET paper level: Primary or Upper Primary.
  • Exam date and shift timing.
  • Reporting time and gate closing instructions, if printed.
  • Exam centre name, district and complete address.
  • Candidate photograph and signature, if printed.
  • Important instructions issued by the commission for the exam day.

Documents to Carry

  • Printed copy of UPTET Admit Card 2026.
  • Valid original photo identity proof such as Aadhaar Card, Voter ID, PAN Card, Driving Licence or other accepted ID.
  • Passport size photograph, if required on the admit card.
  • Application acknowledgement or registration details, if required for verification.
  • Category, PwBD or other supporting document, if mentioned in the official admit card instructions.
  • Any other document specifically mentioned on the official admit card.

Exam Day Instructions

  • Reach the examination centre as per the reporting time printed on the admit card.
  • Do not wait until the last minute to check the centre location and travel route.
  • Carry the printed admit card and a valid photo ID for verification.
  • Follow the entry rules, document verification process and centre instructions strictly.
  • Do not carry prohibited items if they are restricted by the official admit card instructions.
  • Keep the admit card safely even after the exam for future reference.

Duplicate Admit Card / Correction Instructions

The provided official notice does not mention a separate duplicate admit card schedule or correction process. Candidates who face any issue in downloading the admit card should check the official website and follow the instructions issued by Uttar Pradesh Education Service Selection Commission.

  • Duplicate admit card schedule: Check official website.

Frequently Asked Questions

When will UPTET Admit Card 2026 be released?

As per the official notice, UPTET 2026 admit card will be available for download from 30 June 2026.

What is the UPTET 2026 exam date?

The examination will be held on 02 July, 03 July and 04 July 2026 in different shifts.

When can candidates check UPTET exam city?

The advance exam city information was scheduled to be available from 22 June 2026 on the official UPESSC website.

Where can I download the UPTET admit card?

Candidates should download the admit card from the official website: upessc.up.gov.in.

What login details are required?

The exact login details should be checked on the official website. Keep your application or registration details ready.

What is the UPTET exam timing?

The first shift is from 09:30 AM to 12:00 PM and the second shift is from 02:30 PM to 05:00 PM.

What is the reporting time for UPTET 2026?

Reporting time is not mentioned in the provided notice. Candidates must check the reporting time printed on the official admit card.

Can I appear in the exam without admit card?

No. The official notice states that candidates will not be permitted to appear in the examination without the admit card.

Is there any duplicate admit card schedule?

The provided notice does not mention a duplicate admit card schedule. Candidates should check the official website for any helpdesk or duplicate admit card instructions.

Which documents are required on exam day?

Carry the printed admit card, valid photo ID and any other documents mentioned in the official admit card instructions.
