About BPSC 72nd Prelims Exam Date & City Intimation

The Bihar Public Service Commission (BPSC) has officially published the structured examination program notice for the upcoming Integrated 72nd Combined Preliminary Competitive Examination. This massive screening test will open up entry pathways for premium executive administrative posts across various state government channels, incorporating a total pool of 1,186 vacancies following official modifications in the structural department allocations.

As per the signed notification issued by the Controller of Examinations, BPSC, Patna, the competitive prelims test is configured to execute completely on 26 July 2026, Sunday. The event is mapped to finish inside a single uniform timeline window spanning exactly two hours. The primary allocation matrices highlighting the district-level centers are being systematically distributed to enable smooth logistical routing for registered candidates traveling across Bihar.

Applicants must register their presence on the designated commission interfaces to accurately intercept their designated exam city district slip. The dynamic e-admit card parameters highlighting unique room schedules and complex center building information will launch online in subsequent deployment blocks. A mandatory printing of the final e-admit card must be held ready prior to visiting the examination hall.

How to Check BPSC 72nd Exam City and Allotment Notice

  1. Direct your web browser to the formal homepage of the commission at bpsc.bih.nic.in or the online portal at onlinebpsc.bihar.gov.in.
  2. Locate the notification headline tracking the "Examination Program: Integrated 72nd Combined (Preliminary) Competitive Examination".
  3. Click the online application login prompt and submit your verified individual credentials comprising Username/Registration Number and account password.
  4. Locate the dynamic dashboard element designated for "Exam District / City Intimation Information Slip".
  5. Carefully scrutinize the assigned exam city name, general district, and shift time rules printed on the screen.
  6. Retain an electronic snapshot or download copy of the configuration page to smoothly guide your transport and logistical arrangements.

Details Specified on the Exam City / District Slip

  • Full name of the registered candidate.
  • Registration sequence number and individual application identification code.
  • Assigned examination phase title (72nd CCE Preliminary).
  • Official test date (26 July 2026).
  • Allocated examination shift timing window (12:00 PM to 02:00 PM).
  • Allotted exam city region and assigned home district framework in Bihar.
  • Essential preliminary behavior conditions, identification check guidelines, and electronic item exclusions.

Documents to Bring on Exam Day

  • Clear physical printout of the final BPSC 72nd Prelims e-Admit Card (Downloaded from the BPSC database).
  • Valid, non-expired photo identification card issued by official authorities (e.g., Aadhar Card, Voter ID, Driving License, Passport, or PAN Card).
  • Recent color passport-sized photographs matching the uploaded application form metrics, if explicitly mandated in the final text.
  • Standard blue or black ballpoint pens for marking response items on the physical OMR paper matrix.

General Exam Day Guidelines

  • Reach your assigned venue coordinates safely in advance of the deadline, keeping structural check sequences in consideration.
  • Strictly note that entry pathways will immediately close after the final internal boundary deadline marked in the official schedule text.
  • Prohibited articles including electronic computational aids, smartphones, Bluetooth communication devices, luxury watches, smartbands, and textual cheat sheets are banned.
  • Always store a secure copy of your e-admit card safely beyond the exam date for verification stages during subsequent mains evaluations.

    Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)

    What is the official BPSC 72nd Prelims exam date 2026?

    The Integrated 72nd Combined (Preliminary) Competitive Examination is officially scheduled to be held on 26 July 2026, Sunday.

    What are the shift timings for the BPSC 72nd Pre Exam?

    The examination will be conducted in a single sitting from 12:00 Noon to 2:00 PM.

    When can candidates check their BPSC 72nd Exam City details?

    The exam city intimation details and notice have been made available in July 2026, prior to the release of the hall tickets.

    Where can I download the official BPSC 72nd Examination Program Notice?

    Candidates can check and download the official notice from the official website of the Bihar Public Service Commission at bpsc.bih.nic.in or bpsc.bihar.gov.in.

    What credentials are required to check the BPSC exam city and download the admit card?

    Candidates will require their official online application login credentials, including Username/Registration Number and Password.

    How many total vacancies are notified under the BPSC 72nd CCE Recruitment?

    There are a total of 1,186 vacancies available under the 72nd CCE recruitment drive after the withdrawal of 44 Sugarcane Officer posts.

    Is city intimation slip the same as the final admit card?

    No, the city intimation slip or district information notice only tells you the assigned city or district for travel arrangements. The final e-admit card containing your exact examination centre address will be released separately close to the exam date.

    What should I do if there is a mistake on my BPSC information sheet?

    Candidates should instantly establish contact with the BPSC administration helpdesks or dispatch an emergency verification request to the commission's headquarters located in Patna for adjustments before the test window.
