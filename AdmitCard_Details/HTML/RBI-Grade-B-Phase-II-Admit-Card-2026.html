About This Admit Card Update

The Reserve Bank of India (RBI) has issued the online call letters for the Phase-II main examination for the recruitment of Officer Grade B vacancies under Advertisement No. RBISB/DA/01/2026-27. Candidates who successfully cleared the Phase-I preliminary examination are eligible to download this phase 2 admission letter from the online portal.

The examination will be held over two consecutive days. The written test for Officers in Grade B (DR) General will be conducted on 25 July 2026. The examination for specialized streams, namely DEPR and DSIM cadres, will follow on 26 July 2026. This online examination will be managed in two mandatory shifts at designated exam centres across India.

Candidates must log in to the RBI Opportunities portal using their credentials to print out the document. No hard copy of the phase 2 admit card will be dispatched by post or courier services to individual applicants.

How to Download RBI Grade B Phase 2 Admit Card

  1. Visit the official RBI web portal at rbi.org.in.
  2. Scroll down to the footer section and click on the "Opportunities@RBI" link.
  3. Navigate to the "Current Vacancies" tab at the top menu and select "Call Letters".
  4. Click on the specific link titled "Admission Letters, Information Handouts and Mock Test Links for Phase-II Online Examination for Officer posts – Panel Year 2026".
  5. Enter your unique Registration Number or Roll Number along with your Password or Date of Birth.
  6. Solve the captcha validation string and hit the submit button.
  7. Verify your exam date, timing, and allotted centre before downloading the clear copy.

Details Mentioned on Phase II Call Letter

  • Full name of the candidate and roll number.
  • Registration number and category details.
  • Allotted stream (General / DEPR / DSIM).
  • Phase 2 exam date, allocated shifts, and batch timings.
  • Reporting time and gate closing duration.
  • Detailed venue name and full address of the exam centre.
  • Scanned photograph and signature specimen.
  • Important instructions regarding objective and descriptive test modes.

Documents to Carry on Exam Day

  • Clear printed copy of the online RBI Grade B Phase 2 Call Letter.
  • One original valid government-issued photo ID proof (Aadhaar Card, PAN Card, Passport, Voter ID, or Driving License).
  • A clear photocopy of the same photo identity proof to submit at the examination desk.
  • Two recent passport-size colored photographs.
  • PwBD category certificate or scribe documents, if applicable.

Exam Day Instructions

  • Report to the assigned testing center 30 to 60 minutes prior to the reporting time mentioned on the call letter.
  • Entry will be completely blocked once the gate closing window is reached.
  • Candidates must appear in both shifts; missing any shift will lead to disqualification.
  • Electronic items, smartwatches, calculators, bags, and study materials are strictly barred inside the hall.
  • Read the official information handout released along with the admit card to understand the descriptive typing pattern.

Frequently Asked Questions

Is the RBI Grade B Phase 2 admit card released?

Yes, the Reserve Bank of India officially released the Phase II online exam call letters on 15 July 2026.

What is the exam date for RBI Grade B Phase 2 2026?

The Phase 2 exam for the General cadre will be held on 25 July 2026, and for DEPR/DSIM cadres, it will be held on 26 July 2026.

Where can I download the RBI Officer Grade B Phase 2 call letter?

Candidates can download their admission letter online from the official RBI opportunities website at opportunities.rbi.org.in.

What login details are required to download the RBI Grade B admit card?

You need your Registration Number or Roll Number along with your Password or Date of Birth.

What is the exam mode for RBI Grade B Phase II?

The Phase II exam will be conducted online in Computer Based Test (CBT) mode in two shifts containing objective and descriptive questions.

What documents are required at the RBI Grade B Phase 2 exam hall?

Candidates must carry a clear printed copy of the phase 2 call letter, one original government photo ID proof, its clear photocopy, and recent passport photos.

What should I do if my admit card details are incorrect?

You should immediately contact the RBI recruitment board via email at hmrd@rbi.org.in or through their helpline numbers mentioned in the handouts.

Can I choose to skip one shift in the Phase II exam?

No, candidates are required to appear in both shifts. Absence in any shift will lead to rejection of your candidature.
