How to Download Admit Card

  1. Visit the official recruitment portal at recruitment.rajasthan.gov.in.
  2. Click on the Get Admit Card option.
  3. Select the recruitment admit card option for Vanpal Direct Recruitment Examination 2026.
  4. Enter your application number and date of birth as required by the portal.
  5. Download the provisional e-admit card and verify the exam date, centre and personal details.
  6. Take a clear printout and read all exam-day instructions before visiting the centre.

Details Mentioned on Admit Card

  • Candidate name and roll number or exam roll details
  • Application number or registration details
  • Exam name and post name
  • Exam date and exam time
  • Reporting and entry instructions
  • Exam centre name and address
  • Candidate photograph and signature details
  • Important instructions printed by RSSB

Documents to Carry

  • Printed provisional e-admit card
  • Original photo identity card, preferably Aadhaar card with date of birth printed on it
  • One recent colour passport-size photograph of 2.5cm x 2.5cm size
  • Blue transparent ball pen as mentioned in the notice
  • PwBD or scribe-related documents if applicable and officially required
  • Any other document mentioned on the official admit card

Exam Day Instructions

  • Check allotted district information from 22 June 2026 and download the admit card from 24 June 2026.
  • Reach the exam centre at least 2 hours before the scheduled exam time for checking and frisking.
  • Entry will be allowed only up to 1 hour before the examination starts.
  • Do not carry mobile phone, Bluetooth device, calculator, smartwatch or other prohibited electronic items.
  • Wear dress as per the official dress code instructions mentioned by RSSB.
  • Use the answer sheet carefully and fill required circles with appropriate pressure as instructed.
  • Do not believe misleading social media messages; follow only the official RSSB website and official notices.
