About RSSB Women Supervisor Exam City Slip

The Rajasthan Staff Selection Board (RSSB / RSMSSB) has successfully opened the tracking link for checking exam city allotments for the post of Women Supervisor. This preliminary update allows registered candidates to look up their assigned exam district and schedule early travel arrangements before the actual examination begins.

According to the official scheduling tracker, the written test for the 72 direct vacancies (comprising 57 Non-TSP and 15 TSP posts) will take place across the state on 24 July 2026. Note that this city intimation slip should not be treated as the regular hall ticket. It serves exclusively as an informative advance update regarding the test location district.

The standard downloadable e-admit card document, containing the exact institutional test venue details, classroom floor allocations, and final security instructions, will be made live on the server on 20 July 2026. Candidates are requested to keep their structural application prints ready to query their dashboards.

How to Check Exam City Location & Download Admit Card

  1. Go to the direct portal link at recruitment.rajasthan.gov.in or the main board page.
  2. Locate and click on the specific option labeled "Click Here to Know Your District Location (Women Supervisor Exam 2026)".
  3. Provide your unique Application Number and input your exact Date of Birth using the calendar layout.
  4. Enter the given security captcha characters accurately and submit the data query form.
  5. Your allocated exam city / district slip will open up on the screen. Verify the details properly.
  6. Re-login on the portal after 20 July 2026 to print out your comprehensive formal e-Admit Card.

Details Displayed on the City Slip / Admit Card

  • Full name of the applicant along with registration number.
  • Applied category classification and gender status.
  • Name of the recruitment board and advertisement identifier.
  • Allocated Exam City / District Name (Available on the City Slip).
  • Complete institutional address of the center venue (Only on the final Admit Card).
  • Scheduled date of the written test and structural timing window.
  • Specific guidelines and reporting protocols issued by RSSB.

Mandatory Documents to Carry on Exam Day

  • Physical clear paper print of the formal RSSB e-Admit Card document.
  • Valid original government-issued photo identity identification (Aadhaar Card, Voter ID, PAN, or Passport).
  • Recent color passport-sized photographs matching portal specifications.
  • A standard blue or black ballpoint pen, if instructed in the final admit card guidelines.

General Exam Day Rules & Protocols

  • Candidates must travel early to reach their allocated examination center district ahead of time.
  • Adhere strictly to reporting and gates-closure timings mentioned on the hall ticket. No entries will be permitted after the deadline.
  • Do not bring any smart devices, digital watches, textbooks, calculators, Bluetooth apparatus, or ornaments into the test venue premises.
  • The testing guidelines require strict adherence to standard identification verification checks before entry.

    Frequently Asked Questions

    Is the RSSB Women Supervisor exam city information released?

    Yes, the Rajasthan Staff Selection Board released the exam city location details on 17 July 2026.

    What is the exam date for RSSB Women Supervisor 2026?

    The written examination for the RSSB Women Supervisor post is scheduled to be held on 24 July 2026.

    When can I download the actual RSSB Women Supervisor admit card?

    The official admit card download portal will be activated on 20 July 2026.

    Where can I check my allocated exam city?

    Candidates can check their exam city information slip on the official Rajasthan recruitment portal: recruitment.rajasthan.gov.in.

    What login details are required to check the exam city slip?

    Candidates need their application number, date of birth, and select the exam type on the location portal.

    Does the city intimation slip show the complete test centre address?

    No, the city intimation slip only contains the allocated exam city/district and date. The full name and address of the examination center will be printed on the final admit card.

    What should I do if my city slip is not loading?

    Verify your application credentials or try checking via the official Rajasthan Single Sign-On (SSO) portal if the direct server faces high network traffic.
