About RRB Railway ALP CBT 2 Update

The Railway Recruitment Board (RRB) has activated the exam city intimation slip download link for candidates who qualified in the CBT 1 examination for Assistant Loco Pilot (CEN 01/2025). The second stage Computer Based Test (CBT 2) is scheduled to be conducted on 28 July 2026 across various examination centers nationwide.

Candidates can log in using their Registration Number and Date of Birth to check their allocated exam city, test shift, and reporting time. Eligible SC/ST candidates can also download their Travel Pass along with the city slip. The official E-Admit Card will be available for download 4 days prior to the examination date.

How to Download RRB ALP CBT 2 Admit Card / Check City Slip

  1. Visit the official RRB digialm login portal or your regional RRB official website.
  2. Click on the link titled "CBT-2 Exam City & Intimation Slip / Admit Card for CEN 01/2025 (ALP)".
  3. Enter your Registration Number and Date of Birth / Password in the login portal.
  4. Enter the Security Captcha code displayed on the screen and click on Submit.
  5. Your CBT 2 Exam City, Date, Shift Time, and Travel Pass (if applicable) will be displayed on the screen.
  6. Download and print a clear copy of the City Slip / E-Admit Card for exam day requirements.

Details Mentioned on CBT 2 City Slip & Admit Card

  • Candidate Name and Roll Number
  • Registration Number and Father's Name
  • Exam Date, Shift, and Reporting Time
  • Exam City Name and District
  • Exact Exam Center Address (on E-Admit Card)
  • Candidate Photo and Digital Signature
  • Important Exam Day Instructions & Covid Guidelines

Documents to Carry to the Examination Center

  • Printed copy of RRB ALP CBT 2 E-Admit Card.
  • Original Valid Photo ID Proof (Aadhaar Card, Voter ID, Driving License, PAN Card, or Passport).
  • Color Passport Size Photographs (matching application photo).
  • Free Travel Authority Pass (for eligible SC/ST candidates downloading travel pass).

Exam Day Instructions

  • Reach the examination center strictly within the reporting window mentioned on the hall ticket.
  • Aadhaar-linked biometric authentication will be conducted at the exam venue prior to entry.
  • Electronic gadgets, smartwatches, calculators, and bluetooth devices are strictly prohibited.
  • Follow all rules communicated by invigilators inside the examination hall.
