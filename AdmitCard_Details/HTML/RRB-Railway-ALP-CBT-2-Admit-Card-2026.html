About RRB ALP CBT 2 Admit Card 2026

The Railway Recruitment Boards (RRBs) have issued the Stage 2 e-Call Letter for candidates who successfully qualified the CBT 1 examination under Advertisement No. CEN 01/2025. This recruitment drive aims to fill 9,970 Assistant Loco Pilot (ALP) positions across various railway zones nationwide.

Candidates can access their CBT 2 exam city intimation details and download their official admit cards using their Registration Number and Password or Date of Birth on the official RRB login portal. The Stage 2 Computer-Based Test evaluates candidates' technical and general proficiency required for the ALP post.

It is mandatory to bring a clear printed copy of the CBT 2 Admit Card along with a valid original photo identity document to the allocated exam venue. Entry will strictly be prohibited after the gate closing time.

How to Download RRB ALP CBT 2 Admit Card

  1. Visit the official portal at rrbapply.gov.in or your respective regional RRB portal.
  2. Click on the link for "Download CBT-2 e-Call Letter for CEN 01/2025 (ALP)".
  3. Log in using your Registration Number and Password or Date of Birth.
  4. Your CBT 2 Exam City, Shift Timing, and e-Admit Card link will be displayed on screen.
  5. Click to download the PDF admit card file and save it to your device.
  6. Print a clear colored copy on an A4 sheet for verification on the examination day.

Details Mentioned on CBT 2 Call Letter

  • Candidate's Full Name, Roll Number, and Registration Number.
  • Applied Railway Zone and Post Title (Assistant Loco Pilot).
  • CBT 2 Examination Date (28 July 2026) and Shift Details.
  • Reporting Time and Gate Closing Time.
  • Complete Address and Google Location Code of the Exam Centre.
  • Candidate Photograph and Specimen Signature.
  • Official Exam Day Guidelines and Prohibited Items Notice.

Documents to Carry to the Exam Centre

  • Printed copy of RRB ALP CBT 2 e-Call Letter / Admit Card.
  • Original and valid Photo Identity Card (Aadhaar Card, Voter ID, PAN Card, Passport, or Driving License).
  • One recent passport-size photograph matching the application form.
  • Self-declaration and biometric compliance materials as instructed.

Exam Day Instructions

  • Report to the designated examination centre well before the reporting time.
  • Gate closing times are strictly enforced; latecomers will not be granted entry under any circumstances.
  • Electronic devices including smartwatches, mobile phones, calculators, Bluetooth devices, and printed study material are banned inside the hall.
  • Ensure biometric verification (Aadhaar/fingerprint) is completed smoothly at the entry desk.
