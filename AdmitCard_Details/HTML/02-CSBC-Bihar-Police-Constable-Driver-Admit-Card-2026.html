About This Admit Card Notice

CSBC Bihar has released an official notice regarding the written examination date and e-admit card instructions for Advertisement No. 02/2026. The recruitment is for 993 Constable (Driver) posts in Bihar Police Telecommunication (Wireless) and Technical Non-Gazetted Cadre. The earlier exam date has been changed because NEET (UG) 2026 is also scheduled on 21 June 2026.

According to the notice, the written examination will now be conducted on 28 June 2026, Sunday, in one shift. Candidates should check their exam date and exam centre district from 15 June 2026 on the official website by using registration number or registered mobile number with date of birth.

The e-admit card is mandatory for entry into the examination centre. Candidates who cannot download the e-admit card from the website have been given a separate duplicate e-admit card facility at the CSBC office in Patna.

How to Download CSBC Constable Driver Admit Card

  1. Visit the official CSBC Bihar website at csbc.bihar.gov.in.
  2. Open the e-admit card link for Advertisement No. 02/2026 Constable (Driver) written examination.
  3. Enter your Registration Number or registered mobile number along with Date of Birth, as required on the portal.
  4. Check your exam date, exam centre district and other details carefully.
  5. Download the e-admit card from 22 June 2026 and print a clear copy.
  6. Read all instructions printed on the admit card before going to the exam centre.

Details Mentioned on Admit Card

  • Candidate name and registration details.
  • Advertisement number and post name.
  • Exam date and shift details.
  • Exam centre district and centre address.
  • Reporting time and entry instructions.
  • Photograph, signature or identity-related details, if printed.
  • Important exam-day instructions issued by CSBC Bihar.

Duplicate e-Admit Card Facility

Candidates who are unable to download the e-admit card from the website can obtain a duplicate e-admit card from the CSBC office as per the official schedule.

  • Printed e-admit card issued by CSBC Bihar.
  • Valid photo identity card.

Exam Day Instructions

  • Reach the exam centre within the reporting window: 10:00 AM to 11:00 AM.
  • Entry without e-admit card will not be allowed.
  • Check the exam centre district and address before travelling.
  • Follow all instructions printed on the e-admit card and displayed at the centre.
  • Keep the admit card safely even after the exam for future stages.
