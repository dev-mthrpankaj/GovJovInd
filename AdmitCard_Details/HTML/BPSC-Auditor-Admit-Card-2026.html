About This Admit Card

The Bihar Public Service Commission (BPSC) has officially announced the exam execution template and call letter accessibility window for the post of Auditor under Advt No. 09/2026. The Preliminary examination is planned to test candidate competencies through an objective, multiple-choice format evaluation setup across assigned test centers in the state of Bihar.

As per the authorized directive, the initial allocation document (e-Admit Card) shows the basic district configuration from 28 June 2026. To check precise institute configurations and specific hall breakdown, candidates must access their personal dashboard updates which release dynamically within the scheduled window before the test date.

Carrying the printed hall pass is mandatory for authentication purposes at the venue perimeter. Candidates are advised to download the electronic copy well in advance to prevent any network congestion issues on the primary BPSC web servers.

How to Download BPSC Auditor Admit Card

  1. Go to the online portal login page of the commission at bpsconline.bihar.gov.in.
  2. Authenticate your profile access by feeding your registered Username and Password into the login block.
  3. Navigate straight to the 'My Account' segment inside your individual portal dashboard page.
  4. Find the relevant Auditor recruitment notice link and click the specific 'View Button' provided next to it.
  5. Locate the Admit Card download header option and select the 'View/Download' action link.
  6. Ensure that your Roll Number and the security Bar-Code render completely clear and sharp before saving the file.
  7. Generate a clean printout on an A4 sheet and secure an extra copy for verification formalities during the test.

Details Mentioned on Admit Card

  • Full Name of the Candidate
  • Registration Identification Sequence
  • Allotted Roll Number (अनुक्रमांक)
  • High-Resolution Security Bar-Code
  • Assigned Test District Name
  • Detailed Venue Address (Accessible from July 2)
  • Designated Exam Timing and Reporting Slots
  • Candidate Photo and Digital Signature Specimen
  • General Conduct Instructions for Exam Day

Documents to Carry on Exam Day

  • Primary Printout: Original e-Admit Card copy featuring clean graphics and clear barcodes.
  • Additional Copy: One extra physical copy of the admit card to be signed in front of the invigilator.
  • Photo ID: A valid government-issued photo identification proof (Aadhaar, PAN, Voter ID, Passport, etc.).
  • Photographs: Recent passport-size photographs if required or matching instructions on the hall ticket.
  • Stationery: Black/Blue ballpoint pens as permissible for marking the objective type OMR sheets.

Exam Day Instructions

  • Early Arrival: Reach the designated test destination venue 2 hours early (by 08:00 AM) to clear verification loops smoothly.
  • Gate Closure: Access entry channels will close exactly 1 hour prior to the assessment kick-off (09:00 AM strict cutoff). No entry allowed post deadline.
  • Document Handover: Sign the mandatory additional admit card copy inside the exam room and hand it over directly to the invigilator.
  • Prohibited Items: Do not bring smartphones, digital bands, communication chips, logbooks, calculators, or text notes into the exam hall.
  • Bar-Code Integrity: Do not fold, scribble, or smudge the region containing the printed barcode on your document sheet.

Duplicate Admit Card / Correction Instructions

No offline physical admit cards or postal dispatches will be provided by the Bihar Public Service Commission. For correction procedures or access problems concerning invisible barcodes or text fields, please consult the official portal helpdesk or write to the commission promptly before the exam date. For any other complex technical queries, check the official website.
