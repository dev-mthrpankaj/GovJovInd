About This Admit Card Notice

The High Court of Judicature at Patna has successfully published the e-admit card notifications for the Computer Operator cum Typist position under Advertisement Number PHC/02/2026. This selection procedure aims to fill 48 vacant regular positions in the Group C establishment of the High Court. Registered applicants can view their designated examination venue, shift structures, and timing by authenticating through the web portal.

According to the official timeline released, the written examination will take place on 22 July 2026. The online link to access individual admit cards was activated on 15 July 2026. Applicants are advised to keep their application details ready to extract their respective documents securely.

Carrying a physical copy of the downloaded call letter is an absolute mandate for entering the test venue. Entry gates will be sealed strictly according to the designated time windows mentioned inside individual hall tickets.

How to Download Patna HC Computer Operator Admit Card

  1. Go to the official website of the authority at patnahighcourt.gov.in or visit the direct login portal.
  2. Locate and select the Recruitment tab visible on the navigation panel of the homepage.
  3. Click open the link designated for "Admit Card Download for Computer Operator cum Typist Exam 2026".
  4. Provide your generated application credentials, including User ID / Registration Number and Password.
  5. Submit the verified details to visualize your call letter dashboard on the display.
  6. Review all printable aspects carefully, secure a local copy, and print a legible copy for the exam day.

Eligibility & Selection Criteria Overview

Candidates appearing for the test must meet the benchmark criteria specified during the vacancy rollout. The initial screening relies strictly on the structured written and practical criteria detailed below:

  • Graduation Degree in any discipline from a recognized educational institution.
  • Minimum 6 Months long Diploma or Certified Course in Computer Applications.
  • Typing speed proficiency minimum of 40 Words Per Minute in English language.
  • Typing speed proficiency minimum of 30 Words Per Minute in Hindi language.
  • Selection Scheme involves a Written Test, Typing Proficiency Assessment, and an Interview.

    Documents to Carry on Exam Day

    • A clearly visible printed copy of the Patna High Court Call Letter.
    • Valid Government-issued Photo Identity Document (e.g., Aadhar Card, PAN Card, Voter ID, driving license).
    • Recent passport-sized colored photographs, if specifically required by the instructions.
    • Photocopy of identity documentation matching the credentials provided during application.

    Exam Day Instructions

    • Arrive at the designated examination center well ahead of the reporting window.
    • Strictly note that no candidates will be granted entrance inside the test center after gate closure timings.
    • Electronic systems, cellular gadgets, calculator systems, and watch models are entirely prohibited inside rooms.
    • Retain the authenticated copy of the hall ticket carefully until the final stages of enlistment.
