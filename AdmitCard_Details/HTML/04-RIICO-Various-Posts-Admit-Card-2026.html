How to Download Admit Card

  1. Visit the official RIICO website at riico.co.in.
  2. Open the recruitment or call letter link for RIICO various posts written examination.
  3. Enter the login details required on the official portal.
  4. Download the call letter and check post, exam date, shift, centre and reporting time.
  5. Print the call letter and keep an extra copy for safety.
  6. Read the exam centre instructions printed on the call letter before exam day.

Details Mentioned on Admit Card

  • Candidate name and roll/registration number.
  • Post name and written examination date.
  • Shift timing and exam duration.
  • Exam centre and reporting time.
  • Photograph, signature or identity details, if printed.
  • Important exam-day instructions mentioned in the call letter.

Documents to Carry

  • Printed RIICO call letter.
  • Valid photo identity proof.
  • Passport size photograph, if instructed.
  • Application acknowledgement, if required.
  • Any other document mentioned in the call letter.

Exam Day Instructions

  • Check your post-wise exam date and shift before travelling.
  • Follow the reporting time mentioned in the call letter.
  • Carry a printed call letter and valid photo ID.
  • Visit RIICO website regularly for latest updates.
  • Follow centre rules and instructions printed on the call letter.

Duplicate Admit Card / Correction Instructions
