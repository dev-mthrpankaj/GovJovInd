About NEET PG Admit Card 2026

The National Board of Examination in Medical Sciences (NBE) conducts the National Eligibility cum Entrance Test (Postgraduate) or NEET PG. It is the primary entrance examination for admission into MD, MS, and PG Diploma courses.

The NBE has activated the exam city intimation slip link, allowing candidates to view the district where their exam center will be located. This facilitates early travel planning. However, the complete admit card—which includes the exact test center address, shift timings, and vital entry instructions—will be officially released on 24 August 2026.

Candidates must ensure they download their admit card via the official portal only. It is imperative to check all printed details for accuracy, such as the spelling of names, photograph clarity, and center address, and adhere strictly to the guidelines specified.

How to Download Admit Card / Check City Intimation

  1. Visit the official NBE portal or the direct download link provided by NBE.
  2. Look for the section indicating "NEET PG 2026" and click on the Admit Card or City Intimation link.
  3. Enter your registration login credentials accurately.
  4. Upon successful login, your exam city intimation slip or admit card will be displayed on the screen.
  5. Verify your exam date, city, exact centre address, and reporting timing.
  6. Download the PDF and take a clear, legible printout for the examination day.
  7. Read and strictly follow the official instructions printed at the bottom of the admit card.

Details Mentioned on Admit Card / City Slip

  • Candidate's Name and Roll Number / Registration Number
  • Exam Name (NEET PG 2026)
  • Course applied for (MD/MS/PG Diploma)
  • Exam Date (30 August 2026)
  • Reporting Time and Gate Closing Time
  • Exam City / District (available in the city intimation slip)
  • Full Exam Centre Address (available only in the final admit card)
  • Candidate's Photograph and Signature
  • Important Exam Day Instructions

Important: The City Intimation slip is *not* an admit card. It only shows your exam district to help you plan travel. You must download the actual admit card on 24 August to enter the exam hall.

Documents to Carry

  • A clear printout of the NEET PG 2026 Admit Card.
  • An original and valid photo ID proof (such as Aadhaar Card, PAN Card, Voter ID, Passport, or Driving License).
  • Passport size photograph (if instructed on the admit card).
  • PwBD certificate (if applicable and claiming relaxation).
  • Any other specific document mandated in the official admit card instructions.

Exam Day Instructions

  • Reach the examination centre strictly before the reporting time specified on your admit card. No late entries will be permitted.
  • Ensure your photo ID matches the details on your admit card exactly.
  • Do not carry prohibited items such as mobile phones, smartwatches, calculators, or any electronic gadgets into the premises.
  • Follow the seating arrangements and instructions provided by the invigilators at the centre.
  • Keep the admit card safe even after the exam; it will be required during the counselling and admission process.

Frequently Asked Questions

Is NEET PG Admit Card 2026 released?

The NEET PG Admit Card 2026 is scheduled to be released on 24 August 2026. However, Exam City Intimation details are already available.

What is the NEET PG 2026 exam date?

The NEET PG 2026 exam will be conducted on 30 August 2026.

When can I check my NEET PG Exam City?

The exam city intimation link has been active since 11 August 2026 on the official NBE portal.

Where to download the NEET PG admit card?

Candidates can download the admit card from the official website nbe.edu.in once the link is activated on 24 August 2026.

What login details are required?

Candidates must log in using their registered application credentials provided during the form filling process.

What is the exam timing and reporting time?

The exact exam timing and reporting time will be printed on the candidate's individual admit card. Please refer to your admit card once downloaded.

What documents are required at the exam centre?

Candidates must carry a clear printout of the admit card, an original valid photo ID proof, and any other documents specified on the admit card instructions.

Is the city intimation the same as the admit card?

No, the city intimation slip only informs you about the city where your exam will be held so you can make travel arrangements. The actual admit card contains the precise exam centre address and entry times.

What to do if admit card is not downloading?

If you face issues, ensure you are using the correct login credentials and a stable internet connection. If the problem persists, check the official website for technical help or contact NBE support.
