About This Exam City Allotment Update

The National Testing Agency (NTA) has published an official notification hosting the Advance Examination City Intimation Slip for candidates appearing in the Joint CSIR-UGC NET June 2026 examination. This test establishes eligibility for the award of Junior Research Fellowship (JRF), appointment as Assistant Professor, and admissions to PhD programmes across Indian universities.

The advance city slip enables candidates to note down the city where their exam centre will be located. This early communication helps outstation candidates complete their travel and logistics arrangements effectively well in advance of the actual examination days.

Candidates must carefully check that this specific advance intimation slip is NOT the official final admit card required for entry. The actual admit card with complete exam centre buildings, floor numbers, and deep instructions will be issued shortly by NTA.

How to Check CSIR UGC NET June 2026 Exam City

  1. Open your web browser and go to the official website of CSIR NET at csirnet.nta.nic.in.
  2. Find and click the link labeled "Advance City Intimation for Joint CSIR UGC NET June 2026".
  3. You will be redirected to the secure portal login interface.
  4. Input your designated Application Number and your Password or Date of Birth accurately.
  5. Fill in the visible security pin text properly, then submit the details.
  6. Your allotted examination city, along with date and shift schedule, will show up on the screen. Download and print the slip copy.

Details Mentioned on Advance City Slip

  • Candidate Full Name and Application Identification Number.
  • Photograph and Signature references.
  • Subject Name applied for (e.g., Chemical Sciences, Life Sciences).
  • Allotted Exam Date and Shift timings (Shift 1 or Shift 2).
  • Allotted City of Examination and State location.
  • Official general notices issued by the National Testing Agency.

Documents to Prepare for Exam Day

  • Clear printed copy of the final CSIR UGC NET Admit Card (to be downloaded separately once released).
  • One original valid government photo identification card (Aadhaar Card, Passport, PAN Card, Voter ID, or Driving License).
  • Passport size photograph matching the one uploaded in the online application form.
  • PwBD certificate or scribe documents, if applicable under official rules.

Important Exam Day Guidelines

  • The advance city intimation slip is only for reference and travel arrangements; it cannot be used to gain entry at the exam centre.
  • Candidates must arrive at the upcoming final allocated test centre precisely as per the reporting times mentioned on their final admit card.
  • Electronic items, calculators, mobile phones, bags, smartwatches, and loose paper sheets are prohibited inside the CBT test laboratory.
  • Ensure that details printed on both the city slip and the admit card match your official records perfectly.

    Frequently Asked Questions

    Is NTA CSIR UGC NET June 2026 Exam City Slip released?

    Yes, the National Testing Agency (NTA) officially released the advance exam city intimation slip on 8 July 2026.

    What are the exam dates for CSIR UGC NET June 2026?

    The written exam will be conducted on 17 July 2026 and 18 July 2026 in Computer Based Test (CBT) mode.

    Is the city intimation slip the same as the final admit card?

    No. The city intimation slip only informs candidates about their allotted exam city. The official final admit card with exact centre address will be released separately.

    What details are required to check CSIR NET exam city?

    Candidates can log in using their Application Number and Password or Date of Birth on the NTA website.

    Where can candidates download the exam city slip?

    Candidates can download the city slip from the official website csirnet.nta.nic.in or through the direct portal link provided.

    What should I do if there is a problem downloading the city slip?

    Candidates can contact the NTA helpline numbers at 011-40759000 or 011-69227700, or email csirnet@nta.ac.in for support.
