About BPSSC ASI Technical Admit Card 2026

Bihar Police Subordinate Services Commission (BPSSC) has officially declared the examination schedule and e-Admit Card release date for 22 vacancies of Assistant Sub Inspector Technical (Advt. No. 07/2026). The online application process was completed in June 2026, and candidates who successfully submitted their forms are eligible to download the hall ticket.

The written examination will take place on 05 August 2026 across designated centers in Bihar. Candidates can access their hall ticket by logging in to the official portal with their Registration ID or Registered Mobile Number and Password/Date of Birth. Ensure all personal details on the e-Admit card are accurate prior to attending the exam.

Candidates must strictly follow the exam day instructions mentioned on the hall ticket. Bringing a original valid photo identity proof along with the printed copy of the e-Admit Card is mandatory for entry into the exam center premises.

How to Download BPSSC ASI Technical Admit Card 2026

  1. Visit the official portal of BPSSC at bpssc.bihar.gov.in.
  2. Click on the e-Admit Card link for "Assistant Sub Inspector Technical (Advt. No. 07/2026)".
  3. Enter your Registration ID or Registered Mobile Number along with your Date of Birth / Password.
  4. Submit the credentials to view your admit card details on screen.
  5. Check all printed information, including your name, venue details, and roll number carefully.
  6. Download the PDF file and print out 2 clear copies for exam day submission.

Details Mentioned on BPSSC ASI Technical Hall Ticket

  • Candidate's Full Name & Father's Name
  • Roll Number and Registration Number
  • Exam Date, Time, and Shift Duration
  • Examination Centre Name & Full Postal Address
  • Reporting Time & Gate Closing Time
  • Candidate's Photograph and Digital Signature
  • Important Exam Day Rules & Guidelines

Documents to Carry on Exam Day

  • Printed copy of BPSSC ASI Technical e-Admit Card.
  • Original Photo Identity Proof (Aadhaar Card, PAN Card, Voter ID, Driving License, or Passport).
  • Two recent passport-size photographs matching the one uploaded during application.
  • Black / Blue ballpoint pen as specified in the exam instructions.

Exam Day Instructions

  • Reach the examination venue well before the reporting time specified on the hall ticket.
  • No entry will be allowed after the gate closing time under any circumstances.
  • Electronic gadgets such as mobile phones, smartwatches, calculators, and Bluetooth devices are strictly prohibited.
  • Ensure the admit card copy is preserved safely till the entire recruitment process (PET & DV) concludes.
  • Comply with all invigilator instructions inside the examination hall.
