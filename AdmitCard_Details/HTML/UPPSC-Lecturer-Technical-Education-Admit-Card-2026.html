About UPPSC Lecturer Technical Education Admit Card 2026

The Uttar Pradesh Public Service Commission (UPPSC) has activated the admit card download link for the Lecturer Technical Education recruitment examination. The examination is being conducted to fill 513 vacancies across various subjects like Mechanical, Electrical, Civil, Electronics, Computer, and others.

The admit card is a mandatory document that serves as an entry pass to the examination hall. Candidates are advised to download the admit card from the official UPPSC portal using their registration number and date of birth.

Upon downloading, candidates must thoroughly verify all details, including their name, photograph, exam date (06 September 2026), reporting time, and exact exam centre location. Any discrepancies should be brought to the immediate attention of the commission.

How to Download UPPSC Lecturer Admit Card 2026

  1. Visit the official UPPSC website at uppsc.up.nic.in.
  2. Click on the "Admit Card" or "Download Admit Card" link available on the homepage/candidate dashboard.
  3. Enter your Registration Number, Date of Birth, Gender, and the CAPTCHA code for verification.
  4. Click on the "Submit" or "Download Admit Card" button.
  5. Your UPPSC Lecturer Technical Education Admit Card 2026 will appear on the screen.
  6. Verify all details printed, including your Name, Roll Number, Exam Date, Time, and Centre Address.
  7. Download the PDF and take a clear printout on A4-size paper to carry on the exam day.

Details Mentioned on the Admit Card

  • Candidate's Full Name
  • Roll Number and Registration Number
  • Exam Name: UPPSC Lecturer Technical Education 2025-26
  • Subject Name (e.g., Mechanical, Civil, Computer, etc.)
  • Candidate's Photograph and Signature
  • Exam Date (06 September 2026)
  • Exam Time and Shift Details
  • Reporting Time at the Centre
  • Exam Centre Name and Complete Address
  • Important instructions for the examination day

Documents to Carry to the Exam Centre

Candidates must ensure they carry the necessary documents as mandated by UPPSC. Failing to present these may lead to denied entry.

  • A clear, printed copy of the UPPSC Lecturer Technical Education Admit Card 2026.
  • A valid original Photo ID Proof (e.g., Aadhaar Card, PAN Card, Voter ID, Passport, or Driving License).
  • Two recent passport-size photographs (if clearly mentioned in the admit card instructions).
  • A photocopy of the ID proof (if instructed on the admit card).
  • Black/Blue ballpoint pen (check official instructions for permitted stationery).

Exam Day Instructions

  • Reach the examination centre well before the reporting time specified on your admit card.
  • No candidate will be allowed entry after the gate closing time.
  • Electronic devices such as mobile phones, smartwatches, calculators, and bluetooth devices are strictly prohibited.
  • Carry only the permitted documents and stationery to your seat.
  • Follow all instructions provided by the invigilators and the centre superintendent.
  • Do not leave the examination hall until the test time is officially over and permitted by the invigilator.

Duplicate Admit Card / Correction Instructions

If you face issues downloading your admit card or find discrepancies in your details (like photo/signature mismatch), please check the official UPPSC website for resolution steps.

  • Double-check that your Registration Number and Date of Birth match the submitted application exactly.
  • Try accessing the portal using a stable internet connection or a different browser.
  • If details are incorrect, contact the UPPSC helpdesk immediately before the exam date.

Is UPPSC Lecturer Technical Education Admit Card 2026 released?

Where can I download the UPPSC Lecturer Admit Card 2026?
