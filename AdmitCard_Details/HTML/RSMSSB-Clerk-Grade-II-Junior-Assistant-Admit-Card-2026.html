About This Admit Card

The Rajasthan Staff Selection Board (RSSB), Jaipur has issued an official notice detailing the administrative schedule and dress code regulations for the upcoming Clerk Grade-II and Junior Assistant Joint Direct Recruitment Exam 2026. This pertains directly to the recruitment notified under Advt No. 04/2026 dated January 12, 2026.

Candidates who applied for these administrative posts can access information regarding their allotted exam city district from June 26, 2026 onwards. The official provisional e-admit cards will be available for quick download starting July 01, 2026. Board authorities clarified that no physical copy of the admit card will be sent to individuals by post.

The evaluation contains two mandatory papers conducted on the same day. Due to strict security checks, identity verification using advanced facial recognition and biometric methods will take place at the entrance gate. Candidates are highly encouraged to thoroughly evaluate the designated rules to minimize hassles on their exam date.

How to Download Admit Card

  1. Navigate to the official Rajasthan State Recruitment application portal at recruitment.rajasthan.gov.in or log in directly via your SSO portal.
  2. Locate and click on the specific link designated as "Get Admit Card" or follow the dedicated path /rectlogingetadmitcard.
  3. Select the specific link corresponding to the "Recruitment to download the admit card" for the Clerk Grade-II / Junior Assistant 2026 advertisement.
  4. Provide your authentic Application Number and Date of Birth (DOB) in the specified input blocks.
  5. Verify your exam date, shift details, and identity metrics visible on the screen.
  6. Download the digital copy and secure high-quality printouts to present at the examination premises.

Details Mentioned on Admit Card

  • Candidate's Legal Full Name and Parentage.
  • Roll Number and Unique Registration/Application Identification Number.
  • Official Advertisement Number (04/2026) and Post Details.
  • Specified Written Examination Date (05 July 2026).
  • Shift Details and individual timings for Paper-I and Paper-II.
  • Complete Address and specific code of the designated Exam Center.
  • Gate Closure timings and primary reporting windows.
  • Candidate Photo, Signature sample, and primary instruction guidelines.

Documents to Carry on Exam Day

  • Clear physical printout of the online provisional e-Admit Card.
  • Original valid Photo Identity Card — Primary preference is an Aadhaar Card with your Date of Birth explicitly printed. (Alternatively, PAN Card, Passport, Driving License, or Voter ID Card are accepted under exceptional circumstances).
  • One recent passport-sized color photograph ($2.5\text{ cm} \times 2.5\text{ cm}$) for each question paper shift, which must not be morphed, tampered, or older than one month.
  • One basic transparent blue ink ballpoint pen for writing and filling the OMR bubble fields.

Exam Day Instructions

  • Candidates must arrive at least 2 hours prior to the official commencement time to complete automated biometric profiling and face recognition without haste.
  • The exam entry doors will close firmly 1 hour before the exam start. No candidates will be admitted past 08:30 AM for the first shift under any circumstances.
  • Candidates are allowed to securely retain their Question Paper booklets and the carbon duplicate copies of their OMR sheets upon completion of the examination.
  • The OMR sheet fields must be bubbled using steady and appropriate pressure to guarantee a distinct impression on the attached carbon copy.
  • Candidates must strictly sit on their allocated seat matching all digits of their specific Roll Number. Any candidate detected inside a wrong room or wrong shift will face direct disqualification.

Official Exam Dress Code

The board enforces strict rules regarding dress choices. Violations can result in an absolute denial of entry at the center gates.

Frequently Asked Questions

What is the RSMSSB Clerk Grade II and Junior Assistant exam date 2026?

The examination will be conducted on 05 July 2026 (Sunday) in two separate question paper shifts.

When can I download the RSMSSB Clerk & Junior Assistant e-admit card?

Candidates can download their provisional e-admit cards starting from 01 July 2026 through their Rajasthan SSO ID dashboard.

How can I check my allotted exam district before the admit card release?

Candidates can view their allotted exam center district from 26 June 2026 by logging into their SSO ID profile.

What are the exam timings for Paper I and Paper II?

Paper-I will be conducted from 09:30 AM to 12:30 PM, and Paper-II will be conducted from 03:00 PM to 06:00 PM.

What is the required entry closing time at the exam center?

Candidates must report 2 hours before the exam. Entry gates will close exactly 1 hour prior to the start of the examination, after which no entry will be allowed.

What login credentials are required to download the admit card?

You will require your Rajasthan SSO ID credentials, or your Application Number and Date of Birth on the State Recruitment Portal.

What documents must I carry to the exam center?

You must bring a printed copy of the e-admit card, a valid original photo ID card (preferably Aadhaar Card displaying the date of birth), a recent 2.5cm x 2.5cm color photograph for each shift, and a transparent blue ink ballpoint pen.

Is there any duplicate admit card facility provided at the office?

No separate provision for duplicate admit cards is mentioned in the notice; candidates are advised to download and print it online from the official website.
