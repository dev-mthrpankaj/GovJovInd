About This Admit Card

Central Bank of India has issued an exam date notice for Engagement of Apprentices under the Apprentices Act, 1961 for FY 2026-27. The notice states that the examination will be conducted by BFSI SSC on 05 July 2026.

Candidates should use the admit card or call letter only from the official Bank website when it is made available. The admit card is important because it normally carries candidate details, exam centre, reporting instructions and exam-day guidelines.

The official notice also advises candidates to regularly visit the Bank website for updates, notices, addendum, corrigendum and instructions related to this process.

How to Download Admit Card

  1. Visit the official Central Bank of India website at centralbank.bank.in.
  2. Open the Recruitment section and look for the Apprentice FY 2026-27 admit card or call letter link.
  3. Enter the login details required on the official portal.
  4. Verify exam date, exam centre, reporting time and candidate details after opening the admit card.
  5. Download and print a clear copy of the admit card.
  6. Read all instructions printed on the admit card and official notice carefully.

Details Mentioned on Admit Card

  • Candidate name and registration details.
  • Exam or engagement name.
  • Exam date and shift, if printed.
  • Exam centre address and reporting time.
  • Photo, signature and identity-related details, if printed.
  • Important instructions issued by the Bank or exam conducting agency.

Documents to Carry

  • Printed admit card / call letter.
  • Valid photo identity proof.
  • Passport size photograph, if instructed on admit card.
  • Application acknowledgement, if required.
  • Any other document mentioned in the official admit card.

Exam Day Instructions

  • Visit the official website regularly for updates and corrigendum.
  • Follow the reporting time and centre rules printed on the admit card.
  • Carry valid ID proof along with the admit card.
  • Do not rely on unofficial links or screenshots.
  • Keep the admit card safely for future stages.

Duplicate Admit Card / Correction Instructions

Duplicate admit card or correction process is not mentioned in the provided notice. Candidates should check the official Central Bank of India website for any helpdesk, correction or duplicate call letter instructions.
