About This Admit Card Update

Sashastra Seema Bal (SSB) has issued the call letters for the Physical Efficiency Test (PET) and Physical Standard Test (PST) for 827 Constable Tradesman vacancies across various trades including Driver, Veterinary, Gardener, Water Carrier, Cobbler, Tailor, Washerman, Barber, Waiter, Carpenter, and Nursing Orderly.

Candidates who submitted their online applications between 21 March 2026 and 04 May 2026 can now access the admit card portal. The physical test ground venue, date, time, and instructions are clearly detailed on the individual e-admit card downloaded from the official portal.

It is mandatory for candidates to carry the physical copy of the admit card along with valid photo identity proof to the designated SSB selection centre on the date of testing.

How to Download SSB Constable Tradesman Admit Card

  1. Visit the official SSB recruitment website at recruitment.ssb.gov.in or direct portal applyssb.com.
  2. Locate and click on the "Download PET/PST Admit Card for Constable Tradesman 2026" link.
  3. Enter your Registration Number and Password / Date of Birth in the login credentials section.
  4. Enter the Captcha code as displayed on screen and click "Submit".
  5. Your SSB Tradesman PET/PST Call Letter will appear on the screen.
  6. Download the PDF and print at least two copies for exam day submission.

Details Mentioned on Admit Card

  • Candidate's Full Name, Roll Number, and Registration Number.
  • Applied Post Name and Trade.
  • PET / PST Exam Date and Reporting Time.
  • Assigned SSB Selection Centre / Ground Address.
  • Category and Gender of Candidate.
  • Candidate's Photograph and Signature.
  • Important Physical Test Instructions and Requirements.

Documents to Carry for PET / PST Test

  • Printed copy of SSB Constable Tradesman PET/PST Admit Card.
  • Original Photo Identity Proof (Aadhaar Card, Voter ID, PAN Card, Driving License, Passport).
  • 4-6 recent passport-size color photographs.
  • Photocopy of Online Application Form acknowledgment.
  • Educational qualification marksheets and certificates (10th/Matriculation).
  • Trade experience / skill certificates (where applicable).
  • Caste / Category / EWS certificate in prescribed format, if applicable.

PET / PST Exam Day Instructions

  • Reach the assigned SSB Selection Ground well before the reported time mentioned on the call letter.
  • Follow physical fitness requirements and guidelines for running, high jump, and physical standard checks as prescribed for your trade.
  • Ensure all original document sets and self-attested photocopies are kept ready for verification.
  • Electronic gadgets, smartwatches, and unauthorized items are prohibited at the testing venue.
  • Adhere strictly to physical ground decorum and staff instructions during PET/PST.
