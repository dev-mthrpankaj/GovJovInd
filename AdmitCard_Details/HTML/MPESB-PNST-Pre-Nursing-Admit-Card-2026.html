About This Admit Card Update

MPESB PNST Pre Nursing Admit Card 2026 is meant for candidates who applied for the Pre-Nursing Selection Test. The admit card is available online through the official test admit card portal and should be downloaded before the examination day.

The admit card is the main entry document for the examination centre. It generally carries the candidate details, roll number or application details, exam date, shift, reporting time, centre address and important instructions. Candidates should carefully match the details with their application records.

Because exam centre and shift details are candidate-specific, students should not rely only on third-party summaries. The official admit card should be treated as the final source for exam timing, reporting window, photo ID requirement and centre rules.

How to Download MPESB PNST Admit Card 2026

  1. Visit the official MPESB website at esb.mp.gov.in.
  2. Open the Test Admit Card section and select Pre-Nursing Selection Test (PNST) 2026.
  3. Or use the official PNST admit card link given in the important links section.
  4. Enter your Application Number, Date of Birth and other verification details shown on the login page.
  5. Submit the details and check the admit card carefully.
  6. Download the admit card and take a clear printout for exam day.
  7. Read every instruction printed on the admit card before travelling to the centre.

Details Mentioned on Admit Card

  • Candidate name and personal details.
  • Application number, roll number or registration details.
  • Exam name: Pre-Nursing Selection Test (PNST) 2026.
  • Exam date, shift and timing.
  • Reporting time and gate closing instruction, if printed.
  • Exam city and full examination centre address.
  • Candidate photograph and signature details.
  • Important instructions issued by MPESB for the examination day.

Documents to Carry on Exam Day

  • Printed copy of MPESB PNST Admit Card 2026.
  • Valid original photo identity proof such as Aadhaar card, voter ID, PAN card, passport or other ID accepted by the board.
  • Passport size photograph, if instructed on the admit card.
  • Application acknowledgement or other document, if mentioned in the admit card instructions.
  • Category, PwBD or other supporting document, if required for verification.
  • Any additional document specifically mentioned on the official admit card.

Exam Day Instructions

  • Reach the exam centre well before the reporting time printed on the admit card.
  • Entry may not be allowed after the gate closing time, so check the reporting instructions carefully.
  • Carry a clear printed admit card and valid original photo ID.
  • Do not depend on a mobile screenshot of the admit card unless the official instructions allow it.
  • Follow frisking, biometric verification and seating instructions at the centre.
  • Avoid carrying prohibited items such as electronic gadgets, notes, calculators or smart devices unless specifically allowed.
  • Keep the admit card safely after the exam because it may be useful during result, counselling or admission stages.

Duplicate Admit Card / Correction Instructions

No separate duplicate admit card schedule was verified for this page. If the admit card is not downloading, candidates should first recheck login details, refresh the official portal, try a different browser and then contact the MPESB or MPOnline helpdesk through the official website.

If there is any mismatch in name, photograph, date of birth or other details, candidates should contact the official helpdesk before the examination and follow the instructions printed on the admit card.

Frequently Asked Questions

Is MPESB PNST Admit Card 2026 released?

Yes. MPESB PNST Pre Nursing Admit Card 2026 is available through the official MPESB test admit card portal.

What is the MPESB PNST 2026 exam date?

When was MP PNST Admit Card 2026 released?

What login details are required for MP PNST admit card?
