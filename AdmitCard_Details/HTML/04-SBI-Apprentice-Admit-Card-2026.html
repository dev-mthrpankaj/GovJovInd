About This Admit Card

SBI Apprentice Admit Card 2026 is the online exam call letter for candidates who applied for the Engagement of Apprentices under the Apprentices Act, 1961. The call letter is required to appear in the online written examination and is available through the SBI Careers Current Openings section.

The admit card is important because it confirms the candidate's exam date, shift, reporting time, examination centre and important exam-day rules. Candidates should not depend only on third-party information for centre and timing because these details may vary candidate-wise.

Before going to the exam centre, candidates should check their name, roll number, photograph, signature, exam centre address, reporting time and document instructions printed on the call letter. Official verification is necessary because SBI will follow the details mentioned on the official call letter only.

How to Download SBI Apprentice Admit Card

  1. Visit the official SBI Careers Current Openings page at sbi.bank.in/web/careers/current-openings.
  2. Find the section titled Engagement of Apprentices under the Apprentices Act, 1961 for Advertisement No. CRPD/APPR/2026-27/07.
  3. Click on the Download Call Letter link available in the same recruitment section.
  4. Enter your Registration Number or Roll Number and Password or Date of Birth on the login page.
  5. Submit the details after completing captcha or verification, if shown on the portal.
  6. Check exam date, reporting time, exam centre and candidate details carefully.
  7. Download the call letter PDF and take a clear printout for exam day.

Details Mentioned on Admit Card

  • Candidate name and roll number or registration number.
  • Advertisement number and post or engagement name.
  • Online exam date, shift and reporting time.
  • Examination centre address and centre code, if printed.
  • Candidate photograph and signature details.
  • Login or exam instructions for the online test.
  • Documents required for verification at the exam centre.
  • Important instructions related to permitted and prohibited items.

Documents to Carry

  • Printed SBI Apprentice online exam call letter.
  • Original valid photo identity proof such as Aadhaar, PAN Card, Passport, Driving Licence, Voter ID, Bank Passbook with attested photo or other ID accepted by SBI.
  • Self-attested photocopy of the same photo identity proof, as required in the SBI notification.
  • Passport size photograph, if instructed on the call letter.
  • Application acknowledgement or registration details, if mentioned in the call letter.
  • Category, PwBD or scribe-related documents only if applicable and instructed by SBI.

Exam Day Instructions

  • Reach the exam centre as per the reporting time printed on your call letter.
  • Carry the printed call letter and valid photo ID in original as well as photocopy.
  • Do not wait till the last day to download the call letter because the login portal may be busy near the exam date.
  • Check the exam centre address in advance and plan travel accordingly.
  • Follow all instructions printed on the call letter and displayed at the examination centre.
  • No request for change of examination centre will be entertained as per SBI notification.
  • Keep the call letter safely after the exam because it may be useful in later stages.

Duplicate Admit Card / Correction Instructions

SBI has not provided a separate duplicate admit card schedule in the available recruitment notice. If a candidate faces any issue in downloading the call letter, they should check the official SBI Careers page and the call letter login page for latest instructions.

If there is any mismatch in candidate details, exam centre, photograph, signature or other printed information, candidates should follow the official helpdesk or instructions available on the SBI call letter portal. Do not make manual changes on the admit card unless officially instructed.
