About AFCAT 02/2026 Exam City Intimation

The Indian Air Force (IAF) has issued the exam city intimation details for the Air Force Common Admission Test (AFCAT 02/2026 Batch) for courses commencing in July 2027. Candidates who successfully submitted their online applications between 20 May 2026 and 21 June 2026 can now verify their allocated examination city district.

The exam city information has been communicated directly to candidates on their registered email addresses and mobile numbers starting 29 July 2026. Candidates can also access the CDAC login portal to view their exam details. The online written examination for AFCAT 02/2026 is scheduled for 08 August 2026 across various exam centers nationwide.

Please note that the city intimation slip is not the final admit card. It is intended to assist candidates in making travel arrangements in advance. The official AFCAT 02/2026 Admit Card / Hall Ticket with the exact center address, reporting time, and roll number will be made available for download shortly as per schedule.

How to Check AFCAT 02/2026 Exam City Details

  1. Check your registered email inbox or SMS inbox for official communication sent by IAF / CDAC on 29 July 2026.
  2. Alternatively, visit the official AFCAT portal at afcat.cdac.in.
  3. Click on the candidate login link for AFCAT 02/2026.
  4. Enter your registered Email ID and Password along with the Captcha code.
  5. Navigate to the Exam City / Candidate Dashboard section to view your allocated exam city and exam date (08 August 2026).
  6. Note down the exam city and begin your travel plans accordingly.

Details Mentioned on Exam City Slip / Admit Card

  • Candidate's Full Name and Registration Number.
  • Father's Name and Date of Birth.
  • Exam Name (AFCAT 02/2026) and Branch Opted (Flying / Ground Duty).
  • Allocated Exam City and State.
  • Date of Online Written Examination (08 August 2026).
  • Exact Exam Venue Address and Landmark (on Final Admit Card).
  • Reporting Time, Gate Closure Time, and Exam Shift Timings (on Final Admit Card).
  • Photograph, Signature, and Important Exam Instructions.

Documents to Carry to the Examination Centre

When the final hall ticket is released, candidates must ensure they bring the following original documents to the exam center:

  • Printed copy of the official AFCAT 02/2026 Admit Card (preferably in color).
  • Valid original Photo Identity Card (Aadhaar Card, Passport, PAN Card, Voter ID, Driving License, etc.).
  • Two recent passport-size colored photographs (matching the one uploaded in the application).
  • Ballpoint pen (Blue/Black) for rough work / sign-in procedures as specified.
  • Other items permitted by IAF as per instructions on the e-admit card.

Exam Day Instructions & Guidelines

  • Report to the exam venue strictly within the reporting window mentioned on your e-admit card.
  • Late entry after gate closure time will strictly not be permitted under any circumstances.
  • Electronic gadgets such as mobile phones, smartwatches, Bluetooth devices, calculators, and study material are prohibited inside the test hall.
  • Ensure mandatory biometrics and photograph capture are completed before taking your assigned workstation.
  • Keep your admit card safe throughout the examination and retain it for subsequent AFSB interview stages.

Frequently Asked Questions

Is AFCAT 02/2026 Exam City details released?

Yes, exam city intimation details for AFCAT 02/2026 have been sent to registered candidates via email and SMS on 29 July 2026.

What is the AFCAT 02/2026 exam date?

The AFCAT 02/2026 online examination is scheduled to be conducted on 08 August 2026.

When will AFCAT 02/2026 Admit Card be released?

The official hall ticket will be released prior to the exam date as per schedule on the candidate login portal.

Where to check AFCAT exam city intimation?

Candidates can check their registered email inbox, SMS messages, or log in directly to the candidate portal at afcat.cdac.in.

What login credentials are required to download AFCAT admit card?

Candidates require their registered Email ID and Password to log in to the portal.

Is city intimation the same as the admit card?

No, city intimation only informs you about your exam city district. The admit card gives exact center location and reporting details.

What documents are required at the AFCAT exam centre?

Candidates must carry a printed copy of the Admit Card, valid original Photo ID, two passport photos, and specified stationery.

What to do if exam city details or admit card is not received?

Check your email spam folder or log in directly on afcat.cdac.in. If issues persist, contact the official IAF helpdesk.
