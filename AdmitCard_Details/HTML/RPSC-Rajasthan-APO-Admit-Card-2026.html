About This Admit Card & City Intimation

The Rajasthan Public Service Commission (RPSC) conducts the Assistant Prosecution Officer (APO) examination to fill 371 vacancies. For the upcoming Preliminary Examination scheduled on 02 September 2026, RPSC has activated the Exam City Intimation and Admit Card download links.

Checking your exam city slip in advance helps you plan your travel to the allocated district. However, the city intimation is not your admit card. You must download the actual hall ticket, which contains your specific exam center address, reporting time, and mandatory candidate instructions.

Candidates must log in using their Rajasthan Single Sign-On (SSO) credentials. It is highly advised to download and print the admit card clearly and to meticulously cross-check personal details like name, photograph, and signature to avoid issues on the exam day.

How to Download Admit Card & Check Exam City

  1. Visit the official Rajasthan SSO portal at sso.rajasthan.gov.in or the RPSC website at rpsc.rajasthan.gov.in.
  2. For Exam City, click on the "Know Your Center Location" or City Intimation link.
  3. For the Admit Card, log in using your SSO ID (Username) and Password.
  4. Navigate to the "Recruitment Portal" app within the SSO dashboard.
  5. Click on the "Get Admit Card" tab and select the link for the Assistant Prosecution Officer (APO) 2026 exam.
  6. Verify all details such as your exam date, city, center address, and timing.
  7. Download the PDF and take a clear printout (preferably in color) on A4 size paper.
  8. Read the official instructions printed at the bottom or back of the admit card carefully.

Details Mentioned on Admit Card / City Slip

  • Candidate's Full Name
  • Roll Number and Application/Registration Number
  • Exam Name (RPSC Assistant Prosecution Officer - 2026)
  • Pre Exam Date (02 September 2026)
  • Exam Shift and Timing
  • Reporting Time and Gate Closing Time
  • Exam City / District (available on the City Slip)
  • Complete Exam Center Address (available only on the final Admit Card)
  • Candidate's Photograph and Signature
  • Crucial Exam Day Instructions

Note: For city intimation pages, the full exam center address is not provided. It will only indicate your allocated exam district/city.

Documents to Carry to the Exam Center

  • A clear printout of the RPSC APO Admit Card 2026.
  • Original and valid Photo ID proof (e.g., Aadhaar Card, Voter ID, PAN Card, Driving License, or Passport).
  • Recent passport-size photographs (as instructed on the admit card).
  • Category/PwBD certificate (if applicable and explicitly requested for verification).
  • Any other specific document explicitly mentioned in the official admit card guidelines.

Exam Day Instructions

  • Always reach the examination center well before the reporting time mentioned on your admit card. Latecomers will not be allowed entry once the gates close.
  • Ensure your original ID matches the details on your admit card exactly.
  • Do not carry electronic gadgets, smartwatches, calculators, or study materials, as these are strictly prohibited inside the hall.
  • Follow the dress code instructions (if any) specified by RPSC on the admit card to avoid delays during frisking.
  • Follow all guidelines provided by the invigilators and center staff.

Duplicate Admit Card / Correction Instructions

If you face issues downloading the admit card or notice discrepancies (like a misspelled name or blurry photo), you should immediately contact the RPSC helpdesk through the official portal. Check the official website for specific grievance redressal mechanisms.

Frequently Asked Questions

Is RPSC Rajasthan APO Admit Card 2026 released?

Yes, the RPSC admit card and exam city intimation link is active for the Assistant Prosecution Officer (APO) 2026 examination.

What is the RPSC APO 2026 exam date?

The official RPSC exam schedule shows the APO Pre Exam will be held on 02 September 2026, and the Mains Exam is scheduled for 27 December 2026.

When will the RPSC APO admit card be released?

The admit card is released a few days before the exam. For the 02 September 2026 exam, links are currently active on the official website.

Where to download the RPSC APO admit card?

Candidates can download the admit card from the official SSO Rajasthan portal (sso.rajasthan.gov.in) or the RPSC official website.

What login details are required to download the admit card?

You will need your SSO ID, Password, and the Application/Registration Number to log in and download your admit card.

What is the exam timing for RPSC APO 2026?

The exact exam timing and shift details are printed directly on your admit card. Please refer to your personal hall ticket.

What is the reporting time?

The reporting time is explicitly mentioned on the admit card. Candidates must reach the center well before the gate closing time.

What documents are required at the RPSC APO exam center?

Candidates must carry a clear printout of the admit card, an original valid photo ID (like Aadhaar Card, PAN Card, or Voter ID), and passport-size photographs as instructed.

Is exam city intimation the same as the admit card?

No. The city intimation slip only informs you about your exam district/city to help with travel arrangements. The actual admit card contains the exact center address and entry details.

What to do if the admit card is not downloading?

Check your SSO ID and password, ensure you have a stable internet connection, or try clearing your browser cache. If the issue persists, contact the RPSC helpdesk.
