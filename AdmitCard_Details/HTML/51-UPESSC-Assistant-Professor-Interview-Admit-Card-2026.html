About This Admit Card Update

The Uttar Pradesh Education Service Selection Commission (UPESSC) has issued a vital update concerning the recruitment of Assistant Professors under Advertisement Number 51. Following the successful conduction of the written examination on 18 and 19 April 2026, the commission officially intermediate-declared the results on 09 June 2026. A total of 3155 provisionally qualified candidates have been verified to proceed to the next stage.

Shortlisted applicants are required to download their formal interview call letters or e-admit cards starting from 29 June 2026. The interview evaluation process spans across multiple streams and subjects, commencing on 21 July 2026 and wrapping up on 13 August 2026. Candidates are expected to verify their individual dates as per the specific discipline mapping released by the administration.

It is important to emphasize that this ongoing selection process remains bounded under the final judgements of Special Appeal No. 368/2026 (Harvinder Singh & Ors vs State of UP & Ors) in the High Court and Special Leave Petition (Diary) No. 23758/2026 (Dheeraj Chandani & Ors vs State of UP & Ors) in the Supreme Court of India. Keeping copies of identity logs and guidelines handy before moving forward is highly recommended.

How to Download Interview Admit Card

  1. Browse over to the central portal of the commission at upessc.up.gov.in.
  2. Locate the prominent notice option indicating download instructions for Advertisement No. 51 Assistant Professor Interview Letter.
  3. Input your authentic registration parameters, reference identity number, or credentials inside the provided data window.
  4. Cross-examine the designated board time slot, allocation desk, and subject code configuration on screen.
  5. Download the electronic file format and extract a clean print template on an A4 sheet.
  6. Go through the technical instructions and guidelines completely before starting for the board city.

Details Mentioned on Admit Card

Shortlisted individuals should verify that the following data sets are cleanly printed on their generated call sheets:

  • Full name of the candidate alongside recent identity image.
  • Unique application sequence number and roll register log.
  • The assigned discipline/subject allocation name matching Advt 51 lists.
  • Precise interview date, board window, and morning/afternoon reporting hours.
  • Allocated Commission premises address in Prayagraj.
  • Authorized signature lines and authentication stamp placeholders.
  • Official code layout guidelines issued by the selection panel.

Documents to Carry for Interview Board

  • Original clear printed copy of the dynamic Interview Call Letter / Admit Card.
  • Official government identity documentation containing photograph (E.g., Aadhar, Driving License, Voter Card).
  • Academic record sheets, mark lists, certificates, and research degree verifications in original forms.
  • Recent passport-size photographs matching official application layouts.
  • Category validation certificates or reservation documents, if applicable under state rules.
  • Duly signed copies of application acknowledgment slips and files outlined inside the instructions layout.

Exam Day / Interview Instructions

  • Arrive at the designated UPESSC address in Prayagraj well inside the safe reporting hour mentioned on your letter.
  • Entry inside verification rooms will completely be barred without holding a valid structural call letter layout.
  • Maintain order and clear behavioral documentation records while your primary credentials are being verified by officials.
  • Refrain from carrying cellular systems, smart wrist wearables, electronic gadgets, or storage sticks inside evaluation rooms.
  • Preserve standard verification tokens safely until the entire final consolidation lists are prepared by the state panel.

Correction Instructions / Duplicate Admit Card

Candidates experiencing portal accessibility failures or missing name attributes within generated files are advised to look over instructions on the main portal or verify updates from the commission office at 23 Allen ganj, Prayagraj. For any specific queries regarding modifications or duplicate document access, please check the official website.
