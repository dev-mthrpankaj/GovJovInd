About This Admit Card Update

The Bihar Police Subordinate Services Commission (BPSSC) has officially issued the e-admit card for the written competitive examination for Havildar Instructor posts under Advertisement No. 05/2026. Candidates who submitted online applications between 01 May 2026 and 01 June 2026 can now download their hall tickets from the official web portal.

The written examination will take place on 29 July 2026 in a single shift across allocated examination centres in Bihar. Candidates are advised to check their personal details, exam venue, shift timings, and reporting instructions immediately upon downloading the admit card.

It is mandatory to bring a printed copy of the e-admit card along with an original valid photo identification card to the exam hall. Entry without a valid hall ticket will strictly not be permitted under any circumstances.

How to Download BPSSC Havildar Instructor Admit Card

  1. Go to the official website of BPSSC at bpssc.bihar.gov.in.
  2. Click on the "Home Guard" tab on the homepage.
  3. Find and click the link "Download Written Exam Admit Card for Havildar Instructor (Advt. No. 05/2026)".
  4. Enter your Registration ID or Registered Mobile Number.
  5. Enter your Date of Birth and the captcha code as displayed.
  6. Submit the credentials to generate and view your hall ticket.
  7. Download the e-admit card and take 2 clear printouts for examination day.

  • Candidate Name, Roll Number, and Registration ID.
  • Father's / Husband's Name.
  • Photograph and Digital Signature.

  • Reporting Time (09:30 AM) and Gate Closure Time.
  • Name and Full Address of the Allotted Examination Centre.
  • Important Exam Day Instructions and Code of Conduct.

Documents to Carry

  • Printed copy of BPSSC Havildar Instructor e-Admit Card.
  • Original Photo ID Proof (Aadhaar Card, Voter ID, Driving License, Passport, or PAN Card).
  • Two recent passport-size photographs (same as uploaded in the application form).
  • Black or Blue Ballpoint Pen for OMR sheet filling.

Exam Day Instructions

  • Reach the examination centre at least by 09:30 AM for security screening and biometric verification.
  • No entry will be allowed after the gate closure time specified on the admit card.
  • Electronic devices such as mobile phones, smartwatches, calculators, and Bluetooth items are strictly forbidden.
  • Follow the seating arrangement instructions provided at the test center.
  • Retain the admit card safely until the completion of the physical test and final selection process.

Duplicate Admit Card / Correction Instructions

If you face technical issues while downloading the admit card or notice discrepancies in your personal details, verify your login credentials first. In case of unresolved errors or download failures, candidates should check the official BPSSC website notices for instructions or contact the BPSSC helpline well before the exam date.
