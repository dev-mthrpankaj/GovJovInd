About This SSB Head Constable Ministerial Exam Notice

The Office of the Director General, Sashastra Seema Bal (SSB), under the Ministry of Home Affairs, has officially published a fresh circular concerning file number 475/RC/SSB/PET-PST/HC(Min)-20/2024/3454. According to this layout, the competitive Computer Based Test (CBT) for the 2020 Head Constable (Ministerial) recruitment drive is scheduled for execution on July 7, 2026.

Candidates who completed prior testing or applications are strictly instructed to configure their academic schedules in sync with this timeline. The board has clarified that individual venue allocations, time shifts, and access parameters will be embedded within the upcoming official e-admit cards.

To avoid fraudulent updates or outdated information, applicants must track notifications exclusively from the authorized web structures managed by the central security force.

How to Download SSB Head Constable Ministerial Admit Card

  1. Navigate to the primary SSB recruitment hub at recruitment.ssb.gov.in.
  2. Look for the operational link flashing "Download CBT Admit Card for Head Constable (Ministerial) - 2020".
  3. Input your designated identity metrics such as Registration Number, Roll ID, or password sequence as requested.
  4. Submit the secure captcha string to view your profile dashboard.
  5. Verify your personal details, specific reporting time window, and address configuration listed on the screen.
  6. Download the digital document layout and print out clean copies for authentication at the verification gates.

Details to Verify on the Document Slip

  • Full Name of the Candidate and Father's identity tracking information.
  • Unique Registration ID and Roll Number layout.
  • Post Identifier: Head Constable (Ministerial) 2020.
  • Scheduled Date of Test (07 July 2026).
  • Specific Reporting Time and Main Gate Closure Timeline.
  • Computer Lab Center Code and exact geographical venue details.
  • Clear passport photo and official signature imprint.
  • General execution guidelines and standard discipline protocols.

Standard Documents for Verification Gates

  • A clear physical printout of your SSB e-Admit Card.
  • A valid government identification template containing a clear face photograph (Aadhaar Card, Passport, driving license, or Voter ID).
  • Recent passport-sized identical color photos, if explicitly commanded in the primary text.
  • Basic writing gear if allowed inside the infrastructure layout.

General Exam Day Guidelines

  • Arrive well within the early reporting window to avoid biometric screening congestion.
  • Strictly no entry will be permitted post the official gate closing announcement.
  • Electronic systems, cellular equipment, smart wearables, and calculation aids are entirely prohibited.
  • Retain the certified physical admit card securely until all selection stages reach termination.
