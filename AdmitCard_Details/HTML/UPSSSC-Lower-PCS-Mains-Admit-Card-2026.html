About UPSSSC Lower PCS Mains Admit Card 2026

The Uttar Pradesh Subordinate Services Selection Commission (UPSSSC) is conducting the Mains Examination for the Combined Lower Subordinate Services (Lower PCS) to fill 2,587 vacancies across various departments[cite: 1]. Following the release of the eligibility result, UPSSSC has now activated the links for candidates to check their exam city and download their official Mains admit cards[cite: 1].

The exam city intimation allows candidates to plan their travel to the allotted district ahead of time[cite: 1]. However, this intimation is not the admit card. To gain entry into the examination hall, candidates must download the actual admit card, which contains the complete exam centre address, shift timing, and reporting guidelines.

Candidates are strongly advised to download their admit cards early to avoid last-minute server issues on the official UPSSSC portal. It is essential to read all printed instructions carefully and ensure that personal details matching your valid ID proof are correctly displayed on the hall ticket.

How to Download Admit Card / Check City Intimation

  1. Visit the official UPSSSC website at upsssc.gov.in[cite: 1].
  2. Under the "Important Go" or "Notice Board" section, look for the link corresponding to "UPSSSC Lower PCS Mains Admit Card" or "Exam City Intimation"[cite: 1].
  3. Click on the relevant link to open the candidate login portal[cite: 1].
  4. Enter your Registration Number, Date of Birth, select your Gender, and enter the Verification Code shown[cite: 1].
  5. Click on the "Download Admit Card" button[cite: 1].
  6. Verify your exam date, city, exact centre address, and reporting timing.
  7. Download the PDF and take a clear printout to carry on the exam day.

Details Mentioned on Admit Card / City Slip

  • Candidate's Full Name and Father's/Husband's Name
  • UPSSSC Registration Number and Roll Number
  • Exam Name (Combined Lower Subordinate Mains 2026)
  • Exam Date (23 August 2026)[cite: 1]
  • Exam Shift and Timing
  • Reporting Time and Gate Closing Time
  • Exam City / District (For City Intimation Slip)
  • Complete Exam Centre Name and Address (Only on Admit Card)
  • Candidate's Photograph and Signature
  • Specific guidelines and instructions for candidates

Important: The City Intimation slip only provides the exam city/district. The exact exam centre address will only be printed on the final Admit Card.

Documents to Carry

Candidates must carry the following documents to the exam centre. Entry will not be permitted without proper verification:

  • A clear printout of the UPSSSC Lower PCS Mains Admit Card 2026.
  • Original and valid Photo ID proof (e.g., Aadhaar Card, PAN Card, Voter ID, Passport, or Driving License).
  • Two recent passport-size photographs (as per standard UPSSSC instructions).
  • A photocopy of the Photo ID proof (if officially requested in the admit card instructions).
  • Blue/Black ballpoint pen (check admit card guidelines for permitted stationery).

Exam Day Instructions

  • Arrive at the exam centre well in advance of the reporting time printed on the admit card.
  • No candidate will be allowed entry after the gate closing time under any circumstances.
  • Electronic devices, smartwatches, calculators, Bluetooth devices, and study materials are strictly prohibited.
  • Your identity will be verified at the entry gate; ensure the details on your ID match those on the admit card perfectly.
  • Follow the instructions of the invigilators and maintain discipline inside the exam hall.

Duplicate Admit Card / Correction Instructions

Check the official UPSSSC website immediately if you notice discrepancies in your admit card (such as incorrect photo, signature, or name spelling). If you cannot download your admit card, ensure you have successfully completed the Mains Exam Fee payment (deadline was 20 July 2026 for payment start, and originally extended past 25/06/2026)[cite: 1]. Contact UPSSSC official support channels for further assistance if the issue persists.

Frequently Asked Questions

Is UPSSSC Lower PCS Mains Admit Card 2026 released?

Yes, the UPSSSC Lower PCS Mains Admit Card 2026 has been released on the official website.

What is the UPSSSC Lower PCS Mains Exam Date 2026?

The UPSSSC Lower PCS Mains examination is scheduled to be held on 23 August 2026[cite: 1].

When was the UPSSSC Lower PCS City Intimation 2026 released?

The exam city intimation slip for the UPSSSC Lower PCS Mains was released on 14 August 2026[cite: 1].

Where can I download the UPSSSC Lower PCS Admit Card?

Candidates can download their admit card and check their exam city through the official UPSSSC website at upsssc.gov.in.

What login details are required to download the UPSSSC admit card?

To download the admit card, candidates need their Registration Number, Date of Birth, Gender, and the Verification Code shown on the login portal.

What documents are required at the UPSSSC Lower PCS exam centre?

Candidates must carry a printed copy of the official admit card, an original valid photo ID proof, and passport-size photographs as per the official instructions.

What should I do if my UPSSSC admit card is not downloading?

Check your internet connection, ensure your registration details are correct, clear your browser cache, or try accessing the official UPSSSC portal during non-peak hours.

Is the exam city intimation slip the same as the admit card?

No, the city intimation slip only informs candidates about their allotted exam district to help with travel arrangements. The actual admit card contains the exact exam centre address and entry instructions.
