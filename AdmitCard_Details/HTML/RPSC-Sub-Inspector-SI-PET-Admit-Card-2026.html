About RPSC Sub Inspector SI PET Admit Card 2026

The Rajasthan Public Service Commission (RPSC) has published the call letter and schedule for the Sub Inspector SI / Platoon Commander Physical Efficiency Test (PET) 2026 under Advertisement No. 05/2025. Candidates who successfully qualified the written stage are invited to appear for the PET stage.

The physical test is organized to measure physical endurance, fitness, and qualifications as per prescribed police department standards. The PET events are scheduled between 03 August 2026 and 13 August 2026 across designated regional centers in Rajasthan.

Candidates must carry a clear printed copy of their call letter along with valid photo identity proofs and required medical clearance documents to the designated test ground.

How to Download RPSC SI PET Admit Card 2026

  1. Visit the official Rajasthan Recruitment Portal at recruitment.rajasthan.gov.in or RPSC home site.
  2. Click on the "Get Admit Card" section or navigate to the RPSC Sub Inspector SI PET Admit Card 2026 link.
  3. Select the exam type (Physical Test / PET) and enter your Application Number and Date of Birth.
  4. Alternatively, login via Rajasthan Single Sign-On (SSO Portal) and open the Recruitment Stack.
  5. Click on "Get Hall Ticket" for Sub Inspector Combined Competitive Exam 2025-2026.
  6. Verify your ground location, reporting time, and shift details, then print out multiple copies for the ground exam.

Details Mentioned on PET Call Letter

  • Candidate Name, Roll Number, and Registration/Application ID.
  • Father's Name and Category details.
  • PET Venue / Ground Address and Centre Code.
  • Date of PET Exam and Reporting Time.
  • Candidate Photograph and Signature space.
  • Specific Physical Efficiency Test rules and required equipment/dress code.

Documents to Carry for RPSC SI PET

  • Hard copy printout of RPSC Sub Inspector SI PET Admit Card 2026.
  • Original valid Photo Identity Proof (Aadhaar Card, Voter ID, Driving License, or Passport).
  • Recent passport-size photographs as specified in the notice.
  • Medical Fitness Certificate if required by the board.
  • Photocopies of essential educational and category documents if required for spot verification.

Physical Efficiency Test Ground Instructions

  • Report to the assigned police lines or ground venue well before the designated reporting time.
  • Wear appropriate athletic attire and suitable running footwear for physical tasks.
  • Follow all guidelines provided by ground supervisors during running, long jump, chin-ups, or other PET events.
  • Electronic gadgets, smartwatches, and unauthorized items are strictly prohibited inside the physical ground.
