About UPSSSC Stenographer Skill Test Admit Card

The UPSSSC Stenographer Admit Card 2026 is a mandatory document for candidates appearing in the Skill Test (Advertisement No. 13-Exam/2024). It serves as proof of eligibility to sit for the typing and stenography test and contains vital information such as the candidate's roll number, exact exam centre, and reporting time.

Candidates can download their hall tickets from the official UPSSSC portal using their Registration Number and Date of Birth. Prior to the admit card release, UPSSSC also activated the Exam City Intimation slip so candidates could make travel arrangements in advance.

It is strongly advised to print a clear copy of the admit card and read all instructions carefully. Ensure that your name, photograph, and other details match your official ID proof to avoid issues at the exam venue.

How to Download UPSSSC Admit Card / City Slip

  1. Visit the official UPSSSC website at upsssc.gov.in.
  2. Look for the "Examination Interview" segment or the direct Admit Card download link on the homepage.
  3. Select the link for "Download Admit Card for Stenographer Skill Test (13-Exam/2024)".
  4. Enter your Registration Number, Date of Birth, and Gender.
  5. Input the Verification Code (Captcha) and click on the "Download Admit Card" button.
  6. Verify your exam date, city, centre address, and reporting time.
  7. Download the PDF and take a clear printout for the exam day.
  8. Read the official instructions printed on the admit card carefully.

Details Mentioned on the Admit Card

  • Candidate's Full Name
  • Roll Number and Registration Number
  • Exam Name (Stenographer Skill Test) and Advertisement Number
  • Candidate's Photograph and Signature
  • Exam Date and Shift Timings
  • Reporting Time and Gate Closing Time
  • Full Address of the Exam Centre
  • Category and Sub-Category
  • Official Instructions and COVID/Safety guidelines

Note: If you are downloading the City Intimation slip, it will only display your Exam District and Date, not the full centre address.

Documents to Carry

Candidates must ensure they bring the following documents to the UPSSSC exam centre to be allowed entry:

  • A clear, printed copy of the UPSSSC Stenographer Admit Card 2026.
  • Original and valid Photo ID proof (e.g., Aadhaar Card, Voter ID, PAN Card, Driving License, or Passport).
  • 2 recent passport-size color photographs (if mentioned in the instructions).
  • Category or PwBD certificate (only if explicitly required by the admit card instructions).

Exam Day Instructions

  • Reach the exam centre strictly before the reporting time to complete frisking and biometric verification.
  • Latecomers will not be permitted to enter the venue under any circumstances.
  • Electronic devices, smartwatches, Bluetooth devices, and calculators are strictly prohibited.
  • Do not carry any study material, written notes, or bags inside the examination hall.
  • Follow all guidelines provided by the invigilator during the Skill Test/Typing Test.

Duplicate Admit Card / Corrections

If you find any discrepancy in your name, photograph, or category on the admit card, or if you are unable to download it, you should immediately check the official website for helpdesk numbers. UPSSSC generally does not issue duplicate admit cards offline. Ensure your internet connection is stable and try downloading again using the exact details filled in the application form.

Frequently Asked Questions

Is UPSSSC Stenographer Skill Test Admit Card released?

Yes, the UPSSSC Stenographer Skill Test admit card link is active on the official website.

What is the UPSSSC Stenographer Skill Test exam date?

The exam was scheduled for 20 January 2026. Please check your official admit card for the exact shift and timings.

Where can I download the UPSSSC Stenographer admit card?

You can download the admit card from the official UPSSSC website at upsssc.gov.in using your Registration Number and Date of Birth.

What login details are required to download the admit card?

Candidates need their UPSSSC Registration Number, Date of Birth, Gender, and the verification code shown on the screen.

Is City Intimation the same as the Admit Card?

No. The City Intimation slip was released earlier (10 January 2026) to inform candidates of their exam district. The final admit card contains the exact exam centre address and reporting time.

What documents are required at the exam centre?

You must carry a clear printout of your admit card, original valid photo ID proof, and passport-size photographs as per the official instructions.

What should I do if my admit card is not downloading?

Ensure you are entering the correct Registration Number and DOB. Try accessing the site from a desktop browser with a stable internet connection. If the issue persists, contact the UPSSSC helpdesk.

What is the reporting time for the exam?

The exact reporting time is mentioned on your printed admit card. Candidates are advised to reach well in advance.
