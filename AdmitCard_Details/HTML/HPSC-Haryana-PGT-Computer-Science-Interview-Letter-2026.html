About HPSC Haryana PGT Interview Notice

The Haryana Public Service Commission (HPSC) has officially rolled out the interview timeline for qualified Post Graduate Teacher (PGT) candidates specializing in Computer Science. This evaluation follows the written examination held on 17 May 2026 and the subsequent results publication on 04 July 2026. The upcoming assessment determines final selection configurations across two distinct vacancy distributions in Haryana.

The structured personality evaluation is slated to begin on 24 July 2026 and wrap up by 01 August 2026. Eligible candidates are advised to verify their specific reporting assignments by checking the call letters distributed online. Securing a digital printout of this certificate is mandatory to pass individual venue identification steps.

If any verification errors or download disruptions arise on the login portal, individuals should immediately cross-verify registration schedules or look up notice extensions posted directly on the commission's primary portal.

How to Download HPSC PGT Interview Call Letter

  1. Navigate to the official commission space at hpsc.gov.in.
  2. Look up the specialized announcements updates section on the portal homepage.
  3. Select the designated login portal for PGT Computer Science Advertisement No. 23/2026 or jump directly to regn.hpsc.gov.in.
  4. Input your authenticated Registration Number or Mobile Number accompanied by your Password.
  5. Review the allocated interview session dates, individual reporting slots, and assigned board settings.
  6. Click download to preserve your digital PDF copy and execute clear prints for physical display.

Details Mentioned on Interview Letter

  • Candidate Identity Details (Full Name, Father's Name).
  • Roll Number & Assigned Application Sequence Number.
  • Advertisement Number (Advt No 23/2026).
  • Specific Post Designation (PGT Computer Science).
  • Allocated Interview Date and Shift/Batch Classification.
  • Reporting Time limits at the Commission's Office.
  • Official Venue Location details.
  • Important guidelines and document checklist specified by HPSC.

Documents to Carry for Interview

  • Original physical copy of the downloaded HPSC Interview Call Letter.
  • Valid photo identity credentials (Aadhaar Card, Passport, Voter ID, or Driving License).
  • Original set of academic documents (Matriculation certificate, 10+2, Graduation, M.Sc Computer Science / MCA / B.Tech degree sheets).
  • HTET / STET qualification proof documents.
  • Valid Category Certificate / EWS / PwBD parameters verification cards, if applicable.
  • Recent color passport-sized photographs matching portal submissions.
  • Self-attested duplicate photocopies of all academic and reservation proofs.

Exam Day / Interview Venue Instructions

  • Strictly adhere to the dynamic reporting times mentioned on the document grid.
  • Late entry parameters block admission once verification rosters are frozen.
  • Ensure consistent academic record proofs are sequenced systematically for verification.
  • Prohibited electronic items or unauthorized communication tools are strictly barred inside the interview venue.
  • Preserve standard verification sheets properly until final selection rolls are cleared.

Frequently Asked Questions

What are the HPSC Haryana PGT Computer Science interview dates for 2026?

The interviews for HPSC PGT Computer Science will be conducted from 24 July 2026 to 01 August 2026.

How many total posts are announced under HPSC PGT Advt No 23/2026?

There are a total of 1672 posts, including 1594 posts for the Rest of Haryana cadre and 78 posts for the Mewat cadre.

When was the HPSC PGT Computer Science written exam result declared?

The written exam results were officially declared on 04 July 2026.

Where can I download my HPSC PGT Interview Call Letter?

Candidates can download their interview call letters through the official HPSC registration portal at regn.hpsc.gov.in.

What details are required to log in and check the interview schedule?

You will require your Registration Number or Registered Mobile Number along with your Password to access the login portal.

What documents should I carry for the HPSC PGT Interview?

Candidates must carry the printed interview letter, a valid photo identity proof, educational certificates, category certificate (if applicable), and passport size photographs.

What can I do if the portal link rejects my credentials?

Please double-check your initial credentials entry or click on official links posted under the notice tabs of the main hpsc.gov.in dashboard.
