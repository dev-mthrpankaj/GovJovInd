About This Admit Card Notice

The Uttar Pradesh Subordinate Services Selection Commission (UPSSSC) has activated the online link to download e-admit cards for the Assistant Boring Technician Mains Examination 2026. Shortlisted candidates based on their preliminary eligibility test (PET 2025) scores who have successfully processed their online main exam fee payment can access their respective admit cards starting from 8 July 2026.

As per the official notification, the written main exam will be conducted on 12 July 2026 (Sunday) in a single shift across three designated districts of Uttar Pradesh: Ghaziabad, Lucknow, and Varanasi. Candidates are advised to check their exam center location details and reporting requirements as listed explicitly inside their personal hall tickets.

Carrying a physical copy of the admit card is mandatory to gain entrance into the examination venue. No candidate will be allowed to sit for the exam without a valid printed hall ticket and a supported original identity document.

How to Download UPSSSC Assistant Boring Technician Admit Card

  1. Go to the official website of UPSSSC at upsssc.gov.in.
  2. Look at the "Applicant Segment" or live notification dashboard on the homepage.
  3. Click on the link designated for "Download Admit Card for Main Examination (Advt. No. 04-Exam/2026)".
  4. Enter your valid Registration Number, Date of Birth, Gender, and the given Captcha Verification Code.
  5. Click on the "Download Admit Card" button to generate the ticket.
  6. Check all printed personal descriptions carefully and save a high-quality printout.

Details Mentioned on Admit Card

  • Candidate Full Name & Roll Number
  • Registration Number (Mains/PET)
  • Category and Gender classification
  • Photograph and Signature of the applicant
  • Allotted Exam Center Name and Complete Address
  • Exam Date, Shift, and accurate Subject Details
  • Reporting Time & Gate Closing Time
  • Exam day discipline and general guidelines

Documents to Carry on Exam Day

  • Clear hard copy printout of the UPSSSC E-Admit Card.
  • Original valid Photo Identity Card (Aadhaar Card, Passport, Voter ID, Driving License, or PAN Card).
  • One legible photocopy of the same Photo Identity Card.
  • Two recent passport-sized color photographs with the candidate's name and roll number noted behind.
  • Black or Blue ballpoint pen for marking responses on the OMR sheet.

Exam Day Instructions

  • Plan ahead to arrive at the allotted exam center well within the stated reporting window.
  • No candidate will be allowed entry inside the center premises post the closing of the gates.
  • Electronic gadgets, smartwatches, calculators, mobile phones, textual notes, and log tables are strictly prohibited.
  • Carefully read and execute instructions specified for filling up details on the OMR response sheet.
  • Maintain strict compliance with security scanning and biometric attendance systems at the center.
