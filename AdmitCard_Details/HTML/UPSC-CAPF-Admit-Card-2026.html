About This Admit Card Update

The Union Public Service Commission (UPSC) has opened the digital download link for the e-Admit Cards of the Central Armed Police Forces (Assistant Commandants) Examination 2026. This national-level recruitment process is organized to fill 349 vacancies for Group-A level officers across security forces including BSF, CRPF, CISF, ITBP, and SSB.

The written examination is scheduled to be held on July 19, 2026 (Sunday) in two separate shifts. Paper 1 will assess General Ability and Intelligence in an objective format, followed by Paper 2 which tests General Studies, Essay, and Comprehension in a conventional descriptive layout. Registered applicants are required to obtain their hall tickets online, as no hard copies will be sent through post or mail services.

To acquire the document, candidates can navigate to the official portal login window and access their dashboard via three distinct paths: using their Registration ID (RID), Roll Number, or registered Mobile/Email details with a password/OTP. It is mandatory to print and carry a clear physical copy of the document to the examination venue.

How to Download UPSC CAPF Admit Card 2026

  1. Open your web browser and go directly to the UPSC digital login link: upsconline.nic.in Portal.
  2. Alternatively, visit the main administrative website at upsc.gov.in and click the "e-Admit Card Central Armed Police Forces (CAPF)" link under the What's New banner.
  3. Choose your preferred verification path: Registration ID (RID), Roll Number, or Email/Mobile Login mode.
  4. Enter the required system credentials carefully along with your accurate Date of Birth.
  5. Submit the dynamic Captcha security pin text visible on the entry interface.
  6. Read through the official examination guidelines page and click "Yes" to acknowledge.
  7. Your official e-Admit Card will appear on the layout screen. Click the save or print command.
  8. Take 2-3 clear hard copy printouts on a standard white sheet and preserve them safely for the exam day.

Details Mentioned on the e-Admit Card

Candidates must carefully check all fields on the downloaded sheet to make sure there are no typos or printing issues. The page documents:

  • Full Name of the Candidate
  • Roll Number and Registration ID (RID)
  • Examination Name and Post Title
  • Scanned Image of Photograph and Signature
  • Father's and Mother's name details
  • Allocated Exam Venue and City District
  • Exact Test Center Address with Landmark parameters
  • Timings for Paper 1 and Paper 2 sessions
  • Important instructions and general exam code of conduct

Mandatory Documents to Carry on Exam Day

  • Printed Admit Card: A clear physical hard copy of the UPSC CAPF 2026 e-Admit Card.
  • Valid Photo ID Proof: The original government identity card whose number was entered in the online application form (e.g., Aadhaar Card, Voter ID, PAN Card, Driving Licence, Passport).
  • Passport Photographs: Two recent passport-size color photographs identical to the one uploaded during registration, to be used in case of printing ambiguities.
  • Stationery Items: Good quality Black Ball Point Pen for encoding the OMR answer sheets for Paper 1.

Crucial Exam Day Instructions

  • Reach the allocated examination center venue at least 60 minutes prior to the start time of the respective session.
  • Note that entry into the exam hall closes strictly 10 minutes before the scheduled commencement of each session (i.e., 09:50 AM for Paper 1 and 01:50 PM for Paper 2). No entry is allowed after gate closure.
  • Smartphones, smartwatches, digital calculators, bluetooth communication assets, bags, log tables, or text chits are completely forbidden on the testing floor. Only analog watches are permitted.
  • Candidates must retain the stamped admit card safely until the final stages of selection (PET/PST and Personal Interview).

Discrepancies & Correction Instructions

In case of any error, spelling mismatch, or missing photograph elements on the printed hall ticket, candidates should immediately notify the Union Public Service Commission. The communications details are:

  • Office Location: Union Public Service Commission, Dholpur House, Shahjahan Road, New Delhi - 110069.
  • Official Helpline Numbers: 011-23385271 / 011-23381125 / 011-23098543 (10:00 AM to 5:00 PM on working days).
  • Resolution Window: Candidates must address discrepancies promptly to obtain official clearance before the examination date.

Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)

Is the UPSC CAPF Admit Card 2026 released?

Yes, the Union Public Service Commission officially activated the server download links on 10 July 2026.

What is the exact UPSC CAPF 2026 exam date?

The written examination will be held on 19 July 2026 (Sunday).

Where can I access the online login dashboard for downloading?

You can use the official web interface at upsconline.nic.in or upsc.gov.in using your registration credentials.

What login details are required to download the hall ticket?

Input your formal application Registration ID (RID), Roll Number, or use the Email/Mobile OTP confirmation window.

How many vacancies are tied to the UPSC CAPF 2026 notification?

This recruitment drive contains 349 authorized Assistant Commandant (AC) vacancies across corporate paramilitary forces.

What are the exam session timings?

Paper 1 will run from 10:00 AM to 12:00 Noon, and Paper 2 will be organized from 02:00 PM to 05:00 PM.

What should I do if my photo is blurry on the CAPF call letter?

You must bring two identical passport-size photographs along with a valid original government photo ID to the venue for verification.

When does the examination entry gate close?

The entry gate closes strictly 10 minutes prior to the start of each test session (09:50 AM for morning, 01:50 PM for afternoon).
