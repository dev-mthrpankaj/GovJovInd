About This Admit Card

CGPSC has published the time schedule for the preliminary written examination of Subedar, Sub Inspector Cadre and Platoon Commander Recruitment 2024. As per the notice, candidates who were found eligible after document verification and physical measurement will appear in the prelims examination on 12 July 2026.

The admit card is an important document because it confirms the candidate's exam details such as roll number, exam date, centre information, reporting instructions and identity verification rules. Candidates should download the admit card only from the official CGPSC website and take a clear printout before the exam day.

Before going to the exam centre, candidates should check every detail printed on the admit card carefully. If there is any mismatch or confusion, candidates should follow the latest instruction available on the official website instead of relying only on third-party summaries.

How to Download CGPSC Subedar SI Admit Card

  1. Visit the official CGPSC website at psc.cg.gov.in.
  2. Open the admit card or latest notification section on the homepage.
  3. Click the admit card link for Subedar, Sub Inspector Cadre and Platoon Commander Preliminary Examination 2024.
  4. Enter the required registration or login details as asked on the official portal.
  5. Verify exam date, exam centre, reporting time and candidate details carefully.
  6. Download the admit card and print a clear copy for exam day use.
  7. Read all instructions printed on the admit card before visiting the exam centre.

Details Mentioned on Admit Card

  • Candidate name and roll number or registration number.
  • Exam name, post name and advertisement number.
  • Preliminary exam date and exam timing.
  • Reporting time, gate closing time and entry instructions.
  • Exam centre name, centre code and full address.
  • Candidate photograph, signature and identity-related details.
  • Important instructions regarding allowed and prohibited items.
  • Any additional instruction issued by CGPSC for the exam day.

Documents to Carry

  • Printed copy of CGPSC admit card.
  • Valid photo identity proof such as Aadhaar Card, Voter ID, PAN Card, Driving Licence or any document accepted by CGPSC.
  • Passport size photograph, if required in the admit card instructions.
  • Application acknowledgement or registration proof, if required for verification.
  • Category, PwBD or other supporting document, if applicable and instructed.
  • Any other document specifically mentioned on the official admit card.

Exam Day Instructions

  • Reach the examination centre as per the reporting time printed on the admit card.
  • Carry a clear printed admit card and original valid photo ID proof.
  • Check the exam centre route before the exam day to avoid last-minute delay.
  • Do not carry prohibited items if they are restricted in the admit card instructions.
  • Follow identity verification, attendance and seating instructions at the centre.
  • Keep the admit card safe even after the exam because it may be needed in later stages.

Duplicate Admit Card / Correction Instructions

The official schedule notice does not mention any separate duplicate admit card facility or correction window. Candidates who face download issues, spelling errors or mismatch in admit card details should check the official CGPSC website and latest notices for helpdesk or correction-related instructions.
