Steps to Download the DSSSB MTS Hall Ticket

  1. Launch an updated web browser and visit the direct digital verification link hosted at https://cdn.digialm.com//EForms/configuredHtml/1258/101323/login.html or navigate via the main updates pipeline on dsssb.delhi.gov.in.
  2. Locate the explicit link labeled for downloading the E-Admit Card for the MTS multi-phase computer examination.
  3. Input your authentic Application Number as provided on your initial registration form printout.
  4. Provide your designated Password (for fresh activations, the placeholder standard structure follows the Date of Birth formatting rules).
  5. Carefully copy the visible verification alpha-numeric Captcha code into the mandatory checkbox and trigger the 'Login' action.
  6. Upon dashboard resolution, select the 'Admit Card' download tab to render the digital copy.
  7. Generate a clear physical printout on an A4 sheet and systematically preserve the file configuration for future processing.

Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)

Is the DSSSB MTS Admit Card 2026 released?

Yes, the Delhi Subordinate Services Selection Board (DSSSB) has released the admit card link on its official portal for candidates appearing in the multi-phase Computer Based Examination.

What is the exam date for DSSSB MTS 07/2025?

The Computer-Based Examination (CBT) for DSSSB Multi-Tasking Staff is scheduled to be held from 25th July to 1st September 2026 across various designated shifts.

How can I download my DSSSB Multi Tasking Staff Hall Ticket?

Candidates can visit the direct login portal URL provided by DSSSB, enter their official Application Number and Password (default password is Date of Birth for initial setup), fill in the captcha, and click download.

What is the official total vacancy for this recruitment?

This recruitment drive under Advertisement Number 07/2025 is being conducted to fill a total of 714 Multi-Tasking Staff (MTS) positions across various government departments in Delhi.

What documents must be carried to the DSSSB MTS exam centre?

Candidates must bring a clear printout of the DSSSB MTS Admit Card, an original government-issued photo identity proof (Aadhaar Card, PAN Card, Voter ID, or Driving License), and two recent passport-size color photographs.

Is there a negative marking in the DSSSB MTS CBT exam?

Yes, there is a penalty for wrong answers. A deduction of 0.25 marks will be applicable for each incorrect multiple-choice response in the exam.

What should I do if my city intimation details are missing?

If you encounter technical issues or missing info, verify your registration status and contact the DSSSB helpdesk immediately or review official notices on the main website.

Where will the DSSSB MTS written exam be conducted?

The examination will be conducted at multiple online test centres located specifically within the National Capital Territory of Delhi region.
