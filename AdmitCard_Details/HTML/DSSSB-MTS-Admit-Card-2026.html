About This Admit Card Notice

The Delhi Subordinate Services Selection Board (DSSSB) has formally initiated the multi-phase online examination framework for the recruitment of 714 Multi-Tasking Staff (MTS) vacancies associated with Advertisement No. 07/2025. Registered candidates who have applied for Post Code 803/25 can now efficiently check their respective center locations and scheduled shifts via the official admit card download facility.

The online examination phase spans from 25 July 2026 to 1 September 2026. The examination authority organizes the multi-shift schedule structure to seamlessly evaluate candidates across various departments under the Government of NCT of Delhi. Entering the designated testing center without a legitimate printed e-admit card will strictly not be permitted under any circumstances.

Candidates must ensure that all fields matching their identity parameters appear correctly. If any discrepancies concerning the name spelling, photograph alignment, or signature rendering are discovered, it must be reported to the board's controllers immediately through the appropriate channels.

How to Download DSSSB MTS Admit Card

  1. Navigate directly to the official digital portal link provided or go to dsssb.delhi.gov.in.
  2. Locate the "Latest News" or specialized "Recruitment" area visible on the landing page interface.
  3. Select the specific update link denoting "Download E-Admit Card for DSSSB online exam scheduled from 25th July to 1st September 2026".
  4. Enter your unique Application Number along with your designated User Password into the form panels. For first-time account logins, your password defaults to your Date of Birth formatted as DD/MM/YYYY.
  5. Resolve the displayed security alphanumeric Captcha text precisely into the specified prompt box and click on the "Login" button.
  6. Review the presented DSSSB MTS Admit Card on the screen, verifying all dates, shift times, and locations before hitting download. Print out a clear copy.

Details Mentioned on Admit Card

  • Full name of the registered candidate.
  • Unique Application Number and newly generated Exam Roll Number.
  • Advertisement details (Advt No. 07/2025) and Post Name (MTS).
  • Allotted examination date, clear shift timings, and reporting windows.
  • Gate closure deadline timing instructions.
  • Comprehensive postal address of the assigned Computer-Based Examination venue.
  • Candidate's uploaded photograph and clear signature representation.
  • Crucial computer-based assessment system guidelines and behavioral rules.

Documents to Carry on Exam Day

  • A clear physical hardcopy printout of the downloaded DSSSB MTS e-Admit Card.
  • A valid original government-issued photo identity certificate (e.g., Aadhaar Card, Permanent Account Number (PAN) Card, Valid Driving License, or Voter Identity Card).
  • Two recent color passport-size photographs matching the application parameters.
  • Valid original supportive PwBD category documentation credentials, if explicitly applicable for accommodation.

Exam Day Instructions

  • Candidates must plan travel arrangements to reach the specified facility at least 60 minutes prior to the reporting time.
  • Under no circumstances will gate access permissions be extended once the formal Gate Closure time has elapsed.
  • Prohibited electronic items including smartwatches, mobile communication devices, Bluetooth gear, calculators, and storage drives are completely forbidden inside the assessment zones.
  • Maintain the downloaded admit card carefully even after the test wraps up, as the board checks it during the physical document verification stages.

Admit Card Correction Instructions

Should any candidate detect typographical anomalies, blurriness in the picture, or incorrect demographic fields within the hall ticket, they must urgently flag the discrepancy to the board's administrators to ensure smooth validation entry on test day.

  • Official Controller Helpdesk Email: coe-dsssb@delhi.gov.in
  • Direct Telephone Enquiry Helpline: 011-22378382
  • Candidates are advised to prepare their application receipt photocopy along with valid identity cards when communicating for corrections.

Frequently Asked Questions

Is the DSSSB MTS Admit Card 2026 released?

Yes, the Delhi Subordinate Services Selection Board (DSSSB) released the e-admit card on 29 June 2026.

What are the DSSSB MTS exam dates for 2026?

The Computer-Based Examination (CBT) for DSSSB MTS posts is scheduled to be held from 25 July to 1 September 2026.

Where can candidates download the DSSSB MTS Admit Card?

Candidates can download it from the official portal via the direct link provided through the official website dsssb.delhi.gov.in.

What details are required to log in and download the hall ticket?

Candidates need their Application Number and Password (their Date of Birth serves as the default password for first-time login).

What is the structure and duration of the DSSSB MTS exam?

The exam is a One-Tier Computer-Based Test containing 200 objective questions for 200 marks, with a total duration of 2 hours.

Is there negative marking in the DSSSB Multi-Tasking Staff exam?

Yes, there is a negative marking of 0.25 marks deducted for each wrong answer.

What documents must be carried to the examination centre?

Candidates must carry a clear printed copy of their e-admit card, a valid original government photo ID proof, and two passport-size photographs.

Who can candidates contact in case of an error on the admit card?

Candidates can email coe-dsssb@delhi.gov.in or contact the official enquiry number at 011-22378382.
