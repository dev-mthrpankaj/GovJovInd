About This Admit Card Release

Madhya Pradesh Public Service Commission (MPPSC) has officially released the e-admit cards for the Assistant Professor recruitment written examination[cite: 3]. Candidates who have registered for the recruitment drive can now access the download link available on the commission's official portal by providing their application number and date of birth/password[cite: 3].

The written examination is being conducted in accordance with the notified subject-wise schedule[cite: 3]. The next phase of the examination for notified subjects is scheduled for 02 August 2026, following earlier phase examinations[cite: 3]. Candidates are advised to download and print their hall ticket well in advance to avoid last-minute technical issues[cite: 3].

Every candidate must verify the printed details including their name, father's name, roll number, subject code, allotted exam centre venue, and reporting time[cite: 3]. Carrying the admit card along with a valid original photo identity proof is mandatory for securing entry into the examination hall[cite: 3].

How to Download MPPSC Assistant Professor Admit Card

  1. First, open the official Madhya Pradesh Public Service Commission website at mppsc.mp.gov.in[cite: 3].
  2. Go to the "Admit Card" section on the homepage or portal menu[cite: 3].
  3. Click on the link for "MPPSC Assistant Professor Admit Card 2026"[cite: 3].
  4. Enter your Application Number / Registration Number and Password / Date of Birth[cite: 3].
  5. Click on Login or Submit to view your admit card[cite: 3].
  6. Check your exam date, reporting time, and exam centre venue carefully[cite: 3].
  7. Download and take a clear printout (Colour or Black & White) for the exam day[cite: 3].

Details Mentioned on Admit Card

  • Candidate Full Name and Roll Number[cite: 3].
  • Father's / Mother's Name[cite: 3].
  • Application / Registration Number[cite: 3].
  • Exam Name and Subject Name/Code[cite: 3].
  • Exam Date and Shift Timings[cite: 3].
  • Reporting Time and Gate Closure Time[cite: 3].
  • Exam Centre Name, Address, and City[cite: 3].
  • Candidate Photograph and Signature[cite: 3].
  • Important Exam Day Instructions[cite: 3].

Documents to Carry

  • Printed copy of the MPPSC Assistant Professor Admit Card[cite: 3].
  • Valid original photo ID proof (Aadhaar Card, Voter ID, PAN Card, or Driving License)[cite: 3].
  • Passport-size photograph (if specified in the instructions)[cite: 3].
  • Ballpoint pen (Black / Blue) for marking the OMR sheet[cite: 3].

Exam Day Instructions

  • Reach the examination centre well before the reporting time printed on your admit card[cite: 3].
  • Candidates will not be permitted to enter the centre after the gate closure time[cite: 3].
  • Electronic gadgets such as mobile phones, smartwatches, and calculators are strictly prohibited[cite: 3].
  • Follow all guidelines given by the invigilators inside the examination hall[cite: 3].
  • Retain a copy of the admit card safely until the recruitment process is concluded[cite: 3].

Correction / Admit Card Discrepancy

If candidates notice any discrepancy in their personal details or face technical issues while downloading the admit card, they should contact the official MPPSC helpdesk or refer to instructions issued on the official website[cite: 3].
