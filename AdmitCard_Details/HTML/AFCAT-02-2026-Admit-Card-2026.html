About This Admit Card

Indian Air Force has issued the admit card for Air Force Common Admission Test (AFCAT 02/2026) for Flying, Ground Duty (Technical), and Ground Duty (Non-Technical) branches. Candidates who registered during the online application window can now download their e-admit card using their candidate portal credentials.

Prior to the release of hall tickets, exam city intimation details were communicated on 29 July 2026 via candidate portal logins as well as registered Email IDs and SMS alerts. Candidates are advised to review the exact venue address, reporting timing, and shift allocations printed on their e-admit card.

Carrying a hard copy of the admit card along with valid photo identity documentation is compulsory for securing entry to the examination premises. Official verification from the primary portal guarantees candidate safety and accurate information.

How to Download AFCAT 02/2026 Admit Card

  1. Visit the official AFCAT web portal at afcat.cdac.in.
  2. Navigate to the candidate login section for "AFCAT 02/2026".
  3. Enter your registered Email ID, Password, and Captcha code in the respective fields.
  4. Locate and click on the "Download Admit Card" tab available in your candidate dashboard.
  5. Thoroughly inspect all listed details, including your photo, personal information, exam centre address, and shift timing.
  6. Download the PDF and print at least two clear physical copies for examination day.

Details Mentioned on Admit Card

  • Candidate's Full Name and Registration Number.
  • Roll Number and Hall Ticket Identifier.
  • Exam Date, Shift, and Detailed Time Schedule.
  • Reporting Time and Entry Gate Closure Time.
  • Allotted Examination Centre Name and Full Address.
  • Photograph and Scanned Signature of Candidate.
  • Exam Day Instructions and Mandatory Guidelines issued by IAF.

Documents to Carry

  • Printed physical copy of AFCAT 02/2026 Admit Card (preferably color printout).
  • Original and valid Photo Identity Card (e.g., Aadhaar Card, Passport, PAN Card, Voter ID, Driving License).
  • Two recent passport-sized color photographs identical to the one uploaded in the online form.
  • Ballpoint pen for signature and rough work as per guidelines.

Exam Day Instructions

  • Arrive at the examination venue well before the specified reporting time.
  • Entry will strictly be barred post entry gate closure timings.
  • Electronic gadgets such as mobile phones, smartwatches, calculators, and Bluetooth devices are strictly banned inside the hall.
  • Carefully adhere to invigilator instructions and complete biometric verification at the venue.

Frequently Asked Questions

When was the AFCAT 02/2026 Admit Card released?

The AFCAT 02/2026 Admit Card was released on 05 August 2026 at 11:00 AM.

What is the AFCAT 02/2026 exam date?

The written examination for AFCAT 02/2026 is scheduled to be conducted on 08 August 2026.

How can I check my AFCAT 02/2026 exam city details?

Exam city details were released on 29 July 2026 and sent directly to candidates on their registered email ID and mobile number, as well as being accessible via the candidate portal.

Where can candidates download the AFCAT 02/2026 Admit Card?

Candidates can download their e-admit card from the official AFCAT portal at afcat.cdac.in.

What login details are required to download the admit card?

Candidates need their registered Email ID, Password, and Captcha code to log into the official portal.

What should I do if my AFCAT admit card is not downloading?

Verify your login credentials and ensure network stability. If issues persist, check the official portal announcements or contact AFCAT support.

Is exam city intimation the same as an admit card?

No, exam city intimation informs candidates about their allotted exam city and district, whereas the admit card contains exact venue address, roll number, and exam day rules.

What documents are required at the AFCAT exam centre?

Candidates must carry a printed copy of the AFCAT 02/2026 Admit Card, a valid original photo identity proof, passport-sized photographs, and other items specified on the hall ticket.
