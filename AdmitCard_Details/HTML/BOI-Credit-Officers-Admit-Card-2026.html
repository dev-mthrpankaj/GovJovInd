About This Admit Card Update

Bank of India (BOI) has released the admit card for the written test conducted for 779 Credit Officer posts under Project No. 2026-27/01. The recruitment drive covers positions across General Banking Officer (GBO) streams, including Credit Officer MMGS-II, Credit Officer MMGS-III, and Credit Officer SMGS-IV.

The online examination is scheduled to be held nationwide on 16 August 2026. Applicants who submitted online applications between 30 June 2026 and 20 July 2026 can now log into the designated online test portal using their registration credentials to download their call letters.

Candidates must carefully check all details printed on the call letter, including test venue, exact reporting time, roll number, and examination instructions. Bringing a clear printed copy of the e-admit card along with a valid original photo ID proof is mandatory for admission into the exam hall.

How to Download Bank of India Credit Officers Admit Card

  1. Visit the official portal of Bank of India at bankofindia.bank.in or open the direct call letter link.
  2. Navigate to the "Careers" or "Recruitment" section and select "BOI Project No 2026-27/01 Credit Officers".
  3. Click on the link for "Download Online Exam Call Letter / Admit Card".
  4. On the login page, select your preferred language (English/Hindi).
  5. Enter your Registration Number / Roll Number and Password / Date of Birth (DD-MM-YY).
  6. Enter the displayed captcha security code and click on "Login".
  7. Your Bank of India Credit Officer Admit Card 2026 will appear on the screen. Download and print a clean copy for the exam.

Details Mentioned on BOI Call Letter

  • Candidate's Full Name
  • Roll Number and Registration Number
  • Exam Date and Shift Timings
  • Reporting Time and Gate Closing Time
  • Examination Center Name and Complete Address
  • Candidate's Photograph and Digital Signature
  • Space for Left Thumb Impression and Candidate Signature
  • Category (General/OBC/EWS/SC/ST/PWD)
  • Important Instructions for the Online Examination

Documents to Carry to the Examination Center

  • Printed Call Letter: Hard copy of the downloaded e-admit card with photograph pasted in the designated space.
  • Original Photo ID Proof: Aadhar Card, PAN Card, Passport, Driving License, Voter ID, or Bank Passbook with photograph.
  • Photocopy of Photo ID: One clear photocopy of the same photo ID proof attached to the call letter.
  • Passport Size Photos: Extra passport-size photographs matching the one uploaded in the application.
  • Personal Stationery: Simple ballpoint pen as specified in the examination guidelines.

Exam Day Guidelines

  • Reach the assigned test venue well before the specified reporting time. Candidates arriving after gate closure will not be allowed inside.
  • Electronic gadgets such as mobile phones, smartwatches, calculators, Bluetooth devices, and digital bands are strictly prohibited inside the hall.
  • Ensure your name on the original photo ID exactly matches the name printed on the admit card.
  • Follow all rules regarding biometric verification, thumb impression, and seating allocation at the exam center.
  • Retain the admit card copy safely as it may be required during subsequent interview and document verification stages.
