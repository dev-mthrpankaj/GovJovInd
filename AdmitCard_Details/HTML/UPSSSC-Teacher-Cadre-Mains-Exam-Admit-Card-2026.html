About This Admit Card Notice

The Uttar Pradesh Subordinate Services Selection Commission (UPSSSC) has activated the online link to download e-admit cards for the Assistant Teacher (Special Education) and various other Teacher Cadre posts under Advertisement Number 03-Exam/2026. This recruitment drive aims to fill 58 specialized teaching vacancies within the Directorate of Empowerment of Persons with Disabilities, Uttar Pradesh.

As per the official notification and the released exam calendar, the main written examination is scheduled to take place on 12 July 2026 (Sunday). The examination will be held in a single shift in the afternoon. Candidates who were shortlisted based on their UPSSSC PET 2025 scores are eligible to appear for this main examination stage.

The e-admit card acts as the mandatory gate pass for entering the allotted exam centre. Candidates must access the web portal to check their precise centre address, personal credentials, and particular exam day standard operating procedures before proceeding to the venue.

How to Download UPSSSC Teacher Cadre Admit Card

  1. Go to the official website of the commission at upsssc.gov.in or use the direct portal link provided below.
  2. Look for the link titled "Click here to download your admit card for Advt. No. 03-Exam/2026 Main Examination" under the Live Advertisements / Examination segment.
  3. You will be redirected to the dedicated admit card page (`upsssc.gov.in/upssscadmitcard/admitCard.aspx?ID=MAIN`).
  4. Enter your 11-digit Registration Number obtained during the application process.
  5. Select your exact Date of Birth (Day, Month, Year) and choose your Gender.
  6. Input the correct alphanumeric Verification Code (Captcha) shown on the screen.
  7. Click on the 'Download Admit Card' button to fetch your document.
  8. Print a clear, high-contrast copy on an A4 sheet for presentation at the exam hall.

Documents to Carry on Exam Day

  • A clearly printed physical copy of the UPSSSC e-Admit Card.
  • Original valid Photo Identity Proof (e.g., Aadhaar Card, Passport, PAN Card, Voter ID, or Driving License).
  • One clear photocopy of the same Photo Identity Card to submit at the desk if instructed.
  • Two recent passport-size color photographs (with the candidate's name and roll number printed or written on the back).
  • Black or Blue Ballpoint pen for marking responses on the OMR answer sheets.

Exam Day Instructions

  • Candidates must reach the specified examination center well before the closing time of the main gates. No entry will be permitted post the lock-in hour.
  • Electronic items, smartphones, calculators, log tables, Bluetooth bands, smartwatches, and textual materials are completely prohibited inside the test hall.
  • Ensure that the photograph and identity details printed on the hall ticket match your physical identity documents cleanly to prevent rejection during the biometric verification process.
  • Retain the signed copy of the admit card carefully even after the test concludes, as it will be checked during the subsequent Document Verification (DV) stages.

Frequently Asked Questions

Is the UPSSSC Teacher Cadre Admit Card 2026 released?

Yes, the commission officially published the main exam admit card download links on 08 July 2026.

What is the official exam date?

The written examination will be held on 12 July 2026 (Sunday) at designated local centers.

Where will the exam take place?

The main examination is organized at multiple test venues across Lucknow city district, Uttar Pradesh.

What are the exam timings?

The written test will be conducted in a single afternoon block from 03:00 PM to 05:00 PM.

What parameters are needed to log into the admit card server?

You need your unique candidate registration number, date of birth, select your gender, and fill in the active captcha code.

You should immediately report the issues or discrepancy to the UPSSSC helpdesk or visit their main administrative building in Lucknow for corrections.

Can I present a digital format of the admit card on my mobile phone?

No, a physical printed hard copy of the hall ticket is strictly mandatory to gain entrance into the test venue.

How many total vacancies are targeted through this exam?
