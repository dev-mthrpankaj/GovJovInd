About This Admit Card Notice

The Staff Selection Commission (SSC) conducts the competitive examination for the recruitment of Head Constable (Ministerial) in Delhi Police. Candidates who submitted their online applications for the 509 ministerial vacancies are required to download their e-Admit Card to gain entrance to the examination centre.

Prior to releasing the full admit card, SSC often opens the Exam City / Status Intimation window. This allows candidates to view their assigned exam city, test date, and shift timing. The detailed hall ticket containing the complete exam centre name and address is made available for download 3 to 7 days prior to the examination date.

Candidates must ensure that all details on their hall ticket—such as candidate name, roll number, photograph, signature, and category—match their official identity documents before heading to the exam venue.

How to Download SSC Delhi Police HC Ministerial Admit Card

  1. Visit the official Staff Selection Commission website at ssc.gov.in.
  2. Click on the Admit Card section located on the homepage menu or dashboard.
  3. Select the link titled Head Constable (Ministerial) in Delhi Police Examination 2025 - Admit Card / City Status.
  4. Log in using your Registration Number / Roll Number and Password / Date of Birth.
  5. Your examination date, test city, and shift details will be displayed on the screen.
  6. Click on Download Admit Card once full hall ticket downloading becomes active.
  7. Print a clear colour or black-and-white printout of the admit card and read all exam day instructions.

Documents to Carry on Exam Day

Candidates must bring the following mandatory documents to the test center. Without these, entry into the examination hall will not be permitted:

  • Printed copy of SSC Delhi Police Head Constable Ministerial Admit Card.
  • Original valid Photo Identity Card (Aadhaar Card / Printout of e-Aadhaar, Voter ID, Driving License, Passport, PAN Card, Service ID).
  • At least two recent passport-size coloured photographs (matching the admit card photo).
  • Face mask and hand sanitizer (if required under general guidelines).
  • Category or PwBD Certificate (if applicable and specified in instructions).

Exam Day Instructions

  • Reach the examination center well before the specified Reporting Time. Latecomers will not be permitted entry after gate closure time under any circumstances.
  • Verify your exam center location a day prior to avoid last-minute delays on the exam day.
  • Electronic items such as mobile phones, smartwatches, Bluetooth devices, calculators, and study material are strictly prohibited inside the hall.
  • Do not bring metallic ornaments, purses, or bags as storage facilities at exam venues may be limited.
  • Follow the instructions provided by the invigilators and maintain proper decorum inside the computer lab.

Frequently Asked Questions

Is SSC Delhi Police Head Constable Ministerial Admit Card 2025 released?

The Staff Selection Commission releases the admit card for Head Constable Ministerial in Delhi Police Examination 2025 a few days before the scheduled exam date. Check the official SSC website ssc.gov.in for current download status.

What is the exam date for SSC Delhi Police HC Ministerial 2025?

The exact CBT exam schedule is announced officially on ssc.gov.in. Candidates should regularly check the portal for official shift schedules.

Where can I download the SSC Delhi Police HC Ministerial Admit Card?

Candidates can download their hall tickets from the official Staff Selection Commission website at ssc.gov.in.

What details are required to download the admit card?

Candidates need their Registration Number/Roll Number and Password or Date of Birth to log into the SSC portal and download the admit card.

What is the total number of vacancies in SSC Delhi Police HC Ministerial 2025?

There are 509 total vacancies (341 for Male and 168 for Female candidates) across UR, OBC, EWS, SC, and ST categories.

Is exam city intimation slip released separately before admit card?

Yes, SSC usually releases exam city intimation status prior to releasing full hall tickets so candidates can know their exam city and shift.

What documents are required to enter the exam centre?

Candidates must carry a printed copy of the Admit Card, original valid photo ID proof (Aadhaar, Voter ID, Passport, Driving License), and passport-size photographs.

What should I do if my admit card details contain an error?

If there is any error in your hall ticket details, contact the respective SSC regional office immediately through official helpdesk credentials.
