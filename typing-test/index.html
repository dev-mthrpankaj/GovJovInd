Are GovJobUpdates typing tests free?

Yes. Practice tests are free with no hidden fee.

Can I practice both Hindi and English?

Yes — dedicated Hindi and English tests, plus exam pages where both apply.

Which exams are covered?

SSC CHSL, SSC CGL DEST, Delhi Police HC Ministerial, RRB NTPC, UPSSSC JA, UP Police Computer Operator, Railway clerk typing and more.

How is speed measured?

As WPM, with accuracy and errors so you can judge speed and correctness together.

WPM, accuracy, typed words, correct characters, errors and target status.
