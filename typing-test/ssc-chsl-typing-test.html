Evaluation guide

How SSC CHSL typing is evaluated

Use this as a study checklist. Exact software, passage, and evaluation on exam day follow SSC's notified process only.

Practice rules: CHSL LDC/JSA: English 35 WPM or Hindi 30 WPM · 10 minutes (15 for eligible compensatory time) · about 7% mistakes (UR) / 10% (reserved) — SSC mistake percentages, not this site's character accuracy · official qualification is not assessed here.

1

Speed (WPM)

Common practice view: key depressions ÷ 5 = gross words; after mistake deductions you get net WPM. Targets: 35 English / 30 Hindi over the full session.

2

Full mistakes

Wrong, missing, or extra words (and similar substitution errors) are treated as full mistakes in SSC-style discussions - each counts more heavily toward deductions.

3

Half mistakes

Spacing, capitalisation (English), and punctuation-type issues are often discussed as half mistakes (0.5 unit). This site's counts are a practice approximation.

4

Mistake %

Practice bands inspired by result notices: roughly =7% UR and =10% reserved. Always read the latest official notice for your cycle.

What this site shows

After each attempt you get practice Net WPM, character accuracy, and estimated full/half mistakes for the typed portion of the passage. Untyped remaining text is not counted as omission. This is not an official SSC qualification.

About

About this SSC CHSL typing practice

This page is for students preparing for the SSC CHSL skill test (LDC/JSA style). You get timed Hindi and English passages, live WPM and accuracy, and after submit a practice result with estimated full/half mistakes and mistake % bands.

Before you start

  • Use a physical keyboard (not only on-screen).
  • English: aim near 35 WPM with high accuracy first.
  • Hindi: set Mangal / exam-style layout; phonetic is only for casual practice.
  • Finish the full 10 minutes when you want a serious speed check.

FAQs

SSC CHSL typing FAQs

What is the SSC CHSL typing speed requirement?

Practice targets for LDC/JSA style: English 35 WPM or Hindi 30 WPM in 10 minutes (15 for eligible compensatory-time candidates). Confirm every detail in the latest SSC notice.

What mistake percentage is allowed?

Practice bands often cited from result notices: about 7% for UR and 10% for listed reserved categories. These are SSC-style mistake percentages, not plain character accuracy on this site.

How are full and half mistakes counted here?

We estimate them by comparing your typed text to the passage prefix for practice feedback only. This is not official SSC software evaluation.

Is this an official exam?

No. It is free student practice. Qualification is only through SSC's official process.

Is it free?

Yes. No charge. Login is optional for ranked attempts only.
