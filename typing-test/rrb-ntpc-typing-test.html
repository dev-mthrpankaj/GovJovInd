Evaluation guide

How RRB NTPC CBTST is evaluated

Study checklist from CEN 06/2024 NTPC UG CBTST (6 Feb 2026). Exam-day software and scoring follow the official notice only.

Practice rules: 10 evaluated minutes · English 30 / Hindi 25 WPM · min ~300 / 250 words · mistakes = full + half/2, minus 5% word allowance, then −10 words each · editing off · passages may repeat · official speed is not auto-assessed here.

1

Speed targets

Practice benchmarks: English 30 WPM or Hindi 25 WPM over a full 10-minute session, with minimum word counts of about 300 / 250.

2

Mistake formula

Reviewed: total mistakes = full + half/2. A 5% word allowance is subtracted; each remaining mistake costs 10 words from speed.

3

Editing rules

In CBTST-style practice, editing is disabled and finishing a passage can start another round of the same text without resetting the timer.

4

Self-review

The notice does not fully define automatic full/half classification. Use the optional result calculator with your reviewed counts.

What this site shows

Live character errors never become official RRB mistakes. Use reviewed counts for the practice calculator. This is not an official CBTST qualification. Verify your own CEN notice.

About

About this RRB NTPC typing practice

This page supports RRB NTPC CBTST-style preparation with timed Hindi and English passages, disabled editing in the attempt workspace, and an optional self-review calculator for full/half mistakes.

Before you start

  • Use a physical keyboard for serious practice.
  • English: aim near 30 WPM with enough words for the full 10 minutes.
  • Hindi: Unicode practice; set the layout your RRB notice requires in your system/IME.
  • For other CENs, always verify your own notice.
How it works

How to use this typing test

1. Select languageChoose Hindi or English when both options are available.
2. Pick a passageSelect Easy, Medium or Hard practice according to your level.
3. Start typingLogin is optional for practice. The timer starts automatically when you type the first character.
4. Check resultSubmit to review WPM, accuracy, typed words, errors and target status.
FAQs

RRB NTPC typing FAQs

What is the RRB NTPC typing speed requirement?

Practice targets from the retrieved CEN 06/2024 UG CBTST notice: English 30 WPM or Hindi 25 WPM in 10 evaluated minutes, with minimum about 300 / 250 words. Confirm your own CEN notice.

How are full and half mistakes used here?

Official classification is not fully defined in that notice. After an attempt, enter reviewed full and half mistake counts in the optional calculator for a practice speed estimate.

Can I edit while typing?

CBTST-style practice disables editing. Completing a passage may start another round of the same text without resetting the timer.

Is this an official exam?

No. It is free student practice. Qualification is only through RRB's notified process.

Is it free?

Yes. No charge. Login is optional for ranked attempts only.
