Evaluation guide

How Delhi Police HCM typing is evaluated (practice guide)

Use this as a study checklist. Exact software, passage and evaluation on exam day follow the official notification only.

1

Speed targets

Practice targets: English 30 WPM or Hindi 25 WPM in 10 minutes. Supplied matter is about 400 English / 350 Hindi words on the official side.

2

Tentative WPM

Notice-style arithmetic: tentative WPM = (reviewed strokes ÷ 5) ÷ 10. Final speed subtracts reviewed mistakes from that tentative value.

3

Marks slabs

Published slabs award marks from 0 to 25 based on final English/Hindi WPM bands. Fractional gaps that are unspecified return no marks assessment here.

4

Self-review

Automatic official mistake classification and Hindi stroke mapping are not verified. Enter reviewed counts in the optional calculator.

About

About this Delhi Police HCM typing practice

This page is for students preparing for the Delhi Police Head Constable Ministerial typing skill test. You get timed Hindi and English passages plus an optional self-review calculator that follows the notice’s unusual speed and marks arithmetic for practice.

Before you start

  • Use a physical keyboard.
  • English: aim near 30 WPM; Hindi: near 25 WPM.
  • Finish the full 10 minutes before trusting marks-style feedback.
  • Confirm the latest SSC Delhi Police HCM notice for your cycle.
How it works

How to use this typing test

1. Select languageChoose Hindi or English when both options are available.
2. Pick a passageSelect Easy, Medium or Hard practice according to your level.
3. Start typingLogin is optional for practice. The timer starts automatically when you type the first character.
4. Check resultSubmit to review WPM, accuracy, typed words, errors and target status.
FAQs

Delhi Police HCM typing FAQs

What is the Delhi Police HCM typing speed?

Practice targets from the HCM 2025 notice: English 30 WPM or Hindi 25 WPM in 10 minutes. Confirm the latest official notice.

How are marks calculated here?

After a full-duration attempt, the optional calculator uses reviewed strokes and mistakes to estimate notice-style speed and marks slabs (0–25). It is practice feedback only.

Why is the speed formula unusual?

The official example subtracts the mistake count from tentative WPM (strokes÷5÷10). That is not the same as RRB’s full/half allowance formula.

Is this an official exam?

No. It is free student practice. Qualification is only through SSC’s notified Delhi Police process.

Is it free?

Yes. No charge. Login is optional for ranked attempts only.
