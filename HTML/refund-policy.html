Refund Policy for UP Services

Last updated: 19 May 2026

UP Services on GovJobUpdates are online application assistance services only. The service fee is an assistance charge and does not guarantee certificate approval, rejection removal or faster government processing.

Cancellation Before Work Starts

If you request cancellation before document review, payment verification or application assistance begins, refund eligibility may be considered after checking the payment record.

After Assistance Starts

Once we begin document checking, payment verification, form preparation, application submission or support follow-up, the assistance fee is generally non-refundable.

Duplicate or Failed Payment

If a duplicate payment or clear payment failure is confirmed, contact us with UTR, payment screenshot, mobile number and request date for review.

Government Decision

No refund is provided only because the concerned government department raises an objection, rejects the application, delays processing or asks for additional documents.

For refund review, contact us through Contact with payment UTR, screenshot, applicant name and request date.
