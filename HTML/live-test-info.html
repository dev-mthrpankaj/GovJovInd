From registration to prize in 8 steps

Follow this order and you will never be stuck at the last minute.

  1. Register

    Open the portal during the registration window. Fill name, mobile, email, city and state. Duplicate registration is blocked by mobile and email per test.

  2. Pay or use a promo

    Pay the entry fee, or apply a valid promo code or free pass. Your seat is confirmed only after successful payment or valid free registration.

  3. Save your ID

    You get a Registration ID. Keep it safe, because you need it (or your registered mobile) to log in on test day.

  4. Login early

    Verification opens 10 minutes before the start time. Read the instructions and tick the confirmation box.

  5. Test starts

    The exam screen opens only when the official server time reaches the start time. The timer never pauses.

  6. Auto-submit

    When the full-paper timer, or the final section's time, ends, your test is submitted automatically.

  7. Result & rank

    Results are published by admin after verification. Your detailed report can be downloaded after the result is published.

  8. Prize payout

    Eligible Top 3 winners are contacted for payout details and paid after verification.

Test day: do this, avoid that

Most disqualifications happen because of simple mistakes. This is your checklist.

Do this

  • Login as soon as verification opens, 10 minutes before the start.
  • Use a stable internet connection and a charged device.
  • Switch off auto-sleep / auto-lock so your screen stays active.
  • Read the section list, timer mode and marking on the instruction screen.
  • Keep your Registration ID and registered mobile ready.

Avoid this

  • Switching tabs, apps or windows after the test starts.
  • Pressing the browser back button or refreshing again and again.
  • Trying to copy text or using right-click on the exam screen.
  • Sharing your Registration ID or letting someone else sit in on your attempt.
  • Waiting for the last minute. Late login is not extended, and the timer does not pause.

Test pattern: the number of sections, questions, total duration, timer mode (full paper or sectional), marks per correct answer and negative marking are configured per test by the admin and always shown on the instruction screen before you begin. Hindi and English instructions are both provided.

One attempt, one device

The first browser that opens your attempt is locked to it. This stops anyone else from answering your paper, even if your Registration ID leaks.

  • To continue on a different device, verify with a 6-digit code sent to your registered email.
  • The code is valid for 10 minutes and allows 5 tries.
  • A maximum of 3 device changes are allowed per attempt.
  • Use an email address you can open on test day.

In any cheating or dispute case, the final decision rests with GovJobUpdates admin.

Payment, promo codes, refunds and common questions

Tap a question to open the answer.

How do promo codes and free passes work?

Promo codes can be public, limited-use, test-specific or time-limited, and may reduce your fee or make registration free. Some tests offer a free pass to the first confirmed registrations. Each code has an expiry, a total usage limit and a per-user limit tied to your mobile number.

A promo never overrides the registration window, the seat limit or the fair-play rules.

Does my seat get confirmed if payment is slow?

Only successful payments and valid free registrations confirm a seat. Failed, pending or cancelled payments do not. If you started payment inside the registration window, it can still be confirmed up to 30 minutes after the window closes, as long as seats are available and the test is not yet closed.

I paid but I am not registered. What now?

This is usually fixed automatically, with either your registration completed or an automatic refund started. If nothing changes, contact support with your Registration ID or mobile number, payment ID and payment screenshot.

Refunds follow the GovJobUpdates Refund Policy.

Can I register twice or use a friend's mobile number?

Duplicate registration is blocked by mobile number and email for each test. Use your own valid 10-digit Indian mobile number, because it is also used for verification and result lookup.

What if I miss the login time or get disconnected?

The test starts at the official server time for everyone, and the timer does not pause. If you log in late, you get only the time remaining. If you are disconnected, log in again and continue on the same device, or verify by email code to switch.

When can I see my result and report?

Results appear on the leaderboard after admin publishes them. Your complete report can be downloaded only after result publication.

Which rules apply if a test has its own notice?

These rules apply to all GovJobUpdates Live Tests unless a test-specific notice or admin setting says otherwise. The test card in the portal always shows the current fee, window, seats and pattern.
