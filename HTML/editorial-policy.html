Editorial Policy

Last updated: 19 May 2026

GovJobUpdates publishes government exam information, preparation resources and browser-based tools for user convenience. We are not a government website and are not affiliated with any government department, recruitment board or official authority.

Source Review

We try to organize information from official notifications, official websites and public recruitment updates. Users must verify all dates, fees, eligibility, vacancies and instructions from the official source before taking action.

Content Scope

Our pages may include job updates, admit cards, results, answer keys, quizzes, rank predictor notes, document tools and UP Services assistance information. Job detail pages will be handled separately when published.

No False Guarantee

Rank Predictor results are approximate and unofficial. UP Services assistance does not guarantee certificate approval. Official departments and recruitment boards remain the final authority.

Corrections

If a page has an error, contact us with the page URL, official source link and exact correction needed. We review genuine correction requests as soon as possible.

For corrections, use the Correction Policy or Contact page.
