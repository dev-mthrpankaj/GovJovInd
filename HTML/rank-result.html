    How is this estimated?
    • Based on submitted data. Ranks and percentile compare you only with candidates who submitted their marks on GovJobUpdates, not with everyone who appeared.
    • Raw vs normalised. For exams held in several shifts, normalised marks adjust for difficulty differences between shifts using the equipercentile method (percentile in your shift, matched to the same percentile in every shift, then averaged).
    • Confidence. With fewer than 100 submissions the estimate is Low, 100 to 999 Medium, 1,000 or more High. Check again later: your result updates as more candidates submit.
    • Not official. Final answer keys, normalisation, category rules and attendance can change the official result. Always verify with the official notice.
