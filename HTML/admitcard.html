Exam Day Guide

How to download and verify an admit card

Open the official download link for your examination, enter the requested registration credentials, and save a clear printout after checking every detail.

Verify printed details

Confirm your name, photograph, signature, roll number, exam centre, date, shift and reporting instructions.

Carry required ID

Take the printed admit card and the original photo ID allowed in the official instructions.

If not available

Recheck the release notice and login details, then contact the official helpdesk if a valid card remains unavailable.

Admit Card FAQs

When is an admit card normally released?

Release dates vary by authority. Refer to the official exam calendar or admit card notice for the confirmed schedule.

What details should I check after downloading it?

Check candidate information, examination name, roll number, centre address, date, shift, reporting time and ID instructions.

Which photo ID should I carry?

Carry only the ID types permitted in the official instructions, such as Aadhaar, voter ID, passport or driving licence where allowed.

What if my admit card has an error?

Contact the issuing authority through its official helpdesk as early as possible and follow its correction instructions.
