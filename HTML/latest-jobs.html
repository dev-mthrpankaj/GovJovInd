Latest Jobs FAQs

Where should I confirm the final vacancy details?

Use the official recruitment notification and authority website for vacancy count, eligibility, fee and date corrections.

What should I check before applying online?

Check qualification, age as on the cutoff date, category certificate requirements, fee, photo/signature format and closing date.

Can an application date change after publication?

Yes. Recruiting authorities can publish corrigenda or revised schedules, so recheck the official site before the deadline.

Does a listing guarantee selection or eligibility?

No. Eligibility scrutiny, examinations and selection decisions are made only by the recruiting authority.
