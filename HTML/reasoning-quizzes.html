Plan your revision

A focused guide to Reasoning practice

Use these areas to plan your revision. Quiz availability is shown in the practice section above; this guide is not a list of currently published sets.

Verbal reasoning

Revise analogy, classification, series, coding-decoding, blood relations and direction sense.

Analytical reasoning

Use clear diagrams for seating arrangements and puzzles. Check each condition before eliminating a possibility.

Logical and non-verbal reasoning

Practise syllogisms and conclusions alongside figure patterns, mirror images and spatial relationships.

Make your next attempt count

Draw a small diagram or list the conditions. Review where your assumption changed the result, rather than memorising the answer.

Preparing for a specific exam? Use the SSC, Banking, Railway or Police practice pages for exam-based sets. Match your revision topics to your target exam’s syllabus.

Before you start

Are these Reasoning quizzes free?

The quizzes listed here are free to practise. You can browse the page without signing in; an account is required when you attempt a quiz.

How should I choose a topic?

Start with a topic you have revised recently. Use the topic filter to see its published sets, then review mistakes before trying another set.

Why can I not see a quiz for every topic?

The practice filters show topics with published quizzes. If a topic has no published set yet, it will not appear in the filter. You can explore exam-based practice while more sets are added.
