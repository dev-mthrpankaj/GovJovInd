Correction Policy

Last updated: 19 May 2026

We want GovJobUpdates pages to stay clear and useful. If you find an error, please send a correction request with enough detail for review.

What to Send

Include the page URL, exam or service name, the exact incorrect line, the correct information and the official source link or notification reference.

What We Review

We review factual corrections for dates, fees, eligibility, links, answer key notes, result information, tool text, policies and UP Services assistance content.

Review Timing

Correction requests are reviewed based on clarity, urgency and source availability. Time-sensitive official updates are prioritized when possible.

Final Authority

Official recruitment boards, departments and official websites remain the final authority. GovJobUpdates is an independent platform and not a government website.

Send correction requests through the Contact page or email dmagstudio2023@outlook.com.
