UP Certificate Services FAQs

Is GovJobUpdates a government certificate portal?

No. GovJobUpdates provides application assistance only. Certificate approval, rejection and verification are handled by the concerned government department.

What is the UP certificate assistance fee?

The assistance fee shown on this page is Rs. 110 per selected certificate. Payment is collected securely online; the fee covers form filling, document checking and request handling and does not guarantee approval.

Which documents are usually needed?

Aadhaar card, Sabhasad or Pradhan letter pad, passport size photo, Aadhaar-linked active mobile number and email ID are requested on this page. No payment screenshot or UTR entry is required.

Where should I check certificate status?

Certificate status should be checked on the official UP eDistrict portal or through the concerned official channel.
