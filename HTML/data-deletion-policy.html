Data Deletion Policy

Last updated: 19 May 2026

This policy explains how users can request deletion of personal information submitted to GovJobUpdates. Browser-only data can also be cleared directly from your device.

Rank Predictor Data

To request deletion, send your roll number, mobile number, exam name and a clear deletion request. We may need this information to identify the correct submitted record.

UP Services Documents

Uploaded documents are used for certificate application assistance, payment verification and support follow-up. You may request deletion after your request is completed or cancelled, subject to fraud prevention, payment and legal record needs.

Quiz and Browser Data

Quiz attempts and some preferences may be stored in your browser storage. You can delete this by clearing site data, localStorage or browser history for GovJobUpdates.

Contact Requests

Contact form messages may be retained for support, correction tracking and abuse prevention. You can request deletion by providing the submitted contact detail and request subject.

Deletion requests can be sent through Contact or email dmagstudio2023@outlook.com.
