UP Super TET Syllabus 2026: Revised Exam Pattern, Subject-Wise Topics & Marking Scheme

The UP Super TET Syllabus 2026 for Assistant Teacher recruitment is different for Primary (Classes 1–5) and Upper Primary (Classes 6–8). This guide explains the revised 120-question pattern, 360-mark scheme, negative marking and the complete subject-wise syllabus in a preparation-friendly format.

Key Takeaways

  • Revised Paper: Both Primary and Upper Primary papers have 120 MCQs to be completed in 120 minutes.
  • Marking: Each correct answer carries 3 marks and 1 mark is deducted for an incorrect answer, making the maximum score 360.
  • Primary Pattern: The 120 questions are distributed across ten areas including GK, Languages, Maths, Teaching Skills and Child Psychology.
  • Upper Primary Pattern: 30 GK questions are compulsory and the remaining 90 questions come from the selected teaching subject.

1. UP Super TET Exam Pattern 2026 at a Glance

The revised UP Assistant Teacher recruitment pattern covered in the released syllabus material uses a common basic structure for both teaching levels. Candidates get 120 objective-type questions, each carrying 3 marks, and must complete the paper in 120 minutes.

The most important change for preparation is the marking rule: an incorrect answer costs 1 mark. This makes accuracy more important than blind attempts. The paper is presented in Hindi and English, while language-subject content naturally follows the selected language.

Do not confuse Super TET with UPTET/CTET

UPTET and CTET are teacher-eligibility examinations. Super TET/Assistant Teacher recruitment is part of the recruitment and merit process. Eligibility conditions should always be checked separately from the relevant recruitment notification because the syllabus alone does not establish every qualification rule.

2. UP Super TET Syllabus 2026 for Primary Teacher (Classes 1–5)

The Primary Assistant Teacher paper is broad rather than subject-specialised. It tests general awareness, reasoning, languages, Science, Mathematics, EVS/Social Studies and four teaching-oriented areas. This means preparation has to be balanced: ignoring a smaller teaching or IT section can still cost valuable marks.

4. UP Super TET Syllabus 2026 for Upper Primary Teacher (Classes 6–8)

The Upper Primary pattern is more specialised. The paper is split into two parts: a compulsory 30-question General Knowledge section and a 90-question section based on the candidate's selected teaching subject.

For the 90-question specialised section, the relevant options are:

  • Language Teacher: Hindi, English or Sanskrit — one language.
  • Social Studies Teacher: Social Studies.
  • Science & Mathematics Teacher: Science and Mathematics.

9. Primary vs Upper Primary Super TET: What Changes?

The total questions, duration and marking scheme are the same, but the preparation strategy is not. Primary candidates need breadth across ten areas. Upper Primary candidates need strong command over one specialised subject because that section alone contributes 90 of the 120 questions.

10. UP Super TET 2026 Marking Scheme and Attempt Strategy

Each correct answer adds 3 marks and each incorrect answer deducts 1 mark. An unattempted question does not add marks, so the decision to attempt a doubtful question should be based on elimination rather than guesswork.

With 120 questions in 120 minutes, the average available time is approximately one minute per question. Candidates should keep the first pass for direct questions, mark lengthy or uncertain questions for review, and use the remaining time for calculated second-pass attempts.

11. How to Prepare for UP Super TET 2026 Using This Syllabus

Start by deciding the exact post level you are targeting. Then turn each syllabus row into a checklist instead of studying from random topic lists. A syllabus-driven plan prevents over-investing time in familiar subjects while leaving smaller scored areas untouched.

  1. Map the paper first: Memorise your section-wise question distribution so your study hours reflect actual weightage.
  2. Build a syllabus tracker: Mark each topic as not started, learning, revision due or test-ready.
  3. Practise with negative marking: Score every mock with +3/-1 so your attempt strategy develops under the correct risk-reward rule.
  4. Prioritise high-weight areas: Primary candidates should pay special attention to Languages, GK and Mathematics; Upper Primary candidates should make the 90-question selected subject the core of their schedule.
  5. Keep UP-specific GK active: Uttar Pradesh events and geography appear in the syllabus, so maintain short state-focused revision notes.
  6. Revise in layers: First finish concepts, then topic-wise MCQs, then mixed sets and finally full 120-question timed papers.

12. Important Note Before You Start Preparation

The supplied source page contained an older/contradictory FAQ block that mentioned 2.5 hours and no negative marking. Those FAQ statements do not match the revised exam-pattern section, which gives 120 minutes and a 1-mark penalty for each incorrect answer. This article follows the revised 120-question, 360-mark, 120-minute, +3/-1 pattern because that is the consistent latest pattern found across the detailed syllabus material and current cross-checks.

Recruitment dates, vacancy numbers and detailed eligibility have deliberately not been added here because they are not reliably established by the syllabus material itself. Always check the latest recruitment notice on the Commission website before applying.

Frequently Asked Questions (FAQs)

How many questions are there in the UP Super TET 2026 exam?

The revised pattern has 120 multiple-choice questions for both Primary and Upper Primary Assistant Teacher papers.

What is the total mark and duration of UP Super TET 2026?

The paper carries a maximum of 360 marks and the duration is 120 minutes. Each question carries 3 marks.

Is there negative marking in UP Super TET 2026?

Yes. Under the revised pattern used in this guide, 1 mark is deducted for every incorrect answer.

What is the Upper Primary Super TET exam pattern?

It has 30 compulsory General Knowledge questions followed by 90 questions from the selected teaching subject: Language, Social Studies, or Science and Mathematics.

Which subjects are included in the Primary Super TET syllabus?

The Primary paper covers GK and Current Affairs, Logical Reasoning, Hindi/Sanskrit/English, Science, Mathematics, Environmental and Social Studies, Teaching Skills, Child Psychology, Information Technology, and Life Skills, Management and Attitude.

Are Primary and Upper Primary Super TET syllabi the same?

No. Primary uses a broad ten-area distribution, whereas Upper Primary has a 30-question compulsory GK section plus a 90-question specialised subject section.
