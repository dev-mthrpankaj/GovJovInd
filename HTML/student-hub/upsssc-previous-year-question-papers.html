UPSSSC PET Exam Pattern and Eligibility

PET is not a recruitment exam on its own. It is a screening test, and a valid PET score is what lets you apply for many Group C posts in Uttar Pradesh, such as Junior Assistant, VDO and Lekhpal. Since so many recruitments depend on it, we have started this collection with PET papers.

  • Mode: Written exam on an OMR sheet.
  • Questions and marks: 100 multiple-choice questions, 1 mark each.
  • Time: 2 hours (120 minutes).
  • Negative marking: 0.25 mark deducted for each wrong answer.
  • Syllabus: 15 subjects in a single paper.
  • Minimum qualification: Class 10 pass from a recognised board. Age limits and other conditions are given in each year's notification.
  • Score validity: 3 years from the result date for PET 2025 onwards. It was 1 year earlier.

One thing many candidates miss: UPSSSC does not fix a single qualifying mark for PET. Cut-offs are decided category-wise for each recruitment that uses the PET score. So the goal is not just to pass, but to score as high as you can.

After PET, many posts also have a separate post-specific stage with its own paper. We plan to list those papers as separate rows instead of mixing them with PET.

Why Solve UPSSSC Previous Year Question Papers?

A syllabus tells you the topics. An old paper shows you how those topics turn into questions, and that gap is where many candidates lose marks.

  • See the real question style: You notice how many questions are straight facts, how many need a little thinking, and how the options are framed.
  • Feel the time pressure: 100 questions in 120 minutes leaves you about 72 seconds per question. You only understand how tight that is after sitting through a full paper.
  • Learn what to skip: With 0.25 negative marking, four wrong answers wipe out one correct answer. Old papers show you when a guess is worth it and when it is not.
  • Spot repeating areas: Put two or three years side by side and the topics that keep coming back become obvious. Do not expect the same question word for word, but the same concepts do return.
  • Find your weak subjects: A mistake in a real paper shows exactly which chapter needs another round of revision.

How to Solve UPSSSC PYQs for Best Results

Downloading ten papers and never opening them does nothing for your score. This routine works better.

  1. Solve in one sitting: Keep a 2-hour timer, put the phone away and do not look anything up.
  2. Mark your doubts: Tick the questions where you were only guessing, so you can separate lucky answers from known ones later.
  3. Check and sort your mistakes: Put each wrong answer in one of three groups: did not know, knew but slipped, or guessed wrong.
  4. Calculate the real score: Subtract 0.25 for every wrong answer, just as the exam will.
  5. Keep a mistake notebook: Write the concept behind each error in one line. Reading this list before the exam is worth more than reading a new chapter.
  6. Move to the next year: After three or four papers, compare your scores and topics to see what is really improving.

If you have around four weeks, here is a simple way to split the time:

  • Week 1: Revise your weak subjects and attempt the oldest paper as a check of where you stand.
  • Weeks 2 and 3: One full paper every two days, with the next day spent on reviewing mistakes.
  • Week 4: Attempt the latest papers, focus on timing, and go through your mistake notebook daily.

How to Manage 100 Questions in 2 Hours

Knowing the answers is only half the job. PET rewards candidates who also handle time and negative marking well.

  • Go through the paper twice: In the first round, answer everything you are sure about. In the second round, return to the doubtful questions.
  • Do not get stuck: If a question is eating your time, mark it and move on. It will still be there in round two.
  • Guess with a reason: With four options, a blind guess gains only about 0.06 mark on average. If you can eliminate two options, that rises to about 0.375. Guess only when you can cut down the options.
  • Leave time for the OMR sheet: Keep the last 10 minutes to check that every answer you meant to mark is actually bubbled.

Practise this exact routine on old papers. It feels awkward the first time and natural by the third paper.

Common Mistakes Candidates Make with PYQs

  • Only reading, never solving: Going through the questions with the answers marked feels productive, but it does not train speed or accuracy.
  • Memorising answers: Learn the concept behind a question instead. The exact question rarely returns in the same form.
  • Ignoring negative marking: Counting only correct answers gives a score that is higher than the real one.
  • Solving too many papers without review: One paper checked properly teaches more than three papers solved and forgotten.
  • Trusting unverified answer keys: If an answer looks wrong, cross-check it with a standard book or the official key.

Frequently Asked Questions (FAQs)

Where can I download UPSSSC previous year question papers?

Use the table at the top of this page. Papers are listed year-wise, starting with UPSSSC PET 2021, 2022, 2023 and 2025. Click Download in the row of the paper you need.

How many times has UPSSSC PET been conducted?

UPSSSC PET was held in 2021, 2022, 2023 and 2025. No PET was conducted in 2024.

Is UPSSSC PET a recruitment exam?

No. PET is a screening test. A valid PET score makes you eligible to apply for many UPSSSC Group C posts, but final selection depends on the process of the specific recruitment.

Is there negative marking in UPSSSC PET?

Yes. PET has 100 multiple-choice questions for 100 marks in 2 hours, and 0.25 mark is deducted for every wrong answer.

How long is the UPSSSC PET score valid?

From PET 2025 onwards the score is valid for 3 years from the date the result is declared. Earlier it was valid for 1 year. Always confirm the validity in the notification of the recruitment you are applying for.

How many previous year papers should I solve?

There is no fixed number. Try to solve every available year at least once as a full paper, and then re-attempt the latest ones after revising your weak subjects.

Will VDO, Junior Assistant and Lekhpal papers be added?

Yes. Papers of other UPSSSC recruitment exams will be added to the same table as we get clean, readable copies, so the page URL will not change.
