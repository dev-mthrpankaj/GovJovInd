SSC CGL Typing Test Rules Explained: Speed, Accuracy, Errors, and Submission Process

Clearing the SSC CGL Typing Test, officially named the Data Entry Speed Test (DEST), is mandatory for final selection in Tier-2. Even though DEST carries no written marks, failing to meet the accuracy guidelines leads to complete disqualification. This complete guide breaks down speed requirements, category-wise error limits, full vs. half mistake rules, and final submission steps.

Key Takeaways

  • The SSC CGL Typing Test (DEST) requires candidates to type approximately 2000 key depressions in 15 minutes on a computer.
  • DEST is qualifying in nature, but failing it leads to disqualification regardless of Tier-2 written examination scores.
  • Category-wise maximum permissible error percentage limits are 20% for UR, 25% for OBC/EWS, and 30% for SC/ST/Others.
  • Typing errors are categorized into Full Mistakes (1 full error) and Half Mistakes (0.5 error), with auto-submission after 15 minutes.

1. What Is SSC CGL Typing Test (DEST)?

The SSC CGL Typing Test, officially called the Data Entry Speed Test (DEST), is a mandatory qualifying exam conducted in Tier-2 (Paper-I, Module-II of Section-III) to evaluate a candidate's computer typing speed and accuracy. It requires typing a given passage of approximately 2000 key depressions within a fixed duration of 15 minutes.

The examination is conducted strictly according to rules prescribed by the Staff Selection Commission (SSC). Although no marks are added to the final merit list, clearing the DEST is compulsory for selection.

SSC CGL Typing Test Overview

  • Test Name: Data Entry Speed Test (DEST)
  • Conducting Authority: SSC CGL (Tier-2, Paper-I, Module-II)
  • Nature of Test: Qualifying (No Marks)
  • Duration: 15 Minutes
  • Key Depressions: Approximately 2000
  • Mode of Examination: Computer Based

Basic Rules for DEST Candidates

  • The DEST is strictly qualifying in nature.
  • Candidates must use only the computer system provided by SSC.
  • The passage consists of approximately 2000 key depressions.
  • Total time allotted is 15 minutes.
  • The passage is of moderate difficulty, usually based on general topics such as current affairs, general awareness, or day-to-day situations.

2. Typing Speed and Category-Wise Accuracy Rules

To pass the SSC CGL Typing Test, candidates must maintain a minimum speed of approximately 2000 key depressions per 15 minutes while staying below the permissible error percentage for their category. Exceeding the maximum allowed error percentage results in immediate disqualification, even if the entire passage is completed on time.

Errors committed during typing are calculated and converted into percentages based on standard formula guidelines issued by SSC.

SSC CGL DEST Minimum Qualifying Standards

  • UR (Unreserved): Maximum Error Percentage Allowed is 20%
  • OBC / EWS: Maximum Error Percentage Allowed is 25%
  • SC / ST / Others: Maximum Error Percentage Allowed is 30%

Slow typing due to lack of practice, keyboard unfamiliarity, or frequent backspacing often leads to an incomplete passage, which automatically increases the error percentage. Candidates should maintain a steady rhythm throughout the 15-minute period.

3. Full Mistakes and Half Mistakes Rules Explained

SSC CGL typing errors are classified into Full Mistakes, which add 1 full error penalty, and Half Mistakes, which add 0.5 error penalty to the total error percentage. Understanding the exact classification helps candidates avoid common formatting and punctuation pitfalls during the exam.

Full Mistakes (Counted as 1 Full Error)

The following are treated as full mistakes:

  • Omission: Omission of any word, number, or figure from the master passage.
  • Substitution: Substitution of a wrong word or figure (except word transposition).
  • Addition: Addition of any extra word or figure not present in the master passage.
  • Spelling Mistakes: Spelling errors caused by repetition of letters, addition of letters, omission of letters, or substitution of letters (e.g., typing "speling", "seplling", or "seepling" instead of "spelling").
  • Word Repetition: Repetition of a word or number (e.g., typing "I shall shall be grateful").
  • Incomplete Words: Incomplete or half-typed words left due to any reason.

Half Mistakes (Counted as 0.5 Error)

The following are treated as half mistakes:

  • Spacing Errors: No space between two words (e.g., "lhope") or extra/unwanted space within a word (e.g., "I h ave").
  • Wrong Capitalisation: Capital letter used instead of small letter or vice-versa (not applicable for Hindi typing).
  • Punctuation Errors: Missing punctuation mark, extra punctuation mark, or wrong punctuation mark.
  • Transposition Errors: Interchanging word positions (e.g., typing "hope I" instead of "I hope").
  • Paragraph Formatting Errors: Manual spacing used instead of the Tab Key at the beginning of a paragraph. Each such paragraph is counted as one half mistake.

4. Medium, Language, and Paragraph Formatting Rules

Candidates must strictly type in the language medium chosen during application (English or Hindi) and use the Tab Key to format paragraph indents. Using manual spacing instead of the Tab Key at the start of a paragraph is penalized as a half mistake per paragraph.

Medium and Language Rules

  • English Medium: Only English typing is allowed.
  • Hindi Medium: Only Hindi typing is allowed.
  • Typing in any other language or script is treated as an error and leads to disqualification.
  • Words, numbers, figures, and years must be typed exactly as given in the provided passage.

Paragraph Formatting Rules

  • The Tab Key must be used to start a new paragraph.
  • Using manual spacebar presses instead of the Tab Key results in a half mistake per paragraph.
  • Only one space should be given after punctuation marks.
  • The overall formatting style of the given passage must be followed strictly.

5. Revision, Correction, and Final Submission Process

The SSC CGL Typing Test ends automatically after 15 minutes without requiring any manual submission, saving all typed content in real time. Candidates are not required to retype the passage after finishing and should use remaining time solely to revise and correct visible spelling, spacing, and punctuation errors.

Revision and Correction Strategy

  • Candidates are not required to retype the passage after completing it once.
  • If time remains after finishing the text, candidates can revise and correct visible errors.
  • Unnecessary retyping should be avoided, as typing past the passage length creates new addition errors.
  • Focus revision specifically on correcting spelling, spacing, and punctuation errors.

Final Submission Process

  • The test automatically closes and submits after the 15-minute timer expires.
  • No manual click on a submit button is required.
  • Focus on completing the passage properly once and spending leftover seconds on calm revision without panicking.

Frequently Asked Questions (FAQs)

Is SSC CGL Typing Test mandatory for all posts?

Yes, clearing the Data Entry Speed Test (DEST) is mandatory for final selection in SSC CGL Tier-2, Paper-I, Module-II of Section-III. Although it is qualifying in nature and carries no marks, failure to clear DEST leads to disqualification regardless of written scores.

What is the maximum allowed error percentage in SSC CGL Typing Test?

The maximum permissible error percentage in SSC CGL DEST is 20% for Unreserved (UR) candidates, 25% for OBC/EWS candidates, and 30% for SC/ST and other reserved category candidates.

What counts as a Full Mistake in SSC CGL DEST?

Full Mistakes (1 full error) include omission, substitution, or addition of any word, figure, or number, spelling mistakes due to repetition/addition/omission/substitution of letters, repetition of words, and incomplete or half-typed words.

How does the final submission work in the SSC CGL Typing Test?

The typing test automatically ends after 15 minutes, and no manual submission action is required. All typed text is automatically recorded and submitted by the system when time expires.
