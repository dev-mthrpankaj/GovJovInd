SSC CPO 2026 Exam Pattern: What Has Changed

SSC released the Sub-Inspector in Delhi Police and CAPFs Examination 2026 notice on 10 September 2026. It lists 1,871 vacancies: 317 for Sub-Inspector (Executive) in Delhi Police, 1,320 for Sub-Inspector (GD) in CAPFs and 234 for Sub-Inspector (Fire) in CISF. Applications were open from 10 to 30 September 2026, and the date of the computer-based exam was to be announced later.

Why Solve SSC CPO Previous Year Question Papers?

The syllabus lists topics. An old paper shows how those topics become questions, and that gap is where many candidates lose marks.

  • See the real question style: You notice how direct the General Awareness questions are, how long the Reasoning sets run, and how Quant questions are framed.
  • Understand English better: Paper 2 now carries 100 English questions for 200 marks. Old Paper 2 papers show the exact kind of grammar, vocabulary and comprehension asked.
  • Feel the time pressure: 100 questions in 60 minutes means about 36 seconds per question. You only understand how tight that is after a full timed sitting.
  • Learn when to skip: With negative marking in both papers, old papers teach you when a guess is worth it and when it is not.
  • Spot repeating areas: Compare two or three years side by side and the topics that keep returning become clear. The same concept returns, even if the exact question does not.
  • Find your weak sections: A wrong answer in a real paper tells you exactly which chapter needs another round.

How to Solve SSC CPO PYQs for the New Pattern

The old papers have 200 questions, but the 2026 Paper 1 has 100. Do not skip them for that reason. Adapt them instead.

  1. Split each paper into blocks: Take one 50-question section and solve it as two blocks of 25 questions, 15 minutes each. This matches the new sectional timer.
  2. Solve full sets once in a while: Every week, attempt four blocks back to back with a 1-hour timer to build stamina.
  3. Mark your doubts: Tick the questions where you were only guessing, so you can separate lucky answers from known ones.
  4. Calculate the real score: Subtract 0.25 for every wrong answer in Paper 1 style practice and 0.50 in Paper 2 style practice.
  5. Sort your mistakes: Put each error in one of three groups: did not know, knew but slipped, or guessed wrong.
  6. Keep a mistake notebook: Write the concept behind each error in one line. Reading this list before the exam is worth more than reading a new chapter.

If you have around four weeks, this simple split works:

  • Week 1: Revise weak topics and solve the oldest papers in 25-question blocks to see where you stand.
  • Weeks 2 and 3: One full paper every two days, with the next day spent on reviewing mistakes. Add English-only practice from old Paper 2 papers.
  • Week 4: Attempt the latest papers under strict timing and go through your mistake notebook daily.

How to Manage Time and Negative Marking

The new pattern gives each section its own timer, so time management matters more than before.

  • Plan every section separately: In Paper 1, 15 minutes for 25 questions is about 36 seconds per question. Decide in advance how long you will give to each question type.
  • Start with your strongest section: Early confidence helps you stay calm for the rest of the paper, if the exam interface lets you choose the order. Check the instructions on exam day.
  • Do not get stuck: If a question eats your time, mark it and move on.
  • Guess with a reason: In Paper 1, a blind guess among four options gains only about 0.06 mark on average. If you can remove two options, it rises to about 0.375. Paper 2 deducts 0.50 mark, so guess even more carefully.
  • Protect your English marks: English Comprehension is worth 200 of the 320 marks in Paper 2. Daily reading and grammar practice pays off here.

Practise this routine on old papers. It feels awkward the first time and natural by the third paper.

Common Mistakes Candidates Make with PYQs

  • Only reading, never solving: Going through questions with answers marked feels productive, but it does not train speed or accuracy.
  • Memorising answers: Learn the concept behind a question. The exact question rarely returns in the same form.
  • Ignoring negative marking: Counting only correct answers gives a score higher than the real one.
  • Practising only in the old 2-hour format: The 2026 Paper 1 is shorter and sectional, so practise with the new timing too.
  • Skipping Paper 2 papers: Paper 2 is now a full four-subject paper, so it needs its own practice.
  • Trusting unverified answer keys: If an answer looks wrong, cross-check it with a standard book or the official key.

Frequently Asked Questions (FAQs)

Where can I download SSC CPO previous year question papers?

Use the table at the top of this page. Papers are listed date-wise for the 2022, 2023, 2024 and 2025 cycles, covering Paper 1 and Paper 2. Click Download in the row of the paper you need.

Has the SSC CPO exam pattern changed in 2026?

Yes. As per the SSC CPO 2026 notice released on 10 September 2026, Paper 1 has been reduced to 100 questions for 100 marks in 1 hour, with a 15-minute timer for each section. Paper 2 is no longer English-only and now has 160 questions for 320 marks.

Are old SSC CPO papers still useful after the pattern change?

Yes. The four Paper 1 subjects are the same, so topics, difficulty and question style carry over. Only the number of questions and the timing have changed. Practise old papers in 25-question blocks to match the new Paper 1.

What is the negative marking in SSC CPO 2026?

Paper 1 carries a negative marking of 0.25 mark for every wrong answer. Paper 2 carries 0.50 mark for every wrong answer. Always confirm this in the official notice on ssc.gov.in.

How many questions were there in SSC CPO Paper 1 earlier?

In the older pattern used up to the 2025 cycle, Paper 1 had 200 questions for 200 marks, with 50 questions each from Reasoning, General Awareness, Quantitative Aptitude and English Comprehension, in 2 hours.

How many SSC CPO previous year papers should I solve?

There is no fixed number. Solve every available Paper 1 date at least once, starting with the latest, and then re-attempt the papers where you scored lowest after revising those topics.

Will Hindi papers and answer keys be added to this page?

Papers and answer keys will be added to the same table as clean copies become available, so the page URL will not change. Check back whenever SSC announces a new CPO exam date.
