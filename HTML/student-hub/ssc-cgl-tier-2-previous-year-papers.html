SSC CGL Tier 2 Exam Pattern: What Changed From 2022?

The SSC CGL Tier-II examination was substantially restructured for the 2022 examination. The new Paper-I combined Mathematical Abilities, Reasoning and General Intelligence, English Language and Comprehension, General Awareness, Computer Knowledge and the Data Entry Speed Test (DEST). SSC's 2022 notice specifically described Paper-I as compulsory and divided it into multiple sections/modules.

The current SSC CGL scheme continues to use this broad Tier-II structure. In the 2026 notification, Paper-I contains Mathematical Abilities, Reasoning and General Intelligence, English Language and Comprehension, General Awareness, Computer Knowledge and DEST, with Paper-I conducted across two sessions.

SSC CGL Tier 2 paper structure under the current scheme
Paper For Whom Main Components
Paper-I Compulsory for all posts Mathematical Abilities, Reasoning & General Intelligence, English Language & Comprehension, General Awareness, Computer Knowledge and DEST
Paper-II Eligible candidates for JSO / Statistical Investigator-related posts Statistics
Paper-III Eligible candidates for the posts covered by the Finance & Accounts / Economics & Governance paper General Studies — Finance & Accounts and Economics & Governance

For the 2026 examination, SSC states that Paper-I is conducted in two sessions. Session-I covers Sections I, II and the Computer Knowledge module, while Session-II contains DEST. The 2026 notice also states that candidates must qualify all sections of Paper-I separately.

SSC CGL Tier 2: 2021 and Earlier vs 2022 Onwards

The difference between the old and new Tier-II schemes is important when selecting previous year papers. The 2021 notification listed separate Tier-II papers for Quantitative Abilities, English Language and Comprehension, Statistics, and General Studies (Finance & Economics), with Paper-I and Paper-II compulsory for all posts.

SSC CGL Tier 2 Subject-Wise Practice

Previous year papers are more useful when you combine full-paper practice with targeted subject revision. Under Paper-I, the major areas are Mathematical Abilities, Reasoning and General Intelligence, English Language and Comprehension, General Awareness, Computer Knowledge and DEST.

Paper-I areas to analyse after solving PYQs
Area What to Analyse Next Step
Mathematical Abilities Calculation time, repeated concepts, silly mistakes Revise weak chapters and practise timed sets
Reasoning & General Intelligence Question-selection speed and accuracy Practise recurring question types
English Language & Comprehension Grammar, vocabulary, comprehension and error patterns Maintain an error notebook and revise regularly
General Awareness Static GK areas and factual recall Convert mistakes into short revision notes
Computer Knowledge Basic concepts and factual questions Revise fundamentals and practise topic-wise MCQs

How to Use SSC CGL Tier 2 Previous Year Papers Effectively

  1. Attempt before checking solutions: Treat the PDF as a real paper and avoid looking at answers while solving.
  2. Record three numbers: Track correct, incorrect and skipped questions after each attempt.
  3. Mark time-consuming questions: A question can be correct and still reveal a speed problem.
  4. Maintain an error log: Separate conceptual mistakes, calculation errors, reading mistakes and guesses.
  5. Revise before the next paper: Fix the weak areas identified from the previous attempt.
  6. Repeat under exam conditions: The aim is not only to complete PYQs but to make your performance more consistent.

Why SSC CGL Tier 2 PYQs Matter for Mains Preparation

Previous year papers show you the actual style of questions better than a generic practice set can. They help you identify repeated concepts, understand how different subjects are mixed within the current Paper-I structure and measure whether your preparation is translating into accurate attempts.

For a serious Tier-II preparation plan, use PYQs alongside the latest SSC notification and official examination instructions. The SSC website remains the authoritative source for the latest scheme, notices, answer keys and other official updates.

Frequently Asked Questions (FAQs)

From which year are SSC CGL Tier 2 previous year papers included on this page?

This page focuses on SSC CGL Tier 2 papers from 2022 onward because the Tier-II scheme was substantially restructured for the 2022 examination. This page covers SSC CGL Tier 2 papers from 2022 onwards. New examination-year papers will be added to the same page as they become available.

Why are 2021 and older SSC CGL Tier 2 papers not the main focus here?

The older Tier-II structure was different. The 2021 scheme had separate papers for Quantitative Abilities, English Language and Comprehension, Statistics and General Studies (Finance & Economics), while the 2022 scheme introduced the restructured Paper-I with Mathematical Abilities, Reasoning, English, General Awareness, Computer Knowledge and DEST.

Which SSC CGL Tier 2 papers are compulsory for most candidates?

Under the current scheme, Paper-I is compulsory for all posts. Paper-II and Paper-III apply to the specific categories of candidates and posts described in the SSC notification.

Are these SSC CGL Tier 2 PDFs official SSC question papers?

The downloadable resources on this page are prepared and hosted by GovJobUpdates for practice. They should not be treated as official SSC-issued PDFs unless explicitly identified as such. For official notifications, answer keys and examination notices, candidates should refer to the Staff Selection Commission website.

How should I use SSC CGL Tier 2 previous year papers for preparation?

Attempt each paper under exam-like conditions, analyse incorrect and skipped questions, record recurring topics, and revise weak areas before attempting the next paper.
