Student device choose karne se pehle 6 quick checks

  • RAM: 16GB RAM long-term ke liye best hai; 8GB sirf basic study use ke liye.
  • Storage: 512GB SSD minimum rakhein, especially coding, projects aur downloaded lectures ke liye.
  • Display: Reading ke liye 14-inch to 16-inch laptop screen comfortable hoti hai; tablet me 10.9-inch ya larger screen better.
  • Battery: College aur library use ke liye real-life 6-8 hours battery target karein.
  • Weight: Daily carry karna hai to 1.2kg-1.7kg laptop practical rahega.
  • Warranty: Brand service center aur warranty extension options check karna na bhoolen.

Laptop vs tablet: students ko kya lena chahiye?

Agar aapko online forms bharne hain, typing assignments banane hain, coding seekhni hai, Excel/PowerPoint use karna hai, internships karni hain ya multiple windows ke saath work karna hai, to laptop pehle lena better hai. Laptop long-term career work ke liye safer primary device hai.

Tablet un students ke liye best hai jo primarily notes banate hain, PDF read karte hain, video lectures dekhte hain aur pen se writing practice karte hain. Competitive exam aspirants ke liye tablet revision and PDF management me powerful tool ban sakta hai, lekin laptop ka full replacement har student ke liye nahi hota.

Final recommendation

Most students ke liye pehla device 16GB RAM + 512GB SSD wala balanced laptop hona chahiye. Agar budget allow karta hai to laptop ke saath pen-enabled tablet add karna study workflow ko kaafi improve kar sakta hai. Laptop productivity ka base hai, tablet notes aur revision ka booster hai.

Price frequently change hota hai, isliye ₹80,000 ke under laptop select karte time sale price, bank discount, warranty and exact model number zaroor match karein. Same product name ke andar bhi processor, RAM, display aur storage variants alag ho sakte hain.
