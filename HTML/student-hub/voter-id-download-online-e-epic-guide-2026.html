Voter ID Download Online 2026: How to Download e-EPIC Step by Step

Voter ID download online is available through the Election Commission of India’s voter services. This 2026 guide explains what e-EPIC is, where to download it, what details you may need, and what to do if your voter record needs correction.

1. What Is e-EPIC or Digital Voter ID?

e-EPIC is the electronic copy of the Electoral Photo Identity Card (EPIC). The Election Commission of India provides an e-EPIC download service through its voter-facing digital services, allowing eligible electors to keep an electronic copy of their card.

The source PDF describes the digital card as carrying the core voter details found on the physical card and highlights the convenience of keeping the PDF on a phone or other secure personal storage. For electoral participation, however, your name in the electoral roll remains fundamental, so it is sensible to check your electoral-roll entry as well.

2. Official Website for Voter ID Download Online

Use the Election Commission of India’s official Citizen Service Portal for voter services. The current portal visibly provides an E-EPIC Download service described as the digital version of the Electoral Photo Identity Card.

Open the official ECI Voters’ Services Portal. Avoid entering voter credentials, OTPs or personal details on unofficial websites that merely imitate the service.

3. Voter ID Download Online 2026: Step-by-Step Process

The exact screen labels can change as ECI updates its portal, but the official service flow is straightforward:

  1. Open the official ECI Citizen Service Portal and sign in or create an account when required.
  2. Select E-EPIC Download.
  3. Use your EPIC number or, where supported, the reference number of your voter application.
  4. Follow the portal’s identity-verification prompts. The login page currently supports registered mobile number, email ID or EPIC number and uses OTP-based access.
  5. After successful verification, use the download option provided by the portal to save your e-EPIC.
  6. Store the downloaded file securely and avoid sharing it publicly.

Important: the supplied PDF mentions specific OTP/e-KYC screens. Because portal workflows can change, this guide treats those screen-level steps as indicative and relies on the current ECI portal for the final on-screen process.

5. e-EPIC vs Electoral Roll: Do Not Confuse the Two

Your EPIC card is an identity document issued for electors, while the electoral roll is the official list of registered electors for a constituency. Downloading a digital card does not replace the need to ensure that your name and details are correctly recorded in the electoral roll.

ECI’s portal separately provides services to search your name in the voter list, check polling details and download electoral rolls. If you are preparing for an election, checking your current electoral-roll entry is a useful additional step.

6. Common Voter ID Download Problems and What to Do

Common e-EPIC issues and the appropriate next step
IssueWhat to do
Cannot sign inRecheck the registered mobile/email/EPIC details and use the official portal. For unresolved issues, contact ECI on 1950.
Wrong name, age or other voter detailUse Form 8 for correction of entries in the existing electoral roll.
Changed residenceForm 8 also covers shifting of residence.
Need replacement EPICECI’s Form 8 includes the option for issue of replacement EPIC without correction.
Cannot locate voter recordUse ECI’s “Search Your Name in Voter List” service and verify the electoral-roll entry.

7. When Is Form 8 Used?

According to the Election Commission of India, Form 8 is used for shifting of residence, correction of entries in the existing electoral roll, replacement of EPIC, and marking a voter as a person with disability. This is more reliable than trying to solve incorrect voter details only at the e-EPIC download stage.

8. Safety Tips Before Downloading Your Digital Voter ID

  • Use only the official ECI website or official ECI voter applications.
  • Never share an OTP with a person claiming they will download the card for you.
  • Do not post your e-EPIC PDF publicly on social media or messaging groups.
  • Check the website domain carefully before entering personal information.
  • If you notice an electoral-roll error, use the appropriate ECI correction service rather than editing a downloaded file.

Frequently Asked Questions (FAQs)

What is e-EPIC?

e-EPIC is the electronic version of the Elector Photo Identity Card that eligible electors can download through the Election Commission of India voter services.

Can I download Voter ID without an EPIC number?

The Election Commission of India states that an electronic copy of EPIC can be downloaded using an EPIC number or the reference number of the submitted form.

Where can I officially download my Voter ID online?

Use the Election Commission of India Voters’ Services Portal and select the E-EPIC Download service.

What should I do if my Voter ID details are incorrect?

ECI provides Form 8 for correction of entries in the existing electoral roll, shifting of residence, replacement of EPIC and marking of PwD.

What is the ECI voter helpline number?

The Election Commission of India lists 1950 as its toll-free voter helpline number.
