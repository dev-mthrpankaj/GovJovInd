SSC CPO 2026 Exam Pattern Changed: Paper 1, Paper 2, PET/PST & Selection Process

SSC CPO 2026 has introduced a major change in both Paper 1 and Paper 2. Paper 1 is now shorter with sectional timing, while Paper 2 is no longer an English-only paper. This guide explains the new pattern, negative marking, physical test, vacancies, eligibility and final selection in simple terms.

Key Takeaways

  • Paper 1 is now 100 questions for 100 marks: 25 questions each from Reasoning, GK/GA, Quant and English.
  • Paper 1 has sectional timing: 15 minutes for each of the four parts, with a total duration of one hour.
  • Paper 2 has changed substantially: it now includes Reasoning, GK/GA and Quant in addition to 100 English questions.
  • Paper 2 carries 320 marks: 20 questions each in Reasoning, GK and Quant at 2 marks each, plus 100 English questions for 200 marks.
  • Negative marking: 0.25 mark per wrong answer in Paper 1 and 0.50 mark per wrong answer in Paper 2.
  • Final selection is driven by Paper 2: PST/PET and medical stages are qualifying; post/force preferences also matter.

The biggest SSC CPO pattern change is in Paper 2

Until now, many aspirants prepared Paper 2 primarily as an English paper. Under the 2026 notification, Paper 2 contains all four major subjects. That changes preparation strategy, time allocation and the importance of balanced performance across sections.

SSC CPO 2026 Overview

The Staff Selection Commission released the notice for the Sub-Inspector in Delhi Police and Central Armed Police Forces Examination, 2026 on 10 September 2026. Online applications are scheduled from 10 September to 30 September 2026, with online fee payment allowed up to 1 October. The correction window is 8 to 10 October 2026.

The Computer Based Examination date is not fixed in the notice and will be notified later. The recruitment includes SI (Executive) in Delhi Police, SI (GD) in CAPFs and a separate SI (Fire) post in CISF.

What Changed in SSC CPO 2026 Exam Pattern?

The 2026 notification is not a routine repeat of the old scheme. SSC has changed the number of questions, marks distribution, sectional timing and—most importantly—the structure of Paper 2.

Paper 1 now has 100 questions instead of the traditional 200-question structure. Each of the four subjects carries 25 questions and 25 marks. The total time is one hour, and SSC has specified a 15-minute sectional timer for each part.

Paper 2 is the bigger change. It is no longer limited to English Language and Comprehension. Reasoning, General Awareness and Quantitative Aptitude are included again, alongside a larger English section. This makes Paper 2 a broad merit-deciding test rather than a single-subject English paper.

SSC CPO 2026 Paper 1 Pattern

Paper 1 is the first Computer Based Examination. It contains four equal parts. Every part has 25 questions and 25 marks. The entire paper is completed in 60 minutes, but that time is split into 15 minutes per section.

There is a penalty of 0.25 mark for every wrong answer. Because the sectional timer is fixed, candidates cannot treat the one-hour duration as a single free pool of time. Preparation therefore needs to include section-wise speed, not only total-paper speed.

Paper 1 minimum qualifying marks

SSC has prescribed minimum qualifying percentages in both Paper 1 and Paper 2: 30% for UR, 25% for OBC/EWS and 20% for all other categories. These are minimum qualifying marks, not guaranteed cut-offs. SSC can fix higher cut-offs and may also set different cut-offs in individual parts of Paper 1.

SSC CPO 2026 Paper 2 Pattern

Paper 2 is the most important strategic change in the 2026 examination. It contains four subjects, but the weight is not equal. Reasoning, GK and Quant each have 20 questions worth 40 marks. English contains 100 questions worth 200 marks.

The first three parts together take one hour, using a 20-minute sectional timer for each part. English Comprehension has a separate one-hour duration. The notification also states that the difficulty level of Paper 2 will be higher than Paper 1.

Each wrong answer in Paper 2 attracts a penalty of 0.50 mark. Since most Paper 2 questions carry two marks, blind attempts can become costly. Accuracy matters even more because Paper 2 performance is used for final selection and force allocation.

Why Paper 2 changes your preparation plan

Aspirants who previously postponed Reasoning, GK and Quant after Paper 1 cannot afford that approach in 2026. These three subjects return in Paper 2, so concepts must remain fresh after the physical stage. English still dominates Paper 2 with 200 of the 320 marks, but the other three subjects together contribute 120 marks—too much to ignore.

  • English remains the highest-weight subject: 100 questions and 200 marks.
  • Reasoning, GK and Quant each matter: 40 marks per subject.
  • Sectional speed is compulsory: you cannot compensate for a slow section by taking time from another.
  • Paper 2 is a merit paper: your strategy should focus on both accuracy and score maximisation.

SSC CPO 2026 Syllabus: What the Notification Says

The broad subject areas remain familiar. Reasoning includes verbal and non-verbal reasoning, analogies, series, coding-decoding, Venn diagrams, spatial reasoning, syllogistic reasoning and related analytical topics.

General Awareness covers current events and static knowledge related to India and neighbouring countries, including History, Culture, Geography, Economy, Polity, the Constitution and Scientific Research.

Quantitative Aptitude includes arithmetic, percentage, ratio and proportion, averages, interest, profit and loss, time and work, time and distance, algebra, geometry, mensuration, trigonometry and data representation.

English Comprehension includes error recognition, fill in the blanks, vocabulary, spelling, grammar, sentence structure, synonyms, antonyms, sentence completion, idioms and phrases and comprehension.

SSC CPO 2026 Eligibility: Education, Age and Driving Licence

Educational qualification

For SI in Delhi Police and SI (GD) in CAPFs, candidates need a bachelor's degree from a recognised university or equivalent by the closing date of applications.

SI (Fire) in CISF has a separate qualification: a bachelor's degree in Science with Physics, Chemistry and Mathematics, or Matriculation/equivalent plus a three-year diploma in specified engineering disciplines. Candidates should read the exact technical qualification clause before choosing this post.

Driving licence rule for Delhi Police SI male

Male candidates seeking SI (Executive) in Delhi Police must possess a valid LMV driving licence for both motorcycle and car on the date fixed for PE&MT. A male candidate without that licence may still be eligible for CAPF SI posts if all other conditions are fulfilled.

SSC CPO 2026 PET/PST: Physical Standards and Running Test

Paper 1 qualifiers are shortlisted for the Physical Standard Test and Physical Endurance Test. PST/PET is mandatory and qualifying in nature; it does not carry marks. Ex-servicemen are exempt from PET but must undergo PST and satisfy the required medical standards.

Basic height and chest standards

For general male candidates in Delhi Police SI and CAPF SI, the standard height is 170 cm with chest 80 cm unexpanded and 85 cm expanded. General female candidates require 157 cm height; there is no minimum chest measurement for women. Relaxed height/chest standards apply to specified hill-area, Gorkha and Scheduled Tribe categories.

For SI (Fire) in CISF, the general male standard is 170 cm height with 81 cm unexpanded and 86 cm expanded chest. Weight for all posts should correspond to height.

SSC CPO 2026 Medical Standards

Candidates qualifying Paper 2 are called for the Detailed Medical Examination. The medical stage, including Review Medical Examination where applicable, is mandatory but qualifying.

The notification specifies minimum near vision of N6 in the better eye and N9 in the worse eye, and minimum distant vision of 6/6 in the better eye and 6/9 in the worse eye, without correction such as spectacles or surgery to improve visual acuity. Candidates must also be free from knock knee, flat foot, varicose vein and squint, and should possess high colour vision.

SSC CPO 2026 Selection Process and Final Merit

The SSC CPO 2026 selection sequence is important because not every stage contributes marks to final merit.

  1. Paper 1 CBE: used for shortlisting candidates for PST/PET.
  2. PST/PET: mandatory and qualifying; no marks are added.
  3. Paper 2 CBE: used to shortlist candidates for medical examination and is the key merit paper.
  4. DME/RME: mandatory and qualifying medical stage.
  5. Document Verification: conducted by CAPFs, preferably with the DME/RME stage.
  6. Final selection: based on Paper 2 performance, applicable NCC bonus and post/force preference.

This is a critical 2026 point: the notification states that final selection and allocation of post/force are based on Paper 2 performance, including applicable NCC bonus marks, along with candidate preferences. Paper 1 gets you to the physical stage; Paper 2 decides the final competitive ranking for allocation.

NCC Bonus Marks in SSC CPO 2026

NCC certificate holders as on the application closing date receive bonus marks in both Paper 1 and Paper 2. The bonus is added to normalized scores.

How to Prepare for the New SSC CPO 2026 Pattern

The new pattern rewards candidates who can maintain speed across fixed sections and keep all four subjects active throughout the recruitment cycle.

1. Practise Paper 1 in 15-minute blocks

Do not rely only on full 60-minute mocks. Train each subject independently for a 15-minute sprint. Your target should be to recognise which questions to skip within seconds because unused time from one section may not rescue another section.

2. Keep Reasoning, GK and Quant alive after Paper 1

These subjects return in Paper 2. After Paper 1, many candidates naturally shift attention to physical preparation and English. In 2026 that would leave 120 Paper 2 marks underprepared.

3. Give English the largest Paper 2 share

English still carries 200 out of 320 marks in Paper 2, so it remains the highest-value subject. Build grammar, vocabulary and comprehension speed alongside the three 40-mark sections.

4. Start physical training before the written result

The running and jumping standards are fixed qualifying barriers. Waiting for Paper 1 result before beginning physical preparation creates unnecessary risk, especially for the 1.6 km male race and timed sprint events.

5. Control negative marking

The penalty doubles from 0.25 in Paper 1 to 0.50 in Paper 2. Paper 2 questions are worth two marks each, but inaccurate guessing can still damage a strong score. Use mock analysis to identify subjects where your attempt rate is high but accuracy is weak.

Frequently Asked Questions (FAQs)

What is the SSC CPO 2026 Paper 1 pattern?

Paper 1 has 100 questions for 100 marks. Reasoning, General Awareness, Quantitative Aptitude and English each have 25 questions for 25 marks. Total time is 60 minutes with a 15-minute sectional timer for each part.

What is the SSC CPO 2026 Paper 2 pattern?

Paper 2 has 160 questions for 320 marks. Reasoning, General Awareness and Quantitative Aptitude have 20 questions each for 40 marks, while English Comprehension has 100 questions for 200 marks.

Is there sectional timing in SSC CPO 2026?

Yes. Paper 1 has 15 minutes for each of its four parts. In Paper 2, the first three parts have 20 minutes each, and English Comprehension has a separate one-hour duration.

What is the negative marking in SSC CPO 2026?

Paper 1 deducts 0.25 mark for each wrong answer. Paper 2 deducts 0.50 mark for each wrong answer.

What is the SSC CPO 2026 selection process?

The stages are Paper 1, PST/PET, Paper 2, DME/RME, Document Verification and final selection. PST/PET and medical stages are qualifying in nature.

How many vacancies are in SSC CPO 2026?

The notification lists 1,871 tentative vacancies: 205 Delhi Police SI male, 112 Delhi Police SI female, 1,320 SI GD in CAPFs and 234 SI Fire male in CISF.

What is the age limit for SSC CPO 2026?

For Delhi Police SI and CAPF SI GD, the age limit is 20 to 25 years as on 1 August 2026. For SI Fire in CISF, it is 18 to 30 years. Category relaxations apply.

Is graduation compulsory for SSC CPO 2026?

A bachelor's degree or equivalent is required for Delhi Police SI and CAPF SI GD. SI Fire in CISF has a separate science or technical qualification requirement.

Is a driving licence compulsory for SSC CPO?

A valid LMV driving licence for motorcycle and car is compulsory for male candidates seeking Delhi Police SI at the date of PE&MT. Without it, a male candidate may still be considered for CAPF SI posts if otherwise eligible.

Does Paper 1 count in final merit in SSC CPO 2026?

Paper 1 is used for shortlisting to PST/PET. The notification states that final selection and force allocation are based on Paper 2 performance, including applicable NCC bonus marks, together with post/force preferences.
