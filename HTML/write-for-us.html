Editorial Standards

Contributor guidelines

Student Hub should remain useful, trustworthy and readable. Review these requirements before sending a proposal.

01

Submit original work

Your article should be unpublished. Copied, spun or lightly reworded material is not accepted.

02

Write for students, not search engines

Use natural language and practical explanations. Keyword stuffing and filler content will not be accepted.

03

Support factual claims

Recruitment rules, dates, eligibility and statistics should use reliable or official sources where applicable.

04

Aim for depth

Most useful submissions will naturally be around 1,000–1,500 words or more, but completeness matters more than length.

05

Keep promotion limited

Promotional copy, affiliate-style writing, link stuffing and disguised advertisements are not accepted.

06

Expect editorial changes

We may edit titles, structure, grammar, links or wording for accuracy, clarity and reader experience.

Author & link policy

A short author bio may be published. One relevant author website or professional profile may be considered in the bio.

Links are editorially reviewed. We do not guarantee specific anchor text, do-follow links or SEO-focused placements.

Paid link insertion, link exchange schemes and unrelated commercial links are not part of this programme.

Quality Control

What we usually reject

Copied or previously published articles
Thin, generic or mass-produced content
Keyword-stuffed SEO articles
Unverified claims presented as facts
Paid-link or backlink-only proposals
Betting, gambling, adult or unrelated commercial topics

Write for GovJobUpdates FAQs

Does GovJobUpdates accept guest posts?

Yes. We consider original, useful and relevant contributor articles that meet our editorial standards.

Is there a publication fee?

No publication fee is charged for accepted editorial contributions. We do not sell ranking links through this programme.

Will I get an author link?

A relevant website or professional-profile link may be considered in the author bio. Links remain subject to editorial review.

Can I submit a previously published article?

No. Please send original content that has not already been published elsewhere.

Is publication guaranteed?

No. The complete article must pass editorial review for accuracy, originality, quality and relevance.
