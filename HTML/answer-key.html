Score Review Guide

How to use an answer key responsibly

Match your recorded responses with the key published by the official examining authority and apply the notified marking scheme before estimating a score.

Provisional key

A provisional key may allow objections within a stated deadline and can change after expert review.

Final key

The final key is released after objections are considered and is normally used to prepare results.

Estimate chances

Expected marks or a rank prediction may help planning, but only the official result and cutoff are final.

Answer Key FAQs

What is the difference between provisional and final keys?

A provisional answer key is open to objections where permitted; a final key is issued after review and generally forms the basis of result preparation.

How can I raise an objection?

Follow the official notice for deadline, objection portal, supporting evidence and any required fee. Processes vary by examination.

Can an answer key confirm my final result?

No. It can help estimate marks, but official normalization, cutoffs and selection rules are applied by the examination authority.

How can the Rank Predictor help?

It can provide an estimate using entered scores or available data, but it is not an official rank or result service.
