How Hiring Works

Four-Stage Selection Process

Every recruitment can use the following structured selection journey. Only eligible candidates progress to the next stage.

01

Application & Shortlisting

Applications and uploaded documents are reviewed against the published requirement. Shortlisted and non-shortlisted candidates receive status updates.

02

Recruitment Test

Shortlisted candidates may be invited to a 100-question job-related online test with negative marking. Eligibility and test status appear in application tracking.

03

Assignment Submission

Candidates who qualify the test receive a role-related assignment with instructions and a submission deadline.

04

Final Screening

Qualified candidates move to final screening. The final status may be Selected, Waitlisted or Not Selected.
