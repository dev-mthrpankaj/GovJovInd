About This Recruitment

Union Bank of India, one of the leading public sector banks in India, has invited online applications from eligible Indian citizens for the post of Specialist Officer (SO) across various operational and specialized domains. This recruitment drive aims to fill 395 regular vacancies in different scales. Candidates who hold professional degrees such as B.E., B.Tech, M.E., M.Tech, MCA, M.Sc, CA, or MBA, along with post-qualification work experience, can apply online for these key officer roles.

The recruitment process requires candidates to register online via the official IBPS application portal integrated for Union Bank of India. Applicants must complete One-Time Registration, upload scanned photographs, signatures, documents, and pay the requisite registration fee before the stipulated deadline of 10/08/2026. Careful review of post-wise experience and educational requirements is recommended before submitting the form.

Selection will generally be based on online written tests, shortlisting, and/or personal interviews depending on the total number of applicants and official rules. Candidates selected under this project will be posted across various branches and office locations of Union Bank of India as per organizational requirements.
