About This Recruitment

BPSSC Bihar Home Guard Company Commander Recruitment is a state-level recruitment process for appointment in the Bihar Home Guard service cadre under the Home Department, Bihar. The post is notified as Gulm Samadeshak / Company Commander and the selection will be made through a competitive recruitment process conducted by Bihar Police Subordinate Services Commission.

This recruitment is useful for graduates who want a uniformed service opportunity in Bihar with a structured pay level and written-plus-physical selection process. The official notice clearly mentions that only one online application can be submitted by a candidate, so applicants should check personal details, category, gender, date of birth and document information carefully before final submission.

Candidates should not treat this as only a written exam vacancy. The notification includes preliminary examination, main examination, category-wise shortlisting, physical standards, physical efficiency tests, medical examination and character verification. Therefore, applicants should verify both educational eligibility and physical readiness before applying.

Eligibility and Qualification

  • Candidate must be an Indian citizen.
  • Male, female and third gender candidates can apply as per the notification.
  • Candidate must have passed graduation from a recognized university or an equivalent qualification recognized by the State Government by 01.08.2025.
  • Candidate should satisfy the notified age limit, physical standards and category certificate rules.
  • Candidates from outside Bihar claiming reserved category benefit should read the notice carefully because reservation benefit is applicable only as per Bihar rules.

Selection Process

  1. Online applications will be scrutinized by BPSSC.
  2. Eligible applicants will appear in the Preliminary Written Examination.
  3. Candidates qualifying the preliminary exam will be shortlisted category-wise for the Main Written Examination.
  4. Based on main written examination marks, candidates up to six times the vacancies may be called for Physical Efficiency Test.
  5. Candidates must qualify the physical standards and physical efficiency stages.
  6. Final merit list will be prepared on the basis of marks obtained in the main written examination, subject to PET, medical test, character verification and other rules.

How to Apply

  1. Visit the BPSSC official website at bpssc.bihar.gov.in.
  2. Open the Home Guard tab and select the link for Advertisement No. 08/2026.
  3. Keep an active mobile number and email ID ready before registration.
  4. Complete the first part of registration with personal, category, domicile, gender and date of birth details.
  5. Pay the online application fee and save registration details received by SMS or email.
  6. Login again and complete the second part of the application with address and educational details.
  7. Upload recent photograph and required signatures in the format and size mentioned in the official instructions.
  8. Preview every detail carefully and submit the application before the last date.
  9. Print the final acknowledgement and keep it safely for future correspondence.
