About This Recruitment

This MPESB recruitment rulebook covers Forest Department posts such as Forest Guard and Field Guard along with Jail Department posts including Jail Prahari and Assistant Jail Superintendent. The uploaded revised pages clearly show the examination schedule and Forest Guard vacancy section.

Because this is a combined rulebook, candidates must check post-wise eligibility and physical/departmental requirements in the official notification before applying. The Forest Guard section visible in the revised pages lists 10+2 / old Higher Secondary / 10th under 10+2 pattern as qualification.

The examination is online with two shifts. Candidates should keep ID proof, admit card, employment registration and category documents ready, and must verify the exact post-wise rules from MPESB before final submission.
