About This Recruitment

Indian Overseas Bank (IOB) Local Bank Officer Recruitment 2026 is an official recruitment drive conducted under Advertisement No. HRDD/RECT/02/2026-27. This recruitment aims to fill 250 vacancies for the post of Local Bank Officer (LBO) across various state locations including Tamil Nadu, Karnataka, Maharashtra, and Gujarat.

The online application process has commenced on 08 August 2026 and will remain open until 24 August 2026. Interested candidates who hold a Bachelor Degree in any discipline from a recognized university and possess mandatory local language proficiency are eligible to apply online.

Selection will be carried out as per bank recruitment guidelines. Candidates are required to register on the official IOB recruitment portal, submit accurate details, upload scanned photo and signature, and make the application fee payment before the deadline.

How to Apply for IOB Local Bank Officer Vacancy 2026

  1. Visit the official website of Indian Overseas Bank at iob.bank.in/en/careers.
  2. Look for "IOB Local Bank Officer Recruitment 2026 (Advt No. HRDD/RECT/02/2026-27)" and click on the Apply Online link.
  3. Register using a valid Email ID and Mobile Number to obtain Registration credentials.
  4. Log in using your Registration ID and Password.
  5. Fill in the application form carefully with personal details, educational qualifications, and category details.
  6. Upload scanned copies of required documents including passport size photograph, signature, and certificates.
  7. Pay the application fee (Rs. 850 for Gen/OBC/EWS or Rs. 175 for SC/ST/PwBD) via online payment modes.
  8. Submit the form and download/print the final confirmation page for future reference.

Documents Required

  • Scanned Passport Size Photograph and Signature.
  • Graduation Marksheets and Degree Certificate.
  • 10th / 12th Certificate for Date of Birth proof.
  • Caste / Category Certificate (SC/ST/OBC/EWS/PwBD), if applicable.
  • Local Language Proficiency Certificate or Marksheet proof, if applicable.
  • Valid Govt Photo ID Proof (Aadhaar Card, PAN Card, Voter ID, Driving License).
