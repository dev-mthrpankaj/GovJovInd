Selection Procedure & Marks Evaluation Scheme

The selection is executed across three discrete phases, carrying a maximum aggregate score of 100 Marks distributed as follows[cite: 9]:

Phase A: Language, Academics & Co-curricular Activities (20 Marks Weightage)[cite: 9]

This phase evaluates documents uploaded during the registration stage[cite: 9]:

  • Language Score (Max 10 Marks): Evaluates Class 10th or 12th language marks (whichever features a higher percentile score)[cite: 9].
    • Hindi Language: 70%-80% = 1 Mark; >80%-90% = 2 Marks; Above 90% = 5 Marks[cite: 9].
    • English Language: 70%-80% = 1 Mark; >80%-90% = 2 Marks; Above 90% = 5 Marks[cite: 9].
  • Academic Track (Max 05 Marks): Evaluated based on average aggregate scores from 1st to 4th Year (for 5-year LL.B.) or 1st and 2nd Year (for 3-year LL.B.)[cite: 9].
    • 60% to 65% = 1 Mark; More than 65% to 75% = 3 Marks; More than 75% to 80% = 4 Marks; More than 80% = 5 Marks[cite: 9].
  • Co-curricular Merits (Max 05 Marks): Calculated based on Moot Court participation or published legal research papers[cite: 9].
    • College level = 1 Mark; State-level winner/National level = 3 Marks; International level = 5 Marks[cite: 9].

Phase B: Descriptive Screening Test (60 Marks Weightage)[cite: 9]

  • Candidates will undergo a 1-hour offline descriptive examination evaluating conceptual writing potential, legally sound drafting capability, and procedural awareness, carrying a total weightage of 60 marks[cite: 9].

Phase C: Interview Test (20 Marks Weightage)[cite: 9]

  • The interview panel scores candidates out of 100 marks, which is scaled down to a 20% index weightage for final merit[cite: 9]. Fixed step evaluation benchmarks are mapped as: Unsuitable (30 Marks), Passable (40 Marks), Good (60 Marks), Very Good (70 Marks), Exceptional (80 Marks)[cite: 9].

Nature of Work / Duties Assigned

Successful candidates are attached with Hon'ble High Court Judges at the main Prayagraj court or Lucknow Bench[cite: 9]. Core duties include:

  • Performing analytical legal research on specialized points of law and precedents as directed by the attached judge[cite: 9].
  • Attending Judge's chambers and active court hearings to record systematic notes and case pointers[cite: 9].
  • Assisting in drafting, compiling case study notes, interpreting legal statutes, maintaining systematic record files, tracking judicial pronouncements, and summarizing key legal issues[cite: 9].

Important Practice Constraints & Rules

  • Restriction on Practice: Upon termination of the contract, a Research Associate cannot appear in any capacity before the specific judge they were attached with for a continuous period of two years[cite: 9].
  • Professional Restriction: No Research Associate can participate in any other parallel academic, regular, or professional pursuit throughout their contract tenure without prior court approval[cite: 9].
  • Marital Disqualification: Candidates holding more than one living spouse, or married to an individual who has a legally living spouse, are ineligible[cite: 9].
  • Criminal Case Restriction: Any individual who is charge-sheeted or convicted in a criminal trial will face immediate disqualification[cite: 9].
