Selection Process

  1. 1

    Candidates will be shortlisted / assessed through a Computer Based Test (CBT).

  2. 2

    The CBT will be objective type with 100 multiple choice questions for 120 minutes.

  3. 3

    The test will comprise 60% technical / domain specific questions and 40% non-technical questions such as quantitative aptitude, reasoning, general knowledge and English.

  4. 4

    Questions will be available in English and Hindi. For eligible posts, candidates may be called for Trade Test based on CBT performance.

  5. 5

    Final appointment will be subject to document verification, pre-employment medical examination and fulfilment of notified eligibility criteria.

How to Apply Online

  1. 1

    Visit www.nalcoindia.com and open Career Page of NALCO / Current Openings.

  2. 2

    Open “Recruitment of Non-Executive Personnel - M&R Complex, Damanjodi-2026” and click View Advertisement before applying.

  3. 3

    Click “Apply Online”, complete new registration, generate provisional registration number and password, then login again.

  4. 4

    Fill the application carefully, upload photograph, signature, left thumb impression, handwritten declaration and relevant documents as per the prescribed format.

  5. 5

    Pay the fee online if applicable, preview the form, submit final application and take printout of the system generated form for records.

Important Instructions

  • Only one application should be submitted for one post. Multiple applications for a single post may lead to cancellation of candidature.
  • All documents must be uploaded under the correct upload tag and should be clear and readable.
  • Application form details must match certificates and documents. Wrong or incomplete details may lead to rejection.
  • Selected candidates may be posted at NALCO establishments anywhere in India or abroad as per company requirement.
  • No request for change of test centre will be entertained after final submission.
  • Admission to CBT / Trade Test is provisional and subject to verification of eligibility, experience, documents and medical fitness.
