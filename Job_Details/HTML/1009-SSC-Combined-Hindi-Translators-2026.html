About This Recruitment

The Combined Hindi Translators Examination is an prestigious national-level recruitment gateway organized annually by the Staff Selection Commission. It targets proficient bilingual professionals to fulfill Group 'B' Non-Gazetted translation vacancies across essential central line ministries, specialized offices, and departmental secretariats. Roles like Junior Translation Officer (JTO), Junior Hindi Translator (JHT), and Senior Translation Officer (STO) ensure fluent, standard administrative communication across various structures of the Government of India.

Applicants belonging to all parts of India who meet the requisite qualification parameters are eligible to compete. However, specific administrative constraints exist, such as gender-specific eligibility policies noting that only male applicants can be allocated to vacancies within the Border Roads Organization (BRO). Registration requires using the newly established administrative portal of the commission, rendering older credentials obsolete. It is vital for serious language applicants to carefully coordinate their educational certifications and experience records prior to the closing dates.

The operational framework of this recruitment demands a high degree of bilingual competency across reading, translation execution, and structural editing. Unlike basic single-tier formats, applicants face an intensive initial screening process involving combined objective scoring followed by classic hand-written descriptive translations. Understanding the micro-details of age reckoning timelines, technical certification exemptions, and department allocation rules serves as a key stepping stone to avoiding procedural disqualification at later evaluation stages.

Eligibility and Qualification

All core academic requirements must be completely possessed on or before the critical target timeline of 01.08.2026. Below are the structural educational routes allowed:

For Post Codes A, B, and C (Junior Translation Levels):

  • Master's degree from a recognized university in Hindi with English as a compulsory or elective subject or as the medium of examination at the degree level; OR
  • Master's degree from a recognized university in English with Hindi as a compulsory or elective subject or as the medium of examination at the degree level; OR
  • Master's degree in any alternative subject with Hindi medium and English as a compulsory/elective subject or examination medium at the degree level; OR
  • Master's degree in any alternative subject with English medium and Hindi as a compulsory/elective subject or examination medium at the degree level; OR
  • Master's degree in any subject with Hindi and English as compulsory/elective subjects or either of the two as a medium of examination and the other as a compulsory/elective subject at the degree level.
  • AND A recognized Diploma or Certificate course in translation from Hindi to English & vice versa OR 2 years of proven translation work experience in Central/State Government Offices or Government of India Undertakings.

For Post Code D (Senior Translation Levels):

  • Possession of any identical Master's Degree language combination routes listed above.
  • AND A recognized Diploma or Certificate course in translation from Hindi to English & vice versa OR 3 years of extensive translation work experience in Central/State Government Offices or Government of India Undertakings.

Selection Process

Final selection rankings rely on an objective-to-descriptive elimination model. The step-by-step process is structured as follows:

  1. Paper-I Examination: Participation in an objective multi-shift computer-based competitive test.
  2. Paper-II Examination: Descriptive written test evaluating formal translation precision and essay architecture for high-scoring qualified candidates.
  3. Post Preference Submission: Compulsory completion of online options regarding department choices on the SSC portal before result generation.
  4. Document Verification (DV): Checking of original qualification certificates and experience credentials by assigned user departments.
  5. Medical Fitness Screening: Specialized physical/medical evaluations carried out by user frameworks (such as GREF Medical Boards for BRO allocations).

Negative Marking Notice: A deduction penalty of 0.25 marks is applicable for every wrong answer marked in Paper-I. Minimum qualifying cut-off parameters are set at 30% for UR, 25% for OBC/EWS, and 20% for other categories.

Indicative Syllabus Focus: Paper-I evaluates degree-level literature grasp, vocabulary usage, grammatical structures, idioms, and phrase applications. Paper-II tests practical skills through translation passages (English to Hindi and vice versa) alongside formal essay writing.

Physical and Medical Standards (BRO Only)

Candidates opting for positions in the Border Roads Organization (BRO) must fulfill specific physical efficiency and medical reviews. These positions carry mandatory All India Service Liabilities (AISL) across extreme remote terrains.

  • Physical Test: Passing a mandatory 1-Mile run fitness test within 10 minutes at the GREF Centre in Pune.
  • Regional Heights: Minimum height requirement stands at 158 cms for Western Himalayan regions, 152 cms for Eastern Himalayan regions, 162.5 cms for West Plains, and 157 cms for Eastern/Central/Southern plain groups.
  • Chest Measurement: Minimum baseline of 75 cms (76 cms for West Plains) with a mandatory minimum expansion capacity of 5 cms.
  • Visual Scale: Visual acuity standards must not fall below 6/12 in each eye or Right 6/6 and Left 6/24. Spectacles are allowed, provided unaided vision does not fall under 6/60.

How to Apply Online

  1. Navigate to the updated official homepage of the Staff Selection Commission at ssc.gov.in.
  2. Click the "Login or Register" link and choose "Register Now" if you have not created your new OTR profile. Legacy credentials from ssc.nic.in are not valid.
  3. Provide your Aadhaar number, verify your mobile and email contacts using OTPs, set up a secure password, and save your permanent registration code.
  4. Log in using your fresh OTR registration details and click on the "Apply" button under the "Combined Hindi Translators Examination, 2026" section on the dashboard.
  5. Verify your pre-filled personal records, select your choice of 3 examination centres, and provide your highest educational qualification details.
  6. Enter your translation course/diploma or experience duration details precisely.
  7. Capture your live frontal face photograph using your webcam or mobile application following standard lighting guidelines, and upload your signature file.
  8. Check your application using the Preview layout, accept the declaration terms, fill in the captcha code, and click submit.
  9. Pay your application fee of Rs. 100 online via UPI or card channels if applicable. Candidates with fee exemptions will see their status updated immediately.
  10. Download and print a copy of your submitted application form for future reference.

Frequently Asked Questions

What is the SSC Combined Hindi Translators Examination 2026?

It is an open competitive examination conducted by the Staff Selection Commission for direct recruitment to Group B Non-Gazetted posts of Junior Hindi Translator, Junior Translation Officer, Junior Translator, Senior Hindi Translator, and Senior Translation Officer across various central ministries and departments.

What is the last date to apply for SSC JHT 2026?

According to the official notification, the last date and time for submission of online applications is 14 May 2026 up to 23:00 hours.

How many vacancies are released for the 2026 exam?

There are currently 84 tentative vacancies reported by the Commission, which may be updated post-wise and category-wise on the official portal.

What is the required educational qualification?

Candidates must possess a Master's degree in Hindi or English (or a related subject combination with Hindi/English mediums) along with a recognized translation diploma/certificate or 2 to 3 years of translation work experience depending on the post level.

What is the age limit for this recruitment?

The candidate's age must be between 18 and 30 years as on 1 August 2026. Upper age relaxations apply for reserved categories like SC, ST, OBC, and PwBD.

What is the application fee structure?

The application fee is Rs. 100. Women candidates, SC, ST, and PwBD candidates are fully exempted from payment of the application fee.

What are the stages in the selection process?

The selection flow consists of an objective Paper-I (Computer Based Examination), followed by a descriptive Paper-II (Translation and Essay), Online Post Preference selection, and Document Verification conducted by the respective User Departments.

Is there any negative marking in Paper-I?

Yes, there is a negative marking of 0.25 marks for each incorrect response in the objective Paper-I examination.

Are female candidates eligible for all departments under this exam?

Females are highly encouraged to apply for general ministries, but the notice specifies that only male candidates are eligible for vacancies reported in the Border Roads Organization (BRO).

Where can I apply for this translation vacancy?

Eligible candidates must complete their One-Time Registration (OTR) and fill out the online application on the new official portal of the Staff Selection Commission at https://ssc.gov.in.
