About This Recruitment

The Uttar Pradesh Subordinate Services Selection Commission (UPSSSC) has released Advertisement No. 15-Exam/2026 for recruitment to various technical and scientific posts within the Forensic Science Laboratory department under the Government of Uttar Pradesh. A total of 208 vacant posts are being filled through this competitive selection process to strengthen state forensic capabilities.

Shortlisting of candidates for the main examination will be strictly done based on their UPSSSC Preliminary Eligibility Test (PET 2025) valid score card. Applicants must also possess relevant academic credentials in disciplines such as Physics, Chemistry, Biology, Forensic Science, or Computer Science to fulfill basic post-level eligibility standards.

Candidates can register and complete their online application process through the official UPSSSC portal starting from 27 July 2026. The selection methodology involves PET score-based shortlisting followed by a written examination and document verification stages.

Eligibility and Qualification

  • Mandatory PET Requirement: Must possess a valid UPSSSC PET 2025 Score Card.
  • Academic Qualification: B.Sc. or M.Sc. Degree in the relevant discipline (Physics, Chemistry, Biology, Forensic Science, Computer Science, etc.) from a recognized university or institution.
  • Computer Science Stream Posts (if applicable): Bachelor's Degree in Computer Science from a recognized university OR 'A' Level Diploma from NIELIT.
  • Refer to the detailed official notification for specific post-wise educational eligibility mapping.

Selection Process

  1. Shortlisting of candidates based on UPSSSC PET 2025 Marks/Score Card.
  2. Written Main Examination for shortlisted candidates.
  3. Document Verification (DV) of shortlisted candidates.
  4. Final Medical Examination and Character Verification.

Exam Pattern

Detailed exam pattern, scheme of examination, subject-wise weightage, and syllabus details will be published by UPSSSC separately on its official website. Candidates are requested to regularly check the official portal or read the full notification PDF.

Documents Required

How to Apply

  1. Visit the official portal of UPSSSC at upsssc.gov.in.
  2. Click on "Applicant Segment" and choose "Candidate Registration".
  3. Select Advertisement No. 15-Exam/2026 for Forensic Science Laboratory Recruitment.
  4. Authenticate using UPSSSC PET 2025 Registration Details (through Personal Details or OTP).
  5. Fill in all required educational, personal, and category information accurately.
  6. Upload required scanned documents, photo, and signature following specified formats.
  7. Pay the required application fee of Rs. 25 online via available payment gateways.
  8. Review the application preview carefully and click on Final Submit.
  9. Download and print out the application confirmation page for future reference.
