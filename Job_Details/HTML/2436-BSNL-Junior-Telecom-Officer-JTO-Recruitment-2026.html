Selection Process & Training Structure

  1. 1

    Computer Based Test (CBT): All eligible applicants must undergo an objective type multiple choice examination consisting of a single consolidated paper spanning three sections.

  2. 2

    Sectional Shortlisting & Normalization: Normalization procedures will be executed across multi-session configurations if applicable. Selection requires meeting explicit sectional and aggregate marks cut-offs.

  3. 3

    Document Verification: Candidates positioned in the generated final merit lists are systematically subjected to comprehensive original certificate checks and verification processes.

  4. 4

    Surety Bond Execution: Candidates finalized for appointment must complete a service bond worth Rs. 6,000,000/- to serve the organization for at least 5 consecutive years.

  5. 5

    Pre-Appointment Training: Appointees will systematically navigate structured frameworks: JTO Phase-I Training (10 weeks), Field Training (4 weeks), and JTO Phase-II Specialisation Training (6 weeks). A maximum of 2 attempts is granted to qualify the training exams.

Written Examination Pattern & Syllabus

The single-shift test spans 3 hours total duration. For each correct response, 4 marks are added; for each wrong response, 1 mark is deducted (-1 Negative Marking).

Syllabus High-Level Structure

  • Section I (Engineering Stream): Materials and components, Structure and properties of Electronic Engineering materials, Passive components, Physical Electronics, Electron Devices and ICs, Network theory, Network analysis techniques, Electromagnetic Theory, Transmission lines, Electronic Measurements and instrumentation, and Power Electronics.
  • Section II (Engineering Stream): Analog Electronic Circuits, Digital Electronic Circuits, Logic families, Combinational logic circuits, Semiconductor memories, Control Systems, Communication systems (Modulation, Optical, Satellite), Microwave Engineering, Computer Engineering (Number Systems, Data structures, Processor architecture), and Microprocessors.
  • Section III (General Ability): Comprehension and simple exercises in General English, Current Events knowledge, everyday observations, developments in Telecommunications, History of India, and Geography.
