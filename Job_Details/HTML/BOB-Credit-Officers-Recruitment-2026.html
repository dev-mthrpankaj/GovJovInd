About This Recruitment

The Bank of Baroda recruitment advertisement targets skilled specialists to handle responsibilities under its Corporate and Institutional Credit (C & IC) vertical. Selected professionals are responsible for evaluating large or mid-corporate propositions, executing industry-wide risk assessments, managing credit appraisal cycles, and sustaining relations with high-value commercial borrowers. Because this structural recruitment brings candidates under regular executive pay levels, applicants from banking and financial institutions with a relevant history in processing credit components can utilize this track.

Interested individuals should note that the registration process requires uploading accurate bio-data documents along with supporting professional histories before submitting processing charges. Since candidates are liable to be deployed anywhere in India at the absolute discretion of the bank, only applicants ready to meet nationwide deployment criteria should advance their dynamic registration. Furthermore, keeping email IDs active is an essential rule as individual communication pieces regarding test structures, call letters, and presentation stages are executed digitally.

A crucial factor added to this specialized selection cycle is the assessment of applicant credit health. Candidates must meet standard financial background screenings, including a defined credit registry rating, to secure a formal appointment. Joining formalities are bounded by standard probation parameters and an operational security agreement framework to safeguard operational continuity. Candidates are encouraged to check age definitions and qualification baselines accurately before completing their fee payment transactions.

Eligibility and Qualification Standard

All qualifications must be obtained from verified universities or institutes recognized by regulatory authorities like AICTE or Government bodies. The parameters as on 01.06.2026 are defined as follows:

  • Basic Educational Background: Candidates must possess a Graduation Degree in any discipline combined with either a Post Graduate Degree or a Diploma specialized in Finance / equivalent stream. Alternatively, holding a standard professional CA, CMA, CS, or CFA certification makes an applicant eligible.
  • Manager - Credit Analyst (MMG/S-II) Experience: Mandatory minimum of 03 years of functional experience in credit evaluation under any Public, Private, or Foreign Bank, or an active NBFC operational in India.
  • Senior Manager - Credit Analyst (MMG/S-III) Experience: Minimum of 06 years of specialized credit work experience handled across active Banks or recognized Financial Institutions within India.
  • Chief Manager - Credit Analyst (SMG/S-IV) Experience: Minimum of 08 years of robust work background in Banks / Financial institutions, requiring a minimum exposure of 05 years inside large or mid-corporate credit appraisal cycles.
  • Senior Manager - C & IC Relationship (MMG/S-III) Experience: Minimum of 06 years of institutional work experience across Banks / Financial operations, with at least 02 years handled in addressing MSME, Mid, or Large Corporate Credit operations.
  • Chief Manager - C & IC Relationship (SMG/S-IV) Experience: Minimum of 08 years of functional work history in Banks / Financial Institutions, carrying at least 03 years of dedicated experience in supervising MSME, Mid, or Large Corporate credit interactions.
  • Clerical Track Restriction: Experience profiles built within clerical cadres will not be factored into eligibility calculations. Less than six months of experience in any organization shall be disregarded unless part of ongoing employment.

Selection Methodology

The bank holds complete discretionary control to adjust the parameters of assessment. The operational steps follow this structure:

  1. Application Scrutiny: Filtering of received submissions based on age metrics, accurate education records, and required experience parameters.
  2. Online Examination (Tentative Phase): Discretionary competitive testing structured to evaluate professional domain capacities and baseline mental tracking.
  3. Psychometric Evaluation: Personality profile check intended to trace communication skills, professional balance, and placement adaptability.
  4. Group Discussion (GD) / Personal Interview (PI): Interaction patterns designed to evaluate individual expertise. The qualifying benchmark for interviews is mapped at 60% for General applicants and 55% for reserved classes.
  5. Biometric Data Verification: Capture of thumb impressions or Iris configurations on testing or joining instances to check identity.
  6. Document Review: Verification of formal category sheets, original degrees, and verified industrial history certificates during individual interview presentations.
