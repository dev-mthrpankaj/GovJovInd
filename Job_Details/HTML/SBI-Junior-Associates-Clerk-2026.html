About This Recruitment

SBI Junior Associates Clerk Recruitment 2026 is an official recruitment drive conducted by the State Bank of India (SBI) to hire Junior Associates (Customer Support & Sales) under Advertisement No. CRPD/CR/2026-27/17. The recruitment covers 9124 openings across regular and backlog vacancies across various circles nationwide.

Interested and eligible candidates holding a Bachelor's degree in any stream can complete their online application through the official IBPS/SBI registration portal. The application window is open from 11 August 2026 to 31 August 2026. Applicants should ensure they meet the age criteria as of 01 April 2026 and complete the fee payment before the stated deadline.

The recruitment process features a comprehensive online selection structure including a Preliminary Examination followed by a Main Examination and a test of specified Local Language for shortlisted candidates. Candidates are advised to download the official notification PDF and carefully review state-wise vacancy distribution and local language requirements prior to submitting their online forms.

Eligibility and Qualification

  • Educational Qualification: Graduation Degree in any discipline from a recognized University or any equivalent qualification recognized as such by the Central Government.
  • Appearing Candidates: Candidates in the final year/semester of their Graduation may also apply provisionally, subject to passing proof as per the official notification timeline.
  • Language Proficiency: Knowledge of the specified local language of the applied state/circle is essential (reading, writing, speaking, and understanding).

Selection Process

  1. Phase-I: Preliminary Examination

    Objective test conducted online consisting of English Language, Quantitative Aptitude, and Reasoning Ability.

  2. Phase-II: Main Examination

    Online objective examination covering General/Financial Awareness, General English, Quantitative Aptitude, and Reasoning Ability & Computer Aptitude.

  3. Specified Local Language Test

    Test of specified local language for candidates who qualify the Main exam and do not produce proof of studying the local language in 10th or 12th standard.

  4. Document Verification & Medical Test

    Verification of original educational certificates, category credentials, identity proof, and fitness screening.

How to Apply

  1. Visit the official portal of SBI Careers at sbi.bank.in/web/careers/current-openings or the direct IBPS application portal.
  2. Click on "RECRUITMENT OF JUNIOR ASSOCIATES (CUSTOMER SUPPORT & SALES) 2026" and select "Apply Online".
  3. Click on "Click here for New Registration" and enter basic details such as Name, Mobile Number, and Email ID.
  4. Log in using the system-generated Registration Number and Password.
  5. Fill in personal, educational, category, and circle/state selection details carefully.
  6. Upload scanned images of Photograph, Signature, Left Thumb Impression, and Hand-written Declaration according to specified formats.
  7. Pay the required application fee through online payment mode (Credit/Debit Card, Net Banking, UPI).
  8. Verify all entered details and submit the application form. Download and print the confirmation page for future reference.

Documents Required

  • Recent passport-size colored photograph (scanned).
  • Scanned copy of signature on white paper with black ink.
  • Scanned image of Left Thumb Impression.
  • Hand-written Declaration as per the prescribed format in the official notice.
  • Graduation degree certificate and mark sheets.
  • Caste/Category certificate (SC/ST/OBC/EWS/PH) if claiming reservation.
  • Valid photo ID proof (Aadhaar Card, PAN Card, Voter ID, Driving License, or Passport).
