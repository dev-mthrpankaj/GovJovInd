About This Recruitment

The Sashastra Seema Bal (SSB) Constable Recruitment 2026 is intended to fill Group 'C' Non-Gazetted, Combatised positions within the military infrastructure of the force. Eligible male and female candidates can compete for positions varying from highly specialized roles like Veterinary assistants and heavy motor drivers to traditional support trades like Carpenters, Tailors, Gardeners, and Cook/Water Carriers. It offers a structured pathway into the Central Armed Police Forces for matriculate level aspirants.

This recruitment gives a distinct opportunity to serve anywhere across India or even beyond national borders under the structural framework of the SSB Act and operational guidelines. Applications are handled via a strict digital screening process, and submissions made offline are completely dismissed. Aspirants must track multi-phased rigorous evaluations ranging from continuous run tracking to skill demonstration to enter the final merit listings.

Understanding the balance between educational prerequisites and structural trade knowledge is vital. Certain profiles require direct experience certification or vocational ITI diplomas alongside general academic credentials. Aspirants should consult the verified summary parameters on this page to analyze which category perfectly complements their skills before initiating formal portal registration.

Eligibility and Qualification

  • Constable (Driver): Matriculation (10th) passed from a recognized board along with a valid Heavy Vehicle Driving License.
  • Constable (Veterinary): 10th Class passed with Science as a core subject, coupled with 1 year of formal work experience in an animal treatment framework at a recognized Veterinary Hospital.
  • Constable (Tradesmen - Gardener, Water Carrier, Cobbler, Tailor, Washerman, Barber, Waiter, Carpenter): Matriculation or equivalent with either 2 years trade experience OR 1 year ITI/Vocational certificate with 1 year experience OR 2 years ITI Diploma in the specific trade. Must clear the trade test.
  • Constable (Nursing Orderly): 10th pass with a valid First Aid Certificate from St. John's Ambulance Organisation or Red Cross Society of India, and 1 year experience as a Nursing Orderly in a minimum 50-bedded hospital.

Selection Process

  1. Stage I: Physical Efficiency Test (PET) & Physical Standard Test (PST): Evaluates physical efficiency metrics via scheduled runs and measures critical structural metrics like height and chest. Ex-servicemen are exempted from the running stage.
  2. Stage II: Written Examination: A 100-mark objective Computer Based Test (CBT) covering key evaluation fields. Final merit relies completely on written test marks.
  3. Stage III: Documentation & Skill/Trade Test: Verification of educational claims up to 20 times the vacancy count. Includes a mandatory 50-mark qualifying skill demonstration.
  4. Stage IV: Medical Review: Multi-layered Detailed Medical Examination (DME) and Review Medical Examination (RME) to confirm mental and clinical fitness.

Exam Pattern

Qualified candidates from the physical stages proceed to the Written Test matrix. The evaluation spans a 2-hour duration consisting of objective multiple-choice parameters:

Frequently Asked Questions

What is the total number of vacancies in SSB Constable Recruitment 2026?

There are a total of 827 tentative vacancies distributed across various trades like Driver, Veterinary, Gardener, and other tradesmen posts.

What is the qualification required for SSB Constable Driver?

Candidates must have passed 10th class (Matriculation) from a recognized board and must possess a valid Heavy Vehicle Driving License.

What is the application fee for SSB Constable recruitment?

The examination fee is Rs. 100 for UR, EWS, and OBC category candidates. SC, ST, Ex-Servicemen, and female candidates are exempted from paying the fee.

What is the selection process for these posts?

The selection process consists of Stage I: Physical Efficiency Test (PET) & Physical Standard Test (PST), Stage II: Written Examination (CBT), Stage III: Documentation and Skill Test, and Stage IV: Detailed Medical Examination (DME) / Review Medical Examination (RME).

What are the passing marks for the written examination?

The minimum qualifying marks are 50% for UR, EWS, OBC, and Ex-Servicemen candidates, and 45% for SC and ST candidates.

Is there any trade test for the positions?

Yes, tradesmen and technician posts require qualifying a trade/skill test of 50 marks with minimum 60% marks, which is qualifying in nature.

What is the pay scale for an SSB Constable?

Selected candidates receive a basic pay in Pay Matrix Level-3 (Rs. 21,700 - 69,100) along with admissible allowances.

How can I apply for SSB Constable posts?

Eligible candidates must apply online through the official application portal link provided by Sashastra Seema Bal during the application window.
