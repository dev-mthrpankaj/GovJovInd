About This Recruitment

UPSCIDC Junior Engineer Recruitment 2026 is an outsourcing job opportunity organized by the Uttar Pradesh State Construction & Infrastructure Development Corporation (UPSCIDC) under the UP Sewayojan Portal (Rojgar Sangam). A total of 06 vacant positions have been advertised for the post of Junior Engineer (Civil) across Uttar Pradesh.

Interested candidates holding a Bachelor's Degree in Civil Engineering or a 3-Year Diploma in Civil Engineering can apply online. The selection process is strictly managed through the official Sewayojan online application system. There is no application fee required for any candidate category.

Candidates must ensure that their job-seeker profile on the UP Sewayojan Portal is complete with updated educational and personal credentials before applying online prior to the last date of 13 August 2026.

How to Apply for UP Sewayojan UPSCIDC Junior Engineer Vacancy 2026

  1. Visit the official UP Rojgar Sangam (Sewayojan) Portal at sewayojan.up.nic.in.
  2. If you are a new user, click on "Jobseeker Registration" and complete your account creation using a valid email ID and mobile number.
  3. Log in using your registered User ID and Password.
  4. Complete your profile by entering personal details, educational qualifications (Civil Engineering Degree/Diploma), and uploading requisite certificates.
  5. Search for "UPSCIDC Junior Engineer (Civil)" under the Outsourcing Jobs section.
  6. Click on the "Apply" button corresponding to the UPSCIDC JE job post.
  7. Verify your application status and download/print the confirmation slip for future reference.

Documents Required

  • Passport size photograph and signature.
  • Aadhaar Card or Government Identity Proof.
  • Civil Engineering Degree Certificate / Polytechnic Diploma Marksheets.
  • High School (10th) Certificate for Date of Birth proof.
  • Category Certificate (SC/ST/OBC/EWS), if applicable.
  • Domicile Certificate of Uttar Pradesh.
  • Active UP Sewayojan Jobseeker Registration Number.
