Computer Based Test (CBT) Exam Pattern

  • The assessment comprises a single paper format consisting of 100 Multiple Choice Questions (MCQs).
  • Total maximum obtainable weightage is locked at 100 Marks (1 mark allocated per correct solution).
  • The total test duration allocated to solve the questionnaire is exactly 2 Hours (120 Minutes).
  • Negative Marking: There is a negative marking of 0.25 marks (1/4) deducted for every wrong answer.

Selection Process

  1. 1

    First Phase: Computer Based Test (CBT) written examination worth 100 marks across specified exam centers.

  2. 2

    Second Phase: Physical Efficiency Test (PET) and Physical Standard Test (PST) purely qualifying in nature.

  3. 3

    Third Phase: Document Verification (DV) check where all original educational, age, and caste sheets are validated.

  4. 4

    Final Phase: Comprehensive Medical Examination to verify absolute physical fitness before direct appointment orders.

How to Download Admit Card

  1. 1

    Navigate directly to the official digital portal of the Madhya Pradesh Employees Selection Board at esb.mp.gov.in.

  2. 2

    Select your language preference (English or Hindi) to access the main dashboard landing window.

  3. 3

    Locate the prominent active text reading "Admit Card - Van Rakshak & Jail Prahari Combined Recruitment Test 2026".

  4. 4

    Type your 13-digit Unique Application Number alongside your precise Date of Birth (DD-MM-YYYY format).

  5. 5

    Solve the basic verification security math expression and click the "Search" button.

  6. 6

    Your online PDF hall ticket will load on your display. Print at least two high-quality copies for your records.

Important Exam Day Instructions

  • Carrying a clear printed copy of the online hall ticket is mandatory for getting venue entry.
  • Candidates must bring a valid original Photo ID Proof (Aadhaar Card, E-Aadhaar with verification, PAN Card, Driving License, or Voter ID). Photocopies/Scanned versions are strictly invalid.
  • Biometric verification will take place before inside room entry; candidates with heavy mehendi or skin modifications must ensure smooth scanner scanning.
  • Prohibited item structures: Smart gadgets, calculators, electronic papers, cellphones, bluetooth transmitters, or ornament sets are totally banned inside.
  • The shift entry reporting gates shut precisely at the designated time bounds; no late entrants will be allowed entry under any pretext.
