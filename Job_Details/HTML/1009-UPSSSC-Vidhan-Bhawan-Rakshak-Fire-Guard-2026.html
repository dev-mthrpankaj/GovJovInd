About This Recruitment

UPSSSC Vidhan Bhawan Rakshak and Vidhan Bhawan Agnik/Fire Guard Recruitment 2026 is a Group C recruitment process connected with the Secretariat Administration Department in Uttar Pradesh. The notice lists vacancies for Council House Guard type security work and fire guard related posts. Candidates who want a state government job with a written exam, physical standards and PET 2025 score based shortlisting should read the complete notification carefully.

This recruitment is linked with Preliminary Eligibility Test 2025. The notification states that only candidates who appeared in PET 2025 and have been issued a score card can apply for the main examination process. Shortlisting for the written examination will be based on the PET 2025 normalized score, and candidates with zero or negative PET score will not be shortlisted for the main exam.

The selection is not limited to filling the online form. Applicants should be ready for the objective written exam, physical measurement standards and document verification rules. Category, reservation, certificate format, fee adjustment and correction instructions are important in this notice, so candidates should avoid last-day mistakes and keep their PET 2025 registration details, documents and certificates ready before submitting the application.

Eligibility and Qualification

  • Candidate must be a citizen of India as per the general eligibility rules in the notification.
  • Candidate should have appeared in UPSSSC PET 2025 and should have been issued a PET 2025 score card.
  • Candidate should have passed Intermediate from Uttar Pradesh Madhyamik Shiksha Parishad or an equivalent examination recognized by the government.
  • Other things being equal, preference may be given to a candidate who has served in the Territorial Army for at least two years or has obtained NCC B certificate, as mentioned in the notification.
  • Candidates must satisfy category, reservation, certificate and physical standard conditions applicable to the post.

Selection Process

  1. Online application through the UPSSSC website using PET 2025 registration details.
  2. Shortlisting for written examination on the basis of PET 2025 normalized score.
  3. Objective written examination for shortlisted candidates.
  4. Physical measurement test according to the post standards. The physical measurement stage is qualifying in nature.
  5. Document verification and checking of eligibility, reservation and certificates.
  6. Final merit preparation as per the rules mentioned in the official notification.

The notice says that category-wise 15 times candidates against the advertised posts may be shortlisted for the written examination, including all candidates at the final cut-off score up to two decimal places.

Exam Pattern

The written examination will have one objective multiple-choice question paper. Total questions will be 100, total marks will be 100 and the time duration will be two hours. For each wrong answer, negative marking will be 25% of the marks prescribed for that question.
