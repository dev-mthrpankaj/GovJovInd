About This Recruitment

The Indian Computer Emergency Response Team (ICERT / CERT-In) recruitment is an excellent national-level technical career opportunity for engineering and technology professionals seeking a stable Group A position in the government sector. Selected candidates are appointed to the highly regarded technical rank of Scientist B, where they handle specialized tasks related to computer security, incident handling, network monitoring, and cybersecurity policy implementation within the nation's critical digital infrastructure framework.

Applicants holding a four-year Bachelor's degree in Engineering or a relevant technical Master's degree can submit their profiles through the online central portal. As a mandatory screening stage, a valid score from the Graduate Aptitude Test in Engineering (GATE) is essential. Because selection depends heavily on the integration of gate scores and institutional performance parameters, checking specific category-wise guidelines will ensure candidate alignment prior to finalize the formal submission.

The processing architecture avoids conventional multi-stage written competitive tests by routing selection directly through objective criteria. Validated evaluation registers candidate scores dynamically across distinct technical streams, offering fair parity between recent graduates and experienced technical experts. Therefore, strategic candidates should concentrate on documentation matching, category validity, and interface compliance to secure their technical profile successfully within the national system.

Eligibility and Qualification

  • Bachelor's Degree in Engineering or Technology (Minimum 4-year course profile) from a recognized institute OR
  • Master's Degree in Science disciplines from a recognized university or board OR
  • Master's in Computer Application (MCA) degree qualification.
  • Mandatory Requirement: Must possess a valid score in the Graduate Aptitude Test in Engineering (GATE) for the years 2024, 2025, or 2026.

Selection Process

  1. Verification of registration profiles and submitted documentation details through the portal.
  2. Shortlisting of eligible candidates based strictly on their GATE Score in a specified ratio of 1:5.
  3. Discipline-wise and category-wise arrangement of candidate merit lists under equal score weightage guidelines.
  4. Personal Interview / Interaction phase for the shortlisted applicants.
  5. Final merit listing generated from active combined indicators and criteria parameters.

Exam Pattern

There is no standalone computer-based written exam paper managed by ICERT for this drive. Screening relies completely on technical parameters:

  • Equal score weightage is explicitly allocated across GATE 2024, GATE 2025, and GATE 2026 parameters.
  • Shortlisted candidates match specialized interview performance expectations prior to structural assignment.
