RSSB Rajasthan Teaching Associate Contractual Recruitment 2026

Rajasthan Staff Selection Board (RSSB / RSMSSB) has invited online applications for the recruitment of Contractual Teaching Associates under the Rajasthan College Education Society (Raj-CES) across various government colleges in Rajasthan.

Organization: Rajasthan Staff Selection Board (RSSB), Government of Rajasthan

Post Name: Teaching Associate (Contractual) - Equivalent to Assistant Professor roles

Advertisement No.: 06/2026 dated 30-04-2026

Apply Online (SSO Portal) Official Website

Recruitment Snapshot

RSSB Teaching Associate 2026 Details

  • RSSB has notified 3,540 vacancies for Contractual Teaching Associates distributed across 32 distinct subjects.
  • The positions are governed by the Raj-CES Hiring of Man Power Rules 2023 with a fixed initial engagement duration up to 5 years (extendable by 3 years based on satisfactory performance).
  • Only online applications through the Rajasthan Single Sign-On (SSO) Portal will be accepted. Candidates must complete One-Time Registration (OTR) to pay the registration fee.
  • Selection will be processed strictly via a Competitive Written Examination (CBT / TBT / OMR-Based format) followed by specialized Document Verification.

Selection Process Methodology

  1. 1

    OTR & Online Application: Registration and fee payment validation on the Single Sign-On (SSO) Portal.

  2. 2

    Written Examination: Appearance in a state competitive examination hosted via Computer Based Test (CBT), Tablet Based Test (TBT), or conventional OMR based format.

  3. 3

    Document Verification: Shortlisted candidates must present all original academic degrees, NET/SET certificates, identity, and domicile validation tracking documents.

  4. 4

    Character & Police Verification: Satisfactory assessment reports regarding applicant background checks.

  5. 5

    Medical Check-up & Engagement: Final execution of the formal five-year performance-monitored contractual employment tenure.
