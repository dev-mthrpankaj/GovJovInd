About This Recruitment

UP Anganwadi recruitment is not issued as one single combined state form. District Programme Officers publish notices separately for their district, project, gram sabha or urban ward vacancies. Because of this, the number of posts, reservation table and last date can be different for Meerut, Baghpat, Kannauj, Prayagraj, Bijnor, Muzaffarnagar and other districts.

The recruitment is meant for local level Anganwadi services under ICDS. Anganwadi Workers and Helpers support nutrition, pre-school activities, health awareness, immunization coordination and community-level child and women welfare work. Most notices require female candidates and local residence of the concerned gram sabha, ward, nyaya panchayat or project area.

Candidates should not rely only on social media posts or third-party summaries. First open the official UP Anganwadi portal, choose the correct post type and district, download the district notification, check the vacancy table and then submit the online form within the deadline.

Eligibility and Qualification

  • Only female candidates are generally eligible for Anganwadi Worker / Helper posts as per district notices.
  • For Anganwadi Worker posts, district notices commonly mention Intermediate / 10+2 or equivalent qualification.
  • Candidate should normally be a permanent resident of the concerned gram sabha, ward, nyaya panchayat or local area mentioned in the vacancy.
  • For widow, divorced, deserted women, BPL family and local-category priority rules, candidates should check the district PDF carefully.
  • Eligibility, documents and merit rules will be applied according to the government order and district recruitment notice.

Selection Process

  1. Online application submission on the official portal.
  2. District-wise scrutiny of applications and uploaded documents.
  3. Merit preparation as per educational qualification and applicable district rules.
  4. Preference / priority consideration for eligible local category candidates as per notice.
  5. Publication of provisional list or selection list by the district authority.
  6. Document verification and final approval as per Bal Vikas Seva Evam Pushtahar Vibhag rules.

Exam Pattern

No written examination pattern is notified in the verified district notices reviewed for UP Anganwadi Worker selection. Selection is mainly district-wise merit and document verification based. Candidates should still read the official PDF because district instructions and merit rules may vary by post and category.

Salary and Benefits

Anganwadi Worker and Helper posts are generally honorarium-based posts. Exact honorarium, allowances and service conditions should be verified from the latest official district notice and Government of Uttar Pradesh orders. Do not rely on unverified salary claims.

Documents Required

  • Recent passport size photograph.
  • Signature or required declaration upload.
  • High School certificate for date of birth.
  • Intermediate / 10+2 marksheet and certificate.
  • Residence certificate of relevant area, gram sabha, ward or district.
  • Category / caste certificate, if applicable.
  • Income certificate / BPL related document, if applicable.
  • Widow, divorced or deserted woman certificate / court order, if applicable.
  • Aadhaar card or valid ID proof.
  • Any other document listed in the district notification.

How to Apply

  1. Visit the official UP Anganwadi recruitment portal at upanganwadibharti.in.
  2. Select the correct post type such as Anganwadi Worker or Helper.
  3. Choose your district and download the official district notification PDF.
  4. Check project, gram sabha, ward, category and reservation details carefully.
  5. Confirm local residence, age, qualification and document requirements.
  6. Fill the online application form with correct personal, educational and local details.
  7. Upload clear and readable documents as required by the portal.
  8. Submit the form before the district-wise last date and save the final application printout.

Why Apply For This Recruitment

UP Anganwadi recruitment is useful for eligible local women who want to work in community-level child development, nutrition and women welfare services. The recruitment is district-wise and usually does not require a written examination, so candidates with correct local eligibility and complete documents should check their village or ward vacancy carefully and apply within the deadline.

Frequently Asked Questions

What is UP Anganwadi Recruitment 2026?

It is a district-wise recruitment process for Anganwadi Worker and Anganwadi Helper posts under Bal Vikas Seva Evam Pushtahar Vibhag, Government of Uttar Pradesh.

Which department conducts UP Anganwadi recruitment?

The recruitment is issued district-wise by the District Programme Officer under Bal Vikas Seva Evam Pushtahar Vibhag / ICDS, Uttar Pradesh.

What is the official website for UP Anganwadi online application?

Candidates should apply and check district-wise notices through the official portal upanganwadibharti.in.

Who can apply for UP Anganwadi Worker posts?

As per district notices, only female candidates are eligible and the candidate must normally be a permanent resident of the advertised gram sabha, ward or local area.

What is the qualification for UP Anganwadi Worker recruitment?

District notices mention Intermediate / 10+2 or equivalent qualification for Anganwadi Worker posts. Candidates must check their district notification for exact post-wise eligibility.

What is the age limit for UP Anganwadi Recruitment 2026?

Many district notices mention 18 to 35 years on the specified cut-off date. The cut-off date can differ by district notice, so candidates must verify it from their district PDF.

Is there any written exam for UP Anganwadi recruitment?

The district notices indicate selection on merit and document verification basis. No written exam is mentioned for the Anganwadi Worker selection in the verified district notices.

What is the application fee for UP Anganwadi recruitment?

Official district notices should be checked for fee details. Many public updates indicate no application fee, but candidates should rely on the district PDF and official portal before submitting.

Are vacancies the same for every district?

No. UP Anganwadi vacancies are released district-wise and project-wise. The number of posts, reservation and last date are different for each district.

Where can I check village or ward wise vacancy details?

Village, ward and project-wise reservation details are published in the district notification PDF or on the official UP Anganwadi recruitment portal.
