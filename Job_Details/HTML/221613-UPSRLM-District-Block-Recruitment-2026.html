About This Recruitment

UPSRLM Recruitment 2026 is for various technical and administrative roles at the District Mission Management Unit and Block Mission Management Unit levels. These positions are connected with field operations, digital monitoring, livelihood promotion and support work under the National Rural Livelihood Mission framework in Uttar Pradesh.

The notification clearly states that the deployment is on a pure outsourcing basis. Selected professionals will be employees of the designated outsourcing service provider agency, and the notification does not create any direct, regular, temporary or direct contractual employment relationship with the Government of Uttar Pradesh or UPSRLM.

This recruitment can be useful for candidates with qualifications in rural development, management, finance, IT, agriculture, commerce, data entry and related fields. Because qualification differs post-wise, applicants should carefully match their educational background with the exact post before filling the online form.

Eligibility and Qualification

  • District Mission Manager posts require post-wise relevant qualifications such as MSW, MA, MSc, MBA, PGDM, MCA, M.Tech, B.Tech, M.Com, CMA or other listed professional qualifications depending on the theme.
  • District Account Assistant requires Master's Degree in Commerce, Finance or Accounts in regular course from a recognized institute or university.
  • District Data Entry Operator cum MIS Assistant requires a graduate degree in any discipline with mandatory CCC Computer Certificate from NIELIT and operational typing proficiency in Hindi and English.
  • Block Mission Manager posts require post-wise graduate or professional qualifications such as graduate degree, BBA, BA Economics, B.Com, BCA, B.Sc. IT, B.Tech, PGDCA, agriculture or related qualifications depending on the post.
  • Block Account Assistant requires regular Bachelor's Degree in Commerce or Business Administration from a recognized institute or university.
  • Block Data Entry Operator cum MIS Assistant requires regular graduate degree with mandatory CCC Computer Certificate from NIELIT and knowledge of Hindi and English computer typing.
  • The notification states that no prior work experience is required for the listed positions, subject to post-wise educational eligibility.

Selection Process

  1. Initial Sewayojan Portal Screening of basic applications, educational pathways, qualifying fields, marks percentages and uploaded computerized certificates.
  2. Computer Based Test for shortlisted candidates.
  3. Practical skill assessment where applicable, especially for Data Entry Operator cum MIS Assistant posts.
  4. Merit listing or further process as per the category-wise minimum qualifying marks and post-wise rules mentioned in the notification.
  5. Document verification and final engagement process as per UPSRLM or outsourcing agency instructions.

The notification describes a Computer Based Test of 120 minutes with 100 objective type MCQs. The paper is divided into two equal parts.
