About This Recruitment

The Office of the Chief Electoral Officer, Uttar Pradesh, has engaged KND Engineers and Consultants (India) Private Limited to recruit Computer Assistants through the official UP Rojgar Sangam Sewayojan Portal. This engagement provides administrative and technical computer support for election-related workflows across various districts in Uttar Pradesh.

Candidates selected for these contractual positions will be assigned duty in key district administrative setups. Applicants must ensure that they have a registered jobseeker profile on the UP Sewayojan portal before submitting their application, as applications are accepted strictly through the online portal system.

To qualify, applicants must hold a recognized 10+2 Intermediate qualification, possess a valid NIELIT CCC Certificate, and demonstrate proficiency in both Hindi and English typing on computers. The recruitment selection relies on document verification, qualifying criteria checks, and interviews organized by the recruitment agency.

Eligibility and Qualification

  • Educational Qualification: Intermediate (10+2) passed from any recognized Board in India.
  • Computer Certification: CCC (Course on Computer Concepts) Certificate provided by NIELIT (formerly DOEACC).
  • Typing Proficiency:
    • Hindi Typing Speed: Minimum 25 Words Per Minute (WPM).
    • English Typing Speed: Minimum 30 Words Per Minute (WPM).

Selection Process

  1. Online Profile Shortlisting through UP Sewayojan Portal based on eligibility criteria.
  2. Verification of Educational, CCC, and Typing Certificates.
  3. Typing Speed Test (Hindi & English) conducted by the agency.
  4. Interview / Document Verification round.
  5. Final engagement order issuance by KND Engineers and Consultants Pvt. Ltd.

Exam / Skill Test Pattern

There is no written examination conducted by standard service commissions for this post. Selection is based on practical computer typing skills and document verification.

How to Apply

  1. Visit the official UP Sewayojan portal at sewayojan.up.nic.in.
  2. Register as a Jobseeker if you do not already have an account. Fill in personal, educational, and CCC qualification details.
  3. Log in to your Jobseeker dashboard using your User ID and Password.
  4. Navigate to the Outsourcing Jobs section and search for "Computer Assistant" under "KND Engineers and Consultants (India) Private Limited" or "Office of Chief Electoral Officer UP".
  5. Verify your profile details against the requirements (10+2, CCC Certificate, and Typing Speed).
  6. Click on the Apply button.
  7. Save or print the submitted application acknowledgement page for future reference.
