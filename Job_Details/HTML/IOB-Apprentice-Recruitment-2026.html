About This Recruitment

Indian Overseas Bank (IOB), a major Public Sector Bank headquartered in Chennai, has announced the recruitment drive for 750 Apprentice vacancies for the financial year 2026-27 under Advt No. HRDD/APPR/01/2026-27. This 1-year apprenticeship program aims to provide practical, hands-on training to young graduates in core banking operations, customer handling, administrative procedures, and digital banking services across various bank branches nationwide.

Candidates with a graduation degree in any discipline from a recognized university are eligible to apply. The application process requires prior registration on the NAPS/NATS portals followed by registration on the BFSI Sector Skill Council portal. Selected apprentices will receive a fixed monthly stipend based on their branch location, supported partially through Government Direct Benefit Transfer (DBT) under the apprenticeship scheme.

The engagement is strictly contractual for a duration of one year. It is important to note that apprenticeship does not constitute regular employment with Indian Overseas Bank, nor does it guarantee permanent absorption into bank services. However, it offers practical exposure to public sector banking operations and valuable experience for future career growth in the financial domain.

Eligibility and Qualification

  • Educational Qualification: Must possess a Bachelor's Degree (Graduation) in any discipline from a recognized University or equivalent qualification.
  • Graduation result must have been declared on or before 31 July 2026.
  • Proficiency in the local language of the specified State/UT applied for is required.
  • Candidates who have already completed or are undergoing apprenticeship in any organization are not eligible.
  • Candidates having formal work experience or training of 1 year or more after graduation are not eligible to apply.

Selection Process

  1. Online Written Test (CBT): Objective computer-based examination testing General/Financial Awareness, English, Quantitative Aptitude & Reasoning, and Computer Knowledge.
  2. Local Language Test: Test to verify proficiency (reading, writing, speaking, and understanding) in the specified local language of the chosen State/UT.
  3. Document Verification: Verification of original academic certificates, age proof, identity documents, and category certificates.
  4. Medical Examination: Basic physical and medical fitness test as per bank norms.

Exam Pattern

The online written test contains objective-type multiple-choice questions with a total duration of 90 minutes.

How to Apply

  1. First, register on either NAPS portal (apprenticeshipindia.gov.in) or NATS portal (nats.education.gov.in) to generate an enrolment number.
  2. Visit the official BFSI SSC portal or click on the Apply Online link on the official IOB website (iob.in).
  3. Click on "IOB Apprentice Recruitment 2026 Apply Online" link.
  4. Register using your basic details, NAPS/NATS enrolment number, valid email ID, and mobile number.
  5. Log in with your registration credentials and complete the application form accurately.
  6. Upload scanned photo, signature, and educational/category documents in prescribed formats.
  7. Pay the applicable registration fee online using UPI, Debit Card, Credit Card, or Net Banking.
  8. Review all details in the preview window and click submit. Download and print the confirmation page for record.

Why Apply For This Recruitment

The IOB Apprentice engagement offers fresh graduates a structured, practical entry point into the public sector banking domain. Working at Indian Overseas Bank provides hands-on experience in branch administration, digital financial services, customer interface, and core banking applications. Additionally, the fixed monthly stipend ensures financial support during training, and the official apprenticeship completion certificate serves as a valuable credential for future banking and administrative job opportunities.
