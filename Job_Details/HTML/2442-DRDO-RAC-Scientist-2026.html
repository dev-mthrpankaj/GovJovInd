Recruitment Snapshot

DRDO Lateral Recruitment 2026 Details

  • RAC invites online applications for 33 regular vacancies consisting of 25 Regular posts in the DRDS cadre and 08 Encadered posts in the DRDS cadre.

Eligibility / Experience / Qualifications

Candidates must hold at least a First Division Degree from recognized government institutions only. The required experience criteria must be fulfilled after attaining the essential educational qualifications.

Scientist 'C'First Division Bachelor's Degree in Engineering/Technology OR First Division Master's Degree in Science/Mathematics/Physiology as applicable.Minimum 3 Years of target field post-qualification experience in Research, Design, Development, or Production.

Experience Weightage Criteria for Higher Qualifications

Higher qualifications are equated to research experience and granted weightage under strict mandatory criteria:

  • Post Graduate Qualification in Engineering (ME/M.Tech): 2 Years weightage.
  • Post Graduate Degree in Medicine/Physiology (For Medical Post): 3 Years weightage.
  • Doctorate Degree in Science / Pharmacy (Ph.D.): 3 Years weightage.
  • Doctorate Degree in Engineering (Ph.D.): 4 Years weightage.
  • Note on overlapping: The period of active study must not overlap with professional job experience; overlapping periods are systematically removed.

Screening & Selection Process

  1. 1

    Administrative Screening: Initial review of user identities, age limits, mandatory First Division certificates, exact length of post-qualification experiences, and valid CCA declarations.

  2. 2

    Technical Screening (Level-1): An independent Technical Screening Committee evaluates the specific field relevance of job experiences and higher qualification matching profiles in a predefined ratio.

  3. 3

    Technical Screening (Level-2 / Preliminary Online Interview): If the count of eligible applicants is large, a 10-15 minute preliminary online interview is organized to narrow down the list.

  4. 4

    Final Personal Interview: Shortlisted individuals must appear in-person before the Selection Board at RAC, Delhi. No online or alternative interview modes are permitted.

  5. 5

    Final Merit Compilation: Final selection is strictly based on the performance in the Final Personal Interview. Candidates must score a minimum of 75% marks (75 out of 100) in the interview to be considered for merit listing.

Important Instructions

  • Only Indian Nationals are legally eligible to register and apply.
  • Experience certs must contain explicit designations, dates of joining/leaving, precise CTC figures, and clear operational work definitions signed by HR heads/Directors. Letters from Group/Project Heads are rejected.
  • Serving Government or Semi-Govt workers must upload a signed Cadre Controlling Authority (CCA) Declaration during form filling, and produce a structural original NOC at the interview stage.
  • Any disclosure of confidential organizational workflow patterns inside experience descriptions will lead to instant termination of candidature.
  • Any structural name mismatch with matching degree sheets necessitates the upload of a clear notarized affidavit during online application.
