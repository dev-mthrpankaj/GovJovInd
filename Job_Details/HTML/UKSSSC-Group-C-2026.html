About This Recruitment

UKSSSC Group C Recruitment 2026 is a direct recruitment drive by the Uttarakhand Subordinate Service Selection Commission for 553 vacancies across different departments of Uttarakhand. The advertised posts include Computer Assistant, Junior Assistant, Junior Assistant-cum-Data Entry Operator, Senior Assistant, Registration Clerk, Computer Operator, Telephone Operator, Assistant Mineral Supervisor, Land Acquisition Amin/Inspector, Store Keeper, House Mother, Survey Lekhpal and other Group C posts.

The online application window is scheduled from 08 September 2026 to 07 October 2026. Candidates should select the post carefully because educational qualification, typing or computer skill requirements, experience conditions and the minimum age can vary by post.

The written competitive examination is scheduled for 15 November 2026. Candidates should keep their High School certificate, Aadhaar card, reservation certificates and any post-specific experience or eligibility documents ready before starting the form.
