About This Recruitment

Security Printing & Minting Corporation of India Limited (SPMCIL), a Miniratna Category-I CPSE under the Ministry of Finance, Government of India, has published an official recruitment notification for executive positions under Advt No 02/2026. The positions available include Deputy Manager (IT Application Developer) as well as Assistant Managers across several core departments including Human Resources (HR), Finance & Accounts (F&A), Material Management (MM), Information Technology (IT), Official Language (OL), and Safety engineering.

Candidates interested in joining SPMCIL must fulfill the specified educational qualifications, professional certifications, and experience requirements detailed in the official notification. Eligible applicants need to register online through the designated exam portal, provide accurate personal and academic records, upload relevant certificates alongside photo and signature, and pay the required fee prior to the deadline on 24 August 2026.

Eligibility and Qualification

Educational eligibility criteria by post
Post Name Eligibility Criteria
Deputy Manager (IT) Application Developer 1st Class Full-Time B.E./B.Tech in CS/IT/Electronics & Communication with minimum 60% marks or equivalent CGPA. Relevant professional experience required.
Assistant Manager (HR) 1st Class Full-Time Master's Degree in PM & IR / MSW / MBA with HR elective OR 1st Class 2-year Full-Time PG Diploma in Management with HR elective.
Assistant Manager (F&A) Bachelor of Commerce (B.Com) degree along with CA / ICWA qualification.

Assistant Manager (OL) 1st Class Master's Degree in Hindi or English with English/Hindi subject at Graduation level OR 2 years experience in Hindi-to-English and vice-versa translation. Knowledge of Sanskrit/Modern Indian language and computer proficiency in Hindi is desirable.
Assistant Manager (Safety) Recognized Degree in Engineering or Technology with minimum 2 years supervisory factory experience AND a recognized Degree or Diploma in Industrial Safety.
