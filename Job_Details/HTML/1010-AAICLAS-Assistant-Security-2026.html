About This Recruitment

AAICLAS Assistant (Security) Recruitment 2026 is a contract-based engagement by AAI Cargo Logistics and Allied Services Company Limited, a wholly owned cargo subsidiary of Airports Authority of India. The company works in air cargo logistics and ground handling operations, and the notified positions are for airport-related operational support work.

The notification makes it clear that candidates should be willing to serve on an all-India basis. Although the current vacancy table mentions specific stations, AAICLAS may change posting locations or adjust the number of positions according to operational and administrative requirements. Candidates should therefore apply only if they are comfortable with transfer and airport operational duties.

This recruitment is useful for 12th pass candidates who meet the marks requirement and are ready for a practical operations-oriented job profile. The role includes assistance in airport operational work, passenger baggage handling, loading and unloading of baggage and other multitasking duties assigned from time to time. Candidates not willing to perform such physical and operational work should read the official notice very carefully before applying.

Eligibility and Qualification

  • Candidate must be a citizen of India.
  • 12th pass from any recognized Board / University / Institution.
  • Minimum marks: 60% for General candidates and 55% for SC/ST candidates.
  • Candidates must enter 12th percentage in numeric form in the online form, not in CGPA or grade format.
  • Ability to read or speak English, Hindi and/or local language is required.
  • Candidates should be willing to serve on all-India basis and perform airport operational duties including baggage handling, loading and unloading work.

Selection Process

  1. Submit the online application through AAICLAS official website before 02.07.2026.
  2. AAICLAS will examine eligibility and shortlist candidates as per vacancy/location requirement.
  3. The date, time and venue/mode of interaction will be communicated through registered email and AAICLAS website updates.
  4. Candidates will have to appear for interaction and produce original certificates/documents when asked.
  5. The notification states that candidates with higher percentage of marks at graduation level will be called first for online interaction in a phased manner, subject to vacancy availability.
  6. Selection will be done according to the merit list prepared on the basis of marks secured in interview/interaction as per the notification.

Exam Pattern

The official notification does not prescribe a written examination pattern for this engagement. It mentions interaction as the selection method, with communication through registered email ID and updates on AAICLAS website.

Job Profile / Skill Requirement

No separate physical standards, typing test or skill test table is provided in the notification. However, the job profile is operational and can involve physical work at airports.

  • Assistance in operational works at airport.
  • Assistance to passenger baggage handling.
  • Loading and unloading of baggage.
  • Work involving lifting of baggage from conveyor belt and x-ray machine.
  • Other multitasking operational work assigned from time to time.

Candidates not willing to perform such operational and baggage-related work should not apply, as clearly indicated in the notification.

Salary and Benefits

  • First year consolidated emoluments: Rs. 21,500/- fixed.
  • Second year consolidated emoluments: Rs. 22,000/- fixed.
  • Third year consolidated emoluments: Rs. 22,500/- fixed.
  • TA/DA/Lodging and Boarding on tour, if deputed, as per notice.
  • Leaves: 18 Privilege Leave in a year, 12 Half Pay Leave, 9 Casual Leave and 2 Restricted Holidays.
  • Medical insurance reimbursement up to Rs. 10,000 per annum for self and dependent family members on production of certified receipt/tax invoice.
  • Gratuity as per Gratuity Act, if/as applicable.

How to Apply

  1. Visit the AAICLAS official website at aaiclas.aero.
  2. Go to the Career section and find Advertisement No. 02 of 2026 for Assistant (Security).
  3. Open the Apply Now link against the advertisement.
  4. Fill the online application form carefully and choose only one location, because the notice says single application and single location only.
  5. Enter 12th marks percentage in numeric form only.
  6. Upload required photograph, signature and documents as per size and format instructions.
  7. Pay the application fee online through the available payment mode.
  8. Submit the form before 02.07.2026 and keep the confirmation details safe.
  9. Keep the registered email ID active because all recruitment communication will be sent through email and website updates.

Why Apply For This Recruitment

This recruitment can be useful for candidates who want airport-sector work under a well-known public-sector-linked cargo logistics company and are comfortable with a fixed term contract. The role is practical and operations-focused, so it may suit candidates who are disciplined, flexible with location, and ready to perform baggage and airport support duties.
