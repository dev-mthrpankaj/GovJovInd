About This Recruitment

The Railway Recruitment Cell, North Central Railway, Prayagraj has invited centralized online applications from qualified individuals for technical apprenticeship training slots for the academic year 2026-27. This training routine allows aspiring technicians to learn foundational vocational skills in industrial environments directly managed by North Central Railway divisions and workshops. The notification details multiple training opportunities spread across regional lines, specifically the Prayagraj, Agra, and Jhansi divisions along with the dedicated Jhansi Workshop and Track Machine Line operations.

Applications are managed solely via online submission platforms. Candidates possessing the fundamental educational metrics and necessary ITI trade credentials are encouraged to participate. Because this acts as a specialized training contract governed strictly under national apprentice frameworks, completing the stipulated duration gives candidates specialized trade familiarity but does not implicitly obligate Indian Railways to provide formal civil side appointments or regular employment absorption upon contract expiration.

A crucial advantage of this entry mechanism is its simplified selection hierarchy. Aspirants are categorized strictly on a multi-tier calculated merit list that averages prior benchmark academic scores without introducing complex written exams, formal physical screenings, or personal viva-voce assessments. The calculation takes into account both foundational school grades and matching technical certification performance numbers to map specialized trade line assignments transparently.

Eligibility and Qualification Requirements

Candidates must ensure they possess the prescribed valid qualification metrics finalized exactly on the notification issue date (07.07.2026):

  • Educational Profile: Must have cleared SSC/Matriculation/10th Standard exam routine or equivalent lines under the 10+2 framework.
  • Minimum Academic Cap: A minimum of 50% cumulative aggregate marks is mandatory for standard Matriculation evaluation.
  • Technical Qualification: ITI certification/National Trade Certificate matching the designated trade stream is compulsory.
  • Recognized Boards: Certificates must show formal validation from NCVT or SCVT structures approved by the Govt of India.
  • Ineligibility Clause: Individuals whose core metric or ITI final result evaluations are pending as on 07.07.2026 cannot apply. Engineering Graduates and standard Diploma holders are governed under separate provisions and are not eligible here.

Selection Process Details

The structured pipeline completely eliminates traditional testing parameters and relies instead on computerized shortlists:

  1. Merit Mapping: Compilation of average scoring percentages by adding absolute metric marks alongside absolute ITI marks, allowing equal evaluation weighting to both fields.
  2. Ties Resolution: Senior candidates by birth date get structural preference. If birth timelines match, earlier matriculation passing tracks are preferred.
  3. Shortlist Multiplier: Shortlisted aspirants are filtered and called for original Document Verification to the extent of 1.5 times the active vacancies.
  4. Medical Review: Submission of an authentic Medical Fitness Certificate signed by an authorised Government Gazetted Doctor.
  5. Final Unit Allotment: Division listings are assigned unit-wise, trade-wise, and community-wise in absolute descending scale order.

Training Environment, Terms & Stipend

Engaging in apprentice routines implies structured compliance parameters under central legal guidelines:

  • Training Period: Training spans a strict fixed contract duration of exactly 01 (One) year.
  • Stipend Payouts: Monthly stipends are provided throughout the active term, calculated using base state mandates and instructions from the Railway Board.
  • Premature Termination: If an apprentice leaves the program early for reasons within their control, they or their guardian must refund the training costs and stipends received.
  • No Hostel Accommodation: Candidates must arrange their own boarding, lodging, and transport during the training tenure.

Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)

What is the total number of vacancies in RRC Prayagraj Apprentice 2026?

There are a total of 1853 tentative slots notified for training across various divisions and workshops of North Central Railway.

What is the last date to apply online for RRC Prayagraj Apprentice recruitment?

The online application closing date and time is 07 August 2026 up to 23:59 hours.

What is the application fee for general candidates?

The application fee is Rs. 100, which is non-refundable. SC, ST, PwBD, and Female applicants are entirely exempted from paying any fee.

What are the educational qualifications required for this apprenticeship?

Candidates must have passed SSC/Matriculation/10th class examination or its equivalent with a minimum of 50% marks in aggregate, and must possess an ITI certificate in the relevant trade issued by NCVT/SCVT.

How is the merit list prepared for selection?

The selection is strictly based on a merit list prepared by taking the average percentage of marks obtained in both Matriculation (10th) and ITI examinations, giving equal weightage to both. There is no written test or viva.

What is the age limit criterion for applicants?

Applicants must be between 15 and 24 years of age as on 07.07.2026. Upper age relaxations apply for SC/ST (5 years), OBC (3 years), PwBD (10 years), and Ex-servicemen.

Are engineering diploma holders or graduates eligible to apply?

No, engineering graduates and diploma holders are not eligible to apply under this notification because they are governed by a completely separate apprenticeship scheme.

Does completing this training guarantee a permanent job in the Railways?

No, it is not obligatory for the employer to offer regular employment. Act Apprentices trained in railway establishments have no legal claim to seek automatic absorption or absorption rights after completion.
