About This Recruitment

South Indian Bank Limited, one of India's premier private sector commercial banks, has invited online applications for the post of Probationary Officer (CA) Scale-I. This specialized recruitment drive targets Chartered Accountants (CAs) who wish to build a corporate career in financial management, credit assessment, auditing, risk analysis, and corporate banking within the financial services sector.

Selected officers will be appointed in Scale-I and will undergo a structured probation period of 1 year. Confirmation in bank services is contingent upon satisfactory performance during probation. Candidates will be required to sign a service bond agreeing to serve the bank for a minimum period of 2 years or pay a stipulated agreement amount if leaving early.

The application procedure is strictly online via the official recruitment page of South Indian Bank. Candidates must complete registration, accurately enter their educational and ICAI qualification details, upload their recent passport-size photograph, signature, and detailed resume in PDF format, and submit the application prior to the deadline.

Eligibility and Qualification

  • Educational Qualification: Must be a passed Chartered Accountant (CA) from the Institute of Chartered Accountants of India (ICAI).
  • Membership / Registration: Candidates with valid ICAI registration or completion certificate are eligible.
  • Nationality: Candidate must be a citizen of India.
  • Language Skill: Proficiency in English and official local languages is desirable for administrative and operational banking tasks.

Selection Process

  1. Shortlisting: Initial screening of submitted online applications and resumes based on academic records and credentials.
  2. Group Discussion (GD): Shortlisted candidates may be called for a Group Discussion round to evaluate soft skills, general awareness, and decision-making capabilities.
  3. Personal Interview (PI): Final evaluation through a comprehensive personal interview by the bank's selection board.
  4. Document Verification: Verification of original educational mark sheets, CA membership certificates, category certificates, and identity proof.
  5. Medical Examination & Background Verification: Conducted prior to formal appointment.

How to Apply

  1. Visit the official portal of South Indian Bank at southindianbank.com and navigate to the Careers tab.
