Eligibility & Qualification

  • Educational Qualification: Graduate (Any stream) from any Recognized University.
  • Experience Required: Fresher to 2 Years of relevant experience.
  • Technical Skills: Hands-on experience with video editing tools (CapCut, VN, Adobe Premiere Pro) and basic graphic design skills (Canva, Adobe Photoshop).
  • Core Competencies: Good smartphone photography/videography, understanding of visual storytelling, framing, lighting, and short-form content (Reels/Stories). Ability to write engaging social media captions.
  • Other Requirements: Willingness to travel to client locations (schools) for content creation and photo/video shoots.

How to Apply

  1. Visit the official website of UP Rojgaar Sangam at rojgaarsangam.up.gov.in.
  2. Click on the "Social Media Content Creator - Reels & Graphic Design 2026 Apply Online" link.
  3. Register on the portal using a valid email ID and mobile number.
  4. Login using your newly generated registration ID and password.
  5. Fill the application form carefully with your personal, educational, and category details.
  6. Upload scanned documents including your recent photograph and signature in the specified format.
  7. Review all details, submit the application form, and download or print the confirmation page for future reference.

Documents Required

  • Recent passport-size colored photograph (scanned).
  • Scanned copy of signature.
  • Valid Email ID and Mobile Number.
  • Graduation degree certificate and mark sheets.
  • Valid photo ID proof (Aadhaar Card, PAN Card, Voter ID).
  • Relevant experience certificates or portfolio links (if applicable).
