Selection and Training Process

  1. 1

    Computer Based Examination (CBT): Objective multiple-choice testing comprising an Aptitude segment and a Core Technical segment.

  2. 2

    Merit Listing & Normalization: Preparation of a circle-wise/national level stream merit list based on performance. Normalization parameters will be applied if conducted in multiple sessions.

  3. 3

    Document Verification: Shortlisted candidates matching the cutoff margins will be scrutinized against their original transcripts, category certifications and NOCs.

  4. 4

    Medical Scrutiny: Appointments are provisionally granted subject to clearing the BSNL medical examination criteria standard parameters.

  5. 5

    Probation and Training: Selected personnel remain under probation for 2 years, running concurrently with a structured 52-week training protocol (Common Managerial Training: 13 Weeks, Functional Stream Training: 13 Weeks, On Job Training: 26 Weeks).

Detailed Scheme of Examination

The question structure follows a pattern of Multiple Choice Questions (MCQ) with negative marking. Every correct entry awards 1 mark, while every incorrect entry incurs a deduction of 0.25 marks.

1. SET (DR) - Telecom Syllabus Structural Scheme

Paper ComponentSubject Field ParticularsQuestions / Max MarksDuration
Part-I (Common)Aptitude Test (Quantitative Ability, Reasoning, Verbal Ability)40 Marks (40 Questions)180 Minutes Total
Part-II (Core)Technical Test (Telecom Principles, Wireless Communication, Data Networks, NGN, etc)160 Marks (160 Questions)
Total Consolidated Marks200 Marks (200 Questions)3 Hours

Technical Core Topics for Telecom (160 Questions):

  • Basic Telecommunications Concepts (15% Weightage): Signal Processing, Modulation/Demodulation, Multiplexing Techniques, Spectrum Management.
  • Wireless Telecommunications Technologies (15% Weightage): Antenna Principles, GSM, LTE, IP Multimedia Subsystem (IMS), 5G and industrial application use cases.
  • Wireline Telecommunications Technologies (15% Weightage): Soft Switches, Telecommunication Call Routing, Broadband Internet, FTTx, PSTN, SSTP, C4/C5 NGN Architecture.
  • Telecom Transmission Infrastructure (15% Weightage): Optical Fiber Technology, DWDM, OTN, FTTH, GPON, EPON.
  • IP/Data Networks (15% Weightage): Routing Protocols, OSI & TCP/IP models, Caching/Peering, IPv4/IPv6 subnetting, tunneling, MPLS.
  • Emerging Technologies (15% Weightage): IoT, M2M, Block Chain, Software Defined Networks, Cloud Computing, Data Centers, AI in Telecom.
  • Project Management (10% Weightage): Project selection, planning, management tools (Gantt Chart, PERT, CPM).

2. SET (DR) - Finance Syllabus Structural Scheme

Paper ComponentSubject Field ParticularsQuestions / Max MarksDuration
Part-I (Common)Aptitude Test (Quantitative Ability, Reasoning, Verbal Ability)40 Marks (40 Questions)180 Minutes Total
Part-II (Core)Technical Test (Advanced Accounting, Taxation Rules, Cost Accounting, Financial Management)160 Marks (160 Questions)
Total Consolidated Marks200 Marks (200 Questions)3 Hours

Technical Core Topics for Finance (160 Questions):

  • Advanced Accounting (15% Weightage): Financial Projections, Valuations, Depreciation, Accounting for Fixed Assets, Consolidation, Buyback of Securities, Ind AS / GAAP compliance.
  • Taxation Rules (15% Weightage): Indirect Taxes (GST Laws, computation of liability, input tax credit, composition levy), Direct Taxes (computation of income, advance tax, TDS).
  • Cost Accounting (15% Weightage): Preparation of cost sheets, material/employee costing, Break Even Analysis, Marginal Costing, Budgetary Controls.
  • Financial Statement Analysis (15% Weightage): Performance, profitability, liquidity ratios, financing and investment analysis.
  • Corporate & Other Law (15% Weightage): Incorporation of companies, prospectus, allotment of securities, share capital, debentures, SEBI regulations.
  • Audit & Assurance (15% Weightage): Internal controls, engagement standards, risk assessment, fraud responsibilities, audit in automated environments.
  • Accounting Receivable Management (10% Weightage): Credit policy, credit analysis, receivable turnover ratio, dunning protocols.

Minimum Qualifying Cut-Off Thresholds

  • General / Unreserved Candidates: Requires minimum 40% individually in Part-I and Part-II, along with an aggregate combined minimum score of 50%.
  • SC / ST / OBC / PwBD / EWS Candidates: Requires minimum 35% individually in Part-I and Part-II, along with an aggregate combined minimum score of 45%.
