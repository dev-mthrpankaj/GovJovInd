About This Recruitment

The Staff Selection Commission is conducting an open competitive nationwide examination for direct registration into the posts of Stenographer Grade 'C' (classified as Group 'B', Non-Gazetted) and Stenographer Grade 'D' (classified as Group 'C'). These openings are distributed across various Ministries, central departments, attached formations, and legal bodies under the Union Government located throughout different States and Union Territories. This opportunity is exclusively directed towards individuals who possess specialized abilities and mechanical expertise in shorthand dictation and professional transcription metrics.

How to Apply Online via the New SSC Portal

  1. Navigate to the main Staff Selection Commission gateway located at ssc.gov.in.
  2. Click on the Login or Register prompt to initialize your unique profile under the updated One-Time Registration (OTR) system. Note that older registration IDs from ssc.nic.in are not functional here.
  3. Fill in your biographical parameters, configure your security credentials, choose your category track, and enter your permanent address details.
  4. Log in using your newly generated OTR sequence and navigate to the Live Exam tab. Select the Stenographer Grade 'C' & 'D' Examination, 2026 link.
  5. Provide your highest educational background details and confirm your geographical preferences for the examination centers (you must choose 3 distinct locations).
  6. Position yourself in front of your camera when prompted by the application script. Capture a well-lit, clear live photograph that complies with the official guidelines.
  7. Upload your horizontally aligned, clear signature file.
  8. Preview your filled fields using the comprehensive data layout view. Verify accuracy, confirm the declaration statement, and submit the form.
  9. Complete your payment step via online transaction windows if you are not exempted from the application fee. Print your confirmation receipt for your personal records.

Why Apply For This Recruitment

This recruitment path provides a direct access point to specialized corporate desk roles within the core administrative machinery of the Union Government for candidates who have completed their intermediate level training (12th standard). Unlike conventional field jobs, stenographers handle vital office environments, manage executive schedules, and support confidential operational processing for various high-level departments. This career track features structured promotion paths, category-specific pay matrices, and options for global placement. It offers a stable environment where technical skill can serve as a substitute for advanced academic credentials.

Frequently Asked Questions

What is the total number of vacancies for SSC Stenographer 2026?

There are approx 731 tentative vacancies available, which will be updated post-wise and category-wise on the commission's official website in due course.

What are the online registration dates for this exam?

Candidates can submit online applications from 24-04-2026 to 15-05-2026 up to 23:00 hours.

What is the educational qualification required?

Candidates must have passed 12th standard or an equivalent examination from a recognized Board or University as on or before the cut-off date of 01-08-2026.

Is there any negative marking in the Computer Based Examination?

Yes, there is a negative marking of 0.25 marks for each incorrect answer in the objective test.

What is the application fee for reserved categories?

Women candidates and candidates belonging to Scheduled Castes (SC), Scheduled Tribes (ST), Persons with Benchmark Disabilities (PwBD), and Ex-servicemen (ESM) are completely exempted from payment of the fee.

What are the age bounds for Stenographer Grade C and Grade D?

As on 01-08-2026, the age limit for Grade C is 18 to 30 years, and for Grade D is 18 to 27 years, with upper age relaxations applicable category-wise.

When is the Computer Based Examination scheduled?

The scheduled window for the Computer Based Examination is July / August 2026.

What is the transcription time for Stenographer Grade D in English?

The regular transcription time allotted on the computer for Stenographer Grade D in English is 50 minutes.

Where can I find the official help desk contact information?

You can use the toll-free helpline number 1800 309 3063 provided by the commission for application queries.

Are female candidates allowed to apply for the Border Roads Organization (BRO)?

No, the official notice specifies that only male candidates are eligible for the vacancies reported for the post of Stenographer Grade D in the Border Roads Organization (BRO).
