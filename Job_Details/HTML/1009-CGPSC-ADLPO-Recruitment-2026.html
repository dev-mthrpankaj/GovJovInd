About This Recruitment

CGPSC Assistant District Public Prosecution Officer Recruitment 2026 is for direct recruitment under the Chhattisgarh Home Department. The post is a law-related prosecution service post and is suitable for candidates who have completed a Law Graduate degree from a recognized university and want to work in the public prosecution system of the state.

The recruitment is conducted by Chhattisgarh Public Service Commission. Applications will be accepted only through online mode on the official CGPSC portal within the notified dates. Manual applications or applications sent by post are not accepted as per the notification.

Candidates should read the official advertisement carefully before applying because admission to the examination is provisional. The commission checks eligibility documents before interview or at the required stage, and candidature can be cancelled if any eligibility condition is not fulfilled.

Eligibility and Qualification

  • Candidate must be an Indian citizen or belong to categories recognized by the Government of India as mentioned in the notification.
  • Candidate must possess a Law Graduate degree from a recognized university.
  • The required educational qualification certificate must be possessed by the last date of online application.
  • No certificate needs to be uploaded with the online application unless the portal specifically asks for it.
  • Reserved category, local resident, PwD and other relaxation claims must be supported by valid documents at the verification stage.

Selection Process

  1. Online application submission through CGPSC official website.
  2. Objective type examination for eligible applicants.
  3. Shortlisting for interview as per commission rules.
  4. Interview carrying 30 marks.
  5. Document verification before interview or at the notified stage.
  6. Final selection according to examination, interview and official selection rules.

Exam Pattern

The notification mentions a two-stage selection: examination and interview. The examination carries 300 marks and the interview carries 30 marks, making the total selection marks 330.

Salary and Benefits

  • Post: Assistant District Public Prosecution Officer.
