Eligibility & Age Limit

Educational Qualification

Candidates must hold a graduate degree or an equivalent qualification recognized for the Civil Services Examination. Candidates whose qualifying examination result was awaited were required to satisfy the documentary conditions prescribed by UPSC for later stages.

Basic Age Limit

Minimum: 21 years

Maximum: 32 years

Category-wise and other permitted age relaxations apply as provided in the official examination notice.

Attempts

The number of permitted attempts varies by category. Candidates should refer to the official CSE 2026 notification for the attempt rules applicable to their category and circumstances.

Examination Fee

UPSC prescribes examination fees and category-based exemptions in the official Civil Services Examination notification. Candidates should use the exact fee and exemption rules shown in the official notice and the UPSC application portal for the relevant stage.

Selection Process

  1. 1

    Civil Services Preliminary Examination: Objective screening examination consisting of General Studies papers.

  2. 2

    Civil Services Main Examination: Written descriptive examination for candidates who qualify the Preliminary Examination.

  3. 3

    Personality Test / Interview: Candidates qualifying the Main written examination are called for the interview/personality test according to UPSC rules.

  4. 4

    Final Allocation: Final ranking, service allocation, category rules and medical/other requirements are applied as prescribed by UPSC and the Government.

What Qualified Candidates Should Do

  • Use the official Civil Services Main Examination page to check the latest timetable, e-Admit Card and examination instructions.

  • Keep checking the UPSC website for subsequent Main result and interview-stage notices.
