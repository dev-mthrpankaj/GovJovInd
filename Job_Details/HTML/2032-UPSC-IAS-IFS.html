Eligibility, Age & Attempts

Education

Graduate degree or equivalent qualification recognized for the Civil Services Examination.

Age

21-32 years on the notified reference date, with applicable category relaxations.

Attempts

General/EWS ordinarily 6 attempts; OBC ordinarily 9; SC/ST attempts subject to age limits. PwBD rules vary by category.

Identity

Follow UPSC photo-ID and admission-certificate requirements at each stage.

Syllabus Highlights

Prelims GS

Current events, history, geography, polity and governance, economy, social development, environment and general science.

CSAT

Comprehension, reasoning, decision making, basic numeracy and data interpretation.

Main General Studies

Heritage/history/society/geography; governance/Constitution/IR; economy/technology/environment/security; ethics/integrity/aptitude.

Optional Subject

One optional subject selected from UPSC's notified list for Papers VI and VII.
