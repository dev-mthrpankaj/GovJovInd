About This Recruitment

The South East Central Railway (SECR) Nagpur Division and Motibagh Workshop has opened online applications for eligible technical aspirants seeking to complete vocational training as apprentices. This engagement is regulated by the Apprentices Act 1961 and Apprenticeship Rules 1992, providing structured practical exposure across diverse trades such as Fitter, Electrician, COPA, Welder, and Diesel Mechanic. Candidates belonging to different parts of India who possess a verified ITI pass certificate are invited to apply for training slots designated for the 2026-27 annual batch.

The registration process is hosted entirely on the National Apprenticeship Promotion Scheme (NAPS) portal. Interested applicants must complete their profiles, fill in precise educational scores, and map their application to the designated SECR establishment codes. Entering incomplete data or neglecting to provide matriculation or ITI grade points in the system will lead to automatic rejection. It is an excellent opportunity for technical graduates to gain foundational experience in Indian Railways workshops, preparing them for prospective core engineering careers.

Applicants must clearly understand that this engagement scheme does not symbolize a regular public employment recruitment. The official rules emphasize that concluding the apprenticeship training period places no dynamic obligation on the railway administration to extend permanent service or recruitment advantages. The selection focuses entirely on academic parity, verification of original community status certificates, compliance with the benchmark physical health criteria, and clearing document verification boards.

Eligibility and Qualification

  • Academic Criteria: Candidates must clear Matriculation (10th standard exam) or its equivalent structural setup with a minimum of 50% aggregate marks.
  • Technical Criteria: Candidates must hold a valid National Trade Certificate (ITI) in the respective trade issued by the NCVT or a Provisional Certificate issued by NCVT/SCVT.
  • Mandatory Entry Rule: It is mandatory to upload precise 10th and ITI grade/marks figures on the NAPS platform. Forms downloaded with blank marks entries will be rejected.
  • Exclusion: Engineering Graduates and Diploma Holders are ineligible to apply under this notification scheme. Candidates who have already undergone apprenticeship training previously are also restricted.

Selection Process

  1. Merit Compilation: Selection is formulated through a centralized merit list based on the calculated percentage of scores in Matriculation (min 50% aggregate) and ITI trade certificates.
  2. Grade Normalization: For alternative boards lacking clear aggregate standards (e.g., Andhra Pradesh, Kerala), the midpoint of the range of grades awarded will be extracted for average mapping.
  3. Tie-Breaking Resolution: In cases where two candidates post identical combined metrics, the candidate older in age will be preferred. If structural birth dates match, the applicant who passed matriculation earlier is prioritized.
  4. Document Verification: Shortlisted applicants must produce all original technical, identification, and category certificates before verification teams.
  5. Medical Examination: Candidates must clear standard Medical Tests certified by an authorized Gazetted Government Doctor, not below the rank of Assistant Surgeon.

Exam Pattern

There is no written competitive examination or interview scheduled for this recruitment. Selection relies completely on transparent scholastic records. Candidates should double-check their portal entries to avoid automated disqualification.

Salary and Stipend Benefits

  • Selected apprentices will be provided a monthly financial stipend during the training tenure as per standard rules governed by the Central Apprenticeship Council.
  • Duration: The active apprenticeship contract duration is fixed for 1 (one) year only.
  • Accommodation Note: No residential housing or hostel facility will be arranged by the railway board. Candidates must manage their own living arrangements during training.

Documents Required for Verification

  • Valid 10th Standard Certificate / Marksheet (confirming Date of Birth).
  • ITI National Trade Certificate or Provisional Certificate issued by NCVT/SCVT.
  • Recent passport-sized photograph and structural signature scan uploaded on the NAPS portal.
  • Community/Caste Certificate (OBC candidates must produce latest central format caste validation documents).
  • Disability Certificate (PWBD applicants) issued by a valid Central or State constituted Medical Board.
  • Discharge Summary or Service Proof documentation for candidates claiming Ex-Servicemen quotas.

How to Apply

  1. Navigate to the official national apprenticeship web portal at apprenticeshipindia.gov.in.
  2. Create an account profile and fulfill profile verification fields to achieve 100% registration completion status.
  3. Ensure matriculation aggregate marks and exact ITI scores are logged accurately within the technical qualification section.
  4. Search for training opportunities mapping to your specific ITI trade discipline.
  5. Apply to your targeted division by entering the establishment codes: E05202702695 for Nagpur Division or E05202702494 for Motibagh Workshop.
  6. Verify all parameters and execute final submission before the closing hours on 18 June 2026.

Why Apply For This Recruitment

Enrolling in an apprentice program within Indian Railways offers trade certificate holders structural exposure to massive engineering systems, locomotives, and production techniques. It shapes practical technical foundations, helping young technical workers develop standard workplace confidence. Completing this one-year training phase equips candidates with a valuable skill certificate, bolstering their core profile credentials for upcoming mechanical, public, or corporate technician job applications nationwide.

Frequently Asked Questions

What is the last date to apply for SECR Nagpur Apprentice 2026?

The last date for submission of the online application is 18 June 2026 up to 23:59 hours.

How many total vacancies are available in this recruitment?

There are a total of 1079 slots available, with 977 posts in the Nagpur Division and 102 posts in the Motibagh Workshop.

What is the application fee for SECR Apprentice 2026?

The official notification does not mention any application fee, meaning candidates can apply online without a fee on the apprenticeship portal.

What is the age limit required for this engagement?

Candidates must be between 15 to 24 years of age as on 19.05.2026, with relaxation rules applicable for reserved categories.

What is the educational qualification required?

Candidates must be Matriculation (10th) passed or equivalent with a minimum of 50% marks, and possess an ITI National Trade Certificate in the notified trade.

What is the selection process for SECR Apprenticeship?

The selection will be based entirely on a merit list prepared using the average percentage of marks obtained in Matriculation (minimum 50%) and ITI trades.

What is the duration of the training period?

The duration of the training period for the selected trade apprentices will be 1 (one) year only.

Are engineering graduates or diploma holders eligible to apply?

No, engineering graduates and diploma holders are not eligible to apply under this notification as they are governed by a separate apprenticeship scheme.

Where can candidates apply online?

Eligible candidates must apply online through the official NAPS portal at https://www.apprenticeshipindia.gov.in.
