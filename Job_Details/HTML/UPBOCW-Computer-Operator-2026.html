About This Recruitment

The Uttar Pradesh Building and Other Construction Workers Welfare Board (UPBOCW) is conducting a recruitment drive to fill Computer Operator vacancies. This outsourcing recruitment is being managed through the UP Sewayojan (Rojgar Sangam) Portal, bringing a total of 84 posts for qualified candidates across various districts of Uttar Pradesh.

Interested job seekers who meet the eligibility criteria can complete their online application from 24 August 2026 to 01 September 2026. This recruitment is a great opportunity for candidates with a Bachelor's degree and typing skills looking for a secure job within the state. There is no application fee required to apply for these posts.

Candidates are highly encouraged to carefully read the official notification to understand the complete selection process, district-wise job locations, and the required documentation before submitting their forms on the UP Sewayojan Portal.

There are a total of 84 Posts available for the Computer Operator position. Job locations include multiple districts across Uttar Pradesh such as Basti, Siddhartha Nagar, Saharanpur, Muzaffarnagar, Meerut, Agra, Mathura, Varanasi, Gorakhpur, Lucknow, Prayagraj, and more.

Eligibility and Qualification

  • Education: Bachelor Degree in Any Stream from a recognized University or Institute in India.
  • Computer Certification: Valid CCC Certificate from DOEACC/NIELIT or an equivalent government-recognized certificate.
  • Typing Skills: Proficiency in Hindi and English Typing at a speed of 25 words per minute (wpm).
  • Software Knowledge: Working knowledge of MS Word and MS Excel is mandatory.

How to Apply

  1. Visit the official website of UP Rojgar Sangam at sewayojan.up.nic.in.
  2. Look for the "UP Rojgar Sangam UPBOCW Computer Operator 2026 Apply Online" link on the dashboard or recruitment section.
  3. Register on the Sewayojan portal using a valid email ID and mobile number if you are a new user.
  4. Login using your newly created Registration ID and Password.
  5. Fill the application form carefully with your personal, educational, and category details.
  6. Upload all required scanned documents (photo, signature, etc.) in the correct format.
  7. Submit the form (No application fee is required). Download and print the confirmation page for future records.

Documents Required

  • Recent passport-size colored photograph (scanned).
  • Scanned copy of candidate signature.
  • Bachelor's Degree certificates and mark sheets.
  • Valid CCC Computer Certificate.
  • Caste/Category certificate (if applicable).
  • Valid photo ID proof (Aadhaar Card, PAN Card, Voter ID, etc.).
