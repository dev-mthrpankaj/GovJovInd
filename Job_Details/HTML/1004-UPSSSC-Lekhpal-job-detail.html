Important Information

UPSSSC has invited online applications for 7994 Lekhpal posts under the Board of Revenue, Uttar Pradesh.

Only candidates who appeared in UPSSSC PET 2025 and have a valid PET 2025 score card can apply.

Shortlisting for the Main Examination will be done on the basis of PET 2025 normalized score.

Candidates with zero or negative marks in PET 2025 will not be shortlisted for the Main Examination.

Eligibility & Age Limit

Educational Qualification

Candidate must have passed Intermediate / Class 12 from the Board of High School and Intermediate Education, Uttar Pradesh, or any equivalent qualification recognized by the Government.

Preferential Qualification

Preference may be given to candidates who have served at least 2 years in Territorial Army or have obtained NCC “B” Certificate.

Age Limit as on 01-07-2025

Minimum Age: 18 Years

Maximum Age: 40 Years

Age Relaxation: Extra as per UPSSSC / Uttar Pradesh Government rules.

PET 2025 Requirement

Only candidates included in Preliminary Eligibility Test PET 2025 with a valid score card are eligible to apply.

Selection Process

Selection Basis

1. Shortlisting on the basis of UPSSSC PET 2025 normalized score

2. UPSSSC Lekhpal Main Written Examination

3. Document Verification

4. Final selection / further process as per Commission rules

Important PET Score Rule

Candidates having zero or less than zero / negative marks in PET 2025 will not be shortlisted for the Main Examination.

Main Exam Pattern

Total Questions: 100 Objective Type Questions

Total Marks: 100 Marks

Time Duration: 02 Hours (120 Minutes)

Negative Marking: 25% of the marks prescribed for that question will be deducted for each wrong answer.
