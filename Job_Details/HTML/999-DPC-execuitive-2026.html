Selection Process Workflow

  1. 1

    Computer Based Examination (CBE): Objective type multi-choice paper conducted across India.

  2. 2

    Physical Endurance & Measurement Test (PE&MT): Qualifying stage checking height, chest expansion, running speed, long jump, and high jump capabilities.

  3. 3

    Medical Examination: Medical standards evaluation by authorized government surgeons to declare fitness.

  4. 4

    Document Verification: Reviewing original education certificates, category status documents, and driving licenses.

Crucial Instructions for Candidates

  • Passing the computer based test merely qualifies a candidate to sit for the physical examinations.
  • Male candidates who fail to present an authentic LMV Driving License at the time of PE&MT will face instant disqualification.
  • Candidates under reserved categories must ensure they possess certificates conforming strictly to the central government layout formats.
  • Maintain an active verification status on the official SSC dashboard portal to obtain individual scorecards and response sheets when released.
