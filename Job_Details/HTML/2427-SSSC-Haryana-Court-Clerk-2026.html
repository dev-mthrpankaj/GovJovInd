    Selection Process

    1. Computer Based Test (CBT): Initial filtering via a synchronized online assessment including both objective multiple-choice blocks and descriptive English typography tests.
    2. Cross-Sectional Filtering: Evaluation of descriptive subjective papers occurs exclusively for applicants who achieve a minimum threshold of 33% marks within the General Knowledge module.
    3. Computer Proficiency Test (CPT): Mandatory qualifying round checking practical application parameters across spreadsheets and text layouts.
    4. English Typing Test: Achieving a professional formatting target speed of 30 Words Per Minute (WPM) on computerized keyboards.
    5. Testimonial Authentication: Shortlisted candidates undergo original paper verification checkpoints alongside structural interactions.
    6. Final Selection Order: Compiled purely on the absolute normalized performance order recorded in the primary online Computer Based Test (CBT).

    Computer Proficiency & Skill Tests

    Candidates clearing the main written test will face the qualifying Computer Proficiency Test (CPT) which contains two segments:

    • Part-I: Spread Sheet Evaluation: Conducted for 10 minutes containing a maximum value of 10 marks. Candidates must score a minimum of 4 marks (40%) to pass.
    • Part-II: Word Processing & Typing Test: Checked only if a candidate passes Part-I. Requires typing in English at a verified processing speed of 30 Words Per Minute (WPM).
    • The practical speed is derived using the standard formula:
      $$Speed = \frac{\text{Number of words typed} - \text{Mistakes}}{10}$$
    • Locomotor disabled individuals with Both Arms Affected (BA) or certified individuals with specified physical typing limitations can request formal typing exam exemptions by uploading relevant medical certificates from a civil surgeon within 15 days of the written result announcement.

    Normalization Framework

    When the Computer Based Examination is arranged across multiple technical shifts, the mathematical balancing of scores within the objective module is handled using standard deviation calculations up to 5 decimal slots via the following equation:

    $$\tilde{M}_{ij}=\frac{\overline{M}_{t}^{g}-M_{q}^{g}}{\overline{M}_{ti}-M_{iq}}(M_{ij}-M_{iq})+M_{q}^{gm}$$

    This formula dynamically balances variable paper difficulty levels across multiple examination sessions to protect candidate merit integrity.

    Frequently Asked Questions

    What is the last date to apply for SSSC Haryana Court Clerk Recruitment 2026?

    The last date for submission of online applications is 23 June 2026 up to 04:00 PM as per the official notification.

    How many vacancies are advertised for Clerk posts in Haryana Subordinate Courts?

    There are a total of 1265 vacancies, which include 1015 posts as on 31.12.2025 and 250 anticipated posts up to 30.06.2026.

    What is the essential educational qualification required for this post?

    Candidates must possess a Bachelor of Arts, Bachelor of Science, or equivalent degree from a recognized university, along with computer proficiency in Word Processing and Spread Sheets. Additionally, matriculation must be passed with Hindi or Sanskrit as a subject.

    What is the age limit for general category candidates?

    The minimum age limit is 18 years and the maximum age limit is 42 years as on 01.01.2026, with relaxations applicable for reserved categories.

    The selection process involves a Computer Based Test consisting of Objective and Subjective sections, followed by a Computer Proficiency Test comprising a Spread Sheet Test and an English Typing Test.

    The total fee for general/unreserved male applicants is Rs. 1200, which includes Rs. 550 online facilitation charges and Rs. 650 examination fee.
