About This Recruitment

Income Tax Canteen Attendant Recruitment 2026 notification has been released by the Income Tax Department under Notice F. No. Pr.CCIT/MP/CS/Canteen Recruitment/2026-27 for 07 posts of Canteen Attendant. Candidates who are 10th Class (High School) passed from any recognized board in India are eligible to submit online applications for this central government vacancy.

The online application process started on 11 August 2026 and will close on 31 August 2026. Interested candidates can complete the online application form and registration through the official portal. There is no application fee for any category candidate, making it completely free to apply for all applicants across General, OBC, EWS, SC, ST, and PH categories.

Selection will be made on the posts allocated across categories including General (03 posts), OBC (01 post), EWS (01 post), SC (01 post), and ST (01 post). Selected candidates will draw salary under Pay Level-1 (Rs. 18,000/- to Rs. 56,900/- per month). Read all details regarding age limit, eligibility criteria, and step-by-step instructions below before filling out the online application form.

How to Apply

  1. Visit the official website of Income Tax Canteen Attendant at incometaxmpcg.gov.in (or nsltd.in).
  2. Click on the "Income Tax Canteen Attendant 2026 Apply Online" link.
  3. Register using a valid email ID and mobile number.
  4. Login using registration ID and password.
  5. Fill the application form with personal, educational, and category details carefully.
  6. Upload scanned documents including photo, signature, etc.
  7. Pay the application fee through online mode (Rs. 0 / No Fee required for all categories).
  8. Submit the form and download/print the confirmation page for future reference.

Documents Required

  • Recent passport-size photograph and signature (scanned copy).
  • 10th Class (High School) mark sheet / certificate for proof of age and educational qualification.
  • Valid ID proof (Aadhaar Card, PAN Card, Voter ID, etc.).
  • Category certificate (SC / ST / OBC / EWS), if applicable.
  • Disability / PH Certificate, if claiming age or fee concessions.
