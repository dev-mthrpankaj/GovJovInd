Eligibility Criteria

PET 2025 Requirement

Candidate must have appeared in Preliminary Eligibility Test 2025 and must have been issued a valid PET 2025 score card by UPSSSC.

Educational Qualification

Bachelor degree in Agriculture or related recognised agriculture stream such as Agriculture, Horticulture, Forestry, Agricultural Engineering, Home Science / Community Science or equivalent as mentioned in the official rules.

Preferential Qualification

Preference may be given to candidates who have served in Territorial Army for at least two years or have obtained NCC “B” certificate, subject to rules.

Selection Process

  1. 1

    Online application will be accepted only from PET 2025 candidates through the UPSSSC online application system.

  2. 2

    Candidates will be shortlisted for the main examination on the basis of PET 2025 normalized score as per merit and applicable rules.

  3. 3

    The main written examination will be objective type with 100 questions and total 100 marks.

  4. 4

    There will be negative marking of 1/4 mark for each wrong answer.

  5. 5

    Final selection will be completed as per UPSSSC rules, reservation provisions, document verification and other official instructions.

Syllabus Highlights

  • Crop Science: Crop production principles, protection, major crop pests and diseases, seed technology, integrated pest management and related topics.
  • Biotechnology & Plant Breeding: Importance and utility of biotechnology, genetics, plant breeding, photosynthesis, respiration, evaporation and plant anatomy.
  • Soil & Water Conservation: Soil formation, soil erosion, fertilizers, nutrient management, soil survey, irrigation, drainage, watershed management and pollution control.
  • Agriculture Extension: Rural development, extension methodologies, audio-visual aids, training objectives and government welfare programmes.
  • Computer & IT: Computer basics, internet, WWW, hardware/software, email, Word, Excel, e-governance, cyber security, AI, big data, IoT and related innovations.
  • Uttar Pradesh GK: History, culture, art, architecture, festivals, literature, tourism, geography, minerals, climate, environment, economy, social welfare and current events of Uttar Pradesh.
