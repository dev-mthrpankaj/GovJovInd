Eligibility Criteria

PET 2025 Requirement

Candidate must have appeared in UPSSSC PET 2025 and possess a valid PET 2025 score card.

Educational Qualification

Bachelor degree in Agriculture or a recognised related agriculture stream such as Agriculture, Horticulture, Forestry, Agricultural Engineering, Home Science / Community Science or equivalent as prescribed.

Preferential Qualification

Preference may apply for qualifying Territorial Army service or NCC “B” certificate subject to the official rules.

Syllabus Highlights

  • Crop Science: Crop production principles, protection, major crop pests and diseases, seed technology and integrated pest management.
  • Biotechnology & Plant Breeding: Biotechnology, genetics, plant breeding, photosynthesis, respiration and plant physiology.
  • Soil & Water Conservation: Soil formation, erosion, fertilizers, nutrient management, irrigation, drainage and watershed management.
  • Agriculture Extension: Rural development, extension methodologies, training and welfare programmes.
  • Computer & IT: Computer basics, internet, hardware/software, email, office applications, e-governance, cyber security, AI, big data and IoT.
  • Uttar Pradesh GK: History, culture, geography, economy, environment, social welfare and current events of Uttar Pradesh.
