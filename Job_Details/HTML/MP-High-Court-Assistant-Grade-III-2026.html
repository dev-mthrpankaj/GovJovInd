MP High Court Assistant Grade III Eligibility and Qualification

  • Bachelor Degree in any stream passed from any recognized University in India.
  • C.P.C.T. Score card exam passed from M.P. Agency Promotion of Information Technology (MAP-IT) or any other Agency/Institution recognized by the Madhya Pradesh Government.
  • One year Diploma course passed in Computer Application from an Institution recognized by the Madhya Pradesh Government.
  • For complete eligibility conditions, candidates should read the official notification before applying.
