How to Apply

  1. Visit the official website of CSIR Technician at https://www.csir.res.in/en.
  2. Click on the "CSIR Technician 2026 Apply Online" Link.
  3. Register using a valid email ID and mobile number.
  4. Login using your generated registration ID and password.
  5. Fill the application form carefully with your personal, educational, and category details.
  6. Upload the scanned documents (recent photograph, signature, etc.) as required.
  7. Pay the application fee through the online mode (Online Payment / SBI Collect).
  8. Submit the form and download/print the confirmation page for future records.

Documents Required

  • Recent passport-size colored photograph (scanned).
  • Scanned copy of signature.
  • 10th Standard (SSC) marksheet and passing certificate.
  • ITI Certificate / National or State Trade Certificate in the relevant discipline.
  • Caste/Category certificate (SC/ST/OBC/EWS/PH) if claiming reservation.
  • Valid photo ID proof.

Please confirm the exact document specifications from the official notice board before publishing.
