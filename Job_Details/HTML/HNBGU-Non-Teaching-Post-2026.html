How to Apply for HNBGU Non Teaching Vacancy 2026

  1. Visit the official website of HNBGU Non Teaching Post at hnbgu.ac.in.
  2. Click on the "HNBGU Non Teaching Post 2026 Apply Online" Link.
  3. Register yourself on the portal using a valid email ID and mobile number.
  4. Login using your newly generated registration ID and password.
  5. Fill the application form carefully with all your personal, educational, and category details.
  6. Upload scanned documents (recent photograph, signature, etc.) in the prescribed format.
  7. Pay the application fee (Rs.1000 for Gen/OBC/EWS, Rs.500 for others) through the online payment gateway.
  8. Submit the form and download/print the confirmation page for your future records.

Documents Required

  • Recent passport-size colored photograph (scanned).
  • Scanned copy of signature.
  • Relevant educational degree certificates and mark sheets.
  • Caste/Category certificate (if claiming reservation).
  • Valid photo ID proof (Aadhaar Card, PAN Card, Voter ID, Driving License).
  • Experience certificates (if applicable to the applied post).
