Eligibility and Qualification

Specific post-wise mandatory educational limits and domain verification criteria must be satisfied on or before the application deadline:

  • Mining Sirdar T&S Gr. C:
    • Possession of a formal Diploma in Mining Engineering from a government-recognized institution.
    • A valid Overman Competency Certificate issued directly by the DGMS.
    • Valid corporate statutory credentials including Gas Testing Certificate and First Aid Certificate.
  • Assistant Foreman (Trainee) (Electrical) / Electrical Supervisor:
    • A minimum 3-year full-time regular Diploma path in Electrical Engineering from a state/central recognized council.
    • A valid Electrical Supervisory Certificate verified explicitly for mining operations issued by the appropriate licensing board.
