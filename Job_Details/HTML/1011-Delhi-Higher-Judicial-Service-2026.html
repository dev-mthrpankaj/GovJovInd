About This Recruitment

Delhi Higher Judicial Service Examination 2026 is notified by the High Court of Delhi for direct recruitment in Delhi Higher Judicial Service. The notice states that the recruitment will be conducted for 27 vacancies, including existing and anticipated vacancies. This opportunity is meant for eligible advocates and eligible judicial officers who satisfy the experience, citizenship and age conditions stated in the official notice.

The recruitment is important for candidates who have substantial legal practice or judicial service experience and want to enter the higher judicial service cadre in Delhi. Since the eligibility conditions are experience-based, candidates should carefully check whether their seven years of practice or combined experience is counted as per the official rules before filling the online application.

The selection process is conducted in three successive stages: Preliminary Examination, Mains Examination and Viva-voce. Admission at every stage is provisional and depends on verification of eligibility. Applicants should read the official instructions and the Delhi Higher Judicial Service Rules, 1970 as amended, before submitting the form.

Eligibility and Qualification

  • Candidate must be a citizen of India.
  • In case of an Advocate, the candidate must have been continuously practising for not less than seven years as on 15.07.2026.
  • In case of Judicial Officers, the candidate must have already completed seven years in Bar before recruitment in subordinate judicial service, or must have seven years combined experience as a Judicial Officer and an Advocate as on 15.07.2026.
  • In case of a person who has been or who is in judicial service, the candidate must have a combined experience of seven years or more as an advocate or a Judicial Officer as on the last date of receipt of application.
  • Candidates should read the official instructions and Delhi Higher Judicial Service Rules, 1970 as amended up to date before applying.

Selection Process

  1. Delhi Higher Judicial Service Preliminary Examination: Objective type examination with 25% negative marking for selection of candidates for the Mains Examination.
  2. Delhi Higher Judicial Service Mains Examination: Written examination for selection of candidates for calling for viva-voce.
  3. Viva-voce: Final interview stage as per rules and official instructions.
  4. Eligibility verification: Candidature remains provisional and can be cancelled if the candidate does not satisfy the prescribed eligibility conditions.

Exam Pattern

The notice specifically mentions that the preliminary examination will be objective type and will carry 25% negative marking. Detailed pattern, syllabus, marks, relaxation in marks and other conditions are available in the instructions on the online portal / official website.

How to Apply

  1. Visit the High Court of Delhi official website at delhihighcourt.nic.in.
  2. Open the Delhi Higher Judicial Service Examination 2026 online application link when available.
  3. Create a new login and fill the online application form in the prescribed format.
  4. Enter personal, eligibility, experience, category and other required details carefully.
  5. Upload required documents, photograph and signature as per portal instructions.
  6. Pay the non-refundable application fee online through Debit Card, Credit Card, Internet Banking or UPI.
  7. Preview the form and submit it before 15.07.2026 at 1730 hours.
  8. Take a printout of the submitted application form and keep it for future reference. Do not send the printout to the High Court of Delhi.
