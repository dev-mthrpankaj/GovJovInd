About This Recruitment

The Delhi Higher Judicial Service Examination is conducted by the High Court of Delhi for direct recruitment to higher judicial service vacancies. The notice mentions 27 vacancies, including existing and anticipated vacancies.

This recruitment is intended for eligible advocates and judicial officers with the required experience. The examination has three stages: Preliminary Examination, Mains Examination and Viva-voce.

Candidates should read the official instructions and the Delhi Higher Judicial Service Rules before filling the form because eligibility and admission at each stage are provisional until verification.

Eligibility and Qualification

  • Candidate must be a citizen of India.
  • Advocate candidates must have been continuously practising for not less than seven years as on 15.07.2026.
  • Judicial Officers must have completed seven years at the Bar before recruitment in subordinate judicial service, or must have seven years combined experience as Judicial Officer and Advocate as on 15.07.2026.
  • A person who has been or is in judicial service must have combined experience of seven years or more as an advocate or Judicial Officer as on the last date of application.
  • Candidate must meet the age condition of 35 years to below 45 years as on the last date of application.
  • Candidates should read the official instructions and Delhi Higher Judicial Service Rules, 1970 as amended, before applying.

Selection Process

  1. Delhi Higher Judicial Service Preliminary Examination, objective type with 25% negative marking
  2. Delhi Higher Judicial Service Mains Examination, written
  3. Viva-voce
  4. Final eligibility verification as per rules and official instructions

How to Apply

  1. Visit the official High Court of Delhi website at www.delhihighcourt.nic.in.
  2. Open the Delhi Higher Judicial Service Examination 2026 online application link.
  3. Create a new login within the prescribed schedule.
  4. Fill the online application form carefully with personal, eligibility and experience details.
  5. Upload required documents as per portal instructions.
  6. Pay the fee online through Debit Card, Credit Card, Internet Banking or UPI.
  7. Submit the form before 15.07.2026 at 17:30 hours.
  8. Take a printout of the submitted application and keep it for future reference. Do not send the printout to the High Court unless instructed.

Why Apply For This Recruitment

Delhi Higher Judicial Service is a senior judicial service opportunity for experienced advocates and eligible judicial officers. The pay scale is clearly higher than entry-level judicial service posts, and the selection process is designed for candidates with substantial legal practice or judicial experience. Serious candidates should study the rules, prepare for the preliminary objective paper and keep all practice or service documents ready before applying.
