About This Recruitment

The Uttar Pradesh Public Service Commission (UPPSC) conducts competitive state-level examinations to select candidates for technical, administrative, and specialist departments within Uttar Pradesh. The Architectural Assistant and Other Posts recruitment is designed to bring qualified technical personnel into state urban planning, housing, public works, and architectural design departments.

Selection for these technical posts usually involves evaluating academic qualifications in architecture, written examination performance, and professional interviews. Applicants must ensure they hold a valid One-Time Registration (OTR) profile on the official UPPSC web portal before attempting to complete the application form.

Interested applicants should carefully check the official qualification criteria, age relaxations, reservation categories, and document upload requirements. Submitting accurate personal details and authentic certificates during the online form submission is mandatory to avoid rejection at later stages.

How to Apply

  1. Visit the official portal of Uttar Pradesh Public Service Commission: uppsc.up.nic.in.
  2. Complete your One-Time Registration (OTR) if not registered previously on the UPPSC OTR platform.
  3. Navigate to the "All Notifications / Advertisements" section and locate the Architectural Assistant advertisement.
  4. Click on "Apply" and authenticate using your OTR credentials.
  5. Fill in all required academic, personal, and professional details carefully.
  6. Upload digital copies of photograph, signature, and necessary technical qualification certificates.
  7. Pay the online application fee according to your category via available digital payment gateways.
  8. Submit the application form and keep a printed download copy for future recruitment procedures.
