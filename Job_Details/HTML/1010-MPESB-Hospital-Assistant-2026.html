About This Recruitment

This recruitment is for Hospital Assistant posts under the Madhya Pradesh Directorate of Public Health and Medical Education. MPESB conducts the examination and the department later verifies eligibility, documents and other appointment requirements.

The post is useful for candidates who have passed at least High School / 10th class and want a state government support role in health institutions. Candidates should read the official rulebook carefully because eligibility, reservation and document verification are handled strictly as per Madhya Pradesh rules.

The examination is online and the rulebook mentions 100 questions for 100 marks. Candidates should keep application number, employment registration, ID proof and category documents ready because eligibility remains provisional until verification by the department.

Eligibility and Qualification

Selection Process
