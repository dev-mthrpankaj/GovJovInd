How to Apply for Bank of Baroda SO Vacancy 2026

  1. Visit the official website of Bank of Baroda at bankofbaroda.bank.in.
  2. Click on "Bank of Baroda SO Various Post 2026 Recruitment" under the Careers / Current Opportunities section.
  3. Register using a valid email ID and mobile number.
  4. Log in using your registration ID and password.
  5. Fill the application form with personal, educational, and category details carefully.
  6. Upload scanned documents including photograph and signature according to specified specifications.
  7. Pay the application fee through online payment gateways (Net Banking, Debit Card, Credit Card, UPI).
  8. Submit the form and download or print the final confirmation page for future reference.

Candidates must read the official Bank of Baroda SO Various Post Notification 2026 carefully before submitting their application.

Documents Required

  • Scanned copy of recent passport size photograph and signature.
  • Educational qualification certificates and mark sheets corresponding to the post applied for.
  • Work experience certificates / experience documents as specified in the post requirements.
  • Proof of Date of Birth (Class 10th certificate or birth certificate).
  • Category Certificate (SC / ST / OBC / EWS / PH / ESM), if applicable.
  • Professional registration certificates / certification credentials (e.g. CEH, CompTIA, CISA, CA/ICWA, PMP), wherever mandatory.
