About This Recruitment

The Union Public Service Commission (UPSC) has published the official notification (Advt No. 52/2026) for the recruitment of Assistant Provident Fund Commissioner (APFC) in the Employees' Provident Fund Organisation (EPFO). The online application process commences on 22 August 2026, and eligible candidates must submit their application forms before the deadline on 11 September 2026 (06:00 PM). Interested candidates holding a Bachelor's Degree are encouraged to read the detailed notification to understand the selection process and syllabus before applying online through the official UPSC portal.

How to Apply

  1. Visit the official website of UPSC at upsc.gov.in or directly access the UPSC Online portal.
  2. Click on the "UPSC EPFO APFC 2026 Apply Online" link for Advertisement No. 52/2026.
  3. Register as a new user (if not already registered via OTR) using a valid email ID and mobile number.
  4. Login using your registration ID/OTR and password.
  5. Fill the application form carefully with all necessary personal, educational, and category details.
  6. Upload scanned documents, including your photograph and signature, in the prescribed dimensions.
  7. Pay the application fee of Rs.25/- through the online payment gateway (exempt for SC/ST/Women/PwBD candidates).
  8. Submit the final form and download/print the confirmation page for future reference.
