About This Recruitment

The Madhya Pradesh Employees Selection Board (MPESB) conducts competitive examinations for state cadre administrative and executive positions. The Group 2 Sub Group 4 examination fills various crucial positions, including Patwari, Assistant, Junior Assistant, and other state department vacancies. Eligible male and female candidates possessing a graduation degree from a recognized university are invited to apply through the official MP Online portal.

Applicants must complete their One-Time Profile Registration (OTR) on the MP Online portal before submitting the examination form. Ensure all uploaded documents, including photos, signatures, and declaration templates, meet the prescribed dimensions and criteria. The written examination evaluates general knowledge, reasoning, arithmetic, computer proficiency, and domain knowledge.

Selection is purely merit-based, determined by performance in the written examination followed by document verification. Candidates belonging to reserved categories must possess valid domicile and category certificates issued by competent authorities in Madhya Pradesh to claim reservation benefits.

Eligibility and Qualification

  • Educational Qualification: Bachelor's Degree in any discipline from a recognized University in India.
  • Post-wise Specific Qualification: Certain specific posts may require CPCT scorecard, diploma, or relevant subject specialization as detailed in the official rulebook.
  • Domicile: Candidates from all states are eligible to apply under the General/Unreserved category. Reservation benefits are strictly restricted to Madhya Pradesh domicile residents.

Selection Process

  1. Written Examination: Computer Based Written Test (CBT) covering statutory syllabus topics.
  2. Document Verification: Verification of original academic certificates, identity proof, domicile, and category documents.
  3. Final Merit List: Final appointment list issued based on marks scored in the written test and preferences filled.
  4. Medical Fitness: Standard medical check-up as mandated by respective government departments.

How to Apply

  1. Visit the official MP Online Portal for ESB at esb.mponline.gov.in.
  2. Complete Candidate Profile Registration (OTR) if not registered previously.
  3. Log in using your Registration ID and Password.
  4. Find the link for Group-2 SubGroup-4 Recruitment Test 2026.
  5. Fill in personal details, educational qualifications, address, and post preferences accurately.
  6. Upload scanned copies of photo, signature, handwritten declaration, and supporting documents.
  7. Pay the required application fee using Net Banking, UPI, Debit/Credit Card, or via MP Online Kiosk.
  8. Submit the application form and keep a printed copy of the final payment invoice and application form for future reference.
