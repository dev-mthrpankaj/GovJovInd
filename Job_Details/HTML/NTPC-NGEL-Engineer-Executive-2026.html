About This Recruitment

NTPC NGEL Engineer Executive Recruitment 2026 is an official recruitment drive conducted by NTPC Green Energy Limited (NGEL) to hire skilled Engineers and Executives across multiple disciplines. With 147 vacancies available, candidates with backgrounds in Civil, Electrical, Mechanical, Safety, HR, Finance, CSR, and Rajbhasha can apply for these roles.

Interested and eligible candidates holding the required degree with a minimum of 65% marks and at least 3 years of experience can submit their online application. The application window is open from 18 August 2026 to 07 September 2026. Applicants must ensure they meet the eligibility constraints and complete their online form and fee payment before the last date.

The detailed selection process, pay scale, and further recruitment rules are outlined in the official notification. Candidates are strictly advised to download the official notification PDF and read through all the instructions before proceeding with their online application.

How to Apply

  1. Visit the official website of NTPC Green Energy Limited (NGEL) at ngel.in/ngel-home.
  2. Click on the "NTPC NGEL Engineer Executive Apply Online" Link.
  3. Register using a valid email ID and mobile number.
  4. Login using your newly generated registration ID and password.
  5. Fill the application form with personal, educational, and category details carefully.
  6. Upload scanned documents (photo, signature, etc.) as required.
  7. Pay the application fee through the online payment gateway.
  8. Submit the form and download/print the confirmation page for future reference.

Please note that if a link is unclear or unresponsive, please confirm the direct link from the official notice board before submitting your final application.
