About This Recruitment

RITES Limited, a leading Navratna Central Public Sector Enterprise under the Ministry of Railways, has announced recruitment for the post of Assistant Manager (Mechanical) under Advt No. RG/11/26. This drive aims to fill 24 executive-level positions across India for qualified mechanical engineering professionals.

Eligible candidates possessing a degree in Mechanical Engineering or allied disciplines can apply online through the official RITES recruitment portal. Selected candidates will be offered an attractive pay scale along with standard PSU allowances and benefits.

The selection process combines qualifying educational merit, written examination/interview, and document verification. Candidates are encouraged to review the complete eligibility criteria and guidelines provided in the official advertisement prior to submitting their online application.

How to Apply

  1. Visit the official RITES recruitment portal at recruit.rites.com.
  2. Click on the registration link and fill in basic details using a valid email ID and mobile number.
  3. Log in using the generated Registration ID and Password.
  4. Complete the application form by entering personal information, educational qualifications, and experience.
  5. Upload scanned copies of required documents including photo, signature, and certificates.
  6. Pay the prescribed application fee via online payment gateway.
  7. Verify all entered details and submit the application form.
  8. Download and print the confirmation page for future reference.
