About This Recruitment

The Institute of Company Secretaries of India (ICSI) is a statutory body set up under an Act of Parliament to regulate and develop the profession of Company Secretaries in India. ICSI has published notification Advt. No. 04/2026 seeking applications for 20 posts of Executive Assistant across its offices in India. Candidates holding a Commerce degree with professional experience are invited to participate in this hiring drive.

The application procedure is strictly online via ICSI's career portal. Candidates must create or log into their account, submit complete educational credentials, experience details, and necessary scanned certificates before the deadline. Notably, ICSI has waived off application fees for all applicant categories, allowing all eligible candidates to register without any payment burden.

Selected candidates will serve in a crucial administrative capacity, handling professional and operational workflow. Candidates will receive Level-4 pay scale along with standard organization benefits. Applicants should carefully verify their eligibility criteria, work experience requirements, and document standards prior to final online submission.

Eligibility and Qualification

  • Educational Qualification: Bachelor's Degree in Commerce (B.Com) from any recognized University or Institution in India.
  • Experience: Minimum of 3 years of relevant post-qualification work experience.

Selection Process

  1. Initial shortlisting / screening of online applications based on educational qualification and experience.
  2. Written Examination / Skill Test / Personal Interview (as determined by ICSI).
  3. Document Verification of original academic certificates and work experience letters.
  4. Final Merit list preparation and issuance of offer of appointment.

Salary and Benefits

  • Pay Level: Level-4 under 7th CPC Pay Matrix.
  • Pay Scale Range: Rs. 25,500 - 81,100 per month (Basic pay structure: 48480-2000/7-62480-2340/2-67160-2680/7-85920 as specified).
  • Additional allowances, medical benefits, and service perks as per ICSI employee service rules.

Documents Required

  • Valid Mobile Number and Email ID for registration.
  • Recent Passport Size Photograph (Scanned).
  • Scanned Copy of Signature.
  • Class 10th Certificate / Marksheet (for Date of Birth proof).
  • B.Com Degree Marksheets & Graduation Certificate.
  • Work Experience Certificates (Minimum 3 years required).
  • Caste Certificate (OBC / SC / ST / EWS) if claiming reservation.
  • Valid Government Photo ID Proof (Aadhaar Card, PAN Card, Voter ID, etc.).

How to Apply

  1. Go to the official ICSI Career portal at icsi.edu/careers/ or access the application page directly.
  2. Click on the link for "ICSI Executive Assistant Apply Online (Advt. No. 04/2026)".
  3. Register using your functional Email ID and Mobile Number.
  4. Login with generated Registration ID / Credentials.
  5. Accurately complete personal, communication, educational qualification, and experience details.
  6. Upload mandatory scanned documents including photograph, signature, and experience letters in specified formats.
  7. No application fee is required; review the complete online application form carefully.
  8. Submit the application form before the closing date of 12 August 2026 and download/print the confirmation application page for future reference.
