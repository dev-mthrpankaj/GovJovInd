Recruitment Snapshot

SSC JHT 2025 Paper-II Selection Highlights

  • Out of 3,642 candidates who qualified in Paper-I to appear for Paper-II, a total of 1,722 candidates have been provisionally shortlisted for the next stages of recruitment specifically under the CRPF post option.
  • Shortlisted candidates who opted for the post of Sub-Inspector (Hindi Translator) in CRPF will undergo Physical Endurance Test / Physical Standard Test (PET/PST) managed directly by the CRPF.
  • Candidates who fail the PET/PST round will still retain their eligibility for all other civilian translation posts like JHT, JTO, and SHT based on their performance metrics and academic qualifications.
  • Mandatory Requirement: The Commission will open an online Option-cum-Preference window before the final merit list declaration. Candidates who fail to submit their post preferences during this window will not be considered for any post in the Final Result.

Structure of Selection Process

  1. 1

    Paper-I (Computer Based Test): Objective test evaluating General Hindi and General English domains (200 marks total). Completed on 12-08-2025.

  2. 2

    Paper-II (Descriptive Evaluation): Standard offline test looking closely at descriptive Translation and Essay writing proficiency (200 marks total). Completed on 14-12-2025.

  3. 3

    Physical Evaluation (PET/PST): Mandatory exclusively for shortlisted candidates prioritizing the Sub-Inspector post in CRPF. Scheduled sequentially following the 04-06-2026 result announcement.

  4. 4

    Online Option-cum-Preference Submission: Compulsory digital portal validation step prior to final merit compilation. Failure to execute post selections disqualifies the applicant from final matching blocks.

  5. 5

    Document Scrutiny & Final Merit Allocation: Conducted by user departments at later operational segments based on cumulative scoring parameters across Papers I & II.
