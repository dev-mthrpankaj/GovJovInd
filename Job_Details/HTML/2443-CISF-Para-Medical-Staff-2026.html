About This Recruitment

The Central Industrial Security Force (CISF) Para Medical Staff Recruitment 2026 is an all-India level competitive process executed to select eligible male and female Indian citizens. These positions fall under Assistant Sub Inspector (ASI) grades for specialized domains including Pharmacy, Radiography (X-Ray), and Medical Laboratory Technology. Selected candidates are assigned to combatized wings within the force, meaning they are governed under the CISF Act and Rules along with applicable Central Civil Services structures, rendering services anywhere across Indian territories or overseas stations.

Applications are managed entirely through the web interface of the CISF recruitment system. Candidates must satisfy the specific professional requirements, register themselves through the multi-stage portal, provide biometric credentials at initial evaluation points, and keep track of updates since call letters or admit cards are not distributed through postal delivery. It serves as an optimal track for qualified paramedical professionals looking to build a stable administrative and tactical career within a recognized Central Armed Police Force.

The examination design balances mental and physical parameters. Applicants move from a comprehensive OMR or Computer Based written test into strict Physical Standard screening, systematic documentation checks, trade compatibility tests, and comprehensive medical examinations. Because of the multi-tiered structure, aspirants are encouraged to maintain a highly detailed review of the formal qualification thresholds, age parameters, and specific medical regulations before submitting data.

Eligibility and Qualification

Candidates must ensure that all diplomas or degrees originate from institutions officially recognized by Central or State educational frameworks.

  • ASI (Pharmacist): 10+2 Intermediate pass along with a Diploma or Degree in Pharmacy from a recognized institution, and must carry active registration as a Pharmacist under the Pharmacy Act, 1948.
  • ASI (X-Ray Technician): 10+2 Intermediate pass along with a 2-year specialized Diploma in Radiography from a recognized Central or State institution.
  • ASI (Lab Technician): 10+2 Intermediate pass with Science stream, alongside a 2-year Diploma in Medical Laboratory Technology (DMLT) from a recognized framework.

Selection Process

The chronological sequence of these evaluation points can interchange at the discretion of the CISF management depending on prevailing circumstances.

  1. Written Examination (Objective multiple choice model under OMR or CBT mode).
  2. Physical Standard Test (PST) measuring Height, Chest, and Weight.
  3. Documentation stage involving strict checking of all original certificates.
  4. Basic Vocational Aptitude Test (BVAT) which acts as a qualifying phase.
  5. Medical Examination (Detailed Medical Examination / Review Medical Examination).
  6. Final Selection list generation berdasarkan aggregate marks scored in written exam.

Exam Pattern

The written evaluation is structured around testing domain and basic knowledge parameters. Shortlisted candidates for the next stages will be roughly 5 times the total declared vacancy metrics.

  • Total Marks: 100 Marks.
  • Duration: 2 Hours (120 minutes).
  • Question Type: Objective Multiple Choice Type.
  • Languages: Bilingual (Hindi and English).
  • Negative Marking: There is no negative marking.
  • Normalization: Score normalization will apply if testing occurs in multiple shifts.

    How to Apply

    1. Access the official interface of CISF at cisfrectt.cisf.gov.in and select the Login tab.
    2. Initiate the "New Registration" sequence to enter profile details, parentage, date of birth, active email, and mobile tracking numbers.
    3. Secure your auto-generated Registration ID and password delivered through electronic communications.
    4. Log in and navigate to the dedicated link titled "Recruitment of Paramedical Staff in CISF-2026".
    5. Input your professional qualifications, selection of choice post, and complete data fields.
    6. Upload digital copies of your passport photograph (JPEG format, 20-50 KB, featuring the date of photography printed on the image) and signature (10-20 KB).
    7. Upload structural verification certificates in PDF format (under 1 MB limit per file).
    8. Proceed to the payment module to settle the Rs. 100 fee via digital gateways or generate an SBI offline bank challan (exempted groups can skip directly).
    9. Review details via preview parameters, execute the "Final Submission" lock, and download a hard copy of the completed application.
