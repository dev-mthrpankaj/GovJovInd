About This Recruitment

Punjab State Power Corporation Limited (PSPCL), a power generating and distribution utility owned by the Government of Punjab, is conducting an open competitive recruitment drive for young and dynamic engineering professionals. The current notification under advertisement CRA 316/26 seeks to fill 622 vacancies for the position of Junior Engineer/Electrical. Selected candidates will be primary field and administrative links responsible for managing quality and uninterrupted power distribution systems across the state of Punjab.

The application portal operates strictly online. Candidates who possess the required academic background in Electrical Engineering or Electrical and Electronics Engineering can complete their application forms and pay the application fee through the SBI integrated online payment gateway. Candidates are advised by the department not to wait until the concluding hours of the timeline to avoid network gridlocks or transactional delays, as incomplete payments will straightaway result in application dismissal.

The evaluation cycle is objective, relying entirely on a comprehensive multiple-choice computer-based test distributed across various shifts if required. Post evaluation, the Corporation will activate correction or objection tracking windows before declaring the final normalized merit list. Additionally, because specific language thresholds are mandatory for regional administrative roles, having an active confirmation of matriculation-level Punjabi language clearance remains a major eligibility bottleneck that applicants must verify beforehand.

Eligibility and Qualification

All structural professional and language benchmarks must be officially achieved by the final application submission cutoff date.

  • Professional Stream A: Full-Time Regular 3 or 4 years Diploma in Electrical Engineering or Electrical and Electronics Engineering from an institute approved by State or Central Government with a minimum aggregate scoring of 60% marks.
  • Professional Stream B: B.E. / B.Tech. / B.Sc Engineering in Electrical or Electrical and Electronics Engineering with a minimum allocation of 50% marks.
  • Professional Stream C: AMIE in Electrical or Electrical and Electronics Engineering with at least 50% marks from the Institution of Engineers (India) Calcutta.
  • Language Mandatory Criteria: Candidates must have passed Punjabi language up to Matriculation (10th standard) level or its structural equivalent.
  • Awaiting candidates whose results are officially declared by the cutoff date can apply by providing required certification confirmations.

Selection Process

The recruitment process depends entirely on competitive marks obtained in the online screening gateway.

  1. Online Computer Based Test (CBT): All applicants must face an objective exam containing 100 multiple choice questions.
  2. Normalization: Marks from multi-shift test variations undergo mathematical normalization to adjust for structural difficulty variances.
  3. Provisional Merit Ranking: Publication of preliminary selection lists based on normalized performance scores matching minimum qualification thresholds.
  4. Document Checking Process: Candidates must personally appear before the evaluation committee with their original academic transcripts, certificates and caste or domicile declarations.
  5. Final Validation Selection Panel: Formulation of final appointment rosters, valid for one year from structural corporate clearance.

    Salary and Benefits

    The structural basic compensation complies thoroughly with the PSPCL Financial Circular No. 21/2022 dated 17.08.2022 and corresponding Department of Finance guidelines.

    • Minimum Admissible Pay: Rs. 35,400/- per month based on the 7th Central Pay Commission (CPC) / Pay Matrix guidelines.
    • Probation Framework: The newly appointed candidates will undergo a mandatory probation period of 3 years.
    • Probation Draw Restrictions: During probation, candidates draw only the minimum admissible basic pay. Grade Pay, Dearness Allowance (DA), or annual increments are frozen, except for minimal travelling allowances and medical reimbursements.
    • Lien Protection: Candidates already serving internally within PSPCL can protect their pre-existing pay structural parameters if their previous drawn matrix is higher than the minimum induction pay.

    Documents Required for Document Verification

    Candidates shortlisted for document verification must present one self-attested photocopy set along with original documents.

    • Original Matriculation (10th) Certificate showing clearance of Punjabi Language.
    • Original Engineering Diploma or Degree transcripts along with passing proofs.
    • Valid Domicile Certificate of Punjab State (where reservation benefits are claimed).
    • Caste / Category Certificate (SC, BC, EWS, Freedom Fighter) matching official state formats.
    • Income and Asset Certificate for EWS candidates for the financial year 2025-26 (valid for the financial year 2026-27).
    • Sainik Welfare Office validation letters for Ex-Servicemen (Self / Dependents).
    • Sports Gradation Certificate issued by the Director Sports Department, Punjab (must be submitted to the Patiala Recruitment Section on or before the application closing date).
    • Medical Disability Certificate with permanent classification issued by the Civil Surgeon.
    • No Objection Certificate (NOC) from the Head of Department for active government service employees.

    How to Apply

    Follow these structural procedures to successfully complete your application on the official portal.

    1. Navigate to the official website of the utility at www.pspcl.in.
    2. Click on the dedicated Recruitment tab on the homepage.
    3. Select the link titled "Recruitment for the post of Junior Engineer/Electrical in PSPCL against CRA-316/26".
    4. Click on 'NEW REGISTRATION' and submit fundamental details (Name, DOB, Email, Mobile Number, Post Choice).
    5. Enter the One-Time Passwords (OTP) received on your registered mobile and email to verify your profile.
    6. Log in again as a registered user and fill in your academic scores, language confirmations and category attributes.
    7. Upload your scanned color passport size photograph (Size: 20kb to 50kb) and signature signed with a black ink pen on white paper (Size: 10kb to 20kb) in JPG/JPEG formats.
    8. Review your details using 'Save and Preview', agree to the declarations and click SUBMIT (Note: Post submission, modification is blocked).
    9. Click on the payment redirection link and transfer your application fee via Net Banking, Debit or Credit Cards or UPI through the integrated SBI Gateway.
    10. Re-login to check your fee status. Once marked "PAID", click on PRINT APPLICATION FORM and save a physical print copy for corporate document checks.
