Eligibility & Age Limit

Educational Qualification

Candidate must have passed Intermediate / 12th Class from a recognized board established by law in India, or an equivalent qualification. Appearing candidates are not eligible.

General / EWS Age

As on 01-07-2025 after 3-year relaxation:
Male: 18 to 25 years, born between 02-07-2000 and 01-07-2007.
Female: 18 to 28 years, born between 02-07-1997 and 01-07-2007.

OBC / SC / ST Age

For Uttar Pradesh domicile candidates after relaxation:
Male: 18 to 30 years, born between 02-07-1995 and 01-07-2007.
Female: 18 to 33 years, born between 02-07-1992 and 01-07-2007.

Preferential Qualification

Preference in tie cases applies for the qualifications/services prescribed in the official notification, including eligible NIELIT/DOEACC, Territorial Army and NCC credentials.
