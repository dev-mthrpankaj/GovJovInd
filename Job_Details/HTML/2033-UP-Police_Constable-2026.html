Latest Updates

Recruitment Snapshot

  • UPPRPB invited online applications for 32,679 posts across Civil Police, PAC, SSF, Female Battalion, Mounted Police and Jail Warder cadres.
  • Candidates had to complete OTR (One Time Registration) on the UPPRPB portal before filling the application.
  • Candidates had to indicate their order of preference for posts during the application. Post-submission changes in preference are not allowed unless the board opens a correction window.
  • The pay scale is Pay Band 5200-20200, Grade Pay-2000 (New Matrix Rs. 21,700 - 69,100, Pay Level-3).
  • As per the corrigendum dated 05-01-2026, a one-time 3-year age relaxation has been added to the upper age limit for all categories.

Eligibility & Age Limit

Educational Qualification

Candidate must have passed Intermediate / 12th Class from a recognized board established by law in India, or an equivalent qualification. Appearing candidates are not eligible.

General / EWS Age

As on 01-07-2025 after 3-year relaxation:
Male: 18 to 25 years, born between 02-07-2000 and 01-07-2007.
Female: 18 to 28 years, born between 02-07-1997 and 01-07-2007.

OBC / SC / ST Age

For Uttar Pradesh domicile candidates after relaxation:
Male: 18 to 30 years, born between 02-07-1995 and 01-07-2007.
Female: 18 to 33 years, born between 02-07-1992 and 01-07-2007.

Preferential Qualification

Preference is given only in tie cases to candidates with DOEACC/NIELIT "O" Level, at least 2 years Territorial Army service, or NCC "B" Certificate. No extra marks are awarded for these qualifications.

Physical Standards (PST & PET)

After qualifying the written exam, candidates will undergo Document Verification and Physical Standard Test (PST), followed by Physical Efficiency Test (PET).
