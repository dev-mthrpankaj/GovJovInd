About This Recruitment

The Karnataka State Police Department has initialized direct recruitment protocols for the post of Civil Police Constable under the local cadre, allocating reservation rules as per the Kalyana-Karnataka regional mandates. This recruitment cycle aims to induct capable individuals to enforce state laws, protect local communities, and support peace-keeping operations. To execute a comprehensive and structured merit system, the written competitive segment of this evaluation has been entrusted directly to the Karnataka Examinations Authority (KEA).

Prospective candidates with the necessary educational credentials from recognized boards are eligible to complete registration through the KEA digital application engine. The process mandates absolute adherence to deadline protocols and document validation standards, with separate application setups defined for direct candidates, service personnel, and transgender categories. Candidates must satisfy localized eligibility norms, native language understanding, and standard police health measures before progression to active service positions.

The operational framework of selection utilizes an integrated multiple-tier screening structure. Candidates must achieve baseline qualification standards in the OMR-based written entrance examination before gaining eligibility for physical measurement and physical durability tests. Success across all parameters, combined with mandatory clear reports from authorized medical boards and localized verification parameters, governs final allocation into the departmental pay matrix.

Eligibility and Qualification

  • Applicants must have completed PUC (Pre-University Course) or its recognized equivalent 12th-grade certification before the final portal closure date.
  • Accepted equivalents include CBSE Class 12, ISC Class 12, NIOS Higher Secondary certifications, and specific 3-year Polytechnic Diplomas with language validations.
  • Two-year ITI qualifications are recognized only if accompanied by authorized language and open school learning certifications.
  • Candidates must meet compulsory Kannada language exemptions (SSLC level learning or passing previous mandatory examinations) or qualify through the custom 150-mark language test by securing at least 50 marks.
  • Applicants currently awaiting final academic result sheets on the final cutoff date remain explicitly ineligible to apply.

Selection Process Workflow

  1. Competitive Written Evaluation: OMR-based test designed by the KEA to measure basic cognitive intelligence and state syllabus competence.
  2. Language Proficiency Screening: Mandatory language tests for applicants lacking required school academic validation backgrounds.
  3. Shortlist Multiplier: Publication of a provisional list using a 1:5 scale ratio based purely on written examination performance scores.
  4. Physical Standard Test (PST): Absolute measurement checkup covering mandatory height and expansion thresholds.
  5. Endurance Test (ET): Time-bound physical efficiency exercises including running drills, long jumping, and shot-put actions.
  6. Provisional List Selection: Creation of a strict 1:2 merit shortlist grouping based primarily on valid written test scoring.
  7. Medical Review Board: Multi-tiered health scrutiny by specialized medical boards covering optical, auditory, and structural compliance criteria.
  8. Verification & Final Appointment: Background character review and credential checking procedures executed by state offices prior to training induction.

Exam Pattern

The structured examination uses an offline multiple-choice system spanning 90 minutes total duration. Negative marks apply to errors made during the assessment procedure.

Negative Valuation Protocol: Every incorrect answer leads to a deduction of 0.25 marks. Furthermore, an explicit 5th circle option exists for questions left blank; if an unattempted question does not receive a marked 5th circle within the final designated 5 minutes, 0.25 marks will be deducted automatically.

    What is the total vacancy count for the Karnataka Police Civil Constable Recruitment 2026?
