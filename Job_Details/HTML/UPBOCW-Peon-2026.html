About This Recruitment

UPBOCW Peon Recruitment 2026 is an official recruitment drive conducted by the Uttar Pradesh Building and Other Construction Workers Welfare Board (UPBOCW) via the UP Rojgar Sangam (Sewayojan) portal to hire Peons on an outsourcing basis. The recruitment covers 15 openings across various districts including Muzaffarnagar, Mathura, Aligarh, Bijnor, Kanpur Nagar, Farrukhabad, Kaushambi, Jaunpur, Chandauli, Sonbhadra, Lucknow, Maharajganj, and Gorakhpur.

Interested and eligible candidates who have passed their Intermediate (12th) exam can complete their online application through the official Sewayojan registration portal. The online application window is open from 24 August 2026 to 01 September 2026. This recruitment has no application fee for any category, making it an excellent opportunity for job seekers looking for outsourcing jobs in Uttar Pradesh.

Candidates are advised to download the official notification PDF and carefully review the eligibility criteria and district-wise distribution before submitting their online forms.

How to Apply

  1. Visit the official website of UP Rojgar Sangam at sewayojan.up.nic.in.
  2. Click on the "UP Rojgar Sangam UPBOCW Peon 2026 Apply Online" Link.
  3. Register on the portal using a valid email ID and mobile number.
  4. Login using your newly generated registration ID and password.
  5. Fill the online application form with personal, educational, and category details carefully.
  6. Upload required scanned documents such as photograph and signature.
  7. Submit the form and download/print the confirmation page for future reference (No application fee is required).

Documents Required

  • Recent passport-size colored photograph (scanned).
  • Scanned copy of signature.
  • 12th Class (Intermediate) marksheet and certificate.
  • Caste/Category certificate if claiming reservation.
  • Valid photo ID proof (Aadhaar Card, PAN Card, Voter ID, etc.).
