About This Recruitment

Punjab National Bank (PNB), one of India's leading public sector banks, has announced the recruitment drive for 545 Local Bank Officer (LBO) vacancies in the Junior Management Grade Scale-I (JMGS-I). The Local Bank Officer role is designed to cater to local banking demands across specific states. Candidates recruited under this cadre will be deployed in the chosen state, ensuring localized customer support, business development, and smooth administrative operations.

The application mode is strictly online through the integrated IBPS registration portal linked from PNB’s official website. Candidates must verify their eligibility, including state-specific local language proficiency, graduation qualifications, and work experience criteria before submitting the application. Candidates applying under reserved categories can avail of standard relaxation benefits upon providing valid official certificates during document verification.

The recruitment process features multiple evaluation stages to test quantitative aptitude, reasoning, language skills, domain banking knowledge, and local language command. Selected candidates will undergo structured training and probation as per Punjab National Bank service rules before full placement into the officer cadre.

Eligibility and Qualification

  • Educational Qualification: Bachelor's Degree in any discipline from a recognized University or Institute in India.
  • Work Experience: Minimum 1 year post-qualification work experience in Clerical or Officer cadre in a Scheduled Commercial Bank or Regional Rural Bank (RRB).
  • Local Language Proficiency: Candidates must be proficient (reading, writing, and speaking) in the local language of the applied state.

Selection Process

  1. Online Written Examination: Objective test covering aptitude, reasoning, English, and banking awareness.
  2. Screening: Initial scrutiny of documents and experience certificates of written test qualified candidates.
  3. Local Language Proficiency Test (LLPT): Mandatory test for candidates who have not studied the state's local language in 10th or 12th standard.
  4. Personal Interview: Interview carrying 50 marks to assess banking knowledge, personality, and communication skills.
  5. Document Verification & Medical Exam: Final verification of original credentials and medical fitness check.

Exam Pattern

The online written test comprises 150 objective questions for a total of 150 marks with a total duration of 180 minutes (3 hours). Sectional timing is applicable. Penalty for wrong answers is 0.25 marks per incorrect attempt.

How to Apply

  1. Visit the official portal of Punjab National Bank at pnb.bank.in and open the Recruitment section.
  2. Click on the online registration link "PNB Local Bank Officer 2026 Apply Online".
  3. Complete initial registration using a valid email ID and active mobile number.
  4. Log in using the generated Registration ID and Password.
  5. Fill in personal, academic, experience, and local language details accurately.
  6. Upload scanned documents including photograph, signature, left thumb impression, and handwritten declaration.
  7. Pay the required application fee via online payment gateway.
  8. Preview all entered fields, submit the application form, and print a copy for future reference.
