How to Apply

  1. Visit the official website of East Coast Railway Apprentice (eastcoastrail.indianrailways.gov.in).
  2. Click on the "East Coast Railway Apprentice 2026 Apply Online" Link available on the recruitment portal.
  3. Register yourself using a valid email ID and mobile number.
  4. Login using your newly generated registration ID and password.
  5. Fill the application form carefully with your personal, educational, and category details.
  6. Upload scanned documents including photo, signature, and other required credentials.
  7. Pay the application fee (Rs.100 for Gen/OBC/EWS) through online mode.
  8. Submit the form and download/print the confirmation page for future reference.

Candidate must read the East Coast Railway Apprentice Notification 2026 before applying for the application form.

Documents Required

  • Recent passport-size colored photograph (scanned).
  • Scanned copy of signature.
  • 10th / Matriculation Marksheet and passing certificate.
  • ITI passing certificate in the relevant trade.
  • Caste/Category certificate (SC/ST/OBC/EWS) if claiming reservation.
  • Valid photo ID proof (Aadhaar Card, PAN Card, Voter ID, or Passport).
