About This Recruitment

UP Ground Water Department MTS Recruitment 2026 has been published through the Regional Employment Office under the UP Rojgar Sangam Sewayojan Portal (sewayojan.up.nic.in). This recruitment drive aims to fill 07 vacancies for the Multitasking Staff (MTS) position on an outsourcing basis across designated districts in Uttar Pradesh.

Interested eligible candidates who are registered as job seekers on the UP Sewayojan portal can submit their online application forms between 07 August 2026 and 15 August 2026. The application mode is strictly online and no application fee is charged from any candidate category.

Candidates selected under this vacancy will be placed in specified work districts of Uttar Pradesh including Saharanpur, Gautam Buddh Nagar, Aligarh, Moradabad, Prayagraj, Mirzapur, and Gonda with a monthly salary range of Rs. 10,001 to Rs. 20,000/-.

How to Apply

  1. Visit the official UP Sewayojan (Rojgar Sangam) portal at sewayojan.up.nic.in.
  2. Complete the Job Seeker registration process if you are a new user.
  3. Log in using your Registration ID and Password.
  4. Navigate to the Outsourcing Jobs section and search for "UP Ground Water Department MTS".
  5. Click on the job listing link and fill in personal, educational, and category details carefully.
  6. Upload required scanned documents, photo, and signature.
  7. Review all entered information and submit the application form.
  8. Download and print the final confirmation page for future reference.

Documents Required

  • Valid Email ID and Mobile Number.
  • Job Seeker Profile / Registration ID on UP Sewayojan Portal.
  • Educational qualification certificates (Class 10th / Below SSC marksheets).
  • Identity proof (Aadhaar Card, PAN Card, Voter ID, etc.).
  • Category / Caste certificate (if applicable).
  • Recent passport-size photograph and scanned signature.
