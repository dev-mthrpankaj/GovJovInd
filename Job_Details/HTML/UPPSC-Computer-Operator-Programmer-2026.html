About This Recruitment

The Uttar Pradesh Public Service Commission (UPPSC) has published advertisement number Advt. No. A-2/E-1/2026 inviting online applications for technical IT roles under state government departments. The positions filled through this recruitment include Manager System, Senior Programmer, Programmer Grade-II, and Computer Operator Grade-B. These roles are suitable for individuals holding qualifications in computer application, computer science, and related technical domains looking for career opportunities within Uttar Pradesh state administrative services.

Eligible candidates can fill out the application form on the official website of the commission. Applicants are required to complete the mandatory One Time Registration (OTR) process on the UPPSC portal prior to submitting the examination form. Ensuring accurate personal, educational, and category details during registration is essential, as initial screening and fee payments are mapped to the candidate's OTR profile.

The recruitment process encompasses a screening/written examination followed by skill tests, typing tests, or interviews where applicable, as specified for each post under state service rules. Candidates are advised to carefully check post-wise eligibility criteria, age limits, and technical requirements before submitting their application before the deadline.

Eligibility and Qualification

  • Manager System: Degree in Computer Application or equivalent qualification from a recognized university.
  • Senior Programmer: Degree with Diploma in Computer Application from a recognized institution.
  • Programmer Grade-II: Bachelor's Degree from a recognized university along with Post Graduate Diploma in Computer Science or DOEACC 'A' Level Certificate.
  • Computer Operator Grade-B: Bachelor's Degree with Diploma in Computer Science OR Bachelor's Degree with 'O' Level Certificate from NIELIT/DOEACC. Knowledge of data entry in Hindi and English on platforms such as MS Office, Windows, Unix, or Lotus, with a minimum typing speed of 25 WPM in Hindi and 40 WPM in English.

Selection Process

  1. Screening / Written Examination conducted by UPPSC.
  2. Skill / Typing Test (mandatory for Computer Operator Grade-B and specified technical posts).
  3. Document Verification of original educational and category certificates.
  4. Final Merit List prepared based on examination performance and eligibility criteria compliance.

Exam Pattern

Check official notification for detailed post-wise syllabus, distribution of marks, and exam scheme.

Skill Test / Typing Test Standards

For Computer Operator Grade-B, candidates must demonstrate essential computer typing and data entry skills:

  • Hindi Typing Speed: Minimum 25 words per minute.
  • English Typing Speed: Minimum 40 words per minute.
  • Working knowledge of MS Office, Unix, Windows, D-Base, and networking concepts.

How to Apply

  1. Visit the official portal of UPPSC at uppsc.up.nic.in.
  2. Complete the One Time Registration (OTR) process if not completed previously.
  3. Navigate to live advertisements and select advertisement Advt. No. A-2/E-1/2026.
  4. Log in using your OTR credentials and fill out the online application form.
  5. Select the desired post(s) based on your eligibility.
  6. Upload scanned copies of required photograph, signature, and necessary certificates.
  7. Pay the required application fee through the provided online payment gateway.
  8. Verify all filled information and submit the application form before 03 September 2026.
  9. Download and print a copy of the final confirmation form for future reference.
