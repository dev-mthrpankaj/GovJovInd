About This Recruitment

SSC Delhi Police Head Constable Ministerial Recruitment is conducted by the Staff Selection Commission as an open competitive examination for Delhi Police. The post is a ministerial role, which means selected candidates are expected to support administrative, record, office, correspondence and computer-based work within the department. The official notice states that candidates from all parts of India are eligible to apply, making this a national-level opportunity for 10+2 qualified candidates who also have the required typing skill.

The application process is online only through the SSC website. Candidates have to complete or use their One-Time Registration, fill the examination application, provide details carefully, follow photograph and signature rules, pay the fee if applicable and submit before the deadline. Because the recruitment is for a police department, candidates should also read the official standards related to physical endurance, measurement, document verification, medical examination and character verification before deciding to apply.

The selection flow is broader than a written test alone. SSC conducts the Computer Based Examination in English and Hindi, while Delhi Police conducts PE&MT and later skill-related stages. The notice lists Typing Test on Computer and Computer Formatting Test after the physical stage for eligible candidates. Final selection is prepared according to the rules, using the notified marks and qualifying stages. Applicants should therefore prepare for objective exam topics, typing speed, basic computer formatting and document readiness together instead of treating the recruitment as only a single exam form.

Eligibility and Qualification

  • 10+2 Senior Secondary pass or equivalent qualification from a recognized board.
  • English typing speed of 30 WPM or Hindi typing speed of 25 WPM.
  • Essential qualification and professional typing requirement must be possessed as per the closing date and rules in the official notification.

Selection Process

  1. Computer Based Examination conducted by SSC.
  2. Physical Endurance and Measurement Test conducted by Delhi Police.
  3. Typing Test on Computer for candidates qualifying PE&MT and eligible PwBD candidates as per rules.
  4. Computer Formatting Test on MS Word, MS PowerPoint and MS Excel, qualifying in nature.
  5. Document Verification and checking of original certificates.
  6. Police verification of character and antecedents and Medical Examination of finally selected candidates.
  7. Final merit is prepared as per official rules using CBE marks, Typing Test marks and applicable incentive or bonus marks.

Exam Pattern

The Computer Based Examination is an objective-type paper of 90 minutes, containing 100 questions for 100 marks. Negative marking is 0.25 marks for each wrong answer. The paper is conducted in Hindi and English only.

How to Apply

  1. Visit the official SSC website at ssc.gov.in.

  3. Select Head Constable (Ministerial) in Delhi Police Examination under live examinations.

  6. Pay the application fee online if not exempted.

Candidates with steady typing practice and basic computer confidence can align their preparation directly with the official stages.
