About This Recruitment

SBI Clerk Recruitment 2026 has been officially announced by the State Bank of India under Advertisement No. CRPD/CR/SPLDRIVE/2026-27/16. This special backlog recruitment drive aims to fill 1538 vacancies for the post of Junior Associates (Customer Support & Sales). Candidates holding a recognized Bachelor's degree can complete their online application process through the official online application portal before the registration deadline.

The online application form process requires candidates to register using a valid email ID and mobile number, log in, fill in educational and personal credentials, upload photograph and signature, pay the requisite examination fee, and retain a copy of the final confirmation form for official reference.

This recruitment notification carries category-wise vacancies distributed across SC, ST, and OBC categories as detailed in the official schedule. Eligible candidates should carefully verify all instructions, syllabus details, and eligibility norms in the official notification before submitting their applications.

How to Apply

  1. Visit the official State Bank of India career portal or direct apply online link (ibpsreg.ibps.in/sbijasrdj126/).
  2. Click on "Click Here for New Registration" and complete basic registration with a valid email ID and mobile number.
  3. Log in using your system-generated Registration ID and Password.
  4. Fill in personal details, educational qualifications, and category details carefully.
  5. Upload required scanned documents including photograph, signature, left thumb impression, and hand-written declaration.
  6. Pay the application fee through online mode (Net Banking, Credit Card, or Debit Card) if applicable.
  7. Submit the final form and print out a copy of the completed application form for future reference.

Documents Required

  • Recent passport-size photograph (scanned).
  • Scanned signature image.
  • Graduation mark sheet and degree certificate.
  • Category certificate (SC / ST / OBC), if applicable.
  • Valid photo identity card (Aadhaar Card, PAN Card, Voter ID, etc.).
