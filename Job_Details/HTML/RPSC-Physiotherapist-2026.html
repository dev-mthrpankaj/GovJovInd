About This Recruitment

The Rajasthan Public Service Commission (RPSC) has officially published the recruitment notification (Advt No 05/ Exam/Physiotherapist/Med. & Health/RPSC/EP-1/2026-27) for the post of Physiotherapist (Non-Subordinate Area) under the Medical and Health Department of Rajasthan. A total of 20 vacancies have been released for candidates meeting the required technical and academic qualifications.

Eligible candidates holding a Diploma in Physiotherapy along with a Secondary (10th) qualification from recognized institutions can apply online through the Rajasthan Single Sign-On (SSO) portal. Applicants are required to verify details, complete one-time profile registration if applicable, upload scanned documents, and submit fees prior to the established closing date.

The selection mechanism involves evaluating candidate eligibility, followed by written examinations or competitive tests conducted per RPSC recruitment guidelines. Candidates who secure qualifying marks will undergo document verification prior to final posting in Rajasthan state departments.

How to Apply

  1. Visit the official portal of RPSC at rpsc.rajasthan.gov.in or direct SSO portal at sso.rajasthan.gov.in.
  2. Complete One Time Registration (OTR) if applying for the first time.
  3. Search for "RPSC Physiotherapist Recruitment 2026" under active recruitment notices.
  4. Fill in all required educational, personal, and category details carefully.
  5. Upload required scanned passport photograph, signature, and diploma certificates.
  6. Pay the required application fee using available online payment options.
  7. Submit the application form and keep a printed soft/hard copy for future reference.
