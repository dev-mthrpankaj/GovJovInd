About This Recruitment

Rourkela Steel Plant (RSP), a flagship unit of Steel Authority of India Limited (SAIL), has announced a major engagement opportunity for candidates seeking apprenticeship training for the year 2026-27. Under Advertisement Ref. No. L&D/Adv./APP/158 dated 17.07.2026, SAIL RSP invites eligible Indian nationals to apply for one-year apprenticeship training in various technical and non-technical disciplines. This program offers practical industrial exposure in modern steel production, equipment maintenance, and administrative operations.

The recruitment process is conducted completely online via dedicated central government apprenticeship portals. Trade Apprentice applicants must apply through the NAPS portal (Apprenticeship India), whereas candidates applying for Graduate or Technician (Diploma) apprenticeship training must submit their registrations via the NATS portal. There is no written examination for these positions; selection will be finalized based on academic performance and walk-in document verification at Rourkela.

Selected candidates will receive monthly stipends in accordance with Government of India apprenticeship rules. Applicants are strictly advised to ensure that their profile details, educational mark sheets, category certificates, and DBT-enabled bank account details are up to date before applying on the respective web portals.

Eligibility and Qualification

  • Passed ITI, Diploma, or Degree in respective trades or engineering branches from a recognized Institute or Board.
  • Candidates must be regular pass-outs (Passing Year: 2022, 2023, 2024, 2025, or 2026).
  • Result-awaited candidates or candidates currently pursuing their course are not eligible.
  • Candidates who have already undergone or are undergoing apprenticeship training elsewhere, or have one year or more job experience, are not eligible.
  • Must be registered on the official apprenticeship portal (NAPS for ITI, NATS for Degree/Diploma).

Selection Process

  1. Academic Shortlisting: Shortlisting candidates based on merit prepared from academic marks (30% weightage to Class 10th and 70% weightage to ITI/Diploma/Degree marks).
  2. Walk-in Document Verification: Shortlisted applicants report for walk-in verification between 01 and 26 September 2026 at Trainees' Hostel, Sector-5, Rourkela.
  3. Medical Examination: Candidates cleared in verification must meet the medical fitness standards prescribed by SAIL RSP.
  4. Final Engagement: Issuance of contract letters through NAPS/NATS portals.

Exam Pattern

There is No Written Exam or Test for SAIL Rourkela Apprentice Recruitment 2026. The selection is entirely merit-based followed by physical document verification.

How to Apply

  1. For Trade Apprentices (ITI): Visit the NAPS Portal at apprenticeshipindia.gov.in and register/log in. Search for "Steel Authority of India Limited, Rourkela Steel Plant" under Apprenticeship Opportunities, choose your trade, and apply.
  2. For Graduate & Technician Apprentices: Visit the NATS Portal at nats.education.gov.in and register/log in. Navigate to "Find Establishments", search for "Steel Authority of India Limited, Rourkela Steel Plant", select your branch, and click Apply.
  3. Verify all personal details, uploaded documents, and contact information before final submission.
  4. Take a clear printout of the submitted profile and application confirmation page for the walk-in verification process.
