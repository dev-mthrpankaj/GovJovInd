About This Recruitment

NTPC Limited is a Government of India enterprise and one of India's major power sector companies. Through Advertisement No. 09/26, NTPC has invited online applications for Assistant Chemist Trainee posts. The recruitment is suitable for candidates who have completed regular full-time M.Sc. in Chemistry and want to work in a technical chemistry-related role in the power sector.

The notice explains that selected candidates will initially undergo one-year training at various places. After successful completion of training, the final place of posting is decided by NTPC management. Candidates should therefore read not only the qualification details, but also the training, posting and service bond conditions before submitting the form.

The selection process is based on an All India online selection test. The test has two parts: Subject Knowledge Test and Executive Aptitude Test. Candidates must qualify separately in both parts, so preparation should include both core Chemistry concepts and executive aptitude areas.

Eligibility and Qualification

  • Regular full-time M.Sc. in Chemistry from a recognized Indian University or Institute.
  • Minimum 60% marks in aggregate for eligible candidates.
  • SC, ST and PwBD candidates with passing marks are also eligible to apply.
  • Final year or final semester candidates expecting their result by 31.07.2026 may apply, subject to notification conditions.
  • Qualification must be from institutions recognized by appropriate statutory authority.
  • Candidates should ensure they meet all eligibility conditions before applying online.

Selection Process

  1. Submit online application through NTPC careers website.
  2. Eligible candidates appear for the All India based Online Selection Test.
  3. The online test includes Subject Knowledge Test and Executive Aptitude Test.
  4. Candidates must qualify separately in both parts of the test.
  5. Candidates are shortlisted category-wise according to merit and number of vacancies.
  6. Shortlisted candidates have to complete further joining and pre-employment formalities as prescribed by NTPC.

Exam Pattern

Medical Standards, Training and Service Bond

  • Before joining, candidates will undergo medical examination by the NTPC Medical Board or officer.
  • Only candidates found medically fit as per NTPC medical standards will be allowed to join.
  • Selected candidates will undergo one-year training at various places.
  • Final place of posting will be decided after completion of training according to organizational requirements.
  • Service bond for General, EWS and OBC candidates is Rs. 5,00,000.
  • Service bond for SC, ST and PwBD candidates is Rs. 1,25,000.
  • The bond requires selected candidates to serve the company for at least three years after successful completion of one-year training.
