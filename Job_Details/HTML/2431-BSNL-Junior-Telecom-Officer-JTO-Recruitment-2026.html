Recruitment Snapshot

BSNL JTO 2026 Details

  • BSNL will fill up 100 posts of Direct Recruitment quota in the Junior Telecom Officer (Telecom) Cadre from the open market.

Selection Process

  1. 1

    CBT Examination: Candidates must undergo a Online Computer Based Test (CBT) consisting of multiple-choice objective type questions.

  2. 2

    Sectional and Overall Cut-offs: Candidates must secure the minimum qualifying marks in each section and combined total to be shortlisted.

  3. 3

    Final Merit List: A merit list will be prepared centrally based on total marks obtained across all three sections of the CBT test.

  4. 4

    Document Verification: Shortlisted merit candidates will be called for verification of original age, caste, and degree certificates.

  5. 5

    Medical Examination: Final appointment is strictly subject to clearing the BSNL medical standards and color vision test.

Scheme of Examination & Syllabus

The pattern of the question paper will be objective multiple-choice questions (MCQ) with negative marking. For each correct answer, 04 Marks are awarded, and for each wrong answer, (-) 1 Mark is deducted.

Minimum Qualifying Cut-off Marks

  • UR / OBC (Creamy Layer) Candidates: Minimum 40% marks in each individual section and an overall minimum combined aggregate of 50%.
  • SC / ST / OBC (NCL) / EWS / PwBD Candidates: Minimum 35% marks in each individual section and an overall minimum combined aggregate of 45%.

Brief Sectional Syllabus Topics

  • Section I: Materials and components, Physical Electronics, Electron Devices and ICs, Network theory, Electromagnetic Theory, Electronic Measurements & Instrumentation, Power Electronics.
  • Section II: Analog Electronic Circuits, Digital Electronic Circuits, Control Systems, Communication systems, Microwave Engineering, Computer Engineering, Microprocessors.
  • Section III (General Ability): General English comprehension exercises, current events knowledge, everyday observations, scientific aspects, and developments in Telecommunications, History of India, and Geography.

Service Bond & Training Details

  • Service Bond: Recruited candidates will execute a mandatory service bond of Rs. 6 Lakhs (Rupees Six Lakhs only) to serve BSNL for at least 5 years from appointment.
  • Probation Period: Candidates will be put on probation for 2 years.
  • Pre-Appointment Training Scheme: JTO Phase-I training (10 weeks) + Field Training (4 weeks) + JTO Phase-II specialization training (6 weeks). Candidates get a stipend allowance during training.
  • Seniority Weightage Rule: Seniority will be fixed using 60% weightage of CBT exam marks and 40% weightage of Phase-I training marks. In case of a tie, the older candidate is senior.
