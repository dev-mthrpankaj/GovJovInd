About This Recruitment

The Uttar Pradesh Subordinate Services Selection Commission (UPSSSC), Lucknow, has announced a public recruitment scheme under Advertisement Number 12-Exam/2026. This selection process aims to fill vacancies for the positions of Forest Guard (Van Rakshak) and Wildlife Guard (Vanya Jeev Rakshak) under the direct administrative control of the Chief Conservator of Forests and Head of Department, Uttar Pradesh. The recruitment includes a mix of regular vacancies and special selection seats, providing a structural pathway into state administration for intermediate-pass applicants.

A primary criterion for application is that candidates must have participated in the Preliminary Eligibility Test (PET-2025) and hold a valid score card issued by the Commission. Candidates with negative or zero marks in PET 2025 will be excluded from the shortlisting process. The initial application system requires authentication using individual PET registration numbers via OTP verification, ensuring an integrated security check before entry into the system.

The comprehensive screening mechanism is designed to evaluate both theoretical capability and bodily endurance. Following the initial online submission, eligible candidates will face a competitive objective test, an endurance verification run, a strict physical dimensions assessment, and a standard board-approved medical checkup. Aspirants are strongly encouraged to review the performance rules, structural details, and reservation rules prior to submitting their registration forms.

Eligibility and Qualification

  • Mandatory Academic Record: Must have passed the Intermediate Examination conducted by the Board of High School and Intermediate Education, Uttar Pradesh, or any matching qualification officially recognized by the State Government.
  • PET Participation Rule: Possession of an active tracking PET 2025 registration number and an absolute or normalized score greater than zero marks.
  • Preferential Weightage: Candidates serving in the Territorial Army for at least 2 years, holding an NCC 'B' certificate, possessing a computer application certificate from a certified center, or who have represented the state in sporting events will receive preference if their scores are tied.

Selection Process

  1. PET 2025 Verification: Preliminary filtering of all entry applications based on relative position in the normalized score grid.
  2. Main Written Test: Participation in an objective state-wide examination for shortlisted candidates.
  3. Physical Standard Test (PST): Measuring physical features against official parameters using standardized tools.
  4. Physical Efficiency Test (PET): An endurance run assessing physical strength while carrying specified weights.
  5. Medical Examination: Assessment by an appointed District Medical Board to verify physical health.
  6. Document Evaluation: Verification of age certificates, caste claims, and academic records before finalizing the merit list.

Exam Pattern

The written component comprises a single objective test. The duration of the exam is 120 minutes (2 hours). It contains 100 questions for a total of 100 marks. A negative marking penalty of 25% (1/4 mark) applies for each wrong answer.

How to Apply

  1. Navigate your web browser to the official commission platform at upsssc.gov.in.
  2. Locate the Live Advertisement Segment on the homepage and click on Advertisement 12-Exam/2026.
  3. Log in using your PET 2025 registration number through either Personal Details or the OTP Verification option.
  4. Review your pre-filled background data and fill in the required academic entries for your intermediate qualification.
  5. Verify your photograph and signature, which are automatically imported from your PET profile.
  6. Complete the declaration checkboxes after checking all details for accuracy.
  7. Pay the online processing fee of Rs. 25 using integrated channels like Debit/Credit cards, Internet Banking, or UPI.
  8. Confirm payment, reconcile your transaction ID using Double Verification if needed, and print out your completed form.

Frequently Asked Questions

What is the last date to apply for UPSSSC Forest Guard Recruitment 2026?

The last date for online application submission and fee payment is 20 July 2026, as per the official advertisement.

How many total vacancies are announced in this advertisement?

There are a total of 708 vacant posts, which include Forest Guard and Wildlife Guard positions under General and Special Selection.

Who is eligible to apply for these posts?

Candidates must have completed the Intermediate Examination from the Board of High School and Intermediate Education UP or an equivalent recognized exam, and they must have a valid score card from the Preliminary Eligibility Test (PET-2025).

What is the age limit specified for the recruitment?

The required age limit is 18 to 40 years as on 1 July 2026, with age relaxation applicable for reserved categories according to government rules.

What is the application fee for all candidate categories?

An online processing fee of Rs. 25 is uniformly applicable to General, OBC, SC, and ST candidates at the initial application stage. Main exam fees will be collected separately from shortlisted applicants.

What are the physical race standards for male and female applicants?

Male candidates are required to complete a 25 km run carrying a weight of 10 kg within 4 hours. Female candidates must complete a 14 km run within 4 hours.

Is there negative marking in the written examination?

Yes, there is a provision for negative marking where 25% or 1/4 of the marks assigned to a question will be deducted for each incorrect answer.

What is the pay level for the Forest Guard post?

The post belongs to Pay Matrix Level-2, with a minimum salary pay scale of Rs. 19,900 to a maximum of Rs. 63,200.

Can candidates make corrections in their application forms?

Yes, candidates can modify specific fields in their submitted applications up to the correction deadline of 27 July 2026.

Which website should candidates visit to submit the online form?

Candidates must submit their forms through the official website of the Uttar Pradesh Subordinate Services Selection Commission at upsssc.gov.in.
