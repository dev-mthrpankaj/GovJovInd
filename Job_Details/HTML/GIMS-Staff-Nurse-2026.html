About This Recruitment

GIMS Staff Nurse Recruitment 2026 is an official recruitment drive conducted by the Government Institute of Medical Sciences (GIMS), Greater Noida, Uttar Pradesh under Advertisement No. GIMS/2026/Manpower/NT-03/10. The notification invites online applications for 100 posts of Staff Nurse (Non Teaching Post).

The selection process and online registration are managed through the official website. Candidates must fulfill the educational qualifications (B.Sc Nursing or GNM with requisite experience) and registration requirements with the State/Indian Nursing Council. The application process is online, requiring registration with a valid email ID and mobile number, filling up personal and educational details, uploading scanned documents, and submitting the fee before the deadline.

Category-wise vacancy distribution includes General, OBC, EWS, SC, and ST seats under Uttar Pradesh government reservation policies. Candidates are advised to thoroughly read the official notification before filling out the online application form.

Eligibility and Qualification

  • B.Sc Nursing (4 years course) from an Indian Nursing Council / State Medical Faculty recognized Institute / University OR B.Sc Nursing (Post Basic).
  • Registered as Nurse & Midwife in State/Indian Nursing Council.
  • OR Diploma in General Nursing Midwifery (G.N.M) from an Indian Nursing Council recognized Institute / Board or Council.
  • Registered as Nurse & Midwife in State / Indian Nursing Council.
  • Two years experience in a minimum 50 bedded hospital after acquiring the GNM educational qualification mentioned above.

How to Apply for GIMS Staff Nurse Vacancy 2026

  1. Visit the official website of GIMS at gims.ac.in/recruitment.html.
  2. Click on the "GIMS Staff Nurse 2026 Apply Online" link.
  3. Register using a valid email ID and mobile number.
  4. Login using your registration ID and password.
  5. Fill out the application form with personal, educational, and category details.
  6. Upload scanned documents (photograph, signature, certificates, etc.).
  7. Pay the application fee through online payment mode.
  8. Submit the application form and download/print the final confirmation page for future reference.

Documents Required

  • Recent passport-size photograph and signature (scanned).
  • B.Sc Nursing Degree / GNM Diploma marksheets and passing certificates.
  • Nursing Council Registration Certificate (State / Indian Nursing Council).
  • Experience certificate of minimum 2 years in a 50-bedded hospital (for GNM candidates).
  • Class 10th certificate / Date of birth proof.
  • Caste / Category certificate (SC / ST / OBC / EWS), if applicable.
  • Disability certificate (for PH candidates of UP).
