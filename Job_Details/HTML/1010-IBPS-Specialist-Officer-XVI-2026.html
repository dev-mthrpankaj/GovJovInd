About This Recruitment

The Common Recruitment Process for Specialist Officers (CRP SPL-XVI) is a specialized channel administered by the Institute of Banking Personnel Selection to recruit highly qualified domain professionals across six technical cadres in public sector banking infrastructure. Selected personnel are assigned distinct institutional portfolios where technical proficiency is directly required to support security framework checking, corporate law administration, multilingual dissemination, and credit assessment fields.

The portal accepts digital forms during the registration block only. Because individual appointments are executed strictly against verified reporting indices of participating corporate banks, candidates should choose their respective post category with intense precision. Merit listing patterns are fundamentally built upon core professional expertise parameters rather than universal aptitude filters alone.

The progression involves multi-tier sequential stages starting with computer-based objective testing, followed by an in-depth specialized main paper and structured nodal interviews. Scoring algorithms enforce strict sectional limits across all testing units. Candidates preparing for these roles must ensure complete availability of technical certificates and professional qualifications prior to the closing threshold.

Selection Process

The structured assessment timeline consists of the following consecutive implementation stages:

  1. Online Preliminary Examination: Objective qualifying pattern with distinct subject timings.
  2. Online Main Examination: Evaluating core Professional Knowledge through dedicated testing formats.
  3. Common Interview: Conducted by participating institutions under a Nodal Bank with 100 marks weighting.
  4. Identity and Document Verification: Strict verification of biological features and qualification documents.
  5. Provisional Allotment: Prepared with an 80:20 relative weightage assigned to the Main Exam and Interview performance respectively.

c. Online Main Exam Structure

In the Main examination, all tests except Professional Knowledge will be qualifying in nature, and merit listing will be done on the basis of candidates' scores in the test of Professional Knowledge. Main papers contain 150 questions for a total of 225 marks over a 155-minute structural window. Detailed breakdowns include objective blocks (50 questions, 100 marks, 40-45 mins) and descriptive papers (2 questions, 25 marks, 30 mins) as configured per cadre rules.

Wrong answers carry a penalty deduction of 0.25 marks from the objective raw cumulative score.
