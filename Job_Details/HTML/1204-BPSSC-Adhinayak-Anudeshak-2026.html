About This Recruitment

BPSSC Adhinayak Anudeshak recruitment is for Bihar Home Guard service cadre under the Home Department, Bihar. The post carries Pay Level 4 and requires candidates to meet educational, age and physical standards.

The recruitment is suitable for candidates who are comfortable with a police/home guard style selection process. The written examination is qualifying for the physical stage, while final merit is based on physical efficiency test marks as described in the notice.

Candidates should check category, domicile and physical standards carefully. Bihar-specific reservation and women horizontal reservation conditions are important in this recruitment.

Eligibility and Qualification

  • Candidate must be an Indian citizen.
  • Male, female and third gender candidates can apply.
  • Educational qualification: Intermediate pass or equivalent as on 01.08.2025.
  • Candidates must satisfy prescribed physical standards for height, chest and weight where applicable.

Selection Process

  1. Scrutiny of online applications.
  2. Written competitive examination of 100 marks.
  3. Candidates scoring less than 30% in written exam are not qualified for PET.
  4. Candidates up to about five times category-wise vacancies may be called for PET based on written marks.
  5. Physical Efficiency Test of 100 marks.
  6. Final merit list is prepared on the basis of PET marks.
  7. Medical examination, character and antecedent verification before appointment.

Exam Pattern

The written examination is of matriculation level with 100 objective questions for 100 marks and two hours duration. It is qualifying in nature and not the final merit basis.
