About This Recruitment

UP Van Nigam Lucknow Recruitment 2026 is an outsourcing recruitment drive published under the Regional Employment Office through the UP Rojgar Sangam Sewayojan Portal. This recruitment offers employment opportunities across 06 total posts divided into Unskilled Laborer and Semi Skilled Labour roles in Uttar Pradesh.

The online application process is conducted through the official UP Sewayojan Portal (sewayojan.up.nic.in). Interested candidates must register as job seekers on the portal, log in, fill in personal and educational qualification details, and submit their application before the deadline of 08 August 2026.

Candidates should note that these posts are categorized under Outsourcing Jobs. Selected candidates will be assigned duty under UP Van Nigam in Uttar Pradesh with a monthly salary ranging from Rs. 10,001 to Rs. 20,000/-.

How to Apply

  1. Visit the official website of UP Rojgar Sangam Sewayojan Portal at sewayojan.up.nic.in.
  2. Complete the Job Seeker registration process using a valid email ID and mobile number.
  3. Log in using your Registration ID and Password.
  4. Navigate to the "Outsourced Jobs" / "Rojgar Sangam" section.
  5. Search for "UP Van Nigam Lucknow Recruitment 2026".
  6. Fill the application form carefully with personal, educational, and category details.
  7. Upload required scanned documents including photograph and signature.
  8. Submit the form and download or print the final confirmation page for future reference.

Documents Required

  • Recent passport size photograph and signature.
  • Educational qualification marksheet/certificate (High School / Below High School as applicable).
  • Date of birth proof.
  • Aadhar Card / Identity Proof.
  • Category certificate (if applicable).
  • Active UP Sewayojan Job Seeker Registration Profile.
