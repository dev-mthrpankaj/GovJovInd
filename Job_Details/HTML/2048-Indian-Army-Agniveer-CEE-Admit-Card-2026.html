What Happens After the CEE Result?

  1. 1

    Physical Fitness Test (PFT): Shortlisted candidates appear for the prescribed physical fitness events according to their category and recruiting-office instructions.

  2. 2

    Physical Measurement Test (PMT): Height, chest and other applicable standards are checked according to the relevant recruitment rules.

  3. 3

    Document Verification: Original educational, identity, domicile/category and other required documents are verified.

  4. 4

    Medical Examination: Candidates must satisfy the medical standards prescribed for the applicable Army entry.

Candidate Checklist

  • Open the official Army portal and locate the result/list for your recruiting zone or ARO.
  • Confirm your roll number and the exact next-stage instructions issued for your recruiting office.
  • Do not assume another ARO's rally or reporting date applies to you.
  • Keep original documents and the required photocopies ready according to the official call/rally notice.
  • Use the dedicated result page linked above for the CEE result record and follow the official portal for subsequent notices.
