How to Download Indian Army CEE Admit Card 2026

  1. 1

    Open the official login: Visit Join Indian Army website or the CEE 2026 Digialm login link.

  2. 2

    Enter credentials: Enter JIA Roll Number for the applied category, date of birth in DDMMYYYY format as password, and captcha.

  3. 3

    Change password if asked: After first login, create a new password using uppercase, lowercase, number and symbol as required by the portal.

  4. 4

    Login again: Use the new password, open the Admit Card tab and click Download Admit Card.

  5. 5

    Print and read instructions: Take a clear printout and read reporting time, exam centre, ID proof and prohibited-item instructions carefully.

  6. 6

    Multiple applications: If you applied for more than one category, repeat the process and download every category admit card separately.

Details to Check on Admit Card

Prohibited Items

Read exam-day instructions and avoid carrying items that the examination authority has restricted.

FAQs

What is the official website for Indian Army CEE 2026 admit card?

The official website is joinindianarmy.nic.in. The current CEE 2026 login is hosted on the Digialm portal linked by Join Indian Army.

What password is required for first login?

The official instruction PDF says the initial password is date of birth in DDMMYYYY format. After first login, candidates may be asked to change password.

Do I need separate admit card for each category?

Yes. If you applied for more than one category, the official instructions say you must download the admit card for each category separately.
