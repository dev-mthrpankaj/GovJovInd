Selection Process Stages

The Agnipath scheme standardizes evaluation across consecutive assessment windows:

  1. 1

    Stage I (Online Written Exam): Common Entrance Examination (CEE) across designated digital testing labs.

  2. 2

    Stage II (Physical Fitness Test - PFT): 1.6 KM run tracking (Group I: Up to 5m 30s for 60 marks; Group II: 5m 31s to 5m 45s for 48 marks), pull-ups metrics, 9-feet ditch clearance, and zig-zag balance stabilization.

  3. 3

    Stage III (Physical Measurement Test - PMT): Height, chest expansion checkups matching structural regional zone cutoffs.

  4. 4

    Stage IV (Document Verification & Medical Evaluation): Ground documentation scrutiny along with physical board medical clearances.
