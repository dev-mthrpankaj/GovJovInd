Selection Process Stages

  1. 1

    Stage I - CEE: Computer-based Common Entrance Examination.

  2. 2

    Stage II - Physical Fitness Test: Includes 1.6 km run, pull-ups and other prescribed events according to the applicable entry.

  3. 3

    Physical Measurement Test: Height, chest and other measurements are checked according to regional and category standards.

  4. 4

    Document Verification & Medical: Original documents are checked and candidates undergo the prescribed Army medical examination.

What Happens After the CEE Result?

  1. 1

    Check the result/list for your recruiting zone or ARO.

  2. 2

    Read the exact rally/reporting instructions issued by your recruiting office.

  3. 3

    Prepare for PFT and PMT according to the standards applicable to your entry.

  4. 4

    Carry original educational, identity, domicile/category and other required documents.

  5. 5

    Complete medical examination and subsequent selection formalities as directed.

Phase 2 Candidate Checklist

  • Confirm your roll number in the correct ARO/ZRO shortlisted list.
  • Do not assume another recruiting office's rally or reporting date applies to you.
  • Keep originals plus required photocopies of educational and identity documents ready.
  • Carry category, domicile and other applicable certificates in the prescribed format.
  • Follow only official Army instructions for physical, medical and document-verification dates.

Help Desk / Official Support

  • Recruiting Office: Phase 2 reporting and rally instructions should be confirmed from the relevant ARO/ZRO.
  • Website Support: Use contact information published on the official Join Indian Army portal.
