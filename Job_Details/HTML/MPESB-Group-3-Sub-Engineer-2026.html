About This Recruitment

The MPESB Group 3 Sub Engineer Recruitment 2026 is an official drive conducted by the Madhya Pradesh Employees Selection Board (MPESB). The organization has invited online applications to fill 1700 posts comprising Sub Engineer, Sahayak Manchitrakar, Technician, and other equivalent roles. This recruitment is a significant opportunity for candidates holding technical qualifications in Madhya Pradesh.

Interested candidates can fill out their online application starting from 29 August 2026 up to the last date of 12 September 2026. Prior to submitting the application, candidates must ensure they meet the specific eligibility requirements and complete the fee payment. The selection process will primarily be based on an official written examination scheduled for 07 October 2026.

Candidates are strongly advised to read the complete official notification to understand the category-wise vacancy distribution, detailed syllabus, and exam center options. The official notification board and MP Online portal provide all necessary instructions for a successful application.

How to Apply

  1. Visit the official website of MPESB at esb.mp.gov.in.
  2. Click on the “MPESB Group 3 Sub Engineer 2026 Apply Online” link under the latest updates or examinations section.
  3. Register yourself using a valid email ID and mobile number. (Complete MP Profile and Rojgar Panjiyan if not already done).
  4. Login using your registration ID and password.
  5. Fill the application form carefully with personal, educational, and category details.
  6. Upload required scanned documents (photograph, signature, etc.) in the prescribed format.
  7. Pay the application fee through online mode (Debit/Credit Card, Net Banking) or via an authorized MP Kiosk.
  8. Submit the form and download/print the confirmation page for future reference.

Documents Required

  • Recent passport-size colored photograph (with name and date as per MPESB rules).
  • Scanned copy of signature.
  • Educational certificates and mark sheets (ITI/Diploma/Degree).
  • Valid MP Rojgar Panjiyan registration number.
  • Caste/Category certificate (SC/ST/OBC/EWS) if claiming reservation.
  • Valid photo ID proof (Aadhaar Card, PAN Card, Voter ID).
