About This Recruitment

Madhya Pradesh Employees Selection Board (MPESB) has released the official notification for the MPESB Group 3 Sub Engineer Recruitment 2026. A total of 1040 vacancies have been announced. Interested and eligible candidates holding a valid Degree or Diploma can complete their online application process starting from 18 August 2026.

The application process is exclusively online through the MP Online portal. Candidates must ensure they meet all the eligibility parameters detailed in the official notification. Registration involves filling personal and educational details, uploading scanned documents, and successfully completing the fee payment.

The selection process for the Group 3 Sub Engineer post generally involves a competitive written examination, which is scheduled for 06 October 2026. Make sure to regularly check the official portal for admit card downloads and result updates.

How to Apply

  1. Visit the official website of MPESB Group 3 Sub Engineer at esb.mp.gov.in or the MP Online portal.
  2. Click on the "MPESB Group 3 Sub Engineer 2026 Apply Online" link.
  3. Register using a valid email ID and mobile number.
  4. Log in using your new registration ID and password.
  5. Fill the application form carefully with your personal, educational, and category details.
  6. Upload scanned documents including photograph and signature as per the required dimensions.
  7. Pay the application fee through the available online payment modes or at an MP Kiosk.
  8. Submit the form and download or print the confirmation page for future reference.

Documents Required

  • Recent color passport size photograph and scanned signature.
  • Educational certificates (Degree/Diploma mark sheets).
  • Class 10th certificate for Date of Birth proof.
  • Category certificate (OBC / SC / ST / EWS) if claiming reservation.
  • Madhya Pradesh domicile certificate (if applying as a native candidate).
  • Any other relevant documents as specified in the rulebook.
