About This Recruitment

The Indian Navy Technician Recruitment 2026 offers an excellent opportunity for candidates possessing an ITI certificate or an Ex-Naval Apprenticeship. Based on the official Navy Technician.pdf notification (Advt No. 01/2026), the Indian Navy has announced 1266 vacancies for Tradesman Skilled and Technician roles across various Naval Commands including the Eastern, Southern, Western Naval Commands and Naval Dockyard Mumbai.

Candidates can complete their online application process directly via the official portal before the closing date of 02 September 2026. Because this is a central recruitment drive, candidates must carefully check the post-wise trade eligibility and upload the correct technical documents as required in the official notification.

The category-wise vacancy distribution, age limits, selection process, and completely fee-free application process are detailed below as per the verified notification data.

Eligibility and Qualification

  • Matriculation: Passed 10th class (Matriculation) or equivalent from a recognized Board.
  • Technical Qualification: Must possess a certificate from a recognized Industrial Training Institute (ITI) in the relevant trade OR hold an Apprenticeship Certificate obtained as an Ex-Naval Apprentice from Naval Apprentice Schools of the Indian Navy.
  • Candidates must meet all standard medical and physical eligibility rules as stipulated by the Indian Navy.

Salary and Pay Scale

The posts fall under the Group 'C' Non-Gazetted, Industrial category. Selected candidates will be placed in the 7th CPC Pay Matrix Level 2.

  • Pay Scale: Rs.19,900 - Rs.63,200 per month.
  • Allowances such as DA (Dearness Allowance), HRA (House Rent Allowance), Transport Allowance, and standard naval medical facilities will be applicable as per central government norms.

Selection Process

The recruitment involves multiple stages to assess the candidates' qualifications and skills:

  • Application Screening
    Initial shortlisting of forms based on essential qualifications and valid technical certificates.
  • Written Examination
    A computer-based test (CBT) covering General Intelligence, General Awareness, Quantitative Aptitude, English, and Trade-related technical knowledge.
  • Document Verification
    Verification of original ITI, 10th marksheets, age proof, and category certificates.
  • Medical Examination
    Final physical and medical fitness check by authorized naval medical boards.

How to Apply

  1. Visit the official recruitment portal of the Indian Navy at joinindiannavy.gov.in.
  2. Go to the 'Join Navy -> Civilians' section and look for the Tradesman Skilled / Technician 2026 application link.
  3. Complete the new registration using an active email ID and mobile number.
  4. Log in using the provided credentials and fill in your personal, educational, and trade details accurately.
  5. Select only one specific Trade/Yard you wish to apply for.
  6. Upload scanned copies of your passport-size photograph, signature, and mandatory documents.
  7. Review the entire form carefully and click on the final submit button.
  8. Download and take a printout of the application form for future reference.

Documents Required

  • Recent passport-size colour photograph (light background, no cap/goggles).
  • Scanned signature.
  • Matriculation (10th) marksheet and passing certificate (for Date of Birth verification).
  • ITI Certificate in relevant trade OR National Apprenticeship Certificate (NAC).
  • Category/Caste certificate (SC/ST/OBC/EWS) if claiming relaxation.
  • Disability Certificate (for PwBD candidates), if applicable.
  • Valid Photo ID proof (Aadhaar Card, PAN Card, Voter ID, etc.).
