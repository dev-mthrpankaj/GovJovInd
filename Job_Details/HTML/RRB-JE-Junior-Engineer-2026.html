About This Recruitment

The Railway Recruitment Boards (RRBs) recruit engineering graduates and diploma holders for supervisory technical cadres across Indian Railways under Centralised Employment Notice (CEN) No. 04/2026. This recruitment drive covers multiple technical departments including Civil, Electrical, Mechanical, Signal & Telecommunication, and Stores. Selected Junior Engineers oversee infrastructure creation, train operations, safety maintenance, and technical assets across various Railway zones.

Applications must be submitted exclusively through the centralized online application portal. Candidates are strongly advised to complete Aadhaar-based registration and ensure that personal particulars match their Class 10 certificate to prevent technical verification delays at subsequent selection stages. Candidates can apply to only one zonal RRB for the notified posts.

The recruitment process involves two stages of Computer Based Tests followed by strict Document Verification and medical fitness standards relevant to technical Railway cadres. A clear understanding of the discipline-wise technical syllabus, negative marking rules, and stage-wise qualification benchmarks is essential for candidates planning to appear in this nationwide examination.

Eligibility and Qualification

  • Junior Engineer (JE): Three years Diploma in Engineering OR Degree (B.E. / B.Tech.) in relevant streams such as Civil, Mechanical, Electrical, Electronics, Computer Science, or Information Technology from a recognized University/Institute.
  • Depot Material Superintendent (DMS): Three years Diploma in Engineering OR Degree in any discipline from a recognized University/Institute.
  • Chemical & Metallurgical Assistant (CMA): Bachelor's Degree in Science (B.Sc.) with Physics and Chemistry with a minimum required percentage from a recognized University.

Selection Process

  1. 1st Stage Computer Based Test (CBT Stage-1) – Screening / Qualifying Stage.
  2. 2nd Stage Computer Based Test (CBT Stage-2) – Core Subject / Technical Evaluation Stage.
  3. Document Verification (DV) – Authentic credential check of qualified candidates.
  4. Medical Examination (ME) – Medical fitness test as per prescribed Railway standards (A-3 / B-1 / B-2).

Exam Pattern

CBT Stage-1 Structure: Screening test containing 100 Questions for 100 Marks with a total duration of 90 Minutes. Negative marking of 1/3rd mark (0.33) is applicable for each wrong attempt.

Salary and Benefits

  • Pay Level: Level-6 of 7th CPC Pay Matrix.
  • Initial Pay: Rs. 35,400/- per month.
  • Allowances: Dearness Allowance (DA), House Rent Allowance (HRA), Transport Allowance (TA), and Railway Medical/Pass Facilities as per Central Government rules.

Documents Required

  • 10th / Matriculation Marksheet & Certificate (proof of Date of Birth).
  • Engineering Diploma / Degree / B.Sc Marksheets and Passing Certificates.
  • Caste / Community Certificate (SC / ST / OBC-NCL / EWS format).
  • Aadhaar Card (updated with latest photo and biometrics for online registration).
  • Scanned passport-size photograph (clear background) and digital signature.
  • NOC and Service Certificate for existing Railway / Government employees.

How to Apply

  1. Visit the official portal at rrbapply.gov.in or your regional RRB portal.
  2. Click on "Create an Account" and register using a valid email ID, active mobile number, and Aadhaar verification.
  3. Log in with your created registration credentials and select CEN No. 04/2026.
  4. Fill in all personal details, educational qualifications, community category, and choice of Railway Zone.
  5. Upload your recent photograph, signature, and community certificates in the specified dimensions.
  6. Pay the required application fee through online payment modes (UPI, Net Banking, Cards).
  7. Verify all entered details before submitting the application form.
  8. Download and print a copy of the submitted application form for future recruitment stages.
