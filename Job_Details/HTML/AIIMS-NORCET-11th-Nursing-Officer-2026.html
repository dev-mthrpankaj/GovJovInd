About This Recruitment

The All India Institute of Medical Sciences (AIIMS New Delhi) conducts the Nursing Officer Recruitment Common Eligibility Test (NORCET) twice a year as a nationwide selection mechanism. The 11th phase (NORCET-11) aims to fill 2,218 Nursing Officer vacancies across various autonomous AIIMS institutes across India as well as participating Central Government hospitals. Candidates holding qualifying degrees or diplomas in nursing and registered with State or Indian Nursing Councils are eligible to participate in this competitive national process.

The application procedure is entirely online through the official examination portal of AIIMS. Candidates are required to create or log in via One-Time Registration (OTR), complete the recruitment form, upload requisite scanned images along with experience certificates (if applicable), and clear the application fee before the stated deadline. Candidates belonging to SC and ST categories who appear for the Stage-I examination are eligible for a fee refund after the test is successfully conducted.

Selection for AIIMS Nursing Officer posts follows a structured two-tier computer-based examination system: Stage-I Preliminary Exam and Stage-II Mains Exam. Stage-I screens candidates through general nursing concepts, basic aptitude, and general awareness, while Stage-II focuses entirely on core professional nursing competencies and clinical case scenarios. Successful candidates are allocated participating institutes according to merit, preference, and reservation norms.

Eligibility and Qualification

  • B.Sc. (Hons.) Nursing / B.Sc. Nursing from an Indian Nursing Council / State Nursing Council recognized institute or university. OR
  • B.Sc. (Post-Certificate) / Post-Basic B.Sc. Nursing from an Indian Nursing Council / State Nursing Council recognized institute.
  • Must be registered as Nurses & Midwife with State / Indian Nursing Council. OR
  • Diploma in General Nursing Midwifery (GNM) from a recognized board/council AND registered as Nurse & Midwife with State / Indian Nursing Council, PLUS 2 years of experience in a minimum 50-bedded hospital after acquiring the educational qualification.

Selection Process

  1. Stage-I: NORCET Preliminary Examination (Qualifying Screening CBT Exam).
  2. Stage-II: NORCET Mains Examination (Competitive CBT Exam for Merit ranking).
  3. Document Verification of original certificates, qualification documents, nursing registration, and experience certificates.
  4. Medical Examination by allocated AIIMS / Hospital authorities before issuance of final joining letter.

Exam Pattern

The examination is conducted in two separate stages in Computer Based Test (CBT) mode with negative marking of 1/3 mark for every incorrect response.

Documents Required

  • Valid Mobile Number & Email ID for registration.
  • Recent Passport Size Photograph (white background).
  • Scanned Signature & Left Thumb Impression.
  • Class 10th Certificate / Marksheet (for Date of Birth proof).
  • B.Sc Nursing / Post-Basic B.Sc / GNM Marksheets and Degree/Diploma Certificates.
  • Valid Registration Certificate as Nurse & Midwife with State / Indian Nursing Council.
  • Hospital Experience Certificate (min. 50-bedded hospital) for GNM candidates.
  • Category / Caste Certificate (SC/ST/OBC/EWS/PwBD), if applicable.
  • NOC from current employer, if working in Government service.

How to Apply

  1. Visit the official portal of AIIMS Exams at aiimsexams.ac.in.
  2. Click on "NORCET-11th" under the Recruitment tab.
  3. Proceed with One-Time Registration (OTR) if not already registered, generating your Candidate ID and password.
  4. Log in using your Candidate ID and Password to fill the online application form.
  5. Enter personal details, academic qualifications, council registration number, and hospital experience details accurately.
  6. Upload clear scanned copies of photo, signature, thumb impression, and essential certificates according to specified format sizes.
  7. Pay the prescribed application fee via online payment gateway.
  8. Review the application preview carefully before clicking final submission.
  9. Download and print out the completed registration form for future document verification.
