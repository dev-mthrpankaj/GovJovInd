About This Recruitment

Hindustan Copper Limited (HCL), a vertically integrated, multi-Unit, Schedule-A Miniratna Category-I Central Public Sector Enterprise, is conducting this recruitment to onboard qualified professionals in managerial and executive streams. The current openings span cross-functional technical and administrative areas including Mining, Geology, Electrical, Mechanical, HR, Finance, and Corporate Communications. Eligible Indian nationals seeking career paths within a public sector giant are encouraged to check the official guidelines.

The recruitment process expects candidates to possess specialized degrees, valid post-qualification corporate or field experience, and specific statutory credentials depending upon the respective cadre. Applications must be completed online exclusively via HCL's web portal. Since selection relies extensively on background tracking, applicants are advised to render correct service particulars, corporate turnover values where needed, and identical educational profiles during application compilation.

Instead of a formal comprehensive written selection test, candidates will be selected directly through Personal Interviews following structural screening assessments based on years of relevant field exposure and higher education standards. Selected executives are required to accept service placement terms across India or overseas business interests, coupled with execution parameters containing a mandatory minimum structural bond period.

Eligibility and Qualification

  • Mining: Bachelor's Degree in Mining Engineering. For E4, a First Class Mine Manager's Certificate of Competency (Unrestricted) is mandatory.
  • Geology: Post-Graduate Degree in Geology / Applied Geology.
  • Environment Management: Bachelor's Degree in Environment Engineering/Technology OR Bachelor's Degree in Engineering/Technology with PG Degree/Diploma in Environmental Engineering / Management / Science.
  • Safety & Fire: Degree in Engineering/Technology with PG Degree/Diploma in Safety Engineering Management OR B.Tech in Safety / Fire Engineering.
  • Electrical / Mechanical / Civil / Instrumentation: Bachelor's Degree in the respective Engineering field / Technology branch.
  • Systems: Bachelor's Degree in Engineering / Technology (Information Technology / Computer Science) or MCA.
  • Medical & Health Services: MBBS with relevant PG Diploma OR MBBS with PG Degree/MS/MD/DNB.
  • HR: Bachelor's Degree (Arts/Commerce/Science/Engineering/BBA/BCA) along with an MBA specialized in Personnel Management or a relevant PG Degree/Diploma in Personnel Management / Social Work.
  • Finance: Final Examination clearing certificate from ICAI (CA) or the Institute of Cost Accountants of India (ICMAI).
  • Corporate Communications: Bachelor's Degree with a Post Graduate Degree/Diploma in Public Relations or Journalism.
  • Materials & Contracts: Graduate degree or Engineering degree combined with PG Degree/Diploma or MBA in Materials Management / Supply Chain Management.

Selection Process

  1. Screening of online data fields submitted by candidates against mandatory benchmark standards.
  2. Shortlisting for Personal Interview based on a defined ratio (1:5 for multi-vacancy posts; 1:10 for single-vacancy posts).
  3. Application of weighted points for shortlisting: 1 Mark for each completed year of relevant experience beyond minimum thresholds, and up to 2 Marks for higher education attributes.
  4. In corporate private experience scenarios involving ties, the candidate from the firm with a higher monetary turnover takes precedent rank.
  5. Execution of the comprehensive Personal Interview (PI) carrying 100% total grading weightage.
  6. Verification of absolute original documents and eligibility verification at the interview venue.
  7. Verification of personal character traits and medical standards approval before formal placement.
