About This Recruitment

UPTET 2026 is an eligibility examination for candidates who want to become teachers for classes 1 to 5 and classes 6 to 8 in Uttar Pradesh. It is not a direct appointment order or vacancy notice, but qualifying it is one of the important eligibility conditions for relevant teacher recruitment processes.

The official booklet explains online application, fee, eligibility conditions, exam scheme, qualifying marks, applicability and certificate validity. Candidates who want to apply for both primary and upper primary levels can select both papers if they hold the required eligibility.

Candidates should read the official booklet carefully before applying because incorrect category, qualification, language or paper details can affect eligibility and certificate validity.

Age Limit

The booklet does not provide a separate age limit for UPTET in the extracted important section. Eligibility depends mainly on educational/training qualifications and applicable rules.

Vacancy Details

UPTET is an eligibility test and not a post-wise vacancy advertisement. Teacher vacancies are notified separately by the concerned recruitment authority.

Eligibility and Qualification

  • For classes 1 to 5, candidates must hold one of the notified primary-level teacher education qualifications such as graduation with D.El.Ed/BTC or other equivalent routes mentioned in the booklet.
  • For classes 6 to 8, candidates must hold one of the notified upper-primary qualifications such as graduation/post-graduation with required teacher education qualification.
  • Final-year candidates in eligible teacher education courses may be provisionally allowed as per conditions.
  • Only qualifications recognized by NCTE/RCI, where applicable, are considered as per the booklet.

Selection Process

  1. Complete online registration and application through the official portal.
  2. Pay the prescribed fee for selected paper/papers.
  3. Download admit card as per schedule.
  4. Appear in the written MCQ examination.
  5. Secure minimum qualifying marks as per category.
  6. Qualified candidates receive UPTET certificate through electronic medium/DigiLocker as per booklet.

Exam Pattern

Each UPTET paper has 150 MCQ questions for 150 marks, duration 150 minutes. There is no negative marking.

How to Apply

  1. Visit the official UPESSC portal.
  2. Complete One Time Registration if required.
  3. Open the UPTET 2026 application link.
  4. Select paper/papers according to eligibility.
  5. Fill personal, educational, category and contact details carefully.
  6. Pay the prescribed application fee online.
  7. Submit the form and save the confirmation page.
  8. Keep checking the official website for exam district and admit card updates.

Why Apply For This Recruitment

UPTET is a key eligibility certificate for candidates targeting primary and upper-primary teaching opportunities in Uttar Pradesh. Since the certificate validity is stated as lifetime, qualifying the exam can be useful for future eligible teacher recruitment cycles.

Frequently Asked Questions

What is UPTET 2026?

UPTET 2026 is the Uttar Pradesh Teacher Eligibility Test for primary and upper-primary teacher eligibility.

What is the last date to apply?

The booklet lists 26 April 2026 as the last date for registration, fee and application submission.

When is the UPTET 2026 exam?

The exam dates are listed as 02, 03 and 04 July 2026.

What is the fee for General candidates?

The fee is Rs. 1000/- per paper for General, EWS and OBC candidates.

Is there negative marking?

No. The booklet states that there is no negative marking.

How many questions are in each paper?

Each paper has 150 MCQs for 150 marks.

What are qualifying marks?

General candidates need 90 marks out of 150, while reserved categories listed in the booklet need 82 marks.

Is UPTET a job vacancy?

No. It is an eligibility test, not a direct vacancy recruitment.

What is the certificate validity?

The booklet states lifetime validity from the date of result declaration.

Where can candidates apply?

Candidates should use the official UPESSC portal.
