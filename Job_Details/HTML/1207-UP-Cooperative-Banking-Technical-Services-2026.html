About This Recruitment

This recruitment is conducted by the U.P. Co-operative Institutional Service Board for selected posts in District Co-operative Banks and a technical post in U.P. State Construction Co-operative Federation Limited. The notification is a special recruitment notice and should be read carefully by candidates belonging to the eligible reserved categories of Uttar Pradesh.

The vacancies include managerial, junior managerial, assistant, typist and civil engineering posts. Because different institutions and posts have different pay structures and qualifications, candidates should match their education and category status with the exact post before submitting the online form.

The selection process is mainly examination-based, with interview or skill-related stages depending on the post category. Candidates should also keep documents such as category certificate, domicile, educational certificates and computer qualification proof ready before applying.

Eligibility and Qualification

  • Manager / Junior Manager posts require graduation or professional qualifications listed in the official notification, including commerce, economics, mathematics, statistics, computer/IT, management or banking/finance related qualifications as applicable.
  • Assistant Engineer (Civil) requires a bachelor degree in Civil Engineering.
  • Assistant / Cashier and Assistant / Typist candidates should check the exact post-wise educational and computer/typing requirements in the official notification.
  • Candidates must belong to the eligible special recruitment categories mentioned in the notification and should have required Uttar Pradesh category/domicile documents where applicable.

Selection Process

  1. Submit the online application and pay the applicable fee within the schedule.
  2. Appear in the online objective examination. If applications exceed the notified limit, the board may conduct Preliminary and Main examinations.
  3. For Category-1 and Category-2 posts, shortlisted candidates may have to appear for interview as per the notice.
  4. For Category-3 posts, skill or proficiency test may be conducted wherever required.
  5. Final selection will be prepared according to post category, marks and official recruitment rules.

How to Apply

  1. Visit the official U.P. Co-operative Institutional Service Board website.
  2. Open the Combined Co-operative Banking and Technical Services Examination 2026 application link.
  3. Read the advertisement and confirm category, age and qualification eligibility.
