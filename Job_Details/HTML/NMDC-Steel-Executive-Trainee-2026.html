About This Recruitment

NMDC Steel Limited (NSL) has published an official employment notification (Advt No. 05/2026) inviting online applications for 102 Executive Trainee positions. This recruitment offers opportunities for young engineering graduates and qualified professionals across disciplines including Mechanical, Electrical, Finance, Chemical, Civil, HR, Computer Science/IT, Instrumentation, Metallurgy, and Ceramics.

The recruitment drive aims to select talented executive trainees who will undergo orientation and structured training in steel plant operations and administrative domains. Eligible candidates are required to register through the designated online application portal, fill out required background details, upload prescribed certificates, and submit the fee before the deadline.

Candidates are advised to carefully review discipline-wise educational prerequisites, age relaxation norms, and document upload requirements specified in the official PDF notice. Regular tracking of the official portal is recommended for subsequent updates regarding examination schedules and call letter downloads.

Eligibility and Qualification

  • Valid Engineering Degree (B.E. / B.Tech) in the relevant discipline or equivalent professional qualification (CA/ICWA/MBA/MCA) as specified for each post.
  • Educational qualification must be obtained from recognized Universities, Institutes, or Deemed Universities.
  • Candidates must meet the requisite minimum aggregate marks criteria as mentioned in the detailed notice.

Selection Process

  1. Written Test / Computer Based Test (CBT) assessing domain knowledge and general aptitude.
  2. Group Discussion (GD) and/or Personal Interview (PI) for shortlisted candidates.
  3. Document Verification of original academic certificates, identity proof, and category certificates.
  4. Pre-Employment Medical Examination as per NMDC Steel Limited standards.

Exam Pattern

The selection pattern, syllabus breakdown, and evaluation criteria are detailed in the official notice. Candidates can check the official notification PDF for section-wise weightage and duration details.

How to Apply

  1. Visit the official NMDC Executive Trainee recruitment portal at nmdcetr.formflix.org or go through nsltd.in.
  2. Register using a valid email ID and active mobile number.
  3. Log in with your generated credentials and select the desired discipline.
  4. Fill in personal, academic, category, and communication details accurately.
  5. Upload scanned copies of required photograph, signature, and educational documents.
  6. Pay the prescribed application fee through online payment gateways if applicable.
  7. Review all entered details, submit the final application form, and print/save a copy for future reference.
