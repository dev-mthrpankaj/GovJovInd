About This Recruitment

The Assistant District Public Prosecution Officer (ADPO) recruitment is conducted by the Chhattisgarh Public Service Commission as a competitive assessment for filling up important legal officers' posts in the Home Department of the State. This role belongs to the Gazetted Class-II service category. Selected candidates are primarily entrusted with looking after prosecution matters, administrative legal works, and assisting the judiciary on behalf of the state government. Law graduates across India holding valid dynamic eligibility criteria can look up this state-level recruitment opportunity.

The application procedure is strictly online via the commission's official portal. No offline forms or certificates sent by post are accepted. Candidates are advised to confirm their compliance with the guidelines regarding eligibility, live photograph verification, signature size, and category certifications prior to submitting payments. The recruitment process also allows a free window for modifying mistakes, followed by a brief paid window for specific essential fields.

The evaluation methodology comprises a objective written paper alongside an interview session. The written component incorporates questions on General Knowledge (focused on India and Chhattisgarh) as well as broad topics concerning both Major and Minor statutory laws. Success across these cumulative evaluation levels decides final selection list placement under strict reservation metrics mandated by the state government regulations.

Eligibility and Qualification

  • Possession of a Bachelor of Laws (LL.B.) degree from a recognized university or institute.
  • Candidates must acquire their primary essential qualifications and certificates on or before the closing date of the online application form.
  • Basic compatibility with local language, legal procedures, and state service criteria.

Selection Process

  1. Written Examination / Computer Based Test (CBT) consisting of objective type questions.
  2. Interview / Personality Assessment stage for candidates who cross the minimum qualifying thresholds in the written stage.
  3. Document Verification of credentials, dynamic degrees, identity, and caste records before the interview board.
  4. Character checking, verification of antecedents, and formal medical fitness validation.
  5. Final merit preparation using cumulative scores from both the written examination and the interview stage.

Exam Pattern

The examination plan specifies a dynamic structure spanning 3 hours (180 minutes). It contains 150 questions for a total of 300 marks. A negative marking scheme applies where 1/3 mark is deducted for each incorrect response.

    Frequently Asked Questions

    What is CGPSC ADPO Recruitment 2026?

    It is an open competitive selection conducted by the Chhattisgarh Public Service Commission for recruitment to the Gazetted Class-II posts of Assistant District Public Prosecution Officer in the Home Department.

    What is the last date to apply online for CGPSC ADPO 2026?

    As per the official notice, the last date and time for submission of online applications is 21 July 2026 up to 23:59 hours.

    How many vacancies are advertised in this notification?

    There are a total of 15 vacancies available, which include 10 regular fresh vacancies and 5 backlog / carryforward vacancies.

    What is the essential qualification required for CGPSC ADPO?

    Candidates must possess a Bachelor of Laws (LL.B.) degree from a recognized university on or before the application deadline.

    What is the age limit for CGPSC ADPO Recruitment?

    The age limit is 21 to 30 years as of 1 January 2026. However, for local residents of Chhattisgarh, the upper age limit is relaxed up to 40 years.

    Is there a correction window available for application forms?

    Yes, a free correction window is available from 22 July 2026 to 24 July 2026. After that, a late correction window with a fee of Rs. 500 is available from 25 July 2026 to 26 July 2026.

    What is the application fee for outside state candidates?

    Candidates residing outside Chhattisgarh have to pay an application fee of Rs. 400. Local reserved categories of CG pay Rs. 300, and standard portal/GST fees apply to all.

    What is the pay scale for this position?

    Selected candidates will receive salary under Pay Matrix Level-9 (Rs. 38,100 to Rs. 1,20,400) along with applicable dearness and other allowances.

    What is the selection process for CGPSC ADPO?

    The selection process consists of two stages: a objective type written examination (or Computer Based Test) carrying 300 marks, followed by an Interview carrying 30 marks.

    Where can I find the official links to apply?

    Candidates can fill out their online application forms on the official website of the commission at www.psc.cg.gov.in.
