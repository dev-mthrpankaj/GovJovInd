About This Recruitment

The Uttar Pradesh Public Service Commission (UPPSC) has announced the UPPSC Assistant Professor Allopathy Recruitment 2026, targeting medical professionals for 1156 vacancies under Advertisement No. D-1/E-1/2026. The posts available include 1112 for Assistant Professor (Allopathy) and 44 for Professor (Allopathy).

Eligible and interested candidates with a Master's Degree (MD/MS/DNB) in a medicine-related trade can submit their online application starting from 18 August 2026. The last date to complete online registration and fee payment is 01 September 2026. Additionally, a correction window is provided up to 08 September 2026, and candidates must ensure their hard copy reaches the commission by 15 September 2026.

Candidates are strongly advised to download the official UPPSC notification and thoroughly review the specific experience requirements for their respective trade before submitting the online application form.

How to Apply

  1. Visit the official website of UPPSC at uppsc.up.nic.in.
  2. Click on the "UPPSC Assistant Professor Allopathy 2026 Apply Online" link located under the active notifications tab.
  3. Register yourself using a valid email ID and mobile number.
  4. Login using the provided registration ID and password.
  5. Fill the online application form with accurate personal, educational, and category details.
  6. Upload required scanned documents, such as your photograph and signature.
  7. Pay the application fee through the available online payment modes.
  8. Submit the application form, download or print the confirmation page, and ensure the hard copy reaches the commission before 15 September 2026.

Documents Required

  • Recent passport-size colored photograph (scanned).
  • Scanned copy of signature.
  • Educational certificates (MD/MS/DNB and relevant experience letters).
  • Caste/Category/PH certificate if claiming reservation.
  • Valid photo ID proof.
