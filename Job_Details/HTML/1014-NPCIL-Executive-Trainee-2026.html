About This Recruitment

NPCIL Executive Trainee Recruitment 2026 is for engineering graduates who have a valid GATE score in the relevant discipline. The recruitment covers Mechanical, Chemical, Electrical, Electronics, Instrumentation and Civil streams.

Candidates shortlisted on the basis of GATE 2024, GATE 2025 or GATE 2026 score are called for personal interview. NPCIL gives equal weightage to valid scores from these three GATE years for this advertisement.

Selected candidates undergo one year orientation training. After successful training, they are appointed as Scientific Officer/C in Group A, subject to meeting training, bond and service conditions mentioned in the advertisement.

Eligibility and Qualification

  • BE / B.Tech / B.Sc. Engineering / 5-year Integrated M.Tech in one of the notified engineering disciplines.
  • Minimum 60% marks as per university or institute rules.
  • Valid GATE 2024, GATE 2025 or GATE 2026 score in the same discipline as the engineering qualification.
  • Only regular full-time on-campus degrees are considered; correspondence, distance, part-time and similar modes are not eligible as per the advertisement.
  • Final year candidates may apply subject to submitting final result with at least 60% marks by the notified deadline.

Selection Process

  1. Shortlisting in order of merit based on valid GATE 2024/2025/2026 score.
  2. Candidates are shortlisted for personal interview generally in 1:12 ratio.
  3. Discipline-wise and category-wise cut-off is displayed on NPCIL recruitment portal.
  4. Final selection is based on performance and document verification as per NPCIL rules.
  5. Selected candidates must complete training and execute the prescribed bond.

Exam Pattern

There is no separate written examination mentioned in the advertisement. Shortlisting is based on valid GATE scores followed by personal interview.
