About This Recruitment

Uttar Pradesh Expressway Industrial Development Authority (UPEIDA) has announced an outsourcing recruitment notification for Computer Operator and Driver posts for 2026. This recruitment drive is being facilitated via the official UP Sewayojan Portal (Rojgar Sangam). A total of 25 vacancies are available, comprising 10 posts for Computer Operator (Lucknow) and 15 posts for Driver.

Interested candidates can fill out the online application form through the UP Sewayojan Portal starting from 05 August 2026 until the last date, 13 August 2026. There is no application fee required for any category candidate. Candidates must ensure they meet the specific educational qualifications, age criteria, and technical skills like Hindi/English typing or valid driving license before submitting their online applications.

Selection will be carried out through the standard UP Sewayojan outsourcing process. Candidates are advised to complete their profile registration on the Sewayojan portal early to avoid any last-minute technical glitches.

How to Apply for UPEIDA Computer Operator & Driver Vacancy 2026

  1. Visit the official website of UP Sewayojan (Rojgar Sangam) at sewayojan.up.nic.in.
  2. Click on "Job Seeker Registration" if you are a new user and create an account using a valid email ID and mobile number.
  3. Log in using your registration ID and password.
  4. Complete your profile by entering personal, educational, and technical/skill details.
  5. Search for "UPEIDA Computer Operator & Driver Outsourcing Recruitment 2026".
  6. Fill in the application form and upload necessary scanned documents (photo, signature, ADCA certificate, driving license, etc.).
  7. No application fee is required; proceed directly to final review.
  8. Submit the form and download or print the application confirmation page for future reference.

Documents Required

  • High School (10th) Marksheet & Certificate.
  • Graduation Degree Certificate (for Computer Operator post).
  • ADCA Certificate from DOEACC / NIELIT or equivalent institute (for Computer Operator post).
  • Valid Driving License (for Driver post).
  • Sewayojan Portal Registration Number / Profile ID.
  • Recent passport-size photograph and scanned signature.
  • Aadhaar Card or valid Identity Card.
  • Caste / Category Certificate (if applicable).
