About This Recruitment

BPSC 33rd Bihar Judicial Service Recruitment 2026 is for appointment to Civil Judge (Junior Division) posts through the Bihar Judicial Service Competitive Examination. The advertisement lists 173 vacancies.

This recruitment is meant for law graduates recognized by the Bar Council of India and candidates having required bar practice experience as stated in the notice. Candidates should ensure all law degree, bar registration and experience documents are ready before applying.

The selection has preliminary examination, main written examination and interview. The preliminary exam is objective/OMR-based, while the final selection is based on performance in main examination and interview as per rules.

Eligibility and Qualification

  • Law graduate or equivalent examination from a university/institution recognized by the Bar Council of India, New Delhi.
  • At least three years practice experience at the Bar as on advertisement date.
  • Experience is counted from enrollment/registration date in the concerned State Bar Council.
  • Experience certificate should be issued by the competent advocate / court authority as stated in the notice.

Selection Process

  1. Preliminary examination with two objective papers.
  2. Main written examination for candidates qualifying prelims.
  3. Interview / personality test of 100 marks for candidates successful in mains.
  4. Final selection on the basis of marks obtained in main written examination and interview.

Exam Pattern

The preliminary exam has two papers: General Studies of 100 marks and Law of 150 marks. The main exam includes compulsory subjects and optional law subjects as given in the advertisement. Interview is of 100 marks with 35% qualifying marks.
