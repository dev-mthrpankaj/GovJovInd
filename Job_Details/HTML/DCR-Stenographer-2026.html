About This Recruitment

The District Court Rewari (DCR) has invited applications for the recruitment of 18 Stenographer Grade III posts on an ad-hoc basis. The offline application window is open from 28 August 2026 and will close on 17 September 2026 (05:00 PM).

This is an excellent opportunity for candidates holding a Bachelor's Degree in Science, Arts, or Commerce who possess shorthand skills. Candidates must clear an English Shorthand Test at 80 W.P.M. and Transcription at 20 W.P.M. on the computer. There is no application fee required for any category.

Since the application mode is offline, candidates must download the application form, fill it out completely, and send the hard copy along with all required self-attested documents to the specified address before the deadline.
