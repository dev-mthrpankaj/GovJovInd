About This Recruitment

NBEMS is an autonomous organisation under the Ministry of Health and Family Welfare. It conducts postgraduate medical examinations at national level and has invited online applications for administrative, technical, accounts, stenography and assistant-level posts.

This recruitment is useful for candidates looking for a central autonomous body opportunity with different qualification levels. The posts include Group A, Group B and Group C positions, so candidates should check the exact post-wise age, education and skill requirement before applying.

The notification clearly states that applications are accepted online only through the official NBEMS website. Candidates should keep the confirmation page, application printout, admit card and required documents safely for later verification.

Eligibility and Qualification

  • Deputy Director (Medical): Recognized postgraduate medical qualification approved under the Medical Council Act, 1956; training in medical education technology/evaluation techniques is desirable.
  • Junior Programmer: B.Tech / B.E / BCA / DOEACC B or C level / degree in Computer Science, IT, Electronics with computer specialization or equivalent.
  • Junior Accountant: Degree in Mathematics or Statistics, or degree in Commerce from a recognized university, and passing the examination prescribed by NBEMS.
  • Stenographer: Senior Secondary (10+2) with shorthand/typing speed of 80/30 words per minute and qualifying General English / Stenography / Computer Typing test conducted by the Board.
  • Junior Assistant: Senior Secondary (12th) from a recognized board/university with proficiency in computer use and basic software packages, and passing the examination prescribed by NBEMS.

Selection Process

  1. For Deputy Director (Medical) and Junior Programmer, the examination scheme and syllabus will be notified later on the NBEMS website.
  2. For Junior Accountant, Stenographer and Junior Assistant, Stage-I will be a Computer Based Test of 200 questions and 200 marks.
  3. Stage-I duration is 180 minutes with 0.25 negative marking for each wrong answer.
  4. Stage-II will be a Computer Knowledge/Skill Test of 100 marks and 75 minutes for shortlisted candidates.
  5. Five candidates against every vacancy in each category may be called for Stage-II based on CBT merit.
  6. Final merit for Junior Accountant, Stenographer and Junior Assistant will be prepared on the total marks of Stage-I and Stage-II.
