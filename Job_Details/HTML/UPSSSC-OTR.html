Understand the service

What is UPSSSC OTR?

UPSSSC One Time Registration is a candidate-profile system designed to reduce repeated data entry in PET and future recruitment applications conducted by the Uttar Pradesh Subordinate Services Selection Commission. Candidates register core personal, educational and contact information once and receive an OTR number for use in applicable future applications.

OTR is not a vacancy and it is not an application for any particular post. After creating OTR, a candidate still needs to open the relevant recruitment advertisement and complete that recruitment's application whenever the commission invites applications.

Important: Creating an OTR does not mean you have applied for a specific UPSSSC recruitment. OTR and the individual recruitment application are separate processes.

Why it matters

Why UPSSSC OTR is Useful

One Candidate Profile

Maintain a single OTR identity for applicable UPSSSC examination and recruitment applications.

Less Repeated Entry

Core personal, contact and educational information does not need to be typed from scratch in every applicable application.

Faster Applications

A prepared OTR profile can simplify future application workflows when a UPSSSC recruitment opens.

Digital Record

Your registered profile details are linked to a reusable OTR number rather than a separate registration for each notification.

Online Access

Registration and login are provided through the official UPSSSC online portal.

Better Consistency

Using one profile can help reduce avoidable differences in repeatedly entered candidate information.

UPSSSC OTR Login

Existing Candidate

Open the official OTR portal and choose the login option available for registered candidates. Enter the credentials requested by the portal and access your OTR profile.

Login Safely

Check the official UPSSSC domain before entering credentials. Never share your password, OTP or recovery details with another person.

09

Review before final generation

OTR Update & Correction

Candidates should review every personal, educational and contact entry carefully before the OTR number is generated. Current rollout guidance indicates that information may become locked after generation of the OTR number.

If the official portal provides a correction or account-management facility for a particular field, use only that official option. Do not create a second OTR merely to correct information unless UPSSSC explicitly instructs you to do so.

Account access help

Forgot OTR Number or Login Details?

Use Official Account Recovery

Open the official OTR portal and use the recovery/login-help option made available there. Keep access to your registered mobile number and email ID. Never send OTP, password or identity documents to unofficial agents.
