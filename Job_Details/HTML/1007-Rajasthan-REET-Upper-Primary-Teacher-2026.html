Rajasthan Upper Primary School Teacher (Sanskrit Education) Recruitment 2025

The Rajasthan Staff Selection Board (RSMSSB), Jaipur has released the detailed advertisement for the competitive direct recruitment of Upper Primary School Teachers (Level-Second, Classes 6 to 8) for the Sanskrit Education Department. Eligible candidates with required academic qualifications and valid REET certificates can apply online.

Organization: Rajasthan Staff Selection Board (RSMSSB) & Sanskrit Education Department, Govt. of Rajasthan

Post Name: Upper Primary School Teacher (Level-Second, Class 6 to 8) - Various Subjects

Advertisement No.: 09/2025 (F.14(162)RSSB/ARTHANA/SAN.SHI.VI./ADHYAPAK/BHARTI/2024)

Recruitment Snapshot

Key Highlights of Rajasthan Teacher Level-II Recruitment

  • Multiple Subjects Available: Direct recruitment is open for Level-II teachers specializing in Sanskrit, Hindi, English, Social Studies, and Mathematics-Science.
  • SSO Rajasthan System: Form submission and Single-Time Registration (OTR) fees must be transacted natively through the SSO portal.
  • Subject Multiplicity: If a candidate holds standard qualifications for more than one subject, they must submit separate application forms for each distinct subject code.
  • REET Mandate: Passing the Rajasthan Teacher Eligibility Test (REET) Level-2 in 2022 or 2025 with prescribed category-wise baseline marks is an absolute prerequisite.

Competitive Exam Scheme & OMR Structure

The direct recruitment competitive test consists of a single objective multiple-choice question paper evaluated as follows:

Parameter HeadingStructural Detail Framework
Maximum Allocated Marks Weightage300 Marks Consolidated
Total Question Count In Question Bank150 Multiple Choice Questions (MCQs)
Duration of the Competitive Examination2 Hours and 30 Minutes (150 Minutes total)
Negative Marking Operational RuleActive Penalty: 1/3rd (One-Third) of marks deducted for every wrong answer.
5-Option OMR Mandatory RuleEvery question features options A, B, C, D, and E. If a candidate leaves a question unattempted, they must darken option 'E'. Failure to fill any circle across more than 10% of total questions results in immediate disqualification. 10 extra minutes are given to finalize OMR checking.

Step-by-Step Online Application Process

  1. 1

    Go to the official SSO Rajasthan registration page link: sso.rajasthan.gov.in.

  2. 2

    Log in using your active credentials or register a new identity using your Jan Aadhaar or Google account.

  3. 3

    Navigate to the Citizen Apps (G2C) area and select the Recruitment Portal icon link.

  4. 4

    Complete your One-Time Registration (OTR) profile. Ensure your Name, Father's Name, Date of Birth, and Gender match your Aadhaar / Jan Aadhaar profile before paying the OTR registration fee.

  5. 5

    Click on the "Apply Now" button adjacent to the "Upper Primary School Teacher Direct Recruitment 2025" tab.

  6. 6

    Select your preferred subject code and carefully fill in your academic parameters and REET credentials.

  7. 7

    Capture your live photograph using your web-camera interface (ensure a light, plain background and blink twice during the automated timer countdown) and upload your clear signature image (black or deep blue ink, 20 KB to 50 KB file layout size).

  8. 8

    Thoroughly review your form via the preview feature before clicking Final Submit. Always save and print your generated unique application form number safely for all downstream state verification schedules.

Mandatory Operational Instructions

  • NOC Prerequisite: Candidates currently holding temporary, permanent, or contract positions with any state/central government agency must secure an official No Objection Certificate (NOC) and produce it during certificate verification.
  • Live Photo Compliance: Application profiles containing distorted, skewed, blurred, dark, or heavily modified digital photographs edited via third-party applications will face automated rejection.
  • Marital & Children Framework: Applicants must declare their marriage registration status and the number of living children. As per Rajasthan service provisions, candidates with more than two children born on or after 01-06-2002 face strict disqualification protocols.
  • Document Verification Policy: Shortlisted candidates must present authenticated character certificates from their last attended educational institution along with two independent validation statements from gazetted officials issued within the last six months.
