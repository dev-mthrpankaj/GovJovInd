About This Recruitment

Patna High Court Technical Assistant Recruitment 2026 is for Group C technical posts in the High Court establishment. The advertisement lists 53 vacancies with horizontal reservation for women and locomotor disabled candidates.

This post is suitable for candidates with BCA, ITI or Polytechnic Diploma in specified computer/electronics disciplines along with at least one year relevant experience. The High Court will assess whether the claimed qualification and experience are relevant.

Selection can include multiple stages: written test, trade/skill/work proficiency test and interview. If applications are high, a preliminary screening test may be conducted before the written test.
