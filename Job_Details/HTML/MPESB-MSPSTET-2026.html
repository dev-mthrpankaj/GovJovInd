About This Recruitment

The MPESB Primary and Secondary Teachers MSPSTET 2026[cite: 4] is an official examination conducted by the Madhya Pradesh Employees Selection Board[cite: 4]. The official notification indicates that only teachers working in primary and secondary schools will be eligible to apply for this examination[cite: 4]. The exact number of posts available under this recruitment has not been specified in the initial notification[cite: 4].

The online application process will commence on 21 August 2026 and conclude on 04 September 2026[cite: 4]. Candidates will also be provided with an application form correction window, which will remain active until 09 September 2026[cite: 4]. The examination is scheduled to take place on 12 October 2026 across various districts in Madhya Pradesh, including Bhopal, Indore, Jabalpur, Khandwa, Neemach, Ratlam, Rewa, Sagar, Satna, Sidhi, and Ujjain[cite: 4].
