For each trade, candidates should have passed SSC / 10th Standard or equivalent with Science subjects and minimum 55% marks, along with one of the following trade-related requirements.

OptionQualification Requirement

Selection Process

  1. 1

    Applications will be screened by a duly constituted Screening Committee.

  2. 2

    Shortlisted candidates will be invited for a Trade Test.

  3. 3

    Candidates who qualify the Trade Test will be invited for Competitive Written Examination.

  4. 4

    Paper-II and Paper-III will be evaluated only for candidates who secure minimum threshold marks in Paper-I.

  5. 5

    Final merit list will be prepared only on the basis of marks obtained in Paper-II and Paper-III.

Merit & Tie Breaking

  • Final merit will be prepared on marks obtained in Paper-II and Paper-III only.
  • In case of tie, candidate with higher marks in concerned subject / trade will be placed higher.
  • Further tie may be resolved by lesser negative marks, older date of birth, earlier acquisition of essential qualification and alphabetical order of first name as per notification rules.
  • Date, time and venue of Trade Test and Written Examination will be intimated through CSIR-CIMFR website.

  2. 2

  3. 3

    Fill primary registration details carefully, then select the post code for which you are eligible.

  4. 4

    Fill educational and employment details, upload relevant certificates and verify the application before final submission.

  5. 5

    Pay the application fee through SBI Collect wherever applicable and upload e-receipt / proof of payment.

  6. 6

    Submit the online application and retain a printout / payment record for future reference. Hard copy is not required to be sent to CSIR-CIMFR.

Important Instructions

  • Only online applications will be accepted. No other mode of application is accepted.
  • Candidate can apply for multiple post codes subject to eligibility, but separate application and fee are required for each post code.
  • Only one application will be considered for each post code. If multiple applications are submitted for a single post code, the latest completed application will be considered.
  • Incomplete applications or applications without required documents / fee wherever applicable will be rejected.
  • All future announcements, updates, corrigendum or addendum will be published only on the official CSIR-CIMFR website.
  • In case of discrepancy between English and Hindi versions of the advertisement, the English version will prevail.
