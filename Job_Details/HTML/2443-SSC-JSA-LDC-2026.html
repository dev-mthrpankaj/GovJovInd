About This Recruitment

The SSC Senior Secretariat Assistant / Upper Division Clerk Grade Limited Departmental Competitive Examination 2025 is a career-progression opportunity for eligible serving employees in specified central government services and cadres. Unlike open competitive recruitments, this examination is meant for departmental candidates such as Junior Secretariat Assistant, Lower Division Clerk, Junior Passport Assistant, Hindi Typist or equivalent feeder-grade employees whose departments participate in the notified select-list process. The examination helps fill SSA/UDC Grade vacancies in Pay Level 4 across offices such as the Central Passport Organisation, Armed Forces Headquarters Clerical Service, Railway Board Secretariat Clerical Service, Central Administrative Tribunal, Central Vigilance Commission, Election Commission of India, Forest Survey of India, NTRO, Directorate General Defence Estates and the Office of Development Commissioner MSME.

For candidates already working in these departments, this LDCE is significant because it offers a structured route to move into higher clerical and secretariat responsibilities. The role generally involves office procedure, noting, drafting, file work, records handling and coordination duties, so the exam pattern focuses on general awareness, English language comprehension, noting, drafting and office procedure. The official notice also makes service record evaluation part of the final selection process, which means candidates should treat both examination preparation and departmental record accuracy as important.

Applicants should read the official notification carefully before submitting the online form because eligibility is not identical for every cadre. Crucial dates for counting approved service differ by service, and some cadres specify age limits, typewriting-test conditions or educational requirements. After online submission, candidates must also complete the departmental forwarding process through their Service or Cadre Controlling Authority. A timely, accurate application with correct service documents is therefore as important as exam preparation.

Selection Process

  1. 1

    Online application and departmental forwarding: SSC accepts applications provisionally after online submission and proper forwarding by the competent department.

  2. 2

    Paper I Computer Based Examination: Objective multiple-choice test for General Awareness and English Language & Comprehension.

  3. 3

    Paper II Written Examination: Descriptive paper on Noting, Drafting and Office Procedure.

  4. 4

    Service-record evaluation: Candidates shortlisted on Paper I and Paper II performance are considered for Part II APAR / record of service evaluation.

  5. 5

    Final merit: Final selection is based on aggregate performance in Paper I, Paper II and Part II service-record evaluation, subject to vacancies and reservation rules.

Syllabus Overview

  • General Awareness: Current events, everyday observation and scientific aspects, India and neighbouring countries, history, culture, geography, economics, science, general politics and scientific research.
  • English Language and Comprehension: Vocabulary, spelling, grammar, sentence structure, synonyms, antonyms, sentence completion, phrases, idioms and passage comprehension at matriculation level.
  • Noting, Drafting and Office Procedure: Secretariat and attached-office procedure, notes and drafts, Manual of Office Procedure, railway office procedure where relevant, parliamentary procedure references, official Hindi orders and reservation policy orders.
  • Service Record: APAR / record evaluation after qualifying stages, as per SSC and cadre rules.
