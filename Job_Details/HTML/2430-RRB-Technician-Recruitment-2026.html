Selection Methodology

  1. 1

    Computer Based Test (CBT): Single or multiple phase objective-type structured online examinations carrying 100 questions with negative marking of 1/3rd for wrong attempts.

  2. 2

    Document Verification (DV): Shortlisted merit candidates will produce original qualification, matriculation certificates, and community proofs.

  3. 3

    Medical Examination: Strict check-ups matching designated standards (A-3, B-1, B-2, or C-1 based on post categories).

Step-by-Step Online Application Process

  1. 1

    Access the central server at rrbapply.gov.in.

  2. 2

    Click on "Create an Account" and key in your Primary details (Name, Mother's name, DOB exactly matching 10th certificate). Complete mobile & email OTP validation.

  3. 3

    Log in with credentials and choose your single desired **RRB Regional Board** for application.

  4. 4

    Perform live webcam photo verification (ensure clean background, no hats/glasses) and upload digital signatures in black ink (30-49 KB).

  5. 5

    Provide educational layouts, lock trade preferences, complete fee payment, and securely save the system-generated acknowledgement page.
