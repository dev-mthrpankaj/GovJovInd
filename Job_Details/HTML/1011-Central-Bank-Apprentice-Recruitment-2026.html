About This Recruitment

Central Bank of India is a public sector bank with a wide branch network across India. The apprentice engagement is designed to provide banking exposure and on-the-job training under the Apprentices Act, 1961.

This notification is useful for graduates who are registered on the NATS portal and want 12 months of structured banking training. The selection includes an online test and a local language test, so candidates should check their state, language and district preferences carefully.

Apprenticeship is a training engagement and does not give a right to regular employment in the bank. Candidates should read all eligibility, fee and contract conditions before applying.

Eligibility and Qualification

  • Candidate must be an Indian citizen or otherwise eligible as per the nationality rules in the notification.
  • Candidate must be registered on the NATS portal to apply.
  • Graduation in any discipline from a recognized university or equivalent qualification recognized by the Central Government is required.
  • Graduation must have been completed on or after 01 January 2022.
  • Candidate should be proficient in the specified local / official language of the state applied for.
  • Candidates who have already undergone apprenticeship or have one year or more training/job experience after qualification are not eligible as per the notification conditions.

Selection Process

  1. Online objective examination conducted through BFSI SSC.
  2. Test of Local Language for the state applied for, unless exempted by 10th or 12th marksheet language condition.
  3. Merit list as per vacancies and bank cut-off.
  4. Document verification of date of birth, category, educational qualification and other application details.
  5. Medical fitness as per bank requirement.
  6. Digital apprenticeship contract through the government apprenticeship portal before engagement.

Local Language Test / Skill Requirement

Candidates applying for a state must be proficient in reading, writing, speaking and understanding at least one specified local or official language of that state. The local language test is conducted after qualifying the online examination. Candidates who produce 10th or 12th marksheet showing study of the specified local language may not be required to undergo the language test.

Salary and Benefits

  • Apprentices are eligible for a stipend of Rs. 15,000 per month.
  • The duration of engagement is 12 months.
  • No other allowances or benefits are admissible as per the notification.
  • Apprentices are entitled to holidays observed by the bank.
  • One casual leave every month is allowed during apprenticeship.
  • No overtime is allowed.

Documents Required

  • Recent photograph and signature.
  • Graduation marksheets and provisional / degree certificate.
  • Date of birth proof.
  • Valid photo identity proof.
  • NATS registration details.
  • Category certificate for SC / ST / OBC / EWS, if applicable.
  • PwBD certificate, if applicable.
  • Local language proof such as 10th or 12th marksheet, where applicable.
  • Fee payment confirmation.
  • Other documents required by the bank or BFSI SSC during verification.

How to Apply

  1. Register or ensure valid registration on the NATS portal.
  2. Open the Central Bank of India recruitment page and read the apprentice notification carefully.
  3. Go to the BFSI SSC application portal mentioned in the notification.
  4. Fill personal, educational, state, district, category and language details carefully.
  5. Upload documents and pay the applicable online fee.
  6. Submit the form before 22 June 2026.
  7. Save the application form, payment receipt and login details for future use.

Why Apply For This Recruitment

This apprentice engagement can help fresh graduates gain practical exposure to banking operations, customer service, products and branch processes. The stipend and one-year training period make it useful for candidates preparing for banking careers, but applicants should remember that apprenticeship does not guarantee regular employment.
