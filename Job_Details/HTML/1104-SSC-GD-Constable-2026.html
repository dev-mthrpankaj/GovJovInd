Eligibility & Age Limit

Nationality

Candidate must be a citizen of India. Domicile / PRC requirements apply according to the notified State/UT vacancies.

Educational Qualification

Matriculation / 10th Class passed from a recognized Board/University on or before 01-01-2026.

Age Limit

18-23 years as on 01-01-2026. Candidate should be born not before 02-01-2003 and not later than 01-01-2008.

NCC Bonus

NCC A/B/C certificate holders may receive bonus marks as per the official rules where applicable.

Important Selection Note

Final result and force allocation depend on CBE performance, force preference and qualification in PET/PST/Medical/DV stages as per the official notice.

Documents Required at DME / DV

Essential Certificates

Matriculation certificate for age/name/education proof and domicile/PRC certificate as applicable.

Category Documents

SC/ST/OBC/EWS certificate, ESM documents and other prescribed certificates where applicable.

Bonus / Relaxation Documents

Valid NCC certificate and height/chest relaxation certificate in prescribed format where applicable.

Identity & Medical

Original identity documents and medical fitness will be verified during later recruitment stages.

Syllabus Highlights

Reasoning

Analogies, similarities/differences, spatial visualization, problem solving, coding-decoding, non-verbal reasoning and related topics.

GK & Awareness

Current events, India and neighboring countries, sports, history, culture, geography, economy, polity and general science.

Mathematics

Number systems, decimals/fractions, ratio, percentage, profit/loss, interest, averages, time-work, time-distance and basic arithmetic.

English / Hindi

Basic comprehension and language ability in English or Hindi.
