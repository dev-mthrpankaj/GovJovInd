About This Recruitment

Airports Authority of India (AAI), a Public Sector Enterprise under the Ministry of Civil Aviation, is inviting online applications from eligible Indian citizens for Executive and Managerial cadres. This recruitment drive aims to fill 389 vacancies across diverse disciplines including Civil, Electrical, Finance, Information Technology, Architecture, Law, Fire Services, Operations, Commercial, Corporate Planning, Corporate Communications, and Survey & Cartography.

Candidates selected for Manager posts will enter at the Group-A (E-3 level) pay scale, whereas Junior Executives will join at the Group-B (E-1 level) pay scale. The registration process is strictly online through the official AAI web portal. Applicants must ensure they fulfill all educational qualifications, age parameters, and post-qualification experience requirements as on the closing date of application.

The recruitment process will predominantly involve a Computer Based Test (CBT) followed by Document Verification, Interview, Physical Measurement/Endurance Tests, or Driving Tests depending on the specific post requirements. Candidates are encouraged to carefully read the complete detailed notification before filling out their application forms.

  • Degree/Diploma must be from a recognized University / Institution recognized by UGC/AICTE/Government of India.
  • For Managerial positions, 3 years post-qualification relevant work experience is mandatory.
  • For Law posts, candidate must be eligible for enrollment as an Advocate with the Bar Council of India.

Selection Process

  1. Computer Based Test (CBT) objective examination.
  2. Application Verification / Document Verification.
  3. Interview (for Manager posts as applicable).
  4. Physical Measurement & Endurance Test / Driving Test (specifically for Fire Services & Operations posts as applicable).
  5. Medical Fitness Examination.
  6. Final Selection List based on merit and performance in applicable selection stages.

Exam Pattern

The detailed syllabus and post-wise CBT exam pattern will be released on the official AAI portal. Generally, the exam consists of objective multiple-choice questions covering technical domain knowledge and general aptitude (Reasoning, Numerical Ability, General Awareness, and English Language).

How to Apply

  1. Visit the official portal of Airports Authority of India at www.aai.aero.
  2. Navigate to the "Careers" section and click on "Recruitment for Manager and Junior Executive (Advt. No. 12/2026/CHQ/DR-CBT)".
  3. Click on "Online Application Link" and register using a valid Email ID and Mobile Number.
  4. Log in using the system-generated Registration ID and Password.
  5. Fill in all personal details, educational qualifications, category details, and experience carefully.
  6. Upload required scanned documents, photo, and signature in prescribed formats.
  7. Pay the online application fee (if applicable) through UPI, Net Banking, or Debit/Credit Cards.
  8. Verify all details before final submission, submit the form, and download/print a copy for future reference.
