About This Recruitment

Bharat Earth Movers Limited (BEML) has published the official notification for the recruitment of Apprentices under Advertisement No. L&D/01/2026. This recruitment drive aims to fill 1346 vacancies across different BEML complexes in India, including Bangalore, Mysore, KGF, and Palakkad.

Interested and eligible candidates holding an ITI, Diploma, BE, or B.Tech degree in related trades and branches can submit their online applications through the official portal. The online registration process starts on 18 August 2026 and closes on 08 September 2026. Applicants should ensure they meet the age limit criteria as of the last date of application.

There is no application fee required for any category. Candidates are advised to download the official notification PDF and carefully review the eligibility criteria, complex-wise vacancy distribution, and selection process prior to submitting their online application.

How to Apply

  1. Visit the official website of BEML Apprentices at bemlindia.in/careers/.
  2. Click on the "BEML Apprentices 2026 Apply Online" link.
  3. Register using a valid email ID and mobile number.
  4. Login using your newly created registration ID and password.
  5. Fill the application form carefully with your personal, educational, and category details.
  6. Upload scanned documents including your recent photograph and signature in the required format.
  7. Submit the form (No application fee is required).
  8. Download and print the confirmation page for future reference.

Documents Required

  • Recent passport-size colored photograph (scanned).
  • Scanned copy of signature.
  • Educational certificates (ITI/Diploma/BE/B.Tech).
  • Caste/Category certificate if applicable.
  • Valid photo ID proof (Aadhaar Card, PAN Card, Voter ID).
