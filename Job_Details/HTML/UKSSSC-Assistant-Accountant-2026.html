About This Recruitment

The Uttarakhand Subordinate Service Selection Commission (UKSSSC) has released the recruitment advertisement for Group 'C' posts under the Accounts Department. This drive aims to fill 379 vacancies for Assistant Accountant (Sahayak Lekhakar), Accountant, Office Assistant III (Accounts), and Cashier cum Assistant Accountant across various state departments in Uttarakhand.

Candidates possessing a Bachelor's Degree in Commerce (B.Com) or an equivalent relevant qualification from any recognized university are eligible to apply. The recruitment process provides a structured career path in government service, offering competitive salary scales, job security, and official allowances as per Uttarakhand state regulations.

Eligible candidates can fill out the online application form on the official UKSSSC portal starting from 26 July 2026 until 15 August 2026. It is highly recommended to carefully read the official notification regarding category-wise distribution, typing speed parameters (where required), and detailed syllabus components before submitting the form.

Eligibility and Qualification

  • Bachelor's Degree in Commerce (B.Com) or an equivalent post-wise commerce qualification from a recognized university.
  • Knowledge of Hindi written in Devanagari script.
  • Computer typing skills (Hindi/English) as required for specific account clerk/office assistant posts under relevant departmental service rules.

Selection Process

  1. Written Competitive Examination (Objective Type MCQs covering Commerce, Accountancy, and relevant subjects).
  2. Computer Typing Test / Skill Test (applicable for specific Assistant Accountant / Office Assistant posts).
  3. Document Verification of original academic degrees, caste certificates, domicile, and identity cards.
  4. Final Merit List preparation based on written exam marks and reservation guidelines.

How to Apply

  1. Visit the official portal of UKSSSC Recruitment at ukssscrecruitment.in or sssc.uk.gov.in.
  2. Click on the link "UKSSSC Assistant Accountant 2026 Apply Online".
  3. Complete the One Time Registration (OTR) or new registration using a valid Email ID and Mobile Number.
  4. Log in using your generated credentials (Registration ID and Password).
  5. Fill out the detailed application form with personal, educational, category, and contact information.
  6. Upload scanned copies of required documents, including photograph, signature, and educational marksheets.
  7. Pay the required application fee using Net Banking, Debit Card, Credit Card, or UPI.
  8. Review all entered details carefully, submit the online form, and download/print the final confirmation page for future reference.
