About This Recruitment

Bhakra Beas Management Board (BBMB) has released a notification for the recruitment of 09 Hindi Translator posts. Candidates seeking to join BBMB in Chandigarh can apply for this opportunity. The online application window is open from 31 August 2026 to 21 September 2026.

This role demands a combination of specific linguistic educational qualifications (M.A. / B.A. / Law Graduate combining Hindi and English) along with a mandatory 3-year experience in translation. In addition to the online application, applicants must submit a hard copy of their application form to the BBMB office in Chandigarh.

Candidates must carefully review the category-wise vacancy distribution, requisite experience, and offline submission guidelines mentioned on this page before submitting the form.
