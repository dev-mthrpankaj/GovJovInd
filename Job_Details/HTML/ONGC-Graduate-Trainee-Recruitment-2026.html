About This Recruitment

The ONGC Graduate Trainee Recruitment 2026 is launched under Advt No. 2/2026 (R&P) to induct qualified dynamic technical professionals into core executive domains. Operating as a premier public sector enterprise, ONGC offers entry-level executive roles that involve overseeing technical setups, managing drilling or structural designs, geological evaluations, and streamlining operational projects across varied geographical setups in India. The recruitment provides a stable professional platform for technical graduates and post-graduates who possess deep domain knowledge.

The entire submission mechanism is hosted digitally. Candidates are instructed to register using functioning mobile numbers and emails, authenticate their application profiles, upload credentials within the specified storage thresholds, pay the application fees where applicable, and lock their submission. Due to the high compliance parameters of public sector recruitments, details concerning stream eligibility, physical fitness levels, matching marks percentages, and original document verification must be read thoroughly beforehand.

The assessment structure moves away from standard gate rankings by conducting an objective evaluation model. The selection matrix comprises an extensive Computer Based Test assessing technical domains and auxiliary aptitude areas, followed by standard personal interviews and stringent medical validation. A comprehensive preparation strategy tracking general knowledge, verbal familiarity, basic analytical capabilities, and thorough branch subjects forms the basis for securing merit scores.

Eligibility and Qualification

  • Petroleum Engineering: Graduate Degree in Petroleum Engineering or Applied Petroleum Engineering with minimum 60% marks from a recognized institution.
  • Mechanical Engineering: Graduate Degree in Mechanical Engineering discipline scoring at least 60% aggregate marks.
  • Chemical Engineering: Graduate Degree in Chemical Engineering path containing a minimum qualification score of 60% marks.
  • Instrumentation Engineering: Graduate degree qualification in Instrumentation Engineering mapping up to 60% standard marks.
  • Geology Stream: Post Graduate degree / M.Sc / M.Tech in Geology or Geological Technology with an aggregate of 60% marks minimum.

Selection Process

  1. Computer Based Test (CBT): Initial filtering stage mapping multi-subject metrics.
  2. Personal Interview: Assessment evaluating interactive abilities and corporate domain familiarity.
  3. Document Verification: Detailed physical cross-checking of educational, birth, and community certificates.
  4. Medical Examination: Final verification phase assessing compliance with corporate fitness rules.

Frequently Asked Questions

What is ONGC Graduate Trainee Recruitment 2026?

It is a recruitment process conducted by Oil and Natural Gas Corporation (ONGC) via Advt No. 2/2026 (R&P) to select Graduate Trainees for Geologist and Engineer positions.

What is the last date to apply for ONGC Graduate Trainee 2026?

The final deadline for online registration and submission of the application form is 31 July 2026.

How many total vacancies are available under this notification?

There are a total of 52 tentative vacancies distributed across various engineering streams and geology disciplines.

What is the essential educational qualification?

Candidates require a Graduate Degree in relevant Engineering streams (Petroleum, Mechanical, Chemical, Instrumentation) or a Post Graduate/M.Sc/M.Tech in Geology/Geological Technology with minimum 60% marks.

What is the prescribed age limit for applicants?

The age limit is 21 to 26 years for Engineering streams and 21 to 27 years for the Geology stream, with standard relaxations applicable for reserved categories.

What is the application fee amount?

The registration fee is Rs. 1000 for General, OBC, and EWS candidates. Candidates belonging to SC, ST, and PwD categories are completely exempted from payment.

What is the expected salary package?

Selected candidates are placed in the pay scale of Rs. 60,000 to Rs. 1,80,000 per month, supplemented by standard allowances.

What are the stages of the selection process?

The selection methodology includes a Computer Based Test (CBT), Personal Interview, Document Verification, and a Medical Fitness Examination.

When is the CBT exam scheduled?

The Computer Based Test (CBT) is tentatively scheduled to be conducted on 06 September 2026.

Where can candidates fill the online application?

Eligible candidates can fill out the online registration portal hosted at https://ibosreg.ibps.in/ongcju126/ or navigate via the official website ongcindia.com.
