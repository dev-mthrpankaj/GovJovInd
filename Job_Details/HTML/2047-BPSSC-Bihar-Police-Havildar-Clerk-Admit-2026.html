Recruitment Selection Process Flow

The evaluation cycle for selection under the Bihar Home Guard Corps Cadre passes sequentially through the below parameters:

1

Written Competitive Test: 100 Objective multiple-choice questions for filtering screening lists.

2

Physical Standard Test (PST): Category-wise height, chest, and weight verification rules.

3

Physical Efficiency Test (PET): Non-negotiable mandatory sports endurance metrics (Running, High Jump, Gola Fek).

4

Final Merit Document Compilation: Evaluated scores structure alignment maps.

Written Exam Scheme & Syllabus Matrix

  • Academic Baseline Matrix: The difficulty standard corresponds to the 10th Class Matriculation syllabus pattern of the Bihar School Examination Board (BSEB).
  • Structural Format: Completely Objective-type Multiple Choice Questions (MCQs).
  • Question Count: 100 Questions.
  • Maximum Evaluation Range: 100 Marks (1 Mark mapped for every correct option entry).
  • Duration Framework Allocation: 2 Hours (120 Minutes stretch).
  • Compulsory Screening Bar: Minimum 30% Marks required to qualify for physical evaluations stages.
  • Topic Core Coverage: Hindi, English, Mathematics, Social Science (History, Civics, Geography, Economics), Science (Physics, Chemistry, Botany, Zoology), General Knowledge, and Current Affairs.

  • Running Metrics: Male candidates must finish a 1.6 Kilometers run within 6 Minutes. Female candidates must clear a 1 Kilometer run within 5 Minutes.
  • High Jump Bounds: Minimum 4 Feet clearing target for Male participants. Minimum 3 Feet clearing benchmark for Female participants.
  • Shot Put (Gola Fek): Male targets involve throwing a 16 Pound sphere across a minimum distance of 16 Feet. Female targets require throwing a 12 Pound sphere across a minimum distance of 10 Feet.
