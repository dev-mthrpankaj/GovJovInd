Method of Selection

The structured multi-layered selection matrix configured by the BPSSC requires clearing successive assessment stages:

01 Offline Written OMR Exam
02 Physical Standard Test (PST)

Note: The preliminary written test has a mandatory minimum cut-off benchmark of 30% marks to satisfy structural eligibility conditions for physical testing call letters.

Written Examination Scheme

The offline OMR testing structure covers multiple academic domains modeled on matriculation difficulty expectations:

  • Total Marks: 100 Marks
  • Total Questions: 100 Multiple Choice Questions (MCQs)
  • Duration: 2 Hours (120 Minutes continuous timing)
  • Negative Evaluation: No provision for negative marks on wrong inputs.
  • Core Assessment Domains: General Knowledge / General Awareness (40 Qs), Mathematics (30 Qs), General Science (15 Qs), and Social Science (15 Qs). Topics occasionally capture elements of fundamental English and Hindi grammar concepts.

2. Physical Efficiency Test (PET) Performance Benchmarks

  • Running Event (Male): 1.6 Kilometers to be completed within 6 Minutes maximum.
  • Running Event (Female): 1 Kilometers to be completed within 5 Minutes maximum.
  • High Jump Challenge: Minimum 4 Feet for Male candidates; Minimum 3 Feet for Female candidates.
  • Shot Put / Gola Fek Event: Male candidates must throw a 16-pound ball at least 16 Feet. Female candidates must throw a 12-pound ball at least 12 Feet.

How to Download BPSSC Havildar Clerk Call Letter

  1. Access the primary communication interface of the commission at bpssc.bihar.gov.in.
  2. Click on the prominent notification string reading "Download Admit Card for Havildar Clerk in Bihar Home Guard Corps Cadre (Advt. No. 01/2026)".
  3. Enter your secure credentials which consist of your unique Registration ID or registered Mobile Number along with your corresponding Date of Birth.
  4. Correctly input the visible alphanumeric Captcha Security verification string displayed on your login box.
  5. Press the submit option to initialize display parameters.
  6. Review all printed details including exam session codes and your personalized passport layout photograph.
  7. Save a permanent digital copy file onto local storage and secure 2 to 3 legible hard-copy prints to bring directly inside the validation enclosure gates.

Mandatory Hall Guidelines & Instructions

  • Essential Documentation: Candidates must bring a clean, physical copy of the BPSSC Admit Card 2026 alongside a government-issued photo identity certificate (Aadhaar Card, PAN Card, Voter ID, or Driving License).
  • Reporting Window Constraints: The competitive exam shift starts exactly at 10:00 AM on 10 June 2026. The reporting deadline inside centers closes permanently 30 minutes prior to paper distribution times; late validation attempts will be rejected.
  • Prohibited Possessions: Standard digital elements such as wrist smartwatches, communication phones, calculations sheets, notebooks, and dynamic storage accessories are entirely prohibited from storage bays within checking hallways.
  • Writing Materials: Bring transparent blue or black ballpoint pens for accurate OMR bubble marking routines.
