About This Recruitment

Naini Aerospace Limited has invited online applications for various non-executive posts on a Fixed Term Employment basis. NAeL is a wholly owned subsidiary of Hindustan Aeronautics Limited and works in areas such as aircraft/helicopter looms, wire harnesses, LCA stub wings, helicopter structures and drone-related activities. The posts notified in this advertisement are for the factory at UPSIDA Industrial Area, Naini, Prayagraj, Uttar Pradesh.

The engagement is not a permanent appointment. As per the notification, selected candidates will be engaged for a tenure of four years from the date of joining and the tenure will end automatically after completion of four years. Candidates should therefore read the tenure, posting, remuneration, medical and termination-related conditions carefully before applying.

This recruitment is useful for candidates who have the exact ITI trade qualification for Operator SS posts or the required graduation/computer proficiency background for Junior Assistant posts. The company has clearly mentioned that part-time, correspondence, distance education and e-learning qualifications are not eligible, and candidates possessing higher qualifications than the required qualification need not apply for these posts.

Eligibility and Qualification

  • Candidates must possess the exact qualification mentioned against the concerned post/trade/stream.
  • Operator SS posts require 2 year regular/full-time ITI (NTC) after 10th standard in the relevant trade.
  • Junior Assistant posts require the relevant full-time graduation/BCA qualification and, wherever applicable, a minimum 6 month proficiency certificate in computer, typing, stenography or related skill.
  • One year experience in the relevant functional area will be an added advantage as per the notification.
  • Required qualifications should be completed from a recognized university, technical board or vocational training centre on or before 31 May 2026.
  • Part-time, correspondence, distance education, private and e-learning qualifications are not eligible.
  • Candidates whose result is awaited or withheld will not be considered.
  • Candidates possessing higher qualifications than the required qualification indicated in the notification need not apply.

Selection Process

  1. Online applications will be screened according to the notified eligibility criteria.
  2. Candidates meeting the required qualification percentage may be shortlisted for written test.
  3. Written Test will be conducted at Prayagraj; date, time and venue will be informed through email / NAeL website / admit card.
  4. Candidates will have to download the admit card from NAeL website and carry a valid original photo ID.
  5. Selection will be based on marks scored in the Written Test only, in order of merit.
  6. Candidates qualifying in the written test will be called for document verification in the notified discipline/category order.
  7. Provisionally selected candidates must undergo pre-employment medical examination before joining.
