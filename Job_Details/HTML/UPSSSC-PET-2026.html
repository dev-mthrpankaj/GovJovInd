About This Recruitment

The Uttar Pradesh Subordinate Services Selection Commission (UPSSSC), Lucknow conducts the Preliminary Eligibility Test (PET) as a mandatory preliminary eligibility screening test for candidate recruitment to various Group 'C' posts in government departments across Uttar Pradesh. Clearing the UPSSSC PET is an essential requirement for appearing in main written examinations such as UP Lekhpal, Junior Assistant, Village Development Officer (VDO), Assistant Accountant, Enforcement Constable, and various other technical and administrative vacancies.

Candidates holding a minimum of a High School (Class 10th) qualification from a recognized board can apply online for PET 2026. The examination evaluates applicants on general knowledge, elementary mathematics, reasoning, general Hindi, English, current affairs, and graphical interpretation skills. Obtaining a valid percentile score in PET allows job seekers to directly qualify for secondary main examinations and skill tests announced by the Commission throughout the valid scorecard tenure.

The online registration and application process is conducted through One Time Registration (OTR) on the official UPSSSC portal. Candidates are strongly advised to complete their OTR details accurately before submitting the PET application form. Ensure that all personal details, photograph, signature, and category details match official government identity cards to prevent discrepancies during future document verification stages.

Vacancy Details

The Preliminary Eligibility Test (PET) is a screening entrance test and does not represent a single post vacancy count. Candidates who clear PET 2026 will be eligible to apply for upcoming Group 'C' recruitments in Uttar Pradesh.

Eligibility and Qualification

  • Must have passed High School (Class 10th) from a recognized Board in India.
  • Candidates with Higher Educational Qualifications (Intermediate 10+2, Graduation, Post-Graduation, or Diploma) are also eligible to apply.
  • Candidates must meet age criteria between 18 to 40 years as on 1st July 2026.

Selection Process

  1. Appearance in UPSSSC Preliminary Eligibility Test (PET 2026) Written Examination.
  2. Generation and issuance of official UPSSSC PET 2026 Scorecard (Valid for 3 Years).
  3. Shortlisting for specific Group 'C' Main Examinations based on PET Percentile Cut-off marks.
  4. Departmental Skill Test / Typing Test / Physical Efficiency Test (wherever applicable for specific posts).
  5. Document Verification & Final Medical Examination.

Exam Pattern

The UPSSSC PET 2026 examination consists of 100 objective-type questions carrying 100 marks. Total time duration is 2 hours (120 minutes). There is a negative marking of 0.25 marks (1/4th) for every wrong response.

How to Apply

  1. Visit the official UPSSSC online portal at upsssc.gov.in.
  2. Complete One Time Registration (OTR) if not registered previously on the portal.
  3. Click on the link for "Advt No. 16/2026 Preliminary Eligibility Test (PET-2026)".
  4. Fill in all required educational, personal, and communication details accurately.
  5. Upload your scanned photo and signature according to official dimensions.
  6. Proceed to pay the prescribed examination fee using Net Banking, Debit Card, Credit Card, UPI, or e-Challan.
  7. Review the filled application form carefully using the preview feature and submit the final form.
  8. Print and download a copy of the final application form and payment transaction receipt for future reference.
