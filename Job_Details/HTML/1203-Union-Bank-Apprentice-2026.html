About This Recruitment

Union Bank Apprentice 2026 is an apprenticeship engagement, not permanent bank employment. Selected candidates receive on-job training in banking practices, products and processes for one year as per the Apprentices Act.

The notice is useful for recent graduates who want practical exposure in a public sector bank environment. Candidates must select a state and should be proficient in one of the local languages of that state.

The final engagement depends on online test performance, document verification, local language proof/test and medical fitness certificate. Candidates should understand that apprenticeship does not create a right to regular employment in the bank.

Eligibility and Qualification

  • Graduate degree in any discipline from a recognized/approved institute, college or university.
  • Qualification result must have been declared on or before 01.04.2026.
  • Graduation marksheet date should not be prior to 01.04.2022 as per notice.
  • Candidate should be proficient in one local language of the selected state.
  • Candidates who already completed apprenticeship or have one year or more job experience after qualification are not eligible as per notice.

Selection Process

  1. Online objective test of 100 marks.
  2. Local language proficiency proof or test.
  3. Document verification.
  4. Medical fitness certificate from a practicing registered General Physician.
  5. Digital apprenticeship offer and acceptance through the relevant portal.

Exam Pattern

The online test has 100 questions for 100 marks and total duration of 60 minutes. The notice states no negative marking.

Salary and Benefits

  • Rural / Semi-Urban branches: Rs. 15000/- per month stipend.
  • Urban branches: Rs. 18000/- per month stipend.
  • Metro branches: Rs. 20000/- per month stipend.
  • No other allowances or benefits are payable to apprentices as per notice.
