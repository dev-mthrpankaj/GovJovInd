    Selection Process

    1. 1

      Computer Based Examination (CBE): Objective multiple choice test of 100 questions carrying 100 marks with no negative markings.

    2. 2

      Physical Endurance and Measurement Test (PE & MT): Mandatory qualifying stage checking physical fitness benchmarks, race timings, and structural measurements.

    3. 3

      Trade Test: Assessment of dictation proficiency, technical terms handling, and core typing speeds (1000 key depressions/15 minutes).

    4. 4

      Proficiency in Computer Functions: Evaluation of MS Word, Excel, formatting setups, and PowerPoint configurations.

    5. 5

      Medical Examination & Document Scrutiny: Full verification of medical fitment certificates, characters, and structural eligibility documents.

    Physical Fitness Standards (PE & MT)

    For Male Candidates (Up to 30 Years)

    • Race (1600 Meters): Must complete within a maximum duration of 07 minutes.
    • Long Jump: Must cover a minimum length threshold of 12.5 Feet.
    • High Jump: Must cross a vertical elevation of 3.5 Feet.
    • Height Standards: Minimum 170 cms (Relaxable by 5 cms for hilly areas and departmental exceptions).
    • Chest Standards: 81 cms to 85 cms with a mandatory minimum expansion of 4 cms.

    For Female Candidates (Up to 30 Years)

    • Race (800 Meters): Must complete within a maximum duration of 05 minutes.
    • Long Jump: Must cover a minimum length threshold of 09 Feet.
    • High Jump: Must cross a vertical elevation of 3.0 Feet.
    • Height Standards: Minimum 157 cms (Relaxable by 5 cms for hilly region residents and daughters of police personnel).
