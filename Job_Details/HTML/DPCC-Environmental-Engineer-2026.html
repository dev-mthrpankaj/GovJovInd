About This Recruitment

The Delhi Pollution Control Committee (DPCC) has released a recruitment notification inviting applications for the positions of Senior Environmental Engineer, Environmental Engineer, and Assistant Environmental Engineer. A total of 17 vacancies are available under this recruitment drive (Advt No. DPCC/(3)(2)(86)/Admn-26).

Eligible candidates holding a Bachelor's or Master's degree in an engineering related field with relevant experience can apply offline. The application process begins on 22 August 2026, and the last date for submitting the application form is 12 September 2026. There is no application fee for any category.

Interested candidates should carefully review the official notification for complete details on the selection process, post-wise age limits, and required experience before submitting their forms.

How to Apply for DPCC Environmental Engineer Vacancy 2026

  1. Visit the official website of DPCC at dpcc.delhi.gov.in.
  2. Click on the recruitment link and download the official DPCC Environmental Engineer Application Form.
  3. Fill the application form carefully with your personal, educational, and category details.
  4. Attach all necessary scanned documents, including your photograph, signature, and experience certificates.
  5. Since this process may require offline submission, verify the exact address and submission method provided in the notification.
  6. Submit the form before the last date (12/09/2026) and keep a print/copy of the confirmation for future reference.

Please confirm the exact submission instructions and direct link from the official notice board before sending the application.
