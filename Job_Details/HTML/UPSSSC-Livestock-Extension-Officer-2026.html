About This Recruitment

UPSSSC Livestock Extension Officer Recruitment 2026 is an official recruitment process organized by the Uttar Pradesh Subordinate Service Selection Commission under Advertisement No. 17-Exam/2026. A total of 1251 vacancies have been advertised for the post of Livestock Extension Officer across Uttar Pradesh.

Candidates can submit their online application form starting from 09 September 2026 through the official portal. Applicants must hold a valid UPSSSC PET 2025 scorecard, possess an intermediate (10+2) qualification along with a 2-Year Livestock Extension Diploma from a recognized Veterinary University, and have knowledge of Hindi in Devanagari script.

The selection process includes screening through PET scores, written examination, document verification, and medical examination. Candidates are advised to read the detailed official notification before filling out the online application.

How to Fill UPSSSC Livestock Extension Officer Online Form 2026

  1. Visit the official UPSSSC portal at upsssc.gov.in.
  2. Locate and click on the "Livestock Extension Officer Recruitment 2026 (Advt No. 17-Exam/2026)" link.
  3. Log in using your UPSSSC PET 2025 Registration Number / OTR credentials.
  4. Fill in your personal, academic, and category details carefully.
  5. Upload required scanned documents, photo, and signature as per specified formats.
  6. Pay the requisite application fee (Rs. 25/- for all categories) via online payment mode.
  7. Submit the completed application form and print the confirmation page for future reference.

Documents Required

  • UPSSSC PET 2025 Score Card / Registration details.
  • 10th (High School) & 10+2 (Intermediate) Marksheets and Certificates.
  • 2-Year Livestock Extension Diploma certificate from a recognized Veterinary University.
  • Valid Photo Identity Proof (Aadhaar Card, Voter ID, PAN Card).
  • Domicile Certificate of Uttar Pradesh (if claiming reservation).
  • Category Certificate (OBC / EWS / SC / ST / PH), if applicable.
  • Scanned passport-size photograph and signature.
