About This Recruitment

This combined MPESB recruitment covers Nursing Officer posts in government health institutions and autonomous medical colleges, along with Sister Tutor posts in government nursing colleges. It is a professional health-sector recruitment and candidates must satisfy nursing education and registration rules.

The rulebook gives different post codes for Nursing Officer vacancies and a separate post code for Sister Tutor. Candidates should study the qualification section carefully because Sister Tutor requires nursing qualification with experience, while Nursing Officer requires PCB at 10+2 level, nursing qualification and live registration.

The examination is conducted online. Selection is not complete only by scoring in the test; documents, registration, category certificates and departmental verification are important before final appointment.

Eligibility and Qualification

  • Nursing Officer: 10+2 with Physics, Chemistry and Biology.
  • Nursing Officer: B.Sc. Nursing or Post Basic B.Sc. Nursing / General Nursing training as mentioned in the rulebook, with required marks and relaxation for reserved categories.
  • Nursing Officer: live registration with Madhya Pradesh Nurses Registration Council is required.
  • Sister Tutor: 10+2 with PCB and B.Sc. Nursing or Post Basic B.Sc. Nursing.
  • Sister Tutor: one year experience after B.Sc. Nursing / P.B.B.Sc. Nursing as mentioned in the rulebook.
  • Sister Tutor: live registration with Madhya Pradesh Nurses Registration Council is required.

Selection Process

  1. Online examination conducted by MPESB.
  2. Merit / recommendation as per rulebook and reservation rules.
  3. Verification of nursing qualification, registration, experience and category documents.
  4. Medical fitness and appointment formalities by the concerned department/institution.

Exam Pattern

The online exam is for 100 marks. The rulebook shows 25 marks for general subjects and 75 marks for the concerned nursing subject. Negative marking of 0.25 for each wrong answer is mentioned in the examination rules.
