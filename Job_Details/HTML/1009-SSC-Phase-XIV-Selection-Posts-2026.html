About This Recruitment

The Staff Selection Commission conducts the Selection Posts examination annually to recruit suitable personnel for diverse technical, administrative, operational, and clerical vacancies across multi-level ministries and user departments of the Government of India. The Advertisement No. Phase-XIV/2026/Selection Posts acts as a centralized application gateway, combining unique structural demands into standardized assessment frames categorized clearly by foundational academic levels. Candidates can check targeted department briefs such as the Geological Survey of India, Central Ground Water Board, and Central Forensic Science Laboratory within regional frameworks.

The application procedure mandates modern administrative methodologies, integrating real-time webcam captures, biometric tracking parameters, and comprehensive Aadhaar authentication platforms to establish high system transparency. Candidates should read through the criteria specific to individual post codes because qualification levels, prior professional training, and field experience elements cannot be modified interchangeably. The initial verification process remains tentative, meaning deep structural scrutiny is handled post-examination directly by corresponding user departments.

Shortlisted aspirants will undergo strict structural processing, commencing from basic computer-based screening metrics towards specific performance qualifiers. Depending on specialized requirements, particular profiles might necessitate precise technical evaluations like professional typing tests, machine operations, data entry work, or physical parameter clearances. This structural layout provides an equitable channel for candidates possessing distinct vocational skill sets to attain secure placements under the central governmental architecture.

Eligibility and Qualification

Candidates must possess the requisite structural educational prerequisites as on the closing timeline specified for application submission, i.e., 04.05.2026. The open vacancies are broadly mapped under three distinct scholastic categories:

  • Matriculation Level: Minimum successful completion of Class 10th standard board examinations or equivalent validation metrics from an officially authorized educational institution.
  • Higher Secondary Level: Successful qualification of Intermediate / Class 12th (10+2) certificate standard or corresponding equivalent validations from registered boards.
  • Graduation & Above Level: Holding valid foundational undergraduate degrees, professional diplomas, or technical certifications relevant to specific department domains from recognized global or domestic universities.
  • Post-Qualification Experience: For selected specialized job descriptors, verified post-qualification work experience acquired after obtaining the base degree is mandatory. Document parameters must be fully supported during eventual screening.

Selection Process

The step-by-step processing structure structured by the Staff Selection Commission requires continuous performance qualification through the following structural operational levels:

  1. Computer Based Examination (CBE): Objective type scanning test incorporating multiple choice structures across primary cognitive assessment vectors.
  2. Scrutiny Phase: Standardized structural evaluation of hard copies and uploaded documents regarding basic qualification matrices, category proofs, experience histories, and relevant age-relaxation criteria.
  3. Skill Test: Mandatory skill validations (such as professional typing tests, shorthand data entry metrics, or computer efficiency tests) wherever specified as essential parts of special recruitment rules. These are generally qualifying in nature.
  4. Document Verification (DV): Final checking of original physical transcripts conducted directly under the operational management of respective user departments prior to formal induction.

Exam Pattern

How to Apply for SSC Phase XIV 2026

  1. Navigate to the updated online portal of the Staff Selection Commission at ssc.gov.in or install the mySSC application via the store.
  2. Complete the structural One-Time Registration (OTR) workflow if not registered previously, ensuring precise character matching with your Matriculation certificate.
  3. Log into the system using your generated credentials, visit the 'Live Examination' column, and click the 'Apply' link next to Phase-XIV/2026/Selection Posts.
  4. Verify your pre-populated OTR details, choose your structural preference order for the three examination centers, and pick the language medium for your test paper.
  5. Specify your selected application region and correct job code, verifying that your qualification aligns with the unique criteria of that index.
  6. Use the application interface tool to capture a high-quality, real-time photograph via your webcam. Ensure proper illumination, a plain background, neutral posture, and do not wear caps, spectacles, or masks.
  7. Upload your scanned signature file following the specified size boundaries, preview your data fields to eliminate mistakes, and hit the 'Submit' button.
  8. Process the payment of Rs. 100/- through online payment methods if you do not qualify for a fee exemption, and print the generated form for your tracking archives.
