About This Recruitment

The Punjab State Power Corporation Limited (PSPCL) is seeking qualified, young, and progressive personnel for technical operations across its generation and distribution infrastructure in Punjab. This massive employment drive aims to select 3289 qualified technicians for the role of Assistant Lineman. Selected candidates will be responsible for building, extending, maintaining, and correcting power distribution layouts. This recruitment represents a national-level qualification standard requiring a specialized line vocational certificate alongside strict local language parameters.

Applications must be processed exclusively through online modules using the standardized portal provided on the official website. One of the unique criteria emphasizes that only candidates possessing functional knowledge of the regional language up to Matriculation standard can safely qualify. Due to the high number of anticipated applicants, candidate selection relies completely on automated evaluation, objective evaluation structures, and secure multi-shift execution criteria which feature specific balance algorithms like score normalization.

The evaluation cycle is divided carefully into a foundational language filter stage followed by a technical core test. This means candidates will be assessed both on functional language fluency and specialized line distribution mechanics simultaneously. Prospective applicants must review administrative details such as domicile confirmation rules, age exceptions, specific photograph limits, and document preservation rules to ensure smooth screening processing.

    Eligibility and Qualification

    • Academic Foundation: Candidates must have passed Matriculation (Class 10) or equivalent from a recognized educational board.
    • Professional Mandate: Candidates must possess a National Apprenticeship Certificate (NAC) specifically in the Lineman Trade.
    • Language Constraint: Candidates must have cleared Punjabi at least at the Matriculation level or its equivalent.
    • Timeline Deadline: Both the essential trade certification and language qualifications must be completed on or before the application deadline.

    Selection Process

    1. Computer Based Online Test: Divided into dual parts evaluating language basics along with core discipline concepts.
    2. Punjabi Language Filter Screening: A mandatory qualifying test where candidates must secure minimum scores to avoid immediate disqualification.
    3. Objection Review Cycle: Answer keys will be posted online for candidates to challenge before finalizing calculations.
    4. Score Normalization Processing: Adjusted score calculations across multi-shift sessions to ensure uniform grading metrics.
    5. Document Verification Process: Evaluation of original credentials, certifications, local residency records, and identity logs by an appointed committee.
    6. Final Panel Verification: Arrangement of final panels based entirely on normalized Part-II exam performance metrics.

    Exam Pattern

    The online examination consists of two components wrapped into a combined session duration of 3 hours (180 minutes). Crucially, there is no negative marking for incorrect responses.

    Salary and Benefits

    • Initial Pay Scale Level: Pay parameters conform to the financial circular no. 21/2022 directive.
    • Minimum Admissible Pay: The initial base parameter maps down to a standard Rs. 19900/- (Basic Pay) per month.
    • Probation Constraints: During a mandatory 3-year probation period, selected candidates will receive only the minimum admissible pay, without allowances like Grade Pay, DA, or annual increments. Traveling allowances and medical reimbursement remain active.
    • Existing Service Exception: Employees transferring within existing parameters inside PSPCL are eligible for salary protections if their previous secure pay exceeds the new basic starting point.

    How to Apply

    1. Navigate directly to the official digital portal of Punjab State Power Corporation Limited at www.pspcl.in.
    2. Click into the dedicated Recruitment tab clearly located on the homepage structure.
    3. Select the specific notice listed as "Recruitment for the post of Assistant Lineman against CRA 317/26".
    4. Choose the option labeled NEW REGISTRATION and input core identifiers such as full name, date of birth, active email, and mobile number.
    5. Complete validation checks using the dynamic OTP code string received on your registered device.
    6. Log in using your secure registration number to fill in educational specifications, domicile claims, and upload digital photograph/signature files.
    7. Review data correctness before pressing the final SUBMIT confirmation option, as modifications are locked thereafter.
    8. Proceed directly through the integrated payment window using Internet Banking or Credit/Debit channels to process processing fees.
    9. Log back into the profile to confirm the transaction parameter states "Paid", then download a copy of the printed application layout for safety records.

    What is the total number of vacancies for PSPCL ALM Recruitment 2026?

    What is the last date to apply for PSPCL Assistant Lineman 2026?

    What is the educational qualification required for PSPCL ALM?

    Candidates must have passed Matriculation (Class 10) or equivalent and possess a National Apprenticeship Certificate (NAC) in Lineman Trade. Knowledge of Punjabi up to Matriculation standard is mandatory.
