About This Recruitment

Bihar B.Ed CET 2026 is not a direct government job recruitment. It is an entrance test for candidates who want admission into two-year B.Ed courses and Shiksha Shastri programmes offered by participating universities in Bihar. The State Nodal University mentioned in the prospectus is B. R. Ambedkar Bihar University, Muzaffarpur.

The prospectus gives application instructions, eligibility conditions, exam schedule, entrance test pattern, fee details, admit card timing and counselling-related guidance. Candidates should read the prospectus carefully because filling the form or qualifying the entrance test alone does not guarantee admission. Admission is subject to counselling, document verification and eligibility verification.

This page is useful for candidates planning teacher-training admission in Bihar. It keeps the main facts in one place, but candidates must use the official portal for registration, payment, admit card and updates.

Age Limit

The prospectus does not specify a separate age limit in the extracted schedule. Candidates should check official eligibility rules and counselling instructions before applying.

Vacancy Details

This is an admission entrance test, not a vacancy recruitment. Seat/course availability should be checked from official counselling information and participating university details.

Eligibility and Qualification

  • For B.Ed programme, candidates need at least 50% marks in Bachelor degree or Master degree in Sciences, Social Sciences, Humanities or Commerce.
  • Candidates with Bachelor in Engineering/Technology with specialization in Science and Mathematics need at least 55% marks or an equivalent qualification.
  • For Shiksha Shastri, Sanskrit-related qualification requirements apply as described in the prospectus.
  • Candidates must upload required marksheets and documents while filling the online form.

Selection Process

  1. Register online on the official CET-B.Ed portal.
  2. Fill application details and upload required documents.
  3. Pay the entrance test fee online.
  4. Download admit card when released.
  5. Appear in the OMR-based entrance test.
  6. Check answer key, submit objection if applicable, and check result.
  7. Participate in counselling and document verification as per official schedule.

Exam Pattern

The entrance test is of two hours duration with 120 objective multiple-choice questions for 120 marks. There is no negative marking.
