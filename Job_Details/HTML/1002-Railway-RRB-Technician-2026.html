RRB Technician CEN 02/2025 Result & Recruitment Update

Railway Recruitment Boards notified 6,238 Technician vacancies under CEN 02/2025: 183 Technician Grade-I Signal posts and 6,055 Technician Grade-III posts. The CBT was held in March 2026. RRB officially released the Technician Grade-I Signal CBT result, cut-off and score card on 21 May 2026.

Organization: Railway Recruitment Boards, Ministry of Railways

Posts: Technician Grade-I Signal & Technician Grade-III

Notification: CEN No. 02/2025

Official CEN 02/2025 Updates Official RRB Website
Current StatusGrade-I Result Released; Grade-III Result Awaited

Technician Grade-I Signal result, cut-off and score card were released on 21-05-2026. For Technician Grade-III, candidates should rely on the official CEN 02/2025 notice timeline and not on unrelated CEN 02/2024 updates.
