Recruitment Snapshot

RRB Technician 2025 Details

  • RRB has released notifications for 6238 vacancies, consisting of 183 Technician Grade-I Signal posts and 6055 Technician Grade-III posts.
  • Out of 30 post categories listed, 12 categories at Pay Level 2 have been organized as Merged Post Categories (MPCs) to streamline application convenience.
  • Only online applications are allowed through the authorized recruitment portal. Separate applications and fees must be submitted for each individual Pay Level.
  • Selection depends strictly on performance merit in the Computer Based Test (CBT), followed by Document Verification and a Medical Fitness Test.
  • Identity verification via DigiLocker or Aadhaar is strongly recommended for seamless authentication across recruitment stages.

Eligibility / Qualification Criteria

Post NamePrescribed Educational / Technical Qualification
Technician Grade I Signal Bachelor of Science (B.Sc.) in Physics / Electronics / Computer Science / Information Technology / Instrumentation from a recognized University/Institute OR B.Sc. in a combination of any sub-stream of these basic streams OR 3 years Diploma in Engineering OR Degree in Engineering in the basic streams or their combinations.
Technician Grade III Categories (Posts 2 to 30) Matriculation / SSLC plus ITI from recognized institutions of NCVT/SCVT in the relevant trade specific to the post designation (e.g., Fitter, Electrician, Welder, Machinist, Carpenter, Turner, etc.) OR Matriculation / SSLC plus Course Completed Act Apprenticeship (CCAA) in the relevant trades.
Note: For Category 15 Technician Grade III (S & T), a qualification of 10+2 with Physics and Maths is also accepted in addition to the Matriculation plus ITI/CCAA pathway. Diploma/Degree in Engineering is not accepted in lieu of ITI/CCAA for Level 2 Technician posts unless explicitly specified.

Candidates awaiting the final results of prescribed educational/technical qualifications as on the closing date (28-07-2025) are NOT eligible to apply.

Selection Process

  1. 1

    Computer Based Test (CBT): Separate exams conducted for each Pay Level post. Normalization applies across multi-shift schedules.

  2. 2

    Document Verification (DV): Candidates are shortlisted for DV based on CBT merit at a strict 1:1 ratio matching the final vacancies.

  3. 3

    Medical Examination (ME): Provisionally empanelled candidates must qualify in the requisite medical standard tests (A3, B1, B2, or C1) handled by Railway Medical Authorities.

How to Apply Online

  1. 1

    Visit the official centralized portal: https://rrbapply.gov.in.

  2. 2

    Create an Account or enter your existing OTR credentials if already registered from prior RRB notifications.

  3. 3

    Fill out all specific detail modules for eligibility, academic history, and contact coordinates. Choose your one preferred RRB per Pay Level.

  4. 4

    Live Photo Capturing: Follow prompts to capture a live photograph using a webcam or smartphone camera. Wear non-white dark clothing against a plain background. Do not wear caps, masks, or glasses.

  5. 5

    Upload a clear scanned signature in cursive running handwriting (Black ink, white paper, size 30 KB - 49 KB, JPG/JPEG format).

  6. 6

    Pay the examination fee via integrated online gateways (UPI, Net Banking, Debit/Credit Card) before the closing deadline.

Important Instructions

  • Only Indian Nationals or eligible subjects specified by the Government of India are entitled to apply.
  • Submission of multiple applications for the same Pay Level will lead to the automatic rejection of all forms and debarment.
  • Candidates who have undergone LASIK surgery are strictly ineligible for posts needing an A3 medical standard.
  • Banned items inside exam centers include mobile phones, smartwatches, calculators, books, pens, pencils, or water bottles. Pens will be provided in the test hall.
  • Do not apply henna/mehendi on hands or feet to prevent biometrics capturing failure.
