RRB Technician CEN 02/2025 Result & Recruitment Update

Railway Recruitment Boards notified 6,238 Technician vacancies under CEN 02/2025: 183 Technician Grade-I Signal posts and 6,055 Technician Grade-III posts. The CBT was held in March 2026. RRB officially released the Technician Grade-I Signal CBT result, cut-off and score card on 21 May 2026.

Organization: Railway Recruitment Boards, Ministry of Railways

Posts: Technician Grade-I Signal & Technician Grade-III

Notification: CEN No. 02/2025

Official CEN 02/2025 Updates Official RRB Website
Current StatusGrade-I Result Released; Grade-III Result Awaited

Technician Grade-I Signal result, cut-off and score card were released on 21-05-2026. For Technician Grade-III, the official CEN page currently shows the March 2026 response/objection stage; candidates should wait for an official RRB result notice.

Important: RRB advises candidates to rely only on official Railway Recruitment Board websites for recruitment notices. GovJobUpdates is not a government website.
Updated 1 September 2026: The earlier “Grade 3 Result Out” claim has been removed because it could not be verified on the official CEN 02/2025 notice page. The page now distinguishes the confirmed Grade-I result from the Grade-III follow-up.

Verified CEN 02/2025 Timeline

  • 28 June 2025: Online application opened.
  • 28 July 2025: Closing date for online application.
  • 6, 9, 10 and 13 March 2026: Revised CBT dates for Technician Grade-I Signal and various Technician Grade-III categories.
  • 19 March 2026: Technician Grade-III responses and objection tracker notice.
  • 27 March 2026: Technician Grade-I responses and objection tracker notice.
  • 21 May 2026: Technician Grade-I Signal CBT result, cut-off and score card released; shortlisted candidates proceed to Document Verification and Medical Examination.

Selection Process

  1. 1

    Computer Based Test: Separate CBT is conducted according to the applicable pay level/post.

  2. 2

    Document Verification: Candidates are shortlisted based on CBT merit and the applicable vacancy/selection rules.

  3. 3

    Medical Examination: Shortlisted candidates must meet the prescribed Railway medical standard for their post.

What Candidates Should Check Now

  • Technician Grade-I candidates should use their respective RRB's official result/DV notices.
  • Technician Grade-III candidates should not rely on an unverified “result out” claim; check the official CEN 02/2025 timeline for the result notice.
  • Do not confuse this recruitment with CEN 02/2024, where Technician Grade-III DV rounds were already being conducted in 2026.
  • Keep registration details and original eligibility documents ready for subsequent stages.
