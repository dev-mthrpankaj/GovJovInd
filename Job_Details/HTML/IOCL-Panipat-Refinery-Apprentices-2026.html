About This Recruitment

The Indian Oil Corporation Limited (IOCL) has launched this major apprenticeship drive under the framework of the Apprentices Act, 1961/1973. This program forms part of a nationwide skill-building initiative designed to provide structured industrial orientation and technical training to qualified youths. Selected apprentices will undergo rigorous technical training inside the high-tech environments of the Panipat Refinery & Petrochemical Complex, ensuring valuable exposure to industry standards, machinery operations, and strict industrial safety workflows.

This engagement is strictly limited to the training period specified for each trade, meaning it does not guarantee permanent absorption or any regular employment within the corporation after the completion of the program. Aspiring candidates can apply across highly diverse fields ranging from mechanical fitter lines, instrumentation, electrical setups, and refinery chemical processes to office administration roles like data entry operations, secretarial assistance, and accounting trades.

Applications are processed online through two separate administrative frameworks: the national apprenticeship promotion portals (NAPS/NATS) and a mandatory internal application link hosted on the corporate portal. Because the evaluation process relies entirely on academic achievements rather than a dynamic written exam, candidates should verify their aggregate decimal conversion numbers carefully to minimize any risk of disqualification during verification rounds.

Eligibility and Marks Criteria

  • Candidates must possess a regular full-time structural qualification from a government-recognized board or university. Distance education or private certifications are not accepted.
  • General, OBC, and EWS applicants must secure a minimum of 50% aggregate marks in their respective qualifying course semesters.
  • For SC and PwBD candidates applying against reserved seats, the threshold is relaxed to a minimum of 45% aggregate marks.
  • Higher professional qualifiers such as B.E., B.Tech, MBA, MCA, CA, or LLB holders are explicitly ineligible to apply.
  • Applicants who have completed their mandatory qualification more than five years ago (prior to 31 July 2021) cannot participate.

Selection Process

  1. Academic performance evaluation based entirely on the aggregate marks submitted by the applicants.
  2. Generation of a structural discipline-wise merit list based on performance variables.
  3. Publication of shortlisted rosters on the official portal by 20 August 2026.
  4. Mandatory document verification phase at Panipat Refinery to validate original grade sheets, identity cards, and category declarations.
  5. Pre-engagement medical screening tests (PEME) to fulfill basic fitness criteria set by corporate medical boards.
  6. Final dispatch of apprenticeship allocation and engagement orders to the successfully validated candidates.

Exam Pattern and Syllabus

Per the rules of advertisement no. PR/P/Apprentice/60(2026-27), there is no written test, objective exam, computer-based assessment (CBT), or interview round scheduled for this recruitment cycle. Shortlisting relies purely on the academic performance scores of candidates in their qualifying streams.

Salary, Stipend and Benefits

  • Monthly stipends are issued strictly in line with provisions under the Apprentices Act, 1961/1973 and corporate frameworks.
  • Apprenticeship engagement extends for a standard duration of 12 months for technical/trade slots, and 15 months for secretarial roles.
  • Provides industry-standard health safety instructions and comprehensive plant workflows inside a Fortune Global 500 company.

Documents Required for Application

  • Valid active NAPS/NATS profile registration printout.
  • 10th class certificate and mark sheet for formal age validation.
  • 12th class intermediate certificates (for data entry profiles).
  • ITI (Fitter) trade mark sheets or technical engineering diplomas.
  • Consolidated semester grade cards for B.Sc / B.A / B.Com degrees.
  • Recent color passport-sized photograph and clean signature scans.
  • Valid community certificate (SC/ST/OBC-NCL format) or active EWS asset document if claiming structural seat allocations.

How to Apply

  1. Identify your matching post category code from the recruitment brochure text.
  2. For Trade positions, log into the national apprenticeship portal at apprenticeshipindia.gov.in and apply using establishment ID E10150600035.
  3. For Technician roles, access the NATS portal at nats.education.gov.in and select establishment code NHRPPC000003.
  4. Navigate to the official IOCL web application framework hosted at iocl.com.
  5. Fill out the dedicated Microsoft Application Form using the exact phone and email coordinates linked to your apprenticeship account.
  6. Provide accurate percentage values converted down to the appropriate decimal scales based on your university guidelines.
  7. Review the values on screen, click submit, and save a soft copy of the confirmation receipt before 12 August 2026.

Why Apply For This Recruitment

Securing an apprenticeship at the Indian Oil Corporation Limited Panipat Complex allows engineering diploma holders, ITI technicians, and conventional stream graduates to obtain hands-on heavy industry skills. Given that the entire allocation follows pure merit evaluations based on qualifying grade records, candidates can secure an operational seat without undergoing stressful competitive written examinations. This structural program bridges the transition gap between conventional classroom learning and high-level industrial environments, building up technical competence profiles for prospective careers across the core energy and chemical sectors.

What is the last date to apply for IOCL Panipat Refinery Apprentices 2026?
