About This Recruitment

Rashtriya Chemicals and Fertilizers Limited (RCFL), a leading Public Sector Undertaking (PSU) under the Ministry of Chemicals and Fertilizers, has published a recruitment notification for the post of Management Trainee in 2026. This recruitment drive offers 94 vacant positions across technical and administrative fields including Chemical, Mechanical, Electrical, Instrumentation, Finance, Marketing, IT, and other specialized streams.

Selection as a Management Trainee in RCFL provides candidates with an excellent career foundation in the fertilizers and chemicals manufacturing sector. Candidates with a regular BE/B.Tech degree in relevant branches or appropriate professional qualifications who meet the age criteria are invited to apply online within the scheduled timeline.

The application mode is strictly online through the official portal. Candidates must ensure they meet all educational, age, and reservation standards as specified in advertisement 16022026 prior to submitting the online form and fee.

Eligibility and Qualification

  • Full-time engineering degree (BE / B.Tech) in relevant disciplines for technical posts.
  • Relevant professional qualification for administrative and specialized posts like Finance, Rajbhasha, Marketing, etc.
  • Degrees must be recognized by AICTE / UGC / relevant regulatory bodies in India.
  • Detailed percentage rules and trade-wise criteria should be verified from the official notice.

Selection Process

  1. Computer Based Test (CBT) covering domain-specific subject knowledge and aptitude.
  2. Personal Interview (PI) for candidate evaluation.
  3. Document Verification of original academic, category, and identity certificates.
  4. Final Merit List based on combined performance in CBT and Interview as per RCFL rules.
  5. Pre-Employment Medical Examination.

Exam Pattern

Selection will involve a Computer Based Examination. Detailed topic-wise syllabus, marks distribution, test duration, and negative marking rules should be checked directly in the official notification.

Documents Required

How to Apply

  1. Visit the official RCFL portal at rcfltd.com.
  2. Navigate to the Recruitment / HR section and open Advt No. 16022026.
  3. Click on "RCFL Management Trainee Apply Online".
  4. Register using a valid email ID and mobile number to obtain registration credentials.
  5. Log in and complete the application form with accurate personal, educational, and category details.
  6. Upload scanned copies of required documents, recent photograph, and signature.
  7. Pay the required application fee via online mode if applicable.
  8. Review all details in the preview window and submit the form before 24/08/2026 (05:00 PM).
  9. Download and print a copy of the final submitted application form for future reference.
