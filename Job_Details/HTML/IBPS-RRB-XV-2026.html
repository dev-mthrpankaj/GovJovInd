About This Recruitment

IBPS RRB XV Recruitment 2026 is the Common Recruitment Process for Group A Officers (Scale I, II and III) and Group B Office Assistants (Multipurpose) in participating Regional Rural Banks. A total of 13,706 reported vacancies are available across the covered posts.

The online registration and fee-payment window runs from 1 September 2026 to 21 September 2026. Candidates applying for the officer cadre may apply for only one Officer post among Scale I, Scale II and Scale III, while an eligible candidate may separately apply for Office Assistant subject to the notification conditions.

Eligibility differs by post. Office Assistant and Officer Scale I primarily require a recognised bachelor's degree, while Scale II specialist/general banking posts and Scale III carry post-specific qualification and experience requirements. Local-language proficiency is important for Office Assistant and Officer Scale I as prescribed for the participating RRB.

Payment information

Application Fee

Payment mode: Online as permitted in the official notification.

For Office Assistant, the concessional fee category includes SC/ST/PwBD/ESM/DESM candidates. For Officer posts, the concessional fee applies to SC/ST/PwBD candidates.

06

Your path to selection

Selection Process

Office Assistant: Preliminary Examination, Main Examination and provisional allotment subject to eligibility/document requirements. No interview is prescribed for Office Assistant.

Officer Scale I: Preliminary Examination, Main Examination and Interview, followed by provisional allotment.

Officer Scale II & III: Single online examination and Interview, followed by provisional allotment.
