About This Recruitment

The Jammu & Kashmir Services Selection Board (JKSSB) has released Advertisement Notification No. 07/2026 to recruit candidates for various posts under the Disaster Management Relief and Rehabilitation department in Division Kashmir. Key positions include Sub-Inspector, Depot Assistant, Junior Librarian, Junior Statistical Assistant, Library Assistant, Motor Vehicle Traffic Assistant, and PWD Draftsman Civil.

Interested candidates fulfilling the required educational qualification, age criteria, and domicile rules can apply online through the official portal. The selection process will be conducted according to government guidelines, featuring written examinations and skill or physical tests depending on the specific post profile.

Applicants are advised to thoroughly read the official advertisement to confirm their eligibility criteria, post-wise qualification requirements, reservation guidelines, and application procedures before filling out the online application form.

How to Apply

  1. Visit the official JKSSB recruitment portal at jkssb.nic.in.
  2. Click on the link "JKSSB Disaster Management Relief Online Application Form 2026".
  3. New users should register using a valid email ID and active mobile number.
  4. Log in using the generated Registration ID and password.
  5. Fill in personal details, educational qualifications, and post preferences accurately.
  6. Upload necessary scanned documents including photo, signature, and domicile certificate.
  7. Pay the required application fee online through Net Banking, Credit, or Debit Card.
  8. Preview and verify all entered information before submitting the form.
  9. Submit the application form and keep a printed copy for future reference.
