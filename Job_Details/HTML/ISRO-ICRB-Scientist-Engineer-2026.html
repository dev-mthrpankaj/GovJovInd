About This Recruitment

The Indian Space Research Organisation (ISRO) Centralised Recruitment Board (ICRB) has issued Notification Advt No. ISRO ICRB:02(EMC-CEPO):2026 for the recruitment of Scientist/Engineer posts in various engineering disciplines. Eligible engineering graduates across India with a valid GATE score can apply for these prestigious research and development opportunities.

Positions are available across multiple engineering branches including Electronics & Communication, Mechanical, Computer Science, Civil, Electrical, Air Conditioning & Refrigeration, and Architecture. Candidates are required to fill out the online application form accurately through ISRO's official portal and upload required certificates prior to the registration deadline.

Shortlisted applicants will undergo a interview process based on their valid GATE performance and academic qualifications. Joining ISRO provides an extraordinary professional platform to work on cutting-edge space research, satellite launch systems, and national technology missions.

Eligibility and Qualification

  • Degree Qualification: B.E. / B.Tech / B.Arch or equivalent qualification in the relevant engineering branch with a minimum aggregate of 65% marks or CGPA 6.84/10.
  • GATE Qualification: Must possess a valid GATE score in the respective discipline.
  • Final year students fulfilling all essential requirements prior to specified cutoff dates are eligible as per rules.

Selection Process

  1. Shortlisting of candidates based on valid GATE Score card in the corresponding branch.
  2. Personal Interview for shortlisted candidates conducted by ISRO Selection Board.
  3. Document Verification of academic, caste, and GATE certificates.
  4. Final Selection list prepared based on performance in Interview subject to medical fitness and eligibility fulfillment.

Exam Pattern & Interview Structure

Shortlisting for the interview is strictly carried out based on valid GATE performance. Details regarding interview pattern, weightage distribution, and interview center allocation will be communicated directly to shortlisted candidates and published on the ISRO portal.

How to Apply

  1. Visit the official ISRO portal at isro.gov.in.
  2. Navigate to Careers section and click on Notification Advt No. ISRO ICRB:02(EMC-CEPO):2026.
  3. Complete initial online registration by providing basic details, valid email, and mobile number.
  4. Fill in academic credentials, GATE registration number, GATE score, and personal details.
  5. Upload required scanned documents, photograph, and signature as per prescribed specifications.
  6. Pay the application fee of Rs. 250 through online banking / UPI / Debit card.
  7. Verify all entries before final submission and download/print the application confirmation page.
