About This Recruitment

ISRO ICRB Scientist Engineer Recruitment 2026 is an official recruitment drive conducted by the Indian Space Research Organization (ISRO) Centralised Recruitment Board to hire Scientist Engineers under Advertisement No. ISRO:ICRB:03(EMC):2026. The recruitment covers 175 openings across Electronics, Mechanical, and Computer Science disciplines.

Interested and eligible candidates holding a B.E/B.Tech degree in the relevant streams with a minimum of 65% marks can submit their online application through the official portal. The application window is open from 27 August 2026 to 16 September 2026. Applicants must ensure they meet the upper age limit of 28 years as of the closing date and complete the fee payment successfully.

The selection process includes a written examination, and candidates appearing for the exam are eligible for a partial or full refund of their application fee depending on their category. Candidates are advised to download the official notification PDF and carefully review all eligibility criteria before publishing their application online.

How to Apply

  1. Visit the official website of ISRO at www.isro.gov.in.
  2. Click on the “ISRO ICRB Scientist Engineer 2026 Apply Online” link provided in the careers or recruitment section.
  3. Register yourself using a valid email ID and mobile number to generate your login credentials.
  4. Login using your newly created registration ID and password.
  5. Fill the application form carefully with your personal, educational, and category details.
  6. Upload scanned documents, including your recent passport size photo and signature, in the required format.
  7. Pay the application fee of ₹750 through the online payment gateway.
  8. Submit the application form and download/print the confirmation page for future reference.
