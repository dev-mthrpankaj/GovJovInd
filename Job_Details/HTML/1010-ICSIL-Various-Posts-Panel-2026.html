About This Recruitment

ICSIL Various Posts Panel Recruitment 2026 is not a fixed vacancy advertisement in the usual sense. The notice has been issued for creating a panel of eligible candidates so that ICSIL can meet present and future manpower requirements of various client departments. The advertisement specifically mentions that the panel may be used where a client department does not have its own explicit panel or has directed ICSIL to float an independent enquiry.

The panel includes Data Entry Operator, MTS and Driver profiles with different qualification levels. Candidates who match the relevant eligibility can apply online through the ICSIL website under the Career section. Applicants should update their profile properly because the advertisement states that the candidate profile must match the eligibility criteria for the post.

Empanelment should not be treated as a guaranteed job offer. The official terms mention that candidates may be considered according to client requirements, application ID order, document verification and selection process prescribed by the client department. Candidates may be deployed anywhere in Delhi/NCR and may have to work in shifts or rotational duties as per requirement.

Eligibility and Qualification

  • For graduate-level Data Entry Operator, candidates should be graduates in any discipline from a recognized university.
  • For 12th pass Data Entry Operator, candidates should have passed Class 12 and should have minimum 30 WPM English typing speed.
  • For MTS, candidates should have passed Class 10.
  • For Driver, Class 10 pass is desirable and a valid Light Motor Vehicle license is required.
  • For Data Entry Operator entries, a computer course of minimum 3 months is desirable.
  • Post-qualification experience of 1-2 years is desirable for all listed posts.
  • Candidate profile must match the eligibility criteria mentioned in the advertisement for the selected post.

Selection Process

  1. Online application and profile submission through the ICSIL website.
  2. Scrutiny and verification of age, educational qualification, experience and other eligibility conditions.
  3. Empanelment of candidates found eligible.
  4. Calling of empanelled candidates for interview, interaction or any other selection process as prescribed by the client department when requisitions are received.
  5. Document verification with original documents and self-attested photocopies.
  6. Deployment or engagement as per client department requirement and contractual terms.

Exam Pattern

The advertisement does not mention a written examination pattern. It states that empanelled candidates may be called for interview, interaction or any other selection process as prescribed by the client department. Salary and Benefits

  • Remuneration is mentioned as per Minimum Wages of GNCTD.
  • Engagement will be contractual and linked to client department requirements.
  • The advertisement states that fifteen days' salary shall be retained as refundable security deposit in two equal instalments from the first two months' salary.
  • The security deposit is refundable with applicable savings bank interest after completion of contractual obligations and submission of No Dues Certificate from the client department.
  • No additional conveyance allowance shall be payable by ICSIL for deployment anywhere in Delhi/NCR.

How to Apply

  1. Visit the official ICSIL website at www.icsil.in.
  2. Open the Career section and go to the Current Job section.
  3. Read the “How to apply for current Jobs” instructions available on the website.
  4. Deposit the one-time non-refundable registration fee of Rs. 590 as per the ICSIL process.
  5. Complete your profile and enter educational qualification from Class 10 to highest qualification.
  6. Add experience details correctly in the profile, if applicable.
  7. Select the post only if your profile matches the eligibility criteria mentioned in the advertisement.
  8. Submit the online application and keep application details safely for future communication.

Why Apply For This Recruitment

This panel can be useful for candidates looking for contractual manpower opportunities in Delhi/NCR through ICSIL client departments. DEO, MTS and Driver roles have simple qualification requirements, but candidates should apply only after understanding that empanelment is not a guarantee of deployment and final consideration depends on future requisitions, eligibility verification and client department requirements.
