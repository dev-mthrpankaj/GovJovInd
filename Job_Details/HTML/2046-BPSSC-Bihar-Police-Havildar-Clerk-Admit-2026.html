Recruitment Snapshot

BPSSC Bihar Police Havildar Clerk 2026 Details

  • The Bihar Police Subordinate Services Commission (BPSSC) is organizing a state-level recruitment drive to fill up 64 vacant contractual/permanent positions of Havildar Clerk within the Bihar Police department.
  • Candidates must have successfully cleared the Intermediate (10+2 standard) examination or its officially recognized equivalent.
  • The written examination consists of 100 Multiple Choice Questions (MCQs) carrying 100 marks to be completed in 2 hours (120 minutes).
  • Selection will be made through offline OMR written test, Physical Standard Test (PST), Physical Efficiency Test (PET), and final document verification.

Note: The preliminary written test has a mandatory minimum cut-off benchmark of 30% marks to satisfy structural eligibility conditions for physical tests and further stages.

Written Examination Scheme

The offline OMR testing structure covers multiple academic domains modeled on matriculation difficulty expectations:

  • Total Marks: 100 Marks
  • Total Questions: 100 Multiple Choice Questions (MCQs)
  • Duration: 2 Hours (120 Minutes continuous timing)
  • Negative Evaluation: No provision for negative marks on wrong inputs
  • Core Assessment Domains: General Knowledge / General Awareness (40 Qs), Mathematics (30 Qs), General Science (15 Qs), and Social Science (15 Qs)
