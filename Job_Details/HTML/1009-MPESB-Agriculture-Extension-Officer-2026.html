About This Recruitment

MPESB Agriculture Extension Officer Recruitment 2026 is a state-level recruitment process under Group 2 Sub Group 1. The conducting body is Madhya Pradesh Employees Selection Board, Bhopal, and the posts are notified for the Directorate of Farmer Welfare and Agriculture Development, Madhya Pradesh.

This recruitment is useful for candidates who have completed graduation in Agriculture or Horticulture and want to work in agriculture extension services. The post is executive in nature, so candidates should read the official rulebook carefully for eligibility, category reservation, employment registration, document verification and service conditions.

The selection is based on a computer based online examination. The paper carries 200 marks, divided between general subjects and agriculture-related subject knowledge. Candidates should prepare both parts because the final recommendation depends on the examination and fulfillment of all departmental eligibility rules.

Exam Pattern

The rulebook states that there will be only one question paper for the examination. The total paper is of 200 marks, with 100 marks for general subjects and 100 marks for agriculture-related subject questions.
