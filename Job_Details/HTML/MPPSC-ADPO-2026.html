About This Recruitment

The Madhya Pradesh Public Service Commission (MPPSC) is conducting this state-level recruitment for the Home Department to enlist legal professionals as Assistant District Public Prosecution Officers (ADPO). This position serves an essential role in the state's legal administration framework, representing governance parameters across regional public prosecution offices. According to the revised notice released under corrigendum number 01/03/2026, the commission integrated an additional 90 vacancies into the process, significantly expanding the scope for eligible candidates holding formal qualifications in legal disciplines.

Applications for this recruitment have been officially reopened for a specific timeline. Candidates who missed out during the initial publication window can now access the submission portal. As per the structural rules mandated by the General Administration Department, the evaluation and eventual outcomes are organized into two key structural classification modules: the Main Part (constituting 87% of vacancies) and the Provisional Part (constituting 13% of vacancies) to safely handle operational procedures. Existing applicants who managed to submit their documentation in the earlier periods are advised that resubmission is not required, although access to correct errors remains available.

The recruitment process necessitates that all essential qualifications and associated certificates be fully secured by the targeted deadline date. MPPSC has also provisioned an extended staggered late registration policy with escalating fee slabs for specific administrative windows, emphasizing the necessity of early compliance to prevent costly overhead penalties. Prospective candidates are encouraged to verify structural alignment against specified state reservation criteria before establishing portal inputs.

Eligibility criteria and Qualifications

  • Educational Qualification: Candidates must possess a valid degree in Law (LL.B) from a recognized university or statutory institution.
  • Target Deadline Validity: The academic qualification parameter must be successfully achieved and backed by proper documentation on or before the regular application closing timeline of 24.07.2026.
  • State Employment Registration: Valid employment exchange registration terms within Madhya Pradesh state parameters may apply as per core foundational advertisement guidelines.

Selection Process Framework

  1. Written / Screening Test: MPPSC intends to organize an initial testing stage to filter candidates based on relevant legal subjects and general knowledge components.
  2. Evaluation Processing Strategy: Result tabulation will split into a Main Segment (87%) and a Provisional Segment (13%) matching administrative policy updates.
  3. Interview Stage: Shortlisted candidates undergo personality checks and professional capability reviews before a certified evaluation panel.
  4. Document Authentication: Verification of formal state credentials, category records, and law graduation proofs.
  5. Final Merit List: Consolidating consolidated test and interview performance metrics to release the final list of selected public prosecutors.

Exam Pattern
