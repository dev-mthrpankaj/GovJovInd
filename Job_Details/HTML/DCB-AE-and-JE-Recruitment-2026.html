About This Recruitment

The Delhi Cantonment Board (DCB) recruitment drive offers a significant opportunity for engineering professionals looking to secure a core technical role under administrative cantonment jurisdictions. Operating under the structural governance of the Ministry of Defence, the board ensures a highly secure, disciplined, and professional civil construction environment within the national capital region.

Selected Assistant Engineers and Junior Engineers will be deeply involved in supervisory operations, maintenance frameworks, and regular municipal structure optimizations across specialized departments. Aspiring candidates must ensure they fulfill the localized parameters regarding educational scores, specific technical classifications, and standard documents before beginning their submission workflow on the official web infrastructure.

Eligibility and Qualification

  • Assistant Engineer (Civil): Bachelor Degree in Civil Engineering from any recognized University or Institute in India.
  • Assistant Engineer (Mechanical): Bachelor Degree in Mechanical Engineering from a legally recognized technical institution.
  • Junior Engineer (Civil): Bachelor Degree in Civil Engineering OR a 3-year Diploma in Civil Engineering from a recognized board or university.
  • Junior Engineer (Electrical): Bachelor Degree in Electrical Engineering OR a 3-year Diploma in Electrical Engineering from a recognized university or board.

Selection Process

  1. Verification of submitted digital details on the centralized registration matrix.
  2. Conduction of the regular evaluation checks / examinations as configured by DCB guidelines.
  3. Document verification phase covering original institutional marks and degree setups.
  4. Final selection allocation subject to clearance of police verification and medical standards check.
