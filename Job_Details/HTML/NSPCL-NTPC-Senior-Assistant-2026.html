About This Recruitment

NSPCL NTPC Senior Assistant Recruitment 2026 is an official recruitment drive conducted by the NTPC Sail Power Company Limited (NSPCL) to hire Senior Assistants across various technical and administrative disciplines under Advertisement No. 01/2026. The recruitment covers 17 openings for Pan India locations.

Interested and eligible candidates holding a relevant Degree, PG Degree, B.E., or B.Tech can complete their online application through the official NSPCL portal. The application window is open from 07 September 2026 to 22 September 2026. Applicants should ensure they meet the age criteria as of 22 September 2026 and complete the fee payment before the stated deadline.

Candidates are advised to download the official notification PDF and carefully review the eligibility criteria and document requirements prior to submitting their online forms.

How to Apply

  1. Visit the official website of NSPCL at nspcl.co.in.
  2. Navigate to the careers section and click on the "Apply Online" link for NSPCL NTPC Senior Assistant Recruitment.
  3. Register yourself using a valid email ID and mobile number to generate login credentials.
  4. Login using your registration ID and password.
  5. Fill the online application form with your personal, educational, and category details carefully.
  6. Upload scanned copies of required documents including a recent photograph and signature in the specified format.
  7. Pay the application fee of Rs.300 (if applicable) through the online payment gateway.
  8. Submit the application form and download or print the confirmation page for future reference.
