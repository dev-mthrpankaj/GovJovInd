Eligibility and Qualification

  • Mining Sirdar T&S Gr. C:
    • Matriculate or equivalent Examination clearance from any recognized Board of Examination.
    • Valid Mining Sirdarship Certificate of Competency issued by DGMS under Coal Mines Regulation 2017 or alternative statutory Mining authorization certificate.
    • Valid Gas Testing Certificate issued by appropriate statutory authority.
    • Valid First Aid Certificate from a recognized organization.
  • Surveyor T&S Gr. B (Mining):
    • Matriculate or equivalent Examination clearance from any recognized Board of Examination.
    • Valid Surveyors Certificate of Competency (SCC) granted under Coal Mines Regulation 2017 or equivalent mining technical certificate.
