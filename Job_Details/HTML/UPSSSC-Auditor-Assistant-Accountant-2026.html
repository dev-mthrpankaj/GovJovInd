About This Recruitment

The UPSSSC Auditor and Assistant Accountant Recruitment 2026 is initiated as a state-level competitive process to fill essential roles in various administration, finance, and internal accounts departments under the Uttar Pradesh government. As an administrative and audit segment framework, selected professionals take care of accounting records, local fund balances, internal auditing procedures, and computational office management files. Only Indian citizens who actively participated in the UPSSSC PET 2025 cycle and hold a valid qualification score card are invited to clear this main tier filter.

Applying candidates need to systematically access the live portal module via the commission's main interface, log in utilizing their original PET registration records, double-check automated database entries, select post preference indicators, and proceed with completing the mandatory actions. As this is a competitive state job profile, verifying the educational and computer diploma eligibility beforehand is important to make sure the data matches the criteria during standard document assessment windows.

The final operational ranking framework takes into account the performance inside the Main Written Examination. Initially, candidates are filtered using their valid PET score card thresholds, after which shortlisted applicants face the objective format examination. Therefore, preparing with continuous practice around accountancy concepts, basic book-keeping models, auditing guidelines, general awareness, and standard office computational tools is highly recommended for all serious state services job aspirants.

Eligibility and Qualification

  • Must possess a valid UPSSSC PET 2025 Score Card with non-zero or positive marks.
  • Must hold a Bachelor's Degree in Commerce (B.Com) from a university established by law in India, OR a Post Graduate Diploma in Accountancy from a government-recognized university or institute.
  • Must possess an 'O' Level Computer Diploma from a government-recognized institution (NIELIT/DOEACC).
  • Essential credentials, score certificates, and professional qualifications must be fully completed before the specified portal closing date.

Selection Process

  1. Shortlisting phase based on the valid marks acquired in the UPSSSC PET 2025 examination.
  2. Main Written Examination consisting of objective multiple-choice questions (MCQs) for shortlisted candidates.
  3. Document Verification (DV) phase to review all original educational, tier, and computer credentials.
  4. Final merit listing generated as per commission criteria based on the Main Written Examination performance and verification clearance.
