About This Recruitment

The UPSSSC Auditor and Assistant Accountant Recruitment 2026 offers a major career opportunity for Commerce and Accountancy graduates seeking permanent government positions in Uttar Pradesh. Conducted under Advertisement Number 14-Exam/2026, this drive aims to fill 1937 posts in various state departments. Selection is handled through shortlisting based on candidate performance in the UPSSSC Preliminary Eligibility Test (PET 2025), followed by a main written examination and document verification.

Eligible candidates must possess a Bachelor's Degree in Commerce (B.Com) or a Post Graduate Diploma in Accountancy along with a valid NIELIT 'O' Level Computer Certificate. Since shortlisting for the Mains Examination is strictly determined by UPSSSC PET 2025 scores, candidates must register online using their existing PET details or OTR portal before the final registration deadline.

All application steps—including registration, form completion, document uploads, and payment of the standard processing fee—are executed online via the UPSSSC official portal. Applicants are urged to carefully verify their personal data, educational credentials, category documents, and PET score credentials prior to final submission.

Eligibility and Qualification

  • Must have appeared in UPSSSC PET 2025 and hold a valid score card.
  • Bachelor's Degree in Commerce (B.Com) OR Post Graduate Diploma in Accountancy from a recognized University/Institute.
  • Must possess an 'O' Level Certificate in Computer Application awarded by NIELIT (formerly DOEACC).
  • For complete preferential qualification details, refer to the official notification PDF.

Selection Process

  1. Shortlisting of candidates based on valid UPSSSC PET 2025 Score.
  2. Main Written Examination conducted by UPSSSC for shortlisted candidates.
  3. Document Verification (DV) of original academic, identity, and category certificates.
  4. Final Selection List and Departmental Allocation based on Merit List.

  • Pay Level: Level-5 in Pay Matrix.
  • Pay Scale: Rs. 29,900 - 92,300/- (Grade Pay 2800).
  • Admissible allowances include Dearness Allowance (DA), House Rent Allowance (HRA), Medical and State allowances as per UP Government rules.

Documents Required

  • UPSSSC PET 2025 Score Card / Registration Details.
  • Class 10th Certificate (High School) for Age Proof.
  • Class 12th Certificate / Marksheet.
  • Bachelor's Degree in Commerce (B.Com) or Post Graduate Diploma in Accountancy Certificate & Marksheet.
  • NIELIT 'O' Level Computer Certificate.
  • Valid Photo ID Proof (Aadhaar Card, Voter ID, PAN Card, etc.).
  • Scanned Passport Size Photograph and Signature.
  • Domicile Certificate & Caste/Category Certificate (EWS, OBC, SC, ST, PH), if applicable.

How to Apply

  1. Visit the official website of UPSSSC at upsssc.gov.in.
  2. Click on the "Live Advertisements" tab and select "Advt No 14-Exam/2026 (Auditor and Assistant Accountant)".
  3. Authenticate using your UPSSSC PET 2025 Personal Details or via One Time Password (OTP).
  4. Fill in your essential qualification details, NIELIT 'O' Level certification numbers, and personal information.
  5. Upload your latest photograph and signature as per prescribed dimensions.
  6. Pay the required online processing fee of Rs. 25 through Net Banking, Debit/Credit Card, or UPI.
  7. Verify all details carefully in the print preview and submit the application form.
  8. Download and print the final submitted application form for future reference during document verification.
