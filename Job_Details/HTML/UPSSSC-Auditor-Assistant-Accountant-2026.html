About This Recruitment

The Uttar Pradesh Subordinate Services Selection Commission (UPSSSC) invites online applications for recruitment to 1,957 positions of Auditor and Assistant Accountant across various state government departments. This recruitment provides a strong career opportunity for candidates who have completed their graduation and hold computer certifications like NIELIT 'O' Level in Uttar Pradesh state government offices.

Eligibility for shortlisting for the main examination depends primarily on the applicant's score in the UPSSSC Preliminary Eligibility Test (PET) 2025. Candidates must register online through the official UPSSSC portal by providing their PET registration details, followed by submitting personal and educational qualifications alongside scanned documents.

Candidates must carefully review all notification parameters, age relaxation limits, and category reservation criteria before registering. After successful payment of the application processing fee, candidates must retain a printed copy of the final application form for submission during document verification.

Eligibility and Qualification

  • PET Eligibility: Must possess a valid UPSSSC PET 2025 Score Card.
  • Educational Qualification: Bachelor Degree in Commerce (B.Com) or relevant discipline from any recognized University in India.
  • Computer Skill Qualification: NIELIT 'O' Level Diploma Certificate in Computer operations or equivalent as notified by the board.
  • Detailed stream requirements can be verified from the official notification.

Selection Process

  1. Shortlisting of candidates based on UPSSSC PET 2025 Score Card.
  2. Main Written Examination conducted by UPSSSC.
  3. Document Verification (DV) of shortlisted candidates.
  4. Final Medical Examination and departmental posting.

Exam Pattern

Detailed syllabus and examination pattern for the Mains examination will be separately published on the official UPSSSC website. Candidates are recommended to regularly check the official portal or download the official syllabus PDF.

How to Apply

  1. Visit the official portal of UPSSSC at upsssc.gov.in.
  2. Navigate to the "Applicant Segment" and select "Candidate Registration".
  3. Authenticate using your UPSSSC PET 2025 Registration Number either via Personal Details or OTP.
  4. Fill in all educational qualifications, NIELIT 'O' level details, and personal attributes carefully.
  5. Upload required documents including photo and signature as specified.
  6. Pay the required processing fee of Rs. 25 online using Net Banking, Debit/Credit Card, or UPI.
  7. Verify all entered details carefully using the preview feature before submitting.
  8. Download and print the final submitted application form for future reference.
