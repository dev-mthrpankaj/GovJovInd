About This Recruitment

The Indian Air Force Agniveer Vayu Musician Recruitment Rally is an all-India competitive entry process aimed at selecting talented individuals to join the musical domain of the air force. Eligible male and female candidates from all states can participate in this recruitment process. Selected personnel will be incorporated under the customized terms and conditions applicable to the Agniveer Vayu scheme, contributing to the military bands and official ceremonial events of the Indian Air Force.

Unlike standard technical or non-technical tracks, this specific intake prioritizes proficiency in handling musical instruments and demonstrating musical capabilities alongside matching physical efficiency standards. Candidates are required to fill out the registration form through the designated digital portal before the deadline, verifying their credentials and uploading scanned formats of identity templates, educational certificates, and musical proficiency proofs where relevant.

The screening process consists of physical fitness tests, detailed assessment parameters concerning musical aptitude, document verification, and medical testing rounds. Given that the entire recruitment structure revolves around fixed rally venues, applicants must keep track of their entry criteria and confirm that they meet the minimum age boundaries and physical attributes detailed in the central brochure before proceeding with their online application forms.

Eligibility and Qualification

  • Educational Qualification: Must have successfully passed the Class 10th High School Examination or its equivalent qualification from any recognized educational board in India.
  • Musical Capability Requirement: Candidates must possess verified music ability and skills to perform effectively on specified musical instruments. Refer to the official advertisement text for details on specific instrument types.
  • Nationality Factor: Open for citizens belonging to all parts and states of India subject to fulfillment of residence criteria during document check rounds.

Selection Process

  1. Initial Online Registration: Filling up basic credentials and submission of processing fee via the registration link.
  2. Physical Fitness Test (PFT): Verification of running efficiency and endurance components under designated time margins.
  3. Music Ability Evaluation: Assessment rounds verifying capabilities with respect to tone, rhythm, and specified instrument handling.
  4. Document Authentication: Validation of Class 10th marksheets, identity certificates, photo IDs, and music capability records.
  5. Medical Screening Stage: Checking fitness attributes in accordance with strict defense medical guidelines laid out by the IAF.
