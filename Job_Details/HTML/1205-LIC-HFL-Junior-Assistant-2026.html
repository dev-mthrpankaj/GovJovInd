About This Recruitment

LIC HFL Junior Assistant recruitment is a city-wise vacancy based selection process for appointment in LIC Housing Finance Ltd. The role is connected with loan processing, documentation, data entry, customer servicing, EMI follow-up, recovery support, cash counter work and related business activities.

The selection process includes an online examination followed by interview. Final selection is based on combined marks of the online exam and interview, subject to minimum qualifying marks and verification of eligibility.

Candidates can apply for only one vacancy city and must also select an examination state. The interview state will be the same as the vacancy state, so applicants should choose city and state preferences carefully.

Eligibility and Qualification

  • Indian citizen.
  • Age 21-30 years as on 01.04.2026.
  • Graduate with minimum aggregate 55% marks in any discipline from a recognized university.
  • Correspondence, distance or part-time course is not eligible.
  • Operating and working knowledge in computer systems is required.
  • Work experience is preferred.

Selection Process

  1. Online examination.
  2. Shortlisting for interview based on online examination performance.
  3. Interview.
  4. Document verification at interview stage.
  5. Final selection based on combined marks of online examination and interview.

Exam Pattern

The online examination is objective type, in English only, with 200 questions for 200 marks and 120 minutes duration. There is negative marking of 0.25 mark for each wrong answer.
