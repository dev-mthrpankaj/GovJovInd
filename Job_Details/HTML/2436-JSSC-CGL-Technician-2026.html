Selection Methodology

  1. 1

    Preliminary Exam: JSSC will organize a Preliminary multi-choice exam if the net count of valid applicant profiles exceeds 50,000 threshold values.

  2. 2

    Main Examination: Candidates clearing the prelims (or directly if applications are under 50,000) must appear in the comprehensive Main examination comprising 3 specific structural papers.

  3. 3

    Normalisation Processing: In case the CBT evaluation runs across multiple batches or shifts, normalisation formulas will determine fair multi-shift relative grading.

  4. 4

    Document Verification (DV): Candidates achieving minimum category cut-offs will be summoned to produce their original master qualifications, category certificates, and identity cards.

Main Examination Pattern

The JSSC JTGLCCE Main written evaluation involves 3 multi-choice papers administered in distinct scheduling blocks on the same or structured calendar shifts:

Paper ComponentSubject DimensionsQuestionsDuration
Paper 1 (Qualifying Only)Hindi Paragraph & Grammar (60 Qs) + English Paragraph & Grammar (60 Qs)120 Qs120 Minutes
Paper 2 (Merit Counted)Tribal or Regional Language Specialization (Selected from official option matrix)100 Qs120 Minutes
Paper 3 (Merit Counted)Technical/Specialized Core (100 Qs) + General Studies (20 Qs) + General Math (20 Qs) + General Science (10 Qs)150 Qs150 Minutes

Marking Scheme: Each correct response awards 3 marks. A mandatory penalty of 1 mark will be deducted for every incorrect entry. Final merit is prepared combining scores of Paper 2 and Paper 3 only (subject to clearing minimum qualifying benchmarks like 40% for General/UR).

Steps to Apply Online

  1. 1

    Open the official Jharkhand Staff Selection Commission URL: https://jssc.jharkhand.gov.in.

  2. 2

    Navigate to the 'Application Forms (Apply)' link and look for the 'JTGLCCE-2026' online application link.

  3. 3

    Complete your registration by entering core details such as name, email, date of birth, and dynamic mobile number.

  4. 4

    Log in with the generated registration number and fill the academic fields, category claims, and preferred post allocations.

  5. 5

    Pay the requested application fee online after 24-48 hours of initial registration form locking if prompted by the portal workflow.

  6. 6

    Upload clean, scanned editions of your recent passport photograph and signature matching file sizes defined in the interface guidelines.

  7. 7

    Verify all entered data rows carefully, submit the final screen, and take a clear printout copy of your transaction page and dashboard receipt.
