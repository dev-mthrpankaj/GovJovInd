About This Recruitment

Coal India Limited (CIL), a Maharatna Public Sector Undertaking under the Ministry of Coal, Government of India, has declared a recruitment drive for young, energetic engineering graduates. This opportunity allows qualified professionals to step into the role of Management Trainee (Mining) within an entity that serves as the single largest coal producing organization globally. This regular recruitment provides direct entry into the executive cadre (E-1 Grade) for individuals who possess a valid technical background and have proven their technical abilities in competitive platforms.

The application procedure is strictly operational through the online career portal of Coal India Limited. Candidates holding a recognized degree in Mining Engineering are required to complete their profile registration, upload mandatory documents, and clear the requisite application payment within the structural timeline. It is critical to note that registrations submitted without an active or qualifying GATE 2025 score card will be subject to immediate rejection without review. Candidates currently engaged with Government or PSU organizations must verify their specific NOC terms before final documentation steps.

The core selection strategy eliminates separate written technical exams by directly assessing candidates on their Graduate Aptitude Test in Engineering (GATE - 2025) performance. A category-wise merit panel is generated to streamline processing for subsequent phases, including comprehensive certificate checking and predefined medical test parameters. Aspiring executives should review this comprehensive page summary to align their technical credentials, category certifications, and vital dates before approaching the final live links.

Eligibility and Qualification

  • Degree Requirement: Must possess a regular, full-time Degree in Mining Engineering from a University or Institute recognized by proper Indian statutory authorities.
  • Minimum Marks (UR/OBC/EWS): Secure a minimum of 60% aggregate marks in the qualifying engineering degree.
  • Minimum Marks (SC/ST): A relaxation of 5% is granted, requiring a minimum of 55% marks.
  • Passing Criteria: Candidates must have cleared their final semester examination and have degree credentials in hand as on the specified cut-off date.
  • GATE Qualification: Must have qualified the Graduate Aptitude Test in Engineering (GATE-2025) under Mining paper code MN.

Selection Process

  1. GATE Score Evaluation: Mandatory participation and qualifying status in GATE-2025. Scores prior or subsequent to 2025 are invalid.
  2. Shortlisting: Category-wise shortlisting of applicants in a 1:3 ratio based on pure merit matching the corporate vacancies.
  3. Tie-Breaking Protocol: Higher marks/CGPA in engineering graduation acts as the primary tie-breaker. Seniority in age acts as the secondary tie-breaker.
  4. Document Verification (DV): Checking of official transcripts, certificates, conversion proofs, and original category documentation.
  5. Initial Medical Examination (IME): Undergoing compulsory medical and physical standard fitness tracking by the authorized Company Medical Committee.
  6. Final Empanelment: Release of formal Offer of Appointment on the main website following flawless completion of verification and medical checks.

Exam Pattern

There is no separate written or computer-based test administered by Coal India Limited for this recruitment framework. The complete processing depends on the GATE paper structure.

How to Apply

  1. Open your browser and navigate to the official CIL portal at coalindia.in.
  2. Go to the 'Career with CIL' link and check the 'Jobs at Coal India' page segment.
  3. Open the dynamic registration window for Advertisement No. 01/2026.

  6. Upload crisp, legible copies of all self-attested documents, photo, and signature.
  7. Pay the specified processing application fee through online payment channels (if non-exempted).
