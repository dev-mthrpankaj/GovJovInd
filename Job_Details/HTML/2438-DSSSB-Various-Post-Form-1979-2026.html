Examination Pattern & Syllabus

The selection will be based strictly on a One Tier Written Examination (Computer Based Test) consisting of Multiple Choice Questions (MCQs) for 200 Marks. Duration is 2 Hours.

1. One Tier General Scheme (For Post Codes: 34/26, 39/26, 40/26)

  • Total Questions: 200 Objective MCQs | Total Marks: 200
  • Syllabus Framework (40 Marks Each): General Awareness, General Intelligence & Reasoning Ability, Arithmetical & Numerical Ability, Test of Hindi Language & Comprehension, and Test of English Language & Comprehension.

2. One Tier Technical / Teaching Scheme (For Post Codes: 21/26 to 31/26, 32/26, 33/26, 35/26 to 38/26, 41/26 to 45/26)

SectionSyllabus Topics CoveredMarks
Section A 5 General sections of 20 Marks each: (1) General Awareness, (2) Intelligence & Reasoning, (3) Arithmetical Ability, (4) Hindi Language, (5) English Language. 100 Marks
Section B Subject-concerned domain questions matching the minimum educational criteria prescribed for the specific post code (Includes Teaching Methodology/B.Ed for TGT/Teachers). 100 Marks

Minimum Qualifying Marks: UR/EWS: 40%, OBC (Delhi): 35%, SC/ST/PH (PwBD): 30%. For Technical/Teaching schemas, minimum qualifying criteria applies strictly to domain Section-B and the cumulative totals.

How to Apply Online

  1. 1

    Visit the DSSSB OARS registration portal: https://dsssbonline.nic.in.

  2. 2

    Complete your One-Time Registration (OTR) if you are a new applicant. Log in using your credentials if already registered.

  3. 3

    Navigate to 'Apply Online' for Advertisement No. 03/2026 and choose your desired Post Code.

  4. 4

    Fill in accurate demographic details, qualification details, and category claims.

  5. 5

    Upload a clear, recent color photograph against a white or off-white background alongside scanned signatures.

  6. 6

    Pay the application fee of Rs. 100/- via SBI e-pay if applicable, submit your final application before 15 July 2026, and print the confirmation copy.

Important Instructions

  • Candidates can submit only one application for each individual post code. Duplicate submissions will lead to total cancellation.
  • Deduction of 0.25 marks will be carried out for each incorrect MCQ answer option.
  • The official cutoff date to determine age bounds, experience parameters, and academic eligibility is 15-07-2026.
  • Dress Code mandatory: Light clothes with half-sleeves without large buttons, badges, or brooches. Only slippers or sandals with low heels are allowed; shoes are completely banned inside examination centers.
