Recruitment Overview

The advertisement includes teaching, Junior Scientific Assistant, IT/computer and other departmental posts. Educational qualification, experience, age and pay level vary by post code. The advertised application fee is ₹100 for applicable candidates, with exemptions as specified in the official advertisement.

The selection uses post-specific DSSSB examination schemes. Negative marking of 0.25 mark applies for an incorrect MCQ response. Candidates should use the official advertisement and later exam notice for the syllabus applicable to their post code.

What Candidates Should Do Now

  • Identify your exact post code from the submitted application.
  • Check whether a corrigendum applies to that post.
  • Check the official DSSSB Exam Notices page for the actual exam date and e-admit card.
  • Do not treat an unrelated post-code schedule as your confirmed exam date.
  • Keep application, qualification and category documents ready.
