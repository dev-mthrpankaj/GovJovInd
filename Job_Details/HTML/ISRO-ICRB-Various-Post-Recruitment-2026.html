About This Recruitment

The Indian Space Research Organisation (ISRO) Centralised Recruitment Board (ICRB) regularly recruits skilled administrative personnel to manage operational and clerical responsibilities across various ISRO centers and autonomous institutions across India. Under advertisement ISRO ICRB:01(A-JPA):2026, ISRO invites eligible graduates to apply for Assistant, Junior Personal Assistant (JPA), Upper Division Clerk (UDC), and Stenographer vacancies.

Selected candidates will receive competitive pay packages under Central Government regulations along with various allowances such as HRA, DA, transport allowances, and medical support. Applicants with high academic standing (at least 60% or CGPA 6.32/10) and computer proficiency are strongly encouraged to go through the selection framework and apply online prior to the specified cutoff date.

Eligibility and Qualification

  • Assistant / UDC / Assistant (Autonomous): Bachelor's Degree in any stream with a minimum of 60% marks or CGPA 6.32/10 from a recognized university, with proficiency in the use of computers.
  • Junior Personal Assistant (JPA) / Stenographer: Bachelor's Degree in any stream with a minimum of 60% marks or CGPA 6.32/10 from a recognized university. English Stenography speed of 60 WPM and proficiency in computer operation are mandatory.

Selection Process

  1. Written Test / CBT: Objective-type Computer Based Test testing general knowledge, aptitude, reasoning, and computer fundamentals.
  2. Skill Test / Stenography Test: Qualifying test for JPA and Stenographer posts (Typing and Stenography speed checks).
  3. Document Verification: Authentication of original education, caste, and experience certificates.
  4. Final Merit List: Compiled based on marks secured in the written examination subject to qualifying skill tests.

Exam Pattern

The written examination will consist of multiple-choice questions (MCQs) testing core areas such as General English, General Knowledge, Reasoning, Quantitative Aptitude, and Basic Computer Literacy. Detailed subject breakdowns are provided in the official recruitment syllabus document.

  • Pay level: Level-4 in the Pay Matrix (Basic Pay ₹25,500/- per month plus allowances).
  • Allowances include Dearness Allowance (DA), House Rent Allowance (HRA), Transport Allowance (TA), and medical facilities as per government rules.
  • Contributory Pension Scheme benefits as per Central Government norms.

Documents Required

  • Scanned Recent Passport-size Photograph and Signature.

How to Apply

  1. Visit the official ISRO portal at isro.gov.in.
  2. Navigate to the Careers/Recruitment section and select Advertisement No. ISRO ICRB:01(A-JPA):2026.
  3. Complete the online registration using a valid email ID and mobile number.
  4. Fill in your personal, educational, category, and communication details accurately.
  5. Upload scanned copies of required documents, including your photo and signature.
  6. Pay the required application fee through available online payment methods.
  7. Review the complete form, submit it, and download/print the confirmation slip for future reference.
