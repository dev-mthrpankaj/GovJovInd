About This Recruitment

The Rajasthan Staff Selection Board (RSSB) has announced the Rajasthan RSSB Junior Engineer Recruitment 2026, targeting ambitious candidates seeking a government engineering career. The notification features 874 distinct vacancies allocated to critical state divisions, namely the Public Works Department (PWD), Water Resources Department, and Public Health Engineering Department (PHED).

Online application procedures commence on 16 August 2026 and conclude on 14 September 2026. Aspirants possessing a recognized Degree or Diploma in Civil, Mechanical, or Electrical engineering disciplines are eligible. Successful candidates will be selected primarily through a competitive written examination, ensuring merit-based recruitment to Pay Matrix Level-10 positions.

Candidates must carefully review the official notification for comprehensive details on category-wise vacancy distribution, age relaxation regulations, application fee processing, and the complete selection process.

Eligibility and Qualification

  • Educational Qualification: Candidates must hold a valid Degree (B.E./B.Tech) or Diploma in Civil, Mechanical, or Electrical Engineering from a recognized university or institute.
  • Candidates must meet the respective branch requirements as mapped to the open departmental positions.
  • A working knowledge of Hindi written in Devnagri Script and awareness of Rajasthan culture is typically required.

Selection Process

  1. Written Examination Objective-type exam testing technical knowledge and Rajasthan General Knowledge.
  2. Document Verification Authentication of educational certificates, identity proofs, and category reservation documents.
  3. Final Merit List Preparation of the final list based on marks secured in the written examination and successful verification.

How to Apply

  1. Visit the official portal of Rajasthan Staff Selection Board at rssb.rajasthan.gov.in or go directly to the SSO portal.
  2. Register yourself on the Rajasthan Single Sign-On (SSO) system if you haven't already completed One Time Registration (OTR).
  3. Log in using your valid SSO ID and password.
  4. Navigate to the "Recruitment Portal" and find the "RSSB Junior Engineer Recruitment 2026" link.
  5. Fill in your educational, personal, and categorical details carefully without errors.
  6. Upload scanned copies of required documents including a recent passport-sized photograph and your signature.
  7. Pay the required application fee online through the integrated payment gateway.
  8. Review the form and click on 'Submit'. Ensure you download and print the final confirmation receipt.

Documents Required

  • Scanned recent color photograph and clear signature.
  • Engineering Degree or Diploma certificates and mark sheets.
  • Matriculation (10th) certificate for Date of Birth proof.
  • Caste/Category certificate (if claiming reservation).
  • Rajasthan Domicile Certificate (if applicable).
  • Valid ID Proof (Aadhaar Card, Voter ID, or PAN Card).
