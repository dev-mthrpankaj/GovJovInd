About This Recruitment

The State Bank of India Probationary Officers recruitment is widely regarded as one of the most prestigious career paths in the Indian banking sector. Operating through the Central Recruitment & Promotion Department, Mumbai, SBI conducts this national-level competitive framework to select exceptionally brilliant candidates for Junior Management Grade Scale I roles. This employment structure provides accelerated promotions, substantial perks, continuous professional development, and potential options for international banking assignments in the bank's global branches.

Prospective applicants from any academic discipline are permitted to participate in this drive, provided they fulfill the structural age boundaries and educational qualification limits. Candidates in the final year or semester of their degree programs can also join the process provisionally. Because the recruitment timeline progresses directly from multi-stage computer tests to in-depth personality evaluation metrics, applicants are highly encouraged to carefully review the operational guidelines regarding testing formats, regional examination centers, and credit history policies before filing the web form.

The institutional framework places a strong emphasis on continuous excellence, meritocracy, and rigorous vetting. The evaluation relies on an objective evaluation blueprint combined with corporate-style psychometric testing, group exercises, and dynamic panels. Selected candidates are given comprehensive banking training through digital formats prior to active deployment. They are subject to a two-year performance verification and probation period, requiring steady dedication to secure administrative confirmation inside India's largest commercial banking network.

Eligibility and Qualification

  • Graduation degree in any academic discipline from a recognized University, or an equivalent educational qualification approved by the Central Government.
  • Candidates possessing professional qualifications such as Engineering, Medicine, Chartered Accountant (CA), or Cost Accountant are also fully eligible.
  • Applicants in their Final Year or Final Semester of graduation can apply provisionally, subject to presenting documentary proof of passing the exam on or before 30 September 2026 if selected for the interview phase.
  • Candidates must maintain an admissible credit score status. Individuals with defaults in loan repayments, outstanding credit card dues, or adverse records in CIBIL will face rejection unless the outstanding dues are regularized before joining.

Selection Process

  1. Phase-I: Online Preliminary Examination - Consists of a 100-mark objective assessment. It is used exclusively as a screening portal to filter out the top tier of talent.
  2. Phase-II: Online Main Examination - Comprises a 200-mark objective test divided into structured sections and a 30-mark web descriptive paper assessing communication skills.
  3. Phase-III: Psychometric Test - Character and personality profiling conducted for all main-qualified candidates. Its results are presented directly to the interview panel.
  4. Phase-III: Group Exercises & Personal Interview - Practical verbal interactions and deep assessments accounting for a combined pool of 50 marks.
  5. Document Verification & Formal Background Audit - Checking of physical credentials, caste certifications, credit reports, and police verification reports.
  6. Medical Examination & Final Merit Compilation - Candidates must pass formal fitness guidelines. Final placement is calculated by combining normalized scores from Phase-II and Phase-III.

Exam Pattern

The recruitment process features separate objective and descriptive configurations across successive phases. Sectional time limits are enforced, and there are no sectional cut-offs in the preliminary round.

Salary and Benefits

  • Starting Basic Pay: Rs. 48,480/- (with 4 advance increments built in) on the structural scale of 48480-2000/7-62480-2340/2-67160-2680/7-85920 applicable to Junior Management Grade Scale-I.
  • Perks and Allowances: Eligible for Dearness Allowance (DA), House Rent Allowance (HRA) or Lease Rental Facility, City Compensatory Allowance (CCA), Provident Fund (PF), and Contributory Pension (NPS).
  • Additional Benefits: Entitled to Leave Travel Concession (LFC), Medical Facilities, and institutional benefits as per bank regulations.
  • Initial Cost to Company (CTC): The total approximate CTC at the initial scale of pay at the Mumbai center is estimated at Rs. 21.97 Lacs per annum.

How to Apply

  1. Navigate to the official career webpage of State Bank of India at sbi.bank.in/web/careers/current-openings.
  2. Locate the Probationary Officers Recruitment section under Advertisement No. CRPD/PO/2026-27/09.
  3. Click the registration portal to enter personal, educational, communication, and basic category data.
  4. Note the system-generated provisional registration number and password sent to your registered mobile and email.
  5. Upload the scanned copy of your photo, signature, left-hand thumb impression, and the hand-written declaration text exactly as specified.
  6. Capture a live photograph using your webcam or mobile interface when prompted by the application system.
  7. Thoroughly crosscheck all entered fields using the preview panel before final submission, as corrections are barred post-submission.
  8. Process the online application fee of Rs. 750 (if applicable) using digital transaction modes. Print the e-receipt and application summary for future reference.

Frequently Asked Questions

What is the total number of vacancies for SBI PO Recruitment 2026?

There are a total of 1500 tentative vacancies for Probationary Officers available in this recruitment cycle.

What is the application fee for SBI PO 2026?

The application fee is Rs. 750 for General, EWS, and OBC candidates. Candidates belonging to SC, ST, and PwBD categories are exempted from paying the fee.

What are the online application dates for SBI PO 2026?

The online registration of applications and payment of fees is scheduled from 18 June 2026 to 08 July 2026.

What is the educational qualification required for SBI PO?

Candidates must possess graduation in any discipline from a recognized university. Those in their final year or semester can also apply provisionally, provided they produce proof of passing on or before 30 September 2026.

What is the age limit for SBI PO Recruitment 2026?

Candidates must be between 21 and 30 years of age as on 01 April 2026. Relaxation in the upper age limit is applicable for reserved categories as per rules.

Is there any sectional cut-off in the SBI PO Preliminary examination?

No, the official notification states that there will be no sectional cut-off in the Preliminary Examination.

What is the selection process for SBI Probationary Officers?

The selection follows a three-phase process: Phase I is the Online Preliminary Exam, Phase II is the Online Main Exam (with an objective and descriptive test), and Phase III comprises a Psychometric Test, Group Exercises, and a Personal Interview.

Is there a penalty for wrong answers in the examinations?

Yes, a penalty of 1/4th of the marks assigned to that question is deducted for wrong answers in both the Preliminary and Main objective exams. No penalty is deducted for questions left blank.

What bond is required at the time of joining SBI PO?

Selected candidates must execute a bond of Rs. 2.00 Lacs to serve the bank for a minimum period of three years from the date of joining.

Where can candidates apply online for SBI PO 2026?

Candidates can fill out the application form online through the careers page of the State Bank of India website at sbi.bank.in/web/careers/current-openings.
