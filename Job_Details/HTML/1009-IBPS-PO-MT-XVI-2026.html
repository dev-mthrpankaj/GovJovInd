About This Recruitment

IBPS conducts the Common Recruitment Process for PO/MT posts on behalf of participating public sector banks. The recruitment is meant for candidates who want an officer-level banking career with a structured selection process and provisional allotment through participating banks.

For CRP PO/MT-XVI, the online examination has two phases: Preliminary and Main. Candidates shortlisted from the Main examination have to appear in a Personality Test and then a Common Interview coordinated through the participating banks and nodal bank.

Candidates should read the full IBPS notification carefully because eligibility, category claims, certificates, examination rules and provisional allotment conditions are checked at later stages also.

Eligibility and Qualification

  • Nationality / citizenship must match the categories stated in the IBPS notification.
  • Educational qualification as on 21.07.2026: Degree / Graduation in any discipline from a university recognized by Government of India, or equivalent qualification recognized by the Central Government.
  • Candidate must possess a valid marksheet or degree certificate on the day of registration and should mention graduation percentage while filling the online form.
  • Final result of the qualifying examination should have been declared on or before 21.07.2026.
  • Category, age, disability, EWS, OBC NCL and other certificate requirements should be checked from the official notification.

Selection Process

  1. Online Preliminary Examination
  2. Online Main Examination with objective and descriptive paper
  3. Personality Test, mandatory but non-qualifying as per notification
  4. Common Interview conducted by participating banks and coordinated by the nodal bank
  5. Provisional allotment based on vacancies, merit and applicable reservation rules
