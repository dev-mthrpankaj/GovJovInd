About This Recruitment

The Common Recruitment Process for Probationary Officers and Management Trainees (CRP PO/MT-XVI) is an annual competitive gateway conducted by the Institute of Banking Personnel Selection. It serves as the primary mechanism to shortlist eligible graduates for officer-level management positions across major public sector participating banks in India. Candidates from various academic backgrounds look forward to this notification as it opens doors to a structured banking career with long-term professional growth.

The recruitment process is online-only, requiring candidates to register, fill in preferences for participating banks, and upload necessary scanned credentials. Since the ultimate appointing authority lies within the individual public sector banks based on reported business needs, candidates are encouraged to double-check their application categories and certificate formats. Attending and passing subsequent selection tiers requires a comprehensive understanding of core banking guidelines and competitive subjects.

The evaluation cycle spans across multiple progressive stages, starting with a fast-paced online preliminary screening. Shortlisted candidates then face a multi-hour main examination that evaluates professional proficiency alongside a descriptive essay segment. With final selections being derived through a balanced combination of the main objective scores and coordinated common interviews, thorough preparation spanning multiple facets is essential to secure a spot in the final provisional allotment list.

Eligibility and Qualification

Educational qualifications are evaluated as on the final registration date 21.07.2026.

  • A Degree (Graduation) in any discipline from a university recognized by the Government of India or an equivalent qualification recognized by the Central Government.
  • Candidates must be in possession of a valid mark-sheet or degree certificate stating graduation status on the day of online application registration.
  • Fractional values will be ignored during percentage computations (e.g., 59.99% will be treated as less than 60%).

Selection Process

The recruitment process consists of sequential screening tiers, followed by mandatory personality and interactive checking rounds:

  1. Online Preliminary Examination: Objective qualifying screening test.
  2. Online Main Examination: In-depth evaluation containing objective sub-sections and an automated descriptive proficiency paper.
  3. Personality Test: Mandatory self-report profiling test (non-qualifying in nature).
  4. Common Interview: Evaluated out of 100 marks by participating banks coordinated by a Nodal Bank.
  5. Provisional Allotment: Prepared using a combined merit-cum-preference weighted ratio of 80:20 (Main Exam to Interview).

    1. Navigate to the official website at www.ibps.in.
    2. Click on the main menu button labeled "CRP PO/MT" and select the option for "CLICK HERE TO APPLY ONLINE FOR CRP-PROBATIONARY OFFICERS/MANAGEMENT TRAINEES (CRP-PO/MT-XVI)".
    3. Choose "CLICK HERE FOR NEW REGISTRATION" and submit basic personal contact info to generate a unique Registration Number and Password.

    5. Upload your photograph, captured live photograph webcam/phone copy, signature, thumb impression, declaration text, and 10th mark-sheet.
    6. Review carefully using the "Save and Next" button. Click "Complete Registration" to confirm details permanently.
    7. Complete the online transaction fee via debit/credit cards, internet banking, or UPI channels.
    8. Download and securely retain the printout of your system-generated application form and transaction e-receipt.
