About This Recruitment

The Chhattisgarh Public Service Commission issued a schedule notice for Subedar, Sub Inspector Cadre and Platoon Commander Home Police Department Preliminary Examination 2024. The notice is important for candidates who completed earlier stages such as document verification and physical measurement and are now eligible to appear in the preliminary written examination.

According to the notice, online applications were invited from 23 October 2024 to 21 November 2024 under Advertisement No. 02/2024/Exam dated 21 October 2024. Later, applications were reopened or accepted again from 11 December 2024 to 25 December 2024 through a corrigendum. The notice mentions that 1,37,323 total online applications were received.

The Police Headquarters conducted document verification and physical measurement. Out of the candidates who appeared in that process, 63,342 candidates have been found eligible for the preliminary written examination. Candidates should now focus on admit card download, exam centre planning, exam-day documents and revision based on the official syllabus and earlier recruitment notification.

How to Download Admit Card

  1. Visit the official CGPSC website at psc.cg.gov.in.
  2. Open the admit card or examination section on the homepage.
  3. Find the link for Subedar / Sub Inspector Cadre / Platoon Commander Preliminary Examination 2024 admit card.
  4. Enter the required login details such as registration number, date of birth or other details as asked on the official portal.
  5. Download the admit card and check exam centre, reporting time and instructions carefully.
  6. Take a clear printout and carry it with required ID proof on exam day.
