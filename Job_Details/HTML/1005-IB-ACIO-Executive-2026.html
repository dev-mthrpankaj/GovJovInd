IB ACIO-II/Exe 2025 Process Details

  • Intelligence Bureau invited online applications for 3,717 vacancies of Assistant Central Intelligence Officer Grade-II/Executive.
  • The selection follows a multi-tier format consisting of Tier-1 (Objective), Tier-2 (Descriptive Paper), and Tier-3 (Interview).
  • The descriptive Tier-2 test was conducted on 11 January 2026, and results were declared on 05 March 2026.
  • Candidates shortlisted from Tier-2 are called for the Tier-3 Interview assessment round equivalent to 5 times the vacancies.

    Scheme & Pattern of Examinations

    Examination PhaseType & Contents StructureDurationMaximum Marks
    Tier-I Exam Written Objective Test: 100 MCQs divided into 5 segments (20 questions each on Current Affairs, General Studies, Numerical Aptitude, Reasoning/Logical Aptitude, English). Negative marking of 1/4th mark applies for every wrong response. 1 Hour 100 Marks
    Tier-II Exam Written Descriptive Paper: Essay writing (20 marks); English comprehension layout (10 marks) & Long answer questions based on Current Affairs, Economics, Socio-political themes (2 questions of 10 marks each). 1 Hour 50 Marks
    Tier-III Interview Personality test, psychological evaluation parameters, and structured oral evaluation interface. - 100 Marks

    Shortlisting Tiers: Tier-2 selection is restricted to 10 times the vacancies depending on minimum written cut-offs (UR/EWS: 35, OBC: 34, SC/ST: 33). Sholisting for Tier-3 requires a mandatory minimum of 33% marks (17 out of 50) in Tier-2 descriptives, up to 5 times the available posts.

    Tie Resolution Protocol

    1. 1

      In the event of equal cumulative scores in Tier-1, Tier-2, and Tier-3 combination, candidate with higher marks in Tier-III Interview is placed higher.

    2. 2

      If the tie persists, marks obtained in the descriptive Tier-II examination are evaluated next.

    3. 3

      If the tie is still unresolved, normalized performance scores in the written Tier-I objective exam are verified.

    4. 4

      Older candidates based on Date of Birth are placed higher; if identical, alphabetical order of first name determines merit.
