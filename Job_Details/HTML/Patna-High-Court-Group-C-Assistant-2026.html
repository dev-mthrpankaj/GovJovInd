About This Recruitment

Patna High Court Group C Assistant Recruitment 2026 offers an opportunity for candidates with a Bachelor's Degree and basic computer certification to secure a Group C post in Bihar's judicial administration. The post of Ex-Cadre Assistant operates under Pay Level-4 and carries essential administrative and clerical responsibilities within the High Court system.

The online portal for application registration remains open from 28 July 2026 until 27 August 2026. Applicants must complete their forms in online mode through the official Patna High Court recruitment portal. Candidates should verify all credentials, including academic records, computer certificates, age proofs, and reservation category certificates before submitting their application.

Selection is structured through multiple evaluated stages including a Preliminary Screening Test (if required based on application volume), a Written Examination, a Computer Proficiency Test, and a final Interview. Final merit list preparation relies on performance in the Written Examination and Interview, while keeping eligibility standards strictly aligned with official rules.

Eligibility and Qualification

  • Bachelor's Degree in any discipline from a recognized University / Institution in India as on 01/01/2026.
  • Diploma or Certificate of at least 6 months course in Computer Application from a recognized institution as on 01/01/2026.
  • Preference may be given to candidates possessing Law qualification or work experience in legal and administrative environments as per notification rules.

Selection Process

  1. Preliminary Screening Test (MCQ based) — Conducted if the total number of applicants is high (Shortlisting purpose only).
  2. Written Examination — Comprehensive test covering English, Reasoning, Quantitative Aptitude, General Awareness, and Hindi.
  3. Computer Proficiency Test — Qualifying test checking typing skills and basic computer application knowledge.
  4. Interview — Personality and subject knowledge assessment.
  5. Document Verification — Verification of original certificates and credentials.

How to Apply

  1. Visit the official website of Patna High Court at patnahighcourt.gov.in.
  2. Navigate to the "Recruitments" tab on the homepage menu.
  3. Click on the link for "Ex-Cadre Assistant Recruitment Examination, 2026 (Advt. No. PHC/03/2026)".
  4. Select "Apply Online" and click on "New Registration" to fill in basic details.
  5. Login using the generated Registration ID and Password.
  6. Fill in personal details, educational qualifications, address, and category information accurately.
  7. Upload scanned copies of required photograph, signature, and computer qualification certificates.
  8. Pay the required application fee through available online payment gateways.
  9. Review all entered details carefully before final submission and download a copy of the completed application form for future reference.
