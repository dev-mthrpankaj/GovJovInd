About This Recruitment

This notice is not a fresh vacancy notification. It is an important update for candidates who have already qualified for the Civil Services (Main) Examination, 2026 written part. UPSC has made the Common Application Form update and confirmation facility available on its online portal.

Candidates must login to the UPSC portal and update and/or reconfirm the required details. The notice clearly states that submitting the Application Form is mandatory for qualified candidates.

A candidate who fails to update/reconfirm details and submit the form within the notified window will not be allowed to participate in further stages of the examination. Therefore, candidates should complete the process early and keep a copy or proof of successful submission.

Eligibility and Qualification

  • Candidate must have qualified for Civil Services (Main) Examination, 2026 as per UPSC.
  • Candidate must login to the UPSC online portal and update and/or reconfirm the required details.
  • Candidate must submit the Common Application Form within the window from 19 June 2026 to 29 June 2026 up to 18:00 hours.
  • Failure to submit the Application Form will make the candidate ineligible to participate in further stages of the examination as per the notice.

Selection Process

  1. Qualified candidates should login to the UPSC portal within the notified window.
  2. Update and/or reconfirm all required details in the Common Application Form.
  3. Submit the Application Form successfully before 29 June 2026 up to 18:00 hours.
  4. Candidates who do not submit the form will not be allowed to participate in further stages of the examination.
  5. Further stages will proceed as per UPSC Civil Services Examination rules and official notices.
