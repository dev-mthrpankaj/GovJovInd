About This Recruitment

The Indian Air Force Airmen Medical Assistant Selection Test is a national-level recruitment process designed for male Indian and Gorkha candidates seeking to join the medical wing of the regular force. Selected candidates are positioned within Group 'Y' (Non-Technical) in the specialized Medical Assistant trade. It is important to emphasize that this selection test is explicitly designated for Airmen ranks and is not intended for Commissioned Officers, Pilots, Navigators, or Agniveervayu slots. This regular entry promises a long-term deployment tenure structure.

Enrolment via this entry offers an initial contract period of 20 years, subject to service conditions, with a possible extension option up to the age of 57 years. The recruitment demands strict adherence to dynamic educational requirements (specifically medical or pharmacy backgrounds) along with rigid medical, physical endurance, and geographical adaptability traits. Selected airmen will be trained in nursing, first-aid, medical store management, dispensary maintenance, and ward supervision across varying organizational bases globally.

The selection flow spans three primary phases. Candidates must first clear an objective, bilingual Phase-1 online examination covering English and RAGA. Successful individuals transition to Phase-2 testing at specified Airmen Selection Centres, which incorporates physical fitness assessments, strict documentation cross-checks, and psychological adaptability routines. The cycle concludes with detailed clinical laboratory reviews in Phase-3 prior to compiling the final provisional select merit charts.

Eligibility and Qualification

  • For 10+2 Entry: Must have passed 10+2 / Intermediate / Equivalent Examination with Physics, Chemistry, Biology, and English from boards recognized by Central, State, or UT administrations with at least 50% marks in aggregate and minimum 50% marks in English.
  • For Vocational Entry: Must have passed a two-year Vocational Course containing non-vocational streams of Physics, Chemistry, Biology, and English with minimum 50% aggregate marks and 50% marks in English.
  • For Pharmacy Qualification Entry: Candidates must possess a Diploma, B.Pharma, or B.Sc in Pharmacy with at least 50% marks in aggregate from an institution approved by the Pharmacy Council of India, alongside a valid registration with a State Pharmacy Council or the Pharmacy Council of India (PCI) at the time of enrolment. In addition, their base Intermediate (10+2) layer must also fulfill the 50% aggregate and 50% English criterion.
  • Note on Rounding Off: Fractional marks will not be rounded off to the next higher integer (e.g., 49.99% will be treated as 49%).

Selection Process

The recruitment cycle comprises three sequential clearance phases, requiring candidates to qualify in each component independently:

  1. Phase-I: Objective Online Computer Based Examination conducted at designated centers across India.
  2. Phase-II Verification: Preliminary physical reporting involving rigid initial verification of educational credentials and certificates.
  3. Physical Fitness Test (PFT): Categorized performance tracking including timed runs, push-ups, sit-ups, and squats.
  4. Adaptability Profiling: Written objective modules (Adaptability Test-I & Test-II) evaluating suitability for varied deployment terrain and military lifestyles.
  5. Phase-III Assessment: Detailed medical screening by an Air Force Medical Team comprising specialized biochemical, radiological, and biometric diagnostics.

Salary, Stipend and Benefits

  • Training Period: Candidates receive a fixed monthly stipend of Rs. 14,600/-.
  • Post Training Pay: Successful graduates join at a starting gross pay scale minimum of Rs. 26,900/- per month (includes basic scale plus Military Service Pay - MSP).
  • Allowances: Entitled to Dearness Allowance, Transport Allowance, Composite Personal Maintenance Allowance (CPMA), Leave Ration Allowance (LRA), and House Rent Allowance (HRA) where applicable.
  • Perks: Entitled to free rations, clothing, medical facilities, accommodation, CSD canteen provisions, and annual leave (60 days annual, 30 days casual).
  • Insurance: Comprehensive Group Insurance Cover of Rs. 62.5 Lakhs at a monthly premium of Rs. 3,200/-.

How to Apply

  1. Navigate to the official portal link at afrecruitment.edcil.co.in.
  2. Complete Step-1 Registration by inputting primary personal details to generate separate OTPs for mobile and email verification.
  3. Log in with your new system-generated credentials and fill out your educational histories, profile dimensions, and contact endpoints.
  4. Select 5 choice cities for the Phase-1 online test and 14 Airmen Selection Centre (ASC) location choices in order of preference.
  5. Upload your photograph (with slate), signature, and thumb scans matching the file layout parameters. Complete the real-time webcam face-match verification step.
  6. Review the information in the application preview panel to make any necessary corrections.
  7. Pay the examination fee of Rs. 550/- plus 18% GST through the online payment gateway.
  8. Download the automatically submitted application form and print a copy to bring to the Phase-2 testing center.

Frequently Asked Questions

What is IAF Airmen Medical Assistant Recruitment 2026?

It is a selection test conducted by the Indian Air Force to recruit male Indian and Gorkha citizens as Airmen in the Group Y (Non-Technical) Medical Assistant trade for Airmen Intake 02/2027.

What is the educational qualification required?
