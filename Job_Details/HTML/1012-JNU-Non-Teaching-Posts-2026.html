About This Recruitment

JNU Non-Teaching Recruitment 2026 is a university recruitment drive for administrative, library, engineering, technical, laboratory, hostel, office support and other non-teaching posts. The notification covers Group A, Group B and Group C posts with different pay levels, qualifications, experience requirements and selection stages.

The recruitment is useful for candidates from a wide range of backgrounds. Graduates can check posts such as Junior Assistant, Assistant, Stenographer and administrative roles, while candidates with technical, library science, engineering, hotel management, laboratory, trade or 10th/12th level qualifications can check post-wise eligibility carefully.

Because the notification contains 35 different post names, candidates should not rely on one common qualification for all posts. Read the post-wise eligibility, age, fee and exam scheme before filling the online form. Applicants can apply for more than one post if eligible, but the advertisement states that a separate fee is required for each post.

Selection Process

  1. Online application through the official JNU Samarth recruitment portal.
  2. Written Test / Paper I for all non-teaching posts.
  3. Paper II for posts where domain or post-specific paper is applicable.
  4. Skill Test, Trade Test or Practical Test wherever prescribed by the University.
  5. Interview / Personality Test for all Group A posts.
  6. Document verification and eligibility checking at the required stage.
  7. Final selection as per merit, applicable qualifying marks and JNU rules.

Paper II and marking notes

  • Paper I duration: 2 hours.
  • Paper II, wherever applicable: 1 hour 30 minutes, 50 questions and 100 marks.
  • Question paper will be bilingual except the language test portion.
  • Minimum qualifying marks: 40% for General/EWS and 35% for OBC (NCL), SC, ST and PwD.
  • There is negative marking of one-fourth of marks allocated per question in Paper I and Paper II.
  • Written test weightage is 70% and Interview/Personality Test weightage is 30% wherever applicable.

How to Apply

  1. Visit the official recruitment portal at jnunt.samarth.edu.in.
  2. Open the JNU Non-Teaching Advertisement No. 01/RC(NT)/2026 application link.
  3. Register or log in using the details required by the portal.
  4. Select the post carefully and confirm that you meet the post-wise qualification, experience and age criteria.
  5. Fill personal, educational, experience, category and communication details correctly.
  6. Upload required documents and certificates in the prescribed format.
  7. Pay the application fee for each post separately, if applicable.
  8. Submit the form before 27 July 2026 up to 05:00 PM and save the final application printout.

Frequently Asked Questions

What is JNU Non-Teaching Recruitment 2026?

It is Jawaharlal Nehru University Advertisement No. 01/RC(NT)/2026 for direct recruitment to various non-teaching posts in Group A, Group B and Group C.

How many posts are available in JNU Non-Teaching Recruitment 2026?

The official advertisement lists 267 non-teaching posts across 35 post names.

What is the last date to apply online?

The closing date for online application is 27 July 2026 up to 05:00 PM as stated in the official advertisement.

Where can candidates apply online?

Candidates should apply online through the JNU non-teaching recruitment portal at jnunt.samarth.edu.in.

What is the application fee for Group A posts?

For Group A posts, the fee is Rs. 1500 for UR/EWS/OBC, Rs. 1000 for SC/ST/Women and Nil for PwD candidates.

What is the application fee for Group B and Group C posts?

For Group B and Group C posts, the fee is Rs. 1000 for UR/EWS/OBC, Rs. 600 for SC/ST/Women and Nil for PwD candidates.

What is the age limit for these posts?

Most posts carry a maximum age limit of 40 years, while Executive Engineer Electrical and Civil posts carry a maximum age limit of 45 years. Candidates should verify the post-wise age column in the notification.

What is the selection process?

The selection process includes Paper I, Paper II where applicable, skill or trade or practical test where applicable, and interview/personality test for Group A posts.

Is there negative marking in the exam?

Yes. The advertisement states negative marking of one-fourth of the marks allocated per question in Paper I and Paper II.

Will there be interview for Group B and Group C posts?

The advertisement states that no interview shall be held for appointment to Group B and Group C posts.
