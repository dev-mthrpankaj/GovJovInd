About This Recruitment

BEML Limited is a Government of India undertaking under the Ministry of Defence and works in core sectors such as Defence & Aerospace, Rail & Metro, Mining and Construction. The recruitment is for non-executive operator roles on tenure basis.

This opportunity is useful for ITI candidates who have completed the required trade training along with National Trade Certificate and National Apprenticeship Certificate. Since the selection is based on a computer based written test followed by document verification and medical examination, candidates should keep both preparation and documents ready.

The post is tenure based and does not automatically create a regular employment claim. Candidates should read the tenure, posting and medical conditions carefully before submitting the form.

Eligibility and Qualification

  • Only Indian nationals may apply.
  • Age, qualification and experience are counted as on 10 July 2026.
  • Candidates should have completed ITI with first-class 60% marks in the respective trade under conventional Craftsmen Training Scheme.
  • National Trade Certificate (NTC) issued by NCVT is required.
  • National Apprenticeship Certificate (NAC) under the respective trade issued by DGT is required.
  • Minimum percentage is relaxable by 5% for SC, ST and PwD candidates.

Selection Process

  1. Computer Based Written Test.
  2. Document Verification in order of merit and as per notified discipline/category vacancies.
  3. Pre-employment Medical Examination as per BEML health standards.
  4. Final offer of engagement after satisfactory document and medical verification.

Salary and Benefits

  • Selected candidates will be paid Basic Pay of Rs. 16,900 per month.
  • Dearness Allowance will be paid at applicable IDA rates.
  • HRA will be applicable at the place of posting if company quarters are not provided.
  • Perks will be applicable as per company rules.
  • Monthly medical expenses component is Rs. 1,500.
  • Annual increase of 3% on Basic Pay is admissible during tenure subject to satisfactory performance.
  • Other benefits include PF, gratuity and bonus as per applicable rules.

Documents Required

  • Recent photograph.
  • Signature on clear white background in black ink.
  • Caste / category certificate, if applicable.
  • PwD certificate, if applicable.
  • 10th standard marks card.
  • ITI / National Trade Certificate from NCVT.
  • ITI marksheets as a single PDF.
  • National Apprenticeship Certificate issued by DGT.
  • Identity card issued by Government of India such as Aadhaar, Passport, Driving Licence or PAN.
  • Relevant experience certificates as a single PDF, if applicable.

How to Apply

  1. Visit the official BEML website and open the career page.
  2. Read the advertisement KP/S/10/2026 carefully before starting the online form.
  3. Fill the online application form with correct personal, trade, category and contact details.
  4. Upload all required documents in the prescribed format.
  5. Pay Rs. 200 fee online if applicable.
  6. Note the registration number generated after submission.
  7. Save and print the submitted application for future correspondence.

Why Apply For This Recruitment

This recruitment is suitable for ITI trade candidates who want a PSU work opportunity in core manufacturing and technical operations. The post offers structured pay components, tenure-based industry exposure and a selection process focused mainly on trade knowledge and written-test performance.
