About This Recruitment

The Institute of Banking Personnel Selection conducts the Common Recruitment Process (CRP PO/MT-XVI) annually to select Officers in Junior Management Grade Scale-I (JMGS-I) for 11 participating public sector banks across India. Selected candidates undergo a two-year probation period as Probationary Officers or Management Trainees, after which they are integrated into administrative, operational, credit management, and customer banking leadership roles. It offers one of the most competitive career pathways for fresh graduates seeking employment in public sector banking.

Candidates must apply exclusively through the IBPS online registration portal. The online application process involves submitting educational marks, choosing bank preferences, uploading required identity documents, photograph, signature, and paying the designated application fee online. Applicants should verify that all personal details and bank preferences entered during registration are accurate before submitting the application.

Selection is conducted in multiple structured phases, beginning with the Online Preliminary Examination, followed by the Main Examination, a Personality Test stage, and the Common Interview. Successful candidates meeting the composite cutoff marks are provisionally allotted to participating public sector banks based on merit-cum-preference ranking and available category-wise vacancies.

Eligibility and Qualification

  • Educational Qualification: Bachelor Degree in any discipline from a University recognized by the Government of India or an equivalent qualification recognized by the Central Government.
  • Marks & Result Date: Candidates must possess valid marksheets and degree certificates proving graduation completion as per the official cutoff date.
  • Computer Literacy: Working knowledge of computer systems (certificate/diploma/degree or studied computer/IT as a subject in High School/College) is desirable for operational readiness.

Selection Process

  1. Preliminary Examination: Online objective screening test consisting of English Language, Quantitative Aptitude, and Reasoning Ability.
  2. Main Examination: Objective test covering Reasoning, General/Banking Awareness, English, Data Analysis & Interpretation, followed by a Descriptive Paper (Essay & Letter Writing).
  3. Personality Test: Mandatory screening stage conducted prior to the interview phase.
  4. Common Interview: Conducted by participating banks and coordinated by IBPS (100 Marks).
  5. Document Verification & Provisional Allotment: Verification of original certificates and merit-based allocation to participating banks.

Exam Pattern

The examination is conducted in online mode. Negative marking of 0.25 marks is applicable for each incorrect answer in objective tests.

How to Apply

  1. Visit the official portal of IBPS at ibps.in and click on "CRP PO/MT-XVI".
  2. Select the link "Click here to apply online for CRP PO/MT-XVI".
  3. Click on "Click here for New Registration" and register using a valid Email ID and Mobile Number.
  4. Note down the generated Provisional Registration Number and Password sent via SMS/Email.
  5. Log in and carefully fill in basic personal, educational qualification, and category details.
  6. Select participating public sector bank preferences in order of choice.
  7. Upload scanned copies of photograph, signature, left thumb impression, and handwritten declaration.
  8. Pay the required application fee online using Debit Card, Credit Card, Net Banking, or UPI.
  9. Preview the full application form, verify every entered field, and click on final submission.
  10. Download and print the completed application form and fee receipt for future recruitment stages.
