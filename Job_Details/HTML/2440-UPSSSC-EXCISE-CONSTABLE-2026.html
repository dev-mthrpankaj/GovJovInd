Selection Process

  1. 1

    Shortlisting Phase: Shortlisting of candidates for the Main Written Examination based on their UPSSSC PET 2025 normalized marks layout metrics.

  2. 2

    Written Main Exam: Verification through a 100-mark objective test matching the detailed blueprint parameters.

  3. 3

    Physical Evaluation: Shortlisted qualifiers undergo mandatory Physical Measurement Tests (PMT) and Running Endurance Modules (PET).

  4. 4

    Final Merit List: Preparation of the final select list based solely on the scores achieved in the written examination, subject to passing physical benchmarks and document review.

Written Exam Pattern & Detailed Syllabus

The main examination consists of a single paper with 100 objective-type, multiple-choice questions to be completed within 2 hours (120 minutes). Every correct question rewards 1 mark, whereas a penalty of 25% (1/4 mark) applies for wrong answers.

Syllabus Highlights

  • Part-1 Highlights: Comprehensive facts on Indian freedom struggles, urban planning, population distributions, sustainable goals, Panchayati Raj local self-government layout, daily science applications, Disaster Management Act 2005 rules, statistical graphs data extraction, and the United Provinces Excise Act 1910 framework containing current UP excise policies.
  • Part-2 Highlights: Computer basics, history of WWW, operational commands for peripherals, MS Word and MS Excel navigation systems, cyber security protocols, artificial intelligence, big data processing workflows, and machine learning milestones.
  • Part-3 Highlights: Dedicated focus on Uttar Pradesh history, architecture, folk performing arts, geography, mineral assets, wildlife reserves, state specific administrative modules, and recent achievements.

How to Apply Online

  1. 1

    Visit the official online web portal of the Commission: https://upsssc.gov.in.

  2. 2

    Navigate to the "Live Advertisement" segment panel on the homepage layout and select the link corresponding to Advertisement No. 08-Exam/2026.

  3. 3

    Authenticate your profile login using your valid UPSSSC PET 2025 Registration Number through either personal details verification or the registered Mobile OTP path.

  4. 4

    Verify your pre-filled details displayed from the PET master records and input required parameters regarding Class 12 certificates.

  5. 5

    Proceed past the static photo and signature lock page, complete the checkbox declaration block, and pay the online processing fee of Rs. 25/- via credit/debit card, UPI, or Net Banking options.

  6. 6

    Submit your form online before the target closing date of 24-06-2026. Retain a clean printed copy of your final registration page layout.

Important Instructions

  • Only Indian Nationals meeting the detailed residency declarations can file submissions under local vertical or horizontal reservation brackets.
  • Submitting multiple forms will invalidate older applications. The Commission preserves only the final completed system transaction record layout.
  • Certificates detailing vertical or horizontal exemptions must be active and dated prior to the terminal portal shutdown date (24-06-2026).
  • In accordance with rules, female candidates seeking caste preferences must supply caste status documents generated explicitly by their father's side layout records.
  • Candidates currently serving state or federal government departments must obtain a valid No Objection Certificate (NOC) to submit upon demand.
  • Provisions of the Uttar Pradesh Public Examination (Prevention of Unfair Means) Act 2024 are strictly implemented for this selection.
