About This Recruitment

CERSAI is a Government of India undertaking that operates important registry systems related to security interest, Central KYC records and other statutory functions. The recruitment notice is for regular employees who can support operations, IT, legal, HR, risk, compliance, finance and administration functions.

The vacancy is more suitable for experienced professionals because almost every post requires post-qualification experience. Candidates should carefully match the post code, category, educational qualification, certification and experience requirement before applying.

The recruitment process includes an online examination and interview for most posts. The notice also contains a special selection note for the Assistant Manager (PA to MD) post, where written test, skill test and personal interview are mentioned.

Eligibility and Qualification

  • Graduation with minimum 50% marks is required for several operations, SI operations, CKYC/CKYCR operations, accounts/admin and PA roles, along with relevant post-qualification experience.
  • IT posts require BCA/MCA/B.Tech or equivalent in Computer Science, IT, Electronics or related fields with minimum marks and relevant IT experience.
  • AGM (Legal) requires LLB/Bachelor of Law with minimum 50% marks; LL.M. is mentioned as desirable.
  • HR post requires MBA/PGDM in HR Management from a recognized university and AICTE-approved or equivalent degree with minimum 50% marks.
  • Risk and Compliance post requires graduation in relevant fields with MBA/PGDM Finance, M.Tech, CA, CMA, CS or equivalent listed qualifications.
  • Candidates must read the post-wise table in the official notification because experience requirement ranges from 3 years to 13 years depending on the post.

Selection Process

  1. Submit online application through the CERSAI website within the notified dates.
  2. Appear in the online examination having multiple choice questions with negative marking, wherever applicable.
  3. Candidates up to six times the number of vacancies may be called for interview based on written test performance and post-wise cut-off.
  4. Interview carries 100 marks and is scheduled at New Delhi as per the notice.
  5. For Assistant Manager (PA to MD), the notice mentions a written test qualifying in nature, followed by skill test and personal interview.
  6. Final selection is subject to eligibility verification, documents, medical examination and other conditions in the notification.

How to Apply

  1. Visit the official CERSAI website at cersai.org.in/CERSAI/home.prg.
  2. Open the Notification section and use the online application link available from 21 June 2026.
  3. Register using name, contact details and email ID to generate provisional registration number and password.
  4. Fill the application carefully and use Save and Next to verify details before final submission.
  5. Upload photograph, signature, left thumb impression and handwritten declaration in the required format.
  6. Pay the application fee online through the payment gateway.
  7. Submit the application before 20 July 2026 and print the application form containing fee details.
  8. Keep registered email ID and mobile number active till completion of the recruitment process.

Frequently Asked Questions

What is CERSAI Recruitment 2026?

It is Advt. No. 01/2026 for recruitment of 16 regular employee posts in CERSAI.

What is the last date to apply for CERSAI recruitment?

The last date for online registration and application submission is 20 July 2026.

How many vacancies are available?

The notification lists 16 vacancies across DGM, AGM, Chief Manager, Senior Manager, Manager and Assistant Manager posts.

What is the CERSAI application fee?

The fee is Rs. 1000 for general candidates and Rs. 500 for SC, ST and PwBD candidates, with refund conditions for SC/ST/PwBD participation as per notice.

What is the selection process?

The main selection process includes online examination and interview. Assistant Manager (PA to MD) has a qualifying written test, skill test and personal interview as per notice.

What is the exam pattern?

The online test has 125 questions for 150 marks with sections including Numerical Ability, Aptitude, General Knowledge, Professional Knowledge and English qualifying.

What is the posting place?

The notice states that at present CERSAI has only one office in New Delhi and candidates will be posted at the Head Office/Registered Office at New Delhi, subject to transfer conditions.

Where can candidates apply online?

Candidates should apply online through the official CERSAI website at cersai.org.in/CERSAI/home.prg.
