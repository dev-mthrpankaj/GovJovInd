Syllabus Overview

Mathematics

Algebra, Matrices and Determinants, Trigonometry, Analytical Geometry, Differential Calculus, Integral Calculus, Differential Equations, Vector Algebra, Statistics and Probability.

English

Grammar and usage, vocabulary, comprehension and cohesion in extended text to test proficiency in English.

General Knowledge

Physics, Chemistry, General Science, History, Freedom Movement, Geography and Current Events.

Selection Process

  1. 1

    Online application through UPSC Online portal using URN, Common Application Form and Examination Specific Module.

  2. 2

    Written examination consisting of objective type Mathematics and General Ability Test papers.

  3. 3

    Qualified candidates will appear before the Services Selection Board for intelligence and personality test.

  4. 4

    Air Force choice candidates may have to undergo Computerised Pilot Selection System (CPSS) as per rules.

  5. 5

    Final selection will be based on merit, preference, medical fitness, eligibility verification and number of vacancies available.

Examination Centres

UPSC has notified examination centres across India including Agra, Aligarh, Bareilly, Delhi, Ghaziabad, Gorakhpur, Kanpur, Lucknow, Meerut, Prayagraj, Varanasi and many other cities.

Centre Allotment Rule

Centres are allotted on a first-apply-first-allot basis, except Chennai, Dispur, Kolkata and Nagpur where the ceiling rule mentioned in the notice applies differently. Candidates should apply early to get a preferred centre.

Documents & Application Instructions

  • Candidate should keep details of one Photo ID such as Aadhaar Card, Voter ID, PAN Card, Passport, Driving Licence, School Photo ID or other government-issued photo ID.
  • Applicants are strongly advised to use Aadhaar for smooth verification and authentication.
  • Live photograph capture is required while filling the Common Application Form.
  • Signature must be uploaded as per UPSC instructions: three signatures one below another on plain white paper using black ink.
  • No correction / alteration / modification is allowed in the application after submission.

Important Instructions

  • Use black ball pen only for writing and marking answers in the OMR sheet.
  • Mobile phones, smart watches, Bluetooth devices, storage media and similar electronic items are banned in the examination hall.
  • Entry into the examination venue will close 30 minutes before commencement of each session.
  • Face authentication / identity verification and frisking will be mandatory at the examination venue.
  • Question paper / provisional answer key representations can be submitted only through UPSC online representation window within the notified time frame.
