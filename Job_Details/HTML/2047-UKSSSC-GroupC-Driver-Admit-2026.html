Selection Process Framework

  1. 1

    Stage-1 Written Examination: Competitive objective-type screening test comprising relevant subject marks to filter valid candidates.

  2. 2

    Stage-2 Practical Driving Test: Mandatory skill examination administered to gauge technical vehicle operation control and road compliance knowledge.

  3. 3

    Document Verification: Verification of original academic certificates, identity documents, category certifications, and active driving licenses.

Written Examination Pattern

  • Mode of Test: OMR-based offline test or Computer Based Test (CBT) layout mode.
  • Total Marks Allocation: 25 Multiple Choice Questions (MCQs) for a max value of 25 Marks.
  • Test Time Allotment: 1 Hour (60 minutes total duration).
  • Subject Composition: Vehicle Driving principles, Traffic signs/rules, basic Motor Vehicle Act rules, Health/Hygiene impacts (alcohol/tobacco avoidance knowledge), and General Knowledge segments concerning Uttarakhand state.
  • Negative Marking: There is a penalty of 1/4th (0.25) mark deducted for every incorrect objective response recorded.
  • Minimum Qualification Criteria: 45% minimum score required for General/OBC classes, and 35% required for SC/ST classified candidates.

  3. 3

  4. 4

    Provide the accurate values along with the required security Captcha code, then click the submit form action button.

  5. 5

    Verify all personal registration details on the displayed PDF file before sending it to a connected printer on clean A4 sheets.

Vital Exam Instructions

  • Candidates must produce a clear hardcopy printout of the UKSSSC admit card document at the designated test center gates.
  • Bringing a valid original Government photo identification card (such as an Aadhaar Card, Driving License, Voter Card, or PAN Card) along with a photocopy is mandatory.
  • Candidates should carry two recent color passport-sized photographs to the examination center to avoid documentation challenges.
  • Electronic equipment, gadgets, smartphones, smartwatches, calculators, bags, and written materials are strictly banned inside the exam hall bounds.
  • Arrive well ahead of the reporting time printed on the admit card; entry will be prohibited after the gate closure flag trigger.
