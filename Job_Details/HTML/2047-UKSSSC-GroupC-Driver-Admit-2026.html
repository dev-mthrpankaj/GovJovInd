Selection Process

  1. 1

    Written Examination: Candidates must appear for an objective-type written test carrying 25 marks.

  2. 2

    Shortlisting for Driving Test: Candidates qualifying the written exam will be shortlisted for the driving test in a 1:6 ratio (6 times the number of vacancies).

  3. 3

    Driving Test / Practical Assessment: A practical test evaluating vehicle operation and driving skills carrying 75 marks.

  4. 4

    Final Merit Compilation: Merits will be drafted on total marks aggregated out of 100 (Written Exam + Driving Test). Minimum passing rules apply.

  5. 5

    Document Verification: Review of original certificates, driving history logs, license validity, and category credentials.

Exam Pattern & Syllabus

  • Mode of Examination: OMR-based offline test or Computer Based Test (CBT).
  • Total Marks: 25 Marks (25 Questions, Multiple Choice Objective Type).
  • Duration: 30 Minutes (Exam Timing: 11:00 AM to 11:30 AM).
  • Negative Marking: Deduction of 1/4th (0.25) mark for every incorrect answer.
  • Minimum Qualifying Score: 45% for General/OBC candidates, and 35% for SC/ST candidates.
  • Syllabus Structure: Unit I covers Requisite Traffic Rules, Regulations, and Vehicle Maintenance Safety Laws (15 Marks). Unit II covers General Knowledge and Current Affairs (10 Marks).

How to Download UKSSSC Driver Admit Card

  1. 1

    Go to the official UKSSSC online portal at https://sssc.uk.gov.in.

  2. 2

    Click on the designated link labeled "Admit Card for Written Competitive Exam of Vehicle Driver / Enforcement Driver".

  3. 3

    Choose your convenient login method: (a) Registered Email ID & Password, (b) Application Number & Date of Birth, or (c) Candidate Name, Father's Name & Date of Birth details.

  4. 4

    Input the security CAPTCHA code correctly and click on 'Submit'.

  5. 5

    Your UKSSSC Driver Hall Ticket will appear on screen. Review your details, exam center address, and download or print on an A4 sheet.

Important Exam Instructions

  • Candidates must bring a clear printed hard copy of the UKSSSC Driver Admit Card along with a valid original Photo ID proof (Aadhaar Card, Driving License, PAN Card, Voter ID) and its photocopy.
  • Carry two recent colored passport-size photographs matching the one uploaded in the application form.
  • Electronic items, calculators, mobile phones, smartwatches, books, notebooks, or cheating materials are strictly prohibited inside the exam premises.
  • Arrive at the designated examination venue at least 45 to 60 minutes before the scheduled exam commencement time to complete biometric validation and verification securely.
  • Any misbehavior with invigilating staff or attempts at tampering with the admit card information will result in instant disqualification and legal debarment.
