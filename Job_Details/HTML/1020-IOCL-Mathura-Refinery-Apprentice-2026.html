About This Recruitment

Indian Oil Corporation Limited has invited applications for apprenticeship training at Mathura Refinery under the Apprentices Act. The engagement is meant for eligible candidates from specified educational backgrounds such as B.Sc., ITI, diploma, graduation, B.Com and Class XII, depending on the trade or discipline chosen.

The notification covers trade apprentices and technician apprentices, including Chemical, Mechanical, Electrical, Instrumentation, Secretarial Assistant, Accountant and Data Entry Operator categories. Candidates should carefully match their qualification with the correct code because only one trade or discipline can be applied for.

This is an apprenticeship opportunity and not a direct permanent job. The official notice states that IOCL has no obligation to offer employment after completion of the apprenticeship period, so candidates should treat it as skill training and industry exposure.

Eligibility and Qualification

  • Candidates must already possess the prescribed qualification as on 30 June 2026.
  • Class XII, graduation and diploma qualifications should be from a recognized board, university or institute as regular full-time courses.
  • For Class XII / Graduate / Diploma holders, minimum 50% aggregate marks are required; 45% applies for SC/ST and PwBD candidates against reserved seats.
  • For ITI in the relevant trade, pass marks are required and the ITI course must be regular full-time and recognized by NCVET/SCVT.
  • Part-time, correspondence and distance education qualifications are not eligible.
  • Candidates with higher professional qualifications such as BE/B.Tech, MBA, CA, LLB, MCA or equivalent are not eligible as per the notification.
  • Candidates who completed the prescribed qualification more than five years before the eligibility date are not eligible.
  • A candidate can apply for only one trade or discipline.

Selection Process

  1. Candidate completes registration/application on the relevant NAPS or NATS portal and submits mandatory additional details through Microsoft Forms.
  2. There will be no written test.
  3. Merit will be prepared in descending order of percentage of marks in the prescribed qualification for the applied trade.
  4. Preference will be given to candidates applying for apprenticeship training for the first time.
  5. In case of a tie, senior age will be considered first; if date of birth is same, higher percentage in Matriculation will be used.
  6. Shortlisted candidates will be called for document verification.
  7. Final engagement is subject to documents and pre-engagement medical fitness being found in order.

Exam Pattern

The official notification clearly states that there shall be no written test for this apprentice engagement. Selection is merit-based on marks in the prescribed qualification, followed by document verification and medical fitness.

Physical Standards / Medical Fitness

Selected apprentices must meet the minimum physical fitness standards prescribed under the Apprentices Act and IOCL guidelines. Only candidates declared fit in the pre-engagement medical examination will be considered for engagement.

Salary and Benefits

  • Monthly stipend will be paid as prescribed under the Apprentices Act, Apprentices Rules and IOCL guidelines.
  • Apprentices selected against technical plant-related codes may be provided safety shoes and helmet wherever applicable.
  • No HRA or company accommodation will be provided to apprentices.
  • Apprentices will be covered under suitable accident insurance coverage as per the notification.

How to Apply

  1. Read the complete IOCL Mathura Refinery apprentice notification and confirm the correct code for your qualification.
  2. For Codes 101, 102, 103, 108, 109, 110 and 111, register on the NAPS portal and apply for the relevant opportunity.
  3. For Codes 104, 105, 106 and 107, register on the NATS portal and apply to the IOCL Mathura Refinery advertisement from the dashboard.
  4. Use the same active email ID and mobile number in the portal and Microsoft Forms.
  5. After Step I, submit additional information through the Microsoft Forms link provided through IOCL website under the Mathura Refinery apprentice notice.
  6. Make sure both Step I and Step II are completed before the last date, otherwise the application may not be considered.
  7. Keep the portal printout, submitted form details and documents ready for document verification.

Why Apply For This Recruitment

This apprenticeship can be useful for candidates who want practical exposure in a large refinery environment and want to build industry-level skills in technical or office-support disciplines. Since there is no written test, the opportunity is especially relevant for candidates with strong marks in their prescribed qualification and complete documents. Candidates should also remember that apprenticeship is training-oriented and does not guarantee a permanent job.
