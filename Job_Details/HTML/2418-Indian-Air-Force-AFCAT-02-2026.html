Recruitment Snapshot

AFCAT 02/2026 Details

  • Indian Air Force invites Indian citizens, Men and Women, to join as Group ‘A’ Gazetted Officers in Flying and Ground Duty branches.
  • The course will commence in July 2027 and the AFCAT online examination will be conducted on 08 August 2026.
  • Fixed tentative vacancies mentioned for AFCAT Entry and GATE Score Entry are 379; NCC Special Entry seats are as per the percentage rule notified by IAF.

Syllabus Overview

English

Comprehension, error detection, sentence completion, synonyms, antonyms, cloze test, idioms and phrases, analogy, rearrangement, substitution and homonyms.

General Awareness

History, Geography, Sports, National and International Organisations, Art and Culture, Personalities, Environment, Polity, Economy, Science and Defence.

Numerical Ability

Fractions, time and work, average, percentage, profit and loss, ratio, interest, time and distance, probability, number system, mensuration and statistics.

Reasoning & MAT

Verbal and non-verbal reasoning along with Military Aptitude Test topics as per AFCAT standard.

Numerical Ability standard is Matriculation level, while other subjects are of Graduation level.

Selection Process

  1. 1

    Online registration and application submission through AFCAT portal.

  2. 2

    AFCAT online examination for AFCAT Entry candidates.

  3. 3

    NCC Special Entry and GATE Score Entry candidates are exempted from AFCAT written exam and may be directly shortlisted for AFSB testing.

  4. 4

    AFSB testing / SSB process and branch-specific assessment as per IAF rules.

  5. 5

    Medical examination, document verification, merit, suitability and availability of vacancies.

Important Instructions

  • Application details cannot be edited after final submission / payment stage. Candidates should check every field before submitting.
  • If multiple applications are submitted, only the latest application against a particular Aadhaar number will be considered for admit card.
  • Application once submitted cannot be withdrawn and fee will not be refunded.
  • Admit Card can be downloaded from 04 August 2026, 11:00 Hr onwards from the AFCAT website.
  • Candidate must carry e-Admit Card printout, original Aadhaar Card, second original photo ID, two passport size photos and blue/black ballpoint pen to the exam centre.
  • Electronic devices, calculators, watches, mobile phones, Bluetooth devices, books, bags and personal valuable items are not permitted inside the examination hall.
