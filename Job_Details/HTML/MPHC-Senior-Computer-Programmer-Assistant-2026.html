About This Recruitment

The Madhya Pradesh High Court (MPHC) vacancy presents a specialized career opportunity for computer science and IT graduates who want to build a career in judicial administration. The role of a Senior Computer Programmer Assistant centers around managing system configurations, managing database architectures, and deploying custom open-source applications within court networks. This opportunity bridges advanced technical system execution with institutional court administrative flows.

Since the application processing requires submitting a physical application form, candidates must carefully map out their documentation early. Ensuring all educational marksheets, work experience validations from court establishments, and structural details match the system requirements will minimize processing friction. Late applications or incomplete documents will not be processed by the High Court selection cell.

Eligibility and Qualification

  • Possession of a B.E. / B.Tech. / B.Sc. in Computer Science / Information Technology / Electronics and Telecommunications or B.C.A. / M.C.A. / M.Sc. (Electronics / C.S. / I.T.).
  • At least 1 (one) year of professional experience working with Linux, Open Source Software, or Windows operating environments.
  • A minimum of 2 years of active work experience within a District Court Establishment or High Court structure.

Selection Process

  1. Preliminary Screening Examination consisting of objective Multiple Choice Questions (MCQ).
  2. Practical Skills Test targeting systems design, computing, and OS deployment frameworks.
  3. Personal Interview evaluations with the High Court panel.
  4. Verification of Past Performance and continuous service records.

Exam Pattern

The comprehensive syllabus details, distribution weights for questions, duration rules, and testing formats will be fully accessible within the explicit exam scheme sections of the official notification. Candidates should track updates on the MPHC portal for direct pattern notifications.

Salary and Benefits

  • Pay Scale Architecture: Level pay band of Rs. 9300 - 34800 along with Grade Pay of Rs. 3600/- (under the 6th Pay Commission guidelines).
  • Revised Matrix Compensation: Structured salary span between Rs. 36200 - 114800/-.
  • Added Perquisites: Standard allowances, healthcare assistance parameters, and allowances as dictated by the High Court of Madhya Pradesh rules.

Documents Required for Application

  • Duly completed physical offline application printout following the prescribed format.
  • High School Certificate (10th standard) acting as age proof verification.
  • Degree Certificates and detailed marksheets for B.E. / B.Tech / B.Sc / BCA / MCA.
  • Valid experience certificates proving 1 year of Linux/Windows systems work.
  • Official service certificates proving a minimum of 2 years of work experience inside a Court Establishment.
  • Category/Caste certificates issued by authorized departments, if claiming relaxations.
  • Recent passport-sized photographs matching alignment mandates.

How to Apply

  1. Navigate directly to the official recruitment portal of the MP High Court at portal.mphc.gov.in/recruitment-result.
  2. Locate the notification title: **Adv No.61/SCPA/Exam/2026** and download the application form format.
  3. Manually fill out the details on the hard copy form, including your professional experience and category data.
  4. Attach self-attested document photocopies for your educational qualifications and court service certificates.
  5. Double-check that the application lists your work experience accurately to avoid automatic rejection.
  6. Place the form inside an envelope clearly marked with the post title, and mail it to the designated MP High Court address so it arrives before **10 August 2026**.
