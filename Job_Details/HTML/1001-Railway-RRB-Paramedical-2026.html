Age Relaxation Thresholds

The standard reference age parameters are calculated strictly as on the baseline parameter date of 01-01-2026. Permitted category relaxations include:

  • SC & ST Candidates: Max extension up to 5 additional years.
  • OBC (Non-Creamy Layer) Candidates: Max extension up to 3 additional years.
  • PwBD Categories: PwBD-UR/EWS: 10 Years, PwBD-OBC: 13 Years, PwBD-SC/ST: 15 Years relaxation.
  • Ex-Servicemen (ExSM): Service duration deductions plus 3 years for UR/EWS, 6 years for OBC, and 8 years for SC/ST.

    1. 1

      Single Stage Computer Based Test (CBT): Objective assessment reviewing technical proficiency and primary logical sections with 1/3 negative marks active.

      Railway Medical Examination (ME): Passing fitness evaluations matching assigned requirements (such as C1, B1, or C2 markers) inside specified Railway hospitals.
