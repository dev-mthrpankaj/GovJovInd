Eligibility Criteria & Educational Qualifications

Candidates must possess the relevant professional qualification from recognized boards or universities to be eligible for selection:

  • Nursing Superintendent:

    Certificate as Registered Nurse and Midwife having passed 3 years course in General Nursing and Midwifery (GNM) from a school of Nursing or other institution recognized by the Indian Nursing Council OR B.Sc in Nursing.

  • Pharmacist (Entry Grade):

    10+2 in Science stream or its equivalent, along with a Diploma in Pharmacy from a recognized institution and registration as a Pharmacist under the Pharmacy Act, 1948 OR Bachelor degree in Pharmacy (B.Pharm).

  • Health & Malaria Inspector Grade III:

    B.Sc. degree with Chemistry as a Main or Optional subject from any recognized University, plus a 1-year Diploma in Health / Sanitary Inspector OR National Trade Certificate (NTC) in Health Sanitary Inspector.

  • Laboratory Assistant Grade II:

    10+2 with Science stream plus a Diploma or Certificate in Medical Laboratory Technology (DMLT) from a recognized institute.

  • Dialysis Technician:

    B.Sc. plus a Diploma in Haemodialysis OR 2 years satisfactory in-house training / work experience in Haemodialysis work from a reputed institution.

  • Radiographer X-Ray Technician:

    10+2 with Physics and Chemistry plus a Diploma in Radiography / X-Ray Technician / Radio-diagnosis Technology (2 years course) from a recognized board or institute.

  • ECG Technician:

    10+2 / Graduation degree in Science along with a Certificate / Diploma / Degree in ECG Laboratory Technology or Cardiology Technology.

Selection Process & Computer Based Test (CBT) Pattern

The recruitment methodology comprises a streamlined selection process containing a single objective assessment exam followed directly by physical document verification:

Sl. No. Syllabus Component Section Number of Questions Marks Allotted
1 Professional Ability (Core technical subjects related to the post) 70 Questions 70 Marks
2 General Awareness (Current Affairs, Indian History, Geography) 10 Questions 10 Marks
3 General Arithmetic, General Intelligence, and Reasoning 10 Questions 10 Marks
4 General Science (Physics, Chemistry & Life Sciences) 10 Questions 10 Marks
- Grand Cumulative Total 100 Questions 100 Marks
  • Exam Duration: A maximum window of 90 minutes is provided to complete the test (120 minutes for eligible PwBD scribe candidates).
  • Negative Marking: There is a strict penalty of 1/3rd (0.33) mark deducted for every incorrect attempt.
  • Normalisation: Scores are normalized across shifts to equalize variations in difficulty index.

Crucial Post-Result Instructions

Candidates shortlisted in the computer-based evaluation must strictly comply with the following instructions to prevent disqualification:

  • Shortlisted candidates are required to upload self-attested scanned original color copies of their educational and reservation certificates on the RRB DV portal prior to attending the offline check.
  • Candidates must bring 2 complete sets of self-attested photocopies (A4 size dimensions) along with all original academic certificates, category certificates, identity proof documentation, and original CBT admit cards.
  • The Medical Examination will be conducted at nominated Railway Hospitals on the immediate next day following physical document verification. Candidates must come prepared to stay for 3 to 4 days at their own expense.
  • A nominal medical evaluation fee of Rs. 24/- is mandatory to be deposited at the railway reception counter. No other charges are levied.
