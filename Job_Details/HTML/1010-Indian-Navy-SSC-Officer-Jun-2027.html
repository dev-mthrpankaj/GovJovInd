About This Recruitment

The Indian Navy SSC Officer Jun 2027 recruitment is for eligible unmarried men and unmarried women who want to join the Navy as Short Service Commission Officers. The notification covers Executive Branch, Education Branch and Technical Branch entries for the course commencing June 2027 onwards at Indian Naval Academy, Ezhimala, Kerala.

This recruitment is useful for engineering graduates, postgraduates and final-year candidates who satisfy the branch-wise educational criteria. Many entries require BE/B.Tech with minimum 60% marks, while Education and Logistics entries include specific postgraduate, management, commerce, IT and other qualifications. Candidates should check the exact stream list carefully because the notification states that equivalent streams other than those mentioned will not be considered valid.

The selection process is based on shortlisting from normalised qualifying degree marks, followed by SSB interview, medical examination and final merit as per entry-wise vacancies. There is no written exam pattern in the provided notification. Candidates should focus on correct application details, degree marks, document upload quality, SSB preparation and medical eligibility before choosing preferences.

Eligibility and Qualification

  • Eligible unmarried men and unmarried women candidates can apply, subject to nationality conditions laid down by the Government of India.
  • Degree obtained or final-year candidates are eligible, but they must meet the required educational qualification before joining INA Ezhimala by June 2027.
  • Executive GS(X) / Hydro: BE/B.Tech in any discipline with minimum 60% marks.
  • Pilot / NAOO / ATC: BE/B.Tech in any discipline with minimum 60% marks. Pilot and NAOO also require 60% aggregate marks in Class X and XII and minimum 60% marks in English in Class X or XII.
  • Logistics: BE/B.Tech First Class, MBA First Class, B.Sc/B.Com/B.Sc.(IT) First Class with relevant PG Diploma, or MCA/M.Sc(IT) First Class.
  • NAIC: BE/B.Tech with minimum 60% in notified streams or postgraduate degree in Electronics / Physics, with Class X/XII and English conditions as mentioned.
  • Education: MSc/MA/BE/B.Tech/ME/M.Tech qualifications in notified subjects with minimum marks as per the entry group.
  • Technical entries: BE/B.Tech with minimum 60% marks in the specific notified engineering streams for Engineering, Electrical, Submarine Technical and Naval Constructor entries.
  • No equivalent stream other than those mentioned in the notification will be considered valid for selection at any stage.

Selection Process

  1. Online application and branch/cadre preference submission on Indian Navy website.
  2. Shortlisting of applications based on normalised marks obtained in the qualifying degree.
  3. BE/B.Tech candidates are shortlisted using marks up to fifth semester if completed or in final year; postgraduate candidates are considered as per completed or pre-final year performance rules.
  4. Shortlisted candidates are informed about SSB interview through email or SMS.
  5. SSB interview at allotted centre; change of SSB centre is not permitted.
  6. Medical examination as applicable to the selected entry.
  7. Final merit list based on SSB marks, medical clearance and availability of vacancies for the respective entry.
  8. Induction as Sub Lieutenant after clearing induction medical examination at INA Ezhimala, subject to rules.

Exam Pattern

The provided notification does not mention a written examination pattern for this recruitment. Shortlisting is based on normalised marks of the qualifying degree, followed by SSB interview and medical examination.

Physical Standards / Medical Standards

The notification instructs candidates to check medical standards, height, leg length and other requirements before selecting entries, especially Pilot. SSB recommended candidates have to undergo medical examination applicable to their entry. Change of medical hospital or centre is not permissible.

  • Candidates choosing Pilot may be considered for SSB / CPSS / Aviation Medical Examination based on service requirement.
  • Candidates must ascertain branch/cadre eligibility with respect to medical standards before indicating preferences.
  • Tattoo, height and weight relaxation information should be checked from the Indian Navy website.
  • Drug use or possession is banned; candidates may be tested during selection, medical, training or service as per notification.

Salary and Benefits

  • Selected candidates will be inducted in the rank of Sub Lieutenant after clearing induction medical examination at INA Ezhimala.
  • The notification states that initial gross salary of Sub Lieutenant starts from approximately Rs. 1,20,000 per month along with applicable allowances.
  • Pilot / NAOO and Submarine allowance of Rs. 31,250 will be admissible after successful completion of relevant training, as per notification and extant rules.
  • Officers / cadets will be members of Naval Group Insurance Scheme on contribution basis as per rules.
  • Short Service Commission tenure is initially 12 years, extendable by maximum 2 years subject to service requirement, performance, medical eligibility and willingness.

Documents Required

  • Matriculation / Class 10 certificate for personal particulars and date of birth.
  • Class 12 certificate / marksheet where applicable.
  • Degree marksheets up to 5th semester for regular BE/B.Tech and up to 7th semester for integrated BE/B.Tech.
  • All semester marksheets for other degree examinations.
  • CGPA conversion formula for BE/B.Tech, where applicable.
  • Merchant Navy certificate issued by Government of India, Ministry of Shipping and Transport, if applicable.
  • NCC "C" certificate, if claiming NCC relaxation.
  • Recent passport size colour photograph.
  • Application printout to be carried while appearing for SSB interview.
  • Any other document required by the Indian Navy website or SSB call letter.

How to Apply

  1. Visit the official Indian Navy website at joinindiannavy.gov.in.
  2. Register or log in and open the SSC Officer entries for Jun 2027 course.
  3. Read the branch-wise eligibility, medical standards and stream list before choosing preferences.
  4. Fill personal details exactly as given in the Matriculation Certificate.
  5. Enter email address and mobile number carefully because SSB information is sent through email/SMS.
  6. Upload readable scanned documents in the required format.
  7. Preview the application and submit it before the last date because the application once submitted is final.
  8. Take a printout of the submitted application and carry it when appearing for SSB interview.

Why Apply For This Recruitment

This recruitment is a strong opportunity for eligible graduates and final-year candidates who want to join the Indian Navy as officers rather than through a written entrance exam route. It covers several technical and non-technical officer entries, offers structured training at INA Ezhimala and provides a commissioned officer career with pay, allowances, leadership exposure and specialised roles such as Pilot, NAOO, Submarine, Technical and Naval Constructor entries. Candidates should apply only after confirming branch-wise eligibility and medical standards.
