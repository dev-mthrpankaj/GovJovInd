About This Recruitment

RSSB CET Graduate Level 2026 is a common eligibility test for graduate-level posts covered under the Rajasthan subordinate and ministerial services CET rules. The CET score is used as an eligibility score for future main recruitment processes of included services and posts, but the CET itself is not a direct appointment order or final job selection.

The official notification explains that candidates who obtain a CET score may still have to appear in the relevant recruitment agency's written examination, interview or other selection stages whenever a separate post-wise recruitment advertisement is issued. Therefore, candidates should treat this exam as the first eligibility step for multiple Rajasthan government recruitment opportunities.

Applications are accepted through the Rajasthan SSO and Recruitment Portal only. Candidates should complete One Time Registration carefully, check category, domicile and document details, and submit the online form before the deadline. The board has also advised candidates not to wait for the last date and to keep checking the official RSSB website for updates.

Eligibility and Qualification

  • CET Graduate Level is meant for posts where graduation-level eligibility is required under the relevant service rules.
  • Specific educational qualification, age, experience, computer qualification and other post-wise eligibility will be as prescribed in the concerned service rules and future recruitment notification.
  • Appearing in CET and obtaining a score does not automatically make a candidate eligible for every included post.
  • Candidates should verify their eligibility for at least one included post before applying.
  • The best valid CET score before the upcoming recruitment may be considered if the score validity has not expired, as per the official rules.

Selection Process

  1. Online application through SSO ID and Rajasthan Recruitment Portal.
  2. Offline OMR-based Common Eligibility Test conducted by RSSB.
  3. Publication of marks on candidate SSO ID and successful candidates list on the official website.
  4. Use of CET score as eligibility for future main recruitment examinations of included posts.
  5. Separate post-wise recruitment examination, interview or other stages as per concerned service rules.
  6. Document verification, final merit and appointment process as per the concerned department's recruitment rules.

Exam Pattern

The official notification gives an offline OMR-based paper with multiple-choice questions. All questions carry equal marks and one-third negative marking applies for each wrong answer.

Physical Standards / Skill Test / Typing Test

RSSB CET Graduate Level 2026 notification is for a common eligibility test only. Physical standards, skill test, typing test, computer qualification or any other special test will apply only if the concerned post-wise recruitment rules require it. Candidates should check the separate main recruitment notification for the post they want to apply for.

Salary and Benefits

RSSB CET Graduate Level 2026 is only a common eligibility test, so no single salary or pay level is attached to this CET notification. Salary, pay matrix, grade pay and service benefits will be available in the separate recruitment notification for the concerned post such as Patwari, Junior Accountant, VDO, Supervisor or other listed posts.

How to Apply

  1. Visit the Rajasthan SSO portal at sso.rajasthan.gov.in.
  2. Login with your own SSO ID and open Citizen Apps (G2C).
  3. Select Recruitment Portal and click on Apply Now for Common Eligibility Test (Graduate Level)-2026.
  4. Complete or verify One Time Registration details, category, domicile and PwD status carefully.
  5. Fill the CET application form with correct personal, educational and communication details.
  6. Upload documents and provide visible body mark or other required details where asked.
  7. Pay the OTR/application fee if it has not already been paid.
  8. Preview the application, submit it finally and save the generated application number.
  9. Take a printout or PDF copy of the submitted form for future use.

Why Apply For This Recruitment

RSSB CET Graduate Level is useful for candidates who want to stay eligible for multiple Rajasthan graduate-level recruitments through a single eligibility test score. Since the score is valid for three years from the result date and there is no restriction on attempts mentioned in the notification, serious aspirants can use this examination to improve their eligibility score and prepare for post-wise main recruitment exams in a more planned way.

Frequently Asked Questions

What is RSSB CET Graduate Level 2026?

RSSB CET Graduate Level 2026 is the Common Eligibility Test for graduate-level posts included under the Rajasthan subordinate and ministerial services CET rules.

Which board conducts RSSB CET Graduate Level 2026?

The examination is conducted by Rajasthan Staff Selection Board, Jaipur.

What is the application start date?

The online application process is scheduled to start on 04 July 2026.

What is the last date to apply online?

The last date to submit the online application form is 02 August 2026 up to 23:59 hours.

What is the exam date for RSSB CET Graduate Level 2026?

The official notification states that the OMR-based offline examination is proposed from 01 December 2026 to 03 December 2026.

How long will the CET Graduate Level score be valid?

As per the notification, the CET score will be valid for three years from the result date.

Is CET Graduate Level 2026 a job guarantee?

No. The notification clearly states that CET is only an eligibility test and does not guarantee a job.

What is the minimum qualifying mark in CET Graduate Level?

Candidates generally need at least 40 percent marks, while SC and ST candidates get relaxation of up to 5 percent in the minimum marks.

What is the application fee?

The one-time registration fee is Rs. 600 for General and creamy layer OBC/MBC candidates, and Rs. 400 for Rajasthan non-creamy layer OBC/MBC, EWS, SC, ST and all PwD candidates. Candidates who already paid OTR fee do not need to pay again.

What is the exam pattern?

The exam will have 150 multiple-choice questions for 300 marks, duration 3 hours, with one-third negative marking for each wrong answer.
