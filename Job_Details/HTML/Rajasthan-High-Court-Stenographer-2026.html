About This Recruitment

The Rajasthan High Court (RHC) has invited online applications for 163 Stenographer posts across various cadres including Grade II and Grade III in Hindi and English mediums for Non-TSP and TSP areas. This recruitment drive offers an excellent opportunity for candidates looking for stenography careers in the judicial system of Rajasthan.

Candidates applying for these posts should ensure they satisfy all requirements including educational qualification, age limit, and stenographic/typing skills as prescribed by the court. The online registration portal remains open from 22 July 2026 to 10 August 2026, and fee payment can be completed up to 11 August 2026.

Selection will be based on standard procedures outlined in the official notification including skill tests/stenography examination and document verification. Applicants are requested to read the official prospectus carefully prior to filling the online application form.

Eligibility and Qualification

  • Intermediate (10+2) passed from any recognized Board in India.
  • Must possess a good working knowledge of Hindi written in Devanagari Script and knowledge of Rajasthani Dialects.
  • Must possess required speed proficiency in shorthand/typing as specified in the recruitment rules.
  • For detailed qualification and equivalent certifications, kindly refer to the official notification.

Selection Process

  1. Shortlisting of online applications.
  2. Shorthand / Stenography Speed Test and Typing Test.
  3. Document Verification of qualified candidates.
  4. Final Merit List declaration and Medical Fitness.

Exam Pattern / Skill Test

Selection involves Stenography Speed Test and Computer Efficiency/Typing Test as per the Rajasthan High Court Stenographer service rules. Detailed scheme of examination and syllabus can be checked from the official syllabus PDF or notification.
