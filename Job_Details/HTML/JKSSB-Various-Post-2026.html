About This Recruitment

JKSSB Various Post Recruitment 2026 is a combined notification issued by the Jammu & Kashmir Services Selection Board under Advertisement No. 08/2026, covering 518 tentative posts across multiple post names. Because it is a "various post" notice, qualification, pay scale and post-wise eligibility differ from post to post, so candidates should check the specific post they intend to apply for in the official notification rather than assuming a single uniform criterion.

The application process is online only, through the JKSSB website. Candidates register with a valid email ID and mobile number, log in, fill personal, educational and category details, upload the required documents, pay the applicable fee, and submit before the deadline.

Category-wise vacancy under this notice covers Open Merit, SC, ST-1, ST-2, OBC, ALC/IB, RBA and EWS categories as used in Jammu & Kashmir recruitment rules. Candidates should read the official notification for the exact meaning and eligibility conditions of each category before applying.

How to Apply

  1. Visit the official JKSSB website at jkssb.nic.in.
  2. Click on the "JKSSB Various Post Apply Online" link.
  3. Register using a valid email ID and mobile number.
  4. Log in using the registration ID and password.
  5. Fill the application form with personal, educational and category details.
  6. Upload the required scanned documents (photo, signature, etc.).
  7. Pay the application fee through online mode, where applicable.
  8. Submit the form and download or print the confirmation page.

Documents Required

  • Recent photograph and signature (scanned, per JKSSB format).
  • Educational qualification certificates matching the applied post.
  • Date of birth proof.
  • Category certificate (SC / ST-1 / ST-2 / OBC / EWS / ALC-IB / RBA / PH), if applicable.
  • Domicile / permanent resident certificate of Jammu & Kashmir, if required for the post.
  • Other documents mentioned in the official notification.
