About This Recruitment

Shri Krishna AYUSH University (SKAU), located in Kurukshetra, Haryana, is a premier state university established under State Legislature Act 25 of 2017. The university has initiated direct recruitment under Advertisement Numbers NT-01/2026 to NT-03/2026 to fill various regular non-teaching and para-medical positions on its campus. These roles aim to strengthen administrative operational efficiency and medical assistance within university-affiliated institutions.

The recruitment drive offers opportunities for qualified medical professionals and general graduates across three key cadres: Staff Nurse (25 posts), Ayurvedic Pharmacist (6 posts), and Clerk (29 posts). Candidates belonging to Haryana as well as other Indian states are eligible to apply, subject to fulfilling the prescribed education, age, language, and category criteria as per the adopted University Non-Teaching Employees Service Rules.

Selection for these posts will be finalized based on performance in a competitive written test, practical computer typing evaluation (for Clerk roles), and academic achievement score weightage, followed by physical document verification. Applicants must complete registration online, pay the prescribed application fee, and upload a signed copy of the completed application form to confirm their submission.

Eligibility and Qualification

  • Staff Nurse:
    • Matriculation pass from a recognized board.
    • A-Grade Nurse registered with Haryana Nurses Registration Council or equivalent body.
    • Matric with Hindi/Sanskrit as a subject, or 10+2/BA/MA with Hindi as one of the subjects.
  • Ayurvedic Pharmacist:
    • Up-Vaidya or Diploma in Ayurvedic Pharmacy from a recognized University/Institution/Board or Faculty of Indian System of Medicine.
    • 10+2 pass with Physics, Chemistry, and Biology (PCB).
    • Matric with Hindi/Sanskrit as a subject, or 10+2/BA/MA with Hindi as one of the subjects.
  • Clerk:
    • Bachelor's Degree from a recognized University or Board in India.
    • Must qualify a written test of language/General Knowledge of graduation standard prescribed by the authority.
    • Must qualify a data entry test on computers at a speed of 30 WPM.
    • Matric with Hindi/Sanskrit as a subject, or 10+2/BA/MA with Hindi as one of the subjects.

Selection Process

  1. Phase 1: Written Competitive Examination (Objective Multiple Choice Questions).
  2. Phase 2: Computer Skill / Data Entry Typing Test at 30 WPM (Mandatory for Clerk post).
  3. Phase 3: Academic Achievement Evaluation (10 Marks Weightage based on percentage in professional degree/diploma).
  4. Phase 4: Document Verification and checking of original certificates.
  5. Final Merit List preparation based on cumulative score in written exam, typing/skill clearance, and academic criteria.

Exam Pattern

The written examination consists of objective-type multiple-choice questions testing core subject knowledge, general knowledge, reasoning, and basic computer applications.

  • Ayurvedic Pharmacist (Advt. NT-02/2026): Functional Pay Level FPL-6 (Rs. 35,400 to Rs. 1,12,400).
  • Clerk (Advt. NT-03/2026): Functional Pay Level FPL-2 (Rs. 19,900 to Rs. 63,200).
  • Allowances including Dearness Allowance (DA), House Rent Allowance (HRA), and Medical Benefits apply as per Haryana University rules.

How to Apply

  1. Visit the official SKAU recruitment portal at recruitment.skau.ac.in/nonteaching/.
  2. Register using your Parivar Pehchan Patra (PPP) or valid mobile number to generate login credentials.
  3. Log in using your generated registration ID and password.
  4. Select the desired post (Staff Nurse, Ayurvedic Pharmacist, or Clerk) and enter personal, educational, and category details carefully.
  5. Upload scanned copies of required documents, photograph, and signature in prescribed formats.
  6. Pay the applicable application fee through online mode.
  7. Submit the application form and download the generated PDF printout.
  8. Sign every page of the downloaded application printout, scan it, and upload the signed PDF copy back to the portal before 21.08.2026 (04:00 PM) to complete the application process.

Why Apply For This Recruitment

Shri Krishna AYUSH University provides direct recruitment into regular state government services in Haryana's growing AYUSH higher education sector. Positions like Staff Nurse, Ayurvedic Pharmacist, and Clerk offer long-term job stability, structured pay scales under 7th CPC (FPL-2 and FPL-6), attractive allowances, and a professional work environment at the Kurukshetra campus. Eligible candidates with relevant nursing, pharmacy, or graduate degrees should take advantage of this university hiring opportunity.
