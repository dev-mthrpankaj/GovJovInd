About This Recruitment

Nainital Bank Specialist Officer Recruitment 2026 is an official recruitment drive conducted by The Nainital Bank Limited to hire qualified professionals for various Specialist Officer (SO) roles in Grade/Scale I, II, and III. This recruitment drive aims to fill 41 vacancies across multiple domains, including IT, Risk Management, Human Resources, Law, Credit, Data Science, and Civil Engineering.

Eligible candidates who possess the required educational qualifications, such as an MBA, B.E./B.Tech, MCA, LLB, CA, or equivalent degrees, can submit their online application through the official IBPS registration portal. The online application window is open from 15 August 2026 to 07 September 2026. Applicants must ensure they meet the post-specific age criteria as of 01 August 2026 and complete the fee payment before the deadline.

The detailed selection process, post-wise eligibility criteria, and experience requirements are outlined in the official notification. Candidates are strongly advised to download the official notification PDF and read through all the instructions carefully before proceeding with the online application.

Eligibility & Qualification

  • Risk Officer (Scale-I & II): Regular Full Time Post Graduate Degree in MBA (Finance) / Master Degree in Mathematics/Statistics/Econometrics. Certifications like GARP-FRM / CFA are preferred.
  • HR Officer (Scale-I): Regular Full Time Post Graduate degree in MBA with specialization in Personnel Management / HR / Labour Law with minimum 60% marks.
  • IT Officer (Scale-I): BE / B.Tech / M.Tech / M.E. / MCA in Computer Science / IT / ECE / Data Science with min 60% marks. Proficiency in Python, JAVA, .NET, SQL is preferred.
  • Data Scientist/Analyst (Scale-I): Bachelor's or Master's degree in Data Science, Computer Science, Statistics, or related field with min 60% aggregate.
  • Civil Engineer / Architect (Scale-I): BE/B.Tech in Civil Engineering or Bachelor's Degree in Architecture with min 60% marks.
  • Manager - Credit / Forex / Treasury (Scale-II & III): MBA / PGDBM (Finance) or relevant field. Professional qualifications like CA, CMA, CFA, CS are advantageous.
  • Manager / Sr. Manager - Law (Scale-II & III): Regular Full Time Bachelor Degree in Law (LLB) and enrolled as an advocate with Bar Council.
  • Manager - Company Secretary (Scale-II): Associate Member of ICSI.
  • Manager - Security Officer (Scale-II): Bachelor Degree in any stream from a recognized university.

Please review the official notification for comprehensive details regarding mandatory post-qualification work experience required for each scale/grade.

Selection Process

The selection process for the Nainital Bank Specialist Officer Recruitment generally involves evaluating the candidate's professional knowledge and suitability for the role. Please see the official notification for the exact stages applicable to your specific post and grade.

  1. Written / Online Examination

    Depending on the post and number of applications, an online objective test may be conducted assessing professional knowledge, reasoning, English, and quantitative aptitude.

  2. Personal Interview

    Shortlisted candidates will be called for a personal interview to evaluate their domain expertise and overall suitability for the bank.

  3. Document Verification & Medical Test

    Final selection is subject to the verification of all original educational, experience, and category certificates, followed by a medical fitness screening.

How to Apply

  1. Visit the official website of Nainital Bank at www.nainitalbank.bank.in or click the direct Apply Online link provided above.
  2. Click on the "Nainital Bank Specialist Officer 2026 Apply Online" link to reach the IBPS application portal.
  3. Register using a valid email ID and mobile number.
  4. Login using your newly generated registration ID and password.
  5. Fill the application form carefully with your personal, educational, category, and experience details.
  6. Upload scanned documents, including your recent photograph and signature, in the required format.
  7. Pay the application fee of Rs.1500 through the online payment gateway (Debit/Credit Card, Net Banking, UPI).
  8. Submit the application form and download/print the confirmation page for future reference.

Documents Required

  • Recent passport-size colored photograph (scanned).
  • Scanned copy of signature.
  • Graduation/Post-Graduation degree certificates and mark sheets.
  • Valid experience certificates (if applicable to the post).
  • Caste/Category certificate if claiming age relaxation or reservation.
  • Valid photo ID proof (Aadhaar Card, PAN Card, Voter ID, Driving License, or Passport).
