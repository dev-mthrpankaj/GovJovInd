Eligibility & Age Limit

Post Graduation

Post Graduate or equivalent examination recognized by UGC in English, Hindi, History, Political Science, Sociology or Music.

Special Education

B.Ed. Special Education or B.Ed. with two years Diploma in Special Education recognized by the Rehabilitation Council of India (RCI).

Other Requirement

Working knowledge of Hindi written in Devanagari script and knowledge of Rajasthani culture.

RCI Registration

Candidate must be registered with RCI before the recruitment exam date and must submit permanent registration before appointment.

Age Limit as on 01-01-2027

Minimum Age: 21 Years

Maximum Age: Below 40 Years

Selection Process

    Selection will be conducted through a competitive examination as per the relevant service rules and RPSC examination scheme.

  2. 2

    The examination will be objective type. Detailed syllabus and exam scheme will be released separately on the RPSC website.

  3. 3

    RPSC may use scaling, moderation or normalization method during evaluation if required.

  4. 4

    Names of suitable candidates will be recommended to the State Government / appointing authority in merit order based on written examination marks.

How to Apply Online

  1. 1

    Visit the RPSC website rpsc.rajasthan.gov.in and read the detailed advertisement and Instructions for Applicants carefully.

  2. 2

    Click on the Apply Online link or login through Rajasthan SSO and open the Recruitment Portal from Citizen Apps.

  3. 3

    Complete One Time Registration using Aadhaar / Jan Aadhaar based details, live photo, personal details and educational details.

  4. 4

    Upload required signature and left thumb impression details through OTR, fill the online application carefully and preview the form.

  5. 5

    Submit the application, pay the applicable OTR fee if required and save the application number / printout for future use.

Important Instructions

  • Only online applications submitted through the RPSC / SSO recruitment portal will be accepted.
  • Preview of the application is not considered final submission. Application number must be generated after successful submission.
  • Candidates should enter all educational qualifications and experience acquired up to the last date of application.
  • If any mistake is found after submission, correction can be done online during the permitted correction period by paying the prescribed fee.
  • Incorrect information, hiding facts or applying without required eligibility may lead to cancellation and debarment as per rules.
  • Candidates should regularly check the RPSC Exam Dashboard because separate offline communication may not be issued.
  • Reserved category benefits are applicable as per Rajasthan Government rules. Candidates from other states should carefully read the category instructions.
