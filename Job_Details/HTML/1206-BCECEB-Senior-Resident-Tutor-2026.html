About This Recruitment

BCECEB Senior Resident / Tutor 2026 notice relates to tenure posts under Health Department, Government of Bihar. The notice directs candidates to use the Senior Resident / Tutor online portal through the BCECEB website.

The uploaded notice focuses heavily on the online application process, counselling fee, document verification documents and important dates. Eligibility is linked with NMC Teachers Eligibility Qualifications in Medical Institutions Regulations, 2025 and DNB/DNB Super Speciality related provisions mentioned in the notice/prospectus.

Candidates must read the full prospectus from BCECEB before applying because department-wise posts, subject eligibility and reservation details can materially affect candidature.

Eligibility and Qualification

Selection Process

  1. Online registration.
  2. Department selection and multi-step application form submission.
  3. Upload photograph, signature and live photograph as per portal instructions.
  4. Preview, final submit and counselling fee payment.
  5. Download confirmation page and fee receipt.
  6. Online editing opportunity for successfully submitted/paid applications.
  7. Document verification/counselling as per schedule to be notified.

Exam Pattern

The short notice does not describe a written exam pattern. The process is based on online application, counselling fee payment and document verification/counselling as per official schedule.

How to Apply

  1. Visit bceceboard.bihar.gov.in.
  2. Open the Online Portal of Senior Resident / Tutor under Health Dept.
  3. Click Apply for Senior Resident / Tutor and complete new registration.
  4. Verify mobile number and email using verification codes.
  5. Sign in and select department.
  6. Fill personal, contact and educational details.
  7. Upload photograph, signature and live photograph as instructed.
  8. Preview all entries and final submit.
  9. Pay counselling fee online and download confirmation page/fee receipt.
