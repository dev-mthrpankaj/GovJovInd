About This Recruitment

RRB ALP CEN 01/2026 is a national railway recruitment for Assistant Loco Pilot posts. The post has strict medical standards, especially vision requirements, because ALP duties relate to train operations and safety.

Candidates can apply to only one RRB and submit only one online application. The notice clearly warns that multiple applications can lead to rejection and future debarment, so candidates should choose their RRB carefully.

The selection process has several stages: CBT-1, CBT-2, CBAT, document verification and medical examination. CBT-1 is only a screening stage, while final merit uses CBT-2 Part A and CBAT weightage as described in the CEN.

Eligibility and Qualification

  • Matriculation / SSLC plus ITI from recognized NCVT/SCVT institution in the relevant trade as listed in Annexure-A.
  • Matriculation / SSLC plus Course Completed Act Apprenticeship in relevant trades.
  • Matriculation / SSLC plus three years Diploma in Mechanical / Electrical / Electronics / Automobile Engineering or a combination of streams in lieu of ITI.
  • Degree in Engineering in the relevant streams is also mentioned in the CEN.
  • Candidates awaiting final result of prescribed qualification should not apply.
  • Medical standard A-1 is required; candidates with LASIK surgery are not eligible as per the CEN.

Selection Process

  1. First Stage CBT (CBT-1) for screening.
  2. Second Stage CBT (CBT-2) with Part A and qualifying Part B trade test.
  3. Computer Based Aptitude Test (CBAT).
  4. Document Verification.
  5. Medical Examination and final Railway appointment formalities.

Exam Pattern

CBT-1 is a 60-minute screening test of 75 questions. CBT-2 has Part A and Part B for 2 hours 30 minutes with 175 questions. Negative marking of 1/3 mark applies in CBTs. CBAT has no negative marking and requires separate qualifying standards.

Medical Standards and Aptitude Test

ALP has A-1 medical standard with strict vision requirements. CBAT is mandatory for shortlisted candidates and final merit gives 50 percent weightage to CBT-2 Part A and 50 percent to CBAT scores.
