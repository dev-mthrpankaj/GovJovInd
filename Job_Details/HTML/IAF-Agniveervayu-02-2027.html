About This Recruitment

The Agnipath induction methodology offers the nation's youth an exclusive opportunity to experience the military way of life and serve the country within the Indian Air Force cadre for a predefined block of four years. This recruitment cycle caters to Intake 02/2027, looking out for disciplined, physically robust, and bright unmarried male and female candidates. The positions are categorised broadly into Science Subjects and Other than Science Subjects stream paths based on academic qualifications.

Selected candidates form a distinct rank framework within the Indian Air Force structure, distinct from regular operational cadres. On successful completion of the four-year service timeline, up to 25% of the specific batch will be accommodated into regular cadres as Airmen based on organizational needs, decentralized evaluations, and overall performance. The rest will step back into civil streams equipped with specialized tech skills, a comprehensive terminal financial package, and an institutional skill profile.

Age Limit and Marital Mandate

Age is checked tightly with respect to predefined calendar blocks. The baseline parameters require:

  • Date of Birth Block: Candidates born between 01 July 2005 and 01 January 2010 (both calendar dates inclusive) are eligible to apply.
  • Enrolment Upper Limit: If a candidate clears all selection stages successfully, the maximum upper age limit on the exact date of official enrolment must not exceed 22 years.
  • Marital & Pregnancy Conditions: Only unmarried candidates are eligible. They must sign a voluntary undertaking not to marry during the 4-year tenure. Female candidates must undertake not to get pregnant during service; pregnancy leads to disqualification/dismissal.

Candidates must ensure they fulfill the qualifications for either or both of the stream variants specified below:

Crucial Note on Percentages: Fractions or decimal values will not be rounded off. For instance, an aggregate of 49.99% will be read as 49% flat, falling below the mandatory 50% eligibility cutoff.

Selection Process

The evaluation cycle spans three sequential execution filters. Candidates have to secure sectional clear cuts in each tier:

  1. Phase-I: Objective Computer Based Test (CBT) featuring normalized score calculations.
  2. Phase-II: Physical Fitness Test (PFT-1 & PFT-2), Document verification, and Adaptability Tests (I & II).
  3. Phase-III: Comprehensive Medical Examination administered by the Air Force Medical Team.

Phase-I Online Exam Pattern

The questions are objective-type and bilingual (English & Hindi), except for the English language paper. Sectional individual qualification in all distinct papers is mandatory.

Marking Scheme Parameters: 1 mark is granted for every correct response. Unattempted queries earn 0 marks. A penalty deduction of 0.25 marks is applicable for every wrong answer.

Salary Structure and Seva Nidhi Terminal Benefits

Agniveervayu personnel receive an attractive package of Rs. 30,000/- per month during the first year, progressing with a fixed annual increment structure up to Rs. 40,000/- in the fourth year. A non-contributory Life Insurance Cover of Rs. 48 Lakhs is provided throughout the engagement tenure.

How to Apply for IAF Agniveervayu 02/2027

  1. Access the official CASB registration portal link at iafrecruitment.edcil.co.in.
  2. Complete Step-I SignUp by entering basic personal details to generate Mobile and Email OTP verification keys.
  3. Log in with the system-generated credentials and navigate to Step-II of the application interface.
  4. Fill in communications, academic percentages, select five choice exam cities in order of preference, and upload scanned copies of documents.
  5. Photograph Specification: Upload a recent passport photo taken with the candidate holding a black slate in front of their chest, featuring their name and the date of the photograph clearly written in white chalk in capital letters.
  6. Capture a live image verification via a working webcam interface to clear the identity match block.
  7. Upload your signature and left thumb impression images matching the dimensional parameters.
  8. Review all entered entries using the 'Preview' panel, correct errors if any, make the non-refundable payment of Rs. 550/- plus GST, and print out the submission PDF copy.

Frequently Asked Questions

What are the registration dates for IAF Agniveervayu Intake 02/2027?

The online registration starts on 06 July 2026 at 11:00 hours and closes on 26 July 2026 at 23:00 hours.

When will the IAF Agniveervayu 02/2027 online exam be conducted?

The Phase-I online examination is scheduled to be held on 22 and 23 September 2026.

What is the age limit for this intake?

Candidates must be born between 01 July 2005 and 01 January 2010 (both dates inclusive). The upper age limit at the time of enrolment should be 22 years.

What is the application fee for Agniveervayu 02/2027?

The examination fee is Rs. 550/- plus 18% GST, which is non-refundable for all categories.

Can female candidates apply for IAF Agniveervayu?

Yes, both unmarried male and female candidates are eligible to apply. The total number and employability of female candidates are decided based on service requirements.

Is there a negative marking in the online test?

Yes, 0.25 marks will be deducted for each wrong answer. No marks are deducted for unattempted questions.

What is the minimum height required?

The minimum acceptable height is 152 cms for both male and female candidates, with relaxation down to 147 cms for female candidates belonging to North East or hilly regions of Uttarakhand, and 150 cms for candidates from Lakshadweep.

What is the duration of service and terminal benefits?

The engagement period is for 4 years. On completion, individuals receive an approximate Seva Nidhi package of Rs. 10.04 Lakhs (plus interest) along with an Agniveer Skill Certificate.
