About This Recruitment

Haryana State Electronics Development Corporation Ltd. (HARTRON) has published the official notification Advt. No. Hartron/ICTET/2026-27/03 to update the panel of Junior Programmers on a Job Work Basis across all 23 districts of Haryana. This recruitment drive aims to create a pool of qualified computer professionals for deployment in various state government departments, boards, and corporations based on requirement.

Eligible candidates holding degrees or diplomas in Computer Science, IT, or Computer Applications with at least 55% marks can submit their applications online through the official portal. The placement is made purely on a job work basis as per the state reservation policy, with a consolidated remuneration of Rs. 24,100/- per month. Merely clearing the tests and getting empaneled does not guarantee immediate placement, as deployment depends on confirmed requisitions from indenting organizations.

The recruitment involves a multi-stage selection process, including an initial Domain Knowledge Computer-Based Test (MCQ test), followed by document verification, approximately 50 hours of mandatory training at designated centres (Ambala or Gurugram), and a final Practical/Project Test. Candidates are advised to carefully select their district preference while filling out the online application form.

Eligibility and Qualification

Candidates must fulfill at least one of the following educational eligibility conditions with a minimum of 55% aggregate marks:

  • Graduate in any discipline with 55% marks AND PGDCA / PDCA / PGDIT / APGDCA from a recognized University or Institute.
  • BCA / B.Sc (Computer Science / IT) / MCA / M.Sc (Computer Science / IT) / B.Tech / M.Tech (in any stream) with at least 55% marks.
  • 3-Year Diploma in Computer Application / IT from SBTE / recognized University with 55% marks.
  • 'A' / 'B' / 'C' Level Course certification from DOEACC / NIELIT with minimum 55% marks.

Selection Process

The recruitment for HARTRON Junior Programmer follows a systematic multi-step empanelment procedure:

  1. 1st Test (Domain Knowledge CBT): Computer-based objective test evaluating domain knowledge and core computer concepts.
  2. Document Scrutiny: Verification of original academic certificates, identity proof, and category certificates for candidates who successfully pass the 1st test.
  3. Skill Training Phase: Qualified candidates undergo approximately 50 hours of mandatory training over a period of about 1 month at IDDC Ambala or HMSDC Gurugram labs (minimum 75% attendance required). Candidates pay a refundable deposit of Rs. 1,500/- for training.
  4. 2nd Test (Practical / Project Test): Practical test on computer programming and project skills upon successful completion of training.
  5. Final Panel Preparation & Deployment: Final merit list created based on equal weightage to test scores. Candidates are empaneled for 2 years (extendable up to 5 years) and recommended to state government departments upon confirmed requirements.

How to Apply

  1. Visit the official HARTRON recruitment website at deployment.setchartron.in or hartron.org.in.
  2. Click on the link for "Updation of Panel of Junior Programmers (Advt No. Hartron/ICTET/2026-27/03)".
  3. Read the detailed advertisement instructions carefully before starting registration.
  4. Register with a valid Email ID and Mobile Number to generate login credentials.
  5. Fill out the online application form with personal details, educational qualifications, and select your preferred district (only 1 district can be selected).
  6. Upload clean scanned copies of your photograph, signature, and essential qualification certificates.
  7. Pay the requisite application fee online via Credit/Debit Card, Net Banking, or UPI as applicable for your category.
  8. Submit the application form and print a copy of the final confirmation page for future reference.
