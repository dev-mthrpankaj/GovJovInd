About This Recruitment

HPPSC Assistant Professor College Cadre Recruitment 2026 is a subject-wise recruitment drive for teaching posts in the Higher Education Department of Himachal Pradesh. The posts are notified as Group-A Job Trainee posts and the advertisements have been issued separately for different subjects such as English, Chemistry, Hindi, Physics, Mathematics, Commerce, History, Geography and other disciplines.

This combined page is useful because the recruitment is not limited to a single PDF. Candidates must first identify their subject, then read that subject advertisement carefully and apply for the correct post through the HPPSC online portal. The total vacancy count is shown as 373 across 23 subjects, but subject-wise conditions and reservation details should always be verified from the relevant official advertisement.

Candidates with a Master’s Degree in the relevant subject, required marks and NET/SLET/SET or applicable Ph.D. exemption may find this recruitment important. Since Assistant Professor posts involve academic eligibility, reservation certificates, proof of qualification and subject-specific documents, applicants should not rely only on summaries and must read the official notice before fee payment.

Eligibility and Qualification

  • Master’s Degree in the concerned / relevant subject from a recognized Indian University or an equivalent degree from an accredited foreign university, as applicable in the subject notification.
  • Minimum 55% marks or equivalent grade is required for general eligibility. Eligible reserved categories may get relaxation to 50% marks as per the official advertisement.
  • Candidate should have qualified NET conducted by UGC / CSIR, or SLET / SET, where required for the subject.
  • Candidates with Ph.D. may be exempted from NET/SLET/SET only if they satisfy the conditions prescribed in UGC regulations and the HPPSC advertisement.
  • Knowledge of customs, manners and dialects of Himachal Pradesh and suitability for appointment in the State is generally desirable as per HPPSC recruitment practice.
  • Candidates must read the exact subject-wise advertisement because qualification wording may differ for Music, Dance, Tabla, Physical Education and other special subjects.

Selection Process

  1. Submit the Online Recruitment Application for the correct subject advertisement through HPPSC OTR/ORA portal.
  2. HPPSC will conduct Screening Test / Written Test / Subject Aptitude Test as applicable for shortlisting and selection.
  3. Candidates shortlisted as per HPPSC rules will be called for Personality Test / interview and document verification.
  4. Original documents, certificates, category claims and eligibility proof must be produced when demanded by the Commission.
  5. Final selection will be prepared according to HPPSC rules, subject-wise advertisement conditions and performance in the applicable stages.
