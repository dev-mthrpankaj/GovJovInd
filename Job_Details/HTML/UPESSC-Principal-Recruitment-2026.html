About This Recruitment

The Uttar Pradesh Education Service Selection Commission (UPESSC) has published an official notification (Advt. No. 02/2026) inviting online applications for the post of Principal in post-graduate and degree colleges across Uttar Pradesh. This recruitment offers high-level academic administrative roles carrying a Pay Level-14 matrix. Eligible candidates with relevant teaching experience and high research publication profiles can apply online up to 20 August 2026.

Under this recruitment drive, candidates are selected for a initial tenure of 5 years as Principal based on evaluation norms and regulatory standards established by the UGC, BCI (for Law Colleges), and NCTE (for Teacher Training / B.Ed. Colleges). Candidates are urged to register via the official portal, submit necessary research scores, and retain hard copies of documents for verification stages.

The selection framework comprises an objective written examination scheduled for 24 September 2026, followed by academic score evaluation and subsequent selection processes. Candidates are advised to review the college-wise vacancy breakdowns and eligibility requirements thoroughly before submitting the online form.

Eligibility and Qualification

  • Ph.D. Degree: Ph.D. degree in a relevant discipline is compulsory.
  • Teaching/Research Experience: Minimum 15 years of teaching or research experience in a recognized University, College, or Higher Education Institution as Associate Professor or Professor or equivalent.
  • Research Publications: At least 10 research publications in UGC-listed / peer-reviewed journals.
  • Research Score: Minimum 110 research score as per Table 2 norms specified by the commission.
  • Specialized Colleges: For Law Colleges, eligibility complies with Bar Council of India (BCI) norms; for B.Ed. / Teacher Training Colleges, standards comply with NCTE guidelines.
  • Tenure: The appointment will be made for a term of 5 years. Subject to satisfactory evaluation by the University Committee, extension or re-joining in parent cadre at Professor rank will follow rules.

Selection Process

  1. Scrutiny of Online Applications & Evaluation of Academic / Research Score.
  2. Written Examination (Scheduled on 24 September 2026).
  3. Interview / Evaluation as per Commission guidelines.
  4. Document Verification of original certificates, service tenure, and research credentials.
  5. Final Merit List preparation & Appointment.

Exam Pattern

The written examination for UPESSC Principal Recruitment 2026 is scheduled to be conducted on 24.09.2026. The exam evaluates general studies, higher education governance, academic leadership, and administrative norms.

Refer to the official notification PDF for complete topic-wise breakdown and negative marking criteria.

Salary and Benefits

  • Pay Level: Level-14 Matrix.
  • Pay Scale: Rs. 144,200/- to Rs. 218,200/- per month plus applicable allowances.
  • Incentives: Dearness Allowance (DA), House Rent Allowance (HRA), Medical and academic allowances as governed by UP State Higher Education regulations.

Documents Required

  • Scanned Passport Size Photograph & Signature.
  • Class 10th / High School Certificate for Date of Birth verification.
  • Post Graduation Degrees and Marksheets.
  • Ph.D. Degree Award Certificate.
  • Experience Certificates (15 Years Teaching/Research Experience).
  • Proof of 10 Research Publications in peer-reviewed / UGC journals.
  • Documentary proof for 110 Academic/Research Score calculations.
  • Caste / EWS / Domicile Certificate (if applicable for UP candidates).
  • No Objection Certificate (NOC) from existing parent organization/university.

How to Apply

  1. Visit the official portal of UPESSC Principal Recruitment at upessc.up.gov.in or direct link principalhe.upessc.org/otr/.
  2. Complete the One-Time Registration (OTR) process using a valid Email ID and Mobile Number.
  3. Log in with generated Credentials (Registration ID & Password).
  4. Fill out personal, educational, research score, and service experience details accurately.
  5. Upload mandatory scanned documents including photo, signature, Ph.D. certificate, and research proof.
  6. Pay the required application fee of Rs. 3000 online using available payment gateways.
  7. Re-check all filled details in the preview option and submit the form.
  8. Download and print out the confirmation page and application form for future reference.

Why Apply For This Recruitment

The UPESSC Principal recruitment provides an esteemed academic administrative career opportunity within post-graduate and degree colleges across Uttar Pradesh. It commands high organizational respect, offering Pay Level-14 along with an opportunity to lead and elevate institutional academic standards. Senior academicians meeting the 15-year experience and Ph.D. criteria can secure long-term tenure leadership roles under UP State Higher Education Commission governance.
