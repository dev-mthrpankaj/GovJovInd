About This Recruitment

The Cotton Corporation of India Ltd. (CCI) is a premier public sector enterprise functioning under the Ministry of Textiles, Government of India. It performs critical marketing support activities to help agricultural producers get competitive pricing for their kapas produce. This centralized recruitment drive serves to reinforce core competencies across various branch networks spanning numerous market yards nationwide. Candidates chosen will get permanent service opportunities across general administration, accounts processing, technical support, or commercial procurement divisions.

Applying candidates need to possess specific educational criteria corresponding to their chosen profiles, including agricultural sciences, finance streams, commercial accounting, information technology backgrounds, or linguistic specialization. Testing centers are spread wide across eleven critical target cities in major parts of the nation to ease the access parameters. Given the specific timelines allocated, keeping all required academic records ready and completing single registrations without error is essential for securing candidate validity.
