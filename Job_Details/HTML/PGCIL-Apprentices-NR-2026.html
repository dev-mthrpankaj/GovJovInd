About This Recruitment

PGCIL Apprentices NR Recruitment 2026 is an official recruitment drive conducted by the Power Grid Corporation of India Limited (PGCIL) to hire apprentices across the Northern Region under Advertisement No. Apprenticeship/2026-27/NR-I/01. The recruitment covers 35 openings spread across states including Delhi, Haryana, Uttar Pradesh, and Uttarakhand.

Eligible candidates holding an ITI, Diploma, or BE/B.Tech degree in related trades/branches can complete their online application through the official portals. The application window is open from 25 August 2026 to 10 September 2026. This is a great opportunity for candidates seeking apprenticeship training in a premier PSU without any application fee.

Candidates are strongly advised to download the official notification PDF and carefully review the eligibility criteria, state-wise vacancy distribution, and selection process prior to submitting their online forms.

How to Apply

  1. Visit the official website of PGCIL Apprentices NR or the respective apprenticeship portals depending on your qualification.
  2. Click on the "PGCIL Apprentices NR 2026 Apply Online" link once activated on 25/08/2026.
  3. Register yourself using a valid email ID and active mobile number.
  4. Log in using the generated registration ID and password.
  5. Fill the application form carefully with your personal, educational, and category details.
  6. Upload scanned documents, including a recent passport-size photograph and signature, in the prescribed format.
  7. Since there is no application fee, simply verify all the details entered and submit the form.
  8. Download and print the confirmation page for future reference.

Documents Required

  • Recent passport-size colored photograph (scanned).
  • Scanned copy of signature.
  • Educational certificates (ITI/Diploma/Degree mark sheets).
  • Caste/Category certificate (SC/ST/OBC/EWS/PH) if claiming reservation.
  • Valid photo ID proof (Aadhaar Card, PAN Card, Voter ID, or Driving License).
