About This Recruitment

IIT BHU Non Teaching Post Recruitment 2026 is an official recruitment drive conducted by the Indian Institute of Technology (IIT) BHU, Varanasi to hire capable personnel for various Non Teaching roles under Advertisement No. 03/2026-27. Eligibility and Qualification

  • Junior Superintendent: Bachelor’s Degree in any discipline with at least 55% marks, 3 years of relevant experience in Level 5* or 8 years of relevant experience in Level 3* or above.
  • Junior Technician (Group-I): Bachelor’s Degree in Science (with Chemistry / Physics as one of the Subjects) with a minimum of 55% marks.
  • Junior Technician (Group-II): Bachelor’s Degree in Engineering or equivalent in Mechanical / Metallurgical / Ceramic / Materials Science with a minimum of 55% marks.
  • Junior Technician (Group-III): Bachelor’s Degree in Engineering or equivalent in Electrical / Electronics with a minimum of 55% marks.
  • Junior Technician (Group-IV): Bachelor’s Degree in Engineering or equivalent in Civil / Mining or Bachelors in Architecture (B.Arch.) with a minimum of 55% marks.
  • Junior Technician (Group-V): Bachelor’s Degree in Engineering or equivalent in Computer Science / Information Technology with a minimum of 55% marks.
  • Junior Library Assistant: Bachelor’s Degree in Library & Info. Science with 55% marks OR Diploma in Library & Info. Science with 55% marks with Bachelor’s Degree in any discipline OR Two years’ degree in M. Lib. & Info. Sc. (Integrated) with 55% marks.
  • Assistant Engineer: Bachelor’s Degree/ Diploma in Civil/Electrical Engineering with at least 05 years (Degree) or 08 years (Diploma) of relevant post qualification experience at Level – 6* or above.
  • Junior Engineer: B.E./B.Tech. with at least 55% marks in Civil with minimum 02 years post qualification experience in relevant field.
  • Junior Translation Officer: Bachelor’s degree in Hindi with relevant experience of three years’ translation work (English to Hindi and Hindi to English) of technical and nontechnical content.

Please review the official notification for comprehensive experience parameters and equivalent grading requirements.

How to Apply

  1. Visit the official website of IIT BHU Non Teaching Post at iitbhu.ac.in/recruitment_nf.
  2. Click on the “IIT BHU Non Teaching Post Apply Online” Link.
  3. Register using a valid email ID and mobile number to generate login credentials.
  4. Login using your registration ID and password.
  5. Fill the application form carefully with your personal, educational, and category details.
  6. Upload scanned documents (photo, signature, etc.) as per the instructed dimensions and format.
  7. Pay the application fee through the online payment gateway (if applicable to your category).
  8. Submit the form and download/print the confirmation page. Send the hard copy of the application if mentioned in the notification directives before the deadline (24/09/2026).

Documents Required

  • Recent passport-size colored photograph (scanned).
  • Scanned signature.
  • Educational degree certificates and mark sheets (10th, 12th, Graduation, Diploma/B.Tech).
  • Relevant experience certificates.
  • Caste/Category certificate (SC/ST/OBC/EWS/PH) if claiming reservation or fee exemption.
  • Valid photo ID proof (Aadhaar Card, PAN Card, Voter ID, Driving License).
