Recruitment Snapshot

SSC CGL 2026 Details

  • SSC CGL 2026 will fill various Group B and Group C posts in Central Government departments and offices.
  • The notice mentions approximately 12,256 tentative vacancies. Updated post-wise and category-wise vacancies will be available on the SSC website.
  • Selection will be through Tier-I and Tier-II Computer Based Examination followed by post/department procedures such as document verification.
  • Applications must be submitted online only through https://ssc.gov.in or through the mySSC mobile application.

Selection Process

  1. 1

    Candidate submits online application through SSC portal and appears in Tier-I Computer Based Examination.

  2. 2

    Shortlisted candidates appear in Tier-II Computer Based Examination. Paper-I is compulsory for all posts.

  3. 3

    Paper-II is for candidates shortlisted for JSO / Statistical Investigator posts. Paper-III is for AAO / Assistant Accounts Officer posts.

  4. 4

    Final selection and post allocation will be based on merit-cum-preference, eligibility, document verification and post-specific standards.

How to Apply

  1. 1

    Visit ssc.gov.in or use the mySSC mobile application and complete One-Time Registration if not already done on the new SSC portal.

  2. 2

    Login using Registration Number and Password and open the Combined Graduate Level Examination, 2026 apply link under Latest Notifications.

  3. 3

    Fill qualification, category, exam centre preference and other required details carefully. Candidates may opt for Aadhaar based authentication during OTR/application process.

  4. 4

    Capture live photograph as instructed and upload signature in JPEG/JPG format of 10 KB to 20 KB. Photo should be without cap, mask or spectacles.

  5. 5

    Preview the application, accept declaration, pay fee if applicable and keep a printout / copy of application form for future reference.

Important Instructions

  • Applications must be submitted online only through the SSC website or mySSC mobile application.
  • Old OTR generated on ssc.nic.in will not work for the new SSC website; candidates must use the new SSC portal OTR.
  • Women candidates and eligible SC, ST, PwBD and Ex-Servicemen candidates are exempted from application fee.
  • Final post allocation depends on merit-cum-preference and fulfilment of post-specific physical, medical and educational standards.
  • Answer key challenges, if allowed, will be submitted online within the stipulated period with fee as prescribed by SSC.
  • There is no provision for re-evaluation or re-checking of scores of any stage/tier of the examination.
  • Candidates should verify all details from the official SSC notification before final submission.
