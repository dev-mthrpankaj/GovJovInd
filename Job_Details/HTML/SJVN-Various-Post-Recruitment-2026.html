About This Recruitment

Satluj Jal Vidyut Nigam Limited (SJVN), a Mini Ratna Category-I Central Public Sector Enterprise (CPSE) under the Ministry of Power, Government of India, has invited online applications for Executive cadre positions under Advertisement No. SJVN:127/2026. These positions are primarily opened for execution and management duties in thermal, hydro, and renewable energy domains including its subsidiary STPL for the 1320 MW Buxar Thermal Power Project in Bihar.

The total number of notified vacancies is 21, spread across key technical streams such as Mechanical, Electrical, Control & Instrumentation (C&I), and Chemical. Posts include Chief General Manager (E8a Level), Deputy Manager (E4 Level), and Engineer (E2 Level). The recruitment seeks candidates possessing relevant engineering or post-graduate qualifications along with post-qualification executive experience as prescribed in the detailed notification.

The recruitment process involves online registration followed by submission of the physical application along with self-attested documents. Candidates are selected through screening of qualifications and experience followed by a personal interview, document verification, and pre-employment medical checks. Eligible and interested candidates must ensure complete adherence to deadlines for both online submission and hard copy dispatch.

Eligibility and Qualification

  • Chief General Manager / E8a (Electrical/Mechanical): Full-time regular Bachelor Degree in Electrical / Electrical & Electronics / Mechanical Engineering from a recognized University or Institute with relevant post-qualification executive experience.
  • Deputy Manager / E4 (Mechanical): Full-time regular Bachelor Degree in Mechanical Engineering with relevant post-qualification executive experience.
  • Engineer / E2 (Mechanical): Full-time regular Bachelor Degree in Mechanical Engineering from a recognized University/Institution.
  • Deputy Manager / E4 (Electrical): Full-time regular Bachelor Degree in Electrical or Electrical & Electronics Engineering with relevant experience.
  • Engineer / E2 (Electrical): Full-time regular Bachelor Degree in Electrical or Electrical & Electronics Engineering.
  • Deputy Manager / E4 (C&I): Full-time regular Bachelor Degree in Electrical / Electrical & Electronics / Instrumentation & Control / Electronics & Instrumentation Engineering with executive experience.
  • Engineer / E2 (C&I): Full-time regular Bachelor Degree in Electrical / Electrical & Electronics / Instrumentation & Control / Electronics & Instrumentation Engineering.
  • Deputy Manager / E4 (Chemical): Full-time regular Bachelor Degree in Chemical Engineering or M.Sc. in Chemistry from a recognized University/Institution with experience.
  • Engineer / E2 (Chemical): Full-time regular Bachelor Degree in Chemical Engineering or M.Sc. in Chemistry.

Selection Process

  1. Shortlisting and screening of online applications based on educational credentials and experience criteria.
  2. Personal Interview for shortlisted candidates conducted by SJVN selection board.
  3. Document Verification of original academic, experience, identity, and caste certificates.
  4. Pre-Employment Medical Examination as per SJVN medical standards.
  5. Final merit list prepared based on interview performance and verified eligibility credentials.

Exam Pattern

Selection for these executive posts is driven by screening and personal interviews. Should SJVN organize a preliminary written test/screening test due to high candidate volume, details will be communicated directly on the official SJVN portal. Candidates are advised to regularly check the official notification and website updates.

  • Benefits include Industrial Dearness Allowance (IDA), Cafeteria allowances, HRA/Leased accommodation, Provident Fund, Gratuity, Pension, and Medical coverage as per SJVN rules.

How to Apply

  1. Visit the official website of SJVN Ltd at sjvn.nic.in and click on Careers -> Current Job Openings.
  2. Select Advertisement No. SJVN:127/2026 and read the instructions carefully.
  3. Register using a valid email ID and personal mobile number.
  4. Log in using the generated credentials and fill the application form with accurate personal, educational, and experience details.
  5. Upload required scanned documents, photo, and signature in prescribed formats.
  6. Pay the online application fee of Rs. 500 + GST (if applicable).
  7. Submit the online form and download/print the confirmation application page.
  8. Send the printed hard copy along with self-attested documents and fee receipt to: Office of DGM (HR/Recruitment), SJVN Limited, Shakti Sadan, Corporate Head Quarters, Shanan, Shimla, Himachal Pradesh - 171006, so as to reach on or before 15 September 2026.
