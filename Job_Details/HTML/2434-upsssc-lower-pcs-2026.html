Selection Process

  1. 1

    PET Shortlisting: Candidates are initial-screened strictly based on their Normalized Scores in the Preliminary Eligibility Test (PET-2025).

  2. 2

    Main Written Exam: Shortlisted applicants must pay the supplementary examination fees to access the hall ticket for the Combined Objective Test.

  3. 3

    Skill / Typing Test: Applicable only for specific designations (like Clerk-cum-Typist) requiring technical assessment.

  4. 4

    Document Verification: Original educational scorecards, caste credentials, and EWS certificates (issued between 01-04-2026 and 18-06-2026) are scrutinized before final allocation.

Examination Plan & Syllabus

The written examination consists of a single objective question paper carrying 100 questions with a maximum duration of 2 Hours (120 Minutes). There is a negative marking of 25 percent (1/4th mark) for every wrong answer.

How to Apply Online

  1. 1Navigate to the UPSSSC portal homepage: upsssc.gov.in.
  2. 2Click on the applicant login authentication module using your valid PET-2025 Registration Number through Personal Details or OTP mode.
  3. 3Verify pre-fetched data elements (Photo and Signature are imported automatically from PET-2025 records and cannot be altered).
  4. 4Fill out mandatory academic variables and dynamic choice fields for department allocation preferences.
  5. 5Tick checkboxes in the digital declaration module, fill out the dynamic CAPTCHA security code, and click Save.
  6. 6Pay the online processing fee of Rs. 25/- using Credit Card, Debit Card, Internet Banking, UPI, or State Bank E-Challan.
  7. 7Download and print the final submitted application form ledger for subsequent documentation rounds.
