About This Recruitment

BSIP Lucknow Scientist B Recruitment 2026 has been officially announced by the Birbal Sahni Institute of Palaeosciences (BSIP), an autonomous institution under the Department of Science and Technology (DST), Government of India. The recruitment drive aims to fill 19 vacancies for the post of Scientist B under Advertisement No. BSIP/Scientist-B/01/2026. Interested and eligible candidates can submit their online application forms between 10 August 2026 and 04 September 2026.

This scientific recruitment drive offers a prestigious career opportunity for postgraduates and doctoral researchers in Earth Sciences, Geology, Bio Sciences, Physics, Chemistry, and related disciplines. Candidates with a M.Sc. degree in First Division or a Ph.D. in a relevant subject are eligible to participate in the selection process.

The recruitment process includes online registration, document verification, application screening, and subsequent evaluation stages as defined by BSIP guidelines. Candidates are advised to go through the detailed official notification to understand post-wise specialization requirements, selection process stages, and submission procedures.

Eligibility and Educational Qualification

  • M.Sc. or Equivalent: Candidate must possess an M.Sc. degree or recognized equivalent qualification with First Division in Bio Sciences, Geology, Physics, Chemistry, or an allied discipline in Earth Sciences.
  • OR Ph.D. Degree: Candidates possessing a Ph.D. in a related subject/field are also eligible to apply.
  • Candidates must ensure their degree is awarded by a recognized University or Institution on or before the crucial cutoff date.

Selection Process

The selection procedure for BSIP Lucknow Scientist B recruitment consists of the following sequential stages:

  1. Shortlisting / Screening of Applications

    Initial screening of received application forms based on educational qualification, academic records, and specialization alignment.

  2. Written Test / Presentation

    Shortlisted candidates may be called for a written test, research presentation, or domain assessment as per BSIP norms.

  3. Personal Interview

    Final evaluation through a personal interview conducted by the Selection Committee at BSIP Lucknow.

  4. Document Verification & Medical Fitness

    Verification of original educational, age, and category certificates followed by standard medical examination prior to appointment.

How to Apply for BSIP Lucknow Scientist B Vacancy 2026

  1. Visit the official website of Birbal Sahni Institute of Palaeosciences (BSIP) at bsip.res.in.
  2. Click on the "BSIP Lucknow Scientist B 2026 Apply Online" recruitment link on the homepage/career section.
  3. Register yourself using a valid email ID and active mobile number.
  4. Log in using the generated Registration ID and Password.
  5. Fill the application form carefully with your personal details, educational qualifications, and category details.
  6. Upload scanned copies of required documents including passport-size photograph and signature in the prescribed format.
  7. Pay the application fee through online mode (Debit Card / Credit Card / Net Banking / UPI), if applicable.
  8. Review all entered information, submit the application form, and download/print the final confirmation page for future reference.

Documents Required

  • Scanned copy of recent passport-size photograph and signature.
  • 10th / High School Certificate for Date of Birth proof.
  • Educational Certificates and Marksheets (M.Sc. / Ph.D. degree certificates).
  • Caste / Category Certificate (SC / ST / OBC / EWS), if applicable.
  • PWD / Disability Certificate (if applying under disability quota).
  • Experience / Publication records (if applicable).
  • Valid photo ID proof (Aadhaar Card, PAN Card, Voter ID, Passport, etc.).
