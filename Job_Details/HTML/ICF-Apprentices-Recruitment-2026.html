About This Recruitment

Integral Coach Factory ICF Apprentices Recruitment 2026 is an official recruitment drive announced by Integral Coach Factory (ICF) Chennai under Advertisement No. App/01/2026-27. The drive aims to fill 1010 posts for Various Trade Apprentices, divided between Freshers (330 posts including MLT) and Ex-ITI candidates (680 posts including PASAA).

The online application process began on 08 August 2026 and will remain active until 07 September 2026. Eligible candidates can submit their online applications directly through the official website by providing required educational, personal, and category details along with scanned photographs and signatures.

Selection for ICF Trade Apprentices is based on merit prepared on the basis of marks obtained in Class 10th and ITI qualification as applicable. Detailed stipend and trade-wise training conditions are detailed in the official notification.

Eligibility & Qualification

  • For Freshers: Must have passed Class 10th (Matriculation) under 10+2 system with a minimum of 50% aggregate marks including Science & Mathematics. For Carpenter, Painter & Welder trades, 10th pass with 50% marks is required.
  • For Ex-ITI Candidates: Must have passed Class 10th with minimum 50% marks along with a National Trade Certificate (NCVT/SCVT) in the relevant trade.
  • For Medical Laboratory Technician (MLT): Must have passed 10+2 Intermediate examination with Physics, Chemistry, and Biology (PCB) stream.
  • For PASAA: Class 10th pass with 50% marks and ITI Certificate in Computer Operator & Programming Assistant (COPA) / IT / Database System Assistant / Software Testing Assistant.

How to Apply

  1. Visit the official website of Integral Coach Factory (ICF) at icf.indianrailways.gov.in.
  2. Click on the "ICF Apprentices 2026 Apply Online" link available on the portal.
  3. Register using a valid email ID and active mobile number.
  4. Log in using the generated Registration ID and password.
  5. Fill in the application form carefully with personal, educational, and category details.
  6. Upload scanned copies of required documents, recent passport size photograph, and signature.
  7. Pay the application fee of Rs.100 online if applicable (SC/ST/PH/Female candidates are exempted).
  8. Submit the application form and download/print the final confirmation page for future reference.

Documents Required

  • Scanned recent passport-size photograph and signature in JPG format.
  • Class 10th (Matriculation) mark sheet and certificate showing date of birth and 50% aggregate marks.
  • ITI National Trade Certificate (NCVT/SCVT) and mark sheet (for Ex-ITI posts).
  • 10+2 Intermediate mark sheet with PCB stream (for MLT posts).
  • Community / Caste Certificate (SC / ST / OBC / EWS) in prescribed format, if applicable.
  • Disability Certificate (PWD/PH), if applicable.
  • Valid ID Proof (Aadhaar Card, Voter ID, PAN Card, etc.).
