About This Recruitment

Southern Railway Act Apprentice Recruitment 2026 is an apprenticeship training engagement under the Apprentices Act, 1961 for 4,471 slots across designated trades in Southern Railway divisions, workshops and units. The vacancy includes 163 Fresher slots and 4,308 Ex-ITI slots.

Online applications are open from 28 August 2026 to 27 September 2026 up to 5:00 PM. Only candidates residing within the notified geographical jurisdiction of Southern Railway are eligible to apply: Tamil Nadu, Puducherry, Kerala, Andaman & Nicobar Islands, Lakshadweep, SPSR Nellore and Chittoor districts of Andhra Pradesh, and Dakshina Kannada district of Karnataka.

This notification is for apprenticeship training and does not guarantee employment after completion of training. Selection is through a merit list based on qualifying examination marks, followed by document verification and fulfilment of medical/physical fitness requirements.
