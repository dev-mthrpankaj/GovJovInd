About This Recruitment

The Higher Education Department of Bihar along with BSUSC has initialized this direct rapid-hiring drive for Contractual Faculty positions. This is aimed at fulfilling immediate educational requirements inside existing standard state universities alongside 211 newly created Rajkiya Degree Colleges setup across different districts in Bihar. Applications are completely digital and are managed exclusively via the customized Samarth online platform setup for higher education recruitment in the state.

This contractual engagement is focused entirely on merit based shortlisting using detailed academic breakups from Secondary school level exams right up to Post-Graduation degrees, additional weightage parameters like Ph.D. and Junior Research Fellowships (JRF), followed by interactive demonstrations. The hired candidates will directly contribute to university-level pedagogical activities and structural academic assignments across multiple disciplines.

Eligibility and Essential Qualifications

Every prospective applicant needs to strictly meet the dual parameters of standard academic track percentage minimums combined with specified national clearing eligibility clearances:

  • Master's Level Requirement: Must possess a Master's degree or equivalent in a relevant core/allied subject with at least 55% marks (Unreserved / EWS categories).
  • Marks Relaxation: A relaxation edge of 5% marks is allowed at the Master's level (bringing the minimum threshold to 50%) for SC, ST, BC (Non-Creamy Layer), EBC (Non-Creamy Layer), and Physically Challenged variants belonging to Bihar State. Only one relaxation variant can be leveraged.
  • Mandatory Competitive Clearance: Must have successfully cleared the National Eligibility Test (NET) managed by UGC / CSIR, or an equivalent test accredited by the UGC.
  • Exemption Criteria from NET: Candidates possessing a valid Ph.D. degree designed under UGC Minimum Regulations 2009 or 2016 guidelines are exempted from NET eligibility. Regular mode Ph.D. candidates registered prior to 2009 need to present a verified 5-point certificate validated by the Registrar or Dean of their respective award institution.

Selection Process & Performance Weightage Scheme

Shortlisting framework eliminates subjective ranking variables by utilizing a specific 100-point metric, broken down between basic criteria performance charts and interactive skills verification:

  1. Academic and Research Screening Score Matrix (88 Max Marks): Shortlisting scores generated purely via entries submitted by candidates in the online application interface.
    • Post Graduation Degree Level: Max 26 Marks
    • Graduation Degree Level: Max 17 Marks
    • Intermediate (10+2) Marks: calculated based on formula $(13/100) \times \text{Percentage Obtained}$ (Max 13 Marks)
    • Matriculation (10th) Marks: calculated based on formula $(10/100) \times \text{Percentage Obtained}$ (Max 10 Marks)
    • Research Credentials (Ph.D. / JRF / NET Mix): Max 22 Marks (Combinations: Ph.D. + JRF = 22; Ph.D. + NET = 19; NET Only = 8)
  2. Shortlist Ratio Calculation: Qualified applicants will be summoned for interactive processing at a fixed ratio of 1:3 per open post. Tie positions at the borderline position will result in all matching score profiles getting advanced together.
  3. Interview-cum-Demonstration Stage (12 Max Marks): Scheduled to evaluate specialized delivery traits.
    • Teaching Skill Presentation on Spot (Mandatorily Video-Recorded): Max 6 Marks
    • Direct Panel Interaction & Domain Competency Viva: Max 6 Marks

Salary Structure and Job Tenure Conditions

  • Monthly Salary Package: Equivalent to the precise initial entry monthly gross basic structural payout (Basic Pay component added with current applicable Dearness Allowance (DA) only) corresponding to a regularly deployed entry-level permanent Assistant Professor.
  • Tenure Guidelines: The tenure status remains entirely temporary on a contract workflow structure. The contract holds valid across one academic term sequence. Extensions or subsequent session assignments are evaluated based on performance assessment reviews and institutional demand requirements. Continuation is not automated.

How to File the Online Application Form

  1. Direct your web browser toward the official state centralized registration portal system at https://hedbiharrec.samarth.edu.in.
  2. Complete initial setup parameters to construct your baseline applicant identity profile on the Samarth digital university network.
  3. Navigate toward the application window and carefully key in your educational marks breakdown precisely in percentage numbers format. If your institution awarded CGPA, you must convert it using the official conversion formula of your board.
  4. Upload digital scans matching baseline parameters for all verification documents, including the mandatory non-judicial stamp paper Notarised Affidavit.
  5. Enter your 10 Distinct Unique State University Preferences in descending sequence order within the additional structural configuration settings tab. Ensure no duplicates are recorded.
  6. Perform a comprehensive visual draft check to cross verify input details, ensure file sizes align, and submit the portal profile before the system locks down on 15 July 2026. Keep printed physical records of your submitted file for structural cross-referencing during interview reporting.

Frequently Asked Questions (FAQs)

What is the last date to apply for BSUSC Contractual Faculty 2026?

The last date for submission of online applications is 15 July 2026.

How many total vacancies are available under this notification?

There are a total of 3,687 vacancies announced across various subjects and universities in Bihar.

What is the application fee for BSUSC Contractual Faculty?

There is no application fee for filling out the online application portal.

What is the age limit required for this recruitment?

Candidates must be between 21 and 43 years of age as on 01.08.2026, with age relaxations applicable for reserved categories.

Is NET qualification mandatory for all applicants?

No, candidates who hold a valid Ph.D. degree compliant with UGC 2009/2016 regulations or those in disciplines where NET is not conducted can be exempted.

How will candidates be selected for the final merit list?

Selection is based on 100 marks total: 88 marks for Academic Qualifications and 12 marks for the Interview-cum-demonstration.

Where can candidates apply for this position?

Applications must be submitted exclusively via the online Samarth Recruitment Portal at hedbiharrec.samarth.edu.in.

Is a domicile certificate necessary to claim reservation?

Yes, reservation benefits and relaxations are strictly limited to native residents who hold a valid Bihar Domicile Certificate.
