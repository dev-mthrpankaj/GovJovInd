About This Recruitment

The Uttar Pradesh Expressways Industrial Development Authority (UPIDA), Lucknow, operating under the Government of Uttar Pradesh, has announced a recruitment drive for Driver positions. This hiring process is facilitated via the UP Rojgar Sangam Sewayojan portal in partnership with service provider PCS Fintec Consultancy Pvt. Ltd. The drive aims to fill 50 vacant Driver posts to support official transit, expressway operational needs, and administrative vehicle handling across Uttar Pradesh.

Interested candidates possessing High School (10th) qualification from a recognized board alongside valid driving credentials can submit their applications online. The application portal requires job seekers to register on the official Sewayojan portal, create an applicant profile, and select the specific UPIDA Driver vacancy listing. Because this process is managed online, applicants should ensure all personal details, contact information, and educational credentials match their legal documents perfectly.

Since these posts are managed under contractual/outsourced guidelines through the Sewayojan frame, candidates are advised to thoroughly inspect the terms of employment, deployment locations, monthly remuneration range, and skill prerequisites. Timely application before the short submission window closes is essential, as portal registrations close strictly according to the advertised timetable.

Eligibility and Qualification

  • Educational Qualification: Must have passed SSC / High School (Class 10th) or equivalent examination from a recognized Board.
  • Driving License: Valid Driving License (LMV / HMV as required for official vehicle operation).
  • Experience: Practical driving experience and familiarization with route navigation/vehicle maintenance is preferred.
  • Portal Eligibility: Candidate must be registered as a job seeker on the UP Sewayojan Portal (sewayojan.up.nic.in).

Selection Process

  1. Shortlisting: Initial screening based on Sewayojan portal profile, educational merit, and application order.
  2. Driving Skill Test: Practical driving test and physical verification of driving proficiency.
  3. Document Verification: Verification of original educational certificates, driving license, ID proof, and Sewayojan registration printout.
  4. Final Joining: Issuance of deployment order by the contracted agency (PCS Fintec Consultancy Pvt. Ltd.) for UPIDA Lucknow.

Exam Pattern & Selection Norms

Generally, outsourced positions on the UP Sewayojan portal do not involve a written competitive examination. Selection is conducted via skill assessment (Driving Test) and document screening. Candidates should refer to the job card description on the official portal for specific updates.

  • Employment Nature: Outsourced / Contractual under UPIDA Lucknow through PCS Fintec Consultancy Pvt. Ltd.
  • Other Benefits: As governed by UP Sewayojan contract conditions and statutory norms.

How to Apply

  1. Go to the official UP Sewayojan Portal at sewayojan.up.nic.in/jobs.aspx.
  2. If you are a new user, complete the Jobseeker Registration process to obtain your Username and Password.
  3. Log in to your candidate dashboard using your credentials.
  4. Navigate to the "Outsourced Jobs" (आउटसोर्स नौकरियां) section.
  5. Filter search by Department "UPIDA" or Company "PCS Fintec Consultancy Pvt. Ltd." or Post "Driver".
  6. Locate the UPIDA Lucknow Driver Online Form 2026 job post.
  7. Carefully read the requirements and click on the "Apply" (आवेदन करें) button.
  8. Submit the application and save the confirmation/application record for future reference.
