About This Recruitment

The Bihar Police Sub-Ordinate Services Commission (BPSSC) is managing the competitive selection process for the position of Forest Range Officer (Vano ke Kshetra Padahikari) under the Environment, Forest & Climate Change Department. This role falls into an essential administrative and ecological execution tier within the state services. Selected candidates are responsible for ensuring forest protection, field execution of environment policies, and managing range-level personnel within their allocated jurisdictions.

The recruitment application window runs entirely online through the commissions designated tab. Aspirants across India are eligible to apply, but specific statutory reservation metrics apply specifically to the native residents of Bihar state. The eligibility demands a strong background in scientific disciplines, making it a targeted opening for graduates who qualify through engineering, agriculture, forestry, or foundational sciences.

The assessment standard encompasses a structured timeline containing multi-staged evaluations. Candidates face an initial screening via a objective written format, followed by deep personal assessment during an interview. Finally, because the job demands rigorous field presence, candidates are filtered strictly based on physical attributes and walking endurance performance guidelines established under department service rules.

Eligibility and Qualification

  • Candidates must hold Indian nationality.
  • Possess a valid graduation degree achieved on or before the cut-off date of 01.08.2025.
  • The degree must be a Bachelor's degree with at least one subject among Animal Husbandry & Veterinary Science, Botany, Chemistry, Geology, Mathematics, Physics, Statistics, and Zoology.
  • Alternatively, a Bachelor's degree in Agriculture, Forestry, or Engineering specializations from recognized institutions/universities is fully acceptable.

Selection Process

  1. Written Competitive Examination: Consists of two comprehensive papers evaluating linguistic skill and subject intelligence.
  2. Personal Interview: Conducted for selected candidates reaching the threshold performance index.
  3. Physical Endurance Test (PET): Standard evaluation check on walking limits and mandatory height parameters.
  4. Medical Review: Verification of structural soundness by an active medical committee.
  5. Document Verification: Check of original credentials, permanent residential metrics, and category certifications.

Exam Pattern

The written evaluation consists of objective multiple-choice formats divided into two papers. Negative markings are specifically distinct per paper structure.
