About This Recruitment

The Institute of Banking Personnel Selection (IBPS) conducts the Common Recruitment Process (CRP) every year to recruit clerical cadre staff in public sector banks across India. Under the CRP CSA-XVI 2026-27 drive, the designation has been designated as Customer Service Associate (CSA) / Clerk. This is one of the most sought-after national banking opportunities for fresh graduates aspiring to build a stable administrative career in public sector banking.

Selected candidates are responsible for front-desk customer operations, cash handling, documentation, account openings, and general clerical assistance across participating banks nationwide. Since state-wise vacancies are released, proficiency in the local language of the applied state or union territory is an essential parameter during the final joining process.

The selection mechanism is two-tiered, consisting of an Online Preliminary Examination followed by an Online Mains Examination. There is no personal interview round for clerical cadre positions, making final selection purely merit-driven based on performance in the Mains examination subject to meeting qualifying standards in language proficiency and document verification.

Eligibility and Qualification

  • Educational Degree: Bachelor's Degree (Graduation) in any discipline from a recognized University or equivalent qualification recognized as such by the Central Government.
  • Computer Literacy: Operating and working knowledge in computer systems is mandatory (Certificate/Diploma/Degree in computer operations/language or studied Computer/IT as a subject in High School/College).
  • Language Proficiency: Proficiency in the official local language of the State/UT for which candidate applies is preferred.

Selection Process

  1. Phase-I: Preliminary Online Examination (Objective Test - 100 Marks) for shortlisting candidates.
  2. Phase-II: Mains Online Examination (Objective Test - 200 Marks) for final merit listing.
  3. Local Language Test (LPT): Verification of local language proficiency conducted by participating banks.
  4. Document Verification & Medical Examination: Checking original certificates, identity proofs, and medical fitness.

Exam Pattern

The preliminary examination consists of objective tests for 100 marks conducted online with sectional timing. Penalty for wrong answers is 0.25 marks per incorrect response.

How to Apply

  1. Visit the official IBPS portal at ibps.in.
  2. Click on the link "CRP Clerical / CSA-XVI" on the homepage.
  3. Choose "Click here for New Registration" and enter basic credentials (Name, Phone, Email).
  4. Note down the generated Provisional Registration Number and Password received via SMS and Email.
  5. Log in and complete the application form by filling personal, academic, and state selection details accurately.
  6. Upload required documents including photo, signature, left thumb impression, and handwritten declaration.
  7. Pay the requisite application fee using online payment gateway options.
  8. Preview the final application, submit, and save a printed copy of the application form for reference.
