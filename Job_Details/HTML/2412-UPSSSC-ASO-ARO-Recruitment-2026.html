About This Recruitment

The Uttar Pradesh Subordinate Services Selection Commission (UPSSSC), based in Lucknow, has announced the Assistant Statistical Officer (ASO) and Assistant Research Officer (ARO) Statistics Main Examination 2026 under advertisement code 05-Exam/2026. This extensive staffing exercise aims to fill 929 positional vacancies distributed across multiple foundational state directorates and departments. It presents a vital gateway for statistics, commerce, and mathematics postgraduates aiming to secure specialized administrative profiles within government infrastructure.

Shortlisting for this competitive main examination will strictly rely on the normalized performance score cards acquired during the Preliminary Eligibility Test (PET-2025). Aspirants holding non-zero, positive structural marks are permitted to login and complete the process. The administrative flow requires verified candidates to register through secure online application infrastructure. Applications via physical mail or secondary modes are not entertained by the commission.

Beyond elementary test performance, this recruitment involves thorough scrutiny of professional academic specializations, local residency validations, and technical computer training compliance. Aspirants should meticulously review the specific equivalent degree classifications acknowledged by the state authorities before making a formal submittal to ensure compliance during final document screening phases.

Eligibility and Qualification

  • Mandatory PET Prerequisite: Candidates must have an active score card from the UPSSSC PET 2025 verification cycles with positive, valid normalized marks.
  • Core Academic Requirement: Postgraduate Degree in Mathematics, Mathematical Statistics, Commerce, Economics, or Statistics from an institute established by law in India, or an equivalent qualification explicitly recognized by the state (such as MBA Finance/HR, M.Com, M.Sc Applied Math, or specific programs listed in official orders dated 24 June 2024).
  • Technical Qualification: Must hold an 'O' Level Computer training credential awarded by the DOEACC/NIELIT Society, or a minimum 1-year academic training diploma in Computer Science from a recognized board or university structure.
  • Language Competence: Practical, clear working knowledge of the Hindi language written in the standard Devnagari structural script.

Selection Process

  1. Shortlisting of registered applicants based on their UPSSSC Preliminary Eligibility Test (PET-2025) performance metrics.
  2. Main Written Examination featuring objective multiple-choice questionnaires.
  3. Comprehensive Document Verification (DV) check to cross-verify core postgraduate specializations and statutory certificates.
  4. Final preparation of the selection merit list using clean academic performance tallies and applicable vertical/horizontal reservation rules.

Frequently Asked Questions

What is UPSSSC ASO ARO Recruitment 2026?

It is a main examination conducted by the Uttar Pradesh Subordinate Services Selection Commission for recruiting 929 Assistant Statistical Officer and Assistant Research Officer (Statistics) posts across various government departments.

What is the deadline to submit the online form?

The last date for submitting online applications and paying the application process fee is 11 May 2026.

How many vacancies are announced in total?

A total of 929 posts are announced, including both regular selection and special selection categories across multiple departments.

What is the mandatory score card required to apply?

Candidates must possess a valid score card from the Preliminary Eligibility Test (PET-2025) with a positive normalized score to be eligible for shortlisting.

What is the educational qualification needed for ASO and ARO?

Candidates need a Postgraduate degree in Mathematics, Mathematical Statistics, Commerce, Economics, or Statistics from a recognized university, or an equivalent degree recognized by the government.

Is there any computer certificate requirement?

Yes, candidates must possess an 'O' Level diploma from the DOEACC/NIELIT Society, or at least a 1-year diploma in Computer Science from a recognized institution.

What is the age limit for this recruitment?

The age limit is 21 to 40 years as of 1 July 2026, with upper age limit relaxations for reserved categories as per state regulations.

What is the application fee at the initial stage?

All categories need to pay an online processing fee of Rs. 25 at the registration stage. Main exam fees will be collected separately from shortlisted candidates.

What is the structure of the selection test?

Selection is based entirely on a written examination consisting of 100 objective-type multiple-choice questions carrying 1 mark each, with a 2-hour duration.

Where can I register or modify my form?

Candidates can log in via the official UPSSSC student portal at upsssc.gov.in using their PET 2025 authentication parameters.
