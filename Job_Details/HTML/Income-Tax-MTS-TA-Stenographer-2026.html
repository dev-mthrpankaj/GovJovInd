Income Tax Pune Region Recruitment 2026 Overview

Income Tax MTS, Tax Assistant and Stenographer Recruitment 2026 is a sports-quota recruitment listed for the Income Tax Department Pune Region. The source lists a total of 85 posts across Stenographer Grade-II, Tax Assistant and Multi-Tasking Staff. The online application window runs from 15 August 2026 to 15 September 2026.
