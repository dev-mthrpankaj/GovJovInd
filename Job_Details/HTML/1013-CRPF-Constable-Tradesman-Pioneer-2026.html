About This Recruitment

CRPF Constable Technical, Tradesmen and Pioneer Recruitment 2026 is a central force recruitment for multiple trades including Driver, Motor Mechanic Vehicle, Cook, Water Carrier, Barber, Safai Karamchari, bands, trades and Pioneer Wing Mason/Electrician posts.

The recruitment is conducted on a domicile-based pattern for Technical and Tradesmen vacancies, while Pioneer Wing vacancies are on an all-India basis. Candidates should choose the correct State/UT and trade carefully because domicile and trade eligibility are verified later.

Selection includes physical stages, computer-based test, trade/skill test, document verification and medical examination. Final result is based on CBT performance, subject to qualifying all other stages and satisfying eligibility conditions.

Eligibility and Qualification

  • Driver: Minimum Matriculation or equivalent and Heavy Transport Vehicle Driving License; must pass driving test.
  • Mechanic Motor Vehicle: Minimum Matriculation / 10th class and ITI / apprenticeship qualification with practical experience as stated in the notice.
  • Other Tradesmen: Minimum Matriculation or equivalent and proficiency / work in the respective trade.
  • Pioneer Wing Mason / Electrician: Matriculation or equivalent and one-year experience in respective trade; ITI trade certificate gets preference.
  • Qualification must be acquired by the cut-off date mentioned in the notice.

Selection Process

  1. Physical Efficiency Test (PET).
  2. Physical Standard Test (PST).
  3. Computer Based Test (CBT).
  4. Trade / Skill Test.
  5. Document Verification.
  6. Detailed Medical Examination / Review Medical Examination.
  7. Final selection based on CBT marks subject to qualifying other stages.

Exam Pattern

The CBT consists of one objective-type paper containing 100 questions for 100 marks. It is conducted in English and Hindi only. PET/PST qualified candidates are shortlisted for CBT.
