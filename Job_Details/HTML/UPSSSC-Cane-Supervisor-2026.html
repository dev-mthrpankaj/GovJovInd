About This Recruitment

The Uttar Pradesh Subordinate Services Selection Commission (UPSSSC) has officially published Advertisement No. 13/2026 for the recruitment of Cane Supervisors (Ganna Paryavekshak) in the state. This recruitment offers a direct pathway into the state's agricultural and sugarcane administration sector. Eligible candidates who possess a valid UPSSSC Preliminary Eligibility Test (PET) 2025 scorecard can submit their applications online through the official portal.

Selected candidates will serve in key supervisory capacities, monitoring sugarcane production, facilitating departmental schemes, and coordinating between regional farming communities and administration. To be eligible, applicants must hold a Bachelor of Science degree in Agriculture along with a CCC computer certificate issued by NIELIT or DOEACC Society. Shortlisting for the Mains examination will be strictly based on candidates' normalized PET 2025 scores.

Candidates are encouraged to complete their online application, upload the required credentials, and complete the fee payment well before the deadline. Ensure that personal details, registration credentials, and certificates are accurate to avoid discrepancies during the subsequent document verification phase.

Eligibility and Qualification

  • PET Scorecard: Must possess a valid UPSSSC Preliminary Eligibility Test (PET) 2025 Scorecard with non-zero positive marks.
  • Degree Qualification: Bachelor of Science (B.Sc.) in Agriculture from a university or institute recognized by law in India.
  • Computer Certification: CCC (Course on Computer Concepts) examination certificate passed from DOEACC or NIELIT Society.

Selection Process

  1. Shortlisting via PET 2025: Applicants are shortlisted for the Mains Exam based on their normalized PET 2025 scores.
  2. Mains Written Exam: Shortlisted candidates must appear for the UPSSSC Cane Supervisor Mains competitive examination.
  3. Document Verification: Verification of original academic degrees, PET scorecard, caste certificates, and identity proof.
  4. Final Merit List: Preparation of final selection merit list according to marks obtained in the Mains Exam and reservation norms.

Exam Pattern

Detailed syllabus and exam pattern for the Mains examination will be uploaded on the official UPSSSC web portal. Candidates should regularly consult the official website for updates regarding topic distribution, negative marking scheme, and test duration.

Salary and Benefits

  • Pay Level: Pay Matrix Level-4.
  • Pay Scale: Rs. 25,500/- to Rs. 81,000/- plus applicable Dearness Allowance (DA), House Rent Allowance (HRA), and state benefits.
  • Service Group: Group 'C' Non-Gazetted post under Uttar Pradesh State Government rules.

Documents Required

  • UPSSSC PET 2025 Scorecard credentials.
  • Class 10th (High School) Marksheet and Certificate for age verification.
  • Class 12th (Intermediate) Marksheet and Certificate.
  • B.Sc Agriculture Degree Certificate and Marksheets.
  • NIELIT / DOEACC CCC Computer Course Certificate.
  • Recent color passport-sized photograph and signature scan.
  • Caste / Category / EWS Certificate (if applicable).
  • Domicile Certificate of Uttar Pradesh for reservation benefits.
  • Valid government Photo ID proof (Aadhaar Card, PAN Card, Voter ID, etc.).

How to Apply

  1. Visit the official portal of UPSSSC at upsssc.gov.in.
  2. Click on the Live Advertisements / All Notifications section on the homepage.
  3. Locate Advt No. 13/2026 for Cane Supervisor Recruitment and click on 'Apply'.
  4. Authenticate using your UPSSSC PET 2025 Registration Number either via Personal Details or OTP.
  5. Verify your pre-filled details from PET 2025 and fill in the specific qualification information for Cane Supervisor.
  6. Upload the requisite photo and signature if prompted, or verify existing PET records.
  7. Pay the online application processing fee of Rs. 25/- through SBI e-pay gateway.
  8. Submit the application form and download the confirmation page for record-keeping.
