About This Recruitment

The Indira Gandhi Centre for Atomic Research (IGCAR), Kalpakkam, which operates under the Department of Atomic Energy, Government of India, has initiated the recruitment program for ITI Trade Apprentices for the year 2026. This training program complies strictly with the guidelines established under the Apprentices Act, 1961. The project aims to provide practical industrial exposure across various engineering and technical trades to eligible young individuals who have completed their formal ITI certificate training.

Candidates selected for this training program will spend one year at the IGCAR facility in Kalpakkam, Tamil Nadu. The engagement does not promise or provide any guarantee for regular post-training employment within IGCAR or the Department of Atomic Energy. Applicants who have already completed an apprenticeship elsewhere, or who are currently undergoing apprenticeship training in any other institution, are disqualified from applying.

The registration process involves two distinct stages. Every applicant must first possess a unique registration profile on the national Apprenticeship India portal and finish their mandatory Aadhaar e-KYC verification. Once this identity record is successfully verified, the candidate can then navigate to the direct application portal provided by IGCAR to supply their vocational data and complete their submission before the formal closing timeline.

Eligibility and Qualification

  • Candidates must have successfully passed their Class 10th High School examination with a cumulative minimum score of 50% marks.
  • Possession of a formal ITI Pass Certificate in the corresponding relevant trade issued by a recognized NCVT or SCVT institution is mandatory.
  • Applicants must not have undertaken prior apprenticeship training or possess existing industrial experience elsewhere.

Selection Process

  1. Shortlisting is processed exclusively using the marks percentage scored during the ITI qualification course.
  2. In circumstances involving a score tie, applicants showing higher marks percentage during their 10th or 8th standard assessments will secure priority.
  3. If a tie still persists, the older candidate determined via birth records will obtain preference.
  4. Shortlisted candidates will be invited for Document Verification at the designated facility.
  5. Final engagement depends on clearing the standard Medical Examination parameters.

Exam Pattern

Check official notification. There will be no written exam or interview conducted for the recruitment of these apprenticeship positions. Selection is entirely merit-based.

Salary and Benefits

  • Monthly Stipend for 2-Year ITI Training Course Trades: Rs. 11,040/- per month.
  • Monthly Stipend for 1-Year ITI Training Course Trades: Rs. 10,560/- per month.
  • Structured apprentice training certificate valid across government and public sector industries upon completion.

Documents Required for Application

  • Apprenticeship India unique portal registration profile sheet.
  • Scanned Class 10th or 8th marksheets and passing certificates.
  • ITI trade certificate along with consolidated and semester-wise mark sheets.
  • Valid community caste certificates (SC/ST/OBC-NCL) or EWS documents where applicable.
  • Disability certificates issued by credible medical authorities if applying under PwBD status.
  • Clear digital copy of passport-size photograph and candidate signature image.

How to Apply

  1. Navigate to the Apprenticeship India web directory at apprenticeshipindia.gov.in and establish an active profile.
  2. Conclude the compulsory Aadhaar identity verification (e-KYC) on the apprenticeship platform to secure your unique registration code.
  3. Go to the IGCAR application link at igcjrf.in/trade/ during the defined dates.
  4. Supply your unique Apprenticeship registration number and primary credentials.
  5. Complete all individual personal, academic, and specific trade selections accurately.
  6. Upload the necessary educational, community, and identity records formatted as requested in the instructions.
  7. Examine all input elements within the digital form preview to identify mistakes.
  8. Submit your final web application record and retain a physical print out copy of the submission verification page.

Frequently Asked Questions

What is the last date to apply for IGCAR Apprentice Recruitment 2026?

The online application submission closes on 19 August 2026.

How many vacancies are available under IGCAR Trade Apprentice 2026?

There are a total of 200 vacancies across 18 distinct ITI trades.

What is the application fee for IGCAR Apprentice Recruitment?

There is no application fee required for any category candidate.

What is the required age limit for candidates?

Candidates must be between 18 and 24 years old as on 19 August 2026, with age relaxation applicable for reserved categories.

What is the selection process for IGCAR Apprenticeship?

The selection is strictly based on the merit percentage of marks obtained in the ITI course, followed by Document Verification and a Medical Examination.

What is the monthly stipend provided during training?

Apprentices receive a monthly stipend of Rs. 11,040 for a 2-year ITI training course and Rs. 10,560 for a 1-year ITI training course.

Is registration on the Apprenticeship India portal mandatory?

Yes, candidates must register on www.apprenticeshipindia.gov.in and complete Aadhaar e-KYC verification to get a unique registration number before applying.

Where can I apply for IGCAR Apprentice 2026?

Eligible candidates can fill out the online application form on the official website portal at igcjrf.in/trade/ or through the IGCAR website recruitment section.
