About This Recruitment

RBI Grade B is one of the major officer-level recruitments conducted by the Reserve Bank of India Services Board. The 2026 notification covers three streams: General, Department of Economic and Policy Research and Department of Statistics and Information Management.

The recruitment is useful for candidates looking for officer-level roles in India's central bank. Since each stream has different educational requirements and examination papers, applicants should select the stream only after matching their qualification with the official eligibility rules.

The selection process includes Phase-I, Phase-II and Interview. Candidates should plan preparation around objective testing, descriptive writing, stream-specific subjects and interview readiness rather than treating the recruitment as a single preliminary exam.

Eligibility and Qualification

  • Grade B General: Graduation or equivalent technical/professional qualification with 60% marks, 50% for SC/ST/PwBD where reserved, or post-graduation/equivalent with 55% marks as per notification.
  • Grade B DEPR: Master's degree in Economics or Finance-related specified subjects with required marks, or equivalent qualification as listed in the official notification.
  • Grade B DSIM: Master's degree or specified statistical/data science/analytics related qualification with required marks, or equivalent qualification as listed in the official notification.
  • Candidates should verify stream-wise marks, equivalent qualifications and reserved-category relaxations from the official RBI notification.

Selection Process

  1. Submit the online application and pay the applicable fee by the deadline.
  2. Appear in Phase-I examination for the selected stream.
  3. Qualified candidates appear in Phase-II examination as per the stream schedule.
  4. Shortlisted candidates attend the Interview stage.
  5. Final selection is based on marks in Phase-II and Interview, along with applicable official rules.
