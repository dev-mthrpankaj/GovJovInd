About This Recruitment

The Bihar Police Subordinate Services Commission (BPSSC) is managing the competitive selection process for appointing technical professionals into the Bihar Police Radio (Wireless) division. The specialized post of Assistant Sub-Inspector (Technical) falls under Level-5 pay guidelines, demanding specialized engineering diplomas. Eligible Indian nationals from all regions can register, but strict horizontal reservation and age adjustments favor permanent residents of Bihar.

The entry process consists of an online single-form portal system through which applicants complete biometric data verification and registration steps. Since it belongs to a specialized law enforcement tech wing, all eligible candidates face rigorous screening metrics. These encompass dual-tiered objective-type screening exams followed closely by structural physical testing thresholds including track runs, jumps, and equipment throwing metrics.

Securing a high ranking requires fulfilling technical qualification minimums, achieving clearing points on written subjects, and passing all qualifying endurance stages. Candidates should review document guidelines closely-especially pertaining to community classifications and domicile records-to prevent administrative cancellations during the final phase of certificate inspections.

Eligibility and Qualification

  • Applicants must hold Indian Citizenship. Male, female, and third-gender applicants are explicitly invited to register.
  • Candidates must have completed a 3-year Polytechnic Diploma course from a recognized State or Central Government institution by the academic cut-off date of 01.08.2025.
  • Acceptable diploma engineering fields include: Electronics & Communication, Electronics & Tele Communication, Information Technology, or Electrical & Electronics.
  • General, BC, EBC, and EWS applicants require at least 50% aggregate marks in their diploma. SC and ST category candidates require a minimum of 45% aggregate marks.

Selection Process

  1. Preliminary Written Examination: OMR screening exam consisting of general awareness and selected engineering domain questions.
  2. Main Written Examination: Divided into language proficiency testing and deeper technical domain assessments.
  3. Physical Standards and Measurement Verification (PST): Scrutiny of physical height, chest sizes, and weight ratios.
  4. Physical Efficiency Test (PET): Qualifying tracking challenges including distance runs, high jumps, long jumps, and shots-put.
  5. Medical Screening & Background Checks: Disqualifying medical parameter checkups (vision accuracy, flat-foot, color-blindness, stuttering test) alongside regional character verification.
  6. Final Merit Ranking: Prepared strictly on score aggregates achieved within the Main Technical paper (Paper 2) aligned with reservation protocols.

Exam Pattern

Evaluations comprise objective multiple-choice formats with normalized marking distributions across multi-session settings if necessary via the Equi-Percentile Method. Every wrong choice drops score counts by 0.2 marks.

Documents Required

  • Valid personal email identification ID and an active mobile number registered in the applicant's name.
  • Recent colored passport photo (taken within 2 months, white background, size between 15-25 KB in JPEG/PNG format).
  • Scanned legible signature records in both Hindi and English (written with black or blue ink on white backgrounds, 15-25 KB file limit).
  • Matriculation (10th) certificate or equivalent verification proof explicitly displaying date-of-birth variables.
  • 3-Year Polytechnic Engineering Diploma completion degree scroll alongside year/semester marksheets.
  • Valid Character Certificate records and Residential Domicile certificates issued by competent authorities in Bihar.
  • Non-Creamy Layer (NCL) or Category Caste Certificate documentation (married women must provide certificates issued under their father's name and address, not husband's details).
  • EWS income certificates reflecting financial year 2025-2026 calculations where applicable.

How to Apply

  1. Navigate directly to the commission web asset at bpssc.bihar.gov.in and select the dedicated "Bihar Police" section tab.
  2. Click the online application link corresponding to Advt No. 07/2026.
  3. Phase 1 (Registration): Provide your name, nationality, mobile contact number, email ID, Bihar domicile status, category parameters, gender identity, and birth date variables.
  4. Pay the Rs. 100 registration fee online through payment options (Net Banking, Card, UPI). Note down the system-generated Registration ID and Password sent via SMS/Email.
  5. Phase 2 (Application Form): Log in using your credentials to complete administrative details, including parents' names, postal addresses, and diploma credentials.
  6. Upload your scanned passport-size photograph and your signatures in both Hindi and English according to the specified size guidelines.
  7. Review your form entries via the "Edit Details" preview window prior to submission. Confirm your details and submit your application.
  8. Download and print your final application acknowledgment document on an A4 sheet for future verification.

Frequently Asked Questions

What is the application period for Bihar Police ASI Technical Recruitment 2026?

The online application process begins on 21 May 2026 and ends on 21 June 2026.

How many vacancies are announced under advertisement 07/2026?

There are a total of 22 vacancies for the Assistant Sub-Inspector (Technical) post.

What is the salary scale for Bihar Police ASI Technical?

The post carries a Level-5 pay matrix scale.

What is the required qualification for Bihar Police ASI Technical?

Candidates need a 3-year Polytechnic Diploma in Electronics & Communication, Electronics & Tele Communication, Information Technology, or Electrical & Electronics engineering with at least 50% marks (45% for SC/ST candidates).

What is the age criteria for applying?

As on 1 August 2025, the minimum age is 21 years. The maximum age is 37 years for General men, 40 years for General women/BC/EBC, and 42 years for SC/ST candidates.

What is the application fee for this BPSSC vacancy?

The online application fee is Rs. 100 for all categories, including men, women, and third-gender candidates.

Is there negative marking in the written examination?

Yes, there is a negative marking of 0.2 marks for every incorrect answer in both preliminary and main examination phases.

What are the selection stages for the ASI Technical position?

The selection flow includes a Preliminary Written Exam, Main Written Exam, Physical Efficiency Test (PET/PST), medical exam, and character verification.

Can candidates from states other than Bihar claim reservation benefits?

No, reservation benefits including age relaxation and horizontal female reservation are restricted to original residents of Bihar state only. Other state candidates are treated under the Unreserved category.

Where can candidates register and apply?

Eligible applicants can complete registration and apply on the official portal at https://bpssc.bihar.gov.in under the Bihar Police tab.
