Recruitment Snapshot

RPSC APO Recruitment 2026 Overview

  • RPSC has announced a total of 371 temporary vacancies for the post of Assistant Prosecution Officer (APO).
  • The vacancy distribution includes 355 posts for Non-Scheduled Areas and 16 posts for Scheduled Areas (including 15 Scheduled Area general posts and 1 post for Sahariya primitive caste of Baran district).
  • A substantial number of seats are dedicated to the 2024 recruitment backlog, which are carried forward for the first time.
  • Only online submissions processed through the integrated Rajasthan Single Sign-On (SSO) recruitment portal will be accepted.
  • The recruitment features a dual-stage evaluation process consisting of an Objective Preliminary Examination followed by a Written Main Examination.

Selection Process

  1. 1

    Preliminary Examination: Screening test composed entirely of objective multiple-choice questions. Marks obtained here will serve solely to shortlist candidates for the Main phase and will not influence the final selection merit list.

  2. 2

    Main Written Examination: Descriptive written test evaluating law proficiency and language control. Shortlisted candidate counts for the Mains will be limited to 15 times the total vacancies per category class.

  3. 3

    Document Verification & Recommendations: Verification of formal education records, age sheets, category proofs, character documents, and medical check certifications before sending final recommendations to the Appointing Authority.

Critical Multiple-Choice OMR Instructions (Prelims)

  • Each question features 5 response options (1, 2, 3, 4, 5). A blue ballpoint pen must be used to darken the circles.
  • It is mandatory to shade exactly one option. If you do not intend to attempt a question, you must darken the 5th option circle.
  • If a question is left unattempted and none of the 5 circles are shaded, one-third (1/3) marks of that question will be deducted.
  • An extra 10 minutes will be given to candidates post-exam specifically to verify unshaded fields. Any candidate leaving more than 10% of total questions completely unshaded will face automatic disqualification.
