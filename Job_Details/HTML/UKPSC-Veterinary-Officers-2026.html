About This Recruitment

The Uttarakhand Public Service Commission (UKPSC) has published an official notification for the recruitment of Veterinary Officers under Advertisement No. A-2/DR(VO)/S-1/2026. This recruitment drive aims to fill 25 vacancies in the Veterinary department of the state.

Interested and eligible candidates who possess a degree in Veterinary Science can submit their online application through the official UKPSC portal. The online application window remains open from 25 August 2026 to 14 September 2026. Applicants should ensure they meet the age limit, calculated as on 01 July 2026, and fulfill the necessary educational and registration criteria.

The selection process details and specific guidelines are outlined in the official notification. Candidates are strongly advised to download the official PDF, thoroughly review the requirements, and confirm the direct application link from the official notice board before submitting their forms.

Eligibility and Qualification

  • Educational Qualification: Candidates must hold a Bachelor of Veterinary Science & Animal Husbandry (B.V.Sc. & A.H.) from a recognized institution/university.
  • Registration Requirement: The candidate must be duly registered with the Uttarakhand Veterinary Council.

How to Apply

  1. Visit the official website of UKPSC at psc.uk.gov.in.
  2. Click on the "UKPSC Veterinary Officers 2026 Apply Online" link provided in the announcements section.
  3. Register yourself using a valid email ID and mobile number to obtain your login credentials.
  4. Login using your newly generated registration ID and password.
  5. Fill the application form carefully with all required personal, educational, and category details.
  6. Upload the required scanned documents, including a recent passport-sized photograph and signature in the prescribed format.
  7. Pay the application fee via the online payment gateway based on your respective category.
  8. Verify all the information and submit the final form. Make sure to download and print the confirmation page for future reference.

What is the age limit for UKPSC Veterinary Officers 2026?

As on 01 July 2026, the minimum age required is 21 years and maximum age is 42 years. Age relaxation applies for reserved categories as per government rules.
