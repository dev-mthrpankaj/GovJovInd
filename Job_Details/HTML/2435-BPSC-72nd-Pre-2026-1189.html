Recruitment Snapshot

BPSC 72nd CCE 2026 Key Highlights

  • The vacancy list aggregates a grand total of 1230 posts distributed across two primary schedules: 1077 vacancies under General CCE Services (Table 1) and 153 vacancies under Departmental Specialized Services (Table 2).
  • A mandatory 35% horizontal reservation is applicable for permanent resident female candidates of Bihar across corresponding service grades.
  • Applications are explicitly restricted through the BPSC Online Portal via a mandatory One Time Registration (OTR) mechanism.
  • The selection methodology encompasses an objective Preliminary Test (150 Marks) with structural negative marking, descriptive Mains Written Examination (900 Marks), and a final Personality Test/Interview (120 Marks).

Structural Examination Pattern

1. Integrated Preliminary Examination (Objective Nature)

  • Total Marks: 150 Marks | Duration: 2 Hours.
  • Question Format: Multiple Choice Questions (MCQ) containing 5 options (A, B, C, D, E) per query block.
  • Mandatory Non-Attempt Rule: If an applicant does not want to answer a question, choosing Option (E) is mandatory. Leaving all 5 options completely blank will trigger automatic penalty counting.
  • Negative Marking: 1/3rd (one-third) penalty deduction applies for every wrong entry or blank unanswered item.
  • Syllabus Scope: General Science, National/International Current Events, History of India & Bihar, Geography, Rivers system, Indian Polity, Post-Independence Economy of Bihar, National Movement contribution, and General Mental Ability.

Step-by-Step Online Application Process

  1. 1

    One-Time Registration (OTR): Open the BPSC online site gateway at https://bpsconline.bihar.gov.in and click on the "New User Registration" action button.

  2. 2

    Profile Configuration Slabs: Authenticate verification indexes using active mobile numbers and emails to retrieve password configurations via system OTPs.

  3. 3

    DigiLocker Identity Integration: Sync your official certification records inside the profile space using the central MeriPehchan portal interface or continue direct form uploads manually in PDF format.

  4. 4

    Multi-Tier Data Insertion: Complete Personal Info, Address Info, criminal records check disclosure entries, structural Qualification tables, and professional background forms sequentially.

  5. 5

    Live Image Capture: Position your desktop/laptop webcam tracking space against a clear white/light background with standard lighting parameters to save a crisp passport photograph.

  6. 6

    Payment Processing & Locking: Choose preferred post selections (CCE, CDPO, FAO) from the "New Advertisement" tab, process your portal payments online, and lock the final application profile before the last date.

Important Candidate Instructions

  • Only Indian Nationals are eligible to apply. Reservation benefits are strictly restricted to applicants holding a valid permanent domicile of Bihar.
  • Married female candidates seeking reservation relaxation certificates must ensure that their Caste, Domicile, and Non-Creamy Layer (NCL) documents are issued explicitly under their father's permanent name and address, rather than their husband's address.
  • All document attachments, qualification certificates, and caste credentials must be issued on or before the reference closing date (31-05-2026).
  • Keep four copies of your uploaded passport photograph secure for administrative document validation at later stages.
