Complete context

About This Recruitment

Container Corporation of India Limited (CONCOR), a Navratna Central Public Sector Enterprise under the Ministry of Railways, Government of India, has released Employment Notification No. 05/2026 for recruitment to Management Trainee and Assistant Officer posts.

The recruitment covers 77 vacancies in Commercial & Operations, Accounts, Accounts/Compliance, Technical, MIS, Civil and Personnel & Administration disciplines. Of the advertised vacancies, 45 are under the Management Trainee cadre and 32 are for Assistant Officer posts.

Online registration started on 31 August 2026 at 10:00 AM and is scheduled to close on 30 September 2026 at 11:55 PM. Candidates should check the post-specific educational qualification, experience requirement and age criteria before submitting the application.

Management Trainee posts carry a pay scale of Rs.50,000-3%-1,60,000, while Assistant Officer posts carry a pay scale of Rs.40,000-3%-1,36,000.
