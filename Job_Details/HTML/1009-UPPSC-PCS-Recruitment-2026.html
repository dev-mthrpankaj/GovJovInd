About This Recruitment

UPPSC PCS Recruitment 2026 is a state-level recruitment process conducted by the Uttar Pradesh Public Service Commission for Combined State / Upper Subordinate Services. The examination is useful for candidates targeting administrative, revenue, police, development, education, labour, food, supply, welfare, taxation and other important state service posts.

The notification mentions that the application is OTR based. Candidates who do not have an OTR number must complete One Time Registration on the Commission’s OTR portal before applying. The online application is accepted only through the UPPSC website, and candidates should preserve records of OTR, fee payment, final submission and correction/modification for future reference.

The recruitment includes a preliminary examination for screening, a main written examination and interview for eligible posts. Some posts may follow written-exam-based recruitment according to their service rules. Because the advertisement includes several posts with different duties and some specific qualifications or physical standards, candidates should carefully match their eligibility with the official notice before submitting the form.

Eligibility and Qualification

  • Candidates must possess a Bachelor’s degree from any recognised university up to the last date for receipt of application.
  • Specific posts such as Principal Government Intermediate College, Senior Lecturer DIET, Labour posts, Assistant Controller Legal Metrology, Officer on Special Duty (Computer), Assistant Prosecution Officer (Transport), District Programme Officer and Child Development Project Officer have additional or specific qualification requirements.
  • Candidates appearing in the examination of the essential qualification are not eligible according to the important instructions.
  • Reservation, category and sub-category benefits are available only as per Uttar Pradesh government rules and official certificate conditions.
  • Candidates must ensure that all claims made in the online form can be supported by valid documents when asked by the Commission.

Selection Process

  1. Online application through UPPSC OTR based system.
  2. Preliminary Examination at various centres in Uttar Pradesh.
  3. Main Written Examination for candidates declared successful in preliminary examination.
  4. Interview / viva-voce for candidates declared successful on the basis of main written examination, wherever applicable.
  5. Document verification of original certificates and claims made in application.
  6. Medical examination and other post-wise checks as required under service rules.
  7. Final selection as per marks, post preferences, applicable service rules and Commission’s decision.

Exam Pattern

The preliminary examination consists of two compulsory objective type papers on OMR sheets. Paper-II is qualifying in nature with minimum qualifying marks of 33%. Merit for preliminary examination is determined on the basis of marks obtained in Paper-I.

  • Negative marking applies in preliminary objective papers: one-third of the marks assigned to the question will be deducted for a wrong answer.
  • Candidates must appear in both preliminary papers for evaluation.
  • Main Examination is conventional written type. Detailed syllabus and paper structure should be checked from the official notification.

How to Apply

  1. Visit the UPPSC official website and open the relevant notification / advertisement page.
  2. Complete One Time Registration at otr.pariksha.nic.in if you do not already have an OTR number.
  3. Use “Authenticate with OTR” for the PCS 2026 advertisement and verify through OTP or OTR password.
  4. Fill the required post qualification details and submit the application part as instructed.
  5. Pay the examination fee online through the available SBI MOPS payment modes.
  6. After payment, complete final submission and print the application form and payment slip.
  7. Use correction/modification facility only within the notified correction period if a permitted correction is needed.
  8. Keep soft and hard copies safely for future stages and produce documents whenever UPPSC asks.

Frequently Asked Questions

What is UPPSC PCS Recruitment 2026?
