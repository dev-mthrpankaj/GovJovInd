About This Recruitment

MPPSC ADPO Exam is meant for law-qualified candidates seeking prosecution-related government service in Madhya Pradesh. The post is connected with public prosecution work, legal records and court-related departmental responsibilities.

The application process is online through MPPSC. Candidates should read the advertisement carefully because the scanned copy contains legal eligibility, fee, correction and examination instructions that must be followed exactly.

Applicants should focus on law eligibility, age conditions, document readiness and examination preparation together. The official notice also mentions correction charges and exam schedule, so candidates should avoid last-minute form filling.
