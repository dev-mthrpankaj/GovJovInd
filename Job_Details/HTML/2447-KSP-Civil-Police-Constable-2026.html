About This Recruitment

KSP Civil Police Constable Recruitment 2026 is a state police recruitment notification for filling Civil Police Constable vacancies in Karnataka State Police. The notification includes posts for men, women, transgender candidates and local cadre categories, so candidates should carefully check the vacancy table and category rules before filling the form.

This recruitment is useful for candidates who want to join the police department at constable level and are ready for both written examination and physical testing. The written test is mentioned as an Offline OMR type examination, while physical stages include Physical Standard Test and Endurance Test. Candidates should prepare for exam subjects as well as running, jump and other physical requirements together.

The official notification is in Kannada and contains detailed rules related to qualification, age, category relaxation, local cadre, fee, physical standards, endurance events, identity proof and application instructions. Applicants should read the full notification before applying because police recruitment involves strict document verification, physical standards and eligibility conditions.

Eligibility and Qualification

  • Candidate should have passed PUC or an equivalent qualification as mentioned in the official notification.
  • Equivalent qualification rules are explained in the notification. Candidates with non-standard or special qualifications should verify eligibility from the official notice.
  • Kannada language-related conditions may apply as per the notification and government rules.
  • Candidates must satisfy age, category, physical standard, medical fitness and document requirements.

Selection Process

  1. Online application submission through the official recruitment portal.
  2. Offline OMR type written examination.
  3. Kannada language test, if applicable as per notification rules.
  4. Physical Standard Test for shortlisted candidates.
  5. Endurance Test for eligible candidates.
  6. Document verification and identity verification.
  7. Medical examination and other checks as per Karnataka Police recruitment rules.
  8. Final selection will be prepared according to official notification rules.

Exam Pattern

The written examination is mentioned as an Offline OMR type paper. The paper contains 100 questions for 100 marks and the duration is 1 hour 30 minutes. The notification mentions negative valuation of 0.25 marks for each wrong answer.
