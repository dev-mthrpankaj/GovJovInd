About This Recruitment

The Karnataka State Police (KSP) recruitment notification has been published to fill vacancies in the cadre of Police Constable (Civil) under the local reservation structure (Kalyan Karnataka region under Article 371J). To make the process highly smooth and secure, the department has designated the Karnataka Examination Authority (KEA) to manage the competitive written test stage. Candidates who look forward to establishing a career in law enforcement within Karnataka can utilize this opportunity to join the state policing infrastructure.

The application submission method is exclusively online through the authorized portal. Candidates need to verify their eligibility conditions such as age boundaries, native parameters, and academic documents before final submission. It is extremely important to review the parameters since preferences or data inputs cannot be changed or rectified once the application period ends. Aspirants must make certain they fulfill all requirements on or before the designated final registration date.

The selection flow comprises a comprehensive multi-stage screening. Initial filtering relies on the objective competitive test, from which high-scoring applicants are selected in a 1:5 ratio for physical evaluation parameters. Since police duties necessitate sound health and physical readiness, final verification is subject to explicit clinical, medical, and character evaluation checks. Applicants should focus evenly on structural knowledge preparation and daily physical conditioning routines to comfortably qualify through all consecutive stages.

Eligibility and Qualification

  • Academic Standard: Must have completed PUC (Class 12) or equivalent qualification from a recognized board.
  • Recognized Equivalents include: CBSE and ISC Class 12 exams, Class 12 exams from other state boards, and Senior Secondary courses from NIOS.
  • ITI Candidates: 2-year ITI holders must pass one language and one academic subject through NIOS or Pre-University Board to count as PUC equivalent.
  • Diploma Holders: 3-year structural educational Diploma programs recognized by Technical Education department are considered equivalent. Candidates who completed it before 2015 must clear the Kannada language test along with departmental tests.
  • JOC Qualifications: Job Oriented Course (JOC) is treated as equivalent only under explicit conditions such as compassionate grounds or exams determined purely via competitive testing scores.
  • Language Exemption Rule: Candidates who cleared SSLC with Kannada as 1st or 2nd language, or studied in Kannada medium, or cleared prior official recruitment Kannada tests are exempted from the mandatory Kannada test. Others must pass a 150-marks test with at least 50 marks.
  • Result Awaiting Status: Candidates currently waiting for final results or appearing for qualifying exams as on the closing date are strictly ineligible.

Selection Process

  1. Written Competitive Exam: Objective OMR screening managed fully by KEA to assess merit.
  2. Compulsory Kannada Language Test: Applicable only to candidates who do not possess language exemption documents (150 Marks test with 50 marks qualifying bar).
  3. Ratio Selection: Shortlisting for physical stages is processed in a 1:5 ratio matching category trends based on written scores.
  4. Physical Endurance Test (ET) & Physical Standard Test (PST): Qualifying in nature. Passing all parameters sequentially is required.
  5. Roster Shortlist: Development of a 1:2 ratio shortlist based on exam marks for medical tests.
  6. Document Verification: Reviewing original educational, native, 371J, and community certificates.
  7. Medical Examination: Final clinical vetting by an official medical board before issuing appointments.

Exam Pattern

The written evaluation is managed offline via an OMR sheet format. The examination duration is 90 minutes (1 Hour 30 Minutes). It consists of 100 questions totaling 100 marks. A negative scoring penalty of 0.25 marks is applicable for every incorrect choice. A minimum qualifying mark of 30 marks is mandatory to become eligible for further physical evaluation shortlists.
