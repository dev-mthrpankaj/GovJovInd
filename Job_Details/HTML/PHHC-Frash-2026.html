About This Recruitment

Punjab and Haryana High Court Frash Recruitment 2026 is a court establishment recruitment drive for 31 Frash posts at Chandigarh. The online application window is open from 28 August 2026 to 18 September 2026.

This role is suitable for candidates looking for a support-staff opportunity in the court system. The key eligibility mentioned on this page is knowledge of Hindi and the regional language, while category-wise vacancies and fee details should be checked carefully before applying.

Candidates should keep photo, signature, identity proof, category documents, and any required domicile documents ready before starting the online form.
