About This Recruitment

This notice is related to outsourcing driver vacancies in the Jhansi region of Uttar Pradesh State Road Transport Corporation. The office notice explains that earlier outsourcing driver strength changed because some drivers left work or joined other fields, and therefore 99 vacant driver posts are proposed to be filled.

The recruitment is connected with service provider and Sewayojan portal based process. Candidates interested in driver work under the transport corporation system should read the official advertisement carefully because the post is outsourcing based and the payment conditions are different from regular government salary posts.

The notice mentions category-wise vacancy details, minimum educational requirement, merit-based selection through Sewayojan portal list, one week internal training and kilometre-based payment conditions. Candidates should check the latest official advertisement and portal listing before applying.

Eligibility and Qualification

  • Candidate should have High School certificate issued by an institution recognized by the State Government or Central Government.
  • Candidate must satisfy the driver-related eligibility and document requirements mentioned in the official advertisement.
  • Category claim details should be filled correctly on the portal as per the advertisement.
  • Detailed driving licence, experience and other driver-specific requirements should be checked from the official notification / Sewayojan listing.
  • Candidates should keep all original documents ready for verification if shortlisted.

Selection Process

  1. Vacancy advertisement publication in local daily newspapers and listing on the Sewayojan portal.
  2. Candidates apply or register as per the Sewayojan portal / official advertisement process.
  3. Merit list is prepared from the portal list based on intermediate marks / qualifications as mentioned in the notice.
  4. Selection is made from the list received through Sewayojan portal according to merit.
  5. Selected candidates are given one week internal training.
  6. Final engagement and payment are governed by outsourcing and corporation rules.

Exam Pattern

The provided notice does not mention a written examination pattern. Candidates should check the official advertisement and Sewayojan portal listing for any driving test, document verification, medical or skill-related requirement.

Salary and Benefits

  • Payment mentioned in notice: Rs. 1.75 per kilometre.
  • Monthly incentive: Rs. 3000 if 22 duty days, 5000 km and 51% load factor conditions are achieved.
  • Overnight rest allowance: Rs. 68 per night in A-category cities.
  • Overnight rest allowance: Rs. 61 per night in B-category cities.
  • Other rules and conditions will remain under Uttar Pradesh Transport Corporation Headquarters, Lucknow directions issued from time to time.
  • The notice mentions payment on kilometre basis for selected outsourcing drivers.

Documents Required

  • High School certificate and marksheet.
  • Intermediate marksheet, if used for merit as per official process.
  • Valid driving licence and driver-related documents as required in official advertisement.
  • Category certificate for EWS / OBC / SC / ST if applicable.
  • Photo identity proof such as Aadhaar Card, Voter ID or other valid ID.
  • Recent passport size photograph.

  • Any other document mentioned in the official advertisement or portal listing.
